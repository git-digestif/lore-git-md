This is the fourth and final patch in Ævar Arnfjörð Bjarmason’s RFC series refining Git’s `.clang-format` configuration to reduce the delta between its output and the project’s current manual style. The patch disables indentation of `goto` labels, aligning `clang-format` with Git’s convention of flushing them to the left margin. The change is small—three lines added to `.clang-format`—but it trims the formatting diff by roughly 3 lines across 510 files, bringing the total reduction from the full series to about 60 % (from ~32 k to ~12,742 lines of diff).

The patch is narrowly scoped: it targets a single, uncontroversial style rule (unindented `goto` labels) and does not introduce new tooling or workflow changes. It continues the series’ incremental, data-driven approach, using the `style-all-diff-*` Makefile targets (introduced in earlier patches) to measure impact. The commit message is factual and concise, reporting the before-and-after diffstat without editorializing about the broader formatting debate.

This patch does not address the larger questions still open in the thread—whether the remaining ~12,742 lines of deviation are acceptable, how enforcement would work, or how to reconcile `clang-format`’s unavailability on NonStop with Randall S. Becker’s pluggable-formatter counterproposal. Instead, it provides another concrete step toward making `clang-format` output more closely match Git’s style, giving the community a clearer picture of what automation would entail. The series as a whole remains a pragmatic artifact for assessing feasibility, not a declaration of intent to adopt `clang-format`.
