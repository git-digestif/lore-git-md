This is a reply in the ongoing design discussion about adopting an automatic code formatter for Git. Randall S. Becker pushes back against brian m. carlson’s suggestion that NonStop developers use a VM or separate system to run `clang-format`, reiterating that GCC (and by extension, `clang`) will *never* be supported on NonStop platforms due to undisclosed legal or technical constraints expected to persist for at least five years. He argues that the formatting problem is not Git-specific but a general need across many projects and languages, and that Git should instead build a *pluggable*, language-agnostic formatting infrastructure directly into its own tooling—one that could delegate to `clang-format` where available but would not depend on it. This reply does not introduce new technical details but sharpens the platform-compatibility objection and broadens the scope of the proposed solution beyond Git’s immediate contributor workflow.
