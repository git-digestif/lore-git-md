This is a minor follow-up in the ongoing `clang-format` adoption discussion. Ævar Arnfjörð Bjarmason clarifies a small discrepancy in the commit message of his fourth patch, which disables indentation of `goto` labels to match Git’s manual style. Junio C Hamano had questioned why the patch claimed to reduce both the number of changed files *and* the total line changes, which seemed counterintuitive. Ævar explains that the diffstat is accurate but reflects the unpredictable nature of formatting changes—tweaking `.clang-format` rules can sometimes reduce the number of affected files while still trimming the total line count, or vice versa, depending on how the tool reflows code.

The exchange is purely mechanical and does not advance the broader debate about whether to adopt `clang-format` or how to handle its remaining incompatibilities with Git’s style. The patch itself remains uncontroversial, and the series continues to provide concrete data on how closely `clang-format` can be made to match Git’s conventions. No new objections or proposals emerge here.
