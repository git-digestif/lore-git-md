Subject: [PATCH v3] advice: add scope hint for disabling advice messages
Series version: v4 (three patches)
Originating author: Vsevolod Myalitsin
Thread type: patch series

Core problem or goal
Add a scope hint to Git’s advice settings so that when users are told how to disable an advice message (e.g., `git config advice.detachedHead false`), the command includes the correct config scope (`--global`, `--local`, or `--system`). The series has converged on a design debate between two simplifications: (1) a boolean `is_global_hint` to distinguish `--global` from `--local` for individual settings, or (2) a uniform `--global` hint for all settings, eliminating per-setting scope tracking entirely.

Current status
v4 is posted as a three-patch series: (1) refactor `vadvise()` to accept the full `advice_setting` struct, (2) introduce the scope-hint mechanism with the boolean `is_global_hint`, and (3) assign `is_global_hint = true` to the `defaultBranchName` advice. Junio’s mechanical feedback from v3 (commit-message motivation, `vadvise()` documentation, indentation) is addressed, and SZEDER Gábor’s syntax error (unmatched opening brace) is resolved by the boolean simplification. The series remains under review with no evidence of Junio picking it up for integration. The central unresolved question is whether to adopt the boolean `is_global_hint` mechanism or Junio’s proposal to always recommend `--global` in the hint, eliminating the `is_global_hint` field entirely. Jeff King has now explicitly endorsed Junio’s uniform `--global` approach, arguing that *all* advice settings should default to `--global` because advice messages are about silencing chatter for a *user* rather than a specific repository, making `--worktree` or `--system` unlikely to be useful in practice. Junio’s latest reply clarifies that the original 2009 design of the `advice.*` config system never intended the scope-less hint to be a literal cut-and-paste command; users were expected to adapt the command to their own needs. This stance implies that the absence of a scope hint was intentional, and that adding scope hints now is a deliberate enhancement rather than a bugfix. The thread now appears to be converging on the uniform `--global` approach, though Vsevolod has not yet responded to confirm whether he will revise the series accordingly.

Key technical details
- Files touched: `advice.c`, `Documentation/git-config.txt` (tangential)
- New field: `is_global_hint` (boolean) in the `advice_setting` struct, proposed in v4 but now under debate; may be eliminated in favor of a uniform `--global` hint
- Enum: removal of `CONFIG_SCOPE` reuse; `is_global_hint` directly indicates whether the scope should be `--global` (true) or `--local` (false)
- Translatable string: rewritten to avoid string interpolation; no longer uses `"git config %s advice.%s false"`
- Symbols: `advice_setting`, `is_global_hint` (proposed), `advice_config[]`, `vadvise()` interface (now takes `const struct advice_setting *`), `ADVICE_DEFAULT_BRANCH_NAME` with `is_global_hint` set to true (proposed)
- Helper function: `config_scope_name()` (existing) is no longer needed for scope flag construction under either simplification
- Refactoring: `vadvise()` now accepts the entire `advice_setting` struct; call sites in `advise()` and `advise_if_enabled()` updated accordingly
- Build-failure resolutions: (1) the `switch` statement in `vadvise()` is eliminated by the boolean simplification, removing the `DEVELOPER=YesPlease` compilation error; (2) the unmatched opening brace in patch 1 is resolved by the structural change
- Tangential documentation: Junio’s patch adds a paragraph to `Documentation/git-config.txt` clarifying that `<file-option>` refers to `--system`, `--global`, `--local`, `--worktree`, or `--file <filename>`; Jeff King proposed a more concise alternative that groups these options under the same term and notes `--blob` as a special case
