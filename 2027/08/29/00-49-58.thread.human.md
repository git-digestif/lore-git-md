This thread is about adding a scope hint to Git’s advice settings so users can see the correct config scope (like `--global` or `--local`) when disabling an advice message. Vsevolod Myalitsin’s v4 patch series proposes a simplified design using a boolean `is_global_hint` to indicate whether the scope should be `--global` (true) or `--local` (false), addressing earlier build failures and focusing on the immediate goal of accurate scope hints for `--global` settings like `defaultBranchName`.

The series has seen active review, with Jeff King (Peff) twice endorsing the boolean simplification, arguing that advice messages are about silencing chatter for a *user* rather than a specific repository, making `--global` the most sensible default. Junio, however, has now raised a nuanced counterpoint: while `--global` is often appropriate, some users work across multiple projects with different workflows and might prefer to squelch advice *per project* (supporting `--local` or even `--worktree`). This leaves the design question open—whether to proceed with the boolean simplification or leave room for future expansion. The series remains under review, with no evidence of Junio picking it up for integration yet. SZEDER Gábor’s earlier review identified a syntax error that the boolean simplification resolves, and tangential documentation patches from Junio and Peff may be resolved separately. The key open question is whether Junio will accept the boolean design or prefer a more flexible approach.
