This thread is about a three-patch series from Vsevolod Myalitsin that adds a `scope_hint` field to Git’s advice settings. The goal is to make advice messages more accurate by telling users the correct config scope (`--global`, `--local`, etc.) when they’re told how to disable an advice message. For example, the `defaultBranchName` advice now suggests `--global` because it only appears during `git init`, making a local setting ineffective.

The series is now at v4, with Junio’s mechanical feedback from v3 fully addressed. Jeff King (Peff) reviewed v3, raised some technical questions about translation and enum reuse, but has since accepted the approach. The final patch in the series applies the mechanism to `defaultBranchName`, ensuring the advice message suggests the correct scope. No substantive objections remain, but Junio hasn’t yet reviewed v4, so the series is still under review.

The changes are confined to `advice.c` and are minimal, touching only the `advice_setting` struct and related logic. The series doesn’t change any user-visible behavior beyond making the advice messages more accurate. The only open question—whether other advice settings should default to `--global` or `--local`—has been left for future work or maintainer discretion. For now, the series is ready for Junio’s review.
