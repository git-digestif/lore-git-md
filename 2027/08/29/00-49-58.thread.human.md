This thread is about adding a scope hint to Git’s advice messages—telling users whether to use `--global`, `--local`, or another scope when disabling an advice with `git config`. The series is now at v4, posted by Vsevolod Myalitsin as three patches. Early versions debated how to track the correct scope for each advice setting, but the discussion has converged on a simpler approach: always recommending `--global` in the hint, since advice settings are about silencing chatter for a *user* rather than a specific repository. Junio and Jeff King both now endorse this uniform `--global` approach, which eliminates the need for per-setting scope tracking (like the `is_global_hint` boolean in v4) and resolves earlier build failures.

The series touches `advice.c` and refactors `vadvise()` to accept the full `advice_setting` struct. The only remaining advice setting explicitly marked for `--global` in v4 is `defaultBranchName`, but under the new consensus, all advice hints would default to `--global`. Junio’s latest reply formalizes this direction, and the thread now awaits Vsevolod’s response to confirm whether he’ll revise the series accordingly. No major objections remain, and the documentation tweaks around `<file-option>` in `git-config.txt` (posted by Junio and Peff) are tangential but may be resolved in parallel. The next version will likely drop the `is_global_hint` field entirely.
