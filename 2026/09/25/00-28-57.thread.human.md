This thread is about a regression in `git shortlog` (and related revision commands) where unknown options were incorrectly reported as `(null)` instead of their actual name (e.g., `--not-an-option`). The bug was introduced by a recent memory management change in revision option parsing and affects all current integration branches.

Jeff King (Peff) proposed a two-patch series to fix the issue, which also addressed two latent bugs: misreporting known-but-invalid options (e.g., `--max-count=foo`) as unknown, and incorrectly labeling known options with invalid arguments (e.g., `--default` without a value) as "unknown option" instead of showing the earlier error message. Junio C Hamano reviewed and tested the series, confirming the fixes restore the correct behavior, and the patches are now ready for inclusion in `next` and eventual graduation to `master`.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk, who originally reported the bug, acknowledged the fix in a recent email. While his feedback was surface-level—requesting a `.mailmap` update for his canonical email and reflecting on the test case’s clarity—it did not challenge the patch’s correctness or suggest changes. The series remains unchanged and under maintainer approval, with no open technical questions or objections. The only loose end is Kristoffer’s `.mailmap` request, which is a minor contributor detail and does not affect the patch’s integration status.
