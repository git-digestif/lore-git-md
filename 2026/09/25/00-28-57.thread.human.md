This thread is about a regression in `git shortlog` (and other revision commands) where unknown options were incorrectly reported as `(null)` instead of the actual option name (e.g., `--not-an-option`). The bug was introduced by a recent commit and affects all current integration branches. Kristoffer Haugsbakk reported the issue and identified the root cause, while Jeff King (Peff) proposed a two-patch series to fix it.

Peff’s patches address not only the `(null)` regression but also two latent bugs: misreporting known-but-invalid options (e.g., `--max-count=foo`) as unknown, and misreporting known options with invalid arguments (e.g., `--default`) as "unknown option" instead of showing the correct error message. The fixes are minimal and well-justified, with new test cases added to prevent regressions. Junio C Hamano has reviewed and tested the patches, confirming they restore the correct behavior, and his endorsement signals the series is ready for integration. No objections or open questions remain, and the patches are expected to merge soon.
