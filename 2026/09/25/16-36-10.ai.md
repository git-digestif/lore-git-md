This is a substantive review comment on the second patch of Julia Evans' seven-patch series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`. D. Ben Knoble raises two specific concerns about content removed from `git-merge.adoc` that he believes should be preserved or relocated to the new guide.

The first concern is the loss of an explanatory paragraph about what a merge conflict *is* -- specifically, the distinction between non-overlapping changes (incorporated automatically) and overlapping changes (requiring manual resolution). Knoble suggests this foundational explanation, while possibly basic, helps users understand *why* conflicts occur and could be valuable in the new guide.

The second concern is the removal of a tip about using `git log --merge -p <path>` to inspect diffs from each branch during conflict resolution. Knoble highlights this as a practical, discoverable tool for resolving conflicts and suggests the new guide should include it (or a similar example) to help users gain context about the changes on each side of the merge.

Knoble’s feedback is technically grounded and actionable: he is not objecting to the patch’s structure or the cross-reference itself, but rather pointing out omissions in the new guide that could weaken its utility. His comment carries weight because it comes from a reviewer who has already engaged substantively with the series’ goals and design. The thread context shows that the series is otherwise well-received, with mechanical integration issues (build system updates, `command-list.txt` registration) being the main blockers. This review adds a content-level refinement to the discussion.
