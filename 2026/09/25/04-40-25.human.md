This is a maintainer follow-up in the ongoing discussion about a bugfix patch for `git fetch-pack`. Junio C Hamano clarifies his earlier objection to Nathan Froyd’s patch, which proposes marking exact OIDs in refs to align `fetch-pack`’s behavior with `git fetch`. Junio’s concern is not about the patch’s correctness but about its design philosophy: he argues that `fetch-pack` should remain a low-level command that passes ref arguments *exactly* as given to the remote via `want-ref` packets, without applying DWIM (Do What I Mean) heuristics.

The clarification reinforces Junio’s position that `fetch-pack` should not implicitly parse or interpret OIDs, preserving its role as a plumbing command. This stance suggests the patch may face resistance unless it introduces a more explicit mechanism for users to specify OIDs, rather than relying on implicit behavior. The discussion remains open, with the key question being whether `fetch-pack` should prioritize consistency with `git fetch` or maintain its strict, low-level interface. For now, the patch is under review, and no revised version has been posted.
