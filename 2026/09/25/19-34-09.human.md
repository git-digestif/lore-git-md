This is a documentation patch thread, part of Julia Evans' seven-patch series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page consolidating merge conflict resolution guidance. The series aims to replace scattered, duplicated advice across existing man pages with a single, comprehensive guide.

Julia had initially removed this tip because the documentation was unclear, but after investigating, she proposed a user-friendly phrasing: “`git log --merge -p <filename>` will print out all commits which caused the merge conflict for `<filename>`, and the diff of how they changed the file.” Knoble’s approval (“That reads well enough for me!”) signals that this wording is likely to be incorporated in the next revision.

The exchange is constructive and advances the series toward a more complete and practical guide.
