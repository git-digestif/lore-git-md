The review focuses on the **documentation** in `config/remote.adoc` and `fetch-options.adoc`. The proposed wording is included in the review and is **technically accurate**—it aligns with how `--refmap` already works and avoids implying that the configuration is inactive in edge cases.

The code change itself (`builtin/fetch.c`) is **approved as-is** with no requested changes. Junio notes the use of a file-scope static variable (`refmap`) but defers any refactoring to a future effort, calling it "clearly outside the scope of this topic." The review is **not tested** (no mention of building or running the code), but it is **substantive** in its engagement with the design intent and documentation clarity.
