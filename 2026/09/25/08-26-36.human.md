This is a bugfix for the recent regression in `git shortlog` (and other revision commands) where unknown options are reported as `(null)` instead of their actual name. Jeff King (Peff) replies to Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s report with two proposed fixes, acknowledging the bug as his own fault from commit cd439487.

Peff’s first suggestion is a minimal fix: change the error message in `parse_revision_opt()` to use `ctx->out[ctx->cpidx - 1]` instead of `ctx->argv[0]`, ensuring the original option string is still available when the error is formatted. His second, more involved approach modifies `overwrite_argv()` to conditionally null the `value` pointer only when `opt->free_removed_argv_elements` is set, restoring pre-regression behavior for cases where the original `argv` entry was expected to remain valid.

The email is brief but clear: Peff flags the bug as his responsibility, provides actionable patches, and promises a fuller explanation later.
