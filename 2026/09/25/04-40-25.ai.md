This is a maintainer follow-up in the ongoing `fetch-pack` exact-OID thread. Junio C Hamano clarifies his earlier design objection: the `<ref>` arguments in `git fetch-pack <repository> <ref>...` are intended to be passed verbatim to the remote via the `want-ref` packet, without any DWIM parsing. His concern is not just about heuristics in general but about preserving the ability to send `want-ref` requests for arbitrary ref names, including those that happen to look like OIDs. The clarification does not soften his position; it sharpens it by explaining why the current patch’s approach would break existing use cases that rely on exact ref-name transmission. No new technical details or alternatives are proposed here—this is purely a restatement of the objection in more precise terms. The thread remains open, with the fundamental design question still unresolved.
