This is the third patch in Kaartic Sivaraam’s RFC series improving error reporting for invalid Git repository directories when `--git-dir` is explicitly provided. The email is a reply to Junio C Hamano’s surface-level review of the patch, addressing two mechanical issues: the dead `strbuf_release()` call after `die()` and the unconventional manual assembly of the error message.

Kaartic acknowledges the dead code bug, noting that GCC (unlike Clang) no longer warns about unreachable code after `NORETURN` functions. On the error message assembly, he explains his intent was to reduce translator workload by avoiding re-translation of the "not a git repository" string, but concedes the lego-style construction is unconventional and agrees to adopt the project’s standard single-`die()` call with format arguments. The response is brief and focused on the mechanical feedback, leaving the series’ substantive direction unchanged. No new technical concerns or edge cases are introduced; the patch remains in the RFC phase, awaiting further discussion on style consistency and potential expansion to other repository-discovery scenarios.
