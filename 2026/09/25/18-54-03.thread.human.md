This thread is about a two-patch series from Harald Nordgren that improves GitHub Actions CI failure reporting by making leak sanitizer and regular test failures more visible and actionable. The series is now at v2, with the first patch focusing on leak sanitizer improvements (stopping at the first failure and annotating leaks with collapsible output) and the second adding precise file-and-line annotations for regular test failures.

Key participants include Harald Nordgren (author), Phillip Wood (who raised concerns about output truncation and scrolling behavior, now addressed), Ben Knoble (who suggested readability improvements), and Junio C Hamano (maintainer). Junio’s latest review highlights two technical issues in the second patch: potential portability problems with `sed` handling of `\r` and an inconsistency in output redirection style. These are targeted but important nits that need fixing before the series can move forward.

The series is under active review, with most feedback addressed in v2. The remaining open questions are the portability and consistency issues Junio raised, along with lingering requests for better justification of some helper functions. The core goal—making CI failures easier to debug—remains uncontested, and the implementation is pragmatic, but the devil is in the details. No merge date is in sight yet, but the changes are incremental and unlikely to conflict with other work.
