This thread is about a two-patch series from Harald Nordgren that improves GitHub Actions CI failure reporting for Git. The series makes leak sanitizer and test failures more actionable by stopping at the first leak (using `--immediate`) and annotating failures with clickable links to the exact line in the test script where they occurred. The v3 iteration, just posted, addresses all substantive review feedback from Junio C Hamano, Phillip Wood, and D. Ben Knoble, including correctness, portability, and consistency issues.

Junio has now marked the series as ready for `next`, meaning it will enter integration testing soon. The key changes include reordering exit paths in `test_failure_()` to ensure annotations are written before any `--immediate` exit, replacing a non-portable `\r` escape with a literal carriage-return byte, and introducing helper functions to handle cross-platform escaping and precise line-number detection. The series touches `ci/lib.sh`, `t/test-lib-github-workflow-markup.sh`, and `t/test-lib.sh`, with no on-disk format changes or new dependencies.

The remaining open questions are minor: Phillip Wood’s request for a more principled justification of the `--immediate` trade-off (currently addressed pragmatically in the commit message) and D. Ben Knoble’s suggestion to reorder helper functions for readability. Neither blocks progression, and no further changes are expected unless new issues arise during cooking in `next`. The series is narrowly scoped and directly improves the developer experience for CI debugging.
