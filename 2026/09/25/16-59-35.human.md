This is a documentation patch thread, specifically a reply in Julia Evans’ seven-patch series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`. The series aims to consolidate Git’s scattered merge-conflict advice into a single, practical guide.

Julia responds here to D. Ben Knoble’s substantive review of the second patch, which replaces the conflict-resolution section in `git-merge.adoc` with a cross-reference to the new guide. Knoble flagged two omissions: a foundational explanation of what a merge conflict *is* (the distinction between overlapping and non-overlapping changes), and a practical tip for using `git log --merge -p <path>` to inspect branch diffs during conflict resolution. Julia acknowledges both points. She commits to reworking the first (“Will think about this!”) and clarifies her initial confusion about the second—she had misunderstood `--merge` as relating to merge commits rather than conflict context. She now proposes new wording for the guide: a brief description of `git log --merge -p <filename>` under a “Tools to get more context” section, emphasizing its intent (“commits which caused the merge conflict”) while deferring to the man page for specifics.

The exchange is constructive and actionable. Julia’s willingness to address the feedback suggests the new guide will gain two useful additions: a clearer conceptual foundation and a discoverable tool for conflict resolution. No controversy or technical disagreement is evident; this is a routine refinement step in an otherwise well-received series.
