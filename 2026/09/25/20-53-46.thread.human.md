This thread introduces GPG commit-signing support to `git replay` via a new `-S` option, authored by Patrick Monette. The series consists of two patches: the first fixes a latent error-handling bug in `pick_regular_commit()` to ensure signing failures propagate correctly, and the second adds the `-S`/`--gpg-sign` CLI options with comprehensive test coverage. The implementation deliberately ignores `commit.gpgSign` to maintain plumbing command consistency, though this design choice has sparked a discussion about whether user configuration should influence behavior.

The series is under review, with Patrick Steinhardt approving the error-handling fix in patch 1 but raising a policy question about ignoring `commit.gpgSign`. Steinhardt has also flagged a merge conflict risk with Tian Yuchen’s in-flight `git history` signing series, recommending this series be rebased after that work lands. No objections to the technical implementation have been raised, but the author has yet to respond to either the policy question or the conflict notice. The series remains actionable pending resolution of these coordination issues.
