This is the fifth patch in Julia Evans’s seven-patch documentation series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page that consolidates merge conflict resolution guidance. The series aims to reduce duplication by replacing scattered conflict-resolution advice in existing man pages with cross-references to the new guide.

This patch updates `Documentation/git-cherry-pick.adoc` to replace its existing merge-conflict discussion with a link to `gitmergeconflicts(7)`. The removed section described cherry-pick-specific details—such as the `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD` ref, how cleanly applied changes are staged in the index, and the structure of conflict markers—while directing users to `git-merge(1)` for resolution advice. The new link sends users to the centralized guide, which covers general conflict resolution but does not mention cherry-pick-specific mechanics like `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD`.

Junio C Hamano’s review flags a real gap: the patch removes information unique to `git cherry-pick` (notably `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD` and the behavior of auto-resolved paths) without ensuring the new guide covers it. While the guide explains general conflict resolution, it does not address the pseudo-ref or the index state during cherry-pick, leaving users without critical context. Junio suggests retaining at least the details about `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD` and auto-resolved paths, either by keeping the original list (shortened) or by adding cherry-pick-specific notes to the new guide. The patch is otherwise mechanical and aligns with the series’ goal, but this omission risks degrading the documentation’s usefulness for cherry-pick users. The fix will likely require either a revision to this patch or an update to the new guide.
