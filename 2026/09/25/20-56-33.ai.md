This is a bugfix patch targeting the packfile subsystem, specifically addressing resource leaks in temporary pack files during `git gc` or `git maintenance` operations. The patch ensures that temporary files created via `odb_mkstemp()` in `pack-write.c` and `pack-bitmap-write.c` are registered with Git's tempfile subsystem, preventing them from being left behind if the process is terminated (e.g., via SIGKILL).
