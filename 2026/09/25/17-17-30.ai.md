This is the fifth patch in Julia Evans's seven-patch documentation series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page consolidating merge conflict resolution guidance. The patch updates `Documentation/git-cherry-pick.adoc` to replace its existing merge-conflict discussion with a cross-reference to the new guide, but Junio C Hamano raises substantive concerns about the removal of cherry-pick-specific details.

Junio's review identifies two critical omissions in the new guide's coverage of cherry-pick conflicts: the `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD` pseudo-ref (useful for inspecting the commit being cherry-picked) and the behavior of cleanly auto-resolved paths (recorded in the index). These details are not currently addressed in `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, and their removal from the cherry-pick man page would leave users without essential command-specific guidance. Junio suggests retaining at least items #2 and #3 from the original list (describing `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD` and index updates) while still linking to the new guide for general conflict-resolution advice.

The review is substantive, engaging with the technical accuracy and completeness of the documentation rather than surface-level issues. It highlights a tension between the series' goal of centralizing conflict-resolution advice and the need to preserve command-specific nuances. The feedback implies that the new guide may not yet be a complete replacement for the removed sections, particularly for operations like cherry-pick that have unique pseudo-refs and workflows. This could necessitate revisions to either the guide (to incorporate cherry-pick-specific details) or the patch (to retain some of the original content alongside the cross-reference). The thread remains open, with no resolution yet on how to balance these competing priorities.
