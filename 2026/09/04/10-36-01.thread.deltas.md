## 2026/09/04

- [2026/09/04/10-36-01 by Patrick Steinhardt <ps@pks.im>] Patrick Steinhardt posted an 11-patch series unifying ref storage terminology: renaming `--ref-format=` to `--ref-storage=` in git-init/clone/refs/submodule, `--show-ref-format` to `--show-ref-storage`, `default-ref-format` to `default-ref-storage`, `GIT_REFERENCE_BACKEND`/`GIT_DEFAULT_REF_FORMAT` to `GIT_REF_STORAGE`/`GIT_DEFAULT_REF_STORAGE`, and `init.defaultRefFormat` to `init.defaultRefStorage`, keeping the old names as hidden aliases for backwards compatibility, and extending `--ref-storage=` to accept URIs with a payload (e.g. `files://foo/bar`).
