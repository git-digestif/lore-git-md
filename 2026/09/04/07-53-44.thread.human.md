This thread is about a patch series (v3) by Thomas Bachem that defers Git’s auto maintenance until the end of sequencer-driven operations like `rebase`, `cherry-pick`, and `revert`, matching the behavior of the apply backend. The series is technically complete—all feedback from Phillip Wood, Patrick Steinhardt, and Junio C Hamano has been addressed, including design refinements like moving the maintenance call out of the sequencer and into the built-in commands to consolidate exit paths.

The only remaining blocker is commit message polish. Patrick Steinhardt has raised concerns about clarity and authorship, specifically that the messages must justify the design choice of delegating maintenance to callers and read as human-written, not LLM-generated. Thomas has agreed to revise the messages in a v4, explicitly noting that the built-in commands are the sole entry points for sequencer operations. Once this is done, the series is expected to be merged. No other open questions or objections remain.
