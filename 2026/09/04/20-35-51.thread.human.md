This thread is about a patch series from Mark C. Chu-Carroll that aims to modernize Git’s test scripts by replacing older shell test idioms (`test -f`, `! test -f`, `test -d`) with more semantic helper functions (`test_path_is_file`, `test_path_is_missing`, `test_path_is_dir`). The goal is purely syntactic modernization, with no intended behavioral or performance changes.

The series has been rejected in its current form after reviews from Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt. Both reviewers identified a fundamental flaw: the mechanical replacement of `test -f` with `test_path_is_file` is incorrect when the test is used for control flow (e.g., lazy file creation or platform-specific checks) rather than assertions. The helper functions are designed to fail loudly with error messages, which breaks silent conditionals. Specific examples include `t/t0031-lockfile-pid.sh` (checking `/proc/$shell_pid/winpid` on MINGW) and `t/t1007-hash-object.sh` (avoiding regeneration of a 5GB file). Steinhardt also noted procedural issues, such as the missing Signed-off-by trailer and non-imperative commit message style.

The series is now blocked until the approach is rethought. Key open questions include how to distinguish between `test -f` used for assertions versus control flow, and whether the series should be split into smaller, targeted patches addressing only assertion contexts. The author has not yet responded to the feedback. The series is part of the broader community-wide test modernization effort but currently has no integration status.
