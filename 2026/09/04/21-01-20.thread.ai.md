Core problem or goal
The `--force-if-includes` safety mechanism in `git push` has two critical flaws: (1) it incorrectly checks the reflog of the *local* branch whose name matches the *remote* destination branch (e.g., `refs/heads/main` when pushing to `refs/remotes/origin/main`), rather than the branch actually being pushed (e.g., `refs/heads/src` in `src:main`), and (2) it **unnecessarily rejects fast-forward pushes of non-branch refs** (tags, detached HEADs) even when no force is needed, breaking workflows and violating the feature’s documented intent. The series fixes the core logic to consult the reflog of the pushed ref (`ref->peer_ref->name`), exempts fast-forward pushes from `--force-if-includes` checks (resolving an existing bug), and provides actionable advice for rejected cases. The v5 update introduces a third patch to skip reflog checks for fast-forwards, aligning the feature with its safety guarantees (ensuring the remote tip has been integrated locally) while reducing friction for common workflows.

Introduces `advice.forceIfIncludesDetachedHead` (documented as `advice.pushRefUnverifiable`) and updates advice to instruct users to use `--force-with-lease=<ref>:<expect>` or `--no-force-if-includes`. **v5 changes**: None; this patch is carried forward from v4.
3. **New in v5**: Skips `--force-if-includes` checks for fast-forward pushes, resolving the existing bug where fast-forwards of tags or detached HEADs were rejected. This patch ensures the feature only intervenes when force is actually needed, aligning with its documented intent. **Key logic**: Adds a fast-forward check in `apply_push_cas` before invoking `check_if_includes_upstream`.

The v5 series is **technically complete** and addresses the backwards-compatibility concern raised by Knoble (e.g., `git push origin <TAG>:hotfix` now works for fast-forwards without requiring `--no-force-if-includes`). The core design (reflog lookup logic, non-branch rejection policy for force-pushes) remains uncontroversial. The series is based on `maint` and has no external dependencies.
