This thread is about a small but important bugfix to Git’s `--force-if-includes` safety mechanism in `git push`. The feature was incorrectly checking the reflog of the *local* branch whose name matches the remote destination (e.g., `refs/heads/main` when pushing to `origin/main`), rather than the branch actually being pushed (e.g., `refs/heads/src` in `src:main`). This could cause false rejections or unintended data loss in common workflows like pushing from a differently named branch or a detached HEAD.
