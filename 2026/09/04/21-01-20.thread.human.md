This thread is about a bugfix series for Git's `--force-if-includes` safety mechanism in `git push`, now in its seventh and final revision (v7). The series addresses two critical flaws: (1) the mechanism incorrectly checks the reflog of the local branch matching the remote destination name rather than the branch actually being pushed, risking data loss and false rejections, and (2) it unnecessarily blocks fast-forward pushes of non-branch refs like tags or detached HEADs, breaking workflows.

Tyler Cipriani, the author, has posted v7 with only minor typographical fixes and a rebase onto the latest `maint` branch, resolving a trivial conflict with a recent advice addition. The series consists of three patches: the first fixes the core reflog lookup logic and adds extensive test coverage, the second introduces actionable advice for unverifiable refs (e.g., tags), and the third exempts fast-forward pushes from `--force-if-includes` checks. All prior feedback from reviewers—including D. Ben Knoble, Junio C Hamano, and Patrick Steinhardt—has been addressed, and the series is now technically complete and ready for merge. The author signals this is the final version unless new issues arise, though they’re willing to send a rebased v8 if needed to resolve a trivial test conflict with another topic. The design remains uncontroversial, and the advice mechanism resolves a long-standing UX flaw.
