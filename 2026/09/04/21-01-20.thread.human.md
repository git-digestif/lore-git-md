This thread fixes a long-standing bug in Git’s `--force-if-includes` safety mechanism, which was incorrectly rejecting fast-forward pushes of non-branch refs (like tags or detached HEADs) and checking the wrong reflog when pushing differently named branches. The series, now at v5, consists of three patches that correct the core logic, improve advice messages, and exempt fast-forwards from unnecessary checks. All prior review feedback—including concerns about backwards compatibility and edge-case correctness—has been addressed, and the series is technically complete.

The key participants are Tyler Cipriani (the author), D. Ben Knoble, Junio C Hamano (the maintainer), and Patrick Steinhardt. Knoble’s latest review focuses on minor readability improvements, such as fixing a commit message typo and clarifying the `deferred_reject_reason` variable name in the fast-forward exemption logic. While Knoble suggests a v6 reroll for these low-weight tweaks, no technical changes are needed, and the series is ready for final approval. The fix is uncontroversial, well-tested (with 108 lines of new coverage in `t/t5533-push-cas.sh`), and resolves a real-world workflow breakage without affecting `--force-with-lease` or on-disk formats.
