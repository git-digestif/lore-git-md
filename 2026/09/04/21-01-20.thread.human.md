This thread is about a bugfix for Git's `--force-if-includes` safety mechanism in `git push`. The issue is that the feature incorrectly checks the reflog of a local branch matching the *remote* destination name (e.g., `refs/heads/main`) instead of the branch actually being pushed (e.g., `refs/heads/src` in `src:main`). This can cause false rejections or unintended data loss. The series also fixes misleading advice messages for detached HEAD rejections, replacing generic "pull" suggestions with actionable guidance.

Tyler Cipriani posted a two-patch series (v3) to address this. The first patch fixes the core logic in `remote.c` to check the correct reflog and adds special handling for `HEAD`-based pushes and detached HEAD states. The second patch improves advice messages for detached HEAD scenarios, introducing a new config knob (`advice.forceIfIncludesDetachedHead`) and updating all transport layers for consistency. The series includes 65 lines of new test coverage and eight additional test cases.

The series is under review and technically complete, but Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt raised new technical concerns about edge-case correctness and test coverage for the first patch, such as deletion handling and symbolic reference resolution. D. Ben Knoble provided substantive review and accepted the series as ready for merge. Junio’s latest feedback on the second patch is purely stylistic (line-length violations and advice-message boilerplate) and does not challenge the patch’s correctness. The series is based on `maint` and cites prior reports of the same issue.
