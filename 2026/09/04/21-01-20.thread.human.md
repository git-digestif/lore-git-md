This thread is about a long-running bugfix series to correct `--force-if-includes` in `git push`, now in its seventh and final revision. The series addresses two critical flaws: (1) the safety check was incorrectly looking at the reflog of a local branch matching the remote destination name rather than the branch actually being pushed, and (2) it unnecessarily rejected fast-forward pushes of non-branch refs like tags or detached HEADs, breaking workflows.

Tyler Cipriani, the author, has posted v7 with all prior feedback resolved, including minor commit message fixes, a rebase onto `maint` at `v2.56.0`, and improved advice messages for unverifiable refs. The series consists of three patches: the first fixes the core reflog lookup logic, the second adds actionable advice for pushes without reflogs (e.g., tags), and the third—just summarized—exempts fast-forward pushes from `--force-if-includes` checks, resolving the existing bug. The implementation is minimal, with 108 lines of new test coverage verifying edge cases like differently named branches, orphan branches, and tag-to-branch pushes.

Key participants include D. Ben Knoble and Patrick Steinhardt, who provided substantive reviews, and Junio Hamano, who raised edge-case questions. All feedback has been addressed, and the series is now technically complete. The only remaining loose end is a trivial textual conflict with another topic (`as/push-force-if-includes-no-reflog`) in the test file, which the author has offered to resolve in a v8 if needed. The series is ready for merge unless new issues are raised.
