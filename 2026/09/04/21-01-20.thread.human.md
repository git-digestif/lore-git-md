This thread fixes a long-standing bug in Git's `--force-if-includes` safety mechanism, which was incorrectly rejecting fast-forward pushes of non-branch refs (like tags or detached HEADs) even when no force was needed. The v5 series, now complete with three patches, corrects the core logic to check the reflog of the pushed ref rather than a mismatched local branch, exempts fast-forwards from reflog checks entirely, and provides clear advice for rejected cases.

The key participants—Tyler Cipriani (author), D. Ben Knoble, Junio C Hamano, and Patrick Steinhardt—have all engaged with the series, with feedback focused on correctness, edge cases, and documentation clarity. The backwards-compatibility concern raised by Knoble (e.g., `git push origin <TAG>:hotfix` failing for fast-forwards) is now resolved by the fast-forward exemption in patch 3/3, which also fixes an existing bug where the feature violated its own documented intent. Junio and Steinhardt's prior feedback on implementation robustness and style has been addressed, and the series is technically complete with 108 lines of new test coverage.

The only open questions are whether reviewers will request additional test cases for edge scenarios like mixed fast-forward/non-fast-forward pushes or symbolic references. The series is based on `maint` and has no external dependencies, making it a straightforward candidate for merging once final reviews are complete.
