Here’s the updated **human-mode** summary for the thread:

This thread is the third iteration of Patrick Steinhardt’s series to standardize Git’s terminology for "ref storage format" across CLI options, environment variables, config keys, and documentation. The goal is to replace inconsistent names like `--ref-format=` and `GIT_REFERENCE_BACKEND` with `--ref-storage-format=` and `GIT_REF_STORAGE_FORMAT`, respectively, to avoid confusion ahead of a planned `--object-storage-format=` extension. The series also fixes a bug where reinitializing a repository with a different ref storage format or URI payload silently corrupted the repository.

Version 3 addresses all substantive feedback from earlier rounds, including backward-compatible option aliases (using `OPT_ALIAS_F()`), clearer error messages, and test updates. The latest patch (13/13) enables `--ref-storage-format=` to accept URIs like `files://path/to/alt-refs`, matching existing support in repository extensions and environment variables. This closes the silent-corruption bug by enforcing immutability of the ref storage format and payload once set. The series is now feature-complete and under final review, with no major objections remaining. Key participants include Patrick (author), Karthik Nayak (reviewer), Junio Hamano (maintainer), and Kaartic Sivaraam (reviewer), who raised minor edge cases—most of which have been resolved. The only unresolved issues are a direct string comparison in the immutability check (which may reject semantically identical paths) and an inconsistency in the `PARSE_OPT_NONEG` flag across commands. The series is expected to merge once these nits are addressed or accepted.
