This thread is about updating Git's CI infrastructure to use Debian 12 instead of the now end-of-life Debian 11. The change itself is straightforward—updating Docker image references in GitHub Actions and GitLab CI workflows—and is already queued in Junio's `seen` branch with the intent to merge. Jeff King (Peff) authored the patch, which Junio endorsed after Debian 11 jobs began failing.

The discussion took a turn when Junio proposed removing two Ubuntu-based CI jobs: `linux32` (which tested i386 support) and `linux-TEST-vars` (which tested exotic configurations en masse). Junio argued these jobs were no longer viable or realistic. Patrick Steinhardt has now pushed back, contending that i386 coverage (via Debian's supported images) and the `TEST-vars` job (which has caught regressions in the past) remain valuable. While he acknowledges the pragmatic trade-offs, he is willing to send patches to preserve or adapt these jobs rather than remove them outright. The Debian 12 update is likely to proceed, but the fate of the two Ubuntu jobs is now contested.
