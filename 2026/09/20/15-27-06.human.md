This is a CI/build system follow-up in the GitLab Windows CI breakage thread. Johannes Schindelin (Dscho) replies to Karthik Nayak’s tested review of his patch series, which aims to fix Rust-related build failures in GitLab’s MinGW-based Windows jobs.

The series itself is a targeted fix for three specific issues: missing Rust toolchain (`cargo: command not found`), silent data loss in `.git/info/exclude`, and linker unavailability during `gitcore`’s build script. Karthik’s earlier setup of an internal GitLab merge request (MR #671) and pipeline (#2863888081) provided a realistic test environment, addressing the "lightly tested" caveat in the original submission.

In this email, Dscho confirms that the `build:mingw64` job succeeded as intended, though he notes the job name might now be outdated (suggesting `build:ucrt64` as a more accurate label). However, the `build:msvc-meson` job timed out due to credential-related issues—an unrelated problem that doesn’t affect the series’ core goal. The fix remains narrowly scoped to GitLab’s MinGW environment, with no changes to Git’s behavior or other CI platforms.

The thread is now in a "tested and partially validated" state. The MinGW job’s success is a positive signal, but the MSVC timeout may prompt further investigation if it recurs. No technical objections have been raised, and the series appears to address the original breakage effectively. The discussion is likely to focus on job naming conventions and unrelated CI flakiness rather than the patch content itself.
