This is the **v3 cover letter follow-up** for D. Ben Knoble’s five-patch bugfix series targeting `git stash`. The series fixes a bug where autostashing fails to preserve staged index entries when `stash.index=true` is set, particularly during merge operations. The root cause—a race condition in subprocess-based index merging—is resolved by switching to in-core merging via `merge-ort.c`.

The series is now complete and ready for integration. Patch 1 (a cleanup) is already merged to `master`; patches 2–5 are technically sound and address all prior feedback. Key improvements in v3 include a new test (patch 3) validating the corrected tree argument order in `merge_incore_nonrecursive()`, expanded test assertions in patch 4 to verify both worktree and index state preservation, and a memory leak fix in patch 5. The author notes a minor procedural hiccup (the cover letter was sent in reply to v2 rather than v1), but this does not affect the substance of the series.

The patches are well-motivated, thoroughly tested, and endorsed by Junio C Hamano and Phillip Wood. The fix is narrow but critical: it eliminates a SIGSEGV risk during conflicted index merges and ensures staged entries are preserved. No further revisions are expected. If you care about `git stash` reliability or index-state handling, this series is worth reviewing.
