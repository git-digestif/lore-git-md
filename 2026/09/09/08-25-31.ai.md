This is the final patch in a three-patch series (v4 3/3) that disables auto maintenance in commands spawned by the sequencer (`git commit`, `git merge`, and `git exec` during rebase). The series is part of an effort to defer auto maintenance until the completion of sequencer-driven operations (`git rebase`, `git cherry-pick`, `git revert`), aligning their behavior with the apply backend and preventing maintenance from interfering with intermediate steps.

This patch implements the core mechanism: it passes `maintenance.auto=false` via `GIT_CONFIG_PARAMETERS` to all spawned commands, ensuring the setting overrides user configurations and propagates to any further subprocesses. The config string is built on first use and cached in `replay_ctx` as a `const char *` to avoid repeated construction. The patch touches `sequencer.c` to add the `disable_auto_maintenance()` helper and modify the `run_git_commit()`, `do_exec()`, and `do_merge()` functions to inject the environment variable. Tests in `t/t3418-rebase-continue.sh` and `t/t3510-cherry-pick-sequence.sh` are updated to verify that auto maintenance runs exactly once at sequence completion (not during intermediate steps) and that spawned commands (e.g., `git commit` during conflict resolution) do not trigger maintenance.
