This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing review of Vsevolod Myalitsin’s patch series to add scope hints to Git’s advice system. Junio C Hamano responds to Jeff King’s (Peff) suggestion about reusing Git’s existing `CONFIG_SCOPE` enum instead of introducing a new `ADVICE_SCOPE` enum.

Junio agrees with Peff’s observation that the existing `CONFIG_SCOPE` enum has more values than needed for advice messages, but he doesn’t object to Peff’s proposal to use `config_scope_name()` to convert scopes into command-line flags. He remains neutral on the design choice, deferring to the author’s preference. The key takeaway is that Junio expects a small final reroll to address the remaining feedback—likely including Peff’s suggestions about enum reuse and translation clarity, along with the mechanical issues Junio noted earlier (commit-message clarity, indentation, and interface narrowing).

The patch is still under review, and this exchange doesn’t introduce new technical concerns. It simply clarifies that the maintainer is open to either approach for handling scope enums, leaving the decision to the author. The next step is for Vsevolod to send a v4 incorporating the feedback.
