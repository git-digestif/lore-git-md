This is patch 1 of Patrick Steinhardt’s 11-patch v2 series standardizing Git’s terminology for "ref storage format." The patch mechanically renames the `--ref-format=` option to `--ref-storage-format=` in `git init`, retaining the old name as a hidden backward-compatible alias via `OPT_STRING_F` with `PARSE_OPT_HIDDEN`. It touches 15 files: the built-in command (`builtin/init-db.c`), its documentation (`Documentation/git-init.adoc`, `Documentation/config/init.adoc`), and 12 test scripts (`t0001-init.sh`, `t0033-safe-directory.sh`, `t0410-partial-clone.sh`, `t1006-cat-file.sh`, `t1301-shared-repo.sh`, `t1302-repo-version.sh`, `t1402-check-ref-format.sh`, `t1419-exclude-refs.sh`, `t1423-ref-backend.sh`, `t5500-fetch-pack.sh`, `t5606-clone-options.sh`, `t7424-submodule-mixed-ref-formats.sh`).

The patch is uncontroversial and well-motivated: it is the first step in a larger cleanup that will unify terminology across CLI options, environment variables, config keys, and documentation, and it retains backward compatibility. The only discussion point introduced by this patch is the lifecycle of the old `--ref-format=` option. Kaartic Sivaraam suggests either deprecating it with warnings (and eventually removing it) or using `OPT_ALIAS` to clarify its status; Patrick prefers a quiet deprecation (retaining the old option as a hidden alias without warnings or documentation updates) and is open to adding `PARSE_OPT_HIDDEN` support to `OPT_ALIAS` if needed. No technical objections or edge-case concerns have been raised; the feedback remains focused on long-term maintainability and user communication. The series as a whole is under active review, with no merge status reported.
