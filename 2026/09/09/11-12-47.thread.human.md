This thread is about a small but important refactoring to Git’s parse-options API, part of Patrick Steinhardt’s larger effort to unify the ref storage format. The patch introduces support for hidden option aliases—allowing deprecated options to remain functional but hidden from help text—by extending the `OPT_ALIAS()` macro with a new `OPT_ALIAS_F()` variant that accepts flags like `PARSE_OPT_HIDDEN`. The change is minimal, touching only two files, and is purely preparatory for later patches in the series that will use this feature to deprecate old ref-storage options.

So far, the patch has drawn no review feedback, but it’s uncontroversial and appears ready for integration. It’s the first patch in the v3 series, and while the cover letter or subsequent patches will likely show how the new hidden-alias capability is put to use, this patch itself is just plumbing—no behavior changes are introduced yet. The thread is still early, with the rest of the series yet to be posted or reviewed.
