This is patch 4/9 in Patrick Steinhardt’s ODB alternates refactoring series, part of the broader ODB abstraction effort. The patch refactors how `git clone` handles `--reference` and `--reference-if-able` by collecting alternates paths into a `strvec` instead of immediately writing them to the object database.

The core change is mechanical: `setup_reference()` is renamed to `collect_alternates()` and now gathers alternates paths into a `strvec` using a new `struct add_one_alternate_data` to track whether each alternate is required or optional. The immediate alternates writes (`odb_add_to_alternates_file()`) are deferred until after the `strvec` is populated, preparing for later patches that will consolidate alternates handling into the ODB creation step. The patch introduces no new behavior or symbols, but the new `collect_alternates()` function and `add_one_alternate_data` struct will be used in subsequent patches to unify alternates logic across all clone modes (local, shared, and reference-based).

The refactoring is uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ goal of simplifying the ODB interface. The patch is a straightforward intermediate step that enables the consolidation of alternates logic in patches 5-6 and the eventual removal of the `write_alternates()` callback in patch 9. Reviewers may focus on the clarity of the new `collect_alternates()` interface, but the change is incremental and preserves existing behavior. The patch touches only `builtin/clone.c` and is built on the `create_repository()` refactoring introduced in patch 1.
