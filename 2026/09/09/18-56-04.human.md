This is a **build-system patch** in Johannes Schindelin’s 12-part series to upstream Git for Windows adjustments. It is the eighth patch, focused on replacing hard-coded MSYS2 metadata (like installation prefixes and CPU targets) with values derived from MSYS2’s environment variables (`MINGW_PREFIX` and `MSYSTEM`). The patch touches the Makefile (`config.mak.uname`), CMake, and Meson build systems, and is uncontroversial in its goal.

The email is a short back-and-forth between Schindelin and Johannes Sixt (Hannes) about a **cleanup suggestion** raised in Sixt’s earlier review. Sixt pointed out that the CMake and Meson hunks in this patch introduce compiler definitions (`ENSURE_MSYSTEM_IS_SET` and `MINGW_PREFIX`) that are only used in the **twelfth patch**, where they drive C code. He argued these definitions should be moved to the final patch to avoid introducing unused symbols. Schindelin acknowledges the point and agrees to "disentangle" the changes, meaning he will move the CMake and Meson hunks to the twelfth patch in a future iteration.

The discussion is purely about patch organization, not technical correctness. The series remains stable and well-tested: Sixt reports running a subset of these patches in his personal Windows builds since November 2025 without issues, and the full series passes GitHub CI on Windows. Junio C Hamano has not objected to the current approach, though he recently nudged the thread to wrap up the discussion, which has been quiet for three weeks. If Schindelin follows through, the only change will be moving the CMake and Meson hunks to the final patch, with no functional impact. The patch is otherwise ready for integration.
