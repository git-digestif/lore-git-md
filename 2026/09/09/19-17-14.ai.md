This is the **tenth patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 12-patch MinGW/Windows build and runtime adjustment series**, which is now in its third iteration. The patch simplifies the logic for setting `ETC_GITCONFIG` and `ETC_GITATTRIBUTES` paths in `config.mak.uname` by unconditionally defining them for all MSYS2 environments, eliminating redundant special-casing.

The change removes a conditional block that previously only set these paths when the prefix matched `/mingw64` (or similar) and replaces it with a simpler guard (`ifneq (,$(MINGW_PREFIX))`) that applies to all MSYS2 environments. This aligns with the series’ broader goal of reducing hard-coded assumptions in favor of dynamic settings derived from MSYS2’s environment variables. The patch is uncontroversial and ready for integration, with no review feedback or open questions.
