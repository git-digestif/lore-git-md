This is the latest exchange in the now-abandoned `--[no-]range-diff-notes` feature thread. Kristoffer Haugsbakk responds to Junio C Hamano’s detailed usability critique by proposing a simplified redesign that removes the toggle mechanism (`--no-range-diff-notes --range-diff-notes`) and the ability to revert to the default state where range-diffs use the same notes as the patches.

Kristoffer’s proposal has three key changes: (1) `--range-diff-notes` must always take an argument (no standalone `--range-diff-notes` without `<ref>`), (2) `--no-range-diff-notes` will mean “no notes for range-diff” without fallback to `--notes`, and (3) the toggle sequence is eliminated entirely. This aligns with Junio’s earlier suggestion that `--no-range-diff-notes` should have a clear, unambiguous meaning. The redesign also simplifies the option parser by moving the logic out of `revision.c` and into a standard `parse-options` callback.

The email is a constructive follow-up to Junio’s critique, but the thread remains closed. Kristoffer’s willingness to rework the feature is noted, but no new patches have been posted, and the original series was abandoned. If this feature is revived, the redesign sketched here would likely be the starting point. For now, the discussion is purely theoretical.
