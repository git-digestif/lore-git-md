This decision settles the backward-compatibility mechanism and removes the last blocker for the patch’s architectural direction. The core changes — replacing the `global_hint` boolean with a `scope_hint` field and passing the entire `advice_setting` struct to `vadvise()` — remain unchanged and uncontroversial.

He wonders if users would find these instructions annoyingly chatty if they appeared everywhere, but neither he nor Junio proposes a concrete change here. This remains an open question for future discussion, though it does not affect the current patch.
