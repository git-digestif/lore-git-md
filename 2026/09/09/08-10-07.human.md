This is patch 9/11 in Patrick Steinhardt’s v2 series to standardize Git’s terminology for "ref storage format." The patch renames two environment variables—`GIT_REFERENCE_BACKEND` to `GIT_REF_STORAGE_FORMAT` and `GIT_DEFAULT_REF_FORMAT` to `GIT_DEFAULT_REF_STORAGE_FORMAT`—while retaining the old names as backward-compatible aliases. It also adds the missing `GIT_REF_STORAGE_FORMAT_ENVIRONMENT` macro to `environment.h`, addressing feedback from the v1 review.

Kaartic Sivaraam’s reply is a **surface-level review** that raises two minor issues. First, he points out that the error messages in `setup.c` mention only the new environment variable names (`GIT_REF_STORAGE_FORMAT_ENVIRONMENT` and `GIT_DEFAULT_REF_STORAGE_FORMAT`) even when the value is read from the old names (`GIT_REFERENCE_BACKEND_ENVIRONMENT` and `GIT_DEFAULT_REF_FORMAT`). This could mislead users into thinking the new variable was set when the old one was actually used. Second, he suggests renaming `GIT_TEST_DEFAULT_REF_FORMAT` to `GIT_TEST_DEFAULT_REF_STORAGE_FORMAT` in `t/test-lib.sh` for consistency, though he frames this as a question rather than a demand.

The feedback is uncontroversial and purely about clarity and consistency. The patch itself is well-motivated, thoroughly tested, and aligns with the series’ goal of resolving terminology inconsistencies ahead of the upcoming `--object-storage-format=` extension. The backward-compatible aliases ensure no breakage, and the changes are mechanical with no functional impact. This patch is likely to be merged once the minor issues are addressed.
