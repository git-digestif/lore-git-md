He does not see the default behavior changing as a regression, but he also admits he is unsure what criteria determine whether an advice setting should be recommended as `--global` or not. This suggests the enum-reuse question is not purely technical but also touches on the intended semantics of the feature, which may not have been fully settled in earlier discussion.

The email advances the thread by removing the technical objection to enum reuse, but it leaves the door open for further discussion about the *semantic* criteria for assigning scope hints.
