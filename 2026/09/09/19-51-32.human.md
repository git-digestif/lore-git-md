Junio confirms that the current pattern—checking `advice_enabled()` to avoid expensive work, then calling `advise()`—is intentional and should not be converted unless the turn-off instructions are explicitly desired.

The exchange is technical but low-stakes for the immediate patch. It resolves a minor ambiguity about the advice subsystem's design philosophy and confirms that the existing behavior is deliberate.
