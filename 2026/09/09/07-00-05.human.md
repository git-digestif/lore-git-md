This is the first patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s v2 series to standardize Git’s terminology for "ref storage format." The patch renames the `--ref-format=` option in `git init` to `--ref-storage-format=`, retaining the old name as a hidden backward-compatible alias via `OPT_STRING_F` with `PARSE_OPT_HIDDEN`. The change is mechanical: it updates the help text, variable names, and documentation, and touches 15 files (including `builtin/init-db.c`, `Documentation/git-init.adoc`, and 14 test scripts).

The email is a reply to Kaartic Sivaraam’s review, which raised a procedural question about the backward-compatibility strategy: whether the old `--ref-format=` option should be deprecated with warnings (and eventually removed) or simply retained as a hidden alias. Patrick clarifies his intent to quietly deprecate the old option—keeping it functional but hidden—without issuing warnings or planning near-term removal, as he sees little benefit in breaking existing scripts. He also agrees to keep the old option documented for clarity and considers adding `PARSE_OPT_HIDDEN` support to `OPT_ALIAS()` in a future preparatory patch.

The discussion remains focused on maintainability and user communication rather than technical objections. The series as a whole is well-motivated (preparing for an analogous `--object-storage-format=` extension) and uncontroversial, but this exchange introduces a new discussion point about how Git should handle deprecated options long-term. No merge status is reported; the series is still under review.
