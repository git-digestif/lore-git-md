This is the final patch (9/9) in Patrick Steinhardt’s 9-patch series refactoring ODB alternates handling during repository creation. The patch removes the now-unused `odb_source_write_alternates()` function and its supporting infrastructure across all ODB backends, completing the series’ goal of consolidating alternates handling into ODB creation via `odb_create_on_disk_options`.

The patch deletes 117 lines across seven files: `odb.c` (removing `odb_add_to_alternates_file()`), `odb.h` (removing the function declaration), `odb/source-files.c` (removing the "files" backend’s `write_alternate` implementation and its lockfile logic), and the "inmemory", "loose", and "packed" backends (removing their stub `write_alternate` implementations). The `write_alternate` field is also removed from the `odb_source` struct in `odb/source.h`, along with its inline helper function.

This is a mechanical cleanup patch with no behavior changes. All alternates logic has already been moved into the ODB creation process in earlier patches (notably patch 7’s lockfile-based atomic writes and patch 8’s wiring of the alternates `strvec` to `create_object_database()`). The removal of `odb_add_to_alternates_file()` and its backend-specific implementations is uncontroversial, as the function has no remaining callers in the codebase. The patch aligns with the series’ design goal of making alternates an ODB backend implementation detail, simplifying the interface by eliminating the ability to write alternates after repository creation.

Reviewers may focus on verifying that no callers of `odb_source_write_alternates()` or `odb_add_to_alternates_file()` remain, but the patch appears complete and correct. The lockfile API adoption in patch 7 ensures atomicity and safety, addressing the primary security concern raised in v1. This patch is the logical conclusion of the series and is ready for integration.
