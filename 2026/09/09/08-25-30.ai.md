This is the second patch in the v4 series that defers auto maintenance until the end of sequencer-driven operations (`git rebase`, `git cherry-pick`, `git revert`). It moves the `run_auto_maintenance()` call from the sequencer to the built-in commands (`builtin/rebase.c` and `builtin/revert.c`), consolidating maintenance into a single exit path per command. The patch touches the rebase and revert built-ins, adding logic to invoke maintenance only after the sequencer has fully completed (e.g., after the state directory is removed for rebase, or after a successful `--continue`, `--skip`, or pick for cherry-pick/revert). Test coverage is extended in `t/t3418-rebase-continue.sh` and `t/t3510-cherry-pick-sequence.sh` to verify maintenance runs exactly once at sequence completion, using `GIT_TRACE2_EVENT` to assert the absence of maintenance during intermediate steps.

The implementation aligns with Patrick Steinhardt’s preference for a unified exit path and simplifies the rule to "the command runs maintenance once when done." The commit message explicitly justifies the design choice of delegating maintenance to callers, noting that `builtin/rebase.c` and `builtin/revert.c` are the only two entry points for sequencer operations.
