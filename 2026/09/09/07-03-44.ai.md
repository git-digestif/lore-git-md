This is the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s v2 series to standardize Git’s ref storage terminology, and the email is his reply to Kaartic Sivaraam’s **surface-level review** of that patch. The patch itself is a mechanical rename of `--ref-format=` to `--ref-storage-format=` in `git refs migrate`, retaining the old option as a hidden backward-compatible alias. Kaartic’s feedback noted a minor inconsistency: the `PARSE_OPT_NONEG` flag (which prevents the option from being negated, e.g., `--no-ref-storage-format`) is present in `git refs migrate` but absent in other commands that accept the same argument (e.g., `git init`, `git clone`). He suggested dropping the flag for consistency but acknowledged it is not a blocker.

Patrick’s reply is brief and direct: he agrees the inconsistency exists but considers it outside the scope of this series, so he will not address it here. This leaves the `PARSE_OPT_NONEG` flag in place for now, with no immediate plans to harmonize it across commands. The rest of the patch—renaming the option, updating help text, and retaining the old name as a hidden alias—remains uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ goal of eliminating inconsistent terminology. The discussion does not introduce new technical concerns or edge cases, and the series as a whole continues to address a real problem (inconsistent terminology) with clear motivation (preparing for an analogous `--object-storage-format=` extension).
