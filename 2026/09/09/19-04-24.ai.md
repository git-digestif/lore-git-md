This is a follow-up in the now-abandoned `--[no-]range-diff-notes` feature thread. Junio C Hamano clarifies his earlier critique of the toggle mechanism (`--no-range-diff-notes --range-diff-notes`) by walking through both possible interpretations of `--no-range-diff-notes` and confirming that neither achieves the intended "revert to `--notes`" behavior.

Under interpretation #1 (the original design), `--no-range-diff-notes` pretends no `--range-diff-notes` was ever given, so the range-diff falls back to the notes specified by `--notes`. However, the subsequent `--range-diff-notes` (without an argument) then overrides this fallback, resulting in no notes for the range-diff—exactly the opposite of what the toggle sequence was meant to achieve. Under interpretation #2 (Junio’s preferred rule), `--no-range-diff-notes` explicitly means "no notes for the range-diff," and the subsequent `--range-diff-notes` (without an argument) is treated as "no notes," so the toggle sequence still does not revert to the `--notes` behavior.

Junio also notes that the documentation’s claim that the toggle sequence "turns off this notes override behavior" is incorrect under either interpretation and reiterates his earlier suggestion to remove that section. He acknowledges that he initially misunderstood the design as following interpretation #1 but now agrees with Kristoffer Haugsbakk that interpretation #2 is the correct approach. The email does not reopen the feature or propose a path forward; it simply closes the loop on the usability analysis, confirming that the toggle mechanism’s behavior is inherently confusing and cannot be salvaged as originally designed.
