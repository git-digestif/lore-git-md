This is patch 8/9 in Patrick Steinhardt’s nine-patch series refactoring how Git writes ODB alternates during repository creation. The series is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort and aims to simplify the ODB interface by moving alternates handling into the "files" backend.

This patch removes the manual alternates loop in `cmd_clone()` and passes the alternates `strvec` directly to `create_object_database()` via the `odb_create_on_disk_options` struct. The change is mechanical and aligns with the series’ goal of consolidating alternates handling into ODB creation. The patch updates `builtin/clone.c` to call `create_object_database()` with the alternates `strvec` instead of manually iterating over the alternates and calling `odb_add_to_alternates_file()` for each entry. It also updates `builtin/init-db.c` to call `create_object_database()` with `NULL` for alternates, preserving existing behavior for `git init`. The `create_object_database()` function in `setup.c` is updated to accept the alternates `strvec` via the `odb_create_on_disk_options` struct, and the function signature in `setup.h` is adjusted accordingly.

The patch is uncontroversial and prepares for the removal of the `write_alternates()` infrastructure in patch 9/9. It introduces no new behavior or symbols, and the changes are minimal (11 insertions, 9 deletions). The patch is well-motivated by the series’ broader goal of simplifying the ODB interface and making alternates an implementation detail of the ODB backend. Reviewers may focus on the clarity of the updated function signature and the robustness of the alternates handling, but the patch appears to be a straightforward and necessary step in the refactoring.
