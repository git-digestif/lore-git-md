## 2026/09/24

- [2026/09/24/23-52-16 by Jeff King <peff@peff.net>] Jeff King reported that setting per-file `EXTRA_CPPFLAGS` in `config.mak` (such as `imap-send.o: EXTRA_CPPFLAGS += -DNO_OPENSSL`) causes `make` to warn that the precompiled header is not used because the object file's build flags differ from those used to build the precompiled header; he explained this arises when building historical Git versions with OpenSSL disabled and mentioned there may be other related cases.

## 2026/09/25

- [2026/09/25/09-25-56 by SZEDER Gábor <szeder.dev@gmail.com>] SZEDER Gábor explained that Jeff King's `-DNO_OPENSSL` warning occurs because, unlike the `EXTRA_CPPFLAGS` set in the Makefile, `-DNO_OPENSSL` affects "git-compat-util.h".
- [2026/09/25/09-25-56 by SZEDER Gábor <szeder.dev@gmail.com>] He advised that anyone setting custom `EXTRA_CPPFLAGS` that affect "git-compat-util.h" should also append `-Wno-invalid-pch` to silence the warning, since `EXTRA_CPPFLAGS` appear after `-Winvalid-pch` in the object-file rule, and noted he found no way to override `-include precompiled.h`.
- [2026/09/25/09-25-56 by SZEDER Gábor <szeder.dev@gmail.com>] He shared his own config.mak logic that sets `NO_OPENSSL` for revisions predating 1ed2c7b115, said he had not encountered breakages from disabling OPENSSL for the whole build but may not have built old enough versions, and said he would adapt Jeff King's `EXTRA_CPPFLAGS` trick.
- [2026/09/25/09-25-56 by SZEDER Gábor <szeder.dev@gmail.com>] He agreed Jeff King's specific use case was too unusual to warrant special handling, but proposed the general guideline: "If you deliberately set a custom EXTRA_CPPFLAGS that affects 'git-compat-util.h', then you should also add '-Wno-invalid-pch' as well".
- [2026/09/25/09-25-56 by SZEDER Gábor <szeder.dev@gmail.com>] He expressed uncertainty about loosening compilation for 'seen', especially `DEVELOPER=1`, arguing it is best to catch issues while a commit is still only in 'seen', and noted his config.mak does not set `DEVELOPER=1` when bisecting or building a revision reachable from a tagged release.

## 2026/10/03

- [2026/10/03/19-00-21 by SZEDER Gábor <szeder.dev@gmail.com>] SZEDER Gábor reported that the precompiled header does change what gets compiled: with it, `__FILE__` in "git-compat-util.h" expands to "./git-compat-util.h" instead of "git-compat-util.h", a difference visible in `assert()`/`BUG()` error messages (e.g., from `regexec_buf()` or `ALLOC_GROW_BY()`) and in compiler error messages; he judged this acceptable because `__FILE__` is only expanded in error messages that should basically never happen.
