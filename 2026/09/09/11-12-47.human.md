This is a refactoring patch in the parse-options subsystem, part of a larger 13-patch series from Patrick Steinhardt. The series goal (stated in the cover letter, not shown here) is to unify Git’s ref-storage format, but this specific patch is plumbing: it extends the `OPT_ALIAS()` macro so that aliases can be marked as hidden (`PARSE_OPT_HIDDEN`). The change is small—seven lines across two files—and enables future patches in the series to deprecate old option names cleanly by hiding the aliases from help text while keeping them functional. The commit message is clear and the implementation straightforward; no controversy or edge cases are apparent. If you care about ref-storage unification or parse-options internals, this is a foundational piece.
