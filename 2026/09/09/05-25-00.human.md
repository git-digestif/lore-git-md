This is the first patch in a two-part contributor housekeeping series aimed at keeping Git’s documentation infrastructure in sync. The patch adds two missing manual pages—`gitformat-loose(5)` and `gitpacking(7)`—to `command-list.txt`, categorizing them as a developer interface and a guide, respectively. The change is mechanical and confined to a single file, with no runtime or on-disk format impact.

The commit message explains the rationale clearly: these manuals were overlooked in the list, and the author chose not to add `gitweb.conf(5)` due to its confusing short name ("web.conf") in `git help -a`. This decision is pragmatic and avoids clutter in the help output.

The patch is part of the broader `kh/doc-datamodel` effort, which is modernizing Git’s documentation. It’s a small but necessary step to prevent future drift in the documentation build system. The change looks clean and uncontroversial, and the accompanying linter update (in patch 2) will ensure similar omissions are caught early.
