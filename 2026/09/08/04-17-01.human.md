The patch itself remains uncontroversial and well-motivated. The change is purely mechanical, affects only the host-matching logic in the TLS certificate verification path, and does not alter runtime behavior. No substantive concerns have been raised about the patch’s correctness or necessity; the only open question is whether the author will adjust the comment style before the series proceeds.
