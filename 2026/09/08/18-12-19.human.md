This is the fourth patch in Christian Couder’s v3 series introducing `uploadpack.lazyFetchTrusted`, a server-side mechanism to mark repositories as safe for lazy fetching. Junio’s review here is surface-level, focusing on a minor type choice in the recursion safeguard.

The patch itself prevents infinite loops when a repository is configured to lazy-fetch from itself or from another repository that in turn lazy-fetches back. It introduces `GIT_INTERNAL_LAZY_FETCH_DEPTH`, an environment variable that tracks recursion depth, and aborts when it exceeds `MAX_LAZY_FETCH_DEPTH` (set to 5). The implementation is minimal, well-tested, and directly addresses Junio’s earlier concern about recursion risks. The only feedback in this email is Junio’s suggestion to use `int` instead of `unsigned long` for the depth counter, a purely stylistic nit that does not affect functionality. The patch remains a clean, uncontroversial fix and is ready for integration once the type is adjusted.
