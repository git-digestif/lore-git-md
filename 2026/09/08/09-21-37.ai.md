This is a surface-level review reply to patch 2/11 in Patrick Steinhardt’s v2 series standardizing Git’s terminology for "ref storage format." The patch renames `--ref-format=` to `--ref-storage-format=` in `git clone`, retaining the old name as a hidden backward-compatible alias via `OPT_STRING_F` with `PARSE_OPT_HIDDEN`.

Kaartic Sivaraam’s feedback is purely editorial: he suggests either deprecating the old `--ref-format=` option (with a warning) or using `OPT_ALIAS` to clarify its status, echoing a question he raised earlier in the thread about the backward-compatibility strategy for the entire series. He also spots a typo in the test script (`--ref-storage` should be `--ref-storage-format`) and confirms the rest of the patch looks correct. No technical objections or edge-case concerns are raised; the feedback is about long-term maintainability and user communication rather than functionality. The typo fix is trivial and will likely be incorporated in a future revision.
