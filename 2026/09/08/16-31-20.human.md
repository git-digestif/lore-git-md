This is a documentation-adjacent patch in the advice subsystem, part of an ongoing thread to fix the on-screen instructions for disabling the `defaultBranchName` advice. The patch itself is small and uncontroversial in its goal: it ensures the displayed command recommends `--global` instead of `set` so the setting applies globally rather than just to the current repository.

Junio’s reply raises two design questions. First, he suggests renaming the new `global_hint` field to something like `config_scope` or `scope_hint` to accommodate future expansion (e.g., suggesting `--system` scope). Second, he proposes narrowing the interface by passing the entire `advice_setting` struct to `vadvise()` rather than individual fields, making the function more future-proof and self-contained. Neither suggestion is a blocker; both are structural improvements that would make the patch cleaner if adopted.

The thread remains under review, with no objections to the core change. The unresolved question is purely about the preferred syntax in the displayed command (`--global` vs `set --global`), which is a matter of taste rather than correctness. The patch looks well-motivated and low-risk, touching only the advice machinery in `advice.c` and leaving on-disk formats unchanged.
