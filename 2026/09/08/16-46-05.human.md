This is the **second patch in Christian Couder’s five-part series** introducing `uploadpack.lazyFetchTrusted`, a server-side configuration to control lazy fetching. The patch is a **pure refactoring** that extracts the path-matching logic from Git’s `safe.directory` mechanism into a reusable helper, `path_allowlist_apply()`, in `setup.c`. This prepares the ground for the later patches in the series, which will reuse this helper to implement the new `uploadpack.lazyFetchTrusted` configuration.

The patch itself is **uncontroversial and well-motivated**. It consolidates duplicated path-matching code, eliminates nested conditionals, and makes the logic more modular. The extracted function handles wildcards (`*`), single-dot paths (`.`), and absolute paths, mirroring the behavior of `safe.directory` but without tying it to a specific configuration variable. This refactoring is a clear improvement, and the commit message does a good job of explaining the rationale.

He proposed renaming the `is_match` output parameter to `matches` for clarity, and Christian incorporated this change in the v3 resend. The review did not engage with the patch’s integration into the broader series or raise any substantive concerns, which is consistent with the patch’s straightforward nature.

This patch is **ready to be integrated early** if the series as a whole stalls, as it stands on its own and does not depend on the later patches.
