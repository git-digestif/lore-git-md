This is the sixth patch in the v15 `git history squash` series, implementing **ref protection** to prevent local branches from being left dangling after a squash operation. The patch adds logic to detect branches that descend from the squashed range but do not include its tip, and rejects the operation unless `--update-refs=head` is specified. This is a **safety mechanism** that aligns with the series' goal of avoiding silent data loss during history rewrites.

The implementation uses `ref-filter` to enumerate local branches (`FILTER_REFS_BRANCHES`) that would become unreachable after the squash. It sorts the blocking branches alphabetically and prints their short names in the error message, along with an `advice()` hint pointing to `--update-refs=head`. The protection is limited to local branches, matching the scope of the default ref-update behavior in other history-rewriting commands. The patch also introduces a new advice key, `ADVICE_HISTORY_UPDATE_REFS`, to guide users toward the `--update-refs=head` escape hatch.

The test coverage verifies both the detection logic (branches that would be left dangling are correctly identified and listed) and the advice suppression (disabling `advice.historyUpdateRefs` silences the hint). The patch is **well-motivated**: it addresses a real risk (silent branch detachment) with a clear, user-friendly mitigation. The code is cleanly integrated, reusing existing `ref-filter` infrastructure and following Git's established patterns for ref protection (e.g., `git rebase --update-refs`).

This is a **substantive, safety-critical feature** that complements the series' core functionality. The design choice—rejecting the operation by default rather than silently detaching branches—reflects the project's conservative stance on history rewrites.
