This is the first patch in the v8 series introducing the `receive-report` hook. It is a **documentation patch** that plugs a gap in the `git-receive-pack` man page by adding an eight-line section about the existing `proc-receive` hook. The change is purely additive and does not affect code or behavior.

The new `PROC-RECEIVE HOOK` section explains that the hook is invoked when the server has configured `receive.procReceiveRefs` and the push commands match those refs. It clarifies that the hook, not the internal `execute_commands()` function, is then responsible for updating references and reporting results back to `receive-pack`. The section links to `git-receive-pack(1)` for context, following the style of other hook documentation in the same file.

This patch addresses a prior omission noted in review and carries a `Helped-by` credit to Patrick Steinhardt, who suggested the addition. The commit message is concise and accurately describes the change. No controversy or open questions remain; the patch is ready for integration.
