The Git Contributor's Summit 2026 is being organized for September 18th in Lisbon, co-located with Git Merge. Taylor Blau sent the initial announcement, confirming the event's logistics—venue, timing, and AV setup for remote participation. A shared Google Sheet was set up for topic proposals, but access was initially restricted. Taylor has now clarified that the sheet is available to all attendees with a summit ticket; anyone without access should contact him directly. So far, the thread remains in the logistical phase, with no technical discussions or topic proposals yet submitted. The next step is for participants to add their ideas to the sheet.
