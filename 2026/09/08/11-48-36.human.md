This is the final acknowledgment in the `receive-report` hook series, now at version 8. Karthik Nayak sends a brief thank-you to Patrick Steinhardt, confirming receipt of Patrick’s approval for v7. The series—four patches introducing a new `receive-report` hook for server-side status filtering—is feature-complete, queued in `next`, and ready for the next release.

The hook lets GitLab implement MVCC by allowing the server to report a push as failed (`ng`) while still committing ref updates, a deliberate divergence from Git’s traditional consistency model.
