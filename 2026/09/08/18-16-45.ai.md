This is an announcement email for the Git Contributor's Summit 2026, scheduled to take place in Lisbon on September 18th alongside Git Merge.

Taylor Blau shares the event details: the summit will run from 10am to 4pm (UTC+1) at Audrey's, Santiago de Alfama, with lunch provided. The event is open to Git contributors and related projects, with remote participation possible via AV setup—interested attendees should contact Taylor off-list for details. The full Git Merge schedule is available on the conference website.

The summit will follow an unconference format, with topics proposed and voted on by attendees. A shared Google Sheet is provided for adding discussion topics, including name, description, and relevant details. No formal presentation is required; open questions or feedback requests are welcome. Voting and note-taking will be handled during the event.

This email serves as both an invitation and a call for topic proposals, with no prior technical discussion or patch context. The tone is logistical and inclusive, aimed at coordinating participation rather than debating code or features.
