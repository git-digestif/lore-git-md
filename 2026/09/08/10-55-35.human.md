This is the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s v2 series to standardize Git’s terminology for ref storage. The patch mechanically renames `--ref-format=` to `--ref-storage-format=` in the `git refs migrate` command, retaining the old option as a hidden backward-compatible alias. The change is uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ goal of eliminating inconsistent terminology before introducing an analogous `--object-storage-format=` option.

Kaartic Sivaraam’s review is surface-level and tangential. He questions the `PARSE_OPT_NONEG` flag on the new `--ref-storage-format=` option, noting that other commands in the series do not use it. His observation is purely about consistency; he does not argue the flag is harmful or that the option should accept a `--no-ref-storage-format` negation. The rest of the patch—renaming the option, updating the help text, and retaining the backward-compatible alias—meets his approval.

The patch itself touches six files: the `git-refs` man page, an error message in `builtin/fetch.c`, the core implementation in `builtin/refs.c`, and three test scripts. No functional changes are introduced; this is a straightforward refactoring with no known edge cases or regressions. The backward-compatible alias ensures scripts using the old option name continue to work.
