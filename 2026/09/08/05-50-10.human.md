This is the latest review exchange in Thomas Bachem’s patch series that defers auto maintenance until the end of sequencer-driven operations (`git rebase`, `git cherry-pick`, `git revert`). The series is now in its third iteration and addresses all prior technical feedback.

Patrick Steinhardt responds to Thomas’s proposal to move the `run_auto_maintenance()` call out of the sequencer and into the built-in commands (`builtin/rebase.c` and `builtin/revert.c`). This would consolidate maintenance into a single exit path per command, matching the apply backend’s design and simplifying the rule to "the command runs maintenance once when done." Patrick’s main concern is not the design itself—he seems open to the approach—but the quality of the commit message. He stresses that the message must clearly explain the design decisions and their impact on other subsystems, warning against LLM-generated text that lacks meaningful context or human insight. His feedback is procedural rather than technical: he wants the commit message to demonstrate the author’s understanding and to serve as a useful record for future readers.
