This is an announcement for the Git Contributor's Summit 2026, scheduled for Friday, September 18th in Lisbon, alongside the Git Merge conference. Taylor Blau invites all Git contributors (and related projects) to attend in person at Audrey's, Santiago de Alfama, with remote participation available for those who cannot travel. The event runs from 10am to 4pm (UTC+1), including a lunch break from 1:30–2:45pm.

Attendees are encouraged to propose discussion topics in advance via a shared Google Sheet, with no formal preparation required—open questions or feedback requests are welcome. The summit follows the unconference format, where topics will be voted on and prioritized during the event itself. Taylor notes that the venue has AV capabilities for remote participants, though details will be shared closer to the date.

The announcement is straightforward and logistical, aimed at coordinating participation rather than proposing technical changes. If you’re a contributor planning to attend Git Merge, this is a heads-up to mark your calendar and suggest topics of interest. No prior action is needed beyond adding to the topic sheet if you have something to discuss.
