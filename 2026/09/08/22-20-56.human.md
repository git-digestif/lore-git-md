This is the second patch in a two-part bugfix series for `--force-if-includes` in `git push`. The series addresses a long-standing flaw where the safety check incorrectly consults the reflog of the *local* branch matching the *remote* destination name, rather than the branch actually being pushed. This patch focuses on the user-facing advice when the check fails due to a detached HEAD state.

The problem: when pushing from a detached HEAD, the current advice suggests running `git pull`, which is irrelevant and misleading. The fix introduces a new `ref->unverifiable` flag to distinguish detached HEAD rejections from remote-update rejections, and adds a tailored advice message (`advice.pushRefUnverifiable`) that directs users to either specify the expected remote tip with `--force-with-lease=<ref>:<expect>` or bypass the check with `--no-force-if-includes`. The patch updates all transport layers (direct pushes, transport helpers, and send-pack) to handle the new rejection reason consistently, and adds test coverage to verify the new advice appears correctly.
