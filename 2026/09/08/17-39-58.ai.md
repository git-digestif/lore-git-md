This is a **substantive review** of the first patch in Christian Couder’s v3 series introducing `uploadpack.lazyFetchTrusted`. Junio’s feedback focuses on two points: the clarity of the new `try_promisor_remotes()` documentation and the subtle semantics of its parameters (`remaining_oids` and `to_free`).

The review identifies a **documentation ambiguity** in the proposed comment for `try_promisor_remotes()`. Junio finds the phrase *"from the (non-)accepted remotes"* confusing and suggests a clearer alternative that explicitly states the function’s behavior (fetching objects, not just checking) and its return value (true if all objects were fetched, false otherwise). He also flags that the comment omits how `remaining_oids` and `to_free` are used, noting that these parameters have non-obvious semantics—particularly `to_free`, which is tricky to document correctly across the call chain.

No objections are raised to the refactoring itself; Junio explicitly acknowledges that the change to avoid redundant `GIT_NO_LAZY_FETCH` checks and the removal of unnecessary `promisor_remote_init()` calls are improvements. The feedback is purely about **code clarity and documentation precision**, not about the patch’s correctness or design. This is a **surface-level but substantive** review: while the issues are minor, they affect maintainability and require careful wording to avoid future confusion. The patch is otherwise ready for integration once the documentation is clarified.
