This is a brief follow-up in Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch series refactoring `create_repository()` to enforce statelessness. The email is a reply to Karthik Nayak’s surface-level review of patch 6/7, which adjusts `repo_clear()` to fully reset the repository struct by replacing redundant `FREE_AND_NULL()` calls with plain `free()` followed by a single `memset()`.

Patrick clarifies the rationale for the change: the final `memset()` makes explicit NULL assignments redundant, so switching to plain `free()` avoids unnecessary work. He also corrects a minor inaccuracy in the commit message about "already-blank" repositories, though this does not affect the patch’s behavior. The cosmetic header-file reordering of `repo_clear()` next to `repo_init()` is confirmed as intentional but purely stylistic.

The exchange remains entirely surface-level, with no substantive discussion of edge cases, caller expectations, or the broader goal of enforcing statelessness in `create_repository()`. The series continues to look uncontroversial, but the lack of deeper technical engagement—particularly on patch 7/7, which introduces the core interface change—leaves open the possibility that undocumented caller assumptions could surface later.
