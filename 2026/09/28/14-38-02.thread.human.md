This thread is about a small but important fix to stabilize a flaky test in `t5520-pull.sh`. The test, which exercises `git pull --rebase -f`, was failing unpredictably because reflog entries—needed to compute the fork point—were expiring too quickly under the default `maintenance.reflog-expire.auto` setting. The test suite hard-codes commit dates to 2005, making reflog entries immediately eligible for expiration when `git maintenance` or `git gc` runs. Thomas Bachem authored a targeted fix that disables reflog expiration for the test's duration, following established precedent in the test suite.

The patch has been thoroughly reviewed and debugged by Junio C Hamano, D. Ben Knoble, and Phillip Wood, with no technical objections raised. D. Ben Knoble has proposed incorporating the patch directly into his stash-related series to expedite integration, reflecting its readiness for inclusion. The fix is uncontroversial and addresses the root cause identified in the regression report. The only remaining discussion is procedural: whether Junio will accept the direct pick into the stash series or prefer the patch to land independently via `seen`. Either way, the patch is poised for inclusion.
