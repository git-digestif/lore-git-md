This thread is about a small but important fix to stabilize a test in `t5520-pull.sh`. The test, which exercises `git pull --rebase -f`, was failing unpredictably because reflog entries it relied on were expiring too quickly under Git's default maintenance settings. The issue surfaced after a recent change made reflog cleanup more aggressive, exposing the test's dependence on reflog entries that were immediately eligible for expiration due to hard-coded 2005 commit dates.

Thomas Bachem authored the fix, which disables reflog expiration for the duration of the test by setting `gc.reflogExpire=never` and `gc.reflogExpireUnreachable=never`. The approach follows established precedent in the test suite. Junio C Hamano initially reported the regression and later integrated the patch into the synthetic merge base (`4d7270214a`) for D. Ben Knoble’s `dk/stash-apply-index-incore` series, resolving the procedural question of how to land it. Ben Knoble and Phillip Wood assisted in debugging, and all participants agreed the fix was appropriate.

The patch is now effectively merged for integration testing, and the thread is closed. No further discussion or changes are expected.
