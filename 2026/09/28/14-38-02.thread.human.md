This thread is about a simple but important fix to a flaky test in `t5520-pull.sh`. The test, which exercises `git pull --rebase -f`, was failing unpredictably because reflog entries it relied on were being expired too aggressively under the default `maintenance.reflog-expire.auto` setting. Thomas Bachem proposed a targeted fix—disabling reflog expiration for the test's duration by setting `gc.reflogExpire` and `gc.reflogExpireUnreachable` to `never`—following precedent in the test suite.

The patch has been thoroughly vetted: Junio C Hamano reported and bisected the regression, and both D. Ben Knoble and Phillip Wood assisted in debugging. There are no objections or open questions; the fix is uncontroversial and addresses the root cause. Ben Knoble’s recent reply suggests a pragmatic path forward—incorporating the patch directly into his stash-related series to avoid waiting for it to land in `seen`. This reflects the patch’s readiness for inclusion, though the final call on logistics rests with Junio. The fix is minimal, touching only the test setup, and doesn’t affect user-visible behavior or on-disk format.
