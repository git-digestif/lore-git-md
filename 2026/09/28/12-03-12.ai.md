This is a **bugfix follow-up** to the final patch in D. Ben Knoble’s five-part series fixing `git stash` autostash with staged index entries. The email acknowledges a **critical NULL-dereference risk** identified by Junio C Hamano in Patch 5/5, where `merge_incore_nonrecursive()` may return with `result.tree` uninitialized on catastrophic failure (e.g., missing objects), leading to a SIGSEGV when `result.tree->object.oid` is dereferenced. The issue is a systemic gap in error handling: the patch assumes the merge will always succeed or fail cleanly, but the merge machinery can leave `result.tree` undefined on catastrophic failure.

The author confirms the problem and implies intent to fix it, though no specific solution is proposed in this reply. The fix will likely involve checking `result.clean` before dereferencing `result.tree`, possibly combined with a fallback to the original subprocess-based logic or an early error return. This is a **substantive correctness issue** that could affect stability in edge cases (e.g., corrupted object stores or concurrent operations), and it adds a second blocker to the series’ integration alongside the unrelated `t5520` test breakage.

The rest of the series remains uncontroversial, and the design (replacing subprocess-based merging with in-core `merge-ort.c`) is not in question. The author’s response is brief but confirms the issue is understood and will be addressed. No other feedback is discussed.
