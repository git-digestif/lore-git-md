Core problem or goal
Remove the obsolete `gittutorial-2` document and all references to it from the Git codebase to simplify the documentation landscape and enable future tutorial improvements. The series (v3) achieves this by removing `gittutorial-2` from `command-list.txt`, ensuring it no longer appears in the `git(1)` man page while maintaining doc-linter compatibility. The stub in `gittutorial-2.adoc` is preserved for manpage compatibility, explicitly directing users to modern alternatives (`gitdatamodel[7]` and `gitcore-tutorial[7]`). The procedural coordination with Tuomas Ahola’s `ta/command-list-guides-sync-lint` topic is finalized: Tuomas’s rerolled patch was stacked on this series to add `gittutorial-2` as a linter exemption directly in his patch, avoiding the need for an "evil merge."

Current status
Merged to `master` on 2025-06-10. Junio C Hamano confirmed the v3 resend addressed the last integration issue (removal from `command-list.txt`), marking the series as procedurally complete. The maintainer now flags a minor forward-looking merge conflict risk in `gittutorial.adoc` between the already-merged removal and Julia Evans’s planned rewrite of `gittutorial` from scratch. Open questions and loose ends
- **Future of `gittutorial`**: The thread’s unresolved question centers on whether the older `gittutorial` (part one) should be preserved or removed as part of the broader cleanup. Junio C Hamano suggests evaluating its utility: if it is actively misleading, it should not be recommended; if it remains reasonably useful despite its outdated approach (starting a project from a tarball), it could serve as a temporary stopgap until a modern replacement is ready. Julia Evans argues it duplicates basic topics (`git init`, `git add`, `git commit`) already covered by other documentation and is ineffective for teaching branching and collaboration due to its outdated structure. She favors shipping her new draft (covering basic commit/push workflows) incrementally, even if branching/collaboration are not yet included, to avoid delaying progress. No consensus has been reached.
- **Scope and sequencing of follow-up work**: Julia Evans plans to remove `gittutorial` and introduce a modern replacement in subsequent steps. The new draft is already tested with 22 beginners and covers basic commit/push workflows, but branching and collaboration are identified as critical gaps. The maintainer’s latest note requests that future `gittutorial` edits omit the `gittutorial-2` reference removal to avoid merge conflicts with the rewrite.
