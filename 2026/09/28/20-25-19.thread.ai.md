Core problem or goal
Remove the obsolete `gittutorial-2` document and all references to it from the Git codebase to simplify the documentation landscape and enable future tutorial improvements. The broader goal is to modernize the tutorial material: the current series (v2) removes `gittutorial-2` while preserving a minimal stub for manpage compatibility and redirecting users to modern alternatives (`gitdatamodel[7]` and `gitcore-tutorial[7]`). The build break in `seen` (caused by a missing entry in `command-list.txt`) was resolved by applying Tuomas Ahola’s proposed fix as a merge-fix. The side effect of the v2 changes—where `gittutorial-2` appeared in the `git(1)` man page under the GUIDES section as "Obsolete tutorial"—has been resolved by Junio C Hamano via a procedural adjustment: ejecting both Julia’s series and Tuomas’s `ta/command-list-guides-sync-lint` topic from the integration branches, re-merging Julia’s series without adding `gittutorial-2` to `command-list.txt`, and then re-queuing Tuomas’s topic with an "evil merge" that adds `gittutorial-2` back to `command-list.txt` for doc-linter compatibility while keeping it out of the `git(1)` man page. Subsequent work is planned to remove the older `gittutorial` and introduce a new, modern replacement, focusing on current needs (e.g., basic commit/push workflows, branching, collaboration) and avoiding duplication with existing documentation (`gitdatamodel[7]` and `gitcore-tutorial[7]`).

Open questions and loose ends
- **Future of `gittutorial`**: The thread now centers on whether the older `gittutorial` (part one) should be preserved or removed as part of the broader cleanup. Junio C Hamano suggests evaluating its utility rather than dismissing it outright: if it is actively misleading, it should not be recommended even temporarily; if it remains reasonably useful despite its old-fashioned approach (starting a project from a tarball), it could serve as a temporary stopgap until a modern replacement is ready. Julia Evans argues it duplicates basic topics (`git init`, `git add`, `git commit`) already covered by other documentation and is ineffective for teaching branching and collaboration due to its outdated structure and lack of foundational explanations. She favors shipping her new draft (covering basic commit/push workflows) incrementally, even if branching/collaboration are not yet included, to avoid delaying progress. No consensus has been reached on whether `gittutorial` should be kept temporarily or removed outright.
- **Scope and sequencing of follow-up work**: Julia Evans plans to remove `gittutorial` and introduce a modern replacement in subsequent steps. She is open to combining these into a single series but does not feel strongly about the order. The new draft is already tested with 22 beginners and covers basic commit/push workflows, but branching and collaboration are identified as critical gaps that will require additional work. No specific timeline or scope for the replacement is outlined beyond avoiding duplication with `gitdatamodel[7]` and `gitcore-tutorial[7]`.
