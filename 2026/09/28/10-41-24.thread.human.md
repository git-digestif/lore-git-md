This thread is about converting Git’s plain-text `BreakingChanges` document into a proper manpage, `gitbreaking-changes(7)`, to make upcoming breaking changes more visible to users. Kristoffer Haugsbakk proposed the change in a five-patch series, now at v2, which is ready for integration after addressing all reviewer feedback.

The series restructures the document in three steps: first adding manpage boilerplate, then renaming the file and integrating it into the build system (this latest patch), and finally formatting URLs for better readability. The v2 update incorporated all feedback, including replacing message-ID references with plain URLs to avoid rendering issues, reordering sections for clarity, and dropping a reference to `git(1)` due to a parallel change. The only remaining task is resolving trivial merge conflicts with two other topics (`ps/ref-storage-format` and `bc/restrict-hex-to-lowercase`) during integration.

Key participants—Kristoffer Haugsbakk, Patrick Steinhardt, and Junio C Hamano—have all endorsed the series’ goals and technical approach. Junio initially proposed a hybrid URL format but ultimately agreed to plain URLs after testing revealed usability issues. The series is now technically complete, with no open questions or loose ends. The new manpage will be accessible via `git help` and `man`, improving discoverability of Git’s breaking changes.
