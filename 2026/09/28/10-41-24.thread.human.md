The `gitbreaking-changes(7)` RFC series is converting Git’s plain-text `BreakingChanges` document into a proper manpage to improve user discoverability. The series, authored by Kristoffer Haugsbakk, consists of four patches and has been under review as an RFC. All substantive feedback has been addressed—including section reordering, formatting fixes, and lifecycle clarifications—except for a narrow disagreement about how to format message-ID references in the manpage.

The last remaining technical hurdle was resolved in this exchange: Junio C Hamano conceded that his proposed hybrid URL+message-ID format (e.g., `cf. https://lore.kernel.org/git/xmqqa59i45wc.fsf@gitster.g/[<xmqqa59i45wc.fsf@gitster.g>^]`) is unworkable due to Asciidoctor rendering the message-ID portion as a broken `mailto:` link in HTML output. He accepted Kristoffer’s preference for plain URLs (e.g., `cf. https://lore.kernel.org/git/xmqqa59i45wc.fsf@gitster.g/`), prioritizing usability over future-proofing. With this consensus, the series is now technically aligned, and a v2 is expected to follow. The broader goal—making breaking changes more visible via `git help` and `man`—remains uncontroversial and on track. Key participants (Patrick Steinhardt, Junio, and Kristoffer) are all supportive of the approach.
