This thread is about converting Git’s plain-text `BreakingChanges` document into a proper manpage, `gitbreaking-changes(7)`, to make upcoming breaking changes more discoverable to users via `git help` and `man`. The series, now at v2, is technically complete and ready for integration, with all reviewer feedback incorporated. Kristoffer Haugsbakk is the author, and the patches touch documentation files (`gitbreaking-changes.adoc`, `BreakingChanges.adoc`, `git.txt`, `command-list.txt`) and build system updates (`Makefile`, `meson.build`), but no code or on-disk formats.

The main discussion points have been resolved: the document’s structure, the relocation of the "Adding new items" section, and the formatting of links (plain URLs were chosen over message-IDs for usability). The series awaits maintainer integration, which will require resolving minor merge conflicts with `ps/ref-storage-format` (in `master`) and `bc/restrict-hex-to-lowercase` (in `seen`). Reviewers—including Patrick Steinhardt and Junio C Hamano—have endorsed the series, though Junio initially pushed for a hybrid URL+message-ID format before conceding to plain URLs. D. Ben Knoble’s feedback was supportive but non-technical, reinforcing the goal of improving discoverability.

Kristoffer’s latest email is a minor follow-up, restating the trade-off between raw message-IDs and clickable URLs but confirming the series is ready as-is. No further changes are needed from the author, and the core goal of better user visibility remains unchanged. The series is under review, with no evidence of merge to any integration branch.
