This thread is about converting Git’s plain-text `BreakingChanges` document into a proper manpage, `gitbreaking-changes(7)`, to make upcoming breaking changes more visible to users via `git help` and `man`. Kristoffer Haugsbakk proposed the change in a four-patch RFC series, which has now cleared all technical and formatting hurdles after review from Patrick Steinhardt and Junio C Hamano.

The series is ready for a v2: all substantive feedback—section reordering, formatting, a three-step restructuring plan, and the post-Git-3.0 lifecycle—has been addressed. The last open question, how to format message-ID references, was resolved in favor of plain URLs (e.g., `https://lore.kernel.org/git/xmqqa59i45wc.fsf@gitster.g/`) after Junio conceded that his proposed hybrid URL+message-ID format caused usability issues in HTML output. Kristoffer’s follow-up email suggests a footnote-based compromise to preserve raw message-IDs for email-only users, but this is a refinement, not a blocker. Both Patrick and Junio have endorsed the series’ goals and structure, and Kristoffer has accepted all feedback.

The next step is for Kristoffer to post v2, after which the series—now uncontroversial and technically aligned—can be queued for integration. The changes are documentation-only, touching `Documentation/`, `Makefile`, and `meson.build` to introduce the new manpage without altering any code, CLI options, or on-disk formats.
