This thread is an RFC patch series by Kristoffer Haugsbakk to improve the visibility of Git’s upcoming breaking changes by converting the existing plain-text `BreakingChanges` document into a proper manpage, `gitbreaking-changes(7)`. The goal is to make this information more accessible to end users via `git help` and `man`, while clarifying its living-draft nature to prevent confusion from outdated versions.

The series consists of four patches, all now posted for review. The first converts the document into a manpage template, preserving its content while adapting it to manpage conventions. The second replaces commit-message-style message-ID references with clickable URLs to `lore.kernel.org`. The third adds a note clarifying the document’s living-draft status, and the fourth—just summarized—updates `git(1)`’s "SEE ALSO" section to reference the new manpage, completing the integration. The build system and `command-list.txt` are also updated to include the new manpage. No behavior changes or new symbols are introduced; the series is documentation-only.

No substantive feedback has been posted yet, as the thread is still in the early RFC stage. The author is seeking input on the overall approach, particularly the usability improvements like clickable URLs and the living-draft clarification. The final patch is a straightforward and uncontroversial step, ensuring the new manpage is discoverable from the main `git` documentation.
