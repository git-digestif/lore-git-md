This is the fifth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch refactoring series that simplifies the `create_repository()` interface. The patch aligns `git clone` with `git init` by ensuring that leading directories are created with the user’s umask rather than being influenced by the `core.sharedRepository` configuration.

The change is small and mechanical: two calls to `safe_create_leading_directories_const()` in `builtin/clone.c` are replaced with `safe_create_leading_directories_no_share_const()`, a variant introduced earlier in the series that ignores the shared-repository setting. The patch also adds comprehensive tests in `t/t1301-shared-repo.sh` to verify that both `git init` and `git clone` now behave identically—creating leading directories with permissions dictated by the umask, not by `core.sharedRepository`.

The motivation is straightforward: the previous divergence between the two commands was unintentional and inconsistent. By making both commands ignore `core.sharedRepository` for leading directories, the patch removes a subtle behavioral difference that could surprise users. The tests confirm that the repository itself still respects the shared-repository setting, just not the intermediate directories.

This patch is a clean, uncontroversial step in the series. It touches only `builtin/clone.c` and the test file, and the change is well-explained and well-tested.
