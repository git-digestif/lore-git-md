This is a documentation effort focused on pedagogy—the ongoing rewrite of Git’s tutorial to simplify its content. Julia Evans responds to Junio C Hamano’s concern about external links and outdated documentation by proposing a two-phase approach: first, a patch series that removes the current tutorial and all references to it, and second, a separate series that introduces the new, simplified version. The proposal keeps the rewrite self-contained and avoids relying on external links, addressing Junio’s preference for maintainable, in-tree documentation.
