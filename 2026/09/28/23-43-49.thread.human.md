Isabella Caselli, an undergraduate student, has proposed adding an `includeIf.hostname:<pattern>` condition to Git’s configuration system to let users share dotfiles across machines by conditionally including config files based on hostname. The idea has drawn early but constructive feedback, with no objections so far. Jeff King (Peff) confirmed the feature is reasonable and not currently being worked on, while Ignacio Encinas—whose 2024 hostname-normalization series stalled after v3—chimed in to clarify that his effort was abandoned due to complexity, not project pushback, leaving the door open for Isabella to take it forward.

The core technical questions remain unresolved: whether the condition should match short hostnames, fully qualified domain names, or both, and whether to support globbing, regex, or exact matching. Peff suggested exposing Git’s view of the hostname via `git var GIT_HOSTNAME` as a debugging aid, and the 2024 discussion consensus—documenting system dependency rather than enforcing normalization—still stands. No patches have been submitted yet, and Isabella hasn’t indicated whether she plans to implement the feature. If she does, the work would touch `config.c`, extend the `includeIf` test suite, and update documentation, with no new CLI options or on-disk format changes expected.
