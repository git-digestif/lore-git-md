Isabella Caselli’s three-patch series implementing `includeIf.hostname:<pattern>` for Git’s configuration system is now complete and under review. The feature allows users to conditionally include Git configuration files based on the machine’s hostname, addressing the common need to share dotfiles across multiple machines with machine-specific adjustments. The implementation extends Git’s existing `includeIf` mechanism, following the design of `includeIf.onbranch:` with exact, case-sensitive matching and standard glob wildcards. A new `git var GIT_HOSTNAME` debugging aid was added early in the series, as suggested by Jeff King, and the hostname resolution remains system-dependent (short name or FQDN) without normalization, aligning with the 2024 consensus reinforced by Junio Hamano.

The series has addressed all prior feedback from the RFC phase, including test consistency fixes to ensure `git gc` and its tests agree on the hostname value. Junio’s review of the final patch was brief but positive, calling the implementation “neat” for its reuse of existing helpers. No substantive objections have been raised, and the only remaining open question is whether hostname matching should be case-sensitive (current behavior), case-insensitive, or support a `hostname/i:` variant. Isabella has noted the question but not yet proposed a specific change. The series builds directly on Ignacio Encinas’ 2024 work with his explicit permission and appears ready for broader review.
