Core problem or goal: Make Git's upcoming breaking changes more discoverable to end users by moving the existing plain-text `BreakingChanges` document into a manpage (`gitbreaking-changes(7)`), ensuring it is accessible via `git help` and `man`, and integrating it into the documentation build system.
