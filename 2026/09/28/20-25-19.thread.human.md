This thread is about the removal of the obsolete `gittutorial-2` document from Git’s documentation, a cleanup effort led by Julia Evans. The patch series (v3) was merged to `master` on June 10, 2025, removing `gittutorial-2` from `command-list.txt` and preserving a minimal stub in `gittutorial-2.adoc` to direct users to modern alternatives like `gitdatamodel[7]` and `gitcore-tutorial[7]`. The series also updated cross-references in five other documentation files to point to `gitdatamodel[7]` instead of the obsolete tutorial.

The procedural follow-up work is nearly complete. Tuomas Ahola’s rerolled patch (`ta/command-list-guides-sync-lint`) adds `gittutorial-2` as a linter exemption in `command-list.txt` and introduces two new guides (`gitformat-loose(5)` and `gitpacking(7)`). Junio C Hamano has now requested clarification on whether Tuomas’s patch reflects the agreed-upon rebase on top of the removal series. Once this is resolved, the linter exemption will be finalized, marking the end of the procedural work for this thread.

The broader question of whether to remove or preserve the older `gittutorial` (part one) remains unresolved. Julia Evans argues it duplicates basic topics and is ineffective for teaching branching and collaboration, while Junio suggests evaluating whether it is actively misleading or merely outdated but still useful. Julia plans to introduce a modern replacement incrementally, though the scope and timeline for this work are not yet defined. Junio has flagged a minor merge conflict risk in `gittutorial.adoc` between the already-merged removal and Julia’s planned rewrite, requesting that future edits omit the `gittutorial-2` reference removal to simplify integration. No further action is needed on the `gittutorial-2` removal itself.
