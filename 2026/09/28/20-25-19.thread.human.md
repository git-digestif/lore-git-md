This thread began with Julia Evans’s patch series to remove the obsolete `gittutorial-2` document, which was merged into `master`. The series cleaned up 27 files, primarily in `Documentation/`, and redirected users to modern alternatives like `gitdatamodel[7]` and `gitcore-tutorial[7]`. However, a follow-up patch was needed to fix a build break in the `seen` branch caused by a missed reference in `includes/cmd-config-section-all.adoc` and a doc-lint failure in `Documentation/lint-manpages.sh`.

The immediate issue is now clearer: the doc-lint check in `seen` expects `gittutorial-2` to be listed in `command-list.txt` due to its inclusion in `MAN_GUIDES` (added in a recent commit). Since the tutorial was removed, the linter fails. Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s review pinpoints this as a mechanical problem, and the fix will likely involve either removing `gittutorial-2` from `MAN_GUIDES` or adjusting the linter to handle its absence. This should be resolved quickly with no impact on the broader cleanup effort.

The bigger open question remains the fate of the older `gittutorial` (part one). Junio C Hamano has noted that it is still reasonably useful, while Julia Evans argues it duplicates basic topics already covered elsewhere and could cause confusion. No consensus has been reached on whether to keep it temporarily (e.g., for comparison during the development of a replacement) or remove it outright. Julia plans to address this in subsequent work, along with introducing a modern replacement, but the scope and sequencing of these steps are still flexible. For now, the focus is on fixing the build break so integration testing can proceed.
