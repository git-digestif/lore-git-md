This thread is about the removal of the obsolete `gittutorial-2` document from Git’s documentation, a cleanup effort led by Julia Evans. The goal was to simplify the documentation landscape by removing outdated material and redirecting users to modern alternatives like `gitdatamodel[7]` and `gitcore-tutorial[7]`.

The key discussion now centers on whether the older `gittutorial` (part one) should also be removed or temporarily preserved. No consensus has been reached, but Julia plans to introduce a new draft tutorial covering basic commit/push workflows incrementally. The new draft has already been tested with 22 beginners, though branching and collaboration remain gaps for future work.
