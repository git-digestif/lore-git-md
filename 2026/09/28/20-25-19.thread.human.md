This thread is about the removal of the obsolete `gittutorial-2` document from Git’s documentation. Julia Evans authored a three-patch series (now at v3) to clean up references to `gittutorial-2`, simplify the documentation landscape, and pave the way for future tutorial improvements. The series was merged to `master` on June 10, 2025, after addressing the last procedural hurdle—removing `gittutorial-2` from `command-list.txt` to prevent it from reappearing in the `git(1)` man page.

The cleanup touches eight files in the `Documentation/` directory, removing all functional references to `gittutorial-2` while preserving a minimal stub in `gittutorial-2.adoc` that explicitly marks the document as obsolete and directs users to modern alternatives like `gitdatamodel[7]` and `gitcore-tutorial[7]`. The series also updates cross-references in five other documentation files to point to `gitdatamodel[7]` instead of `gittutorial-2[7]`. The procedural coordination with Tuomas Ahola’s related topic was finalized by stacking his rerolled patch on Julia’s series, avoiding the need for an "evil merge."

While the `gittutorial-2` removal is now complete, the thread leaves open the future of the older `gittutorial` (part one). Julia argues that it duplicates basic topics already covered elsewhere and is ineffective for teaching branching and collaboration, favoring its removal in favor of a modern replacement. Junio C Hamano suggested evaluating whether the older tutorial is actively misleading or merely outdated but still useful. Julia plans to address this in follow-up work, including introducing a new draft tutorial already tested with 22 beginners, though the scope and timeline for that effort remain undefined. For now, the focus is on incremental progress rather than waiting for a comprehensive replacement.
