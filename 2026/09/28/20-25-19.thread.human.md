This thread is about cleaning up Git’s documentation by removing the obsolete `gittutorial-2` manpage and its references. Julia Evans posted a three-patch series (v1) that deletes the document, removes cross-references from other manuals, and cleans up translations. The series is under review, with no objections so far.

The broader goal is to modernize Git’s tutorial material. Julia argues that `gittutorial-2` is redundant because its content—low-level object model exploration—is already covered by `gitdatamodel[7]` and `gitcore-tutorial[7]`. She clarified in a recent email that she won’t replace it with new content, as the existing documentation suffices. The discussion has since shifted to whether the older `gittutorial` (part one) should also be removed. Junio Hamano notes it’s still useful despite its dated approach, while Julia sees it as duplicative and potentially confusing. No decision has been made yet, and Julia plans follow-up work to address `gittutorial` and introduce a modern replacement, though the scope and timing remain open.
