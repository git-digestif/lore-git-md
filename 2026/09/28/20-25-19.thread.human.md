This thread is about cleaning up Git’s documentation by removing the obsolete `gittutorial-2` tutorial. Julia Evans proposed a three-patch series to delete the file, remove its cross-references from other documentation, and clean up translations. The series was merged, but a follow-up from Junio C Hamano revealed a build break in the `seen` integration branch: the doc-lint check still expects a link to `gittutorial-2` in `includes/cmd-config-section-all.adoc`, which was overlooked in the original series. This is a mechanical oversight, not a design issue, and will likely be fixed with a small patch to remove or update the link.

The broader goal remains modernizing Git’s tutorial material. The current series removed `gittutorial-2` as a first step, with plans to later remove the older `gittutorial` and introduce a new, streamlined replacement. Junio supports the cleanup but noted that `gittutorial` is still somewhat useful despite its dated approach, leaving its future unresolved. A procedural question about modifying `.po` files without introducing new translatable strings was raised but deferred to Jiang Xin, the i18n coordinator. The thread is now focused on fixing the build break, which should be straightforward and uncontroversial.
