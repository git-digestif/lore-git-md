This is the second patch in Julia Evans's three-part documentation cleanup series to remove the obsolete `gittutorial-2` document. It focuses on eliminating cross-references to `gittutorial-2` from other documentation files, replacing them with links to `gitdatamodel[7]` where appropriate.

The patch updates six files in the `Documentation/` directory, removing or replacing references to `gittutorial-2`. The most significant change is in `gittutorial.adoc`, where a lengthy explanation of Git's object database and index file (previously directing readers to `gittutorial-2`) is replaced with a brief note pointing to `gitdatamodel[7]`. The author notes this update is provisional, as `gittutorial` itself is slated for future removal. Other files (`MyFirstObjectWalk.adoc`, `git.adoc`, `gitcore-tutorial.adoc`, `gitcvs-migration.adoc`, and `gitglossary.adoc`) receive smaller, mechanical updates to remove the now-obsolete reference.

The changes are straightforward and align with the series' goal of simplifying Git's documentation landscape. The patch is uncontroversial and appears well-tested, with no functional behavior changes. The redirection to `gitdatamodel[7]` (a modern, pedagogically sound alternative added in 2024) ensures users are still guided to relevant material. This patch, combined with the series' first and third parts, completes the removal of `gittutorial-2` from the codebase.
