The commit message explains the motivation clearly: users, not developers, are the primary audience for breaking-change information, and making the document discoverable via `git help` or `man` ensures they can find it without digging through development channels.

The patch follows the established convention of double-spacing sentences in the `git(1)` manpage, matching the style used in a recent similar addition (the `gitdatamodel(7)` tutorial). No new content is introduced beyond the cross-reference; the focus is purely on integration.

This is a documentation-only change with no code or behavior impact.
