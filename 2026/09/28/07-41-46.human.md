This is a review reply to Patrick Monette’s two-patch series adding GPG commit signing to `git replay`. The reviewer, Patrick Steinhardt, notes a merge conflict with an in-flight patch series that introduces signing infrastructure for `git history`. While the two efforts target different commands, they touch overlapping code in the commit-creation layer, so Steinhardt suggests waiting for the `git history` series to land before rebasing and resubmitting. The rest of the series—error handling in `pick_regular_commit()` and the plumbing-style `-S` option—remains uncontroversial and well-motivated.
