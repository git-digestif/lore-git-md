This is a **bugfix follow-up** to the final patch in D. Ben Knoble’s five-patch series fixing `git stash` autostash with staged index entries. Junio C Hamano identifies a **critical NULL-dereference risk** in Patch 5/5, where `merge_incore_nonrecursive()` may return with `result.tree` uninitialized on catastrophic failure (e.g., missing objects), leading to a SIGSEGV when `result.tree->object.oid` is dereferenced. The author acknowledges the issue and confirms the fix will involve checking `result.clean` before dereferencing `result.tree`, following the pattern used by all other callers of `merge_incore_nonrecursive()`.

The series remains technically sound and addresses a real bug, but this oversight blocks integration until resolved. The fix is straightforward and uncontroversial, and the rest of the series—including the core design of replacing subprocess-based merging with in-core `merge-ort.c`—has already been reviewed and approved. Once this edge case is handled, the patches should be ready for `next`. The series is currently ejected from Junio’s `seen` branch pending this fix and an unrelated test breakage in `t5520-pull.sh`, now resolved by a separate patch.
