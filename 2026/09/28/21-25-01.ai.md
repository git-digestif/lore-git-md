This is a reply in the ongoing `gitmergeconflicts(7)` documentation series, specifically Junio C Hamano’s clarification to Julia Evans about the utility of `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD` in the context of the fifth patch (updating `git-cherry-pick.adoc`).

Junio corrects Julia’s misunderstanding that `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD` is only relevant for multi-commit cherry-picks. Instead, he explains that it is a general-purpose pseudo-ref available *any* time a cherry-pick stops due to conflict, regardless of whether one or multiple commits are being picked. The key insight is that `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD` serves the same role for cherry-picks as `MERGE_HEAD` does for merges: it provides a reliable, mechanical way to inspect the commit being applied without needing to recall the original command-line arguments. This aligns with the broader goal of making Git’s conflict-resolution workflows consistent and predictable across commands.

The exchange reinforces the earlier agreement that cherry-pick-specific details—particularly `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD` and the behavior of cleanly auto-resolved paths—must be retained in the `git-cherry-pick` man page rather than being moved to the new `gitmergeconflicts(7)` guide. Julia’s next revision will likely include a revised explanation of `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD` that reflects Junio’s clarification, ensuring users understand its general utility.
