This is the second patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s four-part RFC series converting Git’s `BreakingChanges` document into a proper manpage (`gitbreaking-changes(7)`). The patch replaces all message-ID references (e.g., `<abc1234@mail.gmail.com>`) with clickable URLs pointing to the corresponding threads on `lore.kernel.org`. Two message-IDs required URL encoding to avoid breaking AsciiDoc parsing.

The change is purely mechanical: every reference in the document is updated, and the surrounding explanatory text is tweaked to clarify that the links point to the `Message-ID` of the original email. No content is added or removed beyond the URL conversion. The motivation is usability—users no longer need to manually construct URLs from message-IDs, making it easier to jump directly to the relevant discussion.

This patch is a natural follow-up to the first patch, which moved the content into a manpage template. The series remains documentation-only, with no code or behavior changes. The RFC tag indicates the author is still seeking feedback on the overall approach, but this specific patch is uncontroversial and ready to land if the series is accepted.
