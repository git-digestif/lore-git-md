Core problem or goal
A test in `t5520-pull.sh` that exercises `git pull --rebase -f` relies on reflog entries to compute the fork point. Because the test suite hard-codes commit dates to 2005, reflog entries are immediately eligible for expiration under the default `maintenance.reflog-expire.auto` setting (100 entries), causing the test to fail unpredictably when the *reflog-expire* task of `git maintenance` or `git gc` runs. The goal is to stabilize the test by disabling reflog expiration for its duration. The regression was introduced by the geometric reflog expiry change (`452b12c2e0`) and is exacerbated by two upcoming changes: (1) D. Ben Knoble’s `git stash apply --index` series, which reduces reflog entries by merging the index in-core, and (2) auto-maintenance during `git rebase`, which triggers reflog expiration earlier in the rebase process.
