This is a **bugfix follow-up** in the `git stash` autostash series. D. Ben Knoble acknowledges Junio C Hamano’s observation that Patch 5/5 introduces a **verbosity regression**: the `merge_options` struct (`o`) is reused for two separate merges (index and worktree), but the `.verbosity` field is unconditionally set to `0` during the index merge and never restored, causing the subsequent worktree merge to lose its configured verbosity level. The author agrees to fix this by preserving the original verbosity setting for the worktree merge, matching the intended behavior of the original subprocess-based implementation (`diff-tree | apply --cached`), which was inherently quiet for the index but should not affect the worktree merge’s verbosity.

The fix is straightforward and uncontroversial: restore the original verbosity value after the index merge completes. This aligns with the principle of least surprise, ensuring user-configured verbosity (via `merge.verbosity` or `GIT_MERGE_VERBOSITY`) persists for the worktree merge. The adjustment is mechanical and does not require re-testing the entire series—only verifying that verbosity settings now behave as expected in both merges. The series remains **ready for integration** once this fix is applied, alongside the other pending issues (the `t5520` breakage and NULL-dereference risk in Patch 5/5).

- **Behavior change**: The original subprocess-based merge was quiet for the index but respected verbosity for the worktree. The new in-core merge machinery must replicate this by restoring the original verbosity after the index merge.
- **Impact**: Without the fix, scripts or workflows relying on verbose output for the worktree merge would break.
