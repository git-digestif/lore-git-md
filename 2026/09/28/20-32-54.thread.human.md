This thread is about a documentation patch by Julia Evans to rewrite the introduction of the `git(1)` man page, replacing outdated tutorial references with direct instructions for accessing Git’s help system. The patch, now in its second version, has been approved by D. Ben Knoble and incorporates all substantive feedback from Junio C Hamano and Kristoffer Haugsbakk. The goal is to improve discoverability for new users by guiding them toward actionable commands like `git push -h`, `git help push`, and `git help --guides`, while also favoring `git-scm.com/docs` over the less user-friendly `git.github.io/htmldocs/git.html`.

The patch itself is technically complete and uncontroversial, but Junio has requested a third iteration to clarify the commit message. Specifically, while the removal of `gittutorial` and `giteveryday` is well-justified, the patch also drops references to `user-manual`, `datamodel`, and `cli` without explanation. Junio recalls that the `cli` guide was added deliberately and wants the rationale for these removals documented. Once this minor update is made, the patch will be ready for integration. No further technical changes are needed, and the thread’s core design questions—balancing simplicity and functionality—are resolved.
