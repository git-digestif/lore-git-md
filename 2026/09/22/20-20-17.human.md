This is a documentation patch that modernizes cross-references in Git's man pages. Junio C Hamano has reviewed and approved the patch, confirming its focus and correctness. The change replaces informal plain-text references (e.g., "see EXAMPLES below") with AsciiDoc link syntax (`<<EXAMPLES,EXAMPLES>>`) across 38 files in the `Documentation/` directory, ensuring all cross-references are clickable in HTML-rendered man pages.

The patch is part of the ongoing documentation synopsis-style conversion led by Jean-Noël Avila. Junio’s review highlights the patch’s sharp focus on a single, well-defined issue: the inconsistency in cross-references that can lead to broken links when sections are moved or included from other files. The two-part AsciiDoc syntax (`<<target,text>>`) is used consistently to avoid this problem, even when the target is in the same file, for future-proofing.

Julia Evans provided thorough testing, including a script to build all man pages before and after the patch and verify no functional differences. Junio’s review confirms the rationale and implementation, particularly the need for the two-part syntax when referencing sections in included files. The patch is mechanical but well-motivated, with no substantive objections expected.

Junio has indicated he will queue the patch, meaning it is likely to appear in `seen` soon and progress toward `next` and eventually `master`. This is a low-risk, high-value improvement to documentation usability, particularly for web-based readers.
