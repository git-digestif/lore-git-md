This is a small feature patch series from Kristoffer Haugsbakk, currently stalled in Junio’s "What's cooking" report under the `kh/format-patch-range-diff-notes` topic. The series teaches `` `git format-patch` `` a new ``--[no-]range-diff-notes`` option so that users can choose different notes for the cover-letter range-diff than for the patches themselves. The first two patches are minor clean-ups (renaming a struct field and simplifying a helper); the third patch adds the actual user-facing knob. Junio marked it “Expecting a reroll” after an earlier round of feedback, and Kristoffer has just replied that he plans to refresh the series by the weekend. The change is narrowly scoped to the format-patch machinery and does not touch any on-disk formats or network protocols.
