This is the author’s reply to Patrick Steinhardt’s **substantive review** of the second patch in the v5 series that defers auto maintenance until the end of sequencer-driven operations (`git rebase`, `git cherry-pick`, `git revert`). The series is now **code-identical to v4**, with only commit messages and test annotations updated to address feedback.

Thomas **fully clarifies the design constraint** that Patrick’s alternative proposal overlooked: single-pick cases (`cherry-pick <commit>`, `revert <commit>`, and their `--continue`/`--skip` variants) **bypass `pick_commits()` entirely**, exiting early through `continue_single_pick()` or `skip_single_pick()`. This means the sequencer **cannot** reliably trigger maintenance at a single exit point, justifying the current design of delegating maintenance to the built-in commands (`builtin/rebase.c` and `builtin/revert.c`). The explanation is now prominently included in patch 2’s commit message.

The exchange is **constructive and decisive**: Thomas acknowledges Phillip Wood’s earlier approval of the current approach and offers to implement Patrick’s alternative (triggering maintenance in `pick_commits()`, the three early returns, and aborts) if Patrick prefers it. However, the **technical rationale for the current design is now settled**—the sequencer’s lack of a unified exit point is a structural limitation, not an oversight. No new objections have been raised, and the series remains on track for `next` unless Patrick insists on a rewrite.

Key details for readers:
- **Files touched**: `sequencer.c` (specifically `pick_commits()`, `continue_single_pick()`, `skip_single_pick()`), `builtin/rebase.c`, `builtin/revert.c`.
- **Behavior**: Auto maintenance runs **once at sequence completion** (after autostash is applied) via `GIT_CONFIG_PARAMETERS=maintenance.auto=false` during intermediate steps.
- **Test coverage**: Extended in `t/t3418-rebase-continue.sh` and `t/t3510-cherry-pick-sequence.sh` to verify maintenance runs only at the end, with annotations clarifying conflict triggers and sequence stops.
