This thread is about modernizing cross-references in Git’s man pages, replacing informal plain-text references (like "see EXAMPLES below") with formal AsciiDoc link syntax to improve HTML navigation. The patch, originally authored by Julia Evans, updates 38 man pages in the `Documentation/` directory, using the two-part AsciiDoc link form (`<<target,text>>`) to ensure consistent rendering across backends. The change is purely navigational—no functional differences in man page content—and was thoroughly tested by Julia and independently validated by Jeff King (Peff), who confirmed its reliability and documented edge cases with the short form (`<<target>>`), such as broken or nonsensical output in HTML.

The patch was approved by Junio C Hamano and has been queued in the `next` branch for integration. The most recent exchange in the thread focused on refining the commit message to better explain the necessity of the two-part form, addressing Junio’s editorial request. No technical changes were made, and the patch remains unchanged. The broader design question—whether to prioritize link stability (manual anchors) or readability (auto-generated anchors)—remains open but was not revisited in this discussion. The thread is effectively closed, with the patch expected to graduate to `master` in the near future. Kristoffer Haugsbakk offered a surface-level endorsement, but the substantive reviews from Julia, Junio, and Peff carried the most weight.
