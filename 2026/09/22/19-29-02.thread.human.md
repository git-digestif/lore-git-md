This thread is about a small but useful documentation improvement: modernizing cross-references in Git's man pages to make them clickable in HTML output. Julia Evans proposed the change, replacing informal plain-text references (like "see EXAMPLES below") with AsciiDoc link syntax (`<<target,text>>`) across 38 files in the `Documentation/` directory. The patch is mechanical but well-tested—Julia built all man pages before and after the change to confirm no functional differences, and Junio C Hamano approved it after spot-checking the diff.

The key detail here is the choice of the two-part AsciiDoc form (`<<target,text>>`) over the simpler single form (`<<target>>`). Julia clarified that while the single form sometimes works, it’s inconsistent (e.g., `<<REMOTES>>` might render as `[REMOTES]` in some contexts), so the two-part form is the pragmatic choice to avoid broken links. Junio agreed, and the patch is now queued for integration in the `next` branch. Kristoffer Haugsbakk chimed in with a brief, positive comment calling the change "an obvious idea in hindsight," but this was purely surface-level and didn’t affect the patch’s status. No further discussion or revisions are expected unless new concerns arise.
