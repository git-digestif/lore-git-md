This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing bugfix series for `git stash` autostash handling. The author, D. Ben Knoble, clarifies a minor implementation detail in response to his own earlier message. Specifically, he corrects a logic error in his proposed conditional reinitialization of merge options in `do_apply_stash()`: the condition should check `!has_index` (not `has_index`) to avoid redundant initialization when the index merge is not needed. This is a small but necessary adjustment to the patch’s edge-case handling, ensuring the merge options are only reset when appropriate. The correction aligns with the broader goal of making the in-core merge logic robust and race-condition-free, though it does not address the larger open questions about assertion safety or verbosity handling noted in the thread. The fix is uncontroversial and will likely be incorporated into the upcoming v2 submission.
