This is a **follow-up discussion** in the ongoing bugfix series for `git stash` autostash handling. The thread focuses on Phillip Wood’s substantive review of the second patch, which replaces subprocess-based index merging with in-core logic via `merge-ort.c`. D. Ben Knoble responds to three key technical concerns raised by Wood: merge algorithm consistency, assertion safety, and verbosity handling.

Knoble agrees to standardize on the `_ui` variant of the diff algorithm for index merging (matching the worktree merge) to avoid behavioral mismatches, though he initially questioned whether the choice would matter given the conflict results are discarded. On the `verbosity` field in `merge_options`, he opts to retain it for now, citing potential future use and a desire to keep the merge quiet, despite Wood’s observation that it is currently unused by the ort strategy. The most critical discussion revolves around the removed `assert()` calls in `merge-ort.c`—Knoble acknowledges the need to restore fixed ancestor/branch labels in `do_apply_stash()` to prevent crashes, and plans to add a test verifying graceful failure if the index merge fails. The exchange leaves open whether `merge_finalize()` should replace `clear_merge_options()` (Knoble is leaning toward conditional reinitialization) and confirms the author’s intent to address the remaining loose ends before a v2 submission.

The discussion advances the patch toward correctness but does not yet resolve all edge cases. The next steps will likely involve a revised implementation and test coverage for merge failures. Reviewers familiar with `merge-ort.c` (e.g., Elijah Newren) may weigh in further on the assertion and merge algorithm questions.
