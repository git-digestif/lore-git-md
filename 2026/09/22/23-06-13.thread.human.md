This thread is about a small but important CI fix for Git's Debian 12 build job. Johannes Schindelin identified an intermittent HTTP/2 authentication failure in the `t5559` test suite (subtests 15 and 16) that started appearing after the CI environment was upgraded from Debian 11 to Debian 12. The root cause is a behavior change in curl 7.88.1 (shipped with Debian 12), which treats early 401 responses as transport errors rather than allowing authentication retries—a bug fixed in curl 8.3.0.

The fix is minimal: skip the problematic tests in the Debian 12 CI job by setting `GIT_SKIP_TESTS` in `ci/lib.sh`. The patch doesn't touch production code or change Git's behavior; it's purely a CI workaround. Junio Hamano has now reviewed and approved the patch, calling the diagnosis "superb" and agreeing with the approach. He confirmed the patch doesn't need to be fast-tracked into the upcoming v2.56.0 release but should land in `master` soon to reduce developer friction. With no objections or open questions, this fix is ready to be merged.
