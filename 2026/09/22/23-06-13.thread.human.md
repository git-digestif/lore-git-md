This thread is about fixing an intermittent HTTP/2 authentication failure in Git's Debian 12 CI job, specifically in tests `t5559.15` and `t5559.16`. The issue stems from a bug in curl versions 7.88.0 through 8.3.0, where early 401 responses are treated as transport errors rather than allowing authentication retries. Johannes Schindelin initially proposed a workaround, which was refined into a version-range-aware runtime prerequisite (`HAVE_CURL_HTTP2_BUG`) that skips the affected tests on problematic curl versions.

The series is now complete with three patches. The first implements the version-range workaround, the second (just summarized) fixes a quoting bug in the prerequisite snippet that could cause the version check to fail silently, and the third extends the test framework to support here-documents in lazy prerequisites for better readability. Jeff King authored the quoting fix and here-doc support, while Junio C Hamano and Johannes Schindelin reviewed and approved the changes. All patches are confined to test infrastructure and don’t affect production code. The series is ready for integration, with no open questions or loose ends.
