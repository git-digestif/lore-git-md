This thread is about a targeted CI fix for a flaky test in Git’s Debian 12 CI job. Johannes Schindelin identified an intermittent HTTP/2 authentication failure in `t5559` (subtests 15 and 16) after the job was upgraded from Debian 11 to Debian 12. The issue stems from a behavior change in curl 7.88.1, where Apache’s early 401 response and stream closure are treated as a transport error rather than allowing an authentication retry—a problem fixed in curl 8.3.0.

Rather than upgrading curl (which would undermine the job’s purpose of testing older distributions), Johannes proposes a minimal workaround: skipping the problematic tests on Debian 12 by setting `GIT_SKIP_TESTS` in the CI environment. The patch is confined to `ci/lib.sh` and is well-justified, with clear references to the upstream curl fix and the affected tests. The regression was introduced in Git v2.56 but only affects CI, so it doesn’t require urgent inclusion in the release. The patch is part of a single-patch series (PR #2236) and appears ready for integration. No objections or further discussion have emerged so far.
