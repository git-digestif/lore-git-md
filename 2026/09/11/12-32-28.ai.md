This is the second patch in the `dk/use-nsec-runtime` series (v7, 2/3), a **no-op refactoring** of `repo_config_values_init()`. The patch reorders assignments in the function to group them by config section ("core", "sparse", "branch"), aligning the initialization with the `struct repo_config_values` declaration. This improves maintainability by making it easier to validate that all members are initialized, especially as new fields are added in the future.

The change is purely cosmetic and introduces no behavioral differences. It touches only `environment.c`, moving 12 lines of assignments into a more logical structure. The patch is **uncontroversial and self-contained**, with no dependencies on the rest of the series. Earlier feedback from Patrick Steinhardt and Junio Hamano focused on clarity and consistency, and this version addresses those concerns by explicitly labeling sections in comments.

This patch is ready to advance independently and is unaffected by the latent architectural constraint (the `repo_config_values()` limitation for non-`the_repository` repositories) discussed in the series cover letter. It resolves a long-standing readability issue in the codebase and is a clean precursor to future additions to `struct repo_config_values`. The conflict with `ty/repo-config-cleanups` (which removes comments in the same struct) is purely textual and will be trivial to resolve if both topics graduate.
