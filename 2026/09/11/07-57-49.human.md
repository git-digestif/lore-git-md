This is a review reply from Patrick Steinhardt on the first patch of Souma’s v2 series teaching `git history` to sign rewritten commits. The series aims to make `git history` respect GPG signing configuration and command-line options (`-S`, `--gpg-sign`) for operations like `drop`, `fixup`, `reword`, and `split`.

Patrick’s feedback here is minimal and surface-level: he suggests trimming the commit message slightly by removing redundant documentation of the `sign_commit` parameter’s behavior, since it is already clearly described in the code. The patch itself — a preparatory refactoring of the replay API to support signing — meets his approval. No substantive concerns are raised, and the series remains on track for further review.
