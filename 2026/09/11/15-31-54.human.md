This is the first patch in a **bugfix series** targeting the `--force-if-includes` safety mechanism in `git push`. Junio C Hamano’s review raises **substantive questions** about the patch’s early returns and their implications, though he does not dispute the core fix itself.

The patch corrects a long-standing flaw where `--force-if-includes` incorrectly checks the reflog of the *local* branch matching the *remote* destination name (e.g., `refs/heads/main` when pushing to `refs/remotes/origin/main`), rather than the branch actually being pushed (e.g., `refs/heads/src` in `src:main`). This can cause false rejections or unintended data loss. The fix modifies `remote.c` to use `ref->peer_ref->name` (the pushed ref) instead of `ref->name` (the destination ref), with special handling for `HEAD`-based pushes and detached HEAD states.

Junio’s review focuses on three early returns in the updated `check_if_includes_upstream()` function: (1) when `remote->peer_ref` is missing, (2) when pushing a deletion, and (3) when resolving a detached HEAD. He questions whether these cases should silently allow the push (by returning early) or raise a `BUG()` if they represent programming errors. Notably, he observes that the test suite does not cover the deletion case, despite the patch’s comment justifying its behavior. His feedback is technical and precise, targeting edge-case robustness rather than the patch’s overall design.

The series is otherwise **ready for merge**, with prior reviews (including a **substantive review** from D. Ben Knoble) confirming the root cause and fix. This v3 is a procedural resend to correct threading, with no technical changes from v2. The patch includes 65 lines of new test coverage in `t/t5533-push-cas.sh`, covering mismatched local/remote names, `HEAD`-based pushes, and detached HEAD scenarios, but Junio’s questions suggest additional test cases may be needed to validate the early-return logic. The discussion is now focused on clarifying intent and ensuring the code’s behavior aligns with expectations.
