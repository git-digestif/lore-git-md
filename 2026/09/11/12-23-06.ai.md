This is patch 5/10 of Patrick Steinhardt’s ODB fsck pluggability series, responding to Toon Claes’s architectural review. The email is a brief but decisive reply: Patrick accepts Toon’s suggestion to move the `ODB_FSCK_FULL` filtering logic from the `odb_fsck()` helper into the backend-specific `fsck` callbacks. The rationale is twofold: it future-proofs the infrastructure for non-local backends (e.g., reftable, cloud storage), and it aligns with the planned move of alternate handling into the "files" backend, which would have forced the change anyway.

The change is narrow but consequential: the `odb_fsck()` helper will no longer filter sources based on `(options->flags & ODB_FSCK_FULL) || source->local`; instead, each backend’s `fsck` callback will decide for itself whether to run under the given flags. This shifts responsibility from the central helper to the individual sources, making the infrastructure more flexible and consistent with the series’ goal of pluggable ODB backends. The impact will ripple through patches 6–10, where backend-specific verification logic is moved into the sources, but the change is mechanical and does not alter user-visible behavior.

Key symbols affected: `odb_fsck()`, `odb_source_fsck_fn`, `ODB_FSCK_FULL`, and the `local` field in `struct odb_source`. The resolution is clear and uncontroversial, with no further discussion needed. The patch remains under review, but the architectural question is now settled.
