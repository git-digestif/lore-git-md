This is part six of Patrick Steinhardt’s ten-patch series refactoring Git’s object integrity verification (`fsck`) to make consistency checks pluggable per ODB backend. The series is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort and targets infrastructure without changing user-visible behavior.

This patch moves packfile verification from `builtin/fsck.c` into the packed ODB backend (`odb/source-packed.c`). The change threads the progress meter and object callback through the new `struct odb_fsck_options`, ensuring the packed backend respects caller preferences without exposing backend details. The patch also simplifies error reporting by replacing the undocumented `ERROR_PACK` bit with the generic `ERROR_OBJECT` bit, which is already used elsewhere in the codebase. The diff removes 33 lines from `cmd_fsck()` and adds 46 lines to `odb/source-packed.c`, with a small addition to `odb.h` to expose the progress flag and callback fields.

The patch is a pure refactoring that continues the pattern established in earlier patches. It has no unresolved feedback or open questions, and the changes align with the series’ goal of making fsck checks pluggable for future ODB backends. The patch is well-motivated, with a clear commit message explaining the rationale for dropping the `ERROR_PACK` bit and threading the progress meter through the new options structure. No behavior changes are introduced beyond the intentional simplification of error reporting.
