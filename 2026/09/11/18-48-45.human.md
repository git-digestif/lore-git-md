This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about adding a `--matched-only` option to `git range-diff`. The thread is focused on refining the design of the feature after Junio C Hamano proposed a more general refactoring of the existing `--left-only` and `--right-only` options.

Harald Nordgren responds to Junio’s suggestion by expressing concern about the long-term maintenance burden of deprecating options. He proposes an alternative: introduce `--hide-left` and `--hide-right` (without "only") as new flags that can be freely combined, and make `--matched-only` a simple shorthand for using both together. This approach avoids deprecation entirely while still achieving the same goal of cleaner, more composable CLI options. The tone is pragmatic, prioritizing simplicity and avoiding technical debt over strict backward compatibility.

The exchange is still in the design phase—no updated patch has been posted yet. The key question remains whether to adopt Junio’s refactor (with deprecation) or Harald’s lighter-weight alternative (without deprecation). Both approaches would deliver the same end-user functionality, but with different implications for the codebase’s future flexibility and maintenance.
