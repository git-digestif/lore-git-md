This is the final patch (10/10) in Patrick Steinhardt’s v3 series refactoring Git’s fsck checks to make them pluggable per ODB backend. It moves loose-object verification from `builtin/fsck.c` into the loose ODB backend (`odb/source-loose.c`), completing the series’ goal of relocating all backend-specific fsck logic out of the monolithic `cmd_fsck()` function.

The patch removes 90 lines from `builtin/fsck.c` and adds 89 lines to `odb/source-loose.c`, introducing the `ODB_FSCK_VERBOSE` flag to preserve the original behavior of printing status messages when `--verbose` is passed. It also fixes a latent bug in the progress-meter logic: the old code captured the progress meter in the callback data before it was initialized, causing the meter to jump from 0 to 256 at completion; the new code initializes the meter first, restoring incremental updates. The `fsck_loose()` helper is relocated and adapted to use a new `struct fsck_loose_data`, which consolidates callback data and error state, and the `ODB_FSCK_VERBOSE` and `ODB_FSCK_PROGRESS` flags are threaded through `struct odb_fsck_options` to ensure the loose backend respects caller preferences.

Toon Claes’s review raised two minor technical points that remain unresolved: whether `ODB_FSCK_VERBOSE` and `ODB_FSCK_PROGRESS` should be documented as mutually exclusive (Patrick Steinhardt states they are not, as their outputs do not interfere), and whether the `object_cb` callback should be guarded against NULL (Patrick declines, noting no current callers pass NULL). Neither affects correctness or blocks integration. The patch is otherwise ready, with all prior feedback addressed and the series’ scaffolding fully in place. The only remaining work is the unresolved asymmetry in error-handling between `verify_midx()` and `verify_bitmap_files()` noted in patch 9/10, but this is a minor detail unrelated to this patch.
