This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 10-patch series refactoring Git’s ODB fsck checks to make them pluggable. The series is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort and prepares the codebase for future pluggable backends by moving all backend-specific verification logic out of `builtin/fsck.c` and into their respective ODB backends. This patch completes the refactoring by relocating loose-object verification into `odb/source-loose.c`.

The patch introduces the `ODB_FSCK_VERBOSE` flag to preserve the original behavior of printing status messages when `--verbose` is passed, and fixes a latent bug in the progress-meter logic where the meter was captured before initialization, causing it to jump from 0 to 256 at completion. The new implementation initializes the meter first, restoring incremental updates. The `fsck_loose()` helper is relocated and adapted to use `struct fsck_loose_data`, which consolidates callback data and error state, and the patch threads the `ODB_FSCK_VERBOSE` and `ODB_FSCK_PROGRESS` flags through `struct odb_fsck_options`.

Toon Claes’s review raises two unresolved technical points. The first is whether `ODB_FSCK_VERBOSE` should be documented as mutually exclusive with `ODB_FSCK_PROGRESS`. Patrick Steinhardt responds that the flags are not mutually exclusive, as their outputs (log messages and progress updates) do not mangle each other, though they may interleave. The second point is whether the `object_cb` callback in `struct odb_fsck_options` should be guarded against NULL. Patrick declines, noting that no current callers pass a NULL callback and that a guard can be added later if needed. Both questions target robustness and clarity rather than the patch’s core functionality.

The patch is a clean refactoring with no behavior changes beyond the intentional progress-meter fix. All earlier feedback on the series has been addressed, including the architectural question about `ODB_FSCK_FULL` filtering logic (resolved in favor of pushing the check into backend-specific callbacks). The series is now complete, with `builtin/fsck.c` reduced to a thin wrapper around `odb_fsck()`. The unresolved review points are minor and do not block integration.
