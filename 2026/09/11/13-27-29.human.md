This is the fifth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 10-patch v3 series refactoring Git’s object integrity verification (`git fsck`) to make backend-specific checks pluggable. The patch introduces the scaffolding for pluggable fsck checks: a new `fsck` callback in the `struct odb_source` vtable, empty stubs for all existing source types (`files`, `inmemory`, `loose`, `packed`), and the `struct odb_fsck_options` structure with the `ODB_FSCK_FULL` flag. The patch replaces the old `check_full` boolean with the new options structure and adds the `odb_fsck()` helper, which iterates over all sources and invokes their respective `fsck` callbacks.

The patch is a pure refactoring with no user-visible behavior changes. It touches `builtin/fsck.c`, `odb.c`, `odb.h`, and the four ODB source files (`source-files.c`, `source-inmemory.c`, `source-loose.c`, `source-packed.c`), adding 102 lines and removing 5. The key symbols introduced are `odb_fsck()` (the helper that iterates over sources), `struct odb_fsck_options` (with the `ODB_FSCK_FULL` flag), and the `fsck` callback in the `struct odb_source` vtable. The `OPT_BIT` macro replaces `OPT_BOOL` for the `--full` flag, preserving `--no-full` functionality via bitwise negation.

This patch resolves the architectural question from v2 about where to filter sources based on the `ODB_FSCK_FULL` flag. In v3, Patrick moved the check into the "files" backend’s `fsck` callback (in `odb/source-files.c`), making the infrastructure more flexible for non-local backends like reftable or cloud storage. The change is minimal (three lines added to `odb/source-files.c`) and does not alter behavior. Karthik Nayak’s review of v2 raised a substantive but minor question about whether `OPT_BIT` provides equivalent `--no-full` functionality to `OPT_BOOL`; Patrick confirmed it does, and the question is now resolved. Toon Claes’s review raised the architectural filtering question, which is also resolved in this version.

The patch is well-motivated and uncontroversial. It is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort and depends on two merged topics (`ps/odb-eagerly-load-alternates` and `ps/odb-pluggable-pack-generation`). The scaffolding is now in place for subsequent patches to move backend-specific verification logic out of `builtin/fsck.c` and into their respective ODB backends. No unresolved feedback or open questions remain for this patch.
