This thread is about a proposed `--matched-only` option for `git range-diff`, introduced by Harald Nordgren to address a specific workflow pain point: when reviewing rebases or range-diffs, users often want to focus only on how commits that survived the rebase changed, rather than seeing added or dropped commits. The feature filters output to show only commits present in both input ranges, which is particularly useful when rebasing a long-lived branch against an updated `master` where the default output buries relevant changes under hundreds of unrelated commits.

The patch series is now in its third and final version (v3), which resolves the last architectural concern by properly separating error handling between the library and CLI layers. Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, has clarified that existing syntax like symmetric difference (`<A>...<B>`) or the three-revision form of `range-diff` cannot fully replace `--matched-only` in the general rebase scenario, particularly when the base has advanced since the previous round. While the technical implementation is complete and the patch is queued in Junio’s `seen` branch, it remains at risk of being dropped unless a reviewer steps in to validate its conceptual fit for the `range-diff` workflow. No open technical objections remain; the patch is low-risk and additive, but its necessity hinges on whether the workflow it enables justifies a new CLI option.

Key participants include Harald Nordgren (the author), Junio C Hamano (maintainer), Ben Knoble, Matt Hunter, and Kristoffer Haugsbakk. Junio’s feedback has been substantive, focusing on architectural consistency and the practical challenges of rebasing, though he has not explicitly endorsed the feature. Ben Knoble and Matt Hunter initially questioned the feature’s redundancy, but their concerns were addressed by Junio’s clarification of the rebasing scenario. Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s recent reply is a minor syntactic observation about the three-revision form of `range-diff` and does not engage with the broader discussion. The patch remains in limbo pending review, with the ball now in the court of potential reviewers to confirm its value before Junio removes it from `seen`.
