This thread introduces a `--matched-only` option for `git range-diff`, proposed by Harald Nordgren to help users focus on commits that appear in both input ranges (e.g., when reviewing rebases). The initial patch (v1) is under review and touches `builtin/range-diff.c`, documentation, and tests, adding the new flag without introducing new symbols or on-disk changes.

Junio C Hamano has reviewed the patch and suggested a more general refactoring of the existing `--left-only`/`--right-only` options. His key point is that the current implementation’s logic is backwards: these options *show* one side rather than *hiding* the other, making `--matched-only` (which hides unmatched commits) an awkward fit. He proposes introducing `--hide-left-only` and `--hide-right-only` as synonyms, deprecating the original names, and then defining `--matched-only` as a shorthand for combining the new `--hide-*` options. This would improve internal consistency and future extensibility. Junio also flagged a minor mechanical issue (using an existing helper for mutual-exclusion checks) and noted that the new option’s output (numbered gaps for omitted commits) is acceptable, though it may lose some context about match quality. The thread is currently uncontroversial, with no objections or competing proposals raised.
