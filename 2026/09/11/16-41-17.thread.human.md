This thread is about adding a `--matched-only` option to `git range-diff`, which would show only commits present in both input ranges—a feature aimed at making it easier to review rebases by filtering out added or dropped commits. The patch, now in its third and final version (v3), was authored by Harald Nordgren and is currently queued in Junio’s `seen` branch but at risk of being dropped due to lack of review.

The implementation is technically sound and addresses all prior feedback, including a layering violation in error handling that Junio raised. The patch is additive, low-risk, and touches only the presentation layer of `range-diff` without altering on-disk formats or introducing new symbols. However, its necessity remains debated: while Junio and Harald argue the feature solves a real workflow pain point—particularly when rebasing long-lived branches where existing syntax like symmetric difference (`<A>...<B>`) or careful input selection (`origin..old-topic origin..new-topic`) fall short—others, like Ben Knoble and Matt Hunter, note that similar filtering can sometimes be achieved with existing tools. The patch’s fate hinges on whether a reviewer steps in to validate its conceptual fit for `git range-diff`.

Key participants include Junio (maintainer), who has clarified the technical limitations of existing syntax but stopped short of endorsing the feature, and Harald, who has refined the patch to address all objections. Ben Knoble and Matt Hunter have raised questions about redundancy, though their concerns have been largely addressed by Junio’s explanations. The latest exchange—a lighthearted remark from Ben about the three-revision syntax—doesn’t move the discussion forward. The patch remains in limbo, awaiting a reviewer’s verdict on whether its utility justifies inclusion.
