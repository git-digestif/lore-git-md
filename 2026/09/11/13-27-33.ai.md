This is patch 9/10 of Patrick Steinhardt’s v3 series refactoring Git’s fsck checks to make them pluggable per ODB backend. The patch moves multi-pack index (MIDX) verification from `builtin/fsck.c` into the packed ODB backend (`odb/source-packed.c`), completing the pattern established in earlier patches (packfiles, reverse indices, bitmaps). The change replaces the dedicated `ERROR_MULTI_PACK_INDEX` bit with the generic `ERROR_OBJECT` bit and ensures the new implementation respects the `--no-full` and `--connectivity-only` flags. A new test in `t/t5319-multi-pack-index.sh` verifies these semantics: MIDX checks are skipped when `--connectivity-only` is passed and performed when `--no-full` is used.

The patch removes 18 lines from `cmd_fsck()` and adds 27 lines to `odb/source-packed.c`, introducing the `verify_midx()` helper. This helper spawns `git multi-pack-index verify` as a child process, checks the repository’s `core_multi_pack_index` setting, and threads the progress meter flag through the `odb_fsck_options` structure. In response to Toon Claes’s review, Patrick has revised the helper to use early returns instead of a `ret` variable for stylistic clarity and replaced a potential `BUG()` assertion with a call to `prepare_repo_settings()` to ensure repository settings are initialized gracefully. The question of why `verify_midx()` checks for negative returns while `verify_bitmap_files()` does not remains unresolved, but this is a minor implementation detail that does not affect correctness.

The patch is a pure refactoring with no behavior changes beyond the intentional flag-based skipping of checks. It is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort and depends on earlier patches in the series that introduced the `odb_fsck_options` structure and backend-specific `fsck` callbacks. All feedback from v2 has been addressed, and the patch is ready for integration. The only remaining open question is the asymmetry in error-handling between `verify_midx()` and `verify_bitmap_files()`, which Patrick has deferred to avoid introducing new questions.
