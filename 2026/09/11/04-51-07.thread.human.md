This thread is a routine call for translations ahead of the Git 2.56.0 release. Jiang Xin, the Chinese (zh_CN) translation maintainer, has kicked off the localization cycle, giving translators until **26 September 2026** to update their `.po` files. The baseline is `v2.56.0-rc0`, and 163 new or updated catalog entries need attention.

The workflow is straightforward: translators pull the `git-l10n/git-po` repository, refresh their files with `make po-update`, and can use AI-assisted tools (documented in `po/AGENTS.md`) to help with draft translations. All submissions must strip location comments (`msgcat --no-location`) to keep the repository clean, and pull requests are validated by the `git-po-helper` CI pipeline. There’s nothing controversial or technically complex here—just the usual coordination to ensure Git’s translations stay up to date.
