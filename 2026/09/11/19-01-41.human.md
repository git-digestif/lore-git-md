This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing "What's cooking" thread, where Patrick Steinhardt responds to Junio C Hamano’s earlier note about the withdrawn `ps/libgit-in-subdir` series. The series proposed moving Git’s library sources (`libgit.a`) into a dedicated `lib/` subdirectory to improve code organization, but was discarded due to lack of consensus and merge conflicts with other topics.

Patrick acknowledges the subjectivity of such structural changes and reiterates his personal view that separating library sources is a sensible first step, while also conceding that further subsystem-specific grouping (e.g., `refs/`, `odb/`) could coexist with this change. He does not propose reviving the series immediately but hints that the discussion may resurface at the upcoming Contributor’s Summit. The tone is pragmatic, framing the disagreement as a matter of preference rather than technical correctness. No new patches or technical arguments are introduced—this is purely a post-mortem reflection on the series’ reception.
