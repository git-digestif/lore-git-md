This is a surface-level review of the sixth patch in Johannes Schindelin’s 13-patch series updating the MinGW/Windows build system. The series as a whole is already cooking in `next` and on track for `master`; this specific patch refactors `config.mak.uname` to dynamically set `prefix` and `HOST_CPU` from MSYS2 environment variables instead of hard-coding them per variant.

Oswald Buddenhagen’s comment is purely stylistic: he notes that the patch introduces a mix of tabs and spaces in `config.mak.uname`, which violates the project’s preference for consistent indentation. The observation is mechanical and does not question the patch’s correctness or motivation. Since the series is already in `next` and the issue is cosmetic, it is unlikely to block progression.
