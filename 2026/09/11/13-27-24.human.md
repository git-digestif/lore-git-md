This is the v3 iteration of Patrick Steinhardt’s 10-patch series refactoring Git’s object database (ODB) consistency checks to make them pluggable. The series is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort, moving fsck logic out of `builtin/fsck.c` and into the respective ODB source implementations (loose, packed, multi-pack, etc.). The goal is to allow different ODB backends to implement their own consistency checks, preparing the codebase for future pluggable backends like reftable or cloud storage.

The v3 update addresses the last remaining feedback from v2. The key change is moving the `ODB_FSCK_FULL || local` check from the central `odb_fsck()` helper into the "files" backend’s `fsck` callback, making the filtering logic backend-specific. This aligns with the series’ architectural goal of pushing decision-making into the backends themselves. The patch also ensures repository settings are prepared before accessing them (fixing a potential `BUG()` in patch 9/10) and drops a redundant `ret` variable for stylistic clarity. All other patches remain unchanged from v2, including the fixes for the `--full` flag behavior, the progress-meter bug in loose-object verification, and the relocation of packfile, reverse-index, bitmap, and MIDX verification into their respective backends.

The series is now fully reviewed, with all substantive feedback addressed. Karthik Nayak and Toon Claes provided surface-level and architectural reviews, respectively, and their concerns have been resolved. The changes are mechanical and do not alter user-visible behavior, but they lay critical groundwork for future ODB backends. The diffstat shows a net reduction of 259 lines in `builtin/fsck.c`, with new code added to `odb/source-loose.c` and `odb/source-packed.c`. Tests have been extended to verify the new backend-specific checks respect the `--no-full` and `--connectivity-only` flags.

This looks like a well-crafted, thoroughly reviewed refactoring that’s ready for integration. The unresolved minor questions (e.g., the asymmetry in error-handling between `verify_midx()` and `verify_bitmap_files()`) are implementation details that don’t affect correctness or the series’ core goals. The series depends on two earlier topics (`ps/odb-eagerly-load-alternates` and `ps/odb-pluggable-pack-generation`), both of which are already merged.
