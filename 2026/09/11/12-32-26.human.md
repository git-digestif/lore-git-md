This is the v7 cover letter for D. Ben Knoble’s three-patch series converting Git’s build-time `USE_NSEC` macro into a runtime configuration option (`core.useNanosec`). The series is now effectively complete and has been cooking in `next` since July 2024 under the topic `dk/use-nsec-runtime`.

The core goal remains unchanged: users can now enable nanosecond precision on supported filesystems without recompiling Git, reducing "racy Git" problems. This v7 iteration is a final polish release that incorporates a single documentation typo fix ("comparisions" → "comparisons") and formally abandons two previously considered changes: (1) further `#ifdef` finagling to consolidate `NO_NSEC` build-time differences, and (2) a safe default for `is_racy_stat()` when operating on a repository that is not `the_repository`. Both decisions were discussed in earlier rounds and are now settled; the author and Jeff King (Peff) agree the preprocessor guard (`#ifndef NO_NSEC`) is reasonable despite its trade-offs, and the architectural constraint around `repo_config_values()` is documented as a forward-looking limitation.

The series touches 12 files, including `Documentation/config/core.adoc`, `environment.c`, `read-cache.c`, and `statinfo.c`. The first two patches—a Meson build-system fix and a no-op refactor of `repo_config_values_init`—are uncontroversial and ready to advance independently. The third patch introduces the new `core.useNanosec` config key (defaults to false) and preserves existing behavior on platforms lacking nanosecond support by treating the field as false when `NO_NSEC` is defined. The on-disk format is unchanged, and performance is restored to baseline after the v1 regression (~41× slowdown) was fixed by caching the config value in `repo_config_values`.

The only remaining loose end is the latent bug in `is_racy_stat()` when operating on a repository that is not `the_repository`, which the author has documented as a stopgap. Junio and Patrick Steinhardt have flagged this as a blocker for future recursive submodule operations, but no immediate changes are proposed. The series is based on master (2c78326f81) and carries a textual conflict with `ty/repo-config-cleanups` in `seen`, which will require routine resolution if both topics advance. All prior feedback has been addressed, and the author has explicitly declined to produce a v8. The topic is now in its final holding period in `next` pending last-minute feedback.
