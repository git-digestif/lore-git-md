This is the eighth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s v3 series refactoring Git’s fsck checks to make them pluggable per ODB backend. It moves bitmap verification from `builtin/fsck.c` into the packed ODB backend (`odb/source-packed.c`), continuing the pattern established in earlier patches.

The patch relocates `verify_bitmap_files()` from `pack-bitmap.c` to `odb/source-packed.c` and adapts it to operate on a single `odb_source_packed` instance rather than iterating over all sources. This change is necessary because the central `odb_fsck()` helper (introduced in patch 5/10) already loops over all ODB sources, so backend-specific callbacks should not repeat the iteration. The patch also replaces the dedicated `ERROR_BITMAP` bit with the generic `ERROR_OBJECT` bit, simplifying error reporting, and extends the test in `t/t5326-multi-pack-bitmaps.sh` to verify that bitmap checks respect the `--no-full` and `--connectivity-only` flags.

The diff is small and mechanical: 5 lines removed from `builtin/fsck.c`, 3 lines added to `odb/source-packed.c`, and 26 lines changed in `pack-bitmap.c` (mostly to adapt `verify_bitmap_files()` to the new signature). The test extension confirms that the refactoring preserves the intended flag semantics.
