This is a **bugfix patch** for the `--force-if-includes` safety mechanism in `git push`, part of a two-patch series that is now in its third iteration (v3). The patch corrects a long-standing flaw where the check incorrectly consults the reflog of the *local* branch matching the *remote* destination name (e.g., `refs/heads/main` when pushing to `refs/remotes/origin/main`), rather than the branch actually being pushed (e.g., `refs/heads/src` in `src:main`). This can cause false rejections or unintended data loss, as the commit message and tests demonstrate.
