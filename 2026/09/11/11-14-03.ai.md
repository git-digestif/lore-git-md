This is patch 5/10 of Patrick Steinhardt’s ODB fsck pluggability series, now receiving a substantive review comment from Toon Claes. The patch introduces the scaffolding for backend-specific fsck checks—new callbacks, options structure, and the `odb_fsck()` helper—but Toon questions whether the helper should decide which sources to include based on the `ODB_FSCK_FULL` flag, or whether that responsibility should belong to the sources themselves.

Toon’s concern is architectural: the current implementation has `odb_fsck()` filter sources by checking `(options->flags & ODB_FSCK_FULL) || source->local`, but this logic may not generalize to future ODB backends (e.g., reftable, cloud storage) where "local" might not be the right criterion. He suggests pushing the `ODB_FSCK_FULL` check into the backend-specific `fsck` callbacks, letting each source decide whether it should run under the given flags. This would make the infrastructure more flexible and future-proof, avoiding a central policy that might not fit all backends.

The patch itself is a clean, mechanical refactoring with no user-visible impact, but Toon’s feedback targets a design choice that could affect extensibility. The question is narrow—whether the filtering logic belongs in the helper or the backends—but the answer could ripple through the rest of the series, particularly patches 6–10 where backend-specific verification is moved into the sources. No other technical issues or edge cases are raised; the review is focused and constructive.

Key symbols in play: `odb_fsck()`, `struct odb_fsck_options`, `ODB_FSCK_FULL`, `odb_source_fsck_fn`, and the `local` field in `struct odb_source`. The discussion centers on where the `ODB_FSCK_FULL` check should live, not on the correctness of the current implementation. The patch remains under review, with no resolution yet.
