This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing "What's cooking" thread, specifically Patrick Steinhardt’s reply to Junio C Hamano about the now-discarded `ps/libgit-in-subdir` series.

Patrick acknowledges that the series—proposing to move `libgit.a` sources into a new `lib/` directory—failed to gain consensus on the list. He still believes the change is a sensible first step toward better code organization, but concedes that opinions vary on how best to structure the source tree. He suggests that further grouping by subsystem (e.g., refs/, odb/) could complement the top-level `lib/` split, framing the two approaches as compatible rather than competing. The door remains open for revisiting the topic at the upcoming Contributor’s Summit.
