This is the v3 iteration of Patrick Steinhardt’s 10-patch series refactoring Git’s object integrity verification (fsck) to make consistency checks pluggable per ODB backend. The series is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort and targets the `builtin/fsck.c`, `odb/`, and test subsystems. No user-visible behavior changes are introduced; the goal is to move fsck logic out of `builtin/fsck.c` and into the respective ODB source implementations (loose, packed, multi-pack, etc.), preparing the codebase for future pluggable backends like reftable or cloud storage.

The v3 update addresses the last unresolved feedback from v2: the architectural question about where to filter sources based on the `ODB_FSCK_FULL` flag. In v2, the `odb_fsck()` helper iterated over all sources and applied the check centrally; in v3, Patrick has moved the check into the "files" backend’s `fsck` callback, making the infrastructure more flexible for non-local backends. The change is minimal (three lines added to `odb/source-files.c`) and does not alter behavior. Other v3 tweaks include ensuring repository settings are prepared before accessing them (patch 9/10), dropping a redundant `ret` variable (patch 9/10), and preserving all other v2 improvements (commit message clarifications, spelling fixes, and early-return style).

Files touched span `builtin/fsck.c` (259 lines removed), `odb/source-loose.c` (92 lines added), `odb/source-packed.c` (117 lines added), and test scripts (`t1450-fsck.sh`, `t5319-multi-pack-index.sh`, etc.). Key symbols introduced or relocated include `fsck_obj_buffer()`, backend-specific `fsck` callbacks, `struct odb_fsck_options` (with `ODB_FSCK_FULL`, `ODB_FSCK_VERBOSE`, and `ODB_FSCK_PROGRESS` flags), and verification helpers like `verify_reverse_indices()`, `verify_bitmap_files()`, `verify_midx()`, and `verify_loose_objects()`. The series also fixes two latent bugs: the `--full` flag now correctly controls verification of loose objects in alternates (patch 4/10), and the progress meter for loose-object verification no longer jumps from 0 to 256 (patch 10/10).

Review feedback from Karthik Nayak and Toon Claes has been fully addressed. Karthik’s surface-level reviews (editorial nits, spelling, and observational comments) were incorporated in v2, while Toon’s substantive points (architectural filtering logic, early returns, and repository settings initialization) are resolved in v3. The only remaining open questions are minor: why `verify_midx()` checks for negative returns while `verify_bitmap_files()` does not (patch 9/10), and whether `ODB_FSCK_VERBOSE` and `ODB_FSCK_PROGRESS` should be documented as mutually exclusive (patch 10/10). Neither affects correctness or blocks integration.

The series is now complete, with all patches rebased onto Junio’s integration branch as of 2026-08-24 and the two dependency topics (`ps/odb-eagerly-load-alternates` and `ps/odb-pluggable-pack-generation`) merged in. The scaffolding for pluggable fsck checks is fully in place, and the refactoring is ready for further review or integration.
