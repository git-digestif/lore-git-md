This is patch 7/10 of Patrick Steinhardt’s v3 series refactoring Git’s fsck checks to make them pluggable per ODB backend. It moves reverse-index verification from `builtin/fsck.c` into the packed ODB backend (`odb/source-packed.c`), replacing the dedicated `ERROR_PACK_REV_INDEX` bit with the generic `ERROR_OBJECT` bit. The patch also ensures the new implementation respects the `--no-full` and `--connectivity-only` flags, skipping reverse-index checks when either flag is passed. A new test in `t/t5325-reverse-index.sh` verifies these semantics.

The diff removes 37 lines from `builtin/fsck.c` (including the `check_pack_rev_indexes()` function and the `ERROR_PACK_REV_INDEX` bit) and adds 39 lines to `odb/source-packed.c` (the new `verify_reverse_indices()` helper and its integration into `odb_source_packed_fsck()`). The helper iterates over all packfiles in the packed ODB source, loads and verifies their reverse indexes, and reports errors via the generic `ERROR_OBJECT` bit. The progress meter is threaded through the `ODB_FSCK_PROGRESS` flag in `struct odb_fsck_options`, preserving the original behavior.

Toon Claes’s surface-level review of this patch confirmed the architectural fit, noting that negative return values from the callback are converted to `ERROR_OBJECT` in `builtin/fsck.c`, preserving the existing error-reporting contract. No unresolved feedback or open questions remain for this patch. The change is a pure refactoring with no user-visible behavior changes beyond the intentional flag-based skipping of checks, and it continues the pattern established in earlier patches (e.g., patch 6/10’s relocation of packfile verification). The patch is ready for integration.
