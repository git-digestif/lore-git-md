The patch is now ready for integration and addresses a clear workflow pain point: when reviewing a rebase or range-diff, users often want to focus only on how the commits that survived the operation changed, rather than seeing commits that were simply added or dropped.

The implementation is minimal and well-tested. The patch updates the documentation to clarify the relationship between the new option and the existing filtering flags, and extends the mutual-exclusion check to reject combining `--matched-only` with either `--left-only` or `--right-only`.
