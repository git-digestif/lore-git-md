This thread is about a small but important compatibility fix to a development helper script, `Documentation/lint-gitlink.perl`. The patch, authored by Tuomas Ahola, replaces the Perl regex quantifier `{,8}` (introduced in Perl 5.34.0) with `{0,8}` to ensure compatibility with Git's minimum Perl version, 5.26.0. The change is trivial—just one line—but necessary to avoid breaking the script for developers using older Perl versions.

Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, reviewed the patch and found it uncontroversial. He was curious about how Tuomas discovered the issue, given that Perl 5.26.0 is quite old (released in 2017), but didn’t question the fix itself. He confirmed he’ll queue it for merging, effectively closing the thread. No further discussion or revisions are expected, and the patch is ready to land. The fix is well-justified, clearly explained, and poses no risk to production code.
