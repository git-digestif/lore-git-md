This is patch 3 of Patrick Steinhardt’s 9-patch series refactoring ODB alternates handling during repository creation, part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The patch is a preparatory refactoring with no functional changes.

The patch moves the `setup_reference()` function in `builtin/clone.c` further down in the file, closer to `copy_alternates()`, which it will soon call. This avoids the need for a forward declaration and is a mechanical step in preparation for the next patch (4/9), where `setup_reference()` will be renamed to `collect_alternates()` and start gathering alternates paths into a `strvec` instead of immediately writing them to the ODB. The change is trivial—just reordering the function definition—but is necessary for the subsequent consolidation of alternates logic.

The patch touches only `builtin/clone.c` and makes no behavioral changes. It is uncontroversial and appears to be a straightforward cleanup. Reviewers are unlikely to object, though the real discussion will center on the follow-up patch that introduces the new `collect_alternates()` logic. This patch itself is a safe, incremental step toward simplifying the ODB interface.
