This thread is about a patch series from Beat Bolli that fixes OpenSSL compatibility and correctness issues in `git imap-send`. The series is now at version 2, with this email addressing the second patch, which replaces Git’s manual hostname verification with OpenSSL’s built-in `SSL_set1_host()` to achieve full RFC 6125 compliance.

The key development here is a review from Aniket Gupta, who points out a behavioral regression in the new code: when certificate verification fails, the patch loses the hostname-specific error message that the old code provided. The issue arises because `SSL_connect()` fails early under `SSL_VERIFY_PEER` on any verification error, making the subsequent `SSL_get_verify_result()` check unreachable. Aniket suggests printing the OpenSSL verify result when `SSL_connect()` fails to restore the lost context. This is a substantive technical concern about error reporting, not just a stylistic one.

The series remains under review, with the v2 patches uncontroversial in principle but now facing this new feedback. The earlier v1 series is still blocked on unresolved memory-safety and architectural questions, but the v2 patches are expected to proceed independently once this error-reporting issue is addressed. No other participants have weighed in on this specific point yet.
