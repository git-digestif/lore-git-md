This is the second patch in Beat Bolli’s three-part series fixing OpenSSL compatibility and correctness issues in `git imap-send`. The fix is straightforward—allocate a temporary buffer, copy the ASN1_STRING data, and free it after use—without altering the function’s behavior or interface. The commit message cites a recent OpenSSL commit that explicitly documents this assumption as incorrect, reinforcing the patch’s motivation.
