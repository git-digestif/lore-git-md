This is the second patch in a v2 series that implements auto-maintenance deferral for the sequencer backend, covering `git rebase`, `git cherry-pick`, and `git revert`. The patch adds calls to `run_auto_maintenance()` in key exit paths (`sequencer_finish()`, `continue_single_pick()`, `single_pick()`, and `sequencer_continue()`) to ensure maintenance runs exactly once at sequence completion, matching the apply backend’s behavior.
