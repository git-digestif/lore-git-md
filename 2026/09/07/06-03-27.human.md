This is a CI/build system discussion following up on Jeff King’s patch to update Git’s CI from Debian 11 to Debian 12. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Peff’s observations about the `linux32` and `linux-TEST-vars` jobs, which still use Ubuntu 20.04 (now out of LTS).

Patrick agrees that finding a 32-bit alternative is ideal but acknowledges the shrinking options. He suggests Alpine Linux as a viable candidate, noting it already has CI coverage via the `linux-musl-meson` job and could be extended to include 32-bit testing. This could even replace the existing `linux32` job, though it would mean losing glibc coverage. For the `linux-TEST-vars` job, he endorses Peff’s idea of bumping it to the oldest supported LTS, though he notes the lack of a stable tag makes this a recurring maintenance task.

The tone remains pragmatic: no immediate action is proposed, but the discussion surfaces two potential future CI improvements. The Debian 12 patch itself is unaffected and remains on track for merging.
