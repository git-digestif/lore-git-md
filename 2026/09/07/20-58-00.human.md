This is Junio C Hamano’s **surface-level review** of the fourth patch in Karthik Nayak’s `receive-report` hook series, now in its seventh iteration and queued in `next`. The email catches a **hard-coded boolean inconsistency** in the hook’s invocation logic: after the `receive-report` hook fails (non-zero exit), the code regenerates the status report with `generate_report(&buf, commands, unpack_status, false)`, but the original call used the `version` variable that tracks the protocol version (v0 or v2). The `false` literal forces the fallback to v0, which could misreport the status if the client negotiated v2. This is a **purely mechanical nit**—no behavior change is intended, and the fix is trivial—but it risks introducing a subtle bug if the hook fails during a v2 push. The patch is otherwise feature-complete, with all substantive feedback addressed, and Junio’s tone suggests this is the last hurdle before the series graduates to `master`.
