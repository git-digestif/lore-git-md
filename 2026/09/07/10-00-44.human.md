This is a short follow-up in the ongoing refactoring series to unify Git’s terminology around "ref storage." The thread is focused on the first patch, which renames the `--ref-format=` option to `--ref-storage=` in `git init` and related commands.

Patrick acknowledges Karthik Nayak’s observation about the help text placeholder (`argh`) in `builtin/init-db.c`. Karthik pointed out that while the option is now named `--ref-storage=`, the placeholder still uses `"format"`, which could confuse users (e.g., `--ref-storage=<format>`). Patrick agrees that `"format"` is the better choice for the placeholder, especially since the series is now shifting to `--ref-storage-format=` (per Junio’s feedback) to avoid ambiguity with object hash formats. The inconsistency in error messages—where Git already referred to "ref storage format" despite the option being named `--ref-format=`—is noted as a minor irony but not a regression.

The exchange is brief and confirms that the placeholder will be left as `"format"` in the short term, with the understanding that the broader terminology shift to `--ref-storage-format=` will make this choice more intuitive. No other issues are raised, and the discussion remains narrowly scoped to this one detail. The patch itself is uncontroversial and part of a well-motivated cleanup effort.
