This is patch 5/11 in Patrick Steinhardt’s v2 refactoring series that unifies Git’s terminology for “ref storage format.” The patch mechanically renames the `--show-ref-format` option in `git rev-parse` to `--show-ref-storage-format` and retains the old name as a hidden backward-compatible alias. It updates the option’s documentation, shell completions (`contrib/completion/git-prompt.sh`), and 8 test scripts (`t0001-init.sh`, `t0610-reftable-basics.sh`, `t1460-refs-migrate.sh`, `t1500-rev-parse.sh`, `t5601-clone.sh`, `t7424-submodule-mixed-ref-formats.sh`, and the perf-lib helper `t/perf/perf-lib.sh`). The change is purely lexical; no functional behavior is altered. The patch is part of the series’ broader goal of standardizing on `--ref-storage-format=` (and related names) to avoid confusion with the upcoming `--object-storage-format=` extension.
