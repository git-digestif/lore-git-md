This is a minor naming debate in the ongoing patch series that defers auto maintenance during sequencer operations (`git rebase`, `git cherry-pick`, `git revert`). The series is otherwise technically complete and under final review.

The discussion centers on the `git_config_append_parameter()` helper function introduced in the first patch. Phillip Wood argues that the parameter name `env` is appropriate because the function is specifically designed to build `GIT_CONFIG_PARAMETERS` strings (an environment variable), making the name descriptive and intentional. Patrick Steinhardt suggests renaming it to the more generic `buf`, noting that the function doesn’t inherently require its output to be used as an environment variable and could theoretically be reused elsewhere.

The exchange is purely stylistic—neither reviewer disputes the function’s correctness or necessity. Patrick has clarified this is a nitpick and won’t insist on the change. The author, Thomas Bachem, will likely resolve this in the next revision based on whichever naming convention aligns better with the project’s style or maintainer preference. No broader implications for the series’ functionality or scope.
