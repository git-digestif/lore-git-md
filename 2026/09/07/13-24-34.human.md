This is a minor review exchange on the first patch of Thomas Bachem’s v2 series, which introduces `git_config_append_parameter()` to help the sequencer defer auto maintenance until rebase completion. The series as a whole is a well-motivated cleanup that aligns the sequencer’s behavior with the apply backend and eliminates lock contention during multi-step operations.

Phillip Wood pushes back lightly on Patrick Steinhardt’s suggestion to rename the `env` parameter to `buf`. Phillip argues that the function is specifically designed for building `GIT_CONFIG_PARAMETERS` strings, so `env` is a meaningful hint at its purpose. The exchange is purely stylistic; neither reviewer objects to the patch’s substance, and the naming debate is unlikely to block progress. The author can choose either name without risking a reroll.
