This is patch 6/11 in Patrick Steinhardt’s v2 refactoring series that unifies Git’s terminology for “ref storage format.” The patch is a one-line mechanical rename in `help.c` and its corresponding test: the build-info output field `default-ref-format` becomes `default-ref-storage-format` to match the new `--ref-storage-format=` CLI option and related environment/config names introduced in earlier patches.
