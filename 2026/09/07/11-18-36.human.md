This is a refactoring patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s eleven-part series to unify reference storage terminology. It renames the `--ref-format` option in `git clone` to `--ref-storage-format` and keeps the old spelling as a hidden alias. The change touches the man page, the command-line parser, and seven test scripts that exercise the option. The commit message references an identical change already made in `git init` and simply applies the same reasoning here. No behavior is altered; this is purely about naming consistency. The patch is small, mechanical, and well-scoped. If the series as a whole is on your radar, this step is uncontroversial and can be safely skimmed.
