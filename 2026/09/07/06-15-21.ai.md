This is a brief follow-up in the thread improving the `git maintenance` heuristic for the `rerere gc` task. Patrick Steinhardt confirms the clear division of labor between his patch series (v2) and Thomas Bachem’s concurrent effort (pull.2214.v3). His series reduces the *frequency* of `rerere gc` invocations by replacing the hard-coded 60-day cutoff with a dynamic threshold heuristic, while Thomas’s series addresses the *lock contention* issue by introducing a timeout mechanism in `setup_rerere()`. The two efforts are complementary: together they mitigate the race condition between `git maintenance` and `git rebase` from both ends, but neither series alone resolves the full problem.

The email reiterates the technical scope already established in the thread: the dynamic heuristic (sampling `rr-cache` with a "17" prefix heuristic, scaling stale entries by 256) triggers `rerere gc` only when the estimated stale count meets or exceeds `maintenance.rerere-gc.auto` (default: 512). The locking mechanism remains unchanged—`rerere gc` still uses `MERGE_RR.lock` with `LOCK_DIE_ON_ERROR`, so concurrent invocations will abort. No new code changes or test updates are introduced; this is purely a clarification of intent and scope.
