This is the second patch in a three-patch series that defers auto maintenance until the end of sequencer-driven operations (`git rebase`, `git cherry-pick`, `git revert`). The patch itself adds calls to `run_auto_maintenance()` in three exit paths: after a successful sequence, after a single pick, and after conflict resolution. The goal is to match the apply backend’s behavior, where maintenance runs once at completion rather than during intermediate steps.

Patrick Steinhardt’s review raises two main concerns. First, the commit message is unclear—it doesn’t explain what the patch actually changes or why. The current wording is hard to parse and doesn’t clarify whether this introduces new maintenance invocations or just moves existing ones. Second, he questions the number of call sites (three) and suggests there might be a single exit path that could unify maintenance invocation across both the apply and merge backends. His feedback is substantive but focused on clarity and structure rather than functionality.

The patch is technically sound—it achieves the series’ goal of deferring maintenance—but the review highlights opportunities to improve the commit message and possibly reduce code duplication. The author has already addressed prior feedback from Phillip Wood and Junio C Hamano, and this round of comments is minor. The series looks ready to proceed once these points are clarified.
