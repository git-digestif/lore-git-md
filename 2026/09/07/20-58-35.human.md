This is a surface-level review comment on the second patch of Karthik Nayak’s four-patch series introducing the `receive-report` hook for `git-receive-pack`. Junio C Hamano points out a typo in the enum definition: `REPORT_STATUS_UNKOWN` is missing an "N" and should be `REPORT_STATUS_UNKNOWN`. The patch refactors static variables tracking the report status version into an enum to make the protocol-version switch more explicit, and this typo would have introduced a subtle bug if left unaddressed.

The series as a whole is feature-complete and queued in `next`, with all prior feedback incorporated. This is the only remaining nit before the patch can be considered ready for `master`. The fix is trivial and will likely be included in a final v9 iteration or addressed directly by Junio during integration. No substantive concerns remain for this patch or the series.
