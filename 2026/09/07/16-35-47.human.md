This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing patch series that defers auto maintenance until sequencer completion. The series aims to make `git rebase`, `git cherry-pick`, and `git revert` behave consistently by running auto maintenance only once at the end of the sequence, rather than during intermediate steps.

In this email, the author (Thomas Bachem) responds to Patrick Steinhardt’s review comment on the third patch, which disables auto maintenance for commands spawned during a sequencer operation. Patrick questioned whether disabling both `maintenance.auto` and `gc.auto` was necessary, given that Git’s maintenance infrastructure now exclusively uses `git maintenance`. Thomas agrees that disabling only `maintenance.auto` is sufficient, as `git maintenance` checks `gc.auto` only if `maintenance.auto` is unset. He plans to drop the redundant `gc.auto=0` setting in the next revision, simplifying the implementation without affecting correctness.

The change is minor and technical, but it reflects the series’ broader goal of clarity and consistency. The discussion remains focused on polishing the patch rather than revisiting its core design. No new objections or edge cases have been raised here—just a small improvement to the code. The series is still on track for integration, pending the usual final review and testing.
