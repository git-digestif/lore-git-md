This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 9-patch series refactoring ODB alternates handling during repository creation. It removes the now-unused `write_alternates()` infrastructure, completing the series’ goal of consolidating alternates handling into ODB creation.

The patch deletes the `write_alternate` callback and its supporting functions across all ODB backends (`files`, `inmemory`, `loose`, and `packed`). This includes removing `odb_source_write_alternates()` and `odb_add_to_alternates_file()` from the public ODB interface, along with their backend-specific implementations. The changes are mechanical and uncontroversial: all alternates logic has already been migrated to the ODB creation phase in earlier patches, and this patch simply cleans up the leftover code.

Files touched include `odb.c`, `odb.h`, and all ODB source files (`source-files.c`, `source-inmemory.c`, `source-loose.c`, `source-packed.c`, and `source.h`). The deletions total 117 lines, reflecting the removal of the lockfile-based alternates writing logic in `odb/source-files.c` and the corresponding stubs in other backends. No new symbols are introduced; the patch only removes unused code.

This patch is a straightforward cleanup with no user-visible impact. It aligns with the series’ broader ODB abstraction effort by eliminating the ability to modify alternates after repository creation, simplifying the interface and reducing the risk of race conditions. The lockfile-based alternates writing logic (previously in `odb_source_files_write_alternate()`) is no longer needed, as alternates are now written atomically during ODB creation via `odb_create_on_disk_options`.
