This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing patch series that defers auto maintenance until sequencer completion. The email addresses a specific technical point raised by Patrick Steinhardt in his review of the third patch: whether the `disable_auto_maintenance()` helper needs to disable both `maintenance.auto` and `gc.auto` via `GIT_CONFIG_PARAMETERS`, or if disabling only `maintenance.auto` is sufficient.

Thomas Bachem agrees with Patrick’s assessment that setting only `maintenance.auto=false` is enough, since Git’s maintenance infrastructure now exclusively uses `git maintenance` (which respects `maintenance.auto` and will not trigger `git gc --auto` if auto maintenance is disabled). The redundant `gc.auto=0` setting is unnecessary and will be removed in the next revision (v3). This change simplifies the config string and clarifies the intent, though it has no functional impact on the series’ core goal of deferring maintenance until sequence completion.

The exchange is a minor but meaningful refinement to the implementation, reflecting the evolving behavior of Git’s maintenance subsystem. No broader design questions or objections are raised here; the focus remains on code clarity and correctness. The author’s response confirms the change will be included in the next iteration.
