This means a clean sequence (no conflicts, no message edits) never runs maintenance at all, leaving loose objects to accumulate until the next manual commit. The patch changes this by ensuring maintenance runs once at sequence completion, matching the apply backend’s behavior.

This would consolidate maintenance into a single exit path per command, simplify the rule ("the command runs maintenance once when done"), and avoid duplicating logic. The trade-off is moving the maintenance call out of the sequencer and into the built-in commands, which may feel less cohesive but aligns with the apply backend’s design.
