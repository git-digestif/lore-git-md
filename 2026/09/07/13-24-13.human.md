This is a **substantive review** of the third patch in the v2 series that defers auto maintenance until sequencer completion. Phillip Wood raises several clarity and testing concerns, all focused on improving the commit message and test structure rather than questioning the patch's core functionality.

The review starts by suggesting a clearer rewrite of the commit message's problem statement, replacing "works in the background against the sequence itself" with "can cause lock contention with the sequencer" to avoid implying active interference. Phillip then flags confusing phrasing in the motivation section, particularly around "the commit of one `git rebase --continue`" and the conditional nature of `MERGE_RR.lock` contention, asking for more precise language. He also questions the meaning of "The loose objects a sequence creates wait for the run at its end," suggesting it may be unclear or misleading.

On implementation details, Phillip notes that mentioning the shell in the context of `exec` commands is unnecessary and potentially confusing, since Git may bypass the shell entirely. He approves of the `disable_auto_maintenance()` helper and its usage but critiques the function's comment as sounding "like an LLM" rather than natural prose. The most significant testing feedback is that the new tests duplicate coverage already added in the previous patch—Phillip suggests extending those existing tests instead of creating separate ones for the same "maintenance at end" behavior.

No technical objections are raised; the feedback is entirely about clarity, precision, and test organization. The patch remains on track for integration once these readability improvements are addressed.
