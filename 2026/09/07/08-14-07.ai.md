This is a surface-level review of the first patch in the v2 series that introduces `git_config_append_parameter()`. Patrick Steinhardt raises two minor points: the commit message could better explain what `GIT_CONFIG_PARAMETERS` is and how its quoting works, and the parameter name `env` in the new helper function assumes too narrow a use case (suggesting `buf` instead). He also notes that neither the new helper nor the existing `git_config_from_parameters()` documents the actual format used for the config string.

The feedback is purely about clarity and naming, with no objections to the patch’s functionality or structure. The author has already addressed prior substantive feedback in v2, and this review does not introduce new technical concerns. The changes Patrick suggests are straightforward and unlikely to require a v3 on their own. The patch remains a clean refactoring that factors out reusable config-string logic for the sequencer’s upcoming use.
