This is a documentation cleanup follow-up addressing a build break in the `seen` integration branch. Junio does not suggest reverting the series; instead, he is flagging the breakage so the author (or a reviewer) can send a trivial follow-up patch to restore the build. The tone is matter-of-fact, treating this as a routine integration hiccup rather than a design flaw.
