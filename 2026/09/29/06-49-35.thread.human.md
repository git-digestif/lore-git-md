This thread is about a seven-patch series (v4) from Jeff King (Peff) that refactors the xdiff subsystem to replace `unsigned long` with `size_t` for buffer sizes in the `mmfile_t` struct, eliminating integer overflow risks and consolidating the redundant `mmbuffer_t` type. The series also fixes a latent memory leak, adds defensive NUL-termination to buffers, and resolves an error-handling bug in `ll_ext_merge()` where a successful merge driver could return `LL_MERGE_OK` with a `NULL` buffer, leading to undefined behavior. A regression test in `t/t4200-rerere.sh` now reproduces and validates the fix.

Key participants include Peff (the author), Junio C Hamano (the maintainer), Patrick Steinhardt, Phillip Wood, and D. Ben Knoble. Junio has acked several patches, and the series is procedurally complete and ready for merging. The only remaining discussion is a minor, non-blocking debate about commit history aesthetics—whether to squash follow-up patches into the main bugfix patch for cleaner presentation. Junio and Patrick briefly discussed the naming of `mmfile_t` (Patrick suggested alternatives like `mmbuffer_t`), but Junio dismissed the concern, noting that `mmfile_t` remains appropriate since xdiff treats all inputs as file-like. No technical objections remain, and the series is uncontested. A minor textual conflict with `jk/merge-ll-tempfile-cleanup` exists but is non-blocking.
