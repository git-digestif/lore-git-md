This thread is a five-patch series from Jeff King (Peff) that refactors the xdiff subsystem to replace `unsigned long` with `size_t` for buffer sizes in the `mmfile_t` struct, eliminating integer overflow risks during memory operations. The series also consolidates the redundant `mmbuffer_t` type into `mmfile_t`, fixes a latent memory leak in `read_mmfile()`, and adds a defensive NUL-terminator to all `mmfile_t` buffers. The work is purely mechanical, with no intended behavior changes, and touches several files across the xdiff and merge subsystems.

The series is currently under review, with no prior versions posted. Phillip Wood has reviewed patches 4 and 5, calling them "obviously correct" and supporting the simplification. The only open procedural question—integration order with the `jk/merge-ll-tempfile-cleanup` topic—remains unresolved but is minor. D. Ben Knoble’s recent reply dismisses the earlier concern about diverging from upstream xdiff (used by projects like Vim), endorsing the consolidation of `mmbuffer_t` into `mmfile_t`. No substantive objections or requests for changes have been raised, and the series appears to be converging toward acceptance. The next steps will likely involve a rebase to resolve the textual conflict with the `jk/merge-ll-tempfile-cleanup` topic, followed by maintainer pickup.
