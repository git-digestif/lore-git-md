This thread is a seven-patch series from Jeff King (Peff) that refactors the xdiff subsystem to replace `unsigned long` with `size_t` for buffer sizes, eliminating integer overflow risks and fixing several latent bugs. The series consolidates redundant types, plugs a memory leak, adds defensive NUL-termination to buffers, and corrects an error-handling flaw where a successful external merge driver could return a `NULL` buffer, leading to undefined behavior. A regression test was added to catch this edge case, and Junio’s stylistic suggestion to use `rm` instead of `chmod 0` in the test was incorporated.

Key participants include Junio C Hamano, who acked the early patches and required the error-handling fix before proceeding, and Patrick Steinhardt, who raised design considerations about naming and intermediate safety but did not block the series. Phillip Wood and D. Ben Knoble also reviewed, with the former calling the changes "obviously correct." The latest maintainer feedback from Junio is purely editorial—suggesting a clearer commit message for the NUL-termination patch—but the patch itself is uncontroversial and ready for merging. The only open question is whether Junio will squash the final follow-up patches into the bugfix patch for a cleaner history, a procedural choice that doesn’t affect the series’ technical readiness. The series is under review with no outstanding objections.
