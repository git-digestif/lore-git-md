This thread is a refactoring and bugfix series for Git’s xdiff subsystem, converting buffer sizes from `unsigned long` to `size_t` to eliminate integer overflow risks. The series, authored by Jeff King (Peff), consists of seven patches that consolidate the `mmbuffer_t` type into `mmfile_t`, fix a memory leak, add defensive NUL-termination, address a latent error-handling bug in `ll_ext_merge()`, and include a regression test. The work is technically complete and under review, with no major objections.

Key participants include Junio C Hamano (the maintainer), who has acked the first two patches and suggested minor procedural improvements—such as squashing the last two follow-up patches into the bugfix patch for cleaner history—and Patrick Steinhardt, who raised design considerations about naming (`mmfile_t` vs. `mmbuffer_t`) and intermediate safety but did not block the series. Peff responded to Patrick’s naming suggestion by deferring it to a future effort, keeping the current consolidation for simplicity. Phillip Wood and D. Ben Knoble also reviewed parts of the series, endorsing the changes as "obviously correct" or "nice."

The series is ready for integration, with only procedural discussions remaining. Junio’s feedback on patch presentation and commit message clarity is non-blocking, and the author has incorporated his stylistic suggestion for the regression test. A minor textual conflict with another topic (`jk/merge-ll-tempfile-cleanup`) exists but is not a blocker. The main open question is whether to squash the follow-up patches, but this is a matter of patch presentation rather than technical correctness.
