This thread is about a seven-patch series from Jeff King (Peff) that refactors the xdiff subsystem to use `size_t` instead of `unsigned long` for buffer sizes in the `mmfile_t` struct, eliminating integer overflow risks and fixing several latent bugs. The series consolidates the redundant `mmbuffer_t` type into `mmfile_t`, plugs a memory leak in `read_mmfile()`, adds defensive NUL-termination to buffers, and corrects a subtle error-handling bug in `ll_ext_merge()` where a successful external merge driver could return a `NULL` buffer, leading to undefined behavior downstream. A regression test in `t/t4200-rerere.sh` now reproduces the bug, and the series is ready for integration after addressing all maintainer feedback.

Key participants include Peff (the author), Junio C Hamano (the maintainer), Phillip Wood, and Patrick Steinhardt. Junio acked the first two patches and required fixes for the latent bug in patch 4, which Peff addressed with targeted refactoring in `ll_ext_merge()` and a follow-up patch. Phillip Wood called patches 4 and 5 "obviously correct," and Patrick Steinhardt’s recent review raises a minor naming concern—suggesting `mmbuffer_t` or a new `struct xdiff_buf` would better reflect that buffers aren’t always file-backed—but accepts the current approach. The series has no outstanding technical objections, though a minor textual conflict with `jk/merge-ll-tempfile-cleanup` remains unresolved procedurally. All patches are now technically complete, and the series is awaiting a maintainer decision.
