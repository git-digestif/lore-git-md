This thread is a seven-patch series by Jeff King (Peff) that refactors the xdiff subsystem to replace `unsigned long` with `size_t` for buffer sizes in the `mmfile_t` struct, eliminating integer overflow risks and fixing several latent bugs. The series began as a five-patch cleanup but expanded to address a memory leak in `read_mmfile()`, a defensive NUL-terminator addition, and a critical error-handling bug in `ll_ext_merge()` where a successful external merge driver could return `LL_MERGE_OK` with a `NULL` buffer, leading to undefined behavior in downstream callers like `rerere.c`.

Key participants include Junio C Hamano (the maintainer), who acked the first two patches and required the error-handling fix before merging the full series, and Phillip Wood, who reviewed patches 4 and 5 as "obviously correct." The series is now complete, with the latent bug fully addressed by Peff’s targeted refactoring and a regression test added to `t/t4200-rerere.sh`. Junio’s latest comment suggests a minor stylistic tweak to the test—replacing `chmod 0 "$1"` with `rm "$1"` to better align with Git’s test idioms—but this is purely cosmetic and doesn’t affect functionality. The series is otherwise ready for integration, with no outstanding technical objections. A trivial textual conflict with `jk/merge-ll-tempfile-cleanup` remains, but Peff has offered to rebase if needed.
