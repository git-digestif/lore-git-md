This thread is a five-patch series from Jeff King (Peff) that refactors the xdiff subsystem’s buffer handling to eliminate integer overflow risks and improve safety. The series replaces `unsigned long` with `size_t` for buffer sizes in the `mmfile_t` struct, consolidates the redundant `mmbuffer_t` type, fixes a latent memory leak in `read_mmfile()`, and adds a defensive NUL-terminator to buffers as a final safeguard.

The series is now complete and under review, with no prior versions. Phillip Wood reviewed patch 4, calling it "obviously correct," and the same applies to the final patch, which adds the NUL-terminator using `xmallocz()`. The only procedural loose end is the minor textual conflict with the `jk/merge-ll-tempfile-cleanup` topic, but Peff has offered to rebase if needed. No maintainer decision has been made yet, but the series is uncontroversial and likely to be merged as-is. The changes are purely mechanical, with no intended behavior changes or on-disk format impact.
