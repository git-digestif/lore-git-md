This thread is a seven-patch series from Jeff King (Peff) that refactors the xdiff subsystem to replace `unsigned long` with `size_t` for buffer sizes, eliminating integer overflow risks and fixing several latent bugs. The series consolidates redundant types, plugs a memory leak, adds defensive NUL-termination to buffers, and corrects a subtle error-handling bug in `ll_ext_merge()` where a successful merge driver could return a `NULL` buffer, leading to undefined behavior. A regression test was added to catch this edge case, and Junio Hamano’s stylistic suggestion to use `rm` instead of `chmod 0` in the test was incorporated.

The series is technically complete and under review, with all substantive feedback addressed. Patrick Steinhardt raised design considerations about naming and intermediate safety but did not oppose merging, while Phillip Wood and D. Ben Knoble endorsed the changes.
