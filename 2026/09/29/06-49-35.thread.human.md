This thread is a patch series from Jeff King (Peff) that refactors the xdiff subsystem to replace `unsigned long` with `size_t` for buffer sizes in the `mmfile_t` struct, eliminating integer overflow risks and consolidating redundant types. The series also fixes a memory leak in `read_mmfile()` and adds defensive NUL-termination to buffers. Patches 1 and 2 have been acked by Junio Hamano, while patches 4 and 5 were recently reviewed by Phillip Wood, who called them "obviously correct."

The latest development is a substantive review from Junio identifying a latent bug in patch 4/5: if an external merge driver succeeds but produces no output, `read_mmfile()` returns `LL_MERGE_OK` with a `NULL` buffer, leading to undefined behavior in some callers. Junio proposes a follow-up patch to centralize error handling in `ll_merge()`, ensuring `NULL` buffers are treated as errors. This fix is small but critical for correctness. The series remains under review, with no merge decision yet, though the first two patches are ready to go. The only procedural loose end is a minor conflict with `jk/merge-ll-tempfile-cleanup`, which Peff has offered to resolve via rebase.
