This thread is about a seven-patch series from Jeff King (Peff) that refactors the xdiff subsystem to use `size_t` instead of `unsigned long` for buffer sizes, eliminating integer overflow risks and fixing several latent bugs. The series is now in its fourth version, with all prior feedback addressed.

Key changes include consolidating the redundant `mmbuffer_t` type into `mmfile_t`, fixing a memory leak in `read_mmfile()`, adding defensive NUL-termination to all `mmfile_t` buffers, and resolving a latent error-handling bug in `ll_ext_merge()` where a successful external merge driver could return a `NULL` buffer, leading to undefined behavior. The series also adds a regression test to catch this edge case. Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, has acked three of the patches, and the remaining ones are uncontested. The sixth patch, just summarized, is a mechanical refactoring of `ll_ext_merge()` to reorder control flow, preparing for the final fix in patch seven.

The series is procedurally complete and ready for merging. No open questions remain, and the minor textual conflict with another topic (`jk/merge-ll-tempfile-cleanup`) is non-blocking. Reviewers, including Patrick Steinhardt and Phillip Wood, have raised design considerations but do not oppose merging. The work is purely internal, with no user-visible behavior changes beyond the bug fixes.
