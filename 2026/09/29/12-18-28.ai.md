This is the second patch in the v4 series fixing `git stash` autostash handling of staged index entries. The change is purely mechanical: it moves the `init_ui_merge_options()` call and the `quiet` flag assignment up by about 30 lines, eliminating a future code duplication. No behavior change is introduced yet, but the patch is necessary for the subsequent index-merge logic. The diff is small (10 lines, 1 file) and the commit message clearly states the motivation. This patch is uncontroversial and ready for integration.
