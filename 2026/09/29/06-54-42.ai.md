This is the fourth patch in Jeff King’s five-patch series converting xdiff’s `mmfile_t` buffer sizes from `unsigned long` to `size_t`. It delivers the original cleanup goal: replacing a hand-rolled file-reading loop in `ll_ext_merge()` with a single call to `read_mmfile()`, which was made safe in patch 1. The diff is tiny (one file, 11 lines deleted) and purely mechanical; no behavior change is intended, only code simplification. The patch is uncontroversial and should be a straightforward merge once the earlier patches land.
