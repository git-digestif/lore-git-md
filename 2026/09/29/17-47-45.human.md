This is the maintainer's review of the final patch in Christian Couder’s v4 series introducing `uploadpack.lazyFetchTrusted`, a server-side configuration mechanism to mark repositories as trusted for lazy fetching. The patch activates the feature by conditionally setting `GIT_NO_LAZY_FETCH=1` in `builtin/upload-pack.c`, preserving operator control while defaulting to safe behavior for untrusted repositories.

He explicitly praises the clarity of the code logic in `cmd_upload_pack()`, calling it "trivially obvious" and requiring no further explanation.
