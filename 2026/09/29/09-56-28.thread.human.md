This thread is a three-patch series from Patrick Steinhardt that fixes a timezone-handling bug in Git’s reftable backend. The issue: reflog entries were storing timezones in a non-compliant format ("[+-]HHMM"), violating the reftable spec’s requirement for signed minutes. This caused interoperability problems and even a segfault in the `dump-reftable` test helper.

The series is now under review, with v1 posted. Key changes include new helper functions (`parse_timezone_minutes()`, `format_timezone_minutes()`) and a transparent on-disk format change—reflog timezones are now stored as signed minutes, but converted back to "[+-]HHMM" on read to avoid disrupting the rest of Git. Tests cover edge cases like +1400 and +0530, and the segfault in `dump-reftable` is fixed.

Karthik Nayak has reviewed all three patches. His feedback on the first patch flagged a potential sign-handling inconsistency in `local_time_tzoffset()`, but this hasn’t resurfaced as a blocker. On the third patch, he raised a minor test nit (adding a negative timezone like -0530) but otherwise approved the logic. The series looks ready for integration once the nit is addressed, with no major objections or open questions remaining.
