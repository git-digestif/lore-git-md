# Thread deltas

## 2026/09/29

- [2026/09/29/11-25-40 by Scott Chacon <scott@gitbutler.net>] Scott Chacon posted a 4-patch series (mainly AI-generated) porting Sam Reis's sha1dc Rust crate approach to C, claiming ~2.7x faster hashing on a Xeon and 2.85x on an M5 Max, with single-threaded index-pack of git.git going from 24.3s to 12.7s (Xeon) and 16.1s to 8.7s (M5 Max), cutting collision-detection overhead from 1.5-2.5x to about 0.2x.
- [2026/09/29/11-25-41 by Scott Chacon <scott@gitbutler.net>] Patch 1/4 adds sha1dc-accel/ with its own block loop around sha1dc's SHA1_CTX and DV table (portable C backend only, a few percent slower), plus tests comparing against sha1dc/ on real collisions (SHAttered, SHA-mbles, reduced-round) and new test-tool commands sha1dc-backends and sha1dc-compare; DC_SHA1_NO_ACCEL falls back to plain sha1dc/.
- [2026/09/29/11-25-42 by Scott Chacon <scott@gitbutler.net>] Patch 2/4 vectorizes the unavoidable-bitconditions check as SSE2, AVX2, NEON, and new scalar forms driven by solver-picked condition tables (from the crate at commit 426b4afd, MIT-licensed), adding backends portable+avx2, portable+sse2, portable+neon, and portable, for 1.29x on the Xeon and 1.27x on the M5 Max.
- [2026/09/29/11-25-43 by Scott Chacon <scott@gitbutler.net>] Patch 3/4 compresses with SHA-NI on x86-64 (spilling the schedule, saving states at steps 60/64, recompressing flagged blocks in hardware), adding shani+avx2 and shani+sse2 backends; 2.07x on the Xeon, 2.70x overall vs plain sha1dc, with index-pack 1.92x faster single-threaded and 1.76x with 4 threads, and shani+avx2 hashing at 900-1050 MiB/s vs 420-450 for sha1dc/.
- [2026/09/29/11-25-44 by Scott Chacon <scott@gitbutler.net>] Patch 4/4 compresses with the ARMv8 SHA-1 instructions on arm64, adding the armv8+neon backend; on an M5 Max, 1GiB hashing drops from 1.46s to 0.51s (~2 GiB/s, 2.85x overall) and index-pack of git.git goes from 16.1s to 8.7s single-threaded (1.86x) and 6.7s to 5.9s with 18 threads; the Linux runtime check is so far only tested under qemu-user.
