This is a **feature patch follow-up** in the thread extending `` `git branch --delete-merged` `` to detect squash-merged and rebase-merged branches. The email is Harald Nordgren’s brief reply to D. Ben Knoble’s performance concern.

Nordgren acknowledges the algorithm’s cost but reports that his latest implementation is already noticeably faster than earlier versions, making it tolerable on his local Git repository. He then floats the idea of hiding the feature behind a config flag (e.g., `` `branch.deleteMergedDetectSquash` ``) to let users opt in only when they need it, sidestepping the performance question entirely. No decision has been reached; the suggestion remains open for discussion.
