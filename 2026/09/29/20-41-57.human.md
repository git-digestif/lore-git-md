This is a follow-up to the `mmfile_t` refactoring series, specifically addressing a latent bug in patch 4/5 that Junio C Hamano identified during review. The issue: when an external merge driver exits successfully (status 0) but its output file is missing or unreadable, `ll_ext_merge()` returns `LL_MERGE_OK` with a `NULL` buffer, which downstream callers like `rerere.c` then pass to `fwrite()`, causing undefined behavior. Jeff King (Peff) confirms the bug with a reproducer and proposes a targeted fix in two new patches (6/5 and 7/5) that force a driver error (`status = 129`) if `read_mmfile()` fails, avoiding the need to modify every caller.

The fix is narrow—touching only `merge-ll.c` and adding a regression test to `t/t4200-rerere.sh`—and preserves the series’ original goal of safe buffer handling while closing a correctness hole. Patches 1 and 2 of the series are already acked by Junio, and Phillip Wood called patches 4 and 5 "obviously correct" before this bug was discovered. The new patches look well-motivated and minimal, addressing the specific edge case without expanding scope. No merge status is reported yet, but the series now appears technically complete and ready for final review. The only procedural loose end is a minor textual conflict with `jk/merge-ll-tempfile-cleanup`, which Peff has offered to rebase if needed.
