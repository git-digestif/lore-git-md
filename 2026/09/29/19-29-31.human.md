This is the first patch in a two-patch v5 series updating the `git-contacts` contrib script. The patch itself is unchanged from the already-merged v4: it extends `git-contacts` to accept a single dash (`-`) as an explicit stdin marker, enabling mixed file/stdin usage (e.g., `git contacts patch1 - patch3 <patch2`). The implementation is simple and uncontroversial, setting a `$read_from_stdin` flag when `-` is encountered in `@ARGV` and passing the stdin filehandle to the existing `scan_patches()` helper.
