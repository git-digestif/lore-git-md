This is a documentation cleanup thread that advances the plan to remove the obsolete `gittutorial-2` document. Julia Evans replies to Junio C Hamano’s earlier questions, clarifying the roadmap: first delete `gittutorial-2` (the series under review), then later decide whether to replace or remove the remaining `gittutorial`. She argues that `gittutorial-2`’s content is already covered by `gitdatamodel[7]` and `gitcore-tutorial[7]`, so no direct replacement is planned. On `gittutorial` itself, she questions its value, noting that any modern tutorial would cover the same basics (`git init`, `git add`, `git commit`) and that keeping two overlapping tutorials would cause confusion. The tone remains collaborative, and no objections are raised to the current series.
