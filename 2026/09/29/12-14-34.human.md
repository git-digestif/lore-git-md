This is a brief update in the ongoing RFC thread about adding an `includeIf "hostname:..."` condition to Git's configuration system. The email is from Ignacio Encinas, who authored a 2024 patch series that touched on hostname normalization but did not implement the `includeIf.hostname` feature itself.

Ignacio confirms that his 2024 series stalled after v3 due to its unexpected complexity, not because of any technical objections. He apologizes for not communicating this explicitly at the time and expresses hope that the earlier discussion will be useful if Isabella Caselli (the original RFC author) decides to proceed with implementation. The tone is constructive and supportive, with no new technical points raised.

For readers following the thread, this clarifies that the 2024 work is definitively abandoned, removing any ambiguity about whether it might resurface. The earlier consensus on hostname normalization (documenting system-dependency rather than enforcing rules) remains the most relevant guidance for Isabella’s proposal. No objections or alternative approaches are introduced here.
