This is a feature proposal follow-up from Jeff King responding to Isabella Caselli’s RFC about adding a `hostname:` condition to Git’s `includeIf` mechanism.

Jeff endorses the feature as reasonable and confirms no one else is actively working on it. He flags the hostname normalization question as the main technical hurdle, pointing to a 2024 patch series by Ignacio Encinas that grappled with the same issue. That series stalled after v3, but the thread consensus was to document the system-dependent nature of hostname matching rather than enforce normalization. Jeff also suggests exposing Git’s resolved hostname via `git var GIT_HOSTNAME` as a debugging aid, a detail not mentioned in Isabella’s original email.

The tone is constructive and matter-of-fact: no objections to the feature itself, but the hostname ambiguity remains the sticking point. If Isabella picks this up, she’ll need to either adopt the 2024 consensus or propose a new approach to normalization. The email is short and focused, making it clear that the path forward hinges on resolving that single question.
