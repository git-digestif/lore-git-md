This is the third patch in Jeff King’s five-part series refactoring the xdiff subsystem to use `size_t` for buffer sizes. It directly changes the `mmfile_t` struct in `xdiff/xdiff.h` to store its size as a `size_t` instead of a `long`, and updates the one place in `diff.c` that prints that size to use the correct format specifier (`PRIuMAX` with a cast to `uintmax_t`).

The patch is mechanical and low-risk: the type change is the obvious fix for the integer-overflow concerns raised in the cover letter, and the printf adjustment is the only consumer that needed updating. No behavior changes are intended, and the diff is tiny (two lines). This looks like a clean, uncontroversial step in the larger cleanup.
