This thread began as a patch series to extend `git stash create` with `--include-untracked` (`-u`) and `--all` (`-a`) options, aiming to bring parity with `git stash push`. The series reached its second iteration, addressing backward-compatibility concerns raised by Phillip Wood—specifically, ensuring option parsing stops at the first non-option argument (preserving the traditional behavior of treating the rest of the command line as a message) and mapping the internal exit status 1 to 0 for consistency with other stash subcommands. Junio C Hamano’s feedback on the commit message, however, prompted the author, Kazumasa Shigeta, to reconsider the scope of the change.

Shigeta has now stepped back from the original patch, recognizing that the feature’s narrow focus on `-u` and `-a` left broader compatibility questions unanswered. In a recent email, he outlines three potential directions forward: extending `stash create` further to support more options (like `-p` or pathspecs) while using `PARSE_OPT_STOP_AT_NON_OPTION` to avoid breaking existing scripts; introducing a new subcommand (e.g., `git stash make`) to sidestep `create`’s positional message grammar; or adding a `--create-only` flag to `git stash push` to reuse its option conventions without introducing a new subcommand. The thread remains under active review, with no merge status reported, and the next steps will depend on feedback from the maintainers and reviewers. Key participants include Junio, Phillip Wood, and Patrick Steinhardt, with Shigeta seeking guidance on which approach aligns best with the project’s design principles.
