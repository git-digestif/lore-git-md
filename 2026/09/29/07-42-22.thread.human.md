This thread is about extending `git stash create` to support the `--include-untracked` (`-u`) and `--all` (`-a`) options, bringing it closer to the functionality of `git stash push`. The series, authored by Kazumasa Shigeta, has evolved from a straightforward feature patch into a design discussion about how best to expose `do_create_stash()`’s capabilities without breaking backward compatibility.

The core debate centers on whether to incrementally extend `git stash create` with a minimal subset of options (`-m/--message`, `-q/--quiet`, `-u`, and `-a`) or to align it more closely with `git stash push` by supporting all relevant options from the start. Junio C Hamano has pushed back on the minimal approach, arguing that `stash create` should eventually be capable enough to let scripts reimplement `stash push` using `create` and `store`. He questions why any option supported by `push` would need to behave differently in `create`, suggesting that the two commands should be interchangeable for scripting purposes. Phillip Wood has endorsed a middle ground, supporting `-u` and `-m` while ruling out `--patch` and full positional pathspecs, but the thread remains unresolved on the final scope.

The current patch touches `builtin/stash.c`, `Documentation/git-stash.adoc`, and `t/t3903-stash.sh`, with no changes to on-disk formats or core behavior beyond option parsing. The author has addressed backward-compatibility concerns (exit status mapping and `PARSE_OPT_STOP_AT_NON_OPTION`) but has not yet posted a revised patch. The next step is likely further discussion or a new version that either justifies the proposed subset or expands it to match `push` more closely. No merge status has been reported, and the thread remains under active review.
