This thread is about extending `git stash create` to support the `--include-untracked` (`-u`) and `--all` (`-a`) options that are already available in `git stash push` and `git stash save`. The goal is to resolve an inconsistency in the stash subsystem while preserving backward compatibility with existing scripts that rely on `git stash create <message>`’s positional message grammar. The discussion has expanded to address broader design questions, including which subset of `stash push` options should be exposed in a script-focused command and how to avoid breaking existing workflows.

Key participants include the author, Kazumasa Shigeta, who proposed the original patch and is now refining its scope; Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, who has emphasized the need to justify the change’s scope and suggested `--create-only` as a safer alternative to extending `stash create`; and Phillip Wood, who provided substantive feedback on backward-compatibility risks and has helped narrow the proposed changes. Phillip’s latest email rules out `--patch` and positional pathspecs as unsuitable for `stash create`, endorsing `--pathspec-from-file` and `-m`/`--message` instead. He also reaffirms that `PARSE_OPT_STOP_AT_NON_OPTION` is a reasonable way to preserve backward compatibility, as messages starting with `-` are rare in practice.

The thread remains in the design discussion phase, with three potential directions still under consideration: (1) extending `stash create` with a carefully chosen subset of `stash push` options, (2) adding a new subcommand (e.g., `git stash make`) to avoid `create`’s positional message grammar, or (3) adding `--create-only` to `git stash push` to reuse its option grammar while separating creation logic from application. Junio’s earlier feedback described `--create-only` as "the safest" approach, but the latest discussion has focused on refining the `stash create` extension. No merge status has been reported, and the next steps are likely a revised patch or further discussion to finalize the design direction. The original patch touched `builtin/stash.c`, `Documentation/git-stash.adoc`, and `t/t3903-stash.sh`, but future changes may expand or shift scope based on the chosen approach.
