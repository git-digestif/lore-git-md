This is a **feature patch follow-up** in the ongoing discussion about extending `` `git branch --delete-merged` `` to detect squash-merged and rebase-merged branches. The email is a short reply from the author, Harald Nordgren, to D. Ben Knoble’s suggestion of a granular CLI option (e.g., `` `--delete-merged=squashed,rebased` ``).

Nordgren clarifies that his motivation for hiding the new behavior behind a flag is purely performance-related, not use-case differentiation. He reinforces a preference for simplicity, arguing that users should not have to specify detection modes explicitly. His example—`` `git branch --delete-merged` `` and `` `git branch --delete-merged '*/*'` `` should behave identically—illustrates a desire for the feature to work out-of-the-box without additional configuration. The exchange remains conceptual; no implementation or documentation changes are proposed. The key takeaway is that the author leans toward a single, unified behavior rather than fine-grained control, leaving the design question open for further discussion.
