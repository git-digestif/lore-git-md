Peff advises against making this a routine practice, explaining that while it occasionally surfaces useful feedback (as it did here), the more common outcome is either no actionable insight or a conflict that the maintainer must resolve anyway. He notes that the effort rarely justifies the payoff for most contributors. Instead, he suggests that if a contributor is already maintaining a personal integration branch (like his `jch` branch), they might naturally catch such issues early—but this isn’t something he expects everyone to do. For those who want to test ahead of submission, he recommends targeting `next` or Junio’s `jch` branch (a stable subset of `seen`) rather than `seen` itself, which is more ephemeral and may include untested topics.
