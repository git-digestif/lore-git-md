Thread identity: Patch series extending `git stash create` to support `--include-untracked` (`-u`) and `--all` (`-a`) options, authored by Kazumasa Shigeta. Thread type: feature addition to the stash subsystem, now in refined design discussion phase with a narrowed scope proposal.

Core problem or goal: Resolve the inconsistency where `git stash create` lacked support for `--include-untracked` and `--all` options available in `git stash push` and `git stash save`, while preserving backward compatibility with `git stash create <message>`’s positional message grammar. The goal has evolved to expose a minimal, script-friendly subset of `do_create_stash()`’s capabilities without breaking existing scripts, with active debate over the subset of `stash push` options to expose and the preferred design direction.

Current status: Design discussion phase following feedback from Junio C Hamano, Phillip Wood, and others. The original v3 patch is on hold while the author refines the scope. The discussion has narrowed the proposed changes to incrementally extending `git stash create` with a minimal subset of options (`-m/--message`, `-q/--quiet`, `-u/--include-untracked`, and `-a/--all`), ruling out `--patch`, `--staged`, and positional pathspecs. `PARSE_OPT_STOP_AT_NON_OPTION` is confirmed as a reasonable backward-compatibility measure for preserving the positional-message grammar. The author argues that this approach establishes a foundation for future script-oriented options without revisiting backward-compatibility concerns, while Junio C Hamano previously described `--create-only` to `git stash push` as "the safest" approach. The latest email signals a preference for extending `stash create` over adding a new subcommand or `--create-only`, but the thread remains open to all three directions. No merge status reported; the thread remains under active review.
