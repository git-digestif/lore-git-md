This is a surface-level review comment on the second patch in Christian Couder’s five-part v4 series introducing `uploadpack.lazyFetchTrusted`. The patch itself is a clean refactoring that extracts the existing `safe.directory` path-matching logic into reusable helpers (`path_allowlist_apply()` and `path_allowlist_config_apply()`) for use by the upcoming `uploadpack.lazyFetchTrusted` implementation. No functional changes are introduced.

Junio’s comment focuses on a latent design issue inherited from the existing `safe.directory` logic: the silent ignoring of non-existent paths, which could mask configuration errors (e.g., typos, unmounted filesystems) and lead to unintended trust decisions. He suggests a potential improvement—allowing users to explicitly mark paths as optional (e.g., `:(optional)foo/bar)`)—to distinguish between intentional omissions and mistakes, and proposes adding a `NEEDSWORK` comment to flag this for future work. The comment is framed as a minor point and explicitly outside the scope of the current series, though it now risks propagating to `uploadpack.lazyFetchTrusted` if left unaddressed.

The review is purely advisory and does not block the patch. Christian has already acknowledged the concern in the thread and proposed adding a `NEEDSWORK` comment in a follow-up patch. The refactoring remains a strong candidate for early integration if the series stalls, as it introduces no new behavior and its helpers are immediately reusable by the next patch. No substantive concerns remain for this patch itself.
