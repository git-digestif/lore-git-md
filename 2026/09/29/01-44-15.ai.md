This is a substantive review reply in the ongoing `includeIf "hostname:..."` RFC thread. Jeff King (Peff) responds to Isabella Caselli’s inquiry about whether the feature is still relevant and whether anyone is already working on it.

Peff confirms the feature is reasonable and not currently being pursued by anyone else, based on a list archive search. He then addresses the hostname normalization question Isabella raised: whether the condition should match the system-reported hostname exactly or normalize it (e.g., short name vs. fully qualified domain name). Peff points to a 2024 patch series by Ignacio Encinas that touched on this issue, noting that the consensus at the time was to document the system-dependency rather than enforce normalization. He also suggests exposing Git’s view of the hostname via `git var GIT_HOSTNAME` as a debugging aid, a detail not mentioned in the original RFC.

The email clarifies that the 2024 series stalled after v3, implying the author may not have resumed work. Peff’s reply advances the thread by providing historical context, confirming the feature’s relevance, and offering a concrete suggestion for handling hostname ambiguity.
