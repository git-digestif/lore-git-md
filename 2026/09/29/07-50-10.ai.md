This is a maintainer reply to a documentation cleanup series. Junio C Hamano responds to Julia Evans's proposal to remove `gittutorial-2`, asking whether this is the "two series" approach she mentioned earlier. He expresses support for removing the outdated tutorial but emphasizes that the goal is to create a clean slate for a modern replacement, not to replicate the old content. He suggests that the replacement need not cover everything the old tutorial did and should instead focus on current needs.

Junio also raises a question about `gittutorial` (the first part), noting that it is short and still reasonably useful, despite its old-fashioned approach of starting a project from a tarball. He does not advocate for its removal but leaves the decision open. The tone is collaborative, focusing on the broader goal of improving Git's tutorial documentation rather than the mechanics of the patch itself. No objections to the series are raised, but the discussion hints at future work to modernize the remaining tutorial material.
