## 2026/10/04

- [2026/10/04/09-54-10 by Phillip Wood <phillip.wood123@gmail.com>] Phillip Wood concluded that detecting squash- and/or rebase-merged branches is a useful improvement but needs careful implementation to be efficient enough to be practical in large repositories, and that he is unlikely to have time to closely review it.
