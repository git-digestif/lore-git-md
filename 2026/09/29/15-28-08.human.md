This is a **feature discussion follow-up** in the ongoing thread about extending `` `git branch --delete-merged` `` to detect squash-merged and rebase-merged branches.

D. Ben Knoble responds to Harald Nordgren’s suggestion of hiding the new functionality behind a config flag. Knoble floats the idea of a more granular approach: a CSV-style option (e.g., `` `--delete-merged=squashed,rebased` ``) that would let users explicitly opt into the specific merge types they want to detect, rather than a simple on/off toggle. He acknowledges this could serve both performance concerns and use-case flexibility, but also notes that most users likely want a simple "delete merged branches" command without extra thought. The comment remains exploratory—no concrete proposal or objection, just a musing on how to balance performance, usability, and control.
