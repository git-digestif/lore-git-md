Harald Nordgren has posted version 10 of his five-patch series extending `git branch` to detect squash-merged and rebase-merged branches, currently under review through patch 3/5. The series introduces `--delete-integrated <pattern>` alongside the existing `--delete-merged <pattern>`, using change-based detection (tree-entry comparisons for rebases, plain-text `xdl_merge()` for squashes) to identify branches whose content has landed upstream even when their tips do not. Patch 3/5, just posted, relaxes `--dry-run` so it works with plain `-d` and `-D` deletion modes rather than requiring `--delete-merged`, addressing one of Junio C Hamano's open v9 concerns about dry-run semantics and enabling users to preview deletions of integrated branches.

Junio has confirmed the CLI design separating strict tip-reachability from change-based detection, and the series now includes tests for dry-run, quiet mode, and the `branch.<name>.deleteMerged=false` opt-out with the new flag. However, the series remains under review with no integration announced. Open technical questions include whether `branch.<name>.deleteMerged` is the appropriate config key for `--delete-integrated` (Junio has suggested a more generic `deleteDone`), how to mitigate "npm update" false positives via author checks or patch IDs, and whether to document limitations around rename detection and partial-clone blob fetching more explicitly.
