This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s 13-patch series to refactor Git’s object database (ODB) subsystem by removing ad-hoc source linking, a mechanism that allowed temporary object directories (like those for submodules) to be registered in-memory. The goal is to simplify the codebase and prepare for future ODB abstraction work. The series is now in its second version (`v2`), posted on September 5, 2026, with all patches reviewed and no substantive objections remaining.

Key participants include Patrick (the author), Junio C Hamano (the maintainer), Karthik Nayak, and Justin Tobler. Junio gave unqualified approval to two patches in the first version, while Karthik and Justin provided detailed reviews of several patches in `v2`, confirming the correctness of the refactoring and the removal of implicit dependencies on global state. The series touches multiple subsystems—ODB, submodule handling, cache-tree, and test infrastructure—without changing on-disk formats or user-visible behavior, except for a breaking change in `git multi-pack-index --object-dir=`, which now rejects non-alternate directories (aligning with documented behavior).

The only remaining discussion is a minor, surface-level tweak to the commit message of one patch, suggested by Justin and accepted by Patrick. The series is now fully reviewed and ready for Junio’s queue, though no explicit merge intent has been stated yet. It’s uncontroversial and self-contained, with all prior feedback addressed.
