Thread identity: standalone patch and follow-up series addressing GitLab CI documentation job failures and Asciidoctor version pinning modernization, originating with Patrick Steinhardt’s minimal fix (`ps/ci-depends-on-ruby`). The thread evolved into a discussion about CI policy and broader documentation tooling, culminating in Jeff King’s two-patch series (`jk/ci-use-system-asciidoctor`) and a subsequent resend by Tuomas Ahola of Jeff’s second patch as a standalone update.

Core problem or goal: restore and modernize GitLab CI’s documentation build. The immediate goal was to fix a broken CI job by ensuring Ruby is available for Asciidoctor installation. The broader goal is to eliminate an outdated version pin and simplify CI configuration by using the system-provided Asciidoctor package, reflecting what users are likely to have installed locally and reducing maintenance overhead. The thread has also opened a tangential but forward-looking discussion about the long-term viability of supporting both Asciidoc and Asciidoctor.
