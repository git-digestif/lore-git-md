This is a **bugfix** for the `contrib/credential/libsecret` credential helper, part of an ongoing discussion about crashes and silent data loss when retrieving credentials. Daniel Martí clarifies the upstream status of the issue in response to M Hickford’s question: while the libsecret documentation is incomplete (a bug he has submitted a fix for), the behavior of `SECRET_SEARCH_LOAD_SECRETS`—silently skipping locked or inaccessible items—appears to be intentional. This means Git must handle NULL secrets regardless of the flag’s use, and the current patch’s approach (explicitly loading secrets after search) aligns with libsecret’s own `secret-tool` utility.

Martí confirms that the patch’s design is sound: the two-step process (search + explicit load) avoids the silent failures introduced by `SECRET_SEARCH_LOAD_SECRETS` while maintaining the same number of round trips in the common case. He plans to send a v2 with a clearer commit message, addressing Junio C Hamano’s earlier feedback about self-contained explanations. The discussion now appears to be converging on implementation details rather than fundamental design, though the lack of test coverage (due to reproducibility challenges) remains a minor gap. The patch looks well-motivated and technically justified, with Martí’s upstream engagement adding credibility.
