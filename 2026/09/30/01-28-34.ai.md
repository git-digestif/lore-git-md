This is a bugfix series targeting the multi-pack-index (MIDX) and cruft pack machinery. The patches address corner cases where MIDXs can end up with objects not closed under reachability, which can cause failures when generating reachability bitmaps. The series is authored by Taylor Blau and touches `pack-objects.c`, `repack.c`, `repack-midx.c`, and adds new tests in `t5331-pack-objects-stdin.sh` and `t7704-repack-cruft.sh`.

The problem occurs when once-cruft tree or annotated tag objects are included in a MIDX, but objects reachable from them are missing from the input packs. This can happen during incremental repacks or when `.keep` packs are involved. The series introduces a `stdin_packs_context` struct to refactor `pack-objects`'s `--stdin-packs` handling (patch 1), then fixes the reachability closure issues in three specific scenarios: `--stdin-packs=follow` (patch 2), incremental repacks (patch 3), and `.keep` packs (patch 4).
