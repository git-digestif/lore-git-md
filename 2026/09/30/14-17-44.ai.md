This is the **fourth patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s RFC series** converting Git’s `BreakingChanges` document into the new `gitbreaking-changes(7)` manpage. The patch **replaces all message-ID references with clickable URLs** (pointing to `lore.kernel.org` threads) and **moves the "how to add new entries" section to the end** of the document, as previously requested by Patrick Steinhardt. Two message-IDs required URL encoding to avoid breaking AsciiDoc parsing.

The patch is **documentation-only**, touching only `Documentation/gitbreaking-changes.txt` (the new AsciiDoc source for the manpage). No new symbols, CLI options, or on-disk formats are introduced, and no test coverage is added. The changes are mechanical and usability-focused: making the document more navigable for end users by providing direct links to relevant mailing-list threads and improving the document’s logical flow.

Kristoffer has **accepted all feedback** from Patrick’s review, including the section reordering and a minor formatting nit (two spaces after a URL). The patch is now ready for further review, though the series remains tagged as an RFC, indicating the author is still seeking broader feedback on the overall approach before finalizing.

The unresolved forward-looking question about the document’s lifecycle post-Git 3.0 remains open but is not a blocker for this patch. Patrick Steinhardt suggested retaining the manpage and eventually adding a new section for Git 4.0, while Kristoffer proposed keeping a historical section for Git 3.0 and leaving Git 4.0 discussion for later. Neither position is contentious, and the discussion is exploratory rather than a point of disagreement.
