This is a brief reply in the ongoing xdiff buffer-type refactoring series. Jeff King responds to Patrick Steinhardt’s naming feedback on patch 2/5, which consolidates `mmbuffer_t` into `mmfile_t`. Peff acknowledges the semantic inaccuracy of `mmfile_t` but argues that renaming would require a much larger, riskier change with many incidental adjustments (e.g., variable names). He prefers to defer the naming question, keeping the current approach for now. The exchange remains procedural and non-blocking; the series’ technical readiness is unaffected.
