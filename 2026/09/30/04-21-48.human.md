This is a refactoring patch targeting the fetch subsystem, specifically the handling of the `fetch.followRemoteHEAD` configuration setting. Matt Hunter replies to Colin Hinton’s v3 patch, clarifying Junio’s earlier review comment about consistency between the local (`builtin/fetch.c`) and remote-side (`remote.c`) parsers.

Matt agrees with Colin’s assessment that the only substantive difference between the two parsers is the `warn-if-not-$branch` setting, which is remote-specific and not applicable to `fetch.followRemoteHEAD`. However, he suggests Junio’s comment may have been misinterpreted: the intent was likely to apply the same *deferred validation* pattern to the remote-side parser, not to unify the parsing logic itself. The current patch (v3) defers validation in `builtin/fetch.c` but leaves `remote.c` unchanged, which is fine as a first step but should preserve or reword the NEEDSWORK comment to signal that the remote-side work remains.

The patch itself is well-scoped and addresses the core annoyance: spurious warnings during config parsing when the fetch operation never consults `fetch.followRemoteHEAD`. It introduces a clean lazy-validation mechanism (`get_follow_remote_head()`) and fixes a segfault edge case for valueless truth settings. Matt confirms the functionality works as intended. The discussion is constructive and the patch looks ready for merging, with the remote-side follow-up left as a separate effort.
