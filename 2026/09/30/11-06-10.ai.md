This is a **surface-level review** of the fourth patch in Pablo Sabater’s five-patch series adding a `--dry-run` option to `git backfill`. Karthik Nayak’s feedback focuses on code clarity and style rather than the feature’s design or correctness.

The review suggests three minor adjustments:
1. Change the `dry_run` field in `struct backfill_context` from an `int` to a `bool`, since `OPT__DRY_RUN` internally uses `OPT_BOOL`.
2. Rename `dry_run_batch()` to `count_batch()` to better reflect its purpose (counting blobs rather than simulating a dry run).
3. Reorganize the control flow in `do_backfill()` to use a direct `if (dry_run) count() else download()` pattern instead of a `goto`, for improved readability.

No technical concerns are raised, and the patch remains functionally unchanged. The feedback is purely stylistic and could be addressed in a future revision without requiring deeper rework. The series as a whole is still awaiting substantive technical review.
