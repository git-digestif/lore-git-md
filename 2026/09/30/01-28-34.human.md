This is a bugfix series targeting the multi-pack-index (MIDX) and repack machinery, specifically addressing edge cases where cruft-less MIDXs can fail to close under reachability. The series comes from Taylor Blau and consists of four patches that fix scenarios where tree and annotated tag objects, incremental repacks, or `.keep` packs could leave MIDXs in a state where reachability bitmaps cannot be generated reliably.

The problem the series tackles is subtle but important: when a MIDX is written without ensuring all objects reachable from its packed objects are also included, bitmap generation may fail if any selected commit descends from an object outside the MIDX. The fixes involve both low-level changes in `pack-objects` (to handle `--stdin-packs=follow` correctly) and higher-level adjustments in `repack` (to retain cruft packs when necessary).

The first patch is a preparatory refactoring that introduces a `stdin_packs_context` struct in `pack-objects.c`, reducing churn in the subsequent fix. The second patch ensures that when `--stdin-packs=follow` is used, tree and tag objects are properly followed to maintain reachability closure. The third and fourth patches address incremental repacks and `.keep` packs, respectively, ensuring cruft packs are retained in MIDXs when needed to avoid breaking reachability.

The series includes new tests in `t/t5331-pack-objects-stdin.sh` and `t/t7704-repack-cruft.sh`, covering the edge cases explicitly. The commit messages are detailed and explain the failure modes clearly, which is helpful for reviewers. This looks like a well-scoped, well-motivated fix for a real issue in the MIDX/repack code, and the changes appear to be surgical rather than sweeping. Worth attention from anyone working on pack or MIDX internals.
