This is a surface-level review of the already-merged `gittutorial-2` removal series. The reviewer, Tuomas Ahola, points out a minor formatting nit in the patch subject lines: the `[doc]` prefix in the email subjects is dropped by `git am`, so the commit messages end up with redundant "doc:" prefixes (e.g., `doc: [doc] Remove gittutorial-2`). He illustrates the issue by comparing with another of Julia Evans' series, where the subject lines were formatted more conventionally (e.g., `Add new gitmergeconflicts man page`).

The feedback is purely stylistic and does not engage with the substance of the patch. It is aimed at aligning future patch submissions with the project's conventions for commit message formatting in `git shortlog`. The thread context confirms the series itself is merged to `master`, so this comment is retrospective and unlikely to require action unless Julia chooses to adjust future submissions.
