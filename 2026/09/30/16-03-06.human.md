This is the third patch in Kaartic Sivaraam’s RFC v2 series improving Git’s error messages for invalid `--git-dir` directories. Patrick Steinhardt’s review focuses on two technical points: error messages should include `strerror(errno)` to clarify *why* operations like `lstat()` or `readlink()` failed, and the patch’s diff would be clearer if the new `is_git_directory_verbose()` helper were introduced in a separate preparatory commit rather than interleaved with the refactoring of `validate_headref()`.

The review is substantive but narrowly scoped—it does not question the overall direction of the series (adding explanatory "reason:" lines to error messages) or the design of the new helper. Instead, it targets specific omissions in error detail and patch organization that could make the implementation more robust and easier to review. The feedback is actionable and aligns with Git’s conventions for error reporting, where system-level failures are typically paired with `errno` descriptions. No objections to the core idea have been raised, and the series remains in the RFC phase awaiting broader feedback on edge-case coverage.
