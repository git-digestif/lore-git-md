This is a surface-level review of the second patch in Julia Evans' seven-patch series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`. Patrick Steinhardt notes two minor issues: a missing punctuation mark in the commit message ("All of the info about merge conflicts has been moved to the new guide" should end with a period) and the loss of introductory context about what a merge conflict *is* in the `git-merge` man page. The removed sections ("HOW CONFLICTS ARE PRESENTED" and "HOW TO RESOLVE CONFLICTS") previously included brief explanations of merge conflicts, which are now centralized in the new guide. While the patch itself is mechanically sound—replacing duplicated content with a cross-reference—Patrick flags that the `git-merge` man page may now feel abrupt to users who encounter it without prior knowledge of merge conflicts. This feedback aligns with earlier substantive reviews (including his own on the first patch) advocating for foundational explanations in the new guide. The patch is otherwise uncontroversial and directly serves the series' goal of reducing duplication.
