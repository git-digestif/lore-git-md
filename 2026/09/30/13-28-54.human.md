This is a documentation patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s RFC series converting Git’s `BreakingChanges` document into a proper manpage (`gitbreaking-changes(7)`). This specific patch replaces all message-ID references (e.g., `<abc1234@mail.gmail.com>`) with clickable URLs pointing to the corresponding threads on `lore.kernel.org`. Two message-IDs required URL encoding to avoid breaking AsciiDoc parsing.

The change is purely mechanical and uncontroversial—every reference in the document is updated, and the surrounding explanatory text is tweaked to clarify that the links point to the original email’s `Message-ID`. The motivation is straightforward: users no longer need to manually construct URLs from message-IDs, making it easier to jump directly to the relevant discussion. Patrick Steinhardt’s review endorses the usability improvement, noting that while `lore.kernel.org` URLs could theoretically break in the future, the message-ID can still be extracted and adapted if needed.

Patrick also suggests a minor reorganization: moving the instructions for adding new entries to the end of the document, since the target audience is expanding and most readers won’t care about the process. He flags one nit (two spaces after a period), but otherwise the patch is ready to land if the series is accepted. The RFC tag remains in place, indicating the author is still seeking feedback on the overall approach, but this patch itself is a clear win for usability.
