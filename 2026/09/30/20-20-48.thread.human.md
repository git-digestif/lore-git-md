This thread is about a regression in Git's sparse-checkout feature where `git checkout` incorrectly overwrites untracked files within the sparse-checkout cone instead of aborting. The issue was introduced in Git v2.27.0 and has persisted through current versions. Webstrand originally reported the bug with a reproduction script, identifying the culprit commit and describing the expected behavior.

Muhammed Dilshad A has now proposed a fix that modifies `unpack-trees.c` to enforce the same untracked-file protection in sparse checkouts as in full checkouts. The patch ensures the operation aborts when conflicts with untracked files are detected, rather than proceeding and overwriting them. It also addresses the misleading warning message that previously masked the error. The fix includes thorough test coverage, adding a new regression test in `t1092-sparse-checkout-compatibility.sh` and adjusting an existing test to verify consistent behavior across full and sparse checkouts.

The patch is well-scoped and directly targets the regression without introducing new user-facing changes. Given the active development in the sparse-checkout subsystem, the maintainers (Derrick Stolee and Elijah Newren) are likely to review it closely. While the fix appears uncontroversial, reviewers may examine the reordering of sparsity checks for potential side effects. No maintainer response or integration status has been recorded yet.
