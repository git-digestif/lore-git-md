This is a brief reply in the xdiff buffer-type refactoring series, specifically addressing Patrick Steinhardt’s review of patch 2/7. Jeff King responds to Patrick’s suggestion to rename `mmfile_t` to the more semantically accurate `mmbuffer_t` (or even `struct xdiff_buf`) by explaining that such a change would generate a disproportionately large diff with many secondary considerations—like variable renaming—and would introduce unnecessary complexity at this stage. He prefers to defer the naming improvement to a future effort, keeping the current consolidation to `mmfile_t` for now. The exchange remains procedural and non-blocking; the series’ technical readiness is unaffected, and no new design or correctness concerns are raised. The discussion clarifies the author’s rationale for the current approach without altering the patch’s substance.
