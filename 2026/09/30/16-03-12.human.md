This is the fourth patch in Kaartic Sivaraam’s RFC v2 series improving Git’s error messages when an explicitly provided `--git-dir` points to an invalid repository. The patch integrates the new `is_git_directory_verbose()` helper (introduced earlier in the series) into `repo_discover_explicit_gitdir()`, appending a "reason:" line to fatal errors to explain why Git rejected the directory—such as missing `HEAD`, unreadable object directories, or broken symlinks.

Patrick Steinhardt’s reply is a surface-level review. He suggests a minor wording tweak in the commit message ("more easy" → "easier") and questions the two-line error format ("reason:" on a separate line), though he acknowledges this may be subjective. He also proposes renaming the `invalid_gitdir_reason` strbuf to `errbuf` for brevity, but treats this as a nit. His feedback does not challenge the series’ direction or implementation, and the technical concerns he raised in his earlier review of patch 3 remain unaddressed here. The series is still in the RFC phase, awaiting broader feedback before expanding to other repository-discovery code paths.
