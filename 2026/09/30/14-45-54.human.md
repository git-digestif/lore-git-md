This is the final review from Patrick Steinhardt for Tamir Duberstein’s two-patch CI/build system series, now in its third version. The series addresses two CI resource issues: an OOM kill in GitHub Actions caused by `diff` comparing large test outputs, and misaligned parallelism settings across CI platforms.

Patrick’s one-line reply—“I'm happy with this version”—explicitly accepts the revised approach in Patch 2, which now uses **twice the detected CPU count** for both `make` and `prove` parallelism on GitHub Actions and GitLab CI. This change follows cross-platform benchmark data showing a 7 % Linux speedup and avoiding a 26 % macOS slowdown. The earlier version of Patch 2 had proposed simply matching GitLab’s one-job-per-CPU policy, but Patrick’s review of v2 had requested empirical evidence and alignment between the two CI providers, both of which are now addressed.

Patch 1, which replaces `test_cmp` with `test_cmp_bin` in `t/t4205-log-pretty-formats.sh` to prevent OOM kills, remains unchanged and was already endorsed by Jeff King and Junio C Hamano. With Patrick’s sign-off, the entire series is now fully reviewed and ready for integration. No further discussion or revisions are expected.
