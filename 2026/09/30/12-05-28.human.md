This is a **bugfix follow-up** in Patrick Steinhardt’s reftable timezone series, addressing a sign-handling nit raised by Karthik Nayak in the first patch. The change is tiny but important: the `local_time_tzoffset()` function now applies `minutes_to_tz()` *after* multiplying the offset by `eastwest`, rather than before, to avoid unintentionally flipping the sign. This aligns the code with the intended mathematical behavior and resolves the ambiguity Karthik flagged.

The fix is mechanical and uncontroversial — a one-line swap in `date.c` — and Patrick’s reply suggests he agrees with the correction. No other changes are made; the rest of the series (including the new timezone helpers and their call sites) remains untouched. The series is now one step closer to readiness, with only Karthik’s test-coverage nit (a negative timezone with non-zero minutes) still outstanding.
