This is a brief but substantive review comment on Phillip Wood’s two-patch series that teaches `git checkout -m` to remember and reuse the original conflict labels (branch names) instead of generic "base", "ours", and "theirs". The series is under active review, with Junio already flagging documentation and portability concerns.

Johannes Sixt (Hannes) raises a fundamental design question: instead of storing the labels in a new `.git/MERGE_LABELS` file, would an index extension be a better place for this auxiliary merge metadata? His comment is concise but points to a potential architectural shift—index extensions are already used for other merge-related state (e.g., conflict entries), so co-locating the labels there might be more consistent and less prone to race conditions or cleanup issues than a separate file. This is not a surface-level nit; it challenges the storage mechanism itself and could influence how future merge-related state is handled.

The question is open-ended and invites discussion.
