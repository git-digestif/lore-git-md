This is a **substantive review** of the first patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s reftable timezone bugfix series. Karthik Nayak, a reftable backend contributor, engages with the implementation details of the new `tz_to_minutes()` and `minutes_to_tz()` helpers.

The review is narrowly focused on the C89 compatibility dance in `tz_to_minutes()` and a potential mathematical oversight in `local_time_tzoffset()`. Karthik notes that the C89 workaround (flipping the sign before division) is technically unnecessary under C99 but accepts Patrick’s decision to retain it for safety. He also flags what appears to be a sign-handling inconsistency in `local_time_tzoffset()`: the function currently applies `minutes_to_tz()` to the offset *before* multiplying by `eastwest`, which could invert the intended sign. This observation is precise and actionable, though Karthik does not label it as a bug outright.

The rest of the patch — the mechanical conversion of call sites, the new function signatures, and the commit message — is explicitly marked as correct. No other issues are raised, and the review does not question the series’ overall goal or structure. The feedback is technical, targeted, and unlikely to derail the series, but the sign-handling point may require a follow-up patch or a reply from Patrick to clarify intent.
