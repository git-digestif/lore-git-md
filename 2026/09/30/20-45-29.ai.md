This is a **substantive review** from Jeff King (Peff) on the third patch in Taylor Blau’s four-patch bugfix series for MIDX reachability closure. Peff confirms the patch’s core premise—that incremental repacks (but not geometric repacks) can omit cruft packs from the MIDX when `repack.midxMustContainCruft` is false—and endorses the fix as reasonable. However, he identifies an **unaddressed edge case**: the `write_midx_incremental()` function (used by `--write-midx=incremental` and `GIT_TEST_MULTI_PACK_INDEX_WRITE_INCREMENTAL`) does not respect `midx_must_contain_cruft`, meaning the same reachability issue could still occur in those scenarios. Peff’s test with `--write-midx=incremental` reproduces the failure, though the environment variable `GIT_TEST_MULTI_PACK_INDEX_WRITE_INCREMENTAL=1` does not. He frames this as a pre-existing gap rather than a regression, leaving it to the author to decide whether to address it now or defer it to future work. The review is **tested** (Peff verified the behavior locally) and **substantive**, as it surfaces a correctness issue that could undermine the patch’s goal if left unhandled. The tone is collaborative, and the feedback is narrowly scoped to the MIDX-writing logic.
