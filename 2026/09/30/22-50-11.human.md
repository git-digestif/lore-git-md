This is a short follow-up in the ongoing `xdiff` buffer-size refactoring series. Jeff King acknowledges two points raised by Patrick Steinhardt in his review of patch 4/5. First, the patch temporarily removes NUL-termination of `mmfile_t` buffers, which Patrick noted could leave an unsafe intermediate state; Jeff agrees to reorder the series so the NUL-termination fix (currently patch 3/5) comes first, eliminating that window. Second, the new `read_mmfile()` call emits an error message on read failures, whereas the original code silently ignored them. Jeff considers this a usability improvement and plans to document it in the commit message. Neither change alters the technical substance of the series, but both address design and clarity concerns raised during review. The series remains on track for merging once these presentation adjustments are made.
