This is a CI/build system patch series that is cooking in `next` and now has Junio’s explicit approval. The latest email is a brief note from Phillip Wood acknowledging the improvements in v3 but raising two remaining concerns before the series graduates to `master`.

Phillip’s feedback is substantive but narrowly scoped. First, he asks for clarity on what the filename-and-line-number annotations actually do in the GitHub Actions UI: the commit message claims they produce clickable links, but Phillip reports that in practice the links are not clickable in the `linux-leaks` job. This suggests either a mismatch between the annotation format and GitHub’s parser, or an issue with how the annotations are rendered. Second, he questions the selective escaping of annotations: the new `github_escape_message_` helper escapes `%` and `\r` in test descriptions, but existing annotations (like `group::` markers) are not escaped, and test descriptions are single-line by design. Phillip sees this as over-engineering and wants a clearer rationale for the escaping logic.

The email does not block the series—Junio has already marked it ready for `next`—but it flags two usability issues that could affect how actionable the failure reports actually are. If the links are not clickable, the feature’s core value is diminished; if the escaping is unnecessary, it adds complexity without benefit. Neither issue is a correctness problem, but both could lead to follow-up patches if the community agrees they need fixing. The thread is now waiting for either a response from Harald or a decision from Junio on whether these points are worth addressing before the series graduates to `master`.
