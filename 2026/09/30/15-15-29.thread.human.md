This thread is about a performance optimization patch (v3) by Karthik Nayak that speeds up operations rewriting the packed-refs file by avoiding redundant formatting of unchanged references. The patch, which touches only `refs/packed-backend.c`, introduces raw byte writes for unchanged refs while preserving original formatting (including case, whitespace, and malformed refnames), and includes a benchmark showing a ~20% speedup when deleting 100,000 packed refs.

Toon Claes initially raised correctness concerns about side effects of bypassing `fprintf()`, which were addressed in v2 by documenting these effects and splitting the write logic into separate functions. His final review in v3 was surface-level, marking the patch as ready. Patrick Steinhardt has now weighed in with a substantive review, endorsing the patch’s direction and performance gains but identifying two minor issues: the commit message lacks a clear problem statement, and a comment about the `record_start` field in `struct packed_ref_iterator` is misleading, as the code shows it advances independently of `pos`. He also questions the necessity of the trivial `write_packed_entry_raw()` wrapper but does not insist on its removal. No new interoperability concerns were raised, and Patrick’s feedback does not require a reroll, leaving the patch’s fate to Junio’s eventual pickup. The patch remains under review with no maintainer decision recorded.
