This thread is about a performance optimization for Git’s packed-refs file rewrites. Karthik Nayak’s patch (now at v5) speeds up operations like deleting references by avoiding redundant formatting of unchanged refs, achieving a ~20% speedup in benchmarks. The change preserves original formatting—including case, whitespace, and malformed refnames—as a documented side effect.
