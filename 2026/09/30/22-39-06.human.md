This is a historical clarification in the thread adding `@{p}` as a shorthand for `@{push}`. Jeff King (Peff) expands on Junio’s earlier explanation of why `@{push}` originally shipped without a shorthand. He recalls that `@{p}` was reserved during the 2015 discussions to avoid confusion with a separate, never-merged proposal for `@{publish}`, and that `@{pull}` (as an alias for `@{upstream}`) was also floated at the time. Peff confirms that neither of those older proposals is still in play, so the original rationale for omitting `@{p}` no longer applies. He states he has no objection to the current patch, treating it as a straightforward consistency fix rather than a design conflict. The thread remains uncontroversial, with no new technical concerns raised.
