This is the maintainer’s reply to Patrick Steinhardt’s review of the fifth patch in Jeff King’s xdiff buffer refactoring series. The patch itself is a one-line change that adds a defensive NUL-terminator to buffers read by `read_mmfile()`, replacing `xmalloc(sz ? sz : 1)` with `xmallocz(sz)`. Junio’s comment is purely about the commit message’s clarity: he suggests rewording it to explicitly state that the NUL is added by using `xmallocz()`, rather than leaving the mechanism implicit. The feedback is surface-level and does not question the patch’s technical content or motivation. The series as a whole remains ready for merging, with only minor cosmetic improvements suggested.
