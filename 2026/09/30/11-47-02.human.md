This is a review of the first patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s three-part bugfix series for reftable timezone handling. Karthik Nayak’s feedback is **surface-level**: it focuses on code clarity and minor arithmetic details rather than the patch’s correctness or design.

Karthik walks through the patch line-by-line, confirming the logic for converting between Git’s traditional "[+-]HHMM" timezone format and signed-minute offsets. He notes that the C89-compatible division dance (to handle negative values) is technically unnecessary under C99 but agrees it’s safer to retain for portability. His only substantive question is about the order of operations in `local_time_tzoffset()`: whether `minutes_to_tz(offset) * eastwest` would be clearer than `minutes_to_tz(offset * eastwest)`. The difference is purely stylistic—both produce the same result—and he does not insist on a change.

The rest of the review is mechanical: Karthik acknowledges the patch’s intent, approves the helper functions, and marks the series as "looks good" without raising deeper concerns. This is a green light for the preparatory refactoring, but it doesn’t address the larger reftable interoperability issues the series aims to fix (those are tackled in patches 2 and 3). If you’re tracking the reftable backend, this patch is a safe, uncontroversial step forward.
