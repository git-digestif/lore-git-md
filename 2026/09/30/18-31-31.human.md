This is the author’s reply to Junio C Hamano’s review of the final patch in the `git backfill --dry-run` series. Pablo Sabater accepts Junio’s critique of the overloaded `ctx->object_info_enabled` flag and proposes two concrete changes for the upcoming v2: (1) tightening the condition that clears the flag to `if (status != FETCH_OBJECT_INFO_OK || !results->sizes)` so that any non-success status or missing size data disables size reporting, and (2) renaming the flag to `ctx->sizes_valid` to make its purpose unambiguous. The rest of the patch—output format, fallback behavior, and resource cleanup—remains unchanged. This reply resolves the last technical blocker identified in the thread, leaving only the conceptual objection from Derrick Stolee (that the feature may not address a real user need) still open. The series is now technically sound and ready for deeper review once v2 is posted.
