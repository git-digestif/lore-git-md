The thread introduces a `--dry-run` option for `git backfill`, allowing users to preview the number and total uncompressed size of blobs that would be fetched before committing to the actual operation. The feature, proposed by Pablo Sabater in a five-patch series (v1), has undergone technical refinement—including squashing patches, renaming functions, and resolving control-flow correctness issues—but now faces a significant conceptual hurdle. Derrick Stolee, the original author of `git backfill`, argues that the feature does not address a concrete user need and that the uncompressed size estimate may not meaningfully inform a user’s decision. Junio C Hamano has explicitly endorsed Stolee’s position, reinforcing that the feature is unlikely to proceed without evidence of demand from regular `backfill` users. The author has conceded this point, effectively stalling the thread.

The implementation touches several subsystems, including transport logic, fetch-object-info, and the backfill built-in, and introduces a new enum value (`FETCH_OBJECT_INFO_UNAVAILABLE`) and a dedicated flag (`ctx->sizes_valid`) to ensure size reporting is only done when data is complete. While the technical groundwork is sound, the lack of substantive technical reviews or acks—combined with the conceptual objection from two of the project’s most influential voices—means the feature’s future hinges on whether a compelling use case emerges. For now, the series remains under review, with a v2 pending but unlikely to advance without broader support.
