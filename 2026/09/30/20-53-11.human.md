This is a **substantive review** from Jeff King (Peff) on the fourth patch in Taylor Blau’s four-patch series fixing MIDX reachability closure corner cases. The patch ensures cruft packs are included in MIDXs whenever kept packs are present, preventing bitmap-writer failures when kept commits reference objects only in cruft packs.

Peff’s review raises two technical concerns. First, he questions whether the fix might trigger unexpectedly during pushes, where Git temporarily installs `.keep` files to prevent repacks from deleting active packs. If the MIDX suddenly includes cruft packs during these transient states, it could cause performance regressions in large repositories. Second, he wonders why `.keep` packs aren’t simply traversed like other included packs (via `--stdin-packs=follow`), which would naturally ensure reachability closure without special-casing cruft packs.

The concerns are narrowly scoped to implementation trade-offs rather than the patch’s core correctness. Peff does not challenge the problem statement or the need for a fix, but his questions suggest the current approach might have unintended side effects. The review is substantive because it engages with the patch’s behavior in real-world scenarios (pushes) and alternative design choices, rather than focusing on surface-level details. The author will need to address these points to ensure the fix is robust and performant.
