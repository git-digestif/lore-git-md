This is the final review reply in the RFC v2 series improving Git’s error reporting for invalid `--git-dir` directories. Patrick Steinhardt provides two surface-level suggestions on the fourth patch: a grammar nit in the commit message ("more easy" → "easier") and a preference for naming the `strbuf` variable `errbuf` instead of `invalid_gitdir_reason`. He also voices a subjective concern about the two-line error message format ("fatal: ...\nreason: ..."), suggesting it might feel cleaner to combine the lines or use an "error:" prefix before "fatal:".

The review remains narrowly focused on presentation and naming, with no new technical objections raised. Patrick explicitly endorses the motivation ("Which I think is a good motivation") and does not contest the implementation or the RFC’s direction. The feedback is purely cosmetic and does not block the series from moving forward. The thread is still in the RFC phase, awaiting broader feedback on whether to expand coverage to remaining cases like repository discovery or `GIT_ALTERNATE_OBJECT_DIRECTORIES`.
