This is the fourth and final patch in Taylor Blau’s four-patch series fixing multi-pack-index (MIDX) reachability closure corner cases. It addresses a scenario where `.keep` packs can cause MIDXs to omit cruft packs that contain objects reachable from the kept commits, leading to bitmap-writer failures.

The fix is in `repack-midx.c`: whenever the MIDX contains any kept packs, cruft packs are now unconditionally included. This sidesteps the need to verify reachability closure for the kept packs and ensures the MIDX remains closed under reachability. The change is minimal (five lines added) and well-motivated: kept packs may reference objects whose only copy is in a cruft pack, and the MIDX must include all reachable objects to support bitmaps.

The new test in `t7704-repack-cruft.sh` sets up a geometric repack with a `.keep` pack containing a commit and its tree, while the commit’s parent remains in a cruft pack. The test confirms that the repack succeeds and that a bitmap can be written afterward. This scenario previously failed because the MIDX omitted the cruft pack, breaking reachability closure.

The patch is narrowly scoped, touching only the MIDX pack-selection logic and adding test coverage. It complements the earlier patches in the series, which fixed similar issues for `--stdin-packs=follow` and incremental repacks. The series as a whole now comprehensively addresses the reachability closure problem in MIDXs, and this patch closes the last known gap. The changes are confined to the repack and MIDX subsystems, so the risk of unintended side effects is low. The fix looks correct and uncontroversial.
