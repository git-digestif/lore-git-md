This is a follow-up in the `git backfill --dry-run` thread, now at a conceptual impasse. He floats two ideas for improving the object-info protocol—either having the server compute deltified sizes based on what the client already has, or returning the server’s local compressed size—but notes that both would still be estimates and require protocol changes. Without evidence that users want this capability, he concedes the feature might not be worth merging and suggests dropping it unless someone steps forward with a concrete use case.
