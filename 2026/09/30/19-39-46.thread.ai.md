Core problem or goal
Add `@{p}` as a shorthand for `@{push}` in Git revision syntax, mirroring the existing `@{u}` shorthand for `@{upstream}`. The goal is to eliminate the historical asymmetry where `@{u}` was introduced in 2010 alongside `@{upstream}` while `@{push}` (added in 2015) lacked a shorthand. The asymmetry was not deliberate but resulted from the 2015 patch series focusing on the push-model flexibility for Git 2.0 and overlooking the shorthand addition, despite Junio Hamano already noting the inconsistency in 2014. The change improves user convenience without introducing new behavior, aligning with Git’s existing case-insensitive revision suffix handling.
