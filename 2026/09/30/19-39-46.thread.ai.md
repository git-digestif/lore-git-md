Core problem or goal
Add `@{p}` as a shorthand for `@{push}` in Git revision syntax, mirroring the existing `@{u}` shorthand for `@{upstream}`. The goal is to eliminate the historical asymmetry where `@{u}` was introduced in 2010 alongside `@{upstream}` while `@{push}` (added in 2015) lacked a shorthand. The updated commit message in v2 now fully documents the historical rationale: the 2010 introduction of `@{upstream}`/`@{u}`, Junio Hamano’s 2014 note of the asymmetry, and the 2015 focus on push-model flexibility without addressing the shorthand. The change improves user convenience without introducing new behavior, aligning with Git’s existing case-insensitive revision suffix handling.
