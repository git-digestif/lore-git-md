Core problem or goal
Add `@{p}` as a shorthand for `@{push}` in Git revision syntax, mirroring the existing `@{u}` shorthand for `@{upstream}`. The goal is to eliminate the inconsistency where `@{u}` works but `@{p}` does not, improving user convenience without introducing new behavior. The historical asymmetry arose because `@{upstream}` and `@{u}` were introduced in 2010, while `@{push}` was added in 2015 without a shorthand, possibly due to `@{push}` being considered short enough at the time. Later discussions in 2015 considered alternative semantics for `@{p}` (e.g., `@{publish}`) and `@{pull}` as an alias for `@{upstream}`, but these proposals were never implemented, leaving the namespace uncontested.
