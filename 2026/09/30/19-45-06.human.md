This is a **documentation patch discussion** in the ongoing RFC series to create a `gitbreaking-changes(7)` manpage. Junio C Hamano responds to Patrick Steinhardt’s review of the second patch, which replaces message-ID references with clickable URLs.

Junio raises a practical concern: some message-IDs contain characters that must be URL-encoded (e.g., `%` becomes `%25`), and the current patch does not handle this. More fundamentally, he dislikes the idea of *only* showing URLs, arguing that the message-ID itself should remain visible for reference. His suggested compromise is to include both the URL and the message-ID in the source, formatted so that the rendered output shows a readable link while preserving the original identifier.

This is a **surface-level review**—it focuses on presentation and usability rather than the series’ overall goal. The feedback is narrow but actionable: the author will need to rework the URL formatting to satisfy Junio’s preference for dual representation. No objections to the broader RFC (converting `BreakingChanges` into a manpage) are raised here. The discussion remains confined to this single patch; the rest of the series is unaffected.
