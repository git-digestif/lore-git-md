This thread is a four-patch series from Taylor Blau fixing corner cases in Git’s multi-pack-index (MIDX) and cruft-pack machinery where MIDXs can end up missing objects needed for reachability closure. The series targets three specific scenarios: `--stdin-packs=follow`, incremental repacks, and `.keep` packs. The patches are under review, with no prior versions, and touch `builtin/pack-objects.c`, `repack.c`, and `repack-midx.c`, along with new tests in `t5331` and `t7704`.

Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, has reviewed the first two patches, offering minor stylistic feedback on the second and noting a potential future maintenance concern in the first, but nothing that blocks progress. Derrick Stolee’s recent review of the second patch suggests a technical improvement: replacing the `oid_array` used to collect extra roots (trees and tags) with an `oidset` to avoid duplicates and improve efficiency. This change is narrowly scoped to the internal data structure and doesn’t alter the patch’s behavior, but it’s a well-justified tweak that could matter for performance. The series remains uncontroversial, with no open questions or objections, and is likely to move forward once the minor feedback is addressed.
