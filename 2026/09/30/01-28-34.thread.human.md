The series to fix MIDX reachability closure corner cases, now at v2 with eight patches, is at a crossroads after nearly a month of review. Taylor Blau’s updates addressed all substantive feedback from Jeff King, Derrick Stolee, and Junio C Hamano, leaving most patches uncontroversial and ready for integration—patches 1, 3, 4, 6, 7, and 8 are technically sound and have no open objections. However, two sticking points remain: the choice of data structure for `extra_roots` in patch 2 (where Elijah Newren’s test cases showed an `oid_array` is functionally necessary to preserve delta compression quality and attribute handling, while Derrick Stolee and Taylor initially favored an `oidset` for efficiency) and the readability of a double-negative conditional in patch 5 (which Jeff King found confusing and suggested refactoring). Junio’s latest email signals the maintainer’s patience is wearing thin; unless Taylor or another participant can break the logjam on these two issues soon, the series risks being dropped from the integration branches. The ball is now in Taylor’s court to either propose a compromise or clarify whether the current state is the final word.
