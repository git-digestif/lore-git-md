This thread is about a patch series from Taylor Blau that fixes corner cases in Git’s multi-pack-index (MIDX) and cruft-pack machinery where MIDXs can violate their reachability closure invariant, causing bitmap generation failures. The series, now at v2 with eight patches, addresses four specific scenarios: `--stdin-packs=follow`, incremental repacks, `.keep` packs, and `--write-midx=incremental`.

The series has been under review for nearly a month, and Junio recently questioned whether it was still making progress or had stalled. Taylor’s latest response clarifies that the series is ready for integration in its current form, with no further changes planned. Most patches—1/8 (refactoring), 3/8 (incremental repack fix), 4/8 (mechanical efficiency improvement), 6/8 (refactoring in `repack-midx.c`), 7/8 (mechanical refactoring), and 8/8 (fix for incremental MIDX writes)—are uncontroversial and have received maintainer or reviewer approval. Jeff King (Peff) has explicitly endorsed patches 4/8 and 6/8 as correct and sensible, and the rest have either been accepted or carry only minor, non-blocking concerns.

However, two patches remain contentious. Patch 2/8, which introduces a two-walk approach to handle reachability closure, has sparked debate over whether to use an `oid_array` or an `oidset` for tracking `extra_roots`. Elijah Newren’s test cases demonstrate that an `oidset` would introduce functional regressions, such as blobs being written in full instead of as deltas and misapplied path-based attributes. Peff has sided with Newren, emphasizing the importance of preserving pack-order correlation for delta compression quality, though he acknowledges the memory trade-offs. Taylor initially accepted Derrick Stolee’s suggestion to use an `oidset` but now defends the `oid_array` as the correct choice despite its memory overhead. The discussion remains unresolved, though Taylor treats it as non-blocking.

Patch 5/8, which adjusts geometric repack traversal to include ancestors of kept objects, contains a confusing conditional (`if (!(geometry.split_factor && !midx_must_contain_cruft))`) that Peff finds unreadable. Taylor acknowledges the awkwardness but argues that the alternative proposed by Peff is no clearer, tracing the confusion to the naming of the `midx_must_contain_cruft` configuration variable. He suggests renaming it to `midx_may_exclude_cruft` (defaulting to `false`) for greater clarity, but this remains unresolved.

Junio now faces a decision: merge the uncontroversial patches separately or hold the entire series pending resolution of the remaining issues. The thread has reached a stalemate on the two contested patches, with no new technical discussion or code changes forthcoming. The series is well-motivated and narrowly scoped, but the unresolved issues in patches 2/8 and 5/8 risk stalling its progress.
