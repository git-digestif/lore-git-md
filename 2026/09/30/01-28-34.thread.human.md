This thread is a v2 patch series from Taylor Blau fixing corner cases in Git’s multi-pack-index (MIDX) and cruft-pack machinery where MIDXs could end up missing objects required for reachability closure, breaking bitmap generation. The series now stands at eight patches, incorporating all feedback from the v1 review by Jeff King, Derrick Stolee, and Junio Hamano.

The core issue is that MIDXs must contain all objects reachable from their tips, but several scenarios—including `--stdin-packs=follow`, incremental repacks, `.keep` packs, and `--write-midx=incremental`—could violate this invariant. The fixes are narrowly scoped and well-tested, with new coverage in `t5331`, `t7704`, and `t7705` verifying the scenarios. Most patches are uncontroversial: patch 1/8 (a refactoring) and patch 4/8 (a mechanical efficiency improvement) have received maintainer and reviewer approval, and patches 3/8, 6/8, 7/8, and 8/8 address specific edge cases with clear technical rationale. Jeff King’s latest reply on patch 8/8 defers to the test coverage, leaving the patch ready for integration.

Two points remain contested. The most significant is the data structure for `extra_roots` in patch 2/8: Elijah Newren’s test cases show that an `oid_array` (preserving insertion order) is functionally necessary for delta compression and attribute handling, while an `oidset` (as suggested by Derrick Stolee) would introduce regressions. Jeff King has sided with Newren, but Taylor Blau continues to argue for the `oidset` on memory-efficiency grounds. Additionally, three implementation details in patches 2/8 and 5/8—comment placement, a double `prepare_revision_walk()` call, and a confusing conditional—are still under discussion, though none block integration. The series is otherwise complete and ready for Junio to pick up.
