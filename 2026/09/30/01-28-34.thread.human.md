Here’s the human-mode summary for a developer catching up:

This thread is about Taylor Blau’s eight-patch series fixing corner cases in Git’s multi-pack-index (MIDX) and cruft-pack machinery where MIDXs can end up missing objects reachable from their tips, breaking bitmap generation. The series has been through two iterations; v2 incorporated all feedback from Jeff King, Derrick Stolee, and Junio C Hamano.

Six of the eight patches—1/8 (refactoring), 3/8 (fixing `--stdin-packs=follow`), 4/8 (mechanical efficiency improvement), 6/8 (MIDX compaction refactoring), 7/8 (deferred allocation), and 8/8 (incremental MIDX write fix)—are uncontroversial and ready to merge. The sticking points are patches 2/8 and 5/8. In 2/8, Elijah Newren and Jeff King argue that using an `oid_array` for `extra_roots` is necessary to preserve path and namehash ordering for delta compression and attribute handling, while Taylor and Derrick Stolee initially preferred an `oidset` for efficiency. In 5/8, the conditional controlling whether `--keep-pack` arguments are passed during geometric repacks is confusing due to double negation, and no one has proposed a clearer alternative. Taylor has declared the series final, with no further changes planned, but Junio remains unsure whether to merge the uncontroversial patches separately or hold the entire series. The thread is now waiting on Junio’s decision.
