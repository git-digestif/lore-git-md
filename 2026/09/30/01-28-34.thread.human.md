This thread is a bugfix series from Taylor Blau addressing corner cases in Git’s multi-pack-index (MIDX) and cruft-pack machinery where MIDXs can fail to be closed under reachability, breaking bitmap generation. The v1 series (four patches) targets three scenarios: `--stdin-packs=follow`, incremental repacks, and `.keep` packs. Patch 1/4 refactors `--stdin-packs` handling, while patches 2–4 implement the fixes with new tests in `t5331` and `t7704`.

Review has been active, with Junio C Hamano, Derrick Stolee, and Jeff King providing feedback. Junio’s concerns about patch 1/4 were addressed with a documentation tweak, and Stolee’s suggestion to replace an `oid_array` with an `oidset` was accepted. Peff’s **substantive** reviews of patches 2–4 endorsed the fixes as strict improvements but raised deeper questions: the necessity of a two-walk traversal (patch 2/4), an unaddressed edge case in `write_midx_incremental()` (patch 3/4), and potential race conditions with `.keep` packs (patch 4/4). Taylor has acknowledged these limitations, agreeing to defer larger traversal changes but committing to fix the `write_midx_incremental()` gap in v2. The `.keep` pack questions remain open.

The series is still under review, with no merge decisions yet. Taylor plans to post v2 soon, incorporating the `write_midx_incremental()` fix and predatory patches for clarity. The core fixes are uncontested, but the scope of v2 may expand slightly to address Peff’s edge case. No regressions or design objections have been raised; the discussion centers on completeness and future-proofing.
