This thread is a four-patch series from Taylor Blau fixing corner cases in Git’s multi-pack-index (MIDX) and cruft-pack machinery where MIDXs can end up missing objects needed for reachability closure. The bugs surface in three scenarios: `--stdin-packs=follow`, incremental repacks, and `.keep` packs. The series is under review with no prior versions.

Taylor’s patches refactor `--stdin-packs` handling (patch 1/4), fix the `--stdin-packs=follow` scenario by treating trees and tags in included/excluded-open packs as reachability roots (patch 2/4), force cruft packs into the MIDX during incremental repacks (patch 3/4), and ensure cruft packs are included whenever `.keep` packs are present (patch 4/4). The fixes are narrowly scoped, with new tests covering each scenario. Junio C Hamano has reviewed patches 1/4 and 2/4, confirming their correctness and offering only minor stylistic suggestions—no substantive objections have been raised. The series remains uncontroversial and is likely to proceed as-is.
