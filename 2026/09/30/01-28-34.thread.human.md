This thread is a four-patch series from Taylor Blau fixing corner cases in Git’s multi-pack-index (MIDX) and cruft-pack machinery where MIDXs can end up missing objects that are reachable from their tips, causing failures when generating reachability bitmaps. The series targets three specific scenarios: `--stdin-packs=follow`, incremental repacks, and `.keep` packs.

The first patch is a pure refactoring that introduces a `stdin_packs_context` struct to clean up `--stdin-packs` handling in `pack-objects`. The second patch fixes the `--stdin-packs=follow` case by ensuring trees and tags in included or excluded-open packs (or loose objects with `--unpacked`) are treated as roots for the reachability walk. The third patch addresses incremental repacks by forcing cruft packs to be included in the MIDX when `--incremental` is passed to `pack-objects`, fixing a regression introduced in June 2025. The newly summarized fourth patch closes the last gap by ensuring cruft packs are unconditionally included in the MIDX whenever `.keep` packs are present, since kept packs may reference objects whose only copy is in a cruft pack.

The series is under review and remains uncontested so far. It touches `builtin/pack-objects.c`, `repack.c`, and `repack-midx.c`, and adds new tests in `t5331-pack-objects-stdin.sh` and `t7704-repack-cruft.sh`. The changes are narrowly scoped and well-tested, with no open questions or objections raised yet. The fixes are confined to the repack and MIDX subsystems, so the risk of unintended side effects appears low.
