This thread is a well-scoped bugfix series from Taylor Blau addressing corner cases in Git’s multi-pack-index (MIDX) and cruft-pack machinery where MIDXs could violate their reachability-closure invariant. The v2 series, now complete at eight patches, fixes four specific scenarios: `--stdin-packs=follow`, incremental repacks, `.keep` packs, and `--write-midx=incremental` (including `GIT_TEST_MULTI_PACK_INDEX_WRITE_INCREMENTAL`). All substantive feedback from Jeff King, Derrick Stolee, and Junio C Hamano has been incorporated, and the series is now ready for integration.

The core issue—MIDXs containing objects not closed under reachability—was causing bitmap generation failures. Key fixes include ensuring trees and tags in included/excluded-open packs are treated as roots (patch 2/8), forcing cruft packs into the MIDX during incremental repacks (patch 3/8), and adjusting geometric repacks to traverse ancestors of kept objects without copying the kept objects themselves (patch 5/8). The final patch (8/8) closes a previously unnoticed gap in incremental MIDX writes by ensuring all required packs (cruft or kept) are included in the MIDX chain, directly addressing Jeff King’s feedback from v1.

The series is narrowly focused, with each patch targeting a specific scenario and accompanied by targeted tests. Mechanical refactorings (patches 1/8, 4/8, 6/8, and 7/8) improve code clarity and efficiency without altering behavior, while the remaining patches introduce minimal, well-justified changes to maintain reachability closure. The only loose end—a pre-existing edge case involving `--pack-kept-objects` and cruft packs—has been deferred to a separate follow-up. The series is uncontroversial and should graduate to `next` shortly.
