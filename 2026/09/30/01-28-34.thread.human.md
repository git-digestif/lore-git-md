This thread is a four-patch series from Taylor Blau fixing corner cases in Git’s multi-pack-index (MIDX) and cruft-pack machinery where MIDXs can end up missing objects that should be reachable from their tips, breaking bitmap generation. The series targets three specific scenarios: `--stdin-packs=follow`, incremental repacks, and `.keep` packs.

The first two patches have been summarized so far. Patch 1/4 is a mechanical refactoring to prepare for the fixes, introducing a `stdin_packs_context` struct to clean up `--stdin-packs` handling. Patch 2/4 addresses the `--stdin-packs=follow` scenario by ensuring trees and tags in included or excluded-open packs (or loose objects with `--unpacked`) are treated as roots for the reachability walk. Patch 3/4, just posted, fixes a long-standing issue in incremental repacks where cruft packs might be omitted from the MIDX when `repack.midxMustContainCruft` is set to "false". The fix forces cruft packs to be included in the MIDX during incremental repacks, ensuring reachability closure. The change is minimal, touching only `builtin/repack.c` and adding a single test.

The series is under initial review and remains uncontroversial. No objections or requests for changes have been raised yet. The patches are well-motivated, with clear explanations of the bugs and targeted fixes. Test coverage is appropriate, with new tests in `t5331-pack-objects-stdin.sh` and `t7704-repack-cruft.sh` verifying the fixes. The final patch (4/4) addressing `.keep` packs is still pending.
