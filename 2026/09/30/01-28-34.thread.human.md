This thread is a v2 patch series from Taylor Blau fixing corner cases in Git’s multi-pack-index (MIDX) and cruft-pack machinery where MIDXs can end up missing objects required for reachability, breaking bitmap generation. The series addresses four scenarios: `--stdin-packs=follow`, incremental repacks, `.keep` packs, and `--write-midx=incremental`. After substantive feedback from Jeff King, Derrick Stolee, and Junio C Hamano on v1, v2 now includes eight patches, all of which are uncontroversial except for one lingering design question.

The core dispute centers on patch 2/8, where Elijah Newren and Jeff King argue that an `oid_array` (rather than an `oidset`) is necessary to preserve path and namehash ordering for delta compression and attribute handling. Taylor initially accepted Derrick Stolee’s suggestion to use an `oidset` for efficiency, but Newren’s test cases demonstrated functional regressions—blobs written in full instead of as deltas, and misapplied `.gitattributes`—if the order of roots is lost. Jeff King’s latest follow-up sides with Newren, emphasizing that the memory trade-off is justified for cruft packs, though the discussion remains unresolved. Four minor implementation details (comment placement, double `prepare_revision_walk()` calls, a confusing conditional in patch 5/8, and the two-walk traversal approach) were debated but deferred as cleanup items.

The rest of the series is ready for integration. Patch 1/8 (a refactoring) and patch 4/8 (a mechanical efficiency improvement) are explicitly endorsed by Junio and Jeff King, respectively. Patches 6/8 and 7/8 (refactorings in `repack-midx.c`) are uncontroversial after Taylor clarified their necessity for future optimizations. Patch 8/8 fixes a previously unnoticed gap in incremental MIDX writes, ensuring required packs (like cruft packs) are included in the chain. The series is narrowly scoped, well-tested, and addresses all prior feedback, though the `oid_array`/`oidset` debate and the deferred implementation details may require a v3 or follow-up patches.

Key participants: Taylor Blau (author), Jeff King (substantive reviewer), Elijah Newren (technical advocate for `oid_array`), Derrick Stolee (original `oidset` suggestion), and Junio C Hamano (maintainer, yet to review v2). The thread is under active review, with no major objections beyond the `extra_roots` data structure choice.
