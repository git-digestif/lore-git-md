This thread is a four-patch series from Taylor Blau fixing corner cases in Git’s multi-pack-index (MIDX) and cruft-pack machinery where MIDXs can end up missing objects they claim to contain, breaking reachability bitmaps. The series targets three specific scenarios: `--stdin-packs=follow`, incremental repacks, and `.keep` packs. All patches are under review, with no prior versions submitted.

The first three patches have received substantive feedback from Junio Hamano, Derrick Stolee, and Jeff King, addressing refactoring, algorithmic trade-offs, and edge cases. The latest discussion centers on the fourth patch, which ensures cruft packs are included in MIDXs whenever `.keep` packs are present. Jeff King raised two technical questions about this patch: whether temporary `.keep` files installed during pushes could racily trigger the fix, potentially degrading performance, and whether the patch’s unconditional inclusion of cruft packs is the most principled approach. Neither question undermines the patch’s correctness, but they could prompt further refinement.

The series is narrowly scoped, with clear test coverage and no unresolved objections to its core goals. Open questions include Derrick Stolee’s suggestion to replace an `oid_array` with an `oidset` for efficiency, Jeff King’s concerns about the two-walk approach in patch 2/4, and the unaddressed edge case in `write_midx_incremental()` for patch 3/4. The thread remains active, with the focus now on the `.keep` pack handling in patch 4/4.
