This thread is about Taylor Blau’s eight-patch series fixing corner cases in Git’s multi-pack-index (MIDX) and cruft-pack machinery where MIDXs can end up containing objects not closed under reachability. The series, now at v2, addresses feedback from v1 and is narrowly scoped to four scenarios: `--stdin-packs=follow`, incremental repacks, `.keep` packs, and `--write-midx=incremental`.

The series is at a decision point. Six patches (1, 3, 4, 6, 7, and 8) are uncontroversial and ready for integration, with explicit endorsements from Jeff King and Junio. The remaining two—patch 2 (data structure choice for `extra_roots`) and patch 5 (confusing conditional logic)—remain contested. Elijah Newren and Jeff King argue that an `oid_array` is functionally necessary to preserve delta compression quality and attribute handling, while Taylor defends the current implementation as final despite the unresolved debate. Similarly, the double-negation conditional in patch 5 is acknowledged as awkward but treated as non-negotiable.

Junio’s latest email lightly chides Taylor for declaring the series "ready to go" without broader consensus, signaling skepticism about merging it in its current form. The thread is now waiting for either additional reviewer sign-off or a maintainer ruling on whether to merge the uncontroversial patches separately or hold the entire series pending resolution of the remaining issues. No further changes are planned, and the series remains under review.
