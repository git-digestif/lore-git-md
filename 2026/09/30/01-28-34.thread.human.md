This thread is a v2 patch series from Taylor Blau fixing corner cases in Git’s multi-pack-index (MIDX) and cruft-pack machinery where MIDXs could end up missing objects reachable from their tips, breaking bitmap generation. The series addresses four scenarios: `--stdin-packs=follow`, incremental repacks, `.keep` packs, and `--write-midx=incremental`. After v1 feedback from Jeff King, Derrick Stolee, and Junio Hamano, the series expanded to eight patches, incorporating all substantive review points. Most patches are uncontroversial refactorings or narrowly scoped fixes, but one low-level detail remains contested: the choice of data structure for `extra_roots` in patch 2/8.

The core dispute centers on whether to use an `oid_array` (preserving insertion order for delta compression and attribute handling) or an `oidset` (more memory-efficient and semantically cleaner). Elijah Newren’s review demonstrated that an `oidset` would cause functional regressions—blobs written in full instead of as deltas, and misapplied `.gitattributes`—while Taylor argues the `oid_array` isn’t perfect either (path-dependent namehashes can still produce suboptimal deltas). Jeff King’s earlier feedback noted these limitations but endorsed the two-walk approach (commits first, then trees/tags) as a strict improvement over the status quo. The series is otherwise ready for integration, with Junio’s approval in spirit for the uncontroversial parts. The unresolved `extra_roots` debate is the sole blocker, and no hybrid solution has been proposed yet.

Key participants include Taylor (author), Jeff King (substantive reviewer), Derrick Stolee (suggested `oidset`), and Elijah Newren (pushed back with test cases). The series is independent of other in-flight work, though a pre-existing edge case involving `--pack-kept-objects` and cruft packs is deferred to a follow-up. The v2 patches are well-tested, with new coverage for all fixed scenarios.
