This thread is a v2 patch series from Taylor Blau fixing corner cases in Git’s multi-pack-index (MIDX) and cruft-pack machinery where MIDXs can end up containing objects not closed under reachability. The series now consists of eight patches, up from four in v1, after incorporating all feedback from Jeff King, Derrick Stolee, and Junio C Hamano.

The core issue is that MIDXs must contain all objects reachable from their tips, but this invariant can be violated in four scenarios: `--stdin-packs=follow`, incremental repacks, `.keep` packs, and `--write-midx=incremental`. The fixes touch `builtin/pack-objects.c`, `repack.c`, and `repack-midx.c`, with new tests added to verify the behavior in `t5331-pack-objects-stdin.sh`, `t7704-repack-cruft.sh`, and `t7705-repack-incremental-midx.sh`. The first patch is a pure refactoring introducing `stdin_packs_context` to streamline the handling of `--stdin-packs` in `pack-objects`, addressing Junio’s feedback from v1 with a clarifying comment and a minor readability improvement.

The series is now well-motivated and narrowly scoped, with all substantive feedback incorporated. Jeff King’s review confirmed the fixes as strict improvements, though he raised nuanced questions about the two-walk approach in patch 2/8 and the handling of `.keep` packs in patch 4/8. These discussions concluded with mutual agreement to defer deeper traversal machinery changes to future work, while the immediate performance concerns for geometric repacks were addressed in v2. Derrick Stolee’s suggestion to replace an `oid_array` with an `oidset` for efficiency was also implemented. The series is under active review and appears ready for merging, with no major open questions or deferred issues beyond a pre-existing edge case involving `--pack-kept-objects` and cruft packs, which will be addressed separately.
