This thread is about a four-patch series from Taylor Blau that fixes corner cases in Git’s multi-pack-index (MIDX) and cruft-pack machinery where MIDXs can end up containing objects that aren’t closed under reachability. These gaps can break reachability bitmaps, and the series addresses three specific scenarios: `--stdin-packs=follow`, incremental repacks, and `.keep` packs. The patches touch the `pack-objects`, `repack`, and MIDX subsystems, with new tests added to verify the fixes.

The series is under active review, with no prior versions. Junio C Hamano, Derrick Stolee, and Jeff King have all weighed in with substantive feedback. Stolee suggested a technical improvement to patch 2/4—replacing an `oid_array` with an `oidset` for efficiency—while Peff endorsed the series as a strict improvement but flagged several potential edge cases, including an unaddressed gap in `write_midx_incremental()` (patch 3/4) and questions about `.keep` pack handling (patch 4/4). Peff also noted that the fixes, while effective, may not cover every possible scenario, leaving it to Taylor to decide whether to expand the series or defer remaining work.

The open questions revolve around low-level design choices (e.g., the two-walk approach in patch 2/4) and whether to address additional edge cases now or later. No one has objected to the core fixes, and the feedback has been collaborative rather than prescriptive. The series is still in flux, but the direction is clear: these are targeted bugfixes that improve MIDX reliability, with room for minor refinements before merging.
