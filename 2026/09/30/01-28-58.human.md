This is the fourth and final patch in Taylor Blau’s four-part bugfix series targeting MIDX reachability closure corner cases. It addresses a scenario where `.keep` packs can cause MIDXs to omit cruft packs they depend on, leading to bitmap-writer failures when the MIDX claims to contain all objects reachable from its tips but is actually missing some.

The fix is simple and surgical: when the MIDX contains any kept packs, cruft packs are automatically included. This sidesteps the need to prove reachability closure upfront and ensures the MIDX remains consistent. The patch adds a five-line guard in `repack-midx.c` and a new test in `t7704-repack-cruft.sh` that packs a commit and its tree into a `.keep` pack, leaving its parent in the cruft pack. The test confirms that a subsequent geometric repack succeeds and can still write a bitmap.

The change is low-risk: it only affects the MIDX pack-selection logic and does not alter on-disk formats or user-visible behavior. The test coverage is thorough and mirrors the real-world failure mode described in the commit message. This patch, like the rest of the series, looks well-crafted and ready for review.
