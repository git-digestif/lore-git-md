This is a surface-level review in the ongoing thread about removing `gittutorial-2`. Kristoffer Haugsbakk identifies the root cause of the build break in the `seen` branch: a recent change (commit `4ce144a1`) expanded the doc-lint check to include `MAN_GUIDES` (a list of guide-style man pages) alongside `ALL_COMMANDS`. Since `gittutorial-2` was listed as a guide but no longer exists, the linter now fails. The fix will likely involve either removing `gittutorial-2` from the guide list or adjusting the linter to ignore it. This is a mechanical issue, not a design disagreement, and the resolution should be straightforward. No new questions about the broader tutorial cleanup are raised here.
