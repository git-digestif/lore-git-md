This is a brief but substantive follow-up in the thread about Phillip Wood’s `[PATCH 0/2] checkout -m: remember original conflict labels`. Junio C Hamano responds to Johannes Sixt’s suggestion of using an index extension instead of `.git/MERGE_LABELS` to store merge labels.

Junio’s reply is exploratory rather than decisive. He notes that replacing all the special `*_HEAD` files (like `MERGE_HEAD`, `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD`, etc.) with index extensions would "unclutter `$GIT_DIR/`" but also make the information less accessible to users and scripts. His tone is neutral—he calls the idea "an interesting thought" but does not endorse or reject it outright. The trade-off he highlights (cleanliness vs. accessibility) is a classic Git design tension, and his comment invites further discussion rather than settling the question.

This exchange advances the thread by framing the index-extension proposal as a broader architectural possibility, not just a narrow fix for the conflict-label series. It also signals that Junio is open to considering alternative designs, which may encourage Phillip or others to explore the idea further.
