This is the second patch in Taylor Blau’s four-patch series fixing MIDX reachability-closure corner cases. It targets the `--stdin-packs=follow` mode in `git pack-objects`, ensuring that trees and tags in included or excluded-open (`!`) packs (and loose ones with `--unpacked`) are treated as roots for the reachability walk, even when no commit reaches them. The fix prevents MIDXs from omitting objects reachable only from these trees or tags, which could otherwise break bitmap generation.

The patch introduces a new `extra_roots` OID array in the `stdin_packs_context` struct (added in patch 1) to collect trees and tags during the initial pack traversal. These roots are walked *after* the commit traversal to preserve path prefixes for delta attributes and namehashes. The change is confined to `builtin/pack-objects.c`, with corresponding updates to the `git-pack-objects` man page to document the new behavior. Three new tests in `t5331-pack-objects-stdin.sh` verify that the walk correctly handles tree-only input packs, excluded-open tags, and delta attributes for subtree contents, while a fourth test in `t7704-repack-cruft.sh` confirms that geometric repacks rescue descendants of loose trees.

The commit message provides a clear technical rationale, explaining how the bug manifests (e.g., a geometric repack producing a MIDX that isn’t reachability-closed for lone trees) and why the fix works (adding trees/tags as roots to "rescue" their descendants). The tests cover the specific scenarios mentioned in the series’ cover letter, reinforcing the patch’s correctness. This is a well-motivated, narrowly scoped fix with no obvious red flags—it addresses a real issue in the MIDX/cruft-pack machinery without altering on-disk formats or introducing new dependencies. The refactoring in patch 1 sets up this change cleanly, making the diff easy to follow.
