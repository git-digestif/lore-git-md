This is a **substantive review** from Jeff King (Peff) on the third patch in Taylor Blau’s four-patch bugfix series for MIDX reachability closure. The patch forces cruft packs to be included in MIDXs after incremental repacks to prevent bitmap generation failures.

Peff confirms the patch’s core logic—it correctly targets incremental repacks (not geometric ones) and fixes the scenario where a new pack omits cruft objects. However, he identifies a **gap in the fix’s scope**: the `write_midx_incremental()` function (used by `--write-midx=incremental` and the `GIT_TEST_MULTI_PACK_INDEX_WRITE_INCREMENTAL` environment variable) does not respect `midx_must_contain_cruft`, meaning the same reachability issue could still occur. Peff tested this and found the tests fail when `--write-midx=incremental` is used, though the environment variable case does not trigger the bug.

The review is **tested** (Peff ran the tests with the flag) and **substantive**, as it uncovers an edge case where the fix’s logic is incomplete. Peff does not demand an immediate follow-up but flags the issue as something that may need future attention. The patch itself remains a clear improvement, but this feedback suggests the problem is slightly broader than initially addressed.
