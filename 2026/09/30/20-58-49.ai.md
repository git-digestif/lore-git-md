This is a small feature patch extending `git stash pop` to accept the same custom conflict-label options (`--label-ours`, `--label-theirs`, `--label-base`) that `git stash apply` already supports. The implementation is straightforward: the three options are added to the `pop_stash()` function, passed through to `do_apply_stash()`, and documented in the man page. Two new tests verify that the labels appear in conflict markers and that empty labels are handled correctly. The patch is clean, well-tested, and directly mirrors the existing `apply` behavior.
