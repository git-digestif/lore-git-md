This is the third patch in Jeff King’s seven-patch series (now five patches after squashing) converting the xdiff subsystem’s `mmfile_t` buffer sizes from `unsigned long` to `size_t`. The patch itself is a mechanical refactoring: it changes the `size` field in the `mmfile_t` struct from `long` to `size_t` and updates the `printf` format in `emit_binary_diff_body()` to use `PRIuMAX` with an explicit cast to `uintmax_t`. The commit message explains the motivation: `size_t` is the more natural type for buffer sizes, and using `long` creates potential integer overflow risks, particularly on LLP64 systems like Windows where a 2^31+1-byte blob could cause out-of-bounds reads. The patch is uncontested and Junio has already acked the first two patches in the series; this one looks equally straightforward and well-justified. No behavior changes are intended, and the patch is ready for merging as part of the larger series.
