This is the first patch in a four-patch bugfix series for `git stash`, addressing a latent error-handling issue in the recently merged `dk/stash-apply-index-incore` topic. The series is a follow-up to the original work, which replaced subprocess-based index merging with in-core logic to fix an autostash race condition but left a robustness gap in how hard merge errors are handled.

This specific patch is a cleanup: it removes an unused `#include "oid-array.h"` from `builtin/stash.c`. Clang flagged the header as unused, and the author confirms that none of its functions or types are referenced in the file. The transitively included `hash.h` declarations are already covered by a direct include elsewhere in the file. The change is trivial—just one line deleted—and carries no risk of behavior change.

The patch is part of a well-structured follow-up series that also includes mechanical refactoring, a new test, and the substantive fix for the error-handling bug. While this patch itself is minor, it’s a sensible cleanup that aligns with the series’ goal of tidying up after the original merge. Reviewers are unlikely to object, and it should move forward smoothly as part of the larger effort.
