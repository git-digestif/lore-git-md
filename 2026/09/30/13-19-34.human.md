This is the second patch in Julia Evans' seven-patch series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page that consolidates merge conflict resolution guidance. The patch removes redundant conflict-resolution sections from `git-merge.adoc` and replaces them with a cross-reference to the new guide.

Patrick Steinhardt’s reply is a surface-level review. He notes a missing period in the commit message ("All of the info about merge conflicts has been moved to the new guide") and agrees with D. Ben Knoble’s earlier observation that the patch loses some context about what a merge conflict *is*. This feedback aligns with the broader discussion in the thread, where reviewers have highlighted the need for the new guide to include foundational explanations alongside practical advice. The comment is minor and unlikely to require significant rework, but it reinforces the importance of ensuring the new guide is self-contained and accessible to users unfamiliar with Git’s merge mechanics.
