This thread is about adding `@{p}` as a shorthand for `@{push}` in Git's revision syntax, mirroring the existing `@{u}` shorthand for `@{upstream}`. The patch, now in its second version, was authored by Harald Nordgren to address a long-standing asymmetry: `@{u}` was introduced in 2010 alongside `@{upstream}`, but `@{push}` (added in 2015) lacked a similar shorthand. The updated commit message in v2 provides full historical context, including Junio Hamano’s 2014 note about the asymmetry and the 2015 focus on push-model flexibility without addressing the shorthand gap.

The change is minimal—it only adds `@{p}` and `@{P}` as case-insensitive aliases for `@{push}`—and touches `object-name.c`, the revisions documentation, and a test file to verify the new behavior. Junio queued the patch in his `seen` branch on July 10, 2026, with an unconditional intent to graduate it to `next` and later `master`. The sole procedural blocker (updating the commit message) has been resolved, and no technical or design objections remain. D. Ben Knoble’s recent comment was a surface-level observation about case insensitivity, confirming the patch aligns with existing patterns, and required no changes. The patch is now on track for inclusion in the next release.
