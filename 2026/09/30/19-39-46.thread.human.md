This thread is about a small but long-overdue quality-of-life improvement to Git’s revision syntax. Harald Nordgren proposed adding `@{p}` as a shorthand for `@{push}`, mirroring the existing `@{u}` shorthand for `@{upstream}`. The asymmetry dates back to 2015, when `@{push}` was introduced without a shorthand, despite Junio Hamano already noting the inconsistency in 2014. The patch itself is trivial—it adds the new alias in `object-name.c`, documents it in the revisions manual, and tests both lowercase and uppercase variants (`@{p}` and `@{P}`).

The only hurdle was historical context: Junio wanted the commit message to clarify why the shorthand was missing in 2015 (concerns about potential `@{publish}` or `@{pull}` proposals that never materialized). Harald sent a v2 with this rationale, addressing the last procedural blocker. Junio had already queued the original patch in his `seen` branch with clear intent to graduate it to `next` and eventually `master`. No technical objections remain, and the change aligns with Git’s existing patterns for case-insensitive revision suffixes. The patch is now ready to proceed.
