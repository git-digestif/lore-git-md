This thread introduces a small but user-facing convenience feature: `@{p}` as a shorthand for `@{push}` in Git's revision syntax, mirroring the existing `@{u}` shorthand for `@{upstream}`. The patch comes from Harald Nordgren and is a straightforward addition—it modifies the revision parser in `object-name.c`, updates the documentation, and adds a test to verify that `@{p}` (and case-insensitive `@{P}`) resolves correctly.

The change is minimal and uncontroversial, following established patterns without altering existing behavior. It simply adds a new alias for an existing feature, so there’s no risk of breaking workflows. The patch includes test coverage and aligns the push shorthand with the upstream one for consistency. So far, there’s been no discussion or objections, and the implementation appears ready for review.
