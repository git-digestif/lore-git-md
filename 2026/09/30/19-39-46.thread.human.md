The asymmetry dates back to 2015, when `@{push}` was introduced without a one-letter alias, not for any deliberate reason but simply because it was overlooked. Harald Nordgren’s patch, now queued by Junio, closes this gap by making `@{p}` (and `@{P}`) resolve identically to `@{push}`.
