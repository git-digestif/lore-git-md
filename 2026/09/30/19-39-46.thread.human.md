This thread is about adding `@{p}` as a shorthand for `@{push}` in Git's revision syntax, mirroring the existing `@{u}` shorthand for `@{upstream}`. The patch, now in its second version, addresses a long-standing asymmetry: `@{u}` was introduced in 2010 alongside `@{upstream}`, but `@{push}` (added in 2015) lacked a shorthand until now. The updated commit message in v2 provides full historical context, including Junio Hamano’s 2014 note about the inconsistency and the 2015 focus on push-model flexibility without addressing the shorthand gap.

The change is minimal and uncontroversial, touching only `object-name.c`, `Documentation/revisions.adoc`, and a test file. It introduces no new symbols or on-disk format changes, simply adding `@{p}` and `@{P}` as case-insensitive aliases for `@{push}`. Junio has already queued the patch in his `seen` branch (as of July 10, 2026) with clear intent to graduate it to `next` and later `master`. The only prior feedback—Junio’s request for a more detailed commit message—has been addressed, and no technical or design objections remain. The thread is effectively closed, with the patch on track for inclusion.
