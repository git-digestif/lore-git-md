This is the fourth patch in Jeff King’s seven-patch series converting xdiff’s `mmfile_t` buffer sizes from `unsigned long` to `size_t`. The patch itself is a straightforward refactoring: it replaces a hand-rolled file-reading loop in `ll_ext_merge()` with a call to `read_mmfile()`, simplifying the code. Patrick Steinhardt’s review raises two minor points. First, the patch temporarily loses the NUL-termination that `xmallocz()` provided, though Jeff restores it in a later patch (patch 7/7). Patrick suggests reordering the series so the NUL-termination patch comes first, which would avoid the intermediate state. Second, Patrick notes that `read_mmfile()` now emits an error message on failure, a behavioral change not mentioned in the commit message. Neither point is a blocker, but both are worth addressing before the series lands. The patch remains uncontroversial and is ready for integration once the earlier patches are in place.
