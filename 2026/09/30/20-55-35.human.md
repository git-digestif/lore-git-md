This is a follow-up in the ongoing review of Taylor Blau’s four-patch series fixing MIDX reachability-closure corner cases. Jeff King (Peff) replies to the cover letter with a high-level endorsement: all four patches make the code strictly better, but he has identified a few spots where the fixes might be incomplete. He leaves it to the author to decide whether to address these now or defer them to future work.

Peff’s note is brief but substantive. He explicitly agrees with Derrick Stolee’s earlier suggestion to replace the `oid_array` used for `extra_roots` with an `oidset`, since the order of roots is irrelevant and duplicates are possible. While he acknowledges the practical impact is small (duplicates are rare and skipped during traversal), the change would improve semantic clarity and efficiency. Beyond this, Peff does not restate the deeper technical questions he raised in his individual patch reviews—such as the two-walk approach in patch 2/4, the unaddressed edge case in `write_midx_incremental()` in patch 3/4, or the `.keep`-pack race condition in patch 4/4—but his phrasing (“incomplete”) signals that those issues remain open.

The tone is collegial and non-prescriptive; Peff frames the decision as the author’s call rather than a blocker. For a busy reader, this email confirms that the series is on the right track but still has minor loose ends that could prompt a v2.
