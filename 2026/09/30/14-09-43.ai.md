This is a **documentation patch** in the ongoing RFC series to convert Git’s `BreakingChanges` document into the `gitbreaking-changes(7)` manpage. This email is a reply to Patrick Steinhardt’s review of the second patch in the series, which replaces message-ID references with clickable `lore.kernel.org` URLs.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk accepts all of Patrick’s feedback: he will move the "how to add new entries" section to the end of the document (a usability improvement for the now-public-facing manpage) and fix a minor formatting nit (two spaces after a URL). The changes are purely editorial and do not affect the patch’s core goal of improving discoverability. No new technical concerns or open questions are introduced in this exchange.

The patch remains uncontroversial and ready to land if the series is accepted. The files touched are still limited to `Documentation/gitbreaking-changes.adoc`, and the changes are confined to URL formatting, explanatory text, and section ordering. The key names introduced (the URLs themselves) are unlikely to be referenced in future discussion, but the section-move suggestion may resurface if other reviewers weigh in on document structure. No new patch version has been posted yet.
