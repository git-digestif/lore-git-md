This is a review reply for the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s three-patch series fixing timezone handling in the reftable backend. The series addresses a spec compliance issue where Git stores reflog timezone offsets in "[+-]HHMM" format instead of the signed minutes required by the reftable specification.

Karthik Nayak’s review is surface-level. He notes a minor test coverage gap — suggesting the test include a negative timezone with non-zero minutes (e.g., "-0730") — but otherwise approves the patch as written. The rest of his feedback is purely editorial ("nit"), with no engagement with the technical substance of the change. His earlier review of patch 1 was substantive, raising a potential sign-handling issue in `local_time_tzoffset()`, but that concern does not resurface here.

The patch itself is a targeted fix that converts timezone storage to signed minutes on disk while preserving the "[+-]HHMM" format for the rest of Git. It includes a thorough test verifying both the on-disk format (using `dump-reftable -t`) and the round-trip behavior. The series as a whole appears ready for integration, with no unresolved technical objections.
