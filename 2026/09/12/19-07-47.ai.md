This is a **CI/build system** follow-up in the ongoing discussion about the Coccinelle rule that removes `if (!E) free(E)` guards. Junio C Hamano responds to René Scharfe’s **substantive review**, clarifying his reasoning for the original patch and addressing René’s suggestion to remove the `free(E)` call entirely rather than leaving it unconditional.

However, he raises a practical concern: a compiler might flag the empty `if` block (e.g., `if (!E) ;`) as suspicious, especially if `E` is a simple variable, and trigger `-Werror` warnings. This could create noise in the build process or even break CI if warnings are treated as errors.

The email does not propose a new patch or alternative solution—it merely explains why Junio opted for the original approach (removing the rule entirely) rather than pursuing René’s suggestion.
