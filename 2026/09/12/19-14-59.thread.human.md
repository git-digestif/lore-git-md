This thread is about a small but important documentation cleanup: making sure command, option, and subcommand references in the `git-refs` and `git-pack-refs` man pages are consistently quoted with backticks. The goal is purely visual—ensuring uniform formatting in the rendered output—but it’s part of a larger effort to standardize Git’s documentation style.

The series, now at version 3, was posted by Todd Zullinger and touches three files: `git-refs.adoc`, `git-pack-refs.adoc`, and `pack-refs-options.adoc`. The changes are mechanical: converting the SYNOPSIS block to the newer `[synopsis]` style, backtick-quoting subcommands and configuration keys, and leaving command-line options unquoted as requested by Junio. There’s no functional change to Git itself, and no new tests were needed.

The thread has been uncontested since v2, when Todd withdrew a proposed lint script change that Jean-Noël Avila objected to. Junio reviewed the remaining patches, requested a minor correction to the scope of backtick-quoting in `pack-refs-options.adoc`, and has now accepted the updated version. With all feedback incorporated, the series is ready for integration and has been queued for maintainer pickup. There are no open questions or loose ends.
