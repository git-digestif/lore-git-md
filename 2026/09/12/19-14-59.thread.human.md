This thread is a small but important part of the ongoing effort to standardize Git’s man pages on the new synopsis style. Todd Zullinger sent a two-patch series (v2) to ensure consistent backtick-quoting of command and option references in the `git-refs` and `git-pack-refs` documentation. The only remaining issue was a minor correction flagged by Junio C Hamano: in `pack-refs-options.adoc`, backtick-quoting should apply only to configuration keys, not command-line options like `--all` or `--no-prune`.

Todd has now addressed this nit with a mechanical update to the commit message and a revert of the unintended backticks. The series is otherwise uncontested and ready for integration. Jean-Noël Avila, who leads the broader documentation effort, had previously objected to a lint script change in v1, but that patch was dropped, leaving only the straightforward backtick-quoting fixes. No further revisions are expected, and the changes are purely visual—affecting only rendered man page and HTML output, not Git’s functionality. The files touched remain `Documentation/git-pack-refs.adoc` and `Documentation/technical/pack-refs-options.adoc`.
