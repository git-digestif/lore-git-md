This thread is a three-patch documentation series from Todd Zullinger that ensures consistent backtick-quoting of commands and options in the `git-refs` and `git-pack-refs` man pages. The goal is purely cosmetic—fixing visual inconsistencies in rendered output where some commands appeared in bold while others did not—without altering Git’s functionality. The series is part of Jean-Noël Avila’s broader effort to standardize all Git man pages on a consistent AsciiDoc synopsis style.

The first two patches introduced a lint script to enforce backtick-quoting and applied mechanical fixes to `git-pack-refs.adoc`. This third patch completes the work by quoting the remaining unquoted subcommands in `git-refs.adoc` (`list`, `exists`, `optimize`, `create`, `delete`, `update`, and `rename`). The series is uncontroversial and aligns with established project conventions, with no open questions or objections raised so far. It remains under review, awaiting maintainer pickup.
