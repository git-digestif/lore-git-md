This thread is a small but important part of Git’s ongoing effort to standardize its documentation. Todd Zullinger sent a two-patch series (v2) to clean up backtick-quoting in the `git-refs` and `git-pack-refs` man pages, ensuring that command and option references appear consistently in the rendered output. The first patch in v2 (already summarized) handled `git-pack-refs`; this new email is the second patch, which mechanically backtick-quotes all subcommands in `git-refs.adoc` and fixes a placeholder’s formatting.

The series is uncontested and ready for integration. Jean-Noël Avila, who leads the broader synopsis-style documentation effort, had previously objected to a lint script change in v1, but that patch was dropped, leaving only the mechanical formatting fixes. No further revisions are expected, and the changes are purely visual—no functional behavior is altered. The work aligns with the project’s long-running goal of making man pages more consistent and readable.
