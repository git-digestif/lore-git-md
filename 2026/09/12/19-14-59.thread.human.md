This thread is about a small documentation cleanup series from Todd Zullinger, part of the ongoing effort to standardize AsciiDoc formatting across Git’s man pages. The goal is to ensure consistent backtick-quoting of commands and options in the `git-refs` and `git-pack-refs` documentation, which affects how these elements appear in rendered output (e.g., bold vs. plain text).

The series is now at v2, with two uncontested patches that mechanically convert formatting in `Documentation/git-refs.adoc` and `Documentation/git-pack-refs.adoc`. Junio C Hamano recently reviewed the first patch and requested a minor correction: backtick-quoting in the included `pack-refs-options.adoc` should apply only to configuration keys, not command-line options like `--all`. This is a surface-level tweak and doesn’t change the series’ readiness for integration. Jean-Noël Avila, who leads the broader documentation effort, had previously objected to a lint script change in v1, but that patch was dropped, leaving the remaining work uncontroversial.

No further revisions are expected, and the series is effectively ready to merge. The changes are purely cosmetic, with no impact on Git’s functionality or test coverage.
