This thread is a small but important part of the ongoing effort to standardize Git’s man pages on a consistent AsciiDoc synopsis style, led by Jean-Noël Avila. Todd Zullinger posted a three-patch series to fix backtick-quoting inconsistencies in the `git-refs` and `git-pack-refs` documentation, ensuring commands and options appear uniformly in the rendered output. The series itself is uncontroversial and mechanical, touching only documentation files without changing Git’s behavior.

The only hiccup came in Jean-Noël’s review of the first patch, which extends the lint script to enforce backtick-quoting for *commands* as well as options. He pointed out that the current regex would produce false positives in other man pages—like `git-add.adoc` and `gitglossary.adoc`—where definition lists include terms that aren’t commands or options. Backtick-quoting those terms would freeze them for translators, creating unintended side effects. The rest of the series (patches 2 and 3) remains unchallenged, and the goal of consistent quoting isn’t in question—just the mechanical enforcement.

The ball is now in Todd’s court to adjust the lint script, either by restricting its scope to the two files being fixed or refining the regex to avoid over-matching. No other objections or open questions have surfaced, so once this narrow technical point is resolved, the series should be ready to merge.
