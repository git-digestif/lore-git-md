## 2026/09/13

- [2026/09/13/10-26-39 by Jean-Noël AVILA <jn.avila@free.fr>] Jean-Noël AVILA explained that the lint regex was deliberately restricted to options: backticked terms are meant to be immutable for translators, and definition lists also cover real definitions (e.g., in gitglossary.adoc and git-add.adoc), so extending the match to commands triggers false positives — he noted the proposed change flags git-add.adoc, git-push.adoc, git-difftool.adoc, git-daemon.adoc, and git-fetch.adoc.
- [2026/09/13/14-14-05 by Todd Zullinger <tmz@pobox.com>] Todd Zullinger said he does not mind dropping the lint patch if the false positives would be more annoying than skipping the command checks, and that he would wait a little before sending a re-roll with that patch dropped, in case anyone spots issues in the main pack-refs and refs doc patches.
