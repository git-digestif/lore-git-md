This thread is about a two-patch series from Harald Nordgren that cleans up and tightens Git’s CI dependency installation. The goal is to cut log noise by making Perforce, Git LFS, and JGit installation failures fatal on the platforms that need them (ubuntu-* and macos-* runners), rather than allowing silent skips. After four iterations the series has settled: the preparatory patch removes an obsolete macOS quarantine-removal step that has been a no-op since 2022, and the main patch removes redundant error-handling logic and `&&` chains, relying on `set -e` for consistency with the rest of the script.

Junio Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt have both reviewed the changes; Junio called the trade-off reasonable and Patrick, who originally challenged the premise, now supports the stricter approach. In the latest email Patrick simply says “this version looks good to me,” signaling the series is ready. The only remaining step is a formal Reviewed-by or Acked-by from someone other than Junio or Patrick. The patches touch only `ci/install-dependencies.sh` and don’t change Git’s behavior—only how CI fails. Everything requested has been addressed, and the series is uncontroversial.
