This thread is about a two-patch series from Harald Nordgren that tightens Git’s CI behavior to reduce log noise and prevent silent test skips. The series makes installation failures for Perforce, Git LFS, and JGit fatal on the platforms that require them (Ubuntu and macOS), ensuring the build fails immediately if a tool cannot be installed rather than allowing the job to continue with missing dependencies. The v4 iteration, just posted, removes redundant error-handling logic and a long-obsolete macOS quarantine-removal step that has been a no-op since 2022.

The preparatory patch (1/2) drops the `xattr -d com.apple.quarantine` call for macOS Perforce binaries, which stopped doing anything when the install method switched from Homebrew to direct `wget`. The main patch (2/2) simplifies the script by removing explicit `echo "error: ..." / exit 1` blocks and redundant `&&` chains, relying instead on `set -e` (already active via `ci/lib.sh`) to abort on the first failed command. Both patches have been accepted in principle by Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt, who have each requested minor commit-message polish but have no technical objections. The series is now technically unblocked and ready for final review, though it still lacks an explicit Reviewed-by or Acked-by from a contributor outside the maintainer and Patrick. The changes are confined to `ci/install-dependencies.sh` and do not alter Git’s behavior—only CI log clarity and failure handling.
