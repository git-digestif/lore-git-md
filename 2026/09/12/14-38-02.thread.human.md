This thread is about a two-patch series that tightens Git's CI tool installation behavior, now at version 3. The goal is to reduce log noise by ensuring warnings or failures for tools like Perforce, Git LFS, and JGit only appear on platforms that actually require them—failing the build immediately if installation fails rather than allowing silent skips.

The preparatory cleanup patch (1/2) has cleared its last technical hurdle: Junio confirmed the removal of the macOS `xattr -d com.apple.quarantine` step for Perforce binaries is safe, as it was never set by CI runners. He’s now waiting on a minor commit-message polish from Harald Nordgren before giving it a formal review. The stricter behavior patch (2/2) remains ready for final review, with no outstanding technical objections. Both patches modify `ci/install-dependencies.sh` to rely on `set -e` for failure handling, eliminating redundant error-checking logic.

The series is nearly ready to merge, pending a second positive review (Reviewed-by or Acked-by) from a contributor other than Junio or Patrick Steinhardt. A minor stylistic question—whether to drop redundant `&&` chains in the installation steps—remains open but isn’t blocking progress. Harald has acknowledged the redundancy and plans to address it in a future iteration.
