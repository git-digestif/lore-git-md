This thread is about reducing CI log noise by making the installation of Perforce, Git LFS, and JGit mandatory on the platforms that require them (ubuntu-* and macos-*). Harald Nordgren’s v4 series—now down to two patches—has settled on a stricter approach: if a tool cannot be installed, the build fails immediately rather than issuing warnings or allowing silent skips.

The preparatory cleanup (patch 1/2) is now technically unblocked; Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt have accepted it in principle after Harald updated the commit message to clarify that the macOS `xattr` step has been a no-op since 2022. The second patch (2/2), which implements the stricter behavior, is ready for final review. It removes redundant presence checks, warnings, and explicit error-handling blocks, relying instead on `set -e` for failure handling. Both patches touch only `ci/install-dependencies.sh` and have no functional impact on Git itself.

Junio and Patrick support the direction but have not yet provided a formal Reviewed-by or Acked-by. The series is now waiting for a positive review from another contributor before it can be merged.
