This is the second patch in Vsevolod Myalitsin’s three-part v4 series introducing scope hints for Git’s advice messages. The patch implements the boolean `is_global_hint` mechanism proposed earlier to resolve build failures and simplify the design. The author now explicitly aligns the change with Jeff King’s argument that `advice.*` settings were originally intended to be disabled globally, making `--global` the appropriate scope for the hint. The commit message and this reply both dismiss `--system` and `--worktree` as unnecessary (YAGNI) and propose treating all other scopes as local by default. The patch itself touches `advice.c`, adds the `is_global_hint` field to the `advice_setting` struct, and updates `vadvise()` to use the new field when constructing the scope hint. No new technical issues are raised here, but the maintainer’s position on the boolean simplification remains unresolved.
