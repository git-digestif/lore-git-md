This is a documentation patch series targeting the man-page synopsis style conversion effort. Todd Zullinger noticed inconsistent formatting in `git-refs(1)`—some commands were bold while others were not—and traced it to missing backtick-quotes in the AsciiDoc source. The series fixes the linter to catch these inconsistencies, then applies the correction to `git-pack-refs(1)` and `git-refs(1)` (including their shared `pack-refs-options.adoc`).

The motivation is purely cosmetic: ensuring commands and options are rendered uniformly in both man pages and HTML output. The commit messages are clear and the changes are mechanical—no behavior changes, just markup tweaks. This is part of Jean-Noël Avila’s ongoing effort to standardize all Git man pages on the synopsis style; Todd’s patch aligns with that goal by extending the linter and fixing two files that slipped through earlier passes.

The series is small (three patches) and self-contained. If the linter change is accepted, the follow-up patches are uncontroversial cleanup. No test failures or CI issues are expected, and the changes are unlikely to draw substantive review comments—this is the kind of housekeeping that usually lands quietly once the linter rule is agreed.
