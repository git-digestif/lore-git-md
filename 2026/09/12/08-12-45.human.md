This is the second patch in Vsevolod Myalitsin’s three-part v4 series adding scope hints to Git’s advice messages. The series proposes a boolean `is_global_hint` field in the `advice_setting` struct to indicate whether the scope should be `--global` (true) or `--local` (false), replacing the earlier `scope_hint` enum that caused build failures and was criticized as overly complex.

In this reply, Vsevolod directly addresses Junio’s observation that the original 2009 design of the `advice.*` config system never intended the "use `git config advice.foo false`" instruction to be a literal cut-and-paste command. He argues that since advice settings were originally meant to be disabled globally (as Jeff King noted), adding `--global` to the hint aligns with the original intent while making the instruction genuinely usable as-is. He dismisses support for `--system`, `--worktree`, or other scopes as premature (YAGNI), reinforcing the case for the boolean simplification.

The reply does not introduce new technical details but strengthens the rationale for the current design. The series remains under review, with Junio yet to signal whether he will pick it up for integration. The unresolved question is whether the boolean approach will be accepted or if Junio will push for a more flexible mechanism that could later support additional scopes.
