This is the second patch in Todd Zullinger’s three-patch documentation series (v1) that standardizes backtick-quoting in the `git-pack-refs` and `git-refs` man pages. It mechanically converts the SYNOPSIS block from `[verse]` to `[synopsis]`, removes single-quote formatting around the command name, backtick-quotes all option terms in the OPTIONS section, and updates the included `pack-refs-options.adoc` to backtick-quote configuration keys. The only prose change replaces a literal `<branch>` with an italicized placeholder. No functional behavior is altered; the patch strictly affects rendered man-page and HTML appearance. The changes are confined to two AsciiDoc files and align with the ongoing synopsis-style conversion effort led by Jean-Noël Avila. The patch is uncontroversial and ready for review.
