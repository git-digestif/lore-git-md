This is the maintainer’s latest reply in the ongoing discussion about how to add scope hints to Git’s advice messages. Junio acknowledges that the original design of the `advice.*` config system never intended the "use `git config advice.foo false`" instruction to be a literal cut-and-paste command; users were expected to adapt the scope (`--global`, `--local`, etc.) to their own needs. He accepts Vsevolod’s argument that adding `--global` to the hint makes it more user-friendly while preserving the original intent, but goes further: he suggests that the project could simply hard-code `--global` in *all* advice hints without needing to track which advice settings are global and which are local. This would eliminate the need for the `is_global_hint` boolean or any per-advice scope tracking, simplifying the implementation even further. The series remains under review, and Junio has not yet picked it up for integration.
