This is a documentation patch series follow-up, specifically a review comment on Todd Zullinger’s v1 patch series that fixes backtick-quoting inconsistencies in `git-refs` and `git-pack-refs` man pages. The series is part of Jean-Noël Avila’s ongoing effort to standardize Git’s man pages on the synopsis style.

Jean-Noël’s reply raises a technical concern about the lint script change in patch 1. The current regex targets only options, but Todd’s patch extends it to commands as well. Jean-Noël explains that the regex was intentionally restricted to avoid false positives: definition lists in other man pages (e.g., `git-add.adoc`, `gitglossary.adoc`) include terms that are not commands or options, and backtick-quoting them would incorrectly freeze those terms for translators. He lists five files (`git-add.adoc`, `git-push.adoc`, `git-difftool.adoc`, `git-daemon.adoc`, `git-fetch.adoc`) where the proposed change would trigger false positives.

The review is substantive but narrow: it does not question the goal of consistent backtick-quoting for commands and options, only the mechanical means of enforcing it. The implication is that Todd should either restrict the lint check to the two files he is fixing or refine the regex to avoid over-matching. No other patches in the series are contested.
