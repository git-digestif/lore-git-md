This is a documentation patch series follow-up, part of the ongoing effort to standardize Git's man pages on AsciiDoc synopsis style. Jean-Noël Avila, who leads that effort, replies to Todd Zullinger's v1 series with a measured concern: the lint script change Todd proposes would over-match and flag false positives in several existing man pages (git-add, git-push, git-difftool, git-daemon, git-fetch). The core issue—ensuring consistent backtick-quoting of commands and options in `git-refs` and `git-pack-refs`—remains uncontroversial, but the lint rule needs to stay scoped to avoid collateral damage. Expect a v2 that either narrows the regex or restricts the check to the two files that actually need it.
