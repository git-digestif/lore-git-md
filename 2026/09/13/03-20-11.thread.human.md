This thread is a five-patch v3 series from Yoichi NAKAYAMA that fixes a bug in `git worktree repair` where the command could incorrectly modify unrelated worktrees due to missing validation of cross-references between a linked worktree and its administrative data. The series has evolved from an initial two-patch v1 to a clearer five-patch structure, with Junio C Hamano’s review prompting improvements in refactoring and validation robustness.

The core issue is that `git worktree repair` previously operated on administrative files without verifying their relationship to the worktree being repaired, risking corruption in edge cases like manually swapped or copied worktrees. The fix introduces validation to ensure repairs only proceed when the target is confirmed correct. The v3 series addresses Junio’s sole substantive review concern—handling non-canonical path separators in the worktree ID validation—by hardening the `get_worktree_id()` helper to accept both forward and backward slashes, tolerate duplicate separators, and skip trailing slashes.

The series is now under broader review, with no merge status reported. Patches 1–4 refactor the codebase to improve clarity and consistency in `.git` file parsing and backlink inference, while patch 5 adds the critical validation logic. The second patch (summarized here) centralizes the inferred backlink selection logic, ensuring it is computed before validation and preferred when both a valid gitdir and inferred path exist—a key edge case for copied worktrees. No other contributors have weighed in yet, and the series remains untested in broader scenarios, though the separation of concerns in v3 mitigates risks of unintended side effects.
