This thread is about a two-patch bugfix series that resolves a long-standing race condition in Git’s `rerere` subsystem, where concurrent operations could contend for the shared `rr-cache` directory lock. The issue, while exacerbated by Git 2.54’s geometric maintenance strategy, is a general robustness flaw affecting any two simultaneous `rerere` operations.

The final version (v8) introduces two key changes: a configurable timeout (`rerere.lockTimeout`, defaulting to 1000ms) for the `MERGE_RR.lock` file, allowing processes to wait rather than fail immediately, and a hidden `--skip-locked` mode for `git rerere gc` that silently skips a held lock during background maintenance.
