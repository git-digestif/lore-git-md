## 2026/09/06

- [2026/09/06/10-29-32 by Thomas Bachem <mail@thomasbachem.com>] Thomas Bachem confirmed that when "git rerere" fails at a conflict stop, the user can still recover everything by hand: the conflict remains in the index, so running "git rerere" records the skipped preimage, "git rebase --continue" records the resolution, and replaying the same conflict resolves from cache; he verified all of this, but stressed the warning must say to run "git rerere" before resolving, since resolving first removes the conflict markers and records nothing.
- [2026/09/06/10-29-32 by Thomas Bachem <mail@thomasbachem.com>] He argued that failing rather than warning buys only the advantage that the user cannot miss it, since failing also records nothing and leaves the conflict available to record by hand afterwards; he admitted he had agreed to that trade without checking what it costs.
- [2026/09/06/10-29-32 by Thomas Bachem <mail@thomasbachem.com>] He corrected the assumption that a rebase interrupted mid-way is easily recoverable: the "you have staged changes" refusal suggests "git commit --amend" or "git commit", but amend folds the interrupted commit's changes into the previous commit and drops its message while the rebase runs one commit short, and commit keeps the message but not the author; aborting works but requires the user to ignore git's advice. He noted this is existing 2.55 behavior, not something the patch adds, and that waiting only makes it rarer.
- [2026/09/06/10-29-32 by Thomas Bachem <mail@thomasbachem.com>] He stated his position: keep v3's behavior at a conflict (where the command stops anyway and the warning can tell the user to run "git rerere" first), but keep failing after the wait for recordings after resolution ("git am --continue" and a rebase's "git commit" proceed and a leftover MERGE_RR entry would record whatever the file holds at the next rerere run), for explicit "git rerere", "forget" and "clear", for the clears that "am" and "rebase" run, and for the gc (which gives up at once); he offered to build the alternative where all of it fails if Junio still prefers that.
