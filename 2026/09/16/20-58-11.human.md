This is an administrative update in the ongoing discussion about Git’s participation in the Outreachy December 2026 cohort. Christian Couder responds to Kaartic Sivaraam’s proposal to expand the Outreachy application by adding two additional projects originally prepared for Google Summer of Code 2026. The thread has already confirmed mentors for the two original projects—command-line argument parsing and libification—but is now considering whether to include more.

Christian agrees that the first additional project, **"Implement promisor remote fetch ordering"**, is worth proposing, though he notes it appears more challenging than the existing projects. However, he expresses stronger reservations about the second, **"Enhance promisor-remote protocol for better-connected remotes"**, calling it "even more risky and difficult." He defers to Kaartic’s judgment, since Kaartic would be mentoring it, but his tone suggests skepticism about its suitability for an internship.

The email does not resolve whether the second project will be added, nor does it assign mentors to either of the new proposals. The Outreachy application deadline is approaching (September 11, 2026), so this exchange reflects last-minute deliberation about how many—and which—projects to include. The discussion remains open, but Christian’s preference for limiting expansion to the first project may carry weight.
