Subject: Inconsistent behavior between `+` refspec modifier and `--force` in `git fetch`
Series version: N/A (bug report)
Originating author: André Kießling
Thread type: bug report

Core problem or goal
The `+` refspec modifier and the `--force` flag are documented to behave identically, but in `git fetch` they diverge: `--force` updates tags without pruning deleted ones, while `+` in the refspec both updates *and* prunes local tags that no longer exist on the remote. The documentation in `git-push(1)` claims `+` "does the same thing as --force", yet this equivalence does not hold for `git fetch`. The thread has shifted from assuming a bug to investigating whether the behavior is intentional, documented, or environment-specific.

Current status
Under review; no patch or documentation change yet. Junio C Hamano's reproduction attempt on Unix fails to demonstrate the reported pruning behavior, suggesting the discrepancy may stem from an undisclosed configuration or platform-specific issue (André's report was on Windows). The discussion now centers on diagnosing why the reporter's environment produces different results.

- Subsystem: fetch/push refspec machinery
