Subject: Inconsistent behavior between `+` refspec modifier and `--force` in `git fetch`
Series version: N/A (bug report)
Originating author: André Kießling
Thread type: bug report

Core problem or goal
The `+` refspec modifier and the `--force` flag are documented to behave identically, but in `git fetch` they do not: `--force` updates tags without pruning deleted ones, while `+` in the refspec both updates *and* prunes local tags that no longer exist on the remote. This inconsistency contradicts the documentation claim that `+` "does the same thing as --force".
