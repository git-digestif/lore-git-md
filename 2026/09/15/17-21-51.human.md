This is Junio’s review of the v8 patch extending `git var` to support broken-down identity components, multiple variable arguments, and NUL-terminated output. The patch is part of a long-running effort to provide a unified, scriptable interface for Git’s identity and signing configuration, and it has been technically complete since v7, with all prior feedback addressed.

Junio’s main point is structural: the patch is doing too much at once and should be split into at least three smaller patches. His suggested breakdown is clear: (1) add `-z` output mode, (2) add support for multiple variable arguments, and (3) add the new variables (identity components and `GIT_SIGNING_KEY`). He also notes that the commit message reads like a development log rather than a concise explanation for future readers of `git log`, and he emphasizes the importance of engaging in dialogue with reviewers before sending new iterations.

The review is substantive but not critical — Junio is not objecting to the functionality or design, only to the patch structure and commit message quality. His tone is constructive, and the feedback is actionable. The patch itself is ready for integration once the structural changes are made, and there are no unresolved technical concerns. This is a matter of presentation and patch hygiene, not functionality. The series remains in `seen` and is likely to graduate to `next` once the split is done.
