This is the v5 submission of a three-patch bugfix series targeting the `--force-if-includes` safety mechanism in `git push`. The series addresses two long-standing flaws: (1) the feature incorrectly checks the reflog of the *local* branch whose name matches the *remote* destination (e.g., `refs/heads/main` when pushing to `refs/remotes/origin/main`), rather than the branch actually being pushed (e.g., `refs/heads/src` in `src:main`), and (2) it unnecessarily rejects fast-forward pushes of non-branch refs (tags, detached HEADs) even when no force is needed. The v5 update adds a third patch to exempt fast-forward pushes from `--force-if-includes` checks, resolving an existing bug that was exacerbated by the stricter validation in v4.

The core logic in `remote.c` now consults the reflog of the pushed ref (`ref->peer_ref->name`) and unconditionally resolves `peer_ref` with `RESOLVE_REF_READING`, ensuring non-branch pushes are rejected for force-pushes but allowed for fast-forwards. The new fast-forward exemption (patch 3/3) aligns the feature with its documented intent—only intervening when force is actually needed—while reducing friction for common workflows like `git push origin <TAG>:hotfix`. The series also introduces `advice.forceIfIncludesDetachedHead` (documented as `advice.pushRefUnverifiable`) to provide actionable guidance for rejected cases, such as using `--force-with-lease=<ref>:<expect>` or `--no-force-if-includes`.

Key files touched include `remote.c` (core logic), `advice.c`/`advice.h` (new advice message), and `t/t5533-push-cas.sh` (81 lines of new test coverage). The series is based on `maint` and has no external dependencies. All prior review feedback—from Patrick Steinhardt (substantive), Junio C Hamano (surface-level), and D. Ben Knoble (backwards-compatibility)—is resolved or superseded by the v5 design. The only remaining loose end is explicit test coverage for the fast-forward exemption (patch 3/3), which the author notes is pending but logically sound. The implementation is thorough, updating all transport layers (`send-pack.c`, `transport-helper.c`, `transport.c`) and avoiding regressions for `--force-with-lease`. The series is ready for review.
