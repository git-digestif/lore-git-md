This is a design discussion about Git's option-parsing infrastructure, specifically whether `OPT_ALIAS()` should support the `OPT_HIDDEN` flag to allow deprecated option names to be hidden from help text. Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, clarifies that `OPT_HIDDEN` is a general mechanism for deprecation, not just for aliases, and outlines Git's typical deprecation lifecycle: initially, deprecated options remain visible in help text to warn existing users, but after sufficient time, they are hidden to avoid cluttering help output for new users. He confirms that if `OPT_ALIAS()` currently does not support `OPT_HIDDEN`, that is a limitation of the infrastructure, not a policy decision, and suggests it could be fixed later with a `#leftoverbit` (a Git project convention for marking minor technical debt). Junio's reply signals that the infrastructure limitation is not a blocker for Patrick's series, but it leaves open the question of whether hidden aliases are the right approach for this specific deprecation.
