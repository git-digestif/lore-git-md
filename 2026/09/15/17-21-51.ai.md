This is Junio’s review of the v8 patch extending `git var` to support broken-down identity components, multiple variable arguments, and NUL-terminated output. The patch is part of the long-running effort to provide a unified, scriptable interface for Git’s identity and signing configuration, and it addresses all prior feedback from Junio and Phillip Wood.

Junio’s core critique is structural: the patch is too large and should be split into at least three smaller patches. His proposed breakdown is (1) adding `-z` output mode, (2) enabling multi-variable queries, and (3) introducing the new variables (`GIT_AUTHOR_NAME`, `GIT_SIGNING_KEY`, etc.). This is a **substantive, high-weight review** that directly targets the patch’s organization rather than its technical correctness. Junio also notes that the commit message’s "laundry list" format reads like a development log rather than a concise explanation for future `git log` readers, and he emphasizes the importance of engaging in dialog with reviewers before sending new iterations.

The review does not identify any technical flaws in the implementation itself. Junio’s concerns are purely about patch structure and communication process. The v8 patch is otherwise **technically complete and ready for integration**, having resolved all prior feedback on output format, exit codes, and documentation. The series remains in `seen`, and this review does not block its progress but rather requests a reorganization for clarity and maintainability.
