This thread is a bug report from André Kießling about an inconsistency between the `+` refspec modifier and the `--force` flag in `git fetch`. The documentation claims they behave identically, but in practice, `+` in the refspec prunes deleted tags while `--force` does not. The discussion initially treated this as a potential bug, but Ben Knoble suggested the behavior might be intentional and documented in the `PRUNING` section of `git-fetch(1)`.

Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, has now weighed in, arguing that the two are equivalent in their core purpose (allowing forced updates) and that pruning is a separate concern. His reproduction steps on a Unix system failed to replicate the reported behavior—adding `+refs/tags/*:refs/tags/*` to the refspec did not prune local tags—contradicting André’s findings. Junio suspects the discrepancy may stem from an undisclosed configuration or a Windows-specific issue, though he remains skeptical of the latter. The thread now focuses on diagnosing why the reporter’s environment produces different results, with no resolution or patch proposed yet. The key question is whether the pruning behavior is tied to the `+` modifier or another setting (e.g., `fetch.pruneTags`).
