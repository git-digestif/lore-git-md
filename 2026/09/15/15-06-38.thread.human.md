This thread is about an inconsistency in `git fetch` where the `+` refspec modifier and the `--force` flag—documented as equivalent—behave differently: `+` updates *and* prunes deleted tags, while `--force` only updates them. The report, from André Kießling, includes clear reproduction steps and notes the contradiction in the `git-push` man page.

Ben Knoble’s review suggests the discrepancy might be intentional, pointing to the `PRUNING` section of `git-fetch(1)` as a possible explanation. This shifts the discussion from whether this is a bug to whether the behavior is documented and deliberate. If the `PRUNING` section clarifies the difference, the fix may involve updating the documentation rather than changing the code. No patch has been proposed yet, and the thread remains focused on understanding the current behavior. The key open question is whether the inconsistency is by design or an oversight.
