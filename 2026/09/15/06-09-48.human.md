The changes are confined to the build system (`Makefile`, `CMakeLists.txt`, and `.gitignore`), with no impact on Git’s core functionality. The 35% speedup claim is plausible but remains anecdotal; the series does not include detailed benchmarks or CI validation. The precompiled-header logic is straightforward, using a `.gch` artifact and excluding files that don’t include `git-compat-util.h` (like reftable sources). The CMake adjustments may need review from Windows builders, but the patches are otherwise mechanical and unlikely to be controversial.
