This is a documentation patch, part of Todd Zullinger’s two-patch series (v3) that tidies up backtick-quoting in the `git-pack-refs` and `git-refs` man pages. It converts the SYNOPSIS block from `[verse]` to `[synopsis]`, removes single-quote formatting around the command name, backtick-quotes all option terms (`--all`, `--no-prune`, etc.) in the OPTIONS section via the included `pack-refs-options.adoc`, and italicizes the `<branch>` placeholder in the BUGS section. The patch is purely mechanical, touches only AsciiDoc files, and aligns with Jean-Noël Avila’s ongoing synopsis-style conversion effort. No functional changes to Git itself are introduced, and the patch is uncontested and ready for integration.
