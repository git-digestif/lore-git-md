This is the second patch in Todd Zullinger’s v3 documentation series, which ensures consistent backtick-quoting of commands and options in the `git-refs` man page. The patch mechanically applies backticks to all subcommand entries (`list`, `exists`, `optimize`, `create`, `delete`, `update`, and `rename`) that were added after the initial synopsis-style conversion but missed the quoting convention. It also replaces a literal `<branch>` placeholder with an italicized version for stylistic consistency.

The change is purely visual—no functional behavior is altered—and aligns with the ongoing effort led by Jean-Noël Avila to standardize man page formatting. The patch is uncontested and ready for integration, following the incorporation of Junio’s requested corrections in the previous patch. It touches only `Documentation/git-refs.adoc` and is part of a well-scoped, documentation-only series.
