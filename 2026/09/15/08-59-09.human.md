This is the final reply in the two-patch bugfix series that fixes a race in Git’s ODB transaction layer. The series targets a specific edge case: when `core.fsync` batching is enabled, large blobs (exceeding `core.bigFileThreshold`) are written to a packfile in a transaction’s temporary directory, but the transaction migrates to the main ODB before the packfile is finalized, leaving it unflushed and causing a "No such file or directory" error during the rename.

The exchange here is brief and confirms the patch is ready. Justin Tobler declines the tweak, noting the existing `test_when_finished` cleanup (which deletes the entire test repository) makes the extra config cleanup redundant. Karthik acknowledges the point, and the patch remains unchanged.
