This is a bug report discussion about inconsistent behavior between the `+` refspec modifier and the `--force` flag in `git fetch`.

Junio C Hamano, the Git maintainer, responds to André Kießling's report by clarifying that the `+` modifier in refspecs and the `--force` flag are indeed meant to serve the same purpose—allowing forced updates—but are *not* intended to affect pruning behavior. He argues that the documentation is correct and that the discrepancy André observed (where `+` in a refspec prunes deleted tags while `--force` does not) is not a bug but rather a misunderstanding of how pruning is controlled separately.

Junio attempts to reproduce the issue on a Unix-like system but fails, suggesting the behavior might be platform-specific (though he doubts it) or influenced by an undisclosed configuration. His test setup involves a fresh clone with tags, and he shows that adding `+refs/tags/*:refs/tags/*` to the fetch refspec does *not* prune local tags in his environment. This contradicts André's report, leaving the root cause unresolved.

The discussion now hinges on whether the behavior is intentional or a bug, and if the latter, whether it’s specific to Windows or tied to an unmentioned configuration. No patch has been proposed yet, but the thread is likely to continue as contributors investigate the discrepancy further. The issue remains well-motivated and may attract attention from fetch/push refspec maintainers.
