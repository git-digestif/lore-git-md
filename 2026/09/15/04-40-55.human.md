This is a review of the first patch in Justin Tobler’s two-patch series fixing a race in the ODB transaction layer. Karthik Nayak, a frequent contributor to the ref and ODB subsystems, replies with a **substantive review** that engages with the patch’s logic.

The patch itself lifts the `odb_reprepare()` call out of `flush_packfile_transaction()` so that callers can decide when to refresh the ODB view. Karthik’s feedback is narrowly scoped: he observes that `flush_packfile_transaction()` is now called unconditionally in `odb_transaction_files_commit()`, even when no packfile was ever opened, and suggests guarding it with the same `have_packfile` check that the patch already introduces for the reprepare step. The suggestion is mechanical and low-risk—it simply avoids a no-op call—but it tightens the code’s intent and may save a few cycles in the no-packfile case. No controversy or deeper technical concern is raised; the review is constructive and aligns with the patch’s goal of making the logic more explicit.
