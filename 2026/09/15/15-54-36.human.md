This is a bugfix patch for the `git status --ignored` pathspec substring matching issue reported earlier in the thread. René Scharfe identifies the root cause: the regression was introduced in commit `95c11ecc73` (2020-04-01), which optimized `fill_directory()` to skip pathspec matching for excluded directories. The optimization is flawed because it assumes excluded directories don’t need pathspec validation, but pathspecs can still filter ignored items by name.

The patch itself is minimal—just removing the `!excluded` condition in `dir.c` to force pathspec matching for all directories, not just non-excluded ones. René notes this might be a blunt fix and suggests the optimization could potentially be repaired instead, but the patch as written is straightforward and addresses the immediate bug. The reproduction case from the original report confirms the issue, though René also tests a variant in Git’s own repo (where substring matching doesn’t occur, hinting at edge-case complexity).
