This is a **bugfix review** targeting the stash and merge subsystems. D. Ben Knoble, who originally added the `stash.index` configuration, replies to Eli Barzilay’s bug report with a surface-level review and some exploratory debugging.

Knoble confirms the bug is reproducible using the provided script and shares his debugging setup (prepending `bin-wrappers` to `PATH` and using `GIT_DEBUGGER`/`GIT_TRACE2`). He agrees with Eli’s analysis that the root cause lies in `git stash apply --index` invoking `reset_head()`, which triggers `remove_merge_branch_state()` and prematurely deletes the `MERGE_AUTOSTASH` ref. Knoble expresses surprise that `git reset` performs branch-state cleanup even when invoked by `stash apply`, calling it “odd” and noting the manual page does not document this behavior.

He briefly experiments with replacing the `git reset` subprocess call with a direct `reset_working_tree()` invocation but reports no success yet. Knoble also considers disabling `stash.index` for merge autostashes but dismisses it as a superficial workaround. The email ends with an invitation for others to weigh in, positioning this as an early exploration rather than a finished fix. No patch is attached, and the feedback remains at the level of code-path tracing and conceptual discussion.
