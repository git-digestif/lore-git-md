This is the third and final patch in Tyler Cipriani’s v5 bugfix series for `--force-if-includes`. It exempts fast-forward pushes from the reflog reachability check, fixing an existing bug where fast-forwards of non-branch refs (tags, detached HEADs) were incorrectly rejected even when no force was needed.

The patch introduces a `deferred_reject_reason` in `set_ref_status_for_push` to postpone the `--force-if-includes` check until after the fast-forward determination. If the push is a fast-forward, the deferred rejection is discarded; otherwise, it becomes the active rejection reason. This change is minimal and focused, touching only `remote.c` and adding 27 lines of test coverage in `t/t5533-push-cas.sh` for fast-forward pushes of expired-reflog branches and tags.

The implementation is technically sound and aligns with the feature’s documented intent: `--force-if-includes` should only intervene when force is actually required. The deferred-rejection pattern avoids duplicating reflog checks and keeps the change localized. The new tests cover the two most common edge cases (expired reflogs and tag-based pushes) and integrate cleanly with the existing test suite.

This patch resolves the backwards-compatibility concern raised by D. Ben Knoble in v4 and addresses the architectural flaw Tyler identified—fast-forward pushes of non-branch refs now work without requiring `--no-force-if-includes`.
