This is a brief, uncontroversial follow-up to the `receive-report` hook series, now cooking in `next`. Karthik Nayak acknowledges Junio C Hamano’s cleanup patch that removes a redundant null-check before `FREE_AND_NULL(cmd->error_string_owned)` in the `override_cmds_error()` helper. The patch also serves as a model for updating `tools/coccinelle/free.cocci` to enforce this pattern, though the Coccinelle update itself is not included here.

The patch is a minor stylistic improvement with no functional impact. It resolves a small technical debt introduced in the memory-leak fix for the `receive-report` hook and will be merged alongside the main series. The series remains on track for graduation to `master`.
