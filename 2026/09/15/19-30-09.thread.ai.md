Thread identity: standalone bugfix patch for Git protocol v2 `upload-pack` regression in shallow fetch ordering with `uploadpack.allowRefInWanted=true`. Author: Royce Remer.

Core problem or goal: fix a client-side crash (`fatal: expected 'packfile', received 'shallow-info'`) during shallow clones or incremental shallow fetches when `uploadpack.allowRefInWanted` is enabled. The regression is caused by swapped function call order in `upload_pack_v2()`: `send_wanted_ref_info()` was called before `send_shallow_info()`, causing the client to skip the "wanted-refs" section while searching for "shallow-info" (due to the `peek` bit in `process_section_header()`). The patch moves `send_wanted_ref_info()` after `send_shallow_info()` to ensure the client receives sections in the expected order.
