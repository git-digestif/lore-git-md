This is the second patch in SZEDER Gábor’s four-patch **build system** series introducing precompiled headers for `git-compat-util.h`. The patch generalizes CMake’s Makefile parsing to ignore all `*_OBJS` variables (e.g., `XDIFF_OBJS`, `REFTABLE_OBJS`) rather than hardcoding exclusions for `COMPAT_OBJS` alone. The change is mechanical and preparatory: it ensures future patches can safely add new object-file variables without polluting CMake’s source lists.

The patch modifies a single line in `contrib/buildsystems/CMakeLists.txt`, replacing a hardcoded `$(COMPAT_OBJS)` removal with a regex that matches any `$(..._OBJS)` variable. The regex is deliberately constrained to avoid over-matching directory-prefixed variables like `$(UNIT_TEST_DIR)/test-lib.o`. The commit message explains the rationale clearly: this generalization is necessary to accommodate the series’ later patches, which reintroduce `REFTABLE_OBJS` and may add other object-file variables.

No functional changes are introduced, and the patch is unlikely to be controversial. However, it may draw attention from Windows/CMake builders to confirm the regex behaves as intended across platforms. The change is part of a broader effort to reduce build times by ~35%, but this specific patch is purely infrastructure—no performance impact is expected until the final patch (4/4) lands. The series’ v2 iteration made no technical changes; this patch remains identical to its v1 counterpart.
