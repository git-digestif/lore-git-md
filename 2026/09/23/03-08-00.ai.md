This is a **bugfix patch update** addressing Junio C Hamano’s layering concern in the cruft-pack walk fix (patch 2/5 of the v2 series). The patch resets the kept-pack cache after re-marking fresh packs as kept, ensuring the cruft-pack walk reflects the current kept-pack state. The v3 revision moves the cache invalidation logic from `builtin/pack-objects.c` to `packfile.c`, eliminating the unchecked downcast in the ODB source loop and removing the new dependency on the files backend from `pack-objects`. The downcast now sits alongside existing ones in `packfile.c`, which will need to move together when the files backend layering is eventually reworked. The fix remains minimal and correct for the current codebase, and the author argues it is necessary to prevent data loss in cruft repacks today, regardless of future ODB abstraction work. The test in `t5329-pack-objects-cruft.sh` reproduces the bug and confirms the fix. No new technical objections are raised; the architectural concern is now addressed, and the patch is ready for re-review. Likely next steps are acked-by or further discussion on whether the downcast grouping is sufficient for long-term maintainability.
