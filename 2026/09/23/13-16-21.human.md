This is the first patch in a two-patch bugfix series addressing a race in Git's ODB transaction layer. Patrick Steinhardt, a core contributor and leader of the ODB abstraction effort, raises a subtle but important point about error handling in the refactoring.

The patch itself lifts the `odb_reprepare()` call out of `flush_packfile_transaction()` and moves it to the callers, preparing for the actual race fix in patch 2/2. Patrick notes that the refactoring introduces a behavioral change: previously, `odb_reprepare()` was only called if the packfile flush succeeded, but now it is called unconditionally after the flush, even if the flush failed. He questions whether this is safe and suggests that error checking might be needed.

The observation is sharp and relevant. While the change is small and the risk is probably low (a failed flush would likely leave the ODB in a state where repreparation is still safe), it is exactly the kind of detail that can lead to subtle bugs later. Patrick does not demand a change, but he flags the issue for consideration. The author, Justin Tobler, will need to decide whether to add error checking or document why it is unnecessary.

This is a substantive review comment, not a nit. It does not block the patch, but it does raise a legitimate technical question that deserves an answer before the series advances. The rest of the patch remains clean and well-motivated, and the series as a whole is still on track to fix a real race in the ODB transaction layer.
