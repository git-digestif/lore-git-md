This is Christian Couder’s reply to Junio’s review of the first patch in the v2 series introducing `early_scan_options()`, a new `parse-options` sub-API for early argument scanning. The series targets a real bug in `git fast-import` where `--allow-unsafe-features` was silently ignored when preceded by `--depth 5`.

Christian addresses Junio’s two main concerns directly. First, he acknowledges the v1 design’s complexity and limited practical value, explaining that v2 now **requires commands to adopt the full `parse-options` API** before using the early scanner, eliminating the separate `early_scan_option` structure and simplifying the implementation. This aligns with Junio’s preference for reducing programmer burden and avoiding parallel abstractions. Second, he fixes the implementation issues Junio raised: renaming the misleading `options` parameter to `option` (since the function operates on a single array element), and correcting the logic for stuck arguments (`--option=value`) to use the same condition as `parse_options()` (`!(opt->flags & PARSE_OPT_NOARG)`), ensuring consistency with the existing API.

The reply also clarifies that the early scanner’s limitations—such as ignoring abbreviations and negated forms—are intentional trade-offs for simplicity and speed, documented in `parse-options.h` and `api-parse-options.adoc`. Christian proposes adding a comment to `find_early_scan_option()` to clarify that skipping separate values (`--option value`) is the caller’s responsibility, a minor but helpful improvement.

This exchange advances the series toward a more maintainable design, but the broader API’s long-term viability remains unresolved. Junio’s vision of a "dry-run" mode in `parse_options()` is still unaddressed, and the series may need further iteration before landing in `next`. The `git fast-import` bugfix (patch 3/6) is likely ready for integration, but the API patches (1/6 and 2/6) are still under discussion. The series is well-tested, with comprehensive coverage for edge cases like stuck values, optional arguments, and early termination at `--`.
