This is a **bugfix** targeting the HTTP transport layer, specifically the handling of Basic Authentication when credentials are provided with an empty username but a non-empty password.

The author notes that Basic Auth does not technically require non-empty usernames or passwords, making the current behavior counterintuitive and difficult to debug without verbose logging.
