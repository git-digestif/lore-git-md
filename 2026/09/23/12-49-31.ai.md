This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing `reference-transaction` hook redesign thread, specifically addressing two review comments from Karthik Nayak on Maciej Ciemborowicz’s proposed implementation.

Maciej responds to Karthik’s suggestion to avoid exposing `REF_TRANSACTION_FLAG_SKIP_HOOK` as a public mechanism. He explains that the packed-refs transaction is an internal, physical part of a single logical update in the files backend, so the hook should only observe the outer logical change. While restructuring the call chain to avoid the flag entirely would be ideal, Maciej argues it would either duplicate transaction lifecycle logic or hide the hook-suppression decision in a less transparent helper. Instead, he plans to keep the flag internal to the refs implementation, limiting its use to these backend-internal transactions.

Separately, Maciej agrees with Karthik’s feedback that the `int copy` parameter in the rename/copy logic should be a `bool`, since the function operates in only two modes (rename or copy). This change will make the contract clearer in the next version of the patch.

The discussion remains focused on implementation details rather than the underlying goal, which is to ensure the `reference-transaction` hook reports both deletion and creation events during branch renames and copies. No new patch version has been posted yet, but these adjustments are part of the ongoing redesign effort. Key technical details to track include the internalization of `REF_TRANSACTION_FLAG_SKIP_HOOK` and the switch from `int` to `bool` for the copy/rename mode.
