This is a **bugfix patch** that resolves a regression in `git rebase`, `git merge`, and related operations when commit graphs are enabled and submodules are involved. The issue causes fatal errors due to cross-repository commit-graph lookups, where submodule commits are incorrectly parsed in the superproject’s repository. The patch, authored by Orestis Floros, propagates the `struct repository *` parameter through the reachability functions in `commit-reach.c`, ensuring commits are parsed in the correct repository.

The fix is narrowly scoped and well-motivated, addressing the root cause without altering the core logic of the reachability functions. It includes a new test case in `t/t6437-submodule-merge.sh` that verifies the regression is resolved by setting up a superproject and submodule with separate commit-graphs and merging branches that update the submodule pointer. The test coverage is thorough, and the commit message clearly explains the problem and solution.

Patrick Steinhardt’s review confirms the patch is correct and aligns with the ongoing effort to eliminate implicit reliance on `the_repository`. He notes the test catches the bug and follows the existing style in the test file. The patch is uncontroversial and ready for integration, likely into the `next` branch. No performance implications or additional edge cases were raised in this review, though backporting to the maintenance track may be worth considering once the fix lands in `master`.
