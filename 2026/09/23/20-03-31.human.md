This is a **procedural review comment** from Junio on the third patch of Maciej Ciemborowicz’s v4 bugfix series, which restores correct old-OID reporting in the reference-transaction hook for `git fetch --prune` and `git remote prune`. The series as a whole is technically complete and ready for merging, but Junio spots a **build-order dependency issue**: the third patch adds a new parameter (`&failed_refs`) to a call to `refs_delete_refs()`, but the function signature change that enables this parameter was introduced in the **first patch** of the series. Because the patches are being reviewed in isolation, the second patch (which touches `git branch` and `git tag`) does not yet have the updated signature, meaning the series **does not compile between patches 1 and 2**.

The issue is purely mechanical—no design or logic flaw—and is easily fixed by reordering or squashing the patches. The fix itself remains sound: it restores the pre-2.31 behavior (old OID followed by all-zeros in the hook) for all affected commands, preserves the performance gains of the original optimization, and resolves the core design conflict using `REF_TRANSACTION_ALLOW_FAILURE`. All substantive feedback from Junio and Karthik Nayak has been addressed in v4, and the series includes comprehensive test coverage for both files and reftable backends. This is a high-priority regression fix affecting external tools (Gerrit, GitLab, CI systems) and is otherwise ready for `next`.
