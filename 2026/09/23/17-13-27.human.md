This is a CI/build system patch series targeting resource pressure in Git's Linux CI jobs. The author, Tamir Duberstein, addresses two specific problems that arise when running the full test suite with long tests enabled: the `diff` command being killed due to excessive output from `git log`, and disk space exhaustion (`ENOSPC`) during large clone/repack tests.

The series consists of two small patches. The first modifies `t4205-log-pretty-formats.sh` to use `cmp` instead of `diff` when comparing large outputs, avoiding the memory pressure that led to the `diff` process being killed. It also cleans up the large temporary files after the comparison succeeds. The second patch adjusts the parallelism settings for `make` and `prove` in the CI environment to match the available CPU count on Linux runners, aligning with GitLab's existing policy. Both changes preserve the full test coverage while reducing resource contention.

The patches touch only two files (`ci/lib.sh` and `t/t4205-log-pretty-formats.sh`) and are narrowly scoped to the CI infrastructure, making them low-risk for unintended side effects. The commit messages are clear and the rationale is well-explained, though the mention of "Prepared with Codex, including review by a separate Codex agent" is unusual for the Git mailing list and may draw curiosity or skepticism from reviewers.
