This is the second patch in D. Ben Knoble’s four-patch bugfix series for `git stash` autostash handling of staged index entries. It is a small, targeted refactoring that moves the call to `init_ui_merge_options()` earlier in `do_apply_stash()`—from just before the worktree merge to the start of the function. The motivation is to prepare the merge options (`struct merge_options o`) for reuse in a future patch (the fourth in the series) that will perform an in-core index merge alongside the worktree merge. The change is purely preparatory: no behavior is altered, and the patch consists of only two line movements in `builtin/stash.c`. The commit message is clear and concise, explaining the purpose of the refactoring without overstating its scope. This patch is uncontroversial and serves as a logical precursor to the functional fix that follows.
