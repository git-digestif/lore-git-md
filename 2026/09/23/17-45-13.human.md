This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing bugfix series that fixes a race condition in `git repack` leading to data loss. The email is Junio C Hamano acknowledging Qin ShiCheng’s v3 revision of patch 2/5, which addresses Junio’s earlier layering concern about an unchecked downcast in the ODB source loop.

The v3 patch moves the kept-pack cache invalidation logic from `builtin/pack-objects.c` to `packfile.c`, grouping the downcast with existing ones that will eventually need to move when the files backend layering is reworked. Junio accepts this as a pragmatic short-term fix—better to address the immediate data-loss risk with a layering violation than to leave the bug unfixed—but he reiterates that the layering issue remains unresolved in the long term. He invites input from ODB-backend experts (notably Patrick Steinhardt and Justin Tobler) on whether a cleaner architectural solution exists.

No new objections are raised; the patch is now ready for re-review and likely to proceed. The thread remains focused on the cruft-pack walk fix, with the broader series still under active discussion.
