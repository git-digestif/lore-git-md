This is a follow-up to the recently merged `http.sslVerifyStatus` security feature. SZEDER Gábor reports that the new test `t5585-http-ssl-ocsp.sh` fails on his system, specifically the test case that verifies a revoked certificate is accepted when `http.sslVerifyStatus` is disabled.

The failure occurs because libcurl (7.81.0) and Apache (2.4.52) on Gábor’s system reject the revoked certificate even when OCSP staple validation is turned off. The test expects the connection to succeed in this scenario, but instead it fails with a generic certificate verification error. Gábor also questions the robustness of the `SSL_VERIFYSTATUS` prerequisite, which currently passes if `git ls-remote` fails for *any* reason (including "repository not found" or "no OCSP response"), rather than specifically checking for the absence of the expected error message.

The patch itself remains merged to `master`; this email is about a post-merge test flake that may indicate either an overly strict test expectation or a backend-specific quirk in how libcurl handles revoked certificates when OCSP stapling is disabled.
