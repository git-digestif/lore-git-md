This thread is a two-patch series from Tamir Duberstein aimed at reducing resource exhaustion in GitHub Actions CI jobs. The patches address two specific issues: (1) an out-of-memory kill in `t4205-log-pretty-formats.sh` caused by `diff` comparing large outputs, fixed by switching to `cmp` and deleting temporary files early, and (2) over-aggressive parallelism in `make` and `prove` on Linux runners with only 2 CPU cores, fixed by dynamically setting `-j` to the runner’s CPU count.

The series is currently under review in its initial version (v1). Tamir has clarified in a follow-up that the patches were prepared with Codex assistance but will omit such attribution in future submissions—this is a procedural note and doesn’t affect the technical content. No reviews or objections have been recorded yet, and the series remains focused on pragmatic fixes with clear rationale. The changes are minimal, touching only CI scripts and a single test file, with no impact on Git’s core functionality or on-disk formats.
