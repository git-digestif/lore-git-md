This is a CI/build system patch that has just received Junio’s explicit approval.

The patch works around an intermittent HTTP/2 authentication failure in Git’s Debian 12 CI job by skipping two flaky subtests (`t5559.15` and `t5559.16`). The root cause is a bug in Debian 12’s curl 7.88.1 that treats early 401 responses as transport errors rather than allowing authentication retries; the issue was fixed upstream in curl 8.3.0. Johannes Schindelin’s diagnosis is thorough and the proposed workaround—setting `GIT_SKIP_TESTS` in `ci/lib.sh`—is minimal, targeted, and avoids the need to upgrade curl, which would defeat the job’s purpose of testing older supported distributions.

Junio’s reply is brief but unequivocal: he calls the diagnosis “superb,” agrees with the reasoning, and sees no need for fast-tracking into the current release. The patch is now effectively ready for integration and will likely appear in `next` shortly, reducing developer friction from flaky CI runs.
