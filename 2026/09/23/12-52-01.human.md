This is a follow-up in the Git 3.0 planning and Contributor's Summit discussion thread. D. Ben Knoble, a contributor focused on build system and documentation improvements, replies to Johannes Schindelin’s summit recap with three small but practical offers and observations.

He volunteers to reach out to Gentoo maintainers about their interest in a Git 2.x LTS branch, noting that Gentoo’s needs likely extend beyond packaging Git itself to the broader ecosystem of tools that depend on it. This is a concrete step toward addressing the open question Schindelin raised about LTS support.

Knoble also points out that Julia Evans, whose documentation work Schindelin praised, received a Sovereign Tech Fund fellowship in 2026 to continue improving Git’s documentation. This adds context to the ongoing effort and underscores its funding stability.

Finally, he asks whether the new push protocol v2 is less accessible for smaller or local hosts, suggesting that ease of adoption might be worth addressing alongside the protocol’s scalability benefits. The tone is constructive and low-key, offering help rather than critique. The email does not introduce new technical details or shift the thread’s direction, but it signals engagement from a contributor who works on infrastructure and documentation.
