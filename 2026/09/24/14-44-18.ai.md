This is the third patch in a seven-patch documentation series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page consolidating merge conflict resolution guidance. The series aims to replace scattered, duplicated advice across multiple command man pages with a single authoritative guide.

This patch updates `git-rebase.adoc` to remove redundant conflict-resolution details and link to the new `gitmergeconflicts(7)` guide. The change deletes four lines of step-by-step conflict resolution instructions (e.g., using `git diff` to find conflict markers and `git add` to mark files as resolved) and replaces them with a cross-reference to the new guide. The patch retains the unique `--skip` option explanation for `git rebase`, which is not covered by other commands, and adds the new guide to the `SEE ALSO` section.

The patch is mechanically straightforward and aligns with the series' goal of reducing duplication. The removed content is now covered in the new guide, which provides more comprehensive and user-friendly explanations. Reviewers may focus on whether the retained `--skip` explanation is sufficient or if additional rebase-specific details should be preserved. The patch does not alter runtime behavior or introduce new symbols, as it is purely documentation-focused. The series remains under review, with four more patches to update `git-revert`, `git-cherry-pick`, and `git-pull`.
