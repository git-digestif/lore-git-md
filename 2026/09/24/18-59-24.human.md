This is a **CI/build system** follow-up in the ongoing thread about working around Debian 12’s HTTP/2 authentication failures in Git’s CI. Johannes Schindelin responds to Jeff King’s (Peff) earlier clarification about the version-range approach for skipping affected tests. He reports that further investigation (using GPT-6) revealed the bug was introduced in curl 7.88.0, not 7.88.1, and has adjusted the version range accordingly for the next patch iteration.

The core issue remains unchanged: an intermittent HTTP/2 authentication failure in `t5559.15` and `t5559.16` caused by curl 7.88.0–8.3.0 treating early 401 responses as transport errors. The discussion is still unresolved on whether to use the original CI-only skip, Peff’s single-version runtime prereq (`HAVE_CURL_HTTP2_BUG`), or the version-range-aware prereq, but this update refines the technical accuracy of the version bounds. The change is minor and does not shift the trade-offs Peff outlined (false positives vs. false negatives), but it ensures the workaround is precise. No objections to the adjustment itself; the thread’s focus remains on implementation strategy.
