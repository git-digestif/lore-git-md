This thread is about adding an Afrikaans (`af`) translation for `git-gui`, submitted by Stéfan Driaan Turvey. The patch introduces a complete localization (2,733 translated strings) in the form of a new `po/af.po` file, following Git’s l10n conventions. After an initial review by Johannes Sixt (Hannes) flagged procedural omissions—missing registration in `po/meson.build` and un-stripped location metadata—the author posted a corrected v2, which Hannes has now queued for integration.

The patch is procedurally complete and ready for merging, with no technical or infrastructure concerns remaining. The translation itself hasn’t received substantive review for accuracy, but this is standard for l10n contributions, which often rely on post-merge community feedback. The change is confined to the `po/` directory and doesn’t affect Git’s core functionality. Unless new issues arise, the next step is for Junio to pick it up in his integration branches.
