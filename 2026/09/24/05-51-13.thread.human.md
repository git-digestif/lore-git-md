This thread is about adding an Afrikaans (`af`) translation for `git-gui`, making the graphical interface accessible to Afrikaans-speaking users. The initial patch, posted by Stéfan Driaan Turvey, provided a complete translation of 2,733 strings but missed two procedural requirements: registration in `po/meson.build` and stripping of location metadata from the `.po` file.

The second version (v2) addresses these issues, as requested by Johannes Sixt in his review. The translation itself remains unchanged, and no technical or translation-quality objections have been raised so far. The patch is now procedurally complete and ready for final approval, pending potential review from Afrikaans speakers or l10n maintainers to verify the translation’s accuracy. The changes are confined to the `po/` directory and do not affect Git’s core functionality.
