This is the third patch in a seven-part documentation series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page consolidating merge conflict resolution guidance. The series aims to reduce duplication and improve clarity by replacing scattered advice across multiple command pages with a single, comprehensive guide.

This patch updates `git-rebase.adoc` to reference the new guide. It removes a brief, incomplete explanation of conflict resolution (four lines about using `git diff` to find markers and `git add` to mark files as resolved) and replaces it with a cross-reference to `gitmergeconflicts(7)`. The patch retains the rebase-specific steps—including the unique `--skip` option—and adds the new guide to the `SEE ALSO` section.

The change is mechanical and aligns with the series' goal: centralizing conflict-resolution advice while preserving command-specific details. The removed text was already minimal, so the patch doesn’t lose much practical information. The cross-reference is placed immediately after the rebase-specific steps, making it easy for users to find the full guide.

This patch is uncontroversial and likely to be accepted as-is. Reviewers may check for consistency with the other man-page updates in the series (e.g., placement of the cross-reference, phrasing), but there’s little here to debate. The real discussion will center on the new guide itself (patch 1/7) and whether it strikes the right balance between completeness and brevity.
