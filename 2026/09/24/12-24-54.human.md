This is a **translation (l10n)** review for the Afrikaans `git-gui` localization patch. Johannes Sixt (Hannes) provides a **surface-level review** identifying two procedural oversights in the submission: the new Afrikaans translation (`af.po`) is not registered in `po/meson.build`, and the `.po` file includes location metadata that should be stripped for consistency with other translations.

The review is brief and technical, focusing on infrastructure requirements rather than translation quality. Hannes references `po/README.md` for guidance on automating the location-stripping step via Git's clean-filter mechanism, suggesting the author add an entry to `.git/info/attributes` to handle this until the project's `.gitattributes` is updated. Neither issue is controversial, and both are straightforward to address in a follow-up iteration. No substantive concerns about the translation itself are raised, and the patch remains uncontroversial.
