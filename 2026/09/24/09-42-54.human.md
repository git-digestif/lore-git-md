This is a **substantive review** of the test patch in D. Ben Knoble’s four-part bugfix series for `git stash` autostash handling. Phillip Wood identifies two gaps in the test’s verification logic: (1) `git diff-files` alone does not confirm that unstaged changes are identical before and after the failed `stash apply --index`, only that the same files are modified, and (2) the test does not explicitly verify that the *index* state is preserved, leaving a theoretical hole where index and worktree changes could diverge without detection.

Phillip suggests two improvements: adding `-p` to `git diff-files` to compare patch content (not just file lists), and capturing `git diff-index --cached HEAD` to explicitly check the index. These changes would tighten the test’s correctness guarantees without altering its scope or intent. The review is constructive and narrowly focused on test robustness; it does not challenge the patch’s motivation or design. The author can address these suggestions with a trivial update, likely in a reroll or as a follow-up. The patch remains a well-motivated addition to the series.
