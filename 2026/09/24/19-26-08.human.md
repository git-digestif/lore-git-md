This is a **CI/build system** clarification in response to Patrick Steinhardt’s review of the first patch in Johannes Schindelin’s four-patch series fixing GitLab CI breakage for Windows after Rust was enabled. Johannes acknowledges the poor wording in the original commit message and explains that "GNU Rust" refers to the Rust toolchain that produces GCC-compatible output (as opposed to the MSVC-compatible toolchain used for Visual C builds). He commits to rewording the commit message for clarity. He also confirms Patrick’s observation that the current MinGW CI job does not yet use the `install-dependencies.ps1` script (it uses `install-sdk.ps1` instead) and agrees to clarify this in the commit message. The technical substance of the patch—installing the `gnu` Rust target for MinGW builds—remains unchanged and is now better explained.

The discussion is narrow but important: it ensures the CI setup’s toolchain selection is transparent and maintainable. No objections to the patch’s correctness or necessity are raised, and the series’ overall goal (restoring CI functionality) is unaffected.
