This is patch 5/7 in Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch series refactoring `create_repository()` to enforce stateless input. The patch aligns `git clone` with the behavior already established for `git init` in patch 3/7: leading directories are created without applying the `core.sharedRepository` configuration, even when it is set at the global or system level. The change removes two lines of divergence between the two commands, replacing calls to `safe_create_leading_directories_const()` with `safe_create_leading_directories_no_share_const()`. A new test (`t1301.28`) verifies that both `git init` and `git clone` now produce the same permission pattern on leading directories—namely, that the umask is respected rather than overridden by `core.sharedRepository`. The patch is mechanical, touches only `builtin/clone.c` and `t/t1301-shared-repo.sh`, and does not alter on-disk format or repository initialization logic. It is a prerequisite for the final enforcement of statelessness in patch 7/7.
