This is a surface-level review from Junio C Hamano on the second patch in Kaartic Sivaraam’s RFC series to improve Git’s error reporting for invalid repository directories. The series aims to add explanatory "reason" lines to fatal errors when `--git-dir` is explicitly provided, helping users diagnose misconfigurations more easily.

Junio’s feedback focuses solely on code style and indentation in the patch. He notes that the indentation of the new `strbuf_addf` calls in `validate_headref` looks unusual compared to the surrounding code, though he does not object to the style itself if it matches existing patterns. He also flags a minor formatting issue: the `} else {` construct should appear on a single line, not split across two lines.

The review does not engage with the patch’s substance—whether the new `is_git_directory_verbose()` helper is well-designed, whether the error messages are clear and actionable, or whether the approach is worth pursuing. The feedback is purely mechanical and does not affect the series’ direction or readiness. The patch remains in RFC stage, awaiting broader discussion on the implementation and potential expansion to other repository-discovery cases. If the style issues are addressed, the patch itself appears clean and uncontroversial.
