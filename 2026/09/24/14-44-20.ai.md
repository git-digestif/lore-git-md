This is the fifth patch in Julia Evans's seven-patch documentation series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page consolidating merge conflict resolution guidance. The series aims to eliminate redundant conflict-resolution advice scattered across multiple command man pages by centralizing it in a single, example-driven guide.

This patch updates `Documentation/git-cherry-pick.adoc` to replace its existing merge-conflict discussion with a cross-reference to the new `gitmergeconflicts(7)` guide. The removed section described the mechanics of cherry-pick conflicts (e.g., `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD`, conflict markers, and index state) and linked to `git-merge(1)` for resolution advice. The new link directs users to the centralized guide, which covers conflict resolution in detail, including terminology clarification (e.g., "ours" vs. "theirs"), tools like `diff3`/`zdiff3`, and practical examples. The patch also adds `gitmergeconflicts(7)` to the `SEE ALSO` section for discoverability.

The change is mechanical and aligns with the series' goal of reducing duplication while preserving command-specific details (e.g., `--skip` for `git rebase` remains in its man page). The patch does not alter Git's behavior or introduce new functionality—it merely reorganizes documentation. Reviewers may focus on whether the retained link placement is optimal for user discoverability or if additional cherry-pick-specific guidance should be preserved (e.g., notes about `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD` or `--no-commit` edge cases). The series remains under review, with two patches (updating `git-cherry-pick` and `git-pull`) still pending after this one.
