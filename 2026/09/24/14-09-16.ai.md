This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 7-patch CI/build system series improving Meson build speed, test reliability, and GitLab CI stability. It fixes hanging MSVC jobs in GitLab CI by disabling PortableGit’s default credential helper (Git Credential Manager for Windows) after installation. The hang occurs because GitLab Runner 19.x invokes `git credential reject` during cleanup, which triggers GCM’s attempt to use Windows Credential Manager—an operation that fails and blocks indefinitely. The fix adds an explicit `git config unset --all --system credential.helper` call in the PowerShell dependency-installation script, making the cleanup a no-op. The change is minimal (8 lines in `ci/install-dependencies.ps1`) and platform-specific, with no impact on non-Windows builds. The patch directly addresses the CI issue noted in the cover letter and completes the series’ goal of resolving MSVC job hangs.
