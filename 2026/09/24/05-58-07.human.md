Patrick Steinhardt notes that the current implementation lacks error checking for flushing the object directory, and that the refactoring introduces a subtle behavior change: `odb_reprepare()` is now called even if `flush_packfile_transaction()` fails (e.g., due to a failed `tmp_objdir_migrate()`). This wasn’t the case before, where reprepare was conditional on a successful flush.

The exchange clarifies that the behavior change is real but likely inconsequential—repreparing after a failed flush is harmless, though it does highlight a broader issue: `flush_packfile_transaction()` swallows errors by calling `die()` internally, masking potential failures. Justin acknowledges this but defers addressing it to a future series, keeping the current fix focused. No changes to the patch are implied; the discussion is purely informational for reviewers and future contributors. The series remains on track for v2 with its core race fix intact.
