This is the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 7-patch Meson build-improvement series. It extends precompiled-header support (introduced in March 2026 for `libgit.a`) to the test-helper executable. The change is a one-line addition in `t/helper/meson.build` that points the test-tool build at the existing precompiled header (`tools/precompiled.h`). Benchmarking shows a clean-build speedup of ~19 % (6.36 s → 5.33 s), which is the largest single-patch gain in the series so far. No new symbols or on-disk formats are introduced; the patch is purely a build-time optimization.
