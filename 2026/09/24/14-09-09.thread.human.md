This thread is a seven-patch series from Patrick Steinhardt aimed at improving Git’s Meson build system. The focus is on speeding up clean build times (reducing them from ~6.8s to ~5.0s) by cutting redundant compilation, particularly for HTTP-related code and test helpers, while also fixing shell completion test failures and updating subproject wrappers (curl, expat, openssl, pcre2, zlib) to their latest versions. The series also resolves a GitLab CI hang affecting MSVC jobs, though MinGW-based jobs remain broken and are being addressed separately by Johannes Schindelin.

The changes are confined to build infrastructure—`meson.build` files, subproject wrappers, and test helpers—rather than core Git functionality, so the risk to existing behavior appears low. The patches are well-motivated, with measurable performance gains and clear fixes for test and CI issues. No objections or requests for changes have surfaced yet, and the series hasn’t seen any substantive review beyond the initial posting. If the improvements hold up in testing, this is likely to be merged without controversy.
