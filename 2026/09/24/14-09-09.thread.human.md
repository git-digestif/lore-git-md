This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch series improving Git’s Meson build system—speeding up clean builds, fixing shell completion test failures, updating outdated dependencies, and resolving GitLab CI hangs in MSVC jobs. The series is still in its first version and under review.

The most significant change so far is in the first patch, where Patrick refactored HTTP-related sources into a static library (`libgit-curl.a`) to avoid redundant compilation. Karthik Nayak reviewed this patch and confirmed the approach is sound, noting the library is correctly linked into all seven dependent commands. This change alone shaves about 8% off clean build times, and later patches add further optimizations—precompiled headers for test helpers and unit tests bring the total speedup to roughly 35%.

No objections or open questions have surfaced yet, but the series hasn’t seen broad review beyond Karthik’s confirmation. The fixes for shell completion tests and MSVC CI hangs are self-contained and don’t appear to introduce new risks, though the credential-helper workaround is Windows-specific. Johannes Schindelin’s separate work on MinGW CI issues remains unrelated. If the current pace holds, the series could move toward Junio’s integration branches once more feedback arrives.
