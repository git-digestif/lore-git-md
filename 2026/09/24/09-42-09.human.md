This is the **final review feedback** on the last patch of D. Ben Knoble’s four-patch bugfix series for `git stash`. The series fixes a race condition in autostash handling when `stash.index=true` is set, replacing subprocess-based index merges with in-core merges via `merge-ort.c`.

Phillip Wood’s review is **substantive but narrow**: he identifies a **memory leak** in the patch’s error path. The leak occurs because `merge_finalize()` is not called before returning on a conflicted merge, leaving allocations in the `merge_result` strmap unfreed. He also notes a minor **labeling nit** ("Upstream index" for the current index is slightly confusing, but not worth a re-roll). Everything else in the patch is approved.

The fix is **low-risk and localized**: adding `merge_finalize(&o, &result)` before the error return in `do_apply_stash()` resolves the leak without changing behavior. The patch remains a **clear improvement** over the old subprocess-based logic, and the series is otherwise ready for integration. No further changes are needed unless other reviewers raise additional concerns.
