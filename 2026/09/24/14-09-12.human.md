This is the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch Meson build improvement series. It extends precompiled-header support—previously added for `libgit.a`—to the test-helper executable (`test-tool`). Because every test-helper source includes `git-compat-util.h` via `test-tool.h`, the change yields a measurable speedup: clean builds now finish ~19 % faster (6.36 s → 5.33 s). The patch itself is a one-line addition in `t/helper/meson.build` that points to the existing precompiled header. No new symbols or on-disk formats are introduced, and the change is purely a build-time optimization.
