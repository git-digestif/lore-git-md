This is a **surface-level review** of the Afrikaans `git-gui` translation patch. Johannes Sixt (Hannes) points out two procedural omissions in the submission: the new `af.po` file must be registered in `po/meson.build`, and the `.po` file should be stripped of location metadata (line numbers) before committing, as is the convention for other translations in the repository. He references `po/README.md` for instructions on automating this with a Git clean-filter and suggests adding `af.po` to `.git/info/attributes` temporarily until the repository’s `.gitattributes` is updated. The review is purely about adherence to project conventions and does not question the translation’s accuracy or completeness. No technical objections or deeper engagement with the content are raised.
