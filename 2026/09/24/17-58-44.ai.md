This is the **v3 of a bugfix patch** that restores the correct default expiry periods for `git reflog expire`, addressing a Git 2.50 regression where reachable and unreachable reflog entries had their expiry times swapped. The patch directly reverses the misordering of `.default_expire_total` and `.default_expire_unreachable` in `reflog.h`, which was introduced by commit `85658275702b` in April 2025. This version splits the regression test into four independent test cases, covering reachable and unreachable entries both before and after their respective expiry periods (90 days for reachable, 30 days for unreachable).

The fix remains narrowly targeted and uncontroversial, touching only the misordered defaults in `REFLOG_EXPIRE_OPTIONS_INIT()` without altering any other logic or on-disk formats. The expanded test coverage in `t/t1410-reflog.sh` now provides robust regression protection by verifying that 60-day-old reachable entries are retained, 100-day-old reachable entries are expired, 20-day-old unreachable entries are retained, and 40-day-old unreachable entries are expired. These tests use `test-tool date` to simulate aged reflog entries and address Junio C Hamano’s prior feedback about test structure asymmetry.

The commit message continues to clearly identify the regression’s origin and the specific change made, incorporating all prior review feedback from Junio, Jeff King (Peff), and Patrick Steinhardt. The patch is ready for merging, with no remaining technical objections or open questions. The only uncertainty is whether Junio will merge it for the Git 2.56 cycle or defer it to the next one, given the regression’s low real-world impact (it went unnoticed for over a year).
