Junio acknowledges the validity of Patrick’s question but remains **neutral**, noting that the amount of code required to extend `refs_delete_refs()` versus rewriting callers to use transactions would likely be similar. He defers to seeing concrete code before deciding which approach is more reasonable.

The exchange underscores the **architectural tension** in the series: Patrick argues for a clean separation of concerns (transactions for conditional logic, `refs_delete_refs()` for simple deletions), while Junio is open to either path but wants to see the trade-offs in practice. The discussion is **high-stakes**—if Patrick’s view prevails, the series will need a major rewrite to avoid extending `refs_delete_refs()` with new parameters (`old_oids`, `failed_refs`) and instead use transactions directly in callers like `builtin/branch.c` and `builtin/fetch.c`. The thread remains unresolved, with the ball now in Maciej’s court to either defend the current design or pivot to transactions.
