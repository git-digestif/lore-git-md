This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch series improving the Meson build system. It extends precompiled-header support—already applied to test helpers in patch 3—to the unit-test executable (`unit-tests`). The twist is that `clar.c` (a third-party unit-test framework) must not include Git’s `git-compat-util.h`, so the patch moves it into a separate static library (`libclar.a`) with its own build flags, allowing the rest of the unit-test sources to benefit from precompiled headers. Benchmarking shows a 5 % speed-up (5.34 s → 5.08 s) for a full clean build. The change is confined to `t/meson.build` and is purely a build-time optimization with no user-visible effects.
