This thread is a seven-patch series by Patrick Steinhardt that refactors Git’s `create_repository()` function to enforce statelessness, removing its last in/out parameter usage (the propagation of `core.sharedRepository`). The goal is to simplify the interface and prepare for future unification of repository initialization. The series is now at v2, with updates focused on documentation and commit message clarifications.

The first six patches are plumbing prerequisites—relocating logic, simplifying helpers, and aligning behavior between `git init` and `git clone`—all without changing functionality. The capstone, patch 7/7, enforces statelessness by calling `repo_clear()` at the start of `create_repository()`, though this change is constrained by `repo_config_values()` still requiring `the_repository`. So far, reviews from Kaartic Sivaraam and Karthik Nayak have been entirely surface-level, addressing only cosmetic details like man-page references (`free(3)` vs. `free(3p)`) and comment wording. No substantive technical feedback has engaged with the core interface change or the series’ broader implications, leaving open the possibility that patch 7/7 might surface undocumented caller reliance on the old in/out behavior.

The series remains uncontroversial, but the lack of deep technical review on the capstone patch means its risk profile is still untested. The next steps will likely depend on whether reviewers begin scrutinizing the interface change or if the series proceeds as-is.
