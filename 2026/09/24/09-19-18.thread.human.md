Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch series to refactor `create_repository()` continues to progress without controversy, but also without substantive technical review. The goal is to enforce that the function’s input `struct repository` is stateless—currently an in/out parameter—by clearing it at the start, removing the last such usage (propagation of `core.sharedRepository`). The series is now at v2, with improved documentation and commit messages addressing cosmetic feedback from Kaartic Sivaraam and Karthik Nayak.

Patches 1 through 6 lay the groundwork: relocating logic, simplifying helpers, and aligning behavior between `git init` and `git clone` (notably ensuring leading directories respect the umask rather than `core.sharedRepository`). Patch 7, the capstone, adds the `repo_clear()` call to enforce statelessness, though its impact is constrained by `repo_config_values()` still requiring `the_repository`. The only feedback so far has been surface-level—nitpicks about man-page references (`free(3p)` vs. `free(3)`) and comment wording—leaving the core interface change unexamined. No reviewers have engaged with the technical substance of patch 7 or the broader goal of unifying repository initialization, and there’s a lingering risk that the enforcement might surface undocumented caller reliance on the old in/out behavior. The series remains in review, with no merge status reported.
