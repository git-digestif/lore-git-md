This thread is a seven-patch refactoring series by Patrick Steinhardt to simplify the `create_repository()` interface by making it stateless, removing the last in/out parameter usage (propagation of `core.sharedRepository`). The goal is to reduce confusion and prepare for future unification of repository initialization.

The first two patches—mechanical cleanups in `path.c`—are uncontroversial and lay the groundwork. The third patch, just posted, targets `git init` (`builtin/init-db.c`) and replaces a manual workaround for ignoring `core.sharedRepository` with a direct call to the new `safe_create_leading_directories_no_share_const()` helper. This change is purely mechanical, removing boilerplate while preserving behavior, and is also uncontroversial.

The series is still under review, with no merge status reported. The key technical change—enforcing statelessness in `create_repository()`—comes in the final patch (7/7), which may surface undocumented caller reliance on the old in/out behavior. No substantive feedback has been posted yet, but the early patches are straightforward and appear ready to merge once the full series is reviewed.
