This thread is a seven-patch series from Patrick Steinhardt refactoring the `create_repository()` interface to enforce statelessness of its input `struct repository`. The goal is to simplify the function's behavior, eliminate its last in/out parameter usage (for `core.sharedRepository`), and prepare for future unification of repository initialization.

The series is now complete with the v1 posting. The first six patches were plumbing prerequisites: they relocated logic, simplified boilerplate, and overhauled `repo_clear()` to reliably zero out the repository structure. Patch 5/7 aligned `git clone` and `git init` by ensuring both respect the umask for leading directory permissions, removing a long-standing behavioral divergence. The final patch (7/7) enforces statelessness by clearing the input repository at the start of `create_repository()`, a minimal but consequential change that caps the series. No prior versions exist, and the series is under review with no merge status reported yet.

Key technical details include the introduction of `safe_create_leading_directories_no_share_const()` (patch 2/7) and the overhaul of `repo_clear()` (patch 6/7) to use `memset` for robust zeroing. The series touches `builtin/init-db.c`, `builtin/clone.c`, `path.c`, `repository.c`, and `setup.c`, and adds a test (`t1301.28`) to verify permission consistency between `git init` and `git clone`. The on-disk format remains unchanged.

The main open question is whether any undocumented callers relied on the old in/out behavior, though the series' incremental approach and added tests suggest this risk is low. Patrick presents the refactoring as a clean, uncontroversial step toward a larger goal, and the final patch is unlikely to draw objections. The series builds on recent ODB alternates work and sets the stage for future unification of repository initialization.
