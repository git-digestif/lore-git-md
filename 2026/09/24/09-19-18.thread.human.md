This thread is a seven-patch refactoring series by Patrick Steinhardt that simplifies the `create_repository()` interface by enforcing stateless input, removing the last in/out parameter usage (propagation of `core.sharedRepository`). The goal is to reduce confusion and prepare for future unification of repository initialization. The series is now complete and under review, with all patches posted as v1.

The refactoring touches several files, including `repository.c`, `setup.c`, and `path.c`, and introduces minor behavior changes—such as ensuring `git init` and `git clone` respect the umask for leading directory permissions. The capstone patch (7/7) enforces statelessness by calling `repo_clear()` at the start of `create_repository()`, a minimal but consequential change. Tests confirm the new behavior, and the on-disk format remains unchanged.

So far, feedback has been light. Kaartic Sivaraam reviewed the series positively, calling the patches "well-split" and appreciating the removal of input state reliance, but his comments were surface-level and did not engage with technical details or potential edge cases. No substantive objections or open questions have emerged yet, though Patrick noted the possibility of undocumented caller assumptions surfacing with the new enforcement. The series appears uncontroversial but has not yet attracted deeper technical scrutiny.
