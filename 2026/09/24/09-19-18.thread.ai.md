Series version: v2 (updated documentation and commit messages)
Originating author: Patrick Steinhardt
Thread type: patch series

Core problem or goal
Refactor the `create_repository()` function to enforce that its input `struct repository` is stateless (currently used as an in/out parameter), removing the last in/out usage (propagation of `core.sharedRepository`). The goal is to simplify the interface, reduce confusion, and prepare for future unification of repository initialization.

Current status
Under review; v2 posted. The series is complete with all seven patches submitted. Patches 1/7 through 6/7 are plumbing prerequisites that relocate or simplify logic without behavior changes. Patch 7/7, the capstone, enforces statelessness by calling `repo_clear()` at the start of `create_repository()`, directly addressing the series’ core goal. The enforcement is constrained by `repo_config_values()` requiring `the_repository`, preventing a more radical interface change (e.g., allocating a new repository). No substantive technical review has yet engaged with patch 7/7 or the broader interface change. The latest exchange between Patrick Steinhardt and Karthik Nayak (patch 6/7) remains surface-level, focusing on mechanical implementation details (redundant NULL assignments, comment wording, and declaration grouping) without engaging with the patch’s role in the series’ larger goal or potential edge cases. The series remains uncontroversial, but patch 7/7 may surface undocumented caller reliance on the old in/out behavior.
