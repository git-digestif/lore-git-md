Core problem or goal
Refactor the `create_repository()` function to enforce that its input `struct repository` is stateless (currently used as an in/out parameter), removing the last in/out usage (propagation of `core.sharedRepository`). The goal is to simplify the interface, reduce confusion, and prepare for future unification of repository initialization.

Current status
Under review; v2 posted. The series is complete with all seven patches submitted. Patches 1/7 through 6/7 are plumbing prerequisites that relocate or simplify logic without behavior changes. Patch 4/7 (newly summarized) moves the `core.sharedRepository` setting from `cmd_init_db()` in `builtin/init-db.c` into `create_repository()` in `setup.c`, removing the last in/out usage for this value. The change is a minimal three-line adjustment: deleting the setting from `init-db.c` and adding it to `setup.c`, preserving identical behavior for both `git init` and `git clone` (the latter already passes `-1` and does not modify the repository). Changes from v1: improved documentation in `path.h` (grouping and clarifying the four `safe_create_leading_directories_*()` variants under a single explanatory paragraph, addressing Kaartic Sivaraam's feedback) and clearer commit messages for patches 6/7 (explaining the shift from `FREE_AND_NULL()` to `free()` followed by `memset`) and 1/7 (now explicitly noting the inlining of `safe_create_leading_directories_1()`). No functional changes; the update is purely documentary and cosmetic.
