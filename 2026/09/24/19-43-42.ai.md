This is a **bugfix patch reply** addressing **substantive review feedback** on the first patch of a three-patch series that restores correct reference-transaction hook behavior after a 2.31 regression. The series is currently in `next` but faces **architectural objections** from Patrick Steinhardt that could force a major rewrite.

Maciej Ciemborowicz responds to Karthik Nayak’s **substantive review** of the first patch, which refactors `refs_delete_refs()` to allow callers to supply old OIDs for batch deletion. The key points in this reply:

1. **`is_null_oid()` → `NULL` conversion in `ref_transaction_delete()`**
   Maciej concedes to Karthik’s safety concern and will **revert the removal of the `BUG()` macro**, leaving `ref_transaction_delete()` unchanged. Instead, he proposes storing the hook’s observed OID value in **separate fields that do not set `REF_HAVE_OLD`**, avoiding constraints on the transaction. This sidesteps the API semantics debate while preserving the patch’s core functionality.

2. **Redundant failure counting**
   Maciej clarifies that `rejection_data.failures` is needed because `failed_refs` is optional. However, he notes that if the solution moves to the **transaction layer** (as Patrick Steinhardt proposes), both the `failed_refs` parameter and the counter could be eliminated. This hints at a potential pivot toward Patrick’s architectural alternative.

3. **Loop variable nit**
   Maciej agrees to inline the `size_t i` declaration.

The reply is **technical and constructive**, directly addressing Karthik’s concerns while leaving the door open for a larger architectural shift. The most significant concession—retaining the `BUG()` macro—strengthens the patch’s safety but does not resolve the **underlying architectural tension** between Maciej’s caller-centric approach and Patrick’s transaction-layer proposal. Junio C Hamano’s request for **concrete code comparisons** between the two approaches remains unanswered, and this reply does not engage with that broader debate.

**Key takeaways for the thread’s future direction:**
- The first patch will likely be revised to **preserve `ref_transaction_delete()`’s existing safety checks**, addressing Karthik’s immediate concern.
- The **redundant failure counter** may be eliminated if the solution moves to the transaction layer, but this depends on whether Maciej pivots to Patrick’s proposal.
