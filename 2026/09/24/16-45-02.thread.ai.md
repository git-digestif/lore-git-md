Core problem or goal: Add flexible revision-based filtering to the `git repo structure` command, enabling users to include or exclude specific revisions (commits, refs, or ranges) from the repository structure report. The v2 patch replaces the v1 `--no-<reftype>` approach with Git’s standard `setup_revisions()` machinery, addressing the design critique that the original implementation was inflexible and inconsistent with Git’s idioms.
