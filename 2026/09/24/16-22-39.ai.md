This is a **test-infrastructure follow-up** in the `http.sslVerifyStatus` security feature thread. Junio C Hamano replies to SZEDER Gábor’s latest clarification, agreeing with both of Gábor’s suggested improvements to the `SSL_VERIFYSTATUS` prerequisite: tightening the error-message check to look for the absence of “could not enable OCSP status verification” (the second half of the error message) and renaming the prerequisite from `SSL_VERIFYSTATUS` to `CURL_SSL_VERIFYSTATUS` to better reflect its scope (libcurl support, not server support). No new technical issues are raised; the thread remains focused on test robustness. The next step is a follow-up patch implementing these two changes. The core `http.sslVerifyStatus` feature itself is unaffected and remains merged in `master`.
