This is a **substantive review** of the test patch in D. Ben Knoble’s four-patch series fixing `git stash` autostash handling for staged index entries. The review comes from Phillip Wood, who identifies two gaps in the test’s verification logic: (1) `git diff-files` alone does not confirm that unstaged changes are *identical* before and after the failed stash apply—only that the same files are modified—and (2) the test does not explicitly verify that the *index* state is preserved, leaving a theoretical hole where index and worktree changes could align by coincidence without the index actually being untouched.

Phillip suggests two concrete improvements: adding `git diff-files -p` to compare the actual diff content of unstaged changes, and adding `git diff-index --cached HEAD` to explicitly check that the index is unchanged. These changes would make the test more robust by ensuring both the worktree *and* index states are preserved exactly as they were before the failed `stash apply --index`. The review is narrowly scoped to test correctness and does not revisit the series’ broader design or the functional fix in patch 4/4, which Phillip has already approved in principle. The tone is constructive and appreciative of the test’s intent, framing the feedback as a refinement rather than a fundamental critique. No new technical risks are introduced; the review focuses on tightening the test’s guarantees. The author will likely incorporate these suggestions in a v3 reroll.
