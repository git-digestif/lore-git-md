This is a surface-level review of the second patch in Kaartic Sivaraam’s RFC series improving error reporting for invalid `--git-dir` directories. Junio C Hamano’s reply focuses solely on test style: he questions the use of subshells (`(...)`) in the new test cases, noting they appear unnecessary since the test body does not require a separate working directory or environment isolation. The three new tests—covering a missing `HEAD`, a `HEAD` symlink pointing outside `refs/`, and an invalid `GIT_OBJECT_DIRECTORY`—all use subshells despite operating in self-contained directories created by `test_when_finished`.

The feedback is purely mechanical and does not engage with the substance of the patch (the scenarios being tested or the error messages being verified). No objections are raised to the test coverage itself, and the review does not address the series’ broader goal of adding explanatory "reason" lines to error messages. The thread remains in the RFC phase, awaiting further discussion on the implementation or edge-case coverage.
