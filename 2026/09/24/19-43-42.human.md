This is a reply in the ongoing bugfix series that restores correct object IDs to the reference-transaction hook when branches or tags are deleted. The series, now in its fifth iteration, has been merged to `next` but faces architectural objections from Patrick Steinhardt that could force a rewrite.

Maciej Ciemborowicz responds here to Karthik Nayak’s substantive review of the first patch, which refactors `refs_delete_refs()` to allow callers to supply old OIDs for batch deletion. Karthik’s feedback focused on three points: the safety of removing a `BUG()` macro in `ref_transaction_delete()`, redundant failure counting, and a minor loop variable nit. Maciej concedes all three. He will revert the `BUG()` removal, eliminate the redundant counter (once the fix moves to the transaction layer), and inline the loop variable. The most significant concession is abandoning the batch deletion API changes entirely, opting instead to store hook-specific values in separate fields that do not affect the transaction’s core logic. This pivot aligns with Patrick’s architectural objection that `refs_delete_refs()` should remain a simple wrapper, leaving conditional logic to callers or the transaction layer itself. The author’s willingness to rework the patch suggests the series may soon shift toward Patrick’s proposed alternative, which would resolve old OIDs at the transaction layer and ensure the hook always receives correct values.
