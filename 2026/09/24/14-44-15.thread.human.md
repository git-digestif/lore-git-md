This thread introduced `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page consolidating Git’s scattered merge-conflict resolution guidance into a single, practical guide. The six-patch series, authored by Julia Evans and co-developed with Marie Claire LeBlanc Flanagan, is now fully merged to Junio’s `next` branch and ready for graduation to `master`. The final patch, just confirmed, updates `git-pull.adoc` to replace a redundant line about conflict resolution with a cross-reference to the new guide, completing the integration.

The new guide centralizes advice previously duplicated across `git-merge`, `git-rebase`, `git-revert`, `git-cherry-pick`, and `git-pull` man pages, while preserving command-specific details like `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD` and `--skip`. It clarifies foundational concepts (e.g., "ours" vs. "theirs," the role of the common ancestor in `diff3` conflict markers) and actively promotes `merge.conflictstyle=diff3` as a best practice. The series also fixed a 16-year-old bug in `git-merge.adoc` where it referenced non-existent resolution steps. All substantive feedback from prior versions—including Patrick Steinhardt’s concerns about the `zdiff3` explanation and Junio’s editorial tweaks—has been addressed or deferred to future work.

Key participants included Junio (who reviewed and approved all patches), Jeff King (who provided the mechanical fix for a `make check-docs` failure), and D. Ben Knoble and Patrick Steinhardt (who contributed substantive reviews in earlier versions). The series is now cooking in `next` with no open technical objections. Loose ends include potential follow-ups on `zdiff3` documentation, conflict-marker enhancements, and `git status` wording improvements, but these are not blocking graduation.
