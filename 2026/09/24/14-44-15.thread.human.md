This thread introduced `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page that consolidates Git’s scattered merge-conflict advice into a single, practical guide. Julia Evans authored the series, which has now reached its third and final version after incorporating all feedback from Junio Hamano and other reviewers. The guide explains what merge conflicts are, lists every command that can trigger them, walks through resolution steps, clarifies “ours” versus “theirs,” and promotes `merge.conflictstyle=diff3` as a best practice. Junio’s final review included a squashable patch refining the `diff3` example to use neutral language and demonstrate how the common ancestor reveals the intent behind conflicting changes—both sides removing a fruit from the original list, for instance.
