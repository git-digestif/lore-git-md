This thread is about Julia Evans’s patch series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page that consolidates Git’s merge-conflict resolution guidance. The series is now in its third version, with all prior feedback incorporated—including Junio’s final review of v2, which confirmed most patches were ready for merging with only minor refinements. Julia’s latest update (v3’s fifth patch) adopts Junio’s stylistic suggestion for `git-cherry-pick.adoc`, removing a vague introductory sentence about merge conflicts and relocating the cross-reference to the new guide to the end of the conflict-resolution section. This change mirrors an earlier adjustment in `git-revert.adoc` and keeps the focus on centralizing guidance without altering technical content.

The series is effectively complete: Junio has approved patches 2/6 (`git-merge.adoc`), 3/6 (`git-rebase.adoc`), and 6/6 (`git-pull.adoc`) if unchanged in v3, and patches 4/6 (`git-revert.adoc`) and 5/6 (`git-cherry-pick.adoc`) are now ready with the latest wording tweaks. The new guide itself—`gitmergeconflicts(7)`—has been refined through three iterations to address omissions (e.g., `git am -3`, `git checkout -m`, `|||||||` marker in `diff3`), terminology gaps ("original" as common ancestor, "configuration variable"), and stylistic improvements (grouping conflict-resolution tools, cohesive phrasing for Git’s refusal to guess resolutions). Key sections include foundational explanations of merge conflicts, resolution tools (with active promotion of `merge.conflictstyle=diff3`), and clarifications of "ours" vs. "theirs" during merges and rebases. The series is purely additive, with no on-disk or runtime behavior changes, and integrates cleanly into the documentation build system.

Open items are minor and deferred: a 16-year-old bug in `git-merge.adoc` (references to non-existent steps) will be fixed separately, and Junio’s exploratory questions about conflict marker labels and `git status` wording remain as potential follow-ups. The series has broad support, with substantive reviews from Junio, Jeff King, D. Ben Knoble, and Patrick Steinhardt, and is on track for merging after v3’s final submission.
