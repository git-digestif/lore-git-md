This thread is about a new `gitmergeconflicts(7)` man page, introduced by Julia Evans to consolidate Git’s scattered merge-conflict resolution advice into a single, practical guide. The series is now in its fourth and final version, with all substantive feedback incorporated. Junio Hamano has given his final sign-off and applied his `Reviewed-by` to all six patches, confirming the series is ready for merging once a minor mechanical fix is made—adding an empty `SYNOPSIS` section to the new man page to satisfy `make check-docs`.

The guide itself is a 339-line document that replaces duplicated conflict-resolution sections in existing man pages (`git-merge`, `git-rebase`, `git-revert`, `git-cherry-pick`, and `git-pull`) with cross-references, while retaining command-specific details where necessary. It covers the basics of merge conflicts, an exhaustive list of commands that can trigger them (including `git am -3`, which Junio confirmed is a valid three-way merge operation), and step-by-step resolution guidance. The `diff3` example has been refined to use neutral language and demonstrate a scenario where both sides removed an item from a shared list, clarifying how the common ancestor reveals intent. The guide also promotes `merge.conflictstyle=diff3` as a best practice, though whether it will become the default remains an open question.

A few loose ends remain, such as clarifying the behavior of `zdiff3` (deferred to future work) and Junio’s exploratory ideas about improving conflict markers and `git status` output. Patrick Steinhardt’s suggestion to embed commit details in conflict markers is also noted as a potential follow-up. The series is currently cooking in `next` and should graduate to `master` in the next cycle, pending the trivial `SYNOPSIS` fix. No other blockers or objections are outstanding.
