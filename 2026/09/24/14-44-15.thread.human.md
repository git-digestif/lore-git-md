Julia Evans’ patch series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page consolidating Git’s merge conflict resolution guidance, is under review after its initial v1 posting. The series aims to replace scattered, duplicated advice across multiple command man pages (`git merge`, `git rebase`, `git revert`, `git cherry-pick`, and `git pull`) with a single, example-driven guide. Julia’s approach prioritizes clarity—clarifying "ours" vs. "theirs," providing practical examples, and addressing user feedback from sources like Mastodon polls.

Junio C Hamano reviewed the first patch and requested two mechanical adjustments: adding the new file to `Documentation/meson.build` and folding the `.gitattributes` conflict-marker-size setting into the first patch rather than leaving it as a separate, final patch. His follow-up email corrects an earlier misstatement about a Meson build failure (unrelated to this series) but reaffirms the need for these changes to ensure proper build system integration. No substantive feedback on the guide’s content or structure has emerged yet, though Julia has flagged several open questions, including whether `git commit` and `git merge --continue` are always equivalent in conflict resolution, whether `git apply`/`git am` conflicts should be covered, and the balance between brevity and completeness in the new guide.

The series remains in its initial v1 state, with no revised posting yet. The requested changes are minor and uncontroversial, focusing on build system mechanics rather than the guide’s substance. Once addressed, the series will likely proceed to broader review, where the community will weigh in on the guide’s technical accuracy, man-page conventions, and user discoverability.
