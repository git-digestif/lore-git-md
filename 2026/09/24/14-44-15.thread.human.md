This thread introduced `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page consolidating Git’s scattered merge-conflict guidance into a single, practical guide. Authored by Julia Evans with contributions from Marie Claire LeBlanc Flanagan, the series reached its fourth and final version after incorporating all feedback from Junio Hamano, Patrick Steinhardt, and D. Ben Knoble. The guide centralizes advice on conflict markers, resolution tools, "ours/theirs" terminology, and the `diff3` format, while replacing redundant sections in existing man pages (e.g., `git-merge.adoc`, `git-rebase.adoc`) with cross-references. Command-specific details—like `--skip` for rebase or `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD` for cherry-pick—are preserved where necessary.

Junio signed off on all six patches, and the series is now cooking in `next`, with no further changes expected. The guide’s exhaustive list of conflict-causing commands (including `git am -3`) and its side-by-side `diff3` example—now refined to avoid implying intent—are finalized. A few loose ends remain, such as clarifying `zdiff3`’s behavior and tweaking `git status` output, but these are deferred to future work.
