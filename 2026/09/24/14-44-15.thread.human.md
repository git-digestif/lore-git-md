This thread is about Julia Evans’s seven-patch series to introduce `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page that consolidates Git’s scattered merge-conflict advice into a single, example-driven guide. The goal is to reduce duplication across command man pages (`git merge`, `git rebase`, `git revert`, `git cherry-pick`, and `git pull`) while improving clarity—especially around terminology like "ours" vs. "theirs" and tools like `diff3`/`zdiff3`.

The series is now at its fifth patch, with the first four having updated `gitmergeconflicts.adoc` and the man pages for `git merge`, `git rebase`, and `git revert`. This latest patch replaces the conflict-resolution section in `git-cherry-pick.adoc` with a cross-reference to the new guide, mirroring the approach taken in earlier patches. The remaining two patches will update `git-pull` and finalize the series. No technical changes to Git’s behavior are involved—this is purely a documentation refactor.

Key questions still open include whether the guide should cover `git apply`/`git am` conflicts, the accuracy of `diff3`/`zdiff3` examples, and the balance between brevity and completeness in the new man page. Reviewers have yet to weigh in, but expected focus areas include the technical correctness of the examples, the appropriateness of the conversational tone, and the placement of cross-references in the updated man pages. The series is self-contained and doesn’t interact with other in-flight work.
