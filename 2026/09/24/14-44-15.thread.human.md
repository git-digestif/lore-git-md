This thread is about a new `gitmergeconflicts(7)` man page, introduced by Julia Evans to consolidate Git’s scattered merge-conflict guidance into a single, practical guide. The series is now in its final v3 form and ready for merging, with all substantive feedback incorporated. Junio C Hamano has given his final sign-off, and the patches are queued in `next` for integration testing.

The guide centralizes advice previously spread across multiple command man pages, improving clarity on foundational concepts like "ours" vs. "theirs," conflict markers, and resolution tools. Key refinements in v3 include a neutral tone, an exhaustive list of commands that can cause conflicts (including `git am -3`, which Junio recently clarified as a legitimate three-way merge operation), and a side-by-side comparison of `diff3` vs. default merge conflict formats to highlight how the common ancestor reveals intent. The latest updates also address mechanical details like punctuation consistency and spelling out `--patch` in `git log` examples.

No further changes are expected, and the series is fully integrated into the documentation build system. Open questions—such as the handling of `zdiff3`, conflict marker labels, and `git status` wording—have been deferred to future work. The thread has seen high-quality input from Junio, Jeff King, Patrick Steinhardt, and D. Ben Knoble, all of whom have reviewed the content substantively. The guide is now poised to become the go-to resource for users navigating merge conflicts.
