This thread is about Julia Evans’s patch series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page that consolidates Git’s scattered merge-conflict resolution advice into a single, user-friendly guide. The series is in its first version and currently cooking in Junio’s `seen` branch for integration testing.

The core goal—replacing duplicated conflict-resolution sections in existing man pages (`git merge`, `git rebase`, `git cherry-pick`, etc.) with cross-references to the new guide—has broad support, but Junio and D. Ben Knoble have raised two key content concerns: the new guide omits cherry-pick-specific details like `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD` and auto-resolved path behavior, and it lacks a foundational explanation of what a merge conflict *is*. Julia has committed to addressing these, along with mechanical fixes like registering the new man page in `command-list.txt` (which triggered a `make check-docs` failure) and folding the `.gitattributes` change into the first patch. Jeff King identified the `command-list.txt` issue and provided the exact fix needed.

The series is now blocked on these integration adjustments rather than design objections. Julia’s latest email asks a procedural question—whether she should apply her patches to Junio’s `seen` branch before resubmitting—highlighting a minor workflow uncertainty but no new technical issues. Once the mechanical fixes are in, the series should be ready to graduate to `next`.
