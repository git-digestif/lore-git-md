This thread is about a new documentation series by Julia Evans that introduces `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a dedicated man page to consolidate Git’s scattered merge-conflict resolution advice. The goal is to replace redundant sections in existing man pages (`git merge`, `git rebase`, `git revert`, `git cherry-pick`, and `git pull`) with cross-references to this new guide, improving clarity and reducing maintenance overhead. The series is now under review in its initial (v1) form, with seven patches covering the new guide, updates to existing man pages, and build system adjustments.

Junio C Hamano reviewed the first patch and identified a minor but important oversight: while the patch adds the new man page to the Makefile, it omits the corresponding update for the Meson build system in `meson.build`. He also suggested combining the `.gitattributes` conflict-marker-size setting (currently the final patch) with the first patch to ensure build consistency from the outset. This feedback is purely mechanical and doesn’t challenge the series’ content or design. The requested changes are straightforward and won’t impact the core value of the guide—centralizing conflict-resolution advice—but will ensure the new man page is properly integrated into both build systems.

No other reviews have been posted yet, so the open questions from the initial submission remain unresolved. These include whether the guide should cover `git apply`/`git am` conflicts, the accuracy of technical details like `diff3`/`zdiff3` explanations, and the balance between brevity and completeness in the man page’s conversational tone. Reviewers are likely to focus on these aspects, as well as the placement of cross-references in the updated man pages and the sufficiency of retained command-specific details. The series is self-contained and doesn’t interact with other in-flight topics.
