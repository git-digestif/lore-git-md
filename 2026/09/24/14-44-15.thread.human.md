This thread is about a new `gitmergeconflicts(7)` man page, introduced by Julia Evans to consolidate Git’s scattered merge-conflict documentation into a single, practical guide. The series is now at version 3, incorporating all feedback from Junio Hamano’s review of v2. Junio has already approved most patches (2/6, 3/6, and 6/6) for merging if unchanged, and the remaining two (4/6 and 5/6) are ready with minor wording tweaks. The new guide is purely additive—no behavior changes—and is fully integrated into the documentation build system.

The latest email from Junio is a final polish pass on the first patch, focusing on two small but important details: making the list of commands that can cause conflicts exhaustive (adding `git stash pop` and `git revert`) and avoiding contractions like "Here's" in favor of formal phrasing. Julia had already agreed to these changes, so this is just a reminder to ensure they’re included in the final version. The series is otherwise complete and ready for merging once these tweaks are made. Key contributors—Junio, Jeff King, D. Ben Knoble, and Patrick Steinhardt—have all weighed in with substantive feedback, and the guide’s structure, terminology, and examples have been refined through three iterations. Open questions about future enhancements (like conflict-marker labels or defaulting to `diff3`) remain, but they’re separate from this series.
