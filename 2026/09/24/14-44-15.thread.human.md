This thread is about a new `gitmergeconflicts(7)` man page, proposed by Julia Evans to consolidate Git’s scattered merge-conflict advice into a single, user-friendly guide. The series is under active review (v1 is complete and in Junio’s `seen` branch for integration testing), but several issues remain before it can graduate to `next`.

Junio C Hamano and Jeff King identified mechanical integration problems: the new page must be registered in `command-list.txt` to pass `make check-docs`, and the `.gitattributes` addition should be folded into the first patch. More substantively, Junio raised concerns about the removal of cherry-pick-specific details—particularly the `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD` pseudo-ref and index behavior for auto-resolved paths—arguing these should either be added to the new guide or retained in the cherry-pick man page alongside the cross-reference. Julia has committed to addressing these omissions, and D. Ben Knoble’s latest feedback endorses her revised wording for the `git log --merge -p <filename>` section, resolving one of the guide’s technical ambiguities.

The series is otherwise well-received. Knoble’s reviews have been particularly constructive, validating design choices (e.g., deferring `git apply` coverage) and surfacing practical gaps, such as the need for a foundational explanation of what a merge conflict *is*. The guide’s conversational, example-driven approach is intact, but its completeness is still under discussion—especially for edge cases like `REBASE_HEAD` workflows and pull-specific options (`--no-commit`, `--no-ff`). The immediate next steps are mechanical (the `command-list.txt` fix) and content-focused (cherry-pick details and the foundational explanation). No revised version has been posted yet, but the feedback loop is narrowing the remaining work.
