This thread is about the finalization of a new `gitmergeconflicts(7)` man page, consolidating Git’s scattered merge-conflict resolution guidance into a single, practical guide. Julia Evans (via GitGitGadget) authored the six-patch series, now at version 3, which has incorporated all feedback from Junio Hamano and other reviewers.

The series is ready for merging. Junio’s reviews of v2 and v3 confirmed that all patches—including the new guide and updates to existing man pages (`git-merge`, `git-rebase`, `git-revert`, `git-cherry-pick`, and `git-pull`)—are approved once final refinements are applied. The latest email updates `git-pull.adoc` to cross-reference the new guide, a minor but necessary step to ensure users can easily find centralized conflict-resolution advice. This patch is uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ goal of reducing duplication and improving clarity.

Key improvements in v3 include an exhaustive list of commands that can cause conflicts, a formal tone, and retention of prior enhancements like the "WHAT IS A MERGE CONFLICT?" section and a rewritten "OURS" AND "THEIRS" explanation. The series is purely additive, with no runtime or on-disk behavior changes, and has passed `make check-docs` validation. Open questions—such as `zdiff3` deprecation, `merge.conflictstyle=diff3` as a default, and potential follow-ups like conflict-marker enhancements—remain as future work but do not block merging. The series has received high-weight reviews from Junio, Jeff King, D. Ben Knoble, and Patrick Steinhardt, all of whom are credited for their contributions.
