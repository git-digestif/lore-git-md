This thread introduces `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page designed to centralize Git’s scattered merge-conflict resolution guidance into a single, user-friendly guide. The series, now in its third iteration (v3), is led by Julia Evans and has evolved through two rounds of feedback from Junio C Hamano, D. Ben Knoble, and Patrick Steinhardt. The v3 patch addresses all remaining refinements, including clarifying terminology (e.g., "original" as the common ancestor), explaining conflict markers like `|||||||` in `diff3` output, acknowledging semantic conflicts, and deprecating `zdiff3`. Junio has already approved most patches in the series, contingent on minor wording tweaks, and the guide is structurally complete—only mechanical adjustments remain before merging.

The new man page is organized as a practical, example-driven resource, covering foundational concepts (e.g., "WHAT IS A MERGE CONFLICT?"), resolution tools (e.g., `git diff AUTO_MERGE`, `merge.conflictstyle=diff3`), and command-specific nuances. It replaces duplicated advice in existing man pages with cross-references while retaining critical details like `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD` in `git-cherry-pick.adoc`. The series is purely additive, with no runtime or on-disk behavior changes, and integrates seamlessly into the documentation build system. Julia’s responsiveness to feedback—including replacing informal phrasing, harmonizing examples, and updating trailers—has kept the series on track for merging after v3 submission. A separate patch will likely address a 16-year-old bug in `git-merge.adoc` unrelated to the new guide. Open follow-up discussions include Patrick Steinhardt’s proposal to embed commit details in conflict markers and Junio’s advocacy for making `diff3` the default conflict style, but these are deferred for future work.
