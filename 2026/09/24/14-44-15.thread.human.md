Julia Evans authored the series, now at v2, which addresses all feedback from v1—mechanical fixes (build system integration, `.gitattributes`), content refinements (a new foundational section, rewritten "ours/theirs" explanation), and command-specific details (retaining `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD` in `git-cherry-pick.adoc`). The series is complete and ready for merging, with no further revisions planned.
