This thread is about a new `gitmergeconflicts(7)` man page that consolidates Git’s scattered merge-conflict advice into a single, practical guide. Julia Evans authored the series, now at version 3, which has incorporated all feedback from Junio Hamano and other reviewers. The guide explains what merge conflicts are, lists every command that can trigger them, walks through resolution steps, clarifies “ours” versus “theirs,” and promotes `merge.conflictstyle=diff3` as a best practice. Junio’s final review included a small refinement to the `diff3` example—replacing “code” with “lines” and tweaking the illustration to better show how the common ancestor reveals the intent behind conflicting changes.
