This thread is about Julia Evans’s seven-part patch series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page consolidating Git’s scattered merge-conflict resolution advice into a single, user-friendly guide. The series is now complete and under final review, with all substantive feedback addressed. Key participants include Julia (the author), Junio C Hamano (the maintainer), Jeff King (Peff), D. Ben Knoble, and Patrick Steinhardt, who have collectively refined the guide’s clarity, completeness, and technical accuracy.

The series has been merged into Junio’s `seen` branch for integration testing, where it triggered a `make check-docs` failure due to the new man page not being registered in `command-list.txt`. Julia has confirmed she’ll address this by adding `gitmergeconflicts` to the "guides" section, following the pattern of `gitdatamodel(7)`. Junio’s latest email clarifies a minor misunderstanding in the final patch’s title: the `.gitattributes` setting `conflict-marker-size=32` doesn’t "ignore" conflict markers but prevents Git from misinterpreting payload lines (e.g., seven `<` characters) as conflict blocks—a detail that might be worth mentioning in the guide for users writing documentation or scripts. This is a surface-level correction; the patch itself is correct and will be merged as planned, with the title adjusted to avoid confusion.

The guide’s content is now in good shape, with recent improvements including a foundational "WHAT IS A MERGE CONFLICT?" section, Junio’s detailed examples demonstrating `diff3`’s practical utility, and Patrick Steinhardt’s proposed commit-accessibility enhancement (embedding commit details in conflict markers). Julia has revised the "OURS" AND "THEIRS" section to clarify terminology and rebase behavior, and the guide now actively promotes `merge.conflictstyle=diff3` as a best practice. Open questions remain about potential follow-up patches, such as rewording `git status` output during conflicts or updating conflict marker labels, but these are exploratory and not blockers. The series is expected to graduate to `next` once the mechanical `command-list.txt` fix is applied.
