This thread is about the final stages of introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page that consolidates Git’s scattered merge-conflict resolution guidance into a single, user-friendly guide. The series, now at version 3, was authored by Julia Evans and has undergone thorough review and refinement.

The core goal—centralizing conflict-resolution advice to reduce duplication and improve clarity—has been met. The new guide covers foundational concepts (e.g., "ours" vs. "theirs"), lists all commands that can trigger conflicts, explains resolution tools (including a strong endorsement of `merge.conflictstyle=diff3`), and provides practical examples. All substantive feedback from prior versions has been addressed, including Junio’s final refinements to the `diff3` example and Patrick Steinhardt’s editorial suggestions. The series is now cooking in Junio’s `next` branch, with only minor, non-blocking tweaks (like punctuation consistency) remaining.

The latest email from Patrick Steinhardt notes a small editorial nit in the commit message of patch 5/6, but this doesn’t affect the patch’s content or merge status. Junio has already signed off on the series, and all patches are ready for integration. The only open questions are potential follow-ups, such as improving conflict markers or adjusting `git status` output, but these are separate from the current effort. The thread is effectively complete, with the new guide poised to become part of Git’s documentation.
