This thread introduced and successfully merged `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page consolidating Git’s previously scattered merge-conflict guidance. The series, authored by Julia Evans and co-developed with Marie Claire LeBlanc Flanagan, reached its fifth and final version after addressing all substantive feedback. Junio Hamano reviewed and approved all six patches, and the series is now cooking in the `next` branch, ready for the next release.

The new guide centralizes advice on conflict markers, resolution steps, and the distinction between “ours” and “theirs” during merges and rebases. It also clarifies edge cases—such as scenarios where both sides removed an item from a shared list—and promotes `merge.conflictstyle=diff3` as a best practice. While the guide omits `zdiff3` and defers Patrick Steinhardt’s proposed commit-accessibility enhancements, these are acknowledged as potential follow-ups. The only remaining loose ends are Junio’s exploratory questions about updating conflict-marker labels and `git status` wording, neither of which block graduation.

The mechanical work of removing duplicated sections from existing man pages (e.g., `git-merge.adoc`) and integrating the new guide into the build system is complete. No runtime or on-disk behavior changes were introduced; the update is purely additive. The series also fixed a long-standing bug in `git-merge.adoc` where it referenced non-existent merge steps. With all reviews addressed and Junio’s final sign-off, the thread is effectively closed.
