This thread is about introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page designed to centralize Git’s scattered merge-conflict resolution advice. Julia Evans proposed the series to reduce duplication across existing man pages (`git merge`, `git rebase`, `git cherry-pick`, etc.) and improve clarity for users. The initial version (v1) was posted and merged into Junio C Hamano’s `seen` branch for testing, but triggered a `make check-docs` failure due to missing registration in `command-list.txt`. Junio and Jeff King (Peff) identified mechanical fixes—adding the new guide to `command-list.txt` and folding a `.gitattributes` change into the first patch—while D. Ben Knoble provided substantive feedback on content completeness.

The series’ core tension is balancing centralization with command-specific nuances. Junio’s latest email clarifies that `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD` (a pseudo-ref available during *any* conflicted cherry-pick, not just multi-commit operations) must be retained in `git-cherry-pick.adoc` rather than moved to the new guide. This aligns with earlier feedback that the guide should supplement, not replace, command-specific details. Julia has committed to revising the cherry-pick man page to include this clarification while linking to the new guide for general advice. Other open questions include whether to cover `git apply` conflicts (deferred for now) and how to explain foundational concepts like what a merge conflict *is*. The series is now focused on addressing these refinements, along with the mechanical fixes, before a v2 posting. Key participants—Junio, Peff, and Knoble—are all engaged and supportive, with no major objections remaining.
