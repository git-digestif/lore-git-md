This thread is about Julia Evans's patch series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page consolidating Git's scattered merge-conflict advice into a single, practical guide. The series is under active review, with v1 posted and merged into Junio C Hamano's `seen` branch for integration testing. Key participants include Junio (the maintainer), Jeff King (Peff), D. Ben Knoble, Patrick Steinhardt, and Julia herself.

The goal is to replace redundant conflict-resolution sections in existing man pages (e.g., `git merge`, `git rebase`) with cross-references to the new guide, while preserving command-specific details like `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD` for `git cherry-pick`. Early feedback has refined the series: Junio requested mechanical fixes (Meson build updates, `.gitattributes` inclusion), and a `make check-docs` failure revealed the need to register the new page in `command-list.txt`. Substantive discussions have focused on balancing centralization with command-specific nuances, improving terminology clarity (e.g., "ours" vs. "theirs"), and adding foundational explanations—though Julia has pushed back on some suggestions (e.g., 3-way merge theory) in favor of user-tested practicality.

The latest exchange between Junio and Julia zeroes in on two actionable improvements: making commit information more accessible during conflicts (Junio advocates for `git show $commit` and `git diff ...$commit` examples, while Julia prefers commit messages over raw IDs) and promoting `merge.conflictstyle=diff3` as a best practice. Junio’s strong endorsement of `diff3`—and his hint that it might become the default—could elevate its prominence in the guide. The series remains on track, with pending revisions to address the `command-list.txt` registration, cherry-pick-specific content retention, and these new suggestions. No major structural changes are expected, but the guide’s practical utility continues to sharpen through iterative feedback.
