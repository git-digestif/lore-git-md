This thread is about Julia Evans’s patch series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page that consolidates Git’s scattered merge-conflict guidance into a single, user-friendly guide. The series is now in its first version and under active review, with the goal of replacing redundant sections in existing man pages (like `git merge`, `git rebase`, and `git cherry-pick`) with cross-references to the new guide while preserving command-specific nuances.

Key participants include Julia (the author), Junio C Hamano (the maintainer), Jeff King (Peff), D. Ben Knoble, and Patrick Steinhardt. The series has been merged into Junio’s `seen` branch for integration testing, where it triggered a `make check-docs` failure due to the new man page not being registered in `command-list.txt`—a mechanical issue Julia has committed to fixing. Junio and Patrick have also raised substantive concerns about the removal of cherry-pick-specific details (like `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD`) and the need for foundational explanations in the new guide, which Julia is addressing by retaining these details in the cherry-pick man page and refining the guide’s terminology and completeness.

Patrick’s latest review of the second patch flags two minor issues: a missing period in the commit message and the loss of introductory context about merge conflicts in the `git-merge` man page. While the patch itself is uncontroversial—it replaces duplicated content with a cross-reference—his feedback reinforces the need for the new guide to include foundational explanations (e.g., what a merge conflict *is*) to avoid leaving users without prior knowledge at a disadvantage. This aligns with earlier reviews and is unlikely to block progress. The series is otherwise on track, with pending mechanical fixes (like the `command-list.txt` registration) and content refinements (e.g., terminology, cherry-pick-specific details) expected in the next revision.
