This thread introduces `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page consolidating Git’s scattered merge-conflict guidance into a single, practical guide. Authored by Julia Evans and refined through three iterations, the series addresses long-standing user confusion by centralizing advice on conflict resolution, clarifying terminology like "ours" and "theirs," and promoting best practices such as `merge.conflictstyle=diff3`. The final version (v3) incorporates all feedback from Junio Hamano and other reviewers, including a pedagogically improved `diff3` example that demonstrates how the common ancestor prevents mismerges.

The series is now fully reviewed and ready for merging. Junio has signed off on patches 2–6, and the remaining patch (1/6) has been updated with his requested refinements to the `diff3` example and phrasing. The guide is integrated into the documentation build system, and all cross-references in existing man pages (e.g., `git-merge`, `git-rebase`) have been updated to point to the new guide. No substantive changes are expected, though Patrick Steinhardt has suggested optional editorial tweaks, such as punctuation consistency and spelling out `--patch` in `git log --merge`.

Key participants include Junio (maintainer), who provided the final sign-off and squashable refinements; Jeff King, who fixed a `make check-docs` blocker; and D. Ben Knoble and Patrick Steinhardt, who contributed substantive reviews in earlier versions. The series is currently cooking in Junio’s `next` branch, with no open technical objections.
