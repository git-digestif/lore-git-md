This thread is about a new `gitmergeconflicts(7)` man page, introduced by Julia Evans to consolidate Git’s scattered merge-conflict documentation into a single, example-driven guide. The series is now in its initial version (v1) and has been queued in Junio C Hamano’s `seen` branch for integration testing. Junio’s latest email reports a mechanical issue: the `make check-docs` target fails because the new man page isn’t registered in the documentation linting infrastructure. This is a routine integration step—no content changes are needed—and once fixed, the series should move to `next`.

The guide itself is complete and covers practical conflict-resolution advice, including when conflicts occur, how to read conflict markers, tools for resolution, and clarifications like “ours” vs. “theirs” during rebase. Earlier feedback from Junio focused on build-system adjustments (adding Meson support and folding `.gitattributes` into the first patch), which have been addressed. Open questions remain about whether to include `git apply`/`git am` conflicts, the accuracy of `diff3`/`zdiff3` examples, and the balance between brevity and completeness. No substantive objections to the guide’s content have been raised, and the series is otherwise ready for wider review.
