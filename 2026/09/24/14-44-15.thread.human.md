This thread is about a documentation patch series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page that consolidates Git’s merge conflict resolution guidance. The series, now at version 3, is ready for merging after addressing all feedback from prior reviews. Julia Evans is the originating author, with collaborative input from Marie Claire LeBlanc Flanagan and substantial technical reviews from Junio C Hamano, D. Ben Knoble, Patrick Steinhardt, and Jeff King.

The goal is to replace scattered, duplicated advice across multiple command man pages with a single, practical guide. The new `gitmergeconflicts(7)` covers foundational concepts (what a merge conflict is, "ours" vs. "theirs"), an exhaustive list of commands that can trigger conflicts, resolution steps, tools for handling conflicts (including a strong recommendation for `merge.conflictstyle=diff3`), and side-by-side examples of `diff3` vs. `zdiff3` output. The series also fixes a 16-year-old bug in `git-merge.adoc` and updates cross-references in related man pages (`git-rebase`, `git-revert`, `git-cherry-pick`, `git-pull`).

Junio’s final review of v3 confirmed all patches are ready for merging once minor wording refinements are applied. The only remaining discussion points are administrative (attribution preferences) and potential follow-ups, such as Patrick Steinhardt’s proposed enhancement to embed commit details in conflict markers and Junio’s exploratory questions about improving `git status` output during conflicts. These are deferred to future work. The series has no unresolved technical objections and is fully integrated into the documentation build system.
