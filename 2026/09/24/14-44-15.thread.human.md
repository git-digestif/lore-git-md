This thread is about a new `gitmergeconflicts(7)` man page, consolidating Git’s scattered merge-conflict advice into a single, practical guide. Julia Evans (via GitGitGadget) authored the series, now at v3, which refines terminology, adds missing commands (`git am -3`, `git checkout -m`), and improves explanations of conflict markers, resolution tools, and "ours/theirs" behavior. Junio C Hamano’s v2 review confirmed the series is ready for merging once v3 incorporates his final wording tweaks, and all other feedback—from D. Ben Knoble, Patrick Steinhardt, and Jeff King—has been addressed. The guide is purely additive, replacing duplicated advice in existing man pages with cross-references while retaining command-specific details.

The series is now mechanically complete, with `make check-docs` validation passed and no structural changes needed. Key improvements include a rewritten "OURS" AND "THEIRS" section, active promotion of `merge.conflictstyle=diff3`, and a note deprecating `zdiff3`. Open follow-up ideas—like embedding commit details in conflict markers or rewording `git status` output—remain as potential future work but don’t block merging. Ben Knoble’s latest email is a minor process note about email address attribution in trailers, ensuring his `Reviewed-by:` uses the `.mailmap`-preferred address; this has no impact on the series’ technical readiness.
