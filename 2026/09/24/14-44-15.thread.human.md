The `gitmergeconflicts(7)` documentation series, introduced by Julia Evans, aims to centralize Git’s scattered merge-conflict advice into a new man page while preserving command-specific nuances. The v1 series is under review, with mechanical fixes pending (e.g., `command-list.txt` registration, Meson build updates) and content refinements to address feedback from Junio C Hamano and D. Ben Knoble. Key adjustments include retaining cherry-pick-specific details like `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD` in the `git-cherry-pick` man page and clarifying the `git log --merge -p` behavior.

Junio and Jeff King recently clarified workflow best practices for contributors: instead of applying patches directly to integration branches like `seen`, trial-merging a topic branch into `next` or `jch` can reveal conflicts with other in-flight work. While this step is optional and less critical for isolated documentation changes, it’s a useful habit for areas with overlapping efforts. The series remains on track for graduation once the pending fixes are addressed, with no major technical objections outstanding. Julia has committed to revisions, and the maintainers’ feedback has focused on integration mechanics rather than content disputes.
