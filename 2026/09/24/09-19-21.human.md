This is the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-part refactoring series to simplify the `create_repository()` interface. It targets `git init` and replaces a convoluted workaround for ignoring the `core.sharedRepository` setting when creating leading directories. The current code temporarily saves, zeroes, and restores the setting to prevent `safe_create_leading_directories()` from applying shared permissions—an awkward dance that the patch eliminates by switching to the newly introduced `safe_create_leading_directories_no_share_const()` helper.

The change is a clean, mechanical simplification: ten lines of error-prone bookkeeping become two lines of direct function call. It touches only `builtin/init-db.c` and leaves no behavioral footprint—`git init` will still ignore shared permissions for leading directories, just more straightforwardly. The patch is clearly motivated by the series’ broader goal of enforcing statelessness in `create_repository()`, and it looks like a sensible step toward that end. No controversy or edge cases are evident from the diff or commit message.
