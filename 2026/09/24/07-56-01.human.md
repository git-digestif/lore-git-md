This is a governance documentation discussion, continuing the thread about whether and how to document Git’s governance structure.

Antonin Delpeuch responds to Junio C Hamano’s cautious openness by clarifying his intent: he is not proposing to codify clout or formalize decision-making authority, but rather to *describe* existing roles, expectations, and renewal processes—even for informal or ad-hoc ones like the `git-security` list maintainers or `git-scm.com` administrators. He sees value in the *process* of writing the document itself, as it may surface gaps or opportunities for improvement (e.g., identifying new volunteers for overburdened roles) without requiring immediate changes to how the project operates.

The tone remains collaborative and low-stakes. Antonin emphasizes that the document would explicitly state its descriptive (not prescriptive) nature, and that any resulting changes could happen organically, separate from the documentation effort. He reiterates his willingness to lead the drafting process, either solo or with collaborators, and to defer to leadership’s preferences on communication channels and format. The thread is still exploratory—no patch has been proposed, and no decisions have been made about scope, location, or next steps.
