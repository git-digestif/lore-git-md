This is the third patch in Johannes Schindelin’s v2 series fixing GitLab CI breakage for Windows builds after Rust support was enabled. It addresses a specific failure in GitLab’s MinGW job where `cargo` is not found, despite Rust being installed.

The patch modifies `.gitlab-ci.yml` to ensure `cargo` remains accessible after the minimal Git SDK’s login profile resets `PATH`. It adds a call to `./ci/install-dependencies.ps1 -Mingw` in the `before_script` phase and explicitly appends `/c/Rust/bin` to `PATH` in the `script` phase. This ensures the Rust toolchain (installed in earlier patches) is usable during the build.

The change is minimal and targeted: four lines added, one removed. It directly resolves the "cargo: command not found" error observed in GitLab job #16576450182, a regression introduced by the Rust-enabling commit 86909a94db5d. The commit message notes that the fix was assisted by GPT-6, though the technical content appears straightforward and uncontroversial.

This patch depends on the earlier patches in the series (particularly the Rust toolchain setup in `install-dependencies.ps1`), but stands alone in its purpose: making `cargo` available in the MinGW job’s environment. No behavior changes to Git itself are introduced.

The series as a whole is under review and has been validated by Karthik Nayak’s internal GitLab pipeline, where this patch contributes to the restored functionality of the `build:mingw64` job. No objections or questions specific to this patch have been raised in the thread so far.
