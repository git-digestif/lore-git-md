This is the fifth patch in Julia Evans' seven-part documentation series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page consolidating merge conflict resolution guidance. The series aims to eliminate redundant conflict-resolution advice scattered across multiple command man pages by centralizing it in a single, example-driven guide.

This patch updates `git-cherry-pick.adoc` to replace its existing merge conflict explanation with a cross-reference to the new `gitmergeconflicts(7)` guide. The removed section described the low-level mechanics of cherry-pick conflicts (e.g., `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD`, conflict markers, and index state), which are now covered in the centralized guide. The patch also adds `gitmergeconflicts(7)` to the "SEE ALSO" section for discoverability.

The change is mechanical and aligns with the series' goal of reducing duplication while improving user experience. The new guide's practical, example-based approach (e.g., clarifying "ours" vs. "theirs" terminology and demonstrating `diff3`/`zdiff3` output) is likely to be more helpful than the removed technical details, which were already partially duplicated in other man pages. Reviewers may focus on whether the retained cross-reference is sufficiently prominent or if additional cherry-pick-specific guidance should be preserved, but the patch itself is uncontroversial and well-motivated. The series as a whole addresses a clear documentation gap and has been well-received so far.
