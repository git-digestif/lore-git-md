This thread is about a regression in Git 2.50 where the default expiry times for reachable and unreachable reflog entries were accidentally swapped—reachable entries (meant to expire after 90 days) now expire after 30 days, while unreachable entries (meant to expire after 30 days) are incorrectly retained for 90 days. The bug was introduced in April 2025 and went unnoticed for over a year, suggesting it had little real-world impact.

Pushkar Singh authored a fix (now at version 3) that restores the correct defaults and adds thorough test coverage. The patch has been reviewed by Junio C Hamano, Jeff King (Peff), and Patrick Steinhardt, all of whom agree it’s correct and low-risk. Junio merged it to `master` on June 12, 2025, resolving the regression. While the fix is uncontroversial, there was brief discussion about whether to fast-track it for the upcoming Git 2.56 release; Patrick Steinhardt initially proposed this but later withdrew the suggestion, aligning with the consensus that the patch can wait for the next cycle. No further action is needed—the regression is fixed, and the thread is now closed.
