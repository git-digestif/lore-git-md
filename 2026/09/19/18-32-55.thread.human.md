This thread is about a regression in Git 2.50 that swapped the default expiry times for reachable and unreachable reflog entries in `git reflog expire`. The bug, introduced in April 2025, caused reachable entries (documented to default to 90 days) to expire after 30 days, while unreachable entries (documented to default to 30 days) were incorrectly retained for 90 days. The issue was reported by r.norouzi and fixed by Pushkar Singh in a patch that has since been merged to `master` by Junio C Hamano on June 12, 2025.

The fix restores the correct default expiry periods and adds comprehensive test coverage to verify both reachable and unreachable expiry behavior. The regression had low real-world impact, as it went unnoticed for over a year, but the patch was deemed low-risk and ready for merging. Key participants included Junio, who approved the patch after addressing test-structure concerns, and Jeff King (Peff), who confirmed the regression's origin and supported the fix as obvious and uncontroversial. Patrick Steinhardt initially suggested fast-tracking the fix but later aligned with the consensus that it was not urgent.

The most recent email from Patrick Steinhardt is a post-merge review with minor nitpicks about test timing thresholds, suggesting tighter boundaries (89/90 days for reachable, 29/30 for unreachable) for precision. However, he explicitly stated this does not require a reroll, and the patch remains merged. There are no remaining open questions or loose ends.
