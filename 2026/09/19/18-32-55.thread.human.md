This thread reports and fixes a regression in Git 2.50 where `git reflog expire` swapped the default expiry times for reachable and unreachable reflog entries. The bug, introduced by commit `85658275702b` in April 2025, caused reachable entries (documented to expire after 90 days) to expire after 30 days, while unreachable entries (documented for 30 days) were incorrectly retained for 90 days. Pushkar Singh authored a fix (now in its second version) that restores the correct defaults and adds thorough test coverage to prevent regression.

The fix is uncontroversial and has been reviewed by Junio C Hamano, Jeff King, and Patrick Steinhardt, with all feedback addressed. Junio initially requested clearer commit messaging and expanded test coverage, while Patrick later suggested the fix might be fast-tracked for Git 2.56 due to its urgency—this regression affects both `git reflog expire` and `git gc`, which relies on it. The patch is minimal, touching only `reflog.h` and `builtin/reflog.c`, and includes tests verifying the correct retention and expiry of aged reflog entries. No technical objections remain, and the only open question is whether to merge it immediately or wait for the next cycle. The fix is ready for integration.
