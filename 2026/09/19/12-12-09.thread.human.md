Karthik Nayak validated the series in GitLab’s CI environment, confirming both `build:mingw64` and `build:msvc-meson` jobs now succeed. Patrick Steinhardt’s earlier questions about the toolchain and linker were resolved, and no technical objections remain.

The series is narrowly scoped to GitLab’s MinGW environment, with no changes to other CI platforms. The final patch (4/4) ensures the Rust MSI installer includes the `Gcc` feature, resolving a linker error for host executables like `build.rs`. The series is now ready for integration, though an unrelated GitLab Runner regression (credential-clearing timeout) remains unresolved but does not affect the fix.
