This thread is about fixing GitLab CI breakage for Windows builds after Rust support was enabled. Johannes Schindelin (Dscho) submitted a four-patch series (v1) that restores the pipeline by addressing Rust toolchain provisioning for MinGW builds, preventing silent data loss in `.git/info/exclude`, and ensuring `cargo` accessibility. The series was validated as successful by Karthik Nayak, who confirmed both the `build:mingw64` and `build:msvc-meson` jobs now pass in GitLab’s internal testing.

The original breakage is fully resolved, but a separate issue emerged with GitLab Runner 19.4.0~pre.2085.g4d3dddee: a `git credential reject` call during cleanup assumes interactive use, causing CI timeouts when Git Credential Manager opens a dialog. Dscho provided detailed debugging and reproduction steps, and Karthik has now forwarded this to GitLab’s infrastructure team for resolution. The patch series itself is uncontested and ready for integration, but the thread is paused pending GitLab’s response to the Runner regression. No further changes to the series are expected.
