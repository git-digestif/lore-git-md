This thread is about a four-patch series from Johannes Schindelin (Dscho) that fixes GitLab CI breakage for Windows builds after Rust support was enabled. The breakage was specific to GitLab's MinGW-based Windows jobs, where the Rust toolchain (`cargo`) and GNU Rust host-linker support were not properly provisioned, causing build failures and silent data loss in dependency setup.

The series is under review, with no feedback received yet. It targets only the CI infrastructure—no changes to Git's core behavior or user-facing features. The fixes include: (1) ensuring the correct Rust toolchain (`gnu` target) is installed for MinGW builds via a new `-Mingw` switch in the CI dependency script, (2) preserving `.git/info/exclude` contents to prevent data loss, and (3) explicitly adding `/c/Rust/bin` to `PATH` in `.gitlab-ci.yml` to make `cargo` accessible after the minimal SDK resets it. The latest patch addresses a linking failure in the MinGW job by ensuring the `Gcc` feature is included in the Rust installation when the `-Mingw` switch is used, which supplies the necessary linker for host executables like `build.rs`.

The series is described as "lightly tested" due to limited GitLab CI build minutes, so further validation may be needed. Open questions include the robustness of the CI script adjustments and whether the `-Mingw` switch and `Gcc` feature require additional justification. The fixes are narrowly scoped to GitLab's MinGW environment and do not affect other CI platforms, though they are part of the broader Rustification effort led by Ezekiel Newren. No maintainer decision on integration status has been made yet.
