This thread is about fixing GitLab CI breakage for Windows builds after Rust support was enabled. Johannes Schindelin (Dscho) posted a four-patch series (v1) that restores pipeline functionality by addressing Rust toolchain provisioning issues, silent data loss in `.git/info/exclude`, and ensuring `cargo` accessibility. The series has been validated as successful: Karthik Nayak’s internal GitLab merge request and pipeline confirm both the `build:mingw64` and `build:msvc-meson` jobs now pass, fixing the original breakage.

The discussion has since focused on two open questions. First, Patrick Steinhardt raised a substantive point about the "GNU Rust" toolchain (`gnu` target) for MinGW builds, asking why it differs from the MSVC build’s `msvc` target and what "GNU Rust" entails. Second, in the latest email, Patrick questions whether enabling the `Gcc` feature in Rust’s MSI installer introduces a redundant GCC toolchain alongside the existing MinGW-provided one, potentially causing conflicts or side effects. Neither question has been answered yet, and both could influence future CI script design. No maintainer decision on integration status has been made, and no technical objections to the series itself have been raised beyond these clarification requests.

A separate, unrelated issue—a regression in GitLab Runner 19.4.0~pre.2085.g4d3dddee where a `git credential reject` call during cleanup causes CI timeouts—has been escalated to GitLab’s infrastructure team. This does not affect the patch series’ validity and is not expected to require further action on the Git mailing list. The series remains under review, with the key loose ends being the `gnu` target rationale and the dual-GCC scenario.
