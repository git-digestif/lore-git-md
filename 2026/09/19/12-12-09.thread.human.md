This thread began as a fix for GitLab CI breakage on Windows after Rust support was enabled, addressing missing Rust toolchain and linker issues in MinGW builds. Johannes Schindelin (Dscho) submitted a four-patch series that restored the pipeline by adjusting CI scripts to properly provision the GNU Rust toolchain and preserve `.git/info/exclude` entries. Karthik Nayak validated the fix through an internal GitLab merge request, confirming both the targeted `build:mingw64` job and the unrelated `build:msvc-meson` job now pass.

The thread has since uncovered a new, unrelated issue: recent GitLab Runner images (19.4.0~pre.2085.g4d3dddee) introduce a `git credential reject` call during cleanup that assumes interactive use, causing CI timeouts when Git Credential Manager opens a dialog. This affects the `test:msvc-meson` job but is specific to the Runner's cleanup phase, not the original Rust toolchain fixes. Schindelin has documented the problem with links to failing jobs and Runner code, flagging it for GitLab's infrastructure team. The original patch series remains valid for its intended purpose, but this Runner regression may require separate attention to prevent future CI disruptions. No maintainer decision on integration has been made for the series, and no technical objections have been raised.
