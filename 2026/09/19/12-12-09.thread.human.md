This thread is about a four-patch series from Johannes Schindelin (Dscho) that fixes GitLab CI breakage for Windows builds after Rust support was enabled. The series addresses two distinct issues: the original Rust toolchain provisioning breakage (now fixed by v1) and an unrelated regression in GitLab Runner 19.4.0~pre where a `git credential reject` call during cleanup causes CI timeouts.

The v1 series has been validated as successful for its intended purpose—restoring CI functionality for Windows builds—with Karthik Nayak confirming both the `build:mingw64` and `build:msvc-meson` jobs now pass. The patches touch only CI infrastructure (`.gitlab-ci.yml` and `ci/install-dependencies.ps1`) and introduce no changes to Git’s core behavior. The main technical adjustments include ensuring the correct Rust toolchain (`gnu` target) is provisioned for MinGW builds, preserving `.git/info/exclude` contents to avoid silent data loss, and explicitly adding `/c/Rust/bin` to `PATH` to make `cargo` accessible.

Patrick Steinhardt has raised one substantive question about the necessity of the "GNU Rust" toolchain (`gnu` target) for MinGW builds, noting the commit message doesn’t explain why it differs from the MSVC build’s `msvc` target. Otherwise, the series has no technical objections, though the PowerShell `New-Item -Force` behavior and the conditional inclusion of the `Gcc` feature in the Rust MSI installation may prompt further discussion. The unrelated GitLab Runner regression (credential-clearing timeout) has been escalated to GitLab’s infrastructure team, so no further action is expected on the mailing list until they respond. The series remains under review, with no maintainer decision on integration status yet.
