This thread is about fixing GitLab CI breakage for Windows builds after Rust support was enabled. Johannes Schindelin (Dscho) sent a four-patch series (v1) that restores the pipeline by addressing Rust toolchain provisioning, silent data loss in `.git/info/exclude`, and ensuring `cargo` is accessible. The series was validated internally at GitLab by Karthik Nayak, confirming it fixes the original Rust/Cargo issues for both MinGW and MSVC builds.

The discussion has focused on two main areas: substantive questions about the Rust toolchain setup (e.g., the `Gcc` feature and GNU Rust target) and surface-level feedback on script organization. Patrick Steinhardt raised the former, which remains partially open—while Johannes clarified that "GNU Rust" refers to GCC-compatible output, the potential redundancy of the `Gcc` feature in the MinGW environment is still unresolved. The latter, about merging scripts or quoting `PATH` adjustments, was addressed by Johannes, who declined to merge `install-sdk.ps1` into `install-dependencies.ps1` (citing conceptual differences) and explained why the `PATH` adjustment doesn’t need quoting. No further action is expected on these minor points.

A separate, unrelated issue—a GitLab Runner regression causing CI timeouts during credential cleanup—was identified and escalated to GitLab’s infrastructure team. This doesn’t affect the patch series’ validity but highlights an external dependency risk. The series itself is under review, with no maintainer decision yet, and no technical objections beyond the clarification requests. The next steps likely involve waiting for GitLab’s response on the Runner issue and possibly addressing Patrick’s remaining question about the `Gcc` feature.
