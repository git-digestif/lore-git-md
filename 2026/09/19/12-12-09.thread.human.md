This thread is about fixing GitLab CI breakage for Windows builds after Rust support was enabled. Johannes Schindelin (Dscho) submitted a four-patch series (v1) that targets the GitLab CI infrastructure to restore pipeline functionality for MinGW-based Windows jobs. The breakage involves missing Rust toolchain components, silent data loss in dependency setup, and linker unavailability during builds.

The series makes narrow adjustments to CI scripts (`.gitlab-ci.yml` and `ci/install-dependencies.ps1`) to ensure the correct Rust toolchain is provisioned for MinGW builds, preserve `.git/info/exclude` entries, and make the GNU linker available. No changes to Git’s core behavior are proposed. The patches are under review, with no prior versions or maintainer feedback yet.

Karthik Nayak, a contributor on Patrick Steinhardt’s GitLab team, has now tested the series in an internal GitLab merge request (MR #671) and shared the pipeline results (#2863888081). This moves the series from "lightly tested" to "actively validated" in the same environment where the breakage occurred, addressing the original author’s concern about limited CI build minutes. No technical objections have been raised so far, but the MR and pipeline provide a concrete basis for further discussion if issues arise. The next steps will likely involve reviewing the pipeline results and any follow-up feedback from the testing.
