This is a withdrawal notice for the RFC patch series that proposed adding a warning in `git commit` for commits dated before their parents. The author, Yashwanth Sai, is withdrawing the patch after brian m. carlson pointed out that the commit message's AI co-authorship violates Git's contribution guidelines (`Documentation/SubmittingPatches`). The patch was written with significant AI assistance, making it ineligible under the project's Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) requirements.

The series touched `advice.c`, `advice.h`, `builtin/commit.c`, `Documentation/config/advice.adoc`, and `t7520-commit.sh`, introducing a new `advice.clockSkew` config key (default: `true`) to warn users when a commit's date precedes its parent. The feature was designed to address a long-standing usability issue where out-of-order commit dates break history traversal commands like `git log --since`, but it was warning-only and did not enforce date ordering. The implementation included timezone handling, skipped unparseable commits, and added test coverage in `t7520-commit.sh`.

The thread's procedural blocker (AI co-authorship) is now resolved by the withdrawal, but the underlying technical discussion remains open. brian m. carlson endorsed the feature as "useful" and noted it had been discussed on the list before, suggesting the problem is real and worth addressing. However, the series will not proceed in its current form. If Yashwanth revisits the idea, they will need to rewrite the patch without AI assistance. No further technical review or integration branch activity is expected for this iteration.
