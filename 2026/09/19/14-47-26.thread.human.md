This thread implements a "matching-inspired" fetch mode for shallow repositories, addressing slow or hanging `git fetch` operations when remotes have many branches. The series, now at v7, introduces `remote.<name>.refmap` to separate namespace mappings from fetch selection logic, dynamically fetching only branches tracked by local branches at the remote. Harald Nordgren is the author, and the design has evolved through feedback from Junio C Hamano and others.

The core change is in Patch 2/4, which adds logic to infer branches from local `@{upstream}` settings when no explicit refspec is given. Junio’s latest review flags integration issues: the patch causes test failures in `t5505` and `t5586`, and he suggests refactoring to improve clarity and avoid hardcoding `the_repository`. The precedence rule from Patch 1/4—where `remote.<name>.fetch` overrides `remote.<name>.refmap`—remains unresolved, as Junio hasn’t responded to Harald’s proposal. Patches 3/4 (adding `--limited-fetch` to `git remote add`) and 4/4 (defaulting shallow repositories to this mode) are uncontroversial but blocked until the design is finalized.
