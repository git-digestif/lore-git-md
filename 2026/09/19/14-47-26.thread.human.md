The v8 series from Harald Nordgren introduces a "matching-inspired" fetch mode for shallow repositories, aiming to speed up `git fetch` by dynamically fetching only the branches tracked by local branches at the remote. The series is now at a critical juncture: Junio’s latest review of Patch 2/5 identified two issues—a misplaced control-flow reorganization and a missing NULL-check—that require a respin. Harald has acknowledged both points and is preparing a fix, which should be straightforward.

The core design, using `remote.<name>.refmap` to separate namespace mappings from fetch selection, is settled and uncontroversial. The series also adds a `--limited-fetch` flag to `git remote add` (Patch 4/5) and defaults it for shallow repositories (Patch 5/5), both of which Junio reviewed as technically sound. The only remaining blocker is the respun Patch 2/5, which extracts existing logic into a helper without changing behavior. Once that’s addressed, the series is poised for `next`.

Phillip Wood and D. Ben Knoble contributed to earlier design discussions, but their positions on the current implementation aren’t yet clear. The series resolves a prior conflict with Collin’s topic in `seen` and has no other dependencies. If the respin lands cleanly, this could be the final version before merging.
