Harald Nordgren has sent the v13 series for the `remote.<name>.fetchRefmap` feature, which is now under final review for merging to `master`. This thread is about a long-running effort to improve `git fetch` performance in shallow repositories by dynamically fetching only the branches tracked by local branches at the remote being fetched.

The design is finalized and endorsed by Junio C Hamano and Phillip Wood. It introduces `remote.<name>.fetchRefmap` to separate the "what to fetch" logic from the "where to store it" mapping, resolving earlier objections to overloading `remote.<name>.refmap`. The series is currently queued in `next` (as v11) but v13 is the latest and expected to graduate soon. The only remaining high-weight issue is the override semantics for command-line refspecs and colon-separated `remote.<name>.fetch` entries, which the discussion has converged toward resolving in favor of override semantics (where command-line refspecs override `fetchRefmap` entirely).

This first patch in the v13 series adds the core plumbing: the new config key, its parsing in `remote.c`, and fallback logic in `remote_find_tracking()`. The implementation is mechanical and well-tested, with extensive new tests covering edge cases. The patch is ready for review, with no known blockers. The series is expected to graduate in the current cycle once the override semantics are finalized and any remaining feedback is incorporated.
