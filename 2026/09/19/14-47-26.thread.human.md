Harald Nordgren's v15 series, now complete at seven patches, implements a matching-inspired fetch mode to fix slow or hanging `git fetch` operations in shallow clones where a remote offers many branches. The series decouples which refs are fetched from where they are stored by introducing two new configuration keys: `remote.<name>.fetchRefmap` (mirroring how `remote.<name>.fetch` supplies default refspecs) and `remote.<name>.fetchUpstreams` (a boolean enabling fetches of locally tracked upstream branches even without an explicit `fetch` refspec). Patch 6/7 added a `--limited-fetch` flag to `git remote add`, and the final patch 7/7 makes that limited-fetch behavior the default whenever `git remote add` is run inside a shallow repository, setting `fetchRefmap` and `fetchUpstreams` while omitting the traditional wildcard `fetch` refspec; full repositories and the explicit `--no-limited-fetch` flag retain the old behavior.

The implementation spans refspec handling in `builtin/fetch.c` and `remote.c`, including a new `collect_upstream_from_remote()` helper and logic so that a refspec-less `git fetch <name>` retrieves only branches that local branches track when `fetchUpstreams` is enabled. Tests live in `t/t5586-fetch-refmap.sh` and `t/t5505-remote.sh`, covering upstream resolution, deduplication, `@{upstream}` and `@{push}` behavior, and the new shallow-repository default. Junio C Hamano has endorsed the override semantics and `fetchRefmap` design, and the series remains queued in `next` (as v11 in the latest "What's cooking" report), where it currently blocks Collin's `ch/remote-defer-follow-remote-head-validation` topic due to textual conflicts. A proposal to partially merge the foundational patches to unblock that dependent work remains open and unacted upon, and the series awaits final maintainer review before graduation to `master`.
