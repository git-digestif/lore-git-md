This thread is about a patch series by Harald Nordgren that introduces a "matching-inspired" fetch mode for shallow repositories, aiming to speed up `git fetch` by dynamically fetching only the branches tracked by local branches at the remote. The series, now at v11, has been queued in `next` but faces a fundamental design challenge.

The core issue is that the current design overloads `remote.<name>.refmap` to control both ref-namespace mapping and which refs are fetched, a limitation the series was meant to address. Phillip Wood raised a substantive objection, arguing this approach repeats the same inflexibility it seeks to fix. Junio C Hamano has now agreed, proposing a cleaner alternative: a separate `remote.<name>.fetchRefs` config key to decouple the "what" (which refs to fetch) from the "where" (where to store them). This would resolve the refspec-matching order inconsistency and enable per-branch overrides. The series is effectively blocked until this redesign is implemented, though it remains in `next` for now.

Key participants include Harald Nordgren (author), Junio C Hamano (maintainer), and Phillip Wood (reviewer). Junio’s latest reply endorses Phillip’s critique and sketches the new design direction, which will require a v12 revision. The `--limited-fetch` flag and shallow-repository default (introduced in v11) are likely to remain but will operate under the new precedence rules. The next steps are for Harald to revise the series to adopt `remote.<name>.fetchRefs` and submit an updated version.
