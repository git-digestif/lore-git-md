This thread is about Harald Nordgren’s ten-iteration patch series that introduces a “matching-inspired” fetch mode for shallow repositories via the new `remote.<name>.refmap` configuration. The goal is to stop slow or hanging `git fetch` operations in shallow clones by dynamically fetching only the branches that local branches already track at the remote, rather than negotiating history for every remote branch.

The v10 series is technically complete and addresses every prior review point. Junio, the maintainer, has signed off on the design and implementation, but he is now explicitly asking for a second reviewer to Ack the series so it can graduate to the `next` branch. The only change since v9 is a rebase to resolve a merge conflict with Junio’s own `ch/fetch-followremotehead-lazy-validation` topic, which is already in `master`; no functional changes were made. The five patches cover the refmap configuration, a pure refactoring to avoid hard-coded `the_repository` usage, the dynamic branch-inference logic, a `--limited-fetch` flag for `git remote add`, and a default-on behavior for shallow repositories. Test coverage is thorough, and the implementation reuses existing machinery without introducing new technical debt.

All substantive technical concerns have been resolved, and the series is uncontroversial. The remaining open item is purely procedural: Junio wants another established contributor to review and Ack the patches before he merges them to `next`. Once that happens, the series is expected to graduate quickly.
