Harald Nordgren has posted v16 of his seven-patch series introducing a matching-inspired fetch mode for shallow repositories, which decouples which refs are fetched from where they are stored via new `remote.<name>.fetchUpstreams` and `remote.<name>.fetchRefmap` configuration. This iteration directly addresses three concerns Junio C Hamano raised during his review of v15: patch 2/7 now rejects `--refmap` when fetching from a bare URL with no configured refspecs rather than silently ignoring it, fixes the tag-following (`*autotags`) computation so tags are followed only when refs are actually stored through the refmap, and ensures `--prune` does not delete configured `remote.<name>.fetch` destinations when `--refmap` redirects storage elsewhere. The technical core involves new helpers `drop_destinations()` and `map_refs_without_dst()` in `builtin/fetch.c` that strip destinations from configured refspecs before applying the refmap, allowing `--refmap` to override where fetched refs land without changing what is fetched.

The series remains under review; although v15 was queued in `next` according to the 2026-05-15 "What's cooking" report, v16 supersedes it and has not yet graduated to `master`. Phillip Wood previously proposed a partial merge of the foundational patches to unblock the dependent `ch/remote-defer-follow-remote-head-validation` topic, but Junio has not yet acted on that proposal. The thread awaits final maintainer review before any integration decision.
