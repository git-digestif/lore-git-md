This thread is about Harald Nordgren’s patch series introducing a "matching-inspired" fetch mode for shallow repositories, now in its twelfth iteration (v12). The goal is to speed up `git fetch` in shallow clones by dynamically fetching only the branches tracked by local branches at the remote, rather than negotiating history for all remote branches. The design is finalized and endorsed by Junio Hamano and Phillip Wood, with the key change being the introduction of `remote.<name>.fetchRefmap` to separate the "what" (which refs to fetch) from the "where" (where to store them).

The v12 implementation is complete and under active review for merging to `master`. The series is currently queued in `next` (as v11) but deprecated in favor of v12. The design resolves earlier objections by avoiding overloading `remote.<name>.refmap` and clarifying precedence rules: `remote.<name>.fetch` takes priority over `remote.<name>.fetchRefmap`, which in turn takes priority over dynamic branch enumeration. Command-line refspecs override both. The placement of forced updates (`+`) on the refmap side (the "where") is confirmed, though this introduces a minor inconvenience for users who want to reject non-fast-forward updates for some branches while mapping all refs the same way. Documentation updates in v12 address this and other edge cases, though Phillip Wood has requested further clarification on precedence and backwards-compatibility risks.

The only remaining hurdles are final review feedback on v12, including minor documentation tweaks and code nits. Harald has addressed all major design and implementation questions, and the series is expected to graduate in the current cycle. The latest exchange between Harald and D. Ben Knoble was a surface-level review of a helper function in Patch 2/5, focusing on commit message clarity. No new technical concerns were raised, and the series remains on track.
