The v14 series from Harald Nordgren, which introduces `remote.<name>.fetchRefmap` to decouple fetch selection from namespace mapping in shallow repositories, remains under final review with the override semantics now effectively settled. Junio C Hamano has sent a clarification to Phillip Wood confirming his understanding of the agreed behavior: refspecs with explicit destinations (like `+refs/heads/master:refs/heads/main`) will cause the refmap to be ignored entirely, whether they appear on the command line or in configuration, preventing the dual-storage issue where a fetched object would otherwise land in both the explicit destination and the refmap target. Destination-less refspecs (such as `master` or `master:`) remain subject to refmap remapping. This resolves the last substantive technical disagreement between the maintainer and the primary reviewer, with both now endorsing the implicit-override model where `src:dst` entries bypass `fetchRefmap` and command-line `--refmap` entirely.

While the technical design is converging, two strategic questions still await resolution before the series can graduate to `master`. First, Phillip Wood and Junio agree that Patch 3/5's implicit dynamic branch enumeration is architecturally questionable when explicit smart modes exist, preferring explicit `fetchRefmap` configuration instead; however, Matt Hunter has noted that Patch 3/5 is textually necessary to resolve conflicts with Collin's dependent topic `ch/remote-defer-follow-remote-head-validation`. Second, Phillip has proposed a partial merge of Patches 1--3 to unblock that dependent topic immediately while deferring the ergonomic additions in Patches 4--5 and revisiting the implicit behavior later. The series remains queued in `next` as v11, with v14 representing the current implementation under review.
