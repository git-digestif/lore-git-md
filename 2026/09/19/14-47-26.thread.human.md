This thread is about a patch series (v6) by Harald Nordgren that introduces a "matching-inspired" fetch mode for shallow Git repositories. The goal is to speed up `git fetch` operations by dynamically fetching only the branches tracked by local branches at the remote, rather than negotiating history for all remote branches. The series adds a new `remote.<name>.refmap` configuration to define namespace mappings and modifies `git fetch` to use this when no explicit refspec is provided.

The series is technically complete but remains blocked on a design decision: how to handle cases where both `remote.<name>.fetch` and `remote.<name>.refmap` are configured. Harald proposed that `remote.<name>.fetch` should take precedence, but Junio C Hamano has not yet responded. Additionally, the series had a textual conflict with Collin’s recently merged `followRemoteHAD` topic, which Harald has now reviewed and found resolved in Junio’s `seen` branch. The series is still under active review and not queued for integration.

Key participants include Harald (the author), Junio (the maintainer, who raised the design ambiguity and conflict), Phillip Wood (who provided design feedback in earlier versions), and D. Ben Knoble (who contributed to the design discussion). The core behavior—dynamic branch selection via `@{upstream}` tracking—is unchanged, and the series preserves backward compatibility for non-shallow repositories. The next steps depend on Junio’s response to the design proposal.
