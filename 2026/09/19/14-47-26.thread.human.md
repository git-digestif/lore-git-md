This thread is about Harald Nordgren’s patch series introducing `remote.<name>.fetchRefmap` to improve `git fetch` performance in shallow repositories by dynamically fetching only branches tracked by local branches. The **v13 implementation is complete** and under final review for merging to `master`, with the series currently queued in `next` (as v11, though v11 and v12 are deprecated). The design is finalized and endorsed by Junio C Hamano and Phillip Wood, resolving earlier objections to overloading `remote.<name>.refmap`.

The series is blocked from graduating by one remaining high-weight edge case: the **override semantics for command-line refspecs and colon-separated `remote.<name>.fetch` entries**. The discussion has converged toward **override semantics**, where such refspecs replace `fetchRefmap` entirely (Phillip Wood’s preference), rather than coexisting with it (current dual-storage behavior). This aligns with user expectations and preserves backwards compatibility. A v14 revision is expected to implement this change, along with any remaining review feedback. Other edge cases—such as `@{push}` resolution and documentation clarity—are fully resolved in v13.

Key participants include Harald (author), Junio (maintainer, design direction), and Phillip Wood (technical critique, backwards compatibility). D. Ben Knoble’s feedback has been surface-level and non-blocking. The series is self-contained, with no dependencies or related work affecting its progress. The shallow-repository default for `--limited-fetch` and the `@{push}` resolution fix are uncontroversial and well-tested. Graduation to `master` is expected in the current cycle once the override semantics are finalized.
