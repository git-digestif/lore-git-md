This thread is about Harald Nordgren’s patch series introducing a new `+:` refspec mode for `git fetch`, aimed at improving performance in shallow repositories by dynamically fetching only branches tracked by local branches. The series is now in its 12th iteration (v12), with the design finalized and endorsed by Junio Hamano and Phillip Wood. The core change decouples *what* to fetch (`remote.<name>.fetchRefmap`) from *where* to store it (`remote.<name>.refmap`), resolving earlier objections to overloading the `refmap` configuration.

The implementation is complete and under active review, with only documentation clarity and minor edge cases remaining. The most significant resolved issue was the "dual storage" behavior—when both a colon-separated `fetch` entry and `fetchRefmap` are configured, the ref is stored in both destinations, matching existing command-line behavior. The remaining open question is whether the documentation sufficiently explains this behavior, precedence rules, and backwards-compatibility risks. D. Ben Knoble’s recent feedback was surface-level, endorsing the `fetchRefmap` naming for future extensibility but not engaging with technical details.

The series is expected to graduate to `master` soon, pending final review feedback and documentation updates. No major blockers remain, and the design is stable.
