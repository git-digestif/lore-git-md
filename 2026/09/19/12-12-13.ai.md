This is the fourth and final patch in a CI/build system series fixing GitLab CI breakage for Windows builds after Rust was enabled. The series targets GitLab's MinGW-based Windows jobs, where Rust toolchain provisioning (`cargo`) and GNU Rust host-linker support were broken.

This patch specifically addresses a build failure in GitLab's MinGW job where `x86_64-w64-mingw32-gcc` could not be found during linking of `gitcore`'s build script. The issue stems from Cargo's requirement for the GNU MSI's `Gcc` feature, which supplies the necessary linker and platform libraries. The fix modifies `ci/install-dependencies.ps1` to conditionally include the `Gcc` feature in the Rust installation when the `-Mingw` switch is used, ensuring the linker is available for host executables like `build.rs`.

The change is minimal—just one line in a PowerShell script—and is narrowly scoped to the GitLab CI environment. It does not affect Git's core functionality or other CI platforms. The patch is part of the broader Rustification effort but focuses solely on restoring CI stability rather than advancing Rust adoption. The commit message provides clear technical context, including links to the failing job and Rust's MSI installer documentation, which helps justify the fix.

This patch, like the rest of the series, is lightly tested due to limited GitLab CI build minutes, so further validation in the full CI environment may be needed. The series as a whole appears well-motivated and targeted, addressing a real and specific breakage without introducing broader changes. Expect follow-up discussion to focus on the `-Mingw` switch, the `Gcc` feature, and the robustness of the CI script adjustments.
