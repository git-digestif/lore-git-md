This is a **substantive review** of the first patch in Maciej Ciemborowicz’s three-patch bugfix series that restores the old OID in the `reference-transaction` hook for deletions. Karthik Nayak raises a **race condition concern**: the patch changes `refs_delete_refs()` from unconditional deletion to conditional deletion based on the supplied old OIDs, which could cause the transaction to fail if the ref’s value changes between when the caller resolves it and when the transaction runs. The review is technically precise, identifying a potential correctness issue in the ref-deletion path that could affect atomicity and reliability. Karthik also suggests a minor readability improvement (using `refnames->items[i]` directly in the diff), but the core of the feedback is the race condition risk. This is a **tested-level concern**—it directly impacts the safety of the fix and could lead to spurious failures in production workflows. The author will need to address this before the series can proceed.
