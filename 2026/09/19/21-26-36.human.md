This is the second patch in a two-patch bugfix series for `git stash`, addressing a long-standing autostash bug triggered when `stash.index=true` is set. The patch replaces an external-process-based index merge (using `git diff-tree` and `git apply`) with an in-core merge via `merge-ort.c`, eliminating a race condition that could corrupt the `MERGE_AUTOSTASH` ref during concurrent autostash operations.

The change is surgical: it removes 65 lines of subprocess plumbing from `builtin/stash.c` and replaces them with a 20-line call to `merge_incore_nonrecursive()`, plus a few lines of setup. The patch also removes three `assert()` calls in `merge-ort.c` that the author believes are unnecessary for this internal merge, though they invite feedback on whether to retain them. A new test in `t/t7600-merge.sh` verifies the fix by staging a file, triggering an autostash merge, and confirming the stash is correctly applied and cleared.

The commit message now clearly explains the bug’s root cause (non-reentrant filesystem operations during concurrent autostash) and the fix’s two benefits: avoiding subprocess overhead and eliminating the race. The patch looks well-scoped and technically sound, with test coverage for the reported scenario. The only open question is the `assert()` removal, which is minor and unlikely to block acceptance. Reviewers familiar with the merge machinery (Elijah Newren, Phillip Wood) may weigh in, but the change appears uncontroversial.
