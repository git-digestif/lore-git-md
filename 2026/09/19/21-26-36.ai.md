This is the second patch in a two-patch bugfix series targeting `git stash`, authored by D. Ben Knoble. It addresses a long-standing autostash bug where staged index entries are incorrectly handled when `stash.index=true` is set, particularly during merge operations that trigger autostash. The patch replaces external process calls (`git diff-tree` and `git apply`) with in-core merging via `merge-ort.c`, eliminating a race condition that could corrupt the `MERGE_AUTOSTASH` ref and ensuring staged entries are preserved.

The change touches `builtin/stash.c` and `merge-ort.c`, removing 65 lines of subprocess-based logic and replacing it with 23 lines of in-core merge calls. The patch also adds a test case to `t/t7600-merge.sh` that verifies the fix by staging a file before a fast-forward merge with `--autostash` and `stash.index=true`. The author seeks feedback on whether to retain or remove `assert()` calls in `merge-ort.c`, noting that the original assertions (added in 2020) lack explanatory context.

This is a substantive, well-motivated fix that addresses a real correctness issue. The in-core approach is cleaner and more efficient than the previous subprocess-based implementation, and the added test coverage is a strong positive. The patch is likely to attract attention from reviewers familiar with the merge and stash subsystems, particularly Phillip Wood (who provided early feedback) and Elijah Newren. The only open question is the `assert()` handling, which appears to be a minor design choice rather than a functional concern. The series as a whole looks ready for serious review.
