# Thread deltas

## 2026/09/19

- [2026/09/19/18-32-55 by r.norouzi <r.norouzi@proton.me>] r.norouzi reported that `git reflog expire` prunes reachable reflog entries older than 30 days instead of the documented 90, because the default expiry values were swapped in commit 85658275702b (first released in 2.50.0): `reflog.h` now sets `default_expire_total` to `now - 30 * 24 * 3600` and `default_expire_unreachable` to `now - 90 * 24 * 3600`, reversing the previous assignments in `builtin/reflog.c`; observed with Git 2.55.0 (Git for Windows 2.55.0.windows.5), not platform-specific.

## 2026/09/22

- [2026/09/22/16-54-34 by Pushkar Singh <pushkarkumarsingh1970@gmail.com>] Pushkar Singh posted a patch fixing reversed default reflog expiry periods in `REFLOG_EXPIRE_OPTIONS_INIT()` (restoring 90 days for reachable entries and 30 days for unreachable entries) and adding a regression test for a 60-day-old reachable entry.
- [2026/09/22/17-27-38 by Junio C Hamano <gitster@pobox.com>] Junio C Hamano asked whether the breakage dated back to the beginning of the reflog expire feature or was introduced later, and suggested testing both sides of the expiry logic by checking unreachable entries at 20 and 40 days old plus a reachable entry at 100 days old.
- [2026/09/22/18-02-51 by Jeff King <peff@peff.net>] Jeff King answered that the values were flipped by commit 8565827570 ("builtin/reflog: stop storing default reflog expiry dates globally", 2025-04-08) and stated that this should go in the commit message.

- [2026/09/23/12-21-13 by Patrick Steinhardt <ps@pks.im>] Patrick Steinhardt acknowledged the fix, writing "Oops, indeed. Thanks for finding this issue and fixing it!"
