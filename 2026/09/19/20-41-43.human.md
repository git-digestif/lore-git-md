This is a **substantive review** of the first patch in Maciej Ciemborowicz’s three-patch bugfix series, which aims to restore the correct old-OID reporting in the `reference-transaction` hook when branches or tags are deleted. Karthik Nayak, a ref-backend expert, raises a **race-condition concern**: the new behavior—verifying old OIDs before deletion—could fail if the ref changes between the caller’s check and the transaction’s re-check, breaking callers that previously relied on unconditional deletion. His feedback is technical and precise, identifying a real correctness issue rather than a surface-level nit.

The review also includes a **minor style suggestion** (using `refnames->items[i]` consistently) but the core of the comment is the race question. This is not a show-stopper—Maciej’s approach is still the right direction—but it does mean the series may need a v2 to address the safety implications. The thread is now at a decision point: whether to accept the current design or adjust it to avoid the race.
