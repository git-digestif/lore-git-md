This is the first patch in a two-patch bugfix series for `git stash`. It removes an unused `#include "oid-array.h"` from `builtin/stash.c`, a straightforward cleanup that silences a Clang warning. The header is transitively included via other paths, but none of its direct symbols (`oid_array*`) are used in the file. The change is mechanical and uncontroversial, serving as a preparatory step for the functional fix in the next patch. No behavior or test coverage is affected.
