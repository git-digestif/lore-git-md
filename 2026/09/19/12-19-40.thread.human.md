This thread began when Francisco Boni reported that Git’s automatic injection of `LESS=FRX` into the environment—even when the user’s `core.pager` is a third-party tool like `delta`—creates unintended side effects, such as broken alternate-screen handling and disrupted keybindings. The issue stems from Git’s historical behavior of defaulting `LESS` to `FRX` only when the variable is *unset*, not when it’s empty, leading to inconsistent behavior between `LESS` being unset (Git injects `FRX`) and `LESS=""` (Git leaves the environment alone).

After discussion, Francisco accepted **brian m. carlson**’s argument that changing the behavior would risk cross-platform inconsistencies and break backward compatibility. The thread shifted to documentation, where Todd Zullinger proposed a patch to clarify the `LESS=""` workaround in `Documentation/config/core.adoc`. The patch, which explains how users can override Git’s defaults (e.g., `LESS="" delta` or `LESS="RX" delta`) and includes examples for per-command pager configurations, was reviewed and accepted by **brian m. carlson**. Junio C Hamano has now queued the patch in the `next` integration branch, signaling it will undergo testing before graduating to `master`. The thread is effectively resolved, with no further discussion or objections expected. The patch will make the workaround more discoverable for users encountering pager-related issues.
