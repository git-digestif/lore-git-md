Core problem or goal
Fix a latent error-handling bug in `git stash`’s `do_apply_stash()` function, where hard errors (`result.clean = -1`) from `merge_incore_nonrecursive()` are incorrectly treated as conflicts (`result.clean = 0`). This flaw risks a NULL-dereference when accessing `result.tree` after catastrophic merge failures (e.g., out-of-memory, disk errors). The follow-up series introduces explicit tri-state logic to handle hard errors separately, preventing crashes and providing clearer diagnostics. The original v4 series (now in `master`) resolved the autostash race condition but left this robustness gap unaddressed.
