This is a withdrawal notice for the RFC patch series that proposed adding a warning in `git commit` for commits dated before their parents. The author, Yashwanth Sai, acknowledges the procedural issue raised by brian m. carlson regarding AI co-authorship in the commit message, which violates Git's contribution guidelines. As a result, Yashwanth has withdrawn the patch and closed the pull request.

The series aimed to address a real usability problem—commit dates that violate Git's non-decreasing assumption can break history traversal commands like `git log --since`—but the technical discussion never progressed due to the procedural blocker. The patch itself was well-structured, touching `advice.c`, `builtin/commit.c`, and adding test coverage, but the AI authorship issue made it non-viable under current project rules. The author indicates they may revisit the idea later, but for now, the thread is effectively closed. No further review or integration is expected unless the series is resubmitted without AI involvement.
