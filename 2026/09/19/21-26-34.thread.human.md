This thread is about a bugfix series for `git stash` that corrects how autostash handles staged index entries when `stash.index=true` is set. The issue, discovered during merge operations, was caused by a race condition in the subprocess-based logic (`git diff-tree` and `git apply`), which the series replaces with in-core merging via `merge-ort.c`. The author, D. Ben Knoble, submitted the final version (v3) of the four-patch series, which is now technically sound and ready for integration.

The series addresses a critical correctness issue identified by Junio C Hamano: the tree arguments in `merge_incore_nonrecursive()` were backwards, leading to incorrect index state preservation. This was fixed by swapping the arguments from `(head, merge, merge_base)` to `(merge_base, merge, head)`. Phillip Wood, a key reviewer, endorsed the fix and recommended standardizing the argument order across related merge functions to prevent future confusion, though the author opted to treat this as a separate cleanup task. All prior feedback—including a memory leak fix, test robustness improvements, and the inclusion of Junio’s test case—has been incorporated.

Junio’s latest reply clarifies that his test case was a minimal illustration of the bug and encourages the author to develop a more realistic scenario for the test suite. While he doesn’t object to its inclusion as-is, he suggests expanding it to better reflect real-world usage. The series is otherwise complete, with no open questions or procedural blockers. Patch 1 (a cleanup) is already merged to `master`, and the remaining patches are ready for integration.
