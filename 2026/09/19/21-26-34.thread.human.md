This thread is about a bugfix series for `git stash` that addresses a race condition in autostashing when `stash.index=true` is set, causing staged index entries to be mishandled during merge operations. The series, now at v3, replaces subprocess-based logic with in-core merging via `merge-ort.c` to ensure correct index state preservation.

The first patch in the series has already been merged to `master`. The remaining four patches were previously ready for integration but were temporarily ejected from Junio’s `seen` branch due to three issues: a test breakage in `t5520-pull.sh`, a NULL-dereference risk in Patch 5/5, and a verbosity regression (now resolved). The test breakage was traced to an interaction with the `tb/rerere-lock-grace` topic, where auto-maintenance operations prematurely expired reflog entries critical for `git pull --rebase`. Thomas Bachem, the author of that topic, confirmed the root cause and proposed a targeted fix (pinning `gc.reflogExpire` to `never` in the test setup), which has already been submitted as a separate patch. This resolves the last major uncertainty around the breakage.

The remaining blocker is the NULL-dereference risk in Patch 5/5, where `merge_incore_nonrecursive()` may return with `result.tree` uninitialized on catastrophic failure. The author, D. Ben Knoble, has acknowledged the issue and plans to address it by checking `result.clean` before dereferencing `result.tree`. Once this is fixed, the series is expected to re-enter `seen` and proceed toward integration. Phillip Wood and Junio C Hamano have provided substantive reviews, and all prior feedback has been resolved. The series is technically complete and well-tested, with robust coverage for edge cases.
