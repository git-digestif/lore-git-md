This thread is a two-patch bugfix series from D. Ben Knoble that fixes a long-standing issue in `git stash` where autostashing fails to correctly preserve staged index entries when `stash.index=true` is set. The bug surfaces during merge operations that trigger autostash, leading to incorrect index state preservation. The fix replaces external process calls (`git diff-tree` and `git apply`) with in-core merging via `merge-ort.c`, eliminating a race condition and ensuring staged entries are properly preserved.

The series is currently under review, with no prior versions. The first patch is a low-weight cleanup (removing an unused header), while the second is the substantive fix, touching `builtin/stash.c` and `merge-ort.c` and adding a test case to `t/t7600-merge.sh`. The author has sought feedback on whether to retain or remove `assert()` calls in `merge-ort.c`, though this appears to be a minor design choice rather than a functional concern. The fix is well-motivated, addresses a real correctness issue, and includes test coverage, making it likely to attract attention from reviewers familiar with the merge and stash subsystems, such as Phillip Wood and Elijah Newren. The series looks ready for serious review.
