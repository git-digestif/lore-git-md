This thread is about a two-patch bugfix series from D. Ben Knoble that fixes a race condition in `git stash` when autostashing with staged index entries (`stash.index=true`). The bug surfaces during merge operations and can corrupt the `MERGE_AUTOSTASH` ref due to a race in the subprocess-based logic (`git diff-tree` and `git apply`). The series replaces this with in-core merging via `merge-ort.c`, ensuring staged entries are preserved correctly.

The first patch is a minor cleanup (removing an unused header), while the second is the substantive fix, refactoring the index merge logic in `builtin/stash.c` and `merge-ort.c` and adding a test case to `t/t7600-merge.sh`. The series is under review, with no prior versions. Phillip Wood provided a detailed technical review of the second patch, raising concerns about merge algorithm consistency (recommending the `_ui` variant for parity with worktree merges), assertion safety (suggesting the removed `assert()` calls may have been guarding against real crashes), and an unused `verbosity` field. He also praised the cleanup of subprocess calls and confirmed the test coverage is appropriate. The author had previously sought feedback on whether to retain the `assert()` calls, and Wood’s review suggests they may be necessary for safety. The series remains open, with these technical questions likely requiring follow-up from the author. No merge decisions have been made yet.
