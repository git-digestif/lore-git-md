This thread is a follow-up bugfix series for `git stash`, addressing a latent error-handling bug in `do_apply_stash()` that Junio C Hamano identified after the original series (`dk/stash-apply-index-incore`) was merged to `master`. The original series replaced subprocess-based index merging with in-core `merge-ort.c` logic to fix an autostash race condition, but left a robustness gap where hard merge errors (e.g., out-of-memory) could trigger a NULL-dereference crash.

The follow-up v5 series, authored by D. Ben Knoble, introduces explicit tri-state logic to handle hard errors separately from conflicts, preventing crashes and improving diagnostics. The series includes cleanup (removing an unused include), mechanical refactoring (to prepare the codebase), and two new tests: one validating `stash apply --index` behavior on merge failures, and another adding regression coverage for `--autostash`. Phillip Wood reviewed and approved the series, praising the removal of subprocesses from `git stash`. Junio has now signed off, marking the series ready for the `next` branch. It is expected to graduate to `master` in the next integration cycle, with no further changes or open questions remaining. The fix is minimal, well-tested, and aligns with existing code patterns.
