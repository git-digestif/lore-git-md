This thread is about a bugfix series for `git stash` that ensures autostashing correctly handles staged index entries when `stash.index=true` is set. The bug, reported by Eli Barzilay, caused incorrect index state preservation during merge operations that trigger autostash, due to a race condition in the subprocess-based logic. The series, now at version 4, replaces this with in-core merging via `merge-ort.c` to eliminate the risk of SIGSEGV on conflicted index merges.

The series is technically complete and ready for integration. All prior blockers—including a NULL-dereference risk, verbosity regression, bisectability issues, and a test interaction with `t5520-pull.sh`—have been resolved. The test breakage was traced to an unexpected reflog dependency in `git pull --rebase`, where `merge-base --fork-point` relies on reflog entries that were being expired by `git maintenance run --auto`. This was fixed by Thomas Bachem’s patch, which pins `gc.reflogExpire` and `gc.reflogExpireUnreachable` to `never` in the test setup. The fix is merged to `master` and does not affect the stash series’ logic.

Key participants include D. Ben Knoble (author), who incorporated all feedback and confirmed the series’ readiness; Junio C Hamano (maintainer), who merged the first patch and confirmed the series is ready; and Phillip Wood (reviewer), who provided substantive technical review and validated the fixes. The series touches `builtin/stash.c`, `merge-ort.c`, and several test files, with new tests verifying correct tree ordering during index merges. No open questions remain, and the series is now awaiting integration.
