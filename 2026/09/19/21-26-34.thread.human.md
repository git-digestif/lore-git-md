This thread fixes a bug in `git stash` where autostashing fails to correctly handle staged index entries when `stash.index=true` is set, particularly during merge operations. The issue stems from a race condition in the subprocess-based logic (`git diff-tree` and `git apply`), which the series replaces with in-core merging via `merge-ort.c` to ensure staged entries are properly preserved.

D. Ben Knoble posted v2 of the four-patch series, which is now technically sound and ready for integration. The series includes a cleanup patch (already merged to `master`), a refactor of merge option initialization, a test for conflicted index merges, and the functional fix. Junio C Hamano identified and confirmed the resolution of a critical correctness issue in the functional fix—backwards tree arguments in `merge_incore_nonrecursive()`—which has been corrected. The author plans to include Junio’s test case in the series as a preparatory patch with proper attribution.

Phillip Wood’s review of the test patch (3/4) highlighted gaps in verification logic, prompting the author to propose improvements: replacing `git diff-files` with `git diff-files -p` to compare diff content and adding `git diff-index --cached HEAD` to verify index preservation. The author also confirmed a trivial memory leak fix in Patch 4 will be incorporated without a full re-roll. The series is effectively complete, with v3 expected to be the final version pending these minor adjustments. No further re-roll is anticipated unless Junio requests additional changes.
