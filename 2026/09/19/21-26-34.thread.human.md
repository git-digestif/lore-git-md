This thread is a follow-up to a now-merged series that fixed a race condition in `git stash`'s autostash feature. The original series, authored by D. Ben Knoble, replaced subprocess-based index merging with in-core `merge-ort.c` logic, resolving several issues including a bisectability regression and a NULL-dereference risk. That work is now fully integrated into `master`.

The current four-patch series addresses a latent error-handling bug in `do_apply_stash()` that Junio C Hamano identified after the original series was merged. The bug occurs when `merge_incore_nonrecursive()` returns a hard error (`result.clean = -1`), which was incorrectly treated as a conflict, risking a crash. The fix introduces explicit tri-state logic to handle hard errors separately, preventing NULL-dereference crashes and improving robustness. The series also includes cleanup, mechanical refactoring, and comprehensive tests to validate the fix and prevent regressions. Phillip Wood, who reviewed the original series, is likely to approve this follow-up, as it directly addresses the remaining issue with minimal risk. No open questions remain, and the series is ready for integration.
