This thread is a bugfix series for `git stash` that resolves a race condition in autostashing when `stash.index=true` is set, particularly during merge operations. The issue caused staged index entries to be incorrectly preserved due to subprocess-based merging logic (`git diff-tree` and `git apply`). The series, now in its final **v3** iteration, replaces this with in-core merging via `merge-ort.c`, ensuring proper index state preservation and eliminating the risk of SIGSEGV on conflicted merges.

D. Ben Knoble authored the series, which consists of five patches: (1) a cleanup (already merged to `master`), (2) a refactor of merge option initialization, (3) a new test for conflicted index merges, (4) expanded test assertions to verify worktree and index state, and (5) the functional fix. The critical correctness issue—backwards tree arguments in `merge_incore_nonrecursive()`—was identified by Junio C Hamano and resolved by swapping `(head, merge, merge_base)` to `(merge_base, merge, head)`. Junio and Phillip Wood endorsed the fix, and the test case (included as a preparatory patch) confirms its correctness. Phillip also highlighted a memory leak in the original fix, which was addressed by adding `merge_finalize(&o, &result)` before returning on conflict.

The series is technically complete and ready for integration. All feedback has been addressed, including test robustness improvements (using `git diff-files -p` and `git diff-index --cached HEAD` for verification) and the inclusion of Junio’s test case with proper attribution. Phillip Wood proposed an expanded test case for content-level merge conflicts, which the author accepted and plans to include after minor adjustments. The broader argument-order standardization between `merge_ort_nonrecursive()` and `merge_incore_nonrecursive()` remains a separate, future cleanup task, as Junio explicitly endorsed keeping it out of scope. No further revisions are expected.
