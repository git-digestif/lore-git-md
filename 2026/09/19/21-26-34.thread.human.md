This thread began as a five-patch series from D. Ben Knoble to fix a bug in `git stash` where autostashing failed to correctly handle staged index entries when `stash.index=true` was set. The bug, which could cause incorrect index state preservation or even a SIGSEGV during merge operations, was rooted in a race condition in the subprocess-based logic (`git diff-tree` and `git apply`). The series replaced this with in-core merging via `merge-ort.c`, ensuring staged entries are properly preserved. After four iterations and extensive review, the series was fully integrated into `master`.

The final version (v4) resolved all prior blockers: a test breakage in `t5520-pull.sh` (fixed by Thomas Bachem’s patch to pin reflog expiration in the test setup), a verbosity regression (fixed by preserving the original verbosity setting), a bisectability regression (fixed by restructuring tests), and a NULL-dereference risk (fixed by checking `result.clean` before dereferencing `result.tree`). The only remaining issue is a latent error-handling bug in `do_apply_stash()`, where hard errors (`result.clean = -1`) are incorrectly treated as conflicts (`result.clean = 0`), risking a NULL-dereference crash. Junio identified this in his final review, and D. Ben Knoble has agreed to submit a follow-up patch with a three-way conditional to handle hard errors, conflicts, and the happy path separately. All other aspects of the series are now stable and merged.
