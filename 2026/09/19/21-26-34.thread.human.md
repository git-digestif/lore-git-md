This thread is about a bugfix series for `git stash` that addresses a race condition in autostashing staged index entries during merges. The series, now at v3, replaces subprocess-based logic with in-core merging via `merge-ort.c` to ensure staged entries are preserved correctly. While the technical feedback on the series itself is resolved, two critical issues have emerged:

First, the series causes a test breakage in `t5520-pull.sh` when merged into Junio’s `seen` branch. The author has traced this to an interaction with the `tb/rerere-lock-grace` topic, specifically commit `2d1fa0323f`, which introduces auto-maintenance during rebase operations. This commit appears to disrupt the stash series’ assumptions about index or ref consistency, even though the test doesn’t involve stashing. The root cause is still under investigation, but the failure suggests the stash series inadvertently alters rebase’s fork-point detection logic.

Second, a newly identified NULL-dereference risk in Patch 5/5 could cause crashes in edge cases, such as missing objects. The author has confirmed the issue and plans to fix it by checking `result.clean` before dereferencing `result.tree`. Additionally, a verbosity regression in Patch 5 was resolved by preserving the original verbosity setting for the worktree merge.

The series is currently ejected from Junio’s `seen` branch pending resolution of these issues. Patch 1 (a cleanup) is already merged to `master`. Key participants include D. Ben Knoble (author), Junio C Hamano (maintainer), Phillip Wood (reviewer), and Thomas Bachem (author of the conflicting `rerere-lock-grace` topic). The next steps involve coordinating with Thomas Bachem to address the test breakage and implementing the NULL-dereference fix. Until then, the series remains on hold.
