This is a surface-level review of the first patch in Vsevolod Myalitsyn’s three-part v4 series that refactors Git’s advice subsystem to support scope hints. SZEDER Gábor points out a simple but critical build-breaking error: an opening brace at the end of the `if` condition line is not matched by a closing brace in the subsequent block, causing compilation to fail. The patch is otherwise a straightforward refactoring—passing the entire `advice_setting` struct to `vadvise()` instead of separate arguments—but this unbalanced brace makes it unbuildable in its current form.

The review is brief and mechanical, focusing solely on the syntax error. There is no engagement with the patch’s design or motivation, and no discussion of whether the refactoring itself is sound. The feedback is actionable and should be easy to address in a v5, but until the brace is fixed the patch cannot proceed. This is a reminder that even small, uncontroversial patches must pass basic build tests before submission.
