This is a test modernization patch, part of the ongoing community effort to update Git’s shell-based test scripts. This is the first patch in a three-part v2 series (1/3) revising `t/t4001-diff-rename.sh` to use modern test syntax and structural improvements, as mandated by Junio C Hamano’s review of v1.

The patch replaces the old `setup` test with a helper function (`initial_setup`) and merges its steps directly into the main test bodies, eliminating redundant `echo` commands and here-documents. It also adopts modern naming conventions (`actual`/`expect` instead of `current`/`expected`) and replaces multi-line `test_expect_success` blocks with single-quoted strings and proper indentation. The changes are purely stylistic—no functional behavior is altered—but they make the test more concise (26 lines removed) and easier to read.

The structural improvements align with Git’s documented test best practices, particularly around logical grouping of related steps. Junio’s feedback on v1 explicitly called for these deeper changes, not just mechanical modernization, and this patch appears to address those concerns. The patch is small, focused, and well-motivated, making it a strong candidate for acceptance if the rest of the series holds up. No new edge cases or functional issues are introduced.
