Peff notes that the interop suite is rarely run and has hidden gotchas (e.g., building old Git versions), making it impractical for routine testing. His email is **not a call to action**—he explicitly describes the test as a "fun answer" rather than a serious suggestion for the series. The key takeaway is that the `BUG()` flaw was real but untested due to infrastructure limitations, not design oversight. The v9 fix (silently skipping the hook when no report is requested) remains valid, and the series is still on track for `master`.
