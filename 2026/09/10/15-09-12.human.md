This is the second patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s nine-part series refactoring ODB alternates handling during repository creation, part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The patch defers the setup of the object database in `git clone` until after the clone source URI is resolved, addressing two key concerns: forward compatibility with alternate ODB backends that may require object hash information at creation time, and the need to resolve the URI (including any `insteadOf` rewrites) before writing alternates.

The change is minimal—just four lines moved—and uncontroversial. It moves the `create_object_database()` call in `cmd_clone()` to occur after URI resolution and transport setup, ensuring alternates can be written with full context. This also requires deferring `setup_reference()` (which handles alternates, despite its name) until after the ODB is created. The patch preserves existing behavior while preparing for the series’ later consolidation of alternates logic into the ODB creation process.

The patch is a straightforward step toward the series’ goal of simplifying the ODB interface, and it aligns with the broader ODB abstraction effort. No new symbols are introduced, and the change is well-motivated by the commit message. This is a safe, incremental refactoring that should attract little debate.
