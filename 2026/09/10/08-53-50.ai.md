This is the cover letter for v4 of Vsevolod Myalitsin’s three-patch series that adds a `scope_hint` field to Git’s advice settings. The series is now split into three focused patches as Junio requested: (1) API change to pass the full `advice_setting` to `vadvise()`, (2) introduction of the scope-hint mechanism, and (3) assignment of the `ADVICE_SCOPE_GLOBAL` hint to the `defaultBranchName` advice.

The motivation remains unchanged: the current advice message for `defaultBranchName` suggests disabling it with a local config setting, which is ineffective because the advice appears only during `git init`—a one-time event for any given repository. The series now programmatically suggests `--global` for this setting, ensuring the advice suppression applies to future repositories. The cover letter clearly explains the problem, the solution, and the series structure, and it references the earlier discussion that resolved the scope-hint criteria for this specific advice.

No new technical questions are raised here; the cover letter simply restates the rationale and patch organization. The series is ready for review, and the patches themselves are expected to address Junio’s mechanical feedback from v3 (commit-message motivation, `vadvise()` documentation, and indentation). The file touched is still only `advice.c`, and the key symbols introduced are `scope_hint` in the `advice_setting` struct and the reuse of the existing `CONFIG_SCOPE` enum. The translatable string has been rewritten to avoid interpolation, and the helper function `config_scope_name()` constructs the scope flag programmatically.
