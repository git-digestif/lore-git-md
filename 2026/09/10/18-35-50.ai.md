This is a **documentation patch** posted as a tangent by Junio C Hamano in the ongoing thread about Vsevolod Myalitsin’s advice-scope-hint series. The patch clarifies the `<file-option>` placeholder in the `git config` man page’s SYNOPSIS section, explicitly linking it to the scope-limiting options (`--system`, `--global`, `--local`, `--worktree`) and the `--file <filename>` option. Junio notes that the current documentation is ambiguous, with the SYNOPSIS referring to `<file-option>` without defining it, and the body text only hinting at its meaning in passing. The patch adds a short explanatory paragraph early in the document and leaves a `NEEDSWORK` comment about another undefined placeholder, `<display-option>`.

The context for this patch is a discussion between Junio and Jeff King (Peff) about whether the advice-scope-hint mechanism should support `--worktree` or `--system` scopes. Junio’s position is that `--worktree` is more plausible than `--system` (which users often cannot set), but he acknowledges that the `git config` documentation itself does not clearly explain these options, making it harder for users to understand their purpose. This patch is a direct response to that gap, though it is not part of the advice-scope-hint series itself. It is a small, self-contained improvement to the `git-config` man page, with no dependencies on other patches.

The patch is well-motivated and addresses a real documentation deficiency. The commit message is clear, and the change is minimal and focused. There is no controversy here; this is a straightforward cleanup that would likely be merged without debate if posted as a standalone patch. However, it remains a tangent to the main thread, and its inclusion in the discussion does not directly advance the advice-scope-hint series. The next steps for the advice series are still pending: Vsevolod’s proposal to replace the `scope_hint` enum with a boolean `is_global_hint` (to resolve build failures and simplify the design) has not yet received feedback from Junio or Peff. This documentation patch does not interact with that proposal.
