This is a bugfix patch targeting the low-level merge driver infrastructure. It addresses a resource cleanup issue when a long-running external merge driver is interrupted by the user (e.g., via Ctrl+C). The patch ensures temporary files (`.merge_file_*`) are removed even when the merge process is terminated abruptly.

The problem occurs because a SIGINT sent to the external merge driver also propagates to the parent `git merge` process, preventing its cleanup handlers from running. The patch adopts a signal-handling idiom already used in `editor.c`: it detects when the child process died due to a signal (SIGINT or SIGQUIT) and re-raises the signal in the parent after performing cleanup. This ensures temporary files are deleted while preserving the expected interrupt behavior.

The change is confined to `merge-ll.c` and touches only the `ll_ext_merge()` function. It introduces signal chain manipulation (`sigchain_push`/`sigchain_pop`) to temporarily ignore SIGINT/SIGQUIT during child process termination, then conditionally re-raises the signal if the child died from one of these. The patch includes a `Reported-by` trailer crediting Jean Delvare, suggesting this was a real-world issue observed in production.
