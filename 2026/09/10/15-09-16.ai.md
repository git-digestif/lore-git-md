This is patch 6/9 of Patrick Steinhardt’s ODB alternates refactoring series, now in its fifth iteration. The patch completes the consolidation of alternates logic by moving the setup of alternates for non-shared local clones (`git clone --local --no-shared`) into `collect_alternates()`, fixing a latent bug in worktree alternates handling and adding test coverage for the regression.

The core change is mechanical: `copy_alternates()` is renamed to `read_alternates()` and refactored to gather alternates paths into a `strvec` instead of immediately writing them to the ODB. This aligns with the series’ goal of deferring alternates writes until ODB creation. The patch reuses the resolved common directory path from `collect_alternates()` (already computed for shared clones) to handle non-shared local clones, ensuring alternates are correctly resolved for worktrees. The manual alternates handling in `copy_or_link_directory()` is removed, as alternates are now fully handled by `collect_alternates()`.

Two new tests in `t/t5604-clone-reference.sh` verify the bug fix: one ensures alternates are correctly carried over when cloning from a linked worktree, and another confirms relative alternates paths are properly resolved. The patch is uncontroversial and incremental, with no behavior changes beyond the bug fix. The patch is now well-documented and ready for integration, with all substantive feedback addressed in earlier iterations.
