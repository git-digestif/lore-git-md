Vsevolod Myalitsyn clarifies the motivation for assigning `ADVICE_SCOPE_GLOBAL` to `defaultBranchName`: the advice message appears only during `git init`, and suppressing it locally (via `.git/config`) has no effect in future repositories. He acknowledges Peff’s broader point that many advice settings might logically default to `--global` (since users typically want to suppress messages they’ve already seen), but limits the scope of this patch to `defaultBranchName` alone, leaving other settings’ scope hints to future work or maintainer discretion.
