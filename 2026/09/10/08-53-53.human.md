This is the final patch in Vsevolod Myalitsin’s three-part series that adds scope hints to Git’s advice system. It assigns `ADVICE_SCOPE_GLOBAL` to the `defaultBranchName` advice, ensuring the suggested `git config` command uses `--global` rather than a local setting. The motivation is clear: the advice appears only during `git init`, so a local config setting would not suppress it for future repositories. The patch is a single-line change in `advice.c` and directly addresses the problem described in the cover letter.
