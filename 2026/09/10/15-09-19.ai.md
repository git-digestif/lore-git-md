This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 9-patch series refactoring ODB alternates handling, part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The patch removes the `write_alternate` callback and its supporting infrastructure across all ODB backends, completing the series’ goal of consolidating alternates handling into ODB creation.

The patch deletes 117 lines across seven files: `odb.c` (removing `odb_add_to_alternates_file()`), `odb.h` (removing the function declaration), `odb/source-files.c` (removing the "files" backend’s `write_alternate` implementation and its lockfile logic), and the "inmemory", "loose", and "packed" backends (removing their stub `write_alternate` implementations). The `write_alternate` field is also removed from the `odb_source` struct in `odb/source.h`, along with its inline helper function. This is a mechanical cleanup patch with no behavior changes, as all alternates logic has already been moved into the ODB creation process in earlier patches (notably patch 7’s lockfile-based atomic writes and patch 8’s wiring of the alternates `strvec` to `create_object_database()`).

The removal of `odb_add_to_alternates_file()` and its backend-specific implementations is uncontroversial, as the function has no remaining callers in the codebase. The patch aligns with the series’ design goal of making alternates an ODB backend implementation detail, simplifying the interface by eliminating the ability to write alternates after repository creation. The lockfile API adoption in patch 7 resolves the primary security concern from earlier iterations, and the series’ incremental approach (consolidating alternates logic in patches 4-6 before removing the callback) ensures correctness.

No new symbols are introduced, and the patch preserves existing behavior. The series is now complete, with all substantive review feedback addressed and the final patch ready for integration.
