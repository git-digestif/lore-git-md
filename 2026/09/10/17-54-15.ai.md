This is a reply in the ongoing `scope_hint` patch series for Git’s advice subsystem. The author, Vsevolod Myalitsin, responds to Jeff King’s (Peff) question about whether `--system` scope is ever needed for advice settings. Vsevolod proposes simplifying the design by replacing the `scope_hint` enum (`CONFIG_SCOPE_*`) with a single boolean `is_global_hint`, arguing that he cannot identify any realistic scenario where advice should be disabled at the system level. This change would eliminate the build failure under `DEVELOPER=YesPlease` mode (the unhandled `switch` cases in `vadvise()`) and the unmatched brace syntax error in patch 1, while still addressing the original goal of making advice-disablement messages accurate for `--global` settings like `defaultBranchName`. The proposal leaves the door open for future expansion if a use case for `--system` or other scopes emerges, but removes the immediate implementation complexity. Key symbols affected: `advice_setting` struct (replacing `scope_hint` with `is_global_hint`), `vadvise()` logic, and the translatable string construction. The thread remains under review; this reply shifts the discussion toward a simpler, more targeted design.
