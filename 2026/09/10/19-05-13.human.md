This is a reply in the ongoing patch series that adds scope hints to Git’s advice messages. Jeff King (Peff) endorses Vsevolod Myalitsin’s proposal to replace the `scope_hint` enum with a simple boolean `is_global_hint`. Peff agrees that `--system` and other non-global scopes are unlikely to be useful in practice, reinforcing the case for a minimal design that focuses on the immediate goal: accurate scope hints for `--global` settings like `defaultBranchName`. The message is brief but decisive, shifting the series toward a simpler, more targeted implementation. The thread remains under review; Junio has not yet responded to this simplification proposal.
