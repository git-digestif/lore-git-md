This is the fifth patch in the v4 series "MinGW/Windows build and runtime adjustments," a platform compatibility effort targeting the Windows build system. It removes an overly specific linker flag (`-Wl,--pic-executable,-e,...`) that was introduced in 2016 to work around a MinGW limitation but is now redundant: the entry-point symbols (`_mainCRTStartup` for 32-bit, `mainCRTStartup` for 64-bit and ARM64) are already the default in current MinGW toolchains. The change affects only `config.mak.uname`, simplifying the `BASIC_LDFLAGS` definitions for all three MinGW environments (MINGW32, MINGW64, CLANGARM64) by dropping the `-e` argument. No new symbols or behavior changes are introduced; the patch is purely a build-system cleanup. Johannes Sixt is credited with helping refine the change. The patch is part of a larger series that is already cooking in `next` and is positioned as a routine hygiene improvement with no impact on non-Windows platforms.
