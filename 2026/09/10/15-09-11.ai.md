This is patch 1/9 of Patrick Steinhardt’s v5 series refactoring ODB alternates handling during repository creation, part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The patch splits `init_db()` into three focused functions: `create_repository()` (skeleton creation), `create_reference_database()` (refdb setup), and `create_object_database()` (ODB setup). This refactoring eliminates the need for skip flags (`SKIP_REFDB`, `SKIP_ODB`) and the `QUIET` flag, simplifying the interface and deferring ODB/refdb creation to the caller—critical for the series’ goal of consolidating alternates handling into ODB creation.

The patch is mechanical and well-documented, with clear commit messages and updated `setup.h` documentation for the new helpers. It addresses Justin Tobler’s feedback from v3 by clarifying the `reinit_ok` parameter and the purpose of `create_reference_database()` and `create_object_database()`. The changes are uncontroversial and align with the series’ incremental approach, though reviewers may focus on the updated function signatures in `setup.h` and the removal of the `INIT_DB_SKIP_*` flags. No new symbols are introduced beyond the renamed `create_repository()` and the now-public `create_object_database()`.

Key files touched: `builtin/clone.c` (deferring ODB setup until after URI resolution), `builtin/init-db.c` (moving the initialization message into the command), `setup.c` (splitting `init_db()` into three functions), and `setup.h` (updating function signatures and documentation). The patch preserves existing behavior while enabling the deferral of ODB creation, a prerequisite for patches 2/9 and later. The `reinit_ok` pointer is now the only mechanism for conveying whether reinitialization is allowed, simplifying the interface.
