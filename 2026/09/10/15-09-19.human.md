This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 9-patch series refactoring ODB alternates handling, part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The patch removes the `write_alternates()` callback and its supporting infrastructure across all ODB backends, completing the series’ goal of consolidating alternates handling into ODB creation.

The patch deletes 117 lines across seven files: `odb.c` (removing `odb_add_to_alternates_file()`), `odb.h` (removing the function declaration), `odb/source-files.c` (removing the "files" backend’s `write_alternate` implementation and its lockfile logic), and the "inmemory", "loose", and "packed" backends (removing their stub `write_alternate` implementations). The `write_alternate` field is also removed from the `odb_source` struct in `odb/source.h`, along with its inline helper function. This is a mechanical cleanup with no behavior changes, as all alternates logic has already been moved into the ODB creation process in earlier patches (notably patch 7’s lockfile-based atomic writes and patch 8’s wiring of the alternates `strvec` to `create_object_database()`).

The removal is uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ design: alternates are now an ODB backend implementation detail, and the ability to write alternates after repository creation has been eliminated. The patch touches no new files or symbols, and the deleted infrastructure has no remaining callers in the codebase. Reviewers may focus on confirming the mechanical correctness of the removal, but the patch is a straightforward cleanup following the substantive work in earlier patches. The series is now complete and ready for integration, with all substantive review feedback addressed in prior iterations.
