## 2026/09/10

- [2026/09/10/15-06-07 by Michal Koutný <mkoutny@suse.com>] Michal Koutný submitted a patch to merge-ll.c that cleans up `.merge_file_*` temporaries after a Ctrl+C interrupt by pushing SIGINT and SIGQUIT onto the sigchain while the external merge driver runs, then re-raising the signal if the child process died from it so cleanup executes before git-merge exits.
