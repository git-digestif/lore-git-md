This is the third and final patch in Vsevolod Myalitsyn’s v4 series adding a `scope_hint` field to Git’s advice settings. It applies the mechanism introduced in the earlier patches by assigning `ADVICE_SCOPE_GLOBAL` to the `defaultBranchName` advice. The change is a single-line update to `advice.c` that ensures the advice message for `defaultBranchName` suggests disabling it with `--global` rather than a local config setting, which is ineffective because the advice appears only during `git init`—a one-time event per repository.

The patch is narrowly scoped and directly addresses the problem identified in the cover letter: users who disable the advice locally will still see it again when initializing new repositories. The rationale for using `--global` here is clear and uncontroversial, and the patch follows the series’ established pattern of reusing the existing `CONFIG_SCOPE` enum. No new technical questions or edge cases are introduced; this is purely an application of the infrastructure added in the first two patches.

The patch is well-motivated and aligns with the series’ goal of making advice messages more accurate. It touches only `advice.c` and updates the `advice_setting` struct for `ADVICE_DEFAULT_BRANCH_NAME` to include the `CONFIG_SCOPE_GLOBAL` hint. The change is minimal and unlikely to introduce regressions, though it does depend on the earlier patches in the series for context.
