This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing advice-subsystem thread. Junio C Hamano responds to Jeff King’s concern about whether the turn-off instructions shown by `advise_if_enabled()` would feel visually intrusive. Junio’s reply is lighthearted but substantive: he suggests that if users find the instructions unnecessary, they can simply turn the advice off—so the instructions serve their own purpose. More seriously, he argues that the choice between `advise()` (unconditional) and `advise_if_enabled()` (with turn-off instructions) should be made case-by-case, balancing the value of the advice against the cost of the extra output. The thread remains open on this design question, but Junio’s comment signals that he expects the project to settle into a consistent pattern over time. No code changes are proposed here; this is purely a design-discussion reply.
