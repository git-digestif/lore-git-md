This is a surface-level review from Junio on the second patch of Vsevolod Myalitsyn’s three-patch v4 series. The patch introduces a `scope_hint` field to Git’s advice settings, allowing advice messages to suggest the correct config scope (e.g., `--global`) when telling users how to disable them.

Junio’s feedback is mechanical: the `switch` statement in `vadvise()` does not handle all values of the `CONFIG_SCOPE` enum (`WORKTREE`, `COMMAND`, and `SUBMODULE`), causing the build to fail under `DEVELOPER=YesPlease`. He suggests adding a `default` case that calls `BUG()` to catch any unexpected scope values. The fix is straightforward and does not affect the patch’s core functionality—it’s purely about build correctness.

The series as a whole remains under review, with no substantive objections raised since the v3 feedback was addressed. This is a minor hiccup in an otherwise well-structured refactoring. If the author sends a v5 with this fix, the patch will likely be ready for Junio to pick up. The broader goal—making advice messages more accurate—is uncontroversial and aligns with Git’s usability improvements.
