This is a bugfix patch targeting the refs/files backend. The patch avoids taking a lock on `packed-refs` when deleting root refs (like `HEAD` or `refs/heads/main`), since root refs cannot be packed and the lock is therefore unnecessary. The motivation is to prevent spurious errors during post-commit cleanup in linked worktrees where the shared metadata directory is read-only.
