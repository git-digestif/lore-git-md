This thread is about a targeted bugfix for Git’s low-level merge driver infrastructure. Michal Koutný reports and fixes a resource cleanup issue where temporary files (`.merge_file_*`) are left behind when a long-running external merge driver is interrupted by the user (e.g., via Ctrl+C). The problem arises because the SIGINT sent to the child process also propagates to the parent `git merge` process, preventing its cleanup handlers from running.

The fix, confined to `merge-ll.c` in the `ll_ext_merge()` function, adopts a signal-handling pattern already used in `editor.c`: it temporarily ignores SIGINT/SIGQUIT during child process termination, performs cleanup, and then re-raises the signal if the child died from one of these. The patch is minimal, well-motivated, and follows established codebase practices. It includes a `Reported-by` trailer crediting Jean Delvare, indicating this was a real-world issue observed in production.
