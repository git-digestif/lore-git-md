This is patch 2/9 of Patrick Steinhardt’s 9-patch series refactoring ODB alternates handling during repository creation, part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The patch defers the `create_object_database()` call in `cmd_clone()` until after URI resolution and transport setup, enabling alternates to be written with full context and addressing forward compatibility with alternate ODB backends that may require object hash information at creation time.

The change is minimal (4 insertions, 4 deletions) and uncontroversial: `create_object_database()` is moved to occur after the clone source URI is resolved and the transport is initialized, and `setup_reference()` (which handles alternates setup) is now called immediately after ODB creation. This deferral aligns with the series’ goal of consolidating alternates logic into the ODB creation process and eliminates the need for the ODB to be created before the clone source is fully determined. The patch preserves existing behavior while preparing for patch 7/9’s lockfile-based alternates writing and patch 9/9’s removal of the `write_alternates()` callback.

The patch touches only `builtin/clone.c` and introduces no new symbols or user-visible changes. Karthik Nayak’s surface-level review of this patch in v4 confirmed the mechanical correctness of the change but did not engage with the technical rationale or edge cases. The deferral is a direct follow-up to patch 1/9’s refactoring of `init_db()` into `create_repository()` and `create_object_database()`, and it sets the stage for patches 4-6/9’s consolidation of alternates logic into `collect_alternates()`. No substantive feedback or concerns have been raised about this patch, and it is a straightforward step toward the series’ broader goal of simplifying the ODB interface.
