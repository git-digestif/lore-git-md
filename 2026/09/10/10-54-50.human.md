This is patch 5/9 in Patrick Steinhardt’s nine-part ODB alternates refactoring series, now receiving a surface-level review from Karthik Nayak. The patch consolidates the setup of alternates for shared local clones (`git clone --shared`) into the `collect_alternates()` function, moving it out of `clone_local()`. This is a mechanical refactoring that preserves existing behavior while preparing for the eventual removal of the `write_alternates()` callback in patch 9.

Karthik’s feedback is minor and focused on readability: he questions whether the `is_local` check should also guard against `option_shared`, noting that the patch currently initializes `commondir` even when `option_shared` is false. His observation is technically correct but does not affect the patch’s logic—the `commondir` buffer is properly released regardless of whether `option_shared` is set, and the `strvec_pushf()` call is correctly guarded by `option_shared`. The current structure aligns with the series’ incremental approach, where non-shared local clones (`git clone --local --no-shared`) are handled in the next patch (6/9).

The patch itself is uncontroversial and addresses a critical correctness issue raised in v3: the `option_shared` guard ensures alternates are only added for shared local clones, preventing the incorrect addition of alternates for non-shared clones. The change is minimal (14 insertions, 9 deletions) and does not introduce new symbols or user-visible behavior. Karthik’s feedback is unlikely to require significant changes, though Patrick may adjust the conditional structure for clarity in a future iteration.

This patch is part of the broader ODB abstraction effort, which aims to simplify the ODB interface by making alternates an implementation detail of the backend. The consolidation of alternates logic into `collect_alternates()` is a necessary step toward that goal, and the patch’s incremental approach (handling shared clones first, then non-shared ones in patch 6) has been confirmed as correct by Justin Tobler. No substantive objections remain, and the series appears ready for Junio’s queueing.
