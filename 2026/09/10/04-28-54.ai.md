This is a refactoring patch in the advice subsystem, posted by Junio C Hamano as a follow-up to the ongoing `defaultBranchName` advice documentation fix thread. It mechanically converts existing `if (advice_enabled(ADVICE_FOO)) advise(...);` patterns to the newer `advise_if_enabled(ADVICE_FOO, ...)` helper across seven files, reducing boilerplate while preserving behavior. The patch includes a new Coccinelle rule (`tools/coccinelle/advice.cocci`) that automates most of the conversion, though manual edits were needed afterward to fix line wrapping and formatting.

The patch touches `advice.c`, `branch.c`, `builtin/am.c`, `builtin/checkout.c`, `builtin/submodule--helper.c`, `sequencer.c`, and the new Coccinelle rule. No new symbols are introduced or renamed, and there are no user-visible behavior changes—this is purely an internal cleanup. The conversion aligns with the broader advice subsystem design, where `advise_if_enabled()` is intended for cases where turn-off instructions are explicitly desired, while the older pattern (checking `advice_enabled()` before `advise()`) avoids unnecessary work when advice is disabled. Jeff King and Junio previously clarified this distinction in the thread, and this patch unifies existing callers toward the newer helper without altering the design.

The patch is low-risk and self-contained, with no test coverage added. It does not block the core `defaultBranchName` fix, which is effectively ready for merging, but it complements the effort by tidying up the codebase. The manual edits suggest a follow-up cleanup might be needed for formatting, though this is not a blocker. The patch is under review and awaits feedback on the mechanical conversion and the new Coccinelle rule.
