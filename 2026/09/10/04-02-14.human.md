This is a **substantive review** of the `receive-report` hook series, now cooking in `next`. Jeff King (Peff) demonstrates how to trigger the `BUG()` flaw that was fixed in v9 by writing a minimal interop test that pushes from an older Git client (v1.0.0) to a server running the v8 patches. The test succeeds despite the server-side crash because the client does not expect a status report, exposing the gap in the test suite.

Peff’s email is not a call to action—he explicitly calls the interop suite “more trouble than it’s worth” and presents the test as a “fun answer” rather than a serious patch. The value here is diagnostic: it confirms Junio’s explanation that the test coverage gap is real and hard to close without custom tooling. No new objections are raised; the series remains on track for `master`.
