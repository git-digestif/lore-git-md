This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s nine-patch series refactoring ODB alternates handling during repository creation, part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The patch refactors the `--reference` and `--reference-if-able` logic in `git clone` to gather alternates paths into a `strvec` instead of immediately writing them to the object database. This is a mechanical intermediate step that prepares for later patches (5-6/9) consolidating all alternates logic into a single code path and patch 9/9’s removal of the `write_alternates()` callback.

The patch renames `setup_reference()` to `collect_alternates()` to clarify its new role: collecting alternates paths rather than setting them up. It introduces `struct collect_alternates_data` (replacing the old `add_one_reference` callback data) to track whether an alternate is required or optional and to hold the `strvec` of paths. The `collect_one_alternate()` function (renamed from `add_one_reference`) now pushes valid alternates paths into the `strvec` instead of writing them directly to the ODB via `odb_add_to_alternates_file()`. The `cmd_clone()` function is updated to call `collect_alternates()` and then manually write each alternate to the ODB in a loop, preserving existing behavior while enabling the consolidation of alternates logic in later patches.

The changes are minimal (28 insertions, 16 deletions) and touch only `builtin/clone.c`. No new symbols are introduced beyond the renamed `collect_alternates()`, `collect_one_alternate()`, and `struct collect_alternates_data`. The patch addresses Karthik Nayak’s surface-level review feedback from v4 by renaming `add_one_alternate_data` to `collect_alternates_data` and `add_one_alternate` to `collect_one_alternate` for clarity, and by converting the `required` field from `int` to `bool`. The conditional structure remains unchanged, as Karthik acknowledged these adjustments were inconsequential. The patch is uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ incremental approach, though it may attract scrutiny in later patches regarding error handling for invalid alternates paths.
