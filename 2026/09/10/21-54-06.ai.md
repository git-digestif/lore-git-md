This is the first patch in the v10 series introducing the `receive-report` hook. It is a **documentation-only update** that adds an eight-line `PROC-RECEIVE HOOK` section to `git-receive-pack.adoc`, explaining the purpose of the `proc-receive` hook and linking to `githooks(5)` for further details. The patch plugs a gap in the existing documentation, which previously omitted the `proc-receive` hook despite its role in the push process. The change is straightforward and aligns with the surrounding hook documentation style. A `Helped-by` credit to Patrick Steinhardt is included, reflecting his prior feedback on the series. No code or test changes are involved, and the patch is uncontroversial. It is part of a larger effort to introduce the `receive-report` hook, which is now queued in `next` and addresses GitLab’s need for server-side status filtering in MVCC workflows.
