This is a **substantive review reply** from Jeff King (Peff) in the ongoing thread about Vsevolod Myalitsyn’s `scope_hint` patch series for Git’s advice subsystem. The series aims to make advice-disablement messages more accurate by including the correct config scope (e.g., `--global`).

Peff responds to Junio’s earlier review comment about a build failure under `DEVELOPER=YesPlease` mode, where the `switch` statement in `vadvise()` does not handle all values of the `CONFIG_SCOPE` enum. While Junio suggested adding a `BUG()` for unhandled cases, Peff proposes a simpler alternative: treat *all* unhandled scopes (including `LOCAL`, `WORKTREE`, `COMMAND`, and `SUBMODULE`) as equivalent to the default (unadorned `git config` command). His rationale is that these scopes are either irrelevant to advice settings or would never be passed in practice, making a `BUG()` unnecessarily strict. He also notes that using `config_scope_name()` to avoid the `switch` entirely would require more string manipulation and assumes a 1:1 mapping between scope names and CLI flags.

Peff questions the practical need for `--system` scope in advice settings, observing that `--global` is only justified for advice like `defaultBranchName` (which appears during `git init` and thus cannot be disabled via local config). However, he acknowledges that further debate may not be productive, suggesting the series is already well-motivated and that the remaining issues are minor. The discussion remains focused on implementation details rather than the core design, which has already been accepted. The next step is likely a v5 patch addressing the build failure, either via Peff’s `default:` case or Junio’s `BUG()` approach.
