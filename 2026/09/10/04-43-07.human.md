This is a **bugfix-adjacent feature** in the ongoing `advice` subsystem cleanup. Vsevolod Myalitsin replies to Jeff King’s review of the v3 patch, confirming he will reuse Git’s existing `CONFIG_SCOPE` enum and treat `CONFIG_SCOPE_UNKNOWN` as “use the default location” (currently local scope). The key clarification here is the motivation for recommending `--global` for `advice.defaultBranchName`: after a repository is initialized, the advice to disable `defaultBranchName` will never trigger again in that repository, so a local config setting is effectively useless. A global setting, by contrast, prevents the advice from reappearing in future repositories. This rationale is specific to `defaultBranchName` and does not necessarily generalize to other advice messages, but it resolves the semantic ambiguity Peff raised about when to recommend `--global` versus `--local`.
