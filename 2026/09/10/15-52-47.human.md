This is a review reply from Jeff King (Peff) in the ongoing discussion about Vsevolod Myalitsin’s patch series adding a `scope_hint` field to Git’s advice settings. The series aims to make advice-disablement messages more accurate by including the correct config scope (e.g., `--global`).

Peff responds to Junio’s earlier review identifying a build failure under `DEVELOPER=YesPlease` mode, where the `switch` statement in `vadvise()` doesn’t handle all values of the `CONFIG_SCOPE` enum. While Junio suggested adding a `BUG()` for unhandled cases, Peff proposes a simpler approach: treat *all* unhandled scopes (including `LOCAL`, `WORKTREE`, `COMMAND`, and `SUBMODULE`) as equivalent to the default (local) scope, avoiding a `BUG()` call. His rationale is that these scopes are unlikely to be used for advice settings, and the current implementation already silently ignores `LOCAL` and `UNKNOWN`.

Peff also questions the practical need for `--system` scope in advice messages, noting that `--global` is only justified for settings like `defaultBranchName` (which only applies during `git init`). However, he acknowledges the discussion may be over-optimizing at this point and suggests the series is ready for integration once the build issue is resolved. The feedback remains **surface-level**, focusing on code hygiene rather than design. The series is well-motivated and nearly ready; this is a minor polish step before merging.
