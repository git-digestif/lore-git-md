This is the third patch in a three-patch series that defers auto maintenance until the end of sequencer-driven operations (`git rebase`, `git cherry-pick`, `git revert`). The series is now under review after Junio signaled intent to queue it, but a substantive design objection from Patrick Steinhardt remains unresolved.

Thomas Bachem replies to Phillip Wood’s review of the test coverage in `t/t3418-rebase-continue.sh`. Phillip had pointed out that the test scenario—rebasing with a failing exec command—does not actually trigger a conflict, so the test might not verify that auto maintenance is suppressed during intermediate commits. Thomas clarifies that the test *does* trigger a conflict: both the topic and main branches add the same file (`F2`), causing the pick to conflict and the rebase to stop. The `--continue` then spawns `git commit` for the resolution, which is what the test checks for in `mid.txt`. Only after that does the exec command fail, stopping the rebase a second time. Thomas agrees to add a comment to the test to make this sequence clearer.

The exchange is a minor test hygiene clarification, not a design dispute. The series as a whole remains technically sound and well-motivated, but Patrick’s earlier objection about where to trigger maintenance (in `pick_commits()` or in the built-in commands) still needs resolution before merging. This reply does not address that larger question.
