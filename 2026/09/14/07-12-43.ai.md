This is the first patch in the v4 series that defers auto maintenance until sequencer completion, now updated in response to Patrick Steinhardt’s **surface-level review** of the commit message and header comment. The patch itself remains unchanged: it introduces `git_config_append_parameter()`, a reusable helper for building `GIT_CONFIG_PARAMETERS` strings, and refactors the existing `git_config_push_split_parameter()` to use it. What’s new in this reply is Thomas’s pushback on Patrick’s **substantive design critique** from the second patch, which argued that the sequencer *does* have a single exit point (`pick_commits()`) where maintenance could be triggered consistently.

Thomas clarifies that the single-commit paths for `git cherry-pick` and `git revert` (`cherry-pick <commit>`, `revert <commit>`, and their `--continue`/`--skip` variants) bypass `pick_commits()` entirely, exiting early through `continue_single_pick()` or `skip_single_pick()`. Only `git rebase` reliably reaches `pick_commits()`. This means Patrick’s proposed alternative—triggering maintenance in `pick_commits()` and on abort—would miss the single-pick cases unless the sequencer also hooks the three early-return paths. Thomas offers to implement either approach (sequencer hooks or built-in delegation) but asks Patrick to choose. The outcome will determine whether the series requires a v5 rewrite or can proceed as-is. The patch’s technical foundation (the config helper) is uncontroversial and ready.
