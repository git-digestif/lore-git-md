This thread is about a bugfix series that addresses a race condition in `git repack -d` where concurrent pushes could cause data loss by deleting packs whose objects weren’t safely copied elsewhere. The series, now at version 3 (patches 1/5–5/5), replaces the `--honor-pack-keep` mechanism with a snapshot-based approach to manage `.keep` files consistently, while also fixing a latent cruft-pack walk bug that would become critical once the old mechanism is removed.

The patches are technically ready for integration—Junio C Hamano has acked patch 2/5 (the cruft-pack fix), and the remaining patches are well-tested and address clear issues: a traversal logic bug (patch 1/5), a performance bottleneck (patch 3/5), a scalability enabler (patch 4/5), and the final race fix (patch 5/5). However, the series is blocked on Patrick Steinhardt’s `ps/odb-files-alternates` topic, which introduces the object directory list that patch 5/5 depends on. Junio prefers not to carry a temporary merge resolution in his `seen` branch while the ODB topic is still evolving, so the repack series will wait until `ps/odb-files-alternates` graduates to `next` or `master`. The author, Qin ShiCheng, plans to send a v4 rebased onto a synthetic merge once that happens.

The only recent update is a minor clarification about code formatting in the PR description, which doesn’t affect the patches themselves. No new technical feedback or objections have emerged, and the series remains in a holding pattern until its dependency stabilizes. Key reviewers like Patrick Steinhardt and Taylor Blau haven’t weighed in yet, but the patches are unlikely to face major pushback given their focused fixes and thorough test coverage.
