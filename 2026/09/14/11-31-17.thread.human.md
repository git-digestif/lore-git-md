This thread is about a v3 patch series from Qin ShiCheng (qeesung) that fixes a race condition in `git repack -d` where concurrent pushes could cause data loss by deleting packs whose objects weren’t safely copied elsewhere. The series replaces the `--honor-pack-keep` mechanism with a snapshot-based approach to manage `.keep` files consistently, and also fixes a latent cruft-pack walk bug that would become critical once the old mechanism is removed.

The series is now at v3, with five patches (the original first patch was withdrawn in favor of Justin Tobler’s ODB-focused series, which will handle `.keep` file ownership more cleanly). Junio C Hamano has acked patch 2/5—the cruft-pack walk fix—after the v3 revision addressed his layering concerns. The remaining patches (1/5, 3/5, 4/5, and 5/5) are well-motivated, production-tested, and include thorough test coverage, but they’re currently blocked from integration. Junio has identified a textual conflict with Patrick Steinhardt’s `ps/odb-files-alternates` topic, which is still under review. While the resolution is mechanically correct, Junio prefers to wait until the ODB topic stabilizes rather than carrying a temporary merge fix in `seen`. The series will likely proceed once `ps/odb-files-alternates` graduates to `next` or `master`, at which point a clean rebase can be done.

Key participants include qeesung (the author), Justin Tobler (who proposed an alternative architectural direction for `.keep` file handling), and Junio (who has acked the critical fix but is holding the series for merge conflicts). The series is effectively ready for integration once the ODB topic is resolved, with no outstanding technical objections.
