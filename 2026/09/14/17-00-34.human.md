This is a short follow-up from Jeff King (Peff) in the ongoing discussion about how to handle scope hints for Git’s advice messages. Peff agrees with Junio’s suggestion that the hint could *always* recommend `--global` without needing to mark individual advice settings for their intended scope. He argues that while it’s *possible* some advice settings might be better set per-repository (using `--local`), it’s unlikely in practice, and any exceptions could be handled later by introducing a scope hint for those specific cases. This aligns with the minimalist approach Junio proposed, which would eliminate the need for the `is_global_hint` field entirely and simplify the patch series further.

The exchange leaves the design direction unresolved but signals growing consensus toward a uniform `--global` hint. The series remains under review, and the next step will likely be for Vsevolod Myalitsin to either revise the patch to adopt this simpler approach or continue advocating for the boolean `is_global_hint` mechanism, which Peff previously endorsed. For now, the thread is converging on a design that prioritizes simplicity and aligns with the original intent of `advice.*` settings.
