This is the second patch in a six-patch **bugfix series** addressing a data-loss race in `git repack -d` when concurrent pushes are active. The series targets the repack machinery and object database (ODB) subsystems, with this patch laying groundwork for the full fix in patch 6/6.

The patch corrects a behavioral inconsistency in `git pack-objects --stdin-packs=follow`. Currently, packs named with `--keep-pack` are treated as closed under reachability (like `^` packs), which stops traversal at their objects. This is incorrect: `--keep-pack` only means "do not repack this pack," not "this pack is self-contained." When a kept pack holds a commit but not its tree or blob, the traversal fails to rescue those objects, breaking closure and causing bitmap-writing failures.

The fix modifies `add_extra_kept_packs()` to mark `--keep-pack` packs as "kept-open" (like `!` packs) when in follow mode, allowing the traversal to proceed through them. This aligns with how on-disk `.keep` packs have always behaved and prepares for patch 6/6, where `repack` will stop passing `--honor-pack-keep` and instead explicitly list `.keep` packs via `--keep-pack`. The change is minimal: a new `stdin_packs` parameter to `add_extra_kept_packs()` and a conditional flag assignment in `builtin/pack-objects.c`.

The test in `t/t5331-pack-objects-stdin.sh` reproduces the bug by creating a scenario where a kept pack (`B_ONLY`) contains only a commit, while its tree and blob reside in an excluded pack (`B`). Without the fix, the tree and blob are omitted from the output pack; with the fix, they are correctly included. The test is thorough and validates the exact edge case described in the commit message.
