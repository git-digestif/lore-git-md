This is a documentation patch series, part of the ongoing effort to standardize Git’s man pages on the synopsis-style AsciiDoc format. The v2 submission drops the controversial lint-script change from v1 and resubmits only the two mechanical backtick-quoting conversions for `git-pack-refs.adoc` and `git-refs.adoc` (plus the included `pack-refs-options.adoc`). The goal is to eliminate visual formatting inconsistencies—some commands appear bold in man pages and colored in HTML while others do not—by ensuring every command and option reference is consistently backtick-quoted.

The changes are confined to the `Documentation/` directory and introduce no functional differences in Git itself; they affect only the rendered appearance of the man pages and HTML output. Patch 1 converts the `git-pack-refs` synopsis block from `[verse]` to `[synopsis]`, removes single-quote formatting around the command name, backtick-quotes all option terms in the OPTIONS section, and updates the included `pack-refs-options.adoc` to backtick-quote configuration keys. Patch 2 mechanically backtick-quotes all subcommand entries in `git-refs.adoc` (`list`, `exists`, `optimize`, `create`, `delete`, `update`, and `rename`) and replaces a literal `<branch>` placeholder with an italicized version.

The v1 review identified a substantive objection to the lint-script change (patch 1 in v1), which Todd has now withdrawn. The remaining two patches are uncontested and align with Jean-Noël Avila’s ongoing documentation effort. No further revisions are expected; the series is ready for integration once Junio picks it up. Files touched are `Documentation/git-pack-refs.adoc`, `Documentation/git-refs.adoc`, and `Documentation/technical/pack-refs-options.adoc`.
