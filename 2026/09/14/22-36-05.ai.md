This is a **follow-up cleanup patch** for the `receive-report` hook series, now in its v10 iteration and queued in `next`. Junio C Hamano proposes a small mechanical improvement to the `override_cmds_error()` helper in `builtin/receive-pack.c`.

The patch removes a redundant null-check before `FREE_AND_NULL(cmd->error_string_owned)`. The macro is already safe to call on `NULL`, so the explicit check is unnecessary. The change is purely stylistic and does not affect the hook’s behavior. It also serves as a model for updating `tools/coccinelle/free.cocci` to enforce this pattern across the codebase, though the Coccinelle update itself is not included in this diff.

Karthik Nayak had previously offered to submit the Coccinelle patch separately, but Junio’s email includes the cleanup directly, suggesting it will be merged alongside the main series. The patch is uncontroversial and resolves a minor technical debt introduced in the memory-leak fix for the `receive-report` hook. The series remains on track for graduation to `master`.
