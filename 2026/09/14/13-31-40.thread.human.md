This thread is about a bug in `git status --ignored` where pathspecs like `ba` incorrectly match ignored directories whose names *contain* the pathspec as a substring (e.g., `bar/`). The issue was introduced in 2020 by an optimization that skipped pathspec matching for excluded directories. René Scharfe posted a minimal fix (v1) that removes the problematic optimization, restoring exact-match behavior.

The patch is straightforward: it modifies `dir.c` to ensure pathspec matching is applied even for excluded directories with nested repositories, and includes a new test case in `t/t7061-wtstatus-ignore.sh` to prevent regression. René notes the optimization might be revisited later but prioritizes correctness for now. No maintainer feedback has arrived yet, but the fix is technically sound and aligns with Git’s emphasis on correctness. The thread is still under review, with no indication of integration status. Key participants are René (author) and Sean Whitton (reporter).
