This is the third and final patch in Tyler Cipriani’s v6 series fixing `--force-if-includes` in `git push`. It addresses the long-standing bug where fast-forward pushes of non-branch refs (tags, detached HEADs, or branches with expired reflogs) were incorrectly rejected even when no force was needed. The patch defers the reflog reachability check until after fast-forward determination, ensuring that only actual force-pushes are subject to `--force-if-includes` safety checks.

The core change is in `remote.c`: a new `needs_force_reject_reason` variable remembers whether the reflog check failed, but the rejection is only applied if fast-forward rules also reject the push. This preserves existing behavior for force-pushes while allowing fast-forwards, deletions, and new refs to proceed unblocked. The patch adds 27 lines of test coverage in `t/t5533-push-cas.sh` for edge cases like expired reflogs and tag-based fast-forwards.

This patch is uncontroversial and technically complete. All prior review feedback (from Patrick Steinhardt, Junio C Hamano, and D. Ben Knoble) has been addressed in earlier iterations, and the v6 changes are limited to variable renaming and documentation tweaks. The series is now ready for merge unless new issues are raised.
