This is a bugfix series targeting the `--force-if-includes` safety mechanism in `git push`. The v6 reroll addresses the last remaining readability concerns from the v5 review while keeping the technical solution intact. The series now consists of three patches that fix two critical flaws: (1) the feature incorrectly checks the reflog of the local branch named after the remote destination rather than the branch actually being pushed, and (2) it unnecessarily rejects fast-forward pushes of non-branch refs (tags, detached HEADs) even when no force is needed.

The core logic in `remote.c` now consults the reflog of the pushed ref (`ref->peer_ref->name`) and introduces a `needs_force_reject_reason` variable (renamed from `deferred_reject_reason` in v5) to defer reflog checks until after fast-forward determination. This ensures fast-forward pushes are exempt from `--force-if-includes` checks, resolving an existing bug where workflows like `git push origin <TAG>:hotfix` were broken. The series also adds a new `ref->unverifiable` flag and `advice.forceIfIncludesDetachedHead` config knob to provide actionable advice for rejected non-branch pushes.

Key files touched include `remote.c` (core logic), `advice.c`/`advice.h` (new advice message), and `t/t5533-push-cas.sh` (108 lines of new test coverage). The v6 changes are minor: a variable rename for clarity, typo fixes in commit messages, and improved documentation wording. All prior review feedback from Patrick Steinhardt, Junio C Hamano, and D. Ben Knoble is resolved. The series is based on `maint` and has no external dependencies.

The implementation looks solid: it updates all transport layers (direct pushes, transport helpers, and send-pack), maintains backwards compatibility for `--force-with-lease`, and includes thorough test coverage for edge cases like differently named branches, detached HEADs, and tags. The only remaining open question from earlier iterations—the policy for detached HEAD pushes—has been settled in favor of rejection (with configurable advice), which aligns with the feature’s safety guarantees. The series is ready for final review and likely merge.
