- **Bug fixes**:
  - 4GB object commit limit resolved.
  - Stack-smashing crash in parallel checkouts fixed.
  - Vim integration bug (omitting first line of files) fixed.
- **Artifacts**: SHA-256 checksums provided for installers, portable builds, and MinGit variants (32/64-bit, ARM64).
