This is the third and final patch in Tyler Cipriani’s v6 series fixing `--force-if-includes` in `git push`. It exempts fast-forward pushes from `--force-if-includes` checks, resolving the existing bug where fast-forwards of non-branch refs (tags, detached HEADs) were incorrectly rejected even when no force was needed.

The patch defers the reflog reachability check until after fast-forward determination by introducing a `needs_force_reject_reason` variable in `set_ref_status_for_push`. If the push is a fast-forward, deletion, or new ref, the reflog rejection reason is discarded and the push proceeds; otherwise, the rejection reason is preserved. This keeps the change localized to `remote.c` and avoids duplicating reflog checks.

Test coverage adds 27 lines to `t/t5533-push-cas.sh`, verifying fast-forward pushes work for expired-reflog branches, tags, and symbolic references. The implementation is uncontroversial and aligns with the feature’s documented intent ("Force an update only if the tip of the remote-tracking ref has been integrated locally"). All prior review feedback is resolved, and the series is ready for merge.
