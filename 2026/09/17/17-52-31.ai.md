This is the second patch in Johannes Schindelin’s seven-patch series fixing Coverity-reported issues in Git for Windows after v2.56.0-rc0. It addresses a latent buffer-overread defect in GPG signature-prefix matching that was exposed by commit c8762c30df5b (object-file-convert: convert tag objects when writing, 2023-10-01) and became observable after 215d305f450f (odb: compute compat object ID in `odb_write_object_ext()`, 2026-07-17).

The patch replaces the NUL-termination–dependent `starts_with()` helper with the length-aware `starts_with_mem()` in `get_format_by_sig()`, ensuring that signature-prefix matching stays within the bounds of the caller-provided buffer. The change is confined to `gpg-interface.c` and touches only the internal `get_format_by_sig()` function and its callers (`get_signature_format()`, `check_signature()`, and `parse_signed_buffer()`). No new symbols are introduced, and the on-disk format remains unchanged.

The commit message traces the defect’s history, explains why it surfaced now, and cites Jeff King’s earlier observation of the same pattern in a different code path. The fix is narrowly scoped and uncontroversial; it plugs a static-analysis warning without altering behavior. Any follow-up discussion is likely to focus on whether similar length-aware checks are needed elsewhere in the GPG subsystem.
