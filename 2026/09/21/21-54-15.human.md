This is a gentle nudge in the ongoing bugfix thread for the `contrib/credential/libsecret` credential helper. The patch, originally posted by Daniel Martí, addresses a crash and silent data loss caused by the current implementation’s use of the `SECRET_SEARCH_LOAD_SECRETS` flag, which silently discards failures (e.g., locked or deleted items) and leads to assertion failures when accessing or freeing NULL secrets.

Daniel’s reply reaffirms his preference for the original design—dropping the `SECRET_SEARCH_LOAD_SECRETS` flag entirely and explicitly loading secrets with `secret_item_load_secret_sync()`. He clarifies that the flag isn’t a true optimization: it performs two round trips (SearchItems + GetSecrets) and silently discards errors, including client-side skipped items. His approach replaces this with a single SearchItems call plus one explicit GetSecret, keeping the round-trip count identical in the common case while ensuring all errors are surfaced. Junio’s alternative (retaining the flag and conditionally loading secrets) would add a third round trip in rare cases and leave the error-prone path untested, whereas Daniel’s version unifies the code path and guarantees consistent error reporting.

The discussion remains focused on design trade-offs: Junio prioritizes preserving the batch-fetch optimization for the common case, while Daniel emphasizes simplicity and reliability. No technical objections to correctness have been raised, and both parties accept the lack of test coverage due to the race condition’s reproducibility challenges. The next step is likely a v2 incorporating this rationale or further discussion to resolve the design impasse. For now, the patch looks well-motivated and addresses a real problem, but the final implementation may hinge on which trade-off the maintainer prefers.
