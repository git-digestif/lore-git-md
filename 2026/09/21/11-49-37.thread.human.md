This thread is about a small but important bugfix to Git’s line-log subsystem (`git log -L`). Kristofer Karlsson identified an inconsistency: when using `git log -L :funcname:` to view the history of a function, the command currently includes trailing empty lines in the range, while `git grep -W` explicitly skips them. The patch trims those trailing empty lines from the computed range in `parse_range_funcname()`, aligning the behavior of the two commands.

The fix is straightforward, touching only `line-range.c` and its tests. Two new tests in `t/t4211-line-log.sh` verify the change—one ensures trailing empty lines at the end of a file are excluded, and the other confirms empty lines between functions aren’t included in the range. The commit message references a prior fix (`8e5dd94e68`) that made `git grep -W` skip trailing empty lines, framing this as a consistency improvement rather than a bug per se. The patch is under review, with no feedback yet from maintainers or other contributors. There’s no indication of merge status or objections so far.
