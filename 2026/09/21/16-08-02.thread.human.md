This thread is a governance discussion initiated by Antonin Delpeuch, an occasional Git contributor, proposing that the project formally document its governance structure. Currently, Git’s decision-making processes are partially covered in `Documentation/DecisionMaking.adoc`, but other aspects—such as roles, responsibilities, delegation, succession planning, and leadership renewal—remain undocumented. Antonin argues that writing this down could help onboard contributors, reduce conflicts, and improve the project’s bus factor, and he offers to draft a `GOVERNANCE.md` file (or similar) as a patch, either alone or collaboratively.

The thread is still in its early stages, with no responses yet. Key questions likely to emerge include whether the project sees value in codifying governance (some projects prefer informal norms) and how much detail to include, particularly around succession planning. The discussion may also touch on where such documentation should live (e.g., root of the repository) and whether leadership would be willing to answer questions to support its creation. For now, the focus is on whether the project wants this documentation at all, rather than its specifics.
