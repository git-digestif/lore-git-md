This thread is about K Jayatheerth’s GSoC project to add eight new path-related keys to the `git repo info` command, exposing filesystem locations of repository components (e.g., hooks directory, index file, grafts file, superproject root, and relative paths between the top-level working tree and the current directory). The series is now in its seventh version, mechanically complete and correctness-fixed, but at critical risk of being dropped due to persistent lack of reviewer engagement.

The latest update addresses minor mechanical nits in the final patch (`path.cdup`), which implements the inverse of `path.git-prefix`—a key explicitly requested by Junio C Hamano to achieve symmetry with `git rev-parse --show-cdup`. K Jayatheerth incorporated Justin Tobler’s feedback to clean up control flow and test hygiene, leaving the patch uncontroversial and ready for merging. The broader series, however, still faces two unresolved issues: whether to include `path.grafts` (given grafts’ deprecation) and a latent correctness concern in `path.index`. Junio has warned that the series will be dropped from his `seen` branch unless reviewers step up within weeks.

Key participants include Junio (maintainer), who has resolved mechanical issues but raised architectural concerns about shared logic between `git repo info` and `git rev-parse`, and Justin Tobler, who has provided surface-level reviews and questioned the long-term maintenance value of `path.grafts`. The series’ fate hinges on whether substantive technical review materializes soon.
