The `git repo info` patch series adding eight new path-related keys (`path.toplevel`, `path.superproject-root`, `path.hooks`, `path.index`, `path.grafts`, `path.git-prefix`, and `path.cdup`) has reached its seventh iteration (v7) after resolving all high-weight issues from prior versions. Authored by GSoC contributor K Jayatheerth, the series exposes filesystem locations of repository components in a scriptable format, mirroring internal Git struct fields while avoiding direct access to internals. Key improvements in v7 include fixes for a critical `get_superproject_working_tree()` bug, alignment of `path.cdup` with `git rev-parse --show-cdup` behavior, and expanded test coverage. Junio C Hamano has endorsed the design and mechanical cleanliness but now warns that the series will be dropped from his integration branches (`seen`) unless reviewers step up in the next few weeks.

The series is feature-complete and ready for substantive technical review, with no known correctness issues. The only unresolved question is the two-phase refactoring plan proposed by the author: merge v7 as-is, then follow up with a dedicated series to extract shared logic into a new `repo-info.c` helper library. This plan defers architectural deduplication to avoid blocking the current effort, but Junio’s call for reviewers prioritizes immediate feedback on the existing implementation. The lack of engagement since v7 was posted a month ago highlights the risk of the series stalling despite its polish. Potential reviewers can focus on edge-case behavior (e.g., bare repositories, `GIT_INDEX_FILE` overrides) and the symmetry of new keys like `path.cdup`, which now matches `git rev-parse` exactly. Without timely review, the series will not progress to `next` or `master`.
