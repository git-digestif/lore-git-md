This thread is about K Jayatheerth’s GSoC project to add eight new path-related keys to `git repo info`, exposing filesystem locations of repository components in a scriptable format. The series is now at version 7, mechanically complete and correctness-fixed, but stuck in review limbo with a critical risk of being dropped unless reviewers engage soon.

The new keys—`path.toplevel`, `path.superproject-root`, `path.hooks`, `path.index`, `path.grafts`, `path.git-prefix`, and `path.cdup`—mirror internal Git struct fields, avoiding direct access to internals. The series includes `path.cdup` (the inverse of `path.git-prefix`) to address Junio’s prior design objection and achieve symmetry with `git rev-parse --show-cdup`. All high-weight issues from earlier versions are resolved, but three test-related or design questions remain open: a bisectability break in patch 2/8, a debate over how `path.hooks.relative` should handle `/dev/null`, and whether `path.grafts` should exist at all given grafts’ deprecation.

Justin Tobler has been the most active reviewer, raising surface-level nits and substantive design questions. His latest review of patch 8/8 (`path.cdup`) flags minor style and test hygiene issues—unnecessary braces in an `if` statement, a suggested early return, and a `cd` command that leaks into subsequent tests—but nothing that affects functionality. The patch remains uncontroversial and ready for merging once these nits are fixed. No other reviewers have engaged with the series since v7 was posted, and Junio has warned that the series will be dropped from `seen` unless reviewers step up within weeks. The lack of substantive feedback beyond Justin’s committed-but-incomplete review leaves the series in a precarious state.
