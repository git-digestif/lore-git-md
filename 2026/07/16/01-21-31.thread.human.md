The `git repo info` path-keys series (`[PATCH v7]`) is a feature patch set adding eight new path-related keys to the `git repo info` command, exposing filesystem locations of repository components in a scriptable format. The series, authored by K Jayatheerth (a GSoC contributor), is now in its seventh iteration and is mechanically complete and correctness-fixed after addressing all high-weight issues from prior versions. However, it has received no substantive technical review in the past month, and Junio C Hamano has warned that the topic will be dropped from his integration branches (`seen`) unless reviewers engage soon.

The new keys—`path.toplevel`, `path.superproject-root`, `path.hooks`, `path.index`, `path.grafts`, `path.git-prefix`, and `path.cdup`—mirror existing internal Git struct fields but avoid requiring direct access to Git’s internals. Each key supports suffix-based formatting (e.g., `path.toplevel.absolute` vs. `path.toplevel.relative`), and the series includes `path.cdup` (the inverse of `path.git-prefix`) to achieve symmetry, as requested by Junio. The implementation touches `builtin/repo.c`, `submodule.c`, `Documentation/git-repo.adoc`, and test files, and introduces 24 new keys with consistent behavior: returning empty strings for inapplicable contexts (e.g., bare repositories) and unconditional paths for `path.index`, `path.grafts`, and `path.hooks`, aligning with `git rev-parse --git-path` precedent. Key fixes in v7 include resolving a correctness bug in `path.superproject-root` (where `get_superproject_working_tree()` ignored the `repo` parameter) and aligning `path.cdup` behavior with `git rev-parse --show-cdup` when outside the working tree.

The series remains under active review but faces abandonment due to lack of engagement. Justin Tobler, who previously raised substantive design questions (e.g., ODB abstraction concerns, `/dev/null` handling in `path.hooks`), has now committed to a full review within the next day, which may help address the current stalemate. Open questions include architectural duplication (a proposed two-phase refactoring to consolidate shared logic into `repo-info.c`), a latent correctness issue in `path.index` (a `BUG()` invocation in `repo_get_index_file(repo)`), and minor naming or suffix semantics adjustments. The most pressing concern is the risk of the series being dropped from `seen` without reviewer engagement, despite its technical readiness.
