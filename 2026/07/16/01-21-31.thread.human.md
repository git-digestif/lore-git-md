This thread is about K Jayatheerth’s GSoC project to add eight new path-related keys to `git repo info`, exposing filesystem locations of repository components (e.g., hooks directory, index file, grafts file, superproject root) in a scriptable format. The series is now in its seventh iteration (v7), mechanically complete and correctness-fixed, but at critical risk of being dropped unless reviewers engage soon.

The most recent development resolves a usability concern around `/dev/null` handling for the `path.hooks` keys. Justin Tobler and Jayatheerth agreed that when `core.hooksPath` is set to `/dev/null`, both `path.hooks.absolute` and `path.hooks.relative` will return `/dev/null` verbatim, simplifying script detection of disabled hooks. This change diverges from `git rev-parse --git-path hooks` but is uncontested and improves cross-platform consistency. Implementation is pending but straightforward.

Two major issues remain unresolved. First, Justin Tobler questions whether `path.grafts` should exist at all, given that graft files are deprecated and slated for removal. The patch is technically sound but may not justify long-term maintenance. Second, the series still lacks substantive technical review beyond the resolved mechanical issues, and Junio Hamano has warned it will be dropped from his `seen` branch within weeks without further engagement. The author has proposed a two-phase plan—merge v7 as-is, then refactor shared logic into a new helper library—but this depends on the series progressing.

The series touches `builtin/repo.c`, `Documentation/git-repo.adoc`, and several test files, introducing 24 new keys with suffix-based formatting (e.g., `path.toplevel.absolute`, `path.cdup`). All prior feedback has been addressed, including a critical bug in `path.superproject-root` and edge-case discrepancies in `path.cdup`. The only remaining open questions are the `path.grafts` design debate and the broader architectural concern about duplicating logic between `git repo info` and `git rev-parse`.
