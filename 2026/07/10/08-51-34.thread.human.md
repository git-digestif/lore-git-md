This thread is about a patch series (v5) that introduces `uploadpack.lazyFetchTrusted`, a server-side configuration variable allowing operators to explicitly mark repositories as trusted for lazy fetching. The series, authored by Christian Couder, replaces an earlier client-side proposal (`GIT_NO_LAZY_FETCH=fromAccepted`) with a design modeled after Git’s `safe.directory` mechanism, shifting trust decisions entirely to the server.

The series has been marked ready for the `next` branch after Junio C Hamano and Christian agreed it is ready for integration testing. No substantive changes were made since v4—only minor documentation tweaks—and Junio’s final review focused on surface-level wording. The patches refactor lazy-fetch logic, introduce path-allowlist validation, add recursion safeguards (limiting depth to 5), and activate the mechanism with updated documentation. Key files touched include `promisor-remote.c`, `setup.c`, and `upload-pack.c`, with new helpers like `lazy_fetch_objects()` and `upload_pack_lazy_fetch_trusted()`.

Two loose ends remain: a latent design issue in `safe.directory` (silently ignoring non-existent paths) is flagged with a `NEEDSWORK` comment, and Christian has proposed a follow-up patch to explore `:(optional)` prefix syntax. The recursion depth limit may also need adjustment based on real-world use. The series is now queued for `next`, with no outstanding objections.
