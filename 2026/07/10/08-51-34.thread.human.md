This thread is about a patch series introducing `uploadpack.lazyFetchTrusted`, a server-side configuration variable that lets operators mark repositories as safe for lazy fetching, replacing an earlier client-side proposal. Christian Couder is the author, and the series is now at v3, with v4 pending only for a minor environment-handling tweak in the final patch.

The core idea is to shift trust decisions to server operators, modeled after Git’s `safe.directory` mechanism. The v3 series addresses all substantive concerns from Junio’s v2 review—API rigidity, recursion risks, and documentation ambiguities—through callback-based allowlists, stricter path validation, and recursion safeguards. The only remaining open questions are minor: Junio’s preference for an `int` over `unsigned long` for the recursion depth counter (patch 4/5), a design nit in the environment-handling logic (patch 5/5), and lingering conceptual doubts about scalability and configuration-based trust, though these are mitigated by the server-side model.

The latest exchange is a brief, collaborative discussion between Christian and Junio about the recursion depth counter’s type. Christian notes that Git’s environment-helper API lacks a `git_env_int()` function, proposing a cast from `unsigned long` to `int` as a workaround, and asks Junio’s preference. This is the last mechanical nit before the patch can be marked ready. No other substantive reviews have surfaced since v3, and the series remains under review, not yet in `seen`, `next`, or `master`.
