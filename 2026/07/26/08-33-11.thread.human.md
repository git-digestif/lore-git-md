This thread is about a small but useful instrumentation patch that adds Trace2 telemetry to the packfile URI download process in `git fetch-pack`. The patch, authored by Ted Nyman, exposes the number of URIs advertised and the download loop duration during protocol v2 fetches, without generating per-pack spam.

The patch has been under review since August 2026 and was deemed ready for integration after a substantive review by Patrick Steinhardt, who confirmed its utility, correctness, and test coverage. The only minor critique—piggybacking tracing checks on a non-tracing test—was not considered a blocker. Ted Nyman pinged Junio Hamano in mid-September to check for any remaining concerns, and Junio has now explicitly confirmed the patch is ready for integration. It will be queued in the `next` branch for cooking, marking the end of the review process.

The patch touches `fetch-pack.c` and extends an existing test in `t/t5702-protocol-v2.sh` to verify the new telemetry. It uses existing Trace2 APIs and maintains correct use of `the_repository`. No open questions or objections remain, and the patch is effectively approved.
