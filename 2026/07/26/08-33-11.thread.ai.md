Core problem or goal
Add Trace2 telemetry to the packfile URI download process in `git fetch-pack` to expose the number of URIs advertised and the download loop duration in protocol v2 fetches.

Current status
Under review; no prior versions. Patch touches `fetch-pack.c` (new Trace2 region and data event) and `t/t5702-protocol-v2.sh` (extended test to verify telemetry).
