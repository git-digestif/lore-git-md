This thread is about a performance optimization for the experimental `git last-modified` command, which now reuses Git’s Bloom filters to achieve near-parity with `git log` speeds (up to 3.7× faster). Toon Claes posted v4 of the series, addressing all prior feedback—including a test environment fix from Junio C Hamano and correctness improvements for wildcard pathspecs and `--show-trees`—and the series is now under final review.

The latest development is a design suggestion from Patrick Steinhardt, who proposes a cleaner alternative to the current approach of restoring `bloom_filter_settings` after `prepare_revision_walk()` clears them. His patch moves the settings into `struct last_modified`, isolating the state and avoiding reliance on a fragile side effect. While this doesn’t block integration, it’s a maintainability improvement that the author will need to evaluate. All other technical feedback has been resolved, and the series is ready for integration once Patrick’s review is addressed. Key participants include Toon (author), Junio (maintainer), Taylor Blau (who identified the `--show-trees` bug), and Jeff King (who reviewed API design). The only remaining open question is whether Patrick’s alternative design is viable or if the original approach is necessary for other callbacks.
