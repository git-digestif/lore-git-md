This is the final patch (6/6) of Patrick Steinhardt's six-part series on packfile lifecycle and process-wide statistics, part of his broader ODB abstraction and repack machinery overhaul. It is a small bugfix in `packfile.c` that hardens `close_one_pack()` -- the function that enforces the process-wide open-pack limit introduced in the previous patch -- by explicitly skipping any packfile carrying the `do_not_close` flag rather than relying on the incidental fact that `git fast-import` packs never entered the global `all_packfiles` list. The diff is three lines: a single condition added to the LRU scan loop. The commit message is clear that no user-visible behavior change is expected; the goal is to make the design robust instead of fragile. The series as a whole remains at v1 and under review, with no integration-branch status claimed.
