This is a pure refactoring patch, the second of six in Patrick Steinhardt's packfile lifecycle series. It performs a mechanical rename of packfile lifecycle functions to adopt a consistent `packfile_` subsystem prefix: `open_pack_index` becomes `packfile_open_index`, `close_pack_index` becomes `packfile_close_index`, `close_pack_fd` becomes `packfile_close_fd`, `close_pack_windows` becomes `packfile_close_windows`, `close_pack` becomes `packfile_close`, `add_packed_git` becomes `packfile_from_idx`, and the static helper `alloc_packed_git` becomes `packfile_new`. No behavior changes.

The patch touches 21 files with 84 insertions and 83 deletions, updating call sites across `builtin/` (count-objects, fast-import, index-pack, pack-objects, pack-redundant), `commit-graph.c`, `connected.c`, the HTTP transport files (`http.c`, `http-push.c`, `http-walker.c`), `midx-write.c`, `midx.c`, `odb/source-packed.c`, `pack-check.c`, `pack-mtimes.c`, `pack-revindex.c`, `repack-geometry.c`, `repack-midx.c`, and the test helper `t/helper/test-bitmap.c`, alongside the definitions in `packfile.c` and `packfile.h`.

This rename aligns with the series' broader goal of modernizing the packfile API ahead of the ODB abstraction and repack machinery overhauls, making function names consistent with other subsystems that already use prefixes. The author notes that fully modernizing the subsystem (including renaming `struct packed_git` itself) would cause excessive churn, so this incremental step focuses only on lifecycle functions.

There are no open questions or likely points of contention specific to this patch; it is a straightforward naming cleanup that prepares the codebase for the process-wide statistics changes introduced in the final patch of the series.
