This is a refactoring patch, the first of eight in Christian Couder's v2 series to fix the missing `promisor-remote` reply in protocol v2 `fetch` commands. It reworks `write_command_and_capabilities()` in `connect.c` to accept a `struct strvec` of complete capability lines instead of a `string_list` of server options, and adds a new `add_server_options()` helper that converts the old format into the new one. The callers in `fetch-pack.c` and `fetch-object-info.c` are updated to build a `strvec` and pass it through, with no intended behavior change. This is preparatory cleanup: it unifies the three previously duplicated capability-writing paths so that later patches can easily inject the `promisor-remote=...` line into every fetch request. The patch looks clean and well-motivated, directly addressing the maintainer's earlier objection that v1's approach would create technical debt by bypassing the `server_options` mechanism.
