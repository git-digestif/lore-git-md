This is a follow-up reply in an open bug report thread, in which a new contributor volunteers to send a fix patch.

Aniket Gupta responds to Sami Jawhar's report about `git diff-tree --cc` producing wrong output for octopus merges with many parents. Gupta confirms the diagnosis -- that `coalesce_lines()` at `combine-diff.c:249` uses `1<<parent`, shifting a plain `int` into the `unsigned long` field `parent_map`, which overflows beyond 32 parents and invokes undefined behavior at 64 -- and asks whether he may send a patch to correct the shift and additionally handle merges with too many parents. No patch has been sent yet; this email is an offer to contribute, and the thread remains open with no maintainer response recorded.

For future reference, the specific defect is the expression `baseend->parent_map |= 1<<parent` inside `coalesce_lines()` in `combine-diff.c`, where `parent_map` is declared as `unsigned long`. Neighboring functions already use the correct form `1UL<<num_parent`: `give_context()` around lines 544-545, `make_hunks()` around lines 610-611, and `dump_sline()` around line 756. The bug is reproducible on both Git 2.43.0 and current master at commit `e1ec09b23c8e`, manifesting as mismatched hunk headers at 33 and 63 parents and as an empty diff at 64 parents. Gupta's proposed patch would presumably align `coalesce_lines()` with those neighboring sites and may also address the edge case of merges exceeding 64 parents, though no code has been posted to evaluate that scope yet.
