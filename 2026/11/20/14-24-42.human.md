This is a side discussion about Git notes workflows, branching off from the main `fetch` refspec series. Kristoffer Haugsbakk and D. Ben Knoble exchange personal configuration strategies for managing Git notes across multiple remotes and machines. Haugsbakk describes using a broad fetch refspec (`+refs/*:refs/tracking/o/refs/*`) to mirror all remote refs -- including notes -- into a local tracking namespace, noting the limitation that negative refspecs cannot carry destinations. Knoble details a more complex setup separating his own notes from Junio C Hamano's `amlog` notes using multiple `push` refspecs and negative exclusions, highlighting the risk of accidental force-pushes to shared notes and the awkward manual merge process when notes diverge. Both touch on the absence of remote-tracking branches for notes and speculate about collaborative notes use cases, with Haugsbakk referencing `git-appraise` and an LWN article on "history of history" to suggest that shared commit metadata remains niche. The conversation is exploratory and does not affect the status of the `fetchRefmap` patch series under review.
