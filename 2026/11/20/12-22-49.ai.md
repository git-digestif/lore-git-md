This is a bugfix within a larger refactoring series -- specifically the first of seven patches in Patrick Steinhardt's v2 series introducing pluggable commit graphs as part of the ODB abstraction effort. The patch corrects a validation gap in commit-graph chain loading where trailer hashes were only checked when linking a subsequent layer, meaning the topmost layer's hash was never verified and lower layers with mismatched hashes could be used until the mismatch was detected.

The fix moves the trailer-hash comparison out of `add_graph_to_chain()` and into the loading loop of `load_commit_graph_chain_fd_st()`, verifying `oideq(&g->oid, &oids[i])` immediately after each layer is loaded from disk. On mismatch, the layer is freed and the chain is truncated at that point rather than being used. This ensures every layer -- including the base and topmost layers -- has its trailer hash validated against the corresponding entry in the commit-graph-chain file.

Files touched are `commit-graph.c` and `t/t5324-split-commit-graph.sh`. Two existing tests that implicitly relied on the old deferred-validation behavior are updated, and two new tests are added to verify that corrupted trailers are detected specifically in the tip and base layers. The commit message notes this addresses feedback from Junio C Hamano on v1 by making the previously write-only `oid` field in `struct commit_graph` readable and used for validation.

This change is part of a broader series that refactors commit-graph internals to unify error handling and prepare for pluggable `odb_source` backends in a follow-up part 2. No on-disk format changes are introduced. The series remains under review with no merge status reported.
