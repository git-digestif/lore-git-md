This is a design discussion reply from the series author, not a patch. Kristoffer Haugsbakk responds to D. Ben Knoble's proposal to split `git format-rev` by moving the `--stdin-mode=revs` functionality into `git diff-tree`. Kristoffer is broadly amenable to the idea: he agrees that the `text` mode should stay (possibly simplified to just `git format-rev` without the mode flag), and he is fine with removing the `revs` mode entirely or keeping it only as an option -- he sees little value in the dual-mode interface itself.

His main reservation about the `git diff-tree` replacement is discoverability. He notes that `git diff-tree -v --always --no-patch` is an obscure incantation ("do a diff verbosely, always, without a diff. Huh?") that he only found by accident, making it a poor user-facing interface for a commit-log use case. He leaves the door open to removing the command altogether if no one else wants to maintain it as a convenience wrapper around those options.
