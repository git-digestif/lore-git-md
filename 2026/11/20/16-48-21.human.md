This is the final patch (8/8) of Christian Couder's v2 series, and it is a documentation update to `Documentation/gitprotocol-v2.adoc`. It clarifies that because protocol v2 is stateless, the client must send the `promisor-remote=...` reply with every `fetch` command request that asks for a pack -- not just with `ls-refs` or `bundle-uri` as previously documented. It also notes the edge case that negotiation-only fetches (like `git fetch --negotiate-only`) do not request a pack and therefore do not need the reply, and that object-ID-only fetches may skip `ls-refs` entirely.

This patch documents the behavior that patch 7/8 implements in code, closing out the series that fixes the missing `promisor-remote` reply during protocol v2 fetches against stateless servers.
