Dirk Gouders has posted the first patch of a four-part v3 series that hardens Git's UTF-8/ANSI escape-sequence parser against buffer over-reads. The change modifies `display_mode_esc_sequence_len()` in `utf8.c` to accept an optional `end` pointer alongside the existing start pointer; when `end` is NULL the function behaves as before on NUL-terminated strings, but when provided it guarantees the scan never dereferences past that boundary, protecting callers that operate on counted `<string, len>` buffers. All four in-tree call sites are updated to pass the correct boundary, including `utf8_strnwidth()`, `strbuf_add_wrapped_text()`, `strbuf_utf8_replace()`, and `format_and_pad_commit()` in `pretty.c`.

The patch adds a new clar unit-test file, `t/unit-tests/u-utf8-ansi.c`, covering the legacy NULL-end path, explicit end pointers at sequence boundaries, and the edge case of swapped start/end pointers. The commit message notes that bounding the scan also avoids the O(n^2) degradation risk Andy Koppe previously flagged. Co-signed by Junio C Hamano, the small, focused diff touches only the parser, its header, the four call sites, and the new tests, and represents the opening salvo of the v3 series.
