This is a bugfix patch, the fourth of five in Dirk Gouders's v4 series correcting bounds handling in Git's UTF-8 display-width code. The patch fixes `utf8_strnwidth()` in `utf8.c` so that when it calls `utf8_width()`, it passes the remaining length (`remainder = end - string`) as the optional boundary argument instead of NULL. Without this, `utf8_width()` could read past the `len` boundary when a multibyte character (such as a CJK glyph) is chopped by the length argument, returning a width based on bytes beyond the buffer. The patch adds unit tests in `t/unit-tests/u-utf8-width.c` covering exactly that case: `utf8_strnwidth("三bytes long...", 1, 1)` now correctly returns 1 rather than misreading.

The commit message is concise and states the problem and fix plainly. It fits cleanly into the series' overall goal of making `utf8_strnwidth()` and `utf8_width()` respect their length arguments, and the accompanying tests directly verify the boundary behavior. The series remains under review with no integration-branch placement yet.
