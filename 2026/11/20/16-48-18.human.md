This is a **refactoring patch**, the fifth of eight in Christian Couder's v2 series fixing the missing `promisor-remote` reply in protocol v2 `fetch` commands. It reorganizes `get_remote_refs()` in `connect.c` to route its request through the now-unified `write_command_and_capabilities()` function, replacing the older `send_capabilities()` plus an open-coded loop for server options. To support this, it introduces a new `add_promisor_remote_reply()` helper (declared in `connect.h`) that computes and appends the `promisor-remote=...` capability line; this helper will be reused in `fetch-pack.c` in the next patch to deliver the actual bugfix.

The patch changes the wire order of `ls-refs` request lines -- capabilities now appear as `server-option*, promisor-remote, object-format` rather than the previous `object-format, promisor-remote, server-option*` -- and drops a trailing newline after `command=ls-refs`. The author notes both changes are harmless because servers accept capabilities in any order and chomp newlines. Functionally this is a no-op for users; it exists to eliminate the duplicated capability-writing logic that the maintainer objected to in v1, paving the way for `send_capabilities()` to be removed entirely in a follow-up commit. The patch looks clean and well-motivated, directly advancing the series' goal of consolidating protocol v2 request construction before applying the fix.
