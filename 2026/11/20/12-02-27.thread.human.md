Patrick Steinhardt has posted a six-patch series refactoring Git's packfile lifecycle API as part of his broader ODB abstraction and repack overhaul efforts. The series removes packfile statistics from `git fast-import` output, mechanically renames seven lifecycle functions to a consistent `packfile_` prefix, consolidates pack-opening logic into a single function with unified error cleanup, introduces a `packfile_free()` helper, moves process-wide pack limits from per-repository scope to a global list, and fixes `close_one_pack()` to honor the `do_not_close` flag.

Junio C Hamano has reviewed most of the patches and broadly approves the direction while requesting specific fixes. He judged patch 2/6 (the rename) "done very nicely," praising especially the `add_packed_git()` to `packfile_from_idx()` change for clarifying that the function does not add the pack to a store, though he noted a minor nit about an unused forward declaration in `packfile.h`. On patch 1/6 he asked for commit message accuracy corrections and a fix to the `--stats` option string in `fast_import_option[]`. On patch 3/6 he identified a `pack_open_fds` counter underflow bug on the `git_open`-success/`fstat`-failure path but otherwise called the consolidation "looking good." He accepted patch 4/6 with a suggestion to consider NULL-guarding `packfile_free()`, approved patch 5/6's implementation while flagging a conceptual tension between pluggable ODBs and a global packfile list, and on patch 6/6 questioned whether the `do_not_close` check is needed at all, proposing instead that the flag be removed entirely since fast-import packs never enter the global list.

brian m. carlson supported removing the statistics but suggested a future change making all `git fast-import` stats opt-in via `--stats`. The series remains at v1 and under review; a v2 addressing Junio's feedback on patches 1, 3, and 6 is expected.
