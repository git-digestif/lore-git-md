Patrick Steinhardt has posted a six-patch series refactoring the packfile lifecycle subsystem, part of his broader ODB abstraction and repack overhaul work. The series mechanically renames packfile functions to a consistent `packfile_` prefix, consolidates pack opening logic into `packfile_open_pack()`, introduces a `packfile_free()` helper, moves process-wide packfile limits to a global `all_packfiles` list, and fixes `close_one_pack()` to honor the `do_not_close` flag. The first patch also removes packfile statistics from `git fast-import` output since those metrics expose implementation details that become meaningless under pluggable object databases.

Junio C Hamano has reviewed most of the series and signaled general approval while requesting specific fixes for a v2. On patch 1/6, he corrected the commit message's description of what `pack_report()` outputs and pointed out that the `--stats` option string in `cmd_fast_import()` still references packfiles; brian m. carlson supported the removal but suggested a future series make all fast-import statistics opt-in via `--stats` only. On patch 3/6, Junio identified an accounting bug where `pack_open_fds` could underflow if `fstat()` fails after `git_open()` succeeds, though he otherwise called the consolidation "looking good." He accepted patch 4/6 with an "OK" but suggested adding a NULL guard to `packfile_free()`. On patch 6/6, he questioned whether honoring `do_not_close` is necessary since fast-import packs are never in the global list, proposing instead to retire the flag entirely. Most recently, reviewing patch 5/6, Junio approved the implementation--praising the signature change of `close_one_pack()` to take `void` and confirming that routing all allocations through `packfile_new()` and frees through `packfile_free()` prevents dangling pointers--but raised a conceptual concern on record: a global process-wide list of packfiles sits awkwardly with the goal of supporting pluggable object databases that may not have the concept of a packfile at all. He also noted a minor wording nit, suggesting "maximum amount of packfile data" over "maximum number of."

The series remains at v1 and under review, with no integration branch status claimed. A v2 addressing Junio's wording corrections, the `fast_import_option[]` string fix, the `pack_open_fds` underflow, and a decision on whether to keep or remove the `do_not_close` bit is expected.
