Patrick Steinhardt has posted a six-patch series refactoring the packfile lifecycle subsystem as part of his broader ODB abstraction and repack overhaul efforts. The series performs five mechanical cleanups followed by one behavioral fix: patch 1/6 removes packfile statistics from `git fast-import` output, patch 2/6 renames lifecycle functions to a consistent `packfile_` prefix, patch 3/6 inlines a helper to consolidate error handling, patch 4/6 introduces a `packfile_free()` helper combining close and free operations, patch 5/6 moves process-wide packfile limits from per-repository to a global list, and patch 6/6 fixes `close_one_pack()` to honor the `do_not_close` flag.

Junio C Hamano has reviewed three of the six patches so far. On patch 1/6 he required commit message corrections and an update to the `--stats` option string in `builtin/fast-import.c`, though he agreed with removing the internal metrics. On patch 4/6 he accepted the `packfile_free()` helper with a plain "OK" but suggested considering a NULL guard to match `free()` semantics for future call-site safety. On patch 6/6 he questioned whether the `do_not_close` check is necessary since fast-import packs never enter the `all_packfiles` list, proposing instead that the flag might be removed entirely. The series remains at v1 and under review, with no integration branch status claimed.
