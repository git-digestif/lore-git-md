This thread is Patrick Steinhardt's six-patch series refactoring the packfile lifecycle subsystem in `packfile.c`, part of his broader ODB abstraction and repack overhaul work. The first five patches polish the API: dropping packfile stats from `git fast-import --stats`, renaming lifecycle functions to a consistent `packfile_` prefix, inlining `open_packed_git_1()` into `packfile_open_pack()` with unified error cleanup, adding a `packfile_free()` helper, and moving process-wide packfile limits from per-repository ODB iteration to a global `all_packfiles` list. The sixth and final patch layers a small behavioral fix on top: `close_one_pack()` now explicitly skips packs whose `do_not_close` flag is set when enforcing the open-pack limit, rather than relying on the fragile coincidence that `git fast-import`'s manually allocated packs never enter the global list. The series touches around 22 files, heaviest in `packfile.c` and `packfile.h`.
