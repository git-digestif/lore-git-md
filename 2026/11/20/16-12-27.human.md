This is a volunteer commitment in the ongoing bug-report thread about ZIP and `fast-import` date-handling inconsistencies. Aniket Gupta is claiming the still-open third issue -- the gap where `git fast-import --date-format=raw` accepts negative timestamps that `git fsck --strict` later rejects as `badDate`.

Aniket states that nobody appears to be working on this and announces he will send a patch to make the plain `raw` format reject dates that fsck would reject, while leaving the `raw-permissive` variant unchanged. He references the two `NEEDSWORK` comments in `validate_raw_date()` that René Scharfe had pointed out earlier in the thread.

This directly addresses the policy disagreement noted earlier: René had argued against stricter validation, citing Git's existing flexibility with arbitrary decimal timestamp strings in commit objects, while Matthew E. Luallen had proposed rejection to align with fsck. Aniket's plan sides with the stricter-validation camp by distinguishing `raw` from `raw-permissive`. No patch has been sent yet; this email simply signals intent to implement the fix.
