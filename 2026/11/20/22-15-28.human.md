This is a discussion reply in an ongoing feature-request thread, not a patch. Steven Rostedt is responding to Junio C Hamano's rejection of a proposed configuration option for `git reset --hard`, clarifying that he was not asking to change the command's default behavior but rather hoping a pre-command hook mechanism (something like a `.gitrc` that runs before specific commands) might already exist.

Rostedt explains his actual workflow to illustrate why he reaches for `git reset --hard` daily: he frequently creates temporary topic branches, works on them, and then uses `git reset --hard <sha1>` on his target branch to move it to the temp branch's commit. Having received confirmation that no such hook exists and that the maintainer opposes altering `git reset --hard`'s contract, Rostedt concludes he will write his own wrapper script that runs `git diff` and prompts for confirmation before executing the reset. He thanks Junio for the guidance and effectively closes his inquiry, accepting that a personal script is the appropriate solution for his specific workflow rather than a change to Git itself.
