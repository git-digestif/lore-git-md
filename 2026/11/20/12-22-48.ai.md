This is a cover letter for the first half of a v2 patch series that refactors commit-graph internals as part of the ongoing ODB (object database) abstraction effort. Patrick Steinhardt has split the original twelve-patch v1 series into two parts; this seven-patch submission contains only the preparatory refactoring of commit-graph reading and writing logic, while the second half (which will introduce the actual pluggable backend hooks in `odb/source-files.c`) is deferred to a follow-up series.

The seven patches rework `commit-graph.c` to simplify chain management, unify error handling, and modernize interfaces without introducing the pluggability itself yet. The new first commit addresses feedback from Junio C Hamano on v1 by adding verification of all trailer hashes against the chain file, fixing a gap where the base layer's OID was not validated. Subsequent patches expose `commit_graph_validate_chain()`, switch layer mergeability decisions from path-based to depth-based comparisons, introduce a common exit path in `write_commit_graph_file()`, and derive layer information directly from the chain structure rather than maintaining parallel state.

For future reference, the series touches `commit-graph.c`, `commit-graph.h`, `builtin/commit-graph.c`, and `t/t5324-split-commit-graph.sh`. Key new or renamed symbols include `commit_graph_link_base()` (replacing `add_graph_to_chain()`), `commit_graph_validate_chain()`, and an alignment on "0 means success" error conventions. Behavior changes are minimal but include stricter validation of commit-graph chain trailers and a switch from boolean to integer error returns in several functions. The series is built on `ps/commit-graph-write-refactor` and remains under review with no merge status yet; part 2 is expected to add the `read_commit_graph` and `write_commit_graph` callbacks to `struct odb_source` and move path-derivation logic into the "files" backend.
