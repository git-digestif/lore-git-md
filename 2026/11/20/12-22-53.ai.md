This is a **refactoring** patch, part of Patrick Steinhardt's v2 series preparing commit-graph internals for the ODB abstraction effort (part 1 of 2, patch 5 of 7). It does not change on-disk formats or user-visible behavior but removes backend-specific path comparisons from the split-graph merge decision.

The patch modifies `commit-graph.c` to replace directory-path comparisons in `split_graph_merge_strategy()` with depth-based counters. Previously, the function compared `g->dir` against `ctx->dir` to decide whether a layer was local and thus mergeable; now `commit_graph_write()` pre-computes two new fields in `struct write_commit_graph_context`: `mergeable_layers` and `chainable_layers` (both `size_t`). It walks the existing chain once, using `get_commit_graph_filename()` to detect split vs. non-split layers and `strcmp(layer->dir, ctx.dir)` to detect locality, incrementing the counters accordingly. `split_graph_merge_strategy()` then uses a `depth` counter against these pre-computed values instead of inspecting paths directly. The logic for converting a local non-split base into a split layer is preserved but expressed as `if (ctx->new_base_graph && depth >= ctx->chainable_layers)`.

For future thread context: the key symbols introduced here are the `mergeable_layers` and `chainable_layers` fields in `struct write_commit_graph_context` and the `depth` local in `split_graph_merge_strategy()`. The old behavior relied on `strcmp(g->dir, ctx->dir)` and `get_commit_graph_filename()` comparisons inside the merge strategy; the new behavior moves those checks into `commit_graph_write()` so the strategy function itself becomes path-agnostic. This sets up part 2 of the series, where the actual decision of how many layers are mergeable or chainable will be made by the ODB backend (specifically the files backend in `odb/source-files.c`) rather than by generic commit-graph code. No merge status is reported; the series remains under review.
