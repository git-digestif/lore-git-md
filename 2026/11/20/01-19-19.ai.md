This is a **"What's cooking in git.git" integration status report** (Nov 2026, #07) from Junio C Hamano, tracking which topics have graduated to `master`, which are cooking in `next` or `seen`, and which have been discarded.

Eighteen topics **graduated to `master`** this cycle. Notable among them are several steps in the **`the_repository` removal** effort: `jc/unpack-trees-repo` (unpack-trees API), `oa/merge-blobs-stop-using-the-repository`, `oa/merge-without-the-repository`. Performance work also landed, including `js/push-refspec-match-speedup` (strmap-based refspec matching for push) and `ps/repo-count-objects-speedup` (avoiding full tree/blob parsing in `git repo structure`). Bugfixes include `mb/ref-filter-missing-object-double-free` (double free in `for-each-ref` when objects are missing), `aa/for-each-ref-no-format-fix` (segfault on `--no-format`), and `ik/fsmonitor-untracked-cache-trivial-response` (stale untracked cache after trivial FSMonitor response). `ps/commit-graph-write-refactor` (10 commits) modernized commit-graph writer interfaces, and `ps/storage-format-improvements` added payload support for storage formats plus automatic leading-directory creation. `kk/large-pack-assertions` fixed `size_t` truncation in `unpack-objects` for >2GB packs on Windows.

Among **new topics**, `ps/odb-pluggable-commit-graphs` (12 commits, currently only in `seen`, "Waiting for response") makes commit-graph reading/writing pluggable through the ODB backend interface, building on the graduated refactor. `ep/loose-object-size-checks` (5 commits, `seen`, "Needs review") adds size validation when streaming loose objects and switches several paths to `size_t`. `rm/http-retry-improvements` (5 commits, "Needs review") adds exponential backoff and configurable `http.retryStatusCodes`. `hn/branch-delete-merged-squash` proposes a `--delete-integrated` option for `git branch` to detect squash/rebase-merged branches. Several smaller fixes are marked "Will merge to 'next'?" or "Will merge to 'master'", including `nc/ls-tree-format-z` (`%(path)` verbatim with `-z`), `jk/uname-errors-are-negative`, and `mr/diff-lib-fsmonitor-leading-path`.

In the **[Cooking]** section, many topics are in `next` and marked "Will merge to 'master'", including `ar/ls-remote-show-object-format` (new `--show-object-format` option), `tb/config-null-values` (15 commits hardening NULL config values across many subsystems), `ps/ci-speedups` (CI optimizations), `mv/log-follow-merge-opt-in` (makes expensive rename-following across merges opt-in via `--follow=merges`), and `kk/commit-struct-padding` (removes 8 bytes of padding from `struct commit`). Topics still only in `seen` needing review include `cc/pack-index-v3` (13 commits implementing pack index v3 for SHA-1/SHA-256 interoperability), `hn/fetch-refmap` (limited fetch in shallow repos), `tc/push-force-if-includes-fixes`, and `wh/checkout-orphaned-commit-warning` (configurable orphaned-commit checks on detached HEAD). Several topics are marked **"Expecting a reroll"** (`bc/setup-default-hash-algo`, `ta/command-list-guides-sync-lint`, `ch/remote-defer-follow-remote-head-validation`) or **stalled** (`iy/sigchain-recover-concurrent-pops`, `vs/refs-seek-fix`, `cs/read-cache-conversion-size`).

In **[Discarded]**, `mv/log-follow-speedup` was retracted to fast-track the opt-in version, and `js/coverity-overflow-fixes` is expecting a reroll after Coverity-reported overflow fixes.
