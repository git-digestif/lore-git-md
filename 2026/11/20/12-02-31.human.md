This is a **refactoring** patch -- the fourth in Patrick Steinhardt's six-part series polishing the packfile lifecycle API as part of his ongoing ODB abstraction effort. It introduces a new `packfile_free()` helper in `packfile.c` that combines the currently-ubiquitous two-step pattern of calling `packfile_close()` followed by `free()` on a `struct packed_git`. The commit message notes this new function will be extended in the final patch of the series to also maintain a process-wide list of packs, but for now it simply encapsulates the close-and-free sequence.

The patch mechanically updates nine call sites across `builtin/index-pack.c`, `commit-graph.c`, `connected.c`, `http-walker.c`, `http.c`, `midx-write.c`, `odb/source-packed.c`, and `packfile.c` itself to use `packfile_free()` instead of the manual close-then-free sequence. This removes a leaky abstraction where callers had to know that freeing a packfile required closing it first. No behavior changes; it is purely a cleanup that makes the eventual introduction of global pack tracking in patch 6/6 cleaner. The patch is well-scoped and consistent with the incremental approach the author has taken throughout the series.
