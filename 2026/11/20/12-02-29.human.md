This is a refactoring patch, the second in Patrick Steinhardt's six-part series on the packfile lifecycle subsystem. It performs a mechanical rename of the packfile API's lifecycle functions to give them a consistent `packfile_` prefix: `open_pack_index` becomes `packfile_open_index`, `close_pack` becomes `packfile_close`, `add_packed_git` becomes `packfile_from_idx`, and the close/alloc helpers (`close_pack_fd`, `close_pack_index`, `close_pack_windows`, `alloc_packed_git`) receive similar treatment. The commit message notes that packfile function naming has historically lacked subsystem prefixes, and frames this as an incremental modernization rather than a wholesale rewrite of `struct packed_git` itself.

The diff touches 21 files -- `packfile.c`, `packfile.h`, and call sites spread across `builtin/`, `http*.c`, `midx*.c`, `commit-graph.c`, `odb/source-packed.c`, and test helpers -- updating every invocation of the renamed functions. There is no behavior change; the patch is purely cosmetic at the API level.

Within the series, this follows patch 1/6 (which removed packfile statistics from `git fast-import` output) and precedes later patches that inline `open_packed_git_1()`, introduce a dedicated packfile-freeing function, move process-wide limits to global scope, and fix limit handling plus the do-not-close flag. The series as a whole is part of Steinhardt's ongoing ODB abstraction and repack machinery overhaul efforts. It is newly posted and under review; nothing is queued in `next`, `seen`, or `master`.
