This is a review reply in the ongoing discussion of Patrick Steinhardt's v5 series modernizing `git rerere` subcommand handling, specifically addressing the design implications of patch 11/13 which converts `builtin/rerere.c` to use `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` with `PARSE_OPT_SUBCOMMAND_OPTIONAL`.

He notes that the `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` interface already supports this via the `PARSE_OPT_SUBCOMMAND_OPTIONAL` flag that patch 11/13 employs to keep the subcommand optional, which is relevant for future readers tracking how the implicit "perform" behavior is implemented via `cmd_rerere_perform()` as the fallback handler. The email closes the design discussion without raising objections, leaving the 13-patch series under review with no merge status claimed.
