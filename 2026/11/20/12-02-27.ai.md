This is a cover letter for a six-patch refactoring series from Patrick Steinhardt targeting the packfile lifecycle subsystem, with one behavioral fix at its core. The series is part of his ongoing ODB abstraction and repack machinery overhaul efforts. The first five patches polish the packfile API and internals -- renaming lifecycle functions (notably `open_pack_index` to `packfile_open_index`), inlining `open_packed_git_1()`, introducing a dedicated function to free packfiles, and moving process-wide limits to global scope -- while the sixth and final patch fixes how those process-wide limits are handled and skips closing packs marked as do-not-close. The author notes a minor semantic merge conflict with the `seen` branch in `odb/source-packed.c` where the renamed `packfile_open_index()` call needs to replace `open_pack_index()`.

The series touches 22 files with the heaviest changes in `packfile.c` (233 lines modified) and `packfile.h`, alongside `odb/source-packed.c`, `midx-write.c`, `commit-graph.c`, `builtin/fast-import.c`, `builtin/index-pack.c`, and several other call sites in `builtin/`, `http*.c`, and test helpers. Key API changes include the introduction of `packfile_open_index()` and a new packfile-freeing function, plus a global mechanism for maintaining process-wide packfile limits rather than per-repository tracking. The `do-not-close` flag on packfiles is now honored during close operations. Base commit is f7de81456940dd6f3274e461149bba12965de609.
