This is a documentation patch, specifically version 2 of a standalone proposal to codify line-length and paragraph conventions for emailed contributions. Okhuomon Ajayi updates three contributor-facing documents -- `Documentation/CodingGuidelines`, `Documentation/ReviewingGuidelines.adoc`, and `Documentation/SubmittingPatches` -- adding seventeen lines of new guidance that instruct contributors to wrap emailed prose near 70 columns so it remains readable on an 80-column terminal after multiple levels of quoting.

The substantive change from v1 is the addition of rules about blank lines between paragraphs, including the clarification that a block of quoted text counts as a paragraph and should be surrounded by blank lines. This directly addresses the scope concern Junio C Hamano raised in review of v1, where he questioned why inter-paragraph spacing was not also covered. The 70-column figure itself remains unchanged and unchallenged so far. The guidance is inserted in contextually appropriate locations: near the general rules in CodingGuidelines, alongside advice about quoting context in ReviewingGuidelines, and next to the existing MUA word-wrap warning in SubmittingPatches.
