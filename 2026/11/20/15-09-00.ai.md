This is a retraction of a patch series. Dirk Gouders withdraws the `[PATCH v3 0/4]` series he had just posted, explaining that after squashing two commits during final polish he failed to re-run `git send-email --reflect` one more time, leaving the series in a malformed state. He asks readers to disregard v3 entirely.

For context that a future session may need: the withdrawn v3 series was a four-patch bugfix effort targeting `utf8.c` bounds handling in `utf8_strnwidth()` and `utf8_width()`, including the mmapped-region SIGBUS edge case, plus a documentation correction. Its distinguishing feature was an end-pointer API (`display_mode_esc_sequence_len(const char *s, const char *end)`, patch 1 authored by Junio C Hamano) that replaced the earlier counted-length wrapper to avoid O(n^2) scanning. Patch 4 had added `display_mode_esc_sequence_len_all()` for greedily consuming consecutive ANSI SGR sequences and made `utf8_strnwidth()` pass a remainder pointer to `utf8_width()`. Files touched included `utf8.c`, `utf8.h`, `pretty.c`, and new unit tests `t/unit-tests/u-utf8-ansi.c` and `t/unit-tests/u-utf8-width.c`. With this retraction, none of that v3 content is currently under review; a corrected repost would presumably follow.
