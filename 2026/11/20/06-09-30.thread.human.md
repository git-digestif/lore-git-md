This thread introduces a new `%(body:trailers=<bool>)` placeholder for Git's pretty-formatting engine, proposed by Andy Koppe. The atom extends the existing `%b` (raw body) so that, by default, it behaves identically, but with `trailers=no` it strips the commit's trailer block along with the blank line separating it from the preceding message body. Content following the trailer block, such as `Conflicts:` sections or comment lines, is preserved. The implementation lives in `pretty.c` via a `format_body_without_trailers()` helper that reuses the existing trailer-parsing machinery, with documentation in `Documentation/pretty-formats.adoc` and six new test cases in `t/t4205-log-pretty-formats.sh` covering edge cases including empty bodies and unknown-option pass-through.

The patch is motivated by four use cases: hiding trailers in compact log views, reflowing the body with `%w` without disturbing trailers, coloring trailers separately from the body, and enabling scripts or UIs to access the body without trailers without post-processing `%b`. Koppe notes a related but independent patch about lost and leaked colors at line breaks that originally sparked this work, suggesting the two be reviewed separately. As of this initial post, the series is at version 1 and under review, with no maintainer or reviewer feedback yet recorded.
