This is the final patch (5/5) of a five-patch bugfix series (v4) addressing bounds handling in Git's UTF-8 display-width code. It introduces a convenience wrapper and simplifies call sites that previously looped manually.

Dirk Gouders adds `display_mode_esc_sequence_len_all(const char *start, const char *end)` to `utf8.c` and `utf8.h`. The function greedily consumes all consecutive leading ANSI SGR escape sequences up to `end` by repeatedly calling `display_mode_esc_sequence_len()` until no further sequence is found, returning the total consumed length. This replaces three manual `while` loops that called `display_mode_esc_sequence_len()` individually: the ANSI-skipping loop in `utf8_strnwidth()`, the loop in `strbuf_add_wrapped_text()`, and the loop in `strbuf_utf8_replace()`. Each call site now advances by the full `display_mode_esc_sequence_len_all()` result in a single step.

The patch adds unit test `test_utf8_ansi__sgr_len_greedy` in `t/unit-tests/u-utf8-ansi.c`, covering cases with no sequences, single sequences, multiple consecutive sequences, and truncated buffers. Files touched are `utf8.c`, `utf8.h`, and `t/unit-tests/u-utf8-ansi.c`. As the closing patch of the v4 series, it completes the refactoring by consolidating the repeated escape-sequence-skipping pattern into a single helper, reducing the risk of inconsistent bounds handling across the three call sites.
