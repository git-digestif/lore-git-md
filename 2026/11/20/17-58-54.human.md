This is a maintainer review of the third iteration of a documentation patch. Junio C Hamano responds to Okhuomon Ajayi's v3 proposal to codify 72-column line wrapping and paragraph spacing for emailed contributions across `Documentation/CodingGuidelines`, `Documentation/SubmittingPatches`, and `Documentation/ReviewingGuidelines.adoc`.

Junio's review is substantive, engaging with both the commit message philosophy and the diff content. He praises the opening observation that these documents currently lack line-length guidance, but notes that the blank-line rule lacks a "why" and uses the wrong grammatical mood. Since the commit message should instruct the patch author to tell readers what to do, "Leave a blank line" should become something like "Tell readers to leave a blank line between paragraphs." He also explains that line-length limits and paragraph breaks are conceptually linked -- wrapping to 72 columns implies that new paragraphs are signaled by blank lines rather than by single long lines.

On the diff itself, Junio objects to the phrase "patch message" as non-standard terminology not used elsewhere in the documentation, warning that it could misleadingly suggest wrapping diff lines in a patch. He proposes instead referring to "log messages, explanations in cover letters, review comments and their responses," and suggests rewording the bullet to describe "E-mailed text intended for human-to-human communication." He also mildly questions whether the text needs to explicitly state that quoted blocks and responses are separate paragraphs, though he concedes some readers may need that spelled out.

The thread remains open with these wording refinements pending incorporation. No integration branch placement has been announced, and no Reviewed-by or Acked-by trailers have been recorded yet.
