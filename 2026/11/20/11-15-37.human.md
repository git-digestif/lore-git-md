This is a review reply in the ongoing discussion of Patrick Steinhardt's twelve-patch series introducing pluggable commit graphs for the ODB abstraction. Responding to Junio C Hamano's substantive review of the first patch, Patrick agrees to explicitly mention the switch to the "0 means success" error convention in the commit message.

On the more technical point about dropped validation of `cur_g->oid`, Patrick observes that the original check was already flawed: it failed to verify both the base layer and the topmost layer of the chain. He proposes a stricter fix that validates every layer's trailer hash against the chain file, which he considers an improvement in both correctness and clarity. He notes that this stricter check causes a test failure in `t5324` because the test implicitly relied on the base layer not being verified. Patrick says he is inclined to add a preparatory commit to address this, and wonders aloud whether the series has grown too long to review comfortably, hinting he may split it into two parts.
