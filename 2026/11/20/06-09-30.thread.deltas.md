# Thread deltas

## 2026/11/20

- [2026/11/20/06-09-30 by Andy Koppe <andy.koppe@gmail.com>] Andy Koppe submitted a patch adding a `%(body)` placeholder to pretty formats, equivalent to `%b` but with a boolean `trailers` option; with `trailers=no` it omits the trailer block and the preceding blank line while preserving any content after the trailers (such as comment lines or a "Conflicts:" section), and behaves identically to `%b` when no trailers are present. He noted that separately coloring trailers would benefit from the independent "pretty: fix lost and leaked colors at line breaks" patch, but considered the two patches independently useful.
