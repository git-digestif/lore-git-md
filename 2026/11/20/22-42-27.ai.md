This is a documentation patch -- specifically version 4 of a standalone proposal from Okhuomon Ajayi to codify line-length and paragraph conventions for emailed contributions to the Git mailing list. The patch updates three contributor-facing documents (`Documentation/CodingGuidelines`, `Documentation/ReviewingGuidelines.adoc`, and `Documentation/SubmittingPatches`) to state that prose intended for human-to-human communication -- including log messages, cover letters, and review comments -- should be wrapped to a maximum of 72 columns so it remains readable on an 80-column terminal after several levels of quoting, with blank lines separating paragraphs and surrounding blocks of quoted text.

Version 4 incorporates all three revisions Junio C Hamano requested when reviewing version 3. First, it recasts the guidance in imperative mood directed at readers ("Leave a blank line between paragraphs" rather than a bare statement) and adds the rationale that paragraph breaks aid readability. Second, it eliminates the term "patch message" -- which Junio noted does not appear elsewhere in `Documentation/` and could mislead readers into wrapping diff lines -- in favor of the broader phrase "E-mailed text intended for human-to-human communication" and specific examples like "log messages", "explanations in cover letters", and "review comments and their responses". Third, it retains and emphasizes the rule that a block of quoted text counts as a paragraph by explicitly stating "leave a blank line before and after it, too".

The changes remain purely additive, adding twenty lines across the three files with no code or behavior modifications, and the patch is based on commit `7fb6055e678f800320d8ab8cd8f201d9493d1ba0`. The guidance appears near the general rules in `CodingGuidelines`, alongside quoting advice in `ReviewingGuidelines.adoc`, and next to the existing MUA word-wrap warning in `SubmittingPatches`.

No integration-branch placement has been announced; the thread remains open for further review of version 4. No `Reviewed-by` or `Acked-by` trailers are present yet.

For future sessions summarizing this thread: the core 72-column convention has faced no objections, but watch whether the expanded "human-to-human" terminology and the imperative-mood phrasing satisfy Junio's remaining concerns, and whether the explicit "before and after it, too" qualifier for quoted blocks draws further scrutiny or is accepted as clarifying existing intent.
