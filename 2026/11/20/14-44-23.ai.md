This is a bugfix cover letter for the third iteration of a four-patch series fixing buffer-bounds handling in Git's UTF-8 display-width code.

Dirk Gouders posts `[PATCH v3 0/4]`, updating the series that corrects `utf8_strnwidth()` and `utf8_width()` in `utf8.c` so they respect their length arguments and do not read past the end of the input buffer, including the mmapped-region SIGBUS edge case. The series also fixes a misleading comment on `utf8_strnwidth()`.

The significant change in v3 is the API for the ANSI-escape-sequence scanner: patch 1 (authored by Junio C Hamano) replaces the v2 approach -- a `display_mode_esc_sequence_len_counted(const char *s, size_t len)` paired with a retained NUL-terminated wrapper -- with a single `display_mode_esc_sequence_len(const char *s, const char *end)` that takes an end pointer rather than a length. This eliminates the `strlen()` call inside the old wrapper, avoiding the O(n^2) degradation Andy Koppe had flagged, and the sole remaining caller in `pretty.c` switches to passing an explicit end pointer directly. Patches 2 through 4 adjust the documentation, fix the ANSI-skipping loop in `utf8_strnwidth()` to stay within bounds while using the new end-pointer API, and extend the same bounds discipline to `utf8_width()` by passing a remainder pointer.

Files touched are `utf8.c`, `utf8.h`, `pretty.c`, `Makefile`, `t/meson.build`, and new unit tests `t/unit-tests/u-utf8-ansi.c` and `t/unit-tests/u-utf8-width.c`. The series is under review with the technical direction settled on the end-pointer design; v3 also addresses previously noted surface nits such as meson test-suite ordering and stray whitespace.
