This is the sixth and final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's six-part refactoring series on the packfile lifecycle subsystem, functioning as a small behavioral fix layered on top of the API polishing done in patches 1 through 5. The patch modifies `close_one_pack()` in `packfile.c` to skip over any packfile whose `do_not_close` bit is set when selecting a pack to close in order to stay under the process-wide open-pack limit, adding the check `e->pack->do_not_close` alongside the existing `pack_fd == -1` condition in the loop over the global `all_packfiles` list introduced in the previous patch.

The commit message explains that the `do_not_close` flag is currently only set by `git fast-import`, which allocates its packs manually and therefore never adds them to the `all_packfiles` list, meaning `close_one_pack()` would never encounter them anyway. The author characterizes this as fragile coincidence rather than proper design, and the fix makes the limit-enforcement logic explicitly respect the flag regardless of how the pack was allocated. The change touches only three lines in `packfile.c` and is intended to produce no user-visible behavior change.

This patch completes the series goal of moving packfile limit handling to a process-wide scope while making the close logic robust against packs that must remain open. It operates directly on the global `all_packfiles` list established in patch 5/6, closing the loop on the architectural shift away from per-repository ODB iteration. The series as a whole remains under review at v1 with no integration status claimed.
