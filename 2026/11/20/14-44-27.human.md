This is a bugfix patch -- specifically the fourth and final patch in Dirk Gouders's `[PATCH v3 0/4]` series correcting buffer-bounds handling in Git's UTF-8 display-width code.

The patch fixes `utf8_strnwidth()` so that when its `len` argument cuts a multibyte character in half, the underlying `utf8_width()` call receives the remaining byte count via its optional `remainder` parameter instead of `NULL`. Previously `utf8_width()` could read past the supplied boundary, which mattered for mmapped regions where such overreads could trigger a SIGBUS. The change computes `remainder = end - string` inside the loop and passes its address, ensuring the width calculation stays within the caller's buffer.

It also introduces a new helper `display_mode_esc_sequence_len_all(const char *start, const char *end)` that consumes all consecutive leading ANSI escape sequences in one call, returning their total length. `utf8_strnwidth()` and the wrapping utilities `strbuf_add_wrapped_text()` and `strbuf_utf8_replace()` now use this helper to skip escape sequences without looping per-sequence.

The patch adds unit tests in `t/unit-tests/u-utf8-width.c` (including `test_utf8_width__strnwidth_cjk_chopped`, which verifies correct widths when CJK characters are truncated by `len`) and in `t/unit-tests/u-utf8-ansi.c` for the new greedy ANSI scanner. The commit message is concise and the change is tightly scoped to the bounds bug, fitting cleanly into the series' settled end-pointer design.
