This is a substantive review of patch 3/3 in Adrian Ratiu's v2 bugfix series for the `compatObjectFormat` interoperability layer. Junio C Hamano endorses the patch's core direction -- converting object IDs only in headers known to carry them (`tree`, `parent`, `mergetag`) while copying all other headers verbatim -- and agrees that the now-redundant special cases for `author`, `committer`, `encoding`, and `gpgsig` in `convert_commit_object()` (`object-file-convert.c`) can be removed since they fall through to the generic copy path.

However, he questions the specific change from `<` to `<=` in the length checks for `"tree "`, `"parent "`, and `"mergetag "`. He notes that the commit message does not adequately explain why the equality case was added, and asks why a line consisting solely of `"parent \n"` (with nothing after the space) should now enter the `else if` branch and be passed to `parse_oid_hex_algop()` rather than being treated as an unknown header. This suggests the v2 fix for malformed headers may need further justification or adjustment before the patch is considered settled.
