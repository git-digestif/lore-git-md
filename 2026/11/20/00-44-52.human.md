This is a review reply from the maintainer on a config-subsystem bugfix. Junio C Hamano responds to Dominik Maier's patch fixing `repo_config_copy_or_rename_section_in_file()` in `config.c`, where backslash-continued values beginning with `[` were misparsed as section headers during `--remove-section` or `--rename-section`.

The review raises a substantive concern about backward compatibility: Junio notes that while treating those lines as continued values might have been reasonable from the start, changing the behavior now could break existing configuration files that rely on the old parsing. This is exactly the kind of edge-case consideration that determines whether the fix can land as-is or needs a transition plan. He also flags a surface-level coding issue, pointing out that declaring `int was_continued` mid-function triggers `-Wdeclaration-after-statement`, and adds an administrative note asking the author not to Cc random people unless they are specifically familiar with the config-editing code.

The patch itself remains under review with no integration-branch status reported. The open question is whether the behavior change is acceptable or requires mitigation for existing configs that might contain bracketed text on continuation lines.
