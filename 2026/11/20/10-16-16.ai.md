This is a substantive review reply in the ongoing discussion of `[PATCH v2 1/4]` (adding `display_mode_esc_sequence_len_counted()` to `utf8.c`/`utf8.h`), rejecting Dirk Gouders's proposal to give the wrapper an optional `size_t *remainder` parameter modeled on `utf8_width()`.

Andy Koppe argues that the remainder-based compromise is unnecessary: callers who want NUL-terminated-style scanning can already pass `SIZE_MAX` as the length to `display_mode_esc_sequence_len_counted()`, and because the function returns the number of consumed bytes, the caller can subtract that from its own known length to obtain the remainder without the function needing to update an out-parameter. This keeps the API simpler and avoids the `strlen()` quadratic cost that motivated the original objection.

Although the thread has already settled on removing the NUL-terminated wrapper entirely (reverting to Junio C Hamano's v1 form where only the counted variant exists), this reply documents why the middle-ground `size_t *len` signature was considered and dismissed. Future discussion referencing "the remainder approach" or `SIZE_MAX` as a sentinel for unbounded scanning in `utf8.c` will trace back to this exchange.
