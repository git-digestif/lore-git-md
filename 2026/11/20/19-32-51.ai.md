This is a substantive code-structure review of v9 2/4 in Harald Nordgren's `--delete-integrated` series. Junio C Hamano responds to the patch that extends `delete_branches()` in `builtin/branch.c` to accept a `landed_commits` strmap and print "landed as" attribution when deleting branches whose changes were detected upstream.

Junio approves of the empty `if` body the patch uses to handle `--quiet` without deep nesting, calling it a clean way to suppress output. He then raises a semantic question about whether combining `--dry-run` with `--quiet` makes practical sense, suggesting that users paranoid enough to want a preview likely would not also want silence, though he frames this as a musing rather than a blocking concern.

His main suggestion is a structural refactoring of the message-selection logic. Rather than keeping the dry-run check on the outside and cascading through landed/remote/normal message variants inside, he proposes selecting a set of message templates from a two-dimensional array indexed by the dry-run flag, which would flatten the control flow and make the loop body easier to follow. The actual branch-removal side effects would remain guarded separately by the dry-run check.

The review is advisory and focused on readability; it does not identify a bug or request behavioral changes. Within the broader thread, patch 2/4 remains considered uncontroversial and ready for integration, with this feedback offered as a possible polish rather than a required fix.
