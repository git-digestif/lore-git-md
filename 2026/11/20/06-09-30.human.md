This is a feature patch that adds a new `%(body)` placeholder to Git's pretty-printing formats. It behaves like the existing `%b` (body) placeholder by default, but accepts a boolean `trailers` option; with `%(body:trailers=no)` the trailer block as identified by `%(trailers)` is omitted along with the blank line separating it from the preceding text. Content that `%(trailers)` ignores -- such as comment lines or an old-style `Conflicts:` section -- is preserved. When no trailers are present, the two placeholders are identical.

The commit message lists several practical motivations: hiding trailers in compact log views, wrapping the body with `%w` without disturbing the trailers, coloring trailers separately, and avoiding post-processing of `%b` in scripts and UIs. The placeholder name deliberately mirrors the `%(contents:body)` field used by `git for-each-ref`, and the option syntax follows the conventions already established by `%(trailers)` and `%(describe)`.

Implementation touches `pretty.c` (a new `format_body_without_trailers()` helper and parsing logic in `format_commit_one()`), the `pretty-formats` documentation, and `t/t4205-log-pretty-formats.sh` with six new test cases covering equivalence with `%b`, unknown options, `Conflicts:` sections, text after trailers, and trailer-only messages. The patch appears well-crafted: it includes thorough tests, documentation, and a clear commit message explaining the semantics.

The author also references a separate, companion patch about fixing lost and leaked colors at line breaks, noting that while the two are related in motivation (the color fix was partly driven by the desire to color trailers separately), they remain independently useful and are not bundled into a series. The patch is based on a recent master commit and is under review on the mailing list.
