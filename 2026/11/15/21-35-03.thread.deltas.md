# Thread deltas

## 2026/11/15

- [2026/11/15/21-35-03 by Andy Koppe <andy.koppe@gmail.com>] Andy Koppe submitted a patch to `pretty.c` fixing lost and leaked colors at line breaks in custom log formats: when color output is enabled, `repo_format_commit_message()` now scans the output for active color escape sequences and makes each line self-contained by inserting a reset before newlines where colors are active, replaying those colors at the start of the next non-empty line (unless it begins with a reset), and appending a reset at the end if colors remain active, thereby preventing colors from being lost when `--graph` or color-aware pagers reset at line breaks and from leaking into graph markers, edges, or unrelated lines when grepping.
