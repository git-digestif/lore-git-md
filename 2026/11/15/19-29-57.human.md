This is a short follow-up in the integer-overflow hardening series. Johannes Schindelin drops the `signed_mult_overflows` and `mult_overflows` macros entirely from the v2 reroll in response to Phillip Wood’s review.

The review highlighted that the type-generic `mult_overflows` macro relied on GNU `typeof`, making it unavailable on non-GCC compilers, and that the signed variant’s implementation was fragile (multiple argument evaluation, operand-width mismatches). Rather than patching the macros, Johannes chose to remove them, simplifying the series and sidestepping the portability concerns. The remaining patches (1–3 and 5) are unaffected; patch 5 will need a different overflow-checking approach for `date.c`.
