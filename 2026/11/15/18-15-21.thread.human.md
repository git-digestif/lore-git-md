This small test-modernization patch by Hardik Kumar has been reviewed and approved by Junio Hamano. It replaces seven instances of the `!` operator with the more robust `test_must_fail` helper across four test scripts (`t5564-http-proxy.sh`, `t6030-bisect-porcelain.sh`, `t7400-submodule-basic.sh`, and `t9001-send-email.sh`). The change is purely mechanical and improves test reliability by ensuring that unexpected crashes or signals are not mistakenly treated as test passes.

Junio’s feedback confirms the patch is sensible and aligns with the project’s goals for test robustness, though he notes that the selected instances were likely chosen for their simplicity—other `! git` occurrences in the test suite (such as those in `t2206`) involve additional complexities like pipes that hide Git’s exit status, which this patch does not address. No changes were requested, and Junio plans to queue the patch for integration in his next cycle. The patch is now effectively ready for merging.
