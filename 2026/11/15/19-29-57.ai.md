This is a **follow-up in the integer-overflow hardening series**, specifically addressing Phillip Wood’s review of patch 4/5. Johannes Schindelin **drops the `signed_mult_overflows` and `mult_overflows` macros entirely** in response to the feedback, acknowledging the approach as flawed. The decision removes the GCC-only limitation and the operand-width assertion concerns raised by Phillip, but leaves the series without a portable overflow-checking mechanism for signed arithmetic.

The email is brief and decisive: Johannes thanks Phillip for the thorough review, calls the macro approach "doomed," and states the macros are removed from v2. No alternative implementation is proposed here, so the hardening in patch 5/5 (date.c) will need a different solution—likely the `intmax_t` arithmetic Phillip suggested. The thread’s open question now shifts to whether Johannes will revise patch 5/5 to use that approach or leave the date.c hardening unimplemented. The series’ overall goal remains intact, but this patch is effectively withdrawn.
