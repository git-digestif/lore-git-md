This thread is about a bugfix for ANSI color handling in `git log --format=...` output, authored by Andy Koppe. The patch addresses two related issues where color escape sequences are either lost across line breaks or leak into subsequent content, making custom log formats visually inconsistent. The fix modifies `repo_format_commit_message()` in the `pretty` subsystem to track active colors and replay them after line breaks, ensuring each line is self-contained.

Junio C Hamano has reviewed the patch and given it a green light at the design level. His feedback focuses on whether the runtime detection approach is justified, and he concludes that it is, given the current API's limitations—specifically, the lack of hooks for custom format authors to manage color resets at line boundaries introduced by `%w(72)` or `%b`. The patch's implementation and test coverage (which includes line breaks, wrapped lines, empty lines, and `--graph` integration) have not been questioned, and no technical objections or edge cases were raised.
