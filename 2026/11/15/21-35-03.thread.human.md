Andy Koppe has posted the third iteration of his patch fixing ANSI color handling in custom `git log --format=...` output, where bold or colored text would lose its styling across line breaks or leak into graph markers and other unrelated lines. The fix lives in `pretty.c` inside `repo_format_commit_message()`, which now post-processes colored output to insert resets at line breaks and replay active colors on the following line, keeping each line self-contained without expensive buffer copies.

Version 3 is purely a clarity refactor of version 2: the author renamed local variables for consistency (`is_reset` to `reset`, `is_reset_only` to `reset_only`, `end` to `reset_end`) and collapsed separate conditionals into a single if-else chain, with no functional changes. Junio C Hamano had already reviewed the earlier versions substantively and endorsed the runtime approach after acknowledging that the existing placeholder API (`%w`, `%b`) cannot provide per-line color control, so no new objections are expected.

The series remains under review with no merge status reported; it is based on `maint` and adds test coverage in `t/t4205-log-pretty-formats.sh` for wrapped lines, empty lines, manual escape sequences, NUL bytes, and `--graph` integration. Nothing is currently contested or open -- the thread appears to be waiting on Junio to queue it.
