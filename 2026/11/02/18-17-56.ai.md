This is a maintainer proposal to freeze the `repo_config_values` structure in `environment.h` by adding a prominent comment that forbids new members until the ongoing design discussion about decoupling configuration from `the_repository` is resolved. Junio C Hamano responds to Patrick Steinhardt’s forward-looking proposal (summarized in the parent email) by suggesting a tactical pause: no new fields should be added to `repo_config_values` until `repo_config_values()` can operate on arbitrary repositories, not just `the_repository`. The patch itself is minimal (seven-line comment) and carries Junio’s Signed-off-by, signaling it is ready for review. The comment references the thread where Patrick first raised the issue, ensuring future contributors can trace the rationale. This is not a functional change, nor does it block Tian Yuchen’s v3 cleanup series (which remains technically complete and ready for final review); it is a coordination measure aimed at preventing further technical debt in a subsystem whose design is under active reconsideration.
