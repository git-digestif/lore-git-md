This is a refactoring patch series, part of the ongoing ODB (object database) abstraction effort led by Patrick Steinhardt. The goal here is to move loose-object backend logic out of `object-file.c` and into a dedicated `odb/source-loose.c` file, making the backend more self-contained and exposing which subsystems still depend on loose-backend internals.

The series consists of six patches that progressively relocate functions for reading loose objects, deriving object paths, iterating over loose files, and mmap handling. The final two patches remove direct includes of `odb/source-loose.h` and `git-zlib.h` from `object-file.c`, completing the isolation. The net effect is a reduction of ~500 lines in `object-file.c` and a corresponding expansion in `odb/source-loose.c`.

Patrick notes this is preparatory work for similar refactoring of the packed and files backends, which will help identify subsystems that aren’t yet fully backend-agnostic. The series is based on the current master (ebdc087dc99) but conflicts with another in-flight topic (`bc/restrict-hex-to-lowercase`), with a straightforward resolution provided.

This looks like a well-scoped, mechanical refactoring with clear motivation. The commit messages and cover letter are thorough, and the changes align with the established ODB abstraction roadmap. No behavior changes are introduced, so the risk appears low. For anyone following the ODB effort, this is a useful incremental step.
