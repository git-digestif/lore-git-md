This is a gentle nudge from Junio C Hamano about the stalled review of Daniel Martí’s v2 bugfix for the `contrib/credential/libsecret` credential helper. The patch, which prevents crashes and silent data loss by explicitly loading secrets instead of using the problematic `SECRET_SEARCH_LOAD_SECRETS` flag, has been in limbo since its submission on November 2. Junio notes that no reviews have materialized and warns that the topic may be dropped from his `seen` branch if no reviewers step up in the coming weeks.

The patch itself is well-motivated and addresses a real issue: the current implementation can lose credentials due to a race condition or locked keyring, and the v2 version clarifies the design trade-offs (simplicity and reliability over perceived optimization) in response to Junio’s earlier feedback. It is self-contained, touching only the libsecret credential helper, and aligns with libsecret’s own practices. The lack of test coverage is acknowledged but understandable given the difficulty of reliably reproducing the race.

The unresolved question remains whether to retain the `SECRET_SEARCH_LOAD_SECRETS` flag for its batch-fetch path (Junio’s preference) or drop it entirely (Martí’s approach). The patch currently implements the latter, but the discussion is not settled. Without further review, the topic risks being abandoned despite its clear value.
