This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's six-patch refactoring series that moves the loose-object backend into its own source file (`odb/source-loose.c`). This specific patch cleans up header includes by removing the now-unnecessary `#include "git-zlib.h"` from `object-file.h` and adding it explicitly to the nine files that still depend on zlib functionality.

The change is purely mechanical and completes the header hygiene work begun in earlier patches. By removing the blanket include from `object-file.h`, the patch makes dependencies more explicit and reduces unnecessary header exposure. The nine files that receive the new include (`apply.c`, `builtin/fast-import.c`, `builtin/index-pack.c`, `builtin/pack-objects.c`, `diff.c`, `object-file.c`, `odb/source-loose.c`, `pack-check.c`, and `packfile.c`) are all components that directly use zlib compression or decompression.

This patch maintains the series' focus on modularity without altering behavior. It's a small but necessary step in the ODB abstraction effort, ensuring that future backend isolation work won't accidentally rely on transitive includes. The patch is uncontroversial and appears ready for integration, assuming the rest of the series is accepted.
