This is an announcement email for the 140th edition of *Git Rev News*, the project's monthly digest of development activity, community updates, and technical discussions.

The November 2026 edition is now live at [git.github.io/rev_news/2026/10/31/edition-140/](https://git.github.io/rev_news/2026/10/31/edition-140/). Christian Couder, Jakub Narębski, Markus Jansen, and Kaartic Sivaraam curate the newsletter, and this month’s contributors include Pablo Sabater, Okhuomon Ajayi, Björn Fock, Štěpán Němec, FluxGit, Jack Lot, and Spencer Uresk. The email also notes that the next edition’s issue is already open for contributions on GitHub.

*Git Rev News* is a useful resource for developers who want to stay informed about Git’s development without following every mailing list thread. This announcement itself is purely informational—no code changes, patches, or technical discussion are involved.
