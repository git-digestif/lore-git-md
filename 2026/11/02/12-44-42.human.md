Kristoffer Haugsbakk pushes back against the idea that automation (such as AI-assisted self-review or bot pre-checks) can meaningfully address Git’s review bottleneck. He argues that while bots can catch mechanical issues like whitespace errors, they cannot enforce uncheckable soft requirements—such as whether a contributor genuinely self-reviewed their patch or rewrote an AI-generated commit message in their "own voice." His tone is skeptical of automation as a panacea, framing it as a one-sided tool that helps contributors far more than reviewers. He also rejects the notion that Git should "move faster" in terms of features, aligning with Patrick Steinhardt’s earlier stance that the project’s pace and standards are not dictated by contributor volume.
