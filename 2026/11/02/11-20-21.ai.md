This is the fifth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch refactoring series that isolates the loose-object backend into `odb/source-loose.c`. It removes the blanket `#include "odb/source-loose.h"` from `object-file.h` and adds explicit includes in the seven compilation units that still need loose-backend internals (`builtin/pack-objects.c`, `http-walker.c`, `http.c`, `loose.c`, `object-file.c`, `setup.c`, and the header itself). The change is purely mechanical and has no functional impact; its purpose is to make backend dependencies visible so that future abstraction work can target the right subsystems. The patch is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort and builds directly on the previous four patches in the series.
