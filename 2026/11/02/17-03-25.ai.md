This is a maintainer follow-up in the `git describe` regression fix thread. Junio C Hamano acknowledges the correctness-over-performance trade-off raised by Patrick Steinhardt and Derrick Stolee—specifically, the proposal to call `ensure_generations_valid()` to guarantee accurate commit generation numbers, even at the cost of a full commit graph walk when the graph is missing or stale. Junio does not dispute the trade-off but floats a new idea: **amortizing the cost by writing out a fresh commit-graph after the expensive walk**, so future invocations of `git describe` (or other commands) can reuse the updated graph instead of repeating the work.

The email is brief but introduces a concrete technical direction that could resolve the thread’s open question. Junio’s suggestion is not a final decision but a plausible path forward—one that preserves correctness while mitigating the performance penalty. It implies that the fix may need to be extended to include a commit-graph write-back step, which would touch the commit-graph subsystem and potentially require coordination with `git maintenance`. No objections or alternatives are raised here; the focus is on exploring how to implement the trade-off rather than re-litigating it. This is a substantive contribution that advances the thread toward a practical resolution.
