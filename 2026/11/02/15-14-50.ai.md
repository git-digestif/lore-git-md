This is a **refactoring patch** in the `parse-options` subsystem, part of Christian Couder’s v4 series introducing `early_scan_options()` for early command-line argument scanning. This patch (2/4) extracts `lookup_long_opt()` from `parse_long_opt()` to enable reuse in the upcoming `early_scan_options()` API. The extraction is mechanical and behavior-preserving: exact matches, unambiguous abbreviations, negated forms, and ambiguity errors remain unchanged. The new helper returns an enum (`LONG_OPT_EXACT`, `LONG_OPT_ABBREV`, `LONG_OPT_AMBIGUOUS`, or `LONG_OPT_UNKNOWN`) and populates output parameters (`found`, `other`, `stuck`) without side effects (no `get_value()`, `die()`, or `error()` calls). The original `parse_long_opt()` now delegates to `lookup_long_opt()` and acts on its result via a `switch` statement, improving readability.

The patch touches only `parse-options.c` and is a prerequisite for the core API in patch 3/4. It addresses Kaartic Sivaraam’s v3 feedback by replacing an `if`-statement chain with a `switch`, making the control flow clearer. The extracted helper is designed to be reused by `early_scan_options()` to handle negated options, unambiguous abbreviations, and aliases consistently with `parse_options()`. No new tests are added here, as the behavior change is internal and covered by the existing test suite and the new tests in patch 3/4. The series as a whole is **ready for `next`**, with this patch uncontroversial and well-motivated by the broader goal of replacing hand-rolled early argument scans.
