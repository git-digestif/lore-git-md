This thread is a refactoring series by Patrick Steinhardt to isolate Git’s loose-object backend logic into a dedicated source file (`odb/source-loose.c`), part of the broader ODB abstraction effort. The series is now in its first version (v1) and under review, with all patches having received at least surface-level feedback from Kaartic Sivaraam. The refactoring is mechanically sound and maintains existing behavior, though it conflicts with the in-flight `bc/restrict-hex-to-lowercase` topic, for which a resolution is provided.

The latest exchange clarifies minor header hygiene details: `builtin/pack-objects.c` can drop its `#include "object-file.h"` only after patch 6/6 (due to an implicit `git-zlib.h` dependency), the removal of `#include "object-file.h"` from `http-walker.c` belongs in patch 5/6, and `object-file.h` must retain `#include "odb/streaming.h"` despite accidental compilation success. These adjustments will be incorporated into the next revision but don’t block integration. No substantive objections to the refactoring’s architecture or behavior have been raised, though deeper technical review is still pending. The series remains focused on modularizing the ODB layer, with future work planned to further abstract backend internals.
