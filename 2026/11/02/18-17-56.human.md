This is a maintainer follow-up in the ongoing refactoring thread about Git’s repository configuration handling. Junio C Hamano proposes a temporary freeze on the `repo_config_values` structure to prevent further expansion until the broader design questions raised by Patrick Steinhardt are resolved. The patch he attaches adds a prominent comment in `environment.h` explicitly forbidding new fields in `repo_config_values`, citing the unresolved discussion about decoupling configuration from `the_repository` and the confusion between `repo_settings` and `repo_config_values`. Junio’s tone is pragmatic: he acknowledges the current series (Tian Yuchen’s v3 patches) is ready for review and uncontroversial, but wants to prevent future patches from deepening the technical debt while the architectural direction remains unclear. The freeze is a light-touch measure—no code changes, just documentation—intended to signal caution to other contributors without blocking existing work. The thread remains open for final review of Tian’s series, but Junio’s intervention suggests the project is now treating the configuration subsystem as a sensitive area until a more principled redesign emerges.
