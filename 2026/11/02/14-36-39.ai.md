This is a **process discussion reply** in the stalled `advice.shallowHistory` thread. Phillip Wood engages with Patrick Steinhardt’s earlier defense of human review, agreeing that conceptual direction requires human judgment and that automation cannot replace it. He adds two concrete suggestions: (1) contributors should self-review their patches before submission to preempt common issues, and (2) the project could experiment with a "review quota" policy—requiring contributors to provide in-depth reviews of others’ work for every few series they submit. Phillip notes that while such a policy was tried and abandoned by PostgreSQL, it might still be worth testing in Git. The reply does not address the technical content of the `advice.shallowHistory` patch itself, but it advances the broader process discussion by proposing actionable steps to alleviate the review bottleneck.
