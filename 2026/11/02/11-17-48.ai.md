This is the v2 revision of Patrick Steinhardt’s CI/build system cleanup series, now reduced to seven patches after dropping the controversial eighth patch. The goal remains consolidating test environment variables across GitHub Actions and GitLab CI to reduce duplication and improve maintainability.

The v2 series is a direct carry-over of patches 1–7 from v1, with no code changes—only the cover letter and range-diff reflect the dropped eighth patch. The dropped patch proposed a `--feature-testbed` command-line flag to simplify local re-testing of patch series with feature-testbed settings, but reviewers (Jeff King, Junio C Hamano, Phillip Wood) favored a `FEATURE_TESTBED=1` environment variable or Makefile flag instead, citing better alignment with existing tooling and CI reproducibility. Patrick Steinhardt has abandoned the patch entirely, leaving the broader CI consolidation effort (patches 1–7) unaffected.

The seven remaining patches are uncontroversial and have received positive feedback from Jeff King ("obviously good"), Phillip Wood ("nice"), and Toon Claes ("noncontroversial and good"). They centralize hardcoded variable overrides in `ci/lib.sh` using pattern matching, eliminate redundant job-specific configurations, and ensure test environments honor Git’s compiled-in defaults (e.g., ref storage format). The series also adds a `NO_RUST` build variant using glibc to restore test coverage for non-Rust builds after the April 2026 default-enable-Rust commit (32d5b90590).

Files touched include `.github/workflows/main.yml`, `.gitlab-ci.yml`, `ci/lib.sh`, `ci/run-build-and-tests.sh`, and test infrastructure (`t/test-lib.sh`, `t/test-lib-functions.sh`). The changes are mechanical but impactful: they remove 54 lines of redundant configuration while adding 46 lines of centralized logic, with no on-disk format changes. The series is queued for review and likely to be merged as-is, pending Junio C Hamano’s final confirmation. The unresolved tension between developer ergonomics (quick local re-testing) and CI reproducibility remains unaddressed, but the immediate discussion is closed.
