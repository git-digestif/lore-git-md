This is the second patch in Tuomas Ahola’s v3 contributor housekeeping series, now receiving a substantive review from Julia Evans. The series aims to keep `command-list.txt` in sync with non-command manual pages (like `gitformat-loose(5)` and `gitpacking(7)`) by extending the `lint-manpages.sh` script to catch omissions automatically.

Julia’s feedback is narrowly technical: she notes that the patch currently pulls some variables from the top-level `Makefile` and others from `Documentation/Makefile`, and suggests consolidating all variable extraction to the latter. This would simplify the logic and avoid mixing sources. The observation is precise and actionable, though it does not challenge the patch’s overall design or motivation.

The patch itself remains well-scoped and uncontroversial. It introduces a new `MAN_GUIDES` variable, updates the linter to verify completeness, and exempts `gitweb.conf(5)` (to avoid a confusing display name in `git help -a`) and the obsoleted `gittutorial-2(7)`. Junio’s earlier mechanical feedback (quoting fixes) and the `gittutorial-2(7)` exemption are already incorporated. The series is ready for acceptance once this minor variable-source question is resolved.
