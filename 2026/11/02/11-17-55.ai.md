This is the seventh and final patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s v2 CI/build system cleanup series. It removes a redundant override of `GIT_TEST_DEFAULT_INITIAL_BRANCH_NAME` in the `linux-feature-testbed` CI job, as the initial branch name is already controlled by the `WITH_BREAKING_CHANGES` toggle (which sets it to "master" when enabled, "main" otherwise). The change is a one-line deletion in `ci/lib.sh` and aligns with the series’ broader goal of eliminating unnecessary duplication in CI environment variable assignments.

The patch is uncontroversial and mechanically sound: the override was introduced when the default branch name was still in flux, but the `WITH_BREAKING_CHANGES` mechanism now handles this consistently. No behavior change is expected, as the effective value remains "master" for the testbed job. This cleanup follows the pattern established in earlier patches (e.g., removing redundant `linux-reftable-sha256` job assignments) and reinforces the series’ focus on maintainability.

Reviewers have already endorsed the series as a whole (Jeff King called patches 1–7 "obviously good"; Phillip Wood and Toon Claes described them as "nice" and "noncontroversial"). This patch is the smallest in the series and likely to be merged without further discussion, pending Junio C Hamano’s final confirmation. The only remaining open question is whether the series will be merged as-is or if Junio will request minor adjustments (e.g., squashing patches for atomicity), but no such concerns have been raised yet.
