I have had good experiences with private review by bots.

But again the usual problem with an influx of contributions is that
“requiring” this kind of soft check by a bot is not something that
reviewers can check for. They can check if someone skipped the
whitespace error check. But they can’t check that someone skipped a
self-review. It’s like requiring “using your own voice” for commit
messages. People can just say that they “drafted” it with AI but they
rewrote it “in their own voice”. Uncheckable.

Contributors have gotten a hundred-fold more help to write
code. (And even commit messages for some reason.) Meanwhile, what have
reviewers received?
