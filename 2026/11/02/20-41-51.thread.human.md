This thread began when Tuomas Ahola asked whether Git’s `.cirrus.yml` file was still in use, given that Cirrus CI shut down in June 2026. The file, which had configured FreeBSD CI, was confirmed unused by Patrick Steinhardt and Carlo Arenas, and Tuomas posted a cleanup patch to remove it. That patch has now received a final polish review from Patrick, who suggested minor cosmetic tweaks—lowercasing the subject prefix (`ci:` instead of `CI:`) and omitting the three-dash separator in the commit message—while reaffirming the patch is ready for integration. With those adjustments made, the patch is uncontroversial and expected to land in `next` shortly, closing the thread.
