Patrick suggests calling `ensure_generations_valid()` to guarantee consistent behavior, even though it would be expensive in repositories without a commit graph.

The tone is technical and exploratory; Patrick isn’t objecting to the patch, but he’s flagging a real edge case that could confuse users.
