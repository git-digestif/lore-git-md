This is a **contributor housekeeping** follow-up in the bash-completion script thread. Mirko Faina responds to Pablo Sabater’s substantive review with agreement and additional technical context.

Mirko accepts Pablo’s suggestion to move `--graph-indent` and `--no-graph-indent` from the shared `__git_log_common_options` list to the command-specific `__git_log_gitk_options` block, where `--graph` itself resides. He also agrees to investigate and likely add completion for `--graph-lane-limit`, the other `--graph`-dependent option Pablo identified.

The new technical insight in this email is Mirko’s observation about the root cause of the completion system’s limitations: the bash-completion script exists only for options that are *not* handled by Git’s `parse-options.h` machinery. That machinery, which powers `--help` output and argument parsing, does not currently support conditional completion (e.g., suppressing options that require another option to be present). Mirko suggests that implementing such logic in the bash-completion script would be a temporary workaround for a limitation that should ideally be addressed in `parse-options.h` itself. This frames the discussion as architectural rather than purely cosmetic: the long-term goal would be to make the completion system self-maintaining by exposing completion hints directly from the commands, rather than manually curating lists in `git-completion.bash`.

No objections or controversy are present; the tone is collaborative and forward-looking. Mirko plans to reroll the patch with the agreed changes. The thread remains focused on the immediate completion script improvement, but the architectural question about `parse-options.h` integration is now on the table as a potential future direction.
