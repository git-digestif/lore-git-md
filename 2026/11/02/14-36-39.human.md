This is a process discussion reply in the stalled `advice.shallowHistory` thread. Phillip Wood joins the conversation to endorse Patrick Steinhardt’s stance that human review is essential for assessing conceptual direction, not just code correctness. He agrees that automation (CI, linting, coverage tools) can catch mechanical issues but cannot judge whether a patch implements a "bad idea." Phillip also shares Junio’s past suggestion of requiring contributors to provide reviews in exchange for submitting patches, though he notes that PostgreSQL abandoned a similar experiment. His tone is pragmatic: while he supports helping newcomers learn the process, he expects repeat contributors to self-review and anticipate common feedback. The thread remains stalled, with no technical review of the patch itself and no immediate path forward.
