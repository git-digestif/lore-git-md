This is a withdrawal notice for Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s five-patch series adding `--abbrev`, `--color`, and `--date` options to `git format-rev`. The author now believes the command itself may not be necessary, citing unrelated research that suggests `git diff-tree` could be extended to cover the same use case. The series, which had been idle in Junio C Hamano’s `seen` branch since August 2026 and incorporated all prior review feedback, is effectively abandoned.

The withdrawal comes after two maintainer pings (most recently on November 2) asking for reviewers, with Junio explicitly noting he is not the best person to review this experimental command and warning the topic would be dropped without feedback. Kristoffer’s follow-up links to two earlier emails where he explores whether `git diff-tree` could replace `git format-rev --stdin-mode=revs`, though he does not detail the proposed modifications or claim the replacement is complete. No other contributors have weighed in on the replacement idea, and the series itself remains uncontroversial and technically sound.

If the replacement effort gains traction, it would likely render the entire `git format-rev` command redundant, making these patches moot. For now, the topic will be removed from `seen` in the next integration cycle.
