This is the first patch in the **final (v9) series** adding path-related keys to `` `git repo info` ``. It introduces ``path.workingtree.absolute`` and ``path.workingtree.relative``, which expose the repository’s working-tree root in scriptable, strictly formatted form. The implementation is straightforward: it uses ``repo_get_work_tree()`` and the existing ``format_path()`` helper, returning an empty string in bare repositories. Tests cover both absolute and relative paths, including execution from a subdirectory and the bare-repository edge case. The patch is **mechanically clean, uncontroversial, and ready for merging**; all prior feedback on this part of the series has been addressed.

The files touched are ``builtin/repo.c`` (the two new getter functions and their registration), ``Documentation/git-repo.adoc`` (man-page entries), and ``t/t1900-repo-info.sh`` (two new test cases). The new keys are the simplest of the four added in the series, serving as a foundation for the more complex ``path.superproject_workingtree``, ``path.index``, and ``path.prefix`` keys introduced in later patches. For future AI summarization, note the key names, their suffix-based formatting, and the use of ``repo_get_work_tree()`` and ``format_path()``.
