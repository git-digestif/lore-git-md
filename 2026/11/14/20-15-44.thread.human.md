Dirk Gouders has been polishing the `gitcli` man page (`Documentation/gitcli.adoc`) through five iterations of purely editorial fixes: correcting "convention" to "conventions", switching a bullet about short options from the gerund "Splitting" to the imperative "Split short options into separate words" (per Andy Koppe's suggestion of "into" over "to"), and clarifying how ambiguous arguments are disambiguated with `--`. The series is now at v5 and remains under review; it has not been merged to any integration branch.

The one substantive exchange in the thread came when Junio C Hamano objected to a v4 wording change in the disambiguation bullet. V4 said an ambiguous argument "can be disambiguated by placing `--` between them", but Junio pointed out this was misleading -- for `git log main next` where both exist as files and branches, the separator goes between the revision and the path, not simply between the two arguments, while for a single ambiguous argument `--` can go before or after it. Dirk acknowledged the regression was accidental, introduced during a base migration, and v5 reverts to the previously approved phrasing "before or after it".

With that objection resolved, v5 incorporates all maintainer feedback and the remaining changes are purely grammatical and stylistic, with no technical content affected. The only open item is Junio's decision on whether to queue the topic for `next`; no corrected concerns or new objections remain.
