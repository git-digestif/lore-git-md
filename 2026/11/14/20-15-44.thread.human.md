Dirk Gouders has submitted version 4 of a standalone documentation patch cleaning up grammar and style in the `gitcli` man page, and the thread is now down to a single contested wording choice. The series fixes minor grammatical agreement issues ("convention" to "conventions", "When an argument" to "When arguments") and rewords a bullet about splitting short options into the imperative "Split short options into separate words". Junio C Hamano has reviewed v4 and accepts these editorial changes, but objects to one regression in the bullet explaining how to disambiguate arguments that could be either revisions or paths.

Junio argues that v4's new phrasing -- that such arguments "can be disambiguated by placing `--` between them" -- inaccurately describes where the separator may go. Using `git log` examples where a name exists as both a branch and a file, he shows that `git log main next` cannot be fixed by simply inserting `--` between the two arguments as typed; the correct form is `git log next -- main`, placing the separator between the revision and the path. The earlier wording "before or after it" correctly captured that for a single ambiguous argument the `--` may appear on either side, whereas "between them" misleads readers about valid positions. The other v4 changes remain approved, but this bullet needs to revert to the prior phrasing before the topic can be queued. The patch remains under review with no merge to any integration branch.
