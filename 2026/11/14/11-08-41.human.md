This is the fourth and final patch in Harald Nordgren’s series adding `--delete-integrated` to `git branch`, which detects branches whose changes (but not their tips) have been integrated via squash merges or rebases, even when upstream modified the same files. The series is now in its sixth iteration and the CLI design is settled.
