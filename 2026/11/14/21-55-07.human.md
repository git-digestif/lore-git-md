This is a documentation patch under review, touching only the `gitcli` man page. Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, has responded to Dirk Gouders' initial submission with feedback on one of the three proposed changes.

The patch aims to improve grammatical and stylistic consistency in the `gitcli` documentation. Two of the three changes—pluralizing "convention" to "conventions" and rephrasing a bullet point about short option splitting to use imperative form ("Split" instead of "Splitting")—are accepted without comment. The third change, pluralizing "argument" to "arguments" in a rule about disambiguating revisions and paths, drew pushback. Junio argues that the original singular form is more accurate, since the rule describes a single ambiguous argument, and suggests rewording the conclusion instead to maintain clarity without implying multiple arguments must be reordered.

The discussion is narrow and focused, with no broader implications for Git's behavior or design. The patch remains uncontroversial and is likely to be merged with minor adjustments. No further review or testing is needed, as the changes are purely editorial.
