This is the final patch in Harald Nordgren’s 6-part series introducing `remote.<name>.fetchRefmap` for a matching-inspired fetch mode. The patch fixes a bug where `@{push}` resolution fails for remotes configured with `--limited-fetch` (which sets `fetchRefmap` but not `remote.<name>.fetch`). The fix is straightforward: when `remote->fetch` cannot resolve `@{push}`, the code now falls back to `remote->refmap`, ensuring the refname resolves correctly.

Harald acknowledges D. Ben Knoble’s feedback that the fix might logically belong with the earlier patches that introduce `fetchRefmap` (Patches 1–3), since the bug exists as soon as `fetchRefmap` is added, even without `--limited-fetch`. Knoble’s suggestion is purely about patch organization—framing the fix as a bugfix for the plumbing rather than part of the `--limited-fetch` feature—and does not question the patch’s correctness. The fix itself is uncontroversial, well-tested, and ready for merging.
