The v4 text states that arguments which could be either revisions or paths "can be disambiguated by placing `--` between them", but Junio argues this is a regression from the earlier v3 phrasing "before or after it". He illustrates with `git log` examples: for `git log main next` where both exist as files and branches, the separator cannot simply be placed between the two ambiguous arguments in typed order; the correct form is `git log next -- main` (separator between revision and path). For a single ambiguous argument like `git log main`, the `--` can go either before (`git log -- main`) or after (`git log main --`). The earlier "before or after it" phrasing captured both possibilities, whereas "between them" misleads. Dirk Gouders has now acknowledged this objection, explaining that the change was an accidental regression introduced while migrating the topic to a different base ("b4") and that he did not intend to override the maintainer's preferred v3 phrasing; he apologizes for the error.
