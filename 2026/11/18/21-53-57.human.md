This is a review reply in an ongoing patch-series discussion about Git's HTTP retry logic. Ravi Mistry is responding to Patrick Steinhardt's objection to the new `http.retryStatusCodes` config option in patch v3 3/5, which would let users opt in to retrying transient 5xx errors like 502, 503, and 504.

Ravi agrees with Patrick that providers should standardize on 429 for rate limiting, but draws a distinction: 5xx codes signal different infrastructure problems -- dropped connections at reverse proxies or CDNs (502/504) and temporary server overload (503) -- rather than deliberate rate limiting. He argues these should not be retried by default because in some deployments they indicate permanently broken infrastructure, but keeping them configurable lets CI systems and administrators opt in centrally where they know the 5xx responses are transient. The exchange leaves Patrick's architectural push for ecosystem convergence on 429-only versus Ravi's opt-in flexibility unresolved, with the ball in Junio's court to decide which direction the series takes.
