This is a maintainer review of the second patch in a two-patch bugfix series. Junio C Hamano is responding to Elia Pinto's `[PATCH 2/2]`, which changes `parse_tree_buffer()` in `tree.c` to accept a `size_t` for the tree buffer size instead of `unsigned long`.

The patch continues the series' goal of preventing silent truncation of trees larger than 4 GiB, particularly on Windows where `unsigned long` is only 32 bits wide. While the first patch widened the size fields in `struct tree_desc`, this one updates `struct tree` and `parse_tree_buffer()` to match, aligning the type with what `odb_read_object()` already returns.

Junio confirms the change sounds good but notes an important limitation: the sole caller, `parse_object_buffer()` in `object.c`, still passes the size as `unsigned long`. He acknowledges that this patch alone therefore does not fix the end-to-end path, yet accepts it as a valid incremental step under a "one thing at a time" approach. This is a substantive review -- he traces the caller chain and identifies exactly why the fix is incomplete without the follow-up work he previously flagged.

The series remains under review with no objections to the core approach. Junio's acceptance of this patch as an intermediate step suggests the broader `size_t` conversion is on track, pending eventual cleanup of `parse_object_buffer()` to complete the fix.
