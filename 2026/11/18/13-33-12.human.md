This is a substantive review reply from Patrick Steinhardt on the first patch of Elia Pinto's four-part v2 series converting `unpack-objects` to `size_t` for large-object handling on 32-bit-`long` platforms.

Patrick has two main concerns. First, he finds the commit message excessively verbose, noting that while documenting unexpected behavior is valuable, detailing every function touched is redundant since the diff makes that obvious; he suggests the message could be cut to a third of its length without losing information. More significantly, he objects to the `cast_size_t_to_ulong()` guards that Pinto added in `write_object()` to protect downstream calls to `odb_write_object()` and `parse_object_buffer()`, which still accept `unsigned long`. Because Pinto already has a separate series in flight that converts those functions to `size_t`, Patrick worries this patch creates temporary casts that will need to be removed in a third series once both land, and asks whether it would be cleaner to defer this entire series until the `odb_write_object()` conversion has landed first. The exchange highlights a sequencing question: whether to layer temporary casts now or wait for the deeper conversion to avoid churn.
