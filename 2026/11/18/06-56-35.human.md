This is the first patch of a four-patch bugfix series targeting 32-bit `unsigned long` truncation on 64-bit platforms (notably 64-bit Windows), where `size_t` is 64 bits.

Elia Pinto converts `builtin/unpack-objects.c` wholesale from `unsigned long` to `size_t` for object sizes, closing three failure modes that appear when handling objects of 4 GiB or more. Non-streamed objects previously hit an "inflate returned -5" error because `get_data()` allocated a truncated buffer; deltas with bases of 4 GiB or more crashed because `unpack_delta_entry()` downcast the base size; and large delta results were silently truncated in `write_object()`, producing objects with wrong contents and wrong names. The patch fixes the first two directly and the third by inserting a `cast_size_t_to_ulong()` guard in `write_object()`: because `odb_write_object()` and `parse_object_buffer()` still accept `unsigned long`, the guard makes unpack-objects die with a clear "object too large to read on this platform" message instead of writing corrupt data. Full correctness for non-streamed writes awaits follow-up work converting those downstream functions to `size_t`.

The commit message is thorough, describing reproduction via 16-bit size truncation simulation, and explicitly notes that no test is included because triggering the bug requires multi-gigabyte objects on a 32-bit-`long` platform. As patch 1/4 of the v2 series, it focuses solely on the unpack-objects path; the remaining patches address `index-pack` and `git fsck`.
