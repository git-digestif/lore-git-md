This is a review reply in the ongoing `[PATCH v3 0/5]` HTTP transport series. Ravi Mistry responds to Patrick Steinhardt's two implementation nits on patch 2/5 (exponential backoff): he agrees to change the `attempt` parameter from signed `int` to `size_t` in a future v4, and he defends the iterative doubling loop over a single `MIN()` expression, explaining that the loop avoids undefined behavior from bit-shift and multiplication overflow when a user configures a large `http.maxRetries` value such as 100. The exchange is brief and cooperative, with the author accepting one nit and providing a technical rationale for the other; no new design questions are raised.
