Thread identity: v2 patch series (4/4, final patch; cover letter not included in this email), authored by Dirk Gouders <dirk@gouders.net>, fixing a boundary-handling bug in `utf8_strnwidth()` in `utf8.c`.

Core problem: when `utf8_strnwidth()` is called with a `len` that splits a multibyte UTF-8 sequence, the inner call to `utf8_width()` was made without its optional boundary argument, causing it to read past the requested length and mis-count display width.

Current status: second version of the series, under review; no integration branch status mentioned.

Key technical details: the fix in `utf8.c` introduces a `remainder` variable computed as `orig + len - string` at each iteration and passes `&remainder` to `utf8_width()`, so incomplete sequences at the boundary are handled correctly and the function returns `len` in such cases. Test coverage added in `t/unit-tests/u-utf8-width.c` as the clar unit test `test_utf8_width__strnwidth_cjk_chopped`, which exercises the CJK string `"三bytes long, 二columns wide"` with byte boundaries of 1 and 3 (both splitting multibyte characters) and asserts display widths of 1 and 2 respectively.
