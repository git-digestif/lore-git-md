This is a maintainer-review acknowledgment for patch 11/12 of Patrick Steinhardt's merged `git rerere` subcommand modernization series (v4). Patrick Steinhardt replies to two points Junio C Hamano raised in his review of the patch that switches `builtin/rerere.c` from a hand-rolled subcommand ladder to `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()`.

First, Patrick concedes the error-reporting regression: on the `seen` branch, `git rerere unknown` now prints a generic "too many arguments" message instead of listing valid subcommands in the usage string, unlike `master` or other commands such as `git submodule`. He states he will fix this.

Second, he addresses the memory-safety concern in the fallback logic that constructs a synthetic `"perform"` subcommand. The code pushes `"perform"` plus the original `argv` into a `strvec`, then uses `CALLOC_ARRAY(args_copy, args.nr)` and `COPY_ARRAY()` to duplicate it before passing to `parse_options()`. Junio questioned whether `args.nr` includes the terminating NULL pointer required by the C `(ac, av)` convention (`av[ac]` must be NULL); Patrick agrees this likely needs correction to avoid an unterminated array and says he will adapt the code.
