This is a review reply in the ongoing thread for the bounds-safety patch that adds `display_mode_esc_sequence_len_counted()` to `utf8.c`. Dirk Gouders responds to Andy Koppe's technical clarification about ECMA-48 SGR sequence syntax, conceding his earlier doubt about whether colon-separated subparameters could be recognized without extending the finite state machine.

Gouders acknowledges that `"\033[:;m"` is indeed a valid SGR sequence -- comprising two parameters where the first contains two empty subparameters and the second is an empty parameter -- and accepts Koppe's confirmation that `"\033\[[0-9:;]*m"` is the complete regular expression for SGR sequences. He also notes that he now understands the ECMA-48 column/row notation (`3/0` for `0x30`/`'0'`, `3/11` for `0x3B`/`';'`), which makes the standard's parameter syntax legible.

The exchange effectively settles the technical sub-question: merely expanding the accepted alphabet from `[0-9;]` to `[0-9:;]` in the existing single-parameter-state machine suffices to recognize all valid SGR sequences without introducing new parser states. However, no decision has been recorded on whether the patch should actually extend the parser to accept colons; that question remains open for the series.
