This is a **maintainer review** of the second patch in a bugfix series addressing silent truncation of trees larger than 4 GiB. Junio C Hamano approves the change to `parse_tree_buffer()` and `struct tree` in `tree.c`/`tree.h`, which widens the buffer size parameter from `unsigned long` to `size_t` (matching what `odb_read_object()` already returns), but notes that the sole caller `parse_object_buffer()` in `object.c` still passes an `unsigned long`, so this patch alone does not fix the truncation end-to-end; he accepts it as an incremental "one thing at a time" step. The review is substantive in that it identifies the specific remaining call site that still needs conversion, but otherwise offers no objections. For future reference: the patch touches `tree.c` and `tree.h`, changes the signature of `parse_tree_buffer()` from `unsigned long size` to `size_t size`, and leaves `parse_object_buffer()` in `object.c` as known follow-up work; the series remains under review with the maintainer's approval of this approach now on record.
