The patch touches exactly two lines in `t/t1017-cat-file-remote-object-info.sh`, both inside `test_expect_success` blocks that set up repos served by `git daemon` and HTTP. In each case an unquoted redirection target is replaced with a quoted one, and per Junio's coding-guideline correction the redirection operator `>` is placed immediately before the quote with no intervening space: `>"$daemon_parent/hello"` and `>"$HTTPD_DOCUMENT_ROOT_PATH/http_parent/hello"`. The variables involved are `daemon_parent` (derived from `GIT_DAEMON_DOCUMENT_ROOT_PATH`) and `HTTPD_DOCUMENT_ROOT_PATH`; both are now double-quoted where they previously appeared bare after `>`.

Old versus new behavior is purely syntactic: previously `echo_without_newline "$hello_content" > $daemon_parent/hello` would word-split the path on Solaris when it contained spaces, producing "No such file or directory" errors during the subsequent `cd` in the test harness; the new form `>"$daemon_parent/hello"` prevents that splitting. The change aligns with `Documentation/CodingGuidelines`, which mandates quoting shell variables used as redirection targets even though POSIX does not strictly require it.
