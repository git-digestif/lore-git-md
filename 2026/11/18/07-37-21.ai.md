This is a bugfix patch preventing out-of-bounds reads when scanning ANSI escape sequences in non-NUL-terminated buffers, and this reply confirms a change in authorship: Dirk Gouders accepts Junio C Hamano's proposal to take over the patch and repost it as `[PATCH v2 1/2]` under his own name, with his clar unit tests expected to become patch 2/2.

The underlying change adds `display_mode_esc_sequence_len_counted(const char *s, size_t len)` to `utf8.c` (declared in `utf8.h`), which scans exactly `len` bytes without reading at or beyond `s[len]`, returning the length of a complete SGR sequence or 0. The original `display_mode_esc_sequence_len()` remains as a wrapper passing `strlen(s)`. Three internal callers in `utf8.c` -- `utf8_strnwidth()`, `strbuf_add_wrapped_text()`, and `strbuf_utf8_replace()` -- now use the counted variant. Only `utf8.c` and `utf8.h` are touched.

Dirk had previously supplied clar unit tests for `t/unit-tests/u-utf8-ansi.c` (to be registered via `CLAR_TEST_SUITES += u-utf8-ansi` in the `Makefile` and the corresponding `t/meson.build` entry) asserting boundary behavior such as returning 0 for a truncated `"\033[m"` at `len=2` and correct lengths for complete sequences like `"\033[31m"` and `"\033[0;31m"`. With the authorship transfer accepted, these tests are expected to appear in the forthcoming respun series.
