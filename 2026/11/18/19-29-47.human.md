This is a brief review reply in the ongoing discussion of the bounds-safety patch for ANSI escape sequence scanning in `utf8.c`. Dirk Gouders responds to Andy Koppe's explanation of ECMA-48 SGR syntax by acknowledging that he did not previously know `"\033[:;m"` constitutes a valid SGR sequence -- specifically two parameters where the first holds two empty subparameters and the second is empty. Gouders also accepts Koppe's decoding of the ECMA-48 notation (`3/0` for `'0'`, `3/11` for `';'`) and commits to reading the standard.

The exchange confirms the technical validity of colon-separated subparameters but leaves the larger design question open: whether the patch should extend its accepted alphabet from `[0-9;]` to `[0-9:;]`. The underlying `[PATCH v2]` adding `display_mode_esc_sequence_len_counted()` remains under review, with authorship expected to transfer from Junio C Hamano to Gouders for a repost as a two-part series including clar unit tests.
