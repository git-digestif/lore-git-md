This is a bugfix and platform-compatibility patch -- specifically the fourth and final patch of Elia Pinto's `[PATCH v2 0/4]` series addressing object-size truncation on platforms where `unsigned long` is 32 bits (notably 64-bit Windows) while `size_t` is 64 bits. The series as a whole targets the pack-receiving and verification pipeline; this patch focuses on the fsck path.

The change is mechanical but important: it converts the `size` parameter from `unsigned long` to `size_t` in three places -- the `verify_fn` typedef in `pack.h`, the `object_cb` field of `struct odb_fsck_options` in `odb.h`, and the `fsck_obj_buffer()` implementation in `builtin/fsck.c`. On 32-bit-`long` platforms, the old signature caused `git fsck` to pass a truncated size for objects of 4 GiB or more, so `parse_object_buffer()` and `fsck_object()` inspected only a prefix of the object rather than the full content.

The patch explicitly notes a remaining gap: `parse_object_buffer()` and `fsck_object()` themselves still accept `unsigned long`, so until those are converted in a follow-up series (linked in the cover letter but not yet queued), fsck will still inspect only a prefix even with this fix applied. No test is included because reproducing the bug requires objects >=4 GiB on a 32-bit-`long` platform.

This is patch 4/4 of the v2 series based on master at `e1ec09b23c`. The series remains under review with no merge status indicated; v1 received no review comments. The other three patches in the series convert `builtin/unpack-objects.c`, `builtin/index-pack.c` (`write_compressed()`, `sha1_object()`, and related functions) to `size_t` to fix inflate failures, false SHA-1 collision reports, and silent truncation during pack receiving.
