This is a surface-level review reply from Junio C Hamano on the fourth and final patch of Dirk Gouders's v2 series fixing bounds handling in `utf8_strnwidth()` and `utf8_width()`.

Junio approves the substance of patch 4, opening with "Well spotted, and nicely done." The patch fixes `utf8_strnwidth()` so that when it calls `utf8_width()` for the final glyph, it passes the remaining byte count (`orig + len - string`) as the optional boundary argument instead of `NULL`, preventing the width calculation from reading past the caller-supplied end of the buffer. It also adds unit tests covering CJK characters truncated by the `len` argument.

His only feedback is cosmetic: he flags an extra space in `if (skip_ansi )` and suggests breaking the overlong `while` loop condition across lines for readability. He raises no technical objections to the bounds-checking logic. With this patch accepted in principle and only style nits outstanding, the series appears to be converging toward a possible v3 or merge.
