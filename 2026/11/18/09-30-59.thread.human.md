Patrick Steinhardt's four-patch series addresses a severe performance problem where `git maintenance run --auto` spawned `git reflog expire` on every run, causing nearly one-second hangs. The root cause, fixed in patch 3/4, was that the auto-condition in `builtin/gc.c` treated every reflog entry as unreachable because it skipped reachability checks, incorrectly comparing timestamps against `gc.reflogExpireUnreachable` instead of `gc.reflogExpire`. Patches 1/4 and 2/4 optimize the reftable backend to avoid writing new tables during dry-run and no-op expiration invocations, while patch 4/4 fixes a documentation typo. Benchmarks on git.git show the series dropping `git maintenance run --auto` from roughly 385 ms to 18 ms.

Review has been largely positive but remains open on several points. Karthik Nayak provided substantive reviews of patches 1/4 and 2/4 confirming their correctness, and Patrick clarified that a retained "future improvement" comment refers to a finer-grained optimization than the all-or-nothing skip implemented in patch 2/4. Toon Claes substantively approved patch 3/4's decision to skip reachability walks during auto-maintenance, agreeing the performance tradeoff is worthwhile, though he raised unanswered questions about commit message wording on patch 3/4 and a hash-size consistency nit on patch 2/4 that he judged not worth a reroll. Most significantly, maintainer Junio C Hamano has asked Patrick to confirm whether patch 1/4 is purely a performance optimization or actually fixes a correctness bug: specifically, whether the pre-patch code already discarded the committed reftable during `--dry-run` (making the patch a safe I/O elimination) or whether dry-run previously leaked state changes to disk. Patrick has not yet responded to this question.

The series remains under review with no Reviewed-by or Acked-by trailers recorded yet, and it has not been queued in any of Junio's integration branches.
