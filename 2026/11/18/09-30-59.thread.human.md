Patrick Steinhardt has submitted a four-patch series addressing slow reflog expiration during `git maintenance run --auto`, which was spawning `git reflog expire` unnecessarily on every run and costing nearly a second of wall time. The core fix in patch 3/4 reworks `reflog_expire_condition()` in `builtin/gc.c` to stop performing an expensive reachability walk during auto-maintenance and instead compare entry timestamps only against `gc.reflogExpire`, bringing auto-maintenance on git.git from roughly 385 ms down to 18 ms. Patches 1/4 and 2/4 optimize the reftable backend to skip disk writes during dry-run and no-op expirations, and patch 4/4 fixes a documentation typo in `maintenance.adoc`. The series is version 1, submitted for review, and not yet queued in any integration branch.

Review has been largely positive so far. Karthik Nayak provided substantive reviews of patches 1/4 and 2/4 with no objections, raising only a cross-patch question about a retained "future improvement" comment in `refs/reftable-backend.c`, which Patrick clarified refers to a finer-grained per-entry skip rather than the all-or-nothing optimization implemented. Patrick also declined a documentation nit as redundant and confirmed that the test added in patch 2/4 covers the dry-run path of patch 1/4. The most recent email comes from Toon Claes, who flagged a minor style inconsistency on patch 2/4: adjacent lines use `hash_algo->rawsz` for a `memcmp` but the fixed `GIT_MAX_RAWSZ` for a `memcpy`, asking whether they should match; Patrick has not yet responded, so this remains open. No reviews or acks have been recorded yet on patches 3/4 or 4/4, and substantive feedback on the reachability fix at the heart of the series is still pending.
