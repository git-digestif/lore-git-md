This is the cover letter for **v2 of a 4-patch bugfix series** addressing object-size truncation on platforms where `unsigned long` is 32 bits (notably 64-bit Windows) while `size_t` is 64 bits. Authored by Elia Pinto and based on master at `e1ec09b23c`, the series converts size parameters from `unsigned long` (and in one case `unsigned int`) to `size_t` across the pack-receiving and verification pipeline to prevent crashes, false collision reports, and silent truncation when handling objects of 4 GiB or more.

The series touches `builtin/unpack-objects.c`, `builtin/index-pack.c`, `builtin/fsck.c`, `odb.h`, and `pack.h`. Patch 1/4 converts `builtin/unpack-objects.c` to use `size_t` throughout, fixing an "inflate returned -5" failure for non-streamed large objects, a crash when a delta base exceeds 4 GiB, and silent truncation when writing large delta results; it moves a `cast_size_t_to_ulong()` guard into `write_object()` so oversized objects die with a clear error instead of being written truncated, pending updates to `odb_write_object()` and `parse_object_buffer()`. Patch 2/4 changes `write_compressed()` in `index-pack` from `unsigned int` to `size_t` to fix a "serious inflate inconsistency" in `index-pack --fix-thin` when local bases exceed 4 GiB. Patch 3/4 converts `sha1_object()` and `append_obj_to_pack()` in `index-pack` to `size_t` to stop false "SHA1 COLLISION FOUND" errors for large objects already present locally. Patch 4/4 updates the `verify_fn` callback in `pack.h` and `fsck_obj_buffer()` in `builtin/fsck.c` to `size_t` so `git fsck` inspects full objects rather than prefixes.

Changes from v1 include rebasing onto current master, where commit `e07ac16d1c` (by Kristofer Karlsson) already converted `stream_blob()` to take `size_t`; patch 1/4 therefore drops that hunk and rewrites its commit message around the remaining affected paths (non-streamed objects, `unpack-objects -n`, and delta handling). The cast previously removed from `unpack_delta_entry()` is now placed in `write_object()` to protect downstream callees that still accept `unsigned long`. Commit messages were also clarified to specify that the issue arises where "long" is 32 bits while `size_t` is 64. No tests are included because reproducing the bugs requires 4 GiB objects or 32-bit platforms, though the messages describe verifying fixes by truncating sizes to 16 bits in simulation.

The cover letter notes that full correctness for non-streamed objects depends on follow-up series (not yet queued) that convert `odb_write_object()`, `parse_object_buffer()`, and `fsck_object()` to `size_t`; links to those series are provided. Separately, a related `pack-objects` fix for streamed large blobs has already been merged as `ed0aed1e17`. There were no review comments on v1, and the series remains under review with no merge status indicated.
