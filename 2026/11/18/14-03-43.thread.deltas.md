## 2026/11/18

- [2026/11/18/14-03-43 by John Mellor <johnme@google.com>] John Mellor reported that on Linux, when a directory is moved into the working tree from outside, the built-in fsmonitor daemon fails to watch the directory itself (only its subdirectories), making edits to files directly inside it invisible to status, diff, add -u, commit -a, stash, checkout, and rm; he diagnosed the cause in compat/fsmonitor/fsm-listen-linux.c where rename_dir()'s "moved from outside the watch tree" branch does not call add_watch(path) before register_inotify() adds subdirectory watches, and suggested calling add_watch(path, data) in that branch as a possible fix, reproduced on 2.56.1 and next (720f3f593).
