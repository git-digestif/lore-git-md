This thread is a small, standalone documentation patch from Okhuomon Ajayi that adds three lines to the mailing-list etiquette section of `Documentation/MyFirstContribution.adoc`, telling new contributors to leave blank lines between paragraphs and to treat blocks of quoted text as paragraphs needing blank lines around them. The goal is readability: keeping short replies visible amid long quoted threads on `git@vger.kernel.org`. The patch touches only that one AsciiDoc file, with no code or test changes, and is not part of a numbered series.

Junio C Hamano has replied with an endorsement, saying "Will queue," which signals he intends to pick the patch up into his integration workflow, though he does not name a target branch. His reply carries two observations worth noting: he did not expect this guidance to live in MyFirstContribution, since contributors first encounter quoted text when reviewing others' patches rather than when submitting their own; and he finds it surprising that `CodingGuidelines`, `SubmittingPatches`, and `ReviewingGuidelines` say nothing about line-length limits for emailed text. Whether the paragraph-spacing guidance should be cross-referenced in those other documents, and whether the line-length gap deserves its own patch, remains open, but nothing in the thread is contested.
