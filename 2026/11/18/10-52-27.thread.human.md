This thread is about a small documentation improvement to Git's contributor guide. Okhuomon Ajayi proposed adding guidance to `Documentation/MyFirstContribution.adoc` instructing contributors to leave blank lines between paragraphs and around quoted text blocks, so short replies stay visible in long mailing-list threads. Junio C Hamano endorsed the change and said he would queue it, though he questioned whether that etiquette belongs in the first-contribution walkthrough or in `SubmittingPatches` or `ReviewingGuidelines`, where contributors more typically encounter quoted text.

In his latest reply, Okhuomon thanked Junio and reported that a separate patch is already in progress to address another gap Junio had noticed: none of `CodingGuidelines`, `SubmittingPatches`, or `ReviewingGuidelines` currently mention line-length limits for emailed communications. That follow-up will touch all three documents.

The original patch remains queued in Junio's workflow -- not merged to `master` or any named branch -- and there are no other reviewers or objections. The open threads are the placement question for the paragraph-spacing guidance and the pending line-length patch, which may prompt later discussion about keeping the related guidance consistent across the documentation set.
