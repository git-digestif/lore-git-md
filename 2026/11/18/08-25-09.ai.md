This is a review reply from Patrick Steinhardt on the first patch of Christian Couder's three-patch LLP64 platform-compatibility series. The patch changes the `size` parameter of `write_buf_to_worktree()` in `notes-merge.c` from `unsigned long` to `size_t` to prevent silent truncation on 64-bit Windows when writing notes larger than 4 GiB during a delete/modify conflict.

Patrick's review is brief but substantive: he confirms the change is "obviously correct" because the value is passed to `write_in_full()`, which already accepts a `size_t`. This verifies that no narrowing occurs at the downstream boundary, directly addressing the truncation concern raised in the commit message. He also remarks on the general error-proneness of implicit casts in C when mixing `size_t` and `unsigned long`.

Within the series context, this patch prepares `notes-merge.c` for the removal of the `size > LONG_MAX` guard in `xdl_do_merge()` that arrives in patch 3, and builds on recently merged work `jk/xdiff-size_t` (81db32ddce) and `kn/safer-mem-arithmetic-in-xdiff` (1c93cc03ea). No objections or change requests are raised; the review signals clear approval of the approach.

The series remains under review with no integration branch status yet. Testing of the LLP64-specific path requires 64-bit Windows with `GIT_TEST_LONG=1`, which standard Linux and macOS CI cannot exercise.
