This is a substantive review reply from Patrick Steinhardt addressing patch 4/4 of Elia Pinto's `[PATCH v2 0/4]` series, which converts the `verify_fn` callback signature in `pack.h`, the `object_cb` field in `struct odb_fsck_options` (odb.h), and `fsck_obj_buffer()` in `builtin/fsck.c` from `unsigned long` to `size_t` so that `git fsck` passes the full object size rather than a truncated prefix on 32-bit-`long` platforms.

Patrick raises a sharp correctness question about the residual gap acknowledged in the patch: even with the callback now receiving the true 64-bit size, `parse_object_buffer()` and `fsck_object()` still internally accept `unsigned long` and therefore inspect only a prefix of objects >=4 GiB. He asks whether these functions actually detect that they received a truncated buffer and report an error, or whether they silently validate only the prefix and miss corruption in the unexamined tail -- which he notes would itself be a bug.

The question targets the fsck verification path in `pack-check.c` (`verify_pack()`) and its consumer `fsck_obj_buffer()`, probing whether the size-truncation currently causes silent acceptance of incomplete data rather than a hard failure. No resolution is offered in this email; it remains an open question about error-handling behavior in the object-parsing internals that the series depends on but does not yet convert.
