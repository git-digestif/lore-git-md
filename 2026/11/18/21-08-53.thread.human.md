This thread begins with the fourth and final patch of a v2 series from Dirk Gouders that fixes a boundary-handling bug in `utf8_strnwidth()` in `utf8.c`. The problem is that when the function is called with a length that cuts through the middle of a multibyte UTF-8 character, the inner call to `utf8_width()` was not receiving the boundary information, causing it to read past the requested length and potentially miscount the display width. The fix computes the remaining byte count at each iteration and passes it to `utf8_width()`, so incomplete sequences at the cutoff are handled correctly and the function returns the requested length in those cases, as documented.

The patch also adds a clar unit test, `test_utf8_width__strnwidth_cjk_chopped`, to `t/unit-tests/u-utf8-width.c` that verifies the fix using a CJK string with cutoffs of 1 and 3 bytes -- both of which split multibyte characters -- asserting the resulting widths are 1 and 2 respectively. As of this email, no review feedback has appeared yet; the series is under review with no decisions recorded.
