This is a review reply on the first patch of Christian Couder's three-patch series addressing LLP64 (64-bit Windows) truncation bugs.

Patrick Steinhardt responds to the proposal to change `write_buf_to_worktree()` in `notes-merge.c` from `unsigned long` to `size_t`, agreeing that the fix is correct. He notes that the value flows directly into `write_in_full()`, which already accepts a `size_t`, so the widening introduces no new narrowing at the boundary. His tone is casually supportive -- he jokes that nobody would likely hit a 4 GiB note conflict in practice, but agrees the change is the right thing to do regardless, and laments the error-prone nature of implicit casts between `size_t` and `unsigned long` in C.

The review is brief and surface-level in depth: it confirms the technical soundness of the one-line signature change without raising new edge cases or alternative approaches. No objections are recorded, and the patch appears to have a clear path forward within the broader series, which also widens ODB write APIs and removes a now-redundant `LONG_MAX` guard in `xdl_do_merge()`.
