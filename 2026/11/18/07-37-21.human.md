This is a thread-management reply confirming an authorship handoff for a bounds-safety patch to `utf8.c`. Dirk Gouders accepts Junio C Hamano's proposal to take over the patch, agreeing that it should become `[PATCH v2 1/2]` under his name as the first part of a two-patch series.

The underlying work adds `display_mode_esc_sequence_len_counted(const char *s, size_t len)` to scan ANSI SGR escape sequences within a bounded buffer without reading past `s[len]`, addressing out-of-bounds reads in non-NUL-terminated strings. The original `display_mode_esc_sequence_len()` remains as a wrapper, and three internal callers in `utf8.c` now use the counted variant.

With Dirk now committed to reposting the series under his authorship, the patch is poised to advance as a properly attributed contribution, likely incorporating the clar unit tests he previously drafted for `t/unit-tests/u-utf8-ansi.c`. The unresolved question about ECMA-48 colon separators in SGR sequences remains open in the broader thread.
