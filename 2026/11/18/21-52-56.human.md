This is a review reply in the ongoing thread for the HTTP retry bugfix series (`[PATCH v3 1/5]`). Ravi Mistry responds to Patrick Steinhardt's three points of feedback on the first patch. On the suggestion to split the off-by-one counter fix and the Retry-After race fix into separate commits, Ravi declines, preferring to keep them together to avoid churning the series layout. Regarding Patrick's question about returning `HTTP_ERROR` instead of `HTTP_RATE_LIMITED` when `http.maxRetries` is zero, Ravi explains that this preserves existing behavior from the original 429 handling commit: callers like `discover_refs()` continue to print the standard curl error rather than switching to a custom rate-limited message. He acknowledges the coding-style nit about `case` indentation and says he will fix it in v4.
