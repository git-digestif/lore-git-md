This is a substantive review of the second patch in Ravi Mistry's v3 series, which adds exponential backoff for HTTP 429 retries when no Retry-After header is present. Patrick Steinhardt approves of the overall direction -- exponential backoff with a cap via `http.maxRetryTime` -- and casually suggests that adding jitter would also make sense, though he does not require it for this series.

His technical feedback targets the implementation in `handle_rate_limit_retry()`. He notes that the new `attempt` parameter being a signed integer is "a bit weird," and more substantively questions the doubling loop, pointing out that it could be expressed as a closed-form `MIN(http_max_retry_time, delay * (2 << (attempt - 1)))` rather than an iterative loop. These are constructive implementation-level comments, not objections to the feature itself.

The review is engaged and specific, citing the exact code being changed, which signals real attention to the patch. It does not raise any blocking concerns; at most it suggests minor cleanup of the backoff calculation and the counter type. Combined with the earlier feedback from Patrick on patch 1/5, this keeps the series on track, with the only outstanding structural question still being Junio's procedural point about how the 10-second default is introduced across the series.
