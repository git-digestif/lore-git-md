This is a review reply to the second patch in Christian Couder's three-patch series on LLP64 (64-bit Windows) compatibility. Patrick Steinhardt responds to the proposal to widen `odb_write_object()` and related APIs from `unsigned long` to `size_t`.

The review is substantive in a quiet way: Patrick traces the type flow through the call chain and confirms that every downstream consumer of the length parameter -- `hash_object_file()`, `format_object_header()`, `git_zstream`, and the backend `write_object` callbacks -- already accepts `size_t`, so the widening introduces no new narrowing at the boundaries. He notes that `odb_source_write_object()` was the only remaining `unsigned long` holdout and approves of adapting it in the same patch. He raises no objections and requests no changes.

One actionable note stands out: Patrick points out that this work overlaps with a patch series from Elia Spiros (referenced by message ID) that apparently already touched this function, and suggests that Couder rebase his series on top of that work to avoid conflicts. Otherwise, the tone is approval -- "Yeah, makes sense" and "we're good" -- indicating the patch addresses a real truncation hazard on platforms where `long` is 32-bit while `size_t` is 64-bit.
