This thread is a bugfix patch from Dirk Gouders, the third of four in a v2 series, correcting a boundary-handling bug in `utf8_strnwidth()` in `utf8.c`. When the function's `skip_ansi` flag causes it to skip ANSI escape sequences while computing display width, the old code did not check whether consuming those sequences had already advanced the pointer to the end of the allowed `len` boundary. An escape sequence ending exactly at `orig + len` could fall through to a `utf8_width()` call past the boundary, risking an out-of-bounds read. The fix adds an explicit `if (string >= orig + len) break;` after the ANSI-skipping loop, and comes with a new unit test, `test_utf8_width__strnwidth_ansi()` in `t/unit-tests/u-utf8-width.c`, covering the string `"\033[31mhello"` at lengths 4, 5, and 6 -- the length-5 case being the critical regression test where the escape sequence consumes exactly the full allowed length.

The patch is tightly scoped to `utf8.c` plus its test file, with a clear commit message and tests that directly exercise the fixed corner case. The cover letter for the four-patch series has not been seen in this thread, so the broader goal of the series and the other three patches remain unknown. The series is at version 2 and under review; nothing in this email indicates any integration status.
