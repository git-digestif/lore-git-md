This thread is about a bugfix for reflog handling during symref updates in Git's reftable backend. The issue, reported and fixed by 井上司 (Tsukasa Inoue), causes HEAD reflog entries to record the wrong OID or be omitted entirely when HEAD and its target branch are updated in the same transaction. The proposed fix introduces a helper function in `refs.c` to correctly resolve the new OID for reflog entries, ensuring consistency across both reftable and files backends.

The patch is well-prepared, with thorough test coverage and a clear explanation of the root cause: the new OID was being resolved from the *new target* before the transaction committed, leading to incorrect or missing values. Karthik Nayak provided substantive review feedback, focusing on implementation details like using `string_list_lookup()` for efficiency, ensuring reflog updates are scheduled last to avoid edge cases, and adding test coverage for transactions with `REF_TRANSACTION_ALLOW_FAILURE`. These suggestions aim to harden the patch against subtle correctness risks rather than just polishing surface details.
