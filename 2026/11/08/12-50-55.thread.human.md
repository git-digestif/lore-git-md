This thread is about a bugfix for reflog handling during symref updates in Git's reftable backend. The issue, reported and fixed by 井上司 (Tsukasa Inoue), causes HEAD reflog entries to record the wrong OID or be omitted entirely when HEAD and its target branch are updated in the same transaction. The bug affects both reftable and files backends but was masked in the latter due to its lack of atomic transaction support.

The proposed fix introduces a helper function, `ref_transaction_resolve_new_oid()`, in `refs.c` to ensure reflog entries record the correct OID by resolving symref chains within the transaction. The patch has undergone substantive review, with Karthik Nayak providing detailed feedback on implementation efficiency, edge-case correctness, and test robustness. 井上司 has addressed all review points, clarifying why a linear scan is necessary for correctness (due to late symref updates breaking sorted invariants) and confirming that `REF_TRANSACTION_ALLOW_FAILURE` scenarios are handled safely. The test suite has been enhanced to cover these edge cases, including a subshell-based cleanup pattern to improve robustness.

The patch is now ready for a v2 update, which is expected to land in Junio's `next` branch shortly. No merge to `master` has been reported yet. The fix aligns with broader architectural goals of centralizing reflog logic in `refs.c` and could support future transaction-level conflict resolution. Key technical details include the new helper function, its impact on both backends, and the expanded test coverage in `t/t1400-update-ref.sh`. The discussion has been highly technical and collaborative, focusing on correctness and maintainability.
