**Human-readable summary**

This thread is a small refactoring patch from Hussain Sitamouwala that moves Git’s `assume_unchanged` flag out of a global variable and into the per-repository settings struct. It’s part of the long-running effort to remove the `the_repository` global, and the change is purely mechanical—no user-visible behavior changes, no new tests, just plumbing updates in `update-index.c`, `environment.c`, and `read-cache.c`.

So far the patch has received no review feedback; it’s still waiting for someone to look at it. In a separate but related thread, Junio recently asked Hussain whether a sentence he’d agreed to remove from the partial-clone documentation was LLM-generated, but that’s just curiosity and doesn’t affect this patch’s status.
