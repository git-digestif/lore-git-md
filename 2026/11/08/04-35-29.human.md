This is a documentation patch discussion focusing on build toolchain compatibility. Junio C Hamano responds to Jeff King’s observation that the `[[name_only]]` anchor syntax in Julia Evans’s `git-diff(1)` EXAMPLES rewrite works in Asciidoctor but fails in the older Python AsciiDoc processor, causing xmlto validation errors. Junio suggests that the project has repeatedly considered dropping AsciiDoc support entirely, standardizing on Asciidoctor, and directing users who don’t use Asciidoctor to pre-rendered documentation from the `git-htmldocs.git` and `git-manpages.git` repositories. He muses about adding this change to the BreakingChanges document, though he acknowledges that it may not strictly belong there.

The email is brief and process-oriented, not a direct review of the patch itself. It advances the thread by framing the anchor syntax issue as part of a larger, recurring discussion about documentation toolchain support rather than an isolated technical problem. No resolution is proposed, but the message signals that the project may be open to a more decisive shift away from AsciiDoc in the future. The patch remains stalled on this compatibility question, alongside the unresolved presentation format dispute.
