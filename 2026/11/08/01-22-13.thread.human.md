This thread is about a refactoring patch for `git reflog expire` by Laveesh Bansal, which consolidates two separate loops (one for `--all`, one for named refs) into a single loop. The change was suggested by Junio Hamano during review of an earlier fix and is purely mechanical, preserving all existing behavior—error reporting, exit status, output order, and handling of dynamic refs like `@{-1}`.

The patch is under review, with Patrick Steinhardt providing substantive feedback. While he acknowledges the refactoring is sound and behavior-preserving, he suggests two improvements: the commit message should better explain *why* the change is needed (not just *what* it does), and the new unified loop presents an opportunity to deduplicate refs (e.g., if the same ref appears via `--all` and a command-line argument). The author has confirmed full test coverage, including reflog tests with reftable, SHA-256, and leak sanitization.

No objections or design concerns have been raised yet, but the discussion is now focused on whether to take the refactoring further by deduplicating refs in the loop. The patch touches only `builtin/reflog.c` and introduces no new symbols.
