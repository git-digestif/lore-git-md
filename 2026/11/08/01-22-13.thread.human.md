The first patch handles the mechanical refactoring without changing behavior, while the second—just posted as v2—adds deduplication to ensure each reflog is processed only once per invocation, even if named multiple times (e.g., via `--all` and a command-line argument).

Both maintainers have endorsed the approach, with Patrick’s earlier concerns now resolved. The implementation is confined to `builtin/reflog.c` and includes a new test in `t/t1410-reflog.sh` to verify deduplication. The change is justified as a deliberate improvement rather than a bug fix, preserving output order and per-ref reporting while avoiding redundant work. The series has passed the full test suite, including reflog tests with reftable, SHA-256, and leak sanitization.

No open questions or objections remain, and the series is now ready for integration.
