This thread concerns a two-patch v3 series by Laveesh Bansal that refactors `git reflog expire` in `builtin/reflog.c` to use a single loop for all refs instead of separate loops for `--all` and named refs, then adds deduplication so refs appearing both ways are not processed twice. The first patch is a mechanical unification with no behavior change; the second introduces a `strset` to skip already-seen refnames while preserving the original processing order, which the author explains is necessary because `--updateref` can move branch tips between passes and pruning outcomes are order-dependent.

Junio C Hamano originally suggested the refactoring and endorses the deduplication as a deliberate improvement. Patrick Steinhardt raised two substantive concerns in review: the performance characteristics of the dedup approach and whether the `--updateref` behavior change is a bug or an intentional improvement. Bansal's latest email closes the loop by confirming that v3 uses the `strset` approach for O(1) lookups rather than sorting, documents the `--updateref` semantics change explicitly in the commit message, and adds a dedicated test case that exercises the edge case where duplicated ref names previously could lose entries due to tip movement. The series remains under review, with the philosophical classification of the `--updateref` change still unsettled, and known limitations around worktree-prefix aliasing and non-existent names remaining undeduplicated.
