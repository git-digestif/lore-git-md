This is a workflow discussion in the ongoing `test-tool genbytes` series. Junio C Hamano replies to Kristofer Karlsson’s question about how to structure the first patch, which currently splits a file rename (`test-genzeros.c` → `test-genbytes.c`) from the functional changes. Junio prefers a single combined commit that both renames the file and modifies it in place, arguing that this makes the history clearer for future readers who use `git log` or `git show`. He explicitly does not take a position on whether to keep both `genzeros` and `genbytes` or rewrite all callers to use `genbytes`—that decision remains open for later discussion. The message is brief and focused on the mechanics of patch presentation rather than the technical design of the series.
