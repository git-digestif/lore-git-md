This is an Outreachy proposal update from Tanga Joris, refining their plan to improve Git’s command argument scanning and parsing. The proposal targets the December 2026 round and builds on Tanga’s prior contributions (e.g., fixing `git annotate` segfaults with `--git-completion-helper` and removing `NO_PARSEOPT` from built-ins).

The revised plan focuses on three concrete tasks: converting `git stash show` to use `parse-options` (fixing a bug where `-G`/`--pickaxe-regex` values are misparsed as stash names), addressing silent misparsing of numeric arguments in `git index-pack` and `git fetch-pack` (e.g., `--max-input-size=1k` being read as 1 byte), and improving `--no-index` detection in `git diff` (where short options like `-G` or `-S` can mask `--no-index`). The proposal also flags other candidates (e.g., `git daemon`’s manual option parsing) for potential follow-up work.

Tanga’s approach is methodical: each change is preceded by tests that fail on `master`, and the timeline accounts for review cycles. The proposal explicitly seeks feedback on whether to prioritize a full `parse-options` conversion for `git diff` or a targeted fix for its `--no-index` loop. The questions for mentors (e.g., which command to tackle first) reflect a pragmatic focus on execution.
