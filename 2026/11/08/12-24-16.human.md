This is a test infrastructure patch in the ongoing `test-tool genbytes` series. The author, Kristofer Karlsson, responds to Johannes Sixt’s suggestion to narrow the fix to just the performance-critical test (`t4205-log-pretty-formats.sh`) by replacing `tr(1)` with `printf`. Karlsson reports that while the `printf` approach works on Linux, it fails on macOS—outputting only the literal zeros and ignoring the large padding—making it unreliable for cross-platform use. He reiterates that generalizing the existing `genzeros` helper into a unified `genbytes` tool remains the most maintainable path forward, even if the immediate need is limited to one test. The thread is still open on whether the series will proceed as planned or pivot to a narrower fix, but this reply strengthens the case for the broader solution.
