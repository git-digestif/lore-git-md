This is Junio’s reply in the ongoing discussion about Farid Zakaria’s credential-cache daemon race-condition fix. The series (v2 of 3 patches) addresses two races: one where concurrent daemons unlink each other’s sockets, and another where `mkdir()` fails spuriously. Junio zeroes in on Farid’s earlier comment that the edge case—where a daemon’s socket is "stolen" by another process—might only matter during Git upgrades (old client + new daemon). Junio pushes back: any serious deployment would have a babysitter process to respawn dead daemons, so the edge case is not upgrade-specific and should be handled robustly. He also questions Farid’s confidence in ignoring existing code safeguards, given his admitted unfamiliarity with the codebase, and implies that reviewers’ time is better spent on issues the author understands. The tone is direct but not dismissive; the architectural concern about mixing `unix_ss_*` and `tempfile` APIs remains unresolved, and Junio’s reply suggests he expects a more thorough v3.
