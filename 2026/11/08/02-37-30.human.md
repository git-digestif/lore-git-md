This is a documentation patch, part of Julia Evans’s ongoing rewrite of the `git diff` man page’s EXAMPLES section. Jeff King (Peff) replies to the v2 patch with two technical observations about AsciiDoc compatibility and markup placement.

Neither issue affects the substance of the examples or the overall reorganization, but both need to be fixed before the patch can be queued.
