The scope has narrowed since v2, with `git column` excluded (its "must be first" rule for `--command` is intentional) and `git check-ref-format` deferred to Hussain Sitamouwala’s concurrent work. `git bisect` is now the sole priority, with `git unpack-objects` retained as a stretch goal. Cynthia’s preparation is thorough—she has reviewed related patches, contributed a microproject (merged to `master`), and tested edge cases in candidate commands.

Cynthia’s emphasis on backward compatibility (e.g., preserving `BISECT_LOG` contents for replay) and incremental progress (starting with `bisect_terms()` before `bisect_start()`) demonstrates a pragmatic approach.

Cynthia’s hands-on experience—testing command-line edge cases, reviewing patches, and contributing a microproject—suggests she is prepared to address these challenges.
