This is a follow-up ping to a single-patch bugfix series from Laveesh Bansal addressing option-parsing inconsistency in `git rev-parse` and `git shortlog`. The original patch makes both commands accept `--glob <pattern>` as two separate arguments, matching the long-standing behavior of `git log`, whereas previously they only recognized the `--glob=<pattern>` form. The fix touches `builtin/rev-parse.c` and `revision.c` to consume the next argument when `--glob` is encountered, and flips four previously known-breakage tests in `t/t6018-rev-list-glob.sh` to regular passing tests. The commit message cites the 2011 commit 0fc63ec4e7 that introduced the discrepancy.

The author notes that two weeks have passed since submission and confirms the patch still applies cleanly to current master at f7de814569 with t6018 passing. No review feedback or objections have been recorded yet. The email also signals that `--exclude` and `--exclude-hidden`, which share the same two-word parsing gap, will be submitted as a separate follow-up series if this patch is accepted.
