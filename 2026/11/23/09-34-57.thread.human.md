This thread concerns a standalone documentation patch from Gontu Sujith Reddy clarifying that `git commit --amend --date=now` sets the author date to the current time rather than preserving the original commit's date. The patch adds two lines to the `--date` entry in `Documentation/git-commit.adoc` to document this exception to the usual amend behavior.

Junio C Hamano has reviewed the patch and expressed mild opposition to the placement as written, arguing that singling out the amending case in the `--date` description implies it is the option's primary purpose. He suggests the clarification belongs instead in the `--amend` documentation, where it could note that the original author date is normally preserved but can be overridden with `--date`, alongside a mention of the related `--reset-author` option. He also flagged a minor consistency issue: the email envelope spells the author's name "Gontu Sujith Reddy" while the in-body From header and sign-off use "GontuSujithReddy".

The patch remains under review with no new version posted yet; the author has not yet responded to Junio's suggestion to relocate the note or address the name spelling.
