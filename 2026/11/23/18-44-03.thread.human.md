Junio C Hamano's latest "What's cooking in git.git" report (Nov 2026, #08) shows six topics graduating to `master` this cycle, including Taylor Blau's 15-commit series hardening subsystems against NULL config values and Patrick Steinhardt's CI speedups, while new topics queued for review address partial clones over stateless HTTP, object format compatibility checks, and UTF-8 width boundaries. Several architectural efforts remain in cooking, including pluggable ODB commit-graphs, rerere subcommands, and ongoing `the_repository` removal patches, alongside a few stalled or discarded items.

One stalled topic, Curtis Allen Smith's fix for false dirty statuses in `read-cache` when line-ending conversion alters file sizes, drew renewed attention when Torsten Bögershausen asked what was needed to advance it, calling the patch sound and offering to resend a related optimization. Junio replied that the topic had been paged out of his mind and that without the original author responding to comments he cannot affirm the fix resolves the issue; he then asked Torsten whether he would take over the topic by assembling both the original fix and the optimization into a two-patch series, potentially reviving it under new stewardship.
