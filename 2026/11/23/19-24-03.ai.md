This is a substantive review of a standalone documentation patch. Junio C Hamano responds to Gontu Sujith Reddy's proposal to add two lines to the `--date=<date>` entry in `Documentation/git-commit.adoc` clarifying that `--date=now` sets the author date to the current time when amending. Junio is mildly against the update as written, arguing that singling out the amending case in the `--date` description gives the impression that this is the option's primary purpose. Instead, he suggests that the fact that `git commit --amend` preserves the original author date belongs in the description of `--amend` itself, where it could also mention that users can override it with `--date` and that `--reset-author` is another relevant option in that context. He also notes a separate stylistic issue: the envelope Sender header spells the author's name as "Gontu Sujith Reddy" while the in-body From and sign-off use "GontuSujithReddy", and the project prefers consistent spelling across all three. The review identifies the correct placement for the behavioral note (the `--amend` documentation rather than `--date`) and connects it to related options (`--reset-author`), making it a substantive rather than surface-level critique.
