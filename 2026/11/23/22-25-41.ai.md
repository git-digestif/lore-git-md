This is a follow-up reply in the v4 review of a bugfix series targeting the bundle-uri subsystem. Aniket Gupta responds to Junio C Hamano's edge-case observation about patch 3/3, confirming that he can reproduce the issue: when `bundle_list_update()` fails to resolve a relative URI via `relative_url_gently()`, the bundle entry remains in the `list->bundles` hashmap with a NULL `uri` field, causing `fetch_bundle_uri_internal()` to emit a secondary "error: bundle '<id>' has no uri" message after the primary resolution errors. He agrees this additional error is redundant since the earlier messages already identify the problem, and notes that lists passed via `--bundle-uri` do not exhibit this because the whole list is rejected earlier.

Gupta proposes a concrete fix for v5: drop the bundle from the hashmap when its URI cannot be resolved, which he has tested locally with no change in clone behavior and passing bundle-uri tests. He explains that he deliberately avoids suppressing the error inside `fetch_bundle_uri_internal()` because a bundle advertised without any URI should still be reported as an error, preserving existing behavior for genuinely missing URIs. He commits to implementing this cleanup and adding a test case in the next iteration. The series remains under review, awaiting incorporation of this fix before potential queueing.
