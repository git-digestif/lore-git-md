This is a follow-up ping on a bugfix patch that makes `git rev-parse` and `git shortlog` accept `--glob <pattern>` as a separate argument, matching the long-standing behavior of `git log`. Laveesh Bansal sent this two weeks after the original submission to check that it had not been overlooked.

The underlying patch is minimal and well-documented: it teaches `builtin/rev-parse.c` and `revision.c` to consume the next argument when they encounter `--glob`, and flips four previously known-breakage tests in `t/t6018-rev-list-glob.sh` to regular passing tests. The commit message cites the specific 2011 commit (0fc63ec4e7) that introduced the inconsistency. In this ping, the author confirms the patch still applies cleanly to current master and that the tests pass.

The inconsistency being fixed is that `git log --glob 'heads/*'` works, but `git rev-parse --glob 'heads/*'` and `git shortlog --glob 'heads/*'` did not -- only the `--glob=heads/*` form was recognized in those commands. This is a small consistency fix with no on-disk format changes.

The author also signals that `--exclude` and `--exclude-hidden` share the same parsing gap and will be addressed in a separate follow-up series. No review feedback has been recorded yet in this thread.
