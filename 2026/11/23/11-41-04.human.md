This is a **review of a platform-compatibility bugfix** -- specifically Christian Couder commenting on the fourth patch in Elia Pinto's v3 series fixing 64-to-32-bit truncations on Windows (LLP64). The patch converts `struct text_stat` counters and `count_ident()` from `unsigned`/`int` to `size_t` in `convert.c` to prevent theoretical overflow when processing files over 4 GiB.

Couder suggests rewording the commit message to state plainly that on common 64-bit platforms `size_t` is 64 bits while `unsigned`/`int` are 32 bits, making the overflow risk clearer. More substantively, he proposes a concrete `EXPENSIVE,SIZE_T_IS_64BIT` test that generates a 4 GiB file of lone LFs (triggering the 2^32 wraparound scenario) and verifies `git ls-files -o --eol` still reports the file as text with LF line endings. He hedges, however, noting the project may not want to accumulate many such expensive tests. The review is technically engaged but acknowledges the practical cost of testing extreme edge cases.
