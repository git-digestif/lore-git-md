This is a substantive review from Junio C Hamano of Dirk Gouders' v4 documentation patch to `Documentation/gitcli.adoc`. While accepting the other editorial fixes in the series -- the grammatical agreement changes ("conventions", "When arguments") and the imperative bullet about splitting short options -- Junio objects to one specific wording change in the bullet describing how to disambiguate arguments that could be either revisions or paths. The v4 text says such arguments "can be disambiguated by placing `--` between them", replacing an earlier phrasing that said "before or after it". Junio argues the new wording is a regression because it inaccurately describes where the `--` separator may be placed.

He illustrates with concrete `git log` examples. For `git log main next` where both `main` and `next` exist as both files and branches, the user cannot simply insert `--` between the two ambiguous arguments in the order typed; the correct form is `git log next -- main`, placing the separator between the revision and the path. For a single ambiguous argument like `git log main`, the `--` can go either before (`git log -- main`) or after (`git log main --`) the argument. The earlier "before or after it" phrasing captured both possibilities, whereas "between them" misleads readers about valid separator positions.

The email references the prior iteration at https://lore.kernel.org/git/20261115070357.113875-1-dirk@gouders.net/ where the "before or after it" wording appeared, which Junio considers "much less error prone". The thread remains open with this objection pending; the other v4 changes are approved, but the disambiguation bullet needs to revert to the earlier phrasing (or equivalent) before the topic can be queued.
