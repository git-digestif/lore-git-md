This is a review reply on the third and final patch of Wasswa Edson's series converting `git credential` to use `OPT_SUBCOMMAND` from the parse-options API. Laveesh Bansal, who already gave a blanket `Reviewed-by` on the v2 cover letter, offers two non-blocking observations about patch 3/3.

First, he notes that `--end-of-options` now behaves the same as `--` after a subcommand (the behavior change Tanga Joris previously flagged as needing documentation), showing the new parsing accepts both markers equivalently. Second, he points out that while `git credential --git-completion-helper` now lists the subcommands thanks to the `OPT_SUBCOMMAND` conversion, the bash completion script still offers nothing for `git credential <TAB>` -- the same gap that exists for `git hook` -- and asks whether a dedicated `_git_credential` completion function is planned as a follow-up.

The review is surface-level in depth: it confirms the completion helper exposure works but does not dig into the dispatch logic itself. Both points are explicitly marked as not blocking, and the series remains in a converged state pending only the commit-message clarification Tanga Joris requested earlier.
