This is a maintainer review of a documentation patch clarifying `git commit --date=now` behavior. Junio C Hamano replies to Gontu Sujith Reddy's proposal to note the amending case in the `--date` option description, and the review is substantive: he argues the addition is misleading because `--date` overrides the author date whether creating a new commit or amending an existing one, so singling out the amend scenario suggests that is the option's primary purpose. He recommends instead documenting under `--amend` that the original author date and identity are preserved, noting that `--date` can override the date and `--reset-author` can change the author. He also flags a minor formatting issue with the name spelling in the patch's sign-off. The patch remains under review with the maintainer steering toward a different documentation approach.
