This is a small documentation patch. Gontu Sujith Reddy adds two lines to the `git-commit` man page clarifying that `--date=now` sets the author date to the current time when amending a commit, since amending normally preserves the original author date. The change touches only `Documentation/git-commit.adoc` and appears straightforward and well-scoped, filling a minor gap in the existing documentation of the `--date` option.
