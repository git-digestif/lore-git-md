This is the third and final patch in Kristofer Karlsson’s test infrastructure series, completing the introduction of `test-tool genbytes` to replace slow `tr(1)` invocations on macOS. The patch folds the existing `genzeros` command into `genbytes` by teaching the latter to accept "NUL" as a byte specifier, then updates the remaining 10 call sites in the test suite to use `genbytes NUL` instead of `genzeros`. The implementation is straightforward: a new `parse_byte()` helper maps "NUL" to the zero byte, and the usage message is updated to reflect the new syntax.

The patch touches eight files, mostly test scripts that previously relied on `genzeros` for generating large zero-filled files. Two scripts (`t1007-hash-object.sh` and `t5318-commit-graph.sh`) actually depend on the data being NUL bytes for correctness (hashing 5 GB of zeros or zeroing out parts of a commit-graph file), while the others only care about the size. The author preserves the existing behavior in all cases to avoid changing test semantics.

This is a clean, optional cleanup that reduces the test-tool surface by one command. The author explicitly flagged it as easy to drop if the project prefers to keep `genzeros` as a thin wrapper, but the change feels natural and low-risk. The series as a whole is a well-motivated performance improvement for macOS CI jobs, and this patch ties it off neatly.
