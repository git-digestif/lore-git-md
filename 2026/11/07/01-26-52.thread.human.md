This thread is a two-patch refactoring series from Junio C Hamano that removes the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY` macro from `unpack-trees.c` as part of the ongoing effort to eliminate the global `the_repository` variable. The first patch introduced a new helper function, `unpack_repo()`, to replace direct uses of `the_repository` with an explicit repository instance derived from function parameters. The second patch, just posted, is a purely mechanical rename of local variables from `index` to `istate` for consistency, with no functional changes—all tests continue to pass.

The series is now complete and under review. Junio is the sole participant so far, and the patches are uncontroversial, aligning with the project’s long-term refactoring goals. There are no open technical questions, though the temporary paranoid consistency checks in the first patch may be revisited later. The work is part of René Scharfe’s broader `the_repository` removal effort, which has been ongoing for some time. No further changes are expected unless reviewers raise concerns.
