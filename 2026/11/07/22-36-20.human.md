This is a gentle nudge from Junio about the stalled Coverity-inspired integer-overflow hardening series. The series, originally posted by Johannes Schindelin in early November 2026, consists of five patches that introduce new overflow-checking macros and use them to harden several Git subsystems against integer overflows and unsafe arithmetic.

The patches themselves are small, targeted, and uncontroversial in principle—prior Coverity-fix branches from the same author were merged without issue between July and October 2026. However, review feedback on three of the five patches (3/5, 4/5, and 5/5) remains unaddressed. Phillip Wood raised substantive points about platform-specific risks (e.g., `SSIZE_MAX` vs. `size_t` width mismatches on NonStop), macro portability (GCC-only `typeof` dependency), and simpler alternatives for truncation checks in `date.c`. Junio’s message confirms that patch 2/5 (the `http.c` type change) is settled and ready, but the rest are blocked on Johannes’s response.

The series is now in Junio’s "Waiting for response" queue and will expire from his integration branches if no further activity occurs. For anyone following the hardening effort, this is a heads-up that the patches may need a v2 to address the open review points before they can proceed. The absence of test coverage for the hardened edge cases could also become a sticking point if reviewers push for it.
