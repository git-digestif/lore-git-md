This is a **bugfix** for the `merge-ort` strategy, addressing the regression in `--no-overwrite-ignore` that Coy Geek reported. The patch ensures that Git respects the `--no-overwrite-ignore` option during real merges (not just fast-forwards) by propagating the flag through the merge machinery.

Aniket Gupta’s fix adds a `preserve_ignored` bit to `struct merge_options` and wires it up in both `merge-ort.c` and `builtin/merge.c`. The change is minimal: two lines of plumbing in the merge options and two lines to pass the flag from the command-line option to the merge backend. The tests cover both a direct collision and a directory-rename scenario, confirming that the ignored file is preserved and the merge aborts as documented.

The patch is well-scoped and directly addresses the FIXME comments left in the code. It does not touch other merge strategies or callers, so the risk of unintended side effects is low. The commit message and tests are clear, and the reproduction cases match the original report. This looks like a straightforward, correct fix for a real data-loss bug.
