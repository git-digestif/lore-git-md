The `test-tool genbytes` patch series, now at version 4, is ready to land in Junio’s `next` branch. Kristofer Karlsson introduced this two-command helper (`test-tool genzeros` and `test-tool genbytes`) to replace slow `tr(1)` invocations that were causing macOS CI jobs to run 70–79% slower than Linux ones—an 800-second bottleneck reduced to 200 seconds. The series converts 16 test scripts, including a late-breaking fix for a `test-tool genzeros | tr` callsite that appeared in Git 2.50.2.

Junio and Patrick Steinhardt have both reviewed the series and signaled it’s ready for integration. Junio’s final note simply acknowledges the trivial rebase needed to cover the new callsite; no further changes are expected. The implementation uses a static 256 KB buffer and `git_parse_signed()` for idiomatic parsing, and the literal-character interface (`genbytes 'a'`) has won out over more complex alternatives. All prior objections—from `printf` truncation on macOS to thread-safety concerns—have been addressed, and the series is now purely a matter of queueing.
