Core problem or goal
A critical bug in Git’s index subsystem causes silent corruption (null OIDs, missing entries) and crashes (`BUG: wt-status.c:585`) when both `core.splitIndex` and `index.skipHash` are enabled. The corruption occurs when the shared index is written, either explicitly via `git update-index --split-index` or automatically after sufficient changes.

Under review; no prior discussion or related patches identified. Severity is high: index corruption and crashes block users of split-index or skipHash optimizations (e.g., large repos with `feature.manyFiles`).
