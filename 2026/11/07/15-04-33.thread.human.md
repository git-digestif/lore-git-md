This thread is about a documentation patch from first-time contributor Hussain Sitamouwala that clarifies how Git orders fetches from promisor remotes in partial clones. The patch updates `Documentation/technical/partial-clone.adoc` to specify that remotes explicitly accepted from a server are tried first, with fallback to other promisor remotes if a fetch fails.

Junio C Hamano has reviewed the patch and, while he didn’t object to the technical content, raised concerns about its context and coordination. He asked whether the documented behavior is long-standing or newly introduced (referencing Christian Couder’s April 2026 commit `8808e61fd3`), and whether there’s a central coordinator for the recent flurry of promisor-remote patches—some of which appear to be Outreachy contributions. Christian Couder, the domain expert, has been CC’d, suggesting his input will determine whether the patch needs revision or can proceed as-is. No other reviews have been posted yet, and the patch remains under review. The thread’s next steps hinge on Christian’s response to Junio’s questions about the patch’s relationship to prior work and the broader effort around promisor remotes.
