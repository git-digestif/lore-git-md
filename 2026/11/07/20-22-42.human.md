This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about the future of `git format-rev`. D. Ben Knoble proposes a potential compromise to address Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s earlier doubts about the command’s necessity: split its functionality by moving the `--stdin-mode=revs` logic (formatting revision ranges) into `git diff-tree`, while keeping `--stdin-mode=text` (filtering arbitrary input text) in `git format-rev`. This would simplify `git format-rev`’s scope and invocation, making it more focused on its unique text-filtering use case—such as integration with editors or other tools that need to replace hashes with references in arbitrary text.

Knoble’s suggestion is pragmatic but leaves open whether the "revs" mode’s higher-level interface (e.g., parsing revision ranges) should still be available in `git format-rev` as an option. The proposal does not address the technical feasibility of modifying `git diff-tree` (which Haugsbakk found "too difficult"), nor does it comment on the v2 patch series itself, which remains unreviewed. The thread’s focus remains on design rather than implementation, and the series’ fate is still uncertain—it risks being dropped from `seen` without further engagement. For now, the patches’ motivation is preserved by the text mode’s unique functionality, but this exchange may reignite debate about whether the command’s scope should be narrowed.
