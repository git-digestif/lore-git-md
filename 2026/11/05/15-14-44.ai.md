This is a substantive review of the second patch in Joy Gundu’s v3 series, which moves `pack.packsizelimit` into the repository-scoped `repo_config_values` struct. Tanga Joris reports that the patch builds and passes key tests (`t1300`, `t5300`, `t7700`), confirming the change works as intended. However, the review identifies a significant procedural blocker: Junio’s recently queued patch `jc/freeze-repo-config-values` (in `seen`) explicitly prohibits adding new members to `struct repo_config_values` until it can operate on repositories other than `the_repository`. Since Joy’s patch adds `pack_size_limit` to that struct, it directly conflicts with Junio’s freeze, making integration impossible in its current form.

Tanga suggests splitting the series: the preparatory `memset` cleanup (patch 1/2) could proceed independently, while the core refactoring (2/2) would need reworking to avoid violating the freeze. The review also notes two minor issues: a double blank line in `repo_config_values_init()` (which should be fixed in patch 1/2) and an outdated cover letter that still describes v1-to-v2 changes instead of v2-to-v3. The merge conflict with Christian Couder’s `cc/pack-index-v3` in `object-file.c` is mentioned but appears to be a routine integration issue rather than a design problem.

The review is technically thorough and constructive, combining tested feedback with awareness of broader project priorities. The freeze on `repo_config_values` is the critical obstacle here, and Tanga’s suggestion to decouple the preparatory work from the blocked refactoring is pragmatic. The patch’s alignment with the `the_repository` removal effort remains valid, but the timing of Junio’s freeze now requires a tactical adjustment.
