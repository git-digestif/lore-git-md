This is the second patch in Joy Gundu’s two-patch refactoring series (v3) that moves `pack.packsizelimit` from a global variable into the repository-scoped `repo_config_values` struct. The patch itself is technically sound, well-tested, and uncontroversial, but a new obstacle has emerged: Junio’s recent patch `jc/freeze-repo-config-values` (currently in `seen`) explicitly freezes the `repo_config_values` struct to prevent new members until it can work on repositories other than `the_repository`. Tanga Joris reports that Joy’s patch conflicts with this freeze, making it unmergeable in its current form.

Tanga also tested the patch and confirmed that `pack.packSizeLimit` still functions correctly—`git repack -ad` respects the limit when set via `-c pack.packSizeLimit=1m`, producing multiple packs as expected. The tests Joy mentioned (`t1300-config.sh` and `t5300-pack-object.sh`) pass, as does `t7700`. There’s also a minor merge conflict with Christian Couder’s `cc/pack-index-v3` in `object-file.c`, but this is a routine integration issue rather than a design problem.

The preparatory patch (1/2) that uses `memset` in `repo_config_values_init()` is unaffected by the freeze and could potentially be merged independently. Tanga suggests sending it alone, though it’s unclear whether this would be worthwhile without the follow-up patch. The cover letter still references v1-to-v2 changes, omitting v3 updates, and there’s a minor formatting nit (double blank lines in `repo_config_values_init()`) that should be fixed in 1/2 rather than 2/2.

The broader context remains the `the_repository` removal effort, and the patch aligns with Patrick Steinhardt’s ongoing work. However, the freeze complicates the path forward—this isn’t a technical flaw but a coordination issue. The next steps will likely involve either waiting for the freeze to lift or revisiting the design to avoid modifying the struct. For now, the patch is blocked, but the underlying idea is still valid.
