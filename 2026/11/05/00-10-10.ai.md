This is a **design discussion** in the HTTP retry series, specifically about the default value of `http.retryAfter`. Ravi Mistry responds to brian m. carlson’s objection to the current default of `0` (immediate retry) by proposing a compromise: defaulting `http.retryAfter` to **1 second** instead of 30. This would align with curl’s `--retry` behavior (1s, 2s, 4s, ...) and avoid the aggressive backoff that a 30-second initial delay would introduce (e.g., 30s, 60s, 120s over three retries). The proposal acknowledges both Junio’s earlier feedback on v1 (that `0` is incompatible with exponential backoff) and Carlson’s server-operator perspective, while aiming to balance fast recovery with server protection.

The email does not introduce new code or tests but signals a willingness to revise the patch if the compromise is accepted.
