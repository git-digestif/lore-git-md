This thread is about a bugfix series by Aniket Gupta that addresses a regression in Git 2.41’s bundle-uri subsystem. When `git clone` or `git fetch` encounters a bundle list with an unresolvable relative URI (like too many `../` components), it now skips the problematic bundle with an error message—including anonymized URLs to prevent credential leaks—instead of dying. The series, now at v3, is technically complete and ready for Junio to pick up.

Derrick Stolee, the primary reviewer, initially questioned whether skipping unresolvable URIs was the right policy but has since confirmed it aligns with the feature’s original intent. His latest email is a retrospective note, not a request for changes: he acknowledges the series is ready and even suggests a low-priority follow-up (a config toggle to restore the old "die on error" behavior) as a `#leftoverbits` item. No further action is needed on the current series, which awaits maintainer pickup.
