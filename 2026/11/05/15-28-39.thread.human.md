The thread concerns a v4 patch series by Aniket Gupta fixing a regression in Git's bundle-uri subsystem (introduced in 2.41) where `git clone` or `fetch` would die when encountering an unresolvable relative URI in a bundle list. Instead of aborting the entire operation, the series now skips problematic bundles with an error message and falls back to fetching from the remote. The three patches introduce a non-fatal `relative_url_gently()` helper, preserve original URLs in error messages for diagnosability (dropping earlier credential-anonymization logic at Derrick Stolee's suggestion), and modify `bundle_list_update()` to gracefully handle resolution failures. Derrick Stolee has given an unconditional LGTM to the series, and Patrick Steinhardt's concerns about diagnosability have been addressed by naming the original input URL in error output.

Junio C Hamano has now provided maintainer review of patch 3/3, praising the rewritten commit message for clearly explaining that server-advertised lists stop parsing at the bad entry while `--bundle-uri` lists are rejected wholesale. He raised one minor edge-case observation: because `bundle_list_update()` inserts the bundle into the hashmap before attempting URI resolution, a failed resolution leaves a NULL-uri entry that could trigger a secondary "bundle has no uri" error during the subsequent iteration in `fetch_bundle_uri_internal()`. He characterized this as a minor point rather than a blocking objection. The series remains under review and has not yet been queued in `seen`.
