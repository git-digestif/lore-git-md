This thread is a three-patch bugfix series from Aniket Gupta addressing a regression in Git 2.41’s bundle-uri subsystem, where `git clone` or `git fetch` would die when encountering an unresolvable relative URI in a bundle list. The series refactors URI-handling to skip problematic bundles with an error message instead of failing outright, while also improving error message clarity and security.

The latest update focuses on the second patch, which plugs an information-disclosure hole by anonymizing credentials in error messages when `relative_url_gently()` fails. Derrick Stolee’s review is substantive but raises a policy question: whether anonymizing URLs in error messages is necessary, given that users already know the URLs they provided and logs should be sanitized elsewhere. He acknowledges the implementation is correct but questions the value of the anonymization step, especially since bundle-uri lists with credentialed URIs are inherently unsafe. The patch itself is uncontroversial in its mechanics, but its fate now hinges on this policy discussion.

The series remains under review, with the larger policy question—whether to skip unresolvable URIs or halt—still unresolved and blocking approval. Patch 1 (introducing `relative_url_gently()`) is conditionally approved pending resolution of this broader concern, while patch 3 (which implements the skip-or-halt behavior) awaits substantive feedback. The author has addressed prior feedback by splitting the series and tightening tests, but the core design choice remains open for debate.
