This thread concerns a three-patch v4 series by Aniket Gupta that fixes a regression in Git's bundle-uri subsystem (introduced in 2.41) where `git clone` and `git fetch` would die when encountering an unresolvable relative URI in a bundle list. The series introduces a non-fatal `relative_url_gently()` helper in `remote.c` and modifies `bundle_list_update()` in `bundle-uri.c` to skip problematic bundles with an error message naming the original URL, falling back to fetching from the remote instead of aborting the entire operation.

Review has converged positively. Derrick Stolee, who had suggested dropping the credential-anonymization logic in v3 (a change adopted in v4 alongside Patrick Steinhardt's request to preserve the original input URL in error messages for diagnosability), now gives an unconditional "LGTM" to the v4 series. He also confirms that a proposed follow-up configuration toggle to restore the old die-on-error behavior can be deferred as a leftover item rather than included in this series. Junio C Hamano's earlier concerns about credential anonymization are moot since that logic was removed entirely.

The series remains under review and has not yet been queued in `seen`. Once accepted, the change will make bundle-uri operations resilient to malformed relative URIs by logging the specific error and continuing with valid bundles or the standard remote fetch.
