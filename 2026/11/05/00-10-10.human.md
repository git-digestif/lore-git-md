This is a **human-readable digest** of Ravi Mistry’s reply in the HTTP retry series thread.

Ravi acknowledges brian m. carlson’s concern that the current default of `http.retryAfter=0` (immediate retry) risks overwhelming rate-limited servers. He agrees that zero is not a good fit for exponential backoff and proposes a compromise: default `http.retryAfter` to **1 second**, matching curl’s `--retry` behavior. This would yield delays of 1 s, 2 s, 4 s, … on successive retries, avoiding the aggressive 30 s → 60 s → 120 s ramp that a 30-second default would produce. The change is small (one-line config tweak) and keeps the series’ existing doubling logic intact. Ravi is ready to send a v3 if the 1-second default is acceptable.
