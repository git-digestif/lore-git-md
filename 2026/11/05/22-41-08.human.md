This is the ninth patch in Jon Simons' v2 performance series optimizing `git push` refspec matching. Junio C Hamano replies to the patch with a brief but substantive review.

Junio opens by calling the optimization "an obvious thing to do for performance" and notes that the performance numbers "look promising" — a clear endorsement of the patch's direction. The core of his feedback is a single architectural observation: the patch removes all in-file callers of the linear `count_refspec_match()` function but leaves the function itself in place for external callers. He wonders whether those external callers would also benefit from the new `strmap`-based variant (`count_refspec_match_in_map()`), which would require exposing the `ref_map_init()` machinery as well.

This is a forward-looking suggestion rather than a blocker; Junio does not request changes or challenge the patch's correctness or performance claims. The patch itself remains effectively approved, and the series as a whole continues to await broader review for the remaining patches. The question is narrowly scoped to whether the optimization should be extended to external callers in a follow-up effort.
