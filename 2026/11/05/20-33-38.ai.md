This is a refactoring series targeting the notes subsystem as part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. Okhuomon Ajayi posts a six-patch series that directly addresses the root cause of `the_repository` dependencies in `notes.c` by storing the repository in `struct notes_tree` and using it throughout the notes API. The series replaces the withdrawn protective measure patch and earlier attempts to guard unsafe functions in `notes-utils.h`.

The core change is mechanical: `struct notes_tree` now holds a `struct repository *` field, and every function in `notes.c` that previously used `the_repository` is updated to use the repository stored in the tree. This makes the API repository-agnostic without requiring guards. The ref glob helpers and combine_notes callbacks are also updated to take an explicit repository parameter. The final patch removes the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` guard from `notes-utils.c` and `notes-utils.h`, completing the cleanup.

Files touched are `notes.c` (primary), `notes-utils.c`, `notes-utils.h`, `notes.h`, and a handful of callers in `builtin/am.c`, `builtin/notes.c`, `notes-cache.c`, and `sequencer.c`. No user-visible behavior changes, CLI options, or on-disk format changes are introduced. The series is well-scoped and directly addresses Patrick Steinhardt’s architectural concern by fixing unsafe functions at the source rather than guarding downstream callers.

The series is clearly motivated and structured: each patch builds on the previous one, and the commit messages explain the mechanical changes without overreaching. The cover letter explicitly ties the work back to the earlier discussion and withdraws the protective measure patch, making the thread’s progression easy to follow. No merge status yet; the series is under review and likely to attract attention from René Scharfe (the `the_repository` removal driver) and Patrick Steinhardt (who raised the architectural concern).

Key technical details for follow-up discussion: the repository field in `struct notes_tree`, the updated signatures of `write_notes_tree()`, `init_notes()`, `add_note()`, and the ref glob helpers, and the removal of the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` guard. The series also leaves a few remaining uses of `the_repository` in `notes.c` (display notes, `expand_loose_notes_ref()`, and `the_hash_algo` sizes), which may become follow-up work.
