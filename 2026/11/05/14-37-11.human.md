This is a **substantive review** of Daniel Martí’s v2 bugfix for the `contrib/credential/libsecret` credential helper. Phillip Wood, a frequent and thorough reviewer, raises several technical concerns about the patch’s implementation and error handling.

The patch fixes a crash and silent data loss in the GNOME keyring backend by replacing the `SECRET_SEARCH_LOAD_SECRETS` flag with explicit secret loading. Phillip’s review focuses on three main areas: **error reporting**, **code structure**, and **testability**. He questions whether the patch’s error messages are appropriate (using `g_critical()` instead of `g_printerr()`), whether the code properly distinguishes between locked and erased credentials, and whether the race condition could be tested with a locked keyring. He also suggests structural improvements to avoid redundant cleanup paths and to check for NULL secrets even after the fix.

Phillip’s feedback is **technical and actionable**, addressing both correctness and maintainability. While he acknowledges the patch’s goal, he challenges some of its assumptions—such as whether a concurrently erased credential should be treated as an error—and proposes alternatives. His review does not reject the patch but highlights areas where the implementation could be more robust. The discussion now hinges on whether to refine the error handling and code structure before merging.
