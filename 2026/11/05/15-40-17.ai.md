This is a documentation patch—specifically, the second iteration of Julia Evans’s rewrite of the EXAMPLES section in `git-diff(1)`. The patch reorganizes the section into five sub-subsections (uncommitted changes, specific files/directories, branches or commits, summarizing output, and alternative displays), replaces the old footnote-style layout with AsciiDoc horizontal definition lists, and modernizes the examples by using `--merge-base` instead of the `...` syntax. It also restores the `AUTO_MERGE` example (reworded to “merge conflict”) and now mentions both `--staged` and `--cached` side-by-side, treating them as synonyms.

The v2 changes address two of Junio’s three objections: the `AUTO_MERGE` omission is fixed, and `--staged` is no longer promoted over `--cached`—instead both are presented as equally valid. The third objection—the presentation format—remains unresolved: Junio still prefers tables over horizontal definition lists because of line-wrapping and cut-and-paste issues, but Julia has not reverted to tables. No other reviewers have weighed in on the design questions, so the thread is effectively stalled on this single formatting dispute.
