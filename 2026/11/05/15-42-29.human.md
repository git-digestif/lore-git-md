This is a test-fix patch from Kristofer Karlsson that addresses a side effect of the ongoing `git describe` regression fix. The patch ensures that a specific test case (`--candidates=2` with disjoint histories) continues to fail as expected regardless of whether a commit-graph is present. Without this fix, the test passes when a commit-graph exists (due to the generation-number-based ordering introduced by Linus Torvalds' patch) but still fails without one, creating an inconsistent test outcome.

The change is minimal: it unsets `GIT_TEST_COMMIT_GRAPH` for the disjoint-history test, forcing it to run without a commit-graph and thus preserving the expected failure. Kristofer notes that this is a temporary measure; his larger series (which builds on Linus's patch) would remove the unset once the test passes in both modes. The patch is a pragmatic workaround to maintain test hygiene while the broader algorithmic debate (Linus's generation-number fix vs. Kristofer's alternative prototype) remains unresolved.

This is not a substantive review or endorsement of either algorithmic approach, but it does address a practical issue that would otherwise complicate testing. The patch is uncontroversial and likely to be merged as a follow-up to Linus's fix, assuming the latter proceeds. For now, it keeps the test suite consistent while the design discussion continues.
