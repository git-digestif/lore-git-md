This thread is about preparing Git’s test suite to support alternative object storage backends, a key step in Patrick Steinhardt’s ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The five-patch series introduces `GIT_TEST_DEFAULT_OBJECT_STORAGE_FORMAT`, allowing tests to specify non-default backends via payloads (e.g., `files://objdir`). While ~1,100 tests currently fail under this regime, fixing them is deferred to follow-up work.

The series is under review, with Karthik Nayak providing feedback on test design—specifically, a contested `for` loop in `t1423-ref-backend.sh`. Karthik suggested replacing it with `test_detect_ref_format` to avoid redundant CI execution, but Patrick pushed back, arguing the loop is necessary to directly test the ref storage format’s behavior, including payload interaction. The disagreement remains unresolved, though both agree the series is otherwise sound. No merge status has been claimed, and the patches are based on Patrick’s `ps/odb-object-storage-extension` branch. The work is foundational for future ODB backends but doesn’t yet introduce one.
