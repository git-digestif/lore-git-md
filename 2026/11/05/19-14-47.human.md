This is a small but important documentation update in the ongoing completion-fix series. Tanga Joris responds to Junio C Hamano’s feedback on the first patch of v4, which centralizes `--git-completion-helper` handling in `parse-options.c`. The original comment over-emphasized `fast-import`’s quirks (waiting for input, leaving temporary files) as if they were general concerns, when in fact they are unique to that command. The revised comment now clearly states that the function is only needed for programs that must handle help or completion requests *before* calling `parse_options()`, making it more accurate and broadly applicable. This change is purely about clarity and does not alter the patch’s behavior or technical approach. The series remains well-motivated, with all prior feedback addressed, and is still blocked on Christian Couder’s `cc/early-scan-options` series reaching stability.
