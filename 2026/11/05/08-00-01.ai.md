For future discussion tracking, this patch removes the `freebsd_task` definition from `.cirrus.yml`, which included environment variables like `GIT_PROVE_OPTS`, `GIT_TEST_OPTS`, and `MAKEFLAGS`, as well as scripts for installation, user creation, building, and testing.
