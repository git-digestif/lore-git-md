This thread is about a Rust bugfix that prevents a panic when formatting an `ObjectID` with an invalid hash algorithm. The patch, submitted by shrimech, replaces an unwrap with a fallback that displays raw hash bytes and appends an `:invalid-hash-algo-<id>` marker instead of crashing. The fix touches `src/hash.rs` and includes a regression test.

The discussion has clarified that the Rust implementation’s behavior—treating invalid algorithms as an error—is correct and preferred over the C code’s silent fallback to a default. brian m. carlson, the original author of the Rust `hash` module, confirmed this and referenced a related commit on their `sha256-interop` branch that makes the `algo` field non-public to prevent invalid states. Tanga Joris, who reviewed the patch, now suggests shrimech incorporate that commit into a revised version of the patch to address the root cause. Tanga also offered to review the update and asked brian to push the commit to a public branch for easier access.

The patch is still under review, with no maintainer decision yet. The next step is for shrimech to either update the patch with brian’s commit or leave it as a follow-up. No objections remain, but the patch’s readiness depends on whether this suggestion is acted upon.
