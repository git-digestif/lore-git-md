This is a review by Junio C Hamano of a patch that attempts to migrate the `diff_auto_refresh_index` global variable into the `repo_config_values` struct as part of the `the_repository` removal effort. Junio rejects the proposed approach, arguing that it does not meaningfully advance the goal of supporting Git as a library.

The review is substantive: Junio explains that `repo_config_values` is not a suitable location because it cannot be safely accessed with anything other than `the_repository`, which defeats the purpose of eliminating global state. He also notes that the configuration variable is synthesized from system, global, and repository-level settings, making it ill-suited for repository-scoped storage. The feedback is clear and technical, suggesting the patch needs a rethink rather than a minor adjustment.

No alternative solution is proposed in this email, leaving the author to reconsider how to properly scope the `diff_auto_refresh_index` setting. The patch is unlikely to proceed in its current form, and future discussion will likely focus on finding a more appropriate mechanism for repository-isolated configuration.
