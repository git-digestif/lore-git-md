This is the third patch in Andy Koppe’s five-part documentation series (v2) that aligns Git’s terminology with the 2024 redefinition of "pseudorefs" and "root refs." The patch updates six man pages and `gitdatamodel.adoc` to consistently use "root ref" for refs outside the `refs/` hierarchy (e.g., HEAD, ORIG_HEAD) and to clarify the distinction between root refs and pseudorefs.

Patrick Steinhardt’s review of this patch is **surface-level**, focusing on two minor editorial suggestions. First, he proposes dropping the word "also" from the new "root refs" entry in `gitdatamodel.adoc`, as the phrasing no longer flows from a prior list item. Second, he suggests adding cross-references to the glossary for the "root refs" and "pseudorefs" entries, which would help readers navigate between the pedagogical documentation and the formal definitions. Neither suggestion questions the technical content or the patch’s goals; both are stylistic improvements aimed at clarity and consistency.

The patch itself remains uncontroversial and nearly ready for merging. The two unresolved documentation nits from v1—ambiguous phrasing in `git-update-ref(1)` and outdated reflog storage details—are unrelated to this patch and have been deferred by Andy to a future effort. Patrick’s feedback here does not reopen those issues or introduce new technical concerns.
