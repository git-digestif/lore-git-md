It modifies the function’s signature and updates its callers in `builtin/am.c`, `builtin/notes.c`, and `sequencer.c` to pass the appropriate repository. The change is purely mechanical and does not alter behavior.

The patch removes the last remaining dependency on `the_repository` in `notes-utils.c`, allowing the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` macro to be dropped entirely. Callers in `builtin/am.c` and `builtin/notes.c` still pass `the_repository` because those files continue to use the global variable elsewhere, but `commit_post_rewrite()` in `sequencer.c` already has a repository parameter and now passes it through.
