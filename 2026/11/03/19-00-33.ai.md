This is a **grammar clarification reply** in the ongoing refactoring series to mark functions as `static`. Junio C Hamano responds to Patrick Steinhardt’s surface-level nit about the phrase *"A pair of functions ... were created"* in patch 1/4’s commit message. Junio explains that both singular (*"was created"*) and plural (*"were created"*) agreement are grammatically valid here, citing "Syntactic vs Notional Agreement." He notes that while singular is the formal choice, the plural form is justified because the "pair" represents two distinct functions, one of which is being removed while the other remains. The exchange remains purely stylistic and does not affect the technical content of the patch, which continues to be uncontested. No further action or reroll is implied.
