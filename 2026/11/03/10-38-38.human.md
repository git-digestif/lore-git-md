This is a bugfix patch addressing an inconsistency in how `git rev-list` handles replacement objects during connectivity checks. The author, Kristofer Karlsson, responds to Patrick Steinhardt’s substantive review with clarifications that resolve the two main technical concerns raised earlier.

Kristofer confirms that the test change in `t5413` was not written to accommodate a real-world edge case—it was simply preserving the existing (and unintended) behavior when the incremental connectivity check was introduced. This means the new behavior (rejecting packs where replacements mask missing objects) is unlikely to break existing workflows, as the original test expectation was not intentional.

On the second concern—whether removing `OBJECT_INFO_LOOKUP_REPLACE` from `tree-verify.c` affects other callers—Kristofer explains that `tree-verify.c` is only used by `git rev-list --connectivity-check=incremental`. The flag removal is therefore scoped to the connectivity check and does not impact other commands like `fsck` or `index-pack`. He also audited other users of `OBJECT_INFO_LOOKUP_REPLACE` and found no related issues, though he notes a separate potential bug in `git mktree` (where replacements could mask missing objects) that might warrant a future fix.

The patch is now on firmer ground: the design (ignoring replacements for consistency with `fsck` and other commands) remains uncontroversial, and the technical concerns about side effects have been addressed.
