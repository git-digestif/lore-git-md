This is a surface-level review of Johannes Welsch’s RFC proposing `git branch --switch`.

Pushkar Singh replies to the RFC with a polite but skeptical take. He points out that `git switch -c` already provides the exact same functionality and that the `git-branch` man page even recommends it for this use case. His concern is that adding `--switch` to `git branch` would blur the distinction between the two commands, which was a key motivation for introducing `git switch` in the first place. The review is brief and focuses on user-experience consistency rather than technical implementation; it does not engage with edge cases, code paths, or backward compatibility. No testing or alternative proposals are mentioned.
