This is a follow-up in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. Okhuomon Ajayi, an Outreachy contributor, replies to Christian Couder’s surface-level review of their patch that removes hard-coded use of the global `the_repository` in Git’s fetch negotiation logic.

The exchange is brief and purely stylistic. Okhuomon accepts both of Christian’s suggestions: replacing `n->data` with the already-introduced `ns` variable in the `mark_common()` call for improved readability, and moving the new `struct repository *r` parameter to the first position in `default_negotiator_init()` to align with Git’s convention of placing repository parameters first. Neither change affects functionality; both are about code clarity and consistency.

The patch remains under review with no substantive issues raised. The next version will incorporate these minor adjustments, keeping the series on track as part of the broader effort to eliminate `the_repository`. No acks or tested-by trailers yet.
