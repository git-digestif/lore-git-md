This is Junio’s reply to Karthik Nayak’s review of the first patch in Okhuomon Ajayi’s `the_repository` removal series. Junio raises a fundamental concern: the patch is premature because the underlying notes subsystem (`notes.h`) still hardcodes `the_repository` in critical functions like `write_notes_tree()`, `init_notes()`, and `load_subtree()`. Adding a `struct repository *` parameter to `notes-utils.h` functions is therefore misleading—it suggests the code can work with arbitrary repositories, but the implementation still depends on the global `the_repository`.

Junio proposes a protective measure: wrap the entire `notes-utils.h` header in `#ifdef USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` to prevent accidental use of these functions in contexts where `the_repository` is not available. His diff adds this guard, effectively blocking further refactoring until the lower-level notes functions are themselves made repository-agnostic. This is a clear signal that the series, as currently scoped, cannot proceed without deeper changes to the notes subsystem.

The tone is matter-of-fact but firm: Junio is not rejecting the goal, but he is insisting that the work must be done bottom-up rather than top-down. The thread’s earlier momentum (positive feedback from Patrick and Karthik) is now paused pending resolution of this architectural blocker. For a busy reader, this email is the key inflection point—it shifts the discussion from "when will this merge?" to "what needs to change first?" and makes it clear that the series will not land in its current form.
