This is a **terminology clarification** in the ongoing documentation cleanup series to align Git’s glossary with the 2024 distinction between "pseudorefs" (FETCH_HEAD, MERGE_HEAD) and "root refs" (HEAD, ORIG_HEAD). The discussion centers on how to best describe the *semantic* differences between pseudorefs and root refs. Patrick’s alternative emphasizes that pseudorefs can record multiple object IDs and carry metadata (e.g., FETCH_HEAD’s branch names and "not-for-merge" flags), while Junio’s tweaks ("additional" instead of "different", "extra" instead of "additional") aim to avoid implying pseudorefs are strictly less capable than refs. Both agree that pseudorefs are not handled by the ref backend and cannot be written by `git-update-ref` or listed by `git-for-each-ref`.
