This is the fourth and final patch in a **v2 series** improving Git’s HTTP transport resilience. It extends retry logic to **HTTP POST requests**, which carry the actual data during fetch/clone and partial-clone blob downloads. The patch extracts retry-wait logic into a shared helper (`http_retry_wait()`), defers error reporting until retries are exhausted, and ensures the `Content-Encoding: gzip` header is re-attached on retry. Tests verify that POST requests now survive 429 (rate-limiting) and 502 (bad gateway) errors, and that gzipped requests preserve their encoding across retries.

The patch is **well-motivated and technically sound**: it addresses a real gap (POST requests were previously not retried at all), the refactoring is clean, and test coverage is thorough. The only open question from earlier in the series—whether 5xx errors should be retried by default—remains unresolved, but this patch sidesteps controversy by respecting the existing `http.retryStatusCodes` configuration. The changes are narrowly targeted at Junio’s feedback, and the series as a whole now feels polished and ready for further review or integration.
