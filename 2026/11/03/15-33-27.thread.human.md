This thread is about Julia Evans’s effort to modernize the EXAMPLES section of the `git diff` man page. The current examples are outdated and hard to follow, so Julia reorganized them into five clearer subsections, replaced confusing syntax like `...` with `--merge-base`, and added cross-references to option definitions. The patch is documentation-only, touching only the man page files.

Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, has raised three main objections: the new presentation format (horizontal definition lists instead of tables) makes line-wrapping and cut-and-paste harder; the initial use of `--staged` instead of `--cached` was inconsistent unless done throughout the entire man page; and the omission of `git diff AUTO_MERGE`. Julia has conceded the latter two points—restoring `AUTO_MERGE` and reverting `--staged` to `--cached`—but the formatting dispute remains unresolved. Junio insists all three issues must be addressed before the patch can proceed.

The thread is now waiting on Julia’s next revision. She has not yet responded to Junio’s formatting concerns or the broader question of whether `--staged` should replace `--cached` everywhere in the man page. No other reviewers have weighed in, leaving the discussion stalled on these two sticking points. The patch itself is uncontroversial in content, but the presentation and consistency issues are blocking its acceptance.
