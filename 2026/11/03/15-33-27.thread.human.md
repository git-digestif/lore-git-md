This thread is about Julia Evans’s effort to modernize the EXAMPLES section of the `git diff` man page. The v2 patch reorganizes common invocations into five subsections, replaces outdated `...` syntax with `--merge-base`, and clarifies the relationship between `--staged` and `--cached`. Junio Hamano, the maintainer, has objected to the use of horizontal definition lists (citing cut-and-paste issues) and to the inconsistent handling of `--staged` and `--cached` across the man page. Julia addressed two of Junio’s concerns in v2—restoring the `AUTO_MERGE` example and presenting the two options as synonyms—but the core formatting dispute and the broader consistency question remain unresolved.

Tuomas Ahola’s recent email flags a mechanical oversight: the patch updates the full man-page synopsis but misses the corresponding change in the short `-h` help output, causing a test failure. The fix is straightforward and doesn’t touch the larger design questions. No other participants have weighed in on the substantive issues, so the thread is still stalled on Junio’s objections and the unresolved `--staged`/`--cached` consistency question.
