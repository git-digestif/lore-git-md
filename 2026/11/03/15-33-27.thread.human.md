This thread is about a proposed rewrite of the EXAMPLES section in the `git diff` man page, aimed at modernizing outdated examples and improving readability. Julia Evans, the author, reorganized the section into five subsections, replaced confusing syntax with clearer alternatives (e.g., `--merge-base` instead of `...`), and switched from tables to AsciiDoc horizontal definition lists for better terminal display. However, Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, has raised objections to the presentation format, the initial use of `--staged` over `--cached`, and the omission of `git diff AUTO_MERGE`.

The latter two issues have been resolved—Julia reverted to `--cached` and restored `AUTO_MERGE`—but the dispute over presentation format remains. Junio argues that horizontal definition lists introduce line-wrapping and cut-and-paste problems, while Julia has not yet addressed this concern. Additionally, Junio insists that if `--staged` is to be used, it must replace `--cached` consistently throughout the entire man page, not just in one example. No other reviewers have weighed in on the technical or design questions.

Junio’s latest email is procedural, acknowledging the challenge of interpreting historical examples but emphasizing that the mailing list is the appropriate forum for resolving such ambiguities. The thread remains stalled, with no clear path forward on the unresolved issues. The patch is documentation-only, touching `Documentation/git-diff.adoc` and `Documentation/diff-options.adoc`, and has no impact on code or tests.
