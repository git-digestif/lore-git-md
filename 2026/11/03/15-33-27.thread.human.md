This thread is about Julia Evans’s effort to modernize the EXAMPLES section of the `git diff` man page. The patch, now in its second iteration, reorganizes the examples into five clear subsections, replaces the confusing `...` syntax with `--merge-base`, and restores previously omitted elements like `AUTO_MERGE`. Julia has addressed two of Junio C Hamano’s three objections—restoring `AUTO_MERGE` and treating `--staged` and `--cached` as synonyms—but the core dispute over presentation format remains unresolved. Junio prefers traditional tables for better readability and usability, while Julia has stuck with AsciiDoc horizontal definition lists, which she argues avoid line-wrapping issues on 80-character terminals.

The thread is effectively stalled on this single formatting question. No other reviewers have weighed in, and Junio’s latest response reinforces his position without proposing a compromise. Until this is resolved, the patch won’t move forward. The broader question of whether `--staged` should replace `--cached` throughout the man page also remains open, though Julia has deferred that for now. For anyone catching up, the key takeaway is that the content updates are largely agreed upon, but the presentation format is the sticking point.
