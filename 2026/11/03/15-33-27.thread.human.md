Junio’s latest reply adds two more editorial corrections to the `--staged`/`--cached` presentation but doesn’t resolve the broader formatting or consistency questions. His tone remains firm: the patch won’t move forward until all presentation details are settled.

Julia has not yet responded to these latest corrections or the unresolved disputes. No other reviewers have weighed in on the design questions, leaving the thread stalled.
