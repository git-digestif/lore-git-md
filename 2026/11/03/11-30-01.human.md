This is the third patch in Andy Koppe’s five-part documentation series (v2) that aligns Git’s terminology with the 2024 redefinition distinguishing "pseudorefs" (FETCH_HEAD, MERGE_HEAD) from "root refs" (HEAD, ORIG_HEAD, etc.). The series updates man pages, glossary entries, in-code comments, and pedagogical documentation to replace outdated uses of "pseudoref" with "root ref" and correct related inaccuracies.

Patrick Steinhardt’s review of this patch is **surface-level**, focusing on two minor editorial suggestions. First, he proposes dropping the word "also" from the newly introduced "root refs" section in `gitdatamodel.adoc`, arguing that it no longer makes sense in the revised context where "root refs" is its own list entry. Second, he suggests adding cross-references to the glossary for the "root refs" and "pseudorefs" entries, which would help readers quickly access the formal definitions. Neither suggestion questions the patch’s substance or identifies any inaccuracies; both are purely about readability and navigation.

The patch itself remains uncontroversial and appears ready for merging. It updates six man pages and `gitdatamodel.adoc` to consistently use "root ref" for refs outside the `refs/` hierarchy, corrects minor inaccuracies (e.g., removing ORIG_HEAD from `git-update-ref(1)`’s reflog documentation), and incorporates Julia Evans’s proposal to expand the "References" section of `gitdatamodel.adoc`. Two unresolved documentation nits from Patrick’s earlier review—ambiguous phrasing in `git-update-ref(1)` and an outdated reflog storage explanation—are still present but acknowledged as out of scope for this series. The new suggestions here are even more minor and could be addressed in a final revision or as follow-up patches without delaying the series.
