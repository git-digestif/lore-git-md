This is the sixth patch in the v7 `git repo info` path-keys series, focusing on the **`path.grafts` keys**. The email is a reply to Justin Tobler’s review, which raised two points: a mechanical nit about redundant error handling and a **substantive design question** about whether the patch should exist at all, given that graft files are deprecated and slated for removal.

The mechanical nit is resolved: the redundant `if (!graft_file)` checks in `get_path_grafts_absolute()` and `get_path_grafts_relative()` will be removed, as `repo_get_graft_file()` already BUGs on NULL. This is a minor cleanup with no functional impact.

The design question is the crux of the discussion. Justin argues that including `path.grafts.absolute` and `path.grafts.relative` may not be worthwhile because grafts are deprecated and will be removed (per `Documentation/BreakingChanges.adoc`). K Jayatheerth acknowledges this but notes that the keys were included based on earlier discussions and the GSoC idea page. The patch itself is technically sound—it follows the series’ pattern of exposing repository component paths and aligns with `git rev-parse --git-path` precedent—but its long-term utility is now in doubt. The decision hinges on whether the project prioritizes completeness (including deprecated features) or alignment with deprecation plans.

The patch is otherwise ready for review, with no other technical issues raised. If the series progresses, this patch may be dropped or kept based on maintainer preference. The thread highlights the **critical risk of abandonment** for the entire series due to lack of reviewer engagement, making this design question a potential blocker.
