This is a documentation review of Julia Evans's patch to rewrite the EXAMPLES section of `git-diff(1)`. Junio C Hamano raises three substantive concerns in his reply, shifting the discussion from a straightforward readability improvement to a more nuanced design question about how examples should be presented and which command forms should be promoted.

The first concern is about the table format itself: Junio dislikes the line-wrapping behavior on 80-character terminals, which makes the examples impossible to cut-and-paste. He acknowledges that preferences vary, but his objection is concrete and technical, not merely stylistic.

The second concern is the replacement of `--cached` with `--staged` in one of the examples. Junio calls this a "severe regression" and points out that `--staged` is not mentioned in the SYNOPSIS section of the man page, only in the description of `--cached` where it is documented as a synonym. He insists that if `--staged` is to be promoted, it must be done consistently throughout the entire man page, not just in a single example.

The third concern is the omission of `git diff AUTO_MERGE`, which was present in the original EXAMPLES section. Junio asks why it was dropped and suggests it may have been due to an external limitation (e.g., a constraint on table size), but the commit message does not explain the omission.

The review is substantive: Junio engages with the content of the examples, not just their presentation, and identifies specific regressions in functionality and consistency. No tested feedback is provided, but the points raised are precise and actionable. The thread is now at a decision point: whether to proceed with the table format, whether to promote `--staged`, and whether to restore `AUTO_MERGE`. The author will need to address these concerns before the patch can move forward.
