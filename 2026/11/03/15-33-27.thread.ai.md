Core problem or goal
Rewrite the EXAMPLES section of the `git diff` man page to modernize usage patterns, improve readability, and replace outdated syntax. The revised version organizes common `git diff` invocations into five subsections, replaces the `...` syntax with `--merge-base`, and adds cross-references to option definitions. The presentation format (AsciiDoc horizontal definition lists vs. tables) and the broader `--staged`/`--cached` consistency question remain contested.

No maintainer decision or merge status recorded; the thread remains stalled on the formatting dispute, the broader consistency question, and now the `-h` help output oversight.
