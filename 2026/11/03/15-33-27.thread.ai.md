Core problem or goal
Rewrite the EXAMPLES section of the `git diff` man page to reflect modern usage patterns and improve readability. The current examples are outdated and difficult for users to follow; the new version organizes common `git diff` incantations into five subsections, replaces confusing `...` syntax with `--merge-base`, and adds cross-references to option definitions. The presentation format has evolved from a table to AsciiDoc horizontal definition lists to avoid line-wrapping issues on 80-character terminals, though this change remains contested.

Current status
Under review. No prior versions. Julia Evans has conceded the latter two points (reverting `--staged` to `--cached` and restoring `AUTO_MERGE`), but the presentation format dispute (tables vs. horizontal definition lists) and the broader `--staged`/`--cached` consistency question remain unresolved. Junio’s latest email reinforces the collaborative review process but does not signal a shift in his position or propose a resolution. No maintainer decision or merge status recorded.
