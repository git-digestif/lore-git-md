Core problem or goal
Rewrite the EXAMPLES section of the `git diff` man page to modernize usage patterns, improve readability, and replace outdated syntax. The revised version organizes common `git diff` invocations into five subsections, replaces the `...` syntax with `--merge-base`, and adds cross-references to option definitions.
