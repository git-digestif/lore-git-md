This thread is a follow-up to Justin Tobler’s recently merged `jt/odb-keep-lockfile-fix` series, addressing post-merge review feedback with two small but important error-handling improvements in Git’s object-file subsystem. The first patch adds warnings when unlink operations fail, preventing silent failures that could leave behind `.keep` files. The second patch ensures errors from `git index-pack` are properly propagated, even though callers currently ignore the return value—a cleanup Justin now confirms was theoretical rather than a response to a real-world bug.

The series is already cooking in Junio Hamano’s `next` branch, with both patches deemed "trivially correct" and ready for integration. Patrick Steinhardt had raised a substantive question about whether the second patch addressed a latent issue or was merely a cleanup, but Justin’s latest reply clarifies it’s the latter, closing the discussion. Karthik Nayak’s review was surface-level, and no objections remain. The changes are narrowly scoped to `object-file.c`, with no new tests or on-disk format changes, and the prior version of the series remains in `next` as a base. The only loose end is Junio’s minor stylistic preference about preserving an error code, but this doesn’t block progress.
