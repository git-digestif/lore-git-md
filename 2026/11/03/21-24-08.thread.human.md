This thread is a follow-up to Justin Tobler’s recently merged ODB lockfile fix, adding two small patches that tighten error handling in Git’s object-file subsystem. The first patch adds warnings when unlink operations fail, while the second ensures errors from `git index-pack` propagate correctly—even though the latter addresses a theoretical edge case rather than a known bug. Junio Hamano has already deemed both patches "trivially correct," and they are now cooking in the `next` branch for integration testing.

The discussion has been straightforward, with no substantive objections. Patrick Steinhardt initially questioned the motivation behind the second patch but was satisfied after Justin clarified it was a cleanup, not a fix for a latent issue. Karthik Nayak gave a surface-level thumbs-up, and Kaartic Sivaraam chimed in to agree with Junio’s stylistic preference on the second patch’s error-handling flow—but all agreed the change itself was fine as-is. The series is effectively ready to graduate to `master` once its time in `next` completes.
