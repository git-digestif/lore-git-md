This thread is a small follow-up to the recently merged `jt/odb-keep-lockfile-fix` series, addressing minor error-handling improvements in Git’s object-file subsystem. The two-patch series, authored by Justin Tobler, is now cooking in Junio C Hamano’s `next` branch after being deemed "trivially correct" by the maintainer.

The patches focus on preventing silent failures: the first adds warnings when unlink operations fail (replacing silent `unlink()` calls with `unlink_or_warn()`), while the second ensures errors from `git index-pack` are properly propagated rather than masked. The latter was initially questioned by Patrick Steinhardt but was clarified as a theoretical cleanup—no real-world bug was being fixed. Junio raised a minor stylistic concern about error-code relaying in the second patch, but accepted Justin’s explanation that the change was intentional and behaviorally neutral.

The series touches only `object-file.c`, with no on-disk format changes or new symbols introduced. While no test coverage was added, the patches are uncontroversial and ready for integration. The discussion has been surface-level, with no substantive objections or open questions remaining.
