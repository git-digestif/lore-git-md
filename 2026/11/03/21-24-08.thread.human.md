This thread is a small follow-up to the recently merged `jt/odb-keep-lockfile-fix` series, addressing post-merge review feedback with two mechanical error-handling improvements in Git’s object-file subsystem. The first patch, now cooking in `next`, plugs a silent failure mode where `unlink()` calls during ODB transaction aborts could fail without warning, potentially leaving behind `.keep` files that block packfile housekeeping. The fix replaces `unlink()` with `unlink_or_warn()` in three locations in `object-file.c`, ensuring users are alerted to cleanup failures. The change is narrow—no new symbols, no on-disk format changes, and no test coverage added—and is already undergoing integration testing. The second patch in the series (not yet summarized here) will address a related error-propagation issue in `git index-pack`. So far, there are no reviews or objections, and the series is a direct response to feedback on the earlier ODB lockfile work.
