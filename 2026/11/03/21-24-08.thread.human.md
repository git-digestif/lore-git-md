This thread is a follow-up to Justin Tobler’s recently merged ODB lockfile fix (`jt/odb-keep-lockfile-fix`), now cooking in `next`. The two-patch series tightens error handling in the object-file subsystem: the first patch adds warnings when unlink operations fail (replacing silent `unlink()` calls), and the second ensures `git index-pack` failures propagate as errors rather than being masked. Junio Hamano has confirmed both patches are "trivially correct" and ready for integration, though the series remains in `next` for now.

The discussion has focused on stylistic refinements to the second patch, which fixes an inconsistency in the original code where `read_index_pack_report()` overwrote the return value even on success. Justin’s change—using a separate variable to track the report’s return value—was initially met with mild pushback from Kaartic Sivaraam, who preferred the original flow. However, Junio later endorsed the change as a clear improvement after recognizing the inconsistency. Kaartic has since suggested a further tweak (initializing `ret` to `-1` at declaration) to better align with Git’s error-handling conventions, but framed it as a minor digression not worth a re-roll. No substantive objections remain, and the series is poised to graduate to `master` once integration testing completes.
