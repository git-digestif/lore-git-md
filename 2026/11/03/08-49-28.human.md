This is a refactoring patch series, part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The series updates `notes-utils.c` to stop using the global `the_repository` variable by explicitly passing a `struct repository *` parameter through the relevant functions.

The v2 series consists of two patches. The first patch updates `create_notes_commit()` and `commit_notes()` to use the repository parameter they already receive, replacing implicit reliance on `the_repository`. The second patch extends this change to `init_copy_notes_for_rewrite()` and its callers in `builtin/am.c`, `builtin/notes.c`, and `sequencer.c`, fully removing the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` macro from `notes-utils.c`. The callers in `builtin/am.c` and `builtin/notes.c` still pass `the_repository` for now, as those files continue to use the global variable.

The changes are mechanical and do not alter behavior. The commit message and cover letter are clear, and the author has addressed feedback from Patrick Steinhardt by extending the scope of the series to include `init_copy_notes_for_rewrite()`. This series looks well-crafted and aligns with the project's goal of eliminating `the_repository`. No new edge cases or functionality are introduced, and the patches are self-contained. The series is ready for review and appears uncontroversial.
