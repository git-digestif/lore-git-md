This is a brief follow-up to the already-merged `kk/quarantine-alternate-refs` patch, which fixed a performance regression in Git’s object quarantine mechanism by preventing the repository’s own refs from being listed as alternate refs during connectivity checks. The patch is now in Junio’s `seen` branch after he resolved a mechanical merge conflict with Patrick Steinhardt’s concurrent `odb-reload-alternates` series, and this email confirms the author’s review of the resolution.

Kristofer Karlsson, the patch’s author, replies to Junio’s integration update to express approval of the conflict resolution. He reviewed Patrick’s `odb-reload-alternates` changes and found no logical incompatibilities with his own patch, agreeing that Junio’s adjustments correctly preserved the core logic—introducing a `borrowed` flag to exclude the repository’s own object directory from `--alternate-refs`. The confirmation is purely procedural; no new technical concerns or performance data are introduced.

The patch remains on track for `next` and eventual inclusion in `master`. The broader discussion about the fragility of the alternate interface and potential long-term solutions (such as Justin Tobler’s proposed ODB transaction callback) is still open but not advanced by this email. For readers tracking the topic, this follow-up serves as a green light that the merge resolution is uncontroversial.
