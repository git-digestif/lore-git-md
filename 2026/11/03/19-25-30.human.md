The new reproduction uses a synthetic repository with three branches: `main` (commit B), `topic` (commit T), and `orphan` (commit R). The issue manifests regardless of `--graph-indent` settings, confirming it is not a rendering quirk but a logic error in how orphaned commits are handled in the graph layout algorithm.

The reproduction is self-contained and platform-independent, making it easy for other contributors to verify the issue.
