This is a documentation patch refining the recently proposed rewrite of the `git diff` man page’s EXAMPLES section. Julia Evans follows up on her own patch to suggest replacing the table format with Asciidoctor’s horizontal definition lists. The motivation is to keep the compact, cheat-sheet feel while avoiding awkward line wrapping on 80-column terminals. She notes that the change is reversible—removing the `[horizontal]` attribute would revert to a traditional vertical list—so the risk is minimal. No new examples or technical content are added; this is purely a formatting tweak to improve presentation. The patch remains under review with no other feedback yet.
