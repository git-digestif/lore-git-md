This is a small refactoring guardrail patch from Junio C Hamano, part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The patch adds a comment block to `environment.h` that explicitly forbids adding new members to the `struct repo_config_values` until the structure is made usable with arbitrary repository instances, not just `the_repository`. The comment cites a discussion thread (linked in the patch) where Patrick Steinhardt and others explored how to generalize the settings machinery. The patch itself is a single-line change (plus the comment) and carries Patrick’s Ack. It is a clear signal to contributors that any future work on this structure must first address its current limitation.
