This is a **bugfix and performance patch** targeting Git’s object quarantine mechanism. Junio C Hamano reports that he has resolved the merge conflict between Kristofer Karlsson’s patch and Patrick Steinhardt’s concurrent `odb-reload-alternates` series, allowing the patch to proceed toward integration in `next`.

The patch fixes a measurable performance regression in `git-receive-pack`’s connectivity check, where the quarantine environment unnecessarily lists the repository’s own refs as alternate refs. By marking alternates as "borrowed" (from another repository) or not, the patch avoids redundant traversal of hidden refs, delivering a 67.7% speedup in repositories with 200,000 hidden refs and a 17.6% improvement even without hidden refs. The fix repurposes `GIT_QUARANTINE_ENVIRONMENT` to carry the parent repository’s object directory path, avoiding new APIs or fragile path comparisons.
