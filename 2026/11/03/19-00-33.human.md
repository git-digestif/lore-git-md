This is a brief grammatical clarification in the ongoing refactoring series to mark functions as `static` where no longer needed. Junio C Hamano responds to Patrick Steinhardt’s minor nit about subject-verb agreement in the commit message for patch 1/4. Junio explains that while "pair" is grammatically singular, it can also be treated as a plural subject when emphasizing the separable nature of its components—here, the two functions where one is being removed while the other remains. The exchange is purely stylistic and does not affect the technical content of the patch, which remains uncontested and aligned with the series’ goal of tightening encapsulation. No further action or reroll is expected.
