This is Junio’s review of the first patch in Ravi Mistry’s four-part series improving Git’s HTTP transport resilience. The patch fixes two bugs in the existing 429-rate-limit retry logic: an off-by-one retry counter and a race in Retry-After header parsing.

Junio’s feedback is brief and positive. He confirms the off-by-one bug is real and demonstrates it clearly with the new test. He also acknowledges the integer-wrapping risk when `http.maxRetries=0` and accepts the proposed fix. For the Retry-After race, he agrees moving the `CURLINFO_RETRY_AFTER` extraction into `finish_active_slot()` is the correct approach. The only point he questions is the removal of the early `HTTP_ERROR` return when retries are disabled, but he concedes it is redundant after the loop-condition change.

The review is substantive but narrowly focused on the two bugs described in the commit message. Junio does not raise broader questions about the series’ direction or suggest alternative approaches. The tone is matter-of-fact and constructive, indicating the patch is on track for eventual merging once the series as a whole receives sufficient review.
