This thread is a bug report from Jean Abou Samra about a flaw in `git diff --word-diff`. When comparing files that differ by both textual changes and whitespace additions (such as blank lines), the tool produces incorrect output—matching words out of context and omitting large unchanged sections. The report includes a minimal reproduction case with three files and exact commands to demonstrate the issue, which persists regardless of the `--diff-algorithm` setting.

The problem appears to be a genuine bug in Git’s word-level diffing logic, likely tied to how it handles line boundaries or whitespace when computing word-level similarity. The report is clear, well-structured, and actionable, making it a credible candidate for attention from maintainers of the diff subsystem. No responses or reviews have appeared yet, so the issue remains open and unaddressed.
