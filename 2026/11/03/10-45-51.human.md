This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about the future of `git format-rev`. Kristoffer Haugsbakk clarifies the current status of his proposed alternative to the command: while he initially speculated that `git diff-tree` could be modified to replace `git format-rev --stdin-mode=revs`, he now reports that the codebase is too complex for him to implement this change. The key functionality of `git format-rev --stdin-mode=text` (turning hashes into references) remains unsupported by the alternative approach, leaving the original command with a clear niche.

The exchange with Ben Knoble narrows the open question: the series’ motivation is intact for the `--stdin-mode=text` use case, but the broader design uncertainty persists. The patches themselves remain uncontroversial and well-structured, incorporating all prior feedback, but the thread’s fate is still tied to whether the command’s necessity can be resolved. Junio C Hamano’s earlier warning about dropping the series from `seen` due to lack of reviews still applies, and no new technical feedback has emerged in this email. The discussion now hinges on whether the remaining use case justifies the command’s existence—or if further work on `git diff-tree` could eventually subsume it.
