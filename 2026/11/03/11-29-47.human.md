This is the second patch in Andy Koppe’s five-part documentation series to align Git’s terminology with the 2024 distinction between "pseudorefs" (FETCH_HEAD, MERGE_HEAD) and "root refs" (HEAD, ORIG_HEAD, etc.). The series is uncontroversial and appears ready for merging, with all substantive feedback from v1 addressed in v2.

Patrick Steinhardt’s reply here is a **surface-level review** of Patch 1/5, focusing on the phrasing of the glossary’s "pseudoref" entry. He suggests a wording tweak to better capture the semantic difference between pseudorefs and root refs, emphasizing that pseudorefs can record multiple object IDs and carry metadata (e.g., FETCH_HEAD’s branch names and "not-for-merge" flags), while root refs behave like normal refs. His proposed text is more precise than Andy’s v2 version, which only highlighted storage differences. The change is editorial and does not alter the patch’s technical correctness or scope.

The thread’s prior discussion (including Junio’s clarification that pseudorefs are not handled by the ref backend) already established the key technical points Patrick reiterates. His feedback is unlikely to spark further debate, but it may prompt Andy to update the glossary entry in a v3 or as a follow-up. The rest of the series remains unaffected.
