This thread is about a small but important guardrail patch from Junio C Hamano that prevents new members from being added to `struct repo_config_values` in `environment.h` until the structure is made usable with arbitrary repository instances. The patch is part of the ongoing effort to remove the `the_repository` global variable, a long-running refactoring led by René Scharfe.

The patch itself is straightforward: it adds a comment block to `environment.h` forbidding new members until the structure is generalized. Patrick Steinhardt, a key contributor to the `the_repository` removal effort, has reviewed and acknowledged the patch, agreeing it’s the right short-term measure while the larger refactoring continues. There are no open questions or functional changes here—just a protective measure to keep the codebase in a stable state during the transition.

The patch remains under review, with no indication yet of when it will be merged. For now, it’s a quiet but necessary step in the broader cleanup work.
