This is the ninth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 12-part refactoring series that modernizes `git rerere`’s subcommand handling by switching to Git’s standard `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` mechanism. This patch extracts the "diff" action into a dedicated `cmd_rerere_diff()` function with proper `parse_options()` machinery, preserving the existing logic: it calls `rerere_cache_setup()` with `RERERE_READONLY`, iterates over the conflicted paths in `MERGE_RR`, and invokes `diff_two()` on each preimage.

The change is purely mechanical and preparatory for the upcoming `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` conversion (patch 11). It touches only `builtin/rerere.c`, moving 19 lines of code into a new function and updating the hand-rolled `if (!strcmp(argv[0], "diff"))` branch to delegate to the new function. The patch is uncontested and aligns with the series’ goal of internal consistency without behavior changes.
