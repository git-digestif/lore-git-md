The focus is on ergonomics—replacing cumbersome command chains with a single flag—rather than new functionality. No technical objections or design concerns have been raised, but the thread still lacks input from maintainers or core contributors on whether the feature aligns with Git’s design principles.

If Colomar submits a patch, it will likely target `builtin/ls-files.c`, with tests and documentation updates. The thread’s tone is collaborative, and the proposal appears well-motivated, though its acceptance hinges on broader community feedback.
