This is a review follow-up in the "bundle-uri: skip unresolvable relative URIs instead of dying" bugfix series. Derrick Stolee, the primary reviewer, has now fully approved the v3 series after initially raising policy concerns about the behavior change. His message here is a retrospective note rather than a request for changes: he acknowledges that the implemented behavior (skipping unresolvable URIs) aligns with the original intent of the bundle-uri feature, as evidenced by a TODO comment he himself wrote. The series is technically complete and ready for maintainer pickup.

The only new idea introduced in this email is a potential follow-up enhancement: adding a configuration toggle to let users opt back into the old behavior (dying on unresolvable URIs) if they prefer strict validation over graceful continuation. Stolee frames this as a `#leftoverbits` item — a low-priority, non-blocking suggestion — and explicitly states it should not delay the current series. The suggestion is motivated by use cases where users might want to avoid partial fetches from incomplete bundle advertisements, but it is not tied to any immediate need or regression. No action is required on the current series; this is purely a forward-looking note for future consideration.
