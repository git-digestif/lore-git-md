This thread is a pre-RFC discussion about a proposed refactoring in Git’s `fast-import.c` to replace manual pack iteration with the `repo_for_each_pack()` iterator, part of the broader ODB abstraction effort. The proposal, originally suggested by Outreachy applicant Joie "Joy" Joie, aimed to align `fast-import` with the project’s goal of abstracting object database access.

Patrick Steinhardt, the lead of the ODB abstraction effort, has now weighed in with a substantive rejection of the specific change. While he acknowledges the intent, he clarifies that `repo_for_each_pack()` is itself a transitional helper—not a long-term solution—because it still assumes a packfile-based backend. The real architectural goal is to eliminate low-level iteration entirely in favor of higher-level ODB interfaces, particularly ODB transactions, which Karthik Nayak is already developing. This means Joy’s proposed refactoring, while mechanically sound, is premature and will likely be superseded by Karthik’s work. No patch is expected from Joy on this change, and the discussion has shifted to the broader ODB transaction effort.

The thread is now effectively closed for this specific proposal, though it remains open as part of the larger ODB abstraction conversation. No other participants have weighed in yet.
