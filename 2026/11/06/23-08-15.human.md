This is a documentation patch, the third in Andy Koppe’s five-part v3 series that aligns Git’s terminology with the 2024 distinction between *root refs* (e.g., HEAD, ORIG_HEAD) and *pseudorefs* (only FETCH_HEAD and MERGE_HEAD). The patch updates six man pages and `gitdatamodel.adoc` to replace outdated uses of “pseudoref” with “root ref” where appropriate, corrects inaccuracies, and improves clarity.

Junio’s review is overwhelmingly positive, calling the changes “very very much appreciated” and “a perfect fit.” He flags only three small points: (1) the `--refs` description in `git-ls-remote(1)` could be clearer about what is normally shown (HEAD but not ORIG_HEAD), (2) the “root refs” section in `gitdatamodel.adoc` should cross-reference the glossary’s naming-convention discussion, and (3) the `--merge` phrasing in `rev-list-options.adoc` could be slightly more precise. None of these are blocking; the patch is otherwise ready for merging once Andy addresses them. The series as a whole remains uncontroversial and appears on track for `next`.
