This is the second patch in Andy Koppe’s v3 series aligning Git’s documentation with the 2024 terminology change that narrowed "pseudoref" to FETCH_HEAD and MERGE_HEAD while introducing "root ref" for refs outside the `refs/` hierarchy. The series is a documentation cleanup with no behavioral changes, and this patch specifically updates the glossary’s `per-worktree ref` entry to reflect current reality.

Junio C Hamano reviews this patch and suggests two small editorial improvements. First, he proposes adding a note that no new root refs (those whose names do not end with `_HEAD`) are planned, implying that future per-worktree refs should be created under `refs/worktree/`. Second, he suggests clarifying that pseudorefs, despite being per-worktree, are not actually refs by prefixing the existing sentence with "Even though they are not refs, ". These are surface-level tweaks that do not affect the patch’s correctness or readiness; the series as a whole has already incorporated all substantive feedback and is uncontroversial.

The patch itself modernizes the glossary by replacing an outdated prose list of per-worktree refs with a clear, itemized AsciiDoc list. It now explicitly enumerates all per-worktree refs: root refs (HEAD, ORIG_HEAD, etc.), refs under `refs/worktree/` and `refs/rewritten/`, and pseudorefs. This directly addresses prior feedback about the glossary’s accuracy and completeness. The `worktree` entry is also updated to cross-reference the `per-worktree ref` definition, eliminating redundancy. The diff is clean, the commit message is clear, and the patch is part of a well-reviewed series that appears ready for merging once these minor suggestions are addressed.
