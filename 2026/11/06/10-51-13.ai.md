This is a surface-level review comment on the second patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s twelve-patch series modernizing `git rerere`’s subcommand handling. The patch converts the four existing `#define` flags (`RERERE_AUTOUPDATE`, `RERERE_NOAUTOUPDATE`, `RERERE_READONLY`, `RERERE_NOWAIT`) into an `enum rerere_flags` and documents each flag in the enum.

Karthik Nayak’s reply is a nit: he suggests documenting that `RERERE_NOAUTOUPDATE` outputs conflict resolution information to stderr instead of updating the index. The comment currently describes it only as the "reverse of `RERERE_AUTOUPDATE`," which Karthik feels is incomplete. This is a minor, stylistic suggestion about enum documentation; it does not question the patch’s correctness or behavior. The patch itself is uncontested and ready for integration, and this comment is unlikely to require a reroll unless Patrick chooses to incorporate it for clarity.
