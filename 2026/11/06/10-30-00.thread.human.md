This thread is about a patch series from Patrick Steinhardt aimed at speeding up Git's GitLab CI pipelines by optimizing test execution, SDK installation, and build configuration. The series is now in its third version (v3), with previous iterations (v1 and v2) already reviewed.

Key improvements in the series include faster Git for Windows SDK installation (reduced from 3 minutes to 10 seconds), optimized test scripts like `t0027-auto-crlf.sh` (20-25% faster across platforms), and significant speedups for specific tests such as `t5551-http-fetch-smart.sh` (10x faster on macOS). The series also introduces template-based repository initialization for `t1092-sparse-checkout-compatibility.sh` and excludes intermediate build artifacts on Windows, cutting artifact handling time in half. Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, has reviewed the series and stated he has no technical objections, calling the patches "interesting" and "fully made sense," which signals likely inclusion in the `next` branch unless other reviewers raise substantive issues.

Johannes Sixt (Hannes) recently tested the series on his Windows notebook and confirmed the performance gains, reporting a 32% reduction in runtime for `t0027` and an 11% reduction for `t1092`. His feedback aligns with Patrick's reported improvements and adds confidence to the series' effectiveness, particularly for Windows runners, which remain the critical bottleneck in GitLab CI. No new concerns were raised, and the series appears on track for integration. The only remaining open question is the continued slowness of Windows runners, but this is acknowledged as a broader issue not fully addressed by the current series.
