Patrick Steinhardt has posted a seven-patch series aimed at speeding up Git's GitLab CI pipelines, with measurable improvements across macOS, Fedora, and Windows runners. The series targets bottlenecks in test execution, SDK installation, and build configuration, and is now under review in its initial v1 form.

Key changes include optimizing Git for Windows SDK installation (3 minutes down to 10 seconds), caching SDK archives between runs, and streamlining test scripts like `t0027` and `t1092` to cut runtime by 20–40%. The final patch switches two high-ref-count test repositories in `t5551` and `t5559` to the reftable backend, slashing macOS test time from 20 minutes to 2 minutes. Build optimizations like `-Doptimize=debug` and excluding intermediate `.obj` files also contribute to faster Fedora jobs and artifact handling.

Windows runners remain the slowest, but the series delivers clear net gains. No reviews have landed yet, and the patches are not yet in Junio’s integration branches. The work stands alone, with no dependencies on other in-flight efforts.
