This is a documentation patch in Andy Koppe’s v3 series (3/5) that updates man pages and `gitdatamodel.adoc` to consistently use "root ref" instead of the outdated "pseudoref" for refs outside `refs/` (e.g., HEAD, ORIG_HEAD). The patch is uncontroversial and nearly ready for merging, but Junio C Hamano’s review raises a few minor but important clarifications and corrections.

The core goal remains unchanged: aligning documentation with the 2024 terminology change that restricted "pseudoref" to FETCH_HEAD and MERGE_HEAD only. This patch focuses on six man pages and `gitdatamodel.adoc`, ensuring they accurately describe root refs and avoid conflating them with pseudorefs. Junio’s feedback is largely positive, but he identifies three areas needing attention:

1. **`git-ls-remote(1)`**: The `--refs` option’s description ("Do not show peeled tags or refs outside `refs/`") is technically correct but could be clearer. Junio suggests adding a DESCRIPTION section upfront to explain the default behavior (showing all refs under `refs/` and HEAD, with peeled tags) before detailing how `--refs` modifies it. He also proposes tweaking the EXAMPLES section to avoid implying symbolic refs are special ("List all refs under `refs/` and `HEAD`" instead of "including `HEAD` and symbolic refs").

2. **`gitdatamodel.adoc`**: The new "root refs" section should reference the naming conventions (e.g., uppercase/underscore names ending in `_HEAD`) documented in the glossary. Junio also suggests dropping "so-called" from the "pseudorefs" section, as the term is now officially defined.

3. **`rev-list-options.adoc`**: The `--merge` description’s phrasing ("the first of `MERGE_HEAD`, `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD`, ...") is slightly awkward. Junio recommends rewording it to explicitly state that `<other>` is the first *existing* ref among those listed.

The patch touches seven documentation files and makes no behavioral changes. Junio’s feedback is editorial and does not challenge the series’ direction. The unresolved points are minor and should be straightforward to address in a v4. The series as a whole remains uncontroversial and appears ready for merging once these tweaks are incorporated. Key files updated: `git-worktree(1)`, `git-ls-remote(1)`, `git-update-ref(1)`, `config/core.adoc`, `for-each-ref-options.adoc`, `rev-list-options.adoc`, and `gitdatamodel.adoc`.
