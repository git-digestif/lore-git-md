This thread fixes a Windows-specific bug in `unpack-objects` where blob sizes over 4 GB were incorrectly truncated due to a type mismatch (`unsigned long` instead of `size_t`), and modernizes the test script `t5608-clone-2gb.sh` to ensure it actually verifies large-pack handling. The series, authored by Kristofer Karlsson, consists of two patches: one plumbing fix and one test assertion update.

Patrick Steinhardt reviewed the series, confirming the core issue and endorsing the fix. He raised a question about whether `fetch.unpackLimit`'s lack of effect on `git clone` was intentional, but Kristofer resolved this by citing historical commits showing the behavior is by design—`git clone` has always kept received packs, and `fetch.unpackLimit` was never meant to apply to it. The only remaining discussion point is a minor stylistic preference about test structure, which Kristofer addressed but did not consider significant enough to warrant a reroll. No further objections or technical concerns have been raised, and the series appears ready to proceed.
