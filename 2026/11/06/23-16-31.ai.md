This is a documentation/terminology patch in Andy Koppe’s v3 series (4/5) that updates in-code comments and help text to replace outdated references to "pseudorefs" with "root refs" where appropriate. Junio C Hamano’s reply focuses on one specific change in `revision.c`: the error message for `git log --merge` when no merge is in progress.

Junio points out that simply removing the word "pseudorefs" from the error message could confuse users, as the revised phrasing ("--merge requires one of MERGE_HEAD, CHERRY_PICK_HEAD, ...") might imply that the user must explicitly supply one of these refs as an argument. He suggests two alternatives: either retain the word "pseudorefs" (e.g., "one of the pseudorefs MERGE_HEAD, ...") or reword the message to be more operational ("--merge works only in the middle of a merge, rebase, etc."). The latter would avoid technical jargon entirely and clarify the actual requirement—namely, that the command only works when one of those refs already exists due to an ongoing operation.

The rest of the patch (updates to `builtin/for-each-ref.c`, `refs.h`, and `contrib/completion/git-completion.bash`) receives no objections and remains uncontroversial. This is a surface-level review comment on wording, not a substantive concern about the patch’s direction or correctness. The series as a whole is otherwise ready for merging, pending Andy’s response to this and a handful of similar editorial suggestions in other patches.
