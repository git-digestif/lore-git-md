This thread is about an Outreachy contributor’s proposal to modernize the argument parsing in `git column`. Fien Dora suggests replacing a manual early check for `--command=` with Git’s `early_scan_options()` API, which would allow more flexible option forms (like `--command branch` or `--comm=branch`) while keeping existing behavior for conflicting values. The change is small and self-contained, targeting `builtin/column.c` and likely adding tests to `t/t7003-column.sh`.

Fien has done their homework: they’ve tested edge cases, referenced prior work (including their own `t1402` test patch), and flagged a potential overlap with another Outreachy applicant’s proposal. The main open question is whether to preserve the current restrictions or adopt the full flexibility of `parse_options()`. This is a reasonable design decision to surface early, and the maintainers may weigh in on it. So far, the proposal looks uncontroversial and well-scoped for an Outreachy follow-up. No prior decisions or objections have been raised yet.
