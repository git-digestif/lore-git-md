This is patch 4/12 in Patrick Steinhardt’s v3 series modernizing `git rerere`’s subcommand handling. The patch removes the unused `--[no-]rerere-autoupdate` flag from the "status" and "diff" subcommands, hard-coding `RERERE_READONLY` in their call sites instead. The change is purely internal: no behavior change, no new CLI options, and no on-disk format changes. It touches only `builtin/rerere.c` (two lines) and is a mechanical refactoring that simplifies the codebase by eliminating dead flag propagation.

The motivation is preparatory: the flag was previously accepted but ignored by these subcommands, and removing it now makes the subsequent `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` conversion (patch 11) easier to reason about. The patch is uncontested and aligns with the series’ goal of bringing `git rerere` in line with Git’s standard subcommand-handling conventions. All callers of `rerere_cache_setup()` in "status" and "diff" now pass `RERERE_READONLY` directly, preserving the existing read-only semantics.
