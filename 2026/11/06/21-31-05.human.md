The change replaces a flat prose list with an itemized AsciiDoc list for better readability and future extensibility, and it updates cross-references in the "worktree" entry to point to the new definition.

The patch is uncontroversial and directly addresses prior review feedback. It aligns with the series' goal of consistent terminology around root refs and pseudorefs, following the 2024 redefinition (commit 993d57eded).
