This is a review exchange on the first patch of Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-part series to speed up GitLab CI pipelines. The patch replaces PowerShell’s `Expand-Archive` with `tar(1)` for extracting the Git for Windows SDK, cutting extraction time from three minutes to ten seconds, and adds caching to avoid re-downloading the SDK on every run.

Junio’s review is brief but raises two questions. First, he playfully questions whether the choice of tool (`tar` vs. `Expand-Archive`) should matter so much, though he clearly acknowledges the dramatic performance difference. Second, he asks whether the cached SDK could go stale—an important operational concern for CI reliability. The review also notes a minor style change (uppercasing parameter names in the PowerShell script) but doesn’t object to it.

The exchange is lighthearted and focused on practical concerns rather than technical correctness. The patch itself remains uncontroversial and well-justified by its measurable speedup. No substantive objections have been raised, and the discussion is likely to resolve quickly with a v2 that clarifies the cache-invalidation strategy. This is a low-risk, high-impact CI optimization that looks ready to move forward.
