This is a test modernization patch for `t5608-clone-2gb.sh`, part of a two-patch series. The patch addresses a long-standing but silent test breakage in Git’s large-object clone tests. Originally added in 2009 to verify that clones of repositories with packs larger than 2 GB work correctly, the test setup stopped producing the intended large packs after a 2016 change to `fast-import`’s default unpack limit. As a result, the test has been passing for years while actually testing something far smaller (under 10 MB) than its name suggests.

Kristofer Karlsson adds explicit assertions to verify that the packs involved are indeed larger than 2 GB (or 4 GB, in the follow-up tests added in 2026). The patch also fixes the setup so it once again generates the intended large packs by disabling `fast-import`’s unpack limit, and replaces a misleading `git clone` invocation with `git fetch` in the unpack-objects variant to ensure the test actually exercises the intended code path. The changes make the test more robust and cheaper to run—halving the setup time from 80 to 40 seconds in the author’s local testing—while ensuring it fails visibly if the setup ever regresses again. The patch is well-motivated, clearly explained, and touches only test infrastructure, making it a straightforward improvement.
