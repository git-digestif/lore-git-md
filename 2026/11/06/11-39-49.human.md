This is a brief follow-up in Patrick Steinhardt’s twelve-patch series refactoring `git rerere`’s subcommand handling. The discussion centers on the second patch, which converts four `#define` flags into an `enum rerere_flags` and documents each flag.

Karthik Nayak suggested expanding the documentation for `RERERE_NOAUTOUPDATE` to clarify its behavior—specifically, that it outputs conflict resolution information to stderr instead of updating the index. Patrick responds that the stderr output is not unique to `RERERE_NOAUTOUPDATE`; both it and `RERERE_AUTOUPDATE` print messages, just at different points in the code. He agrees, however, that the enum comment should clarify that `RERERE_NOAUTOUPDATE` prevents index updates while still updating the working tree. This is a minor documentation tweak and does not affect the patch’s correctness or behavior. The series remains uncontested and ready for integration.
