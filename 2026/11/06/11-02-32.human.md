This is a brief but substantive exchange in the ongoing ODB abstraction effort, specifically about Patrick Steinhardt’s five-patch series preparing the Git test suite for alternative object storage backends. The series introduces `GIT_TEST_DEFAULT_OBJECT_STORAGE_FORMAT` to let tests specify non-default backends, and adds payload support (e.g., `files://objdir`) to expose tests that hard-code assumptions about `.git/objects`. About 1,100 of 35,000 tests currently fail under this regime, but fixing them is deferred.

The email is Patrick’s reply to Karthik Nayak’s review, which called the series “a very easy read” and suggested a mechanical test improvement in patch 1/5 (replacing a `for` loop in `t1423-ref-backend.sh` with `test_detect_ref_format` to avoid redundant CI execution). Patrick declines the suggestion, arguing the loop is necessary to test the ref storage format’s behavior directly, not just Git’s compatibility with alternate stores. The disagreement is narrow and unresolved, but neither party objects to the broader series.

The series itself is well-motivated and touches key infrastructure (`setup.c`, `rev-parse.c`, `t/test-lib.sh`) to enable future ODB backends. The payload mechanism is a pragmatic placeholder until real alternatives exist. While the ~1,100 failing tests are a known loose end, the series is clearly a stepping stone rather than a final solution. No merge status is claimed, but the work looks serious and aligned with the ODB abstraction roadmap.
