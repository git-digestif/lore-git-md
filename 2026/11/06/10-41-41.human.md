This is a review reply from Karthik Nayak on Patrick Steinhardt’s five-patch series preparing the Git test suite for alternative object storage backends. The series is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort and introduces the `GIT_TEST_DEFAULT_OBJECT_STORAGE_FORMAT` environment variable to let tests specify a non-default backend.

Karthik’s feedback is minimal and positive. He calls the series “a very easy read” and has only one comment, on the first patch, which he notes could be left as-is. His earlier review of that patch suggested a mechanical improvement to `t1423-ref-backend.sh`—replacing a `for` loop with a single test using `test_detect_ref_format` to avoid redundant execution in CI—an idea that aligns with existing test patterns and looks sound. Beyond that, he confirms the change to `builtin/rev-parse.c` “makes sense” and was an obvious omission.

The series itself remains under review, with no merge status claimed. It is based on the 15th batch (2026-11-04) with Patrick’s `ps/odb-object-storage-extension` branch merged in. The ~1,100 failing tests are explicitly deferred to follow-up work; the current patches focus on plumbing and test infrastructure. Karthik’s review is substantive on the test script and surface-level on the rest, but his comments are narrowly scoped and do not raise objections.
