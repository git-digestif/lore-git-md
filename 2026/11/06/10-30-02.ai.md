This is a CI/build system patch, the second in Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch series aimed at speeding up Git’s GitLab CI pipelines.

The patch addresses a bottleneck in the Windows CI workflow: the build artifacts archive currently includes ~1,800 intermediate “*.obj” files that test runners never need. By excluding these files from the artifact, the download and extraction time is cut in half—from 34 seconds to 17 seconds per test job. The change is a simple two-line addition to `.gitlab-ci.yml` under the `build:msvc-meson` job’s artifact configuration. No other files or behaviors are touched, and the patch carries no risk of breaking the build or tests.
