This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch CI speedup series. It targets the `t0027-auto-crlf.sh` test script, which exercises Git’s CRLF conversion logic across 2600 test cases and is a known bottleneck on Windows, where it previously took over 10 minutes to run.

The patch replaces a loop that spawned ten separate processes (one `rm` and one `git checkout` per file) with a single `rm` and a single `git checkout` that handles all five files at once. The change is purely mechanical—no new behavior is introduced—but it cuts the test’s runtime by about 20 % across all platforms and saves roughly 2.5 minutes on Windows. The diff is confined to the `checkout_files()` helper in `t/t0027-auto-crlf.sh` and does not touch any other part of the codebase.
