This is the third patch in Andy Koppe’s v3 documentation series (5 patches total) that aligns Git’s terminology with the 2024 redefinition of "pseudoref" and "root ref." This patch updates six man pages to stop using "pseudoref" to describe root refs (like HEAD and ORIG_HEAD), replacing it with the now-official term "root ref." The changes are mechanical but important for consistency, as the glossary and other documentation have already been updated in earlier patches.

The patch touches `git-update-ref(1)`, `git-worktree(1)`, `git-ls-remote(1)`, `for-each-ref-options(7)`, `rev-list-options(7)`, `config/core.adoc`, and `gitdatamodel.adoc`. Key updates include:
- Correcting `git-update-ref(1)` to clarify that only HEAD (not ORIG_HEAD) gets a reflog by default, and fixing the scope of `core.logAllRefUpdates` to include root refs.
- Updating `git-worktree(1)` to explicitly state that both root refs and pseudorefs are per-worktree, and removing the outdated claim that they live directly under `$GIT_DIR` (which doesn’t hold for reftable).
- Fixing `git-ls-remote(1)` to describe `--refs` as omitting refs outside `refs/` (rather than "pseudorefs") and correcting a typo ("symbolics" → "symbolic refs").
- Expanding `gitdatamodel.adoc` to distinguish root refs and pseudorefs in separate subsections, with cross-references to the glossary for definitions.

The changes are uncontroversial and address prior review feedback. The only remaining loose ends are two minor editorial suggestions from Patrick Steinhardt (dropping "also" in `gitdatamodel.adoc` and adding glossary cross-references), which are surface-level and don’t block merging. This patch is ready for inclusion and completes the terminology cleanup for the man pages.
