This is Junio C Hamano weighing in on the policy question raised in patch 2/3 of Aniket Gupta’s bundle-uri bugfix series. The patch proposes anonymizing credentials in error messages when `relative_url()` fails, but Derrick Stolee questioned whether this is necessary. Junio’s reply is substantive and skeptical.

He challenges Derrick’s assumption that log-storing environments already sanitize command output, asking how they could do so without knowing the strings in advance. He then points out that the bundle-uri feature itself is unsafe if the bundle list contains credentialed URIs, making this patch’s protection moot in that context. The core question: is this patch fixing a non-existent problem?

Junio also surveys where `transport_anonymize_url()` is already used in Git. The existing call sites are all user-facing output (reflog entries, fetch/push reports, verbose messages), not error messages. This suggests the patch would introduce anonymization in a new context—error output—without clear precedent.

The tone is probing rather than dismissive. Junio is not rejecting the patch outright but is clearly signaling that the burden of justification is on the author. The thread’s next move will likely determine whether patch 2/3 survives in its current form or is dropped. The series as a whole remains technically ready for merging (v3 addressed all feedback), but this policy discussion could prompt a v4 if Aniket chooses to revise.
