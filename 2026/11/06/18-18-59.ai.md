This is a **maintainer response** in the ongoing `rev-list` option-parsing bugfix thread. Junio C Hamano challenges the architectural premise of the proposed v5 refactor by questioning whether the "early scan" is necessary at all. Instead of sharing option-recognition logic between the early scan and the main parser, he suggests eliminating the early scan entirely and solving the underlying problem (`.fetch_if_missing` and related defaults) in a different way: record explicit option settings during normal parsing, then synthesize the final configuration at the end. He points to the original commit that introduced the early scan (df11e19648, 2017) as a starting point for reevaluating whether the approach was ever optimal. The email does not endorse either the v4 patch or the author’s proposed v5 direction, but instead opens a new architectural possibility that would avoid duplicating parser logic altogether. The thread remains unresolved, with the maintainer’s suggestion now the primary open question.
