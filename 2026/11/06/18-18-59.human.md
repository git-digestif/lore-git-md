He suggests that the underlying problem (options like `--missing-only` needing to know about other options such as `--grep` or `--src-prefix`) might be better solved by recording explicit option settings and synthesizing the final configuration at the end of parsing, rather than scanning arguments twice. The email points to the original commit that introduced the early scan (`df11e19648`, 2017) as a starting point for reevaluating whether the two-pass approach is necessary at all. Junio does not reject the author’s proposed refactor outright but clearly signals that a simpler, single-pass solution may exist. The thread remains unresolved, with the architectural direction now open to rethinking from first principles.
