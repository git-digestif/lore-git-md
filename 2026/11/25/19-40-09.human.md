This is a maintainer approval for a bugfix series. Junio C Hamano has reviewed version 6 of Dirk Gouders's four-patch series fixing out-of-bounds reads in `utf8_strnwidth()` and `utf8_width()` and given it his green light.

The email focuses on one specific refinement in patch 1: in `strbuf_add_wrapped_text()`, Gouders reverted to using the NUL-terminated `display_mode_esc_sequence_len()` wrapper (which internally passes `SIZE_MAX` to the new counted variant) rather than manually computing `strlen(text)` and calling the `_counted` variant with an explicit length. Junio endorses this choice, noting it avoids the redundant `strlen()` call and lets the bounded scanner handle the termination check cheaply.

With the comment "All looks good. Will replace," Junio signals that the series is accepted and will be queued in his integration branch, replacing the previous version. This marks a transition from "under review" to effectively merge-ready. The series itself addresses real memory-safety issues where ANSI escape sequences or truncated multibyte characters at the buffer boundary could cause reads past the end of the input, and it comes with new unit tests in `t/unit-tests/u-utf8-ansi.c` and `t/unit-tests/u-utf8-width.c` covering the edge cases.
