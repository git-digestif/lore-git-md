This is patch 1/6 of the v5 series removing `the_repository` from the notes subsystem, a refactoring within the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. Okhuomon Ajayi introduces a new static helper `notes_tree_or_default()` in `notes.c` that encapsulates the repeated pattern of substituting `&default_notes_tree` when the passed `struct notes_tree *t` is NULL and then asserting `t->initialized`. Six public entry points -- `add_note()`, `remove_note()`, `get_note()`, `for_each_note()`, `write_notes_tree()`, and `prune_notes()` -- now call this helper instead of inlining the check. The commit message notes that `notes_tree_init()`, `free_notes()`, and `format_note()` deliberately keep their own initialization logic. This refactoring sets up the fix for the `copy_note()` NULL-dereference bug identified in the previous iteration by ensuring all public paths route through the default-tree substitution before any dereference, and it is a prerequisite for threading an explicit `struct repository *` through these functions in later patches of the series.
