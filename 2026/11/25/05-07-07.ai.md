This is a review reply from Junio C Hamano on the final patch (4/4) of Jean-Noël Avila's four-patch documentation series converting `git-fast-export.adoc` to the standard synopsis style. The review is largely surface-level but identifies several specific defects in the rewritten prose: a double space and the phrase "in addition of" (instead of "in addition to") in the `--mark-tags` option description, a stray tab character between "which" and "already" in the `--reference-excluded-parents` description, and an awkwardly truncated sentence "When asking to one of the following values:" in the `--reencode` option that reads like an incomplete edit of the original "When asking to 'abort'..." phrasing. Junio also questions whether the remaining single-quoted references to `'git rev-parse'` and `'git rev-list'` in the `<git-rev-list-args>` description should be converted to backticks for consistency with the rest of the conversion. He does praise the `[synopsis]` markup on the `gpgsig` line as clean. The series remains v1 and under review with no integration branch status mentioned; this feedback awaits a response or v2 from the author.
