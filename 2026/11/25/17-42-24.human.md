This is a substantive maintainer review of a bugfix patch, part of Dominik Maier's two-patch v2 series fixing `git archive` ZIP handling.

Junio C Hamano replies to René Scharfe's feedback on patch v2 1/2, confirming that the fix for the out-of-bounds pointer advance in `write_zip_entry()` is correct and straightforward. The patch prevents `path + args->baselen` from stepping past the end of the path buffer when `--prefix` is longer than an extra or virtual file's archive path, which previously caused `entry_is_binary()` to look up gitattributes along an arbitrary virtual path.

On the technical details René raised, Junio agrees that if the NULL-path handling were meant as an optimization, it belongs inside `userdiff_find_by_path()` rather than guarded at the call site in `entry_is_binary()`, though he notes the difference is not significant for current workloads. He also concurs with René's out-of-scope architectural observation: introducing a dedicated `struct archive_entry` prepared by `write_archive_entry()` would be a good longer-term direction to eliminate the per-entry mutations of `struct archiver_args` and the associated pointer arithmetic in the tar and zip handlers.

The thread remains in a converged state with no outstanding objections or change requests for this patch; both reviewers have signaled approval, and the discussion has shifted to optional future refactoring rather than correctness issues.
