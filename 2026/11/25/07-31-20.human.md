This is a surface-level review of a bugfix patch for `git submodule update --jobs`. Jacob Keller replies to Abhishek Agarwal's proposal with a `Reviewed-by:` trailer and a brief comment on the semantics of the default: he notes that treating "a reasonable default" as the number of CPUs (via `online_cpus()`) might be debatable, but since it matches the wording and behavior of `git fetch --jobs`, he finds it acceptable.

The patch under review fixes two broken behaviors introduced in v2.39: `--jobs=0` currently aborts with a `BUG()` crash, and negative values produce a misleading "data too large to fit into virtual memory space" error. The fix adds validation in `module_update()` within `builtin/submodule--helper.c` to reject negative values with a clear message and to substitute `online_cpus()` when zero is given, aligning the option with the existing `submodule.fetchJobs` config handling. It also updates the man pages for `git-submodule(1)` and `git-clone(1)` and adds three test cases in `t/t7406-submodule-update.sh`. With Keller's review, the patch now carries one `Reviewed-by` and remains under review with no maintainer decision yet.
