This is a brief review reply from René Scharfe in the ongoing discussion of Dominik Maier's two-patch v2 bugfix series for `git archive`. He concurs with Junio C Hamano's assessment that the fix for the out-of-bounds pointer in `write_zip_entry()` is correct and straightforward.
