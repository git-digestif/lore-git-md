Julian Maurin has posted a single-patch fix for a long-standing bug where `git push --no-thin` was silently ignored over smart HTTP remotes. The root cause was twofold: `transport-helper.c` still listed `TRANS_OPT_THIN` among unsupported options (a leftover from 2009), and `remote-curl.c` unconditionally passed `--thin` to `send-pack`. The patch removes the exclusion so the option reaches helpers, adds handling in `remote-curl.c` to toggle `options.thin`, documents the new `option thin` capability, and adds a trace-based test in `t/t5541-http-push-smart.sh`. The motivating scenario is pushing from a shallow treeless partial clone over HTTPS, where a thin pack triggers a lazy fetch per missing tree; `--no-thin` avoids that overhead but was not effective for HTTP.

Junio C Hamano reviewed the patch and responded "Looking good. Thanks.", confirming the fix is clean and correct. He suggested condensing the commit message to lead with the partial-clone scenario, and Maurin has now accepted that wording for a forthcoming v2. Both agree that a minor `#leftoverbits` tangent about `strncmp()` prefix-matching in `set_option()` is out of scope for this series.
