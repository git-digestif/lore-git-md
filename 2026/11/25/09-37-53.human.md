This is a surface-level review reply -- a bare "LGTM" -- approving the second iteration of a bugfix for Git's ASCII graph renderer.

The patch under review, from Dominik Maier, fixes incorrect `git log --graph` output for left-skewed octopus merges with three or more parents. In `graph.c`, `graph_insert_into_new_columns()` misplaces parents when the merge is skewed left, causing a swallowed edge with three parents and an out-of-bounds read at `graph->new_columns[-1]` with four or more. The fix adds an `idx < 0` guard so a fusion shortcut only applies to existing non-merge columns. The v2 revision drops a defensive `BUG()` that v1 had added, expands the commit message with ASCII-art examples of both failure modes, and adds two regression tests to `t/t4215-log-skewed-merges.sh`.
