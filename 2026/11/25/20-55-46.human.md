This is a bugfix patch -- specifically the second of five in Johannes Schindelin's v3 series addressing integer overflows flagged by Coverity across several subsystems. The patch makes a one-line change in `http.c`, converting the `posn` variable in `fwrite_sha1_file()` from `int` to `size_t` so that the byte-position counter matches the `size_t` type of the buffer length it tracks. The commit message notes that if a single curl callback ever delivered more than `INT_MAX` bytes, the old `int` could overflow and wrap negative, causing the subsequent pointer arithmetic to under-read the buffer; while curl typically delivers data in small chunks, matching the types eliminates the theoretical defect.

The patch sits within a larger hardening effort that also touches `archive-tar.c`, the wrapper I/O functions in `wrapper.c`, and date parsing in `date.c`. Junio C Hamano had previously approved this patch individually and suggested rewording the commit message to clarify that `posn` resets on every callback invocation -- a clarification that is incorporated in this v3 iteration. Patrick Steinhardt also contributed to the fix, and the thread summary notes that he confirmed the `size_t` change is correct. With Junio's earlier approval of this patch and the v2 direction generally, this v3 update appears to be converging toward readiness for queuing, though the series as a whole still awaits final review.
