Alessio Attilio has posted a second version of a single-patch bugfix for a crash in `git pack-objects` under partial clone. The problem is in `is_not_in_promisor_pack_obj()` in `builtin/pack-objects.c`, which called `BUG()` when it failed to read an object's info -- but in a partial clone, objects missing from the local promisor pack are expected to be absent, so the crash was spurious. The fix replaces the `BUG()` with `return 0`, so the object is treated as not belonging to a promisor pack and skipped during pack creation.

Version 2 refines the fix by adding `OBJECT_INFO_SKIP_FETCH_OBJECT` to the `odb_read_object_info_extended()` call, so the lookup no longer triggers a lazy fetch of the missing object. The test coverage also moved from `t/t0410-partial-clone.sh` to `t/t5616-partial-clone.sh` and was expanded to cover filtered `--unshallow` and `--deepen=1` fetches of a moved ref, plus a scenario where a subsequent fetch repacks links to promisor commits whose trees are missing -- the conditions under which index-pack previously hit the `BUG()`.

The patch applies on top of commit 1630431f326e15fcde608827b5ff38422528eb59 and remains under review with no recorded feedback yet.
