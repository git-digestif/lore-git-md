This is a review acknowledgment from Junio C Hamano closing out a NULL-safety discussion on patch 2/5 of the five-patch series that removes `the_repository` from the notes subsystem.

Junio concedes that he missed the call-path guarantees Okhuomon Ajayi traced for the functions converted in patch 2/5. Specifically, he accepts that `get_note()` substitutes `&default_notes_tree` for a NULL tree pointer and asserts `t->initialized` before reaching `note_tree_find()`, so the mechanical substitution of `t->repo` for `the_repository` in that patch is safe as written.

He confirms that `copy_note()` in patch 3/5 is now the only unsafe conversion remaining in the series -- the one place where the patch dereferences `t->repo->hash_algo` without first ensuring `t` is non-NULL. That bug, along with the documentation gaps and the `notes-cache.c` enumeration, is queued for the next iteration, which will be labeled "v5" per the version-labeling discussion earlier in the thread.

The series remains under active review with no merge status; it is part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort and currently scoped to `notes.[c,h]` with the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` guard intentionally retained for functions tied to the global `default_notes_tree`.
