This is a measurement follow-up in the test modernization thread. Tanga Joris replies to Karthik Nayak's 20-patch series splitting the monolithic `t9300-fast-import.sh` into smaller files, specifically addressing the open question about how much overhead the new per-test repository creation adds. Having run the fast-import tests on a 4-core machine, Tanga reports that sequential execution slowed from 17.8s to 21.1s, while `prove -j4` went from 11.0s to 11.9s, so the parallelism gains do not fully offset the setup cost at that level. The email closes by accepting the trade-off: the slight slowdown is worth the readability improvements, effectively resolving the performance concern with real numbers rather than leaving it as an unknown.
