This is a review from Junio C Hamano endorsing Wonseok Oh's two-patch v2 series that fixes how the sequencer handles unborn branches. Junio approves of the decision to split the fix into two separate patches -- one correcting the uninitialized OID written to `.git/sequencer/head` and another ensuring `.git/sequencer/abort-safety` also stores the null OID rather than remaining empty -- saying the separation makes both changes straightforward to verify individually. He also confirms that the reworked regression test in patch 1, which now checks that `.git/sequencer/head` contains `$ZERO_OID` and that `git cherry-pick --abort` fails with the expected "cannot abort from a branch yet to be born" message, aligns well with the scenario described in the commit message. The review is positive overall, with no requests for further changes, signaling that the series addresses the v1 feedback cleanly and is likely ready for integration.
