This is a review of patch 3/4 in a four-part documentation series from Jean-Noël Avila, converting `git-fast-import.adoc` to the project's standard synopsis style. Junio C Hamano is reviewing the patch, and his feedback is mostly surface-level cleanup mixed with a few consistency observations.

Junio notes several small defects that likely warrant a v2. He points out that "Load marks like `--import-marks` but ... silently skips" mixes the imperative mood with third-person singular and should read "silently skip" to match "Load". He also catches that the final `<count>` in the raw data description should use the `_<count>_` placeholder form, and asks whether other placeholders like `<idnum>`, `<object-identifier>`, and `<time>` elsewhere in the file should similarly be converted from `<placeholder>` to `_<placeholder>_`. He flags a missing backtick on `--import-marks=` and notes that a few lines switched from a leading tab to two spaces, breaking the file's indentation consistency. Aside from these nits, he seems satisfied with the direction, closing one hunk with "Thanks."

The patch itself is purely mechanical: converting the SYNOPSIS from `[verse]` to `[synopsis]`, switching option markup to backtick-quoted `` `--option`:: `` form, and normalizing placeholders. It is part of Jean-Noël Avila's ongoing project-wide effort to standardize all man pages, and this particular patch also touches `Documentation/config/fastimport.adoc`. The series remains under review with no integration-branch status recorded.
