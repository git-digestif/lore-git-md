This is a review acknowledgment in the ongoing `the_repository` removal series for the notes subsystem. Okhuomon Ajayi is responding to Christian Couder's correction about patch 2/5, which converts five read-path functions in `notes.c` to use `t->repo` instead of the global `the_repository`.

Ajayi concedes that her initial safety argument was incomplete: she had claimed `load_subtree()` and `add_non_note()` were only reachable during initialization, but Couder pointed out that subtrees are unpacked lazily on demand during ordinary lookups via the chain `get_note()` -> `note_tree_find()` -> `note_tree_search()` -> `load_subtree()`. She accepts the cleaner framing that the safety of the converted functions derives not from the reachability of individual helpers but from the public entry points -- `add_note()`, `remove_note()`, `get_note()`, and the internal `format_note()` -- all of which substitute `&default_notes_tree` for a NULL `t` and assert `t->initialized` before any downstream code dereferences `t->repo`.

She confirms that `copy_note()` remains the sole public function lacking this NULL guard, which is the bug Junio C Hamano identified in patch 3/5 and which will be fixed in the next iteration. Ajayi agrees to document this call-path safety argument in the commit message for patch 2/5 when the series is re-sent as v5, ensuring future readers understand why the mechanical substitution does not introduce a NULL-dereference risk. The series remains under active review with no merge status, and the next iteration is expected to be labeled v5 per Junio's housekeeping note.
