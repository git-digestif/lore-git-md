This is a substantive review comment on the ninth patch of Karthik Nayak's v2 series splitting `t9300-fast-import.sh` into smaller test files. Tanga Joris responds to the patch's approach of replacing the `verify_packs()` helper with an inline loop over `.git/objects/pack/*.pack` in the "big blob" tests, noting that the same loop now appears in four places across the newly created `t9307-fast-import-validation.sh`, `t9308-fast-import-stream.sh`, and `t9311-fast-import-refs.sh`. Rather than duplicating the pack-verification logic, Tanga suggests extracting `verify_packs()` into a small shared helper file that the split-out test scripts can source, particularly if additional test groups are moved out of `t9300` in future patches. The comment is constructive and forward-looking, flagging a maintenance concern about code duplication that will grow as the series progresses, but it does not block the current patch. A future summarizer should note that this introduces a potential follow-up: creating a shared test utility (possibly in `t/lib-*.sh` or similar) for pack verification, and that the four current duplication sites are in the fast-import validation, stream, and refs test files.
