This is a cover letter for a six-patch refactoring series, part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. Okhuomon Ajayi is rerolling the notes-subsystem work as `[PATCH v5 0/6]` after the previous round (mislabeled v2, should have been v4) drew review feedback. The series makes `notes.[c,h]` operate on a repository stored in `struct notes_tree` instead of the global `the_repository`, deliberately leaving display-notes code, `notes-utils.[c,h]`, `notes-merge.[c,h]`, and parts of `notes-cache.c` for future series.

The biggest change since the last iteration is the addition of a `notes_tree_or_default()` helper that factors the repeated NULL-tree check into one place. This directly fixes the NULL-dereference bug in `copy_note()` that Junio C Hamano and Christian Couder flagged in patch 3 of the previous round, and routes all public entry points (`add_note()`, `remove_note()`, `get_note()`, `write_notes_tree()`, `prune_notes()`, `copy_note()`) through the same guard. The series also documents the new `struct repository *` and `flags` parameters of `notes_tree_init()`, explains the call-path safety argument for the read-path conversions in the commit message, and lists `notes-cache.c` among the remaining unconverted files.

The six patches add the helper, store the repository in `struct notes_tree`, thread it through the read, write, prune, ref-glob, and combine-notes paths, and update direct callers in `builtin/notes.c`, `notes-cache.c`, and `notes-utils.c` to pass the repository explicitly. No user-visible behavior changes; the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` guards remain in the files intentionally left for later.

The series looks well-crafted and responsive: it incorporates Patrick Steinhardt's interface feedback from earlier rounds and resolves the specific correctness and documentation concerns raised in review. It remains under review with no merge status, and Patrick Steinhardt's confirmation on the scoping of the remaining guard is still the gating item before the next iteration.
