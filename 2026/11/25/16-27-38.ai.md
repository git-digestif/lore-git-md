This is a surface-level review by Junio C Hamano of `[PATCH 1/4]` in Jean-Noël Avila's four-patch series converting `Documentation/git-svn.adoc`, `Documentation/git-fast-import.adoc`, and `Documentation/git-fast-export.adoc` to AsciiDoc synopsis style. The review targets only patch 1/4 (the `git-svn.adoc` conversion) and flags six consistency defects likely to require a v2.

Junio first notes a verb disagreement introduced by changing "This fetches" to imperative "Fetch" while leaving "rebases" on the following line; he suggests "rebase" to match. He then objects to leaving lines like `config key: svn-remote.<name>.commiturl` inside a `[synopsis]` block, because the synopsis attribute causes Asciidoctor to highlight prose words as code; he recommends backtick-quoting the key names there as done elsewhere in the patch, specifically citing the `svn.pushmergeinfo` entry under `--mergeinfo`.

On placeholder style, Junio points out that the patch uses `<filename>` in running text where his earlier series (commit e9a99ef2da, "doc: convert git-format-patch synopsis and options to new style") established the `_<filename>_` form, and asks which convention to standardize on. He also catches a likely typo where `<rev-num-1>` was written as `<ref-num-1>` in the `--revision` description, and notes an inconsistency between "Name _<email>_" in the `--authors-prog` entry versus the backticked "Name <email>" form used in patch 2/4. Finally, he observes that renaming the option parameter from `<filename>` to `<program>` leaves the second paragraph referring to "a relative filename," which should presumably be "a relative _<filename>_" or similar to avoid a dangling reference.

No merge status, trailer, or approval is recorded; the series remains v1 under review.
