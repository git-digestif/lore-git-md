This is a substantive review comment on patch 2/5 of Okhuomon Ajayi's five-patch v2 series removing `the_repository` from the notes subsystem. Christian Couder is refining the justification for why the converted functions are safe to dereference `t->repo` without explicit NULL checks.

He corrects Ajayi's earlier argument that `load_subtree()` is only reachable from `init_notes()`, pointing out that subtrees are also unpacked on demand via the path `get_note()` -> `note_tree_find()` -> `note_tree_search()` -> `load_subtree()`. The stronger safety argument, he suggests, is that all public entry points -- `add_note()`, `remove_note()`, `get_note()`, and the internal `format_note()` -- already replace a NULL `t` with `&default_notes_tree` and assert `t->initialized` before proceeding. This makes the mechanical replacement of `the_repository` with `t->repo` safe across those paths.

Couder notes that `copy_note()` appears to be the only public function lacking this guard, which connects directly to the NULL-dereference bug Junio C Hamano identified in patch 3/5. He closes by suggesting that the commit message for patch 2/5 in the next iteration (v5) should briefly explain this safety reasoning so future readers understand why the conversions are sound.
