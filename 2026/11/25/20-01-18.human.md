This is a documentation patch that restores pointers to the tutorial and data model pages in the main `git` man page. Julia Evans adds three lines to `Documentation/git.adoc` directing readers to `gittutorial[7]` for getting started and `gitdatamodel[7]` for understanding Git's underlying data model and terminology.

The change is a direct follow-up to commit e2e5e9f740, which had removed these references in October 2026 because the tutorial content was outdated. Now that a refreshed tutorial exists, the author brings back those two links while explicitly leaving the other previously-removed documents out, since they remain stale.

The patch is small, focused, and well-motivated: the commit message clearly traces the history and explains exactly why these specific pages are being restored versus others. It touches only the AsciiDoc source for the top-level `git` documentation, making it a low-risk change that improves discoverability for new users.
