This is a surface-level documentation review of patch 3/4 in Jean-Noël Avila's four-part series converting `git-fast-import.adoc` to AsciiDoc synopsis style.

Junio C Hamano reviewed the conversion of `Documentation/git-fast-import.adoc` and `Documentation/config/fastimport.adoc` and found the intentions clearly stated but flagged several consistency and formatting defects likely needing a v2. He noted a mood mismatch in the `--import-marks-if-exists` description ("Load ... but ... skips" should be "silently skip"), an inconsistency where the final `<count>` in the raw data grammar section was not converted to `_<count>_` while others were, and asked whether other placeholders like `<idnum>`, `<object-identifier>`, `<time>`, `<offutc>`, and `<tz>` should also use the `_<placeholder>_` form throughout. He also caught a missing backtick on `--import-marks=` and indentation drift from tabs to two spaces in the `feature import-marks` description. The review is purely stylistic with no technical objections; the series remains v1 under review with no recorded response from the author.
