Substantive review reply correcting the safety argument for patch 2/5 in the `the_repository` removal series for the notes subsystem.

Christian Couder responds to Okhuomon Ajayi's defense of patch 2/5, which converts five read-path functions in `notes.c` (`note_tree_find()`, `add_non_note()`, `load_subtree()`, `remove_note()`, `format_note()`) to use `t->repo` instead of `the_repository`. While agreeing the patch is safe, Couder corrects Ajayi's claim that `load_subtree()` is only reachable from `init_notes()`, pointing out it is also invoked on demand during reads via the chain `get_note()` -> `note_tree_find()` -> `note_tree_search()` -> `load_subtree()` when subtrees are unpacked lazily. He clarifies that the actual safety guarantee comes from the public entry points (`add_note()`, `remove_note()`, `get_note()`, and the internal `format_note()`) all substituting `&default_notes_tree` for a NULL `t` and asserting `t->initialized` before any dereference of `t->repo`, rather than from the specific reachability of individual helpers.

Couder reiterates that `copy_note()` remains the lone public function lacking this NULL guard, which is precisely the bug Junio C Hamano identified in patch 3/5. He requests that the commit message for patch 2/5 in the next iteration (v5) briefly document this call-path safety argument so future readers understand why the mechanical `t->repo` substitution does not introduce a NULL-dereference risk. This is a substantive review: it traces specific function call chains across `notes.c`, distinguishes between initialization-time and on-demand reachability, and connects back to the already-tracked `copy_note()` issue, but it does not surface new bugs beyond those already acknowledged for v5.

The series remains under active review with no merge status; it is part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, and the next iteration is expected to be labeled v5 per Junio's housekeeping note.
