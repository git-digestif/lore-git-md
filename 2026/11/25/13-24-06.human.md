This is a surface-level review approving the third patch in Elia Pinto's four-part series fixing 64-to-32 truncations on Windows (LLP64 platforms). Toon Claes confirms that `read_blob_data_from_index()` has no callers beyond the three converted in the patch, verifies that `read_attr_from_buf()` already accepts `size_t`, and endorses Christian Couder's suggested wording tweak for the commit message.

The patch itself converts file-conversion helpers in `convert.c`, `attr.c`, and `read-cache.c` from `unsigned long` to `size_t` to prevent silent misbehavior when processing files larger than 4 GiB on Windows, where `unsigned long` is only 32 bits. Without this fix, large binary files could be misdetected as text, leading to incorrect CRLF handling or skipped keyword expansion, and staged blobs over 4 GiB could crash when `core.autocrlf` is enabled.

Toon's review is mechanical and focused on API consistency rather than deep behavioral analysis, checking that the type change is complete and that no other call sites were missed. He finds the patch technically sound and ready, adding no new concerns beyond the minor wording suggestion already raised by Couder. The series as a whole remains under review pending resolution of the separate commit-message authorship dispute, but this specific patch appears to have cleared technical review.
