This is a substantive review reply from Junio C Hamano confirming the correctness of patch v2 1/2 in Dominik Maier's two-patch bugfix series for the archive subsystem. The patch fixes out-of-bounds pointer arithmetic in `write_zip_entry()` (`archive-zip.c`) when `--prefix` is longer than the path of `--add-file` or `--add-virtual-file` entries, which previously caused `entry_is_binary()` to receive an invalid `path_without_prefix` and perform spurious `userdiff_find_by_path()` lookups.

Junio agrees with René Scharfe's assessment that the fix is correct and straightforward, offering no objections to the approach. He clarifies his earlier suggestion about moving the NULL-path check into `userdiff_find_by_path()` rather than guarding it at the call site in `entry_is_binary()`: the call-site guard is not a meaningful optimization, and if optimization is desired it belongs inside `userdiff_find_by_path()` itself, with the caveat that skipping `attr_check_initl()` sanity checks for virtual-file-only workloads (e.g., `git archive --add-virtual-file=foo:bar $EMPTY_TREE`) may not be a net positive.

He also endorses René's out-of-scope architectural observation about introducing a dedicated `struct archive_entry` prepared by `write_archive_entry()` to eliminate per-entry mutations of `struct archiver_args` (such as setting `args->base` to NULL) and the associated pointer arithmetic in the tar and zip handlers, calling it a "good longer-term direction" for addressing the current design where `write_archive_entries()` (`archive.c`) mutates shared `archiver_args` fields inside the loop over extra and virtual files.

The thread remains converged with no outstanding change requests for the patch itself; both reviewers have signaled approval, and the discussion has shifted to optional micro-optimizations and future refactoring directions. No merge or integration status is stated.
