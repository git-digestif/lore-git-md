This is a cover letter for the fifth iteration (`[PATCH v5 0/6]`) of a six-patch series in the notes subsystem, part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. Okhuomon Ajayi re-labels the series as v5 to correct a versioning collision from the previous round (which was mislabeled v2 when it should have been v4).

The series stores a `struct repository *repo` field in `struct notes_tree` (patch 2/6) and threads it through the core read, write, prune, ref-glob, and combine paths in `notes.c`. Patch 1/6 introduces a new static helper `notes_tree_or_default()` that encapsulates the repeated `if (!t) t = &default_notes_tree(); assert(t->initialized);` pattern; this helper is now used by all public entry points including `copy_note()`, which directly fixes the NULL-dereference bug identified in the previous iteration where `copy_note()` dereferenced `t->repo` without the NULL guard. Patch 3/6 (reading) includes a commit message explanation of why the converted helpers (`note_tree_find()`, `add_non_note()`, `load_subtree()`, `remove_note()`, `format_note()`) are safe when `t` is NULL, tracing the safety guarantee to the public entry points substituting `&default_notes_tree` before any dereference. Patch 2/6 adds doc comments for the `r` and `flags` parameters of `notes_tree_init()`. Patches 4/6 through 6/6 pass explicit repository parameters to `string_list_add_refs_by_glob()`, `string_list_add_refs_from_colon_sep()`, and the `combine_notes_fn` typedef and its four built-in implementations.

Files touched are `notes.c`, `notes.h`, `notes-utils.c`, `notes-cache.c`, and `builtin/notes.c`. The `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` guards remain in `notes.c` and `notes-utils.[c,h]` for functions tied to the global `default_notes_tree` or lacking repository propagation; the cover letter explicitly enumerates remaining unconverted work including `notes-cache.c` functions (`notes_cache_match_validity()`, `notes_cache_write()`, `notes_cache_get()`, `notes_cache_put()`), display-notes code, hash-size helpers, and `notes-merge.[c,h]`. The series remains under active review with no merge status; Patrick Steinhardt's confirmation on the scoping of the remaining guard is still pending.
