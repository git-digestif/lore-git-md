This is a **the_repository removal** refactoring patch, specifically the fifth of six patches in Okhuomon Ajayi's v5 series that stores a `struct repository *` in `struct notes_tree` and systematically removes implicit uses of the global `the_repository` from the notes subsystem.

Patch 5/6 converts the two ref-glob helper functions to take an explicit repository pointer as their first argument. `string_list_add_refs_by_glob()` and `string_list_add_refs_from_colon_sep()` now accept `struct repository *r` before their existing parameters, and internally replace calls to `get_main_ref_store(the_repository)`, `repo_get_oid(the_repository, ...)`, and the recursive call between themselves with uses of the passed `r`. The declaration changes appear in `notes.h`, while the implementations and call sites are updated in `notes.c` and `notes-utils.c`.

The call sites updated in this patch span both `notes.c` and `notes-utils.c`. In `notes.c`, `notes_display_config()` and `load_display_notes()` pass `the_repository` when populating `display_notes_refs` from config, environment variables, and extra notes refs. In `notes-utils.c`, `notes_rewrite_config()` and `init_copy_notes_for_rewrite()` pass `the_repository` when building the rewrite ref list from config and the `GIT_NOTES_REWRITE_REF` environment variable. These temporary uses of `the_repository` are intentional stepping stones; the series intends to replace them with `t->repo` or other propagated repository pointers in subsequent work.

This patch sits between patch 4/6, which converted the write/prune/copy paths and fixed the `copy_note()` NULL-dereference via the new `notes_tree_or_default()` helper, and patch 6/6, which will convert the `combine_notes_fn` typedef and its four built-in combinators to take a repository parameter. The remaining `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` guards in `notes.c` and `notes-utils.[c,h]` are scoped to functions tied to the global `default_notes_tree` or awaiting further propagation, with Patrick Steinhardt's confirmation on that scoping still pending.
