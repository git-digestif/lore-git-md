This is a substantive review of the cover letter for a five-patch `the_repository` removal series in the notes subsystem. Junio C Hamano responds to Okhuomon Ajayi's framing of the work as preparatory, agreeing that splitting out the remaining conversion work makes the scope clear.

He adds one new observation beyond the previously discussed `copy_note()` NULL-dereference risk: `notes-cache.c` still uses `the_repository` directly in `notes_cache_match_validity()`, `notes_cache_write()`, `notes_cache_get()`, and `notes_cache_put()`, even though `notes_cache_init()` now threads its repository parameter into `notes_tree_init()`. This suggests the cache layer needs attention in a future iteration even though the core `notes.c` conversion is largely complete.

Apart from the NULL check needed in patch 3, Junio assesses that the series looks in good shape, signaling general approval of the approach while flagging these specific remaining gaps.
