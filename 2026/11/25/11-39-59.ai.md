This is a substantive review reply from René Scharfe responding to Junio C Hamano's feedback on the first patch of Dominik Maier's two-patch v2 series. The email addresses the ZIP prefix-stripping fix in `archive-zip.c` and `archive.c`.

René agrees with Junio's assessment that the fix is correct and straightforward, offering no objections to the current approach. He engages with Junio's suggestion to move the NULL-path check inside `userdiff_find_by_path()` rather than guarding it at the call site in `entry_is_binary()`. René notes this would indeed help workloads that call `userdiff_find_by_path()` without a path -- specifically citing `git archive --add-virtual-file=foo:bar $EMPTY_TREE` as a case where only virtual files are processed -- but he expresses uncertainty whether skipping the one-time `attr_check_initl()` sanity checks for those workloads is a net positive, leaving the question open.

He also raises a broader architectural observation, explicitly marking it out of scope for this series: he wonders whether it is time to introduce a dedicated `struct archive_entry` prepared by `write_archive_entry()` so that the tar and zip handlers no longer need per-entry mutations of `struct archiver_args` (such as setting `args->base` to NULL) or the associated pointer arithmetic in `write_zip_entry()`. This reflects a longer-term concern about the current design where `write_archive_entries()` in `archive.c` mutates shared `archiver_args` fields inside the loop over extra and virtual files.

The thread remains in a converged state with no outstanding change requests; both reviewers have signaled approval of patch v2 1/2, and the discussion has shifted to optional micro-optimizations and future refactoring directions rather than correctness issues. No merge status is stated.
