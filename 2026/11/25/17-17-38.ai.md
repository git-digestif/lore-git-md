This is a substantive review by Junio C Hamano of the v2 cover letter for Wonseok Oh's two-patch bugfix series targeting the sequencer subsystem. The series addresses the handling of unborn (orphan) branches during multi-commit `git cherry-pick` sequences: patch 1 ensures `sequencer_pick_revisions()` in `sequencer.c` writes the null OID to `.git/sequencer/head` instead of an uninitialized value when HEAD does not yet exist, and patch 2 makes `update_abort_safety_file()` write the null OID to `.git/sequencer/abort-safety` rather than leaving it empty.

Junio endorses the v2 restructuring, noting that separating the `.git/sequencer/head` fix from the `.git/sequencer/abort-safety` update makes both changes straightforward to verify individually. He specifically approves the test adjustments in patch 1, observing that they match the scenario described in the commit log well. His review engages directly with the patch organization and test design rather than offering only surface-level comments, and he raises no objections or requests for further changes to the series as presented. The series remains under review with no integration status reported.
