This is a review reply from Junio C Hamano on the cover letter of Okhuomon Ajayi's five-patch series removing `the_repository` from the core of `notes.[ch]` (part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort). He endorses the "preparatory series" framing as making the scope clear, but adds `notes-cache.c` to the enumerated list of remaining unconverted code: `notes_cache_match_validity()`, `notes_cache_write()`, `notes_cache_get()`, and `notes_cache_put()` still reference `the_repository` even though `notes_cache_init()` now threads its repository parameter into `notes_tree_init()`. He reiterates the previously identified NULL-dereference risk in `copy_note()` (patch 3/5, where a NULL `t` could be dereferenced without falling back to `default_notes_tree`) and otherwise judges the series to be in good shape. The review is substantive but concise; it confirms the series direction while refining the inventory of what remains for future work, and does not raise new objections beyond these points.
