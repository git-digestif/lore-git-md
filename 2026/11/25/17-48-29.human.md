This is a bugfix, and the first patch in Ravi Mistry's five-part v5 series hardening Git's HTTP transport. It corrects two small defects introduced by the original HTTP 429 retry logic in commit 640657ffd0.

The first bug is an off-by-one in the retry counter: `http_request_recoverable()` used a pre-decrement on `rate_limit_retries`, so `http.maxRetries=1` performed zero retries and `http.maxRetries=0` could wrap the counter to -1 (truthy in C), causing a 429 after a 401 re-auth to loop unexpectedly. The patch switches to a post-decrement guarded by `http_max_retries > 0`, and moves the zero-check after the loop so an initial 429 with retries disabled fails cleanly without logging a misleading "retries-exhausted" Trace2 event. The second bug is a logging timing issue: `CURLINFO_RETRY_AFTER` was queried after `handle_curl_result()` had already written `retry_after` to Trace2, so the event always showed -1. The extraction now happens in `finish_active_slot()`, initializing the field to -1 and populating it before any consumer sees it.

Tests in `t/t5584-http-429-retry.sh` verify that `http.maxRetries=1` retries exactly once, that exhaustion behaves correctly after multiple failures, and that Trace2 now records the actual `Retry-After` value. This patch is unchanged from v4 and was already marked "Looking good" by Junio, so it appears settled and ready to land pending the rest of the series.
