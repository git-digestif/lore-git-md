This is a maintainer response regarding the status of a stalled integration topic. In reply to Torsten Bögershausen's query in the "What's cooking in git.git (Nov 2026, #08)" thread, Junio C Hamano addresses the fate of `cs/read-cache-conversion-size`, a patch from Curtis Allen Smith that modifies `read-cache` index refresh logic to avoid treating file size changes as conclusive proof of modification when content conversion (such as line-ending conversion) is active, which previously caused false dirty statuses.

Torsten had asked what steps are needed to advance the topic, stating that the patch appears sound because it fixes a long-standing problem and includes test cases. He also noted that he had previously sent a separate optimization patch on top of the fix and offered to resend it if there is interest. Junio responded that the topic has been entirely paged out of his mind and, importantly, that without the original author responding to comments raised in subsequent discussion, it is difficult to affirm that the patch actually fixes the problem.

Junio observed that the original author appears to have gone silent and proposed a concrete path forward: he asked Torsten whether he would be willing to take over the topic and assemble a two-patch series combining the original fix with the optimization to move it toward integration. This shifts the topic from stalled to potentially active under new stewardship, contingent on Torsten's willingness to shepherd both patches through review.
