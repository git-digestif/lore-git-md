This is a documentation patch reply in which Julia Evans responds to D. Ben Knoble's substantive pedagogical review of her `gittutorial-branches` beginner tutorial, agreeing to several structural changes for the next version.

The email belongs to the second half of Evans's two-part beginner tutorial effort (the first part, `gittutorial`, is already merged); this patch adds a hands-on guide to branches aimed at users without a computer science background. Evans accepts Knoble's concern that Step 8 confusingly references hypothetical branches and merges before merges have been introduced, and she will replace that with a simpler statement that new branches should usually start from `main` to keep work independent. She agrees that stacked branches are too advanced for this audience.

She also confirms a reorganization of the tutorial's back half for v2: merge conflicts move into a note, the "3 ways to think about a branch" material moves inline after Step 6, the collaboration workflows section gets a clearer title, and a new overview at the top lays out the document's four-part structure so readers can see the shape of the guide upfront. The exchange is constructive and shows the author engaging seriously with structural feedback rather than defending the original draft; the patch remains under review with no merge status change indicated.
