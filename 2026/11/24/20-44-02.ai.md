This is a substantive review of the third patch in Yoichi NAKAYAMA's v2 series on worktree-specific submodule configuration. Junio C Hamano endorses the patch's overall direction -- noting that the transition to `extensions.worktreeConfig` is safer with the new warning and that the test coverage for main-worktree and linked-worktree migration looks solid -- but identifies a concrete correctness hazard in the new helper `submodule_git_dir_matches_current_worktree()`.

The concern is a canonicalization mismatch between the two paths being compared. `repo_worktree_path_append()` resolves `the_repository->worktree` via `real_pathdup()` during repository setup, but `absolute_pathdup(repo_get_git_dir(the_repository))` relies on `PWD`/`getcwd()` when the gitdir is relative, or copies an explicit absolute `GIT_DIR` verbatim, without resolving symlinks or normalizing `.`/`..` components. In scenarios such as running `GIT_DIR=.git git submodule init` from a symlinked directory, or with `GIT_DIR=.git/../.git`, the `super` and `submodule` buffers can diverge, triggering a false mismatch warning and generating a new gitdir instead of copying the valid existing one. He suggests canonicalizing both sides with `real_pathdup(repo_get_git_dir(the_repository), 1)` for `super` and `strbuf_realpath_forgiving()` for `submodule`, acknowledging this may produce uglier absolute paths but ensures the comparison is robust.
