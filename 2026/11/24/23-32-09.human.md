This is a refactoring patch, the first of three in the fourth iteration of Erik Cervin-Edin's series to let `git commit --fixup` accept message options like `-m` and `-F`. The patch restructures `prepare_to_commit()` in `builtin/commit.c` so that the `--fixup` subject line (`fixup! ...` or `amend! ...`) is written up front in its own block before the if/else-if chain that handles message sources, mirroring how `--squash` already behaves. This removes the need for `!fixup_message` guards on the `-m` and `-F` arms later in the chain and lets the follow-up patches in the series enable those options cleanly. The error for combining `-m` with `--fixup=amend:` is moved into the new subject block since the `-m` arm now runs before the fixup arm. The commit message is thorough, citing the original 2010 commits that introduced `--fixup` and `--squash` and explaining the historical asymmetry. The diff is clean and mechanical, and the thread summary indicates all prior feedback from Phillip Wood and Junio Hamano has been addressed, leaving the series production-ready and awaiting final review.
