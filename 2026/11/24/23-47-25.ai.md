This is a maintainer review of patch 2/6 in Patrick Steinhardt's six-patch packfile lifecycle refactoring series. Junio C Hamano approves the mechanical rename of seven lifecycle functions to the `packfile_` prefix -- `open_pack_index` to `packfile_open_index`, `close_pack_index` to `packfile_close_index`, `close_pack_fd` to `packfile_close_fd`, `close_pack_windows` to `packfile_close_windows`, `close_pack` to `packfile_close`, `add_packed_git` to `packfile_from_idx`, and `alloc_packed_git` to `packfile_new` -- noting that limiting the scope to lifecycle functions avoids the churn of renaming `struct packed_git` itself while still modernizing the API. He specifically praises the `add_packed_git()` to `packfile_from_idx()` rename because the old name misleadingly implied the function added the pack to a store. He confirms that all callers across the 21 files touched by the patch (spanning `builtin/`, `commit-graph.c`, HTTP transport files, `midx-write.c`, `odb/source-packed.c`, and others) are consistently updated with behavior preserved. The review flags one minor cleanup: a forward declaration of `struct object_database` that moved position in `packfile.h` is already unused because `odb.h` is included at the top of the header, so the move can be skipped or the declaration dropped separately. Aside from this nit he judges the step "done very nicely." This is a substantive review that verifies call-site consistency and naming semantics, though concise.
