The patch under approval hardens `approxidate_str()` and its helpers against signed integer overflow when parsing relative dates like "4000 weeks ago". It modifies `approxidate_alpha()` in `date.c` to compute the unit-length multiplication in `uintmax_t` using `unsigned_mult_overflows()` and verify the result fits `time_t` via `date_overflows()` before casting, returning `NULL` on failure. `approxidate_digit()` now rejects parsed numbers exceeding `INT_MAX` before narrowing to `int`, and `approxidate_str()` propagates these failures by setting `*error_ret` so callers such as `git reflog expire` report "invalid timestamp" rather than computing a wrapped offset. Tests in `t/t0006-date.sh` cover both a large-but-valid "4000 weeks ago" case on 64-bit `time_t` platforms and rejection of "18446744073709551615 seconds ago".
