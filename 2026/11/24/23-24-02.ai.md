This is a **substantive review** of patch 5/6 in Patrick Steinhardt's packfile lifecycle refactoring series. Junio C Hamano examines the introduction of the global `all_packfiles` list and the removal of repository-scoped iteration from `close_one_pack()` and `unuse_one_window()`.

Junio raises one wording nit and one conceptual concern. He finds "maximum number of packfile data" awkward in the commit message, suggesting "maximum amount of" instead. More substantively, he flags an impedance mismatch: while the series aims to support pluggable object databases that may not have the concept of "packfile", this patch assumes a global process-wide list of packfiles consuming address space. He notes he cannot think of a way to rectify this tension but wants it on record.

On the implementation, Junio is approving. He praises the signature change of `close_one_pack()` from taking a `struct repository *` to `void`, observing it makes clear the function selects a pack regardless of owning repository. He confirms that routing all allocations through `packfile_new()` and frees through `packfile_free()` means `all_packfiles` will not retain dangling pointers, and that removing the `odb_source_files_downcast()` calls cleanly solves the cross-repository limit enforcement problem.
