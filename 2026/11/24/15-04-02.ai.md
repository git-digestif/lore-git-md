This is a substantive review by Junio C Hamano of the v15 2/7 patch in Harald Nordgren's seven-patch series on matching-inspired fetch. The patch removes the fatal error in `get_ref_map()` (in `builtin/fetch.c`) when `--refmap` is supplied without command-line refspecs, instead applying the refmap to configured `remote.<name>.fetch` entries via new helpers `drop_destinations()` and `map_refs_without_dst()`.

Junio raises three concerns about the change. First, with no configured `remote.<name>.fetch`, `git fetch --refmap=... <URL>` would now fetch HEAD into FETCH_HEAD while silently ignoring the refmap, rather than erroring out. Second, automatic tag following may behave unexpectedly: `*autotags` is computed during the loop over `remote->fetch` based on whether each entry's `dst` is non-empty, but `drop_destinations()` later clears `peer_ref`, so passing `--refmap=""` would leave `*autotags` at 1 while clearing destinations, and destinationless configured entries with a supplied refmap would leave it at 0. Third, in `do_fetch()`, when `rs->nr` is 0, `prune_refs()` receives `&transport->remote->fetch` rather than the refmap, so `git fetch --prune --refmap=... origin` would prune based on the configured refspecs rather than the refmap-derived hierarchy, potentially leaving the actual fetched hierarchy unpruned.
