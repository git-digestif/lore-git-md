This is a refactoring patch, specifically part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort in the notes subsystem. It is the second patch in a five-patch v2 series from Okhuomon Ajayi.

The patch makes five internal functions in `notes.c` -- `note_tree_find()`, `add_non_note()`, `load_subtree()`, `remove_note()`, and `format_note()` -- read objects and hash algorithms from the repository stored on the notes tree (`t->repo`) rather than from the global `the_repository`. This is a mechanical change with no behavior difference; it simply continues the work started in patch 1/5 (which added the `repo` field to `struct notes_tree`) by threading that field through the read paths. The series remains under review with no merge status, and this patch is a straightforward continuation of the architectural cleanup Patrick Steinhardt requested.
