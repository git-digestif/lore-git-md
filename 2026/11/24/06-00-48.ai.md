This is a brief acknowledgment reply from Dirk Gouders to Junio C Hamano's substantive review objection on the v4 documentation patch to `Documentation/gitcli.adoc`.

Gouders admits that the controversial change -- replacing the v3 phrasing "before or after it" with "between them" in the bullet describing how to disambiguate arguments that could be either revisions or paths using `--` -- was an accidental regression. He explains that he had attempted to migrate the topic to a different base (referenced as "b4") and inadvertently reverted that specific line to an earlier iteration, not realizing Junio had already approved the "before or after it" wording in v3. He apologizes and confirms he did not intend to override the maintainer's preferred phrasing.

The thread remains open with this objection unresolved. The other v4 changes (grammar fixes: "conventions", "When arguments", and the imperative "Split short options into separate words") stand accepted, but the disambiguation bullet requires correction -- either reverting to the v3 "before or after it" text or an equivalent that correctly conveys that `--` may appear before or after a single ambiguous argument, and that for multiple ambiguous arguments the separator placement may require reordering (as in `git log next -- main`). No corrected version has been submitted yet, and the topic has not been queued to any integration branch.
