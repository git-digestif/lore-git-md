This is a substantive review from Junio C Hamano of the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt's six-part packfile API refactoring series. The patch under review inlines `open_packed_git_1()` into its caller and renames the result `packfile_open_pack()`, introducing a unified `out:` exit label to ensure the packfile descriptor is closed on error paths. Junio agrees that the change makes file descriptor ownership much easier to follow and that the inlining is appropriate.

He does, however, spot a subtle accounting bug in the new error handling. When `git_open()` succeeds but the following `fstat()` fails, the code jumps to the `out:` label before `pack_open_fds` is incremented, yet the cleanup routine `packfile_close_fd()` unconditionally decrements that counter, which could underflow it to UINT_MAX. He notes that this may not be a newly introduced bug since the old code leaked the descriptor rather than corrupting the counter, but he suggests fixing the accounting while the area is being modified. With that caveat noted, he otherwise finds the patch good to go.
