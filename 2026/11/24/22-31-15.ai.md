This is a substantive review of patch 2/5 in the v4 HTTP retry series, in which Junio C Hamano approves the exponential backoff implementation. The email focuses on two specific aspects of the diff: first, he acknowledges that the `attempt` parameter in `handle_rate_limit_retry()` is now typed as `size_t` rather than `int` (a change made between v3 and v4), and second, he explicitly endorses the overflow-safe doubling loop that computes the backoff delay. The loop guards against integer wraparound by checking `if (delay >= http_max_retry_time - delay)` before doubling, clamping to `http_max_retry_time` when the next doubling would exceed it. Junio concludes with "Looking good," indicating his approval of this patch.

The technical context is that patch 2/5 modifies `handle_rate_limit_retry()` in `http.c` to accept the 1-based retry attempt count and, when no `Retry-After` header is present, compute the wait as `http.retryAfter * 2^(attempt - 1)` with the aforementioned overflow protection. The call site in `http_request_recoverable()` passes `(size_t)(http_max_retries - rate_limit_retries)` as this attempt counter. With this approval, both patch 1/5 (the off-by-one retry count fix) and patch 2/5 (exponential backoff) now have the maintainer's sign-off, while patches 3/5 through 5/5 (configurable retry status codes, POST retries for read-only RPCs, and the new 10-second default `http.retryAfter`) remain under review.
