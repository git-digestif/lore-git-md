Jean-Noël Avila has posted a four-patch series converting the man pages for `git-svn`, `git-fast-import`, and `git-fast-export` to the project's standard AsciiDoc synopsis style, as part of his ongoing effort to standardize documentation across Git. The series also extracts the `svn.*` and `fastimport.*` configuration documentation into shared include files under `Documentation/config/`. The changes are purely mechanical: converting `[verse]` blocks to `[synopsis]`, switching command references from single quotes to backticks, normalizing placeholders to `_<placeholder>_` form, and restructuring option descriptions.

Junio C Hamano has reviewed the final patch (4/4, covering `git-fast-export`) and flagged several small defects in the rewritten prose that will need fixing in a v2: a double space and the phrase "in addition of" instead of "in addition to" in the `--mark-tags` description, a stray tab character in `--reference-excluded-parents`, and a truncated sentence "When asking to one of the following values:" in `--reencode` that appears to be an incomplete edit. He also asked whether remaining single-quoted references to `'git rev-parse'` and `'git rev-list'` should be converted to backticks for consistency, while praising the `[synopsis]` markup on the `gpgsig` line as clean. The series remains at v1 and under review with no integration status yet.
