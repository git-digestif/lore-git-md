This is a substantive review from the maintainer, Junio C Hamano, approving patch 1/5 of Ravi Mistry's v4 series on HTTP retry handling.

Junio confirms that the proposed fix in `http_request_recoverable()` correctly resolves the off-by-one bug introduced by commit `640657ffd0`. By switching from pre-decrement (`--rate_limit_retries`) to post-decrement guarded by an explicit `http_max_retries > 0` check, the loop now performs the full N retries when `http.maxRetries=N` and avoids the 0-to-negative wrap that occurred when retries were disabled. He specifically highlights that moving the `http_max_retries <= 0` guard to after the loop prevents a 401 (re-auth) followed by a 429 from wrapping the counter or emitting a misleading "retries-exhausted" Trace2 event when `http.maxRetries=0`. His "Looking good" signals acceptance of this specific patch within the broader five-patch v4 series that reworks HTTP 429/backoff/POST retry behavior.
