This is a **refactoring** patch in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically the fourth of five in Okhuomon Ajayi's v2 series to make the notes subsystem repository-agnostic.

The patch modifies the two ref-glob helpers `string_list_add_refs_by_glob()` and `string_list_add_refs_from_colon_sep()` so they accept an explicit `struct repository *` as their first parameter instead of implicitly walking refs in `the_repository`. The internal calls to `get_main_ref_store()` and `repo_get_oid()` now use that passed-in repository. Callers in `notes.c` and `notes-utils.c` are updated to pass `the_repository` for now, keeping the change mechanical and behavior-neutral while establishing the signature that future patches can thread a real repository through.

Within the series, this follows the structural groundwork of patches 1/5 through 3/5, which added the `repo` field to `struct notes_tree` and converted the core read, write, and prune paths. Patch 4/5 extends that plumbing to the ref-discovery helpers used by the display-notes configuration and rewrite machinery. The remaining `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` guard in `notes.c` is still present because these callers still use the global; that is intentional and enumerated as future work in the cover letter.

The series remains under active review with no merge status. This patch looks clean and well-scoped: it changes only signatures and call sites, touches three files with a small diff, and does not alter any user-visible behavior.
