This is a maintainer review of a security bugfix. Junio C Hamano replies to Dominik Maier's patch that hardens `git filter-branch --state-branch` against path-traversal via malformed `filter.map` entries, and gives his approval.

The review is substantive. Junio confirms the vulnerability is real, pointing out two specific failure modes the patch addresses: an empty key like `:foo` triggers an "Is a directory" error inside the loop without calling `die()`, and keys containing `../` can write outside the temporary map directory into the working tree. He also asks Dominik to clarify in the commit message why spaces are permitted in the `to_commit` field -- presumably because a single source commit can map to multiple replacement commits, consumed later by an unquoted `$(map ...)` expansion -- before concluding "Looks good. Thanks."

The patch remains under review; Junio's approval is a strong signal that the fix is technically sound and likely to move toward integration once the commit message tweak is incorporated.
