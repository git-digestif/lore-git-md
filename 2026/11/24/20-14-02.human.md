This is a substantive review of patch 2/3 in Yoichi NAKAYAMA's v2 series implementing worktree-specific submodule configuration. Junio C Hamano examines the diff and raises two correctness concerns plus one robustness issue. He first flags that `is_submodule_active()` is designed to take a submodule path rather than its name, and notes that the patch passes `sub->name` to it in two places -- when setting the active flag during initialization and when checking activity in `deinit_submodule()` -- which would misbehave for submodules whose name and path differ, such as after a rename in the superproject. He suggests adding test coverage for that scenario in `t/t2405-worktree-submodule.sh`.

He then points out that the new logic in `copy_filtered_worktree_config()` for stripping `submodule.<name>.gitdir` entries when spawning a linked worktree relies on single-value config replacement and would miss duplicate entries in the source file; he recommends switching to `repo_config_set_multivar_in_file_gently()` with `CONFIG_FLAGS_MULTI_REPLACE` to unset all of them. Finally, he asks whether the patch should document in `Documentation/config/submodule.adoc` how these `submodule.<name>.*` keys interact with `extensions.worktreeConfig`. The review is technically precise, identifying concrete bugs rather than style preferences, and no integration status is indicated.
