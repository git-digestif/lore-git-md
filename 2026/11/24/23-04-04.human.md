This is a maintainer review of a patch series cover letter -- specifically a positive, surface-level acknowledgment that the series is in good shape.

Junio C Hamano replies to Ravi Mistry's v4 cover letter for the five-patch HTTP retry series, noting that while the HTTP transport code is not his strongest area, he reviewed it and found little questionable thanks to the author's responsiveness to Patrick Steinhardt's earlier feedback. The email effectively confirms that patches 1/5 through 4/5 have been individually approved and the series as a whole is ready from the maintainer's perspective, with only patch 5/5 (which changes the default `http.retryAfter` to 10 seconds) still awaiting his explicit sign-off.

The series addresses four concrete gaps in Git's HTTP resilience: an off-by-one retry counter bug, missing exponential backoff, lack of configurable retry status codes beyond 429, and no retries for read-only POST requests used in fetch/clone and partial-clone operations. The v4 iteration has already incorporated substantive review feedback, including restricting POST retries to `git-upload-pack` and `git-upload-archive` while excluding push, and handling explicit `Retry-After: 0` headers correctly. With Junio expressing satisfaction and no new objections raised in this email, the series appears poised for integration pending his final decision on the 10-second default delay.
