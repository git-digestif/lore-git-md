This is a maintainer review of patch 2/5 in Ravi Mistry's v4 series on HTTP retry handling. Junio C Hamano approves the patch, specifically noting two things: the `attempt` parameter is now `size_t` rather than `int` (a fix from v3), and the backoff calculation uses a careful loop that doubles the delay while checking against `http_max_retry_time` to avoid integer wraparound. His "Looking good" signals the patch is accepted from his perspective.

The patch itself adds exponential backoff to the 429 retry path in `http.c` when the server does not send a `Retry-After` header. Instead of always using the configured `http.retryAfter` value, the wait now doubles on each successive attempt (`http.retryAfter * 2^(attempt-1)`), clamped at `http.maxRetryTime`. An explicit `Retry-After` header still takes precedence. The review is brief but substantive -- Junio engaged with the overflow-safety of the arithmetic rather than just rubber-stamping.

Within the broader series, this is the second of five patches addressing HTTP transport resilience. Patch 1 (the off-by-one retry count fix) was already approved in the previous email; patches 3-5 (configurable retry status codes, POST retries for fetch/clone, and changing the default `http.retryAfter` to 10 seconds) remain under review. The series as a whole is still awaiting final integration decisions.
