This thread introduces a feature patch by gremat (via GitGitGadget) that adds `git range-diff` support to `contrib/diff-highlight`, the Perl helper that colorizes diff output. Previously the tool passed range-diff lines through unhighlighted because it did not recognize the commit-pair headers (e.g. `1:  abc1234 ! 1:  def5678`) or the four-column indentation and dual outer/inner markers that characterize range-diff output.

The patch modifies `DiffHighlight.pm` to detect the commit-pair header and strip the four-column indent much as it handles a `--graph` prefix, relaxes the hunk-header regex to tolerate ANSI color codes, and updates `is_pair_interesting()` so either the outer or inner marker can qualify a removed/added pair. A new `show_inner()` fallback handles inner-diff pairs (e.g. `+-old` with `++new`) when the outer hunk cannot be matched line-for-line. All new logic is guarded by a `$range_diff` state flag, so plain diffs and graph output are unaffected, and three new tests in `t/t9400-diff-highlight.sh` cover cross-version, within-version, and colored range-diff input. The series is currently under review with no maintainer decision recorded yet.
