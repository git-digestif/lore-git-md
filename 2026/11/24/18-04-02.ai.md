This is a maintainer acknowledgment from Junio C Hamano on the v2 cover letter for Johannes Schindelin's five-patch series hardening Git against Coverity-flagged integer overflows in `archive-tar.c`, `http.c`, `wrapper.c`/`wrapper.h`, and `date.c`. Junio confirms the v2 direction is a clear improvement over v1, praising the removal of the ad-hoc `signed_mult_overflows()`/`mult_overflows()` macros and the separation of `read_in_full_or_error()` from `read_in_full()` as making the wrapper APIs cleaner and easier to verify.

He offers two minor wording corrections to the cover letter itself: the claim that "Most callers of `read_in_full()`" were converted overstates the scope, since five callers were switched to `read_in_full_or_error()`, `strbuf_read()` now clamps `want` to `SSIZE_MAX`, the remaining callers operate on small fixed sizes, and `worktree.c:should_prune_worktree()` remains as a possible additional conversion; and "could been simplified" should read "could have been simplified". He dismisses these nits as insignificant because cover letters do not become part of the permanent commit record.

The email contains no new technical objections and signals the maintainer's approval of the series' overall approach. The series remains in a "waiting for response" state, with patches 1 and 2 previously approved pending commit-message tweaks and patches 3 through 5 revised in v2 but still awaiting resolution of earlier commit-message corrections regarding return-type documentation and the `BUG()` contract in `read_in_full()`.
