This is a brief acknowledgment in an ongoing `the_repository` removal thread. Okhuomon Ajayi thanks Christian Couder for his reply that "This version 2 looks good to me" on the v2 patch removing the global `the_repository` from the default fetch negotiator.

The patch itself is a mechanical refactoring of `fetch-negotiator.c`, `negotiator/default.c`, and `negotiator/default.h`: the `negotiation_state` struct gains a `repo` field, `default_negotiator_init()` takes an explicit `struct repository *`, and internal calls now use that pointer instead of the global. It is part of the broader effort to eliminate `the_repository` and is an Outreachy contribution.

This email adds no new technical content -- it simply closes the loop on Couder's informal approval. That approval is positive but surface-level, with no `Reviewed-by` trailer or detailed commentary, so the patch remains under review with no stated graduation to `next`, `master`, or any other integration branch.
