This is a review follow-up in the `the_repository` removal series for the notes subsystem. Okhuomon Ajayi agrees with Christian Couder's substantive review of patch 5/6, acknowledging that the `copy_note()` modification--switching from the global hash algorithm to `t->repo->hash_algo` for constructing the null object ID--does not logically belong with the `combine_notes` callback signature updates. She commits to moving that change into patch 3/6 (the writing and pruning patch) in the next iteration, since `copy_note()` modifies the tree through `add_note()` and therefore aligns better with the tree-mutation operations covered in that earlier patch. The email also quotes Christian's original suggestion that relocating the change to patch 2 or 3 would make the commit title match its content, and confirms the plan to adjust the commit title accordingly when rebasing. This exchange resolves one of the two substantive review points Christian raised on the series; the other open question--whether patch 6/6 should be dropped to avoid a mixed state between `notes-utils.c` and `notes.c`--remains pending alongside Patrick Steinhardt's confirmation on the remaining `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` guard scope in `notes.c`.
