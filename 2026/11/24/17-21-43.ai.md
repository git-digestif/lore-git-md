This is a **refactoring patch** in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically the second patch (`[PATCH v2 2/5]`) of Okhuomon Ajayi's five-patch series to store the repository in `struct notes_tree`.

The patch converts five core functions in `notes.c` -- `note_tree_find()`, `add_non_note()`, `load_subtree()`, `remove_note()`, and `format_note()` -- to read hash algorithms and objects from `t->repo` instead of the global `the_repository` and `the_hash_algo`. Concretely, `the_repository->hash_algo` becomes `t->repo->hash_algo` in `hasheq()`, `oidread()`, `oidclr()`, and `oid_set_algo()` calls; `the_hash_algo->rawsz` becomes `t->repo->hash_algo->rawsz`; `fill_tree_descriptor()` now receives `t->repo`; and `odb_read_object()` reads from `t->repo->objects`. No behavior changes; this is purely mechanical substitution enabled by the `repo` field added to `struct notes_tree` in patch 1/5.

The series remains under review with no merge status. Patrick Steinhardt's interface feedback from the previous iteration has been incorporated, and the author is waiting on his confirmation regarding the scoping of the remaining `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` guard before proceeding. The patch is clean and focused, touching only `notes.c` with 10 lines changed.
