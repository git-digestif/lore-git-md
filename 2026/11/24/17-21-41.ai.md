This is a cover letter for a five-patch refactoring series (labeled `[PATCH v2 0/5]`) in the notes subsystem, part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. Okhuomon Ajayi presents this as a preparatory series that converts the core of `notes.[ch]` and its direct callers to use a repository stored in `struct notes_tree` rather than the global `the_repository`.

The series stores a `struct repository *` field in `struct notes_tree` (patch 1/5) and threads it through the reading, writing, and pruning paths in `notes.c` (patches 2/5 and 3/5), the ref glob helpers `string_list_add_refs_by_glob()` and `string_list_add_refs_from_colon_sep()` (patch 4/5), and the `combine_notes_fn` callbacks including `concatenate_notes()`, `overwrite_notes()`, `ignore_notes()`, and `cat_sort_uniq_notes()` (patch 5/5). The `copy_note()` change to use `t->repo->hash_algo` for its null object ID has been moved from patch 5/5 into patch 3/5 per Christian Couder's review. The final patch from the previous iteration -- which would have converted `notes-utils.c` -- has been dropped entirely to avoid a mixed state where `notes-utils.[c,h]` appears fully converted while still calling into guarded `notes.c` code; as a result, the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` guards remain in both `notes.c` and `notes-utils.[c,h]`.

Files touched are `notes.c`, `notes.h`, `notes-utils.c`, `notes-cache.c`, and `builtin/notes.c`. No CLI options, config keys, or on-disk format changes are introduced. The cover letter explicitly enumerates remaining work for future series: the display-notes code and `default_notes_tree` global in `notes.c`, hash-size helpers relying on `the_hash_algo`, `expand_loose_notes_ref()`, callers in `log`, `format-patch`, `diff-tree`, and `format-rev` that receive but do not propagate a repository to `format_note()`, and the entirely unconverted `notes-merge.[c,h]` and `notes-utils.[c,h]`.

The series remains under review with no merge status; Patrick Steinhardt's confirmation on the scoping of the remaining `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` guard in `notes.c` is the key open question for the next iteration.
