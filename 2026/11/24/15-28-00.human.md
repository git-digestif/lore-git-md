This is a substantive review of patch 5/6 in an ongoing `the_repository` removal series targeting the notes subsystem. Christian Couder replies to Okhuomon Ajayi's explanation of why the series leaves the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` guard in `notes.c`, and while he accepts her scoping rationale, he raises a coherence concern about the current patch boundaries.

Couder points out an apparent inconsistency: the series removes the guard from `notes-utils.[c,h]` in patch 6/6, yet leaves it in `notes.c` even though some functions still relying on the global are reached from `notes-utils.c` code paths. He specifically notes that the hash-size helpers, which Ajayi left on `the_hash_algo`, are called from `create_notes_commit()` through `write_notes_tree()`, meaning the cleanup is not as cleanly separated as the patch structure suggests.

Rather than pushing to expand the series scope, Couder suggests the opposite: limit the v2 to just the first five patches and reframe the cover letter to explicitly present the work as a preparatory series. He recommends the cover letter list precisely what remains undone in `notes.c`, `notes-utils.[c,h]`, and `notes-merge.[c,h]` and why, so future series can address those gaps explicitly. This would make the series' incremental nature clearer and avoid the impression that the guard removal is complete when it is only partial.

The feedback is constructive and focused on series organization rather than code correctness. It acknowledges that the first five patches do substantial, well-executed work in `notes.[c,h]` and its callers, while suggesting that shipping patch 6/6 as-is muddies the message. Couder closes by encouraging a v2 that incorporates reviewer suggestions but drops the sixth patch to maintain a cleaner, more honest scope.
