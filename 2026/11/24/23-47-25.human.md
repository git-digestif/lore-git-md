This is a review reply from Junio C Hamano on patch 2/6 of Patrick Steinhardt's six-patch packfile lifecycle refactoring series. The patch performs a mechanical rename of seven lifecycle functions to adopt a consistent `packfile_` prefix, touching 21 files across `builtin/`, `commit-graph.c`, HTTP transport code, and the ODB source layer.

Junio C Hamano approves the scoping decision to limit the rename to lifecycle functions rather than attempting a full modernization of `struct packed_git` itself, noting that this makes the patch much easier to review. He specifically praises renaming `add_packed_git()` to `packfile_from_idx()`, pointing out that the old name was misleading because the function never actually added the pack to a store. He confirms that all callers of the renamed symbols are consistently updated and that behavior is preserved.

He identifies one minor cleanup opportunity: a forward declaration for `struct object_database` that moved within `packfile.h` is already unnecessary because `odb.h` is included at the top of the header, so moving it can be skipped or the declaration dropped separately. Aside from that, he considers the step done very nicely and signals clear approval for this patch in the series.
