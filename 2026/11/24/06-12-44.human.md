This is a bugfix, specifically the first of three patches in the v5 series that fixes a data-loss race in `git repack -d`. Patch 1/3 targets the cruft-pack reachability walk in `git pack-objects`, where a stale kept-pack cache prevented the traversal from seeing packs that had been unmarked between the collection and walk phases.

The core issue is that `pack-objects` builds a cache of kept packs when collecting fresh objects, and the subsequent cruft walk reuses that cache even after unmarking unlisted packs. As a result, the walk would stop at an unlisted pack and fail to rescue expired objects reachable only through it, leaving them to be deleted with their pack. The patch adds `repo_invalidate_kept_pack_caches()` in `packfile.c` and calls it after unmarking so the walk correctly traverses all packs except the fresh ones. It also treats `--keep-pack` packs as "open" kept packs so the walk passes through them without copying their objects, matching the behavior of `--honor-pack-keep`.

The fix deliberately allows the cruft walk to traverse alternate object directories and `--keep-pack` packs, which can significantly slow down `repack -d -l --cruft` in shared clones, but this is an intentional safety trade-off. Tests in `t/t5329-pack-objects-cruft.sh` cover traversal through unlisted packs, `--keep-pack` packs, and alternates with and without `--local`.

Jeff King has provided a Reviewed-by for this patch, and the commit message is thorough, explaining both the cache invalidation and the handling of alternates and keep-packs. Combined with the series context, this looks well-crafted and ready for integration.
