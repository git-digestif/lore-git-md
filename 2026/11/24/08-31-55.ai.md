This is a documentation patch, the fifth iteration of an editorial cleanup to the `gitcli` man page (`Documentation/gitcli.adoc`). Dirk Gouders submits v5 to restore a wording fix in the disambiguation rule that was accidentally regressed in v4. Where v4 had changed the text to say an ambiguous argument "can be disambiguated by placing `--` between them", v5 reverts to the earlier approved phrasing "it can be disambiguated by placing `--` before or after it", directly addressing Junio C Hamano's objection that "between them" was misleading for cases like `git log main next` where both arguments exist as files and branches and the separator must be placed between the revision and the path.

Beyond that restoration, the patch applies three other minor grammatical and stylistic fixes to the same file. It corrects "convention" to "conventions" in the opening description, adjusts the disambiguation bullet to maintain singular agreement ("When an argument... it can be disambiguated"), and rewrites the short-option splitting rule from the gerund "Splitting short options to separate words" into the imperative "Split short options into separate words" with adjusted punctuation, incorporating Andy Koppe's suggestion that "into" reads better than "to".

The commit message explicitly lists these changes and notes that the v5-specific change restores a lost edit caused by tooling issues during a base migration. The range-diff against v4 confirms that only the disambiguation bullet changes in this iteration, reverting to the v3 text while the other three lines remain as accepted in v4.

The topic remains under review with no merge to any integration branch. This iteration appears to resolve the sole open objection from the maintainer, leaving the patch as a clean, purely editorial improvement with no technical behavior changes. For future reference, the key locations are `Documentation/gitcli.adoc` around lines 8, 32, and 79, where the old text used singular "convention", "between them", and "Splitting... to separate words", now replaced with "conventions", "before or after it", and "Split... into separate words" respectively.
