This is a substantive review of patch 3 in Johannes Schindelin's v2 series hardening Git against Coverity-flagged integer overflows. Phillip Wood endorses the core change -- altering `write_in_full()` to return `int` (0 or -1) instead of `ssize_t` to avoid overflow when accumulating byte counts -- but he goes further by auditing the call sites and identifying several that still treat the return value as a byte count or could be simplified.

Wood notes that `config.c`'s `write_section()` and `write_pair()` propagate the result unchanged and might switch to `int` for consistency, while `entry.c`, `parallel-checkout.c`, and `odb/streaming.c` still store the return in a `ssize_t` despite the new prototype. Most interestingly, he points out that `refs.c`, `sequencer.c`, and `transport.c` already stored the result in an `int` and checked for negative values, meaning this patch quietly fixes potential bugs in those callers. His feedback is precise and file-specific, signaling he traced the actual usage patterns rather than just skimming the diff.
