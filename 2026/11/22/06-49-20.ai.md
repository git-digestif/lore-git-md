Substantive review of the `[PATCH v4 0/5]` utf8 bounds-fix series. Andy Koppe replies to Dirk Gouders's v4 cover letter, arguing the series has become unfocused and recommending it concentrate on three items: the comment fix for `utf8_strnwidth()`, the optional end pointer for `display_mode_esc_sequence_len()` with corresponding caller updates, and the end guard plus bounded `utf8_width()` call inside `utf8_strnwidth()`. He questions whether the new `display_mode_esc_sequence_len_all()` helper (patch 5) is needed at all. Most concretely, he reports that patch 4/5 fails to build because the variable `skip` is undeclared, a blocker for the series as posted. The review is substantive in that it pinpoints a specific compilation error and proposes a scope reduction, though it does not address the unit tests or the `remainder` mechanism in detail.
