Thread identity: fourth and final patch of a v5 series by Dirk Gouders <dirk@gouders.net>, a bugfix to the UTF-8 width calculation code in Git.

Core problem or goal: `utf8_strnwidth()` in `utf8.c` previously ignored the caller-supplied `len` boundary by calling `utf8_width(&string, NULL)`, allowing the decoder to read past the buffer end and consume bytes of an incomplete multibyte sequence as if they formed a complete glyph. The fix computes `remainder = orig + len - string` and passes `&remainder` to `utf8_width()`, so a truncated multibyte character at the boundary is not counted.
