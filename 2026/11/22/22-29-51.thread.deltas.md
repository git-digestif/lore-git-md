# Thread deltas

## 2026/11/22

- [2026/11/22/22-29-51 by Stepan Koltsov via GitGitGadget <gitgitgadget@gmail.com>] Stepan Koltsov posted a patch (PR #2501) that skips the prep_exclude() call in valid_cached_dir() when fsmonitor is enabled and the untracked cache entry is still valid, and modifies invalidate_stale_dirs() to also invalidate any directory that has a .gitignore (because editing a .gitignore in place does not change the directory's stat data), reporting that on macOS with a tree of about 89,000 directories and a warm untracked cache, "git status --untracked-files=all" went from about 310 ms to about 65 ms and time in read_directory() from about 255 ms to about 8 ms.
