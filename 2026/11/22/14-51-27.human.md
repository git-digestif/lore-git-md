Bugfix in the UTF-8 width calculation code, and the fourth and final patch of a v5 series.

This patch fixes `utf8_strnwidth()` in `utf8.c` so that it respects the length boundary when measuring display width. Previously, the function called `utf8_width()` without passing its optional boundary argument, which meant the width calculation could read past the specified end of the buffer when it encountered an incomplete multibyte sequence at the boundary. The fix computes the remaining bytes as `remainder = orig + len - string` and passes that pointer to `utf8_width()`, ensuring that chopped sequences are handled correctly rather than reading beyond the buffer.

The patch also adds two unit tests to `t/unit-tests/u-utf8-width.c` using the clar framework, covering CJK characters that are invalidated by the length argument. These verify that `utf8_strnwidth("三bytes long, 二columns wide", 1, 1)` returns 1 and that a length of 3 returns 2, confirming the boundary is now enforced.

As the final patch in its series, this appears to complete a focused effort to harden UTF-8 handling. The change is small and well-motivated -- a five-line modification to `utf8.c` plus test coverage, with a clear commit message that explains both the problem and the mechanism of the fix. It reads as a solid, ready-to-merge bugfix.
