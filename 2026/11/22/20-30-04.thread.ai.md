Core problem: Two tests fail in the built-in fsmonitor daemon test suite -- test 43 (`Matrix[uc:false][fsm:true] move_directory_up`) and test 56 (`Matrix[uc:true][fsm:true] move_directory_up`) -- because the Linux fsmonitor backend crashes fatally during the `move_directory_up` operation. Current status: Root cause identified by Gouders; no fix or patch proposed yet.
