This is a follow-up ping on the split-index/`index.skipHash` corruption bugfix thread. Tanga Joris replies to his own patch with a gentle request for someone to verify that the fix resolves the reported problem on their side, effectively asking for test confirmation or review feedback before the patch can progress.

The email itself contains no new technical content; it serves as a reminder that the proposed fix -- ensuring `write_shared_index()` always computes and includes the trailing checksum when naming the shared index file, even when `index.skipHash` is set -- remains unverified by anyone other than the author. The underlying issue is that with both `core.splitIndex` and `index.skipHash` enabled, the shared index filename becomes all zeros, causing the split index to record a null base OID and silently discard entries, eventually triggering `BUG: wt-status.c:585` crashes. The patch modifies `do_write_index()` and `write_shared_index()` in `read-cache.c` to pass a `skip_hash` flag that is forced off for the shared index, and adds two tests to `t/t1700-split-index.sh` covering the hash behavior and first-entry preservation. No maintainer has yet weighed in, and the patch has not been queued in any integration branch.
