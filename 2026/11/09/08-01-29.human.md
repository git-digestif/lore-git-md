This is a short follow-up in an ongoing patch series that aims to speed up `` `git log --follow` `` when tracking renames in non-linear history.

The series, now at v5, is led by Miklos Vajna. In this email, Miklos acknowledges Patrick Steinhardt’s feedback from v4: he has squashed the final two patches (v4’s 5/6 and 6/6) into a single commit so that no patch in the series fixes a regression introduced by an earlier patch in the same series. He also switched from an interdiff to a range-diff in v5, as Patrick requested, to make it easier to spot which commits changed between iterations.

The thread itself is still under active review; no merge signal has been given yet.
