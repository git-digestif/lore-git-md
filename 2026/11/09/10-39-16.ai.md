This is the second patch in Aniket Gupta’s three-patch bugfix series for the bundle-uri subsystem, now under active policy debate. The patch addresses a minor information-disclosure risk: when `relative_url_gently()` (introduced in patch 1) fails to resolve a relative URI, the error message it generates now anonymizes any credentials present in the original URL before printing it. The implementation is correct and the test coverage is appropriate, but the necessity of this change is now in question.

Derrick Stolee’s substantive review raised two key points: (1) the user already knows the problematic URL (since they provided it), and (2) environments storing logs should already be sanitizing command output. Aniket acknowledges this in his reply, noting that the anonymization only applies to this specific error message and that other parts of Git (e.g., "failed to download bundle from URI" warnings) still print raw URLs containing credentials. He also points out that Git already hides passwords in other contexts (e.g., the "From" line of `git fetch`), but concedes the patch is separable and could be dropped if reviewers deem it unnecessary.

The patch itself is uncontroversial in its mechanics—it calls `transport_anonymize_url()` on the original URL and frees the temporary string—but the policy question remains open. Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt have not yet weighed in on this specific patch, but their broader critiques of patch 2/3 (credential anonymization and URL-trimming) suggest this patch may need revision or splitting in a v4. The series as a whole is still under review, with no merge in sight. Key files touched: `remote.c` and `t/t0060-path-utils.sh`.
