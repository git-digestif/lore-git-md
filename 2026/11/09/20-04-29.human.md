This is a **refactoring follow-up** in the ongoing discussion about Laveesh Bansal’s patch to unify the reflog expiration loops. Junio weighs in on Patrick Steinhardt’s suggestion to **deduplicate refs** in the new single-loop design.

Junio agrees with Patrick that the current two-loop model is buggy when the same reflog appears multiple times (via `--all` and a command-line argument, or repeated command-line arguments). He endorses deduplication as a natural and nearly cost-free improvement enabled by the collect-then-process pattern. No new technical objections are raised; the focus is now on whether deduplication should be folded into the current patch or treated as a separate follow-up. The author’s earlier preference for a separate patch (to preserve `--verbose` output order) is now countered by Junio’s view that duplicate processing is itself a bug.
