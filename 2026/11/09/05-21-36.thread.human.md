This thread began as an Outreachy internship proposal from Fien Dora to improve Git's command-line argument parsing by converting the manual option loops in `git diff-index` and `git diff-tree` to the shared `parse_options()` API. Fien, whose earlier microproject was accepted into `next`, is mentored by Christian Couder and Siddharth Asthana, and the proposal remains under discussion with no technical objections or reviews yet; the key open questions involve preserving edge-case behavior such as `--merge-base`/`--stdin` mutual exclusivity, abbreviated option names, and the interaction with `setup_revisions()` and `diff_merges_suppress_m_parsing()`.

A brief side discussion about mailing-list formatting conventions also unfolded in the thread. Richard Kerry asked for blank lines in long quoted reply blocks, Junio C Hamano confirmed the existing 80-column wrapping guidance and his preference for blank lines between paragraphs including quoted text, and Okhuomon Ajayi volunteered to formalize this in the contributor documentation. Ajayi has now sent that patch, adding three lines to `Documentation/MyFirstContribution.adoc` instructing contributors to leave a blank line before and after quoted blocks, which closes the formatting side discussion.
