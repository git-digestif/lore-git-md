This is a substantive review of a proposed refactoring in `fast-import.c`, part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. Patrick Steinhardt rejects the specific change Joy suggested—replacing manual pack iteration with `repo_for_each_pack()`—but clarifies the broader architectural direction.

Patrick explains that `repo_for_each_pack()` is itself a transitional helper, not a long-term solution, because it still assumes a packfile-based backend. The real goal is to eliminate such low-level iteration entirely in favor of higher-level ODB interfaces. For `fast-import`, this means using ODB transactions, an area Karthik Nayak is already exploring. The rejection is technical and forward-looking, not a critique of Joy’s idea in isolation.

Key technical context for follow-up: the functions involved (`store_object()`, `stream_blob()`), the proposed iterator (`repo_for_each_pack()`), and the alternative approach (ODB transactions).
