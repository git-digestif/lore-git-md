This is a surface-level review comment on the first patch of Patrick Steinhardt’s v2 CI speedup series. Junio C Hamano notes that the patch replaces `Expand-Archive` with `tar -xf` to extract the Git for Windows SDK, a change that reduces extraction time from ~3 minutes to ~10 seconds. He acknowledges the improvement but raises a portability concern: the script (`ci/install-sdk.ps1`) currently assumes the presence of a BSD-derived `tar` that can handle ZIP archives, which may not hold across all CI environments. Junio suggests adding a comment to document this assumption, ensuring future maintainers are aware of the dependency. The comment is purely precautionary—no functional issue is identified, and the patch itself remains uncontroversial. The broader series (seven patches targeting GitLab CI bottlenecks) is otherwise well-received, with Junio previously stating no technical objections.
