This is a follow-up in the ongoing bugfix series "bundle-uri: skip unresolvable relative URIs instead of dying." Patrick Steinhardt chimes in to clarify two points about patch 2/3, which adds credential anonymization to error messages when URI resolution fails.

First, he gently corrects Aniket Gupta’s earlier reply: the quoted phrase “I'm less convinced on this factor” does not appear in Derrick Stolee’s review, and the context was stripped, making the exchange harder to follow. This is a minor procedural note rather than a technical objection.

Second, he weighs in on the substance. While he has no strong opinion on whether credentials should be anonymized in error messages (deferring to Stolee), he firmly supports the other half of patch 2: printing the *original* URL in the error message rather than the partially processed one. Without this, users cannot diagnose why a relative URI failed to resolve, since the error message would show only a stripped-down path that no longer contains the problematic `../` components. This part of the patch is uncontroversial and should remain.
