This is the second patch in Junio C Hamano’s two-patch v2 series targeting the `the_repository` removal effort. It is a purely mechanical refactoring that renames every local variable named `index` to `istate` in `unpack-trees.c` (94 lines changed, 47 insertions, 47 deletions). The change is confined to a single file and does not alter behavior or test coverage; all existing tests continue to pass.

The patch follows directly from the first patch, which introduced the `unpack_repo()` helper and eliminated direct uses of `the_repository`. By standardizing on `istate` for `struct index_state *` variables, it removes a source of visual ambiguity—`index` could be misread as an integer offset rather than a pointer to the index state. The commit message is minimal but sufficient: it states the goal (“Make them consistent”) and nothing else.

Review feedback on v1 was positive and non-blocking. Derrick Stolee endorsed the rename as helpful cleanup, and Patrick Steinhardt’s earlier feedback on patch 1/2 (whitespace nits) has already been addressed in this v2. No new concerns have surfaced; the patch is stable and appears ready to proceed to `next` alongside its companion.
