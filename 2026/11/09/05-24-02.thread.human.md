This thread is a small but long-overdue bugfix for option parsing consistency in Git. Laveesh Bansal identified that `git log --glob <pattern>` has always accepted the glob as a separate argument, but `git rev-parse` and `git shortlog` only recognized the `--glob=<pattern>` form. Users who tried `--glob 'heads/*'` as two words would get either a literal ref error (rev-parse) or an unknown option error (shortlog). The fix teaches both commands to accept the detached form, making the behavior uniform across all three commands. The change is minimal—just a few lines in `builtin/rev-parse.c` and `revision.c`—and flips four tests that had been marked as known breakages since 2011.

The patch is straightforward and uncontroversial, addressing a clear inconsistency without introducing new edge cases. The author notes that similar gaps exist for `--exclude` and `--exclude-hidden` but leaves those for a separate patch, while `--branches`, `--tags`, and `--remotes` remain in the `=` form because they take optional arguments. There’s been no review traffic yet, so the patch is still awaiting feedback from the usual suspects (likely Peff or Junio, given the low-level nature of the change). If it lands, it’ll be a quiet but welcome cleanup for users who’ve tripped over this inconsistency.
