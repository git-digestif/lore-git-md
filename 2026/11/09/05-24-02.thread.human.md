This thread is a single-patch bugfix from Laveesh Bansal that makes `git rev-parse` and `git shortlog` accept `--glob <pattern>` as two separate arguments, matching the long-standing behavior of `git log`, where previously only the `--glob=<pattern>` form worked. The change is small: `builtin/rev-parse.c` and `revision.c` now consume the next argument when `--glob` is seen, and four tests in `t/t6018-rev-list-glob.sh` that were previously marked as known breakage now pass and have been converted to regular tests. The commit message points to the 2011 commit 0fc63ec4e7 that introduced the inconsistency.

The author has sent a follow-up ping noting that two weeks have passed since submission; the patch still applies cleanly to current master at f7de814569 and t6018 passes, but no review feedback or objections have been recorded yet. Laveesh also notes that `--exclude` and `--exclude-hidden` share the same two-word parsing gap and will be addressed in a separate follow-up series if this patch is accepted.
