Stéphane Glondu, a Debian developer, suggests **Belenios** as a third voting platform option alongside CIVS and devotee. Belenios is a Condorcet-based system that Debian already uses for its own elections, and it’s available as a hosted service (with a Debian instance at `vote.debian.net`) or as self-hosted software.

The suggestion is **brief and technical**, adding a new candidate to the platform debate without weighing in on eligibility criteria or other governance questions. Belenios’s key advantage is its **alignment with Debian’s practices**—a point that may appeal to contributors who value proven, community-run infrastructure. Like devotee, it avoids the platform bias concerns raised by GitHub-based voting, and like CIVS, it’s available as a hosted service (reducing setup overhead).
