This is the seventh and final patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s v2 series to speed up GitLab CI. It targets the `t5551-http-fetch-smart.sh` test, which creates nearly 100,000 references to verify HTTP fetch behavior. The patch switches the test repositories from the traditional "files" ref backend to the reftable backend, reducing the runtime on macOS from 20 minutes to 2 minutes (a 10x speedup) and halving the runtime on Linux. The change is minimal—just two lines in the test script—and does not affect the test’s core functionality, as the focus is on HTTP negotiation rather than ref backend performance. This patch, like the rest of the series, is a low-risk optimization with clear before/after metrics, reinforcing the series’ goal of measurable CI speedups.
