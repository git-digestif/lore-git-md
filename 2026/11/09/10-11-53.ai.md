This is a reply in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort targeting the notes subsystem. The series, now complete at six patches, refactors `notes.c` and `notes-utils.c` to store a `struct repository *` in `struct notes_tree` and update all notes API functions to use it, eliminating the need for downstream guards. This email responds to Patrick Steinhardt’s substantive review question about why the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` guard in `notes.c` itself remains.

Okhuomon Ajayi explains that the series was scoped to code paths that already receive a `struct notes_tree *`, leaving out functions that rely on the global `default_notes_tree` or do not propagate a repository parameter. Specifically, `display_notes()` maintains state in globals, and commands like `log`, `format-patch`, `diff-tree`, and `format-rev` receive a repository but do not pass it through to `format_note()`, which falls back to the global `default_notes_tree`. The `expand_loose_notes_ref()` helper only takes a `strbuf`, and hash-size helpers still use the global `the_hash_algo`. The reply acknowledges these limitations and signals that removing the remaining guard would require broader refactoring beyond the current series’ scope.

The explanation is pragmatic and technically grounded: the series delivers a clean, incremental step toward repository-agnostic notes APIs, but the global `default_notes_tree` and its downstream dependencies remain as follow-up work. This positions the series as a well-scoped architectural improvement rather than a complete overhaul, and the remaining guard is now clearly marked as a known limitation rather than an oversight. The thread is likely to continue with discussion about whether the guard should be removed in a future series or if the current approach is sufficient for now.
