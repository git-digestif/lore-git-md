Joy has agreed to revise the patch using compound literals, listing all struct members explicitly—including those set to zero—rather than relying on `memset()`. Junio’s reply here is a brief acknowledgment of Joy’s plan, with a gentle reminder about mailing-list etiquette (no top-posting). The technical substance is settled: the v2 patch will adopt compound literals, aligning with Git’s current style preferences.

The patch itself is a small, uncontroversial refactoring with no behavior change, CLI impact, or test coverage implications.
