This is the first round of review feedback on Okhuomon Ajayi’s six-patch series to refactor the notes subsystem as part of the `the_repository` removal effort. Patrick Steinhardt, whose architectural concern about unsafe `the_repository` usage in the notes API motivated the series, responds to the first patch with two focused suggestions.

Patrick’s feedback is narrowly technical and constructive. He suggests reordering the parameters in `init_notes()` so that `struct notes_tree` comes first, arguing that the function belongs to the notes subsystem and should follow its conventions. He also proposes renaming the function to `notes_tree_init()` to make the ownership clearer. For `load_notes_trees()`, he agrees with the new signature but suggests the analogous rename to `notes_tree_load()`. Neither suggestion changes the substance of the patch—only the clarity and consistency of the API.

The review is substantive but lightweight: it engages with the patch’s design (parameter order, naming) without questioning the overall direction. Patrick does not raise deeper concerns about the `repo` field or the series’ approach, and his only aside about the “cursed” default notes tree is observational rather than prescriptive. The feedback is actionable and should be straightforward to incorporate in a reroll.

This is a positive signal for the series: the reviewer whose concern sparked the work is now reviewing the implementation and offering only minor, mechanical improvements. The patch remains under active review; no merge status yet.
