This is the second patch in a two-part refactoring series targeting the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The patch removes the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` macro from Git’s archival modules by replacing implicit use of the global `the_repository` with explicit `struct repository *repo` parameters. The changes span seven files in the archive subsystem: `archive-tar.c`, `archive-zip.c`, `archive.c`, `archive.h`, `builtin/archive.c`, `builtin/upload-archive.c`, and `diagnose.c`.

The patch is mechanical and follows the established pattern of prior `the_repository` removal patches. Functions like `init_tar_archiver`, `init_zip_archiver`, and `init_archivers` are updated to accept a `struct repository *repo` parameter, and callers are adjusted to pass the correct repository context. The changes are straightforward and do not alter behavior, though one notable adjustment is in `archive-tar.c`, where `the_hash_algo->hexsz` is replaced with `args->repo->hash_algo->hexsz` to ensure the correct hash algorithm is used for the given repository.
