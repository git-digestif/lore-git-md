This is a review reply in the ongoing six-patch series that refactors Git’s notes subsystem to remove implicit uses of the global `the_repository`. The series, authored by Outreachy intern Okhuomon Ajayi, is part of the broader `the_repository` removal effort.

Patrick Steinhardt’s question is brief but pointed: he asks why the series stops short of removing the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` guard in `notes.c` itself. The guard was introduced as a protective measure to prevent unsafe downstream callers from using functions that still implicitly relied on `the_repository`. The current series fixes the root cause by storing the repository in `struct notes_tree` and updating all core functions to use it, which should make the guard unnecessary. Patrick’s expectation is that the guard should be removed as part of this work, and his question suggests he sees this as a logical completion of the series.

The question does not block the patch or the series, but it does highlight a loose end: the guard remains in place even though the unsafe functions it was protecting have been fixed. This could create confusion for future contributors who might wonder why the guard is still present. The answer may lie in the series’ scope (addressing only `notes-utils.c` and its callers first) or in remaining uses of `the_repository` in `notes.c` (such as `display_notes()` and `expand_loose_notes_ref()`), but the email does not provide that context. Okhuomon’s next reply will likely clarify whether this is an oversight or a deliberate staging choice.
