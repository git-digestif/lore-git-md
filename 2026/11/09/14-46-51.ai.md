This trades a small increase in script complexity for potentially greater performance gains, though Torsten notes he has not benchmarked the change. The patch also removes the `rm` calls for `.expect` files, as they are now static and reused throughout the test.

This review is substantive because it engages with the patch’s core optimization goal (reducing process overhead) and suggests a different technical approach.
