Junio responds to Jeff King’s earlier feedback about AsciiDoc processor compatibility by queuing a squashable fix-up commit that adopts Peff’s proposed `--name-only[[name_only]]::` syntax for anchor definitions. The patch touches `Documentation/diff-options.adoc`, converting all anchor definitions from the `[[anchor]]` form to the compatible `--option[[anchor]]::` form for four options: `--stat`, `--name-only`, `--name-status`, and `--word-diff`. The misplaced `[[ignore_all_space]]` anchor (noted by Peff as a pre-existing issue) is also relocated to the correct option, though this correction is incidental to the build compatibility fix. No other files or behavior are affected.

Junio’s fix is mechanical and uncontroversial; it directly implements Peff’s tested workaround without engaging the broader policy question about whether to drop Python AsciiDoc support. The patch is now technically ready for queuing, though the thread remains stalled on two unresolved disputes: Junio’s objection to the horizontal definition list presentation format (due to cut-and-paste issues) and the broader toolchain policy question.
