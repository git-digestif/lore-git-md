This is a follow-up in the `test-tool genbytes` thread, focused on test infrastructure. Johannes Sixt (Hannes) concedes that his proposed `printf`-based alternative to `test-tool genbytes` fails on macOS, as demonstrated by Kristofer Karlsson: when given an extremely large padding value, macOS `printf` silently truncates output to nine literal zeros instead of generating the expected 2 GB of data. Hannes reports that his own CI run passed, but notes he cannot confirm whether the relevant `EXPENSIVE` tests were actually executed. He attaches a revised version of the problematic test (`t4205-log-pretty-formats.sh`) that splits the large padding into three smaller `printf` invocations (each 715,827,883 bytes) to work around the macOS truncation issue. The patch is offered as a technical curiosity rather than a serious counterproposal; the thread remains settled on implementing `test-tool genbytes` with the hexadecimal byte-specification interface already agreed upon in earlier discussion. No new design questions or workflow adjustments are introduced.
