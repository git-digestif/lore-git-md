This is a **test infrastructure patch** addressing a macOS-specific performance bottleneck in the Git test suite. The author responds to Junio C Hamano’s surface-level review of the first patch in the v2 series, which flags the 256 KB stack-allocated buffer in `test-genbytes.c` as potentially problematic on platforms with smaller stack limits (e.g., Windows). Kristofer Karlsson outlines three options for resolving the issue: (1) declare the buffer `static` and document its non-thread-safety, (2) retain the current stack allocation and accept the risk of breakage on constrained platforms, or (3) shrink the buffer size while keeping it on the stack. He expresses no strong preference and is open to any of the three, noting that the current implementation (option 2) is the smallest change but acknowledging the trade-offs. The response does not engage with Patrick Steinhardt’s earlier design proposal for an escape-sequence interface or the mechanical suggestion to use `git_parse_signed()`; those remain open for v3. The tone is collaborative and pragmatic, focusing on implementation hygiene rather than core functionality.
