This is a thread update from Miklos Vajna in the ongoing `[PATCH v4 0/6]` series aimed at speeding up `` `git log --follow` `` for rename detection in non-linear history.

Miklos acknowledges Patrick Steinhardt’s feedback from the v4 review: future iterations will reply directly to individual review comments rather than summarizing responses only in the cover letter. In response to Patrick’s concern about a regression introduced and then fixed within the same series, Miklos has squashed the final two patches (v4’s 5/6 and 6/6) into a single patch (v5’s 5/5), eliminating the intermediate regression. He also switched from an interdiff to a range-diff in v5, making it easier for reviewers to spot changes between iterations. The next version (v5) is now structured so that no patch in the series fixes a bug introduced by an earlier patch in the same series.
