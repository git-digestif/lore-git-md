This is a test infrastructure discussion about the ongoing effort to eliminate a macOS-specific performance bottleneck in Git's test suite. Johannes Sixt (Hannes) replies to Kristofer Karlsson's proposal for a chunked-`printf` helper that avoids the memory overhead of the naive `printf` approach. Hannes rejects the chunked version, arguing that spawning 2000 `printf` processes (if `printf` is not a shell builtin) would be worse than the 2.1 GB RSS cost of the naive approach. He declares the bikeshedding over and endorses Kristofer's earlier suggestion to keep both `test-tool genzeros` (for NUL bytes) and `test-tool genbytes` (for arbitrary bytes) as the cleanest solution. This email effectively closes the `printf`-based alternative discussion and reaffirms the two-command design direction for the v3 series. No new technical details or open questions are introduced; the thread now awaits Kristofer's v3 submission.
