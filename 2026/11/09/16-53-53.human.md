This is a surface-level review comment on the first patch of Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-part CI speedup series. Junio notes that the patch replaces `Expand-Archive` with `tar -xf git-sdk.zip` in `ci/install-sdk.ps1`, achieving a dramatic speedup (3 minutes → 10 seconds). He expresses mild surprise that `tar` can extract zip files, then raises a portability concern: the script currently assumes a BSD-derived `tar` that understands zip archives, but this assumption is not documented. Junio suggests adding a comment to clarify the requirement, ensuring future maintainers understand the dependency. The comment is purely precautionary—no technical issue is identified, and the change itself is uncontroversial. The patch remains a straightforward, well-motivated optimization targeting GitLab CI’s Windows runners.
