This is a brief exchange in the ongoing RFC thread about adding `--verbose` support to `git history reword`. Phillip Wood chimes in to agree with D. Ben Knoble’s earlier concern that the term "verbose" might be ambiguous in the context of `git history`, where it could imply a broader range of behaviors (like a post-execution summary) rather than just showing the diff in the editor during reword. He suggests `--show-diff` as an alternative flag name, though he acknowledges it may not be a clear improvement over Knoble’s proposals (`--commit-verbose` or `--diff-in-editor`).

Phillip also endorses Knoble’s point that the feature should support the `commit.verbose` configuration option from the start, aligning with `git commit --verbose` and avoiding a future breaking change. He doesn’t engage with the implementation details of the patch itself but reinforces the user-interface discussion, signaling that the flag naming and config support are likely to be sticking points before this lands. The tone is collaborative, and the exchange keeps the focus on design clarity rather than technical correctness.
