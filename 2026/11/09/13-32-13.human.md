This is a review of the twelfth patch in Thomas Bachem’s 15-patch series fixing Git segfaults when config keys lack values. The patch targets `scalar reconfigure --all`, which crashes when `scalar.repo` is unvalued. Derrick Stolee’s review is surface-level: he confirms the use of `config_error_nonbool()` is correct and approves the test, but does not engage with the broader series or edge cases. The fix itself is minimal and uncontroversial, following the established pattern of rejecting missing values with a clear error message. Since `scalar register` always writes `scalar.repo` with a path, this change cannot break existing setups. The patch is ready for merging once the earlier patches in the series land.
