This is the latest reply in the ongoing bundle-uri bugfix series, specifically addressing the second patch, which anonymizes credentials in error messages when URI resolution fails. Aniket Gupta acknowledges Derrick Stolee’s skepticism about the necessity of this change, noting that other parts of Git (like the "failed to download bundle from URI" warning) still print raw URLs containing credentials. He points out that Git already hides passwords in some contexts (e.g., the "From" line in fetch output) and suggests this patch aligns with that existing behavior. However, he concedes the change is minor and offers to drop the patch if reviewers deem it unnecessary.

The patch itself remains technically sound and uncontroversial in its implementation, but its value is now in question. The discussion has shifted from *how* to anonymize credentials to *whether* it’s worth doing at all in error messages. Aniket’s reply doesn’t resolve the policy debate but clarifies that the anonymization is limited to a single error message and won’t affect other parts of Git’s output. If the maintainers decide this isn’t a meaningful improvement, the patch will likely be omitted from the series. The rest of the bugfix (skipping unresolvable URIs instead of dying) is unaffected by this decision.
