This is a CI/build system patch (part 3 of 7 in Patrick Steinhardt’s GitLab CI speedup series). It addresses a specific bottleneck in the `fedora-breaking-changes-meson` job, which runs roughly twice as long as other Linux jobs due to unoptimized Meson builds.

The patch changes the Meson build type from the default "debug" (`-O0`) to "debugoptimized" (`-O2`), cutting the Fedora job’s runtime nearly in half (from 2100 to 1000 seconds). The improvement is most dramatic for SHA256 hashing (500MB blob: 9.6s → 1.5s), while SHA1 is less affected. Linux and macOS jobs see smaller but measurable gains (~10%), and Windows remains roughly neutral overall despite a slight build-time regression.

The change is minimal: one line added to `ci/run-build-and-tests.sh` and one flag adjusted in `.gitlab-ci.yml`. No new symbols or on-disk formats are introduced. The series as a whole has already received positive feedback from Junio, and this patch was unchanged from v1, suggesting it’s uncontroversial. The performance impact is well-documented and aligns with the series’ goal of reducing CI runtime without risking correctness.
