This is a reply in the ongoing RFC thread about adding `--verbose` support to `git history reword`. D. Ben Knoble responds to Karthik Nayak’s proposal to make diff display the default behavior, pushing back with a practical concern: long diffs or users who prefer faster editor startup may not want the diff shown every time. He reiterates his preference for keeping the feature opt-in, aligning with his earlier position that the flag’s name should clearly communicate its purpose (e.g., `--diff-in-editor` or `--show-diff`). The exchange sharpens the opt-in vs. opt-out debate but does not resolve it; the thread now has three distinct positions (opt-in with flag, opt-in with config, opt-out) and no clear consensus on either the interface or the default behavior. The discussion remains focused on user experience rather than implementation details.
