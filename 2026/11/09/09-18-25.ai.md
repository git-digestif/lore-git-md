This is a **substantive review** of the first patch in Okhuomon Ajayi’s six-patch series refactoring the notes subsystem to remove implicit `the_repository` usage. The series is part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort.

Patrick Steinhardt’s review focuses on two key interface design choices in the patch. First, he argues that `init_notes()` should retain `struct notes_tree *` as its first parameter (rather than `struct repository *`) because the function belongs to the notes subsystem, and suggests renaming it to `notes_tree_init()` for consistency. Second, he approves the signature change for `load_notes_trees()` (adding a `struct repository *` parameter) and suggests renaming it to `notes_tree_load()` to match the naming pattern.

The review also notes a broader architectural concern: the global `default_notes_tree` in `notes.c` is "cursed" due to its implicit global state, though Patrick acknowledges that fixing this is outside the scope of the current series. He confirms that the patch correctly initializes the default notes tree via `init_notes()`, ensuring the `repo` field is set.

This feedback is **substantive but not blocking**—it proposes interface improvements rather than identifying bugs or regressions. The suggested renames would improve consistency with Git’s naming conventions, and the parameter order change aligns better with subsystem ownership. The review does not require a major rewrite, but the next iteration of the patch will likely incorporate these adjustments. No merge status yet; the series remains under review.
