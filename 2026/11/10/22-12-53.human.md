This is a brief follow-up to Junio’s security release announcement for Git 2.56.1 and related maintenance versions. Todd Zullinger notes that the GitLab work item links included in the announcement (such as the one for CVE-2026-113408) are currently inaccessible to regular users, likely due to internal permissions. He flags this as unintended and copies Patrick Steinhardt, suggesting GitLab might need to adjust the visibility settings so the details are publicly available. Beyond this minor access issue, Todd expresses gratitude to the contributors involved in the release. No technical concerns or objections are raised—this is purely a housekeeping observation about the linked advisories.
