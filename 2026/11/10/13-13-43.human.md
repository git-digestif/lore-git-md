This is a brief exchange in the ongoing RFC thread about adding `--verbose` support to `git history reword`. Karthik Nayak agrees with Phillip Wood’s latest position: making diff display the default behavior for `git history reword` and omitting the option entirely, at least for now. He frames this as a user-friendly default for an experimental command, aligning with the idea that `git history` can evolve based on feedback. Both acknowledge that scripted editing might eventually need a way to suppress the diff (e.g., a `--no-verbose` flag), but they consider that a follow-up concern. The discussion remains focused on the opt-out vs. opt-in question, with no new technical feedback or engagement with D. Ben Knoble’s naming concerns. The tone is collaborative, but the fundamental disagreement about whether diff display should be the default remains unresolved.
