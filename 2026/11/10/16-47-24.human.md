This is a **surface-level review** of the first patch in Jasper Mayone’s four-patch series tightening safety around `git worktree remove`. The patch itself is a **bugfix** that prevents deletion of worktrees in the middle of a rebase, `git am`, bisect, cherry-pick, or revert unless `--force` is given, closing a real safety hole. Phillip Wood’s feedback focuses on **documentation formatting** and **consistency in command naming**, not the patch’s technical substance.

Phillip suggests two editorial improvements to the updated `git-worktree(1)` man page:
1. Each bulleted condition should be a self-contained sentence, so the trailing comma and “, or” should be dropped.
2. The formatting of `git am` (in back-ticks) differs from the other command names (plain text), which looks odd in the rendered HTML. He floats the idea of splitting the list into separate items for each command but isn’t sure whether that would improve clarity.

The rest of the patch—implementation, error messages, and tests—receives no criticism. The review is brief and targeted, so it doesn’t change the assessment that the patch is ready for integration. If the author adopts the suggested wording tweaks, the documentation will be slightly clearer without altering the patch’s behavior or scope.
