This is a surface-level review of the fifth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch series refactoring the Git object database to isolate the loose-object backend. The patch removes the blanket inclusion of `odb/source-loose.h` from `object-file.h` and adds explicit includes in the seven files that still depend on loose-backend internals. Kaartic Sivaraam’s feedback is purely about header hygiene and does not engage with the patch’s functional correctness or the broader ODB abstraction goals.

The review identifies three minor cleanup opportunities: `builtin/pack-objects.c` and `loose.c` no longer need to include `object-file.h`, and `object-file.h` itself may not need `odb/streaming.h`. None of these are blocking issues, and the patch remains a straightforward, mechanical step toward making backend dependencies visible for future abstraction work. The changes align with the series’ goal of modularizing the ODB layer, though the broader effort to fully hide backend internals from callers like the dumb transport is still ongoing.

If you care about the ODB abstraction effort, this patch is a small but necessary piece of the puzzle. The feedback is nit-level and does not suggest any functional problems, so the patch looks ready to proceed once the minor header cleanups are addressed or deferred.
