This is a **surface-level review reply** in the ongoing thread about Jasper Mayone’s four-patch series to improve `git worktree` safety and filtering. Ben Knoble disagrees with Phillip Wood’s earlier suggestion to reformat the bulleted list in the `git worktree remove` documentation.

Phillip had proposed dropping the trailing comma and the ", or" before the final list item to make each bullet a self-contained sentence. Ben argues that the current phrasing ("it has untracked files or modifications in tracked files, it has submodules, or it is in the middle of a rebase...") is clearer and more natural, treating the list as a series of parallel clauses rather than standalone sentences. He sees no need to rewrite the list introduction or items to conform to strict sentence grammar.

The exchange is purely stylistic and does not affect the patch’s technical content. The underlying implementation—making `git worktree remove` refuse deletion of in-progress worktrees unless `--force` is given—remains uncontroversial and well-motivated. This minor documentation debate is unlikely to block the patch, but it may prompt Jasper to reconsider the formatting in a future reroll.
