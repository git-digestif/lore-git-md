This is a follow-up in the RFC thread about adding `--verbose` support to `` `git history reword` ``. Karthik Nayak agrees with Phillip Wood’s latest position: make diff display the **default behavior** for `` `git history reword` ``, citing the command’s experimental status and the principle of better defaults for users. He acknowledges the scripted-editing concern Phillip raised (where the diff could interfere with automated edits) but suggests deferring any opt-out mechanism (e.g., a `--no-verbose` flag or environment variable) to a future patch. The reply does not engage with D. Ben Knoble’s naming or opt-in arguments, nor does it address the technical feedback about config support or diff-generation edge cases. Instead, it reinforces the opt-out proposal, positioning it as a user-experience improvement rather than a technical necessity. The thread now has two reviewers (Karthik and Phillip) advocating for default diff display, while the author and D. Ben Knoble maintain the opt-in position.
