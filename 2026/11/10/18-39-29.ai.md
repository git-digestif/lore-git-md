This is the second patch in Srujith A Poojary’s v3 series removing `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` from Git’s archival modules. It directly addresses Junio C Hamano’s feedback on v2 by simplifying the design: the archiver initialization functions no longer take an explicit `struct repository *` parameter, instead relying on `the_repository` internally. The patch touches two files (`archive-tar.c` and `builtin/archive.c`) and makes three key changes:

1. Moves the `repo_config(the_repository, git_tar_config, NULL)` call to the start of `init_tar_archiver()`, ensuring configuration is loaded even when `cmd_archive()` is invoked outside a repository context (a potential edge case Junio flagged in v2).
2. Replaces `the_hash_algo->hexsz` with `args->repo->hash_algo->hexsz` in `write_global_extended_header()`, aligning with the series’ goal of eliminating implicit global state.
3. Adds a temporary `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` macro in `builtin/archive.c` to maintain compatibility while other archival modules are converted.

The changes are mechanical and follow established patterns from prior `the_repository` removal patches. The patch is uncontroversial in its current form, as it resolves the edge case Junio identified while avoiding the complexity of passing repository parameters through the archiver initialization API. The temporary macro in `builtin/archive.c` remains a loose end, but this is explicitly acknowledged as a transitional measure. No functional changes to user-visible behavior are introduced. Reviewers may focus on verifying the safety of the simplified design, particularly the moved `repo_config()` call, though the v3 revision explicitly addresses this concern.
