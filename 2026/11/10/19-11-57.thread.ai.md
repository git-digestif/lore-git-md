Thread identity: Patch series introducing `checkout.orphanedCommitWarning` configuration to disable orphaned commit warnings during `git checkout`/`git switch` for performance in large mono-repos. Series version: v1 (initial). Originating author: Wyatt Holden (Microsoft). Thread type: feature series.

Core problem/goal: Address performance bottleneck in `git checkout`/`git switch` when leaving detached HEAD by adding a configurable opt-out for the reachability check that warns about orphaned commits. The check can consume >90% of checkout time in large mono-repos (e.g., 193s out of 205s in Microsoft’s internal repo). Goal is to preserve default safety while allowing opt-out for performance-critical workflows, though Junio C Hamano has raised the alternative of optimizing the reachability check itself (e.g., early cutoff in commit-traversal loop) rather than making it optional.
