This is the second patch in Srujith A Poojary’s v3 series removing `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` from Git’s archive subsystem. Junio C Hamano’s reply is a **substantive review** that raises sharp questions about the patch’s clarity and correctness, particularly around the order of configuration loading in `init_tar_archiver()` and the unexplained removal of the macro from `archive-tar.c`.

The patch claims to clean up `archive-tar.c`, `archive-zip.c`, and related files, but Junio points out that `archive-zip.c` is untouched in the diff, making the commit message misleading. He flags three specific changes as unexplained: the removal of the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` macro from `archive-tar.c` (which appears cosmetic), the reordering of `repo_config()` and built-in filter configuration in `init_tar_archiver()` (which reverses the intended precedence of user config vs. defaults), and the addition of the macro in `builtin/archive.c` (which the commit message does not justify). Only the change to `write_global_extended_header()`—replacing `the_hash_algo` with `args->repo->hash_algo`—receives explicit approval as a correct step toward eliminating global state.

The review suggests the patch is **under-motivated and sloppy**: the commit message overpromises, the diff omits advertised changes, and key hunks lack rationale. While the series’ goal (removing `the_repository` usage) is uncontroversial, this iteration appears rushed and may need a thorough rewrite.
