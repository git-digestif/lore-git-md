This is a brief but substantive review from Junio C Hamano responding to Wyatt Holden’s three-patch series that introduces `checkout.orphanedCommitWarning` to disable the orphaned-commit reachability check during `git checkout` and `git switch`. Junio acknowledges the series is well-structured and clearly motivated—it addresses a real performance bottleneck in large mono-repos where the check can consume over 90% of checkout time. However, he flags some design choices in the first patch as questionable, though he doesn’t specify which ones in this email. Earlier in the thread, he raised two main concerns: whether the config knob is the right solution (suggesting instead that the reachability check itself could be optimized with an early cutoff), and why there’s no command-line option to override the setting. This reply leaves those questions open, so the thread remains unresolved. The tone is constructive, and the praise for clarity suggests the series is technically sound, but the design debate is still live.
