This is a test modernization patch series targeting the `git fast-import` test suite. Karthik Nayak is splitting up the sprawling `t9300-fast-import.sh` (currently ~4,000 lines) into smaller, self-contained test files, each focused on a specific aspect of `fast-import` functionality.

The series consists of 20 patches that progressively detach groups of tests from `t9300` and relocate them to new or existing test files (`t9301`, `t9304`, `t9307`, etc.). Each patch isolates one logical cluster—marks, ref updates, stream handling, notes, submodules, and so on—so that tests no longer depend on the state left by earlier ones. The final patch drops the old “series letter” naming convention from the moved tests.

The net result is a much shorter `t9300` (shrinking by ~2,800 lines) and eight new test files that are easier to navigate and maintain. Karthik notes that more cleanup is possible but has stopped here to keep the series manageable; he plans follow-up work based on how this batch is received. The patches are kept separate for readability, though squashing could be considered later.
