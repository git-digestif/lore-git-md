This thread is a 20-patch series by Karthik Nayak to modernize Git's test suite by splitting the unwieldy 4000-line `t9300-fast-import.sh` into smaller, self-contained test files. The series has strong support from Junio Hamano, who called the cleanup "long overdue," and is now at its sixteenth patch.

The work is purely organizational—no changes to Git's behavior or on-disk format—but aims to make the test suite more maintainable by eliminating shared state between tests. Each patch isolates a group of related tests (e.g., marks, stream handling, notes, or command-line options) into new files like `t9301-fast-import-notes.sh` or `t9304-fast-import-marks.sh`, with setup helpers to ensure consistent configuration. The latest patch (v2's sixteenth) refactors the "feature" and command-line option tests, wrapping each in a subshell with a fresh repository to prevent state leakage. The series has already reduced `t9300` to about 1200 lines, with the remaining patches expected to complete the cleanup.

While the series is under review, no major objections or open questions have surfaced. Reviewers are focusing on verifying the isolation logic (e.g., subshell scoping and cleanup) and ensuring no regressions are introduced. The work is part of Git's broader test modernization effort, which includes adopting modern test patterns and tools like `clar` for unit testing.
