Karthik Nayak has posted v2 of a 20-patch series that splits the ~4000-line monolithic `t9300-fast-import.sh` into smaller, self-contained test files such as `t9301-fast-import-notes.sh`, `t9307-fast-import-validation.sh`, and `t9311-fast-import-refs.sh`, grouping tests by functionality (marks, stream handling, notes, submodules, refs, and so on). Each moved test now runs in a fresh repository with explicit setup helpers, eliminating the side effects and shared state that made the original file hard to maintain, and the legacy "series letter" prefixes are being dropped from test names. Maintainer Junio C Hamano has strongly endorsed the direction ("cleaning up this script is long overdue"), but the series remains under review with no merge status reported.

Review so far has been substantive but has surfaced open questions rather than objections. Kaartic Sivaraam approved the early refactoring steps -- including moving the `fastimport.unpackLimit` configuration to the first test that needs it and making the "series B" and "series T" tests self-contained -- with brief "looks good" replies, though he has not recorded formal Reviewed-by trailers. He raised two unresolved points: whether later patches will switch persistent `git config` calls to `test_config` for better self-containment, and what the performance cost is of initializing roughly 16 additional repositories across the series. No other reviewers have weighed in yet.

Still open is how the remaining test groups left in `t9300` (A, C, D, H, L, M, N, U) will be disentangled, since they still share state, and whether the performance and `test_config` questions get addressed before the series graduates.
