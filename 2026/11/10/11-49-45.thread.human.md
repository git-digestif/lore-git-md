Karthik Nayak's twenty-patch v2 series continues to refactor the monolithic `t/t9300-fast-import.sh` test file into smaller, self-contained scripts grouped by functionality, reducing the original ~4000-line file to about 1200 lines while adding new files such as `t9307-fast-import-validation.sh` through `t9311-fast-import-refs.sh`. Junio C Hamano has strongly endorsed the effort, calling the cleanup "long overdue," and the series remains under review with no merge status yet reported. The refactoring eliminates shared state between tests by giving each its own fresh repository and reusable setup helpers, and drops legacy "series letter" prefixes from test descriptions.

The one substantive open question—raised by Kaartic Sivaraam about the performance cost of creating and tearing down sixteen additional repositories in the early patches—has now been answered with concrete data. Tanga Joris measured the impact on a 4-core machine and found that running the fast-import tests sequentially increased from 17.8 to 21.1 seconds, while parallel execution with `prove -j4` went from 11.0 to 11.9 seconds. He concludes that the modest overhead—about 3.3 seconds sequential and under a second parallel—is worth the significant readability and maintainability gains. Other review feedback from Kaartic Sivaraam has been largely surface-level, confirming the mechanical correctness of the moves and isolation logic without formal Reviewed-by trailers, and Karthik has agreed to minor commit-message clarifications without re-rolling the series solely for wording.
