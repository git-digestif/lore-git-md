This thread is about a 20-patch series from Karthik Nayak that modernizes the `t9300-fast-import.sh` test file by splitting it into smaller, self-contained test files. The series aims to address the unmaintainability of the current ~4000-line monolithic file, where tests depend on side effects from earlier tests. The sixth patch in the series makes the "series Q" tests (notes-related fast-import functionality) independent by introducing a `setup_notes_repo()` helper and removing shared state dependencies. This is a purely mechanical change with no behavior modifications, preparing the ground for future relocation of these tests to a separate file.

The series is still in its early stages, with the initial version (v1) posted and under review. No maintainer decisions or integration status (e.g., `pu`, `next`, `master`) have been reported yet, and no reviews or objections have surfaced so far. The focus remains on ensuring the refactoring doesn’t introduce regressions, given the series' size and the mechanical nature of the changes. This effort is part of the broader **test modernization** initiative in the Git project.
