Karthik Nayak's twenty-patch v2 series continues the effort to split the monolithic `t9300-fast-import.sh` into smaller, self-contained test files grouped by functionality. The series makes each test run in a fresh repository via helpers like `setup_marks_repo()` and the newly introduced `setup_checkpoint_repo()`, eliminates cross-test state leakage, and drops the legacy "series letter" prefixes from test descriptions. Junio C Hamano has endorsed the direction with strong praise, but the series remains under review with no merge status reported.
