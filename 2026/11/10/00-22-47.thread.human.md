Justin Tobler's six-patch v2 series proposes eagerly creating the temporary object directory at the start of ODB transactions rather than lazily on first write, ensuring all transactional loose object writes are staged in the temporary directory regardless of fsync configuration. The series expands from four to six patches, incorporating feedback from the v1 review cycle: it renames the default prefix from "bulk-fsync" to "transaction," refactors `tmp_objdir_create()` to preserve `errno` on failure, cleans up die() messages in `odb/transaction.h` to say "object" instead of "ODB," and moves the actual directory creation into `odb_transaction_files_begin()` while removing the lazy paths from the write functions. The changes touch `object-file.c`, `tmp-objdir.c`, `odb/source-loose.c`, and the unwritable-directory test, with a small measured performance cost on `git add`.

Patrick Steinhardt, who raised substantive concerns during v1 about batched-fsync scope and code structure, has since endorsed the respun version as sensible, though without a formal Reviewed-by trailer. Junio C Hamano has now acknowledged Patrick's review with "Will queue," signaling the maintainer intends to pick the series up into his integration queue, though no specific branch was named and no merge to `master` has occurred. Karthik Nayak's fundamental design question -- whether eager creation is architecturally justified over the lazy approach -- remains unanswered and unaddressed by the latest replies, but has not blocked the series from advancing.
