This is a surface-level review of the second patch in Wyatt Holden’s three-patch series introducing `checkout.orphanedCommitWarning`. Derrick Stolee points out a minor whitespace alignment issue in the `advice.c` file, where the new `ADVICE_CHECKOUT_ORPHANED_COMMIT_WARNING` entry doesn’t match the existing indentation style. The comment is purely cosmetic—no functional or behavioral concerns are raised.

The series as a whole targets a real performance bottleneck in large mono-repos, where the orphaned-commit reachability check can consume over 90% of checkout time. This patch adds an advice message that triggers when the check exceeds two seconds, guiding users to the new config knob. The feedback here is trivial and won’t require significant rework. If you’re following the series for its performance implications, this review doesn’t change the substance of the proposal.
