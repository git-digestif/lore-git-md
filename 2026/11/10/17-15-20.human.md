This is a reply in the ongoing RFC thread about adding `--verbose` support to `` `git history reword` ``. D. Ben Knoble weighs in on the opt-in versus opt-out debate, pushing back against the recent shift toward making diff display the default behavior. He accepts the opt-out approach *only if* it includes immediate config support, allowing users to disable the feature globally. This introduces a new condition to the opt-out proposal: config-based control must be part of the initial implementation, not deferred to a future patch. His stance aligns with earlier concerns about long diffs and scripted editing but adds a concrete demand for user control via configuration. The thread now has two reviewers (Karthik Nayak and Phillip Wood) advocating for default diff display, while D. Ben Knoble and the original author (Jelle van der Waa) maintain the opt-in position, with Knoble’s latest reply tightening the requirements for any opt-out compromise.
