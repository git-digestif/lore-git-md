He also describes how GNU `yes` achieves high throughput via zero-copy techniques, but acknowledges that macOS’s `yes` and `tr` implementations are slower and lack these optimizations.

The email does not propose changes to the Git series itself; it is purely informational, offering context on why `printf` and `tr` behave differently across systems. This background reinforces Kristofer’s design choice: `test-tool genbytes` is more predictable and maintainable than shell-based workarounds, especially for generating arbitrary bytes (including NUL) and handling large outputs.
