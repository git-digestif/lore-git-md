Aniket Gupta has sent a two-patch series fixing incorrect combined-diff output for merges with many parents, a bug originally reported by Sami Jawhar. The series is based on `maint` and remains under review with no integration status claimed. With more than 32 parents, `git log --cc` shows lines in the wrong columns and hunk headers that disagree with their contents; with 64 parents, no hunks appear at all.

The first patch fixes the root cause in `coalesce_lines()` in `combine-diff.c`, where the lost-line mask was computed with `1 << parent` -- an `int` shift that is undefined for 32 or more parents -- and changes it to `1UL << parent` to match the `unsigned long` type of the `parent_map` field. The second patch, just posted, handles merges that exceed the mask capacity entirely: a new `MAX_PATCH_PARENTS` macro (bitsizeof of `unsigned long` minus two, so 62 parents on 64-bit platforms and 30 on 32-bit `long` platforms like Windows) causes `diff_tree_combined()` to print an error and suppress only the patch output for that merge, while raw-format output and the rest of the log run continue unaffected. Both patches add tests in `t/t4038-diff-combined.sh`, including a forty-parent merge test guarded by a `LONG_IS_64BIT` prerequisite and a 63-parent merge test verifying the new error message and that raw output survives.

The author reports the test suite passing and CI green on Windows, macOS, and Linux. No reviewer feedback has been recorded yet, so the series is awaiting review.
