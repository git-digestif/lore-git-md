This is a brief review-response from Harald Nordgren on patch v9 1/4 of his `--delete-integrated` series. Addressing Junio C Hamano's concern that the helper function name `branch_opted_out()` was too generic -- creating an impedance mismatch with the specific `branch.<name>.deleteMerged` config key it reads -- Harald confirms he adopted Junio's first suggested resolution and renamed the function to `opted_out_of_delete_merged()`. The change resolves the naming concern for now while keeping the door open for future destructive-operation opt-outs to use their own explicitly-named helpers.
