This is a follow-up ping on a standalone bugfix patch currently under review. Aniket Gupta is noting that his fix for `git check-ignore -v` incorrectly matching directory paths with trailing slashes against blank lines in `.gitignore` files has still not received maintainer attention.

The underlying patch modifies `add_patterns_from_buffer()` in `dir.c` to skip calling `add_pattern()` when a line is empty after `trim_trailing_spaces()` removes carriage returns and trailing whitespace, ensuring blank lines never match any path regardless of line-ending format. It adds a test case in `t/t0008-ignores.sh` covering CRLF and mixed-whitespace blank lines. The bug was reported by Mike Beaton and confirmed present in Git 2.51.1 and 2.56.0, ruling out a recent regression.

No maintainer or ignore-subsystem expert has yet weighed in. The change is narrowly scoped to runtime parsing logic and does not affect on-disk formats, comment lines, or negation patterns. Future discussion will likely focus on whether the single guard clause is sufficient and whether additional whitespace edge cases need coverage.
