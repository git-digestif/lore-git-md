This is a follow-up discussion email seeking design approval for a feature series that adds reverse-order traversal to `git submodule foreach`. He also notes that upstream has advanced since v2 (a new t7427 test file appeared), so he plans to renumber his tests to t7428 and rebase the build files for v3, which he will send once the design is agreed upon or in about a week absent objections.
