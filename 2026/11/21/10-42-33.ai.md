This is the first patch of a two-patch bugfix series targeting the combined diff machinery in `combine-diff.c`. It fixes a long-standing bug reported by Sami Jawhar where `git log --cc` produces incorrect output for merges with more than 32 parents, causing lines to appear in the wrong columns and hunk headers to disagree with their contents.

The root cause lies in `coalesce_lines()`, which records which parents lost a line by setting a bit in the `parent_map` field of an `lline`. The bit was computed with `1 << parent`, an `int` shift that is undefined for shift counts of 32 or more; in practice the 33rd parent and beyond collided with the bit of another parent. The fix changes the expression to `1UL << parent`, matching the `unsigned long` type of `parent_map` and consistent with the other masks in the file.

The patch adds a test in `t/t4038-diff-combined.sh` that constructs a merge with forty parents where two of them (the second and the fortieth) lose the same line, then verifies that the combined diff output marks exactly those two columns. The test carries the `LONG_IS_64BIT` prerequisite and is skipped on platforms where `long` is not 64 bits, since the masks cannot represent that many parents there.

As noted in the cover letter, the series is based on `maint` because the bug is old. The companion second patch handles merges that exceed the mask capacity entirely (more than 62 parents on 64-bit platforms, more than 30 on Windows) by printing an error and skipping that merge without aborting the entire `git log --cc` run.
