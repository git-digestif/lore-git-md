This is a design discussion follow-up on the `git submodule foreach` traversal feature series. William Hatfield is asking the list whether the overall design is acceptable before resending the series as v3, with the flag naming being the sole remaining open question.

The specific point under discussion is the name `--reverse-traversal`. Hatfield explains that unlike a tree-walk order (which would imply pre/in/post variants), this option literally reverses the existing traversal order, siblings included, and behaves the same whether or not `--recursive` is given. The options no longer require `--recursive`, a change from v1. He is seeking confirmation that this semantic justifies the "reverse" terminology rather than algorithmic alternatives like "postorder" or directional terms like "bottom-up" that Junio C Hamano had previously suggested.

Practically, Hatfield notes that upstream has advanced since v2 was posted -- a new `t7427` test file now exists -- so he plans to renumber his test to `t7428` and rebase the trivial `t/meson.build` change when preparing v3.

He states he will send v3 once the design is agreed upon, or automatically in about a week if no objection is raised. The email effectively puts the ball in the maintainers' court regarding the `--reverse-traversal` and `--reversive` naming before further iterations are produced.
