This is a substantive review reply to Andy Koppe's v2 bugfix for the `%w()` pretty-format indentation regression in `utf8.c`. Tanga Joris confirms the fix resolves their case (preserving both spaces after "- foo" followed by an indented line), then identifies an edge case where removing the `space++` increment would cause `%w(20)aa%n%nbb` to emit " bb" instead of "bb" -- an unwanted leading space after a paragraph break. They propose a regression test comparing `printf "aa\n\nbb\n"` against `git log -1 --format="%w(20)aa%n%nbb"` to catch this. They also suggest tightening the new wording in `Documentation/pretty-formats.adoc`: the rule that newlines followed by letters or digits become spaces should specify this happens "when wrapping," since with width 0 (e.g., `%w(0,2,2)`) newlines are preserved verbatim. Aside from those two refinements, the reviewer considers the patch good.
