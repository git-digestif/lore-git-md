This is a **substantive review** of a standalone bugfix patch for `git archive --remote` argument validation ordering. René Scharfe engages with the proposed behavior change and suggests an alternative implementation strategy.

The patch under review (by Dominik Maier) modifies `archive.c` to reject `--add-file` and `--add-virtual-file` when combined with `--remote` immediately inside `add_file_cb()` rather than after `parse_options()` returns. It introduces a new `struct add_file_data` bundling `archiver_args`, the `base` pointer, and the `is_remote` flag, passed as the option callback value. The late check `if (is_remote && args->extra_files.nr)` in `parse_archive_args()` is removed. A test in `t/t5000-tar-tree.sh` verifies that `--remote=. --add-file=no-such-file` dies with the incompatibility message rather than "File not found", and that `--add-file` followed by `--no-add-file` still errors.

René raises two points. First, the current approach rejects `--remote --add-file=foo --no-add-file`, which arguably should be allowed since `--no-add-file` clears the extra-files list and cancels the earlier option. Second, he suggests moving the `stat(2)` call out of the callback to after option parsing completes (but before archive content writing begins), refusing to stat if `is_remote` is set. This would preserve the ability to use `--no-add-file` to override `--add-file` even with `--remote`, while still preventing filesystem access on the server. He notes that `--add-file` entries can be identified by `content == NULL` in the `extra_file_info` struct, distinguishing them from `--add-virtual-file`.

The thread remains open with no resolution yet; the author has not responded to René's alternative proposal.
