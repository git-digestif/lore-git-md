This is a status ping on a standalone bugfix patch that is still awaiting review. Aniket Gupta is nudging the thread to note that his fix for `git check-ignore -v` incorrectly matching directory paths against blank `.gitignore` lines has not yet received maintainer attention.

The underlying patch addresses a parsing bug in `add_patterns_from_buffer()` within `dir.c`, where blank lines using CRLF endings or containing only whitespace were being turned into ignore patterns that could match paths ending in a trailing slash. The fix adds a guard to skip empty lines after `trim_trailing_spaces()` has stripped carriage returns and trailing whitespace, restoring the documented behavior that blank lines never match anything. A new test in `t/t0008-ignores.sh` covers CRLF and mixed-whitespace blank lines, and the bug was confirmed present in recent releases (2.51.1 and 2.56.0) by the original reporter, Mike Beaton.

At this point the patch remains under review with no substantive feedback from Junio or other ignore-subsystem experts. The change itself is minimal and surgical -- a single condition preventing empty strings from becoming patterns -- and appears to align with documented semantics, but it still needs maintainer sign-off before it can move forward.
