This is a cover letter for a two-patch bugfix series targeting the combined diff machinery. Aniket Gupta addresses a long-standing bug reported by Sami Jawhar where `git log --cc` (and related combined-diff output) produces incorrect results for merges with many parents: with more than 32 parents, lines appear in the wrong columns, and with 64 parents no hunks are shown at all.

The first patch fixes the root cause in `combine-diff.c`: the shift operation that sets the bit for a parent in the mask of a lost line used an `int` shift, so the 33rd parent and beyond collided with the bit of another parent. The second patch handles merges that exceed the capacity of the masks entirely (more than 62 parents on platforms where `long` is 64 bits). Instead of emitting wrong output, it now prints an error and skips the patch for that merge, but deliberately does not abort the entire operation, so `git log --cc` continues past a huge octopus merge rather than stopping halfway. On Windows, where `long` is 32 bits, the effective limit drops to 30 parents and the relevant tests are skipped.

The series is based on `maint` because the bug is old. It touches `combine-diff.c` and adds test coverage in `t/t4038-diff-combined.sh`. The author reports that the test suite passes and CI is green on Windows, macOS, and Linux. For future reference, the key technical details are the parent-bit mask shifting logic in `combine-diff.c` and the new error-and-skip behavior for merges exceeding the mask width, which varies by platform (`long` size).
