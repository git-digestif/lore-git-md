This is a review discussion reply in the `git reflog expire` refactoring thread, where Laveesh Bansal responds to Patrick Steinhardt's technical concerns about the v2 deduplication patch.

Laveesh addresses three specific points from the review. First, he agrees with Patrick that the behavior where `--updateref` produces different pruning results depending on how many times a refname is passed should be explicitly documented in the commit message as a potential regression risk, and notes that v3 moves this into the commit message. Second, he explains why the patch must preserve the current processing order rather than sorting the refs: with `--updateref`, expiring a branch can move its tip, and because `HEAD` is checked against every ref tip during expiration, processing `HEAD` before or after that branch can yield different results. To maintain this order while still deduplicating efficiently, v3 uses a `strset` for O(1) lookups rather than sorting the list. Third, he confirms that v3 adds a test covering the `--updateref` edge case that Patrick suggested.

The response is substantive and technically grounded, directly engaging with the semantics of reflog expiration and the ordering constraints imposed by `--updateref`. It indicates that the v3 iteration of the series incorporates the feedback by documenting the behavior change and adding test coverage, while preserving the ordering guarantees that the maintainer and reviewer consider important.
