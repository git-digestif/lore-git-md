This is a review reply within Harald Nordgren's six-patch series on `git status`/`git push` handling of rebased push branches, specifically addressing the performance concern about patch 2/6's `stat_outside_upstream()` helper.

Aniket Gupta is responding to Harald Nordgren's worry that a two-phase optimization (counting diverged commits first, then computing patch-ids only for small counts) would produce confusing, inconsistent UX where the "rebased cleanly" message appears unpredictably. Gupta concedes the point and effectively withdraws his earlier performance objection: he notes that the expensive `--cherry-mark` patch-id computation only runs on commits outside the upstream, so a normal rebased branch with a handful of local commits stays fast. The 5-8x slowdown he previously measured only manifests when there are thousands of diverged commits on each side. He concludes the cost is "probably acceptable as is," leaving the series without the proposed optimization. The thread remains open with no decision on thresholds, but the immediate pressure to implement the two-phase approach has been relaxed.
