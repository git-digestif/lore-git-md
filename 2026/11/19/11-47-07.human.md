This is a refactoring patch -- specifically the sixth of thirteen in Patrick Steinhardt's v5 series to modernize `git rerere`'s subcommand handling. The patch extracts the "clear" subcommand from the monolithic `cmd_rerere()` dispatch into a dedicated `cmd_rerere_clear()` handler in `builtin/rerere.c`, mirroring the structure introduced for "forget" in the preceding patch. The new function sets up its own `parse_options()` call with an empty options array, rejects any extra arguments with a "too many arguments" error, and then delegates to `rerere_clear()`.

This is purely preparatory restructuring with no behavior change. It continues the incremental pattern of pulling each subcommand into its own function ahead of the eventual switch to the standard `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` mechanism planned for later in the series. The diff is small and contained, touching only `builtin/rerere.c`, and the commit message is brief but clear about its place in the larger effort.
