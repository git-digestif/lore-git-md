This is a documentation patch that adds explicit guidance about line-wrapping length to three contributor-facing documents. Okhuomon Ajayi notes that `Documentation/CodingGuidelines`, `Documentation/SubmittingPatches`, and `Documentation/ReviewingGuidelines.adoc` previously did not specify how long emailed lines should be, and adds a recommendation in each to keep text near 70 columns so that it still fits within an 80-column terminal after a few levels of quoting.

The patch is small and mechanical: eleven added lines across the three files, each inserting a short paragraph at a contextually appropriate location (near other advice about email composition in `SubmittingPatches`, alongside general contribution norms in `CodingGuidelines`, and within the review-specific guidance in `ReviewingGuidelines.adoc`). The rationale is purely practical -- ensuring readability in threaded email discussion where indentation from quoting consumes horizontal space.

The change addresses a real gap in the project's contributor documentation, codifying a long-standing informal convention. It is unlikely to be controversial, as it does not alter any code behavior or impose new tooling requirements, merely documents existing best practice.
