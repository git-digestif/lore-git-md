This is a follow-up reply in the review thread for a bugfix patch series that corrects bounds handling in Git's UTF-8 display-width functions (`utf8_strnwidth()` and `utf8_width()`). Dirk Gouders confirms the resolution of an open design question about the ANSI escape-sequence scanning API: he will remove the NUL-terminated wrapper `display_mode_esc_sequence_len()` entirely and switch its sole remaining caller in `pretty.c` to the length-bounded `display_mode_esc_sequence_len_counted()` variant. By doing so, he notes, the change effectively reverts to the approach Junio C Hamano originally proposed in v1 of the series rather than the wrapper-based design in v2.

The exchange closes the performance objection Andy Koppe had raised earlier -- that retaining the wrapper as a `strlen()`-based shim would make repeated scanning quadratic in time. With both participants now agreeing the wrapper should be dropped, that concern is settled, and Koppe has separately indicated he will update his own proposed `make_line_colors_self_contained()` function to call the counted variant directly. Koppe's postscript suggestion to also drop the `_counted` suffix and fold the `len` parameter directly into `display_mode_esc_sequence_len()` is noted but not debated here.

The series remains under review and is expected to see a v3 that incorporates this wrapper removal along with several minor outstanding items: a meson build ordering nit, a documentation wording fix in patch 2, and a couple of style nits from Junio on patch 3. No technical objections remain to the core bounds-handling fixes in patches 3 and 4.
