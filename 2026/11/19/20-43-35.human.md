The email reinforces the resolved design principle that refspecs with explicit `src:dst` destinations bypass both command-line `--refmap` and the new `remote.<name>.fetchRefmap` configuration, preserving a clean separation where configuration sets defaults and command-line options override them. This agreement removes the last substantive objection to the v14 design for Patch 1/5.
