This thread concerns a single documentation patch from Okhuomon Ajayi that proposes codifying line-length and paragraph conventions for emailed contributions across three contributor-facing documents: `Documentation/CodingGuidelines`, `Documentation/ReviewingGuidelines.adoc`, and `Documentation/SubmittingPatches`. The patch, currently at version 2, would instruct contributors to wrap emailed text near 70 columns so it remains readable on an 80-column terminal after quoting, and to separate paragraphs with blank lines, including treating blocks of quoted text as paragraphs. Version 2 directly addresses a scope concern Junio C Hamano raised on v1 by adding the inter-paragraph blank-line guidance.

Andy Koppe has now offered substantive review of v2, suggesting the phrase "near 70 columns" be replaced with "to a maximum of 72 columns" to remove the ambiguity of "near" while aligning with the existing convention for commit messages, which are likewise quoted in email replies. Koppe's feedback targets only the column-count figure and does not address the blank-line guidance added in v2. The thread remains open with no Reviewed-by or Acked-by trailers recorded yet, and whether 72 columns is the precise right number is now the active point of discussion.
