This thread is a standalone documentation patch from Okhuomon Ajayi that codifies two long-standing informal conventions for emailed contributions to the Git list: wrap prose to a maximum of 72 columns so it stays readable on an 80-column terminal after quoting, and separate paragraphs -- including blocks of quoted text -- with blank lines. The patch touches three contributor-facing documents: `Documentation/CodingGuidelines`, `Documentation/ReviewingGuidelines.adoc`, and `Documentation/SubmittingPatches`. It is now at version 4, which implements all three revisions Junio C Hamano requested after reviewing version 3: the guidance is recast in imperative mood with a readability rationale, the misleading term "patch message" is replaced with phrases like "E-mailed text intended for human-to-human communication" and examples such as log messages, cover letters, and review comments, and the quoted-text rule is kept with an explicit "leave a blank line before and after it, too." Earlier versions had already addressed Junio's scope concern about blank lines (added in v2) and Andy Koppe's suggestion to say "maximum of 72 columns" (incorporated in v3).

The core 72-column convention has drawn no objections from anyone, and Junio praised the v3 wording while asking for the fixes now present in v4. The series remains under review with no Reviewed-by or Acked-by yet, and no integration-branch placement has been announced; the open question is simply whether v4 satisfies Junio's remaining concerns and attracts the trailers needed for it to be picked up.
