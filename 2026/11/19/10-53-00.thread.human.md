Okhuomon Ajayi has proposed a small documentation patch codifying the informal convention that emailed text -- patch commit messages and review replies alike -- should be wrapped near 70 columns so it stays readable on an 80-column terminal after quoting. The patch adds eleven lines across three contributor-facing documents: Documentation/CodingGuidelines, Documentation/ReviewingGuidelines.adoc, and Documentation/SubmittingPatches. It is under review but has not been placed in any integration branch, and no Reviewed-by or Acked-by has been recorded.

Junio C Hamano reviewed substantively and did not object to the 70-column figure itself, but questioned the scope: why specify line length while ignoring the related convention of blank lines between paragraphs. In response, Ajayi explained that the blank-line rule is already codified in the MyFirstContribution documentation, which is why it was omitted here -- but he has committed to expanding the next revision (v2) to cover both formatting rules together in all three files. The thread remains open pending that resend, with no alternative column count proposed and no other loose ends outstanding.
