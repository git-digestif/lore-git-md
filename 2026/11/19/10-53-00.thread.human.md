A new documentation patch from Okhuomon Ajayi proposes codifying the long-standing informal convention that emailed contributions -- patch commit messages and review replies alike -- should be wrapped near 70 columns so they stay readable on an 80-column terminal after several levels of quoting. The patch is purely additive, adding eleven lines across three contributor-facing documents: `Documentation/CodingGuidelines`, `Documentation/ReviewingGuidelines.adoc`, and `Documentation/SubmittingPatches`, each placed in a contextually appropriate spot near existing formatting guidance. No code or behavior is touched.

Since this is the thread root, nothing has been decided yet; the likely points of discussion will be whether 70 columns is the right number and whether the wording is precise enough, though the proposal matches established practice and is unlikely to draw much opposition.
