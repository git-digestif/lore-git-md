Okhuomon Ajayi has posted version 3 of a standalone documentation patch proposing to codify line-wrapping and paragraph conventions for emailed contributions to the Git mailing list. The patch updates three contributor-facing documents -- `Documentation/CodingGuidelines`, `Documentation/ReviewingGuidelines.adoc`, and `Documentation/SubmittingPatches` -- with guidance that emailed text including commit messages and review replies should be wrapped to a maximum of 72 columns to remain readable on 80-column terminals after quoting, that paragraphs should be separated by blank lines, and that blocks of quoted text count as paragraphs requiring surrounding blank lines.

Version 3 incorporates Andy Koppe's suggested wording change from "near 70 columns" to "to a maximum of 72 columns, matching the commit message convention," removing ambiguity about whether the figure is a hard cap. The blank-line guidance, added in v2 to address a scope concern raised by Junio C Hamano during his review of v1, remains unchanged. The patch is purely additive, adding nineteen lines across the three files with no code changes, and is based on commit 7fb6055e678f800320d8ab8cd8f201d9493d1ba0.

The thread remains open and under review with no integration-branch placement announced yet. No Reviewed-by or Acked-by trailers have been recorded, though the patch has been responsive to both maintainer feedback and reviewer suggestions. Future discussion will likely focus on whether the specific "maximum of 72 columns" phrasing is accepted verbatim and whether the quoted-text paragraph rule draws further scrutiny.
