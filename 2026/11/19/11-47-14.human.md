This is a documentation patch, specifically the thirteenth and final patch of Patrick Steinhardt's v5 series modernizing `git rerere`'s subcommand handling. It removes a stale claim from `Documentation/git-rerere.adoc` stating that `git rerere diff` passes additional arguments to the system `diff` command. That behavior actually disappeared back in 2006 when `git rerere` was converted to a builtin (commit 658f3650273), apparently unintentionally, yet the documentation continued to advertise it for two decades. Since nobody has complained about the missing passthrough in all that time, Patrick updates the man page to match current behavior, a change suggested by Junio C Hamano. The patch is small and well-motivated, touching only one line of documentation, and serves as a clean conclusion to the series by ensuring the user-facing docs accurately reflect how the subcommands now work after the broader `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` conversion.
