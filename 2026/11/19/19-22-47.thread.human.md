This thread addresses a rendering bug in `git log --graph` where left-skewed octopus merges could trigger an out-of-bounds array access in `graph.c`, leaving a `-1` in the `graph->mapping[]` array. Dominik Maier posted a single-patch fix that adds an `idx < 0` guard in `graph_insert_into_new_columns()` to stop a fusion branch from misfiring on the merge's own column, inserts a defensive `BUG()` in `graph_draw_octopus_merge()`, and adds a regression test to `t/t4215-log-skewed-merges.sh`.

Caspar Duregger provided substantive review confirming the `idx < 0` guard is the correct approach, but demonstrated the bug reproduces with as few as three parents rather than four, supplying a simpler test case using `test_commit` and `commit-tree`. He questioned whether the defensive `BUG()` should crash rather than degrade gracefully, deferring to project policy on the matter, and noted the commit message is highly technical and may obscure the practical impact for readers unfamiliar with the graph internals.

The series remains at v1 and under review; no maintainer decision, Reviewed-by, or Acked-by has been recorded, and it has not been queued in any integration branch. Open items include whether to retain the `BUG()` assertion or handle the error more gracefully, whether to adopt the simpler three-parent test, and whether to revise the commit message for broader clarity.
