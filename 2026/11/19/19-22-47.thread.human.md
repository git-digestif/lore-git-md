Dominik Maier has posted version 2 of a single-patch bugfix correcting how `git log --graph` renders left-skewed octopus merges with three or more parents. The bug lived in `graph.c`: a fusion shortcut in `graph_insert_into_new_columns()` was firing on the wrong column, which with three parents silently dropped an edge from the graph and with four or more parents shifted subsequent parents left, leaving a `-1` in `graph->mapping[]` that caused an out-of-bounds read in `graph_draw_octopus_merge()`. The v2 patch adds an `idx < 0` guard so the fusion only applies to existing non-merge columns, drops the defensive `BUG()` that had been added in v1, includes ASCII-art examples of both failure modes in the commit message, and expands the regression tests in `t/t4215-log-skewed-merges.sh` to cover both the 3-parent and 4-parent cases.

Caspar Duregger, who had provided the simplified reproduction and confirmed the fix approach during the v1 discussion (and is credited with `Helped-by`), has now replied with a bare LGTM on v2, signaling that the patch is ready from his perspective. No `Reviewed-by` or `Acked-by` trailer has been added, and the series remains under review with no maintainer feedback or integration-branch placement recorded yet.
