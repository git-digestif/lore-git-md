This is a refactoring patch, specifically the fifth in Patrick Steinhardt's thirteen-part v5 series to modernize `git rerere`'s subcommand handling. The series aims to replace the command's home-grown subcommand dispatch with the standard `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` mechanism from the parse-options subsystem; this particular patch is a preparatory step that extracts the "forget" subcommand into its own dedicated `cmd_rerere_forget()` function and routes it through `parse_options()` with a proper usage string, rather than handling it inline within `cmd_rerere()`.

The change touches only `builtin/rerere.c` and introduces no behavioral differences -- it simply moves the existing pathspec parsing and `rerere_forget()` invocation into a standalone handler that accepts the standard `(argc, argv, prefix, repo)` signature. The commit message notes that the remaining subcommands ("clear", "status", "remaining", "diff", "gc") will follow in subsequent commits until the final switch to `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()`.
