This is a refactoring patch, specifically the third of six in Justin Tobler's v2 series that shifts ODB transaction temporary-directory creation from lazy to eager as part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. The patch inlines the static helper `setup_tmp_objdir()` into its sole caller `tmp_objdir_create()` in `tmp-objdir.c`, moving the `mkdir` of the `pack` subdirectory directly into the creation function. This is preparatory cleanup: the commit message explains that a subsequent patch will make `tmp_objdir_create()` preserve `errno` on failure, and inlining means `errno` only needs to be saved in one place rather than across a helper boundary.

The change touches only `tmp-objdir.c`, removing 13 lines and adding 6, with no behavior change. Mechanically it replaces the call to `setup_tmp_objdir(t->path.buf)` with the explicit `xstrfmt`, `mkdir`, and `free` sequence inline. Within the series context, this sits between the prefix rename (patch 1/6) and the batched-fsync flush extraction (patch 2/6) on one side, and the eventual eager-creation logic in patch 6/6 on the other. The series as a whole ensures all transactional object writes go through the temporary directory immediately rather than only when batched fsync is active.

No `Reviewed-by` or `Acked-by` trailers are present yet, and the thread's open design question from Karthik Nayak about lazy versus eager creation remains unresolved at the series level, though this particular patch is purely mechanical and unlikely to be contentious on its own.
