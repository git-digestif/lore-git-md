Dominik Maier has posted a two-patch v2 series fixing two related bugs in `git archive`: an out-of-bounds pointer advance in ZIP output when `--prefix` is longer than an extra or virtual file's path, and premature filesystem `stat()` calls for `--add-file` that produced confusing error messages or ran on the server with `--remote`. The series evolved from an earlier single-patch proposal after René Scharfe suggested deferring the validation until after option parsing, which v2 implements while also preserving `--no-add-file` cancellation and allowing `--remote --add-virtual-file`.

Both patches have now received substantive approval from the list's senior reviewers. Junio C Hamano examined the code in detail, confirming the fixes are correct and cleanly implemented, concluding "nicely done" on the ZIP fix and "Looking good" on the deferred-validation patch, with only minor non-blocking observations about commit message wording and stylistic points. René Scharfe, who had driven the v1 redesign, has now replied to Junio's feedback on the first patch agreeing with the assessment and raising no objections; he notes that moving the NULL-path handling deeper into `userdiff_find_by_path()` would be a micro-optimization for virtual-file-only workloads but questions whether skipping the one-time attribute sanity checks is worth it, and separately floats a longer-term architectural idea of a dedicated `struct archive_entry` to avoid mutating shared `archiver_args` fields per entry, which he explicitly marks as out of scope for this series.

The thread is converged with no outstanding change requests and no recorded Reviewed-by or Acked-by trailers yet. The series remains under review with no integration status stated; if picked up, it would touch `archive.c`, `archive-zip.c`, and the tar and zip test scripts, with relevance to server-side handling in `git-upload-archive`.
