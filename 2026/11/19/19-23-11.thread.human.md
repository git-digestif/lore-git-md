This thread concerns a standalone bugfix by Dominik Maier that fixes argument validation ordering in `git archive --remote`. The patch moves the incompatibility check for `--add-file` and `--add-virtual-file` so it triggers immediately during option parsing rather than after, preventing confusing "File not found" errors and ensuring `--no-add-file` cannot silently suppress the check.

René Scharfe has provided a substantive review raising a design question about the proposed behavior. He notes that the current approach would reject `--remote --add-file=foo --no-add-file`, even though `--no-add-file` arguably cancels the earlier option and should be permitted. As an alternative, he suggests moving the `stat()` call out of the option callback to after parsing completes (but before writing archive content), refusing the stat only when `--remote` is set. This would preserve the ability to override `--add-file` with `--no-add-file` while still preventing filesystem access on the server side.

The discussion remains open; the author has not yet responded to René's alternative proposal, and no merge or integration status has been stated.
