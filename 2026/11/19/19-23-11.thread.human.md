This thread began as a single-patch bugfix from Dominik Maier correcting argument validation ordering in `git archive --remote`, where `--add-file` and `--add-virtual-file` were being statted on the filesystem before the incompatibility check ran, producing confusing errors and allowing `--no-add-file` to suppress the rejection entirely. After René Scharfe reviewed the first version and proposed deferring the `stat()` call until after option parsing so that `--no-add-file` could still cancel an earlier `--add-file` (and so that `--add-virtual-file` could work with `--remote`, since it needs no filesystem access), Maier reworked the change into a two-patch v2 series.

The first patch fixes an out-of-bounds pointer advance in `write_zip_entry()` in `archive-zip.c` that occurs when `--prefix` is longer than the path of an extra or virtual file -- a bug discovered while testing `--remote --add-virtual-file --format=zip`. The second patch implements Scharfe's suggested approach in `archive.c`: the option callbacks now merely record entries, and after `parse_options()` completes, `parse_archive_args()` skips virtual-file entries, dies with the proper incompatibility error for real files under `--remote`, and only then performs the filesystem `stat()`. Tests in `t/t5000-tar-tree.sh` and `t/t5003-archive-zip.sh` cover the incompatibility error, the `--no-add-file` cancellation both locally and remotely, `--remote --add-virtual-file` succeeding, and the zip prefix fix.

The series remains under review with no merge or integration status stated. Scharfe's design feedback has been incorporated, and no Reviewed-by, Acked-by, or outstanding objections are recorded.
