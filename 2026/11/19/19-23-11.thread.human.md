This thread is Dominik Maier’s two-patch v2 bugfix series for `git archive`, superseding an earlier single-patch proposal after René Scharfe’s review. Patch 1/2 fixes out-of-bounds pointer arithmetic in `write_zip_entry()` in `archive-zip.c` when `--prefix` is longer than the path of an `--add-file` or `--add-virtual-file` entry, which could make `entry_is_binary()` inspect gitattributes along an invalid path. Patch 2/2 defers `--add-file` validation out of option parsing so `--remote --add-file` fails with the clear incompatibility message, `--no-add-file` still cancels an earlier `--add-file`, and `--remote --add-virtual-file` works without touching the server filesystem unnecessarily.

Junio C Hamano has now substantively reviewed both patches and found them clean: he endorsed the zip pointer fix and the deferred-stat design, validated the timing of `prefix_filename()` and the handling of virtual-file entries, and concluded there are no outstanding change requests for the patches themselves. René Scharfe agreed with the fix and left only optional refinements open: whether the NULL-path check belongs inside `userdiff_find_by_path()` rather than at the call site, and whether skipping `attr_check_initl()` for virtual-file-only workloads is a net positive. Both also noted a larger, out-of-scope cleanup direction: a dedicated archive entry structure could stop tar/zip handlers from relying on per-entry mutation of shared `archiver_args` fields.

The series remains at v2 and under review; no `Reviewed-by`/`Acked-by` trailers or integration-branch status are stated, and nothing here should be read as merged. The remaining discussion is about optional micro-optimizations and longer-term refactoring rather than blockers.
