This is a review reply in the ongoing bugfix series for UTF-8 display-width calculation. Andy Koppe explicitly concurs with Dirk Gouders's proposal to remove the NUL-terminated wrapper `display_mode_esc_sequence_len()` entirely rather than keeping it as a `strlen()`-based shim over the new `display_mode_esc_sequence_len_counted()`. Koppe accepts the performance rationale that the wrapper would turn repeated scanning from linear into quadratic time, and agrees that the sole remaining caller in `pretty.c` should switch to the counted variant directly.

The email resolves a design question left open in the v2 series: patch 1 currently introduces the counted variant while retaining the original function as a wrapper, but with this concurrence both participants now agree the wrapper should be dropped entirely. A v3 incorporating this change has not yet been posted, but the direction is settled with no remaining technical objections.

Koppe adds that he will also update his own separately proposed `make_line_colors_self_contained()` function -- referenced via a lore.kernel.org link to commit e9f88b533d -- to call the counted variant directly, avoiding the same performance pitfall in his own work.

In a postscript, Koppe suggests a further simplification: once the old wrapper is removed, the `_counted` suffix could be dropped from the new function name, effectively just adding a `len` parameter to `display_mode_esc_sequence_len()` itself.

This is a substantive review engagement focused on API design and performance characteristics rather than surface-level style. With the wrapper-removal question now resolved and patches 3 and 4 already free of technical objections, the series appears to be converging toward a v3 that addresses the accumulated minor formatting and documentation nits.
