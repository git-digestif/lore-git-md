This is a maintainer response proposing a fix direction for the commit-graph corruption bug. Patrick Steinhardt acknowledges the report and outlines a concrete solution: moving commit-graph generation into `odb_optimize()` so the code can detect whether objects were discarded during maintenance and only rewrite the commit-graph in that case. He notes this aligns with his in-flight series making commit-graphs pluggable as an implementation detail of the object database backend, which would make writing them part of `odb_optimize()` natural. The fix is blocked on that prerequisite series landing first to avoid conflicts; once it does, he plans to send a patch series addressing the reported issue. The response touches the commit-graph and ODB subsystems, references the ongoing ODB abstraction effort, and identifies the key decision point: checking for discarded objects to determine whether commit-graph regeneration is necessary.
