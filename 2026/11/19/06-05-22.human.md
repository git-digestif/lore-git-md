This is a review reply within a bugfix patch series, specifically addressing patch 2/4 of the v2 series that fixes truncation of objects >=4 GiB on 32-bit-`long` platforms. Elia Pinto is responding to Patrick Steinhardt's substantive review of the `write_compressed()` `size_t` conversion in `builtin/index-pack.c`.

Patrick raised two concerns about the patch. First, he noted that the sole caller adds the function's new `size_t` return value to `obj[1].idx.offset`, which is a signed `off_t`, and asked whether the code should guard against overflow. Elia agrees this is worth doing, pointing out that `index-pack` already performs a similar check in `use()` via `signed_add_overflows()`, and commits to adding the same protection in `append_obj_to_pack()` in the next version. Second, Patrick challenged the absence of test coverage, asking whether an `EXPENSIVE` test could exercise the 4 GiB+ scenario. Elia accepts this too, describing a test that would use a hand-written thin pack with a delta against a 4 GiB local base, guarded by its own test variable similar to `GIT_TEST_CLONE_2GB` in t5608.

The exchange confirms the plan recorded earlier in the thread: patches 2/4 and 3/4 will be resent standalone as v3 with these review comments addressed, while patches 1/4 and 4/4 are deferred pending dependent `size_t` conversion series for `odb_write_object()` and `parse_object_buffer()`. No merge status is indicated; the series remains under review.
