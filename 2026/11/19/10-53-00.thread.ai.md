Thread identity: Single documentation patch (not a series) from Okhuomon Ajayi proposing to codify the line-length and paragraph conventions for emailed contributions. Currently at version 4, accepted by the maintainer for queuing.

Core problem or goal: Add explicit guidance to three contributor-facing documents stating that E-mailed text intended for human-to-human communication -- including log messages, explanations in cover letters, and review comments and their responses -- should be wrapped to a maximum of 72 columns so it remains readable on an 80-column terminal after a few levels of quoting, and that paragraphs (including blocks of quoted text) should be separated by blank lines. This codifies existing informal conventions.
