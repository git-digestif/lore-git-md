A notable technical detail surfaced in this reply is Sivaraam's analysis of an edge-case race in the stale-lock recovery: if a lock holder is SIGKILLed while two or more scripts are waiting, both waiters may read the same dead PID before either acts; one could then delete the lock, recreate it, and write its own PID, while the other -- still holding the stale PID -- deletes that live lock and takes its place. Sivaraam concludes this narrow window is too rare to warrant extra complexity, accepting the worst case of two `EXPENSIVE` tests running concurrently instead of one. Patrick Steinhardt had previously approved the overall approach and explicitly rejected splitting the prerequisite into `EXPENSIVE_MEM` versus `EXPENSIVE_DISK` as unnecessary complexity, so the thread now stands at "approach approved, minor cleanups pending" with no integration branch mentioned yet.
