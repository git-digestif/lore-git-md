This is a refactoring patch -- specifically the ninth of thirteen in Patrick Steinhardt's v5 series that modernizes `git rerere`'s subcommand handling by moving away from custom string comparisons toward the standard `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` mechanism.

The patch extracts the `diff` subcommand logic out of the monolithic `cmd_rerere()` into a dedicated `cmd_rerere_diff()` function. The new handler runs `parse_options()` with an empty option set, rejects extra arguments via `usage(_("too many arguments"))`, sets up the rerere cache in read-only mode, and iterates over the merge_rr list to call `diff_two()` for each path. The main dispatcher now simply forwards to this function when `argv[0]` equals `"diff"`, continuing the same extraction pattern established by the preceding patches for `forget`, `clear`, `status`, and `remaining`.

This is purely preparatory restructuring with no intended behavior change; it paves the way for the final switch to `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` later in the series. The v5 series as a whole is under review and addresses follow-up items from the already-merged v4 iteration, including a memory-safety fix and a documentation correction, though this particular patch only touches the internal dispatch structure of `builtin/rerere.c`.

The patch looks mechanically clean and consistent with the rest of the series -- it follows the established pattern precisely and includes the same argument-validation tightening seen in the other extracted subcommands. A busy reader tracking the rerere modernization effort can treat this as routine progress toward the series' goal rather than a point of controversy.
