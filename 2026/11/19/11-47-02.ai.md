This is a refactoring patch, the first of thirteen in version 5 of Patrick Steinhardt's series to modernize `git rerere`'s subcommand handling. It renames the function `repo_rerere()` to simply `rerere()`, since it was the only function in `rerere.h` lacking the `rerere_` prefix and the `repo_` prefix suggested it was a repository-method rather than a rerere-specific operation. The patch mechanically updates all call sites across `apply.c`, `builtin/am.c`, `builtin/commit.c`, `builtin/merge.c`, `builtin/rerere.c`, `builtin/stash.c`, and `sequencer.c`, while moving and expanding the function's documentation into `rerere.h` to clarify that it records and reuses conflict resolutions during merge operations. No behavior changes; this is purely preparatory naming cleanup ahead of the larger `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` migration in the same series.
