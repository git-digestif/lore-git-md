This is a process-level review reply in the ongoing discussion of Elia Pinto's four-patch v2 series fixing large-object truncation on 32-bit-`long` platforms. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Elia's plan to defer patches 1/4 and 4/4 until two dependent `size_t` conversion series land while sending patches 2/4 and 3/4 standalone as v3.

Patrick suggests holding off the entire series until the dependencies land rather than splitting it. He argues that running multiple similar series in parallel does not actually speed things up; instead, the opposite tends to happen because reviewing capacity is limited and tedious for this kind of mechanical type-conversion work, so some series end up receiving no review at all. He notes this does not diminish the usefulness of the changes, only reflects the practical bottleneck of reviewer attention.

The reply adds a strategic consideration to the sequencing discussion: rather than fragmenting the work into a partial v3 now and deferred pieces later, consolidating everything until the `odb_write_object()` and `parse_object_buffer()`/`fsck_object()` conversions are merged might avoid leaving orphaned patches in flight. No technical objections to the code itself are raised here, and no merge status is indicated; the series remains under review.
