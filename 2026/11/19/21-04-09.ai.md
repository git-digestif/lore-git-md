This is a substantive review reply in the ongoing bugfix thread for the v2 series fixing bounds handling in `utf8_strnwidth()` and `utf8_width()`. Andy Koppe explicitly concurs with Dirk Gouders's proposal to remove the NUL-terminated wrapper `display_mode_esc_sequence_len()` entirely rather than retaining it as a `strlen()`-based shim over `display_mode_esc_sequence_len_counted()`, accepting the performance rationale that the wrapper turns repeated scanning from linear into quadratic time. Koppe adds that he will also update his own separately proposed `make_line_colors_self_contained()` function (referenced via a lore.kernel.org link to commit e9f88b533d) to call the counted variant directly, avoiding the same performance pitfall. No technical objections remain to the direction of patch 1; the resolution -- switching the sole remaining caller in `pretty.c` to the counted variant and dropping the wrapper -- is now agreed upon by both participants, though a v3 incorporating this change has not yet been posted.

Koppe appends a postscript suggesting a further simplification: if the wrapper is removed, the `_counted` suffix could be dropped and the `len` parameter added directly to `display_mode_esc_sequence_len()` itself, collapsing the two functions into one. This is a naming/API-design suggestion only, not a new technical objection. The email touches `utf8.c`, `utf8.h`, `pretty.c`, and the `clar` unit-test files `t/unit-tests/u-utf8-ansi.c` and `u-utf8-width.c` indirectly via the series context, but introduces no new files or symbols of its own. The key open item this email closes is the wrapper-removal question; outstanding items remain the meson ordering nit, the "null-terminated" wording in patch 2's comment, and Junio C Hamano's style nits on patch 3.
