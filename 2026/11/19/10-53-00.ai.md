This is a documentation patch that codifies an existing informal convention about line length in emailed contributions. Okhuomon Ajayi adds a short paragraph to three contributor-facing documents -- `Documentation/CodingGuidelines`, `Documentation/ReviewingGuidelines.adoc`, and `Documentation/SubmittingPatches` -- stating that emailed text, including patch commit messages and review replies, should be wrapped near 70 columns so it remains readable on an 80-column terminal after a few levels of quoting. The change is purely additive: eleven lines across three files, no code or behavior modified.

The patch touches the documentation that governs how contributors format their mailing-list submissions, so future discussion is likely to center on whether 70 columns is the right number or whether the wording is precise enough, though the proposal matches long-standing practice and is unlikely to be controversial. The commit message and diff are clear and consistent across all three files, each inserting the guidance in a contextually appropriate spot: near the general rules in CodingGuidelines, alongside advice about quoting context in ReviewingGuidelines, and next to the existing warning about MUA word-wrap corruption in SubmittingPatches.
