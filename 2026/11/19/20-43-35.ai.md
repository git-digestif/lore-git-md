Technically this touches `remote.c` and `builtin/fetch.c` precedence logic involving `remote->fetchRefmap`, command-line refspecs, and `--refmap`. The key behavioral distinction is between destination-less refspecs (shorthand `master` or `master:`), which remain subject to refmap/`fetchRefmap` remapping, and refspecs with explicit `src:dst` destinations, which now bypass the refmap entirely whether passed on the command line or in configuration. This resolves the dual-storage concern while maintaining the principle that explicit destinations take precedence over remapping rules.
