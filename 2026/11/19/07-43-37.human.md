This is a reply in the ongoing review of the v2 bugfix series fixing bounds handling in `utf8.c`'s display-width functions, specifically addressing patch 1 which introduces `display_mode_esc_sequence_len_counted()`.

Andy Koppe had raised a substantive performance objection to keeping `display_mode_esc_sequence_len()` as a wrapper that calls `strlen()`, noting this turns repeated scanning from linear into quadratic time. Dirk Gouders responds by proposing to resolve this: he notes there is only one caller of the wrapper in `pretty.c`, suggests switching that caller to use the `_counted` variant directly with an explicit length, and dropping the NUL-terminated wrapper entirely. He mentions Junio C Hamano's earlier version already contained such a change he could adopt. The exchange leaves the API design question open pending agreement on removing the wrapper, while the rest of the series (patches 3 and 4) has already received approval.
