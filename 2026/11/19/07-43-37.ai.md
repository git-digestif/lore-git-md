This is a reply in the review thread for a bugfix patch series, in which the author agrees to a proposed API change. Dirk Gouders responds to Andy Koppe's performance objection against keeping `display_mode_esc_sequence_len()` as a `strlen()`-based wrapper around the new `display_mode_esc_sequence_len_counted()`. Dirk confirms that only one caller in `pretty.c` uses the wrapper and proposes switching that call site to the counted variant directly, allowing the wrapper to be dropped entirely. He notes that Junio C Hamano's earlier v1 already contained this change and offers to reuse it, asking whether that approach would be acceptable. The email advances the discussion of patch 1 in the v2 series by conceding the quadratic-time concern and proposing a concrete resolution: remove the NUL-terminated wrapper and require the explicit length parameter at all call sites.
