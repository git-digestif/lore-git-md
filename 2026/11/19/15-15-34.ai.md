This is a reply providing historical context to Junio C Hamano's documentation patch. Kristoffer Haugsbakk traces the "clever tricks" clause back to the original CodingGuidelines commit 6d0618a8 from November 2007, noting that Junio himself objected to the `!!` prohibition in the very first review of that document, arguing the idiom was already established in the codebase. Haugsbakk also surfaces Andreas Ericsson's original clarification that the concern was specifically about arithmetic expressions like `(!!a + !!b + !!c)`, where the intent of summing multiple normalized values is less obvious than normalizing a single expression to a boolean. The reply adds useful archaeology to the thread -- showing the rule was contentious from inception and that the "arithmetic constructs" wording was meant to target a narrower case than plain `!!value` -- but offers no technical critique of the deletion itself. The patch remains under review with no objections raised.
