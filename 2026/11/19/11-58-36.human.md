He explains that while one possible fix would be to start calling that function, the series instead takes a different approach by skipping the reachability determination entirely and comparing entry timestamps only against `gc.reflogExpire`.

The email resolves the wording ambiguity in the commit message without introducing new technical content or changing the patch itself. It confirms that the chosen solution trades precise reachability analysis for speed, accepting that some unreachable entries may not be counted as prunable during auto-maintenance because performing a foreground reachability walk would defeat the purpose of fast heuristics. No new version of the patch is sent in this message; it simply answers the reviewer's question about the intent behind the existing code.
