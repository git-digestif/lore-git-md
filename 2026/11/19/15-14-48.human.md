This is a brief reply from Jasper Mayone confirming that patch 4/5 (`%(currentworktree)`) has been dropped from the v5 series. He explains that the patch only existed to support patch 5/5 (the `git branch` output change), which Junio C Hamano objected to, so with 5/5 gone, 4/5 is withdrawn as well. The series now stands at three patches (1/5–3/5).

Jasper acknowledges Phillip Wood's substantive review of the dropped patch, noting that Phillip's suggestion to make the atom boolean like `%(HEAD)` -- or to extend `%(HEAD)` itself to cover rebasing/bisecting branches -- is interesting and will be kept in mind should he revisit the idea later. He also notes Phillip's implementation concerns (the hashmap overhead and potential inconsistency with `%(worktreepath)`) without further elaboration, since the patch is no longer in play.
