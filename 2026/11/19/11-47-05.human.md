This is a refactoring patch, specifically the fourth of thirteen in Patrick Steinhardt's v5 series to modernize `git rerere`'s subcommand handling. The series as a whole replaces the command's custom subcommand parser with the standard `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` mechanism, and this particular patch tidies up how the `--[no-]rerere-autoupdate` flag is handled by the `status` and `diff` subcommands.

Currently, `git rerere status` and `git rerere diff` receive the autoupdate flags even though they silently ignore them. The patch removes `flags |` from the two `rerere_cache_setup()` calls in those subcommand handlers, passing only `RERERE_READONLY`. Since the flags had no effect on these code paths, there is no behavior change; the commit message frames this as preparatory cleanup that makes the upcoming refactorings easier to follow.

This sits within the broader v5 effort that addresses post-merge follow-ups from the already-integrated v4 work. The series renames functions, converts flag macros to an enum, removes `the_repository` usage, and fixes a NULL-termination bug in the fallback subcommand path. Patch 4/13 itself is a small, self-contained two-line change in `builtin/rerere.c` with no test modifications, and it looks like a safe, well-motivated step in the larger restructuring.
