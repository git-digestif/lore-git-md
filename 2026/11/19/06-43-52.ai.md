This is the v3 cover letter for a four-patch bugfix series that fixes silent 32-bit integer truncation in Git's large-file handling paths. The series remains under review with no merge status; it is based on master at 50fe5cc63f and reportedly merges cleanly with current master and `seen`.

The most significant change from v2 is in patch 1/4. Instead of widening `hashwrite()`'s count parameter from `uint32_t` to `size_t` (which would have required updating the Rust FFI bindings in `src/csum_file.rs`), the patch now leaves `hashwrite()` unchanged and instead modifies its three callers that can pass buffers larger than 4 GiB -- `write_no_reuse_object()` and `copy_pack_data()` in `builtin/pack-objects.c`, and `store_object()` in `builtin/fast-import.c` -- to loop and call `hashwrite()` in chunks of at most 4 GiB. This addresses the design concern raised by Junio C Hamano and brian m. carlson in v2.

Patch 2/4 (complete-rewrite diff path) is unchanged in code from v2 but has a shortened commit message; it widens `size_t` through `count_lines()`, `emit_rewrite_lines()`, `count_trailing_blank()`, and the `struct emit_callback` fields, with EXPENSIVE tests in `t/t4022-diff-rewrite.sh` gated on 64-bit `size_t` and `long`.

Patch 3/4 now also widens the `cnt` member of `struct spanhash` in `diffcore-delta.c` from `unsigned int` to `size_t`, addressing Junio's concern that the span count could wrap to zero after four billion hash collisions on repetitive content, making a populated bucket appear empty and causing missed renames.

Patch 4/4 (diffstat totals and graph scaling in `diff.c`) is unchanged in code but its commit message now explains why a `uintmax_t` accumulator cannot overflow in practice (each line is at least one byte read) and documents the `graph_width` capping behavior, addressing two open concerns from Junio. A cheap test in `t/t4052-stat-output.sh` covers the `scale_linear()` overflow case. Torsten Bögershausen's `Tested-by` trailer is present on this patch.

Files touched: `builtin/fast-import.c`, `builtin/pack-objects.c`, `diff.c`, `diff.h`, `diffcore-delta.c`, `t/t4022-diff-rewrite.sh`, `t/t4052-stat-output.sh`. Key symbols: `hashwrite()` (unchanged signature, callers now chunk), `count_lines()`, `emit_rewrite_lines()`, `count_trailing_blank()`, `struct spanhash.cnt`, `print_stat_summary()`, `scale_linear()`. The series still coordinates with a separate loose-object-size series whose patch 6/6 will be dropped to avoid conflict with `count_trailing_blank()`.
