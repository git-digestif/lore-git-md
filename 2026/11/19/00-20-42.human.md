This is a small message-wording patch, the fifth of six in Justin Tobler's v2 series on eagerly creating the ODB transaction temporary directory. It changes three `die()` messages in `odb/transaction.h` so that failures refer to an "object transaction" rather than an "ODB transaction", since "ODB" is jargon unlikely to be understood by average users. The change is purely cosmetic -- three lines swapped, no behavior change -- and was added in v2 in response to earlier review feedback asking for more user-friendly error text. Within the broader series (which moves temporary-directory creation from lazy to eager so all transactional writes land in the temp dir regardless of `core.fsyncMethod`), this patch is housekeeping: it makes the new error paths introduced by the series read naturally to end users. It is uncontroversial and unlikely to draw substantive review on its own, though Karthik Nayak's open design-level question about the eager-vs-lazy motivation for the series as a whole remains unresolved.
