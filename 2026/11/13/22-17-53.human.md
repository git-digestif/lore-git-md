This is a documentation discussion follow-up in the now-concluded thread about improving Git’s explanation of detached HEAD state. Julia Evans responds to Junio C Hamano’s earlier email about the challenges of writing documentation for two distinct audiences—beginners who accidentally enter detached HEAD and advanced users who intentionally use it for temporary work.

Julia clarifies that her original goal was to create a new guide, accessible via `git help detached-head`, specifically to help confused users. She acknowledges that framing the content as an improvement to `git-checkout` might have been a misstep, as the goals of a reference manual and a user guide are different. She proposes removing the changes to `git-checkout.adoc` and reframing the series as a dedicated guide for users confused by detached HEAD, which she describes as an easy change.

The email does not propose any new patches or revisions to the already-merged documentation. Instead, it reflects on the broader documentation philosophy discussed earlier in the thread and suggests a more focused approach for future work. The thread remains closed, with no further action required on the existing detached HEAD documentation.
