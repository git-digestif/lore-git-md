The series targets a regression introduced in commit 393bbb21 (June 2025) where out-of-bounds reflog entries (e.g., `HEAD@{999999}`) caused fatal errors instead of being reported as "missing" like other invalid references.

This patch enables `GET_OID_GENTLY` in `builtin/cat-file.c`, ensuring reflog lookups beyond available history fail gracefully rather than crashing the entire batch process. The commit message clearly documents remaining limitations (e.g., `@{upstream}` on detached HEAD still dies) while emphasizing this change aligns with Git's philosophy of non-fatal error handling. The test coverage is thorough, verifying both boundary cases (`HEAD@{2}` resolves to the oldest entry) and extreme values (`HEAD@{999999}` reports as missing), including edge cases with peel suffixes and emptied reflogs.
