This is a **design discussion reply** in the ongoing promisor-remote protocol v2 bugfix series. Christian Couder responds to Junio C Hamano’s architectural objection to patch 1/3, where Junio proposed generalizing the `server_options` string list to avoid adding a hardcoded `promisor_remote_reply` parameter to `write_command_and_capabilities()`.

Christian acknowledges the technical debt in the client-side capability-writing code—three separate functions (`send_capabilities()`, `get_remote_refs()`, and `write_command_and_capabilities()`) duplicate logic for sending common capabilities like `server-option`, `agent`, and `object-format`. He agrees that a refactor to unify this under a single capability table (similar to the server-side `capabilities[]` in `serve.c`) would be ideal, but argues it should be a **separate follow-up series** rather than blocking the current bugfix. To unblock the fix, he proposes sending a v2 of the series with a `NEEDSWORK` comment in `write_command_and_capabilities()` to document the planned refactor, while keeping the `promisor_remote_reply` parameter for now.

The core trade-off is clear: **expediency vs. API purity**. Christian’s position prioritizes getting the fix into `maint` (where it’s urgently needed for HTTP/JGit compatibility) over a larger refactor that would delay it. Junio’s counter-proposal remains on the table but is not yet implemented. The thread’s next step is likely a v2 of the series with the `NEEDSWORK` comment, unless Junio insists on the generalized `server_options` approach before merging. The fix itself (sending `promisor-remote=...` during `fetch`) is uncontested.
