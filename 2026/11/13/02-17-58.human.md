This is a small performance and correctness cleanup in the HTTP backend. The patch removes a manual `strbuf` pre-sizing step in `get_info_packs()` that was added in 2009 and is no longer justified. The pre-sizing assumed SHA-1 line lengths (53 bytes), undercounting SHA-256 repositories (77 bytes), and used unchecked arithmetic. Since `strbuf_addf()` already handles dynamic growth, the extra counting loop and `strbuf_grow()` call are redundant. The change is inspired by a similar cleanup in `add_rfc2047()` earlier this year.

The patch touches only `http-backend.c`, removing seven lines of code. It’s a straightforward refactoring with no behavior change, but it eliminates a potential source of bugs in SHA-256 repositories and slightly reduces overhead by avoiding a second iteration over the pack list. The commit message clearly explains the rationale and cites prior art, making it easy to assess. This looks like a well-motivated, low-risk improvement.
