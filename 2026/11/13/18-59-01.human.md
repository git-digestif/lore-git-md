This is Junio’s review of the final patch in Jasper Mayone’s five-part series adding `--merged`/`--no-merged` filtering to `git worktree list`. The patch itself is a small cleanup that suppresses the redundant `+` marker and worktree path when `git branch` is run inside a worktree that is rebasing or bisecting a branch.

Junio’s main concern is usability: he argues that the branch name appearing twice—once in the `(no branch, rebasing topic)` header and once in the list with a `+`—is not actually redundant because the header scrolls off-screen when the branch list is long. Removing the `+` marker from the list would make it harder to spot the branch being rebased among hundreds of others. He suggests that if the new `%(currentworktree)` atom was introduced only to support this change, the series might be better off stopping at patch 3/5.

The review also includes two minor commit-message nits: a vague phrase (“marks a branch”) and a request to avoid referring to earlier patches by title alone since their object names are not yet final. These are surface-level and easily fixed. The substantive question is whether the visual cleanup justifies the loss of discoverability in long branch lists.
