This is a follow-up in the now-concluded documentation thread about Git’s detached HEAD state. Junio C Hamano responds to Julia Evans’s procedural proposal by reaffirming his position on what constitutes essential information in user-facing documentation. He clarifies that his concern is not about omitting low-level mechanics (e.g., how `HEAD` stores a branch name or commit ID) but about ensuring the documentation explains the *practical consequences* of detached HEAD—specifically, that commits made in this state do not affect any branch and can be easily discarded. Junio argues that this behavior is the "most important consequence" of detached HEAD and has "real impact" on users, making it inappropriate to omit from an introductory document. His tone is firm but not adversarial, framing the omission as a "disservice" rather than a technical error. The email does not reopen the patch or propose new changes; it is a philosophical rebuttal to Julia’s suggestion that such details belong in a separate, more technical man page (`gitdatamodel(7)`). The thread remains closed, with no further revisions planned, but the underlying tension about documentation scope is left unresolved.
