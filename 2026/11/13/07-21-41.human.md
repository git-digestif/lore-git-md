This is a **bugfix for the reftable and files backends**, addressing a correctness regression in reflog handling during symref updates within transactions. The patch is part of an ongoing thread where 井上司 (Tsukasa Inoue) identified and fixed an issue where HEAD reflog entries recorded the wrong OID (or were omitted entirely) when HEAD and its target branch were updated in the same transaction. The problem was masked in the files backend due to its lack of atomic transaction support but surfaced clearly in the reftable backend.

The email is a **collaborative follow-up** to Karthik Nayak’s substantive review, where Tsukasa clarifies implementation choices and commits to addressing feedback in a v2. Key points: the linear scan in `find_ref_update()` is retained because `ref_transaction_prepare()` sorts `refnames` only at the start, and later updates (e.g., from `split_symref_update()`) are appended, breaking binary search. Tsukasa also explains why `update->rejection_err` is sufficient to catch failed updates, as rejections are recorded during preparation before reflogs are written. The discussion confirms that the patch’s core approach—introducing `ref_transaction_resolve_new_oid()` in `refs.c`—remains sound, but v2 will tighten edge-case handling and test coverage.

Test improvements in v2 will include a subshell for cleaner setup/cleanup, blank lines for readability, and a new scenario for `REF_TRANSACTION_ALLOW_FAILURE` to verify reflog behavior when a branch update fails after HEAD is pointed at it. The patch is well-motivated, touches critical subsystems (`refs.c`, `refs/files-backend.c`, `refs/reftable-backend.c`), and includes thorough test coverage. It looks ready for `next` after v2, assuming no further architectural objections arise. The broader architectural goal—moving all reflog logic into `refs.c`—is deferred but not blocked by this fix.
