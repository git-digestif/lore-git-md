This is a small performance and correctness cleanup in the HTTP backend. The patch removes an unnecessary pre-sizing step in `get_info_packs()` that was introduced in 2009 and no longer serves a purpose. The function previously iterated over all packs twice—once to count local packs and pre-size a `strbuf`, and again to format the output—despite `strbuf_addf()` already handling dynamic growth. The pre-sizing logic was also incorrect for SHA-256 repositories (underestimating line length) and used unchecked arithmetic.

The change is straightforward: it drops the counting loop and the `strbuf_grow()` call, leaving only the formatting loop. The patch touches only `http-backend.c` and is inspired by a similar cleanup in `add_rfc2047()` (a9ce8526dc). The commit message clearly explains the rationale and cites the relevant prior work. This looks like a well-motivated, low-risk improvement.
