This is Junio C Hamano’s review of the fifth and final patch in Jasper Mayone’s v5 series, which cleans up `git branch` output by suppressing redundant `+` markers and paths for branches being rebased or bisected in the current worktree.

Junio raises two concerns about the commit message. First, he suggests rewording the reference to an earlier patch in the series to avoid relying on an unfinalized commit OID. Second, he questions whether the commit message clearly describes the context (the `git branch --list` output) and whether the change introduces a regression that could be avoided by reordering the series. He also expresses skepticism about the change itself: in his workflow, he finds the `+` marker useful for identifying bisected branches even when scrolling through a long list, and he does not see the redundancy as a problem. He suggests that if the new `%(currentworktree)` atom (introduced in patch 4/5) exists only to support this cleanup, the series might be better off stopping at patch 3/5.

The review is substantive: it questions the user-facing behavior and the architectural necessity of the patch, not just its implementation. Junio’s tone is measured but clearly negative on the value of the change. The patch is not rejected outright, but the feedback suggests the series may need to drop this final step or provide a stronger rationale for its inclusion.
