This is a maintainer review of a bugfix patch that prevents a segmentation fault in `git for-each-ref` and `git refs list` when `--no-format` is used.

Junio's review is brief but substantive. He first confirms the bug is real ("That is bad") and considers two possible fixes: using `PARSE_OPT_NONEG` to reject `--no-format` outright, or making `--no-format` restore the default format (the approach the patch takes). He then questions whether `git branch` and `git tag` should also reject `--no-format` rather than treating it as a valid option, suggesting the current behavior in those commands might be inconsistent or unintended.

The review also includes a procedural note: Junio asks the author to review the Developer Certificate of Origin and real-name requirements in `Documentation/SubmittingPatches`, indicating the patch will need a resubmission with a corrected sign-off before it can be merged. This is a routine request and does not reflect on the technical quality of the patch.

The patch itself remains a clean, minimal fix for a long-standing crash, with good test coverage. The only open question is whether the project wants to standardize `--no-format` behavior across all ref-listing commands, which could lead to a small follow-up series. For now, the patch is under review and not yet ready for merging.
