This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s two-patch series (`v2`) improving the performance and memory usage of `git repo structure`. The series optimizes object-counting logic by delaying parsing until necessary, achieving an 80% memory reduction and 20% speedup in large repositories—benchmarks confirmed by Kristofer Karlsson, who provided substantive feedback on the initial version.

The updates in `v2` address the only outstanding technical feedback: a reworded comment in patch 1 to clarify the role of `repo_parse_commit()` as a failsafe. Kristofer’s latest reply confirms the change satisfies his concerns, and he restated his endorsement. The series is now technically complete, with no blocking issues. A minor, low-priority design choice remains open—whether to convert a silent fallback into a `BUG()` assertion—but this has no impact on functionality or performance and doesn’t block maintainer pickup.

Derrick Stolee’s recent reply offers a lightweight endorsement, noting the impressive memory reduction but not engaging with the code or design. The series is ready for Junio’s review and integration.
