This thread is about a small but useful documentation patch from YikunWei that improves the discoverability of three `fmt-merge-msg` configuration variables. Currently, `merge.branchdesc`, `merge.log`, and `merge.suppressDest` are only documented in the `git-fmt-merge-msg(1)` man page, making them easy to miss for users who check `git-config(1)` for configuration options. The patch adds a two-line include directive in `Documentation/config.adoc` to pull in the relevant section from `config/fmt-merge-msg.adoc`, placing it alphabetically between the `filter` and `format` sections.

The change is uncontroversial and aligns with the project’s practice of maintaining a complete, centralized configuration reference. There’s no prior discussion or feedback on the patch yet, but it’s a straightforward fix that addresses a clear gap in the documentation.
