This thread is about a small but well-justified cleanup in Git’s HTTP backend. Yusuke Hayashi proposed removing an unnecessary pre-sizing step in `get_info_packs()`—a function that generates the `objects/info/packs` response for dumb HTTP clients. The pre-sizing logic, introduced in 2009, was both incorrect (it miscalculated line lengths for SHA-256 repositories) and pointless (the overhead of dynamic `strbuf` growth is negligible in this one-shot CGI handler).

The patch has now received clear approval from both Patrick Steinhardt, who initially challenged the justification, and Junio Hamano, who explicitly agreed with the rationale. The second iteration (v2) addressed Patrick’s concerns by clarifying why the cleanup is safe and beneficial, and no further changes are expected. The patch is a pure cleanup—it touches only `http-backend.c`, removes a counting loop and `strbuf_grow()` call, and leaves the output unchanged. It’s covered by existing tests and is ready for merging into Junio’s `next` branch. The thread is now fully converged, with no open questions or objections.
