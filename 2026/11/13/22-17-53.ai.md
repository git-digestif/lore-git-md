This is a documentation follow-up in the now-concluded thread about improving Git’s explanation of detached HEAD state. This would resolve the tension Junio C Hamano highlighted about manual pages needing to cater to both beginners and advanced users, while still providing a dedicated resource for the "oops, I’m lost" audience.

The proposal is minimal and practical: it does not alter the substance of the already-merged v6 patch, nor does it reopen the broader documentation philosophy discussion. Instead, it narrows the scope of the original effort to focus on a single, well-defined goal—helping confused users—by decoupling the guide from the `git-checkout` manual page. This change is uncontroversial and aligns with Junio’s suggestion that a separate FAQ or "survival guide" might better address the needs of inexperienced users. The thread remains closed, with no further revisions or patches planned beyond this reframing.
