This is a follow-up in the now-concluded thread about rewriting Git’s documentation for detached HEAD state. Junio C Hamano responds to Julia Evans’s latest clarification request by reaffirming his position on what the documentation should cover. He does not dispute the accuracy or clarity of the merged text but argues that omitting the *consequence* of detached HEAD—specifically, that commits made in this state do not affect any branch—is a disservice to users. His concern is not about technical completeness but about practical impact: users need to understand that temporary work in detached HEAD can be easily lost (by checking out a branch) or intentionally abandoned. This, he says, is the "most important aspect" of detached HEAD and should not be left out of an introductory explanation, even if the document is not meant to be encyclopedic. The email does not propose new wording or reopen the patch; it is a philosophical defense of including user-relevant consequences in documentation, even at the cost of some brevity. The thread remains closed, with no further changes planned.
