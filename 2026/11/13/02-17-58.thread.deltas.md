## 2026/11/13

- [2026/11/13/02-17-58 by Yusuke Hayashi via B4 Relay <devnull+yusuke8h.gmail.com@kernel.org>] Yusuke Hayashi posted a patch to drop the strbuf pre-sizing from `get_info_packs()` in http-backend.c, removing the pack-counting loop and `strbuf_grow()` call because the pre-sizing under-counts 77-byte SHA-256 lines, uses unchecked arithmetic, and `strbuf_addf()` already grows the buffer automatically.
- [2026/11/13/07-03-52 by Patrick Steinhardt <ps@pks.im>] Patrick Steinhardt replied that while the behavior is buggy, the pre-sizing hint is intended as a performance improvement to allocate the buffer properly from the start, and asked why it is fine to skip it in this particular function rather than fixing the bug, noting that the "others do it" argument is weak.
