This is the fourth and final patch in Justin Tobler’s ODB transaction series, and Karthik Nayak’s review of it. The patch itself removes the now-unused lazy-creation logic (`odb_transaction_files_prepare()`) and makes the temporary object directory creation eager at transaction start, ensuring all transactional object writes go through the temporary directory.

Karthik’s review is substantive but narrowly focused. He praises the commit message’s clarity—calling it a better explanation than the cover letter—but then raises a pointed question about the performance claim: why are large numbers of loose objects not expected in ODB transactions? This is not a code-level nit; it challenges the patch’s implicit assumptions about typical workloads and could affect how reviewers weigh the 7 % slowdown reported in the micro-benchmark. The rest of his feedback is surface-level: he calls the changes themselves “good” without further elaboration, and he does not engage with the test updates or the removal of the lazy-creation plumbing. No Reviewed-by or Acked-by is given, and the question about loose-object expectations remains open.
