This is the fifth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s now-merged 12-patch series modernizing `git rerere`’s subcommand handling. The patch extracts the "forget" subcommand into a dedicated function, `cmd_rerere_forget()`, that uses Git’s standard `parse_options()` machinery. The change is purely mechanical: the existing warning about deprecated usage (`'git rerere forget' without paths`) and pathspec handling are preserved exactly, and the function’s option table is empty because "forget" currently accepts no options. This is a preparatory step; the remaining subcommands will follow in later patches before the series switches to `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` in the final commit. The patch is low-risk, well-scoped, and aligns with the series’ overall goal of internal consistency.
