This is the first patch in the now-merged 12-patch refactoring series that modernizes `git rerere`'s subcommand handling. The patch renames the function `repo_rerere()` to `rerere()` to align with the naming convention of other functions in `rerere.h` (all of which use the `rerere_` prefix). The change is purely mechanical: the function’s signature, behavior, and call sites are unchanged. The commit message clarifies that the `repo_` prefix was misleading because, while the function does take a repository parameter, that alone does not justify the prefix.

The patch updates nine files: `apply.c`, `builtin/am.c`, `builtin/commit.c`, `builtin/merge.c`, `builtin/rerere.c`, `builtin/stash.c`, `rerere.c`, `rerere.h`, and `sequencer.c`. In `rerere.h`, the documentation for `rerere()` is expanded to explicitly describe its role as the main entry point for recording and reusing conflict resolutions. The diff is minimal and focused, replacing every call to `repo_rerere()` with `rerere()` and updating the function’s header comment.

This is a low-risk, high-consistency refactoring that sets the stage for the rest of the series. The change is uncontroversial and aligns with Git’s naming conventions. The expanded documentation in `rerere.h` improves clarity without altering behavior. The patch is part of a larger effort to replace `git rerere`'s custom subcommand-parsing logic with Git’s standard `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` mechanism, but this specific change is limited to renaming and documentation. No new functionality or on-disk format changes are introduced.
