This is a **follow-up in the `git history --verbose` RFC thread**, specifically D. Ben Knoble’s reaction to Patrick Steinhardt’s clarification about the required output format.

Ben acknowledges Patrick’s point that the output format must be generalizable to future `git history` subcommands (e.g., `split`, `move`) and suggests that `git range-diff`’s structured, multi-commit format could be valuable. However, he also notes that the original proposal—showing a simple unified diff in the editor, mirroring `git commit --verbose`—remains useful as a quick reminder for all subcommands. He does not take a firm position on which approach should win, but his phrasing ("decisions, decisions…") signals that the thread now faces a tension between two design goals: a lightweight, familiar diff display for immediate use, and a more ambitious, forward-looking format that can handle complex rewrites. The unresolved question is whether these two goals can coexist, or if one must be prioritized over the other.
