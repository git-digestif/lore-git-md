This is the fifth patch in the now-merged 12-patch refactoring series that modernizes `git rerere`'s subcommand handling. The series replaces the command's custom subcommand-parsing logic with Git’s standard `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` mechanism, and this patch extracts the "forget" subcommand into a dedicated function with its own `parse_options()` machinery.

The change is purely mechanical: `cmd_rerere_forget()` is introduced with an empty option table (preserving the existing behavior), and the hand-rolled pathspec parsing and `rerere_forget()` call are moved verbatim into the new function. The only behavioral note is that the deprecation warning for `'git rerere forget' without paths` remains unchanged. The patch does not alter the command’s CLI, documentation, or on-disk behavior—it is a preparatory step for the later switch to `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` in patch 11.

The diff is clean and minimal, touching only `builtin/rerere.c`. The new function follows the established pattern of other subcommand extractions in the series (patches 6–10), and the commit message clearly states the goal: to align `git rerere` with other multi-subcommand built-ins like `git notes`. Since the series is already merged to `master`, this patch is part of the final, approved implementation. Reviewers familiar with the series’ earlier patches will recognize this as a routine step in the refactoring.
