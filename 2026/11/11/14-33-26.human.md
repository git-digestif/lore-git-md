This is a review comment on Justin Tobler’s four-patch series that moves ODB transaction temporary-directory creation from lazy to eager. The reviewer, Karthik Nayak, asks a single question: why is eager creation preferable to lazy? He notes that, on the surface, lazy creation seems like a reasonable design choice.

The question is substantive but brief. Karthik does not engage with the code or the correctness rationale spelled out in the cover letter (ensuring all transactional writes go to the temporary directory, not just those with batched fsync). Instead, he signals limited familiarity with the ODB transaction machinery and invites the author to restate the motivation. This positions the exchange as an early-stage clarification rather than a deep technical critique.
