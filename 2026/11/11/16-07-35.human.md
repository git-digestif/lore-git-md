This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing RFC thread about adding `--verbose` support to `git history reword`. Patrick Steinhardt, responding to D. Ben Knoble, clarifies his earlier point with a touch of self-deprecating humor—he admits he was posting while unwell and may not have been at his sharpest.

The substance remains unchanged: he reiterates that the **output format** (not just the option name) must be generalizable across all `git history` subcommands (`split`, `move`, etc.) and suggests `git range-diff`’s structured, multi-commit format as a model. This reinforces the thread’s central tension—whether to prioritize a lightweight unified diff (mirroring `git commit --verbose`) for immediate use or a more ambitious, forward-looking format that can handle complex rewrites. The author, Jelle van der Waa, has not yet responded to this constraint, leaving the design direction unresolved. For now, this email is a minor footnote; the real action awaits the author’s next move.
