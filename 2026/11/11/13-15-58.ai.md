This is a reply in the ongoing ODB transaction series from Justin Tobler. Patrick Steinhardt responds to the first patch, which renames the default temporary directory prefix from `"bulk-fsync"` to `"transaction"`. His feedback is brief and entirely supportive: he agrees the old name would become misleading once the directory is used for all transactions, not just batched fsyncs, and endorses the generic `"transaction"` prefix as the right choice. He briefly considers but dismisses the idea of making the prefix caller-dependent, calling it unlikely to be useful. The review is surface-level—it engages only with naming and motivation, not with code behavior or edge cases—but it is positive and aligns with the series' direction. No changes are requested; this patch is effectively approved.
