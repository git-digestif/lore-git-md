This is a **surface-level review** of the final patch in Harald Nordgren’s v13 series introducing `remote.<name>.fetchRefmap`. The reviewer, D. Ben Knoble, focuses on **patch organization and test clarity** rather than the technical substance of the fix itself.

The patch ensures `@{push}` resolution works for remotes configured with `--limited-fetch` (which sets `remote.<name>.fetchRefmap` but not `remote.<name>.fetch`). Knoble suggests this fix might belong earlier in the series—perhaps with the first three patches that introduce `fetchRefmap`—since the issue arises from the interaction between `fetchRefmap` and the lack of a traditional `fetch` refspec, not specifically from `--limited-fetch`. His feedback is **non-blocking** and reflects a preference for logical grouping rather than a technical objection. He also asks whether the fallback behavior (checking `remote->refmap` if `remote->fetch` doesn’t resolve the refname) matches the fetch-side logic, but this is a **clarification question**, not a critique of the implementation.

The patch itself is **mechanical and uncontroversial**: it adds a two-line fallback in `remote.c` and a new test in `t/t1514-rev-parse-push.sh` to verify `@{push}` resolution after pushing to a `--limited-fetch` remote. The fix is **ready for merging** and has no unresolved technical concerns. The series as a whole remains blocked by the **override semantics discussion** and the **cover letter documentation critique**, neither of which affect this patch. Knoble’s suggestion to reorganize the series is **low-priority** and unlikely to be acted on at this stage, given the series is in its thirteenth iteration and under final review.
