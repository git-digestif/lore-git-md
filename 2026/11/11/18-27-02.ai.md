This is a **surface-level review** of the sixth patch in Harald Nordgren’s v13 series introducing `remote.<name>.fetchRefmap`. The patch fixes a bug where `@{push}` resolution fails for remotes configured with `--limited-fetch` (which sets `remote.<name>.fetchRefmap` but not `remote.<name>.fetch`). D. Ben Knoble’s feedback is **non-blocking** and focuses on **patch organization** and **clarity of intent**, not the technical correctness of the fix.

Knoble suggests that the `@{push}` resolution fix might logically belong with the earlier patches that introduce `fetchRefmap` (Patches 1–3), rather than being grouped with the `--limited-fetch` feature (Patches 4–6). His reasoning is that the bug exists as soon as `fetchRefmap` is introduced, even without `--limited-fetch`, and framing the fix as a **bugfix for an earlier patch** would make its motivation clearer. He also notes that this reorganization could help reviewers focus on the `fetchRefmap` plumbing first, deferring the `--limited-fetch` feature to later in the series.

The feedback is **purely structural** and does not question the patch’s correctness. Knoble confirms that the fallback logic (using `remote->refmap` if `remote->fetch` does not resolve the refname) aligns with the precedence rules established in earlier patches, and he does not identify any edge cases or inconsistencies. His suggestion to reorder the patch is **a preference for review clarity**, not a technical objection, and would require only minor adjustments to the test (replacing `--limited-fetch` with a direct `fetchRefmap` configuration).

The patch itself remains **ready for merging**. It is a **mechanical, uncontroversial fix** that addresses a gap in the v12 implementation, and the test coverage (a new test in `t/t1514-rev-parse-push.sh`) is sufficient. The only remaining blocker for the series is the **override semantics discussion** for command-line refspecs, which is unrelated to this patch. Knoble’s feedback does not introduce new technical concerns or delay the series’ progress.
