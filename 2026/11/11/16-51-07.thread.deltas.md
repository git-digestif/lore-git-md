# Thread deltas

## 2026/11/11

- [2026/11/11/16-51-07 by kongfankaikai <kongfankaikai@foxmail.com>] kongfankaikai reported that `git repack --drop-filtered` aborts with a fatal error when a blob selected for dropping is referenced by the current index, which prevents other eligible blobs from being dropped, and proposed that Git instead skip index-referenced blobs and continue dropping the remaining eligible objects, optionally emitting a warning, asking whether there is a specific reason the condition is treated as fatal.

## 2026/11/12

- [2026/11/12/07-29-21 by Siddharth Shrimali <r.siddharth.shrimali@gmail.com>] Siddharth Shrimali identified the reported abort as a known limitation with a fix already planned, citing Samuel Bronson's September RFC patch for the same issue on an llvm-project clone and his own earlier agreement with that direction.
- [2026/11/12/07-29-21 by Siddharth Shrimali <r.siddharth.shrimali@gmail.com>] Siddharth Shrimali explained that the fatal abort has no correctness justification: the guard merely avoids dropping a blob that the next command would lazy-fetch back, making the abort a conservative but poor trade-off.
- [2026/11/12/07-29-21 by Siddharth Shrimali <r.siddharth.shrimali@gmail.com>] Siddharth Shrimali stated the intended fix is to replace the die() with a warning and drop the remaining eligible blobs, and that he is working on the patch and will post it to the list.
