This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing RFC thread about adding `--verbose` support to `git history reword`. D. Ben Knoble reacts to Patrick Steinhardt’s clarification that the **output format** for diff display must be generalizable across all `git history` subcommands (`split`, `squash`, `fixup`, etc.) and suggests `git range-diff`’s format as a model.

Knoble acknowledges the appeal of a `range-diff`-style summary but notes that the original proposal was closer to `git commit --verbose`—a simple diff in the editor. He sees value in both approaches: the `range-diff` format for complex operations (like `split` or `move`) and the simpler diff as a reminder for simpler edits (like `reword`). The comment leaves the design question open, implying that the thread still needs to reconcile these two visions before moving forward. No new technical objections or proposals are raised; this is primarily a reflection on the trade-offs.
