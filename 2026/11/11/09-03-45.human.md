This is a **feature patch series update** for the `git repo info` command, specifically the `[PATCH v8 0/7]` series adding path-related keys. The author, K Jayatheerth, has **conceded to Phillip Wood’s design objections** and will **drop three patches** from the series: `path.hooks` (patch 4/7) and `path.cdup`/`path.prefix` (patches 6/7 and 7/7). This reduces the series from seven patches to four, focusing on the remaining uncontroversial keys: `path.working_tree`, `path.superproject_working_tree`, and `path.index`.

The decision to drop `path.hooks` resolves the most contentious unresolved objection in the thread. Phillip argued that the key was outdated and encouraged scripts to use `git hook run` or config-based hooks instead of querying the hooks directory directly. Jayatheerth initially defended the key as a migration path for scripts using `git rev-parse --git-path hooks`, but ultimately agreed to remove it to avoid encouraging an outdated pattern. The removal of `path.cdup` and `path.prefix` follows from Phillip’s observation that `path.cdup` offered no practical benefit over `path.working_tree.relative` and that its behavior (e.g., dumping users in their home directory when run from the repository root) was surprising and unintuitive.

The remaining four patches are **mechanically complete, design-resolved, and uncontroversial**:
1. `path.working_tree.absolute`/`path.working_tree.relative` (patch 1/7)
2. The `get_superproject_working_tree()` refactoring (patch 2/7, a critical correctness fix)
3. `path.superproject_working_tree.absolute`/`path.superproject_working_tree.relative` (patch 3/7)
4. `path.index.absolute`/`path.index.relative` (patch 5/7)

The series is now **simplified and focused**, removing the risk of abandonment due to unresolved design objections. However, the **lack of reviewer engagement** remains a concern, and the author may need to rebase the series to reflect the dropped patches and address Junio’s naming convention enforcement (underscores instead of hyphens).
