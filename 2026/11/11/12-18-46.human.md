This is the tenth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s now-merged twelve-patch series modernizing `git rerere`’s subcommand handling. The patch turns the "gc" subcommand into a proper subcommand using Git’s `parse_options()` machinery, a mechanical but necessary step toward the series’ goal of replacing the hand-rolled subcommand ladder with `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()`.

The change is straightforward: the "gc" logic is extracted into a new `cmd_rerere_gc()` function that defines its own option table (including the hidden `--skip-locked` flag) and validates arguments before calling `rerere_gc()`. The top-level parser no longer needs the global `--skip-locked` flag, so it is removed. The test that verified `--skip-locked` was only accepted by "gc" is also removed, as the new parser enforces this automatically.

This is a clean, low-risk refactoring that preserves existing behavior while preparing for the final switch to `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` in patch 11. The patch is well-motivated by the series’ overall goal and fits neatly into the progression of earlier patches that converted the other subcommands. Since the series is already merged to `master`, this patch is effectively final and requires no further attention from reviewers.
