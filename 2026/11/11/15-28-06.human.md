This is the first patch in Jasper Mayone’s v5 series, a **worktree safety fix** that prevents `git worktree remove` from deleting a worktree in the middle of a rebase, `git am`, bisect, cherry-pick, or revert unless `--force` is given. The patch closes a real data-loss risk: these operations store their state in the worktree’s `$GIT_DIR`, so removing the worktree discards that state even when the files appear clean. The implementation reuses existing helpers (`wt_status_check_rebase`, `wt_status_check_bisect`) and adds direct checks for `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD`, `REVERT_HEAD`, and the `sequencer` directory. The documentation is updated to list the conditions requiring `--force` in a bulleted format, and the tests cover every operation with both conflicted and resolved-but-in-progress states.

The patch is well-motivated, thoroughly tested, and aligns with Git’s general principle of protecting uncommitted or in-progress work. The only open question is whether any scripts rely on the current ability to delete in-progress worktrees without `--force`; the commit message explicitly asks about this, signaling awareness of the backward-compatibility risk. The change looks ready for integration.
