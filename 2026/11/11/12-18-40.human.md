This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s now-merged 12-patch series modernizing `git rerere`’s subcommand handling. The patch is a small, targeted refactoring that stops passing the `--[no-]rerere-autoupdate` flag to the `status` and `diff` subcommands, which ignore it anyway.

The change is purely internal: the flag is hard-coded to `RERERE_READONLY` in both call sites, removing the redundant `flags |` bitwise OR. This simplifies the code and makes the next steps in the series (switching to `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()`) easier to follow. There are no behavior changes, no new CLI options, and no on-disk format changes. The patch touches only two lines in `builtin/rerere.c` and is a straightforward, low-risk cleanup.
