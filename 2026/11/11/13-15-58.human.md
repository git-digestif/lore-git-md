This is the first review reply in Justin Tobler’s four-patch series to make ODB transaction temporary directory creation eager. Patrick Steinhardt responds to the initial patch, which renames the default temporary directory prefix from `"bulk-fsync"` to `"transaction"`.

Patrick agrees with the rename, calling the old name misleading given the directory’s expanding role beyond just batching fsyncs. He briefly considers but dismisses the idea of making the prefix caller-dependent, endorsing the simpler, more generic `"transaction"` name instead. The review is surface-level—focused on naming clarity rather than deeper design or behavior—but it signals alignment with the series’ direction. No changes are requested; this is a straightforward acknowledgment that the patch is on the right track.
