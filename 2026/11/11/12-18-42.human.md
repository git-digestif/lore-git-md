This is the sixth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s now-merged 12-patch series modernizing `git rerere`’s subcommand handling. The patch turns the "clear" subcommand into a proper subcommand using Git’s standard `parse_options()` machinery, a purely mechanical refactoring that prepares for the later switch to `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()`.

The change is minimal and safe: it extracts the existing `rerere_clear()` call into a new `cmd_rerere_clear()` function, adds an empty option table (since "clear" takes no arguments), and updates the subcommand ladder to call the new function. The behavior is unchanged, and the patch includes the same `string_list_clear()` cleanup as the original code. This is a textbook example of a well-executed preparatory refactoring—small, focused, and clearly motivated by the series’ overall goal.

The patch touches only `builtin/rerere.c` and is part of a larger effort to eliminate hand-rolled subcommand parsing in favor of Git’s standard infrastructure. Since the series is already merged to `master`, this patch is effectively final and requires no further attention from reviewers. It’s worth noting only as a reference point for anyone studying the series’ progression or the rerere subsystem’s internals.
