This is a **test infrastructure patch** that has reached its final form. Patrick Steinhardt reviews the latest iteration of Kristofer Karlsson’s `test-tool genbytes` series and gives it a clear thumbs-up.

The patch introduces `test-tool genbytes <char> [<count>]` to replace slow `tr(1)` invocations in the test suite, particularly on macOS where generating large byte sequences (e.g., 2 GB of NULs) was a measurable performance bottleneck. The interface is now settled: a single literal character for the byte value, with NULs still handled by the existing `test-tool genzeros` for backward compatibility. The implementation is straightforward—shared buffer, idiomatic parsing, and strict input validation—and all 16 test-script callers have been updated to use the new command. Patrick’s review focuses on the clarity of the interface and the readability of the updated test scripts, noting that the literal-character design is both intuitive and future-proof. No further changes are requested; the series is ready for integration.
