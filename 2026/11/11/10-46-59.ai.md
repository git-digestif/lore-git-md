This introduces a new constraint on the design: whatever diff-display mechanism is chosen must not be `reword`-specific. The thread now has three reviewers (Karthik Nayak, Phillip Wood, Patrick Steinhardt) favoring default behavior with opt-out, one reviewer (D. Ben Knoble) conditionally accepting it, and the author (Jelle van der Waa) still advocating for opt-in via `--verbose`. The unresolved questions now include (1) whether config support should be added now or later, (2) how to generalize the diff format across subcommands, and (3) whether the experimental status of `git history` justifies deferring opt-out mechanisms.
