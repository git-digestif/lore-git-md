**Summarizer brief mode**

This is a **follow-up in the `git history --verbose` RFC thread**, specifically Patrick Steinhardt’s brief reply to Ben Knoble’s acknowledgment of the output format constraint.

Patrick clarifies that his earlier message about generalizing the output format was indeed intended to refer to the **editor template presentation** (how diffs and other information are rendered in the editor buffer) rather than just the option name. He reiterates that the format must accommodate future `git history` subcommands (`split`, `move`, etc.) and their use cases: changes to commit layout, commit order, commit messages, and updates to multiple references. His earlier suggestion of `git range-diff`’s structured, multi-commit format remains the proposed model. This reply does not resolve the tension between the lightweight unified diff (mirroring `git commit --verbose`) and the structured format, nor does it address the opt-in vs. opt-out debate or the scripted-editing concern. It serves primarily as a confirmation of the technical requirement that the output format be forward-compatible with all future `git history` subcommands. The author (Jelle van der Waa) has not yet responded to this constraint.
