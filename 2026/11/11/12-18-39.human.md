This is the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s now-merged 12-patch series modernizing `git rerere`’s subcommand handling. The patch removes the last references to the global `the_repository` variable in `builtin/rerere.c` by passing the repository pointer that is already handed to `cmd_rerere()` down to every helper function. The change is purely mechanical: no behavior change, no new options, and no on-disk format changes. It is part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort and keeps the codebase consistent with the project’s goal of eliminating implicit globals. The patch is small (24 lines, 13 deletions) and uncontroversial; it was already reviewed and merged as part of the larger series.
