This is a brief review-response email in an ongoing patch series, specifically Justin Tobler accepting a refactoring suggestion for patch 3/4 of the "odb: eagerly create tmp-objdir at transaction start" series (part of the ODB abstraction effort).

Justin Tobler agrees with Karthik Nayak's proposal to restructure `tmp_objdir_create()` in `tmp-objdir.c`, replacing the current pattern of saving and restoring `errno` around each syscall with a single `goto fail` exit path that sets `errno` only at actual failure points. Justin confirms he will incorporate this change in the next version of the series. This resolves the code-clarity suggestion Karthik had raised on patch 3/4, though no technical objection to the patch's correctness was ever raised.

With this acceptance, Karthik's remaining open questions on the series are now limited to the design-level challenge to the eager-creation premise itself (why switch from lazy to eager temporary-directory creation) and his challenge to the performance claim in patch 4/4 regarding expectations about loose object counts in ODB transactions. Patrick Steinhardt's earlier objection to "ODB" jargon in `t0004-unwritable.sh` error messages also remains pending a v2 update.
