This is a contributor housekeeping reply about mailing-list email formatting, not a technical patch. In the Outreachy proposal thread on improving Git's argument parsing, Richard Kerry had noted that in his Outlook client the proposal arrived as an unbroken wall of text without indentation or blank lines, and asked whether the project guidelines could request that contributors break up large text blocks with blank lines for readability.

Junio C Hamano responds by pointing to the existing convention that all text -- commit messages, code, docs, and emails -- must be line-wrapped to fit comfortably on an 80-column terminal after a few levels of `>` quoting, which practically means keeping lines to around 70 columns. He notes that he line-wrapped Kerry's message in his reply to demonstrate. Junio adds that he personally prefers a blank line between paragraphs, including blocks of quoted text, and says he would not mind someone adding an explicit rule about this to the documentation near where the line-length limit is discussed. The email contains no commentary on the Outreachy proposal itself.
