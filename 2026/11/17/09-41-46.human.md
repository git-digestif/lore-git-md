This is a mailing-list etiquette note, not a patch -- a reply in the Outreachy proposal thread from Richard Kerry complaining that Fien Dora's draft proposal arrived in his Outlook client with no indentation and no blank lines, making it a wall of text that is hard to read. He points out that a few frequent contributors habitually omit blank lines around their additions within long `>`-quoted blocks, which makes new lines blend into the mass of quoted text, and asks whether the project guidelines could reasonably request at least some visual breaking of large slabs of text in both plain emails and long replies.

The email carries no technical content about Git itself; it is purely about readability of list traffic. It is a polite, low-stakes suggestion aimed at improving the signal-to-noise ratio for readers whose mail clients render quoted text poorly.
