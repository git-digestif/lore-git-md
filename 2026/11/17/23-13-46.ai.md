This is a substantive review of patch 4/4 in Elia Pinto's v2 bugfix series on integer truncation in large-file handling, specifically targeting the diffstat totals and graph scaling changes in `diff.c`. The review engages directly with both the completeness of the fix and the adequacy of the commit message documentation, leaving these questions open for the author to address.
