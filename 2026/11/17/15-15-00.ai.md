This is a review response in an ongoing bugfix thread for the two-patch series fixing bounds handling in `utf8_strnwidth()`. Dirk Gouders replies to Junio C Hamano's substantive critique of the first patch, conceding that the blame for the out-of-bounds read is better placed on the missing guard after the ANSI-escape-skipping loop rather than on `display_mode_esc_sequence_len()` itself lacking an explicit boundary parameter. He frames the underlying issue as a "contract mismatch": `utf8_strnwidth()` accepts a `len` boundary but fails to enforce it when calling `display_mode_esc_sequence_len()` (which has no boundary) and `utf8_width()` (which has an optional boundary that is not used). Gouders also agrees with Junio's concern that the proposed fix is "too late" in the specific case where `string` points to the end of an mmapped region, because reading `string[len]` inside `display_mode_esc_sequence_len()` could trigger a `SIGBUS` before the new bounds check is reached. He states he is preparing a v2 that addresses all these points, including the memory-safety edge case, and asks for patience to avoid wasting reviewers' time. The thread remains open with no alternative fix yet proposed; the next expected step is Dirk's v2 respin of the `utf8.c` bounds fix and its accompanying unit test in `t/unit-tests/u-utf8-width.c`.
