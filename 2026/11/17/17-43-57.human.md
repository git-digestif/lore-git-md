This is the third and final patch in Christian Couder's three-patch series addressing 64-bit Windows (LLP64) compatibility. It removes a now-obsolete `size > LONG_MAX` guard from `xdl_do_merge()` in `xdiff/xmerge.c` and updates the corresponding tests in `t/t4300-merge-tree.sh`.

The check was originally added after 288266c11d to prevent truncation when the merge result size was stored in `mmbuffer_t`, whose `size` field was a `long`. Since 81db32ddce replaced `mmbuffer_t` with `mmfile_t` (which uses `size_t`), and since the preceding patches in this series changed `odb_write_object()` and `write_buf_to_worktree()` to accept `size_t`, the `LONG_MAX` comparison no longer prevents any actual truncation. Removing it allows merges producing results larger than 2 GiB to succeed on platforms where `long` is 32-bit, such as 64-bit Windows.

The patch also cleans up `t/t4300-merge-tree.sh` by dropping the `LONG_IS_64BIT` prerequisite from two EXPENSIVE tests and removing a third test that explicitly expected `git merge-tree` to fail on LLP64 platforms when the buffer exceeded `LONG_MAX`. The remaining guard against genuine overflow on 32-bit `size_t` systems is preserved via the `st_add*()` calls introduced in b1313872ce.

As the closing piece of the series, this completes the effort to ensure `size_t` flows unbroken from the merge computation through the object database and worktree write paths. The series is under review with no recorded objections yet, and testing the LLP64-specific paths requires 64-bit Windows with `GIT_TEST_LONG=1` since Linux and macOS CI cannot exercise them.
