This is a bug report targeting the combine-diff machinery, specifically `git diff-tree --cc` output for octopus merges with many parents. The reporter, Sami Jawhar, demonstrates that at 33 and 63 parents the hunk headers disagree with the hunk bodies (deletion columns are marked incorrectly), and at 64 parents the diff produces no hunks at all. The root cause is identified in `coalesce_lines()` at `combine-diff.c:249`, where `baseend->parent_map |= 1<<parent` shifts an `int` rather than an `unsigned long`; on 64-bit systems this overflows once `parent` reaches 32, and shifting by 64 at the 64-parent boundary is undefined behavior. The email notes that neighboring functions -- `give_context()` around lines 544-545, `make_hunks()` around lines 610-611, and `dump_sline()` around line 756 -- already use the correct `1UL<<num_parent` form, suggesting the fix is to make `coalesce_lines()` consistent. A self-contained reproduction script is included that builds octopus merges with 32, 33, 63, and 64 parents and verifies header/body agreement. The bug is present in both Git 2.43.0 and current master at commit e1ec09b23c8e.
