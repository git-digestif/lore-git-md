This is patch 1 of a 3-patch platform-compatibility series addressing LLP64 (64-bit Windows) truncation bugs, specifically a bugfix to `notes-merge.c`.

Christian Couder changes the `size` parameter of `write_buf_to_worktree()` in `notes-merge.c` from `unsigned long` to `size_t`. The function has two callers within the same file: `write_note_to_worktree()` and `ll_merge_in_worktree()`. Since commit c6a4629e32 switched note reading to `size_t`, the first caller now passes a `size_t` that was being implicitly narrowed to `unsigned long`; on LLP64 platforms where `unsigned long` is 32-bit, a delete/modify conflict on a note of 4 GiB or more silently writes a truncated note into `NOTES_MERGE_WORKTREE`. The second caller passes the `size_t` result of `ll_merge()`, which cannot exceed `LONG_MAX` due to a guard added in 1c93cc03ea, but this patch prepares the codebase for removing that guard in the series' third patch.

The diff is a single-line signature change in `notes-merge.c`. This patch is the first in a series that also widens ODB write APIs to `size_t` (patch 2) and removes the `size > LONG_MAX` check in `xdl_do_merge()` (patch 3), building on the recently merged `jk/xdiff-size_t` (81db32ddce) and `kn/safer-mem-arithmetic-in-xdiff` (1c93cc03ea) efforts.
