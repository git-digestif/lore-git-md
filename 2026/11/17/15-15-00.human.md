This is a bugfix discussion for Git's UTF-8 handling code, specifically the second email in a two-patch series addressing bounds handling in `utf8_strnwidth()` in `utf8.c`. Dirk Gouders is responding to a substantive review from Junio C Hamano regarding the first patch, which attempts to ensure the function respects its `len` argument when skipping ANSI escape sequences.

Junio's review identifies a subtle but important flaw in the proposed fix: while adding a boundary check before calling `utf8_width()` is better than nothing, it may come too late. If the input string sits at the end of an mmapped region, `display_mode_esc_sequence_len()` itself could read beyond the mapped memory and trigger a `SIGBUS` before the new guard ever runs. He suggests the root cause is better characterized as a missing guard after the escape-sequence skipping loop rather than solely a missing boundary parameter in `display_mode_esc_sequence_len()`.

Dirk Gouders acknowledges that Junio's assessment is correct, agreeing that the check is indeed too late in mmapped scenarios. He states he is preparing a v2 that will address all the raised concerns, and indicates he wants to give the revision sufficient time to avoid introducing new errors or wasting reviewers' time.

The exchange demonstrates a healthy review dynamic: the maintainer has caught a memory-safety edge case that the initial patch missed, and the author is responsive and planning a revision rather than defending the incomplete fix. The thread remains open pending the v2 respin.
