Sami Jawhar filed a bug report about `git diff-tree --cc` producing incorrect output for octopus merges with many parents: hunk headers disagree with their bodies at 33 and 63 parents, and at 64 parents the diff disappears entirely. The root cause is a plain `int` shift (`1<<parent`) being assigned to an `unsigned long` field in `coalesce_lines()` at `combine-diff.c:249`, which overflows past 32 parents and triggers undefined behavior at 64. The bug reproduces on both Git 2.43.0 and current master.

Aniket Gupta has replied confirming the diagnosis and volunteered to send a fix patch that would correct the shift to use `1UL<<parent` like the neighboring functions already do, and possibly handle merges exceeding 64 parents. No patch has been posted yet, no maintainer has responded, and the thread remains open awaiting a proposed fix.
