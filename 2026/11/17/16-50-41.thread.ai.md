Core problem or goal: prevent out-of-bounds reads when scanning for ANSI display-mode/SGR escape sequences such as `\033[1;31m` in non-NUL-terminated buffers. Version 2 adds `display_mode_esc_sequence_len_counted(const char *s, size_t len)` in `utf8.c`, declared in `utf8.h`, which scans exactly `len` bytes and never reads at or beyond `s[len]`, returning the length of a complete sequence at the start of the buffer or 0 otherwise. The original `display_mode_esc_sequence_len(const char *s)` remains as a wrapper passing `strlen(s)`.
