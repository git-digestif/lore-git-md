This is a **bugfix** cover letter for the second iteration of a series fixing silent integer truncation when Git handles files larger than 2–4 GiB.

Elia Pinto sends v2 of the "widen 32-bit sizes and counts" series, expanding it from three to four patches. The first and third patches—widening `hashwrite()` in `csum-file` and `hash_chars()` in `diffcore-delta`—remain unchanged from v1 and still target packfile corruption and rename-detection failures on huge objects. The substantive change is patch 2/4, which now follows Phillip Wood’s review and widens not just the `count_lines()` parameter but the entire complete-rewrite path in `diff.c` (including `emit_rewrite_lines()` and `count_trailing_blank()`) to `size_t`, closing the overflow hole that v1 inadvertently created in the line counter. Patch 4/4 is new: it switches diffstat totals and graph scaling in `diff.c` to `uintmax_t` so that `--stat` output no longer overflows when a single file exceeds `INT_MAX` lines, and adds a cheap regression test for `scale_linear()`.

The series now includes two `EXPENSIVE` tests in `t/t4022-diff-rewrite.sh` and a case in `t/t4052-stat-output.sh`, addressing the lack of test coverage in v1. It is based on master at 50fe5cc63f and remains self-contained, with no reported merge status. The cover letter directly acknowledges and fixes the critical flaw identified in the v1 review, which makes this version substantially more sound than its predecessor.
