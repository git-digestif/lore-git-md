This is a post-merge follow-up in the now-closed `ps/builtin-rerere-subcommands` series, which modernized `git rerere`'s subcommand handling by replacing custom parsing logic with Git’s standard `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` mechanism. The change is minor but user-facing. Previously, running `git rerere unknown` would print the full usage synopsis, listing all valid subcommands. Now, it fails with a terse "too many arguments" message, which could confuse users trying to discover available subcommands. Junio flags this as a potential regression, though he does not demand a fix—his tone suggests it’s an observation rather than a blocker. The thread remains closed; no further patches are expected, but the note may inform future documentation or error-message improvements. The broader series remains a well-executed refactoring that eliminated technical debt without altering behavior or on-disk formats.
