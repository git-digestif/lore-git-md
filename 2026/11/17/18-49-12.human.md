This is a small documentation patch -- contributor housekeeping for mailing-list etiquette. Okhuomon Ajayi adds three lines to `Documentation/MyFirstContribution.adoc` advising contributors to leave a blank line between paragraphs in email replies, explicitly noting that a block of quoted text counts as a paragraph and should be separated from surrounding text by blank lines.

The patch fulfills a commitment Ajayi made earlier in the thread: after Junio C Hamano expressed a preference for blank lines around quoted blocks and welcomed a documentation change to formalize the convention, Ajayi volunteered to write it. This email delivers on that promise, placing the new guidance right next to the existing advice about trimming quoted context in `MyFirstContribution.adoc`.

It is worth noting that this sits on a side branch of the larger thread, which centers on Fien Dora's Outreachy proposal to convert `git diff-index` and `git diff-tree` to the shared `parse_options()` API. The formatting-convention discussion is now effectively closed pending this patch; the substantive technical proposal remains the main open topic with no reviews yet.
