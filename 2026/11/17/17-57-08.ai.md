This is a thread follow-up in the bugfix series for `utf8_strnwidth()` bounds handling. Dirk Gouders replies to Junio C Hamano's illustrative patch, conceding that Junio's approach of threading a `size_t len` parameter through `display_mode_esc_sequence_len()` is acceptable, and abandoning his own planned v2 alternative.

Gouders had intended to introduce two new functions in `utf8.c` -- `ansi_sgr_nlen(const char *str, size_t len)` and `ansi_sgr_nlen_multi(const char *s, size_t len)` -- to replace `display_mode_esc_sequence_len()` entirely. The first would parse a single bounded ANSI SGR sequence (requiring at least 3 bytes, checking for ESC, `[`, digits or semicolons, and a terminating `m`), while the second would loop to consume all leading SGR sequences without requiring callers to write their own loops. Since Junio has already posted a working illustration of the length-threading approach, Gouders defers to that instead.

The thread remains open pending Gouders's v2 respin, which is still expected to address the mmapped-region SIGBUS edge case and the separate post-loop guard inside `utf8_strnwidth()` that both parties agree remains necessary regardless of which mechanism is used to bound the escape-sequence parser.
