This thread concerns a bounds-safety fix in Git's `utf8.c` to prevent out-of-bounds reads when scanning for ANSI escape sequences in non-NUL-terminated buffers. Junio C Hamano posted `[PATCH v2]` adding `display_mode_esc_sequence_len_counted()`, which scans a bounded buffer safely, and has proposed handing authorship to Dirk Gouders, who has accepted and plans to repost the work as `[PATCH v2 1/2]` with his clar unit tests as `2/2`. The series remains under review with no integration branch status reported.

The discussion has touched on several technical points: Junio rejected Dirk's proposed early-return micro-optimization, accepted his clar tests targeting `t/unit-tests/u-utf8-ansi.c`, and the colon-separator question for ECMA-48 SGR sequences has been actively debated. Andy Koppe established that the complete SGR regex is `"\033\[[0-9:;]*m"` and confirmed that sequences like `"\033[:;m"` are valid under the standard. In his latest reply, Dirk conceded this point, acknowledging that simply expanding the accepted alphabet from `[0-9;]` to `[0-9:;]` in the existing parser would recognize all valid SGR sequences without new states -- though no decision has been made on whether the patch should actually accept colons.

Still open is Dirk's expected repost as a two-part series under his name, and the unresolved question of whether colon-separated subparameters should be supported in this change or left for future work.
