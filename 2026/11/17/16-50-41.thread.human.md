This thread is a maintainer-driven bounds-safety fix to ANSI escape sequence handling in `utf8.c`. Junio C Hamano originally posted a v2 patch introducing `display_mode_esc_sequence_len_counted(const char *s, size_t len)`, which scans exactly `len` bytes for SGR sequences without reading past the buffer end, while keeping the old `display_mode_esc_sequence_len()` as a wrapper for NUL-terminated callers. Three internal callers -- `utf8_strnwidth()`, `strbuf_add_wrapped_text()`, and `strbuf_utf8_replace()` -- now use the counted variant, and the change touches only `utf8.c` and `utf8.h`.

Review has been constructive but unresolved. Dirk Gouders initially suggested an early-return micro-optimization plus a clar unit test suite for the new function; Junio rejected the optimization as unproven and unnecessary, and Dirk has since conceded that point. Junio has agreed to incorporate Dirk's clar tests in a forthcoming version. In his latest reply, Junio has gone further and suggested Dirk take over authorship entirely, proposing the patch become a `[PATCH v2 1/2]` series under Dirk's name. Andy Koppe separately asked whether the parser should accept colons as parameter separators per ECMA-48 and xterm behavior; Junio questioned whether Git itself ever generates such sequences, and that question remains open.

The series is still at v2 and under review, with no integration branch status mentioned. The likely next step is a v3 under Dirk's authorship that adds the clar test suite in `t/unit-tests/u-utf8-ansi.c`, while the colon-separator question awaits a final decision.
