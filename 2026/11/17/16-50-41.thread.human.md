The thread concerns a bounds-safety fix in `utf8.c` to prevent out-of-bounds reads when scanning ANSI SGR escape sequences in non-NUL-terminated buffers. The patch adds `display_mode_esc_sequence_len_counted(const char *s, size_t len)`, which scans exactly `len` bytes, and updates the three internal callers in `utf8.c` (`utf8_strnwidth()`, `strbuf_add_wrapped_text()`, and `strbuf_utf8_replace()`) to use it; only `utf8.c` and `utf8.h` are touched.
