This thread covers Christian Couder's three-patch series fixing latent 64-bit truncation bugs on LLP64 platforms like 64-bit Windows, where `long` is 32-bit but `size_t` is 64-bit. The series widens the data path from `ll_merge()` through the object database so `size_t` flows unbroken end to end, touching `notes-merge.c`, the ODB write functions in `odb.c` and related files, and removing a now-redundant `LONG_MAX` guard in `xdiff/xmerge.c`. All three patches are posted and under review; nothing has been queued in any integration branch.

Patrick Steinhardt has reviewed patches 1 and 2 substantively, calling the first "obviously correct" and tracing the data flow in the second to confirm every downstream consumer already accepts `size_t`, raising no objections. He did flag that patch 2 overlaps with a separate series from Spiros Gitter that also converts ODB write functions to `size_t`. Couder has now replied acknowledging he overlooked Gitter's work and committed to rebasing his own series on top of it, so a v2 is expected that may shift the exact context and scope of what remains in his patches. Patch 3 has not yet received review.
