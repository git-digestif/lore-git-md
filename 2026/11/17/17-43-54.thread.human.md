Christian Couder has posted the second patch in his three-part series addressing 64-bit Windows (LLP64) compatibility, where `long` is 32-bit but `size_t` is 64-bit. Following patch 1, which fixed a truncation bug in `notes-merge.c`, this patch widens the length parameters of the object-database write APIs from `unsigned long` to `size_t` across `odb.c`, `odb.h`, `odb/source.h`, `odb/source-loose.c`, and `odb/source-inmemory.c`. The affected functions include `odb_write_object()`, `odb_write_object_ext()`, `odb_source_write_object()`, and `write_loose_object()`, along with the `size` field of `struct inmemory_object`. The change is mechanical and prepares the ground for the third patch, which will remove the `size > LONG_MAX` guard in `xdl_do_merge()` now that merge results can flow through `size_t` all the way to the ODB layer.

Both patches build on recently merged work: `jk/xdiff-size_t` (commit 81db32ddce) and `kn/safer-mem-arithmetic-in-xdiff` (commit 1c93cc03ea). The series remains under review with no recorded acks or reviews yet. As with the first patch, Linux and macOS CI cannot exercise the LLP64-specific paths; the author notes that testing requires 64-bit Windows with `GIT_TEST_LONG=1` and sufficient memory and disk for merge results over 4 GiB.
