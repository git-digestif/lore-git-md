This is a maintainer review approving the fifth and final patch of Elia Pinto's five-patch v2 series on loose object size handling and LLP64 truncation fixes. Junio C Hamano responds to patch 5/5 ("notes: avoid truncating the size of concatenated notes") by endorsing the use of `st_add3()` overflow-safe arithmetic when widening the buffer length in `combine_notes_concatenate()` from `unsigned long` to `size_t`, and concludes with "Looking good," signaling approval with no requested changes.

The approved change addresses a heap overflow in `notes.c` where concatenating notes totaling 4 GiB or more on LLP64 platforms (where `long` is 32-bit) caused `buf_len` to truncate while `memcpy()` still used full 64-bit lengths. The fix computes the size with `st_add3()` in `size_t` and passes it through `cast_size_t_to_ulong()` only at the `odb_write_object()` call, which still accepts `unsigned long`; this converts the previous silent truncation into a clean die on affected platforms. No automated test accompanies the patch because reproduction requires both a 32-bit `long` platform and notes exceeding 4 GiB, though the commit message documents manual reproduction via notes trees with multiple fanout levels or fetching a notes ref.

With this approval, all five patches in the series have now received positive maintainer feedback: patches 1/5, 2/5, and 4/5 were previously approved, patch 3/5 remains under rework discussion regarding error-reporting placement in `read_istream_loose()`, and patch 5/5 is now cleared. The series touches `object-file.c`, `odb/source-loose.c`, `builtin/cat-file.c`, and `notes.c`, with tests in `t/t1006-cat-file.sh` and `t/t1060-object-corruption.sh` covering the cross-platform fixes while the LLP64-specific scenarios rely on documented manual reproduction.
