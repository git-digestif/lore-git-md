This is a substantive review reply from Junio C Hamano on patch 3/4 of Elia Pinto's v2 series fixing integer truncation in large-file handling. The patch itself widens the local variable `sz` in `hash_chars()` within `diffcore-delta.c` from `unsigned int` to `size_t` so that rename detection correctly hashes the full buffer for files of 4 GiB or more rather than silently truncating to a prefix. Junio's review goes beyond the proposed change to probe the underlying data structure: he notes that the `cnt` member of `struct spanhash` remains `uint` and could theoretically wrap down to zero after four billion hash collisions, making a populated bucket appear empty. He asks whether this counter should be widened to `size_t` in the same patch, or whether there is a reason it can safely remain 32-bit -- drawing a distinction with the `hashval` member, which he suggests can stay `uint`.

The review is substantive because it identifies a concrete overflow edge case in the hash table implementation rather than commenting on style or wording, and it cites the specific struct member and the exact condition under which the bug would manifest. No resolution is reached in this email; the question of whether to also widen `cnt` remains open for Elia Pinto to address.
