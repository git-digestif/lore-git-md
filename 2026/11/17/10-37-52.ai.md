This is a cover letter for a v2 bugfix series (4 patches) that widens 32-bit size and line-count types to `size_t` or `uintmax_t` to prevent integer truncation when Git handles files or buffers larger than 2–4 GiB. The series is based on master at 50fe5cc63f and builds directly on v1, incorporating feedback from Phillip Wood's review.

The most significant change in v2 is the rewrite of patch 2/4. Where v1 only widened the size parameter of `count_lines()`, v2 extends `size_t` through the entire complete-rewrite path of `git diff -B`, including `emit_rewrite_lines()` and `count_trailing_blank()`, ensuring line counters and sizes remain wide enough to avoid the overflow Phillip identified. This patch also adds two EXPENSIVE tests to `t/t4022-diff-rewrite.sh`. Patch 4/4 is new and addresses two related overflow issues surfaced after 2/4: diffstat totals in `--stat` output are summed in `int` and can overflow once single files exceed `INT_MAX` lines, and `scale_linear()` can overflow for small files when the graph is wide; a cheap test in `t/t4052-stat-output.sh` covers the latter case.

Patches 1/4 and 3/4 remain unchanged from v1. They fix `hashwrite()` in `csum-file.c`/`csum-file.h` (changing the byte count parameter from `uint32_t` to `size_t`) and `hash_chars()` in `diffcore-delta.c` (changing the buffer size from `unsigned int` to `size_t`). These still require objects of 4 GiB or more and were verified by simulating 16-bit truncation rather than by committed tests, as generating multi-gigabyte test inputs is impractical.

The author notes a coordination point with a separate series ("loose object size checks and a few size truncations"): patch 6/6 of that series will be dropped from its v2 because `count_trailing_blank()` is now modified here, avoiding conflicts between the two series.

The series remains under review with no merge status reported. Key symbols touched are `hashwrite()`, `count_lines()`, `emit_rewrite_lines()`, `count_trailing_blank()`, `hash_chars()`, and the diffstat total accumulation plus `scale_linear()` in `diff.c`. Future discussion will likely focus on whether the widened types introduce any new edge cases in the low-level I/O and diff code paths, and whether the EXPENSIVE tests in `t4022` adequately cover the complete-rewrite path.
