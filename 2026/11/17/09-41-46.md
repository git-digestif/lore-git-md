I don't know whether it's because of my mail reader, which is Outlook (I know it isn't a favourite in these parts but this is a corporate machine), but this email has come through to me with no indentation and no blank lines between any lines.

I don't know whether the guidelines explicitly mention this but for myself I find both these properties make it difficult to read.

There are a small number of fairly frequent contributors to the list who are also in the habit of not putting blank lines around additions (or even putting just one after the addition) within long blocks of text with reply marks (ie '>' characters).  That also doesn't help me be able to easily read contributions.  If there is a mass of text with all but a handful of lines starting with '>' they are very difficult to spot.

Would it be reasonable for me to ask that the guidelines should request at least a little bit of breaking of large slabs of text, whether plain text, as here, or long replies, as elsewhere?
