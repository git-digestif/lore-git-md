This is a **bugfix** in the `pretty` subsystem, now in its third iteration (`v3`). Andy Koppe is fixing how ANSI color escapes behave across line breaks in custom `git log --format=...` output, where colors currently get lost when pagers or `--graph` reset them at newlines, or leak into subsequent lines.

The `v3` changes are purely cosmetic compared to `v2`: variable renames (`is_reset` to `reset`, etc.) and combining the reset/replay conditionals to make the algorithm in `make_line_colors_self_contained()` easier to follow. The behavior is unchanged -- when color output is enabled, `repo_format_commit_message()` scans the formatted string, inserts a reset before any newline where colors are still active, and replays those colors at the start of the next non-empty line. Tests in `t/t4205-log-pretty-formats.sh` cover wrapped lines, empty lines, NUL bytes, manual escape sequences, and `--graph` interaction.

The patch remains under review with no merge status reported yet.
