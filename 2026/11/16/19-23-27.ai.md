This is the author's response in the RFC thread about adding `--verbose` support to `git history reword`. Jelle van der Waa announces a pivot: the next version of the series will drop the `--verbose` option entirely and make diff display the default behavior for `git history reword`, `split`, and `fixup` (but not `drop`). The behavior will respect the existing `commit.verbose` configuration, aligning with `git commit --verbose`. This decision resolves the opt-in vs. opt-out debate in favor of opt-out, with configurability as a concession to reviewers who insisted on user control. The author does not address the unresolved output format requirement (generalizability to future subcommands via a structured format like `git range-diff`) or the tension between the lightweight unified diff and the structured format, leaving those questions open for the next iteration. The response also does not engage with the flag-naming debate or the scripted-editing concern, focusing instead on the immediate consensus around defaults.
