This is a reply from the author, Jasper Mayone, to Junio C Hamano’s review of the final patch in the `git worktree list` filtering series. Jasper concedes Junio’s point: the patch’s only purpose was to eliminate redundant output in `git branch` (showing the same branch twice when it is being rebased in the current worktree), but Junio’s workflow actually relies on seeing the `+` marker for bisected branches even when they are in the current worktree. Since the `%(currentworktree)` atom (patch 4/5) exists solely to support this cleanup, Jasper agrees to drop both patches 4/5 and 5/5, leaving the series at three patches. He confirms that Junio’s feedback on patches 1/5 and 2/5 does not require changes, so no v6 will be sent unless Junio requests one. The series is now effectively complete at v5 1–3/5.
