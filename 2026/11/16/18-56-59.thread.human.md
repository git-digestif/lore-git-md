Dirk Gouders has posted v6 of a four-patch series fixing out-of-bounds reads in Git's UTF-8 display-width code, with patch 1 authored by Junio C Hamano. The series makes `utf8_strnwidth()` and `utf8_width()` respect their length arguments, including the mmapped-buffer SIGBUS case, and adds the length-bounded `display_mode_esc_sequence_len_counted()` while keeping the NUL-terminated wrapper. The latest email, patch 4/4, threads a `remainder` value into `utf8_width()` so a multibyte character chopped by `len` cannot cause reads past the buffer, and adds a CJK chopped test showing bounded behavior; its commit message also explains why the earlier escape-sequence bounds check must remain even with the remainder argument.

v6 incorporates the mechanical fixes Junio requested after reviewing v5: trailer ordering, removal of a redundant include, `cl_assert_equal_u()` for size_t assertions, the `len <= i` comparison style, dropping the `_counted` variant where NUL termination suffices, and clearer commit messages including the concrete `"\033[31mhello"` example. Junio had called v5 "a very good shape" and "closer to the finish line," so v6 reads as convergence toward merge readiness, but it remains under review and is not merged or queued in `master`, `next`, or `seen`.

Open items are limited: whether v6 fully satisfies reviewers and when Junio queues it, plus Andy Koppe's stated intent to update his separate `make_line_colors_self_contained()` work to the new counted API. There is also a small test TODO about switching assertions to `cl_assert_equal_u()` if the width functions ever change return type from int to size_t, which Dirk currently wants to avoid.
