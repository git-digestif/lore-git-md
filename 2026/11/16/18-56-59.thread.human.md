This thread concerns a v2 four-patch series by Dirk Gouchers that fixes bounds handling in Git's UTF-8 display-width functions, `utf8_strnwidth()` and `utf8_width()`, ensuring they respect their length arguments and do not read past the end of the input buffer -- including an mmapped-region SIGBUS edge case -- along with a documentation correction. The series incorporates a counted variant of the ANSI escape-sequence scanner, `display_mode_esc_sequence_len_counted()`, authored by Junio C Hamano, and extends the same boundary-respecting treatment to both width functions.

The main design question -- whether to retain a NUL-terminated wrapper around the counted variant -- is now resolved. Andy Koppe has explicitly concurred with Dirk Gouders's proposal to drop the `strlen()`-based wrapper entirely and switch its sole remaining caller in `pretty.c` to the counted variant directly, accepting the performance rationale that the wrapper would make repeated scanning quadratic. Koppe added that he will update his own separately proposed `make_line_colors_self_contained()` to use the counted variant as well, and in a postscript suggested that if the wrapper is removed, the `_counted` suffix could also be dropped and the `len` parameter folded into `display_mode_esc_sequence_len()` itself, collapsing the two functions into one.

Patch 4 has received Junio's approval with no changes requested, and there are no outstanding technical objections to the bounds-handling logic in patches 3 or 4. What remains before a possible v3 are minor surface-level items: Junio's style nits on patch 3, a meson build ordering fix for the new unit test files, and removing the inaccurate "null-terminated" phrasing from the `utf8_strnwidth()` documentation, which Gouders has already acknowledged.
