This thread concerns a four-patch v5 series by Dirk Gouders that fixes bounds handling in Git's UTF-8 display-width functions (`utf8_strnwidth()` and `utf8_width()`) to prevent out-of-bounds reads, including a mmapped-region SIGBUS edge case, and corrects misleading documentation. The series replaces the retracted v3 and a broken v4, dropping a fifth patch entirely and reverting from an end-pointer API to a length-based `_counted` approach using array index/length to avoid pointer-arithmetic undefined behavior and O(n^2) degradation.

Junio C Hamano, who authored the first patch introducing `display_mode_esc_sequence_len_counted()`, has now reviewed all four patches and sent a cover-letter response stating that aside from minor mechanical nits already detailed -- trailer reordering and test assertion changes in patch 1, wording tweaks in patch 2, a commit-message clarification in patch 3, and a title adjustment in patch 4 -- the series is in "a very good shape" and "we are getting closer to the finish line." Andy Koppe, whose simplified bounds checks were incorporated, will update his own related work to the new API. No version has been merged or queued in any integration branch yet.

The series remains under review at v5, with the remaining open items being the specific requests Junio enumerated: reordering commit trailers to chronological custodial order, switching `cl_assert_equal_i()` to `cl_assert_equal_u()` for size_t values, rewording test comments from "end pointers" to "length limits", moving a `strlen()` call after an early return in `strbuf_add_wrapped_text()`, using "bounded" instead of "terminated" in patch 2's commit message, clarifying the externally observable symptom in patch 3's commit message, and tightening patch 4's title to "utf8: bound utf8_width() calls in utf8_strnwidth()". A v6 addressing these points or direct queuing by Junio appears imminent.
