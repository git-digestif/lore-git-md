Dirk Gouders has posted v2 of a four-patch series fixing bounds handling in Git's UTF-8 display-width code, expanding on the original two-patch v1. The series addresses a real bug where `utf8_strnwidth()` and `utf8_width()` could read past the end of their input buffer -- including a potential SIGBUS when the input sits at the end of an mmapped region -- and corrects misleading documentation that claimed `utf8_strnwidth()` returns `strlen()` on invalid UTF-8 when it actually returns the `len` argument. Patch 1, authored by Junio C Hamano, introduces `display_mode_esc_sequence_len_counted()` so ANSI escape sequences can be scanned without reading at or beyond a given length; patch 3 applies that counted variant inside `utf8_strnwidth()`'s loop with a guard preventing the pointer from advancing past `orig + len`; patch 4 extends the same boundary-respecting treatment to `utf8_width()`; and patch 2 is the documentation fix. New unit tests in `t/unit-tests/u-utf8-ansi.c` and `t/unit-tests/u-utf8-width.c` cover truncated escape sequences and chopped CJK characters.

Junio C Hamano has now reviewed the series positively. After driving the counted-variant design himself, his latest replies offer no technical objections: he flagged only two surface-level formatting nits on patch 3 (an excess space and a long line to wrap) and replied to patch 4 with "Well spotted, and nicely done." Dirk Gouders incorporated Junio's patch and extended the fix to `utf8_width()` in response to the earlier discussion, having abandoned his own alternative helper approach in favor of the counted-variant design.

The series remains under review and has not been merged to any integration branch. The only outstanding items are the two cosmetic style suggestions on patch 3; there are no open technical questions. The thread now awaits either a v3 addressing the whitespace nit or Junio queuing the series.
