This thread concerns Dirk Gouders's v5 four-patch series fixing bounds handling in Git's UTF-8 display-width code (`utf8_strnwidth()` and `utf8_width()`), ensuring the functions respect their length arguments and do not read past buffer ends, including a mmapped-region SIGBUS edge case. The series also corrects misleading documentation and adds unit tests in `t/unit-tests/u-utf8-ansi.c` and `t/unit-tests/u-utf8-width.c`. Patch 1, authored by Junio C Hamano, introduces `display_mode_esc_sequence_len_counted()` using an index/length API rather than end pointers, avoiding both pointer-arithmetic undefined behavior and O(n^2) degradation on NUL-terminated strings. v5 drops the controversial fifth patch from v4 and restores a missing variable that broke compilation.

Junio C Hamano has reviewed each patch individually and pronounced the series to be in "a very good shape," stating "we are getting closer to the finish line," while enumerating mechanical nits: reordering commit trailers, removing a redundant include, switching test assertions to `cl_assert_equal_u()`, rewriting comparisons as `len <= i`, moving a `strlen()` call after an early return, and various wording tweaks. In his latest reply, Gouders agreed to address all of these points and briefly noted he would test whether the already-computed `strlen(text)` in `strbuf_add_wrapped_text()` could substitute for the `_counted` variant there. Andy Koppe, who had previously objected to pointer-arithmetic edge cases, accepts the length-based design and will update his own separate patch to match.

The series remains under review and has not been merged or queued in any integration branch. The core technical approach -- the length-based `_counted` API -- appears settled and approved pending the mechanical cleanups, which Gouders is prepared to incorporate in a potential v6 or during integration.
