This thread is Dirk Gouders’s two-patch v1 series to fix bounds handling and documentation in `utf8_strnwidth()` in `utf8.c`, with a new unit test in `t/unit-tests/u-utf8-width.c`. The series addresses two issues: `utf8_strnwidth()` ignoring its `len` argument for valid UTF-8, and its docs wrongly claiming a `strlen()`-like return in some cases. It remains under review, not merged.

Junio C Hamano gave substantive review arguing that the more important defect is the missing guard after the loop that skips ANSI escape sequences, and warning that a fix placed too late could still read past the end of an mmapped region and risk `SIGBUS`. Dirk Gouders has now replied, agreeing that the blame is better framed as a contract mismatch: `utf8_strnwidth()` accepts `len` but does not enforce it when calling `display_mode_esc_sequence_len()`, which has no boundary, or `utf8_width()`, whose optional boundary is unused. He accepts that the proposed check can be “too late” when `string` points near the end of an mmapped region, and says he is preparing a v2 that addresses the bounds enforcement and the memory-safety edge case. The open item is therefore Dirk’s v2 respin; no alternative fix has been proposed and no integration status has changed.
