Dirk Gouders posted v5 of his four-patch series fixing bounds handling and documentation in `utf8_strnwidth()` and `utf8_width()`, dropping v4's fifth patch as agreed, restoring a `skip` variable that broke compilation, and shifting to a length-based `_counted` API that avoids both pointer-arithmetic undefined behavior and O(n^2) scanning. Patch 1, authored by Junio C Hamano, introduces `display_mode_esc_sequence_len_counted()`; patches 2 through 4 cover the documentation fix, the bounded `utf8_strnwidth()`, and the main loop's remainder handling, with new unit tests in `t/unit-tests/u-utf8-ansi.c` and `t/unit-tests/u-utf8-width.c`. The series remains under review with no version merged or queued in any integration branch.

Junio has now reviewed the first two patches. On patch 1 he requested a handful of style and test adjustments -- reordering commit trailers to chronological custodial order, comparing as `len <= i` rather than `i >= len`, removing a redundant include, using `cl_assert_equal_u()` for size_t assertions, and moving a `strlen()` call after an early return -- which would presumably be addressed in a v6. On patch 2, the documentation-only fix for the `utf8_strnwidth()` comment, he approves the change and suggests only a wording tweak in the commit message, replacing "terminated" with "bounded"; with that adjustment he considers the patch ready. Andy Koppe, whose earlier objections to the end-pointer design helped shape the current approach, plans to update his own related work to the new API.

What remains open is whether Dirk sends a v6 incorporating Junio's patch 1 requests, and whether the series then satisfies the remaining reviewers and proceeds toward integration.
