Dirk Gouders has posted version 3 of a four-patch series fixing buffer-bounds handling in Git's UTF-8 display-width code, specifically in `utf8_strnwidth()` and `utf8_width()`. The series addresses reads past the end of input buffers, including a mmapped-region SIGBUS edge case, and corrects misleading documentation. Patch 1, authored by Junio C Hamano, establishes an end-pointer API `display_mode_esc_sequence_len(const char *s, const char *end)` that eliminates a quadratic `strlen()` performance issue previously flagged by Andy Koppe. Patch 4 completes the bounds fix by passing an explicit remainder to `utf8_width()` and introduces `display_mode_esc_sequence_len_all()` to greedily consume consecutive ANSI SGR sequences, with new unit tests covering CJK truncation and greedy ANSI handling. All substantive technical questions are resolved around this end-pointer design, surface nits from earlier reviews are addressed, and the series remains under review with no v4 posted yet.
