Dirk Gouders has posted v4 of a five-patch series fixing bounds handling in Git's UTF-8 display-width code, replacing the retracted v3. The series targets `utf8_strnwidth()` and `utf8_width()` in `utf8.c`, ensuring they respect their length arguments and never read past the end of the buffer, including the mmapped-region SIGBUS edge case. Patch 1, authored by Junio C Hamano, introduces the end-pointer variant `display_mode_esc_sequence_len()`; patch 2 corrects the misleading documentation; patch 3 adds a guard in `utf8_strnwidth()` to break out of the loop when ANSI escape skipping consumes the entire remaining bounded string, preventing reads past the `end` boundary; patches 4 and 5 split the former v3 patch 4 to add `remainder`-based boundary fixes and the new `display_mode_esc_sequence_len_all()` helper. The series includes unit tests in `t/unit-tests/u-utf8-ansi.c` and `u-utf8-width.c`. Andy Koppe, who previously raised the performance objection that led to the end-pointer design, has concurred and plans to update his own code to the new API. The series remains under review with no integration status yet.
