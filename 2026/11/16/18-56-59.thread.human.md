Dirk Gouders has reposted the UTF-8 display-width bounds-fix series as v4, a five-patch respin of the withdrawn v3 that keeps the end-pointer design for `display_mode_esc_sequence_len()` while splitting the former fourth patch into two for clarity. The series fixes out-of-bounds reads in `utf8_strnwidth()` and `utf8_width()` when length arguments chop through multibyte characters or ANSI escape sequences, corrects misleading documentation, and consolidates three manual ANSI-skipping loops into a new greedy helper `display_mode_esc_sequence_len_all()`. Patch 1, authored by Junio C Hamano, introduces the bounded scanning primitive; the remaining patches add unit tests in `t/unit-tests/u-utf8-ansi.c` and `u-utf8-width.c` covering truncated sequences and chopped CJK characters.

The latest exchange closes a small API design question: Andy Koppe had argued that adding an optional `size_t *remainder` out-parameter to the scanning function was unnecessary because callers can simply pass `SIZE_MAX` as the bound and derive any remainder themselves. Junio C Hamano has now explicitly endorsed that simpler approach, confirming the end-pointer signature should stand without the extra parameter. The series remains under review with no integration-branch status reported.
