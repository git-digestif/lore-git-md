This thread concerns a four-patch v2 series from Dirk Gouders fixing bounds handling in Git's UTF-8 display-width functions, `utf8_strnwidth()` and `utf8_width()`, so they respect their length arguments and avoid reading past buffer ends -- including a potential SIGBUS on mmapped regions. The series also corrects misleading documentation on `utf8_strnwidth()`. Patch 1, authored by Junio C Hamano, introduces a counted variant `display_mode_esc_sequence_len_counted()` that scans ANSI escape sequences without reading beyond the given length; patches 3 and 4 apply this to the width functions with post-loop guards.

The main design question -- whether to keep a NUL-terminated wrapper around the counted variant -- is now resolved. Andy Koppe had objected that the wrapper's `strlen()`-based scanning makes repeated calls quadratic, and Dirk Gouders has confirmed he will drop the wrapper entirely and switch its sole remaining caller in `pretty.c` to the counted variant directly, which is exactly what Junio's v1 already did. Koppe has concurred, and also suggested that the `_counted` suffix could then be dropped by folding the length parameter into the original function. Patch 4 has been approved by Junio with no changes requested, and patches 3 and 4 have no remaining technical objections.

Only surface-level items remain before a possible v3: fixing the alphabetical ordering of `u-utf8-ansi.c` in `t/meson.build`, removing the inaccurate "null-terminated" phrasing from patch 2's comment block, and two minor formatting nits from Junio on patch 3. The series remains under review, with the technical direction for all four patches now settled.
