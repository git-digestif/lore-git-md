Dirk Gouders has reposted the UTF-8 display-width fix as v4, a five-patch series aimed at making `utf8_strnwidth()` and `utf8_width()` respect their length arguments and avoid reading past buffer ends, including the mmapped-region SIGBUS case, plus correcting the misleading `utf8_strnwidth()` documentation. The series carries forward the end-pointer design settled in v3, with the old patch 4 split into two: one fixing `utf8_strnwidth()` boundary handling and another introducing a greedy `display_mode_esc_sequence_len_all()` helper. No version has been merged or queued in any integration branch.

Review is still active with concrete blockers. Andy Koppe previously flagged that patch 4/5 as posted fails to build due to an undeclared `skip` variable, and questioned whether the new `_all()` helper in patch 5 is necessary, suggesting the series narrow the scope to the comment fix, the bounded `display_mode_esc_sequence_len()`, and the in-loop end guard. In his latest note, Koppe also pointed out two test-only nits in the new `u-utf8-ansi.c` unit tests for patch 1: comparing pointers to distinct string literals (technically undefined behavior) and using `sizeof` on array parameters that decay to pointers, which yields the pointer size rather than the string length. These do not affect the core logic but should be corrected before the series advances.
