This thread tracks a standalone patch in the ongoing effort to remove `the_repository` from Git's codebase, authored by Srujith A Poojary. The first version replaced the global with `istate->repo` in `overlay_tree_on_index()` within `read-cache.c`; the second version, now under review, expands the cleanup to also cover `add_to_index()` and `refresh_index()`, swapping the global for the repository pointer already available through the `index_state` parameter in calls to functions like `repo_ignore_case()`, `repo_get_oid()`, and `read_tree()`. The change remains purely mechanical with no behavioral difference and no new tests.

Junio C Hamano reviewed the initial patch and, while offering cautious approval, questioned whether future cleanups in `read-cache.c` should batch related functions together rather than proceed one at a time. Version 2 appears to respond directly to that feedback by grouping the three index-operation functions into a single commit. Derrick Stolee also reviewed with a brief LGTM. The series remains under review with no merge status indicated.

The broader architectural question Junio raised remains open: while higher-level functions that already receive an `index_state` can be converted mechanically, lower-level helpers in the file lack both `istate` and `repo` parameters, making a comprehensive removal of `the_repository` from `read-cache.c` a non-trivial design challenge that has yet to be resolved.
