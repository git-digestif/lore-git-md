This is a **substantive review** of the second patch in Harald Nordgren’s `v5` bugfix series for `git imap-send`. The patch introduces `has_required_headers()`, a helper function that scans email headers in any order and now correctly handles both LF (`\n\n`) and CRLF (`\r\n`) line endings to comply with RFC 5322 and IMAP’s RFC 2060. The series as a whole is ready for re-integration into `next` after addressing a latent CRLF bug in this patch, and Patrick Steinhardt’s feedback here is the final technical hurdle.

Patrick’s review identifies a subtle readability issue in the line-iteration logic of `has_required_headers()`. The current implementation increments `line` with `*++line` and then checks for `"\r\n"`, which he suggests could be clearer if rewritten to check for `"\n\n"` and `"\n\r\n"` directly before incrementing. His proposed alternative avoids the pre-increment and makes the termination conditions more explicit. This is a **surface-level improvement**—the logic is functionally correct, but the code’s intent would be easier to follow with the suggested change.

The rest of the patch is uncontroversial. It replaces the rigid `strstr()`-based header detection with order-agnostic parsing, fixes the CRLF edge case that blocked `v4`, and includes thorough test coverage for both the new logic and the CRLF handling. The series has already been endorsed by Junio C Hamano and Patrick himself in earlier iterations, and this feedback is the last remaining action item before the patch can graduate to `next`. Harald’s response will likely be a straightforward revision to address the readability concern.
