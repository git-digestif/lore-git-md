Patrick Steinhardt has sent a twelve-patch series that moves commit-graph storage into the ODB backend layer, enabling future storage backends to control how commit graphs are read and written. The series is currently at v1 and under review, with Junio C Hamano having provided substantive feedback on the first patch regarding error-handling conventions and a potential validation gap in the `struct commit_graph` oid field. In his latest reply, Patrick agrees to document the switch to the standard "0 means success" error convention in the commit message, and he proposes fixing the validation gap by adding an `oidcmp` check inside `load_commit_graph_chain_fd_st()` so that every layer's trailer hash is verified against the chain file.

Patrick notes that this proposed check is actually stricter than the previous behavior, which skipped verification of both the base and topmost layers, but he sees this as an improvement in rigor. However, the change breaks test t5324, which implicitly relied on the base layer not being validated, leading him to consider adding a separate preparatory commit to address this. He also wonders aloud whether the twelve-patch series has grown long enough to warrant splitting into two smaller series for easier review. A v2 addressing Junio's feedback is expected, with the core design direction of pluggable commit-graph callbacks remaining intact but validation and packaging details still open.
