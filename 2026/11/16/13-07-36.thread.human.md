This thread is Patrick Steinhardt’s 12-patch series introducing pluggable commit graphs as part of the ODB abstraction effort. The goal is to move commit-graph storage logic into the ODB backend layer, allowing alternative ODB implementations to control where and how commit graphs are read and written. The series is now at its penultimate step: the eleventh patch mechanically relocates all path-dependent logic from `commit-graph.c` into the "files" ODB backend (`odb/source-files.c`), a prerequisite for the final patch that will complete the abstraction.

The first ten patches were infrastructure-only refactors, isolating commit-graph handling and introducing pluggable callbacks (`read_commit_graph` and `write_commit_graph`) in the `struct odb_source` interface. The eleventh patch moves the remaining path helpers, file operations, and loading logic into the backend, leaving only temporary declarations in `commit-graph.h` for cleanup in the next patch. No behavior changes are introduced; the patch is purely preparatory, though its size (437 lines removed, 428 added) may attract review scrutiny.

The series remains under review, with no merge status reported. Open questions include whether the design is sufficiently flexible for backends that might avoid the commit-graph data structure entirely, and whether the depth-based merge strategy will generalize across all possible backends. Patrick has framed the series as a necessary step for ODB abstraction, with the current patch positioned as a mechanical refactor critical for later backend-specific work. The broader ODB abstraction effort, driven by Patrick and Justin Tobler, continues to progress, with this series as a foundational piece.
