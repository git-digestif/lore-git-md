This thread is Patrick Steinhardt’s 12-patch series introducing pluggable commit graphs as part of the broader ODB abstraction effort. The goal is to move commit-graph storage logic into the "files" ODB backend, enabling alternative ODB implementations to control where commit graphs are read from and written to. The series is under review, with the first six patches now posted; the sixth patch, just arrived, refactors the split commit-graph write path to eliminate redundant bookkeeping by deriving layer information directly from the commit-graph chain.

Patrick is the sole participant so far, and the series builds on his prerequisite branch `ps/commit-graph-write-refactor`. The first five patches laid the groundwork with mechanical refactors: simplifying chain-linking logic, refactoring the internal API, exposing validation logic, and introducing depth-based layer selection for the split commit-graph merge strategy. The sixth patch continues this work by removing redundant arrays in `struct write_commit_graph_context` and replacing them with local variables and string lists populated by walking the commit-graph chain. This change is purely internal, with no user-facing or performance impact, but it reduces the API surface area and prepares the codebase for backend-specific logic in later patches.

Key technical details include the removal of five fields from `struct write_commit_graph_context`, the use of `string_list` for tracking kept filenames and chain hashes, and a behavioral change where `expire_commit_graphs()` is now called only on success. The patch is well-structured and unlikely to be controversial, though reviewers may ask for additional comments or encapsulation. Open questions from earlier patches remain, particularly around the flexibility of the depth-based merge strategy and whether the commit-graph data structure itself should be abstracted further. No merge status has been reported yet.
