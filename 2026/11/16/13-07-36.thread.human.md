This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s 12-patch series introducing pluggable commit graphs as a foundational step in Git’s ODB abstraction effort. The series refactors commit-graph storage logic to allow alternative ODB backends to control how commit graphs are read and written, with no user-facing changes in this iteration. All patches are infrastructure-only and currently under review in their initial (v1) submission.

Junio C Hamano has begun reviewing the series with a detailed look at the first patch. His feedback focuses on technical clarity and correctness: he notes the switch to a standard error-return convention, questions the continued necessity of the `oid` field in `struct commit_graph` after its validation is removed, and praises the shift to an unsigned type for layer counting. While his review doesn’t challenge the series’ direction, it raises points that may require adjustments in a v2, particularly around error handling and validation. No other reviews have appeared yet, so the discussion remains open. The series depends on Patrick’s earlier `ps/commit-graph-write-refactor` branch and is part of the broader ODB abstraction work.
