This is the second patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s v2 series improving performance and memory usage of `git repo structure`. It targets `builtin/repo.c` and avoids fully parsing tree objects when only counting their entries, yielding a further 20 % wall-time improvement and an 80 % peak-memory reduction (5.1× less) on top of the gains from patch 1. The change is purely internal: no CLI options, config keys, or on-disk formats are touched. Benchmarks by both the author and Kristofer Karlsson confirm the combined effect; the series is now technically complete and ready for maintainer pickup.
