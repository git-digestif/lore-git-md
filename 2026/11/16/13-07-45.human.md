This is the ninth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 12-patch series introducing **pluggable commit graphs** as part of the **ODB abstraction** effort. The patch marks incomplete commit-graph chains directly in the `struct commit_graph` instead of passing an `incomplete_chain` flag through an out-parameter. This is a small but important refactor to prepare for the upcoming pluggable interface, where exposing such a flag would feel awkward.

The change is straightforward: the `load_commit_graph_chain_fd_st()` function now sets a new `incomplete` boolean field in the commit-graph structure, and callers (currently only `git commit-graph verify`) check this field instead of the out-parameter. The patch touches three files (`builtin/commit-graph.c`, `commit-graph.c`, and `commit-graph.h`) and removes a dozen lines while adding a single field to the struct. No behavior changes—just a cleaner internal API.

This is a well-motivated cleanup that aligns with the series’ goal of hiding backend-specific details behind a pluggable interface. The commit message clearly explains why the out-parameter is problematic for future ODB backends, and the implementation is mechanical and safe. No controversy expected; the patch looks like a clear improvement.
