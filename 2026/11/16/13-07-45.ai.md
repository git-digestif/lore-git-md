This is the ninth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 12-patch series introducing **pluggable commit graphs** for the ODB abstraction effort. It shifts the tracking of incomplete commit-graph chains from an out-parameter (`incomplete_chain`) to a new boolean field (`incomplete`) in the `struct commit_graph` itself. The change is purely internal and preparatory: it paves the way for the next patch, which will hide `load_commit_graph_chain_fd_st()` behind a pluggable interface, where passing an out-parameter would be awkward.

The patch touches three files: `builtin/commit-graph.c` (removes the local `incomplete_chain` variable and checks the new `graph->incomplete` field instead), `commit-graph.c` (drops the `incomplete_chain` parameter from `load_commit_graph_chain_fd_st()` and sets the `incomplete` field directly), and `commit-graph.h` (adds the new field). The diff is small (10 insertions, 12 deletions) and mechanical, with no behavior change beyond the internal representation.

This is a clean, low-risk refactor that aligns with the series’ goal of isolating backend-specific logic. The motivation is clearly stated in the commit message, and the change is narrowly scoped. No new tests are added, but the existing `git commit-graph verify` logic continues to warn about incomplete chains as before. The patch is well-positioned for review as part of the broader ODB abstraction effort.
