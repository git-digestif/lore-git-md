This is a reply in the ongoing thread about Jasper Mayone’s five-patch series adding filtering capabilities to `git worktree list` and related safety checks. The series is now at v5, addressing all prior feedback except Junio’s latest concerns about the final two patches and some refactoring suggestions.

This specific email responds to Junio’s forward-looking design questions about the first patch, which adds a safety check to `git worktree remove` to prevent deletion of worktrees in the middle of a rebase, bisect, cherry-pick, revert, or `git am` operation. Jasper clarifies that the new `check_no_operation_in_progress()` helper and the existing `die_if_checked_out()` protection serve different purposes and should remain separate for now. The former checks whether removing a worktree would lose state (including detached HEAD rebases and `git am`), while the latter prevents checking out the same branch in multiple worktrees. They overlap only in the case of a rebase or bisect on a named branch, where both refuse the operation.

Jasper acknowledges the potential for a reusable helper in `worktree.c` that could report the operation a worktree is in (and the branch, if any) and agrees this would be a good follow-up. He proposes doing this in a separate series after the current one lands, which aligns with Junio’s `#leftoverbits` tag marking it as future work. The tone is collaborative and pragmatic, focusing on incremental improvement rather than overhauling the design in this series.

The patch itself remains uncontroversial and ready for integration. The discussion here is about future maintainability, not current correctness. If you’re following the series for its user-facing features, this email doesn’t change the status of the safety check—it’s still a clear improvement and likely to land as-is. If you’re interested in the longer-term refactoring of worktree safety checks, this exchange sketches the direction but doesn’t block the current work.
