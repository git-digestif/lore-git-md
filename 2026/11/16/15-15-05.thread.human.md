This thread is Junio C Hamano's November 2026 "What's cooking in git.git" report, the authoritative snapshot of every in-flight topic in his integration branches. A batch of topics has graduated to `master` for the next release, including Christian Couder's unified early-scan option parsing, fixes to large-blob handling on 32-bit platforms, CI repairs for Ubuntu 26.04, and Patrick Steinhardt's `git stash` optimization. Once Steinhardt's review of the HTTP retry series lands, that topic becomes the next likely candidate for graduation to `next`.
