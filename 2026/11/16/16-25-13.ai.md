Junio engages directly with the patch's core design choice: whether runtime detection of active colors (and their replay after line breaks) is the right approach.

Junio's initial skepticism centers on whether custom format authors should bear responsibility for managing line-break color resets themselves. This gap justifies the patch's runtime approach, which mimics how default formats already handle colors.

The review also surfaces a minor tension in the problem statement: color loss at line breaks (first issue) versus color leakage into subsequent content (second issue). Junio notes the apparent contradiction but does not press it further; the patch's solution (resetting before breaks, replaying after) addresses both without conflict.
