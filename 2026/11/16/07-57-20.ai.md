This is a follow-up in the now-merged `ps/builtin-rerere-subcommands` series, addressing the UI regression Junio identified: the new subcommand-based parser rejects `git rerere --skip-locked gc` (old syntax) while accepting `git rerere gc --skip-locked` (new syntax). Patrick acknowledges the regression and frames it as a deliberate trade-off to fix what he considers a design flaw—accepting options that are ignored for most subcommands. He notes that `--skip-locked` is newly introduced and unlikely to have users, making now the right time to enforce option ordering. However, `--rerere-autoupdate` has existed for years, so if the breakage is deemed too risky, he proposes grandfathering that flag to retain the old behavior. The tone suggests he is open to revisiting the design but expects pushback on the principle rather than the mechanics. No patch is attached; this is a discussion of whether and how to mitigate the regression.
