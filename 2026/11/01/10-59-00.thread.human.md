This thread is about a two-patch series from Pushkar Singh that improves observability and consistency in `git maintenance run` lock handling. The series adds a Trace2 event for `--auto` skips due to lock conflicts and aligns manual invocations with `git gc`’s fail-fast behavior when the lock cannot be acquired.

The v2 series, posted in response to Patrick Steinhardt’s initial review, is now under review again after Patrick provided a substantive follow-up. His latest feedback focuses on code organization and clarity: he suggests splitting the second patch into two (one for the behavioral change, one for the Trace2 event) and reducing code duplication in error-message formatting. He also prefers using `strerror(err)` directly in the Trace2 event’s `reason` field rather than a ternary operator that hard-codes `EEXIST`. These are mechanical refinements; the core functionality remains uncontroversial and fully addresses the latent lock-handling inconsistency Patrick flagged earlier.

The series touches `builtin/gc.c` and `t/t7900-maintenance.sh`, introducing a new Trace2 event (`"lock/skip": {"reason": "...", "errno": <code>}`) and making manual `git maintenance run` fail with exit code 128 when the lock is held, matching `git gc`. Tests verify both the Trace2 event and the new exit-code behavior. No design objections or alternative proposals have surfaced, and the series is on track for merging once the refinements are incorporated.
