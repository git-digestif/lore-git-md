Pushkar Singh has posted the second half of his v4 two-patch series on `git maintenance` lock handling. The series tackles consistency and observability when `git maintenance run` cannot acquire its lock: patch 1/2 makes scheduled background runs skip silently instead of failing, and stops prefetch child fetches from triggering nested maintenance or spurious warnings via `--no-auto-maintenance`; patch 2/2, just arrived, adds a Trace2 `"lock/skip"` data event under the `"maintenance"` category for `--auto` and `--schedule` invocations so skipped runs leave a machine-readable record even under `--quiet`. Manual runs still fail fast with exit code 128 and deliberately emit no Trace2 event.

Patrick Steinhardt drove earlier iterations with substantive review requests that have been incorporated through v4, and Aniket Gupta reported the v3 regression that prompted the current `opts->schedule` fix, but neither has yet confirmed v4 or offered a Reviewed-by or Acked-by, so the series remains under review. Whether the schedule branch fully resolves the background-run regression and whether the Trace2 coverage for scheduled runs matches maintainer expectations are the open questions awaiting reviewer feedback.
