This thread is about improving the observability and consistency of `git maintenance run` when it encounters lock conflicts. Pushkar Singh sent a two-patch series (v2) that adds a Trace2 event for `--auto` skips due to existing locks and makes manual invocations fail fast—matching `git gc`—when the lock can't be acquired. The design question of whether `--auto` and manual modes should handle locks differently has been resolved in favor of consistency.

Patrick Steinhardt reviewed the series and suggested mechanical refinements: splitting the patch into two (behavioral change and Trace2 event), simplifying the Trace2 event format to use `strerror(err)` unconditionally, and deduplicating error-message formatting. Pushkar has agreed to these changes and plans to send a v3 after giving other reviewers time to comment. The series remains uncontroversial and under review, with no maintainer decisions or merge status reported yet. The key technical changes involve `builtin/gc.c` and the test file `t/t7900-maintenance.sh`, with no on-disk format changes.
