This thread is a two-patch series from Pushkar Singh, now at v4, that makes `git maintenance run` handle lock contention consistently with `git gc` while adding Trace2 observability. The series touches `builtin/gc.c` and `t/t7900-maintenance.sh`. When the maintenance lock at `objects/maintenance.lock` cannot be acquired, the patch establishes three distinct behaviors: manual invocations fail with a non-zero exit and an error via the shared `unable_to_lock_message()` helper (with `--quiet` suppressing the message but still failing), `--auto` warns and exits 0, and scheduled background runs initiated by `git maintenance start` stay completely silent and exit 0 through a new `opts->schedule` check. Separately, `fetch_remote()` now passes `--no-auto-maintenance` to its child `git fetch` processes so prefetch does not trigger nested automatic maintenance or emit spurious lock warnings while the parent holds the lock. The tests in `t/t7900-maintenance.sh` cover all four lock-failure scenarios plus the new fetch flag and quiet-mode behavior.

The v4 iteration exists because Aniket Gupta identified a regression in v3: background scheduled runs were incorrectly failing when another maintenance process held the lock. Pushkar posted v4 directly addressing that report, and the key open question is whether reviewers -- especially Aniket -- confirm the `opts->schedule` branch and the `--no-auto-maintenance` fix fully resolve it. Patrick Steinhardt reviewed earlier versions substantively and his requested refinements were incorporated, but no Reviewed-by or Acked-by from an established reviewer has been given yet, so the series remains under review. The second patch adds a Trace2 `"lock/skip"` event under the `"maintenance"` category for `--auto` and `--schedule` modes; whether that coverage meets maintainer expectations is also still open.
