This thread is about a two-patch series from Pushkar Singh that improves observability and consistency in `git maintenance run` lock handling. The series adds a Trace2 event for `--auto` skips due to lock conflicts and aligns manual invocations with `git gc`’s fail-fast behavior when the lock cannot be acquired. After two rounds of review and mechanical refinements, the series was considered uncontroversial and ready for final review.

However, Aniket Gupta’s recent review reveals a significant regression: the patch causes scheduled background maintenance runs (started by `git maintenance start`) to fail with an error exit code instead of silently skipping, breaking documented behavior. Additionally, manual prefetch tasks now emit spurious lock warnings due to stricter lock-checking logic being inherited by internal `git fetch` calls. These issues challenge the prior consensus that `--auto` should skip silently while manual runs should fail, as the patch appears to have over-applied the fail-fast logic to background runs. The author will need to clarify whether background runs are treated as `--auto` or manual invocations and adjust the lock-checking logic to restore the expected behavior. The series remains under review, with no maintainer decision yet.
