This is the third patch in Andy Koppe’s five-part documentation series (v2) that aligns Git’s terminology around "pseudorefs" and "root refs" following the 2024 terminology change. The patch updates six man pages to replace outdated uses of "pseudoref" with "root ref" and corrects related inaccuracies in reflog and per-worktree ref documentation. The changes are uncontroversial and address prior review feedback, though two minor issues remain unresolved.

The patch touches `git-update-ref(1)`, `git-worktree(1)`, `git-ls-remote(1)`, `for-each-ref-options(7)`, `rev-list-options(7)`, and `config/core.adoc`. Key updates include clarifying that `--refs` in `git-ls-remote` omits refs outside `refs/`, removing ORIG_HEAD from `git-update-ref(1)`’s reflog documentation, and correcting `core.logAllRefUpdates`’s scope to include root refs. The `git-worktree(1)` update now explicitly states that both root refs and pseudorefs are per-worktree and drops an outdated claim about their location under `$GIT_DIR`.

Patrick Steinhardt’s review of v1 highlighted two lingering issues in `git-update-ref(1)`: the phrasing of `core.logAllRefUpdates`’s logic remains ambiguous, and the reflog storage explanation is outdated for the reftable backend. Andy acknowledges these but considers them out of scope for this series. The `git-worktree(1)` prose list of per-worktree ref exceptions is now converted to an itemized AsciiDoc list in v2, addressing Patrick’s editorial feedback. No new technical objections have been raised, and the patch appears ready for merging once the remaining nits are either resolved or deferred.
