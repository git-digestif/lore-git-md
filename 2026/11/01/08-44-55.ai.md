This is a two-patch bugfix series targeting the `grep` and `diff` subsystems on 64-bit Windows (and any platform where `long` is 32 bits but `size_t` is 64 bits). The patches address silent truncation of file sizes at 4 GiB, which caused incorrect results in `git grep` and misleading diffs in `git diff`.

Patch 1/2 changes `git grep` to use `size_t` for buffer sizes, ensuring it correctly searches files larger than 4 GiB in the working tree, with `--no-index`, and in blobs. The commit message confirms the fix was verified by simulating truncation at 16 bits.

Patch 2/2 makes `git diff` die with a clear error when encountering files of 4 GiB or larger in the working tree, textconv, `--no-index`, or combine-diff. This matches the existing behavior for blobs and prevents the generation of misleading patches that would truncate files when applied. The change affects `combine-diff.c`, `diff-no-index.c`, and `diff.c`.

The series is self-contained, merges cleanly with current `master`, and does not depend on other in-flight work. No tests are included due to the impracticality of generating 4 GiB inputs, but the commit messages detail the verification method. The remaining `-Wshorten-64-to-32` warnings in `diff` and `grep` are either unreachable or in xdiff internals, where input sizes are already capped at 1023 MiB. Widening `diff_filespec.size` to `size_t` for full 4+ GiB support is acknowledged as a larger effort left for later.
