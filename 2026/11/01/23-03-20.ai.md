This is the second patch in Andy Koppe’s v2 series updating Git’s documentation and terminology to align with the 2024 redefinition of "pseudoref" (now only FETCH_HEAD and MERGE_HEAD) and the introduction of "root ref" (HEAD, ORIG_HEAD, etc.). This patch specifically updates the glossary entries for "per-worktree ref" and "worktree" to reflect the current reality: all root refs, refs under `refs/worktree/` and `refs/rewritten/`, and pseudorefs are per-worktree, not just HEAD and bisect refs as the old entry claimed.

The change replaces the outdated prose list of per-worktree refs with an itemized AsciiDoc list for clarity and future extensibility, and it cross-references `git-worktree(1)` for how to access per-worktree refs of other worktrees. The "worktree" entry is also updated to describe HEAD as a per-worktree ref and MERGE_HEAD as a pseudoref, removing the redundant separate listing. The patch is purely textual and introduces no behavioral changes.

This patch fully addresses Patrick Steinhardt’s feedback from v1, which suggested using itemized lists and simplifying the "worktree" entry. The result is a clearer, more accurate glossary that matches the codebase’s current behavior and the terminology established by the 2024 change. No open questions or loose ends remain for this patch.
