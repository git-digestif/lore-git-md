This is a maintainer review in the `git describe` commit-distance bugfix thread. Junio C Hamano responds to Tuomas Ahola’s proposed test rewrite, identifying a technical flaw and critiquing the rewrite’s clarity.

The review is substantive but narrowly scoped: Junio points out that `test_config` inside a subshell will fail to register a clean-up action, which could leave the test environment dirty. More importantly, he argues that the original test’s explicit hard-coded dates are superior for this specific case because they make the relative ordering of commits immediately visible in the source. The rewritten version, which manipulates `$test_tick`, obscures the exact skew (3600 seconds) and forces the reader to reason about `test_commit --annotate` internals to determine whether the skew is 3599, 3600, or 3601 seconds. Junio concludes that the rewrite is counterproductive for a test whose sole purpose is to verify behavior under timestamp skew.

No new technical concerns about the core patch are raised; the discussion remains confined to test readability and correctness. The thread’s status is unchanged: Linus’s one-line fix is still under review, and no maintainer decision has been made.
