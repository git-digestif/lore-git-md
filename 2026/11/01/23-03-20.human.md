This is the second patch in Andy Koppe’s v2 series to align Git’s documentation with the 2024 terminology change that split "pseudorefs" (now only FETCH_HEAD and MERGE_HEAD) from "root refs" (HEAD, ORIG_HEAD, etc.). This patch updates the glossary’s "per-worktree ref" and "worktree" entries to reflect the current reality: all root refs, refs under `refs/worktree/` and `refs/rewritten/`, and pseudorefs are per-worktree, not just HEAD and bisect refs as the old text claimed.

The change is a straightforward documentation fix. The "per-worktree ref" entry now lists the three ref prefixes (`refs/bisect/`, `refs/rewritten/`, `refs/worktree/`) in an itemized AsciiDoc list for clarity and future extensibility, and explicitly states that root refs and pseudorefs are also per-worktree. The "worktree" entry is simplified to avoid listing examples (HEAD, MERGE_HEAD) and instead cross-references the "per-worktree ref" section. Both entries now correctly describe HEAD as a per-worktree ref and MERGE_HEAD as a pseudoref.

The patch addresses all of Patrick Steinhardt’s v1 feedback on these entries—his suggestions for itemized lists and a more concise "worktree" entry are fully incorporated. No behavioral changes or new test cases are introduced; this is purely about accuracy and readability. The update looks ready for merging alongside the rest of the series.
