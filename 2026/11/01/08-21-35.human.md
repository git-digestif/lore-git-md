This is the second patch in Harald Nordgren’s four-patch bugfix series for `git imap-send`, now in its fifth iteration (`v5`). The patch makes the header-parsing logic order-agnostic, allowing `Subject` to appear before `Date` (or `From`) in email headers, which is valid per RFC 5322 but previously caused `git imap-send` to reject messages produced by tools like `b4`. The `v5` update specifically fixes a latent CRLF line-ending bug in the helper function `has_required_headers()`, ensuring it correctly detects the end of the header block in both LF and CRLF mboxes.

The change replaces the original rigid `strstr()`-based scanning with a new helper function that scans the header block line-by-line until it hits the first empty line (either `\n\n` or `\r\n`), checking for the presence of `From:`, `Date:`, and `Subject:` headers in any order. The test suite is expanded with two new cases: one verifying that a real-world `b4`-style mbox (with `Subject` before `Date`) is accepted, and another ensuring that header-like text in the message body of a CRLF mbox is ignored. The patch touches only `imap-send.c` and `t/t9001-send-email.sh`, with no on-disk format changes.

This is a targeted, well-tested fix that directly addresses the interoperability issue with `b4` and similar tools. The CRLF edge-case fix in `v5` resolves the blocking issue identified by Junio after the series was merged to `next`, and the patch is explicitly endorsed by Patrick Steinhardt. The series as a whole is uncontroversial and appears ready for re-integration into `next`.
