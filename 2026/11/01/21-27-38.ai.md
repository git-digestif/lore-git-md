This is a process-oriented reply from Junio C Hamano in the ongoing Rust reorganization thread. The patch series (v5) to move Git's Rust infrastructure from `src/` to a dedicated `rust/` subdirectory has been explicitly put on notice: Junio states that unless someone else advocates for it, the patch will likely be dropped. This follows his earlier queuing of the patch in `next` and reflects his current effort to reduce the number of in-flight topics. The decision is framed as a response to the lack of broader project enthusiasm and the unresolved forward-compatibility discussion around Git 3.0's mandatory Rust components, rather than any technical deficiency in the patch itself. Mike Hommey's reaffirmation of personal interest is acknowledged but deemed insufficient to justify retention. No new technical feedback is offered; this email is purely about the patch's uncertain future.
