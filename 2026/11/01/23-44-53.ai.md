This is the **reply to a substantive review** of the third patch in Andy Koppe’s five-part documentation series aligning Git’s terminology around "pseudorefs" and "root refs." The patch updates man pages (`git-update-ref.1` and `git-worktree.1`) to replace outdated uses of "pseudoref" with "root ref."

Andy acknowledges Patrick Steinhardt’s feedback on two issues in `git-update-ref.1`. First, the LOGGING UPDATES section’s explanation of `core.logAllRefUpdates` is ambiguous and outdated, particularly for the reftable backend (which doesn’t store reflogs in `$GIT_DIR/logs/<ref>`). Andy agrees the section is outdated but considers it out of scope for this series, deferring broader documentation updates to a future effort. Second, Andy accepts Patrick’s suggestion to convert the prose list of per-worktree ref exceptions in `git-worktree.1` into a bulleted AsciiDoc list, noting this was already implemented in v2 of the series.

The reply does not address Patrick’s suggestion to replace the detailed `core.logAllRefUpdates` explanation with a cross-reference to `git-config(1)`, leaving that issue unresolved. The patch’s core goal—terminology alignment—remains uncontroversial, but the unresolved documentation nits may require a follow-up or a v3 if Junio or Patrick consider them blocking.
