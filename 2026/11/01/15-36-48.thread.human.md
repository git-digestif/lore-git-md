This thread is Elia Pinto's v2 series of four patches fixing silent integer truncation in Git when handling files and buffers larger than 2--4 GiB. The series widens 32-bit size and line-count types to `size_t` or `uintmax_t` across packfile writing (`hashwrite()`), the complete-rewrite diff path, diffstat totals and `scale_linear()` in `diff.c`, and rename detection (`hash_chars()`). Version 2 expands on v1's three patches, incorporating Phillip Wood's review feedback by propagating `size_t` through the entire complete-rewrite path, and adds a new fourth patch for diffstat overflow that became reachable once files can legitimately exceed 2^31 lines. The series remains under review on master at 50fe5cc63f with no merge status reported.

So far the only substantive review came from Phillip Wood on v1, whose identified flaw has been addressed in v2. Since then, Torsten Bögershausen sent a brief "looks good to me" on patch 4/4, noting the test passed on macOS -- a surface-level approval with no technical engagement. Patches 1/4, 3/4, and 4/4 still await substantive technical review, particularly around `hashwrite()`'s block-based writing, `hash_chars()`'s similarity scoring, and whether `apply.c`'s continued use of `int` for its own stat totals is acceptable. Test coverage added in v2 includes EXPENSIVE tests in `t/t4022-diff-rewrite.sh` and a cheaper `scale_linear()` case in `t/t4052-stat-output.sh`; the multi-gigabyte failure modes were verified via 16-bit truncation simulation rather than committed tests.
