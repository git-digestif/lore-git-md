This thread is a bugfix series from Elia Pinto addressing silent integer truncation in Git when handling files larger than 2–4 GiB. The three patches target `hashwrite()` (packfile generation), `count_lines()` (`git diff -B --stat`), and `hash_chars()` (rename detection), changing function signatures from 32-bit types to `size_t` to prevent corruption.

Phillip Wood’s review has uncovered a critical flaw in the second patch: while the `count_lines()` parameter was widened to `size_t`, the return type and internal line counter remain `int`. This creates a new overflow risk for files with more than `INT_MAX` lines (about 2 billion), causing incorrect diffstat results even for files smaller than 2 GiB. Phillip’s feedback highlights that the series’ mechanical approach—changing parameter types without analyzing downstream effects—can introduce new bugs, and he suggests the patch needs rework, though no specific alternative has been proposed yet.

The other two patches (`hashwrite()` and `hash_chars()`) remain unreviewed, and no test cases are included due to the impracticality of generating multi-gigabyte test inputs. The series is still under review, with no merge status reported, and the identified flaw casts doubt on its readiness. Elia has documented the failure modes (packfile corruption, incorrect diffstats, and missed renames) but has not yet responded to Phillip’s critique.
