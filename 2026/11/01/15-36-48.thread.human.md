This thread is Elia Pinto's v3 four-patch series fixing silent integer truncation in Git's large-file handling, covering files and buffers larger than 2--4 GiB. The series widens 32-bit size and line-count types to `size_t`/`uintmax_t` across the complete-rewrite diff paths in `diff.c`, rename detection in `diffcore-delta.c`, and diffstat accumulation and `scale_linear()` scaling, while fixing packfile generation by chunking `hashwrite()` calls at the caller level in `builtin/pack-objects.c` and `builtin/fast-import.c` rather than widening the function's signature. The latest email in the thread is patch 4/4 itself, which carries the diffstat/graph-scaling fixes plus a cheap new test in `t/t4052-stat-output.sh` and a `Tested-by` from Torsten Bögershausen on macOS.

The series is under review at v3, based on master at 50fe5cc63f. v3 resolves all substantive concerns raised on v2: Junio C Hamano's and brian m. carlson's preference for caller-side chunking of `hashwrite()` (which also keeps the Rust FFI bindings untouched), Junio's overflow concern about `struct spanhash.cnt` in rename detection, and his two commit-message questions on the diffstat patch (why a `uintmax_t` accumulator cannot overflow, and the `graph_width` capping behavior). Phillip Wood's earlier feedback on `count_lines()` was addressed in v2's patch 2/4 and carried forward unchanged, though he has not yet re-reviewed it.

What remains open is simply that final re-review of patch 2/4 and the maintainer's decision on the series as a whole; no merge status has been reported, and the patches that require multi-gigabyte inputs to exercise remain verified only via truncation simulation rather than committed tests.
