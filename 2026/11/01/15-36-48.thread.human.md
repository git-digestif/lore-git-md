This thread tracks Elia Pinto's v2 series of four patches fixing silent integer truncation in Git when handling files larger than 2--4 GiB, widening 32-bit types to `size_t` or `uintmax_t` across packfile writing, complete-rewrite diff paths, diffstat totals, and rename detection. The series remains under review with no merge status reported; v2 addresses Phillip Wood's earlier critique of v1 by propagating `size_t` through the entire rewrite path and adds a fourth patch covering diffstat and graph-scaling overflows that become reachable once the rewrite path is fixed.

Junio C Hamano has now provided substantive review on two of the four patches. On patch 1/4 (`hashwrite()`), he flagged that the Rust FFI bindings in `src/csum_file.rs` still declare the length parameter as `u32` and must be updated, and he questioned whether callers holding buffers over 4 GiB should instead loop `hashwrite()` in chunks rather than widening the parameter type; neither point is resolved. His review of patch 4/4 raises two new open questions: what happens when the widened `uintmax_t` accumulator itself eventually overflows, and why the behavioral change in `graph_width` assignment logic--capping `max_change` at the available width--is not documented in the commit message despite being more than a type change.

Patches 2/4 and 3/4 have not yet received substantive technical review, and Torsten Bögershausen offered only a surface-level "looks good" on patch 4/4. The series waits on the author to address Junio's questions on patches 1/4 and 4/4 and on reviewers to engage with the complete-rewrite and rename-detection fixes.
