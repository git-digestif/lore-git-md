This thread is a two-patch bugfix series from Elia Pinto addressing a long-standing issue on platforms where `long` is 32-bit but `size_t` is 64-bit (notably 64-bit Windows). The patches fix silent truncation or corruption of files 4GiB or larger in `git archive` (both tar and zip formats) and `git apply` by replacing `unsigned long` with `size_t` for file-size variables. The changes are mechanical and confined to `apply.c`, `archive-tar.c`, and `archive-zip.c`, with no behavior changes on platforms where `long` is already 64-bit.

The series is based on `master` and merges cleanly with other topics. While no new tests are included, the author verified the fixes by simulating truncation and notes that the existing `t5004` test (currently disabled on 32-bit `long` platforms) could now be enabled for 64-bit Windows with a `SIZE_T_IS_64BIT` guard. The patches address a real, user-visible issue without adding complexity, and the thread has seen no review activity yet—meaning the fixes are still awaiting substantive feedback.
