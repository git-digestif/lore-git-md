This thread is about a bugfix series for the Git credential-cache daemon, now at version 2, authored by Farid Zakaria. The series fixes a race condition where concurrent `git credential-cache store` commands could cause one daemon to unlink another’s socket, leading to lost credentials and orphaned processes—an issue that surfaces during parallel submodule fetches over HTTPS. The v2 series also addresses a pre-existing permission-handling bug in socket directory creation, ensuring user-specific directories are created with mode `0700` regardless of `core.sharedRepository` settings.

The series is under active review, with the core fixes—ignoring `EEXIST` from `mkdir()` and using `unix_ss_create()` for safe socket creation—confirmed as correct by Jeff King and Patrick Steinhardt. Junio C Hamano has raised an architectural concern about mixing `unix_ss_*` and `tempfile` APIs in the third patch, arguing it could risk socket removal by a dying daemon. The author has acknowledged this but not yet addressed it in the current iteration. The preparatory commit (patch 1) and documentation improvements (patch 3) have been well-received, with reviewers noting the clarity and correctness of the changes.

The series is narrowly scoped, well-tested, and addresses a real, user-visible issue. The only remaining open question is whether the author will adjust the socket management logic to avoid the `tempfile` API, aligning with Junio’s preference and the precedent set by `fsmonitor--daemon.c`. No maintainer decision has been recorded yet, but the uncontroversial parts of the series appear ready for integration once the architectural concern is resolved.
