This thread is about a bugfix series from Farid Zakaria that addresses a race condition in Git's credential-cache daemon, where concurrent commands could cause one daemon to unlink another’s socket, leading to lost credentials and orphaned processes. The issue particularly affects users with large repositories during parallel submodule fetches over HTTPS. The series also fixes a pre-existing permission-handling issue in socket directory creation.

The series is now at v2, with three patches: a preparatory commit fixing the permission issue, the core race-condition fixes (unchanged in logic but improved in documentation), and tests. While patches 1 and 2 are uncontroversial and confirmed correct by reviewers Jeff King and Patrick Steinhardt, Junio Hamano raised an architectural concern about patch 3—specifically, the mixing of `unix_ss_*` and `tempfile` APIs, which could risk socket removal by a dying daemon. Farid’s latest reply acknowledges this concern but notes the race might only occur during Git upgrades, leaving the decision on whether to address it in a v3 to Junio. The thread remains under review, with no maintainer decision yet on whether the current approach is sufficient or if further changes are needed.
