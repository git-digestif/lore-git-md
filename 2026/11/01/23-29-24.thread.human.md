This thread is a two-patch bugfix series from Farid Zakaria that fixes a race condition in Git’s credential-cache daemon, which can cause lost credentials and orphaned daemons during parallel submodule fetches over HTTPS. The issue surfaces in large repositories where concurrent `git credential-cache store` commands can lead to one daemon unlinking another’s socket.

The first patch (already summarized) handles a race in creating the socket directory by making the daemon ignore `EEXIST` from `mkdir()` and proceed with permission checks. The second patch, now under discussion, prevents socket hijacking by replacing direct socket creation with `unix_ss_create()`, a helper from the simple-ipc subsystem. This ensures that if a second daemon detects an existing socket, it exits cleanly, allowing its client to communicate with the running daemon instead. The patch also ensures sockets are registered for cleanup only after binding succeeds, preventing a losing daemon from removing the winner’s socket.

The series includes new tests in `t/t0301-credential-cache.sh` that exercise the race condition, verifying that credentials remain accessible after the fix.
