This is the first patch in v2 of Andy Koppe’s five-patch series to align Git’s documentation with the 2024 terminology change that narrowed "pseudoref" to FETCH_HEAD and MERGE_HEAD while introducing "root ref" for refs outside the `refs/` hierarchy. The patch adds a formal "root ref" glossary entry and clarifies the "pseudoref" entry to reflect the current definition, addressing feedback from Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt on v1.

The patch updates `Documentation/glossary-content.adoc` to define "root ref" as a ref located in the root of the ref hierarchy (e.g., HEAD, ORIG_HEAD), moving the naming rules and list of irregular root refs from the "ref" entry. It explicitly states that pseudorefs are *not* root refs and cross-references `gitrevisions(7)` for descriptions of commonly used root refs and pseudorefs. The "pseudoref" entry is reworded to no longer call them refs, adds `git-for-each-ref(1)` as an example of how they are not proper refs, and explains that using a pseudoref containing multiple object IDs yields the first one. The changes are mechanical and do not alter behavior, but they resolve inconsistencies introduced by the 2024 terminology change.

Feedback from v1 is fully addressed: Junio’s objection to omitting "different semantics" in the pseudoref definition is resolved by explicitly listing supported operations (direct reading via `git log`/`git diff`) and unsupported ones (no `git-update-ref` or `git-for-each-ref`). Patrick’s suggestion to clarify the glossary structure is implemented by splitting the "ref" entry and adding cross-references. The patch is uncontroversial and appears ready for merging, assuming no new objections arise. The glossary now provides a clear, accurate foundation for the rest of the series.
