Cynthia Uche has refined her Outreachy proposal to extend Git’s `early_scan_options()` API to more commands, building on Christian Couder’s `cc/early-scan-options` series (now at v4 in `seen`). The proposal targets `git column`, `git bisect`, `git check-ref-format`, and—time permitting—`git unpack-objects`, with a focus on eliminating ad-hoc argument parsing and enabling consistent option handling.

Key updates since the first draft include Cynthia’s merged microproject (fixing exit code suppression in `t2104`) and her active reviews of the `early-scan-options` series and related patches, demonstrating her engagement with the codebase. The proposal now reflects the latest state of dependencies: Fien Dora’s `t1402` tests for `check-ref-format` are in `next`, and Patrick Steinhardt’s `ps/rerere-subcommands` series is under review.

The most contentious points remain the "must be first" rule for `git column` and whether `git check-ref-format` should accept options after `<refname>`. Cynthia plans to address these through incremental conversions (e.g., starting with `bisect_terms()` before `bisect_start()`) and test-first development to document current behavior. The timeline is realistic, with overlapping weeks to accommodate review cycles, and the only blocker is the `cc/early-scan-options` series graduating to `next`. No objections or reviews have been posted yet, but the proposal is well-prepared for discussion.
