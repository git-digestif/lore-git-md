Elia Pinto has posted a two-patch series fixing silent 4 GiB truncation in `git grep` and `git diff` on 64-bit Windows and similar platforms where `long` is 32 bits but `size_t` is 64 bits, and has now sent a follow-up ping since the series has received no review comments. The first patch widens `grep_source.size` and related buffer-size fields to `size_t` so `git grep` correctly searches files larger than 4 GiB; the second patch leaves `diff_filespec.size` as `unsigned long` but adds `cast_size_t_to_ulong()` checks that make `git diff` die with a clear error when it encounters working-tree, textconv, or `--no-index` inputs of 4 GiB or more, matching the existing behavior for blobs and preventing the generation of patches that would corrupt files if applied.

The series remains at v1 and under review with no merge status reported; Pinto confirms it still applies cleanly to current `master` and merges with `seen`. The key open question, which Pinto explicitly asks reviewers to weigh in on, is whether the "die early" strategy in patch 2/2 is acceptable as an intermediate step, or whether the full widening of `diff_filespec.size` to `size_t` -- a larger change touching `diff.c`, `combine-diff.c`, and `diff-no-index.c` -- should be required instead. No design objections or alternative proposals have been raised yet, and no tests accompany the patches since generating 4 GiB inputs is impractical.
