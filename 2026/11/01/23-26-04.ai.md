This is the v2 update to Patch 2/5 in Andy Koppe’s five-patch series that aligns Git’s documentation with the 2024 terminology change distinguishing “pseudoref” (FETCH_HEAD, MERGE_HEAD) from “root ref” (HEAD, ORIG_HEAD, etc.). The patch updates the glossary entries for “per-worktree ref” and “worktree” to reflect the new terminology and address Patrick Steinhardt’s editorial feedback from v1.

The changes are purely presentational: the “per-worktree ref” entry now uses an itemized AsciiDoc list to enumerate root refs (`HEAD`, `ORIG_HEAD`, etc.), refs under `refs/bisect/`, `refs/worktree/`, and `refs/rewritten/`, and explicitly notes that pseudorefs are also worktree-specific. The “worktree” entry no longer lists examples (HEAD, MERGE_HEAD) and instead cross-references the “per-worktree ref” section, avoiding the awkward distinction between per-worktree refs and pseudorefs. The patch touches only `Documentation/glossary-content.adoc` and makes no behavioral changes.

All of Patrick’s feedback is incorporated: the prose list is now an itemized list, and the “worktree” entry is simplified. The patch is uncontroversial and appears ready for merging alongside the rest of the series. No new technical issues were raised in this iteration.
