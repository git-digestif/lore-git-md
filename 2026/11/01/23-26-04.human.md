This is the second patch in Andy Koppe’s v2 series updating Git’s glossary and documentation to align with the 2024 terminology change that distinguishes “pseudoref” (FETCH_HEAD, MERGE_HEAD) from “root ref” (HEAD, ORIG_HEAD, etc.). The patch focuses on the “per-worktree ref” and “worktree” glossary entries, incorporating Patrick Steinhardt’s editorial feedback from the v1 review.

The changes are purely presentational: the “per-worktree ref” entry now uses an itemized AsciiDoc list to enumerate the categories of per-worktree refs (root refs, refs/bisect/, refs/worktree/, refs/rewritten/), making the list easier to scan and extend. The “worktree” entry no longer lists specific examples (HEAD, MERGE_HEAD) and instead cross-references the “per-worktree ref” section, avoiding the awkward distinction between per-worktree refs and pseudorefs. The patch also clarifies that pseudorefs are worktree-specific, though they are not refs.

No technical objections remain; Patrick’s feedback was fully addressed. The patch is uncontroversial and appears ready for merging alongside the rest of the series. It touches only the glossary file (`Documentation/glossary-content.adoc`) and does not affect code or on-disk formats.
