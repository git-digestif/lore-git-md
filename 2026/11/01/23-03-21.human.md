This is a documentation patch, part 3 of 5 in a series that aligns Git’s terminology around "pseudorefs" and "root refs" with a 2024 redefinition. The series is under review and this patch updates six man pages to replace outdated uses of "pseudoref" (now reserved for FETCH_HEAD and MERGE_HEAD) with the correct term "root ref" for refs like HEAD and ORIG_HEAD.

The changes are mechanical but important for consistency. The patch corrects minor inaccuracies: `git-update-ref(1)` now correctly states that only HEAD (not ORIG_HEAD) gets a reflog by default, `git-ls-remote(1)` clarifies that `--refs` omits refs outside `refs/`, and `git-worktree(1)` drops a backend-specific description of root refs that does not hold for reftable. The `core.logAllRefUpdates` documentation is also fixed to include root refs, and `gitdatamodel.adoc` now explicitly distinguishes root refs from pseudorefs in its data-model guide.

The patch looks well-crafted and uncontroversial. It addresses all feedback from Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt on the terminology changes, though Patrick’s suggestions about ambiguous phrasing in `core.logAllRefUpdates` and outdated reflog storage explanations remain unaddressed. Those are minor editorial points that do not affect the core goal of the patch. The series as a whole appears ready for merging once the remaining loose ends are tied up.
