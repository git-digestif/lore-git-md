This is the fourth and final patch in Harald Nordgren’s **v5 bugfix series for `git imap-send`**, addressing RFC 5322 compliance by making header-name matching case-insensitive. The series as a whole fixes interoperability issues with tools like `b4` that produce non-canonical email headers (e.g., `Subject` before `Date` or mixed-case field names like `FROM:`).

This patch specifically replaces the exact-case string-matching calls (`starts_with`) in the `has_required_headers()` helper with case-insensitive versions (`istarts_with`). The change is minimal—just three lines in `imap-send.c`—and is accompanied by a new test in `t/t9001-send-email.sh` that verifies the fix works with lowercase headers (`from:`, `date:`, `subject:`). The test coverage is thorough and uncontroversial, mirroring the pattern already established in earlier patches for order-agnostic parsing and CRLF edge cases.

The case-insensitive matching is a straightforward compliance fix with no backward-compatibility concerns, and the test ensures the change works as intended.
