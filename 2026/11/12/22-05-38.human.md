This is the second patch in Andy Koppe’s five-part documentation series (v4) that aligns Git’s terminology around "pseudoref" and "root ref" with the 2024 redefinition. This specific patch updates the glossary’s "per-worktree ref" entry to reflect the current reality: all root refs (HEAD, ORIG_HEAD, etc.), refs under `refs/worktree/`, and refs under `refs/rewritten/` are per-worktree, not just HEAD and `refs/bisect/` as the old text claimed. The entry now explicitly lists every per-worktree ref prefix, states that the list is exhaustive and will not be extended, and directs future additions to `refs/worktree/`. It also clarifies that pseudorefs, while not true refs, are worktree-specific.

The change is purely editorial—no code or behavior is touched—but it removes a long-standing inaccuracy in the glossary and provides clear guidance for contributors. The updated entry is structured as an itemized AsciiDoc list for clarity and future maintainability, and it cross-references the `worktree` entry to eliminate redundancy. All prior review feedback from Junio and Patrick is incorporated, and the patch is uncontroversial. If the series lands, this will be a small but useful improvement for anyone reading the glossary to understand Git’s ref hierarchy.
