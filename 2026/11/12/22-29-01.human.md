This is a documentation patch series, now in its fourth iteration, that aligns Git’s terminology with a 2024 change narrowing "pseudoref" to only `FETCH_HEAD` and `MERGE_HEAD` while introducing "root ref" for refs outside the `refs/` hierarchy (e.g., `HEAD`, `ORIG_HEAD`). The series updates the glossary, man pages, code comments, and tests to consistently use the new terms and clarify their distinctions. Andy Koppe’s latest reply here closes the last open editorial feedback from Junio C Hamano on the glossary’s "pseudoref" entry, removing redundant contrasts with root refs and adding remote-tracking branches to the "ref" entry’s examples. The series is uncontroversial, thoroughly reviewed, and ready for merging—no behavioral changes, just clearer documentation. If you care about precise Git terminology or teach Git internals, this is worth a quick skim; otherwise, it’s safe to skip.
