This is a follow-up in the now-concluded documentation thread about Git’s detached HEAD state. Junio C Hamano responds to Julia Evans’s earlier pushback on the idea that detached HEAD requires a "developer’s mindset."

Junio clarifies that by "developer’s mindset," he means the curiosity to not just observe but also experiment—touching, modifying, and even breaking things—while exploring code. He reiterates that detached HEAD is designed precisely for this kind of hands-on experimentation, not just passive inspection. His workflow avoids clobbering others’ branches by using detached HEAD to safely test changes (e.g., creating temporary commits) without affecting the original branch. The exchange highlights a philosophical difference: Julia views detached HEAD primarily as a tool for observation, while Junio emphasizes its role in active experimentation. Neither proposes changes to the already-merged documentation, but the discussion underscores the challenge of writing documentation that accommodates diverse workflows. The thread remains closed, serving as a footnote to the earlier effort to improve Git’s explanation of detached HEAD.
