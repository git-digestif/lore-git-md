This is a follow-up to a bug report about `git repack --drop-filtered` behavior. The issue is already acknowledged, and a fix is in progress.

Siddharth Shrimali confirms that the current fatal error when a filtered blob is referenced by the index is not a correctness requirement but a conservative design choice. The error prevents dropping blobs that would immediately be lazy-fetched again, but it unnecessarily blocks other eligible objects from being dropped. This limitation was previously reported by Samuel Bronson in September, and Siddharth agreed with the proposed direction to replace the fatal error with a warning and continue processing.

The fix will modify `builtin/repack.c` to skip blobs referenced by the index while dropping the remaining eligible objects, improving usability in partial clones with large working trees. Siddharth is actively working on the patch and plans to post it to the list soon. No new technical questions or edge cases are raised in this email; the focus is on clarifying the rationale and confirming the planned solution. The repack machinery and partial clone support are the relevant subsystems.
