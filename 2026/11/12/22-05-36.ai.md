This is a documentation and terminology cleanup patch series (v4 of 5) that finalizes the alignment of Git’s glossary, man pages, code comments, and tests with the 2024 redefinition of "pseudoref" (now limited to `FETCH_HEAD` and `MERGE_HEAD`) and "root ref" (refs outside `refs/`, like `HEAD` and `ORIG_HEAD`). This iteration addresses all remaining editorial feedback from Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt, including removing redundant contrasts between pseudorefs and root refs in the glossary, clarifying the exhaustive nature of the root ref list, and rewording the `--merge` error message in `revision.c` to avoid implying users must supply one of the listed refs as an argument. The series also improves `git-ls-remote(1)`’s documentation to describe default behavior (showing `HEAD` and refs under `refs/`) and peeling behavior, and drops the term "so-called" from `gitdatamodel.adoc`.

Key changes in v4:
- The glossary’s "pseudoref" entry no longer contrasts pseudorefs with root refs, instead focusing on their unique semantics (multi-object storage, metadata). The "ref" entry now explicitly mentions remote-tracking branches alongside local branches and tags.
- The "per-worktree ref" entry states that the list of root ref prefixes (e.g., `HEAD`, `ORIG_HEAD`) will not be extended, directing future additions to `refs/worktree/`.
- `git-ls-remote(1)`’s documentation now describes what is shown by default (including peeling) and clarifies `--refs` in those terms. The `--merge` description in `rev-list-options.adoc` and its error message in `revision.c` are reworded to avoid implying users must supply one of the listed refs as an argument.
- The series touches 22 files, including man pages (`git-update-ref`, `git-worktree`, `git-ls-remote`), the glossary, test scripts, and in-code comments. No behavioral changes are introduced—this remains a pure terminology cleanup.
