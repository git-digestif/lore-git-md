This is a follow-up to Okhuomon Ajayi’s Outreachy patch that aligns the naming of the `credentialStore.lockTimeout` configuration variable with the convention established by the recent `rerere.lockTimeout` patch. The patch itself is a straightforward contributor housekeeping change—it introduces `credentialStore.lockTimeout` as the preferred setting while keeping `credentialStore.lockTimeoutMS` as a deprecated alias—but Junio C Hamano’s reply raises a fundamental question about the project’s broader timeout naming conventions.

Junio’s concern is whether all Git timeout variables are consistently measured in milliseconds. If they are, dropping the unit suffix (`MS`) makes sense; if not, the change could create confusion by obscuring the unit. He asks the author to justify the decision in the commit message, not just cite precedent, and to explain why the change is the right direction for the project. The reply is not a rejection but a request for deeper reasoning—readers should understand the rationale, not just the mechanical change.

The patch is uncontroversial in its immediate goal (aligning the two subsystems), but Junio’s question suggests the project may need to clarify its timeout naming policy more broadly. Until that justification is provided, the patch is effectively stalled, though the technical work itself is complete.
