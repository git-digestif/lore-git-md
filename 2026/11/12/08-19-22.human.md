This is a CI/build system patch series targeting GitLab CI speedups. Johannes Sixt (Hannes) reports tested results for the v2 series on a Windows notebook, providing concrete before-and-after metrics that confirm the performance improvements Patrick Steinhardt claimed. The review is substantive and tested: Hannes built and ran the code, measured runtime reductions, and explicitly states the series "is looking good."

The key takeaway is that the optimizations translate well to real-world hardware, not just GitLab's runners. On Hannes's machine, `t0027` (auto-CRLF handling) dropped from 4 minutes 45 seconds to 3 minutes 14 seconds (a ~32% reduction), and `t1092` (sparse-checkout compatibility) improved from 6 minutes 15 seconds to 5 minutes 33 seconds (~12% faster). These numbers align with the series' advertised gains, though the absolute times differ from GitLab's runners due to hardware differences. `t5551` (HTTP fetch) was skipped in Hannes's setup, so no data is available for the reftable-backed speedup there.

The feedback is positive and unqualified, with no new concerns raised. Hannes's results reinforce the series' low-risk profile: the changes touch only CI configuration and test scripts, introduce no new symbols or on-disk format changes, and deliver measurable speedups without altering test logic or behavior. This tested review, combined with Junio's earlier "no technical objections" stance, makes the series a strong candidate for `next` unless other reviewers surface issues.
