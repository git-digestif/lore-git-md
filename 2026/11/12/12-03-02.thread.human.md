Yoichi NAKAYAMA has posted a three-patch v2 series teaching Git to store worktree-specific submodule settings in `config.worktree` when `extensions.worktreeConfig` is enabled, with `submodule.<name>.gitdir` recorded as a relative path so linked worktrees survive directory moves. Junio C Hamano has now reviewed all three patches and is broadly supportive of the direction, particularly endorsing patch 3's safer transition behavior and its test coverage for main-worktree versus linked-worktree migration, but he has flagged concrete correctness issues in each patch that remain unresolved. In patch 1 he wants the static-buffer lifetime hazard in `create_default_gitdir_config()` closed by unconditionally duplicating `worktree_git_path()` results; in patch 2 he identified that `is_submodule_active()` is called with the submodule name instead of its path and that `copy_filtered_worktree_config()` fails to remove duplicate gitdir entries; and in patch 3 he points out a canonicalization mismatch in `submodule_git_dir_matches_current_worktree()` where `repo_worktree_path_append()` resolves symlinks via `real_pathdup()` but `absolute_pathdup(repo_get_git_dir())` does not, which can produce false mismatch warnings when `GIT_DIR` is relative or contains `.`/`..` components -- he suggests canonicalizing both sides with `real_pathdup()` and `strbuf_realpath_forgiving()`. No other reviewers have weighed in yet, and the series remains under review with no merge status.
