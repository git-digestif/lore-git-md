This is a documentation patch that finalizes the terminology cleanup for "pseudoref" vs. "root ref" in Git's manual pages. Andy Koppe responds to Junio C Hamano's review of the v3 patch (3/5 in the series) with a clear, point-by-point incorporation of all requested editorial tweaks. The changes are minor but important for clarity and consistency.

The patch updates seven documentation files—`git-ls-remote(1)`, `git-worktree(1)`, `git-update-ref(1)`, `config/core.adoc`, `for-each-ref-options.adoc`, `rev-list-options.adoc`, and `gitdatamodel.adoc`—to stop using the outdated term "pseudoref" for refs outside the `refs/` hierarchy (like `HEAD` and `ORIG_HEAD`). Instead, it uses the now-official term "root ref," aligning with the 2024 terminology change that restricted "pseudoref" to `FETCH_HEAD` and `MERGE_HEAD` only.

Andy’s responses address Junio’s feedback directly:
- In `git-ls-remote(1)`, the `--refs` option now reads "Do not show `HEAD` or peeled tags in the output," and the DESCRIPTION section is expanded to explain the default behavior (showing `HEAD` and all refs under `refs/`).
- The EXAMPLES section no longer treats symbolic refs as special, now reading "List `HEAD` and all refs under `refs/`" instead of implying they are a distinct category.
- In `gitdatamodel.adoc`, the "so-called" qualifier is dropped from the pseudorefs section, and the glossary cross-reference is reworded to apply to both root refs and pseudorefs.
- The `--merge` description in `rev-list-options.adoc` is tweaked to clarify that `<other>` is the first *existing* ref among the listed options, avoiding any implication that `MERGE_HEAD` et al. are not refs.

The patch is uncontroversial and nearly ready for merging. Junio’s feedback was entirely editorial, and Andy’s responses are precise and complete. The series as a whole (v4) is now cooking in `next` and appears on track for inclusion in the next release.
