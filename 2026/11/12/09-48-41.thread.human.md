This thread is about a fix for a double-free crash in `git for-each-ref` that was introduced in Git v2.53.0. The bug occurs when processing refs pointing to missing objects, and the patch from Mustafa Bayar resolves it with a minimal change to `ref-filter.c` and a regression test.

The patch is now queued in Junio’s `next` branch for integration testing and is intended for the `maint` branch to address the issue in the v2.53.x stable releases. Junio queued it after interpreting Karthik Nayak’s review as confirmation that the patch had already undergone thorough internal iteration at GitLab. Karthik’s follow-up email clarifies that his earlier comment was indeed meant to convey that the patch had been well-vetted internally, reinforcing Junio’s decision to advance it. There are no open questions or further feedback, and the patch remains unchanged since its submission.
