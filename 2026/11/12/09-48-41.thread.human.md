This thread is about a critical bugfix for a double-free crash in `git for-each-ref`, a regression introduced in Git v2.53.0 and worsened by a subsequent patch. The issue occurs when processing refs pointing to missing objects, leading to a dangling pointer in `oi->content` that triggers a second free. Mustafa Bayar’s patch, targeting the `maint` branch for stable releases, adds a single-line fix in `ref-filter.c` and a regression test in `t/t6302-for-each-ref-filter.sh`.

Karthik Nayak, a ref backend expert at GitLab, has given the patch a surface-level "looks good to me" after internal review, but his feedback doesn’t engage with the technical details or confirm independent testing. The patch is otherwise uncommented on, leaving its correctness and robustness still under review. No objections or open questions have been raised, but the lack of substantive technical review means the fix hasn’t been independently verified beyond GitLab’s internal process. The patch remains queued for broader scrutiny.
