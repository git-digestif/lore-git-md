This is a performance optimization patch that shrinks `struct commit` by reordering its fields to eliminate padding on 64-bit platforms. The author, Kristofer Karlsson, replies to Derrick Stolee’s review of the patch, confirming that the only ordering constraint—keeping `struct object` first—is preserved. The patch itself is minimal: it moves the `unsigned int index` field into the 4-byte gap after `struct object`, reducing the struct’s size from 80 to 72 bytes (a 10% reduction) without changing behavior.

Kristofer’s reply adds a small but useful experiment: he tested what happens if `struct object` is *not* first, and the results are predictably catastrophic (segfaults, runtime errors). This reinforces the safety of the current patch, which leaves `object` in its required position. He also suggests a compile-time enforcement mechanism—using `BUILD_ASSERT_OR_ZERO` in `init_commit_node()`—to catch similar mistakes in the future, though this isn’t part of the current patch.
