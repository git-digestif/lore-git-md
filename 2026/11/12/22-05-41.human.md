This is the final patch in Andy Koppe’s five-part documentation and terminology cleanup series, now in its fourth iteration. The series aligns Git’s documentation, code comments, and tests with the 2024 redefinition that narrowed "pseudoref" to `FETCH_HEAD` and `MERGE_HEAD` only, while introducing "root ref" for refs outside the `refs/` hierarchy (e.g., `HEAD`, `ORIG_HEAD`).

This patch updates test scripts to stop calling root refs "pseudorefs" and corrects lingering references to `FETCH_HEAD` and `MERGE_HEAD` as "special refs." It also renames test refs in `t1400-update-ref.sh` and `t4013-diff-various.sh` from `PSEUDOREF` to `ROOT_REF_HEAD` and from `REVERSE` to `REVERSE_HEAD` to follow root ref naming conventions, including updating the expected output file in `t4013`. The changes are mechanical and do not alter test behavior—only terminology and naming.

The series as a whole is uncontroversial, thoroughly reviewed, and ready for merging.
