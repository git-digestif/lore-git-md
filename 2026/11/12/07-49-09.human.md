This is a brief follow-up in a bug-report thread about `git repack --drop-filtered`.

The original report described a scenario where `git repack --drop-filtered` aborts entirely if any blob selected for dropping is referenced by the current index, even if other eligible blobs could still be dropped. The reporter asked why this condition is treated as fatal rather than merely skipping the affected objects.

Siddharth Shrimali replies that this is a known limitation, not a correctness requirement. The fatal error was a conservative choice, but it prevents other eligible blobs from being dropped unnecessarily. He confirms that a fix is already in progress: replacing the `die()` call with a warning and continuing to drop the remaining eligible blobs. The fix is motivated by a real-world issue encountered in an `llvm-project` clone, previously reported by Samuel Bronson in September. Siddharth links to the earlier RFC patch and his agreement with its direction.

The thread itself is low-volume and collaborative; no controversy or design debate is evident. The original reporter acknowledges the update and plans to monitor the discussion. For anyone tracking partial-clone performance or `git repack` behavior, this fix looks like a straightforward improvement.
