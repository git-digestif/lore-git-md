This is the twelfth patch in Karthik Nayak’s twenty-patch series that splits the sprawling `t9300-fast-import.sh` into smaller, self-contained test files. The patch isolates the “pack edges” tests—those that verify the `--export-pack-edges` option—so they no longer depend on state left by earlier tests.

The changes are mechanical but careful: each test now starts with a fresh `git init -b main` repository inside a subshell, sets `fastimport.unpackLimit=0` to ensure a pack is written, and replaces the hard-coded `from refs/heads/branch` with `from refs/heads/main` to match the new default branch. The `test_when_finished` hooks clean up the temporary repositories, and the pack-edge verification is inlined rather than relying on the now-removed `verify_packs` helper. The diffstat shows fifty-six lines added and thirty-four removed from `t9300`, reflecting the boilerplate needed to make the tests truly independent.

This is a textbook example of test modernization: no behavior change, just better isolation and maintainability. Junio has already praised the overall direction, and the patch looks ready for review.
