This is a documentation patch addressing the final editorial feedback on Andy Koppe’s v4 series (5/5) to align Git’s documentation with the 2024 terminology change that restricted "pseudoref" to `FETCH_HEAD` and `MERGE_HEAD` while introducing "root ref" for refs outside `refs/` (e.g., `HEAD`, `ORIG_HEAD`). This email resolves the last three minor tweaks Junio C Hamano requested in his review of v3 Patch 3/5, completing the series’ readiness for merging.

The patch updates `git-ls-remote(1)` and `gitdatamodel.adoc` to clarify default behavior, avoid misleading phrasing, and improve cross-references. In `git-ls-remote(1)`, the DESCRIPTION now explicitly states that the command shows `HEAD` and all refs under `refs/` by default, with annotated tags peeled. The EXAMPLES section is reworded to avoid implying symbolic refs are special ("List `HEAD` and all refs under `refs/`" instead of "including `HEAD` and symbolic refs"). In `gitdatamodel.adoc`, the glossary cross-reference is reworded to apply to both root refs and pseudorefs ("See linkgit:gitglossary[7] for the root ref naming rules and further details on pseudorefs"), and the redundant "so-called" is dropped from the pseudorefs section. The `--merge` description in `rev-list-options.adoc` is tweaked to avoid calling `MERGE_HEAD` a ref ("the first *one* of `MERGE_HEAD`, `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD`, ...").

These changes are purely editorial and do not alter the series’ core goal: ensuring documentation consistently uses "root ref" for refs outside `refs/` and reserves "pseudoref" for `FETCH_HEAD`/`MERGE_HEAD`. The updated phrasing is more precise and aligns with Junio’s preferences, addressing his concerns about clarity and completeness. With these tweaks incorporated, the series is now fully ready for `next`; Junio’s "What's cooking" report (2025-06-25) already marked it as such. The files touched in this patch are `git-ls-remote(1)`, `gitdatamodel.adoc`, and `rev-list-options.adoc`.
