This is a follow-up in the now-concluded documentation thread about Git’s detached HEAD state. Junio C Hamano responds to Julia Evans’s pushback on the idea that detached HEAD requires a "developer’s mindset." He clarifies that his use of the term refers to the curiosity to experiment with code—touching, breaking, and modifying it—rather than just observing. Junio reiterates that detached HEAD was designed to support this kind of hands-on exploration, not just passive inspection, and that his personal workflow avoids clobbering others’ branches by using detached HEAD for such experimentation. The exchange remains philosophical, with Junio emphasizing that detached HEAD’s utility extends beyond "pure sightseeing" to include active modification, even if the user isn’t making changes in a given moment. No further revisions to the documentation are proposed, and the thread is effectively closed. The discussion serves as a footnote to the earlier patch, illustrating differing perspectives on how detached HEAD is used in practice.
