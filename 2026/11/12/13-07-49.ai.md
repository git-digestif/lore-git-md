This is a **bugfix patch** in response to a **tested, substantive review** of Aniket Gupta’s GSoC contribution. The patch itself remains unchanged; the email is a reply clarifying the edge case Laveesh Bansal identified—namely, that in partial clones the reported object count includes blobs that were only *referred to* by trees, not actually *checked* for integrity.

Aniket confirms the behavior: `git fsck --verbose` reports 9 objects in Laveesh’s partial-clone example, while `git cat-file --batch-check` only sees 6 locally present blobs. The discrepancy arises because `fsck` counts every object reachable from the refs, regardless of whether it was downloaded or even examined. He suggests rewording the commit title to something like “report the number of objects in the connectivity message” to avoid implying that every counted object was verified.

The tone is collaborative and pragmatic. Aniket does not dispute the edge case but notes that “checked” might be equally misleading, since the missing blobs are counted but not validated. He leaves the final wording to Junio, indicating no strong preference and signaling readiness to send a v2 if requested. No code changes are proposed or needed; the discussion is purely about commit-message precision.
