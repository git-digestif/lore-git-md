Adrian Ratiu posted v2 of a three-patch series fixing bugs in Git's SHA-1/SHA-256 interoperability layer (`extensions.compatObjectFormat`). The patches address a crash that made repositories unusable when `compatObjectFormat` and `objectFormat` are set to the same value (in Rust builds every command aborted with a `BUG()` assertion), a silent incorrect OID returned by `git rev-parse --output-object-format` when mapping fails, and inconsistent handling of unknown commit headers during conversion, which had made `git hash-object -w` refuse commits that `git commit --amend` would write but then fail to read back. The series touches `setup.c`, `builtin/rev-parse.c`, and `object-file-convert.c`, with new tests in `t1302`, `t1500`, and `t1016`.

Junio C Hamano has now reviewed the series and given his approval: he endorsed the crash fix (agreeing the trigger is a misconfigured repository, not a `BUG()`), approved the `rev-parse` mapping-failure fix and called its test "clever and clean", and endorsed patch 3's direction of converting only known object-ID headers (`tree`, `parent`, `mergetag`) while copying all other headers verbatim. In his latest message he said the v2 changes, which incorporate Patrick Steinhardt's feedback, "looked reasonable" and approved the series overall. Patrick Steinhardt reviewed earlier and his feedback was fully incorporated in v2; he also floated a future `x-obj-` prefix convention for custom headers containing object IDs.

What remains open is narrow. Junio questioned the `<=` change in patch 3's header-length checks -- asking why a bare `"parent "` line with an empty value should be passed to the OID parser rather than treated as an unknown header -- and wants better commit-message justification. Adrian had specifically asked Eric Sunshine or brian m. carlson to weigh in on patch 3's removal of the 2023 safety check that rejected unknown headers; Junio noted he is neither of them but approved the direction regardless, so that extra review is still technically pending. Absent further objections, the series appears on track for the maintainer's integration queue, though it is not yet reported as merged or queued in any branch.
