This thread covers Adrian Ratiu's v2 series fixing three bugs in Git's `compatObjectFormat` interoperability layer, which handles SHA-1/SHA-256 conversions. The first two patches -- fixing a crash when `compatObjectFormat` equals `objectFormat`, and correcting `git rev-parse --output-object-format` when mapping fails -- have received Junio C Hamano's approval and are considered settled.

The third patch, which changes how unknown commit headers are handled during conversion, remains under discussion. Junio endorses the core approach of copying unknown headers verbatim and removing the now-redundant special cases for standard headers, but he questions the specific change from `<` to `<=` in the length checks for `tree`, `parent`, and `mergetag` headers. He asks why a line containing only `"parent "` with nothing after the space should be classified as a known header and passed to `parse_oid_hex_algop()` rather than treated as an unknown header, suggesting the v2 fix for malformed headers needs further justification before the patch can be considered ready.
