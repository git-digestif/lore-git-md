Adrian Ratiu's v2 series fixes three bugs in Git's SHA-1/SHA-256 interoperability layer (`compatObjectFormat`): a crash when `compatObjectFormat` matches `objectFormat` that left repositories unusable, a silent incorrect OID returned by `git rev-parse --output-object-format` when mapping fails, and inconsistent handling of unknown commit headers during conversion that caused `git hash-object -w` and `git commit --amend` to disagree. Junio C Hamano has approved the first two patches outright and endorsed the overall series direction, though he explicitly noted he is neither Eric Sunshine nor brian m. carlson -- the two reviewers Ratiu specifically requested for the third patch's removal of the 2023 unknown-header rejection check -- so that request for additional eyes remains technically open.

In his latest reply, Ratiu clarified Junio's question about patch 3's `<=` length-check change, explaining that the stricter comparison intentionally routes malformed headers like `"parent \n"` (empty value) into the object-ID parsing path where they fail and reject the commit, rather than copying them verbatim as unknown headers and producing unreadable commits. Acknowledging the v2 commit message did not explain this distinction clearly, he committed to restructuring v3 to split the `<=` change into a separate preparatory patch establishing the boundary semantics, followed by the patch that unifies unknown-header copying between `convert_commit_object()` and `git commit --amend`. Patrick Steinhardt's earlier feedback on patches 1 and 2 has been fully incorporated in v2, including his test design for `t1500-rev-parse.sh` and clarifications that the rev-parse fix covers multiple explicit single revisions but not range arguments like `A..B`.

The series remains under review awaiting v3. The changes touch `setup.c`, `builtin/rev-parse.c`, and `object-file-convert.c`, with new tests in `t1302-repo-version.sh`, `t1500-rev-parse.sh`, and `t1016-compatObjectFormat.sh`. While the crash fix and rev-parse fix have maintainer approval, patch 3's approach of copying unknown headers verbatim rather than rejecting them continues to await the specific additional review Ratiu requested, even as the maintainer has approved the direction overall.
