This is the third patch in a five-part documentation series (v4) that aligns Git’s terminology with the 2024 redefinition of "pseudoref" (now limited to `FETCH_HEAD` and `MERGE_HEAD`) and "root ref" (refs outside `refs/`, like `HEAD` and `ORIG_HEAD`). This patch updates seven manual pages to replace outdated uses of "pseudoref" with "root ref" and correct related inaccuracies.

The changes focus on clarifying the distinction between root refs and pseudorefs in user-facing documentation. Key updates include:
- **`git-ls-remote(1)`**: Now describes default behavior (showing `HEAD` and refs under `refs/`) and peeling behavior, and clarifies `--refs` in those terms. The term "commit IDs" is replaced with "object IDs" to account for tags.
- **`git-worktree(1)`**: Explicitly states that both root refs and pseudorefs are per-worktree, drops the outdated description of their storage location (which doesn’t apply to the reftable backend), and clarifies that the list of per-worktree prefixes (e.g., `HEAD`, `ORIG_HEAD`) will not be extended.
- **`git-update-ref(1)`**: Corrects the scope of `core.logAllRefUpdates` to include root refs and removes the incorrect mention of `ORIG_HEAD` from the reflog documentation.
- **`gitdatamodel.adoc`**: Distinguishes root refs and pseudorefs in the "References" section, lowercases the "other references" entry for consistency, and adds cross-references to the glossary for further details.

The patch touches `Documentation/config/core.adoc`, `for-each-ref-options.adoc`, `git-ls-remote.adoc`, `git-update-ref.adoc`, `git-worktree.adoc`, `gitdatamodel.adoc`, and `rev-list-options.adoc`. No behavioral changes are introduced—this is purely a terminology and accuracy cleanup. The updates are uncontroversial and address all prior review feedback from Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt. The series is ready for merging.
