This is the second draft of Cynthia Uche’s Outreachy proposal to extend the `early_scan_options()` API to additional Git commands. The proposal targets the December 2026 Outreachy round and builds on Christian Couder’s `cc/early-scan-options` series, which is currently in `seen` and expected to graduate to `next` soon.

Key updates since v1:
- The `cc/early-scan-options` series has advanced to v4, incorporating Cynthia’s review feedback (e.g., documenting why `EARLY_SCAN_STOP_AT_NON_OPTION` is passed in `fast-import`, adding a test for `--allow-unsafe-features` after a non-option, and calling `parse_options_check()` in `early_scan_options()`).
- Cynthia’s microproject (fixing exit code suppression in `t2104`) is now merged to `master`.
- She has reviewed three additional patches: the `early-scan-options` series (v3 and v4), a `test-chmtime` conversion to `parse_options()`, and an Outreachy patch improving `--git-completion-helper` behavior. These reviews demonstrate her engagement with the codebase and the specific APIs involved in her project.
- The candidate inventory now reflects that Fien Dora’s `t1402` tests (for `git check-ref-format`) are in `next` and marked for `master`, and that Patrick Steinhardt’s `ps/rerere-subcommands` series (which would convert `git rerere` to `OPT_SUBCOMMAND`) is under review.

The proposal focuses on four commands:
1. **`git column`**: Currently rejects `--command=foo` unless it is the first argument. Cynthia questions whether this restriction is intentional and plans to replace the hand-rolled scan with `early_scan_options()`. This would allow abbreviations (e.g., `--com=foo`) and `--command` to appear after other options, unless the "must be first" rule is explicitly preserved.
2. **`git bisect`**: Targets `bisect_terms()`, `bisect_run()`, and `bisect_start()` in that order. The conversion would accept abbreviations and `--no-` forms (e.g., `--no-checkout`), but must preserve the original arguments written to `BISECT_LOG` for replay.
3. **`git check-ref-format`**: Currently rejects abbreviations, `--`, and options after `<refname>`. Cynthia proposes converting it to `parse_options()` with `OPT_BIT` for flags and `OPT_ALIAS` for `--print`, which would accept these forms unless `PARSE_OPT_STOP_AT_NON_OPTION` is used. The `--branch` option would need special handling to maintain its current error behavior.
4. **`git unpack-objects`**: A stretch goal, contingent on time and ensuring backward compatibility with callers like `index-pack` and `fetch-pack`.

The timeline is realistic, with overlapping weeks to account for review cycles and flexibility to drop lower-priority tasks. Cynthia emphasizes test-first development (e.g., writing tests to document current behavior before converting `bisect_terms()`) and incremental progress (e.g., starting with `bisect_terms()` before tackling `bisect_start()`).

Open questions for the list:
- Is `git column`’s "must be first" rule intentional?
- Should `git check-ref-format` accept options after `<refname>`?

The proposal is well-motivated and technically grounded. Cynthia’s hands-on experience (testing edge cases, reviewing related patches, and contributing a microproject) suggests she is prepared for the work. The main risks are user-visible behavior changes, which she plans to mitigate with thorough testing and list discussion. The dependency on `cc/early-scan-options` graduating to `next` is the only blocking factor.
