This is the second patch in Farid Zakaria’s v2 three-patch series fixing race conditions in the credential-cache daemon. It targets the early window in which two daemons race to create the socket directory: the second daemon now ignores `EEXIST` from `mkdir()`, checks the directory’s permissions, and proceeds if they are safe (mode `0700`). The change is narrow—twelve lines in `builtin/credential-cache--daemon.c`—and leaves the on-disk format and client protocol untouched. Jeff King and Patrick Steinhardt have both confirmed the logic is correct; no substantive objections remain.

The patch is part of a well-motivated bugfix addressing real-world credential loss during parallel submodule fetches. It builds on the preparatory commit (patch 1) that switched to `safe_create_leading_directories_no_share_const()` to avoid incorrect permission inheritance from `core.sharedRepository`. The test in `t/t0301-credential-cache.sh` exercises the race, although the test may not have failed pre-patch due to the client’s ping mechanism masking the issue. The fix itself is uncontroversial and ready for integration once the architectural concern about API mixing in patch 3 is resolved.
