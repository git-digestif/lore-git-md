This is a substantive review reply from Jeff King (Peff) on the second patch of Farid Zakaria’s two-patch series fixing a race condition in the Git credential-cache daemon. The patch replaces direct socket creation with `unix_ss_create()`, a helper from the simple-ipc subsystem, to safely handle concurrent daemon startup.

Peff’s review is positive and confirms the core logic: the brief lockfile held by `unix_ss_create()` is a reasonable trade-off to avoid stale locks, and the "ok" response when a second daemon detects an existing socket is correct. He suggests minor cosmetic improvements—adding a comment to clarify the "ok" response or using `exit(0)` for clarity—but does not object to the current implementation. The tempfile registration is also correctly moved inside the conditional block, ensuring only the winning daemon cleans up the socket.

The test coverage is noted as sufficient, though Peff observes that the test case might not have failed before the patch due to the client’s ping mechanism. This reinforces that the race is subtle and primarily occurs during explicit daemon startup, as in parallel submodule fetches.

No new edge cases or architectural concerns are raised; Peff’s feedback is focused on clarity and alignment with existing patterns. The patch remains uncontroversial and well-motivated, with all substantive review feedback from Patrick Steinhardt and Junio C Hamano still pending resolution in the anticipated v2.
