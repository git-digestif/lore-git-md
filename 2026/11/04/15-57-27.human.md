This is the fifth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s ten-patch series refactoring the commit-graph write code to consolidate options into a single struct. The series is part of the broader ODB abstraction effort, preparing the commit-graph subsystem for pluggable backends.

This patch merges the two existing commit-graph writer functions—`write_commit_graph()` and `write_commit_graph_reachable()`—into a single entry point. The `write_commit_graph_reachable()` function, which writes a commit graph for all reachable commits, is replaced by a new flag, `COMMIT_GRAPH_WRITE_REACHABLE`, in the `commit_graph_write_options` struct. All callers are updated to use the unified `write_commit_graph()` function, passing the new flag when they previously called the reachable variant.

The change is mechanical and internal: no user-visible behavior changes, and the on-disk format remains untouched.
