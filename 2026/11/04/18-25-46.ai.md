This is a **bugfix patch update** in Farid Zakaria’s two-patch series addressing a race condition in the Git credential-cache daemon. This email confirms the author’s plan to incorporate Patrick Steinhardt’s feedback from the review of patch 1/2.

The duplicated permission-checking logic (a minor concern raised by Patrick) remains unchanged, as no cleaner alternative was identified.
