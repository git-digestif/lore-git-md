This is a **critical bugfix** addressing a regression in the promisor remote lazy-fetch mechanism for partial clones, introduced during the ODB abstraction effort. Patrick Steinhardt confirms the issue and traces its root cause to a stale loose object cache that fails to update when new objects are written. The problem predates the bisected commit (`5946a564`) but was masked until the ODB abstraction work stopped flushing the cache during object lookups.

The fix, sent as a separate patch series (`20261104-pks-odb-loose-stale-cache-v1`), updates the loose object cache’s oidtree after writing objects to listed subdirectories. This aligns with the ODB abstraction’s design by preserving cache consistency without requiring full invalidation. The regression’s delayed manifestation in Kubernetes CI—appearing only after days of successful operation—remains unexplained, but the fix addresses the immediate blocker.
