This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s ten-patch series refactoring Git’s commit-graph write code to consolidate scattered flags and global variables into a unified `struct commit_graph_write_options`. The goal is to modernize the internal API, fix a latent bug in verification, and prepare the commit-graph subsystem for pluggability via the object database (ODB) backend. The series is part of Patrick’s broader ODB abstraction effort.

The series is now at v1, with all patches posted and under review. Derrick Stolee gave it a surface-level "LGTM" but did not engage with technical details or edge cases, leaving questions about deeper validation unanswered. Kristofer Karlsson recently reviewed the sixth patch, praising its unification of call sites but offering only stylistic feedback (suggesting a `switch` statement over an `if/else` chain). His review, like Stolee’s, was positive but shallow, with no substantive critique or testing.

No merge status has been reported, and the series remains under review. The changes are internal-only, with no user-visible impact or on-disk format changes. Open questions include whether the unification of writer functions via the `COMMIT_GRAPH_WRITE_REACHABLE` flag introduces edge cases, as Stolee hinted in an earlier review. No other concerns have been raised.
