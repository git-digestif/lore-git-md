This thread is Patrick Steinhardt’s ten-patch series refactoring Git’s commit-graph write internals to consolidate scattered flags and global variables into a unified `struct commit_graph_write_options`. The work is part of his larger ODB abstraction effort, which aims to make commit-graph reading and writing pluggable via the object database backend.

The series is currently at v1, with seven of the ten patches posted. The first six patches introduced the new options struct, moved existing flags into it, eliminated redundant parameters, and merged the two writer functions into a single entry point. The seventh patch, just posted, fixes a latent bug in the verification code: `verify_commit_graph()` was incorrectly reusing the `COMMIT_GRAPH_WRITE_PROGRESS` flag instead of having its own dedicated `COMMIT_GRAPH_VERIFY_PROGRESS` flag. The patch also modernizes the verify flags by converting them from a loose `#define` bitmask to a proper `enum commit_graph_verify_flags`, improving type safety. The change is small, mechanical, and purely internal—no user-visible behavior is altered.

No review feedback has surfaced yet, but the series is expected to be uncontroversial. The refactoring is focused on interface hygiene, and the bugfix in the seventh patch is a clear improvement with no downsides. The remaining three patches in the series are still to come, and the thread will likely continue to focus on mechanical consolidation rather than substantive design questions.
