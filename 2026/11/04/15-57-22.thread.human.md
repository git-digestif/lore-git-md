This thread is Patrick Steinhardt’s ten-patch refactoring series that cleans up the commit-graph writing internals as part of the larger ODB abstraction effort. The goal is to replace scattered flags and global variables with a unified `struct commit_graph_write_options`, fix a latent bug in verification, and move commit-graph verification into the pluggable ODB backend. The first nine patches, posted as v1, introduced the new options struct, merged redundant writer functions, fixed the verification flag bug, and relocated verification logic from `builtin/fsck.c` to `odb/source-files.c`.

The just-posted tenth patch completes the series by renaming commit-graph functions to follow Git’s modern `<subsystem>_<verb>` convention—`parse_commit_graph()` becomes `commit_graph_parse()`, `write_commit_graph()` becomes `commit_graph_write()`, and `free_commit_graph()` becomes `commit_graph_free()`. These are purely internal, mechanical changes that touch nine files and update all callers without altering behavior or on-disk format. The series is now under review; no objections or requests for changes have surfaced yet, and the refactoring is expected to proceed smoothly given its focus on interface hygiene rather than new functionality.
