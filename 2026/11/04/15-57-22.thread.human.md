This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s ten-patch series refactoring Git’s commit-graph write code. The goal is to clean up scattered flags and global variables by consolidating them into a single `struct commit_graph_write_options`, fix a latent bug in verification, move verification logic into the pluggable ODB backend, and modernize function naming to follow Git’s conventions. The series is part of Patrick’s broader ODB abstraction effort.

The patches are now under review, with no merge status reported. Derrick Stolee reviewed the series and gave it a high-level "LGTM," calling the cleanups "carefully constructed" and a step toward a more modern style. However, his review was surface-level—he didn’t dig into technical details, edge cases, or test results, and he didn’t address the unification of writer functions or the verification logic changes that were previously flagged as needing scrutiny. No other reviewers have weighed in yet.

The series touches `commit-graph.c`, `commit-graph.h`, and several built-in commands, introducing new symbols like `COMMIT_GRAPH_WRITE_REACHABLE` and modernizing function names (e.g., `parse_commit_graph()` → `commit_graph_parse()`). There are no user-visible changes, and the on-disk format remains unchanged. The only open question is whether the unification of writer functions might introduce edge cases, but so far, no substantive concerns have been raised.
