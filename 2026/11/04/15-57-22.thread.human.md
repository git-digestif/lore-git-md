This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s ten-patch refactoring of Git’s commit-graph writing internals, part of his larger effort to make the object database pluggable. The series consolidates scattered flags and global variables into a unified `struct commit_graph_write_options`, fixes a latent bug in verification, moves verification logic into the pluggable ODB backend, and modernizes function naming to follow Git’s `<subsystem>_<verb>` convention. There are no user-visible changes—this is purely internal interface hygiene.

The series is still under review in its initial v1 form. Junio C Hamano reviewed patch 4 and endorsed the removal of redundant flags as a readability improvement, while Derrick Stolee gave the series a surface-level "LGTM," calling it "carefully constructed" but without engaging with technical details or edge cases. Kristofer Karlsson suggested a minor stylistic tweak in patch 6, which Patrick acknowledged but didn’t adopt. The only recent activity is a procedural note from Patrick asking Stolee to preserve the Cc list when replying—no technical discussion or new concerns have emerged.

The main loose end is Stolee’s earlier observation that the unification of writer functions via the `COMMIT_GRAPH_WRITE_REACHABLE` flag might need deeper scrutiny for edge cases, though his "LGTM" didn’t follow up on this. Patrick also floated a future design idea (using a tagged union for write modes) but didn’t propose it for this series. No other substantive feedback has been raised, and the series hasn’t been queued for integration yet.
