This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s ten-patch series refactoring Git’s commit-graph write code to consolidate scattered flags and global variables into a unified `struct commit_graph_write_options`. The work is part of his larger effort to make the object database pluggable and modernizes function naming to follow Git’s `<subsystem>_<verb>` convention. A latent bug in verification was fixed, and the series moves verification logic into the pluggable ODB backend.

The series is still under review at v1. Junio Hamano and Derrick Stolee have both given surface-level endorsements—Junio praised the removal of redundant flags in patch 4, and Stolee called the series “carefully constructed” but provided no substantive critique or testing. Kristofer Karlsson suggested a stylistic tweak in patch 6, which Patrick acknowledged but did not adopt, and floated a future design idea (a tagged union for write modes) that isn’t part of this series. No other reviewers have weighed in, and the most recent exchange is purely procedural, with Patrick acknowledging Stolee’s explanation about Thunderbird’s reply behavior. The series touches many call sites but makes no user-visible changes, and no new test failures or edge-case concerns have been raised beyond Stolee’s early note about potential flag interactions, which remains unresolved.
