This is an **Outreachy microproject discussion** about environment variable handling in Git worktrees. The thread remains exploratory, with no code or test patches submitted yet.

Immaculate Mecha, an Outreachy applicant, proposes using their reproduction and investigation of a `GIT_DIR` inheritance issue in shell aliases as their contribution. The issue occurs when a shell alias runs `git -C <unrelated-repo> rev-parse` from inside a linked worktree: the nested command incorrectly reports the parent worktree’s `GIT_DIR` and `GIT_COMMON_DIR`, while `--show-toplevel` correctly identifies the target repository. Unsetting `GIT_DIR` resolves the issue, confirming it as the root cause. Priestess (Cynthia) previously confirmed the behavior and noted that `handle_alias()` in `git.c` intentionally exports `GIT_DIR` to aliases, framing this as a design question rather than a straightforward bug.

The email seeks guidance from Outreachy mentors on whether this investigation qualifies as a suitable contribution, given the tight deadline and the broader design implications. Immaculate acknowledges that changing the current behavior would require further discussion but hopes the investigation itself could serve as their microproject. The thread has added mentors (Christian Couder, Kaartic Sivaraam, Siddharth Asthana, and Usman Akinyemi) to weigh in on scope and suitability. No decision has been reached yet, and the discussion remains open-ended.
