I use the

     base..old base..new

Form as well.[1] Then it indeed does not matter if you have rebased or
not, as far as unrelated commits are concerned. You won’t get unrelated
commits.

But I don’t rebase topics here since I was told to not do it unless it
is needed. And also because then I get to use the often more readable
`--interdiff` output as well.

† 1: The repetition seems a bit inevitable and annoying, but I use a
     script for assembling all of the options to git-format-patch(1).
