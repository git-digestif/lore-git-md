This is a brief, uncontroversial follow-up to a recently merged ODB lockfile fix. The series consists of two small patches that tighten error handling in `object-file.c`: one adds warnings when unlink operations fail, the other ensures errors from `git index-pack` are properly propagated rather than silently ignored.

Karthik Nayak, whose post-merge feedback prompted these changes, has now reviewed the patches and calls them "good." The series is already cooking in `next`, meaning it is undergoing integration testing but not yet final. No new functionality is introduced, and the changes are purely mechanical—replacing silent failures with warnings or error returns. The patches are well-scoped and directly address the feedback they were meant to resolve. For anyone following the ODB lockfile work, this is a minor but necessary cleanup; for others, it is unlikely to be of broader interest.
