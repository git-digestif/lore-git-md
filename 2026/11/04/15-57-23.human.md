The diff shows a net reduction in lines (13 insertions, 11 deletions), as the `INIT` macro replaces manual initialization in one spot. No behavior changes are introduced, and the on-disk format is unaffected.

This patch sets the stage for the rest of the series, which will progressively migrate all commit-graph write parameters into this struct and merge redundant writer functions.
