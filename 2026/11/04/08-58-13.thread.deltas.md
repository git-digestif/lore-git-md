# Thread deltas

## 2026/11/04

- [2026/11/04/08-58-13 by Tatchou Yopa Nelly <yopatatchou@gmail.com>] Yopa Nelly proposed two config shapes for promisor fetch ordering—`remote.<name>.promisorPriority` (number) and `promisor.fetchOrder` (comma-separated list)—as part of her Outreachy application for the promisor remote fetch ordering project with Kaartic Sivaraam.
- [2026/11/04/08-58-13 by Tatchou Yopa Nelly <yopatatchou@gmail.com>] She expressed a preference for the `promisor.fetchOrder` list, arguing it is easier to read and override across system/global/local config files than numeric priorities and maps directly onto the existing linked list of remotes.
- [2026/11/04/08-58-13 by Tatchou Yopa Nelly <yopatatchou@gmail.com>] She described the current behavior as keeping remotes in a linked list in config order with `extensions.partialClone` moved to the end, and `lazy_fetch_objects()` iterating twice—once for server-specified remotes and once for the rest.
- [2026/11/04/08-58-13 by Tatchou Yopa Nelly <yopatatchou@gmail.com>] She raised open questions about handling non-promisor remotes named in `promisor.fetchOrder`, whether an explicit list entry can override `extensions.partialClone`'s default placement at the end, and whether different orders may be needed depending on which remote is being fetched from.
