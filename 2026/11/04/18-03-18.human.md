This is a surface-level discussion about commit message formatting in the thread removing Git’s defunct `.cirrus.yml` file.

Ben Knoble chimes in to explain Patrick Steinhardt’s passing remark about the “three dashes” in Tuomas Ahola’s commit message. He clarifies that the three hyphens (`---`) are a plain-text substitute for an em-dash, a convention borrowed from typewritten documents where proper typographic dashes were unavailable. The comment is purely stylistic and has no bearing on the patch’s content or its readiness to land; the cleanup patch itself remains uncontroversial and is already acked by both Patrick and Carlo Arenas.
