This is a refactoring patch series advancing the `the_repository` removal effort. The series, now at v4, consists of two patches that introduce `repo_for_each_remote()` and update `unique_tracking_name()` to use it, eliminating the function's reliance on the global `the_repository`.

The only change since v3 is a minor stylistic adjustment in patch 1: unwrapping lines in `repo_for_each_remote()` that became short enough after replacing `the_repository` with the explicit `repo` parameter. This addresses Christian Couder's surface-level feedback about readability but does not affect functionality or correctness. Patch 2 remains unchanged.

The series continues to resolve the earlier design tension by providing a proper abstraction for iterating over remotes in arbitrary repositories, rather than documenting limitations. The new `repo_for_each_remote()` function ensures `unique_tracking_name()` can safely accept any repository instance, and the backward-compatible `for_each_remote()` wrapper preserves existing callers. Files touched include `remote.c`, `remote.h`, `checkout.c`, `checkout.h`, `builtin/checkout.c`, and `builtin/worktree.c`.

No new tests are added, as this is a pure refactoring expected to pass existing tests. The series is now likely ready for merging, pending any further discussion of the stylistic tweak or broader adoption of `repo_for_each_remote()` in future patches. The introduction of this function may influence other `the_repository` removal efforts touching the remote subsystem.
