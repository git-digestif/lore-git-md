Wasswa Edson's three-patch series converting `git credential` to use the parse-options API's `OPT_SUBCOMMAND` mechanism has reached v2 and is effectively converged. The series incrementally refactors the manual `strcmp()`-based dispatch in `builtin/credential.c` into per-subcommand functions and finally switches to `OPT_SUBCOMMAND`, which enables subcommand-specific help text and shell completion while fixing minor documentation gaps. All feedback from v1 has been addressed, and the tests now verify subcommand-specific usage, error messages, and rejection of extra arguments.

Tanga Joris reviewed the final patch and confirmed the v2 tests pass, offering approval with one minor caveat: he identified an undocumented behavior change where passing `--` (the end-of-options marker) after a subcommand, as in `git credential fill --`, previously failed with a usage error but now runs successfully. He suggests the author note this in the commit message's list of behavior changes. This is the only remaining open item; otherwise the series is uncontroversial and ready for merging once that documentation tweak is either incorporated or deemed unnecessary. No merge status has been reported, so the series remains under review.
