This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s ten-part refactoring series to consolidate commit-graph write options into a single struct, part of the broader ODB abstraction effort. The series is under review (v1) and targets internal interface hygiene without changing user-visible behavior.

This patch removes redundant flags from the `write_commit_graph_context` struct. Previously, the context tracked individual boolean flags (`append`, `report_progress`, `split`) that were simple aliases of flags already present in the `commit_graph_write_options` struct. Now that the options struct is always available in the context, these duplicate flags are dropped, and the code directly checks the options struct’s flags instead. The diff shows 32 lines removed and 29 added, reflecting the mechanical replacement of `ctx->flag` with `ctx->opts->flags & COMMIT_GRAPH_WRITE_FLAG` throughout `commit-graph.c`.

The change is straightforward and aligns with the series’ goal of eliminating scattered state. No new edge cases or behavioral changes are introduced, and the patch maintains the same progress reporting and split-graph logic. This is a clean refactoring step that reduces code duplication and makes the relationship between the context and its options clearer. Reviewers are unlikely to find substantive issues here, though they may comment on naming or style preferences.
