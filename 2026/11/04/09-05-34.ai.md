This is the second patch in Okhuomon Ajayi's v4 two-patch series advancing the `the_repository` removal effort. It updates `unique_tracking_name()` in `checkout.c` to accept an explicit `struct repository *` parameter, eliminating its reliance on the global `the_repository`. The function now uses the new `repo_for_each_remote()` helper (introduced in patch 1) to iterate over remotes from the specified repository, and passes the repository through the `tracking_name_data` struct to `repo_get_oid()` for object lookups. The three callers in `builtin/checkout.c` and `builtin/worktree.c` are updated to pass `the_repository` explicitly. The `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` macro in `checkout.c` is removed as it is no longer needed.

The patch is a pure refactoring with no behavior changes or new tests. It touches `checkout.c`, `checkout.h`, `builtin/checkout.c`, and `builtin/worktree.c`, and depends on the new `repo_for_each_remote()` function introduced in patch 1. The changes are mechanical and well-scoped, addressing Junio's earlier design concern by providing a proper abstraction rather than documenting limitations. The series is now likely ready for merging, pending any further discussion of the stylistic tweaks in patch 1 or broader adoption of the new helper in future patches.
