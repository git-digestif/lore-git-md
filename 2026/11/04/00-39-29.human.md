This is a brief follow-up to Patrick Peroša’s bug report about `git push --force-with-lease` being silently ignored when pushing to a remote group. Tyler Cipriani announces that he has already sent a fix in a separate thread (not as a reply here, so the two threads are currently disjoint). The fix targets the exact issue Patrick described: the bare `--force-with-lease` flag is dropped during the internal decomposition of a group push into individual remote pushes, while `--force-with-lease=<refname>` and `--force` are forwarded correctly. No review feedback has appeared yet on Tyler’s patch, but the problem itself is now confirmed and addressed.
