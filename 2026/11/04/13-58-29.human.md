This is a substantive review of the second patch in Elia Pinto’s three-patch bugfix series addressing integer truncation in large-file handling. The series aims to fix silent corruption in packfile generation, diffstat output, and rename detection when processing files larger than 2–4 GiB.

Phillip Wood’s review identifies a critical oversight in this patch: while the `size` parameter is correctly widened to `size_t` to avoid truncation, the function’s return type (`int`) and the line counter variable remain unchanged. Since a file’s line count can theoretically approach its size (e.g., a 2 GiB file with one-byte lines), the counter can overflow, leading to incorrect diffstat results even after the patch. The review also expresses broader concern that the series may rely too heavily on mechanical type changes without sufficient manual analysis of edge cases.

The patch itself is minimal (one line) and well-motivated, but Phillip’s feedback suggests it needs rework to address the overflow risk. No test cases are included due to the impracticality of generating multi-gigabyte test files, but the issue was confirmed via simulation. The series remains under review with no prior versions or external dependencies.
