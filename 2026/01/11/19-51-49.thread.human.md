This thread covers two related macOS compatibility fixes, both now with Junio Hamano’s attention. The first, a two-patch series for an ISO-2022-JP iconv bug in utf8.c, is technically complete and ready for merging; it preserves internal state during buffer growth and automatically detects Homebrew/MacPorts/Fink libiconv at build time.

The second, an RFC patch from Torsten Bögershausen refining Curtis Allen Smith’s work on CRLF conversion in read-cache.c, is under active review. It introduces a `size_change_is_conclusive()` helper that shifts verification work from `git add` to `git status`, adding about 20ms overhead per file. Junio’s feedback focuses on commit message clarity and performance: he questions whether the examples given truly leave content unchanged and asks for data on the impact in repositories without clean filters. He also seeks clarification on whether Torsten is taking over the series or co-authoring it. The core approach remains unchallenged, but the performance trade-off may require further discussion before the patch graduates from RFC status.
