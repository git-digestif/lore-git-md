William Hatfield has opened a design discussion on the `git submodule foreach` traversal feature series, seeking confirmation that the overall approach is sound before he invests in a v3. The series, now at v2 with three patches, adds `--reverse-traversal` for post-order processing of nested submodules, `--include-superproject` to optionally run the command in the superproject itself, and a `--reversive` shorthand combining those with `--recursive`. The implementation, documentation, and tests are complete with no technical objections raised, leaving flag naming as the sole open question.

Hatfield is specifically defending the name `--reverse-traversal` against algorithmic alternatives like "postorder" that Junio C Hamano had suggested. He argues that unlike a tree-walk order which would imply pre/in/post variants, the flag literally reverses the existing traversal order including siblings, and now works independently of `--recursive` (a change from v1). He has also noted that upstream has since added a `t7427` test file, so he plans to renumber his test script to `t7428` and rebase when preparing v3.

The ball is now in the maintainers' court: Hatfield has stated he will send v3 once the design is agreed upon, or automatically in about a week if no objections are raised. Until then, the series remains under review with the `--reverse-traversal` and `--reversive` naming awaiting final maintainer decision.
