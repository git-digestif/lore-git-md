This thread is about a four-patch bugfix series for `git imap-send` that resolves long-standing interoperability issues with tools like `b4`. The series, authored by Harald Nordgren and now at version 4 (`v4`), ensures `git imap-send` correctly handles non-canonical email header orders (e.g., `Subject` before `Date`) and case-insensitive header names (e.g., `FROM:`), while also fixing a tunnel-command memory leak and removing a redundant `git-send-email` special case.

The series is now complete and ready for integration. Patch 1/4, which fixes the tunnel process lifecycle, was already merged to `next` on June 10, 2025, and was further refined in `v4` to address defensive coding concerns. Patches 2/4 (order-agnostic header parsing), 3/4 (removal of the `git-send-email` special case), and 4/4 (case-insensitive header matching) have been explicitly endorsed by Patrick Steinhardt and are now queued in `seen`, with graduation to `next` expected soon. Junio C Hamano has confirmed the series is ready for integration, and no further discussion or revisions are anticipated.
