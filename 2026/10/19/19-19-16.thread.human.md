This thread is about a two-patch bugfix series for `git imap-send` that addresses non-canonical email header order (allowing `Subject` before `Date`) and a minor memory leak, improving interoperability with tools like `b4`. The series, now at `v2`, is authored by Harald Nordgren and remains under active review.

The core issue is that `git imap-send` previously failed when processing patches where the `Subject` header appeared before the `Date` header—a valid but non-standard order produced by some email tools. The fix makes the header-parsing logic order-agnostic and case-insensitive, while also removing a special-case workaround that ignored `Subject` headers for messages starting with `From git-send-email`. This removal has drawn scrutiny from Patrick Steinhardt, who questions its backward-compatibility implications and suggests splitting it into a separate patch for clarity. Another unresolved concern is the tunnel-command lifecycle: the current patch plugs a memory leak but omits `finish_command()`, leaving the child process potentially unreaped.

Harald has incorporated feedback from Junio (case-insensitive header matching and memory leak fixes) and acknowledged Patrick’s concerns, though the tunnel process and special-case removal remain open questions. The series touches `imap-send.c` and adds a test in `t/t9001-send-email.sh`, but the test’s prerequisite handling is also flagged as needing refactoring. No merge decisions have been made yet, and the series is still in the early review phase.
