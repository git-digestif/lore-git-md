This thread is about a two-patch bugfix series for `git imap-send` to handle non-canonical email header order (e.g., `Subject` before `Date`), improving interoperability with tools like `b4`. The series also fixes a minor memory leak and ensures RFC 5322 compliance by making header name matching case-insensitive. Harald Nordgren is the author, and the latest version (`v2`) incorporates feedback from Junio Hamano, the maintainer, who approved the design and flagged the case-sensitivity issue.

The series is still under review, with two key technical questions unresolved. Patrick Steinhardt raised concerns about the tunnel process lifecycle (whether `finish_command()` is needed to reap the tunnel process) and the removal of a special-case logic that ignored `Subject` headers for messages starting with `From git-send-email`. The latter change is contested for its backward-compatibility implications and may need to be split into a separate patch. A third, non-blocking issue—refactoring the test suite’s prerequisite handling—was clarified in this thread: Harald acknowledged Patrick’s suggestion to use `skip_all` at the script’s start (instead of repeating `$PREREQ` in every test) and tagged it as `#leftoverbits` for future cleanup. The core fix for header order handling remains uncontroversial and ready to proceed once the open questions are addressed.
