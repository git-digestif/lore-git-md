The `git imap-send` bugfix series, which was merged to `next` on June 18, 2025, has hit a snag. The series was intended to fix non-canonical email header order (e.g., `Subject` before `Date`) and case-insensitive header names (e.g., `FROM:`) in `git imap-send`, while also addressing tunnel-command lifecycle and memory leaks. Harald Nordgren posted a four-patch `v4` that resolved all prior feedback, including defensive coding improvements and expanded test coverage, and the series was merged to `next` after receiving explicit endorsement from Patrick Steinhardt.

However, Junio C Hamano later spotted a latent bug in Patch 2/4: the `has_required_headers()` helper function assumes LF line endings (`\n\n`), but IMAP messages use CRLF (`\r\n`). This mismatch could cause the function to scan past the header block into the message body, leading to false positives or negatives in header detection. Harald has acknowledged the issue and plans to post a `v5` with a straightforward fix—adjusting the line-iteration logic to account for CRLF. The rest of the series, including case-insensitive header matching, tunnel process fixes, and the removal of the `git-send-email` special case, remains unaffected. Once `v5` is posted, the series should be ready to graduate to `master`.
