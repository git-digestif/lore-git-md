This thread is about a four-patch bugfix series for `git imap-send`, now at version 4, authored by Harald Nordgren. The series ensures RFC 5322 compliance by making the tool accept email headers (`From:`, `Date:`, `Subject:`) in any order and any case, which is particularly important for interoperability with tools like `b4`. It also fixes a tunnel-command memory leak and removes a special-case exemption for `git-send-email` messages that previously ignored `Subject` headers.

The series is now complete and ready for integration. The first patch, which fixes the tunnel process lifecycle, was merged to `next` on June 10, 2025, and was further revised in `v4` to address defensive coding feedback. The remaining three patches—order-agnostic header parsing, removal of the `git-send-email` special case, and case-insensitive header matching—are uncontroversial and have all prior feedback resolved. Patrick Steinhardt, who provided detailed reviews of earlier versions, has confirmed that `v4` addresses all his concerns, including expanded documentation for backward-compatibility and additional test coverage for mixed-case headers. Junio C Hamano previously validated the design in the first version of the series.

The changes are minimal and focused, touching only `imap-send.c` and the test suite (`t/t9001-send-email.sh`). The new behavior treats all messages uniformly, ensuring `git imap-send` works reliably with non-canonical header formats while maintaining backward compatibility. The series is currently queued in `seen` and is expected to graduate to `next` soon. There are no remaining open questions or blocking feedback.
