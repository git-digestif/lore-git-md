This thread is a two-patch bugfix series for `git imap-send`, addressing a failure when processing patches with non-canonical email header order (e.g., `Subject` before `Date`), a format produced by tools like `b4`. The series also fixes a minor memory leak in tunnel-command handling. Authored by Harald Nordgren, the patches were initially submitted as ready for review, but Junio C Hamano identified a protocol-compliance issue in the second patch: the new `has_required_headers()` helper uses case-sensitive header matching, violating RFC 5322.

Harald has now acknowledged Junio’s feedback and agreed to replace `starts_with()` with `istarts_with()` in the helper function. He proposes skipping a test for case insensitivity, arguing that `istarts_with()` is already trusted elsewhere in the codebase. The fix is confined to `imap-send.c` and does not require additional test coverage. With this change, the series will address the sole remaining issue and should be ready for a `v2` update. No other design or implementation concerns have been raised, and the patches maintain backward compatibility. The series remains under review, with no merge status recorded.
