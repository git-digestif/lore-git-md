This thread is about a four-patch series from Harald Nordgren that fixes bugs in `git imap-send` to handle non-canonical email header order (like `Subject` before `Date`) and case insensitivity, while also fixing tunnel-command lifecycle issues and memory leaks. The goal is to ensure RFC 5322 compliance and interoperability with tools like `b4`.

The series is nearly complete. The first patch (tunnel process lifecycle fix) was merged to `next` on June 10 but may get a minor follow-up tweak for defensive coding. The second and fourth patches (order-agnostic header parsing and case-insensitive header matching) are ready for integration. The third patch (removing a `git-send-email` special case) is under review but uncontroversial—Patrick Steinhardt raised a minor documentation concern about backward compatibility, which Harald acknowledged but didn’t address in this reply. The entire series is queued in `seen` and should move to `next` soon.

Key changes are in `imap-send.c`, where the header-parsing logic now accepts any order and case for `From:`, `Date:`, and `Subject:` headers. Tests confirm backward compatibility, though Patrick noted a small gap in testing mixed-case headers (non-blocking). No major objections remain, and the series is on track for graduation.
