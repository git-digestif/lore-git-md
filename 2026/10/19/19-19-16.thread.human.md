This thread is about a five-patch series (`v5`) from Harald Nordgren that fixes bugs in `git imap-send` to ensure it properly handles email headers in non-canonical order (e.g., `Subject` before `Date`) and with case-insensitive names (e.g., `FROM:`). The series also addresses a tunnel-command memory leak and removes a special-case exemption for `git-send-email`. The goal is to achieve RFC 5322 compliance and improve interoperability with tools like `b4`.

The series was previously merged to `next` as `v4` on June 18, 2025, but a latent CRLF line-ending bug in one of the patches was later identified by Junio C Hamano. This `v5` iteration resolves that issue and is now ready for re-integration into `next`. The first patch (tunnel process lifecycle fix) was already merged to `next` on June 10 and remains unchanged. The remaining patches—order-agnostic header parsing (now with CRLF support), removal of the `git-send-email` special case, and case-insensitive header matching—have been reviewed and endorsed by Patrick Steinhardt. The latest patch (4/4) completes the series by making header parsing case-insensitive, with test coverage confirming the fix works for mixed-case headers.

All prior feedback has been addressed, including defensive coding for the tunnel process, expanded documentation for the special-case removal, and test coverage for order-agnostic and case-insensitive parsing. The series is uncontroversial, thoroughly tested, and expected to graduate to `master` once re-queued in `next`. Key participants include Harald Nordgren (author), Junio C Hamano (maintainer), and Patrick Steinhardt (reviewer).
