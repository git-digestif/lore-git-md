This thread is about a now-merged four-patch series from Harald Nordgren that fixes bugs in `git imap-send` to handle non-canonical email header order (e.g., `Subject` before `Date`) and case-insensitive header names (e.g., `FROM:`), while also addressing tunnel-command lifecycle and memory leaks. The series was merged to Junio’s `next` branch on June 18, 2025, and was expected to graduate to `master` in the next release cycle.

However, Junio has just identified a latent bug in the second patch of the series, which introduces the `has_required_headers()` helper function. The function assumes LF line endings (`\n\n`) to detect the end of headers, but IMAP messages use CRLF (`\r\n`). This mismatch could cause the function to scan past the header block into the message body, risking false positives or negatives. The issue is blocking, as it undermines the series’ core goal of RFC 5322 compliance, and will require a new iteration (`v5`) to fix. The other patches in the series (case-insensitive headers, tunnel process fixes) are unaffected.

Key participants include Harald Nordgren (the author), who addressed all prior feedback in `v4`, and Patrick Steinhardt, who endorsed the series as ready for integration after `v4`. Junio, who initially validated the design and merged the series, has now raised this critical issue. The fix is expected to be straightforward, but the series will need to be re-rolled before it can graduate to `master`.
