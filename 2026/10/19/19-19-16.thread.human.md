This thread is about a two-patch bugfix series for `git imap-send`, authored by Harald Nordgren, that addresses interoperability issues with tools like `b4` by making the header-parsing logic order-agnostic (accepting `Subject` before `Date`) and case-insensitive. The series also fixes a minor memory leak in tunnel-command handling. Version 2 (`v2`) incorporated Junio Hamano’s feedback on case-insensitive header matching and the memory leak, but new review comments have surfaced technical questions about the design and scope of the changes.

The core issue—failing to process patches with non-canonical header order—is now resolved in principle, with Junio validating the cursor-handling logic and test coverage. However, Patrick Steinhardt’s reviews of both patches have raised unresolved questions. On the first patch, he asked whether the tunnel process lifecycle is intentionally left unreaped (omitting `finish_command()`). On the second patch, he flagged two concerns: (1) the removal of a special-case logic for messages starting with `From git-send-email` (which previously ignored `Subject` headers) without explanation, and (2) the test suite’s inefficient use of `$PREREQ` guards in `t/t9001-send-email.sh`. These points touch on backward compatibility and edge-case behavior, not the core fix, but they remain open and require clarification from the author.

The series is still under review, with no merge status recorded. Key participants include Harald (author), Junio (maintainer, who approved the design direction), and Patrick (who is probing implementation details). The changes touch `imap-send.c` and the test suite, with no on-disk format impact. The unresolved questions are the main barrier to progression; once addressed, the series is likely to be queued for `next`.
