This thread is about a five-patch bugfix series for `git imap-send` that ensures it correctly handles email headers in non-canonical order (e.g., `Subject` before `Date`) and with case-insensitive names (e.g., `FROM:`), while also fixing tunnel-command lifecycle issues and memory leaks. The series, now at `v5`, is authored by Harald Nordgren and addresses RFC 5322 compliance and interoperability with tools like `b4`.

The first patch (tunnel process lifecycle fix) was merged to `next` on June 10 and remains unchanged. The full `v4` series was merged to `next` on June 18, but a latent CRLF line-ending bug in the second patch’s `has_required_headers()` helper was later identified by Junio. This bug, which caused false positives/negatives in header detection, is now fixed in `v5` by adjusting the line-iteration logic to handle both LF (`\n\n`) and CRLF (`\r\n`) line endings. The series also removes a special-case exemption for `git-send-email` messages (patch 3/4) and implements case-insensitive header matching (patch 4/4), both of which are now uncontroversial and well-tested.

Patrick Steinhardt recently reviewed the CRLF-handling logic in patch 2/4 and suggested a minor readability improvement to make the termination conditions more explicit. While this is a stylistic tweak rather than a correctness issue, it’s likely to be incorporated in a `v6` or applied directly by Junio. The series is otherwise ready for re-integration into `next`, with all prior feedback addressed and graduation to `master` expected soon. The changes are minimal, focused, and maintain backward compatibility while improving interoperability.
