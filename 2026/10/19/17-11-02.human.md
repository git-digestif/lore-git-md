This is a **bugfix follow-up** to the ZIP archive timestamp handling effort. René Scharfe acknowledges Matthew E. Luallen’s proposed test infrastructure fix—replacing `TIME_IS_64BIT` with `TIME_T_IS_64BIT`—as a pragmatic solution to unblock integration of the patch that omits the ZIP Extended Timestamp (UT) field for out-of-range commit times. The fix ensures tests pass on 64-bit platforms and are skipped on 32-bit systems where `zipinfo(1)` lacks UT field support, resolving CI failures on Ubuntu 20.02 i386 and Debian i386.

René notes that while the fix is technically sound, it sidesteps a deeper issue: Git’s `archive-zip.c` currently relies on `time_t`, which may not be the right abstraction for archive logic. He suggests future refactoring could eliminate `time_t` entirely, possibly by using UTC instead of the archiver’s local timezone (though this would break caching schemes). For now, the two-line adjustment is accepted as a minimal, low-risk change that keeps the UT field omission patch on track for integration. The broader thread’s unresolved issues—ZIP legacy DOS date overflow and `fast-import` negative timestamp validation—remain unaffected.
