This is a documentation discussion in the ongoing thread about Julia Evans’s detached HEAD rewrite. Julia responds to Junio C Hamano’s request for a clearer explanation of why she prefers her phrasing over his proposed alternative for defining HEAD in the new `gitdetached-head(7)` man page.

Julia grounds her argument in user research she conducted in March 2024, where she collected informal explanations of HEAD from experienced Git users. She presents a curated selection of these quotes to illustrate the variety of "technically wrong but useful" mental models users hold. Her central claim is that effective documentation should build on the language and concepts users already use, rather than introducing new terminology (e.g., "records the state from which your current changes started") that does not align with their existing understanding.

The email is not a patch or a review, but a methodological defense of her approach. Julia acknowledges the challenge of justifying every wording choice with user research and asks Junio for suggestions on how to communicate her reasoning more concisely. The quotes she includes reveal common themes: HEAD as a pointer to the current commit, confusion around detached HEAD, and uncertainty about the distinction between HEAD and lowercase "head." Her framing positions these user perspectives as essential context for evaluating the clarity of any proposed definition.
