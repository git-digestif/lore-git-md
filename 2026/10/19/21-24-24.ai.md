This is a follow-up discussion in the now-merged documentation thread about rewriting Git’s detached HEAD explanation. Junio C Hamano replies to Julia Evans’s methodological defense of her phrasing choices, which was grounded in March 2024 user research.

Junio’s email is a mix of lighthearted acknowledgment and probing questions. He accepts Julia’s “Trust me” appeal as sufficient given their working history, but then challenges the relevance of the user quotes she presented. His core question is whether the quotes support an argument that Git’s documentation should *avoid* explaining HEAD altogether, given that many users get by with only a vague understanding. He also asks what specific point she intended to illustrate with the curated examples, noting that some users clearly do grasp HEAD while others remain confused. The tone is inquisitive rather than dismissive, but the questions push Julia to clarify how her research findings should translate into concrete documentation decisions. No new patches or technical changes are proposed; this is purely a meta-discussion about documentation philosophy.
