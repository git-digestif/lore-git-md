This is a **surface-level review** of the first patch in Beat Bolli’s `[PATCH v3 0/3]` series for `git imap-send` OpenSSL compatibility. Patrick Steinhardt’s reply focuses on **process and presentation**, not the patch’s technical content. He criticizes the lack of a cover letter explaining the series’ goals and the changes between v2 and v3, and recommends using `b4` to automate patch management. He also encourages replying to review feedback directly rather than sending a new version without context.

The patch itself (replacing `SSLv23_method()` with `TLS_method()`) remains uncontroversial and well-motivated, but the **series as a whole is now blocked on process issues** as well as the unresolved **security regression** in patch 2/3 (certificate stripping). Patrick’s feedback is procedural rather than technical, but it is **high-weight** given his role in the project and his prior substantive reviews of the series. The absence of a cover letter and range-diff makes it harder for reviewers to track what has changed, particularly the addition of the new patch 3/3 (verification callback) and the OpenSSL 4.0 compatibility macros.

If you are following this series, the key takeaway is that **v3 is not yet ready for further technical review** until the process issues are addressed. The security regression in patch 2/3 remains the primary blocker, but Patrick’s feedback adds a secondary hurdle. The patch itself is a safe, incremental step, but the series’ presentation needs improvement before it can progress.
