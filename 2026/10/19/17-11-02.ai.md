This is a **bugfix follow-up** to the ZIP archive date-handling thread, specifically addressing the test infrastructure for René Scharfe’s patch that omits the ZIP Extended Timestamp (UT) extra field for out-of-range commit times. The email confirms the pragmatic solution to a blocking issue: replacing `TIME_IS_64BIT` with `TIME_T_IS_64BIT` in the test prerequisites to correctly handle 32-bit platforms where `time_t` (used by `zipinfo(1)`) differs from Git’s internal `timestamp_t`.

René acknowledges Matthew E. Luallen’s proposed fix as a reasonable short-term solution, noting that while `time_t` is not the ideal abstraction for Git’s archive logic, eliminating it entirely would require either a 64-bit `localtime_r(3)` polyfill on 32-bit systems (excessive effort) or switching to UTC (which would break caching schemes). The two-line test adjustment is now the agreed path forward, unblocking integration of the UT field omission patch. This change is narrowly scoped and does not affect the broader unresolved issues in the thread (ZIP legacy DOS date overflow or `fast-import` negative timestamp validation).

Key technical details: the fix ensures tests pass on 64-bit platforms and are skipped on 32-bit systems where `zipinfo(1)` lacks UT field support or is missing. The email reinforces that the UT field omission patch itself remains technically sound and ready for integration, with no remaining blockers. René’s comments hint at future refactoring opportunities but frame them as non-urgent.
