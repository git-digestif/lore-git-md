This is the v3 iteration of Igor Wiedler’s two-patch bugfix series that resolves a multi-minute performance regression in shallow fetches when `--alternate-refs` forces a full history walk of large alternates (e.g., 7 M objects). The series now treats alternate refs as a boundary in *all* connectivity checks, not just deepening fetches, and teaches `odb_for_each_alternate_ref()` to ignore refs from shallow or grafted alternates. The only change since v2 is a mechanical test fix: replacing `$TRASH_DIRECTORY` with `$(pwd)` and relative paths to make the Windows CI pass. No code logic was altered, and the series retains the approvals from Patrick Steinhardt and Justin Tobler for the core regression fix (patch 1) while incorporating Junio’s design feedback in patch 2. The series is now unblocked and ready for final review; it looks like a clean, well-motivated fix for a real-world CI pain point.
