Subject: [PATCH] status: default to comparing against both @{upstream} and @{push}
Series version: v1 (initial submission; next iteration expected as v2)
Originating author: Harald Nordgren
Thread type: feature patch

Core problem or goal
Make `git status` more useful in triangular workflows (where the push destination differs from the upstream) by defaulting to comparing the current branch against *both* `@{upstream}` and `@{push}` instead of only `@{upstream}`. When the two destinations resolve to the same branch, only one comparison is shown, preserving the experience for most users.
