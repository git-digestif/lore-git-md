Core problem or goal
Make `git status` more useful in triangular workflows (where the push destination differs from the upstream) by defaulting to comparing the current branch against *both* `@{upstream}` and `@{push}` instead of only `@{upstream}`. When the two destinations resolve to the same branch, only one comparison is shown, preserving the experience for most users. The v2 commit message establishes historical context: the 2026 commit (3ea95ac9c5) made the feature opt-in due to noise concerns, with the intention of revisiting the default later.
