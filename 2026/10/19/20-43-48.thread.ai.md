Core problem or goal
Clarify the intended contribution workflow in `MyFirstContribution.adoc` by moving the "Sending v2" instructions after the "Responding to Reviews" section and strengthening the language to discourage sending updated patch versions before engaging with review feedback. The goal is to reduce premature v2 submissions from new contributors.
