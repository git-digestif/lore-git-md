This is a bugfix for a memory safety issue in `git bundle create`. The patch, originally posted by Olof Fairbrook, fixes an uninitialized stack memory free that could occur when the command encounters an unrecognized argument. The issue was introduced in commit 11ee9a75e7 (2024-06-11) and could lead to valgrind warnings or crashes depending on stack contents.

Patrick Steinhardt reviewed the patch and provided feedback. His review is surface-level, focusing on minor stylistic nits in the commit message and test script rather than the core fix. He confirms that the fix itself is correct and that the other variables released on exit (`revs` and `lock`) are properly initialized. Patrick also notes that the nits he raised are optional and do not need to be addressed for the patch to be acceptable.

The fix is minimal and well-targeted: it initializes the `revs_copy` struct at declaration in `bundle.c` and includes a new test case in `t/t5607-clone-bundle.sh` to verify error handling without leaving cruft. The patch is self-contained, clearly motivated, and addresses the issue fully. No controversy or open questions remain, and the patch looks ready for merging.
