This is the author's reply to Patrick Steinhardt's **substantive review** of the fifth patch in the `git stash` shell completion series. The patch removes Bash completion for the deprecated `git stash save` subcommand, and Patrick raised a design question about whether option autocompletion should still work when the user manually types `save`.

Ben Knoble acknowledges the oversight and plans to test the behavior of `git stash save --<TAB>` with the patch applied. He suggests that if option completion still works (presumably due to `GIT_COMPLETION_SHOW_ALL`), that might address Patrick’s usability concern. The response is brief and focused on clarifying the patch’s actual behavior rather than defending the design choice. The thread is now waiting on the author’s follow-up after testing, which will likely inform a v2 update. No new technical objections or alternatives are proposed.
