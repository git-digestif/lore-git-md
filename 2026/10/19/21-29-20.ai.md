This is a **substantive review** of the second patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch series introducing the `extensions.objectStorage` mechanism for pluggable object database backends. The review comes from Karthik Nayak, a contributor with domain expertise in ref backends and related plumbing.

Karthik’s feedback focuses on three technical aspects of the patch. First, he questions whether the `files` backend’s payload handling should use `strbuf_realpath()` to normalize relative paths to absolute paths, mirroring the behavior of the ref storage extension. This is a design-level observation about consistency and robustness, though it does not identify a concrete bug. Second, he notes that the `gettext.h` include in `odb/source.c` appears unnecessary, as the patch compiles without it — a minor cleanup suggestion. Finally, he raises test coverage gaps: the patch lacks tests for the `files` backend’s payload handling (both valid and invalid cases) and for absolute vs. relative path resolution. He also questions whether the `DEFAULT_REPO_FORMAT` prerequisite is redundant in tests that explicitly set `core.repositoryformatversion=1`.

The review is narrowly scoped to this patch and does not engage with the series’ broader architectural goals. While the points are technical and actionable, they are not showstoppers; the patch remains functionally sound as-is. The feedback is likely to be addressed in a future revision, particularly the test coverage suggestions, which are straightforward to implement. No objections or conditional approvals were expressed, and the tone is collaborative rather than contentious. The review’s depth suggests careful attention to detail but does not signal deeper concerns about the patch’s direction.
