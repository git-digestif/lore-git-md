Core problem or goal
Replace the `_()` gettext wrapper with `Q_()` in the ZIP backend’s “path too long” error message so that translators can supply language-specific singular and plural forms. The message is emitted when a path exceeds the ZIP format’s 16-bit length limit (0xffff bytes). No behavior change for English users.
