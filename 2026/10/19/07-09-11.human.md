This is a refactoring patch, part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The patch itself is a clean, mechanical conversion of `prune_packed_objects()` to accept an explicit `struct repository *` parameter instead of relying on the global `the_repository` variable. It touches five files—`builtin/prune-packed.c`, `builtin/prune.c`, `builtin/repack.c`, `prune-packed.c`, and `prune-packed.h`—and introduces a small helper struct (`prune_data`) to work around the single-`void*` callback limitation in `for_each_loose_file_in_source()`.

The patch is well-scoped and uncontroversial, with no behavior changes or test coverage implications. The commit message clearly explains the motivation (libification) and the technical workaround, though Patrick Steinhardt’s review suggests two minor tweaks: avoid the word "now" in the commit message and use the author’s full name in the sign-off. Neither affects the substance of the change.

The only open question raised in the review is whether the commands that call `prune_packed_objects()` (`git prune-packed`, `git prune`, and `git repack`) can ever be invoked outside a repository, which would make the `repo` parameter potentially `NULL`. This is a valid edge case to check, but it doesn’t undermine the patch’s correctness—just its completeness. The patch is otherwise ready to advance, assuming the author addresses the review nits and confirms the `NULL` handling. No other feedback has surfaced yet.
