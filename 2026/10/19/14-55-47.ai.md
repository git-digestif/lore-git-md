This is the first patch in a **documentation** series (v2) replacing the outdated `gittutorial` man page with a new beginner-focused guide. The patch itself is a straightforward deletion of the existing `Documentation/gittutorial.adoc` file, clearing the way for the new content in the next patch.

The deleted tutorial is a 671-line document that walks users through basic Git operations—initializing a repository, making commits, branching, merging, and collaborating—using a mix of command-line examples and conceptual explanations. While comprehensive, the content is dated (e.g., references to CVS-like workflows, no mention of modern forges like GitHub/GitLab) and lacks the user-tested clarity of the replacement. The patch’s commit message explains the deletion is intentional to avoid diff noise, as none of the old content is reused.

No technical changes are introduced here; the patch is purely preparatory. The next patch in the series will introduce the new tutorial, which addresses modern workflows, accessibility, and common beginner pain points. This deletion is uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ goal of providing a more practical introduction to Git.
