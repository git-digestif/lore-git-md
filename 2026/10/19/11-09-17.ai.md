This is the first patch in Igor Wiedler’s v3 two-patch bugfix series targeting the connectivity-check performance regression in deepening fetches. It implements the immediate regression fix: negating `--alternate-refs` in *all* connectivity checks, not just non-deepening fetches, by moving the `--not` argument earlier in the `rev-list` command line. The change is minimal (6 lines in `connected.c`) and directly addresses the 227-second delay reported in CI workflows when fetching from large alternates (7M objects).

The patch moves `--alternate-refs` next to `--all` to group the boundary arguments, and adds two new tests in `t/t5618-alternate-refs.sh` that use `GIT_TRACE2_EVENT` to verify the `rev-list` arguments for both regular and deepening fetches. The tests confirm the regression is fixed but do not assert behavioral differences (the alternate remains fully connected, so the outcome is unchanged). Patrick Steinhardt and Justin Tobler’s approvals from v1 carry forward, and Junio’s design feedback is fully incorporated.

This is a well-motivated, low-risk fix for a real-world performance pain point. The patch is ready for integration; no technical concerns remain. The broader design flaw (alternate refs from shallow or grafted repos) is addressed in patch 2.
