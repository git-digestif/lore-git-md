Patrick responds directly to Junio C Hamano’s skepticism about the jobs’ value. His tone is pragmatic and low-key, effectively conceding that the jobs, as currently implemented, are not delivering meaningful benefits.

The open question is whether the jobs themselves are worth the maintenance overhead. Junio’s original concern — that disabling them might lead to a flood of poorly formatted patches — remains unresolved, but Patrick’s reply suggests that the current checks are not effective gatekeepers anyway. Unless another participant steps in to defend the jobs’ utility, the thread is likely to conclude with the patch queued and the jobs left in place, but with an implicit understanding that their long-term future is uncertain.
