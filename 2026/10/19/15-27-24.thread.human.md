This thread is about a small internationalization (i18n) improvement in Git’s ZIP archive backend. Keryle Fondji submitted a patch that replaces a basic `_()` gettext wrapper with `Q_()`, Git’s plural-form helper, for an error message triggered when a path exceeds the ZIP format’s 16-bit length limit. While the condition ensures the path length is always greater than `0xffff` (making the plural form technically unnecessary in English), the change allows translators to provide language-specific singular and plural forms if needed. The modification is a single-line change in `archive-zip.c` and doesn’t alter behavior for English users. No reviews or objections have been posted yet.
