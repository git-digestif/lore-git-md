This is a discussion about documentation methodology in the now-merged `gitdetached-head(7)` rewrite thread. Junio C Hamano responds to Julia Evans’s earlier email where she defended her phrasing choices by presenting user research quotes. His reply is skeptical of the value of those quotes for the immediate task of refining the man page text.

Junio questions whether the variety of user mental models—ranging from “HEAD is mysterious” to “HEAD is a reference to a commit”—actually helps decide how to explain detached HEAD. He suggests that the fact users get by with vague understanding might argue *against* explaining HEAD at all, rather than for any particular wording. The tone is light (“Trust me ;-)) but the underlying point is serious: user research can show what people *currently* think, but it does not automatically tell us how to write better documentation. Junio seems to be nudging Julia to distill the research into actionable guidance rather than presenting raw quotes.

No code or patch is involved; this is purely a meta-discussion about how to evaluate documentation clarity. The thread remains closed (the patch is already in `master`), so this exchange is unlikely to produce further changes. It may, however, inform future documentation efforts by highlighting the tension between user language and technical precision.
