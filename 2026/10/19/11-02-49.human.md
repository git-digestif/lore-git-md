This is a brief reply from Igor Wiedler to Jeff King’s test-fixup suggestion for the second patch in his two-patch series. The series targets a performance regression in connectivity checks for deepening fetches, where `--alternate-refs` was forcing full history walks of large alternates (e.g., 7M objects). Patch 2 expands the fix to treat alternate refs as a boundary in *all* connectivity checks and teaches `odb_for_each_alternate_ref()` to ignore refs from shallow or grafted alternates.

Igor acknowledges Peff’s proposed fix for the Windows CI test failure—a mechanical path normalization issue in the new test—and confirms he will incorporate it into the next version of the series. The failure was not a design flaw but a portability quirk, and Peff’s suggestions (replacing `$TRASH_DIRECTORY` with `$(pwd)` or rewriting the test more idiomatically) are straightforward and backward-compatible. This reply removes the last technical blocker for the series, though Junio’s earlier procedural reminder (requiring Igor to engage in discussion before posting updates) still applies.

The series retains approvals from Patrick Steinhardt and Justin Tobler for the core regression fix (patch 1) and addresses Junio’s design feedback in patch 2. Peff’s review of patch 2 was substantive and praised the low-level fix, though he did not give an explicit "Reviewed-by". With the test fix now agreed, the series looks ready to proceed once Igor posts v3. The patch touches `odb.c`, `connected.c`, and `t/t5618-alternate-refs.sh`, and the deferred edge case of partial clones (promisor remotes) remains a known limitation.
