Thread identity: bugfix patch series (single patch) addressing a correctness issue in Git's skipping negotiator during fetch negotiation. Originating author: Taylor Blau. Thread type: bugfix.

Core problem or goal: fix a subtle ordering bug in the skipping negotiator's queue management where parent commits were added to the queue before their date fields were parsed, causing recent common ancestors to be incorrectly prioritized behind older ref tips. This could lead to premature termination of negotiation without discovering the most recent common commit.

Current status: initial patch posted; under review. Patrick Steinhardt has raised a design question about whether the commit parsing should be moved to `rev_list_push()` to cover all callsites and align with the default negotiator, which would also allow removing an existing `repo_parse_commit()` call in `get_rev()`. The patch is otherwise considered well-motivated and self-contained.

Key technical details:
- Files touched: `negotiator/skipping.c` (main fix), `t/t5552-skipping-fetch-negotiator.sh` (new test case).
- Subsystem: fetch negotiation machinery, specifically the skipping negotiator.
- New behavior: parent commits are now parsed (and thus have their correct date set) before being added to the queue, ensuring proper prioritization by date.
- Test coverage: new test case creates a scenario with 32 old ref tips and verifies that the two most recent commits (HEAD and its grandparent) are offered in the first two "have" lines during negotiation.
- Architectural consideration: Patrick Steinhardt suggests moving commit parsing from `push_parent()` to `rev_list_push()` to cover all callsites and simplify `get_rev()`.

Open questions and loose ends:
- Whether to adopt Patrick's suggested refactoring to move commit parsing to `rev_list_push()` for broader consistency and simplification.

Participant positions:
- **Patrick Steinhardt**: substantive review; suggests moving commit parsing to `rev_list_push()` to cover all callsites and align with the default negotiator, which would also allow removing an existing `repo_parse_commit()` call in `get_rev()`. Also notes a minor test script nit (`sed -n "1,2p"` could be `head -n2`).
