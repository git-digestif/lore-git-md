This thread is about a small but important internationalization fix in Git’s ZIP archive backend. The patch, now in its sixth version, replaces a hardcoded singular/plural error message in `archive-zip.c` with Git’s `Q_()` macro, ensuring proper pluralization support for all languages—not just English. The error occurs when a path exceeds the ZIP format’s 65,535-byte limit, and while the English singular case (`pathlen == 1`) is unreachable in practice, the change is still valuable for languages with more complex plural rules, like Polish.

The patch is minimal—just six lines changed in `write_zip_entry()`—and has no dependencies or interactions with other work. All prior feedback has been addressed, including Junio’s mechanical nit about indentation and Phillip Wood’s procedural note about patch applicability. The v6 patch applies cleanly to `master` and is ready for inclusion in `next`. Both Junio and Phillip have endorsed the approach, and no further discussion is expected. This is a straightforward, well-motivated fix that improves Git’s i18n support without altering logic, tests, or documentation.
