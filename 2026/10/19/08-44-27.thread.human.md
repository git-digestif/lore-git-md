This thread is about a small but important i18n improvement to Git’s ZIP archive backend. The patch, now in its fourth version, replaces a hardcoded singular/plural error message with Git’s `Q_()` macro to support proper pluralization in all languages, not just English. The change is confined to `archive-zip.c` and has no logic, test, or documentation implications.

All prior feedback from Phillip Wood and Junio C Hamano has been addressed: the commit message now clearly explains why `Q_()` is valuable even though the English singular case is unreachable, and minor mechanical issues (whitespace, line length, macro alignment) have been fixed. Both reviewers have endorsed the approach, and the patch is ready for Junio to pick up for `next`. The author has sent a follow-up ping to confirm there are no remaining blockers, signaling the patch is complete and uncontroversial.
