The decision is purely logistical; no technical objections remain, and the v4 series itself remains uncontested and ready for integration.

The exchange clarifies that the fix will land in Patrick’s next CI-related series, which will also tackle other deferred improvements like structural refactoring of the CI scripts. For now, the Rust coverage gap remains open, but the path forward is clear.
