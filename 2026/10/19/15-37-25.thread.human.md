This small i18n improvement for `git fsck` is now ready to land. Aniket Gupta's patch replaces a hardcoded plural form in the verbose "Checking connectivity" message with Git's `Q_()` macro, allowing translators to handle languages with more complex plural rules than English. Junio has reviewed and approved the change, noting that while the displayed count isn't perfectly accurate (it reflects the object hash table size rather than the true object count), using `Q_()` is still the right approach for internationalization. He suggests the count's inaccuracy could be flagged with a `NEEDSWORK` comment in the future but agrees it's a separate concern from this patch.

The change is minimal—just one line in `builtin/fsck.c`—and doesn't affect English output or on-disk formats. It's part of a broader GSoC effort to improve Git's translation support. Junio's "Will queue" means the patch will soon appear in his integration branches, starting with `seen` before moving to `next` and eventually graduating to `master`. No further discussion or changes are expected.
