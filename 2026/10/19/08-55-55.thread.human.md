This thread introduces a new mechanism for pluggable object database backends in Git, allowing alternative storage systems (like cloud storage) to be used in place of the default `.git/objects` directory. The series, authored by Patrick Steinhardt, defines the `extensions.objectStorage` configuration and implements a baseline `files` backend that supports relocating objects via a URI-like payload (e.g., `files://objs` moves objects to `.git/objs`). It also adds user-facing tools to inspect the configured backend, environment variable overrides, and submodule integration.

The series is now complete at version 1 and under active review. Karthik Nayak has provided the most substantive feedback so far, raising design questions about path normalization consistency, naming clarity in inspection tools, and test coverage gaps—particularly around environment variable handling and payload validation. Junio C Hamano recently chimed in on a minor code hygiene point (an unnecessary `gettext.h` include), defending its inclusion as a matter of explicitness but also questioning whether the subsystem should expose user-facing strings at all. The exchange was narrow and doesn’t affect the series’ core functionality.

No major objections or conditional approvals have emerged yet, and the series remains uncontroversial in its baseline implementation. The local clone restriction (disabling hardlinks/copies when non-default storage is used) may attract further discussion, but for now, the open questions are largely about design consistency and robustness rather than fundamental flaws. The next steps will likely involve Patrick addressing Karthik’s feedback or clarifying the design choices before the series moves toward integration.
