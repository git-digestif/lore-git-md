This is a small i18n (internationalization) patch targeting the ZIP archive backend in Git. The change replaces a simple `_()` gettext wrapper with `Q_()`, which is Git’s plural-form helper. The error message in question is triggered when a path exceeds the ZIP format’s 16-bit length limit (0xffff bytes). Although the condition guarantees `pathlen` is always greater than 0xffff, the patch ensures that translators can supply language-specific singular and plural forms if needed. The modification is confined to a single line in `archive-zip.c` and carries no behavior change for English users.
