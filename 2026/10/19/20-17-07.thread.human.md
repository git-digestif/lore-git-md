This thread is about a bugfix patch from Taylor Blau that addresses a subtle ordering bug in Git's skipping negotiator, a component of the fetch machinery. The issue involves how parent commits are queued: they were being added to the queue before their date fields were parsed, leading to incorrect sorting and potential premature termination of negotiation. The fix ensures parents are parsed (and thus have correct dates) before being queued, preventing recent common ancestors from being delayed behind older ref tips.

The patch is narrowly scoped, touching only `negotiator/skipping.c` and adding a test case to `t/t5552-skipping-fetch-negotiator.sh`. The test demonstrates the bug by creating a scenario with 32 old ref tips and verifies that the two most recent commits (HEAD and its grandparent) are offered early in negotiation. The commit message and test coverage are both thorough, making the problem and solution clear. There are no review comments or objections yet, so the patch appears uncontested and ready for integration.
