This thread addressed a correctness bug in Git's skipping negotiator during fetch negotiation, where parent commits were added to the queue before their date fields were parsed, leading to incorrect prioritization of recent common ancestors. Taylor Blau authored the fix, which evolved from a targeted patch into a broader refactoring that moves commit parsing from `push_parent()` to `rev_list_push()`. This ensures all commits are parsed (and thus have correct dates) before being added to the queue, aligning the skipping negotiator with the default negotiator and eliminating a redundant commit-parsing call.

The patch, now at v2, has received full review and approval. Patrick Steinhardt proposed the architectural refactoring, which Junio C Hamano endorsed, and both reviewers signed off on the final version. The test script was updated for robustness, replacing `sed -n "1,2p"` with `head -n 2` to verify the two most recent commits are offered first during negotiation. No open questions remain, and the patch is ready for merging. Key participants included Taylor Blau (author), Patrick Steinhardt (reviewer), and Junio C Hamano (maintainer). The fix touches `negotiator/skipping.c` and the test script, with no unresolved loose ends.
