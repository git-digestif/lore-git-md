This is the author’s reply to Patrick Steinhardt’s review of the fifth patch in a five-patch series improving `git stash` shell completion. The series refactors completion logic, fixes a Zsh-specific bug, and removes support for the deprecated `git stash save` subcommand.

D. Ben Knoble acknowledges Patrick’s concern about whether the patch should preserve option autocompletion for manually typed `git stash save` (e.g., `git stash save --<TAB>`). He plans to test the behavior with `GIT_COMPLETION_SHOW_ALL` set and suggests that if option completion still works in that case, it might address the usability trade-off Patrick raised. The response is brief and focused on clarifying the patch’s behavior rather than defending a design choice, leaving the door open for a v2 adjustment. No new technical details or objections are introduced.
