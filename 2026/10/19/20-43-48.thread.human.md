This thread is a small but telling documentation tweak to `MyFirstContribution.adoc`. Junio Hamano noticed that new contributors often rush to send a v2 patch before engaging with review feedback, so he proposed reordering the tutorial to place the "Responding to Reviews" section before "Sending v2" and sharpening the language to say "Do not send v2 before you are done" instead of the softer "It is good manners to do so."

Patrick Steinhardt reviewed the patch and suggested splitting the structural reordering from the wording changes to make review easier, which Junio agreed was a good idea. Patrick also mildly objected to the blanket advice "do not start coding" during review, noting that some exploratory coding can be productive, but he conceded the stricter wording might be justified given what the project has observed. Otherwise, his feedback was surface-level—typos, grammar, and formatting—with no technical objections. Junio plans to revise the patch in a few days to incorporate any further feedback, but the core goal remains unchanged: to discourage premature v2 submissions. The thread is still under review, with no decisions finalized yet.
