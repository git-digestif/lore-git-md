This thread is a small but telling documentation tweak to `MyFirstContribution.adoc`, led by Junio C Hamano. The goal is to nudge new contributors toward engaging with review feedback before rushing out a v2 patch—a pattern the project has seen too often. The series is now at v2, split into two patches: the first simply reorders the tutorial sections (moving "Sending v2" after "Responding to Reviews"), and the second sharpens the wording to discourage premature updates.

The reordering patch is uncontroversial and effectively done; Patrick Steinhardt’s latest reply is a quick thumbs-up, noting the change is trivial and easy to follow. The second patch, which tightens the language ("Do not send v2 before you are done discussing"), still has mild pushback from Patrick, who points out that some exploratory coding during discussion can be productive. Junio has kept the stricter phrasing, arguing it matches observed contributor behavior. There’s also a minor stylistic suggestion to retain a removed sentence for context, but no one has objected to its removal. Nothing here is blocking integration—both patches are under review, with the first essentially ready to go.
