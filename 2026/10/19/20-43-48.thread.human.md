This thread updates the `MyFirstContribution` tutorial to better guide new contributors through the review process. Junio C Hamano posted a two-patch series (now at v2) that reorders the tutorial to emphasize responding to reviews *before* sending a v2, and strengthens the language to discourage premature updates. The first patch, a simple structural reordering, drew no objections and appears ready to merge. The second patch, which tightens the wording—including a blanket "do not start coding" until discussion is complete—sparked mild pushback from Patrick Steinhardt, who argued that some exploratory coding during review can be productive. Junio kept the stricter phrasing, citing observed contributor behavior.

The changes are purely editorial, touching only the tutorial’s prose in `Documentation/MyFirstContribution.adoc`. No code, tests, or new symbols are involved. The series remains under review, with no merge to `master` or integration branches reported. The core goal—encouraging discussion before iteration—is uncontroversial, but the phrasing may see further tweaks.
