This thread proposes changing the default behavior of `git status` to compare the current branch against both `@{upstream}` and `@{push}` by default, making it more useful in triangular workflows where the push destination differs from the upstream. The author, Harald Nordgren, sent a second version (v2) of the patch that addressed all substantive feedback from the first round, including expanding the commit message to provide historical context and adding explicit test cases for both triangular and common-case scenarios.

The patch was previously considered ready for further review or integration, with no technical objections remaining. However, Junio C Hamano has now raised a fundamental challenge to the patch's justification. He argues that the benefit of showing both comparisons by default is unclear, particularly since `@{push}` is typically under the user's control and less likely to introduce surprises than `@{upstream}`. Junio also questions whether users new to triangular workflows are less likely to discover the `status.compareBranches` setting than those who have already configured it. His review reopens the discussion on the patch's core premise, suggesting that the patch may need a v3 or further discussion to address his concerns about the default change. The thread is now focused on whether the proposed benefit justifies the potential disruption to existing users.
