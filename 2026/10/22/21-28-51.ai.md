This is the third patch in Andy Koppe’s five-part documentation series aligning Git’s terminology around "pseudorefs" and "root refs" with the 2024 redefinition (commit 993d57eded). The patch updates six manual pages—`git-update-ref(1)`, `git-worktree(1)`, `git-ls-remote(1)`, `for-each-ref-options(7)`, `rev-list-options(7)`, and `core.logAllRefUpdates` in `config/core.adoc`—to replace outdated uses of "pseudoref" with "root ref" and correct related inaccuracies.

The changes are mechanical but precise: HEAD and ORIG_HEAD are now consistently called "root refs," the `--refs` option in `git-ls-remote` is clarified to omit refs outside `refs/` (rather than "pseudorefs"), and `git-worktree(1)` now states that both root refs and pseudorefs are per-worktree without tying them to a specific on-disk location (reftable compatibility). The patch also fixes minor inaccuracies, such as removing ORIG_HEAD from `git-update-ref(1)`’s reflog documentation (only HEAD gets a reflog by default) and correcting the scope of `core.logAllRefUpdates` to include root refs.

No behavioral changes are introduced; the patch is purely about terminology consistency. The diff is small (16 insertions, 18 deletions) and touches only documentation files, making it low-risk. The series as a whole is uncontroversial and appears well-scoped, addressing a lingering source of confusion for users and contributors. This patch is a straightforward follow-up to the 2024 terminology change and should integrate cleanly.
