This is the v2 iteration of a bugfix patch targeting the skipping negotiator in Git's fetch machinery, addressing a correctness issue in commit queue prioritization.

The patch implements the agreed refactoring to move commit parsing from `push_parent()` to `rev_list_push()`, ensuring all commits are parsed (and thus have their correct date set) before being added to the queue. This broader change supersedes the original targeted fix, covers all callsites, aligns the skipping negotiator with the default negotiator, and eliminates a redundant `repo_parse_commit()` call in `get_rev()`. The commit message now clearly explains the architectural rationale and the NULL-return handling required by the skipping negotiator's `rev_list_push()` signature.

Key technical details: the patch touches `negotiator/skipping.c` (moving commit parsing to `rev_list_push()` and adding a NULL check in `push_parent()`) and updates the test in `t/t5552-skipping-fetch-negotiator.sh` to use `head -n2` instead of `sed -n "1,2p"` for robustness. The test creates 32 old ref tips to demonstrate the bug and verifies that the two most recent commits (HEAD and its grandparent) are offered in the first two "have" lines during negotiation, confirming correct prioritization by date.

The range-diff against v1 shows the evolution from a targeted fix to the broader refactoring, with improved commit message clarity and test script robustness. All prior feedback has been addressed, including Patrick Steinhardt's architectural proposal and the minor test script nit. The patch is ready for final review and integration.
