This is the first patch in a four-part bugfix series for `git imap-send`, targeting a process lifecycle issue in the tunnel-command handling. The patch ensures the tunnel command (specified via `imap.tunnel`) is properly reaped when the IMAP connection closes, fixing a long-standing leak where the child process and its argument list were never waited for. The fix is minimal: it stores the `child_process` struct in the `imap` context (rather than on the stack) and calls `finish_command()` during `imap_close_server()`. No test coverage exists for the tunnel functionality, so the leak went unnoticed until Patrick Steinhardt flagged it in review.

The change is straightforward and correct: the tunnel command is now managed like other Git subprocesses, and the leak is plugged at the right place (connection teardown). The commit message clearly explains the history (the bug dates back to the 2009 conversion to the `run-command` API) and the rationale for the fix. This patch resolves one of the three open technical questions from the `v2` review, and its isolation makes it easy to verify. No backward-compatibility concerns or new behaviors are introduced.
