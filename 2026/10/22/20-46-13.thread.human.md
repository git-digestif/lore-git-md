This thread is about a small but useful refactoring in Git’s log subsystem. Miklos Vajna is cleaning up how merge commits display their diffs—either a remerge diff or a combined diff—by moving the existing logic into a new helper function, `log_tree_merge_diff()`. The patch itself is the second in a 4-patch series (v3) and is purely preparatory: it introduces no functional changes, just extracts code to avoid duplication before a later patch reuses it.

The change is confined to `log-tree.c` and is straightforward: 14 lines of conditional logic are lifted into the new helper, which is then called in their place. The helper preserves the exact same behavior—skipping remerge-diff for octopus merges, calling `do_remerge_diff()` when appropriate, and falling back to `do_diff_combined()` otherwise. So far, there’s been no review traffic on this version, so the patch is still waiting for feedback before it can move forward.
