This is the final patch in Andy Koppe’s five-part documentation and terminology cleanup series, which aligns Git’s tests with the 2024 redefinition of "pseudoref" and "root ref." The series as a whole is a well-scoped, mechanical follow-up to commit 993d57eded, ensuring consistency across glossary entries, man pages, code comments, and now the test suite.

Patch 5/5 updates ten test scripts to replace outdated uses of "pseudoref" with "root ref" where the old term referred to refs outside the `refs/` hierarchy (e.g., HEAD, ORIG_HEAD). It also renames test refs in `t1400-update-ref.sh` and `t4013-diff-various.sh` from `PSEUDOREF` and `REVERSE` to `ROOT_REF_HEAD` and `REVERSE_HEAD`, respectively, to follow the naming conventions for root refs. The patch includes a file rename in `t4013/` to match the new ref name in expected output. Additionally, it corrects lingering references to FETCH_HEAD and MERGE_HEAD as "special refs," now calling them pseudorefs as defined in the glossary.

The changes are purely terminological and do not alter test behavior or coverage. The patch is uncontroversial and appears to complete the series’ goal of eliminating inconsistent terminology. If the earlier patches in the series were well-received, this one should be a straightforward merge. No new questions or loose ends are introduced.
