His stance aligns with the RFC author’s preference for a simpler, more explicit modifier like `--into origin/master`, though he does not restate that position here.

The email does not advance the discussion toward a resolution but reinforces the tension between **extensibility** (Knoble’s composable syntax) and **simplicity** (the RFC’s original proposal).
