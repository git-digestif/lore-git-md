This is the next round in the CLI design discussion for Harald Nordgren’s `git branch --delete-merged` series, which aims to detect squash-merged and rebase-merged branches. The thread is now focused on naming the new flag and structuring its granularity.

Harald Nordgren responds to Junio C Hamano’s naming proposals, expressing a strong preference for `--delete-integrated[=<level>]`. He argues that "integrated" is unambiguous, aligns with Git’s existing documentation, and clearly conveys the goal of branch maintenance ("getting your work integrated"). He dismisses "done" as unclear, associating it with script completion rather than branch deletion. While he acknowledges Junio’s concern about the word’s length, he prioritizes clarity and familiarity over brevity.

The email does not revisit the hierarchical structure (`merged`, `rebased`, `squashed`) or the unresolved questions about whether the new flag should enable both squash and rebase detection by default. It also does not address the algorithmic trade-offs (e.g., patch IDs, author checks, or `git range-diff` for rebase detection) or the performance implications of the chosen approach. The discussion remains narrowly focused on naming, with Harald’s preference now clearly stated. The next steps will likely involve broader input from the list on the naming question before the series can proceed to implementation.
