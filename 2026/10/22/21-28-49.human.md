This is the first patch in a documentation cleanup series that aligns Git’s glossary with a 2024 terminology change distinguishing "pseudorefs" (now only FETCH_HEAD and MERGE_HEAD) from "root refs" (refs outside the `refs/` hierarchy like HEAD and ORIG_HEAD).

The patch adds a new "root ref" glossary entry, moves the naming rules and irregular refs list from the existing "ref" entry into it, and clarifies the "pseudoref" entry to state that pseudorefs are not refs at all. It also updates cross-references to point to `gitrevisions(7)` for descriptions of common root refs and pseudorefs. The changes are mechanical but thorough, reflecting the project’s current terminology.

This patch is well-scoped and uncontroversial, focusing on documentation consistency rather than behavior changes. It’s a logical follow-up to the 2024 refactoring and should help avoid confusion for users and contributors. The commit message is clear, and the diff is clean. No functional regressions or new features are introduced.
