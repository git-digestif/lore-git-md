Subject: [PATCH v3 0/4] log --follow: performance improvements for rename following in merge-heavy histories
Series version: v4 (six patches)
Originating author: Miklos Vajna
Thread type: patch series

Core problem or goal
Improve performance of `git log --follow` when tracking file history across complex merge topologies (e.g., diamond merges). The current implementation is slow because it performs redundant diff calculations when following renames through non-linear histories; the series also fixes three bugs: `--follow` losing history on the second parent of a merge, incorrect behavior of `git log -m --follow`, and `--diff-merges=first-parent` losing history when combined with `--follow`.

Patrick Steinhardt’s procedural feedback (inline replies, `range-diff` preference) has been noted but not yet acted upon.
