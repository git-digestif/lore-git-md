On granularity, Junio reiterates his preference for a hierarchical `--delete-foo=<mode>` structure (`merged`, `rebased`, `squashed`), where each broader mode includes all narrower ones. This ensures users don’t accidentally exclude normally merged branches when opting into looser detection. He avoids extending `--delete-merged` with an optional value due to Git’s convention against such options.
