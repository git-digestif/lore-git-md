This is a documentation patch, part of Andy Koppe’s five-patch series to align Git’s glossary and scattered documentation with the 2024 terminology change that narrowed “pseudoref” to FETCH_HEAD and MERGE_HEAD only, while introducing “root ref” for refs outside the `refs/` hierarchy.

Patch 2/5 updates the glossary’s “per-worktree ref” entry, which was still claiming that only HEAD and refs under `refs/bisect/` are per-worktree. The patch brings it up to date by listing all root refs (HEAD, ORIG_HEAD, etc.) plus refs under `refs/worktree/` and `refs/rewritten/` as per-worktree, and clarifies that pseudorefs are also worktree-specific. It also tweaks the “worktree” entry to describe HEAD as a per-worktree ref and MERGE_HEAD as a pseudoref, rather than listing them separately.

The change is mechanical and uncontroversial: it simply corrects outdated glossary text to match the current code and the 2024 terminology shift. No behavior changes or new features are introduced. If the rest of the series lands, this patch will help users and contributors avoid confusion when reading the glossary.
