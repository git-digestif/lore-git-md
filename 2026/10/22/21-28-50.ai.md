This is a documentation patch, part of Andy Koppe’s five-patch series to align Git’s glossary and scattered documentation with the 2024 terminology change that narrowed “pseudoref” to FETCH_HEAD and MERGE_HEAD only, while introducing “root ref” for refs outside the refs/ hierarchy.

Patch 2/5 updates the “per-worktree ref” glossary entry, which was still claiming that only HEAD and refs under refs/bisect/ are per-worktree. The patch brings it up to date by listing all root refs (HEAD, ORIG_HEAD, etc.) plus refs under refs/worktree/ and refs/rewritten/ as per-worktree, and clarifies that pseudorefs are also worktree-specific. It also tweaks the “worktree” entry to describe HEAD as a per-worktree ref and MERGE_HEAD as a pseudoref, rather than listing them separately. The change is purely textual, touching only Documentation/glossary-content.adoc, and adds a cross-reference to git-worktree(1) for accessing other worktrees’ refs.

The patch is mechanical and uncontroversial, matching the rest of the series in scope and intent. It should help users and contributors avoid confusion when encountering outdated terminology in the glossary. No functional changes or new test coverage are introduced.
