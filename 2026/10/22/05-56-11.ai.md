This is the final review approval from Patrick Steinhardt for Justin Tobler’s **v4 four-patch bugfix series** targeting a race condition in Git’s ODB transaction machinery. The series fixes a bug where concurrent `git-receive-pack` processes could delete each other’s `.keep` lockfiles, leaving newly written packfiles vulnerable to deletion.

Patrick explicitly states he is **"happy with this version"**, confirming all prior feedback—including the final stylistic tweak (explicitly assigning the `ret` variable in `write_pack_lockfile()` on all exit paths)—has been addressed. This approval clears the series for integration; Junio C Hamano had already signaled readiness to merge once the v4 reroll was submitted.

The series touches `object-file.c`, `pack-write.c`, `pack.h`, and `t/t5547-push-quarantine.sh`, introducing or renaming helpers like `read_index_pack_report()` (now a public API), `write_pack_lockfile()`, and `write_pack_lockfiles()`. The test now verifies no `.pack` file exists alongside the `.keep` file in concurrent scenarios. The only lingering concern—whether leading directories for lockfiles are guaranteed to exist during quarantine—was flagged by Patrick in earlier reviews but not revisited here, and the author has not provided further justification or directory-creation logic. This is not expected to block merging.

The patches are part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort led by Patrick Steinhardt. With this approval, the series is now **technically complete and ready for merging** into Junio’s integration branches.
