This is the fifth and final patch in D. Ben Knoble’s five-part series improving `git stash` shell completion. The patch removes completion support for the long-deprecated `git stash save` subcommand (deprecated since 2017). The author has now tested the behavior of option autocompletion when a user manually types `git stash save --<TAB>` and reports that, even after removing `save` from the list of completable subcommands, option autocompletion still works. This resolves the last open design question raised by Patrick Steinhardt, who had suggested documenting the role of `GIT_COMPLETION_SHOW_ALL` if completion were disabled. Since option autocompletion remains functional, no additional documentation is needed. The author plans to update the commit message to reflect the observed behavior before sending a v2 of the series. The patch itself is uncontroversial and appears ready to proceed.
