This thread is about a documentation cleanup series that aligns Git’s terminology with a 2024 change distinguishing "pseudorefs" (only `FETCH_HEAD` and `MERGE_HEAD`) from "root refs" (refs outside the `refs/` hierarchy, like `HEAD` and `ORIG_HEAD`). The series, now in its fourth version, updates the glossary, man pages, code comments, and tests to consistently use these terms and correct related inaccuracies. Andy Koppe, the author, has incorporated all feedback from Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt, including editorial tweaks to the glossary and expanded explanations in `gitdatamodel.adoc` based on Julia Evans’s suggestions.

The changes are purely terminological and touch 36 files, including the glossary, seven man pages, four code files, ten test scripts, and the bash completion script. Key updates include a new "root ref" glossary entry with an exhaustive list of irregular root refs and naming rules, a rewritten "pseudoref" entry emphasizing their unique semantics (multi-object storage, metadata), and consistent terminology updates across documentation and error messages. The series also clarifies the scope of `core.logAllRefUpdates` and corrects minor inaccuracies in man pages. No behavioral changes are introduced, and the series is uncontroversial and ready for merging. The only deferred issue is Git’s current leniency in treating arbitrary files outside `refs/` as refs, which Andy notes as a potential future tightening.
