This thread is a documentation cleanup series by Andy Koppe that aligns Git’s terminology with a 2024 change distinguishing "root refs" (like HEAD and ORIG_HEAD) from "pseudorefs" (like FETCH_HEAD and MERGE_HEAD). The series updates the glossary, man pages, code comments, and tests to replace outdated references to "pseudoref" with the new terminology, incorporating Julia Evans’s proposal to expand the `gitdatamodel.adoc` guide to cover both categories explicitly.

Version 2 of the series, posted on June 17, 2025, addresses all review feedback from Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt except for two minor documentation nits in Patch 3/5. Junio praised the v1 series as "a pleasant read" but hasn’t reviewed v2 yet, while Patrick’s suggestions—such as tightening commit messages and improving glossary entries—are fully incorporated in v2. The series remains uncontroversial and appears ready for merging, with no behavioral changes or on-disk format modifications.

Julia Evans, whose pedagogical documentation proposal was integrated into v2, recently asked a clarifying question about the on-disk representation of root refs versus pseudorefs. While both ORIG_HEAD (a root ref) and MERGE_HEAD (a pseudoref) appear as plain files in `.git/`, the documentation describes them as stored differently. This isn’t a blocker for the series but highlights a potential gap in the documentation’s technical accuracy, which could be addressed in a future follow-up. The thread otherwise remains focused on terminology consistency and is poised for graduation once the remaining nits are resolved.
