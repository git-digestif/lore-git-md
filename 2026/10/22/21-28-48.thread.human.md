This thread is a five-patch documentation series from Andy Koppe, posted on June 10, 2025, that aligns Git’s terminology with a 2024 change narrowing "pseudoref" to just `FETCH_HEAD` and `MERGE_HEAD` while introducing "root ref" for refs outside the `refs/` hierarchy (like `HEAD` and `ORIG_HEAD`). The series updates the glossary, man pages, built-in commands, and test scripts to replace outdated terminology and clarify related concepts like per-worktree refs and reflog behavior.

The third patch, just summarized, focuses on six manual pages—`git-update-ref(1)`, `git-worktree(1)`, `git-ls-remote(1)`, and others—to consistently use "root ref" for `HEAD` and `ORIG_HEAD`, correct inaccuracies (e.g., removing `ORIG_HEAD` from reflog documentation), and clarify that `core.logAllRefUpdates` applies to root refs. The changes are mechanical and low-risk, with no behavioral impact. The series has not yet attracted substantive review but is positioned as uncontroversial and well-scoped, addressing lingering confusion from the 2024 terminology shift. No open questions or objections have been raised so far.
