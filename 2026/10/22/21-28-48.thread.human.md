This thread is a five-patch documentation cleanup series by Andy Koppe, updating Git’s terminology to align with a 2024 change that narrowed "pseudoref" to FETCH_HEAD and MERGE_HEAD while introducing "root ref" for refs outside the `refs/` hierarchy (e.g., HEAD, ORIG_HEAD). The series targets the glossary, man pages, built-in commands, refs internals, test scripts, and pedagogical documentation, incorporating Julia Evans’s proposal to expand `gitdatamodel.adoc` to cover these concepts explicitly.

Version 2, posted on June 17, 2025, addresses all review feedback from Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt. The patches are uncontroversial and appear ready for merging, though Junio has not yet reviewed v2 or explicitly acked the series. The latest update (v2 of Patch 2/5) refines the glossary entries for "per-worktree ref" and "worktree" to use itemized AsciiDoc lists, clarifying the distinction between root refs and pseudorefs while simplifying the prose. Patrick’s editorial suggestions—such as converting the prose list to an itemized format and streamlining the "worktree" entry—are now fully incorporated.

No behavioral changes are introduced, and the series remains focused on consistency and clarity. The only remaining loose ends are minor documentation nits in Patch 3/5 (ambiguous phrasing in `git-update-ref(1)` and outdated reflog storage explanations for reftable), which the author has noted but not yet resolved. The series depends on the 2024 terminology change (commit 993d57eded) and has no external dependencies.
