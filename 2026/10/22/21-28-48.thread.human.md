This thread is a documentation cleanup series by Andy Koppe, posted on June 10, 2025, to align Git’s terminology with a 2024 change that narrowed "pseudoref" to FETCH_HEAD and MERGE_HEAD while introducing "root ref" for refs outside the `refs/` hierarchy (like HEAD and ORIG_HEAD). The series updates the glossary, man pages, code comments, and tests across 36 files, replacing outdated uses of "pseudoref" and clarifying related concepts like per-worktree refs. Junio C Hamano has signaled general approval ("a pleasant read"), but the series remains under review with no merge status reported.

The key open questions revolve around glossary phrasing and editorial refinements. Junio and Patrick Steinhardt both objected to the glossary’s "pseudoref" definition omitting "different semantics," which they argue is critical to explaining why pseudorefs can’t be written with `git update-ref`. Patrick also suggested converting prose lists of per-worktree refs into itemized AsciiDoc lists for better readability and maintainability, and flagged outdated reflog storage explanations in `git-update-ref(1)`. These issues remain unaddressed. Julia Evans recently chimed in to note that the pedagogical `gitdatamodel.adoc` document should also be updated to cover pseudorefs and root refs explicitly, as it currently only mentions ORIG_HEAD in passing. This would be a straightforward addition to the series.

The series has no behavioral changes—only terminology and documentation updates—but the glossary’s structure and clarity are still under discussion. No new patch version has been posted yet.
