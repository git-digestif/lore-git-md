The series updates the glossary, man pages, built-in commands, refs internals, and test scripts to consistently use the new terms and clarify their behavioral differences—pseudorefs can store multiple object IDs and metadata, while root refs are simpler and handled by the ref backend. Julia Evans’s proposal to expand the pedagogical documentation in `gitdatamodel.adoc` is fully incorporated, and all prior review feedback from Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt has been addressed.
