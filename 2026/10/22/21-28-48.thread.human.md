This thread is a five-patch series by Andy Koppe to align Git’s documentation, code comments, and tests with a 2024 terminology change that narrowed "pseudoref" to FETCH_HEAD and MERGE_HEAD only, while introducing "root ref" for refs outside the "refs/" hierarchy (e.g., HEAD, ORIG_HEAD). The series updates the glossary, man pages, built-in commands, refs internals, and test scripts to replace outdated terminology and correct related inaccuracies.

Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, has signaled general approval of the series as "a pleasant read" but has not yet provided a formal ack or merged it. Patrick Steinhardt has reviewed the first three patches substantively, reinforcing Junio’s feedback on the glossary phrasing (suggesting the term "different semantics" be restored) and proposing editorial improvements to the "per-worktree ref" and "worktree" glossary entries. His latest review of the fourth patch is editorial, focusing on tightening the commit message while approving the code changes themselves. The fifth patch remains unreviewed.

The series touches 36 files, including the glossary, six man pages, built-in commands, refs internals, and test scripts. Key changes include updated glossary definitions, corrected documentation in man pages (e.g., clarifying that `--refs` in `git-ls-remote` omits refs outside `refs/`), and in-code comment updates. No behavioral changes are introduced, and the on-disk format remains unchanged. Open questions include whether the glossary’s phrasing adequately explains why pseudorefs cannot be written with `git update-ref` and whether the "per-worktree ref" documentation should be restructured for better readability. The completion helper `__git_pseudoref_exists()` remains named as-is due to ambiguity about a term covering both root refs and pseudorefs.
