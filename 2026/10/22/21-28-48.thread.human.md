This thread is a five-patch documentation cleanup series by Andy Koppe, now at v2, that aligns Git’s terminology with a 2024 change narrowing "pseudoref" to FETCH_HEAD and MERGE_HEAD while introducing "root ref" for refs outside the `refs/` hierarchy (e.g., HEAD, ORIG_HEAD). The series updates the glossary, man pages, built-in commands, refs internals, test scripts, and pedagogical documentation, incorporating Julia Evans’s proposal to expand `gitdatamodel.adoc` to cover pseudorefs and root refs explicitly.

The series is under review and largely uncontroversial. Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt have both provided positive feedback on v1, and v2 addresses all substantive review comments except for three lingering documentation nits: ambiguous phrasing in `git-update-ref(1)` about `core.logAllRefUpdates`, outdated reflog storage details for the reftable backend, and a commit message mismatch in Patch 2/5 (claiming MERGE_HEAD is described as a pseudoref while the diff removes it). Andy considers the first two out of scope for this series, deferring them to a future effort. The latest exchange with Patrick Steinhardt adds two minor editorial suggestions for Patch 3/5—dropping "also" in `gitdatamodel.adoc` and adding glossary cross-references—but these are stylistic and don’t affect the technical content. The series appears ready for merging once Andy incorporates these final tweaks. No merge status has been reported, and the thread remains focused on documentation clarity rather than behavioral changes.
