This thread is a documentation and terminology cleanup series by Andy Koppe, aiming to align Git’s glossary, man pages, code comments, and tests with a 2024 terminology change that narrowed "pseudoref" to FETCH_HEAD and MERGE_HEAD while introducing "root ref" for refs outside the "refs/" hierarchy (e.g., HEAD, ORIG_HEAD). The series, now at v1, updates 36 files, including the glossary, six man pages, built-in commands, refs internals, and test scripts, without introducing behavioral changes.

Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, has reviewed the series and called it "a pleasant read," indicating general approval. However, he previously raised a substantive objection to the glossary’s phrasing in Patch 1/5, arguing that omitting "different semantics" obscures why pseudorefs like FETCH_HEAD and MERGE_HEAD cannot be written with `git update-ref`. This objection remains unresolved, though Junio did not revisit it in his latest reply. The series is still under review, with no merge status reported.

The key open question is whether the glossary’s wording adequately explains the distinction between pseudorefs and root refs, particularly for users unfamiliar with the 2024 terminology change. The series also leaves the completion helper `__git_pseudoref_exists()` named as-is, reflecting ambiguity about what term should cover both pseudorefs and root refs. No other major objections or requests for changes have been raised so far.
