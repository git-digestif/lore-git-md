This is a documentation and terminology cleanup series. Andy Koppe addresses lingering confusion between "pseudorefs" (now strictly `FETCH_HEAD` and `MERGE_HEAD`) and "root refs" (refs like `HEAD` and `ORIG_HEAD` that live outside the `refs/` directory). The series updates the glossary, man pages, code comments, and tests to consistently use the modern terminology introduced in 2024.

The five patches are mechanical and focused: the first two add and clarify glossary entries, the next three sweep through documentation, source code, and test scripts replacing outdated references. The changes are small (mostly one- or two-line tweaks) but touch 21 files across the tree, including user-facing docs like `git-update-ref(1)` and `git-worktree(1)`.

The motivation is clear: since the 2024 redefinition, inconsistent terminology has persisted in the codebase, which could mislead users and contributors. The series looks well-scoped and uncontroversial; it’s the kind of cleanup that quietly improves clarity without changing behavior.
