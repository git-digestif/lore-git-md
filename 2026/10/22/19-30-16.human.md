Junio also questions whether the patch stops at the right boundary—whether it should address the `for_each_remote()` limitation now rather than leaving it for later.

Junio’s proposal, if adopted, would make the codebase more robust but could complicate ongoing work by surfacing hidden dependencies earlier. The patch itself remains unchanged for now, but the discussion suggests future iterations may need to address the `for_each_remote()` issue or at least document it more prominently.
