This is a documentation patch series targeting `MyFirstContribution.adoc`, now in its fourth iteration. The goal is to reorder and tighten the tutorial to make it clearer that new contributors should engage in review discussion before sending a v2 patch, rather than treating v2 preparation as a mechanical next step after v1.

The series consists of two patches. The first moves the "Sending v2" sections (for both `git send-email` and `b4` workflows) from immediately after "Sending v1" to after the "Responding to Reviews" section. This structural change is uncontroversial and has been confirmed as easy to follow with `git diff --color-moved`. The second patch rewrites the "Reviewing" section with stronger, more direct language: replacing "It is good manners to do so" with "Do not send v2 before you have finished discussing the current one," and trimming about 20% of the word count without losing substance. The latest revision incorporates Junio’s own phrasing tweak—*"so that both sides know what to expect in the next round"*—which Patrick Steinhardt accepted, resolving the last open editorial concern.

The motivation is practical: the mailing list has seen an uptick in new contributors sending updated patch versions without first addressing review feedback, which wastes time for both authors and reviewers. The series directly addresses this by making the intended workflow explicit in the tutorial. No code or test changes are involved; this is purely a documentation improvement.

All feedback has been addressed, and the series is now ready for integration. The patches are well-motivated, clearly explained, and uncontroversial. If you care about onboarding new contributors, this is worth a quick look.
