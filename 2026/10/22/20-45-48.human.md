This is a test patch, part of a four-patch series from Miklos Vajna. It expands the test coverage for `git log --follow` in merge scenarios ahead of a later optimization in the series.

The patch adds 112 new test cases to `t/t4219-log-follow-merge.sh`, exercising three previously untested situations: following renames through forks with combined diff formats (`-c`, `--cc`, `--remerge-diff`), following renames taken from the second parent of a `--no-ff` merge (in both possible commit orders), and following a path that was kept in the merge but renamed on the other side. The commit message is clear and specific about what is being tested and why—these are regression tests for behavior that already works, so the upcoming optimization doesn’t accidentally break it.
