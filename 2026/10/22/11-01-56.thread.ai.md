Core problem or goal

The goal is to make the test runner silently ignore unrecognized flags (specifically `-l`/`--long-tests`) so that common Meson test invocations work as expected without altering test behavior. The series also includes a preparatory cleanup to maintain alphabetical order in the NOOP argument list.
