This is a documentation patch (part 1 of 2) updating `MyFirstContribution.adoc` to reorder the tutorial flow. The patch moves the "Sending v2" section *after* the "Responding to Reviews" section, reinforcing the expectation that contributors should engage with feedback before sending a new version. This is purely a structural change—no text is altered, only the placement of existing content blocks.

The motivation is clear: senior contributors have observed an uptick in new contributors sending v2 patches prematurely, without addressing review comments first. This reordering is a small but deliberate step to guide newcomers toward the intended workflow. The change is uncontroversial and aligns with prior feedback; Patrick Steinhardt confirmed the reordering is trivial and easy to follow with `git diff --color-moved`.

This patch is ready for integration and pairs with the second patch in the series, which tightens the language to explicitly discourage premature rerolls. Together, they address a real pain point in contributor onboarding without introducing new technical complexity. The series as a whole looks well-crafted and likely to be queued soon.
