This is a **follow-up discussion** in the six-patch series adding `transfer.connectivityCheck=incremental` (and `auto`) to speed up fetches and pushes. The thread is now focused on two **forward-looking maintainability concerns** raised by Junio C Hamano in his review of patch 5/6, both related to how the incremental mode interacts with promisor objects and lazy fetching.

Kristofer Karlsson **agrees with both points** and proposes adjustments. For the first issue — the unconditional `repo->fetch_if_missing = 0` in `parse_connectivity_check()` — he suggests simplifying further by **always disabling lazy fetching during any connectivity check**, since no check should trigger remote calls. This would remove the implicit assumption that only non-`full` modes avoid lazy fetching, making the behavior more explicit and future-proof.

For the second issue — the use of `odb_read_object_info()` in `verify_blob()` — Kristofer acknowledges that an `_extended()` variant with `OBJECT_INFO_SKIP_FETCH_OBJECT` would be clearer, but notes that **commit parsing lacks a similar flag** (`repo_parse_commit_gently()` has no `SKIP_FETCH` equivalent). He suggests deferring this refactoring to a **follow-up patch**, arguing that the current series is already large and the existing `fetch_if_missing = 0` approach is sufficient for now. He offers to add the change in a new commit if Junio prefers, but frames it as a **cleanup rather than a correctness fix**.

The tone remains **constructive and collaborative**. No fundamental objections are raised; these are **polish-level concerns** rather than blockers. The series as a whole — including the newly added **auto mode with a 1,000-commit cutoff** (patch 6/6) — is still **ready for integration** pending these minor adjustments. Kristofer’s responses suggest he will likely address both points in a **v5 reroll**, possibly as a single follow-up patch rather than a full series revision. The thread is **narrow in scope** but important for long-term maintainability.
