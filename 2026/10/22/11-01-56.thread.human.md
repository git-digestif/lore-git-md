This thread is about a small but practical fix to Git’s unit-test infrastructure. Kaartic Sivaraam sent a two-patch series (v2) that makes the `clar`-based test runner silently ignore the `-l`/`--long-tests` flag when invoked via `meson test --test-args='-ilx'`, ensuring compatibility without changing test behavior. Junio has already queued the series for integration, and no further changes were requested.

Jeff King (Peff) chimed in after the fact to note that he has just sent a separate, more ambitious patch that would make the entire NOOP argument mechanism—and thus this series—obsolete. He doesn’t ask for the current series to be dropped, but his message flags a potential future direction. For now, the series remains on track, and the fix will land as planned. If Peff’s larger patch gains traction, it could replace this approach in a later cycle.
