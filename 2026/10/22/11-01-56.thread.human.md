This thread is about a small but practical fix to Git’s unit test infrastructure. Kaartic Sivaraam sent a two-patch series (v1, now v2) that makes the `clar`-based unit test runner silently ignore the `-l`/`--long-tests` flag when it’s passed via `meson test --test-args='-ilx'`. Without the fix, users see a hard failure because the test binary rejects the unrecognized flag. The change is minimal—just four lines in `t/unit-tests/unit-test.c`—and follows an established pattern for NOOP arguments introduced in late 2024.

The series is uncontroversial. Patrick Steinhardt reviewed v1 and gave a surface-level thumbs-up, noting only a commit message typo (now fixed) and that the NOOP argument list was properly alphabetized. No one has raised substantive objections or requested changes. CI pipelines (GitHub, GitLab) confirm no regressions, though some macOS runners didn’t execute—unrelated to the patch. The series is ready for Junio to pick up.

The only loose end is a suggestion from Patrick for a future `--features-testbed` switch to simplify CI-like test configurations, but that’s explicitly out of scope here. The patches are part of the ongoing test modernization effort, specifically the adoption of the `clar` unit test framework.
