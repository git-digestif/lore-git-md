This thread is a small but practical fix to Git’s unit-test infrastructure. Kaartic Sivaraam sent a two-patch series that makes the `clar`-based test runner silently ignore the `-l`/`--long-tests` flag when it’s passed through `meson test --test-args`. The change is purely about Meson compatibility—no test behavior is altered—and consists of a four-line tweak to `t/unit-tests/unit-test.c` plus a preparatory cleanup to keep the NOOP argument list alphabetized.

The series has been uncontroversial from the start. Patrick Steinhardt reviewed both patches and gave a thumbs-up, noting only a typo in the commit message (`prerequiiste` → `prerequisite`). Kaartic has now fixed that typo, so the series is ready to be picked up. There are no open technical questions or objections; the only loose end is a tangential suggestion from Patrick about a future `--features-testbed` switch, which Kaartic acknowledged but deferred. CI pipelines have already confirmed no regressions, and the change is part of the broader effort to modernize Git’s test suite with the `clar` framework.
