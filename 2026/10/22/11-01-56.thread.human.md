This thread is about a small but practical fix to Git’s unit test infrastructure. Kaartic Sivaraam sent a two-patch series (now at v2) to make the `clar`-based unit test runner silently ignore the `-l`/`--long-tests` flag when invoked via `meson test --test-args='-ilx'`. The flag is forwarded by Meson but not recognized by the test binary, causing a hard failure; the fix adds it as a NOOP argument, following an established pattern from an earlier commit. The series also includes a preparatory cleanup to keep the NOOP argument list alphabetically ordered.

The change is uncontroversial and narrowly scoped, touching only four lines in `t/unit-tests/unit-test.c`. Patrick Steinhardt reviewed the first version, noting only a commit message typo and the alphabetical ordering—his feedback was surface-level, and no substantive objections or requests for changes have been raised. The series is ready for integration, with CI pipelines confirming no regressions. The only loose end is a suggestion from Patrick for a future `--features-testbed` switch to simplify CI-like test configurations, but that’s explicitly out of scope for this series. The work is part of the ongoing test modernization effort to adopt the `clar` unit test framework.
