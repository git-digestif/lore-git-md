This thread is about a bugfix for Git’s fsmonitor and untracked cache subsystems. The issue occurs when the fsmonitor daemon sends a trivial response (`"/"`), typically after a restart or event loss: Git marks all index entries as dirty but fails to invalidate stale untracked cache entries, causing untracked files to persist in `git status` output even after their directories are removed, and spurious warnings about missing directories.

The fix, authored by Ilia, introduces a new function `untracked_cache_invalidate_stale_dirs()` that recursively checks each cached directory against its on-disk stat data before the untracked cache is written to the index. This ensures stale entries are invalidated, with minimal overhead (one `lstat()` per cached directory, incurred only once per trivial response). The patch has been updated to address review feedback, including handling `repo_get_work_tree()` failures by invalidating the entire cache and removing redundant test comments. Trace2 instrumentation was also added to log invalidated directories.

The patch has received substantive review from Derrick Stolee, who confirmed the design’s performance and correctness, and from Junio C Hamano, who endorsed the final implementation. It is now ready for integration, with no further revisions expected unless new edge cases emerge during testing. The fix is narrowly scoped, well-motivated, and thoroughly tested, covering both hook-based and built-in fsmonitor backends.
