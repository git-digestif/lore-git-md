This thread is about a small but important bugfix for Git’s fsmonitor feature. When the fsmonitor daemon restarts or loses events, it sends a trivial response (`"/"`), which marks all index entries as dirty but previously left stale untracked cache entries untouched. This could cause `git status` to show phantom untracked files or emit spurious warnings about missing directories. The fix, authored by Ilia, adds a targeted invalidation of stale untracked cache directories when such a response is received.

The patch has been thoroughly reviewed and is now in its final form (v2). Derrick Stolee confirmed its performance impact is negligible, and Junio C Hamano endorsed the design and implementation details. The patch is currently in the `next` integration branch, meaning it’s undergoing final testing before graduating to `master`. Junio’s latest email is a procedural note: contributors should avoid rebasing patches already in `next` or `seen` unless there’s a genuine conflict, as rebasing disrupts the maintainer’s workflow. The patch itself remains unchanged and is on track for the next release.
