This thread is about a bugfix for Git's fsmonitor feature, where a trivial response from the fsmonitor daemon (like after a restart) fails to invalidate stale untracked cache entries. This causes `git status` to show incorrect untracked files or emit spurious warnings. The patch, authored by Ilia, has been through two iterations and is now in its final form (v2).

The fix introduces a new function, `untracked_cache_invalidate_stale_dirs()`, which ensures stale untracked cache entries are cleared when the daemon sends a trivial response. Derrick Stolee reviewed the patch substantively, measured its performance impact (confirming no measurable penalty), and gave it his LGTM. Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, also reviewed the patch and endorsed its design and implementation. The patch has been cooking in `next` and is on track for graduation to `master` after the usual testing period.

In the latest update, Junio performed a procedural adjustment—reverting and reapplying the same patch to preserve rerere history—but the patch itself remains unchanged. There are no open technical questions or loose ends; all feedback has been addressed, and the patch is ready for integration. The fix touches the fsmonitor and untracked cache subsystems, adds Trace2 instrumentation for logging, and includes test coverage for both hook-based and built-in fsmonitor backends.
