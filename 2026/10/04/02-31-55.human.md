This is a documentation discussion in the ongoing RFC series to create a `gitbreaking-changes(7)` manpage. The thread is now focused on a narrow formatting question: how to present message-ID references in the new manpage.

Junio C Hamano acknowledges that the hybrid URL-plus-message-ID format he proposed earlier does not work as intended. In HTML output, the message-ID portion is incorrectly rendered as a `mailto:` link, which would confuse users and defeat the goal of making references clickable. He also concedes that users may not recognize raw message-IDs as links at all. While he still prefers having the message-ID available for cut-and-paste (to support workflows that use email clients or newsreaders), he now accepts that the primary goal—usable, clickable links—should take precedence. He explicitly withdraws his earlier suggestion and endorses the simpler approach of using plain URLs, even though they are less elegant.

No new patch has been posted yet, but the technical disagreement is now resolved in principle. The next step is for Kristoffer Haugsbakk to update the patch to use plain URLs throughout. The broader series goal—making Git’s breaking changes more discoverable—remains uncontroversial and well-supported.
