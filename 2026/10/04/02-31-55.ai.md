This is a **documentation discussion** in the ongoing `gitbreaking-changes(7)` RFC series. The email is a reply from Junio C Hamano to Kristoffer Haugsbakk, addressing the unresolved formatting question for message-ID references in the new manpage.

Junio concedes that the hybrid URL+message-ID format he proposed is not viable due to Asciidoctor’s behavior: the message-ID portion is incorrectly rendered as a `mailto:` link in HTML output, making the link unusable. He also acknowledges Kristoffer’s concern that users may not recognize raw message-IDs as clickable links. As a result, Junio withdraws his objection to the plain-URL approach, accepting it as the pragmatic solution. He reiterates that while preserving raw message-IDs for future-proofing was a secondary goal, the primary goal—providing users with functional, recognizable links—takes precedence. The email closes by thanking Kristoffer for testing the hybrid format and catching the issue early.

The discussion is now resolved in favor of plain URLs, removing the last technical obstacle to the series. The next step is likely a v2 of the series incorporating this consensus, unless Kristoffer chooses to drop the patch entirely (though Junio’s concession makes that unlikely). No new files or symbols are introduced; the change remains purely about documentation formatting in `Documentation/gitbreaking-changes.adoc`. The broader goal of the series—making Git’s breaking changes more discoverable via a manpage—remains uncontroversial and on track.
