This is the first patch in Harald Nordgren’s v5 series extending `git branch --delete-merged` to detect squash-merged and rebase-merged branches. It is a pure refactoring: the `branch_opted_out()` helper extracts the existing `branch.<name>.deleteMerged` opt-out check from `delete_merged_branches()` so the same logic can be reused when the new `--delete-integrated` flag is introduced in patch 3. The patch touches only `builtin/branch.c` and makes no behavior change. It is uncontroversial and ready for integration; the thread AI summary confirms that the first two patches in the series are considered stable.
