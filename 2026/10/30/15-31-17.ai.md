This is a minor follow-up in the four-patch bugfix series that fixes a loose-object-idx migration bug in the ODB transaction layer. Justin Tobler replies that he does not feel strongly about the change and will leave the name as-is, noting that the suggestion alone does not warrant a reroll. The exchange is purely stylistic and has no impact on the series’ technical content or integration status. The naming inconsistency remains, but it is explicitly acknowledged as non-blocking.
