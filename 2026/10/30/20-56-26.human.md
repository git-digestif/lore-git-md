This is the first patch in the **final version (v5)** of Julia Evans’ documentation series replacing the outdated `gittutorial` man page with a modern, beginner-focused guide. The patch **deletes the old tutorial** in preparation for the new content, which is introduced in the second patch. This is a straightforward, uncontroversial change—no behavior is altered, and the old tutorial remains accessible in Git’s history until a future cleanup.

The deletion is structured as a separate commit to improve mailing-list readability, since the old and new content share no overlap. The patch touches only `Documentation/gittutorial.adoc`, removing 671 lines of text. All substantive feedback on the series has been addressed in prior versions, and this patch is purely mechanical.

Junio has already marked the series as ready for `next`, and the v4 version was merged to his `seen` branch. The only remaining loose end—a missing cross-reference update in `Documentation/git.adoc` that breaks `make check-docs`—was acknowledged by Junio but not treated as a blocker. The series is now queued for integration testing, with post-merge refinements planned based on user feedback.
