Junio identifies a latent bug in the new `has_required_headers()` helper: it assumes Unix-style line endings (`\n\n` to mark the end of headers) but `imap-send.c` historically expects IMAP’s CRLF line endings. The current implementation could scan past the header block into the message body, potentially misclassifying messages that happen to contain the strings `"From: "`, `"Date: "`, or `"Subject: "` in their body text.

The issue is narrowly scoped to this one function and does not invalidate the series’ core goal (order-agnostic header parsing), but it does introduce a correctness risk. Junio’s observation is precise and actionable, suggesting the fix will likely be a targeted adjustment to the line-ending logic rather than a redesign.
