This is a **bugfix and performance patch** targeting Git’s object quarantine mechanism, specifically addressing a regression where hidden refs are unnecessarily loaded during push connectivity checks. Jeff King (Peff) replies to Kristofer Karlsson’s v2 proposal, offering historical context and endorsing the current approach.

Peff confirms that the quarantine environment variable (`GIT_QUARANTINE_PATH`) was originally designed to carry more than just a boolean flag—it was intended as a rendezvous point for hooks to share data, such as lists of large objects rejected during `index-pack`. While this full vision was never upstreamed, the variable’s path-based design was deliberate, making it a natural fit for Kristofer’s fix. Peff explicitly approves of deducing the real object directory from the quarantine directory’s parent path, calling it "quite sensible" and aligning with Git’s existing logic for filtering self-referential alternates.

The discussion remains focused on the **architectural soundness** of the v2 approach, with no objections raised to its performance claims or test coverage. Peff’s feedback reinforces the patch’s direction, suggesting it is on track for integration. The only remaining question is whether ODB maintainers (Patrick Steinhardt, Justin Tobler) will weigh in on the parent-path assumption, though Peff’s endorsement makes this less likely to be a blocker. Expect this patch to land in `next` soon unless new concerns emerge.
