This specific patch targets `refresh_cache_entry()` in `read-cache.c`, which was made public in 2006 to support the now-removed "recursive" merge strategy and has had no external callers since April 2025.

The rationale for the reordering is future-proofing: if the code later moves around, the function will already be in the correct position. While a forward declaration might have been less noisy in the short term, Junio’s approach eliminates a potential maintenance burden. The patch is uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ goal of reducing global symbol exposure without changing behavior.
