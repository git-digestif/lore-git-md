This is the final patch in Harald Nordgren’s v13 series introducing `remote.<name>.fetchRefmap` for matching-inspired fetch mode. The patch fixes a specific edge case: `@{push}` resolution fails when a remote is added with `--limited-fetch` (which configures `remote.<name>.fetchRefmap` but not `remote.<name>.fetch`), because `tracking_for_push_dest()` only checks the traditional `fetch` refspecs.

The fix is minimal and mechanical: it extends the fallback logic in `tracking_for_push_dest()` to check `remote->refmap` if `remote->fetch` does not resolve the refname, mirroring the behavior already implemented in `get_ref_map()` for fetches. This ensures that `@{push}` works correctly for `--limited-fetch` remotes, just as it does for traditional remotes. The patch adds a new test in `t/t1514-rev-parse-push.sh` that verifies `@{push}` resolution after pushing to a `--limited-fetch` remote, covering the edge case explicitly.

The change is uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ design goal of making `fetchRefmap` a first-class citizen alongside `fetch` and `refmap`. It does not alter the precedence rules or core behavior of the series, and the test coverage is thorough. This patch is ready for merging and completes the v13 implementation. The series as a whole remains on track for graduation once the override semantics discussion (affecting earlier patches) is finalized in v14.
