This is the first patch in the now-merged four-part series that fixes a critical bug in Git’s ODB transaction layer. The patch itself is a small, uncontroversial change: it replaces an indefinite lock timeout (`-1`) with a finite 1000-millisecond timeout when locking the loose-object-idx file, preventing processes from hanging forever if a stale lockfile is left behind by a crashed writer.

Karthik Nayak’s reply is brief and surface-level. He notes that while a configurable timeout via Git config would be preferable (echoing an earlier suggestion from Patrick Steinhardt), the current approach is a reasonable incremental step. His comment is marked `#leftoverbits`, signaling that this is a minor follow-up idea rather than a blocker. The patch is already in `seen` and ready for integration; the suggestion here is purely forward-looking and does not affect the current series.
