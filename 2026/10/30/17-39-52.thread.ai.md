Thread identity
Subject: [PATCH 0/4] mark functions as static where no longer needed
Series version: v1 (initial submission)
Originating author: Junio C Hamano
Thread type: patch series (refactoring)

Core problem or goal
Mark four functions as `static` in their respective source files (`ident.c`, `add-interactive.c`, `read-cache.c`, `unpack-trees.c`) because they are no longer called from outside those files. The goal is tighter encapsulation and reduced global symbol exposure, with no behavior changes.

Current status
Under review; queued in `seen`. No prior versions. The series is complete with all four patches submitted. All substantive and surface-level reviews have endorsed the changes without requesting modifications. Junio’s latest reply resolves the last open stylistic point (a missing relative pronoun in patch 2/4’s commit message) as a matter of clarity, not correctness, and confirms no reroll is needed. The series is ready to proceed as-is.

Key technical details
Files touched: `ident.c`, `ident.h`, `add-interactive.c`, `add-interactive.h`, `read-cache.c`, `read-cache-ll.h`, `unpack-trees.c`, `unpack-trees.h`.
Symbols modified: `author_ident_sufficiently_given()` (deleted from both `.c` and `.h`), `init_add_i_state()` (moved from header to `.c` and marked `static`), `clear_add_i_state()` (moved from header to `.c` and marked `static`), `struct add_i_state` (moved from header to `.c`), `refresh_cache_entry()` (now `static` in `read-cache.c` and removed from `read-cache-ll.h`), `verify_uptodate()` (now `static` in `unpack-trees.c` and removed from `unpack-trees.h`).
The reordering in patch 3/4 moves the `refresh_cache_ent()` helper and its wrapper `refresh_cache_entry()` above their first use, eliminating the need for a forward declaration. Patch 4/4 is a straightforward header cleanup: `verify_uptodate()` is marked `static` and its declaration removed from `unpack-trees.h` after the removal of the "recursive" merge strategy (commit `ad45b327c0`, April 2025) eliminated its last external caller. No new options, config keys, or test coverage changes are introduced.

Open questions and loose ends
None. The grammatical discussions in patches 1/4 and 2/4’s commit messages are resolved as stylistic choices with no action required.
