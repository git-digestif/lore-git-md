The series fixes a critical migration failure in the "loose-object-idx" file when Git is configured with a compatibility hash (e.g., SHA-1/SHA-256 interop) and batch fsync of loose objects is enabled. This approval clears the way for the series to progress to `next` and eventually `master`.
