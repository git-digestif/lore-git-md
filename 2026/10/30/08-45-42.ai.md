This is a design discussion reply in the `sigchain` race-condition thread. Oswald Buddenhagen joins the conversation to clarify a technical point about SIGPIPE: while Phillip Wood and ian4hu have agreed to block all signals (including SIGPIPE) during thread creation so that only the main thread handles them, Oswald points out that SIGPIPE is inherently thread-directed and therefore requires separate reasoning even if the practical outcome is the same. He does not object to blocking SIGPIPE but insists that the distinction be acknowledged in the design rationale. The thread remains in the proposal stage, awaiting feedback from Jeff King and Junio before a v2 patch series is posted.
