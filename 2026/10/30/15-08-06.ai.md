This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing RFC thread proposing `^/` syntax for root-relative submodule URLs. Stanislav Aleksandrov clarifies a key semantic distinction between the proposed `^/` syntax and traditional relative paths (`../`). The `^/` syntax is explicitly designed for submodules that remain in their original location on the remote server, resolving dynamically against the superproject’s remote URL root. In contrast, relative paths like `../lib.git` continue to work as expected when both the superproject and submodule are forked together, preserving the existing behavior for forked dependencies. This distinction addresses Jacob Keller’s earlier concern about fork isolation: `^/` does not silently inherit upstream dependencies, making it more predictable for users who want to keep submodules pinned to their original locations. The exchange reinforces the design rationale without introducing new technical objections or changes to the patch. The thread remains focused on refining the motivation and use cases for the feature.
