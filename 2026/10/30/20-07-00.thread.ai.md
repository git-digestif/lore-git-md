Core problem or goal
`git log --follow` (which tracks file renames across history) became significantly slower when processing merges after commit 304812ed33 (June 2026). The series fixes two correctness bugs—`-m --follow` losing history from non-first parents and `--follow` missing edits on dropped sides of merges—and introduces a targeted optimization that reduces the number of rename-detection passes needed when following a file through merges. The optimization cuts runtime for a real-world linux.git case from ~3 minutes to ~37 seconds.
