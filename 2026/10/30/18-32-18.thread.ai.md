Core problem or goal
Replace the global `the_repository` with the explicit `istate->repo` parameter in three functions in `name-hash.c` (`hash_index_entry`, `lookup_lazy_params`, `remove_name_hash`) so that the `core.ignoreCase` setting is read from the repository that owns the index rather than from the global variable. This is a mechanical step in the long-running `the_repository` removal project.
