This is a brief follow-up in the ODB transaction bugfix series, now in its final form after being merged to `seen`. Justin Tobler responds to Karthik Nayak’s surface-level review of patch 4/4, which suggested two minor naming and error-message tweaks.

Justin explains that he deliberately kept the function name `loose_object_idx_merge()` (rather than `loose_object_map_merge()`) because the function only merges the on-disk loose-object-idx files and does not update any in-memory map. He also declines to change the error message from “unable to write” to “unable to commit/rename” for the `commit_lock_file()` failure, arguing that the different file paths in the two error messages already provide enough context. He notes that neither change is worth a reroll, and the series remains ready for integration. The exchange confirms the series is technically complete and uncontroversial.
