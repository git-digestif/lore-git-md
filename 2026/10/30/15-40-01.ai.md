This is a **follow-up discussion** in the already-merged ODB transaction bugfix series. Justin Tobler responds to Karthik Nayak’s surface-level review of patch 4/4, clarifying the rationale behind two minor naming and error-message choices.

The exchange confirms that the function name `loose_object_idx_merge()` is intentional: unlike the other `loose_object_map_*()` helpers, it does not update the in-memory loose object map, so the different name signals that distinction. On the error message, Justin acknowledges the ambiguity but prefers to retain the current wording ("unable to write"), arguing that the differing file paths in the messages are sufficient to distinguish the two failure modes. He explicitly states he does not consider either nit worth a reroll, and the series remains ready for integration. No functional changes are proposed or needed.
