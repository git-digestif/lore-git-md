This thread tracks Elia Pinto's five-patch v3 series fixing loose object size validation and LLP64 truncation bugs. The series addresses missing bounds checks in loose object parsing (patches 1/5 and 3/5, cross-platform) and type-width mismatches affecting 64-bit Windows (patches 2/5, 4/5, and 5/5), including a buffer overread in `cat-file --batch` for 2--4 GiB objects and a heap overflow in notes concatenation. Only patches 1/5 and 3/5 carry automated tests; the others document manual reproduction steps because they require multi-gigabyte objects or a 32-bit `long` platform.

Junio C Hamano has approved four of the five patches: 1/5, 2/5, 4/5, and 5/5 all received positive reviews with no blocking objections, though he requested rework on 3/5 during v2 review. The new v3 addresses that feedback by removing the direct `error()` call from `read_istream_loose()` and instead signaling failure silently via `z_state = ODB_LOOSE_READ_STREAM_ERROR`, which also eliminates the `oid` struct member added in v2. Patch 1/5 now emits a specific size-mismatch message, patch 2/5's commit message explains why `st_add()` is unnecessary, and patch 4/5 has a trimmed commit message. Patrick Steinhardt's earlier substantive review of patch 1/5 questioned stream mechanics that Junio later clarified and suggested `memchr()` over `strlen()`, the latter now deferred to a separate patch.

The series is now at v3, rebased onto `master` at e1ec09b23c, and remains under review with no integration decision yet. The only open item is whether v3 satisfies Junio's requested changes to patch 3/5; no new objections have been raised against the other four patches.
