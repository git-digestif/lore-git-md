Elia Pinto's v2 five-patch series fixes loose object handling and size truncation bugs, with two patches affecting all platforms and three specific to LLP64 systems like 64-bit Windows. The series is under review, rebased onto `master` at e1ec09b23c. Patch 1/5, which adds post-inflation validation to `unpack_loose_rest()` to catch size mismatches and prevent uninitialized memory exposure, has received maintainer approval from Junio C Hamano ("Looking good. Thanks."), and now also a substantive review from Patrick Steinhardt. Patrick engaged with the bug mechanics, questioning the description of the case where the header claims fewer bytes than the stream contains and asking why the zlib stream would have already ended in that scenario. He endorsed the core approach of comparing `total_out` against the expected size and treating mismatches as corruption, noting it mirrors `unpack_compressed_entry()`, and offered two refinements: replacing `strlen()` with `memchr()` on the bounded header buffer, and making the error message more specific when a size mismatch is the cause. Patch 2/5 widening loose-object size variables to `size_t` was found sensible by Junio, who signaled approval but asked about overflow-safe arithmetic. Patch 3/5 remains the only one requesting rework, as Junio prefers the low-level streaming reader stay silent on size mismatches and let higher-level callers report errors, making the added `oid` field unnecessary. Patches 4/5 and 5/5 have both been approved by Junio, the former as "trivially correct" and the latter with endorsement of the `st_add3()` overflow-safe arithmetic. The series addresses issues reviewed on `git-security` and deemed non-security-sensitive, with only patches 1/5 and 3/5 carrying automated tests while the LLP64-specific fixes rely on documented manual reproduction steps.
