Elia Pinto has posted a five-patch v2 series fixing loose object size handling and truncation bugs on LLP64 platforms such as 64-bit Windows. The series addresses missing bounds checks in loose object parsing and streaming that could expose uninitialized memory or silently truncate objects, an `int` overflow in `cat-file --batch` for objects between 2 and 4 GiB, and a heap overflow in notes concatenation when sizes exceed 4 GiB. Only the first and third patches include automated tests; the LLP64-specific and multi-gigabyte fixes rely on manual reproduction steps documented in the commit messages.
