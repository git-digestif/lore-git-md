The series is under review, rebased onto `master` at e1ec09b23c, and has drawn line-by-line attention from Junio C Hamano, who has approved patches 1/5, 4/5, and 5/5 and requested a rework of patch 3/5 that v3 implements. The one open point is a quality-of-contribution concern Patrick Steinhardt raised on patch 2/5, the `unsigned long` to `size_t` widening of the loose object reading path. Patrick does not dispute the code itself, but objects to the commit message's explanation for why no automated test is included, calling it uninformative and bearing the hallmarks of machine-generated text, and asks the author to rewrite it in their own voice. He frames this as a broader signal that affects his willingness to invest review effort. So while the technical substance of the series has effectively converged with maintainer approval across the board, patch 2/5's commit message still awaits a human rewrite before the series can be considered fully polished.
