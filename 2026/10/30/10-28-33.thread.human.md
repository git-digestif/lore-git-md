Elia Pinto's five-patch v2 series fixing loose object handling and size truncation bugs on LLP64 platforms (where `long` is 32-bit but `size_t` is 64-bit, as on 64-bit Windows) has now received positive maintainer feedback on every patch. The series addresses missing bounds checks in loose object parsing and streaming that could expose uninitialized heap memory or silently truncate objects, an `int`-truncation buffer overread in `cat-file --batch` for 2--4 GiB objects, and a heap overflow when concatenating notes totaling 4 GiB or more. The patches touch `object-file.c`, `odb/source-loose.c`, `builtin/cat-file.c`, and `notes.c`, with automated tests in `t/t1006-cat-file.sh` and `t/t1060-object-corruption.sh` covering the cross-platform fixes; the LLP64-specific scenarios are documented with manual reproduction steps since they can't be exercised in normal CI.

Junio C Hamano has approved patches 1/5 (post-inflation validation in `unpack_loose_rest()`), 2/5 (widening loose-object size variables from `unsigned long` to `size_t` throughout the reading path), 4/5 (`batch_write()` taking `size_t` instead of `int`, which he called "trivially correct"), and now 5/5 (using `st_add3()` overflow-safe arithmetic for the concatenated-notes buffer length in `combine_notes_concatenate()`), which he endorsed with "Looking good." The only outstanding item is patch 3/5, where Junio requested a rework: he wants the low-level `read_istream_loose()` to stay silent on size mismatch and set `st->z_state = ODB_LOOSE_READ_STREAM_ERROR` for a higher-level caller to report, which also makes the patch's added `oid` struct member unnecessary. The author has not yet responded to that rework request, and no maintainer decision on the series as a whole has been announced, so the series remains under review.
