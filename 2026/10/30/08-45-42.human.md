This is a design discussion reply in the ongoing effort to fix a race condition in Git’s `sigchain` subsystem. Oswald Buddenhagen joins the thread to clarify a technical point about SIGPIPE: unlike other signals, SIGPIPE is thread-directed, so its behavior under the proposed signal-blocking redesign needs separate reasoning even if the practical outcome (blocking it during thread creation) ends up the same. He does not object to blocking SIGPIPE but wants the rationale explicitly spelled out rather than folded into a blanket statement about "all signals."

The thread remains in the design phase, awaiting feedback from Jeff King (Peff) and Junio before the author posts a v2 patch series. No alternative approaches or objections have been raised; this is a refinement of the existing proposal.
