This is the third patch in Harald Nordgren’s v5 series introducing `git branch --delete-integrated`, a new flag that deletes branches whose changes (but not their tips) have been integrated upstream via rebase or squash merges. The patch itself remains under review; the **CLI design is finalized** and uncontroversial, but the **author-check mitigation** (Jasper Mayone’s proposal to require that the landing commit’s author also authored at least one commit on the branch being deleted) is still missing. Junio’s nit does not affect functionality—it is purely about code readability and consistency with Git’s naming conventions. The change is mechanical and will likely be incorporated in the next iteration.

If you are following the series, this patch is the core of the new behavior: it implements the change-based detection logic that distinguishes `--delete-integrated` from the existing `--delete-merged`. The algorithm (tree-based diffing for rebase detection, three-way merge approximation for squash merges) is unchanged from v4, and the patch is still missing the author-check safety net for forge workflows and the "npm update" scenario. Junio’s feedback here is minor and does not reopen any settled questions.
