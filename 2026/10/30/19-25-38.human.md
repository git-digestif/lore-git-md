This is Junio’s **substantive review** of the fourth patch in Harald Nordgren’s v5 series, which implements the **path-by-path three-way merge approximation** for `git branch --delete-integrated` to handle the upstream modification problem. The review identifies a **potential correctness issue**: the code passes `the_repository->index` to `ll_merge()`, but the index state (`istate`) may not be populated at that point, which could prevent `ll_merge()` from reading `.gitattributes` and applying custom merge drivers. Junio suggests either ensuring the index is read beforehand or explicitly documenting why attributes are being ignored.

The concern is technical and specific to Git’s low-level merge machinery. If the index is not read, `ll_merge()` will not see any `.gitattributes` settings, which could lead to incorrect merge results for files with custom merge drivers or conflict markers. This is not a theoretical edge case: it affects real-world repositories that use `.gitattributes` to configure merge behavior (e.g., `merge=union` for changelogs or `merge=ours` for generated files).

The patch is otherwise complete and addresses the last major correctness gap in the series, but this feedback may require a small but important fix before merging. The rest of the series (patches 1–3) remains uncontroversial and ready for integration.
