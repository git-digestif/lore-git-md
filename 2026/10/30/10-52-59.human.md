This is the first patch in Harald Nordgren’s v5 series extending `git branch --delete-merged` to detect squash-merged and rebase-merged branches. It is a pure refactoring patch that moves the existing `branch.<name>.deleteMerged` opt-out check into a new helper function, `branch_opted_out()`. The helper will be reused in the third patch, which introduces the new `--delete-integrated` flag. No behavior changes; the patch is uncontroversial and ready for integration. It touches only `builtin/branch.c` and is a straightforward code move with no functional impact.
