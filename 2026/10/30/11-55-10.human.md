This is a **surface-level review** of the final patch in Justin Tobler’s four-part bugfix series for the ODB transaction layer. The series, already merged to `seen`, fixes a critical issue where temporary object directory migration fails when Git uses compatibility hashes (e.g., SHA-1/SHA-256 interop) and batch fsync of loose objects.

Karthik Nayak’s feedback focuses on two minor naming and messaging nits in the new `loose_object_idx_merge()` function (patch 4/4). First, he suggests renaming the function to `loose_object_map_merge()` to align with the other `loose_object_map_*()` helpers introduced earlier in the series. Second, he proposes tweaking an error message to clarify whether the failure occurred during writing or committing the lockfile, avoiding ambiguity for users. Neither suggestion affects functionality—they’re purely about consistency and clarity.

The rest of the patch passes muster, and Karthik explicitly notes the implementation is otherwise sound. Since the series is already in `seen` and addresses a high-priority bug (preventing ODB transaction failures and potential data loss), these nits are unlikely to block integration. They could be addressed in a follow-up cleanup or left as-is if the maintainer prefers minimal churn at this stage. For readers tracking the ODB abstraction effort, this is a routine final-polish exchange on a series that’s otherwise ready to go.
