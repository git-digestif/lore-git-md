This is a review of patch 1/3 in Patrick Steinhardt’s three-patch bugfix series that fixes `git grep --recurse-submodules` when grepping nested submodule trees. The reviewer, Toon Claes, confirms the regression (introduced in Git 2.56) and the correctness of the fix: a single-line change in `builtin/grep.c` that switches tree reading from `the_repository->objects` to `repo->objects`. Toon also verified that the new test fails without the patch and passes with it, and he suggests minor test-script improvements (mentioning the regression in the commit message and using `test_when_finished` for cleanup). The review is substantive and tested; Toon’s feedback is positive and the patch looks ready for merging. The other two patches in the series, which defensively apply the same pattern to sparse directory trees and `.gitmodules` file reading, are also endorsed as worthwhile.
