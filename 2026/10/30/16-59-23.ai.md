This is a refactoring patch contributing to the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The patch updates `partial_clone_register()` and `partial_clone_get_default_filter_spec()` to accept a `struct repository *` parameter instead of implicitly using the global `the_repository`, and modifies their callers in `builtin/clone.c` and `builtin/fetch.c` to pass `the_repository` explicitly. This is a purely mechanical change that moves the dependency on the global one level up the call stack without altering behavior.

The patch touches four files: `list-objects-filter-options.c`, `list-objects-filter-options.h`, `builtin/clone.c`, and `builtin/fetch.c`. The key change is the addition of a `struct repository *` parameter to the two functions, with all internal references to `the_repository` replaced by the passed-in `repo` parameter. The `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` macro, which was previously defined but unused after this change, is now removed.

This version (v2) incorporates feedback from Okhuomon Ajayi, who tested the patch and confirmed that `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` could be safely removed. The change is straightforward and aligns with the project's long-term goal of eliminating global state. The patch is well-scoped, compiles cleanly, and follows Git's coding conventions. No new edge cases or behaviors are introduced, and the on-disk format remains unchanged.
