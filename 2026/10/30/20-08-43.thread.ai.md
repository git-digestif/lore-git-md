Core problem or goal
Fix a severe performance regression in `git log --stat --follow` on merge-heavy repositories (28 s → 6 m 43 s in linux.git). This patch is the fourth in the series and removes the redundant `propagate_follow_pathspec_to_parent()` helper and its loop that called `diff_tree_oid()` once per parent of every merge commit. The change is purely internal: no user-visible behavior change, but it eliminates the extra diff pass that caused the regression, preparing the ground for the actual performance fix in patch 5/6.
