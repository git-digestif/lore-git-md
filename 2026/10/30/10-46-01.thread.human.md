This thread began when Ulrich Windl asked whether Git’s `.gitignore` patterns treat the `*` wildcard as matching an empty string. The discussion quickly produced a documentation patch from Junio C Hamano that rewrites the relevant section of `gitignore(5)` to state explicitly that `*` matches “strings of any length” (or, in Tuomas Ahola’s refined phrasing, “strings of length of zero or more”). Ulrich has now confirmed that either wording resolves the original ambiguity to his satisfaction. The patch touches only the documentation file (`Documentation/gitignore.adoc`) and makes no behavioral changes; it simply clarifies the long-standing rule that wildcards do not match slashes while `*` can match zero or more characters. With no objections or open technical questions, the patch is uncontroversial and awaits Junio’s final wording choice and merge.
