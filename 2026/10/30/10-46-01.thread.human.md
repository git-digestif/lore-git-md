This thread began when Ulrich Windl asked whether Git’s `.gitignore` patterns treat the `*` wildcard as matching an empty string. Junio C Hamano responded with a patch to clarify the `gitignore(5)` man page, explicitly stating that `*` matches "strings of any length (including zero)" while keeping the existing rule that wildcards don’t match slashes. The patch is documentation-only, touching only `Documentation/gitignore.adoc`.

Tuomas Ahola has now suggested a minor but useful refinement to Junio’s wording, proposing "string of length of zero or more" instead of "string of any length" to remove any lingering ambiguity about whether "any" includes zero. The change is purely editorial and keeps the patch’s focus on clarifying existing behavior. No objections have been raised, and the patch remains under review, not yet queued for merging. If accepted, this tweak will make the documentation even clearer without altering Git’s actual pattern-matching rules.
