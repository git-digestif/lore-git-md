This thread began when Ulrich Windl asked whether the `*` wildcard in `.gitignore` patterns matches an empty string. The discussion quickly settled on the need to clarify the `gitignore(5)` man page, which previously relied on an external `fnmatch(3)` reference without explicitly stating the behavior. Junio C Hamano proposed a patch that rewrites the relevant section to say `*` matches "strings of any length," arguing this phrasing already includes zero-length strings. Tuomas Ahola then suggested a tweak to "strings of length of zero or more" for explicitness, citing similar language in `glob(7)` man pages as a model. Ulrich confirmed both phrasings are unambiguous, but the maintainer’s preference remains unresolved. The patch touches only `Documentation/gitignore.adoc` and leaves code behavior unchanged. No merge has occurred yet, and the thread is now focused solely on the most precise wording for the documentation.
