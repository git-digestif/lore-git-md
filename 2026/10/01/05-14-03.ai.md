This is a **documentation patch discussion** in the ongoing `gitmergeconflicts(7)` series. The email advances two substantive improvements to the new man page: **commit accessibility** and **`merge.conflictstyle=diff3` as a best practice**.

Patrick Steinhardt builds on Junio C Hamano’s earlier suggestion to make commit information more accessible during conflict resolution. He proposes a concrete format for embedding commit details directly in conflict markers, e.g.:
```
<<<<<<< HEAD: abcdefg (fruits: add apple, 2026-10-01)
    "cherry",
=======
    "banana",
>>>>>>> add-fruit: 12345678 (fruits: add banana, 2024-02-03)
```
This approach avoids requiring users to learn special refs like `MERGE_HEAD`, provides context via commit messages and dates, and aligns with Git’s existing conflict-marker syntax. Patrick plans to prototype this in GitLab to gather user feedback. The proposal is a significant enhancement—it transforms conflict markers from passive indicators into active tools for resolution, addressing a long-standing pain point for users.

Separately, Patrick and Junio both strongly endorse making `merge.conflictstyle=diff3` the default. Junio’s earlier comment ("I feel about this strongly enough") and Patrick’s agreement signal a potential future change in Git’s default behavior. The `diff3` style, which includes the common ancestor in conflict markers, is widely praised for improving resolution clarity, and Julia Evans’ user feedback ("every time I show people diff3 someone tells me how happy they are") reinforces its practical value. The guide is now expected to actively promote `diff3` as a best practice, and this discussion may foreshadow a broader shift in Git’s conflict-resolution defaults.
