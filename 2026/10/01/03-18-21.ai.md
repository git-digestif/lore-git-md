This is a **follow-up reply in a substantive review thread** about patch 2/4 of Taylor Blau’s four-patch series fixing MIDX reachability-closure corner cases. The patch ensures trees and tags in `--stdin-packs=follow` mode are treated as roots to maintain reachability closure, preventing MIDXs from omitting objects reachable only from these trees or tags.

Taylor Blau **acknowledges Jeff King’s (Peff) feedback** and **clarifies the patch’s intent** without making functional changes. Peff’s review confirmed the core problem (a "timebomb" scenario where MIDXs could omit objects reachable only from trees/tags, breaking later bitmap generation) and raised two technical nuances: (1) whether blobs should be handled similarly to trees/tags (answer: no, blobs can’t reference other objects), and (2) whether the two-walk approach (commits first, then `extra_roots`) is necessary or if a single traversal with all objects could suffice. Taylor agrees with Peff’s assessment that the two-walk approach doesn’t fully resolve path context ambiguities (e.g., tags pointing to commits could reintroduce the same issue) but defends the current design as a **strict improvement** over the status quo. The discussion concludes with mutual agreement that rethinking the traversal machinery is best deferred to future work.

The **key technical details** remain unchanged from the parent email: the patch introduces an `extra_roots` OID array in `stdin_packs_context` to collect trees and tags during pack traversal, deferring their walk until after the commit traversal to preserve path prefixes for delta attributes and namehashes. The patch touches `builtin/pack-objects.c` and the `git-pack-objects` man page, and its behavior change is narrowly scoped to `--stdin-packs=follow` mode. No new edge cases or unaddressed issues are introduced in this reply; the focus is on **clarifying the existing design** rather than revising it.
