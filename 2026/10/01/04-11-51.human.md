This is the fifth patch in Taylor Blau’s eight-patch v2 series fixing MIDX reachability-closure corner cases. It targets a specific performance pitfall in geometric repacks when `.keep` packs are present.

When `repack.midxMustContainCruft` is false and a geometric repack runs, Git uses `--stdin-packs=follow` to copy objects from cruft packs that are needed for reachability closure. However, `.keep` packs were not participating in that walk, even though they are included in the resulting MIDX. This could lead to MIDXs that are not closed under reachability if a commit in a `.keep` pack reaches an object whose only copy is in a cruft pack. The bitmap writer would then fail because the MIDX lacks all reachable objects.

The patch adjusts the geometric repack to pass excluded kept packs to the follow walk, using `!` for packs outside the existing MIDX and `^` for packs already covered by it. This ensures that needed objects are copied out of cruft packs without copying objects in the kept packs themselves, avoiding the performance hit of including all cruft packs merely because a concurrent push has installed a temporary `.keep` file. The change is narrowly scoped to geometric repacks and does not affect full repacks or incremental repacks.

The implementation is clean: it adds a new block in `builtin/repack.c` that iterates over `existing.kept_packs` and writes the appropriate exclusion markers to the `--stdin-packs` input file. The `repack-midx.c` helper is updated to include cruft packs when kept packs are present, ensuring the MIDX remains reachability-closed. Two new tests in `t7704-repack-cruft.sh` verify that geometric repacks follow kept packs to cruft objects and that full repacks retain cruft packs in the MIDX when unreachable kept objects depend on them.

This patch directly addresses Jeff King’s feedback from v1, where he raised the concern about temporary `.keep` files during pushes causing performance issues. The fix is well-motivated, narrowly targeted, and includes thorough test coverage. It looks ready for integration and should be uncontroversial.
