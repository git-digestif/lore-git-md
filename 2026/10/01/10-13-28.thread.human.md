This thread is a small but precise bugfix from Patrick Steinhardt targeting Git's command-line option completion. The issue is a long-standing formatting quirk in `--git-completion-helper`: when the first option in the list is skipped (for example, because it's a group header or a hidden option), the helper can emit a leading space that breaks shell completion scripts. Patrick's fix is minimal—just a new boolean flag to track whether anything has been printed yet—and comes with a test case that uses an `OPT_GROUP` as the first option to verify the behavior. The patch is self-contained, carries his sign-off, and looks ready for review. No discussion or objections have appeared yet.
