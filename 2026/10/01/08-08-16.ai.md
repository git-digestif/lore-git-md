This is the **author's response** to Patrick Steinhardt's substantive review of the second patch in Thomas Bachem's three-part series fixing a `rerere` race condition. The patch introduces `--auto` mode for `git rerere gc`, allowing auto-maintenance tasks to skip the operation if the lock is held.

Thomas accepts Patrick's feedback on both points: **documentation clarity** and **flag naming**. He will remove the caller-specific phrasing from `git-rerere(1)` and instead document the behavior in the `rerere.lockTimeout` config entry, explicitly naming `git maintenance run --auto` and `git gc --auto` there. More significantly, he agrees to rename the flag from `--auto` to `--skip-locked` and mark it as hidden (undocumented), addressing Patrick's concern that "auto" misleadingly implies heuristic behavior. The `RERERE_SKIP_LOCKED` flag in patch 3 will be renamed to `RERERE_WARN_LOCKED` to avoid confusion with the new `RERERE_NOWAIT` flag.

Thomas also explored Patrick's alternative suggestion of calling `rerere_gc()` directly from `builtin/gc.c` but found it impractical: it would cause `git maintenance run` to die with exit code 128 and a lockfile message, breaking existing tests and user expectations. He plans to keep the `--skip-locked` option for this series and will send a v6 update after a brief pause for additional feedback.

The patch remains **well-motivated and technically sound**, addressing a real race condition with clear test coverage. The changes here are cosmetic (documentation and flag naming) and do not alter the core fix. The series is now one step closer to finalization, with only minor tweaks remaining.
