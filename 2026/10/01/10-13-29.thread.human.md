This thread is about a small but useful refactoring to Git’s parse-options API, authored by Patrick Steinhardt. The patch (second in a three-part series) extends the existing `OPT_GROUP()` mechanism so that subcommands—not just options—can be grouped in the help output. Previously, only options could be organized under descriptive headings; now subcommands defined with the new `OPT_SUBCOMMAND_H()` macro can also carry help text and appear under the appropriate group.

The change is confined to the parse-options machinery (`parse-options.c` and `parse-options.h`), adds a single test case (`t/t0040-parse-options.sh`), and updates the API documentation. It’s backward-compatible: subcommands without help text remain invisible in the usage message, so no existing callers are affected. The patch is narrowly scoped, well-tested, and appears uncontroversial. No reviews or objections have surfaced yet, so it’s likely to land as-is once the full series is posted.
