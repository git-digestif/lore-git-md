Hello Git Developers,

I am writing to seek clarification and share feedback regarding unexpected
requirements for force-pushing (--force or --force-with-lease) encountered
by myself and several colleagues across different projects.

In our workflows, developers are encountering situations where a standard git
push is rejected due to history divergence, prompting a need to force push,
despite no local history rewrites or explicit manual force operations being
executed prior.

Given that this has occurred independently across multiple users and
separate project repositories, we are looking to better understand the
underlying triggers within Git's tracking mechanism or upstream reference
updates that might cause local and remote histories to diverge so
unexpectedly.

Could this behavior be related to how underlying reference updates, fetch
tracking, or concurrent background updates interact in recent Git versions?
Any insights or guidance on how to audit or prevent this behavior at the
Git client level would be greatly appreciated.
