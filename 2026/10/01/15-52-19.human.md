This is a review of the fifth iteration of a bugfix series for `git stash`, specifically targeting a latent error-handling flaw in `do_apply_stash()` that could cause crashes on hard merge failures. The series is now complete and ready for integration, with Phillip Wood’s review marking it as expected and uncontroversial.

The core issue is a tri-state logic flaw in how `merge_incore_nonrecursive()` results are handled. The function returns `result.clean` as 1 (success), 0 (conflict), or -1 (hard error), but the original code only checked for non-zero values, treating hard errors as conflicts. This could lead to a NULL-dereference when accessing `result.tree` after catastrophic merge failures (e.g., out-of-memory or disk errors). The fix in Patch 4/4 adds an explicit check for `result.clean < 0` to handle hard errors separately, preventing crashes and providing a clearer error message. The change is minimal—just three lines in `builtin/stash.c`—and aligns with existing patterns in `merge-ort.c`.

The series consists of four patches.
