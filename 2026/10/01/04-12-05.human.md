This is the final patch in an eight-patch bugfix series targeting the multi-pack-index (MIDX) subsystem. The series addresses corner cases where MIDXs can end up with objects not closed under reachability, breaking bitmap generation.

This patch fixes a previously unnoticed gap in incremental MIDX writes: when `--write-midx=incremental` is used (including via `GIT_TEST_MULTI_PACK_INDEX_WRITE_INCREMENTAL`), the append plan only includes newly written packs, omitting preexisting packs that may contain objects reachable from the selected commits. The geometric plan can similarly omit kept and cruft packs, and both plans fail to consult `midx_included_packs()`, which governs cruft pack retention in ordinary MIDX writes. The fix modifies both plans to include all required packs not already in the MIDX chain, ensuring reachability closure.

The patch updates `repack-midx.c` to call `midx_included_packs()` with the base MIDX layer, then adds any missing packs to the write step. It also updates the documentation for `git-repack` to clarify that incremental MIDX writes include both newly written packs and any other required packs not already in the chain. Two new tests in `t7705-repack-incremental-midx.sh` verify that incremental MIDX writes include cruft packs even when no new pack is written, and that geometric incremental MIDXs retain cruft when replacing their tip layer.

The fix is narrowly scoped, well-motivated, and directly addresses the edge case Jeff King identified in his review of the v1 series. The patch is uncontroversial and looks ready for integration. The series as a whole is now comprehensive, with thorough test coverage and all substantive feedback incorporated.
