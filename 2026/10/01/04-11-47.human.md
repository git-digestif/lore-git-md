This is a refactoring patch in the MIDX reachability closure series, specifically patch 4/8 of v2. The patch prepares the codebase for a future caller by replacing a linear search through `--keep-pack` arguments with a binary search. The change is purely internal: it sorts the `keep_pack_list` once (using `fspathcmp()`) and then uses `string_list_has_string()` instead of a manual loop. The commit message notes that the performance difference is negligible in practice, but the new structure is cleaner and avoids duplicating the search logic when the upcoming caller is added.

The patch touches `builtin/repack.c` (sorting the list), `repack.c` (replacing the loop with `string_list_has_string()`), and `repack.h` (documenting the requirement that `extra_keep` be sorted). No behavior changes are introduced, and the existing tests continue to pass. This is a straightforward, well-justified cleanup that aligns with the series' goal of making the MIDX machinery more robust. The patch is uncontroversial and ready for integration.
