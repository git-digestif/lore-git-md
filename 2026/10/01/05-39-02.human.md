This is the final patch in a three-part bugfix series targeting the reftable backend. It fixes a long-standing mismatch between Git’s on-disk storage of reflog timezones and the reftable specification, which requires signed offsets in minutes rather than the traditional `[+-]HHMM` format.

The patch updates `refs/reftable-backend.c` to convert reflog timezones to minutes before writing them to disk, using the new `tz_to_minutes()` helper introduced earlier in the series. On read, `minutes_to_tz()` converts them back to `[+-]HHMM` to maintain compatibility with the rest of Git. This ensures the reftable backend adheres to the spec while minimizing user-visible impact, as reflog timezones are only used for display purposes.

The test coverage is thorough: a new test in `t/t0610-reftable-basics.sh` verifies round-trip behavior for edge cases like `+1400`, `+0530`, and `-0830`, including the negative timezone with non-zero minutes (`-0530`) requested in review. The `dump-reftable` helper is also updated to avoid zero-padding the offset, reducing the risk of misinterpretation.

This patch is well-motivated, clearly explained, and addresses all prior feedback. The series as a whole is effectively ready for integration, with no open questions or objections remaining. The change is mechanical, well-tested, and unlikely to be controversial.
