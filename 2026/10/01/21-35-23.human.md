This is a **workflow tool discussion** about whether Git should include a helper script for incremental rebase conflict resolution in its `contrib/` area. Nico Williams clarifies the algorithm behind his `bisect-rebase.sh` script (now deprecated in favor of Alejandro Colomar’s `git-bisect-rebase`). The core idea is to repeatedly attempt a full rebase, and if conflicts arise, use a bisection-like approach to identify the *first* upstream commit that introduces a conflict, reducing the number of manual resolutions needed.

The email is brief and technical, focusing on the script’s behavior rather than its suitability for inclusion. Nico notes that while the name `bisect-rebase` is somewhat misleading (the worst-case complexity is O(N log N) rather than the expected O(log N) for a true bisection), it remains a reasonable choice given the intent. This clarification reinforces the thread’s convergence on the bisection-based approach as the most promising candidate for `contrib/`, though the broader question of whether Git should include *any* such helper remains unresolved. Junio C Hamano’s earlier endorsement of the third-party tool `git-imerge` as a proven solution still looms over the discussion, and no maintainer decision has been made. The email does not advance the thread’s social or procedural aspects—it is purely a technical footnote.
