This is a review of a proposed `contrib/` script (`git-rebase-walk`) that automates an iterative rebase workflow.

Patrick Steinhardt replies to Alejandro Colomar’s RFC with a substantive comparison: he points out that the proposed script’s goal—resolving large rebase conflicts incrementally—is already addressed by `git-imerge`, a third-party tool that has not been updated in over six years but likely still works. The review is brief but technical, identifying a pre-existing solution and implicitly raising the question of whether Git’s `contrib/` area needs another tool for the same purpose. No code-level feedback is given; the focus is on workflow overlap and project scope.
