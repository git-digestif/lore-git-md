This is patch 5/8 in the v2 series fixing MIDX reachability closure corner cases. It addresses a performance pitfall in geometric repacks when `.keep` packs are present, particularly during concurrent pushes that install temporary `.keep` files.

The patch modifies `builtin/repack.c` to pass excluded kept packs to the `--stdin-packs=follow` walk during geometric repacks when `repack.midxMustContainCruft` is false. Kept packs are marked with `!` (if outside the existing MIDX) or `^` (if already covered by it), allowing the walk to copy objects reachable from kept commits out of cruft packs without copying the kept objects themselves. This avoids the previous behavior of unconditionally including all cruft packs, which could cause performance issues during pushes. The patch also updates `repack-midx.c` to ensure cruft packs are included in the MIDX when kept packs are present, even in non-geometric repacks, and adds two new tests in `t7704-repack-cruft.sh` to verify the behavior.

The change is narrowly scoped to the geometric repack traversal logic and is well-motivated by the race condition it resolves. The test coverage is thorough, with one test verifying that geometric repacks follow kept packs to cruft objects and another ensuring full repacks retain cruft packs in the MIDX for unreachable kept objects. The patch is uncontroversial and directly addresses Jeff King’s feedback from v1 about the performance implications of temporary `.keep` files during pushes. It does not alter the on-disk format and maintains the MIDX’s reachability closure invariant.
