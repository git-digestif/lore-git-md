This is the eighth and final patch in Taylor Blau’s v2 series fixing MIDX reachability closure corner cases. It addresses a previously unnoticed gap in incremental MIDX writes (both geometric and append-only) where required packs—particularly cruft packs and kept packs—could be omitted from the MIDX chain, breaking bitmap generation.

The patch modifies `repack-midx.c` to consult `midx_included_packs()` when building the write step for both append and geometric plans. This ensures that packs not already in the chain (e.g., cruft packs when `repack.midxMustContainCruft` is false, or kept packs needed for reachability) are included. The logic skips packs already present in retained base layers but includes those from a replaced tip. The patch also updates object counts in the compaction step to reflect the added packs, though it does not alter the preferred pack selection.

The documentation in `git-repack.adoc` is updated to clarify that incremental MIDX writes now include "newly written packs and any other required packs not already in the chain." New tests in `t7705-repack-incremental-midx.sh` verify that incremental MIDX writes include cruft packs even when no new pack is written, and that geometric incremental repacks retain cruft when replacing the MIDX tip. The existing "kept packs are excluded from repack" test is renamed and extended to confirm that kept packs are included in the MIDX and that bitmaps remain valid.

This patch directly addresses Jeff King’s feedback from v1, which identified the edge case in `write_midx_incremental()`. The fix is narrowly scoped, well-tested, and uncontroversial. The series as a whole is now complete and ready for integration.
