This is a process-oriented reply from Patrick Steinhardt to the author of the `git stash create` feature patch (Kazumasa Shigeta). Patrick notes that while the v2 patch itself was sent, the author did not include two standard follow-up elements: a direct reply to Phillip Wood’s review acknowledging the feedback, and a range-diff showing what changed between v1 and v2. He frames this as a netiquette expectation in a mailing-list workflow where human-to-human engagement can feel mediated by AI tooling, and as a courtesy that makes it easier for reviewers to verify how their concerns were addressed. The tone is constructive and matter-of-fact, not critical. No technical discussion of the patch itself is included.
