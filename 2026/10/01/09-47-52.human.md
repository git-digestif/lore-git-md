This is a review reply in the ongoing discussion about whether to extend `git stash pop` with custom conflict-label options. Phillip Wood weighs in on Junio C Hamano’s skepticism about the feature’s usefulness, agreeing that while the options make sense for `git stash apply` (primarily to support `git checkout -m`), they’re unlikely to be practical for `git stash pop`. He suggests that users are unlikely to type out long label arguments when popping a stash, and scripts should already use `apply` instead of `pop`, making the extension redundant. The exchange reinforces the design question: is this a feature users actually need, or just a technical symmetry with no real-world benefit? No new technical concerns are raised, but the thread’s focus remains on the user-facing justification rather than implementation details.
