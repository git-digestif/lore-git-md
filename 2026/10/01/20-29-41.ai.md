This email is a **workflow tool discussion** in the ongoing thread about incremental rebase conflict resolution helpers for Git’s `contrib/` area. Alejandro Colomar shares his own implementation of a bisection-based rebase script (`git-bisect-rebase`), inspired by Nico Williams’ `bisect-rebase.sh` but simplified and aligned with Git’s `git rebase` interface (single-parameter usage). The script is 52 lines of Bash, uses pipes instead of shell arrays, and follows a similar O(k) conflict-resolution strategy. Colomar demonstrates its behavior on a real-world rebase, showing it narrows down conflicts efficiently. He expresses a preference for submitting his version to Git due to its interface consistency and lack of employer paperwork, while encouraging Nico to do the same for his script.

The email advances the thread by introducing a third candidate implementation and clarifying the trade-offs: Colomar’s script prioritizes simplicity and Git-like ergonomics, while Nico’s offers more verbose output and a two-parameter interface. The unresolved question remains whether Git should include *any* such helper in `contrib/`, and if so, which one—given Junio’s endorsement of the third-party `git-imerge` and the performance/robustness trade-offs between the two shell scripts. The discussion is now focused on comparing these three implementations (Colomar’s, Nico’s, and `git-imerge`) rather than debating the workflow’s general utility. No maintainer decision has been made, and the naming convention (`git-bisect-rebase` vs. `git-rebase-walk`) is still open.
