This is a bugfix series targeting the multi-pack-index (MIDX) and cruft pack machinery, now expanded to eight patches in its second iteration. The series addresses corner cases where MIDXs can end up with objects not closed under reachability, which breaks bitmap generation. The v2 incorporates feedback from Jeff King, Derrick Stolee, and Junio C Hamano, and now handles more edge cases, including incremental MIDX writes and geometric repacks with `.keep` packs.

The core problem remains the same: MIDXs must be closed under reachability to generate bitmaps, but certain repacking scenarios (like `--stdin-packs=follow`, incremental repacks, and `.keep` packs) can omit objects reachable from trees, tags, or kept packs. The v2 series refines the fixes by:
- Using an `oidset` instead of an `oid_array` for tracking extra roots (suggested by Stolee), avoiding duplicates and improving efficiency.
- Documenting the non-NULL requirement for `stdin_packs_context.revs` (addressing Junio’s feedback).
- Adjusting geometric repacks to traverse kept packs rather than unconditionally including cruft packs, avoiding performance pitfalls during pushes.
- Fixing the `write_midx_incremental()` edge case (identified by Peff), ensuring incremental MIDX writes respect reachability closure.
- Adding tests to verify bitmap generation in incremental and geometric repack scenarios.

The series touches `builtin/pack-objects.c`, `repack.c`, `repack-midx.c`, and adds tests in `t5331-pack-objects-stdin.sh`, `t7704-repack-cruft.sh`, and `t7705-repack-incremental-midx.sh`. The changes are narrowly scoped to the pack and repack subsystems, with no on-disk format changes. The commit messages are detailed, explaining the technical rationale and edge cases, and the test coverage is thorough.
