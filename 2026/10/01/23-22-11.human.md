This is a **substantive review** of Taylor Blau’s patch 2/8 in the MIDX reachability-closure series, focusing on the choice of data structure for collecting extra roots (trees and tags) in `--stdin-packs=follow` mode. Elijah Newren responds to Derrick Stolee’s suggestion to replace the `oid_array` with an `oidset`, arguing that the `oid_array` is necessary to preserve pack-quality invariants—specifically, namehash-based delta selection and path-based attributes.

Elijah demonstrates with two new tests that switching to an `oidset` would introduce pack-quality regressions. The first test shows that an `oidset` disrupts namehash ordering, causing blobs that could be delta-compressed to be stored in full. The second test reveals that an `oidset` loses path context, preventing `.gitattributes`-based delta rules from applying correctly. Both tests are deterministic and use contrived but realistic scenarios to isolate the issue.

The review is **tested** in spirit: Elijah does not claim to have run the tests on the actual patch, but the examples are self-contained and reproducible. The argument is narrowly scoped to the data structure choice and does not challenge the patch’s core goal of ensuring reachability closure. The discussion is now a technical trade-off between correctness (preserving pack quality) and efficiency (avoiding duplicates). Taylor Blau will need to decide whether to retain the `oid_array` or explore an alternative that satisfies both constraints.
