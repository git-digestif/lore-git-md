This is a small feature patch that adds `@{p}` as a shorthand for `@{push}` in Git's revision syntax, mirroring the existing `@{u}` shorthand for `@{upstream}`. The patch is now effectively complete, with Junio C Hamano having queued it for integration and signaled his intent to graduate it to `next` and eventually `master`. The only remaining step is a minor update to the commit message to incorporate the historical context Junio provided—specifically, that `@{upstream}` and `@{u}` were introduced together in 2010, while `@{push}` (added in 2015) lacked a shorthand not due to any deliberate decision but simply because the asymmetry was overlooked.

The patch itself is uncontroversial and well-scoped, touching only `object-name.c` to implement the alias, updating `Documentation/revisions.adoc` to document it, and adding test coverage in `t/t1514-rev-parse-push.sh`. The change aligns with Git’s existing case-insensitive handling of revision suffixes (e.g., `@{U}` also works for `@{upstream}`), so `@{P}` will work as well. No new behavior is introduced—just a convenience for users who expect symmetry between upstream and push shorthands.
