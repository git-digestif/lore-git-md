This is a minor but clarifying follow-up in the reftable timezone bugfix series. Karthik Nayak and Patrick Steinhardt quickly agree on a stylistic tweak to the `local_time_tzoffset()` function in `date.c`: instead of multiplying the offset by `eastwest` before converting to the "+HHMM" format (`minutes_to_tz(offset * eastwest)`), they will convert first and then multiply (`minutes_to_tz(offset) * eastwest`). The change is mathematically equivalent but makes the code’s intent clearer.

The discussion is purely about readability, not correctness or behavior. No other parts of the series are revisited, and the resolution is uncontroversial. With this settled, the only remaining open item from the v2 review is the addition of test cases for negative timezones with non-zero minutes (e.g., "-0530"), which Patrick has already committed to including. The series remains on track for integration, currently queued in Junio’s `seen` branch.
