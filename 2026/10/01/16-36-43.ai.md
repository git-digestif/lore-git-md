This is a process-oriented reply from Junio C Hamano to Patrick Steinhardt’s earlier netiquette note about the `git stash create` patch series. Junio clarifies the expected timing for responding to review feedback: the author should reply to reviewers *after* digesting the feedback on v1 but *before* sending v2, not after or immediately before. He implies that sending the response after v2—or in close proximity to it—risks appearing perfunctory or tool-assisted rather than a genuine engagement with the feedback. The tone is matter-of-fact, reinforcing the project’s long-standing practice rather than criticizing the author. No technical discussion of the patch itself is included.
