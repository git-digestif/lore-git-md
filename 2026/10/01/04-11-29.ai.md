This is a bugfix series targeting the multi-pack-index (MIDX) and cruft pack machinery, now at v2 with eight patches. The series addresses corner cases where MIDXs can end up with objects not closed under reachability, which breaks bitmap generation. The v2 incorporates all substantive feedback from v1, including structural improvements, additional test coverage, and fixes for previously unaddressed edge cases.

The core problem remains the same: when `repack.midxMustContainCruft` is set to "false", geometric repacks can omit cruft packs from the MIDX, but objects in those packs may still be reachable from commits, trees, or tags in retained packs. This violates the MIDX's invariant that it contains all objects reachable from its tips, causing bitmap generation to fail. The series now handles three additional scenarios: incremental MIDX writes (both geometric and append-only), `.keep` packs, and the `--write-midx=incremental` edge case Jeff King identified in v1.

Key changes since v1 include:
- Replacing the `oid_array` for `extra_roots` with an `oidset` (patch 2/8), as suggested by Derrick Stolee, to avoid duplicate OIDs and align the data structure with its semantic purpose.
- Documenting the non-NULL requirement of `stdin_packs_context.revs` and simplifying `add_loose_object()` (patch 1/8), addressing Junio C Hamano's surface-level feedback.
- Clarifying the two-walk approach in patch 2/8, acknowledging that tags can introduce commits in the second walk, making path context best-effort.
- Following Jeff King's suggestion to traverse kept packs during geometric repacks (patch 5/8), avoiding the performance pitfall of unconditionally including cruft packs when temporary `.keep` files are present during pushes.
- Fixing the `--write-midx=incremental` edge case (patch 8/8), ensuring that incremental MIDX writes include all required packs, not just newly written ones.
- Adding new tests to verify bitmap generation in incremental MIDX scenarios (patches 7/8 and 8/8) and ensuring cruft packs are included when replacing a MIDX tip layer.

The series now spans eight patches, with the first three largely unchanged from v1 (though patch 2/8 includes the `oidset` change). Patches 4/8 through 7/8 are new, introducing structural improvements to the MIDX write steps and handling the geometric repack traversal of kept packs. Patch 8/8 consolidates the fixes for incremental MIDX writes, addressing the edge case Jeff King identified and ensuring all required packs are included in the MIDX chain.

Files modified include `builtin/pack-objects.c` (patches 1/8, 2/8, and 5/8), `repack.c` (patch 3/8), `repack-midx.c` (patches 4/8, 5/8, 6/8, 7/8, and 8/8), and the man pages for `git-pack-objects` and `git-repack` (patches 2/8 and 8/8). New tests are added to `t5331-pack-objects-stdin.sh` (patch 2/8) and `t7704-repack-cruft.sh` (patch 3/8), with additional coverage in `t7705-repack-incremental-midx.sh` (patches 7/8 and 8/8).

The series is well-motivated and addresses a real issue with clear technical rationale. The v2 improvements make the fixes more robust and comprehensive, particularly around incremental MIDX writes and `.keep` pack handling. The test coverage is thorough, and the patches are narrowly scoped to the MIDX and repack subsystems, minimizing the risk of unintended side effects. The series looks ready for serious review and is likely to be uncontroversial.
