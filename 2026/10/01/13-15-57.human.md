This is the fifth patch in Jeff King’s xdiff buffer-size refactoring series, now in its fourth iteration. The patch replaces manual file-reading logic in `ll_ext_merge()` with a call to `read_mmfile()`, reducing 16 lines of open/stat/read/close boilerplate to a single function call. The change is mechanical but delivers two concrete improvements: `read_mmfile()` uses `xsize_t()` to catch truncation risks, and it emits an error message to stderr when the file cannot be read, whereas the old code silently returned NULL. Patrick Steinhardt’s reply acknowledges the change and notes that the new error message may result in duplicate output in some call paths, but considers this acceptable. The patch is uncontested, procedurally ready, and part of a well-reviewed series that addresses integer overflow risks, fixes a latent memory leak, and resolves an error-handling bug in external merge drivers. The series as a whole looks solid and worth merging.
