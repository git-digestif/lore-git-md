This is a process-oriented reply from Junio C Hamano, the Git maintainer, clarifying the expected timing for responding to review feedback in a patch series. Junio agrees with Patrick Steinhardt’s point about the importance of acknowledging reviewer feedback but refines the sequence: the author should reply to reviews *after* digesting the feedback but *before* sending the next version (v2). He notes that replying immediately before or after v2 can signal rushed or superficial engagement, which may not fully address the reviewer’s concerns. The tone is matter-of-fact, reinforcing mailing-list etiquette without criticizing the original author. This exchange doesn’t touch the technical content of the `git stash create` patch itself but serves as a reminder of the project’s collaborative norms for future contributors.
