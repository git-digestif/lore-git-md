This is the third patch in Taylor Blau’s v2 eight-patch series fixing MIDX reachability-closure corner cases. It targets a regression introduced in commit 5ee86c273bf (June 2025) where incremental repacks could omit cruft packs from the MIDX when `repack.midxMustContainCruft` is set to "false", breaking bitmap generation.

The patch forces `midx_must_contain_cruft` to `1` when `--incremental` is passed to `pack-objects`, ensuring cruft packs are included in the MIDX even if no new packs were written. This is a narrow, well-justified fix: incremental repacks do not copy already-packed objects, so cruft packs may be required to form a reachability closure. The change is minimal—just six lines in `builtin/repack.c`—and adds a targeted test in `t7704-repack-cruft.sh` to verify that incremental repacks can successfully write reachability bitmaps.

Jeff King’s review of v1 confirmed the patch’s premise and endorsed it as reasonable. The fix is uncontroversial and addresses a clear gap in the existing logic. The test coverage is appropriate, exercising the exact scenario that previously failed. This patch is ready for integration and should be uncontroversial.
