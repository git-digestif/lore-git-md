This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing MIDX reachability closure bugfix series from Taylor Blau. The series targets corner cases where multi-pack-indexes (MIDXs) can end up missing objects that are reachable from their tips, breaking bitmap generation. The thread is now focused on preparing a v2 revision.

Taylor acknowledges Jeff King’s substantive review feedback and confirms that all identified gaps will be addressed in v2. This includes the unhandled edge case in `write_midx_incremental()` (patch 3/4) and the refinements to `.keep` pack handling (patch 4/4) to avoid performance pitfalls during pushes. The v2 series will also incorporate Derrick Stolee’s suggestion to replace the `oid_array` for `extra_roots` with an `oidset`, a mechanical change confined to `builtin/pack-objects.c`.

The only deferred issue is a pre-existing, independent bug involving `--pack-kept-objects` and cruft packs that are themselves kept. Taylor estimates this fix is too complex to include in the current series and will tackle it separately.

No new technical objections or design questions are raised in this email; it serves primarily as a status update confirming the scope of v2. The series remains a targeted bugfix effort touching MIDX, cruft packs, and repack machinery, with test coverage in `t5331` and `t7704`. The v2 revision is expected to be slightly longer but maintains the same core goal of ensuring MIDX reachability closure.
