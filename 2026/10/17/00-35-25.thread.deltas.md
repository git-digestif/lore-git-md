## 2026/10/17

- [2026/10/17/00-35-25 by DonQuinleone <don@donquinleone.com>] DonQuinleone proposed an RFC to extend `git branch --delete-merged` to delete gone-upstream branches when their tip is reachable from an explicitly named ref, arguing that "upstream gone" should select candidates but only a named ref provides proof of merge (agreeing with Junio's earlier point that a vanished upstream cannot serve as a safety check); he presented two designs—(A) a `--into <ref>` modifier on `--delete-merged` and (B) composable `--upstream-gone`/`--merged=` filters on `-d`—leaned toward (A), and asked whether the "selection is not proof" basis is acceptable and which shape or name the list prefers.

## 2026/10/22

- [2026/10/22/12-46-22 by D. Ben Knoble <ben.knoble@gmail.com>] D. Ben Knoble questioned why `--delete-merged` needs to consult `topic@{upstream}` when a merge commit already has the topic as a parent, then noted the manual shows `--delete-merged` actually deletes branches whose tip is reachable from their upstream matching the pattern; he also recommended `push.default=current` so `@{upstream}` is the merge target and `@{push}` the push target in triangular workflows, and argued the interface should stay composable, preferring `--option=value,value,value` syntax such as `git branch --delete-merged='origin/*,into:origin/master'` (or `--delete-merged=into:origin` with DWIM) over a standalone `--into` modifier, while declining to take a position on whether `--delete-merged` should delete gone-upstream branches at all.
- [2026/10/22/13-43-34 by Harald Nordgren <haraldnordgren@gmail.com>] Harald Nordgren agreed that most GitHub/GitLab PR/MR workflows are triangular underneath, and argued `push.default=current` should likely be the default, `branch.autosetupmerge=inherit` would give `@{u}` as upstream/master from the start, and `status.comparebranches=@{upstream} @{push}` should be default so switching tracking doesn't lose the comparison against `@{push}`.
- [2026/10/22/14-38-22 by Harald Nordgren <haraldnordgren@gmail.com>] Harald Nordgren called the proposed `--delete-merged='origin/*,into:origin/master'` syntax "completely chaotic" and a "kitchen sink".

- [2026/10/22/17-52-02 by Harald Nordgren <haraldnordgren@gmail.com>] Harald Nordgren clarified that his objection was specifically to `--delete-merged=into:origin/master`, which he compared to hacking code into an argument like `find . -exec something`.
