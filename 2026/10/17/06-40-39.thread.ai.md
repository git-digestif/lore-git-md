Core problem or goal
The error message in `git difftool --dir-diff` when used with combined diff formats (`-c` or `--cc`) was wrapped in `N_()` (extractable for `.po` files) but not in `_()` (runtime lookup), so it always appeared in English regardless of the user’s locale. The patch replaces `N_()` with `_()` to enable the existing translation.
