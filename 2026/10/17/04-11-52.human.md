This is a short follow-up in the integer-overflow hardening series. Junio replies to Simon Richter’s suggestion to use `off_t` for the `posn` variable in `http.c`, reinforcing the original choice of `size_t`. He explains that `posn` is used to offset into an in-core buffer (`ptr + posn`) whose allocation size is measured in `size_t`, so `size_t` is the correct type to avoid undefined pointer arithmetic. The discussion is now resolved in favor of `size_t` without introducing new edge cases or requiring code changes.

The broader series remains under review, with two open technical points still needing resolution: the platform-compatibility question about `SSIZE_MAX` vs. `size_t` width mismatch on NonStop (patch 3), and Phillip Wood’s feedback on the GCC-only `mult_overflows` macro and its portability (patches 4 and 5). This reply does not affect those discussions.
