The issue causes fetches to take minutes instead of seconds due to inefficient history traversal when advertising shallow commits. The patch, now in its second version (v2), corrects a protocol-layer API misuse and aligns protocol v2 behavior with protocol v0 by directly advertising shallow grafts when no specific depth is requested.

The v2 patch replaces `packet_write_fmt` with `packet_writer` to preserve sideband multiplexing and includes a new test case to guard against regression. The performance improvement is dramatic—reducing fetch time from 7 minutes to 1 second in the reported scenario—and the underlying approach remains uncontroversial.
