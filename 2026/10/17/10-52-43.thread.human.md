This thread is about a small but important refactoring patch by Okhuomon Ajayi that removes an implicit dependency on Git’s global `the_repository` variable in `merge-blobs.c`. The patch is part of the ongoing project-wide effort to eliminate all such implicit references, making the codebase more modular and easier to maintain.

The patch itself is straightforward: it modifies `fill_mmfile_blob()` to accept a `struct repository *` parameter (named `r`, per the codebase’s idiomatic style) instead of relying on the global `the_repository`. The change is mechanical and uncontroversial, with no substantive objections raised during review. The only minor discussion was about parameter naming (`r` vs. `repo`), but Junio C Hamano rejected the suggested change, arguing that `r` is the established convention for `struct repository *` parameters. The reviewer, Usman Akinyemi, conceded, and the patch was approved as-is.

Junio has now marked the topic as ready for the `next` branch, meaning it has passed review and is expected to graduate to `master` in the near future. The patch remains unchanged from its original submission, and no further discussion is anticipated. This is a clean, self-contained step in the larger `the_repository` removal effort, driven primarily by René Scharfe.
