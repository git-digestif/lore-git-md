This thread is about a small refactoring patch by Okhuomon Ajayi that removes an implicit dependency on the global `the_repository` variable in `merge-blobs.c`, part of the ongoing project-wide effort to eliminate such globals. The patch itself is uncontroversial: it changes `fill_mmfile_blob()` to take an explicit `struct repository *` parameter (originally named `r`) and updates its caller accordingly.

The only point of discussion was a reviewer’s suggestion to rename the parameter from `r` to `repo` for clarity. Junio C Hamano has now weighed in, pushing back on that change. He argues that `r` is already the idiomatic choice for `struct repository *` parameters across the codebase, particularly as the libification effort advances, and that consistency with this convention outweighs the minor readability benefit of `repo`. The author had previously agreed to the rename, but Junio’s reply implies the original naming should stand. No other technical concerns have been raised, and the patch’s logic remains unchanged.

The thread is effectively resolved: the patch is ready for a `v2` that retains the `r` parameter name, and no further changes are expected. The series is still under review, with no indication of merge status.
