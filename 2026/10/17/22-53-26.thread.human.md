This thread is a small refactoring series by Okhuomon Ajayi that removes the hidden `the_repository` dependency from checkout’s `unique_tracking_name()` as part of the wider `the_repository` removal effort. The current v4 has two patches: the first adds `repo_for_each_remote()` in `remote.c`/`remote.h`, an explicit-`struct repository *` variant of `for_each_remote()` while keeping the old wrapper for existing callers; the second changes `unique_tracking_name()` to take and use a repository pointer, drops the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` macro in `checkout.c`, and updates callers in `builtin/checkout.c` and `builtin/worktree.c`. It is a mechanical modularity change with no intended behavior or on-disk change and no new tests.

Junio C Hamano’s earlier substantive concern was that the refactoring still leaned on `for_each_remote()`’s implicit repository; v3/v4 address that by introducing the explicit-repository abstraction rather than merely documenting the limitation. Christian Couder gave only low-weight line-wrapping feedback, which v4 incorporated by unwrapping lines in `repo_for_each_remote()`. Usman Akinyemi’s earlier note was surface-level and added no technical verification. The series is now at v4 and remains under review: the latest email is just Okhuomon pinging Junio and Christian to ask whether any further changes are needed, and it reports the topic sitting in `seen` as “Needs review,” which means proposed/queued only, not merged or guaranteed to reach `next` or `master`.

Nothing is currently contested, but no Reviewed-by/Acked-by or merge commitment is recorded. The main open item is simply review closure, with a possible future question of whether `repo_for_each_remote()` should be adopted more broadly across the remote subsystem.
