This thread is about a two-patch refactoring series (v3) by Okhuomon Ajayi that advances Git's ongoing effort to remove the global `the_repository` variable. The series targets the `unique_tracking_name()` function in `checkout.c`, which previously relied on the global repository. The key change is introducing `repo_for_each_remote()`, a new function that takes an explicit `struct repository *` parameter, replacing the hardcoded `the_repository` dependency in `for_each_remote()`. This ensures `unique_tracking_name()` can safely operate on arbitrary repository instances, addressing a design concern Junio raised in his review of v2.

The series is now complete and under review. Patch 1/2 introduces `repo_for_each_remote()`, while patch 2/2 updates `unique_tracking_name()` to use it, removing the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` macro and adjusting callers in `builtin/checkout.c` and `builtin/worktree.c` to pass `the_repository` explicitly. The changes are mechanical and follow established patterns in the `the_repository` removal project. No behavior changes or new CLI options are introduced, and the series is expected to pass existing tests.

Junio's earlier design concern—about the patch's scope and the limitation of `for_each_remote()`—has been fully resolved in v3. The series is self-contained, with no unresolved technical dependencies, though the new `repo_for_each_remote()` function may influence future patches in this effort. The only review so far was a surface-level comment from Usman Akinyemi on v1, while Okhuomon and Junio have engaged substantively. The series appears ready for merging once review discussion concludes.
