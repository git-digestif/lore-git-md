Okhuomon Ajayi has been iterating on a small two-patch refactoring series, now at v4, that advances the project-wide effort to eliminate the global `the_repository` variable. The series introduces a new `repo_for_each_remote()` function in `remote.c`/`remote.h` that takes an explicit `struct repository *` parameter (with `for_each_remote()` kept as a backward-compatible wrapper), and then uses it to give `unique_tracking_name()` in `checkout.c` an explicit repository parameter, removing the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` macro and updating the callers in `builtin/checkout.c` and `builtin/worktree.c`. It is a pure refactoring with no behavior change, no new tests, and no on-disk impact.

The design settled over several versions: after Junio C Hamano raised a scope concern on v2 about the hardcoded `the_repository` dependency inside `for_each_remote()`, Ajayi resolved it in v3 by adding the `repo_for_each_remote()` abstraction itself rather than documenting the limitation. Christian Couder suggested unwrapping a few lines in that new function for readability, and v4 incorporated that change. Couder has now replied to v4 with a brief acknowledgment that the series "looks good," specifically approving the line-unwrapping in patch 1 -- a surface-level sign-off with no new technical feedback.

The series currently sits in Junio's `seen` integration branch marked "Needs review," and Ajayi has pinged the list asking whether anything further is needed; no progression to `next` or `master` has been reported. With Junio's design concern addressed and no outstanding objections or requests for changes, the thread is effectively waiting on the maintainer to pick the series up. The new `repo_for_each_remote()` may also serve as a template for similar explicit-repository abstractions elsewhere in the remote subsystem as the `the_repository` removal effort continues.
