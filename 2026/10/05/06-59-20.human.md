This is a minor follow-up in the ongoing `git history` signing series, now at v5. Souma replies to Junio C Hamano’s clarification question about the completion test in the previous email. The question was whether the three periods (`...`) in `--no-...` are meant to appear literally in the test output or serve as a placeholder for the dynamically generated `--no-gpg-sign` option.

Souma confirms that `--no-...` is a deliberate literal placeholder, not a real option. It signals to users that additional `--no-*` options are available, and typing `--no-` in the shell will expand them (e.g., to `--no-gpg-sign`, `--no-dry-run`, etc.). This matches the established behavior of `git rebase` and `git commit` completion, where the helper lists all options before `--` and generated negated options after it. The clarification resolves the last open question about the test wording, leaving no remaining blockers for the series to graduate from `seen` to `next`. The series itself remains unchanged; this is purely a documentation detail for the test script.
