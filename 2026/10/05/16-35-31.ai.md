This is the second patch in Phillip Wood’s two-patch refactoring series `pw/stash-optimize-check_changes-calls/v1`. It removes the duplicate call to `check_changes()` in `do_push_stash()` and adjusts the return-value convention of `do_create_stash()` so that 2 now signals “no changes to stash” while 1 continues to mean “nothing was stashed” (e.g., `--patch` with no hunks selected or `--staged` when the index matches HEAD). The patch touches only `builtin/stash.c`, shifting the single remaining `check_changes()` call into `do_create_stash()` and updating the caller’s logic to distinguish the two cases. A minor behavior change—attempting to create the reflog entry before discovering there is nothing to stash—is noted but deemed harmless.
