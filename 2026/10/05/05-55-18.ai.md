This is a design discussion email in the `git stash create` feature thread. The author, Kazumasa Shigeta, steps back from the original patch (which added `--include-untracked` (`-u`) and `--all` (`-a`) support to `git stash create`) after Junio C Hamano’s feedback highlighted that the scope and backward-compatibility implications were not fully considered.

Shigeta now recognizes that the original patch was too narrowly focused on `-u` and `-a` and did not account for the broader compatibility contract of `git stash create <message>`, which treats the rest of the command line as a message. He outlines three possible directions for making more of `do_create_stash()`’s capabilities available while preserving that contract:

1. **Extend `stash create` further** – Add more options (e.g., `-p`, pathspecs) while using `PARSE_OPT_STOP_AT_NON_OPTION` to avoid breaking existing scripts. This would require explicit disambiguation (e.g., `--`) for messages starting with option-like arguments.
2. **Add a new subcommand** – Introduce a separate command (e.g., `git stash make`) that avoids `create`’s positional message grammar, allowing it to follow `stash push`’s option conventions without breaking backward compatibility.
3. **Add `--create-only` to `git stash push`** – Reuse `push`’s option grammar while separating its creation-only logic from the rest of its behavior. This avoids adding a new subcommand but may blur the distinction between creation and application.

Shigeta asks which direction seems most plausible and whether this thread is the right place to continue the discussion. The email does not propose a new patch yet—it is purely a design exploration. The thread remains under active review, with no merge status reported. The next step will likely be a follow-up discussion or a revised approach based on feedback.
