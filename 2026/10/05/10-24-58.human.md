This is a build system patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s ongoing Meson optimization series. The email is Patrick’s reply to Kaartic Sivaraam’s substantive review comment on patch 4/7, which extends precompiled-header support to the unit-test executable by refactoring `clar.c` into a separate static library (`libclar.a`).

Patrick acknowledges Kaartic’s observation that `clar.c` no longer inherits `libgit_c_args` compiler flags but argues the change is actually an improvement. He points out that `libgit_c_args` contains Git-specific flags (e.g., `-DWITH_BREAKING_CHANGES`) irrelevant to the vendored `clar.c` unit-test framework, and that the omitted flags are primarily warning-related, which he considers unimportant for a third-party dependency. While he concedes a few flags *might* be relevant, he judges the trade-off acceptable and suggests the commit message should have noted this design choice.

The response is pragmatic and well-reasoned, treating the concern as a minor design decision rather than a correctness issue. No further changes to the patch appear likely, though Patrick may tweak the commit message for clarity. The exchange leaves the patch’s ~5% build speedup intact and resolves the only substantive question raised during review.
