This thread is about a small but necessary cleanup to the experimental `git repo` command. Patrick Steinhardt sent a patch renaming its configuration key from `references.format` to `references.storageFormat`, aligning it with the rest of Git’s ref-storage-format abstraction that was merged earlier. The change is mechanical—updating the man page, the C code, and the test suite—and breaks backward compatibility, but everyone agrees that’s fine because `git repo` is still marked experimental.

Karthik Nayak reviewed the patch and noted the compatibility break was acceptable. Patrick replied to confirm that stance, briefly considering but rejecting the idea of temporarily supporting both keys. There’s no controversy or open questions; the patch is ready for Junio to pick up whenever he gets to it. This is a follow-up to the larger ref-storage-format effort, but it stands on its own as a one-off cleanup.
