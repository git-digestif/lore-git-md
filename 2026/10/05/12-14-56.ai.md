This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing RFC thread about improving Git’s error reporting for invalid `--git-dir` directories. The email is a reply from the author, Kaartic Sivaraam, to Patrick Steinhardt’s substantive review of the third patch in the v2 series, which introduces `is_git_directory_verbose()`.

Kaartic acknowledges Patrick’s three technical points: (1) the need to include `strerror(errno)` in error messages for system-level context (e.g., "Permission denied"), (2) the interleaved implementation of `is_git_directory_verbose()` with `validate_headref()` modifications, which complicates review, and (3) the implied requirement for consistent error reporting across all paths in the new helper. He agrees to address the first two points in the next iteration and notes the third. This response does not engage with Junio C Hamano’s broader design critique (reported in the thread summary) about whether detailed failure reasons are the right approach; the focus here is purely on implementation details.

The exchange confirms that Patrick’s feedback is uncontested but unresolved, and that Kaartic intends to refine the patch before expanding the RFC’s scope to other repository discovery cases. No new technical concerns or objections are raised in this reply. The unresolved design question—whether the series should focus on detailed failure reasons or on reporting the inspected path and its origin—remains the primary open issue.
