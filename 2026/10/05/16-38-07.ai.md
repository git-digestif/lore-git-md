Phillip Wood engages directly with Kazumasa Shigeta’s proposed design directions, offering technical feedback on which options from `git stash push` make sense to expose in a script-focused `stash create` command.

On backward compatibility, he endorses `PARSE_OPT_STOP_AT_NON_OPTION` as a reasonable way to preserve the existing positional-message grammar, noting that messages starting with `-` are rare in practice. He explicitly recommends adding `-m`/`--message` for consistency with other Git commands.

The review is narrowly scoped to the first of Shigeta’s three directions (extending `stash create`), offering no opinion on the new-subcommand or `--create-only` alternatives. Phillip’s feedback is pragmatic and grounded in script use cases, making it a substantive contribution to the design discussion rather than a surface-level nit.
