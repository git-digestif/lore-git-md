This is a refactoring patch series targeting the `git stash` command. Phillip Wood removes redundant checks for unstaged or uncommitted changes in `git stash push` and `git stash create`. Currently, both commands check for changes at startup and again when creating the stash; the series eliminates the duplicate effort. The motivation stems from a recent patch by Kazumasa Shigeta (referenced in the cover letter), though this series does not depend on it.

The series consists of two patches, both touching `builtin/stash.c`. The changes are mechanical: they consolidate the change-detection logic into a single call rather than repeating it. No behavior change is intended, and the commit messages do not mention any measurable performance impact, so this is purely a code hygiene improvement. The patches are small (15 insertions, 12 deletions) and focused on the `do_create_stash()` and `push_stash()` functions.

Phillip is a well-regarded contributor with deep expertise in the sequencer and stash subsystems, so the patches are likely to be technically sound. The series is marked as `pw/stash-optimize-check_changes-calls/v1`, indicating it is a first iteration and may receive review feedback. No prior discussion or review is mentioned in the cover letter.

Key technical details for follow-up: the series modifies `do_create_stash()` and `push_stash()` in `builtin/stash.c`, removes calls to `check_changes()`, and introduces no new functions or options. The base commit is `c46c1e37724f0478939de636ab8ea5a89086d532`.
