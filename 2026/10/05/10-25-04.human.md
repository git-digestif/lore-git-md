This is the sixth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch Meson build improvement series, focusing on build speed, test reliability, and CI fixes. The patch itself updates the bundled subproject wrappers (curl, expat, openssl, pcre2, zlib) to their latest versions available in Meson’s wrapdb, a routine maintenance task with no behavioral changes.

Patrick’s reply to Kaartic Sivaraam’s earlier concern about the version gap between wrapdb and upstream releases is pragmatic. He acknowledges the lag (e.g., curl 8.12.1 vs. 8.22.0, openssl 3.0.10 vs. 3.0.22/4.0.1) but notes that these wrappers are primarily used for GitLab CI’s Windows builds, not production deployments. His position is to leave the current setup as-is for now, with a nod to potential future collaboration with wrapdb upstream to reduce the gap. No immediate action is planned, and the patch remains uncontroversial.

The discussion highlights a minor but recurring tension in Git’s build infrastructure: the trade-off between convenience (using Meson’s wrapdb for dependency-free builds) and currency (keeping dependencies up-to-date). For now, the project appears content to prioritize CI reliability over cutting-edge versions, but this could resurface if security vulnerabilities or missing features in the older versions become pressing. The thread leaves the door open for follow-up, though no concrete steps are proposed yet.
