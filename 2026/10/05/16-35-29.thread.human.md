This thread is a seven-patch refactoring series by Phillip Wood, `pw/stash-optimize-check_changes-calls/v2`, aimed at eliminating redundant index operations in `git stash push` and `git stash create`. The series consolidates duplicate `check_changes()` calls, reduces unnecessary index loads and refreshes, and refines the return-value convention of `do_create_stash()` with an `enum create_result` to distinguish between "no changes to stash" and "nothing stashed." The changes are confined to `builtin/stash.c` and focus on mechanical performance improvements, reducing `lstat()` calls per changed path by up to eight for `push` and seven for `create`, and cutting untracked-file walks by one.

The series is now complete with the final patch simplifying the `reset_tree()` function signature by removing two unused parameters (`update` and `reset`), as its only caller, `do_apply_stash()`, does not use them. This aligns with the series' broader goal of streamlining stash internals without altering user-visible behavior.
