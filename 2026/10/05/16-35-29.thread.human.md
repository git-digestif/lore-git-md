This thread is about Phillip Wood’s seven-patch series `pw/stash-optimize-check_changes-calls/v2`, which refactors `git stash push` and `git stash create` to eliminate redundant index operations and `lstat()` calls. The goal is to consolidate duplicate change-detection logic, index loads, and refreshes, reducing overhead while maintaining identical user-visible behavior. The series has evolved from an initial four-patch version to address feedback and expand the scope of optimizations.

Phillip, an established contributor with deep expertise in Git’s stash and sequencer subsystems, has incorporated all feedback from Junio C Hamano, the maintainer. The fifth patch, just posted, replaces `reset_tree()` with `create_index_from_tree()` in `git stash create`, aligning it with the logic already used by `git stash push -p`. This change simplifies the code, avoids an extra `lstat()` per changed path, and preserves intent-to-add entries without altering behavior.
