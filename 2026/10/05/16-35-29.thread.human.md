This thread is a refactoring series by Phillip Wood aimed at optimizing redundant change-detection logic, index loads, and refreshes in `git stash push` and `git stash create`. The series, now in its third iteration (`v3`), consists of seven patches that consolidate duplicate operations, reducing `lstat()` calls per changed path by up to eight for `push` and four for `create`, and eliminating redundant untracked-file walks. The work is confined to `builtin/stash.c` and introduces an `enum create_result` to refine the return-value convention of `do_create_stash()`, distinguishing between "no changes to stash," "nothing stashed" (e.g., `--patch` with no hunks selected), and success/error states. The series also corrects a minor behavioral inconsistency where `create_stash()` previously exited with status 1 when no changes were detected, despite having already determined this via `check_changes()`; it now exits 0 in such cases, aligning with documented behavior.

The current iteration addresses all feedback from Junio C Hamano on earlier versions, including commit message clarifications and the removal of redundant index operations. The fourth patch, just posted, focuses on eliminating duplicate `preload_index()` calls by shifting index-reading responsibility to callers, cutting two unnecessary `lstat()` calls per changed path. The series is uncontroversial, with no technical objections or edge cases raised, and is expected to proceed smoothly toward integration.
