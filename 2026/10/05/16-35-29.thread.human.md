This thread is about a refactoring series by Phillip Wood to optimize redundant change-detection logic, index loads, and refreshes in `git stash push` and `git stash create`. The goal is to eliminate unnecessary `check_changes()` calls, index operations (`repo_read_preload_index()` and `refresh_index()`), and untracked-file walks, reducing `lstat()` overhead by up to eight calls per changed path. The series also introduces an `enum create_result` to clarify exit statuses and fixes a minor behavioral inconsistency where `create_stash()` previously exited with status 1 when no changes were detected, despite its documented behavior.
