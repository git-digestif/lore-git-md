The series is now cooking in Junio C Hamano’s `next` branch after receiving positive feedback and is on track to graduate to `master` unless issues emerge during integration testing.

Phillip, an established contributor with deep expertise in Git’s stash and sequencer subsystems, authored the series. This change preserves intent-to-add entries and reduces `lstat()` calls by one per changed path, aligning with the series’ goal of optimizing performance without altering user-visible behavior. Junio praised this patch in his review of `v3` as "the most interesting step in the series," noting its elimination of in-core index flipping and side effects.
