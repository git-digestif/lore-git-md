The `pw/stash-optimize-check_changes-calls/v2` series by Phillip Wood continues to refine the performance of `git stash push` and `git stash create` by eliminating redundant filesystem operations. This seven-patch series, now in its second iteration, targets duplicate `check_changes()` calls, unnecessary index loads, and superfluous refreshes that inflate `lstat()` overhead—cutting seven `lstat()` calls per changed path for `create` and eight for `push`, along with one fewer untracked-file walk. The series also introduces an `enum create_result` to clarify exit statuses, ensuring `create_stash()` exits 0 when there are no changes to stash, a behavioral correction that aligns with its documented intent.
