This thread is about a small refactoring series by Phillip Wood that eliminates redundant change-detection logic in `git stash push` and `git stash create`. The goal is to consolidate multiple `check_changes()` calls into a single call for cleaner code, with no intended behavior change. The series is now at its first iteration (`v1`) and consists of two patches touching `builtin/stash.c`.

Phillip, an established contributor with deep expertise in the stash subsystem, authored the series. Junio C Hamano reviewed it and caught a newly introduced inefficiency: the second patch causes `git stash push` to refresh the index twice—once in `do_push_stash()` and again in `do_create_stash()`. Phillip acknowledged this and agreed to address it in a follow-up patch by making callers responsible for refreshing the index, which would also avoid redundant `preload_index()` calls. Junio’s feedback was otherwise positive, focusing on commit message wording in the first patch and the technical inefficiency in the second. No other reviewers have weighed in yet.

The series is uncontroversial and under active review. The only loose end is the planned follow-up to fix the redundant index refresh. The changes are mechanical, touching only `builtin/stash.c`, and do not introduce new options, symbols, or on-disk format changes. The base commit is `c46c1e37724f0478939de636ab8ea5a89086d532`.
