This thread is a small but focused refactoring effort by Phillip Wood to clean up redundant change-detection logic in `git stash push` and `git stash create`. The series, now at its first iteration (`v1`), consists of two patches that remove duplicate calls to `check_changes()`—one in each command—without altering the user-facing behavior. Phillip, a well-established contributor with deep expertise in the stash and sequencer subsystems, authored the series after a recent patch by Kazumasa Shigeta highlighted the redundancy.

The changes are mechanical, touching only `builtin/stash.c` and adjusting how `do_create_stash()` and `push_stash()` handle return values to distinguish between "no changes to stash" and "nothing was stashed" (e.g., due to `--patch` with no hunks selected). The second patch, summarized here, consolidates the remaining `check_changes()` call into `do_create_stash()` and tweaks the caller’s logic to account for the new return-value convention. A minor behavioral quirk—creating the reflog entry before confirming there’s work to stash—is acknowledged but considered harmless.

So far, the series has not drawn any review feedback, which isn’t surprising given its narrow scope and lack of behavioral changes. The main risk would be unintended side effects in edge cases, but none have been raised yet. If the series proceeds smoothly, it’s likely to be queued for integration once reviewers have a chance to weigh in.
