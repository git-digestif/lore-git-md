This is the second patch in Julia Evans’s already-merged v2 series removing the obsolete `gittutorial-2` document. It scrubs every cross-reference to `gittutorial-2` from the documentation tree and replaces them with a pointer to `gitdatamodel[7]`, which is the modern, actively maintained explanation of Git’s object model. The files touched are `MyFirstObjectWalk.adoc`, `git.adoc`, `gitcore-tutorial.adoc`, `gitcvs-migration.adoc`, `gitglossary.adoc`, and `gittutorial.adoc`—all in the `Documentation/` directory.

The patch is purely mechanical: no new concepts are introduced, and the only behavior change is that users who follow old links will now land on `gitdatamodel[7]` instead of the soon-to-be-deleted `gittutorial-2`. The update to `gittutorial.adoc` itself is deliberately minimal because the entire document is slated for removal in a follow-up series; Julia simply rewrites the “Next Steps” section to drop the now-irrelevant discussion of the object database and index file, replacing it with a single sentence directing readers to `gitdatamodel[7]`.

Since the series is already merged to `master` and the build break in `seen` was resolved via a merge-fix, this patch is effectively final. The only remaining discussion in the thread centers on whether the older `gittutorial` (part one) should also be removed or temporarily preserved until a modern replacement is ready. This patch does not engage with that question; it is a self-contained cleanup step that leaves the documentation tree in a consistent state.
