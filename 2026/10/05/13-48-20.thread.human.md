This thread reports three date-handling bugs in Git’s ZIP archive generation and `git fast-import` logic, with real-world tool compatibility risks. The most progress has been on the ZIP extended timestamp wrap issue (bug #2), where René Scharfe’s patch omits the Extended Timestamp (UT) extra field for out-of-range commit times to prevent silent misinterpretation. However, the patch’s tests initially failed on 32-bit platforms due to an incorrect prerequisite assumption.

Matthew E. Luallen has now submitted a follow-up fix adjusting the test prerequisites to use `TIME_T_IS_64BIT` instead of `TIME_IS_64BIT`. This ensures the tests skip correctly on systems where `zipinfo(1)` lacks UT field support, resolving the CI breakage. With this change, the UT field omission logic is technically sound and ready for integration. The patch remains narrowly scoped and does not address the other two bugs: the ZIP legacy DOS date overflow (bug #1) and `fast-import` negative timestamp validation (bug #3). René now advocates rejecting out-of-range timestamps for the legacy DOS date, but no consensus has emerged, and the `fast-import` issue remains contentious. Junio C Hamano’s report of CI breakage has been addressed, leaving the UT field fix unblocked.
