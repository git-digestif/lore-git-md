This is a maintainer response to the narrowed RFC for porcelain wrappers to share Git stashes through remotes. Junio C Hamano aligns with **Johannes Sixt (Hannes)**'s opposition, stating that branches already provide robust support for discovery, transfer, and cleanup of shared work. He sees no justification for adding parallel functionality to stashes, reinforcing the view that stashes should remain personal and ephemeral. The message is brief but decisive, signaling that the proposal in its current form is unlikely to gain traction without a compelling counterargument to the "use branches" position. No technical objections to the implementation are raised; the rejection is purely philosophical.
