This is the fourth and final patch in Kaartic Sivaraam’s RFC v2 series improving Git’s error reporting for invalid `--git-dir` directories. The patch integrates the new `is_git_directory_verbose()` helper into `repo_discover_explicit_gitdir()` to append explanatory "reason:" lines to error messages (e.g., "reason: cannot access object directory '/does/not/exist' set via $GIT_OBJECT_DIRECTORY"). The thread remains in the RFC phase, with Junio C Hamano’s earlier critique questioning whether detailed failure reasons are the right solution—he argues that reporting the inspected path and its origin (e.g., "inspecting '/path/to/dir' (from $GIT_DIR)") would be more diagnostically useful.

Patrick Steinhardt’s review here is purely surface-level, focusing on presentation rather than design or correctness. He suggests two minor tweaks: correcting a grammar nit in the commit message ("more easy" → "easier") and renaming the `strbuf` variable from `invalid_gitdir_reason` to `errbuf`. He also expresses a subjective preference for combining the "fatal:" and "reason:" lines or using an "error:" prefix, though he acknowledges this is a matter of taste. Kaartic acknowledges the feedback and plans to address it in the next iteration. No technical objections are raised, but Junio’s unresolved critique may necessitate a significant rethink of the series’ direction before further implementation work proceeds. The patch touches only `setup.c` and does not alter on-disk formats or existing APIs.
