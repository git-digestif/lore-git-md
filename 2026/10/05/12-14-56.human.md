This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing RFC thread about improving Git’s error messages for invalid `--git-dir` directories. The email is a reply from the author, Kaartic Sivaraam, to Patrick Steinhardt’s substantive review of the third patch in the v2 series, which introduces `is_git_directory_verbose()`.

Kaartic acknowledges Patrick’s three technical concerns: missing `strerror(errno)` in error messages, interleaved implementation of the new helper with `validate_headref()` modifications, and the need for consistency in error reporting. He agrees that including system-level errors (e.g., "Permission denied") would improve diagnostic value and plans to address this in the next iteration. He also accepts Patrick’s suggestion to reorganize the code for better reviewability, likely by splitting the introduction of `is_git_directory_verbose()` into a separate preparatory commit.

The exchange is purely technical and does not engage with Junio C Hamano’s broader design critique from earlier in the thread. Kaartic’s responses are brief and focused on implementation details, leaving the larger question of whether the series’ direction aligns with user needs unaddressed for now. No new objections or alternatives are raised here, but the unresolved design-level pushback from Junio remains the elephant in the room. If the series proceeds, expect a v3 that incorporates these fixes while potentially rethinking its approach to error reporting.
