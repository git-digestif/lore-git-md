This is a bugfix patch for `git stash create`. Phillip Wood identifies and removes a redundant check for unstaged or uncommitted changes that was introduced in ef0f0b4509 (2019). The commit message explains that the double-check—once before refreshing the index and again afterward—serves no clear optimization purpose and can even be unreliable. The patch restores the original return-code handling to ensure `git stash create` exits with status 0 when there are no changes to stash, matching the expected behavior. The change is small (8 lines in `builtin/stash.c`) and well-motivated, with a clear reference to the problematic commit. It looks like a straightforward cleanup that should be uncontroversial.
