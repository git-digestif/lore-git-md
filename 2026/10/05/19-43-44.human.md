This is a review comment on the second patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 13-patch series refactoring Git’s object database to move alternates handling into the files backend. The series aims to eliminate a long-standing architectural mismatch where alternates were managed at the ODB source level, complicating data structures and blocking future ODB work.

Karthik Nayak’s feedback is substantive but narrowly scoped. He points out a potential inconsistency in `git_test_write_commit_graph_or_die()`: the function now takes only a `struct repository *` but internally uses `repo->objects->sources->path` to derive the object directory path. His suggestion is to make the caller explicitly pass the path, aligning with the patch’s broader goal of decoupling the commit-graph subsystem from `struct odb_source`. This would also make the function’s behavior more transparent and avoid hidden dependencies on the ODB source structure.

The comment is technically sound and directly engages with the patch’s architectural intent. It doesn’t challenge the overall direction of the series but flags a small but meaningful detail that could affect future maintainability. The change itself is minor—likely a one-line adjustment—but it reinforces the patch’s stated goal of making the commit-graph subsystem backend-agnostic. No other concerns are raised, and the feedback doesn’t appear to block progress. Patrick will likely address it in a reroll.
