This is a surface-level review reply from Kaartic Sivaraam to Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch Meson build improvement series. The email acknowledges the series as a whole and offers two minor observations. First, Kaartic notes that the `compat` directory is intentionally left out of precompiled-header support, reasoning that including it would create recursive compilation issues due to `git-compat-util.h`’s dependency on `compat/posix.h`; he judges the effort to refactor the build code to resolve this as not worthwhile given the relatively small amount of code in `compat`. Second, he briefly mentions that other small targets like `common-main` and `git-curl` are also not worth optimizing in this series. Beyond these points, he restates that the series looks good to him, echoing his earlier feedback on individual patches. No new technical concerns or edge cases are raised, and the tone remains positive but non-substantive.
