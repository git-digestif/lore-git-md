This is a design discussion in the ongoing thread about extending `git stash create` to support `--include-untracked` (`-u`) and `--all` (`-a`). Junio C Hamano responds to Kazumasa Shigeta’s exploration of three possible directions for exposing more of `do_create_stash()`’s capabilities without breaking backward compatibility.

Junio agrees with Shigeta’s framing: the core issue is not just adding `-u` and `-a`, but how to make more of `do_create_stash()`’s functionality available while preserving `git stash create <message>`’s positional message grammar. He suggests that forbidding messages starting with `-` (with an escape hatch like `-m`) would not be a major backward-compatibility break, though he does not endorse this as the final solution. He also notes that adding a new subcommand (option 2) mirrors how `git stash save` evolved into `git stash push`, and calls option 3 (`--create-only` for `git stash push`) "the safest."

The email does not settle on a direction but clarifies Junio’s leanings: he sees all three options as viable, with a slight preference for `--create-only` as the least disruptive. The thread remains in the design phase, with no patch or merge status yet. The next step will likely be further discussion or a revised proposal from Shigeta.
