Mark acknowledges that this shift was motivated by Patrick’s feedback, prioritizing flexibility over the simpler but less powerful type-based filtering. This pivot resolves the central design objection from v1, making the feature’s goal uncontroversial in principle. However, the new implementation will need scrutiny around **reachability logic** (e.g., how included/excluded revisions interact) and **performance implications** for large repositories.
