This thread is about a bugfix patch that resolves an infinite loop in Git's `sideband.allowControlCharacters` configuration parser, which was introduced in March 2026. The bug caused `git fetch`, `git push`, `git clone`, and `git ls-remote` to hang indefinitely for users with malformed configurations, such as typos ("colour") or unsupported keywords.

The patch, authored by Thomas Bachem, fixes the issue by advancing the parser to the next comma-separated entry after warning about an unrecognized keyword, rather than looping endlessly on the problematic entry. The warning message has also been tightened to show only the unrecognized keyword. A new test verifies the fix by ensuring the parser skips unrecognized keywords (e.g., "bogus") and still processes valid ones that follow.

The patch has been reviewed and endorsed by Junio C Hamano, who dismissed a minor style concern about the use of the `!!` operator, calling it "compelling" and easier to read. Tuomas Ahola, who initially raised the style nit, later volunteered to submit a separate patch to update the `CodingGuidelines` to remove the warning about `!!`. The patch is now ready for integration and has been marked for the `next` branch. No substantive concerns remain, and the fix is minimal, focused, and uncontroversial.
