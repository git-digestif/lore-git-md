This is a CI/build system patch from Patrick Steinhardt. It fixes a long-standing annoyance in GitLab CI where the "check-whitespace" and "check-style" jobs fail for merge requests with more than 50 commits. The root cause is that GitLab's default shallow clone (depth=50) prevents the jobs from accessing the base commit they need to compare against. The fix is simple: set `GIT_DEPTH: 0` for both jobs to force a full history fetch.

The patch is well-motivated and clearly explained. It addresses a real pain point for GitLab's internal development (where large merge requests are common) while also improving the reliability of the public CI pipeline. The change is minimal and targeted, touching only the two affected jobs in `.gitlab-ci.yml`. No controversy is expected here—this looks like a straightforward fix that should just work.
