This is a CI/build system follow-up discussion about whether to intentionally reintroduce a `NO_RUST` job in Git’s CI configurations. The thread stems from Kaartic Sivaraam’s observation that Rust has been *accidentally* disabled in CI for six months due to a leftover `NO_RUST=UnfortunatelyYes` flag tied to a now-removed job (`linux-clang`). Junio C Hamano suggests folding this fix into the current series, arguing it aligns with the series’ goal of ensuring test coverage (like patch 8/8’s reftable coverage) and would prevent future oversight. He proposes adding the flag to either the `linux-feature-testbed` or `linux32` job, noting the effort is small and temporary—until Rust becomes mandatory.

Patrick Steinhardt had previously acknowledged the issue but preferred to address it in a follow-up series, citing consistency with other deferred improvements. The discussion is now a logistical question: whether to include this change now or later. No technical objections exist; the decision hinges on whether the fix is seen as a natural extension of the current housekeeping effort or a separate concern. Junio’s tone suggests a preference for immediate action, framing it as a low-risk addition that would avoid future forgetfulness. The thread remains uncontested on the broader series, which is already marked for `next`.
