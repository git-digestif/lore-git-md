This is a CI/build system follow-up discussion about a latent issue in Patrick Steinhardt’s recently finalized CI housekeeping series. Junio suggests addressing this now by intentionally reintroducing `NO_RUST` in an existing Makefile-based job—either `linux-feature-testbed` (the renamed `linux-TEST-vars` job) or `linux32`—to ensure the non-Rust build path remains tested. His rationale is pragmatic: the effort is small, it aligns with the series’ goal of maintaining test coverage, and it prevents the issue from being forgotten. He also notes that this is temporary, as Rust will eventually become a hard requirement.

The discussion remains low-stakes and forward-looking. There is no disagreement, only a question of timing and placement. If Patrick or another contributor picks this up, expect a one-patch follow-up that moves the `NO_RUST` flag to an active job, with minimal churn and no behavioral changes. For now, this is a gentle nudge rather than a blocker.
