This is a **bugfix patch update** in Justin Tobler’s three-patch series addressing a race condition in Git’s ODB transaction machinery. The series targets the `.keep` lockfile cleanup logic, where concurrent `git-receive-pack` processes could delete each other’s lockfiles, leaving newly written packfiles vulnerable to deletion.

This email is Justin’s reply to Patrick Steinhardt’s **substantive technical review** of the second patch. The patch refactors `odb_transaction_files_write_pack()` to write `.keep` lockfiles explicitly rather than relying on `git-index-pack --keep`, a preparatory step for moving lockfile creation to the transaction commit phase (patch 3/3). Justin accepts all of Patrick’s requested changes: renaming `write_pack_lock()` to `write_pack_lockfile()` for consistency, fixing a style nit (`if (close(fd) < 0)`), and consolidating error-handling paths to simplify resource cleanup. He declines to move the `safe_create_file_with_leading_directories()` call to the final patch, arguing that keeping it here is more defensive and reduces overall churn, though he acknowledges the assumption that leading directories exist during quarantine is safe.

The discussion remains focused on incremental polish; there are no objections to the patch’s core logic or goals. The series is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort and touches `object-file.c`, `pack-write.c`, and `t/t5547-push-quarantine.sh`. Key symbols introduced or modified include `write_pack_lockfile()` (new helper) and `odb_transaction_files_write_pack()` (refactored to defer lockfile creation). The fix ensures lockfiles are only written during transaction commit, eliminating the race condition. Test coverage in `t5547` verifies lockfile deferral and concurrent-process safety. The thread is on track for minor revisions before merging.
