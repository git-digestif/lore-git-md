This is a **surface-level review** of the first patch in D. Ben Knoble’s five-part series improving `git stash` shell completion. The patch refactors Bash completion to use Git’s built-in `--git-completion-helper` mechanism instead of a hard-coded list of subcommands, reducing duplication and aligning `_git_stash` with other completion helpers.

Patrick Steinhardt’s feedback is minor: he suggests a grammar tweak in the commit message ("bringing" instead of "bring") and notes that the patch incidentally fixes an omission—the "store" subcommand was missing from the hard-coded list and will now be included. He also clarifies the behavior around the deprecated "save" subcommand: while it won’t appear in default completions (e.g., `git stash sa<TAB>`), its options will still complete if the user explicitly types `git stash save --<TAB>`. The review is positive but doesn’t engage with deeper technical trade-offs; the patch itself is uncontroversial and likely to be accepted as-is. No further discussion or revisions are expected for this specific change.
