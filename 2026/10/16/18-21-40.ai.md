This is a reply in the ongoing design discussion about inconsistent branch name validation in Git's porcelain commands. Kristoffer Haugsbakk shares a personal workaround (`git symbolic-ref refs/H HEAD`) as a pragmatic response to the usability trap of being unable to check out a branch named `@` via porcelain commands. The reply does not engage with the core design debate—whether to tighten validation to reject bare `@` as a branch name—but instead offers a low-friction alternative for users who encounter the issue.

The workaround itself is a minor technical detail: it creates a symbolic ref (`refs/H`) pointing to `HEAD`, allowing the user to type `git switch H` as a substitute for `git switch @`. While this sidesteps the porcelain limitation, it does not address the underlying inconsistency (the discrepancy between `check-ref-format` and porcelain commands) or the broader question of whether `@` should be allowed as a branch name. The reply is surface-level, providing no new arguments for or against stricter validation, and does not advance the thread toward a resolution.
