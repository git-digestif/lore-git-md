This is a review follow-up to the second patch in Johannes Schindelin’s five-part series fixing integer overflows flagged by Coverity. The patch changes the `posn` variable in `http.c` from `int` to `size_t` to match the type of the total size parameter, eliminating a latent heap under-read risk when HTTP response bodies exceed INT_MAX (~2 GB).

Junio’s reply is brief but substantive. He initially questions whether the loop in `fwrite_sha1_file()` should clamp the byte count passed to `xwrite()` to avoid exceeding `ssize_t` range early in the loop, but after checking `git-compat-util.h`, he confirms that `xwrite()` already handles this internally via `MAX_IO_SIZE`. His conclusion aligns with Patrick Steinhardt’s earlier review: the type change is correct and no further adjustments are needed. The patch remains uncontroversial and ready for integration, with no new open questions introduced. The broader `SSIZE_MAX` platform-compatibility concern raised in patch 3 is still unresolved, but this specific patch is not affected.
