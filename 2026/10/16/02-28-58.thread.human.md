This thread is about a bugfix series from Justin Tobler that fixes a race condition in Git’s object database (ODB) transaction machinery. The issue occurs when concurrent `git-receive-pack` processes delete each other’s `.keep` lockfiles, leaving newly written packfiles vulnerable to deletion by housekeeping processes. The fix moves lockfile creation to the transaction commit phase, ensuring lockfiles are only written after the transaction is guaranteed to succeed.

The series is now in its fourth version (v4) and has received explicit approval from Patrick Steinhardt, who reviewed all four patches and called the series technically sound and well-structured. The v4 changes incorporate a final stylistic tweak Patrick requested—explicitly initializing the `ret` variable in `write_pack_lockfile()` to enable compiler warnings. The test coverage in `t/t5547-push-quarantine.sh` verifies the fix, including scenarios with concurrent processes. The only loose end is an unaddressed assumption about leading directories for lockfiles during quarantine, but this was not revisited in Patrick’s final review and is not considered a blocker.

Junio C Hamano has noted that the series is "Expecting a reroll" for the minor stylistic adjustment, signaling readiness to merge once v4 is submitted. The series is otherwise complete and ready for integration. Key participants are Justin Tobler (author) and Patrick Steinhardt (reviewer), with no substantive objections remaining.
