This thread began as a four-patch series (v4) by Justin Tobler to fix a race condition in Git’s object database transaction machinery, where concurrent `git-receive-pack` processes could delete each other’s `.keep` lockfiles, leaving newly written packfiles vulnerable to deletion. The fix moved lockfile creation to the transaction commit phase, ensuring lockfiles are only written after the transaction is guaranteed to succeed. The series was reviewed by Patrick Steinhardt, who approved all four patches, and Karthik Nayak, who requested minor clarifications and two trivial fixes. Junio C Hamano signaled readiness to merge the series once the v4 reroll was submitted, and it is now cooking in `next`.

The follow-up email from Justin Tobler addresses one of Karthik’s minor requests: replacing `unlink()` with `unlink_or_warn()` in the error path of `write_pack_lockfile()` to avoid silently failing if the unlink operation itself fails. This change is purely defensive and does not affect the core logic or correctness of the series. Justin plans to send a standalone patch for this fix. The only remaining loose end from the original series—the assumption that leading directories for lockfiles exist during quarantine—was flagged by Patrick but not addressed, though it is not considered a blocker. The series is otherwise complete and ready for integration.
