This thread is a routine maintenance update to Git’s GitHub Actions CI workflow, switching the macOS runner image from `macos-14` to `macos-15` in `.github/workflows/main.yml`. The change is prompted by GitHub’s deprecation of the older image and is purely mechanical—replacing `pool: macos-14` with `pool: macos-15`—with no functional impact on Git’s codebase. Junio C Hamano posted the patch, which remains untested due to lack of macOS access.

Brian m. carlson chimed in with a surface-level endorsement, agreeing that `macos-15` is the safer choice over `macos-latest` (currently `macos-26`) to avoid potential compatibility issues with newer macOS features. He noted that POSIX-related functionality is stable across versions, so the upgrade should not introduce problems—assuming the CI jobs pass. However, the patch still lacks testing, and no merge status has been assigned. The only file touched is `.github/workflows/main.yml`, affecting the `osx-clang`, `osx-reftable`, `osx-gcc`, and `osx-meson` jobs. No open questions or technical concerns have been raised yet, but the untested nature of the change leaves room for platform-specific quirks to surface later.
