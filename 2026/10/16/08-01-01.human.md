This is a **review reply** to the third patch in D. Ben Knoble’s five-part series improving `git stash` shell completion. The patch fixes a long-standing Zsh-specific bug where stash names (e.g., `stash@{0}`) failed to complete correctly due to Zsh’s brace expansion behavior. Patrick Steinhardt’s review is **surface-level**: he catches a minor typo in the commit message (`s/things/thinks/`) and asks whether the author explored a solution that would work for both Bash and Zsh, avoiding the need for a Zsh-specific override. The question is reasonable but does not engage with the technical substance of the fix itself (removing the `-Q` flag from `compadd` to allow Zsh to quote metacharacters). No objections or approvals are raised, and the patch remains uncontroversial. The review does not test the change or suggest alternatives, so it is unlikely to derail the series, but it does signal that the author may need to justify the platform-specific approach in a follow-up.
