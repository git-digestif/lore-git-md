This is Junio’s integration notice for the `ps/odb-files-alternates-cleanups` series. The five-patch refactoring and optimization of alternates handling in the Git object database’s "files" backend is now **ready for `next`**, meaning Junio has queued it for integration testing alongside other topics. No further changes are expected; the series has received substantive, tested reviews from Justin Tobler and surface-level but actionable feedback from Johannes Schindelin, and all prior feedback has been addressed.

The series targets performance inefficiencies in reading and streaming objects when alternates are present, achieving a 1.89x speedup for `git cat-file --batch-check` in benchmarks. Key changes include reordering loops to prioritize packfiles over loose objects, confining second-read logic to `odb/source-files.c`, removing a redundant read loop in `do_oid_object_info_extended()`, and eliminating the `local` flag from the generic ODB source struct. The new test `t/t5615-alternate-env.sh` verifies the performance-critical behavior, and the series has been validated on both SHA-1 and SHA-256 repositories.

The only deferred concern—the long-term viability of the `ODB_FOR_EACH_OBJECT_LOCAL_ONLY` flag—was explicitly scoped as a follow-up effort by Patrick Steinhardt, with Justin Tobler’s agreement. The series is uncontroversial and touches 12 files (excluding the test script), primarily in the ODB subsystem (`odb.c`, `odb/source-files.c`, `odb/source-packed.c`, `midx.c`, and `repack.c`). No user-visible behavior changes or on-disk format modifications are introduced. The patches are now in Junio’s queue and will appear in the next "What’s cooking" report.
