This is a review follow-up to the second patch in Johannes Schindelin’s five-patch series hardening Git against integer overflows flagged by Coverity. The patch changes the `posn` variable in `http.c` from `int` to `size_t` to match the total size parameter, eliminating a latent heap under-read risk when HTTP response bodies exceed INT_MAX (~2 GB).

Junio’s reply is substantive but brief. He initially questions whether the loop should clamp the byte count passed to `xwrite()` to avoid exceeding `ssize_t` range early in the loop, but after consulting `git-compat-util.h`, he confirms that `xwrite()` already handles clamping via `MAX_IO_SIZE`. His conclusion is that the type change is correct and no further adjustments are needed. The patch remains uncontroversial and ready for integration, with no new open questions introduced. The broader platform-compatibility concern about `SSIZE_MAX` vs. `size_t` width mismatch (raised in patch 3) is not addressed here but remains the only unresolved technical point in the series.
