This thread began with Patrick Steinhardt’s patch to fix GitLab CI job failures in merge requests with more than 50 commits by forcing full clones (`GIT_DEPTH: 0`). The patch itself is a straightforward, uncontested fix to `.gitlab-ci.yml` that mirrors an existing GitHub CI adjustment. Junio C Hamano has indicated he will queue it, likely to `next` or `seen`.

The discussion has since expanded into a broader, quieter debate about whether the "check-whitespace" and "check-style" jobs are worth maintaining. Patrick, the patch author, now concedes that both jobs have limited utility: "check-style" is non-authoritative due to Git’s inconsistent clang-format adoption, and "check-whitespace" is unreliable. Junio initially questioned whether the jobs might encourage poorly formatted patches, but in his latest reply he softens slightly, noting he hasn’t seen false positives from "check-whitespace" himself. Karthik Nayak argues the jobs still offer marginal guidance, catching issues "here and there" despite their flaws. No one is strongly defending the jobs’ current value, but no one is pushing to remove them either. The patch will proceed, and the jobs will remain in place for now, their long-term fate still unresolved.
