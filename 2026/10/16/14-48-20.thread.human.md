This thread is about a small but practical fix to GitLab CI jobs that check for whitespace and style issues. Patrick Steinhardt noticed that these jobs fail in merge requests with more than 50 commits because GitLab’s default shallow clone (depth=50) prevents the jobs from accessing the full history they need to compare against the base commit. His patch forces full clones by setting `GIT_DEPTH: 0` in `.gitlab-ci.yml`, mirroring a similar fix already in place for GitHub CI.

Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, has acknowledged the annoyance the failure causes but raised a broader question: are these jobs even worth keeping? He wonders if disabling them entirely would just lead to a flood of poorly formatted patches. Despite his skepticism, he plans to queue the patch, so it will likely move forward unless someone argues strongly for removing the jobs instead. No other reviewers have weighed in yet, so the patch’s fate hinges on whether Junio’s comment sparks further discussion. For now, the fix looks set to merge, but the thread leaves open the possibility of a larger conversation about the value of these CI checks.
