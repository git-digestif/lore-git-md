This thread is about a small but practical fix to GitLab CI jobs that check for whitespace and coding style issues. Patrick Steinhardt submitted a patch to force full clones in these jobs, preventing failures in merge requests with more than 50 commits. The fix itself is uncontroversial and will likely be merged soon.

The more interesting discussion is whether these jobs are worth keeping at all. Junio C Hamano questioned their value, suggesting they might encourage sloppy patches if they’re not strict enough. Patrick responded by acknowledging that he personally ignores both checks: the style job isn’t authoritative since Git doesn’t fully follow clang-format, and the whitespace job has been unreliable. While he sees potential in the style check if the project ever adopts clang-format, he didn’t push back strongly on Junio’s skepticism. For now, the jobs will stay, but their long-term usefulness is in doubt. No other contributors have weighed in, so the patch will probably proceed without further debate.
