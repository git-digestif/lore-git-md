This is a follow-up in the `git repack --drop-filtered --dry-run` bugfix thread. The patch (v2) is already cooking in `next` with a `Reviewed-by` and is on track to graduate to `master`. This email is a direct reply to Patrick Steinhardt’s observation about redundant `dry_run` checks after the v2 fix.

Siddharth Shrimali acknowledges Patrick’s point: the early `return` added in v2 makes two later `dry_run` checks redundant. One is the `(!dry_run)` guard in the call to `repack_promisor_objects()`, and the other is the `if (!dry_run) delete_redundant = 1` assignment. Neither check can ever be reached when `dry_run` is true, so they serve no purpose. Siddharth plans to remove both in a v3 patch.

The redundancy is purely a code hygiene issue; it does not affect the correctness of the current fix. The v3 patch will be a minimal cleanup that can be applied independently of the current topic’s merge status. No new behavior or test coverage is introduced.
