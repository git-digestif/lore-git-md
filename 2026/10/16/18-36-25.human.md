This is a surface-level review comment on the proposed `--historical-branch` output format in Harald Nordgren’s reflog usability series. D. Ben Knoble points out that the current design (placing the branch name in parentheses immediately after the commit hash) disrupts the fixed-width alignment of the `HEAD@{n}` column, making it harder to visually scan the output. He suggests three alternative formats that preserve column alignment: placing the branch name at the end of the line, immediately after the `HEAD@{n}` column, or after the operation prefix but before the message. Each alternative has trade-offs—end-of-line placement makes the branch name less prominent, while mid-line placement risks misalignment with long branch names.

The comment is narrowly focused on presentation rather than functionality, and Knoble frames it as a suggestion rather than a critique, acknowledging the inherent tension between readability and alignment. This feedback is unlikely to block the series but could influence the final output format if other reviewers share the concern. The thread’s broader discussion about scope and architectural fit remains unresolved, but this particular point is purely about user experience and could be addressed with a simple tweak.
