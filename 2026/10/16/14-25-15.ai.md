This is a surface-level review of the bugfix patch for the sideband protocol's control-character configuration parser.

Tuomas Ahola points out a minor style nit in the patch: the use of the `!!` operator to convert a character to a boolean (0 or 1) for pointer arithmetic. The Git `CodingGuidelines` discourage such "clever tricks" unless there is a compelling reason. In this case, the `!!` is used to skip past a comma after an unrecognized keyword, but the reviewer suggests it could be written more clearly.

The underlying patch itself remains uncontroversial and well-motivated. It fixes an infinite loop in the `sideband.allowControlCharacters` parser that breaks basic Git operations (`fetch`, `push`, `clone`, `ls-remote`) for users with malformed configurations. The fix advances the parser past unrecognized keywords after warning about them, and the new test verifies this behavior. No substantive concerns about the logic or scope of the change have been raised—only the readability of a single line of code. The author may address this with a follow-up iteration or the maintainer may adjust it during integration.
