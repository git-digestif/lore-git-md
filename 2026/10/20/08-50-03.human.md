This is a **follow-up discussion** in Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch series introducing the `extensions.objectStorage` mechanism for pluggable object database backends. The email is a reply to Karthik Nayak’s substantive review of the second patch, which implements the baseline `files` backend and its configuration plumbing.

Patrick responds to three of Karthik’s points. On the question of whether the `files` backend should normalize relative paths to absolute paths (mirroring the ref storage extension’s behavior), Patrick acknowledges the inconsistency but defers the change, citing backward compatibility with existing user-facing paths and test breakage. This leaves the door open for future cleanup but keeps the current implementation pragmatic. On the `gettext.h` include, Patrick confirms its necessity for translatable strings in `odb_source_new()` and aligns with Junio’s preference for explicit includes, even if the header is implicitly available. Finally, regarding the `DEFAULT_REPO_FORMAT` prerequisite in tests, Patrick justifies it as future-proofing: once additional backends are introduced, the prerequisite will prevent subtle bugs when switching between backends. He also clarifies that the current patch’s tests focus on validating the extension’s activation, while payload handling (including absolute/relative paths) is tested in a later patch.

The exchange is collaborative and clarifies design trade-offs without escalating into controversy. Karthik’s review remains the most substantive feedback on the series so far, and Patrick’s responses address the technical concerns while leaving room for future refinement. The discussion does not alter the series’ core direction but underscores areas where the implementation might evolve in subsequent iterations. Readers tracking the object storage extension effort should note that the `files` backend’s path handling is intentionally conservative for now, and the test coverage for payload semantics will expand in later patches.
