This is the second patch in Phillip Wood’s seven-part refactoring series (`pw/stash-optimize-check_changes-calls/v3`) that targets redundant change-detection logic in `git stash`. The patch removes a long-standing duplicate check for unstaged or uncommitted changes in `git stash create`, which has been present since ef0f0b4509 (2019). The earlier commit claimed the duplicate check was an optimization, but Phillip’s analysis shows it was neither necessary nor reliable—`check_changes_tracked_files()` was called before refreshing the index, making its results potentially inaccurate.

The change is straightforward: the call to `check_changes_tracked_files()` is removed from `create_stash()`, leaving the single, reliable check in `do_create_stash()` (which runs after the index is refreshed). The patch also cleans up `check_changes_tracked_files()` by removing its redundant `repo_read_index()` call, since all remaining callers now ensure the index is read and refreshed beforehand. The diff is minimal—just five lines removed from `builtin/stash.c`—and the commit message clearly explains the rationale and history.
