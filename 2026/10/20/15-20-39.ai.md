This is the fourth patch in Phillip Wood’s seven-patch refactoring series (`pw/stash-optimize-check_changes-calls/v3`) that eliminates redundant index operations in `git stash push` and `git stash create`. The patch removes duplicate calls to `preload_index()` by shifting responsibility for reading the index to the callers of `do_create_stash()` and replacing `repo_read_index_preload()` with `repo_read_index()`. This change cuts two unnecessary `lstat()` calls per changed path, reducing the total number of `lstat()` calls from three to one during a typical `git stash push`.

The patch is a straightforward mechanical optimization confined to `builtin/stash.c`. It modifies `do_create_stash()`, `create_stash()`, and `do_push_stash()` to avoid redundant index preloading, with no intended behavior changes.
