This is the second patch in a two-part documentation update to `MyFirstContribution.adoc`, focusing on wording improvements to clarify the review process for new contributors. The patch strengthens the guidance around when to send a v2 patch, replacing the mild "it is good manners to do so" with a firmer "Do not send v2 before you are done discussing the current iteration with reviewers." It also reorganizes the "Responding to Reviews" section to emphasize that reviewing is a discussion, not just a checklist of fixes.

The changes are editorial and uncontroversial in intent, though Patrick Steinhardt mildly objected to the blanket advice "do not start coding" in the v1 review, arguing that some exploratory coding during discussion can be productive. Junio retained the stricter phrasing, noting it reflects observed contributor behavior. The patch also includes minor copyediting fixes (typos, subject-verb agreement) from the v1 feedback.

This patch complements the structural reordering in patch 1/2 (already summarized), which moved the "Sending v2" instructions after the "Responding to Reviews" section. Together, the series aims to reduce premature v2 submissions by setting clearer expectations in the tutorial. The changes are purely to `Documentation/MyFirstContribution.adoc` and introduce no new code or tests. The series is under review, with no merge to `master` or integration branches reported yet.
