This thread is about a small but urgent bugfix to restore the pre-2.56 behavior of `git init` printing the absolute path (e.g., `/home/user/repo/.git`) instead of the relative `.git/` path in its output message. The regression was introduced in the current development cycle, making the fix time-sensitive.

The patch, now at v2, is complete and uncontroversial. The author, Matt Hunter, has incorporated all feedback—including a regression test—and the fix has drawn favorable reviews from Patrick Steinhardt and Junio Hamano, who both agree the change is necessary. Patrick’s latest review suggests only minor cosmetic tweaks to the test, which the author can address without further discussion. With no open questions or objections, the patch is ready for Junio to merge.
