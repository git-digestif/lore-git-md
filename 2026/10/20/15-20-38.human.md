This specific patch eliminates a duplicate call to `check_changes()` in `do_push_stash()`, reducing the number of untracked-file walks from two to one and cutting two `lstat()` calls per changed path. It also removes an unnecessary index refresh (`repo_refresh_and_write_index()`) that was performed before the duplicate change check.

The patch leverages the `enum create_result` introduced in the first patch to distinguish between "no changes to stash" (`CREATE_NO_CHANGES`) and "nothing stashed" (`CREATE_EMPTY`), ensuring consistent exit statuses: `git stash push --patch` (no hunks selected) and `git stash push --staged` (index matches `HEAD`) still exit with status 1, while a no-op `git stash push` now correctly exits 0. The change is narrowly scoped to `builtin/stash.c` and introduces no new edge cases or user-visible behavior changes beyond the corrected exit status.
