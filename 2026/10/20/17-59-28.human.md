This is a **documentation** patch in the ongoing effort to replace Git’s outdated `gittutorial` with a beginner-focused guide. This email is Julia Evans’ reply to Junio C Hamano’s **surface-level review** of the second patch in her two-part series (v2 2/2). The discussion remains focused on minor usability and formatting tweaks, with no engagement yet on the larger open questions about the tutorial’s design.

Julia responds to three of Junio’s nitpicks. On **SSH key generation**, she clarifies that `ed25519` has been the default in OpenSSH since 2023 and argues for sticking with the default rather than hardcoding a key type, noting that future post-quantum cryptography changes could make `ed25519` obsolete. This is a pragmatic stance, though it leaves the tutorial’s instructions slightly more ambiguous for users on older systems. For **key copying**, she acknowledges Junio’s suggestion to replace `ls ~/.ssh/*.pub` with `cat ~/.ssh/id_ed25519.pub` to reduce the risk of users pasting filenames instead of key contents, adding it to her list of changes to consider. She also accepts the **capitalization fix** for the "push" section header.

The unresolved open questions from the cover letter -- whether to start with `git init` or `git clone`, how to handle authentication generically, and whether to adopt newer `git config set`/`get` syntax -- remain untouched in this exchange. Julia’s responses suggest she is treating these as optional refinements rather than blockers, consistent with her earlier framing of the tutorial as "good enough" for merging. Junio’s feedback, while detailed, is explicitly framed as "leftover nitpicks," implying the core content is uncontroversial. The next step is likely a v3 incorporating these minor tweaks, after which the series may be ready for merging.
