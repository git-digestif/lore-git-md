This is the sixth patch in Phillip Wood’s seven-patch refactoring series `pw/stash-optimize-check_changes-calls/v3`, which targets redundant index loads and refreshes in `git stash push` and `git stash create`. The series is a well-motivated performance optimization, eliminating unnecessary `lstat()` calls and index operations without altering user-visible behavior.

This patch removes redundant index operations in `create_index_from_tree()`. The function is always called from `do_create_stash()`, which already loads and refreshes the index, so the additional `repo_read_index_preload()` and `refresh_index()` calls here are unnecessary. The change saves three `lstat()` calls per changed path, a small but measurable improvement. Junio C Hamano’s review of this patch in v2 was unreservedly positive, calling the redundancy "doubly bad" and "embarrassingly stupid," with no technical objections raised.

The patch is narrowly scoped, touching only three lines in `builtin/stash.c`, and introduces no new edge cases or behavioral changes. It is a straightforward cleanup that aligns with the series’ goal of consolidating index operations. The broader series has already addressed Junio’s feedback from earlier iterations, and this patch is uncontroversial. The refactoring is likely to proceed smoothly to `next`.
