This is a **tested review follow-up in the reftable backend bugfix thread**, adding new technical observations about Patrick Steinhardt’s proposed fix for the buffer overflow issue in `reftable_alloc_size()`.

Aniket Gupta reports testing Patrick’s suggested change (changing `reftable_alloc_size()` parameters to `uintmax_t`) on a 32-bit build. The fix works for `REFTABLE_ALLOC_ARRAY`, but `REFTABLE_CALLOC_ARRAY` bypasses the helper and calls `reftable_calloc()` directly with a `size_t`, which could still truncate 64-bit values. While no current callers pass values large enough to trigger this, Aniket suggests noting the limitation in the commit message. He also points out that the existing test passes due to a redundant bounds check (`count > in.len`), which may be removed in a revised patch. To maintain coverage, he proposes adding a direct test for `reftable_alloc_size()` to verify overflow detection on 64-bit systems, where truncation does not occur.

The review is substantive and tested, identifying a subtle edge case in the proposed fix and suggesting improvements to test coverage. This feedback refines the technical approach and may influence the final implementation. Key details for future discussion include whether the `REFTABLE_CALLOC_ARRAY` truncation risk warrants a separate fix, how to adjust the test suite, and whether the commit message should document the limitation.
