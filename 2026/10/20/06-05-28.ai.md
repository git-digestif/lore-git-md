This is a brief follow-up in the `git stash` shell completion series, specifically addressing the fifth patch (removal of `git stash save` completion). Patrick Steinhardt responds to D. Ben Knoble’s acknowledgment of a potential usability concern: whether option autocompletion should still work when a user manually types `git stash save --<TAB>`.

Patrick clarifies that he is open to either outcome—disabling option completion entirely or preserving it—but emphasizes that if completion is removed, the role of `GIT_COMPLETION_SHOW_ALL` (which would restore it) should be explicitly documented. The tone is pragmatic and leaves the final decision to the author after testing. This exchange resolves the last open design question for the patch, pending Ben Knoble’s verification of the current behavior. No new objections or alternatives are introduced.
