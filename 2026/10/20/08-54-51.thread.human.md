This thread is a focused bugfix series from Patrick Steinhardt to restore dynamic reloading of alternate object databases in Git’s files-based backend, a feature accidentally broken during the ODB abstraction work in March 2026. The regression, though rare, has been observed in production where the alternates file is modified while Git is running.

The series consists of four patches: a preparatory refactoring, the core fix, and two performance optimizations that bring lookup times for missing objects nearly back to pre-regression levels (197ms vs. the original 180ms). Junio reviewed the final patch and raised a minor correctness concern about when the `stat_validity` tracker is updated; Patrick has now addressed this by adjusting the control flow to update the tracker only after a successful read. The change is small and doesn’t affect the series’ overall design or performance. With this resolved, the series appears ready for integration, pending a v2 or follow-up patch incorporating the fix. No other objections or open questions remain.
