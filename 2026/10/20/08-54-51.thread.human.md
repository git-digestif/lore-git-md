This thread is a four-patch bugfix series from Patrick Steinhardt that restores dynamic reloading of alternate object databases in Git’s files-based ODB backend. The regression was introduced in March 2026 during the ODB abstraction effort and has since been observed in production systems where the alternates file may be modified while Git is running.

The series is now complete. The first two patches refactored internal function names and restored the core functionality, while the third and fourth patches addressed a performance regression introduced by the fix. The final patch uses `struct stat_validity` to avoid unnecessary re-reads of the alternates file, bringing lookup times for 20,000 missing objects back to 197ms—nearly matching the pre-regression baseline of 180ms. The series is based on current `master` and Patrick’s earlier ODB cleanup branch, with no prior versions submitted.

All patches are narrowly scoped, well-explained, and include concrete performance data and test coverage. The regression, fix, and optimizations are thoroughly documented in the commit messages and cover letter. No open questions or objections have been raised so far, and the series appears uncontroversial. The next step is likely review from other ODB subsystem contributors, with an eye toward eventual integration into Junio’s `next` branch.
