This thread is about a bugfix series from Patrick Steinhardt that restores dynamic reloading of alternate object databases in Git’s files-based ODB backend—a regression introduced during the ODB abstraction effort in March 2026. The series was initially uncontroversial, with Junio Hamano raising only a minor correctness concern on the final patch, which Patrick addressed. However, Christian Couder’s recent review has surfaced a significant behavioral regression in `git receive-pack` caused by the interaction between Patrick’s fix and an earlier change to object quarantine handling.

The core issue is that when a temporary primary ODB (like the quarantine directory in `receive-pack`) is in use, Patrick’s fix reloads alternates from that directory—which has none—rather than from the repository’s real alternates. This breaks object lookups for objects that have moved to an alternate since the quarantine was created, a problem that didn’t exist before the ODB abstraction regression. Christian proposes a design change to always reload the repository’s real alternates, which would restore the pre-regression behavior for `receive-pack` and avoid additional I/O costs thanks to the `stat_validity` tracking added in Patrick’s fourth patch.

The series is now at a crossroads: while the original fix was narrowly scoped and performance-optimized, Christian’s feedback reveals a correctness gap that could affect production workflows like `git push`. Patrick will likely need to revise the series to address this, potentially requiring a v2. The thread’s key participants—Patrick and Christian—are both deeply familiar with the ODB subsystem, so the discussion is likely to remain focused on technical trade-offs rather than design disputes. For now, the series remains under review, with no merge in sight until the quarantine interaction is resolved.
