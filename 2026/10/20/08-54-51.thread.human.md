This thread is a bugfix series from Patrick Steinhardt that restores dynamic reloading of alternate object databases in Git’s files-based ODB backend. The regression, introduced in March 2026 during the ODB abstraction effort, was rare but observed in production systems where the alternates file might be modified while Git is running. The series consists of four patches: a preparatory refactoring, the core fix, and two performance optimizations that nearly restore the pre-regression lookup speed (from 309ms back to 197ms, close to the original 180ms).

Junio has reviewed the series and raised a minor but substantive edge-case concern about the fourth patch. He points out that the `stat_validity` tracker is updated even if reading or parsing the alternates file fails, which could mask transient errors. His feedback is narrowly scoped to a single function and does not challenge the overall design or motivation. The series remains uncontroversial and production-ready; this is a small polish item that Patrick can address in a follow-up or a v2 if he agrees. No other open questions have been raised, and the series is otherwise well-motivated, thoroughly tested, and performance-optimized.
