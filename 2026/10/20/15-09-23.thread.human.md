This thread is about a small but long-overdue documentation fix for the `git-rebase` man page. Omri Sarig noticed that the manual still referenced `--preserve-merges` (removed in 2021) and incorrectly listed `--allow-empty-message` as incompatible with other options, while omitting the actual incompatibility between `--onto` and `--keep-base`. After three iterations and feedback from Phillip Wood and Junio Hamano, the patch is now queued for the `maint` branch (Git 2.56.0). Junio made minor editorial tweaks to the commit message, and the final version sticks to the existing AsciiDoc style for consistency with the older `maint` branch. There are no open questions or objections, and the patch is ready to merge.
