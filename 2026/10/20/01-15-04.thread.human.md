This thread is about a bugfix patch series (v2) from tsukasa-ino that addresses two usability issues in `git switch` and related commands. The first patch fixes a misleading hint message when tracking configuration fails, showing short branch names (e.g., `origin/feat`) instead of full ref names (e.g., `origin/refs/heads/feat`).
