This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about the inconsistent state left by `git switch` and `git checkout` when tracking configuration fails. 井上司 (Tsukasa Inoue) clarifies the control flow that D. Ben Knoble questioned, confirming that the worktree and index are updated *before* the branch ref is created and HEAD is moved. The update happens in `merge_working_tree()`, which is called on every branch switch, not just during merges. The "merge" here refers to the 2-way merge in `unpack_trees()`, not the `-m` flag, which only adds an autostash step.

井上司 also confirms that the `-m` (merge) case suffers from the same issue: if tracking setup fails, the worktree is left in a conflicted state, HEAD remains on the old branch, and changes stay in the stash. Attempting to retry the command in this state either complains that the branch already exists or that the index needs resolution, leaving the repository stuck. This reinforces the earlier observation that the current control flow is fragile and that a fix would need to address not just `setup_tracking()` but the broader sequence of worktree, index, ref, and HEAD updates. No new patch or design proposal is introduced here—this is purely a technical clarification to inform the ongoing discussion.
