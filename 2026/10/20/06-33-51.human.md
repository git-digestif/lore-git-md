This is a follow-up in the ongoing `git switch`/`git checkout` tracking-configuration-failure discussion. The author, 井上司 (Tsukasa Inoue), acknowledges Junio C Hamano’s preference for atomic all-or-none behavior but argues that implementing full atomicity would require a large, invasive change. Instead, he proposes a smaller, incremental step: modifying `setup_tracking()` in `branch.c` to return an error rather than calling `exit()`, allowing `git switch` and `git checkout` to behave like other commands (`git push -u`, `git clone`, `git fetch`) that continue despite tracking-configuration failures. He suggests deferring full atomicity to a future effort.

The email does not include a patch, but it outlines a clear technical direction: refactor `setup_tracking()` to propagate errors rather than aborting, and leave the worktree and HEAD updates intact when tracking setup fails. This approach avoids the complexity of rolling back worktree and ref updates but still improves consistency across Git commands. The proposal is pragmatic and well-motivated, though it leaves the original atomicity goal unaddressed for now. Future discussion is likely to focus on whether this incremental step is sufficient or if a more comprehensive fix is needed.
