This is a design discussion reply in the ongoing `git switch`/`git checkout` tracking-configuration-failure thread.

Junio responds to the RFC from 井上司 (Tsukasa Inoue) with a clear preference: the command should behave atomically, either completing the entire switch (including tracking setup) or failing cleanly without touching the worktree or index. He considers this "all-or-none" approach more desirable than the current halfway state or a partial fix that completes the switch but skips tracking. The reply does not address backward compatibility concerns or implementation specifics, but it firmly settles the high-level design question raised in the original email. Future discussion in this thread is likely to focus on how to achieve atomicity rather than whether to pursue it.
