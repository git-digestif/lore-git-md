This is the final review from Patrick Steinhardt on the v3 series fixing a race condition in Git’s ODB transaction machinery. The series, authored by Justin Tobler, moves `.keep` lockfile creation to the transaction commit phase to prevent concurrent `git-receive-pack` processes from deleting each other’s lockfiles.

Patrick acknowledges the two minor stylistic tweaks Justin made in v3—explicitly assigning the error return value in `write_pack_lockfile()` and removing an extraneous newline—but notes that the error-handling change doesn’t fully match his suggestion. He doesn’t insist on a reroll, however, and explicitly considers the series ready for integration. All substantive feedback from earlier rounds has been addressed, and Patrick’s earlier approval stands.

The only unresolved concern—the assumption that leading directories for lockfiles exist during quarantine—was flagged in prior reviews but not revisited here. Patrick’s tone suggests this is no longer a blocker. The series is technically complete, well-tested, and part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort. Expect it to land in `next` shortly.
