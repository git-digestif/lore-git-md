This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing series introducing Git’s object storage extension (`extensions.objectStorage`). The email is a short design discussion between Karthik Nayak and Patrick Steinhardt about two naming and encapsulation issues raised in Karthik’s review of patch 4/7.

The first topic is the subtle naming distinction between `object.format` (hash algorithm) and `objects.storageFormat` (storage backend) in `git repo`’s output. Both agree the difference is too slight and likely to confuse users, but Karthik suggests leaving any renaming of the config options out of this series. Patrick had earlier floated the idea of renaming `object.format` to `objects.format` for consistency but deferred it due to scope. The discussion confirms the current naming is a pragmatic compromise rather than a deliberate choice, and the issue remains open but non-blocking.

The second topic is the hard-coded default (`files`) exposed in inspection tools. Karthik had questioned why the `object_storage_format` field in `struct repository` might be unset, expressing discomfort with exposing the default outside the repository and ODB subsystems. Patrick had already agreed this is awkward and committed to exploring a fix—likely ensuring the field is always initialized. Karthik’s reply does not revisit this point, so the plan stands.

No new technical details or objections emerge; the exchange is purely about design trade-offs. The tone is collaborative, and the feedback is actionable but not urgent. The series remains uncontroversial in its core goal, and this patch is unlikely to block progress. Future iterations may tweak the naming and initialization logic, but the inspection tools’ functionality is not in question.
