This is the fourth patch in Phillip Wood’s seven-part refactoring series (`pw/stash-optimize-check_changes-calls/v3`) targeting redundant index operations in `git stash push` and `git stash create`. The patch eliminates duplicate calls to `preload_index()` by shifting responsibility for reading the index to the callers of `do_create_stash()` and replacing `repo_read_index_preload()` with `repo_read_index()`. This change reduces the number of `lstat()` calls per changed path by two, cutting unnecessary filesystem overhead.

The patch is narrowly scoped to `builtin/stash.c` and modifies only three lines. It removes the `repo_read_index_preload()` call from `do_create_stash()` and adds `repo_read_index()` calls in its two callers, `create_stash()` and `do_push_stash()`. The change is mechanical and well-motivated: `do_create_stash()` previously loaded and preloaded the index, only for its callers to repeat the same work. By making callers responsible for reading the index, the patch ensures the index is loaded exactly once, avoiding redundant `lstat()` operations.

This is a clean performance optimization with no intended behavior changes. The patch builds on earlier work in the series (notably the introduction of `enum create_result` and the removal of redundant `check_changes()` calls) and aligns with the series’ goal of consolidating index operations.
