This is a surface-level review of the third iteration of a translation (l10n) patch. The patch itself is uncontroversial and ready for inclusion—it replaces a hardcoded singular/plural error message in the ZIP archive backend with Git’s `Q_()` macro to support proper pluralization in languages beyond English. Prior feedback from Phillip Wood and Junio C Hamano has already been incorporated, including clarifications in the commit message about the i18n rationale and the unreachability of the English singular case in this context.

In this reply, Phillip Wood points out a minor formatting issue: the indentation in the patch is inconsistent, mixing spaces and tabs, and the alignment of the `Q_()` macro arguments is off. He also notes that the lines exceed the recommended 80-character limit. These are purely mechanical fixes—no changes to the logic or behavior are needed. The patch remains well-motivated and technically sound, addressing a real gap in Git’s internationalization support for the ZIP backend. Once the whitespace and line-length issues are corrected, it should be ready to merge.
