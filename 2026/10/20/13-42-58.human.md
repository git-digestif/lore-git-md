This is the final patch in Harald Nordgren’s v10 series introducing a "matching-inspired" fetch mode for shallow repositories. The patch makes `--limited-fetch` the default when adding a remote in a shallow repository, ensuring that `git fetch` does not negotiate history for every branch the remote has.

The change is small and surgical: it modifies `builtin/remote.c` to check `is_repository_shallow(the_repository)` when the `limited_fetch` flag is unset (`-1`), defaulting to `--limited-fetch` unless explicitly disabled. The documentation in `git-remote.adoc` is updated to explain the new default behavior, and four new tests in `t/t5505-remote.sh` verify that the shallow-repository default works as intended, does not interfere with explicit `-t` or `--no-limited-fetch` usage, and preserves the traditional wildcard refspec in full repositories.

This patch is uncontroversial and ready for merging. It reuses existing machinery (the `limited_fetch` flag and `is_repository_shallow()` helper) and introduces no new technical debt. The backward compatibility story is clean: full repositories continue using wildcard refspecs by default, and the new behavior is opt-in for shallow repositories. The series as a whole is now technically complete and awaiting final maintainer feedback before graduating to `next`.
