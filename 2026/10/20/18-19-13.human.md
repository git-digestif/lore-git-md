The patch ensures that the boolean config keys `hook.<name>.enabled` and `hook.<name>.parallel` accept empty values (treated as `true`), aligning their behavior with Git's standard boolean config handling. The motivation is clear and uncontroversial: consistency with Git's established config conventions. The implementation is straightforward and well-tested, covering both the command-line (`git -c hook.lint.parallel`) and config-file (`[hook "lint"] parallel`) syntaxes. This patch complements the first patch in the series, which fixed `hook.<event>.enabled` to correctly disable hooks in the hooks directory.
