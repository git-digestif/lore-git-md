Junio sends the fourth iteration of a routine maintenance update to GitHub Actions workflows, splitting macOS CI jobs between explicit runner versions `macos-15` and `macos-26`. The motivation is clear: GitHub is deprecating `macos-14`, and the project needs to test across two macOS releases for backward compatibility (`macos-15`) and forward coverage (`macos-26`). The final design assigns `osx-clang` and `osx-meson` to `macos-15`, and `osx-reftable` and `osx-gcc` to `macos-26`. This split was settled in the thread after Patrick Steinhardt argued for explicit versions over floating tags to match GitLab CI’s constraints.

The patch itself is minimal: two lines changed in `.github/workflows/main.yml`. No new configuration keys, CLI options, or on-disk formats are introduced, and the change is purely infrastructural. Because the patch is untested (Junio does not use macOS), there is a small risk that the new macOS versions could expose latent platform-specific issues, but the risk is low given the mechanical nature of the update.
