This thread is a small but important step in Git’s ongoing effort to remove global state, specifically migrating the `pack.packsizelimit` configuration value into the repository-scoped `repo_config_values` struct. The work is being done by Joy Gundu, an Outreachy contributor, as part of a two-patch series (v2). The first patch is a preparatory cleanup that replaces manual zero-initialization of the `repo_config_values` struct with a single `memset()` call, reducing boilerplate and making future additions to the struct less error-prone. The second patch, which does the core refactoring, has already received technical approval from Patrick Steinhardt, who called it "reads well" with no further feedback.

The series is uncontroversial and aligns with Patrick’s broader work on reducing global state. All substantive feedback from the v1 review has been addressed, including Patrick’s suggestion to split the `memset` cleanup into its own patch. The only remaining work is cosmetic: Joy will update the commit message and remove a redundant comment in the preparatory patch, as requested by Patrick. Once these minor nits are addressed, the series is ready for integration. Kristoffer Haugsbakk also reviewed the v1 series, praising the commit message’s clarity but raising no technical concerns. The patches touch `builtin/pack-objects.c`, `environment.c`, and `environment.h`, with no changes to on-disk format or behavior, and Joy has confirmed the changes pass relevant tests.
