This thread is about a small but meaningful refactoring to reduce global state in Git. Joy Gundu, an Outreachy contributor, sent a two-patch series (v2) that moves the `pack.packsizelimit` configuration value from a standalone global variable into the repository-scoped `repo_config_values` struct. The first patch is a preparatory cleanup that uses `memset()` to standardize initialization of the struct, while the second patch does the core refactoring.

The series has received positive feedback from both Kristoffer Haugsbakk and Patrick Steinhardt. Kristoffer noted the clarity of the commit message, while Patrick confirmed the change aligns with his own work and suggested the `memset` cleanup, which Joy incorporated. The patches are uncontroversial, pass relevant tests (`t1300-config.sh` and `t5300-pack-object.sh`), and introduce no behavioral changes. The only minor detail is the use of `the_repository` in one spot, but that’s consistent with the current codebase and will be addressed by the broader `the_repository` removal effort.

This is a clean, incremental step in the ongoing effort to make Git’s internals more modular. The patches are ready for integration, and Joy has been responsive to feedback throughout the process.
