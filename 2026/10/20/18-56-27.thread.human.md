This thread is about a two-patch refactoring series by Joy Gundu (an Outreachy contributor) that moves the `pack.packsizelimit` configuration value into Git’s repository-scoped `repo_config_values` struct, reducing global state. The v2 series now includes a preparatory patch that standardizes initialization of the struct using `memset()`, addressing feedback from Patrick Steinhardt.

The series is uncontroversial and aligns with Patrick’s ongoing work to eliminate global state. The first patch in v2—a mechanical cleanup—replaces 12 explicit field assignments in `repo_config_values_init()` with a single `memset()` call, making future additions to the struct less error-prone. The second patch (not yet detailed in the latest email) will handle the core refactoring of `pack.packsizelimit`. Both Kristoffer Haugsbakk and Patrick have reviewed earlier versions, with Patrick proposing the `memset()` improvement, which Joy adopted. No technical objections or edge cases have been raised, and Joy reports successful test runs. The series is under review and ready for integration.
