This thread is about Joy Gundu's (an Outreachy contributor) v3 patch series that refactors the `pack.packsizelimit` configuration value to reduce global state by moving it into Git's repository-scoped `repo_config_values` struct. The series consists of two patches: a preparatory cleanup using `memset()` to standardize initialization, and the core refactoring itself.

The series had been uncontroversial and technically ready for integration, with all prior feedback addressed—including Patrick Steinhardt's explicit approval of the second patch in v2. However, Tanga Joris's recent review identifies a critical procedural blocker: Junio's recently queued `jc/freeze-repo-config-values` patch (now in `seen`) prohibits adding new members to `struct repo_config_values` until it can operate on repositories beyond `the_repository`. Since Joy's patch adds `pack_size_limit` to that struct, it directly conflicts with Junio's freeze, making the core refactoring unmergeable in its current form. Tanga suggests splitting the series, allowing the preparatory `memset` cleanup to proceed independently while the second patch is reworked to comply with the freeze. The review also notes minor issues (a double blank line and an outdated cover letter) but confirms the patch builds and passes key tests (`t1300`, `t7700`, `t5300`).

The thread is now at a crossroads: the technical direction remains sound and aligned with Patrick Steinhardt's ongoing global-state-removal efforts, but the timing of Junio's freeze requires a tactical adjustment. The preparatory work could still land, but the core refactoring will need to wait or be redesigned to avoid the freeze. No objections to the underlying goal have been raised, and the feedback remains constructive.
