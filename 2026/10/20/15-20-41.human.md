This is the sixth patch in Phillip Wood’s seven-part refactoring series (`pw/stash-optimize-check_changes-calls/v3`) that eliminates redundant index loads and refreshes in `git stash push` and `git stash create`. This specific patch removes an unnecessary `repo_read_index_preload()` and `refresh_index()` call in `create_index_from_tree()`, since the index is already loaded and refreshed by its caller, `do_create_stash()`. The change cuts three redundant `lstat()` calls per changed path, a small but measurable performance improvement.

The patch is a straightforward cleanup with no behavior changes. It follows Junio C Hamano’s enthusiastic review of the same change in the previous iteration, where he called the redundancy “doubly bad” and “embarrassingly stupid.” The fix is narrowly scoped to `builtin/stash.c` and aligns with the series’ broader goal of consolidating duplicate index operations. No edge cases or objections have been raised, and the patch is uncontroversial. This is a well-motivated, low-risk refactoring that fits cleanly into the larger effort.
