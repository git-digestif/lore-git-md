This thread is a two-patch bugfix series from Thomas Bachem that aims to fix inconsistencies in Git’s hook configuration system. The patches address two issues: ensuring `hook.<event>.enabled` disables hooks in the hooks directory (not just config-defined hooks), and making boolean config values like `hook.<event>.enabled` and `hook.<event>.parallel` accept empty values as `true`, aligning with Git’s standard boolean config behavior.

The series was posted on July 10, 2025, and is currently under review. Junio C Hamano has raised a potential design disagreement, suggesting that the current behavior—where `hook.<event>.enabled=false` only disables config-defined hooks—might have been intentional rather than a bug. He implies the documentation may have been written too narrowly, reflecting the feature’s original scope. While Junio hasn’t raised technical objections, his response signals that the change could be more controversial than the author assumed.
