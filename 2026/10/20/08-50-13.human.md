This is a **design discussion reply** in Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch series introducing the `extensions.objectStorage` mechanism for pluggable object database backends. The email responds to Karthik Nayak’s review of the fourth patch, which adds user-facing inspection tools (`` `git rev-parse --show-object-storage-format` `` and `` `git repo objects.storageFormat` ``) to query the configured backend.

Patrick acknowledges Karthik’s concern about the subtle naming distinction between `object.format` (hash algorithm) and `objects.storageFormat` (storage backend) in `` `git repo` ``’s output. He explains that the naming was even more confusing before a recent patch series renamed `references.format` to `references.storageFormat` to avoid a similar conflict. While he considered renaming `object.format` to `objects.format` for consistency, he ultimately decided against it to avoid further churn.

On the more significant architectural point — why the `object_storage_format` field in `struct repository` might be unset, forcing the inspection tools to hard-code the default (`files`) — Patrick agrees that exposing the default outside the repository and ODB subsystems is awkward. He commits to revisiting the design to encapsulate the default more cleanly, though he does not yet propose a specific solution.

The discussion remains **design-focused and non-blocking**. No functional bugs or test failures are at issue; the feedback targets long-term maintainability and user experience. The tone is collaborative, and Patrick’s willingness to address the concerns suggests the next iteration may include a follow-up patch to clean up the default-handling logic.
