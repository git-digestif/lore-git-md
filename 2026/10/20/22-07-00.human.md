This is the fifth patch in Phillip Wood’s seven-part refactoring series targeting redundant index operations in `git stash push` and `git stash create`. The patch replaces the use of `reset_tree()` with `create_index_from_tree()` in `stash_working_tree()`, eliminating reliance on the global `set_alternate_index_output()` mechanism and avoiding unintended side effects on the in-memory index. A new `reset` boolean parameter is added to `create_index_from_tree()` to control whether the index is reset or merged, preserving intent-to-add entries. The change also removes an unnecessary `repo_read_index_preload()` call, saving one `lstat()` per changed path.

Junio C Hamano’s review is brief but positive, calling this "the most interesting step in the series" and praising the elimination of in-core index flipping and its side effects. The patch is small (11 lines removed, 4 added) and confined to `builtin/stash.c`, with no user-visible behavior changes. It aligns the implementation with the existing pattern used for `git stash push -p` and simplifies reasoning about index state by avoiding global state manipulation.

This patch is part of a well-motivated series that has already incorporated all prior feedback.
