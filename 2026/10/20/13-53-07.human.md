This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing thread about converting Git’s `BreakingChanges` document into a proper manpage (`gitbreaking-changes(7)`). Kristoffer Haugsbakk replies to D. Ben Knoble’s expression of support, noting that the link-formatting discussion (URLs vs. message-IDs) was the most labor-intensive part of the series. He acknowledges that the core information is already accessible via the existing plain-text document, but argues that the extra step required to find it (using `git --html-path`) undermines discoverability. The tone is matter-of-fact: he is not pushing for further review, but simply clarifying the trade-off involved in the link-formatting decision. The series itself remains technically complete and ready for integration, with no new changes proposed or requested. The only remaining action is for the maintainer to resolve the minor merge conflicts noted earlier.
