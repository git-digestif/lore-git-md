This is a small but well-motivated i18n patch targeting the ZIP archive backend. The author has sent a v4 that addresses the last remaining mechanical issues: proper indentation (tabs instead of spaces), alignment of the `Q_()` macro arguments, and line length under 80 characters. No new strings are added to the translation catalog; the existing message is split into singular and plural variants.
