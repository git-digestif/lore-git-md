This is a documentation patch (part 1 of 2) updating `MyFirstContribution.adoc` to reorder the tutorial’s sections. The goal is to emphasize that new contributors should engage with review feedback before preparing a v2 patch, rather than treating v2 as a mechanical follow-up.

The change is purely structural: no text is altered, only the order of the sections. The diff is large (204 lines) but mechanical, using `--color-moved` to make the reordering easy to follow. This patch addresses the first half of the series’ goal—clarifying the intended workflow—while leaving wording improvements to patch 2/2.

The motivation is practical: senior community members report an uptick in new contributors sending v2 patches without first addressing review feedback, wasting time and reviewer effort. The reordering is uncontroversial; the only discussion so far has been about phrasing in the follow-up patch, not the structural change here. No code, tests, or new symbols are introduced, and the patch is under review with no merge to `master` or integration branches reported.
