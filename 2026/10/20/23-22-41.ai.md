This is the first patch in Junio C Hamano’s v2 documentation series updating `MyFirstContribution.adoc`. It performs a **pure structural reordering** of the tutorial: the "Sending v2" sections (for both `git send-email` and `b4` workflows) are moved from their current position—immediately after "Sending v1"—to a new location after the "Responding to Reviews" section. The patch contains **no text changes**; only the ordering of existing content is adjusted, making the diff easy to follow with `git diff --color-moved`.

The goal is to **emphasize review discussion as a prerequisite** to sending a v2 patch, rather than presenting v2 as a mechanical follow-up to v1. This change directly addresses an observed uptick in new contributors sending updated patches without first engaging with review feedback. The reordering is uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ stated purpose of clarifying the intended contribution workflow.

No new symbols, CLI options, or on-disk formats are introduced. The patch touches only `Documentation/MyFirstContribution.adoc` and is purely editorial. Future discussion is likely to focus on the phrasing improvements in patch 2/2, but this patch itself appears ready for integration. It is part of a two-patch series; the second patch strengthens the cautionary language around premature v2 submissions.
