This is the **final version (v7)** of a well-crafted bugfix series targeting the `--force-if-includes` safety mechanism in `git push`. The series is now **technically complete** and ready for merge, with all prior review feedback resolved. Tyler Cipriani signals this is the last iteration unless new issues emerge.

The core problem is that `--force-if-includes` currently checks the reflog of the *local* branch whose name matches the *remote* destination (e.g., `refs/heads/main` when pushing to `origin/main`), rather than the branch actually being pushed (e.g., `refs/heads/src` in `src:main`). This causes two critical issues: **data loss** (allowing destructive pushes when the wrong local branch has integrated the remote tip) and **false rejections** (blocking valid pushes when the correct local branch is up-to-date but the wrong one is stale). The series fixes this by consulting the reflog of the pushed ref (`ref->peer_ref->name`) instead.

Beyond the core fix, the series addresses two long-standing bugs:
1. **Unnecessary rejections of fast-forward pushes** (e.g., tags or detached HEADs) by deferring the reflog check until after fast-forward determination (patch 3/3).
2. **Poor UX for unverifiable refs** (e.g., detached HEADs, tags) by introducing a new `advice.pushRefUnverifiable` message that provides **actionable guidance** (`--force-with-lease=<ref>:<expect>` or `--no-force-if-includes`) instead of a misleading `git pull` suggestion (patch 2/3).

The implementation is thorough, touching all transport layers (`builtin/push.c`, `send-pack.c`, `transport-helper.c`, `transport.c`) and adding **108 lines of new test coverage** in `t/t5533-push-cas.sh` for edge cases like differently named branches, orphan branches, detached HEADs, tags, and deletions. The series is based on `maint` and resolves trivial textual conflicts with `a85a43c480` (advice alphabetical ordering) and `as/push-force-if-includes-no-reflog` (test additions).

The only behavioral change is that **force-pushes of non-branch refs (tags, detached HEADs) are now rejected by default**, with clear advice on how to override the check. This breaks some workflows that *appeared* to work before (e.g., `git push --force-with-lease --force-if-includes origin <tag>:hotfix`) but were actually unsafe. Fast-forward pushes of non-branch refs remain allowed, as they pose no risk.

The series has been reviewed by Junio C Hamano, D. Ben Knoble, and Patrick Steinhardt, with all concerns addressed in prior iterations. The v7 changes are minor (typo fixes, rebase on `maint`), and the author explicitly states this is the final version unless new issues arise. The design is uncontroversial, and the implementation looks solid. **This is a clear candidate for `next` and eventual merge to `master`.**
