This thread is about a two-patch series by Colin Hinton that defers validation of the `remote.<name>.followRemoteHEAD` configuration setting until it’s actually needed (during `git fetch`), eliminating spurious warnings in commands like `git push` or `git remote -v`. The series is now in its fourth iteration (v4), addressing all prior feedback—including Junio C Hamano’s refactoring suggestion to split the change into a helper function—and is technically ready for integration.

The only remaining blocker is the graduation of Harald Nordgren’s `hn/fetch-refmap` topic from `seen` to `next` (or `master`), as the two series have a mechanical conflict that v4 fully mitigates via the new `determine_follow_remote_head()` helper. Junio has confirmed the conflict is resolved, and all substantive review feedback has been addressed, including Matt Hunter’s structural question about helper placement. In the latest email, Hunter proposes an alternative implementation: folding the new logic into the existing `get_follow_remote_head()` function instead of introducing a standalone helper. His patch simplifies the call site in `do_fetch()` but keeps the core behavior identical. While Hunter’s approach is a valid design choice, Junio has already endorsed the standalone helper, and no further changes are expected unless the author prefers Hunter’s version. The series is otherwise settled, with no open technical questions or loose ends.
