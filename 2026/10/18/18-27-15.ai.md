The patch touches seven files: `builtin/branch.c`, `builtin/checkout.c`, `builtin/rebase.c`, `object-name.c`, `reflog.h`, `sequencer.c`, and `wt-status.c`. It introduces five new constants in `reflog.h` (`REFLOG_SWITCH_PREFIX`, `REFLOG_RENAME_PREFIX`, `REFLOG_STEP_START`, `REFLOG_STEP_FINISH`, `REFLOG_STEP_ABORT`) and updates all code that writes or parses reflog messages to use them. The changes are mechanical and localized, with no new logic or edge-case handling.

The commit message now clearly states the motivation upfront (addressing feedback from v1) and explains why the refactoring is necessary: the next patch will add a new consumer of these strings, and keeping them in sync manually would be error-prone.
