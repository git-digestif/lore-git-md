This is the **v7 final revision** of a **bugfix series** targeting the `--force-if-includes` safety mechanism in `git push`. The series is now **technically complete**, with all prior feedback resolved and only minor typographical fixes and rebase adjustments since v6. The author explicitly signals this is the **final version** unless new technical issues are raised.

The core problem remains: `--force-if-includes` incorrectly checks the reflog of the *local* branch whose name matches the *remote* destination (e.g., `refs/heads/main`), rather than the branch actually being pushed (e.g., `refs/heads/src` in `src:main`). This causes two critical issues: **data loss** (allowing pushes that clobber remote history) and **false rejections** (blocking valid pushes when the local branch is behind or missing). Additionally, the feature unnecessarily rejects **fast-forward pushes of non-branch refs** (tags, detached HEADs), breaking workflows and violating the feature’s documented intent.

The v7 series consists of three patches:
1. **Patch 1/3**: Fixes the core logic in `remote.c` to consult the reflog of the pushed ref (`ref->peer_ref->name`). Unconditionally resolves `peer_ref` with `RESOLVE_REF_READING`, rejecting non-branch pushes for force-pushes but allowing them for fast-forwards. Includes **81 lines of new test coverage** in `t/t5533-push-cas.sh` for edge cases like differently named branches, HEAD-based pushes, orphan branches, detached HEADs, and tags. Explicitly handles deletions (e.g., `git push origin :main`) by checking `is_null_oid(&remote->peer_ref->new_oid)` and allowing them to bypass reflog checks.
2. **Patch 2/3**: Introduces a new `ref->unverifiable` flag and **actionable advice** (`advice.pushRefUnverifiable`) for pushes lacking a reflog (e.g., detached HEAD, tags). Updates all transport layers to emit consistent advice, instructing users to use `--force-with-lease=<ref>:<expect>` or `--no-force-if-includes`. Adds `REF_STATUS_REJECT_UNVERIFIABLE` status and `REJECT_REF_UNVERIFIABLE` reject reason, plumbed through the entire stack. Tests verify the rejection message and advice for detached HEAD and tag pushes.
3. **Patch 3/3**: Exempts fast-forward pushes from `--force-if-includes` checks, resolving the existing bug where fast-forwards of tags or detached HEADs were rejected. Introduces `needs_force_reject_reason` (renamed from `deferred_reject_reason` in v5) in `set_ref_status_for_push` to defer the reflog check until after fast-forward determination. Adds **27 lines of test coverage** for fast-forward pushes of expired-reflog branches, tags, symbolic references, and initial pushes.
