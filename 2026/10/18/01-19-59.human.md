This is a procedural update from Junio on Igor Wiedler’s two-patch series fixing a performance regression in connectivity checks for deepening fetches. The second patch, which extends `odb_for_each_alternate_ref()` to ignore refs from shallow or grafted alternates, has a test that fails on Windows CI due to a path-handling issue. The test uses `$TRASH_DIRECTORY/shallow/.git/objects`, but Windows path normalization breaks the comparison.

Junio has ejected the series from the `seen` integration branch, pausing its progress until the test is fixed. No technical objections to the patch itself were raised—this is purely a portability fix. Jeff King (Peff) already identified the problem in an earlier review and suggested two straightforward solutions (replacing `$TRASH_DIRECTORY` with `$(pwd)` or rewriting the test to use environment variables and `--no-local`). The ball is now in Igor’s court to send a v3 with the corrected test.

The series remains well-motivated and addresses a real-world performance issue; the delay is mechanical, not conceptual. Once the test is fixed, the patches are likely to proceed quickly, as the core design has already been reviewed and endorsed by Junio and Peff.
