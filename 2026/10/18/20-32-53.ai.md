This is the second patch in the **v7 final revision** of Tyler Cipriani’s three-patch bugfix series for `--force-if-includes`. It introduces **actionable advice** for pushes that lack a reflog to consult (e.g., detached HEAD, tags) and ensures consistent rejection messages across all transport layers.

The patch adds a new `ref->unverifiable` flag to distinguish between two distinct failure modes: (1) the remote ref was updated since checkout (`unreachable`), and (2) the push is of a non-branch ref with no reflog to check (`unverifiable`). It then plumbs this distinction through the entire transport stack (`builtin/push.c`, `send-pack.c`, `transport-helper.c`, `transport.c`), introducing a new `REF_STATUS_REJECT_UNVERIFIABLE` status and `REJECT_REF_UNVERIFIABLE` reject reason. The new advice message (`advice.pushRefUnverifiable`) instructs users to use `--force-with-lease=<ref>:<expect>` or `--no-force-if-includes`, replacing the misleading `git pull` suggestion that previously appeared for these cases.

Test coverage in `t/t5533-push-cas.sh` verifies the rejection message and advice for detached HEAD and tag pushes. The patch also updates `Documentation/config/advice.adoc` to document the new advice knob. All prior review feedback (variable naming, advice wording, test formatting) has been addressed; this patch is technically complete and ready for merge.
