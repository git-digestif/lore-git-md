This is the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s five-patch ODB refactoring series, part of the broader ODB abstraction effort. The patch moves the second object read logic—previously controlled by the `OBJECT_INFO_SECOND_READ` flag in the generic ODB layer—into the "files" backend, where it belongs now that alternates are an implementation detail of that backend. The change simplifies the ODB source API by removing the flag entirely and replaces its use in the "packed" source with `OBJECT_INFO_QUICK`, which determines when to scan packfiles covered by a multi-pack index (MIDX) for objects that might be hidden due to a race with a concurrent maintenance task.

Justin Tobler’s review is **substantive and tested**, confirming the technical rationale and implementation. He raises a minor style nit about early loop termination in the "files" backend but otherwise endorses the patch, calling the removal of the leaky `OBJECT_INFO_SECOND_READ` flag a welcome cleanup. The review clarifies that the fallback logic in the "packed" source now triggers on the *absence* of `OBJECT_INFO_QUICK` rather than the presence of `OBJECT_INFO_SECOND_READ`, which could theoretically cause an extra scan on the first read but is expected to have minimal impact in practice. The patch is mechanical, well-motivated, and aligns with the series’ goal of simplifying ODB internals. No controversy or open questions remain.
