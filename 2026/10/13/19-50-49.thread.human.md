This thread is about adding bash completion support for the `--graph-indent` and `--no-graph-indent` options to `git log` in the `contrib/completion/git-completion.bash` script. The patch itself is straightforward and uncontroversial, but the discussion has expanded to consider broader improvements to the completion system.

Mirko Faina, the patch author, initially proposed adding these options to the shared `__git_log_common_options` list. Pablo Sabater, the original implementer of `--graph-indent`, suggested moving them to the command-specific `__git_log_gitk_options` block alongside `--graph` and also proposed adding completion for `--graph-lane-limit`. Mirko agreed with these suggestions and plans to reroll the patch accordingly.

The conversation has also touched on deeper architectural questions. Mirko noted that the bash-completion script exists because Git’s `parse-options.h` machinery doesn’t support conditional completion (e.g., suppressing options that require another option to be present). This limitation means the completion system isn’t self-maintaining, and options must be manually curated in the script. While the immediate focus remains on improving the current script, the discussion has opened the door to future work on integrating completion hints directly into `parse-options.h`, which could make the system more robust and easier to maintain.

No objections have been raised, and the tone remains collaborative. The patch is under review, and the next version will likely include the agreed-upon changes while leaving the broader architectural questions for future exploration.
