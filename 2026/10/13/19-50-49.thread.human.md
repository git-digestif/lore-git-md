This thread is about adding bash-completion support for three `git log` options: `--graph-indent`, `--no-graph-indent`, and `--graph-lane-limit`. The patch, now in its second version by Mirko Faina, has reached consensus after incorporating feedback from Pablo Sabater and others. The changes move these options into the `__git_log_gitk_options` block in `contrib/completion/git-completion.bash`, aligning them with `--graph` as reviewers suggested.

The discussion also touched on broader architectural limitations of Git’s completion system—specifically, the lack of conditional logic in `parse-options.h` to suppress options like `--graph-indent` unless `--graph` is present. While this was acknowledged as a general issue affecting other commands, it didn’t block the immediate patch, which is now ready for Junio to pick up. Toon Claes’s recent "Looks good to me" marks the final review approval, confirming the patch is uncontroversial and addresses a clear gap in the completion script. No further changes are expected unless a new substantive concern arises.
