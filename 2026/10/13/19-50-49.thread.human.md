This thread is about adding bash completion support for the `--graph-indent` and `--no-graph-indent` options to `git log`. The patch, submitted by Mirko Faina, updates the shared completion variable `__git_log_common_options` in `contrib/completion/git-completion.bash` to include these options, which were previously missing.

The patch has been under review and remains uncontroversial so far, with the original feature author, Pablo Celayes, cc'd for awareness. However, Pablo Sabater, the original implementer of `--graph-indent`, has now raised substantive technical concerns. He questions whether these options should be in the shared `__git_log_common_options` variable, noting that `--graph` itself is not included there but appears in a command-specific block. He also suggests adding completion for `--graph-lane-limit`, another missing `--graph`-dependent option, and raises a UX issue: autocompleting `--graph-indent` or `--graph-lane-limit` without `--graph` will produce an error. He wonders if the completion script should suppress these options unless `--graph` is already present, similar to how other dependent options might be handled.

The discussion is still at the design level, with no objections to the current patch but a call for a broader rethink of how `--graph`-dependent options are managed in the completion script. Toon Claes has also been cc'd, likely due to his past work on `--graph` options. No decisions or changes have been made yet, and the thread remains open for further feedback.
