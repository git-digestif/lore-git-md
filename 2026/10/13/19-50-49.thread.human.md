This thread is about adding bash completion support for the `--graph-indent` and `--no-graph-indent` options to `git log`. The patch itself, from Mirko Faina, is a straightforward addition to the `__git_log_common_options` list in `contrib/completion/git-completion.bash` and remains uncontroversial. What’s emerged since is a broader discussion about how `--graph`-related options should be handled in the completion script.

Pablo Sabater, who originally implemented the `--graph-indent` option, reviewed the patch and suggested two improvements: grouping `--graph`-dependent options separately (perhaps in a `__git_log_gitk_options` block) and making their completion conditional on `--graph` already being present on the command line. Ben Knoble chimed in to clarify that `__git_log_common_options` isn’t about frequently used options but about options shared across multiple commands, which helps frame where these new options belong. He also floated a longer-term idea: if Git commands exposed their own completion hints via `parse-options`, the completion script could become self-maintaining, eliminating the need for manual curation and conditional logic.

The patch is still under review, with no objections to its immediate goal. The open questions—whether to group `--graph`-dependent options, whether to add `--graph-lane-limit`, and whether completion should be conditional—are all forward-looking and don’t block the current change. No decisions have been made yet, but the discussion has shifted from a simple housekeeping fix to a design conversation about how completion should evolve.
