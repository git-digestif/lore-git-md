This thread is about adding bash-completion support for three `git log` options (`--graph-indent`, `--no-graph-indent`, and `--graph-lane-limit`) in the `contrib/completion/git-completion.bash` script. The patch itself is uncontroversial and has evolved to its second version after review feedback.

The key participants are Mirko Faina (the author), Pablo Sabater (who implemented the original `--graph-indent` option and suggested improvements), and Toon Claes, who recently weighed in to endorse the architectural framing of the discussion. All agree that the current bash-completion script is a stopgap for options not handled by Git’s `parse-options.h` machinery, and that conditional completion (like suppressing `--graph`-dependent options unless `--graph` is present) would require changes to that underlying system. For now, the patch moves the three options into the `__git_log_gitk_options` block alongside `--graph`, which is the agreed-upon grouping.

The patch is ready for another look, with no open objections. The broader questions about how to make the completion system more self-maintaining remain speculative and forward-looking, but they don’t block the immediate change.
