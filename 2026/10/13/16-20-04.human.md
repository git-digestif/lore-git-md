The series is a pragmatic stopgap that prioritizes correctness: if the lock remains held after the timeout, commands fail outright rather than proceeding or skipping silently. This conservative approach avoids disrupting concurrent operations while ensuring that users are not misled by silent failures. The test suite now covers both auto-maintenance and manual scenarios, and the API design has been simplified by reverting to a shared `RERERE_NOWAIT` flag.
