# Thread deltas

## 2026/10/13

- [2026/10/13/16-01-09 by jyotish kumar <jyotishkumar725015@gmail.com>] jyotish kumar raised an RFC noting that `get_tracking_branch()` in `builtin/pull.c` assumes remote branches follow the default mapping from `refs/heads/<branch>` to `refs/remotes/<remote>/<branch>`, which may cause `get_rebase_fork_point()` to look up an incorrect or nonexistent remote-tracking ref (such as `refs/remotes/origin/custom/main` instead of `refs/remotes/origin/main`) when a custom fetch refspec like `+refs/heads/*:refs/remotes/origin/custom/*` is configured, and asked whether resolving the configured fetch mapping would be the correct direction to address this.
