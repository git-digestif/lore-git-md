This is Junio C Hamano's final sign-off on the two-patch series that fixes the `rerere` race condition. The series—introducing a configurable lock timeout (`rerere.lockTimeout`) and a `--skip-locked` mode for `git rerere gc`—is now explicitly marked as ready for the `next` integration branch. The series addresses a long-standing locking flaw in the `rerere` subsystem, exacerbated by the geometric maintenance strategy but fundamentally a robustness issue for any concurrent `rerere` operations. Files touched include `rerere.c`, `rerere.h`, `builtin/gc.c`, documentation, and tests in `t4200-rerere.sh` and `t7900-maintenance.sh`. The conservative design (failing outright if the lock remains held after timeout) prioritizes correctness, and the test suite covers both auto-maintenance and manual scenarios.
