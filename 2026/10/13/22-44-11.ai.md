This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s five-part ODB refactoring series, and the email is Justin Tobler’s **substantive review** of it. The patch itself removes the `local` flag from the generic `struct odb_source` and moves it into the packed source (`struct odb_source_packed`), completing the internal API cleanup begun in earlier patches.

Justin’s review raises a design-level concern about the `ODB_FOR_EACH_OBJECT_LOCAL_ONLY` flag, which he calls "leaky" and "awkward" now that the `local` flag is no longer part of the generic ODB source. He questions whether all call sites using this flag have legitimate "files" backend-specific reasons, and whether the in-memory source should ever be iterated when only "local" objects are requested. His questions are probing the abstraction boundary rather than objecting to the patch as written, and he explicitly states the patch "looks reasonable" and "seems to be moving us in the right direction." The tone is collaborative, not critical.

The review does not request any code changes, but it surfaces a potential follow-up discussion about whether the `ODB_FOR_EACH_OBJECT_LOCAL_ONLY` flag should be reworked or removed in a future series. No other open questions or objections are raised.
