This is a **CI/build system** discussion in the ongoing CI housekeeping series. Junio C Hamano responds to Patrick Steinhardt’s request for alternative names for the `linux-TEST-vars` job, which tests Git with non-default `GIT_TEST_*` variables. The current proposal (`linux-exotic`) was criticized by SZEDER Gábor as misleading, prompting a search for clearer terminology.

Junio offers a structured list of 14 candidate names, grouped by focus: **feature/capability** (e.g., `linux-experimental-features`, `linux-feature-testbed`), **build/configuration** (e.g., `linux-extra-knobs`, `linux-custom-vars`), and **testing/robustness** (e.g., `linux-stress-features`, `linux-edge-cases`). His personal favorite is `linux-feature-testbed`, which explicitly ties the job to testing experimental features. The suggestions prioritize clarity over brevity, aiming to avoid ambiguity about whether the job tests an exotic platform or Git’s optional features.

No technical objections are raised—this remains a naming dispute. The discussion is now open for further input, but no consensus has emerged yet. The broader CI series (v3) is otherwise ready, pending resolution of this naming question.
