This is a translation (l10n) patch from an Outreachy applicant, now with maintainer feedback. The patch converts the hardcoded English pluralization message in `delta-islands.c` from the simple gettext wrapper `_()` to the ngettext wrapper `Q_()`, allowing translators to handle singular and plural forms correctly. The change itself is uncontroversial and well-motivated: the commit message explains why the original string cannot be properly translated in languages with more than two plural forms.

Junio C Hamano praises the commit message but questions the value of including the grep command used to find the issue. He notes the pattern produces many false positives (e.g., `%d` for line numbers or other non-count uses) and could mislead others into attempting similar "fixes" without understanding the context. The patch itself, however, he calls "sensible" and leaves no technical objections.

This is a standalone patch, not part of a larger series, and touches only one file. The change is mechanical and requires no test coverage beyond verifying the English output remains unchanged. The discussion is now focused on whether the grep command should remain in the commit message, not on the substance of the patch. Given Junio's approval of the change itself, this patch looks likely to be merged once the commit message is adjusted.
