This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s five-part series refactoring alternates handling in Git’s object database. The patch removes the `local` flag from the generic `struct odb_source` and moves it into the packed source (`struct odb_source_packed`), completing the internal API cleanup begun in earlier patches.

Justin Tobler’s review is substantive but narrowly focused. He endorses the change as a step forward but raises a pointed question about the `ODB_FOR_EACH_OBJECT_LOCAL_ONLY` flag, which he calls “leaky” and awkward. He wonders whether all call sites using this flag are tied to the “files” backend, and whether iterating over the in-memory source with this flag set ever makes sense. His questions are technical rather than design-level objections, and he explicitly calls the patch “reasonable” and “moving us in the right direction.”

The patch itself is mechanical and uncontroversial: it removes a flag that no longer belongs in the generic ODB source layer, retains it only where it is still needed (the packed source), and updates the loose and in-memory sources to match. No user-visible behavior changes are expected, and the patch relies on existing test coverage. The only open question is whether the `ODB_FOR_EACH_OBJECT_LOCAL_ONLY` flag can be simplified or removed in a future cleanup.
