Core problem or goal: fix hardcoded English plural forms ("tags"/"commits") inside `_()` gettext macros in two verbose (`--debug`) messages, which cause grammatical errors (e.g., "found 1 tags") and prevent proper pluralization in languages with different plural rules.

No merge status reported; series remains in "under review" state.
