This email is **Curtis Smith's response to Junio Hamano's process clarification request** about the collaboration model for the CRLF conversion bugfix in `read-cache.c`. Curtis confirms that there was no off-list collaboration with Torsten Bögershausen and that Torsten did not seek permission before sending v3. He characterizes v3 as Torsten's reworking of Curtis's original patch (v1) with an optimization removed but the `size_change_is_conclusive()` helper retained. Curtis disagrees with this approach and plans to send v4 with his preferred fix: inlining the logic directly into `ie_modified()` rather than introducing a new helper function. He will credit Torsten with a `Helped-by` trailer in v4.

Curtis also addresses Junio's performance concern about the overhead of attribute lookups in the `would_convert_to_git()` call. He clarifies that the lookup only occurs for files whose size has changed (not for clean trees) and provides benchmark data showing negligible overhead: 26ms for stock Git versus 24ms with the fix when running `git status` on 10,000 files with size changes and no conversion configured. This data will be included in v4.

The email advances the thread by resolving the collaboration question and signaling Curtis's intent to reassert ownership of the series with a revised approach. The next step is Curtis's v4 patch, which will likely reignite the architectural debate (helper function vs. inlined logic) and the broader question of whether the fix belongs in `git status` or `git add`.
