This thread is about a bug in `git log --graph --all --oneline` that causes orphaned branches to be misrendered with a spurious parent connection. The issue was reported by herr.kaste with a clear reproduction case and later diagnosed by Pablo Sabater as a problem in `graph.c`'s handling of the `GRAPH_PRE_ROOT` state.

A fix has now been proposed by the original reporter, Caspar Duregger (herr.kaste). The patch modifies `graph_is_visual_root()` to prevent unnecessary indentation when there are columns to the right of an orphaned commit, which was causing the misalignment. The change is minimal and includes a new test case in `t/t4218-log-graph-indentation.sh` to verify the corrected behavior. The commit message thoroughly explains the root cause and solution, referencing the problematic commit (a34c00d96) and Pablo Sabater's earlier diagnosis.

The patch appears well-crafted and narrowly scoped, addressing the specific issue without introducing broader changes. Pablo Sabater, who identified the root cause, has offered to review the patch, though he remains unavailable to work on it himself until next week. Given the clarity of the bug, the minimal fix, and the included test coverage, this should be a straightforward review and is unlikely to be controversial.
