This thread is about a bug in `git log --graph --all --oneline` that misrenders orphaned branches, causing a spurious parent connection in the graph output. The issue was reported by Caspar Duregger (herr.kaste), who also submitted a patch to fix it. The bug occurs when an orphaned commit (like commit R in the test case) is rendered with an incorrect indentation row, making it appear connected to a nonexistent parent. The root cause was traced to a misalignment in `graph.c` introduced by commit a34c00d96 ("graph: indent visual root in graph", 2026-07-14).

Caspar’s patch modifies `graph_is_visual_root()` in `graph.c` to avoid spurious indentation for orphaned commits with columns to their right. The fix is minimal, surgical, and includes a new test case in `t/t4218-log-graph-indentation.sh` to verify the corrected behavior.
