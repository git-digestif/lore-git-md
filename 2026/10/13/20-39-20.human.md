This is the maintainer's **final approval** of the v6 revision of the patch series that makes `git blame` default to `.git-blame-ignore-revs` (or `HEAD:.git-blame-ignore-revs` in bare repositories) when no ignore file is configured. Junio C Hamano explicitly praises the **code clarity improvements** in v6, particularly the removal of the "cute hack" that used `.util = &sb` as a flag and the propagation of `i->string` to avoid hardcoded paths. He confirms the new design is cleaner and more maintainable while preserving all functionality, including the security hardening (NUL-byte rejection, promisor fetch prevention, 100MB size cap) and edge-case handling (bare repositories, subdirectory invocations, committed symlinks).

The series is now **marked ready for `next`**, meaning it will be integrated into Junio’s `next` branch for broader testing. This is the final step before graduation to `master` and inclusion in the next Git release. No further objections are expected, and the only remaining loose end—the NUL-byte check—is a minor detail the author has already offered to adjust in a follow-up if needed. The patch is **feature-complete, well-tested, and aligned with hosting platform conventions** (GitHub, GitLab), making it a low-risk, high-value addition to the blame subsystem. Busy readers can safely assume this will land soon.
