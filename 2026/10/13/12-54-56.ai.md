This is a documentation patch that revises Git’s explanation of detached HEAD state. Julia Evans responds to Junio C Hamano’s review, accepting most suggestions as style refinements and planning a follow-up iteration. Key changes agreed: rewording the opening definition of HEAD to avoid circular phrasing, framing detached HEAD as advantageous for experimentation rather than a “problem,” and adding a concrete recovery example that quotes Git’s warning message when commits are left behind. Julia also removes the word “unintentionally” from the accidental-detachment scenarios and plans to add a cross-reference to `git help datamodel` for deeper context. The diagrams removed from `git-checkout(1)` are not being restored in this iteration; Julia suggests user feedback would be needed to guide any future diagram work. Build-system omissions (meson/Makefile) remain unaddressed in this reply but are acknowledged as critical for integration. The patch is still under review and not yet ready for merging.
