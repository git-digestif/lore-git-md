This thread is about a five-patch series from Patrick Steinhardt that refactors and optimizes how Git's object database handles alternates in the "files" backend. The goal is to improve performance—particularly when reading objects stored in alternate packfiles—without changing user-visible behavior. The series has now reached its third version, with all substantive feedback addressed, including the addition of a new regression test (`t/t5615-alternate-env.sh`) to verify the performance gains.

Key changes include reordering loops to prioritize packfiles over loose objects, simplifying error-handling logic, and removing redundant code from the ODB source API. The series touches 12 files and achieves a modest net reduction in lines of code while delivering a measurable speedup: benchmarks show a 1.89x improvement for `git cat-file --batch-check` when objects are primarily stored in alternate packfiles. Justin Tobler, who reviewed every patch in detail, has now explicitly declared the series "ready" for integration, and no further changes are expected.

The only loose end is the long-term fate of the `ODB_FOR_EACH_OBJECT_LOCAL_ONLY` flag, which both Patrick and Justin agree is redundant but too complex to remove in this series. That work is now scoped as a follow-up effort. The series is uncontroversial, has been tested on both SHA-1 and SHA-256 repositories, and is awaiting Junio’s merge into the integration branches.
