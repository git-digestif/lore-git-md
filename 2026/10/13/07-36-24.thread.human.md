This thread is a six-patch refactoring and performance series by Patrick Steinhardt, focusing on the Git object database’s "files" backend and its handling of alternates. The series aims to optimize object lookups and streaming when alternates are present, then clean up internal ODB APIs. Version 2 is currently under review, with no user-visible behavior changes intended.

The first two patches deliver measurable performance improvements: a 1.89× speedup in `git cat-file --batch-check` by reordering loops to prioritize packfile checks over loose-object `stat()` calls, and a similar optimization for object streaming. The next three patches refactor internal logic, moving the second-read mechanism into the "files" backend, eliminating a redundant read loop in `do_oid_object_info_extended()`, and tightening error handling to fail immediately on repository corruption—a fix prompted by Justin Tobler’s review of v1. The final patch removes a now-unused `local` flag from the generic ODB source struct, completing the API cleanup.

Justin Tobler has provided substantive reviews for all six patches, endorsing the design direction and verifying the technical rationale. The only open question concerns the long-term viability of the `ODB_FOR_EACH_OBJECT_LOCAL_ONLY` flag, which Patrick has identified as potentially removable but deferred that work. The series is uncontroversial and appears headed for Junio’s `next` branch once review concludes.
