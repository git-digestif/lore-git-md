This thread covers Patrick Steinhardt’s five-patch series refactoring and optimizing alternates handling in Git’s object database "files" backend. The series is now ready for integration after addressing all feedback. Junio C Hamano has queued it for the `next` branch, meaning it will undergo integration testing alongside other topics before graduating to `master`.

The patches target performance inefficiencies when reading or streaming objects from alternates, achieving a 1.89x speedup for `git cat-file --batch-check` in benchmarks. Key changes include reordering loops to prioritize packfiles over loose objects, confining second-read logic to `odb/source-files.c`, removing a redundant read loop in `do_oid_object_info_extended()`, and eliminating the `local` flag from the generic ODB source struct. The new test `t/t5615-alternate-env.sh` verifies the performance-critical behavior, and the series has been validated on both SHA-1 and SHA-256 repositories.

All substantive feedback has been addressed, with Justin Tobler providing thorough, tested reviews and endorsing the series as ready for integration. Johannes Schindelin contributed surface-level but actionable feedback, focusing on test coverage. The only unresolved concern—the long-term viability of the `ODB_FOR_EACH_OBJECT_LOCAL_ONLY` flag—was explicitly scoped as a follow-up effort by Patrick, with agreement from Justin. The series is uncontroversial and introduces no user-visible behavior changes or on-disk format modifications. It touches 12 files, primarily in the ODB subsystem.
