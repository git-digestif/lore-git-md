This thread is a five-part patch series from Patrick Steinhardt aimed at refactoring and optimizing how Git handles alternates in its object database (ODB) files backend. The series targets performance inefficiencies introduced when alternates handling was previously migrated into the files backend, along with internal code cleanup.

The first patch, now under review, addresses a specific regression: Git was checking loose objects (which require a `stat()` syscall) for each alternate before moving to packfiles, slowing down cases where objects are mostly packed. The fix reorders the loops to scan all packfiles across alternates first, yielding nearly a 2x speedup in benchmarks without changing behavior or on-disk format. The change is confined to a single function and appears uncontroversial, given Patrick’s expertise in this area.

The series is part of Patrick’s broader ODB abstraction effort and builds on his earlier work migrating alternates handling into the files backend. No reviews or objections have been posted yet, but the initial patch’s clear performance improvement and mechanical nature suggest smooth progress. The remaining patches likely focus on further optimizations and cleanup.
