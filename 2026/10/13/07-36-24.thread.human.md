This thread is a five-patch series from Patrick Steinhardt refactoring and optimizing how Git’s object database handles alternates in the "files" backend. The goal is to improve performance—particularly in object lookups and streaming when alternates are present—while cleaning up internal APIs. The first two patches have now received substantive, tested reviews from Justin Tobler, who confirmed the technical rationale and implementation of both the direct-read optimization (patch 1/5) and its streaming counterpart (patch 2/5). Both changes reorder loops to prioritize packfile checks over loose-object `stat()` calls, achieving measurable speedups without altering user-visible behavior.

The series remains uncontroversial and under active review. Patches 3/5 through 5/5 continue the internal cleanup, removing redundant flags and code paths to simplify the ODB source API. No open questions or objections have been raised, and the changes are confined to plumbing-level code with no on-disk format or test-coverage gaps. The work builds on Patrick’s earlier alternates migration and is part of his broader ODB abstraction effort.
