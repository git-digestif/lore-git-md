# Thread deltas

## 2026/10/13

- [2026/10/13/13-39-28 by Erik Cervin-Edin <erik@cervined.in>] Erik Cervin-Edin reported that with the built-in fsmonitor daemon on Linux, edits in subdirectories of a renamed directory are deterministically missed from `git status` and `diff` (not a race), persisting until fsmonitor state is invalidated; he identified the cause in `add_watch()` in `compat/fsmonitor/fsm-listen-linux.c`, where subdirectory watches follow the inode across the rename, `inotify_add_watch()` fails with EEXIST when re-registering under the new paths, and the early return leaves the descriptor-to-path map with the old path, noting that after Patrick Steinhardt had asked about exactly this in v5 review, the question went unanswered and v15 still returns early on EEXIST.
