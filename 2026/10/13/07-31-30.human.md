This is the second patch in the final v7 series fixing a long-standing race condition in Git’s `rerere` subsystem. The patch introduces a hidden `--skip-locked` flag for `git rerere gc` to let auto-maintenance tasks (like `git maintenance run --auto`) skip silently when the `MERGE_RR.lock` is held, instead of waiting or failing.

Patrick Steinhardt’s reply is a surface-level review: he retracts his earlier suggestion to use a dedicated `RERERE_GC_NOWAIT` enum and now prefers the simpler shared `RERERE_NOWAIT` flag that the patch already implements. The change is purely mechanical—no behavior or documentation is affected—and the patch remains technically complete and ready for integration.
