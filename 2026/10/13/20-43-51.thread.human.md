This thread is about a small but important i18n fix for `git describe`. Antoni Borowski posted a patch to replace hardcoded English plural forms in two verbose messages with Git's plural-aware `Q_()` macro, preventing grammatical errors like "found 1 tags" in non-English locales. The change is minimal—just two lines in `builtin/describe.c`—and uncontroversial.

Jeff King (Peff) reviewed the patch and called it "obviously correct," though he questioned whether translating debug messages is worthwhile at all. He floated the idea of removing the `--debug` flag entirely as a future cleanup, but didn’t oppose the current fix. No other reviews have come in yet, and the patch is still under review with no merge status reported. Unless someone raises a technical objection, this fix will likely proceed as-is.
