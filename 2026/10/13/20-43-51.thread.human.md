While no objections have surfaced yet, the patch raises a design-level question about consistency across Git’s debug output—why some subsystems (like `transport-helper.c`) leave debug messages untranslated while others don’t. Antoni’s argument is practical, and the patch aligns with precedent, but the thread could yet spark broader discussion about project-wide conventions. For now, it looks like a clean, well-justified cleanup that addresses a real pain point for developers.
