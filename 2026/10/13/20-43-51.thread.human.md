This thread began as a cleanup of i18n handling in `git describe`'s `--debug` output, with Antoni Borowski proposing to remove gettext wrappers entirely to keep debug messages untranslated. The initial patch was well-received and is now cooking in `next`, but Junio C Hamano has just spotted a small but important oversight: while the patch removes translation from the `fprintf()` calls, it leaves the `prio_names[]` array marked for translation (`N_()`) and still translates its elements in the debug output. Since the array is only used for debug output, this is inconsistent with the patch’s goal.

Junio’s review is technical and constructive, suggesting three follow-up actions to fully align the patch with its own rationale and the precedent in `transport-helper.c`: remove the `N_()` markers from `prio_names[]`, remove the remaining `_()` calls around its elements, and consider moving the array into the `if (debug)` block (or renaming it to clarify its debug-only purpose). The patch remains uncontroversial, and no objections to the broader goal have been raised. The thread is now focused on this refinement, with the next step likely being a v2 patch from Antoni addressing Junio’s feedback.
