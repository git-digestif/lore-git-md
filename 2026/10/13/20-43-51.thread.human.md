Antoni Borowski posted the patch, which fixes grammatical errors like "found 1 tags" in non-English locales. Jeff King reviewed it, calling it "obviously correct" but questioning whether debug messages should be translated at all. The patch itself is uncontroversial and ready to proceed, with no technical objections or requests for changes. The only open question is a tangential one: whether debug messages should be translated in the first place, which Junio framed as a potential future cleanup rather than a blocker.
