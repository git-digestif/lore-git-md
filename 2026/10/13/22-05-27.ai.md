This is a maintainer feedback email on Jun Wu’s configurable index lock timeout series. Junio C Hamano raises two main concerns about the clarity and precision of the cover letter, not the code itself.

First, he points out that the cover letter omits the explicit problem statement and proposed solution that readers would expect. The opening lines describe the IDE dilemma but never state that the series will introduce a timeout mechanism; instead, the solution is only hinted at in the patch descriptions. Junio’s reply effectively says, “Tell me up front what you are doing and why, not just the problem.”

Second, he flags ambiguity in the description of the race-avoidance logic. The phrase “if the index is read before locking, the wait will be skipped” does not make clear which process (background `git status` or a foreground command) is doing the reading and locking. Junio wants the cover letter to spell out whose behavior is being modified so reviewers can follow the control flow without guessing.

No code-level objections or merge status are mentioned; this is purely a documentation-of-intent issue. The author will need to revise the cover letter to address these points before the series can move forward.
