This is a small but well-targeted i18n (internationalization) bugfix for the `git describe` command. Antoni Borowski fixes two verbose messages that incorrectly hardcoded English plural forms inside `_()` — the gettext wrapper for translatable strings. The patch switches both messages to use `Q_()`, Git’s pluralization helper, so that translators can supply language-specific singular and plural forms rather than being forced into English grammar.

The change is mechanical and minimal: only two lines in `builtin/describe.c` are touched, replacing `_("traversed %lu commits\n")` with `Q_("traversed %lu commit\n", "traversed %lu commits\n", seen_commits)` and similarly for the "found %i tags" message. The commit message clearly explains both the grammatical problem ("found 1 tags") and the i18n motivation, making the rationale obvious without digging into the code.

This is a classic microproject — a bite-sized, self-contained fix ideal for a first-time contributor. The patch is clean, the test coverage is already present (the existing test suite will exercise these messages), and the change aligns perfectly with Git’s i18n practices. No controversy is expected; it looks like a straightforward improvement that should be uncontroversial to merge.
