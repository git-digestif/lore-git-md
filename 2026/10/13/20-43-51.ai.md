This is a small i18n (internationalization) bugfix for `git describe`. The patch addresses two verbose messages in `builtin/describe.c` that incorrectly hardcode English plural forms ("tags"/"commits") inside the `_()` gettext macro. This causes grammatical errors (e.g., "found 1 tags") and prevents proper pluralization in languages with different plural rules.

The fix replaces `_()` with `Q_()`, Git's plural-aware gettext macro, which selects the correct singular or plural form based on the count variable. The change affects two messages: one reporting the number of commits traversed, and another reporting the number of tags found when the search is abandoned.

The patch is minimal (4 insertions, 2 deletions) and well-targeted, touching only the pluralization logic without altering program behavior. It includes a clear commit message explaining both the grammatical and i18n motivations. This is a straightforward improvement that aligns with Git's ongoing efforts to make its messages translatable. The fix is likely uncontroversial and should integrate smoothly.
