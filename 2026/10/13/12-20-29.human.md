This is a documentation patch discussion, specifically a follow-up in the ongoing review of Julia Evans’s rewrite of Git’s detached HEAD state explanation. The email zooms in on a single, narrow point: the naming convention for the new `gitdetachedhead(7)` man page.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk points out that the existing Git concept guides listed by `git help --guides` all use hyphens (`core-tutorial`, `cvs-migration`, `remote-helpers`, etc.), not single unhyphenated words. He argues that the new guide should follow the same pattern (`gitdetached-head(7)`) for consistency, rather than the unhyphenated form (`gitdetachedhead(7)`) currently proposed in the patch. The distinction between section 1 commands (no hyphen after `git`) and section 7 guides (hyphen after `git`) is already established, so this is not a new rule but an application of an existing one.

The exchange is brief and technical, with no broader implications for the patch’s goals or structure. Julia Evans has not yet responded to this specific naming question, so the hyphenation issue remains unresolved. The rest of the patch—its core improvements to detached HEAD documentation, the standalone guide, and the advice-message pointer—is unaffected by this detail.
