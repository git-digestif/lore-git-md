This is the second patch in Jun Wu’s two-patch series introducing a configurable index-lock timeout (`core.indexLockTimeout`). It modifies `git commit` to lock the index *before* reading it, ensuring the timeout mechanism (added in Patch 1) applies to `commit` as well.

The patch consolidates four separate lock acquisitions in `builtin/commit.c` into a single lock taken at the start of `prepare_index()`. The commit message traces the lock-order history: the original C port (2007) locked before reading, but a later partial-commit fix (2007) accidentally moved the read first. This patch restores the earlier, safer order. The test script `t/t0032-index-lock-timeout.sh` is updated to reflect that `git commit` now waits for the lock when `core.indexLockTimeout` is positive, rather than failing immediately.

The change is small (9 lines removed, 4 added) and touches only `builtin/commit.c` and the test. It completes the series’ goal of making common commands (`add`, `commit`, etc.) wait briefly for the index lock, reducing IDE-induced contention. The lock-order adjustment is the only potential risk point; the author argues it is a regression fix, not a new behavior.
