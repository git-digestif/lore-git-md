However, Patrick's feedback suggests the migration may have uncritically carried forward complexity that is no longer justified. The review does not object to the Clar migration itself—Junio and Patrick previously endorsed that direction—but rather to the specific implementation choices in this patch. No alternative approach is proposed yet; the ball is back in the author's court to simplify the test logic and rewrite the commit message in clear, human-readable prose.
