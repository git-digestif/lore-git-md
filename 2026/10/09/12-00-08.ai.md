This is the first patch in a six-part documentation series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page that consolidates Git’s merge-conflict resolution guidance. The patch creates the new guide (`Documentation/gitmergeconflicts.adoc`) and integrates it into the build system (Makefile, meson.build, command-list.txt, and .gitattributes). The guide is 333 lines long and covers foundational concepts, resolution workflows, tooling advice, and command-specific nuances, all refined based on v1 feedback.

The new page directly addresses user-reported pain points: scattered advice across multiple man pages, inconsistent terminology ("ours" vs. "theirs"), unclear conflict markers, and missing explanations of tools like `diff3`. Key sections include "WHAT IS A MERGE CONFLICT?" (a lightweight, example-driven introduction), "MERGE CONFLICT MARKERS" (with side-by-side examples), "HOW TO RESOLVE A MERGE CONFLICT" (a four-step process), and "TOOLS FOR HANDLING MERGE CONFLICTS" (covering `git log --merge -p`, `git show :1:filename`, and `merge.conflictstyle=diff3`). The guide also clarifies the "ours/theirs" terminology, explaining their relationship to `HEAD` and the other commit, and includes a rewritten "DIFF3 AND ZDIFF3" section with concrete examples demonstrating how the common ancestor reveals the *intent* behind conflicting changes.

Build-system integration is complete: the new page is registered in `command-list.txt` (under "guides"), added to both the Makefile and Meson build, and protected by `.gitattributes` with `conflict-marker-size=32` to prevent false positives in tools like `rerere`. The patch folds the `.gitattributes` change into the first patch (addressing v1 feedback) and resolves the `make check-docs` failure from v1.

The guide is purely additive—no runtime behavior changes—and is designed to replace redundant sections in existing man pages (e.g., `git-merge.adoc`, `git-rebase.adoc`) with cross-references. Command-specific details (e.g., `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD` for cherry-picks) are preserved in their respective man pages while linking to the new guide for general advice. The series is now ready for final review, with all v1 feedback addressed and no open technical questions. The guide’s structure and content reflect a mature, user-tested effort, and it is likely to graduate soon. Key symbols introduced: the `gitmergeconflicts(7)` man page (no CLI options or config keys).
