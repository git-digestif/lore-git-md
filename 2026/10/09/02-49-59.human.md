This is the author announcing a decision on the first patch of her three-patch v2 series that fixes Git’s global config listing inconsistency. Delilah confirms she will drop the Windows path normalization patch entirely and instead adjust the tests in the final patch to export `XDG_CONFIG_HOME` with forward slashes only. This resolves the maintainer’s concern about whether the normalization was truly necessary for the core fix, keeping the series tightly scoped to its stated goal of making `git config list --global` read both `$HOME/.gitconfig` and `$XDG_CONFIG_HOME/git/config`.

The change is purely preparatory for the test suite and does not affect the actual config-reading logic. No new code will land in `path.c`; the adjustment will be confined to the test scripts (`t1300-config.sh` and `t1306-xdg-files.sh`). The decision reflects the project’s preference for minimal, focused patches and avoids introducing platform-specific behavior unless absolutely required. The thread remains active on the remaining two patches, which implement the core fix and its error handling.
