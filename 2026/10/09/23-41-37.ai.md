This is a **licensing policy clarification** from Junio C Hamano, the Git maintainer, responding to D. Ben Knoble’s substantive review of the `sha1dc-accel` series. Junio resolves the licensing ambiguity that Knoble raised by explaining how Git’s GPL-2-only stance interacts with permissively licensed components like the MIT-licensed `sha1dc` and `sha1dc-accel` code.

Junio confirms that Git’s **entire package** (binaries, combined sources) must be distributed under GPL-2 due to the copyleft requirement, but the **individual permissively licensed files** (e.g., `sha1dc` and `sha1dc-accel`) retain their original MIT licenses and copyright notices. This means downstream distributors (like Gentoo) are correct to label the Git package as GPL-2-only, while still being able to extract and reuse the MIT-licensed components elsewhere under their original terms. The key takeaway is that **mixed-license components are permissible** as long as the original licenses are preserved and the combined work is distributed under GPL-2.
