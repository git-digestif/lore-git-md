This is a performance optimization patch in the remote subsystem, part of a 15-patch series. It replaces linear traversals of ref lists in `match_explicit_refs()` with `strmap` lookups, reducing the time complexity from O(R*N + R*M) to O(N + M + R*rules) when matching R refspecs against N local and M remote refs. The patch consolidates helper functions (`match_explicit_lhs_map` is merged back into `match_explicit_lhs`) and introduces a small behavior change: newly matched destination refs are now added to the `dst` strmap so later refspecs can see them, which wasn’t possible with the old linear traversal.

The commit includes benchmark results from a synthetic test (t/perf/p5516) showing dramatic speedups (up to 79%) when many refspecs are used, with no regression in the single-refspec case. The change touches only `remote.c` and relies on `count_refspec_match_in_map()` and `ref_map_init()` helpers introduced earlier in the series. This is the 12th patch in the series; without the cover letter we don’t know the overall goal, but the local optimization is clear and self-contained.
