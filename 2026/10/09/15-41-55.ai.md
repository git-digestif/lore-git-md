This is a process-oriented reply from Junio C Hamano to the v2 cover letter of the `gitmergeconflicts(7)` documentation series. Junio clarifies the correct use of the `Reviewed-by:` trailer in Git’s contribution process.

The email explains that `Reviewed-by:` should only be added by reviewers themselves when they are fully satisfied with the patch after a detailed review. Junio points out that while D. Ben Knoble and Patrick Steinhardt provided substantial feedback that improved the series, neither explicitly offered a `Reviewed-by:` trailer in their replies. Instead, their contributions should be acknowledged with `Helped-by:` trailers, which credit reviewers for input that led to improvements, even if they did not formally approve the final version.

Junio references the review thread to illustrate that both reviewers engaged deeply with the content but did not explicitly state `Reviewed-by:`. He also quotes the project’s documentation (`SubmittingPatches` and `ReviewingGuidelines.adoc`) to reinforce the distinction between `Reviewed-by:` and `Helped-by:`. The email is a gentle correction, ensuring the patch series follows Git’s conventions for crediting contributors. No technical changes to the series are requested; this is purely about process.
