This thread is about a 15-patch series from Jon Simons that aims to speed up refspec matching in `git push` when dealing with many deletions, addressing a scalability bottleneck that causes O(n²) behavior. The series is still under review and hasn’t been picked up in any integration branch yet.

The key improvements come from replacing linear traversals with `strmap` lookups, with the biggest performance gains (55–82% of the total 10–100x speedup) delivered early in the series by removing `mkpath()` overhead in `refname_match()` and fixing a long-standing quirk where leading `./` in refnames were silently stripped. Later patches add test coverage for server-side behaviors and measure `--force-with-lease` overhead to establish baselines for further optimizations. The most recent patch (14/15) refactors `apply_push_cas()` to prepare for the final `strmap` optimization.

So far, the only feedback has been a surface-level comment from Kristoffer Haugsbakk suggesting that regression tests and their fixes should be combined into single commits, rather than split across two as Jon did. No substantive technical reviews have been posted yet, and the series is still awaiting deeper feedback. The behavioral changes—like rejecting duplicate destinations client-side and no longer stripping leading `./`—are guarded by tests, but the removal of `mkpath()` may need careful review to ensure no edge-case regressions.
