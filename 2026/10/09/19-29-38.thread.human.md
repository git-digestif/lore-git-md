This thread is about a 15-patch series from Jon Simons that speeds up refspec matching in `git push` when dealing with many branch deletions. The series targets a scalability bottleneck where the current linear traversals lead to O(n²) behavior, achieving 10–100x speedups by replacing them with `strmap` lookups. It also fixes correctness gaps in refspec ordering and duplicate-destination handling, some of which were exposed by a 2025 server-side change that now rejects multiple updates to the same ref.
