This is a design discussion reply from Junio C Hamano in the ongoing `git stash create` feature thread. Junio steps back to clarify the long-term vision for `git stash create`: it should eventually be capable enough to let script writers reimplement `git stash push` using `create` and `store`. He questions the author’s rationale for limiting the exposed options, asking why any option supported by `stash push` would need to behave differently in `stash create`—unless the concern is simply the effort required to implement and test them all upfront.

The email pushes back on the idea that deferring options avoids "overspecifying" without concrete use cases. Junio argues that if `stash push --foo` creates a certain stash shape, `stash create --foo` should create the same shape, making the two commands interchangeable for scripting. The implication is that the author’s proposed minimal subset (`-m`, `-q`, `-u`, `-a`) might be artificially narrow, and that a more principled approach would be to align `create`’s options with `push`’s where they make sense. No technical objections to the implementation are raised, but the design direction remains unresolved. The next step is likely further discussion or a revised patch that either justifies the subset or expands it to match `push` more closely.
