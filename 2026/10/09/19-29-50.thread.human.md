This thread is about a performance optimization in Git’s remote subsystem, submitted by Jon Simons as part of a 15-patch series. The patch replaces linear traversals of ref lists in `match_explicit_refs()` with `strmap` lookups, cutting the time complexity from O(R*N + R*M) to O(N + M + R*rules) when matching R refspecs against N local and M remote refs. It also consolidates helper functions and tweaks behavior so newly matched destination refs are visible to later refspecs—something the old linear approach couldn’t do.

Jon includes benchmark results from a synthetic test (t/perf/p5516) showing up to 79% speedups with many refspecs, with no regression in the single-refspec case. The change is confined to `remote.c` and depends on helpers (`count_refspec_match_in_map`, `ref_map_init`) introduced earlier in the series. So far, there’s no review feedback or maintainer response, and without the cover letter, the broader goal of the series remains unclear. The patch itself is a self-contained win, but its place in the larger effort is still unknown.
