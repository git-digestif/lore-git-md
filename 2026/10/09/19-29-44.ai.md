This patch is part of a performance optimization series targeting the `remote` subsystem. It converts `check_push_refs()` from a linear scan of local refs to a `strmap`-based lookup, reducing the time complexity from O(R * N) to O(R * rules) when matching R explicit refspecs against N local refs. The change is motivated by measurable speedups in the `mirror:refspecs:100` benchmark (31% faster) and introduces helper functions like `count_refspec_match_in_map()` and `match_explicit_lhs_map()` to support the new implementation. A follow-up commit will consolidate the temporary duplicate functions (`match_explicit_lhs()` and `match_explicit_lhs_map()`).

The commit message includes clear before/after complexity analysis and benchmark results, making the rationale and impact easy to assess.
