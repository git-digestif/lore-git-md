This is the second patch in Julia Evans’s v2 series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page consolidating Git’s merge-conflict resolution guidance. The patch updates `git-merge.adoc` to replace its 122-line “HOW CONFLICTS ARE PRESENTED” and “HOW TO RESOLVE CONFLICTS” sections with a three-line cross-reference to the new guide (`linkgit:gitmergeconflicts[7]`). The only remaining merge-specific content is the brief mention of `--abort` and `--continue` in the opening paragraph, which now points users to the guide for further details. The patch also adds the new guide to the SEE ALSO section at the end of the file.

The change is mechanical and uncontroversial: it removes duplicated material that is now covered in the new guide while preserving the minimal context needed to orient users. No command-line behavior or build-system integration is affected. This patch is part of a broader effort to centralize conflict-resolution advice across multiple Git commands, reducing maintenance burden and improving user experience. The series as a whole is now complete, addresses all v1 feedback, and is ready for final review.
