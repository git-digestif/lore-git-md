This is a test fix targeting the SHA-256 transition effort. The patch modifies `t/t5004-archive-corner-cases.sh` to skip a test that extracts SHA-1 objects from a ZIP archive when running in a SHA-256 repository. The test in question involves objects larger than 4GB and has several prerequisites (64-bit `long`, unzip with 64-bit support, `EXPENSIVE` enabled), but was failing silently in the Fedora CI job due to a typo that disabled breaking changes—once fixed, the job would default to SHA-256 and break.

The change adds the `SHA1` prerequisite to the test, ensuring it only runs in SHA-1 repositories. This is part of a larger series (1/8) addressing CI housekeeping, specifically fixing the Fedora job’s configuration. The commit message clearly explains the context: the test’s reliance on SHA-1 objects makes it incompatible with SHA-256 repositories, and the upcoming fix to the Fedora job would expose this issue. The patch itself is minimal (one-line change) and well-motivated, with no behavioral impact beyond skipping the test where it would fail. The series’ broader goal appears to be improving CI reliability, particularly around SHA-256 testing.
