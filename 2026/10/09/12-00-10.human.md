This is the third patch in Julia Evans’s v2 documentation series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page consolidating Git’s merge-conflict resolution guidance. The patch updates `git-rebase.adoc` to replace its existing conflict-resolution advice with a cross-reference to the new guide, while preserving rebase-specific details—particularly the `--skip` option, which is unique to `git rebase` and not covered in the general guide.

The change removes four lines of redundant explanation (how to identify conflict markers, edit files, and mark resolutions with `git add`) and replaces them with a single line directing users to `gitmergeconflicts(7)` for a "full guide to handling merge conflicts." The patch also adds the new guide to the "SEE ALSO" section at the end of the man page. The rebase-specific steps (resolve, `--continue`, `--abort`, `--skip`) are retained, as they reflect workflows not shared by other commands like `git merge` or `git cherry-pick`.

This patch is a straightforward documentation refactoring that aligns with the series’ goal of centralizing conflict-resolution advice while preserving command-specific nuances. The change is uncontroversial and follows the pattern established in earlier patches (e.g., `git-merge.adoc` in patch 2/6). No new technical details or open questions are introduced here; the focus is purely on reducing duplication and improving maintainability. The patch is ready for review and likely to be merged as part of the series.
