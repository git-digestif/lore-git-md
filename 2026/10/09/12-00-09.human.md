This is the second patch in Julia Evans’ six-part documentation series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page consolidating Git’s merge-conflict resolution guidance. This patch updates `git-merge.adoc` to replace its lengthy, duplicated conflict-resolution sections with a single cross-reference to the new guide.

The change is mechanical but meaningful: 122 lines of conflict-resolution advice—covering conflict markers, resolution steps, and tooling—are removed, leaving only a brief mention of `--abort`/`--continue` and a link to `gitmergeconflicts(7)`. The patch preserves the introductory context about when merges stop (conflicts or `--no-commit`) and the behavior of `git merge --abort`, ensuring the man page remains self-contained for basic usage. The link is presented in two forms: the formal `linkgit:gitmergeconflicts[7]` macro and a parenthetical note suggesting `git help mergeconflicts` as a user-friendly alternative.

This patch is part of the series’ broader goal of centralizing conflict-resolution advice to reduce duplication and improve clarity. The new guide (introduced in patch 1) now handles the removed content, including detailed explanations of conflict markers, resolution steps, and tooling like `git mergetool` and `git diff AUTO_MERGE`. The series has already addressed all v1 feedback, including mechanical fixes (e.g., `command-list.txt` registration) and content refinements (e.g., a new foundational section, rewritten "ours/theirs" explanation). This patch is uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ design: command-specific details remain in the man page, while general advice moves to the guide. The series is now ready for final review and likely to graduate soon.
