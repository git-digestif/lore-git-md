This is a brief but pointed meta-discussion about contributor workflow, specifically the pattern of posting new patch versions without engaging with reviewer feedback. Karthik Nayak, a domain expert on ref backends, weighs in to reinforce Junio C Hamano and Patrick Steinhardt’s earlier concerns.

The comment is wry but underscores a serious point: the project expects contributors to engage in dialogue, not just iterate mechanically.

The email does not revisit the technical merits of the `reference-transaction` hook series; it is purely about the social contract of patch submission. By echoing Junio and Patrick, Karthik signals that the process concern is now a shared sentiment among reviewers, not an isolated objection.
