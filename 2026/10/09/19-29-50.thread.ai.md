Thread type: patch series (performance optimization in the remote subsystem)

Core problem or goal
Replace linear traversals of ref lists in `match_explicit_refs()` with `strmap` lookups, reducing time complexity from O(R*N + R*M) to O(N + M + R*rules) when matching R refspecs against N local and M remote refs. The change also consolidates helper functions and enables newly matched destination refs to be visible to later refspecs.

- New behavior: `strmap` lookups; newly matched destination refs are added to the `dst` strmap so later refspecs can see them.
