This is a design discussion in the ongoing effort to extend `git stash create` with `--include-untracked` (`-u`) and `--all` (`-a`) options. Junio C Hamano responds to Kazumasa Shigeta’s proposal to incrementally extend `git stash create` with a minimal subset of options (`-m/--message`, `-q/--quiet`, `-u`, and `-a`), questioning whether the "start small" approach aligns with the long-term goal of making `stash create` and `stash store` a complete, scriptable foundation for `git stash push`.

Junio argues that the long-term vision should be to make `git stash create` capable of replicating all of `git stash push`’s functionality, so that script writers can build their own `push`-like workflows using `create` and `store`. He presses for consistency: if `stash push --foo` creates a stash entry of a certain shape, `stash create --foo` should create the same shape. The implication is that deferring options like `--patch` or `--staged` now could lead to inconsistencies later, forcing scripts to handle different stash-entry formats depending on which options were used. Junio acknowledges that a "start small" approach is reasonable if the concern is implementation effort, but he does not see how "we might overspecify without knowing the need" applies here, since the options already exist in `stash push` and their behavior is well-defined.

The email does not resolve the design direction but sharpens the tension between incrementalism and long-term consistency. Shigeta’s next move will likely determine whether the patch proceeds as a minimal extension or pivots to a more ambitious scope. The thread remains under active review, with no merge status reported.
