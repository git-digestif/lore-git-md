This is the third patch in the v2 series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page consolidating Git’s merge-conflict resolution guidance. The patch updates `git-rebase.adoc` to replace its existing conflict-resolution instructions with a cross-reference to the new guide, while preserving rebase-specific details.

The change removes four lines of redundant advice (how to find conflict markers, edit files, and mark resolutions) and replaces them with a single link to `gitmergeconflicts(7)`. It retains the three-step resolution flow unique to `git rebase`—`--continue`, `--abort`, and `--skip`—since the `--skip` option is not available in other conflict-generating commands. The patch also adds the new guide to the `SEE ALSO` section at the end of the man page.

This is a straightforward documentation refactoring that aligns with the series’ goal of centralizing conflict-resolution advice. The rebase man page now defers to the new guide for general guidance while keeping its command-specific workflow intact. No behavior changes or new symbols are introduced. The patch is uncontroversial and likely to be merged as part of the series.
