This is a documentation patch in Julia Evans’s v2 series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page consolidating Git’s merge-conflict resolution guidance. This specific patch updates `git-merge.adoc` to replace its 122-line “HOW CONFLICTS ARE PRESENTED” and “HOW TO RESOLVE CONFLICTS” sections with a three-line cross-reference to the new guide (`linkgit:gitmergeconflicts[7]`). The change is mechanical and uncontroversial, removing duplicated material now covered in the new guide while preserving the minimal context needed to orient users.

Junio’s reply is a **substantive review** of the patch’s claim that “all of the info about merge conflicts has been moved to the new guide.” He methodically compares the removed sections with the new guide, identifying three specific omissions or disconnects: (1) the `|||||||` marker in `diff3` output (used to show the common ancestor) is not explained in the new guide, (2) the original text’s nuanced phrasing (“Git cannot randomly pick one side over the other, and asks you to resolve it”) is more cohesive than the new guide’s separate paragraphs, and (3) the grouping of `git diff AUTO_MERGE` with other tools in the new guide blurs the distinction between static and dynamic views of the conflict. He suggests these points should be carried back into the new guide to preserve clarity and completeness.

The review is **constructive and actionable**, with Junio explicitly marking the missing explanations as things that “should be preserved somehow.” His feedback does not block the patch but signals that the new guide will need minor refinements in v3 to fully replace the removed material. The patch itself is ready to merge once those refinements are made.
