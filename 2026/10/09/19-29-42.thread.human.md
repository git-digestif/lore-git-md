This thread is about a performance optimization series for Git's refspec matching during `git push`. Jon Simons has introduced a new performance test (`t/perf/p5516-push-delete-refspec.sh`) as part of a 15-patch series. The test measures the client-side cost of matching explicit delete refspecs during `--dry-run` pushes against a server with 100,000 branches, comparing two scenarios: an empty client and a mirror client (with a full local copy of server refs).

The baseline results show a significant performance gap, especially as the number of refspecs increases—the mirror client takes nearly three times longer (6.85s vs. 2.50s) when pushing 100 delete refspecs. The test is designed to be reproducible, using reftable storage and avoiding side effects that could skew measurements. While the test itself is thorough, the broader goal of the series remains unclear beyond targeting refspec matching performance improvements. So far, there are no reviews or discussions on this patch.
