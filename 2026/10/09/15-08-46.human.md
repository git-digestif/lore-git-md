This is a cleanup and test modernization series targeting Git’s test infrastructure. The author, Muhammed Dilshad A, has restructured the series into four patches for v3, addressing feedback from Patrick Steinhardt and Junio C Hamano. The goal is to migrate mergesort tests from the shell-based test suite to the Clar unit-test framework, simplify the test design, and remove unused code.

The series now drops the earlier memory-leak fix (since the helper it patched will be removed) and splits the Clar migration into three logical steps: first moving the tests, then simplifying them, and finally adding edge-case coverage for empty and small lists. The fourth patch removes the obsolete `p0071-sort.sh` performance benchmark and the `test-mergesort.c` helper, which had no remaining users. The net result is a significant reduction in code size—477 lines removed versus 170 added—while preserving all prior test coverage and adding new edge cases.

The commit messages have been rewritten for clarity, and the test design has been streamlined as requested. The old certification test’s complex grid of input distributions and transformation modes is replaced with direct tests for sorted, reversed, equal-value, and repeatable random inputs, plus explicit checks for empty and small lists. The series looks well-motivated and thorough, directly implementing the feedback from earlier rounds. It should be uncontroversial and ready for final review.
