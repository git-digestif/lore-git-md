This is the final patch in Julia Evans’s six-part documentation series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page that consolidates Git’s merge-conflict resolution guidance. The patch updates `git-pull.adoc` to link to the new guide, replacing a terse reference to abort commands with a cross-reference to the comprehensive `gitmergeconflicts(7)` page.

The change is minimal—just two lines—but it completes the series’ goal of replacing scattered, duplicated advice across multiple man pages with a single, user-tested resource. The new link is unobtrusive and follows the established `linkgit:` macro style used elsewhere in the documentation. No command-specific details are removed from `git-pull.adoc`, preserving its focus on pull-specific behavior while deferring general conflict-resolution advice to the new guide.

This patch is purely mechanical and uncontroversial. All substantive feedback on the series has already been addressed in earlier patches, including the foundational “WHAT IS A MERGE CONFLICT?” section, the rewritten “OURS” AND “THEIRS” explanation, and the retention of command-specific details in other man pages. The series is now fully integrated into the documentation build system and ready for final review. Expect it to graduate to `next` soon.
