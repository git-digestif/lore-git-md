Core problem or goal
Convert `check_push_refs()` from a linear scan of local refs (O(R * N) time) to a `strmap`-based lookup (O(R * rules) time) when matching R explicit refspecs against N local refs. The change is purely internal refactoring with no behavior change, motivated by a 31 % speedup in the `mirror:refspecs:100` benchmark.
