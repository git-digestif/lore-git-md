This is a documentation patch in Julia Evans’s v2 series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`. The patch updates `git-cherry-pick.adoc` to replace its existing merge-conflict advice with a cross-reference to the new guide, but Junio’s review suggests a more radical simplification: removing the entire introductory conflict paragraph ("When it is not obvious how to apply a change...") and moving the cross-reference to the end of the "When a merge conflict happens" section.

The current patch retains the command-specific details (e.g., `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD` behavior, index updates, and clean-worktree requirements) while delegating general conflict-resolution guidance to `gitmergeconflicts(7)`. Junio’s feedback targets the awkward phrasing "obvious to whom" and proposes eliminating the redundant introductory paragraph altogether, arguing that the cross-reference would be more naturally placed at the end of the conflict-description section. This change would streamline the man page without losing any technical content, as the new guide already covers all the removed material.

The patch is part of a well-received series that consolidates scattered merge-conflict advice into a single, user-friendly guide. Junio’s suggestion is a minor refinement—likely to be incorporated in v3—rather than a substantive objection. The series remains on track for merging once these wording tweaks are addressed.
