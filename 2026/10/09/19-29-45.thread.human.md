This thread is a small but focused part of a larger 15-patch series by Jon Simons testing edge cases in `git push`. The specific patch under discussion adds test coverage for a scenario where two refspecs attempt to create the same new branch on the remote—either in full (`main:refs/heads/frotz`) or abbreviated (`main:frotz`) form. The tests confirm that Git’s client-side validation correctly rejects such pushes when the remote advertises at least one ref, but reveals a bug: when pushing to an empty repository, the validation fails, allowing the push to proceed only to be rejected server-side. The failing tests are marked as `test_expect_failure` and will be updated once a later patch in the series fixes the issue.

So far, the thread is just the patch itself—no replies or reviews have arrived yet. The series is still in its early stages, with this patch (part 7) documenting the problem rather than solving it. If you’re following the series, keep an eye out for the later patch that flips these tests to `test_expect_success`; that’s where the actual fix will land.
