This is the first patch in Phillip Wood’s two-patch v3 series, which refactors `remove_branch_state()` to replace its `verbose` boolean argument with an `unsigned flags` field. The change is purely preparatory: it introduces `REMOVE_BRANCH_STATE_VERBOSE` as the sole flag and updates all call sites, but has no user-visible behavior change. The patch is uncontroversial and ready for integration; it enables the second patch to add a new flag for preserving conflict labels without breaking existing callers. Files touched: `branch.h`, `branch.c`, and `builtin/checkout.c`.
