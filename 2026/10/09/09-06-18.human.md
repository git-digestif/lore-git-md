The series as a whole is ready for integration, but Patrick Steinhardt now suggests dropping this specific patch (v6 3/3) and deferring its behavioral change to a later release. However, Patrick’s feedback raises a valid question about whether the cure is worse than the disease. If the first two patches reduce lock contention to negligible levels, then allowing commands to proceed with a held lock—even with a warning—could introduce subtle edge cases without clear user benefit. The thread’s earlier consensus (including Junio’s input) favored the compromise, but Patrick’s late objection suggests the project may now prefer a more conservative approach.

For a busy reader, the key takeaway is that the series’ core fix (patches 1 and 2) is uncontroversial and likely to land soon, while this patch’s fate hangs on whether the community expects real-world users to hit the race condition in practice.
