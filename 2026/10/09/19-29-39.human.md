This is a bugfix patch, the first in a 15-patch series by Jon Simons. It tightens validation of the `<refname>` argument in `git push --force-with-lease=<refname>[:<expect>]` by running it through `check_refname_format()` before the lease is applied. The motivation is an upcoming change (later in the series) that removes `mkpath()` from `refname_match()`, which currently masks invalid refnames like `./refs/heads/main` by silently stripping the leading `./`. After that change, such refnames would no longer match the intended remote ref, causing the lease to be ignored rather than enforced. The patch makes the command fail fast with a clear error message both before and after the `mkpath()` removal. A new test in `t/t5533-push-cas.sh` verifies the rejection of invalid refnames. The change is small, targeted, and comes with a clear rationale and test coverage.
