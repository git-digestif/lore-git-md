This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s v2 CI housekeeping series. It switches the `linux32` job from the now-unsupported `i386/ubuntu:20.04` image to `i386/debian:latest`, ensuring 32-bit platform coverage until at least 2030. The change touches both GitHub Actions and GitLab CI workflows, removes obsolete Ubuntu-specific logic from `ci/install-dependencies.sh`, and updates `ci/lib.sh` to recognize the new image format. The patch is purely mechanical and uncontroversial; it maintains the same test coverage while simplifying dependency management.
