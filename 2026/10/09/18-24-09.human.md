This is a documentation patch, part of Julia Evans’s v2 series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page that consolidates Git’s merge-conflict resolution guidance. The patch updates `git-revert.adoc` to link to the new guide, replacing the command’s previous scattered advice with a cross-reference.

Junio’s review raises a minor but valid point: the current wording ("If there have been new commits since the reverted commit, there may be a merge conflict") over-specifies the scenario. Revert operations can trigger conflicts regardless of whether the reverted commit is the tip or buried deeper in history, and the phrasing risks confusing users by implying that conflicts only occur in one specific case. A simpler, more general statement ("Reverting a commit may result in a merge conflict") would be clearer and more accurate.

The rest of the patch is uncontroversial. It follows the series’ established pattern of centralizing conflict-resolution advice while preserving command-specific details, and the mechanical changes (adding the cross-reference and `linkgit:` macro) are correct. No other feedback has targeted this specific change, and the patch is otherwise ready for integration. The fix is trivial and should be addressed in the upcoming v3 revision.
