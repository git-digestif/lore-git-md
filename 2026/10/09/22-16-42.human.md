This is a brief follow-up in the shallow-fetch regression thread. Karthik Nayak confirms Justin Tobler’s explanation of why tag backfilling cannot skip negotiation: nested annotated tags create chains of objects that require fetching intermediate objects to resolve. The protocol requires the client to report all objects it possesses (via the `have` section) to avoid redundant transfers, and since these objects are only known after references are committed to disk, the two-transaction approach in the original patch remains the correct fix. The exchange here closes the conceptual question Patrick Steinhardt raised and reinforces the technical rationale behind the patch. No new code or tests are introduced; this is purely a clarification for the record.
