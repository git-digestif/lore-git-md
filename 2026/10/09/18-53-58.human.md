This is the first patch in Julia Evans’ six-part documentation series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page consolidating Git’s merge-conflict resolution guidance. The patch is part of a well-received effort to address long-standing user pain points: scattered advice, inconsistent terminology, and missing explanations. Julia’s latest reply confirms she will incorporate Junio’s feedback into v3, including adding `git am -3` and `git checkout -m` to the list of conflict-causing commands, defining "original" (common ancestor) upfront, explaining the `|||||||` marker in `diff3` output, acknowledging semantic conflicts, and deprecating `zdiff3`. She also plans to adjust phrasing around conflict-resolution options and improve example consistency.

The patch is **ready for merging** once these refinements are applied. Junio’s review was substantive but constructive, focusing on completeness, clarity, and user experience rather than structural issues. The series has already passed mechanical checks (`make check-docs`), and Junio’s `Reviewed-by:` will apply to the updated patch if his wording suggestions are adopted verbatim. No behavior changes are introduced—this is purely additive documentation.
