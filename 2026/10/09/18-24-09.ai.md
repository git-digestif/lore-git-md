This is a documentation patch in the ongoing `gitmergeconflicts(7)` series. Junio’s reply to the fourth patch in Julia Evans’s v2 series flags a wording issue in the new cross-reference added to `git-revert.adoc`. The current phrasing—"If there have been new commits since the reverted commit, there may be a merge conflict"—is overly specific, implying that conflicts only arise when reverting the tip commit. Junio suggests a simpler, more general alternative: "Reverting a commit may result in a merge conflict," which better reflects the reality that conflicts can occur regardless of the commit’s position in history. The change is minor and editorial, with no impact on the patch’s technical correctness or the series’ overall goal of centralizing merge-conflict guidance. The patch remains ready for integration, pending this small wording refinement in the next iteration.
