This is a **substantive review** of Karthik Nayak’s bugfix patch for the shallow-fetch regression. Patrick Steinhardt engages directly with the root cause and the proposed solution, raising a conceptual question about whether tag backfilling could skip negotiation entirely (e.g., for nested annotated tags) but ultimately accepts the two-transaction approach as a reasonable trade-off. He also notes a missing benchmark comparison (HEAD~ vs HEAD) to quantify the performance cost of the fix, though he does not insist on it as a blocker. The review confirms the correctness of the transaction-handling logic and suggests a more direct test assertion (verifying no re-fetch of objects rather than counting “have” lines), but does not identify any functional flaws. The patch remains well-motivated, with clear test coverage and a measured performance impact.
