This is a performance optimization patch in the remote subsystem, part of a 15-patch series. The patch replaces linear traversals of ref lists with `strmap` lookups in `match_explicit_refs()`, reducing the time complexity from O(R*N + R*M) to O(N + M + R*rules) when matching R refspecs against N local and M remote refs. Benchmark results show dramatic speedups (up to 79% faster) in scenarios with many refs and multiple refspecs, while cases with few refs remain neutral or show minor overhead.

The change consolidates helper functions introduced earlier in the series, removing now-redundant variants like `match_explicit_lhs_map()` and `match_explicit_lhs_count()`. The core logic remains unchanged, but the ref-matching now uses the `strmap`-backed `count_refspec_match_in_map()` instead of the list-based `count_refspec_match()`. A small but important detail: newly matched destination refs are added to the `dst` strmap so later refspecs can see them, preserving the original behavior.
