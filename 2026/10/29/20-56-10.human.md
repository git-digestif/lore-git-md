This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about the `git notes` race condition fix. Farid Zakaria, the author of the original patch, responds to Junio C Hamano’s proposal for a retry mechanism that would allow non-conflicting concurrent note additions to proceed.

Farid acknowledges the merit of Junio’s suggestion—using Git’s existing merge/rebase machinery to retry failed note updates—but defers implementing it, suggesting Junio or others take it on. He expresses confidence in the current patch as a net-positive interim solution, noting that it already prevents silent data loss, which was the core issue. He also hints at using AI assistance for future work, though this is more of a passing remark than a substantive point.

The key new development here is Farid’s offer to share two additional bugfixes he discovered using the same tool (Rewind, a deterministic VM for exposing concurrency bugs). These are:
1. A race condition in `credential-cache` that leaves a stale daemon and credential behind.
2. An issue where the `fsmonitor` daemon keeps running after its worktree is deleted or moved.

Both patches are described as similar in size and complexity to the notes fix, including test cases. This suggests Farid is shifting focus to these other bugs, leaving the notes retry mechanism as a potential follow-up for someone else. The thread now has a clear fork: the original notes patch remains under review as a safe, immediate fix, while the broader concurrency model discussion awaits a new patch. Meanwhile, two unrelated but similarly motivated bugfixes are now on the table.
