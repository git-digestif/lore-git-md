This is the **final review exchange** in the four-patch bugfix series targeting the ODB transaction layer. Patrick Steinhardt provides **substantive feedback** on the core fix (patch 4/4), focusing on two technical points:

1. **Locking scope**: He observes that the current implementation reads both the source (`src`) and destination (`dst`) loose-object-idx files *under the lock*, which unnecessarily extends the critical section. He suggests moving the `src` read outside the lock to minimize contention, since `src` (the temporary object directory) is not modified by other processes during migration. This is a **minor but meaningful optimization** to reduce lock hold time without sacrificing correctness.

2. **Documentation**: He requests a comment for the new `loose_object_idx_merge()` function in `loose.h`, noting that its purpose (merging two loose-object-idx files during ODB migration) is not self-evident from the signature alone. This is a **surface-level improvement** to aid future maintainers.
