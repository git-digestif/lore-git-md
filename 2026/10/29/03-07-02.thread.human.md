This "What's cooking" thread continues to track the integration status of Git topics for the upcoming release. The broader integration report remains largely unchanged since the last update. Another seventeen new topics are listed, with thirteen now ready for `next` after blanket approvals from Patrick Steinhardt and confirmation from Karthik Nayak on the `kn/fetch-tag-backfill-batch-fix` series. One stalled ref-transaction topic was evicted due to insufficient author understanding, and several others remain stalled or await rerolls. Ongoing efforts like `the_repository` removal, rerere libification, CI security hardening, and unit-test integration continue to progress across multiple topics. The only remaining loose end for this update is the `hn/imap-send-header-fix` series, which is now cleared for integration.
