This is a **review reply** to the first patch in Justin Tobler’s four-part bugfix series for the ODB transaction layer. The patch replaces the indefinite lock timeout for the "loose-object-idx" file with a finite 1000ms timeout, addressing a hang risk if a process dies while holding the lock.

Patrick Steinhardt’s reply is **brief and positive**: he agrees that a finite timeout is a clear improvement over indefinite blocking. He notes that making the timeout configurable would be ideal but accepts deferring that enhancement, calling the current approach "fine for now." This is a **surface-level review**—it engages with the patch’s motivation and scope but does not question the implementation details (e.g., the 1000ms value or the lack of direct test coverage).

The patch itself remains **uncontroversial and ready for integration**. It’s a small, targeted fix that resolves one of Patrick’s earlier blocking concerns, and his feedback signals no further changes are needed. The series as a whole is a high-priority ODB stability fix, and this patch is a necessary foundation for the atomic-write improvements in later patches.
