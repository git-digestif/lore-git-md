This is a refactoring patch, part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. Okhuomon Ajayi sends v2 of their patch to eliminate all remaining uses of the global `the_repository` in `bundle-uri.c`. The patch is mechanical: it updates four functions (`unbundle_from_file`, `fetch_bundle_uri`, `find_temp_filename`, and `copy_uri_to_file`) to use an explicitly passed `struct repository *r` instead of the global, and drops the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` define. The changes are exactly those Patrick Steinhardt suggested in his review of v1, and Okhuomon confirms the patch builds successfully.

The patch touches only `bundle-uri.c` and makes no behavior changes. It updates calls to `get_main_ref_store`, `trace2_region_enter`/`leave`, `odb_mkstemp`, and `copy_file` to use the passed repository parameter. No new symbols are introduced, and no tests are added or modified. The commit message notes one remaining dependency on `the_repository` through `fetch_pack_fsck_objects`, but that is outside the scope of this patch and not a blocker.

This v2 supersedes v1, which was queued in Junio’s `next` branch but will now be dropped in favor of the more complete version. The patch is ready for review and integration; there are no open technical questions or objections. It advances the project-wide goal of eliminating implicit global state and is a clean, uncontroversial step in that effort.
