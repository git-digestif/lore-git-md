This is the first patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s v2 series modernizing `git rerere`’s subcommand handling. It renames the `repo_rerere()` function to the simpler `rerere()`, aligning it with the naming convention of other functions in `rerere.h`. The change is purely mechanical: the function’s signature and behavior remain identical, but its documentation is moved and slightly expanded to clarify its role as the main entry point for recording and reusing conflict resolutions.

The patch touches nine files: `apply.c`, `builtin/am.c`, `builtin/commit.c`, `builtin/merge.c`, `builtin/rerere.c`, `builtin/stash.c`, `rerere.c`, `rerere.h`, and `sequencer.c`. All callers are updated to use the new name. This is a low-risk refactoring that prepares the ground for the subsequent patches in the series, which will introduce Git’s standard `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` mechanism.

The rename addresses Junio C Hamano’s feedback from v1, where he objected to the `repo_` prefix as misleading and suggested the simpler `rerere()` name. The updated documentation in `rerere.h` now explicitly describes the function’s purpose, making the codebase slightly more self-documenting. No behavior changes, new options, or test modifications are introduced in this patch.

This is a routine refactoring step in a larger series that is now uncontested and ready for integration. The patch itself is trivial, but it is a necessary prerequisite for the more substantial changes in later patches. The series as a whole brings `git rerere` in line with Git’s standard subcommand-handling patterns, eliminating custom parsing logic in favor of the established `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` infrastructure.
