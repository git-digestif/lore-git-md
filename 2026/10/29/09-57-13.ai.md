This is a mechanical cleanup that simplifies the codebase by eliminating dead flag-passing logic, making the subsequent subcommand refactoring (patches 5–12) easier to follow.

The change is confined to `builtin/rerere.c` and affects only two call sites: `cmd_rerere_status()` and `cmd_rerere_diff()`. Both subcommands already ignore the autoupdate flag, so the patch has no behavioral impact. The diff is minimal (two lines changed), and the commit message clearly explains the rationale: the flag was forwarded to these subcommands despite being unused, which could confuse future readers. By removing it now, the patch reduces cognitive overhead for the later patches that introduce dedicated subcommand parsers.
