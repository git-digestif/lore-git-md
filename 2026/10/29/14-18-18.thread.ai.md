Core problem or goal
Eliminate Git’s reliance on global state—particularly the `the_repository` global variable and related configuration globals—to facilitate libification (making Git’s core functionality reusable as a library). The proposal continues the ongoing community effort to thread repository instances through call chains, move configuration into repository structures, or pass only needed values rather than relying on globals.

Current status
Under review. Ajayi has already contributed 12 patches to this effort during the Outreachy contribution period:
- 1 patch merged to `master`
- 6 patches in `seen` (some marked “Will merge to 'next'” in Junio’s reports)
These patches remove `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` from files such as `merge.c`, `diffcore-rename.c`, and `bundle-uri.c`, or move repository-specific settings (e.g., `commit_encoding`) into more appropriate structures.
