This is a refactoring series targeting the `git rerere` built-in command. Patrick Steinhardt has posted the second version of his 12-patch series to modernize `git rerere`'s subcommand handling, replacing its custom parsing logic with Git’s standard `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` mechanism. The series is now fully aligned with Junio C Hamano’s feedback: the fallback subcommand is renamed from `rerere_record()` to `rerere()`, and the enum documentation in patch 2 has been extended to cover all four flags. All other patches remain unchanged from v1 and are uncontested.

The changes are mechanical and confined to `builtin/rerere.c`, `rerere.h`, and callers in `apply.c`, `builtin/am.c`, `builtin/commit.c`, `builtin/merge.c`, `builtin/stash.c`, and `sequencer.c`. The man page (`Documentation/git-rerere.adoc`) and test suite (`t/t4200-rerere.sh`) receive minor updates to reflect the new interface. No behavior changes, new functionality, or on-disk format changes are introduced.

This is a well-scoped, low-risk cleanup that brings `git rerere` in line with other multi-subcommand built-ins like `git notes` and `git maintenance`. The series is now effectively ready for integration once Junio picks it up. Reviewers familiar with Git’s command-line parsing or the rerere subsystem may want to verify the mechanical correctness of the patches, but no substantive discussion is expected.
