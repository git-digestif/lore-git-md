This is a refactoring patch in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, specifically addressing feedback on Okhuomon Ajayi’s recent change to `bundle-uri.c`. Patrick Steinhardt reviews the patch and suggests extending it to eliminate the last two remaining uses of `the_repository` in the file, which would allow removing the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` define entirely from this subsystem.

The original patch updated `unbundle_from_file()` and `fetch_bundle_uri()` to use the passed repository parameter instead of the global `the_repository` for ref storage and trace2 regions. Patrick now proposes converting two additional helper functions—`find_temp_filename()` and `copy_uri_to_file()`—to accept a repository parameter, replacing their internal use of `the_repository` with the passed `r`. His attached diff shows the mechanical changes needed: updating function signatures and replacing `the_repository` with `r` in calls to `odb_mkstemp()` and `copy_file()`.

The discussion is constructive and uncontroversial. Patrick’s suggestion is a natural extension of the original patch, addressing the last vestiges of global state in this file. The change remains purely internal plumbing with no behavior or on-disk format impact. The patch is already queued in Junio’s `next` branch, but this feedback could lead to a v2 that completes the cleanup for this subsystem. For readers tracking the `the_repository` removal effort, this is a small but meaningful step forward.
