This thread is about an Outreachy internship proposal from Okhuomon Ajayi to continue the long-running effort to reduce Git's global state—particularly the `the_repository` global variable—as part of Git's libification goals. Ajayi has already made significant contributions to this effort during the Outreachy contribution period, submitting 12 patches that systematically remove `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` or move repository-specific settings into more appropriate structures. One of these patches has already been merged to `master`, and six others are in Junio's `seen` branch, with some marked as "Will merge to 'next'."

The proposal itself is detailed and well-structured, demonstrating a clear understanding of the technical challenges involved. Ajayi outlines three main strategies for reducing global state—threading repositories through call chains, moving configuration into repository structures, or passing only needed values—and applies them methodically to specific subsystems like `merge.c`, `diffcore-rename.c`, and `bundle-uri.c`. The plan includes a realistic timeline with concrete milestones, from finishing current patches to tackling harder cases like `have_git_dir()`. Ajayi also shows strong engagement with the project, having published a "Learn C by reading Git" guide and actively reviewed other applicants' work, which suggests they would be a collaborative contributor.

The proposal is particularly notable for its transparency about past mistakes, such as rushed patches in 2025, and how those were addressed. This level of self-awareness, combined with the quality of the patches already submitted, makes the proposal strong and well-aligned with Git's long-term goals. If accepted, Ajayi's work would directly advance the `the_repository` removal effort, which touches nearly every subsystem in Git and is critical for enabling libification. The thread is currently focused on evaluating the proposal itself, with no objections or competing perspectives raised so far.
