This Outreachy internship proposal to reduce Git's global state for libification has evolved into a well-structured and ambitious plan. Okhuomon Ajayi has significantly expanded the scope after feedback from Christian Couder and Patrick Steinhardt, now including refactoring reusable code from `builtin/` into library helpers—a direction that aligns with Junio's recent discussions about libification. Ajayi's technical preparation is strong, with 18 patches already submitted (1 merged to `master`, 6 in `next`, and 7 in `seen`), all targeting the removal of `the_repository` and related globals.

The revised proposal is methodical, breaking down the work into clear strategies—threading repositories through call chains, moving configuration into repository structures, or passing only needed values—and providing a realistic timeline. Ajayi's transparency about past mistakes and lessons learned, along with their active community engagement (reviewing others' patches, maintaining a "Learn C by reading Git" guide), adds credibility. The main open questions revolve around handling complex globals like `git_log_output_encoding` and `have_git_dir()`, as well as identifying which `builtin/` code is suitable for conversion into library helpers. So far, the feedback from established contributors (Junio, Patrick, Karthik) has been positive, and the proposal appears well-positioned for the internship.
