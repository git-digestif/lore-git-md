The project aims to eliminate reliance on globals like `the_repository` while also refactoring reusable code from `builtin/` into library helpers - a scope expansion that addresses earlier concerns about the proposal's completeness.

Ajayi has already demonstrated strong technical preparation, with 18 patches submitted during the contribution period (2 merged to `master`, 4 in `next`, 6 in `seen`, and 6 awaiting review). The proposal's strength comes from her hands-on experience with the codebase and her transparent approach to learning from past mistakes, such as rushing patches in 2025.
