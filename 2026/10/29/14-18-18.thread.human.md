This Outreachy internship proposal by Okhuomon Ajayi aims to reduce Git’s global state—particularly the `the_repository` global variable—to enable libification. Ajayi has already made progress, contributing 12 patches to this effort during the Outreachy contribution period: one has been merged to `master`, and six are in Junio’s `seen` branch, with some marked for promotion to `next`. The work so far focuses on removing reliance on `the_repository` in files like `merge.c`, `diffcore-rename.c`, and `bundle-uri.c`, and moving repository-specific settings (e.g., `commit_encoding`) into more appropriate structures.

The proposal is under review, and while no technical objections have been raised, Christian Couder’s recent feedback introduces a new consideration: whether the project should also target `builtin/` code, not just library files. Junio’s recent discussion on this topic suggests that even `builtin/` code might benefit from refactoring into reusable library helpers, which could expand the scope of Ajayi’s work. This feedback is substantive but not a rejection—it simply highlights a nuance that may require further discussion or a revised project plan. Ajayi’s progress is otherwise well-regarded, and the patches already in flight remain on track for integration. Open questions include how to handle `git_log_output_encoding` and `have_git_dir()`, but these are technical challenges rather than roadblocks.
