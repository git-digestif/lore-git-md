This Outreachy internship proposal by Okhuomon Ajayi aims to reduce Git’s global state—particularly the `the_repository` global variable—to enable libification. The project builds on an ongoing community effort and has already seen progress, with 12 patches submitted during the Outreachy contribution period: one merged to `master` and six in Junio’s `seen` branch, some marked for promotion to `next`.

The core goal remains removing reliance on globals like `the_repository` and `commit_encoding`, but the scope has expanded following **substantive** feedback from Christian Couder. Initially, the proposal excluded `builtin/` code from refactoring, but Christian argued that even `builtin/` code could benefit from being restructured into reusable library helpers. Ajayi has accepted this suggestion, committing to include `builtin/` refactoring in the project plan. This adjustment addresses the most significant open question in the thread, though technical challenges like handling `git_log_output_encoding` and `have_git_dir()` remain unresolved.

The patches already in flight—targeting files like `merge.c`, `diffcore-rename.c`, and `bundle-uri.c`—are unaffected and continue to progress. No new objections or technical concerns have been raised, and the tone of the discussion remains constructive. The next step is a revised proposal (v2) incorporating the expanded scope. With Christian’s feedback addressed and Junio’s queueing of early patches, the project appears on track for the Outreachy internship.
