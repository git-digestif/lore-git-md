This is a maintainer review of the follow-up patch in Tanga Joris's two-part series fixing Bash completion for `fast-import`. Junio C Hamano proposes a more general solution: instead of adding a special-case check for `--git-completion-helper` in `cmd_fast_import()`, he suggests extending `show_usage_with_options_if_asked()` (the helper function that already handles `-h` and `--help`) to also handle the completion helper flags. His proposed patch moves the logic into `parse-options.c`, making it available to every command that uses `parse-options` without requiring per-command boilerplate.

The change is small (six lines in `parse-options.c`) and eliminates the need for the four-line addition in `builtin/fast-import.c`. Junio reports that the new test in `t9300-fast-import.sh` still passes with this approach, indicating the behavior is preserved. This refactoring would make the fix more maintainable and consistent, as the completion helper logic would be centralized rather than scattered across individual command implementations.
