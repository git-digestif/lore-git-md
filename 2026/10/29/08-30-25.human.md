This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing `--delete-integrated` series for `git branch`. The author, Harald Nordgren, acknowledges two minor usability nits raised by Jasper Mayone in his tested review of v4: the `--delete-integrated` flag is missing from the `git branch -h` usage output, and the error message when both `--delete-merged` and `--delete-integrated` are given is overly verbose. Harald confirms he will address both issues, but the email does not include updated patches or a timeline.

The thread remains focused on polishing the series for integration. The core functionality—splitting strict tip-reachability (`--delete-merged`) from change-based detection of rebased and squashed branches (`--delete-integrated`)—is finalized and confirmed by Junio. The two nits are cosmetic and do not affect correctness or performance; they are the last remaining blockers before the series can be queued. No new technical concerns or design questions are introduced.
