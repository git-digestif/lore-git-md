This is an Outreachy internship proposal for the "Reduce Git's global state to enable Git's libification" project. The author, Okhuomon Ajayi, presents a well-structured, detailed plan to continue the ongoing effort to eliminate Git's reliance on global state (particularly `the_repository` and related globals) to facilitate libification.

The proposal demonstrates strong preparation: Ajayi has already contributed 12 patches to this effort during the Outreachy contribution period, with one merged to `master` and six others in `seen` (some marked "Will merge to 'next'"). These patches systematically remove `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` from files like `merge.c`, `diffcore-rename.c`, and `bundle-uri.c`, or move repository-specific settings (e.g., `commit_encoding`) into more appropriate structures. The work shows clear understanding of the three main strategies for reducing global state: threading repositories through call chains, moving config into repository structures, or passing only needed values.

Ajayi's technical approach is methodical. They analyze specific challenges (like `git_log_output_encoding`, which is set in multiple places and used across subsystems) and propose incremental solutions with clear criteria for choosing strategies. The proposal includes a realistic timeline with concrete milestones, from finishing current patches to tackling harder cases like `have_git_dir()`. Their "Learn C by reading Git" guide (published during the contribution period) suggests deep engagement with Git's codebase.

The proposal stands out for its transparency about past mistakes (e.g., rushed patches in 2025) and how they were addressed, as well as active participation in reviewing other applicants' work. This suggests Ajayi would be a collaborative contributor. The project aligns perfectly with the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort and would directly benefit Git's long-term goal of libification. Given the quality of the patches already submitted and the thoroughness of the plan, this proposal appears strong and well-positioned for the internship.
