This is a maintainer review of the follow-up patch in Tanga Joris's two-part series fixing Bash completion for `fast-import`. Junio C Hamano suggests a more general approach: instead of adding a special-case check for `--git-completion-helper` in `cmd_fast_import()`, the logic should live in `show_usage_with_options_if_asked()` in `parse-options.c`. His proposed patch moves the completion-helper handling into that function, making it available to *all* commands that use `parse-options` without requiring per-command boilerplate.

The change is small (six lines in `parse-options.c`) and eliminates the need for the four-line addition in `builtin/fast-import.c`. Junio reports that the new test in `t9300-fast-import.sh` still passes with this approach, indicating the behavior is preserved. The motivation is architectural: completion-helper support should be a natural part of the `parse-options` API rather than an afterthought bolted onto individual commands. This looks like a cleaner, more maintainable direction for the series. No controversy is apparent; the suggestion is a clear improvement over the original patch.
