This is a **bugfix acknowledgment** for the first patch in the `git repo structure --concern` feature series. The author, Mark C. Chu-Carroll, confirms Junio C Hamano's observation of a copy-paste error in the table-printing logic: the concern level for the largest tree size was incorrectly computed using `objects->largest.commit_size.value` instead of `objects->largest.tree_size.value`. This mechanical mistake would have caused the tree-size concern indicator to reflect commit-size data, misleading users about repository structure issues.

The fix itself is trivial—correcting the field reference—but the error was substantive enough to block the patch from proceeding. Mark's response is brief and to the point, acknowledging the oversight and committing to fix it. No other issues are raised or addressed in this email.

The series as a whole remains under review, with all prior technical feedback from Patrick Steinhardt already incorporated in v4. This specific error was the last known correctness blocker; once fixed, the patch should be ready for maintainer consideration. The feature—adding configurable, empirically derived concern indicators to `git repo structure`—is well-motivated and thoroughly tested, but still awaits a final maintainer verdict.
