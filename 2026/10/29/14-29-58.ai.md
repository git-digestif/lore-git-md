This is the maintainer's merge confirmation for Harald Nordgren's four-patch bugfix series targeting `git imap-send`. Junio C Hamano picks up Patrick Steinhardt's explicit endorsement of the fourth iteration (`v4`) and signals that the series is now queued for integration. The patches address two long-standing interoperability issues: `git imap-send` previously failed when processing patches where the `Subject` header preceded the `Date` header (a valid but non-canonical order produced by tools like `b4`) or when header field names were not lowercase (e.g., `FROM:`). The series also fixes a tunnel-command lifecycle bug and removes a `git-send-email` special case that had become redundant.

The series is now technically complete, with all prior feedback resolved. Patch 1/4 (tunnel process lifecycle fix) was already merged to `next` on 2025-06-10 and was further revised in `v4` to store `imap->tunnel` as an allocated pointer, eliminating edge cases around `finish_command()`. Patches 2/4 (order-agnostic header parsing), 3/4 (removal of the `git-send-email` special case), and 4/4 (case-insensitive header matching) are now explicitly endorsed by Patrick Steinhardt and are ready for integration. The files touched are `imap-send.c` and `t/t9001-send-email.sh`, with expanded test coverage verifying backward compatibility and RFC 5322 compliance. The series is queued in `seen` and is likely to graduate to `next` in the next integration cycle. No further discussion or revisions are expected.
