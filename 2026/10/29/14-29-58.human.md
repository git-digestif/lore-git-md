This is the maintainer's confirmation that the fourth version of Harald Nordgren's four-patch series fixing `git imap-send` is now ready for integration. The series addresses two long-standing interoperability issues: `git imap-send` previously failed when processing patches where the `Subject` header appeared before the `Date` header (a valid but non-canonical order produced by tools like `b4`) or when header field names were not lowercase (e.g., `FROM:` instead of `from:`). It also fixes a tunnel-command lifecycle bug that could leak processes and removes a special-case exemption for `git-send-email` messages that was no longer necessary.

Patch 1/4, which fixes the tunnel process management, was already merged to `next` earlier and has been further refined in this version to store `imap->tunnel` as an allocated pointer, addressing Patrick Steinhardt's defensive coding feedback. The remaining three patches—making header parsing order-agnostic, removing the `git-send-email` special case, and adding case-insensitive header matching—are now explicitly endorsed by Patrick and have no outstanding concerns. The series is technically complete, with expanded test coverage in `t/t9001-send-email.sh` to verify mixed-case headers and the `--imap-sent-folder` workflow.

The changes are minimal and focused, touching only `imap-send.c` and the test suite. The new behavior ensures RFC 5322 compliance and interoperability with `b4` and similar tools, while maintaining backward compatibility. The series is queued in `seen` and likely to graduate to `next` shortly. For anyone using `git imap-send` with patches from `lore.kernel.org` or other sources that produce non-canonical header order, this is a straightforward bugfix worth paying attention to.
