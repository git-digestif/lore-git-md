Thread identity: Bugfix for concurrent signal handling in Git's threading code (`sigchain` subsystem). Series version: v1 (v2 planned). Originating author: ian4hu. Thread type: patch series (bugfix).

Core problem or goal: Fix a race condition where multiple threads may concurrently pop the same signal from Git's `sigchain` stack, leading to undefined behavior or missed signals. The revised design aims to prevent signals from being delivered to worker threads during their creation, while preserving expected `SIGPIPE` behavior for the main thread.

Current status: Under active review. The v1 patch proposed a narrower fix but was revised based on feedback from Phillip Wood. The new approach blocks `SIGINT`, `SIGHUP`, `SIGTERM`, and `SIGQUIT` (but not `SIGPIPE`) during thread creation using a guard pattern, with a `git_pthread_create()` wrapper for single-thread call sites. Key subsystems affected: `preload_index()`, `index-pack`, `pack-objects`, `grep`, `name-hash`, and the read-cache entry loader. Long-running threads like `fsmonitor--daemon` and `simple-ipc` are excluded for now. The author plans to send v2 after incorporating further feedback from Jeff King and Junio Hamano.

Key technical details:
- Files/subsystems touched: `sigchain` subsystem, threading utilities (new `git_pthread_create()` wrapper), and multi-threaded commands (`preload_index.c`, `index-pack.c`, `pack-objects.c`, `grep.c`, `name-hash.c`, `read-cache.c`).
- New symbols: `git_pthread_create()` wrapper function.
- Behavior changes: Signals are blocked during thread creation to prevent delivery to worker threads; `SIGPIPE` is explicitly preserved for the main thread. The `sigaction` handling is made more robust by saving/restoring the entire `struct sigaction` (including `sa_mask` and `sa_flags`).
- Limitations: Threads created by external libraries (e.g., libcurl's threaded resolver) cannot be protected by this mechanism; this will be documented in the cover letter.

Open questions and loose ends:
- Whether the guard pattern is sufficient for all call sites or if additional per-thread blocking is needed.
- Feedback from Jeff King and Junio Hamano on the revised design (v2 not yet posted).
- Potential edge cases in signal masking/restoration, particularly for `sa_mask` and `sa_flags`.
- Documentation of the limitation regarding external library threads.
