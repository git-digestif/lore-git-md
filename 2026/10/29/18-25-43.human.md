This is Junio C Hamano’s reply to Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s design question about the notes concurrency fix. Junio sketches a lighter-weight alternative to the retry loop Haugsbakk proposed: instead of repeatedly checking for conflicts, the losing process could simply merge or rebase its own note commit on top of the winning one. This keeps the notes ref always fast-forwardable and avoids the complexity of a retry loop, while still allowing non-conflicting updates to proceed. The tone is playful (“;-)”) but the suggestion is concrete and directly addresses the trade-off Haugsbakk raised. The thread now has two competing visions for how to handle concurrent note additions: the strict “fail on any conflict” approach in the current patch, and Junio’s more permissive “merge or rebase the loser” alternative.
