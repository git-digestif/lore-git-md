This is a design discussion about fixing a race condition in Git’s `sigchain` subsystem, specifically targeting threaded code. Phillip Wood replies to ian4hu’s proposed redesign, agreeing with the overall approach but suggesting one key change: all signals should be blocked during thread creation, not just SIGINT, SIGHUP, SIGTERM, and SIGQUIT. Phillip’s feedback is brief but clear, endorsing the guard-pattern implementation (blocking signals once per spawn loop rather than per-thread) and the use of `sigaction` with `sa_mask = sigfillset()` to prevent handler interruption.

Phillip’s suggestion to block *all* signals during thread creation is a notable refinement, as it simplifies the design by ensuring no signal handlers are inherited by worker threads.
