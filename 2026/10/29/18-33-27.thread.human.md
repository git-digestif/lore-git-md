This thread is about a small but useful refactoring to the `git check-ref-format` command. The patch, submitted by newcomer Hussain Sitamuwala as part of an Outreachy microproject, converts the command's option parsing from a hand-rolled loop to Git's standard `parse-options` API. The goal is to eliminate duplicated argument-handling logic and align the command with the rest of Git's built-ins—all without changing any existing functionality like normalization, branch-name support, or refname validation.

The change is confined to `builtin/check-ref-format.c` and is minimal: 28 lines added, 17 removed. It replaces the manual `argv` loop with a `struct option` array and updates the positional argument check to match the API's conventions. The patch is self-contained and carries only the author's Signed-off-by, with no prior discussion or review activity visible in this email. As of now, it's waiting for feedback from the community.
