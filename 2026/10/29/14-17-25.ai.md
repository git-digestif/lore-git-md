This is a **contributor housekeeping** follow-up in the bash-completion script thread, specifically a reply to Pablo Sabater’s substantive review of Mirko Faina’s patch. Ben Knoble weighs in on two points.

First, he clarifies the meaning of `__git_log_common_options`: it is not about options that are "commonly typed" but about options that are "common to multiple commands" (i.e., shared across `git log`, `git show-branch`, and similar). This is a terminological note rather than a technical objection, but it helps frame the discussion about where `--graph-indent` and `--no-graph-indent` belong.

Second, he floats a forward-looking idea: instead of manually curating completion lists, Git commands that use `parse-options` could eventually expose their own completion hints, similar to how `--help` already works. This would sidestep the need for conditional logic in the completion script and make the system more self-maintaining. The comment is speculative and does not propose a concrete implementation, but it introduces a potential architectural direction for future work. No objections are raised to the current patch or to Pablo’s suggestions.
