Core problem or goal
Refactor the `git diff-files` built-in command to replace ad-hoc option parsing in `cmd_diff_files()` with Git’s standard `parse-options` API. The change is purely mechanical, preserving existing behavior for `--base`, `--ours`, `--theirs`, and `-q` while improving consistency, help text generation, and shell completion support.
