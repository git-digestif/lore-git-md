This is the second patch in Tanga Joris's two-part series fixing a Bash completion regression for four Git built-in commands (`patch-id`, `diff-pairs`, `fast-import`, and `var`). The patch removes the `NO_PARSEOPT` flag from the `commands[]` table entries for `patch-id`, `diff-pairs`, and `var` in `git.c`, restoring their completion behavior now that they use the `parse-options` API. The `fast-import` case is handled separately in the follow-up patch (already summarized) due to its unique edge case with `--git-completion-helper`.

The change is mechanical and well-justified: each of the three commands was converted to `parse-options` in prior commits (cited in the commit message), but the `NO_PARSEOPT` flag was never removed. The patch aligns the `commands[]` table with the actual implementation, ensuring Bash completion will now offer the correct options for these commands. The diff is minimal—just four flag removals—and the commit message clearly explains the motivation and history.

This patch depends on the follow-up for `fast-import` (which addresses René Scharfe's edge case) but is otherwise independent. No test coverage is added here for the completion behavior of `patch-id`, `diff-pairs`, or `var`, though the new test in `t/t9300-fast-import.sh` for `fast-import` is sufficient for that command. The series as a whole is under review and blocked on Christian Couder’s `cc/early-scan-options` series reaching stability in `next`. The work is clean, focused, and addresses a real user-visible regression.
