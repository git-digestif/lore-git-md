This is a bugfix for `git reflog expire` that corrects a minor but confusing UI inconsistency. The patch ensures that `--dry-run --verbose` consistently reports "would prune" for all reflog entries, whether they are specified via `--all` or by naming individual refs on the command line. The issue was introduced in 2021 when the verbose output logic was moved out of the ref backend, and the fix itself is a one-line change to propagate the `dry_run` flag into the callback struct for named refs.

The patch has already been accepted and is in Junio’s integration pipeline. Patrick Steinhardt’s review here is surface-level, focusing on the test coverage and minor wording in the commit message. The tests added in this patch are thorough, verifying both the correct output wording and that no reflog entries are actually removed during dry runs. The review confirms that the fix is correct and the tests are appropriately defensive.

This is a low-risk, well-motivated fix that addresses a real (if small) usability issue. The discussion has concluded on the immediate fix, and the patch is ready for merging. A follow-up refactoring to consolidate the `--all` and named-refs loops has been proposed but is not yet submitted.
