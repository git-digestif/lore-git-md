This is a test modernization patch. It updates `t/t5402-post-merge-hook.sh` to replace low-level `test -e` and `! test -f` checks with the higher-level `test_path_exists` and `test_path_is_missing` helpers. The change is mechanical and confined to three test assertions in the post-merge hook test script.

The motivation is purely diagnostic: when a test fails, the new helpers will report which path was unexpectedly missing or present, whereas the old `test -e`/`test -f` idioms fail silently. The patch does not alter the logic of the tests, only their error reporting.

This is a first-time contribution from Tilak Kumar, a GSoC participant. The patch is small, well-scoped, and aligns with the ongoing community-wide effort to modernize Git’s test suite by replacing legacy shell patterns with more descriptive helpers. No controversy or follow-up discussion is expected; the change is routine and uncontroversial.
