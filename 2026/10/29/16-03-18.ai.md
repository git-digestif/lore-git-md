This is a design discussion reply in the ongoing effort to fix a race condition in Git’s `sigchain` subsystem. Phillip Wood endorses the proposed redesign: he agrees that all signals (including SIGPIPE) should be blocked during thread creation so that only the main thread handles signals, and he approves the guard-pattern implementation that blocks once per spawn loop rather than per-thread. The conversation is now waiting for feedback from Jeff King (Peff) and Junio before the author proceeds with a v2 patch series.

Key technical details for follow-up: the redesign will block SIGINT, SIGHUP, SIGTERM, SIGQUIT, and SIGPIPE during thread creation in the targeted thread pools (`preload_index()`, `index-pack`, `pack-objects`, `grep`, `name-hash`, and the read-cache entry loader), while leaving `fsmonitor--daemon` and `simple-ipc` threads unchanged. The blocking will use a guard pattern with `sigaction` and `sa_mask = sigfillset()` to prevent handler interruption. The known limitation—threads created by external libraries (e.g., libcurl’s threaded resolver)—will be documented in the cover letter. No objections or alternative approaches have been raised yet.
