Alejandro Colomar acknowledges Junio C Hamano’s explanation of the bisect algorithm’s final-step optimization: when the culprit commit is already at `HEAD`, Git skips moving `HEAD` but still updates `refs/bisect/bad` to point to that commit. This behavior initially surprised Alejandro, who expected every bisect step to reposition `HEAD`, but he confirms it is acceptable since `refs/bisect/bad` reliably holds the culprit hash. The thread remains a discussion of existing behavior, not a patch or feature request.
