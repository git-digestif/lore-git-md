This is the final patch in Taylor Blau’s eight-part v2 series fixing MIDX reachability closure corner cases. The patch addresses a previously unnoticed gap in incremental MIDX writes (both geometric and append-only) where required packs—particularly cruft packs and kept packs—could be omitted from the MIDX chain, breaking bitmap generation. The fix modifies `repack-midx.c` to consult `midx_included_packs()` when building the write step, ensuring that packs not already in the chain (e.g., cruft packs when `repack.midxMustContainCruft` is false, or kept packs needed for reachability) are included. The logic skips packs already present in retained base layers but includes those from a replaced tip. The documentation in `git-repack.adoc` is updated to clarify that incremental MIDX writes now include "newly written packs and any other required packs not already in the chain."

Jeff King’s surface-level review expressed difficulty following the patch’s logic but deferred to the test coverage, noting that the key change—consulting `midx_included_packs()`—appears correct. Taylor Blau’s reply confirms the patch’s intent and acknowledges the complexity of the code, but the patch itself remains uncontroversial. The series as a whole is complete, well-motivated, and ready for integration, with all substantive feedback from v1 addressed. The only remaining open questions are implementation details in earlier patches (data structure choice for `extra_roots` and four minor issues in patches 2/8 and 5/8), none of which block this patch.
