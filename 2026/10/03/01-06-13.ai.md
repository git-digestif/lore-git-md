This is a **substantive follow-up** in the discussion of patch 2/8 of Taylor Blau’s MIDX reachability closure series, focusing on two implementation details in the `pack-objects` traversal logic. Jeff King and Taylor Blau reach a tentative resolution on both points, leaving them as minor cleanup items rather than blockers.

The first issue—comment placement—is acknowledged as awkward but left unchanged. The explanatory comment about deferring tree/tag roots is currently in `read_stdin_packs()`, where the deferred walk happens, rather than in `add_object_entry_from_pack()` or `add_loose_object()`, where the deferral decision is made. Both agree there is no clean solution, and the comment’s location is not a correctness concern.

The second issue—the safety of calling `prepare_revision_walk()` twice on the same `rev_info` struct—is resolved by precedent. Taylor points out that the pattern already exists in `builtin/pack-objects.c::get_object_list()` and `reachable.c::mark_reachable_objects()`, and Jeff accepts that the current implementation is likely safe enough for now. Both acknowledge that the behavior is under-documented and could break if the revision-walk machinery changes, but neither treats it as a blocker.

No new technical objections are raised, and the exchange reinforces that the patch’s core design remains uncontroversial. The unresolved `oidset` vs. `oid_array` debate for `extra_roots` (and its implications for delta compression quality) is still the primary substantive discussion point for this patch.
