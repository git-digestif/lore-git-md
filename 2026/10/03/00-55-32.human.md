This is a **substantive follow-up** in the ongoing review of patch 2/8 in Taylor Blau’s series fixing MIDX reachability closure corner cases. The patch ensures that `git pack-objects --stdin-packs=follow` includes all tree and tag objects reachable from the specified packs, closing a gap that could break MIDX bitmap generation.

Taylor responds to Jeff King’s two technical concerns about the current implementation. First, on comment placement, Taylor acknowledges the awkwardness of having the explanatory comment in `read_stdin_packs()` rather than where the deferral decision is made, but defers to Peff’s judgment on whether to relocate it. Second, on the double `prepare_revision_walk()` call, Taylor confirms the current approach works in practice and points to two existing spots in the codebase (`get_object_list()` and `mark_reachable_objects()`) that already rely on this undocumented behavior. While functional, Taylor concedes the pattern is fragile and could break if the revision-walk machinery changes, labeling it as a potential future cleanup item.

The exchange leaves both issues unresolved but clarifies the trade-offs: the current implementation is correct and consistent with existing code, but could be made more robust. No new objections are raised, and the discussion remains narrowly focused on these two implementation details rather than the patch’s core design. The series as a whole is still on track for integration, with these points likely to be addressed in a final revision or deferred as follow-up work.
