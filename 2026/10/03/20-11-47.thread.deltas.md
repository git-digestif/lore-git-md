## 2026/10/08

- [2026/10/08/22-18-49 by Alejandro Colomar <alx@kernel.org>] Alejandro Colomar posted [RFC v3] git-bisect-rebase, renaming the tool from git-brebase and improving usability for two use cases: moving a single branch and moving a tree of branches.
- [2026/10/08/22-18-49 by Alejandro Colomar <alx@kernel.org>] He implemented --abort/--continue/--quit options that act on the bisect-rebase operation rather than on a single rebase; --continue resumes a tree rebase without respecifying the target and flags, and --quit terminates the operation at the current point after advancing the branch, allowing a switch to descendant branches to minimize conflict resolution.
- [2026/10/08/22-18-49 by Alejandro Colomar <alx@kernel.org>] He provided command-line algorithms for both use cases: a multi-step procedure for moving a tree of branches (using --quit, temporary tags, and git rebase --onto) and a simpler loop for moving a single branch (using --continue).
- [2026/10/08/22-18-49 by Alejandro Colomar <alx@kernel.org>] The script was split into three smaller scripts: git-bisect-rebase as the entry point, with internal helpers git-bisect-rebase--callback and git-bisect-rebase--continue, hosted in the Linux man-pages repository.
