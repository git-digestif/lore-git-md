This is a follow-up to Souma's `git history` signing series, now at v5. The series itself is unchanged from the version previously cooking in `seen`; these two patches are a targeted fix for the sole remaining blocker: the `t9902-completion.sh` regression that surfaced during integration testing.

Patch 1/2 is the replay API plumbing (carried forward verbatim from v4). Patch 2/2 is the main feature patch, now with two additional lines in `t/t9902-completion.sh` that add `--gpg-sign` and the generated `--no-gpg-sign` to the expected completion output for `git history split`. This matches the behavior already established for `git rebase` and `git commit`, and resolves the test failure that was preventing the series from graduating to `next`.

The conceptual trade-off—whether signing rewritten commits that retain the original author identity is desirable or misleading—remains documented but unresolved. Junio and Patrick have both indicated the infrastructure is mechanically sound; the completion fix is the last technical hurdle before the series can move forward.
