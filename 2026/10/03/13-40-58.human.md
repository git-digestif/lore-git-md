This is the main feature patch in Souma’s v5 series teaching `git history` to sign rewritten commits. It adds `--gpg-sign` and `--no-gpg-sign` support to all five subcommands (`drop`, `fixup`, `reword`, `split`, `squash`) and makes them respect the `commit.gpgsign` configuration variable. Command-line options override config, and the last option wins.

The patch touches `builtin/history.c` to thread the signing flag through the commit-creation plumbing (`commit_tree_ext`, `commit_tree_with_edited_message`, `split_commit`, etc.), adds a custom `history_config` callback to read `commit.gpgsign`, and introduces the `OPT_HISTORY_GPG_SIGN` macro for command-line parsing. Documentation is updated to list the new options in each subcommand’s synopsis and to explain their behavior. Five new GPG-gated test scripts (`t3451`–`t3455`) verify that signing works with configuration, command-line overrides, explicit key selection, and the no-new-commit case for `drop`. The patch is part of a series that is now cooking in `seen` and appears ready to graduate to `next`.
