This is the first of two follow-up patches to the `git history` signing series, now at v5. It extends the replay API (`replay.c` and `replay.h`) to allow callers to specify a signing key when creating commits. The change is mechanical: a `sign_commit` field is added to the `replay_revisions_options` struct, and the field is plumbed through the call chain (`pick_regular_commit` → `create_commit` → `commit_tree_extended`). The patch is unchanged from v3 of the original series and is purely infrastructure; no user-visible behavior changes yet. It prepares the ground for the next patch, which will wire up the signing options in `git history` itself. The commit message is concise and the diff is small (14 lines added, 5 removed), touching only the replay internals. No controversy or open questions remain on this piece.
