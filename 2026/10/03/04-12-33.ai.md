This is a documentation thread discussing a minor wording choice in Julia Evans’ `gitmergeconflicts(7)` guide. Junio C Hamano replies to D. Ben Knoble’s endorsement of the term "unstaged" in the new "WHAT IS A MERGE CONFLICT?" section, acknowledging that while "unstaged" can be ambiguous, Knoble’s interpretation (changed but not updated in the index) is reasonable in context. Junio’s concern stems from the verb "to unstage" having multiple potential meanings—reverting to `HEAD` or removing a path from the index entirely—neither of which aligns with how Git handles conflicted paths (which remain staged in three versions). However, he concedes that the term’s ambiguity is less problematic in the guide’s example-driven phrasing, where the intended meaning ("not yet resolved") is clear. This exchange resolves the last open stylistic question about the new section, leaving Julia’s wording intact for the v2 revision. No technical or structural changes are implied; the discussion is purely about readability and precision.
