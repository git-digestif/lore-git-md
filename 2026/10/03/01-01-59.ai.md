This is the final patch in Taylor Blau’s eight-part v2 series fixing MIDX reachability closure corner cases. The patch addresses a previously unnoticed gap in incremental MIDX writes (both geometric and append-only) where required packs—particularly cruft packs and kept packs—could be omitted from the MIDX chain, breaking bitmap generation. The fix modifies `repack-midx.c` to consult `midx_included_packs()` when building the write step, ensuring packs not already in the chain (e.g., cruft packs when `repack.midxMustContainCruft` is false, or kept packs needed for reachability) are included. The logic skips packs already present in retained base layers but includes those from a replaced tip. The documentation in `git-repack.adoc` is updated to clarify that incremental MIDX writes now include "newly written packs and any other required packs not already in the chain."
