This is a **substantive follow-up** in the ongoing review of Taylor Blau’s patch 5/8 in the v2 series fixing MIDX reachability closure corner cases. The email exchanges views on the readability of a conditional in `builtin/repack.c` that controls whether `--keep-pack` arguments are passed to `git pack-objects` during geometric repacks.

Taylor acknowledges the awkwardness of the double-negation in the original conditional (`if (!(geometry.split_factor && !midx_must_contain_cruft))`) but argues that the alternative proposed by Jeff King (`if (!geometry.split_factor || midx_must_contain_cruft)`) is no clearer. He traces the confusion to the naming of the `midx_must_contain_cruft` configuration variable, which defaults to `true` and would be more intuitively named `midx_may_exclude_cruft` (defaulting to `false`). This naming issue, rather than the conditional’s structure, is identified as the root cause of the readability problem. The patch’s core behavior—following kept packs to cruft objects without copying the kept objects themselves—remains uncontroversial and well-motivated, with thorough test coverage. The discussion is narrowly scoped to code clarity and does not challenge the patch’s correctness or design. No resolution is reached, leaving the conditional’s final form as an open implementation detail for future revision.
