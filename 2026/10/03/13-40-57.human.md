This is the first of two follow-up patches to Souma’s `git history` signing series, now at v5. The patch extends the replay API (`replay.c` and `replay.h`) to accept a signing key from callers, plumbing it through `pick_regular_commit` and `create_commit`. No behavior changes yet—this is pure infrastructure so that the next patch can teach `git history` to sign rewritten commits. The diff is small (14 lines added, 5 removed) and mechanical: the new `sign_commit` field in `replay_revisions_options` is passed down the call chain, replacing the hard-coded `NULL` that previously disabled signing. The commit message is clear and concise, and the patch is already cooking in `seen` alongside the rest of the series.
