This email is a continuation of the `git brebase` RFC design discussion. Alejandro Colomar responds to Nico Williams' hypothetical framing by clarifying his own workflow and reinforcing the distinction between `git rebase` and the proposed `git brebase`. He reports using `git brebase` as his default for rebasing but still relying on `git rebase` for fine-grained history editing and conflict resolution within a `git brebase` session. This usage pattern leads him to conclude that the two tools serve fundamentally different purposes: `git rebase` is for precise, manual history manipulation, while `git brebase` automates the incremental resolution of complex rebases. His argument hinges on the claim that the current conflation of the two tools is an accident of history rather than an intentional design choice. The email does not introduce new technical proposals or address Nico’s counterarguments about integration; instead, it deepens the philosophical divide by treating the tools as complementary rather than interchangeable.
