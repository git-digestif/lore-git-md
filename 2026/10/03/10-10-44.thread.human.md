This thread is a user support discussion about finding a stable, version-independent plumbing command to check whether `git bisect` has narrowed the search to exactly one commit—including handling skipped commits. The inquiry was raised by Alejandro Colomar, who initially proposed a pipeline using `git rev-list` and `git for-each-ref` to count remaining candidates by directly inspecting the `bisect/bad`, `bisect/good-*`, and `bisect/skip-*` refs.

D. Ben Knoble refined the plumbing approach, and Kristoffer Haugsbakk later suggested exploring `git rev-list --bisect` (or its variants like `--bisect-all` or `--bisect-vars`) as a more direct plumbing interface for querying the bisect state. However, it remains unclear whether these options reliably handle skipped commits or provide the exact count of remaining candidates. No maintainer or domain expert has yet weighed in to confirm or reject either proposal, so the question remains open. The thread is still focused on identifying a robust, future-proof solution that avoids porcelain commands.
