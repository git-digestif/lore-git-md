This is a refactoring patch in the ongoing effort to clean up the handling of `fetch.followRemoteHEAD`. The author, Colin Hinton, acknowledges Junio C Hamano’s feedback that the current patch (v3) only fixes half the problem—deferring validation in the local config parser (`builtin/fetch.c`) but leaving the remote-side parser (`remote.c`) unchanged. While v3 eliminates spurious warnings for fetches that never consult the setting, the user-facing annoyance remains for remote-side configurations until both parsers are refactored.

Colin plans to post v4 shortly, which will restore a `NEEDSWORK` comment in `remote.c` to highlight the remaining defect in `handle_config()` and add a brief note in `fetch.c` for context. This keeps the patch focused on the local parser while clearly signaling that the remote-side work is still pending. The change is mechanical and low-risk, touching only comments and not altering behavior. If the goal is to merge incremental improvements rather than wait for a complete solution, v4 looks like a reasonable next step.
