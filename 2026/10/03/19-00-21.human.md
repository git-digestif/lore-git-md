This is the latest update in SZEDER Gábor’s **build system** series introducing precompiled headers for `git-compat-util.h`. The patch itself remains unchanged from v2, but Gábor reports an unexpected side effect: the precompiled header alters the value of `__FILE__` in error messages, changing it from `git-compat-util.h` to `./git-compat-util.h`. This affects assertions (e.g., in `regexec_buf()`) and compiler error messages, though the functional behavior remains identical.

The change is minor and only visible in error paths that should rarely trigger (e.g., `BUG()` or `assert()`). Gábor considers it acceptable, noting that `__FILE__` is only used in error contexts where the exact filename is less critical than the caller’s location. The observation is presented as a heads-up rather than a bug, and no follow-up patches are proposed. The series’ core goal—a 35% build-time speedup—remains intact, and this quirk does not undermine the optimization. Reviewers focused on correctness may want to confirm whether the `__FILE__` change could obscure debugging in edge cases, but the patch itself is unlikely to face opposition.
