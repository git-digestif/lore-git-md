This is a follow-up to Souma’s `git history` signing series, now at v5. The series itself is unchanged from v4 except for a two-line addition to `t/t9902-completion.sh` that teaches Git’s completion script to handle the new `--gpg-sign` and `--no-gpg-sign` options for `git history`. This patch addresses the last remaining blocker that Junio identified when he merged the series to `seen`: the completion regression that prevented graduation to `next`. With this fix in place, the series should be ready to move forward.
