This is a **substantive follow-up** in the review of patch 6/8 of Taylor Blau’s MIDX reachability closure series. Jeff King (Peff) acknowledges that Taylor’s explanation clarifies the necessity of the refactoring: the existing code marks *multiple* preferred packs (one per MIDX layer during compaction) and selects the *last* marked entry, making the choice order-dependent. Sorting the string list would preserve the marks but could change which pack is last, breaking the intended behavior. Peff’s initial question about whether the refactoring was strictly necessary is now resolved, and he accepts the design as correct.

The patch itself remains uncontroversial and ready for integration. It introduces a `preferred_pack` field in the `midx_compaction_step` struct to explicitly track the preferred pack, removing dependency on string-list order and enabling future optimizations such as sorting for faster membership checks. The discussion is narrowly scoped to this refactoring and does not affect the rest of the series. No further revisions are expected for this patch.
