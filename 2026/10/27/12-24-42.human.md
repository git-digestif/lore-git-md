This is the third patch in Harald Nordgren’s v12 series implementing the "matching-inspired fetch mode" for Git. The series is now finalized in design and under active review for merging, with Junio C Hamano and Phillip Wood having endorsed the approach.

This patch implements the core logic: when a remote is configured with `remote.<name>.fetchRefmap` but *not* `remote.<name>.fetch`, a refspec-less `git fetch` infers which branches to fetch by looking at local branches whose `@{upstream}` points to that remote. The refmap itself only controls where the fetched refs land, not which branches are selected. The patch adds a new test file (`t/t5586-fetch-refmap.sh`) with thorough coverage of edge cases: branches checked out from one-time fetches, multiple local branches tracking the same upstream, branches tracking a different remote, and prefetch behavior.

The implementation is clean and well-structured, touching `builtin/fetch.c` and `remote.c` to integrate the new logic with the existing refmap machinery. The commit message and documentation updates clearly explain the behavior and its interaction with other configuration keys. This is a high-quality, well-motivated contribution that addresses a real pain point for users of shallow repositories. The series is ready for review and likely to graduate to `master` once any remaining nits are addressed.
