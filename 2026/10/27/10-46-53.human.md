This is the tenth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s twelve-patch series modernizing `git rerere`’s subcommand handling. It converts the "gc" action into a proper subcommand (`cmd_rerere_gc()`) that uses Git’s standard `parse_options()` machinery, matching the pattern already applied to the other rerere actions in earlier patches.

The patch moves the `--skip-locked` option from the top-level command into the new `cmd_rerere_gc()` function, where it belongs, and removes the now-unnecessary check that enforced its use only with "gc". It also drops the redundant `string_list_clear()` call from the top-level function, since the list is now scoped to the gc subcommand. The test suite loses one negative test that verified the old restriction; that check is no longer relevant.

The change is purely mechanical and follows the established refactoring pattern of the series. No behavior changes, no new options, and no on-disk format changes are introduced. The patch is a small, uncontroversial step toward the final goal of switching `git rerere` to `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` in patch 12.
