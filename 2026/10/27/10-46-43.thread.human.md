This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s twelve-patch series that modernized `git rerere`’s subcommand handling by replacing its custom parsing logic with Git’s standard `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` mechanism. The goal was to bring `git rerere` in line with other multi-subcommand built-ins like `git notes` and `git maintenance`, with no new functionality or behavior changes. The series is now complete and merged to `master` as of June 25, 2025.

Key participants included Patrick (the author), Junio C Hamano (the maintainer), Karthik Nayak, and Mark C. Chu-Carroll. Junio’s feedback was decisive, particularly around naming (`rerere()` was accepted as the main entry point) and the final safety check in v4, which added a `BUG()` to catch invalid flag combinations. Karthik suggested minor documentation and code-structure improvements, while Mark’s feedback was surface-level and resolved early.

The refactoring touched `builtin/rerere.c`, `rerere.h`, and the man page, introducing dedicated functions for each subcommand ("forget", "clear", "status", "remaining", "diff", "gc") and a fallback `cmd_rerere_perform()`. The three-state autoupdate flag system was preserved, and the series also removed the global `the_repository` variable and redundant CLI flags. The final patch (12/12) added per-subcommand usage strings to the man page and C code, making the new structure fully discoverable. All prior feedback was addressed, and the series is now closed.
