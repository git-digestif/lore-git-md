This thread is about modernizing `git rerere`'s subcommand handling by replacing its custom parsing logic with Git’s standard `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` mechanism. The series, authored by Patrick Steinhardt, was merged to `master` on June 25, 2025, after three iterations (v1–v3). It refactors `builtin/rerere.c` and related files to align `git rerere` with other multi-subcommand built-ins like `git notes` and `git maintenance`, without introducing new functionality, behavior changes, or on-disk format modifications.

The key changes include converting hardcoded subcommand parsing to `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()`, introducing dedicated functions for each subcommand ("forget", "clear", "status", "remaining", "diff", "gc", and the fallback "perform"), and removing dead or redundant flags (e.g., `--[no-]rerere-autoupdate` from "status" and "diff"). The series also adds per-subcommand usage strings to the man page and C code, completing the modernization. All prior feedback was addressed, including Junio C Hamano’s concerns about the fallback subcommand name (`rerere()`) and the documentation of `enum rerere_flags`.

The only remaining loose end is a minor documentation gap in `rerere.h`: the comment for `RERERE_NOAUTOUPDATE` describes its behavior (updates the working tree but not the index) but not its *purpose* or how it differs from omitting `RERERE_AUTOUPDATE`. Junio’s latest email acknowledges this gap, noting that the flag’s role as an explicit override for `rerere.autoUpdate` should be clarified. Patrick has proposed revised wording, but the discussion remains open as a low-stakes polish for future reference. Other participants, including Mark C. Chu-Carroll and Karthik Nayak, raised surface-level concerns or minor suggestions, all of which were resolved in the final version. The series is now complete and merged.
