This thread is about a now-completed refactoring of `git rerere` to use Git’s standard subcommand-handling machinery. The series, authored by Patrick Steinhardt, modernizes the command’s internal structure without changing its behavior or on-disk format. After four iterations addressing feedback from Junio C Hamano, Karthik Nayak, and Mark C. Chu-Carroll, the series was merged to `master` on June 25, 2025.

The refactoring replaces hand-rolled subcommand parsing with the `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` mechanism, bringing `git rerere` in line with other multi-subcommand built-ins like `git notes`. Key changes include removing the global `the_repository` variable, converting flags to enums, and introducing dedicated functions for each subcommand ("forget", "clear", "status", "remaining", "diff", and "gc"). A latent safety issue—where both `RERERE_AUTOUPDATE` and `RERERE_NOAUTOUPDATE` flags could be set simultaneously—was addressed in the final version with a `BUG()` check. The series also removes dead flags and adds per-subcommand usage strings to the man page.

The only remaining loose end is a minor typo in the commit message of patch 2/12 (`s/update/Update`), which Junio has agreed to fix directly in the integration tree. No further changes or discussions are expected.
