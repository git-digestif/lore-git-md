This thread is about a small but important documentation follow-up to the recently merged series that modernized `git rerere`'s subcommand handling. The series itself—a 12-patch refactoring by Patrick Steinhardt—replaced the command's custom subcommand-parsing logic with Git's standard `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` mechanism, bringing it in line with other multi-subcommand built-ins like `git notes` and `git maintenance`. The work was purely internal, with no new functionality or behavior changes, and was merged to `master` on June 25, 2025.

The follow-up focuses on a single gap in the documentation for `enum rerere_flags`, introduced in patch 2 of the series. Junio Hamano pointed out that while the comment for `RERERE_NOAUTOUPDATE` correctly describes what the flag *does* (updates the working tree but not the index), it fails to explain *why* it exists or how it differs from simply omitting `RERERE_AUTOUPDATE`. The distinction is subtle but meaningful: omitting the flag means Git does nothing after recording a conflict resolution, while `RERERE_NOAUTOUPDATE` actively updates the working tree (without touching the index) and prints messages to stderr. Patrick has proposed revised wording to clarify this, framing the flag as an explicit override for `rerere.autoUpdate`, but the discussion remains open on whether this fully addresses Junio's concern.

Since the series is already merged, this exchange is not a blocker but rather a minor polish for future reference. The change would only affect the in-code documentation in `rerere.h` and `builtin/rerere.c`, with no impact on behavior, CLI options, or on-disk formats. Unless Junio signals dissatisfaction with Patrick's proposed wording, this thread is likely to wrap up quietly.
