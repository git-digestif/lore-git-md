This thread is a refactoring series from Patrick Steinhardt to modernize `git rerere`'s subcommand handling by replacing its custom parsing logic with Git’s standard `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` mechanism. The goal is purely internal restructuring—no new functionality, behavior changes, or on-disk format changes—bringing `rerere` in line with other multi-subcommand built-ins like `git notes` and `git maintenance`.

The series, posted as v1 on June 17, 2025, is under review and depends on the already-merged `ps/rerere-remove-global-state` topic, which eliminated the global rerere cache state and made this refactoring possible. All patches except the first are uncontested and ready for integration. The sole remaining open item is the naming of the fallback subcommand: Junio C Hamano has rejected both `rerere_record()` and Patrick’s subsequent proposal `rerere_perform()`, instead suggesting a return to the original pre-2018 name `rerere()`. This change is purely mechanical—renaming the function in `rerere.h` and its callers—with no logic or behavior impact.

The technical work involves converting flags to enums, removing `the_repository` usage, and updating documentation and test suite references. A minor cosmetic adjustment to patch 2—extending the enum’s documentation to cover all four flags—was proposed by Patrick and confirmed by Junio, but this does not affect functionality. The series touches `builtin/rerere.c`, `rerere.h`, and callers in several other files, introducing standard subcommand handling for "forget", "clear", "status", "remaining", "diff", and "gc" while removing redundant global flags. Once the naming question is resolved, the series is expected to proceed to integration.
