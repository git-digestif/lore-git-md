Patrick Steinhardt’s twelve-patch series to modernize `git rerere`’s subcommand handling by replacing its custom parsing logic with Git’s standard `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` mechanism is now under light review. The series, posted on June 17, 2025, is a pure refactoring with no behavior changes, building on the recently merged `ps/rerere-remove-global-state` topic that eliminated the global rerere cache and made this restructuring possible.

So far, only one review comment has surfaced: Mark C. Chu-Carroll suggested a minor cosmetic improvement to the second patch, which converts the existing integer bit-flags into a C `enum rerere_flags`. He proposes adding an explicit `RERE_MERGE_LOCK_TOKEN` enum value to represent the `MERGE_RR.lock` case, rather than documenting it only in a comment. This is a stylistic suggestion aimed at improving the enum’s self-documenting quality, not a functional critique, and Patrick may choose to incorporate it in a future version. Beyond this, the series remains uncontested, with no substantive objections or additional reviews yet. The patches are mechanical, guideline-aligned refactorings, and the only remaining step is for reviewers to verify their correctness.
