This thread is about a 12-patch refactoring series from Patrick Steinhardt that modernizes the `git rerere` command’s subcommand parsing. The goal is to replace its custom logic with Git’s standard `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` mechanism, aligning it with other multi-subcommand commands like `git notes` or `git maintenance`. The series builds on Patrick’s earlier work (merged as `b948c10c8b3`) that removed `rerere`’s global state, making this conversion possible.

The changes are mechanical and focused: functions are renamed, flags are converted to enums, `the_repository` usage is dropped, and each rerere action ("forget", "clear", etc.) becomes a proper subcommand with its own usage string. The work touches `builtin/rerere.c` (the bulk of the changes), `rerere.h`, and callers in several other files, along with minor updates to the man page and test suite. There’s no new functionality—just internal restructuring to improve consistency.

The series is self-contained and uncontroversial, with no open design questions or dependencies beyond the already-merged global-state removal. It’s a low-risk cleanup that should appeal to reviewers familiar with Git’s command-line parsing or the rerere subsystem. No feedback has appeared yet, so the next step is likely review from other contributors.
