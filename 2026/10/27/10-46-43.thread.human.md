This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s 12-patch series that modernized `git rerere`’s subcommand handling by replacing its custom parsing logic with Git’s standard `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` mechanism. The series is now complete and has been merged to `master` as of June 25, 2025. The goal was purely internal refactoring—no new functionality, behavior changes, or on-disk format changes were introduced.

The key technical work involved converting legacy `#define` flags into a proper `enum rerere_flags`, adding per-subcommand usage strings, and introducing a `BUG()` check to catch invalid flag combinations (where both `RERERE_AUTOUPDATE` and `RERERE_NOAUTOUPDATE` were set). The series also clarified the three-state system for autoupdate behavior (follow default, force autoupdate, or force no-autoupdate) and documented the purpose of each flag. All feedback from reviewers—including Junio C Hamano and Karthik Nayak—was addressed, with the final version (v4) resolving a latent safety concern identified after the initial merge.

The series touched `builtin/rerere.c`, `rerere.h`, and `Documentation/git-rerere.adoc`, along with callers in several other files. No open questions remain, and the work is self-contained, with its only dependency (`ps/rerere-remove-global-state`) already merged. The refactoring brings `git rerere` in line with other multi-subcommand built-ins like `git notes` and `git maintenance`.
