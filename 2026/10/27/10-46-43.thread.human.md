This thread is a refactoring series from Patrick Steinhardt to modernize `git rerere`'s subcommand handling, replacing its custom parsing logic with Git’s standard `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` mechanism. The series is purely internal—no new features, behavior changes, or on-disk format changes—and depends on Patrick’s earlier `ps/rerere-remove-global-state` topic, which was merged in late May.

The v1 patches were posted on June 17 and have been uncontested except for one open naming discussion: Junio C Hamano objected to the proposed `rerere_record()` name for the fallback subcommand, arguing it was too narrow for a function that both records and resolves conflicts. Patrick has since proposed `rerere_perform()` as a replacement, citing the man page’s phrasing, but Junio hasn’t yet responded. All other patches—including the flag-to-enum conversion, `the_repository` removal, and subcommand extractions—are ready for integration once the naming is settled.

Patrick’s latest email is a minor follow-up to patch 2, proposing to extend the documentation for the new `enum rerere_flags` to cover all values (`RERERE_AUTOUPDATE`, `RERERE_NOAUTOUPDATE`, `RERERE_READONLY`, and `RERERE_NOWAIT`). This is purely cosmetic and doesn’t affect the patch’s correctness. The series remains under review, with the naming discussion as the sole blocker.
