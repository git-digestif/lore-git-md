This thread is a 12-patch refactoring series by Patrick Steinhardt to modernize `git rerere`'s subcommand handling, replacing its custom parsing logic with Git’s standard `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` mechanism. The series is purely internal, with no behavior or on-disk format changes, and builds on the already-merged `ps/rerere-remove-global-state` topic, which eliminated the global rerere cache state.

The first nine patches have already restructured most of the command’s subcommands ("forget", "clear", "status", "remaining", and "diff") into dedicated functions using `parse_options()`. The tenth patch, just posted, applies the same treatment to the "gc" action, extracting it into `cmd_rerere_gc()` and removing a redundant global flag. The final two patches will complete the transition to `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()`. The series is under review but has not yet received feedback; given its mechanical, guideline-aligned nature, substantive objections are unlikely. All changes are confined to `builtin/rerere.c` and related callers, with minor updates to documentation and tests.
