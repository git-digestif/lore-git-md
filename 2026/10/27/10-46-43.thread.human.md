This thread is a refactoring series from Patrick Steinhardt to modernize `git rerere`'s subcommand handling by replacing its custom parsing logic with Git’s standard `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` mechanism. The series is purely internal—no new features, behavior changes, or on-disk format changes—and depends on Patrick’s earlier `ps/rerere-remove-global-state` topic, which was merged earlier this cycle.

The v1 patches were posted on June 17, 2025, and are now under review. All patches except the first are uncontested and ready for integration once the sole remaining issue is resolved: the naming of the fallback subcommand. Junio C Hamano objected to Patrick’s initial choice of `rerere_record()` as too narrow, since the function both records *and* resolves conflicts. Patrick has since proposed `rerere_perform()` as a replacement, citing the man page’s phrasing, but Junio hasn’t yet responded. The rest of the series—including converting flags to an enum, removing `the_repository` usage, extracting subcommands ("forget", "clear", "status", "remaining", "diff", and "gc"), and updating the man page—has received only cosmetic feedback, such as a suggestion to document all enum flags for clarity, which Patrick has incorporated.

The changes touch `builtin/rerere.c`, `rerere.h`, and callers in several other files, but no new CLI options or config keys are introduced. The test suite (`t/t4200-rerere.sh`) has been updated to reflect the removal of an unused global flag. Once the naming discussion concludes, the series should be ready for Junio to queue.
