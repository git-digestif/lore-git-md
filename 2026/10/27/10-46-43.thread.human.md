This thread is a refactoring series by Patrick Steinhardt to modernize `git rerere`'s subcommand handling, replacing its custom parsing logic with Git’s standard `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` mechanism. The series, now at v2, is purely internal—no new functionality, behavior changes, or on-disk format modifications—and depends on the already-merged `ps/rerere-remove-global-state` topic, which eliminated the global rerere cache state.

All feedback from v1 has been addressed, including Junio C Hamano’s requests to rename the fallback subcommand to `rerere()` and expand documentation for the `enum rerere_flags`. The series has received substantive reviews from Junio and Karthik Nayak, with no remaining objections. Karthik’s latest reply is a brief "Looks to be in good shape!"—a surface-level acknowledgment that doesn’t alter the series’ status. The refactoring is uncontested, mechanically correct, and ready for Junio to pick up for integration. Key changes include removing `the_repository` usage, extracting subcommands into dedicated functions, and updating the man page with per-subcommand usage strings.
