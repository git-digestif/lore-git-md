This thread is a 12-patch refactoring series by Patrick Steinhardt to modernize `git rerere`'s subcommand handling, bringing it in line with Git's standard `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` mechanism. The series is now in its second version (v2), posted on June 17, 2025, and addresses all feedback from Junio C Hamano on the initial version. The goal is purely internal restructuring—no new functionality, behavior changes, or on-disk format changes—making `git rerere` consistent with other multi-subcommand built-ins like `git notes` and `git maintenance`.

The series has progressed smoothly, with all substantive feedback resolved. Junio initially objected to the naming of the fallback subcommand in patch 1 but accepted Patrick's revised choice of `rerere()`. Patch 2, which converts flags to enums, was clarified to document all four flags (`RERERE_AUTOUPDATE`, `RERERE_NOAUTOUPDATE`, `RERERE_READONLY`, `RERERE_NOWAIT`), and Junio accepted Patrick's proposed cosmetic follow-up. The latest email covers patch 3, a mechanical refactoring that removes all remaining uses of the `the_repository` global variable from `builtin/rerere.c`, replacing them with an explicit repository pointer. This aligns with the broader `the_repository` removal effort and is uncontested.

The series touches `builtin/rerere.c`, `rerere.h`, and `Documentation/git-rerere.adoc`, along with callers in several other files. It introduces no new CLI options, config keys, or test files, and the test suite (`t/t4200-rerere.sh`) receives only minor updates. With all feedback addressed and no open questions, the series is ready for integration once Junio picks it up. Key participants include Patrick (author), Junio (maintainer), and Mark C. Chu-Carroll (surface-level review in v1). The series depends on the already-merged `ps/rerere-remove-global-state` topic, which made this refactoring possible.
