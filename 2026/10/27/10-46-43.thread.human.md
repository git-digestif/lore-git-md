This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s 12-patch series modernizing `git rerere`’s subcommand handling. The goal is to replace the command’s custom subcommand-parsing logic with Git’s standard `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` mechanism, bringing it in line with other multi-subcommand built-ins like `git notes` and `git maintenance`. The series is purely internal refactoring—no new functionality, behavior changes, or on-disk format changes—and is now in its third version, addressing all prior feedback.

The series has been uncontested and is ready for integration. Key changes include renaming the fallback subcommand to `rerere()` (as Junio C Hamano requested), converting rerere flags to an enum with expanded documentation (addressing Karthik Nayak’s feedback), removing all `the_repository` usage, and extracting each subcommand ("forget", "clear", "status", "remaining", "diff", "gc") into dedicated functions with standard `parse_options()` machinery. The final patch, just posted, adds per-subcommand usage strings to the man page and C code, completing the series. Junio and Karthik have reviewed the changes, and while Karthik suggested minor structural tweaks (e.g., reducing nesting in the subcommand dispatch logic), Patrick declined those in favor of clarity, and no substantive objections remain. The series is now a textbook example of a clean, mechanical refactoring.
