This is a refactoring series targeting the `git rerere` built-in command. Patrick Steinhardt converts the current ad-hoc subcommand parsing in `builtin/rerere.c` to use Git’s standard `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` mechanism, making it behave like other multi-subcommand Git commands.

The series consists of 12 patches. The first three lay groundwork: renaming a helper function, converting a bitfield to an enum, and removing the implicit `the_repository` global. Patches 4–10 turn each of the six existing subcommands (`forget`, `clear`, `status`, `remaining`, `diff`, and `gc`) into proper subcommands with their own `cmd_rerere_<subcommand>` entry points. Patch 11 switches the command-line parser to `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()`, and the final patch adds proper usage strings for each subcommand.

The net effect is internal cleanup rather than user-visible change; the commit message explicitly states that the goal is modernization and consistency. The series is based on the recent `ps/rerere-remove-global-state` topic, indicating that Patrick is systematically removing global state from the rerere subsystem. No new features or bug fixes are introduced, and the test suite (`t4200-rerere.sh`) is only touched to remove now-redundant tests.
