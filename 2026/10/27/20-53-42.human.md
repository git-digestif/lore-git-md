This is a brief but important follow-up in the ongoing RFC thread about `git cat-file --remote-object-info` batching. Junio C Hamano responds to Pablo Sabater’s recent proposal for a server-side OID limit to prevent resource exhaustion. He reassures Pablo that raising such concerns is exactly what review is for—helping the project think through system-wide implications, not just the mechanics of the patch itself.

The email itself doesn’t introduce new technical details or code, but it signals Junio’s openness to the server-side cap idea. By framing Pablo’s "brainstorm" as a legitimate and valuable part of the review process, Junio implicitly endorses further exploration of the proposal. This keeps the door open for the next step: deciding whether to implement the server-side limit, how to advertise it (likely via a new capability during handshake), and what the client’s behavior should be when the user’s batch size exceeds the server’s advertised limit.

For a busy reader, this is a green light: the thread is still active, the design question is now officially on the table, and the next round of discussion or patches will likely focus on the server-side cap. No merge status is implied; this remains an RFC under review.
