This is the first patch in Harald Nordgren’s four-patch bugfix series for `git imap-send`, now in its fourth iteration (`v4`). The patch plugs a long-standing tunnel-command lifecycle issue: when `imap.tunnel` is configured, `git imap-send` starts the tunnel process but never waits for it to exit, leaking both the process and its argument list. The fix is minimal and defensive: the `imap` struct now stores the tunnel as an allocated `struct child_process *` (rather than a stack-allocated local), and `imap_close_server()` calls `finish_command()` followed by `free()` to ensure the tunnel is reaped. The change is uncontroversial and has already been merged to `next` (2025-06-10), but `v4` revises it to address Patrick Steinhardt’s defensive-coding feedback by converting the tunnel to an allocated pointer, eliminating edge cases around `finish_command()` on uninitialized memory. No test sets `imap.tunnel`, so the leak was not caught by the test suite. The patch touches only `imap-send.c` and introduces no new symbols or on-disk format changes. It is a low-risk, well-motivated fix that aligns with Git’s process-management conventions.
