This is the author’s response to the maintainer’s design discussion about the `remote.<name>.refmap` fetch-mode series. Harald Nordgren confirms he will implement the agreed redesign in the next version (v12). Specifically, he will introduce a separate `remote.<name>.fetchRefs` config key to control *which* refs are fetched, while `remote.<name>.refmap` reverts to its original role of specifying *where* fetched refs are stored. He also confirms the series will support the use case Junio described—explicitly listing refs to fetch (e.g., `master next seen`) alongside a configured refmap—with a new test to verify this behavior.

The patch series is now unblocked: the maintainer and key reviewer (Phillip Wood) have endorsed the design direction, and the author has committed to implementing it. The current v11 remains in `next` but will not graduate to `master` until v12 is submitted. This email marks the transition from design discussion to implementation; the next step is Harald’s v12 submission, which should resolve the core inflexibility of the original design. The series targets shallow repositories but has broader implications for fetch configurability.
