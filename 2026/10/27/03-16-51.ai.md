This is the first patch in Pablo Sabater’s RFC series lifting the 10 k OID limit for `git cat-file --remote-object-info`. It is a pure refactoring that extracts transport ownership from `get_remote_info()` into its caller `parse_cmd_remote_object_info()`. The patch also renames the transport variable from `gtransport` to `transport`, moves the smart-options check up to the caller, and changes `get_remote_info()` to die on failure instead of returning an error code. The diff touches only `builtin/cat-file.c` and is a prerequisite for the batching logic that follows in patch 2.
