Core problem or goal
The `@{push}` revision suffix and `git push` behave inconsistently in triangular workflows when `push.default=simple` and `remote.pushDefault` points to a different remote than the branch’s upstream. `git push` correctly pushes to the same-named branch on the push remote, but `@{push}` fails to resolve, causing `git status` to omit the push-branch comparison and display misleading hints. The series aligns `@{push}`’s behavior with `git push` by making it resolve to the same-named branch on the push remote in this scenario, while also improving the readability of the underlying code.
