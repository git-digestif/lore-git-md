This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s v1 series consolidating CI test environment variables. It refactors `ci/lib.sh` to merge three separate `case` statements over the `$jobname` variable into a single one, eliminating 25 lines of redundant code while adding 16 lines of consolidated logic.

The patch is a mechanical cleanup with no functional changes: it moves the `linux-musl-meson` and `linux-feature-testbed` job configurations into the primary `case` block, reducing duplication and making the script easier to maintain. The `linux-musl-meson` job now disables Rust support (`-Drust=disabled`) alongside its existing UTF-8 locale setting, while the `linux-feature-testbed` job retains all its existing test environment overrides (e.g., `GIT_TEST_SPLIT_INDEX=yes`, `GIT_TEST_COMMIT_GRAPH=1`).

This is part of the broader goal of centralizing CI environment variables to ensure consistency across GitHub Actions and GitLab CI workflows. The patch follows the pattern established in earlier patches (e.g., platform-wide rules for `GIT_TEST_USE_SET_E`) and prepares the ground for future job additions by using a single switch instead of scattered conditionals. No objections or feedback have been raised yet, but the change is uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ stated goal of reducing maintenance overhead.
