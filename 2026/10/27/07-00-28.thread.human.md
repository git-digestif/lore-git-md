This thread is a targeted bugfix for a crash in `git pack-objects` when used with `--exclude-promisor-objects-best-effort` in partial clones. The issue surfaces when the revision walk encounters missing objects—such as trees filtered out by `tree:0` or lying beyond a shallow boundary—triggering a `BUG()` assertion in the `is_not_in_promisor_pack_obj()` callback. Patrick Steinhardt, the primary maintainer of the promisor/partial-clone subsystem, authored the fix, which replaces the `BUG()` with a graceful skip of missing objects, aligning with existing behavior elsewhere in the codebase.

The patch is minimal and narrowly scoped, addressing only the crash-prone callback without altering broader promisor-object logic. It traces the regression to two prior commits (from 2024 and 2026) and includes two new test cases in `t/t5616-partial-clone.sh` to verify the fix: one exercises `--unshallow`/`--deepen` with a `tree:0` filter, and another explicitly tests missing-tree scenarios. Both tests confirm repository consistency post-fetch via `git fsck`. The fix is uncontroversial and grounded in the project’s existing assumptions about missing objects in partial clones. No objections or alternative approaches have been raised in the thread so far.
