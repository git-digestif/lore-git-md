This is a CI/build system patch series that has reached its final form. The v4 cover letter and Patrick Steinhardt’s reply confirm the series is now technically complete and ready for integration.

The goal is to reduce CI log noise by making installation failures for Perforce, Git LFS, and JGit fatal only on the platforms that require them (ubuntu-* and macos-*). The preparatory patch (1/2) removes a long-obsolete macOS quarantine-removal step that has been a no-op since 2022, and the main patch (2/2) drops redundant error-handling logic, relying instead on the script’s existing `set -e` setting to abort on the first failed command. Both patches touch only `ci/install-dependencies.sh` and do not alter Git’s behavior—only CI failure handling.

Patrick Steinhardt’s reply is a terse but clear approval: “this version looks good to me.” The series still lacks an explicit Reviewed-by or Acked-by trailer, but Patrick’s endorsement is the strongest signal yet that it is ready to cook. Junio C Hamano had already accepted the preparatory patch in principle and invited a second review; with Patrick’s approval, the series now has the necessary support to move forward. Expect it to appear in `seen` shortly and, if no new issues surface, graduate to `next` within the next integration cycle.
