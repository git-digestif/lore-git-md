This is a review reply from Junio C Hamano on the first patch in Pablo Sabater’s RFC series introducing `git cat-file --remote-object-info` batching. The review is substantive but narrowly focused on code clarity and error messaging.

Junio’s feedback centers on three points. First, he agrees with the core refactoring goal—moving transport ownership from `get_remote_info()` to its caller—to enable connection reuse in later patches. Second, he endorses the variable rename from `gtransport` to `transport`, noting that the original prefix misleadingly suggested a global instance. Third, he questions the new error message `"smart options required"`, calling it cryptic for end users who would not know what went wrong or how to fix it. The review does not engage with the batching logic itself (that comes in patch 2) or the overall design, but the critique of the error message is concrete enough that the author will likely need to revise it before the series can proceed.
