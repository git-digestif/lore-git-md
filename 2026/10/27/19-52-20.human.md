This is a maintainer's reply to Patrick Steinhardt’s twelve-patch series that modernizes `git rerere`’s subcommand handling by switching from custom parsing to Git’s standard `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` mechanism. Junio C Hamano, the project maintainer, writes to acknowledge the series as a whole.

Junio’s tone is positive: he calls the patches “well done” and praises the incidental removal of `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` in `builtin/rerere.c` as a clean, low-effort win. He explicitly notes that he does not consider eliminating `the_repository` a high priority, so the fact that the series achieves it with minimal churn is a bonus rather than a requirement. The message is brief and carries no new technical feedback; it is essentially a maintainer-level thumbs-up that the direction is sound and the execution is tidy.

The only substantive discussion still open in the thread is Junio’s earlier objection to the name `rerere_record()` (and by extension the fallback `cmd_rerere_record()`), which he argues misrepresents the function’s dual role of recording *and* resolving conflicts. That naming question remains unresolved, but this particular email does not revisit it. For now, the series is under review, with Junio’s overall assessment signaling that it is on track for eventual integration.
