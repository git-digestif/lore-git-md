This is a bugfix series targeting the sequencer subsystem. Phillip Wood addresses a reported issue where `REBASE_HEAD` and `AUTO_MERGE` refs are not properly cleaned up after a rebase completes, potentially leaving stale refs behind. The motivation is clear: a user reported `REBASE_HEAD` persisting after a rebase, and Phillip confirmed the same could happen with `AUTO_MERGE`.
