This is a review reply from Junio C Hamano to the first patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 12-patch series modernizing `git rerere`’s subcommand handling. The patch renames `repo_rerere()` to `rerere_record()` for naming consistency, but Junio questions the new name’s accuracy. He argues that "record" is too narrow because the function does more than record—it also resolves conflicts when a recorded resolution exists. He suggests inventing a new verb like "to rerere" or "to perform rerere" to better capture the function’s autonomous behavior. The review is substantive, engaging with the function’s purpose rather than just naming style, and sets up a potential naming discussion for the rest of the series. No alternative implementation is proposed yet, and the thread remains open for Patrick’s response.
