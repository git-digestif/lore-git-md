This is a reply in the ongoing RFC thread about lifting the 10k OID limit in `` `git cat-file --remote-object-info` `` by introducing internal batching. The email is a short, focused response from the author, Pablo Sabater, to Junio C Hamano’s review of the first patch.

Pablo acknowledges Junio’s concern about the error message “smart options required” being too cryptic for end users. He explains that the check exists to fail early when the remote is a bundle (which does not support object-info), and that without it the command would die later with a generic “remote does not support object-info” message. He proposes an alternative wording: “object-info is not supported on bundles.” This keeps the early failure but makes the reason clearer. The change is small and targeted—no other parts of the patch or series are revisited in this reply.
