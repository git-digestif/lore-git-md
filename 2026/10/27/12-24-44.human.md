This is the final patch in Harald Nordgren’s v12 series introducing `remote.<name>.fetchRefmap` for matching-inspired fetch mode. It makes `--limited-fetch` the default when adding a remote to a shallow repository, addressing the original pain point: slow or hanging `git fetch` in shallow clones of repositories with many branches.

The patch is small and focused. It checks `is_repository_shallow(the_repository)` in `builtin/remote.c` and, if true and neither `-t/--track` nor `--mirror` is given, sets `--limited-fetch` automatically. The documentation is updated to explain the new default and the `--no-limited-fetch` override. Tests cover the shallow-repository default, the override, and edge cases (explicit `-t`, full repositories).

This is a well-motivated, low-risk change that completes the series. The design is finalized and endorsed by Junio and Phillip Wood; the implementation is clean and thoroughly tested. No blockers remain, and the series is expected to graduate to `master` once any remaining review nits are addressed.
