This is the fourth and final patch in Harald Nordgren’s four-patch bugfix series for `git imap-send`, addressing case-insensitive header matching. The patch replaces the exact-case string matches (`starts_with`) with case-insensitive ones (`istarts_with`) for the `From:`, `Date:`, and `Subject:` headers in `imap-send.c`, ensuring RFC 5322 compliance. It also adds a new test in `t/t9001-send-email.sh` that verifies the fix by sending a message with lowercase header names (`from:`, `date:`, `subject:`) and confirming `git imap-send` processes it successfully.

The change is minimal and targeted: three lines in `imap-send.c` and fifteen lines of test coverage. The test exercises the `--imap-sent-folder` workflow via a fake IMAP server, confirming the fix works end-to-end. This patch directly addresses Patrick Steinhardt’s feedback from earlier iterations, which flagged the lack of test coverage for mixed-case headers. The series is now technically complete, with all prior feedback resolved and no open questions. The patch is ready for integration and should graduate to `next` alongside the rest of the series.
