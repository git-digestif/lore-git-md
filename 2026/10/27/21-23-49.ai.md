This is a **substantive review** of the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s CI/build system series. Jeff King (Peff) engages with the design of the `--feature-testbed` flag introduced in patch 8/8, questioning whether the approach is worth the added complexity. Peff suggests an alternative design: a `FEATURE_TESTBED=1` environment variable that would control both build knobs and test environment variables, avoiding the need for a command-line flag and ensuring all relevant settings are toggled together. His critique targets the patch’s conceptual coherence rather than its implementation details, framing the discussion as a trade-off between developer convenience and code simplicity.
