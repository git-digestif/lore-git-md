This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing review of a bugfix patch targeting the delta compression subsystem. The patch replaces manual integer arithmetic in `diff-delta.c:create_delta_index()` with safe helpers (`st_mult`, `st_add`, `st_add3`) to prevent silent overflows during allocation size calculations. The author, Srujith A. Poojary (an Outreachy contributor), has posted a v6-ready correction to the commit message after Junio C Hamano pointed out a factual error: the commit message incorrectly stated that `(hsize + 1)` was passed to `st_add3()`, when it is actually passed to `st_mult()`. Srujith acknowledges the correction and confirms the fix is ready for the next iteration.

The patch itself remains unchanged; this is purely a documentation fix. The central question—whether the patch should proceed as a precautionary measure despite no observed overflows—is still unresolved and hinges on Junio’s evaluation of the refined justification. The commit message now provides a detailed, step-by-step breakdown of the arithmetic, struct layout, and bounds interaction, including the theoretical risk of overflow under 8-byte struct padding on 32-bit systems. No new technical concerns have been raised in this exchange.
