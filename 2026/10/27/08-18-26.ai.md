This is a CI/build system patch, the sixth in Patrick Steinhardt’s eight-patch series consolidating Git’s CI test environment variables. It restores test coverage for non-Rust builds by adding a `NO_RUST=Yes` flag to the `linux-asan-ubsan` job in `ci/lib.sh`. The change is a direct response to the April 2026 default-enable-Rust commit (32d5b90590), which left Alpine Linux with musl libc as the sole CI job still testing without Rust. The `linux-asan-ubsan` job was chosen because its memory-safety focus makes it a better fit for C-specific checks than Rust builds. The patch is a single-line addition with no functional side effects beyond the intended coverage expansion.
