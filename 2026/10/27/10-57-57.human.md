This is the third and final patch in Phillip Wood’s three-part bugfix series for the sequencer subsystem. It ensures that the `AUTO_MERGE` ref—created by the merge machinery during conflict resolution—is properly cleaned up when a rebase finishes, just as the previous patch did for `REBASE_HEAD`.

The change is small and surgical: `AUTO_MERGE` is now deleted once, at the start of the `pick_commits()` loop, rather than on every iteration. This matches the pattern already established for `REBASE_HEAD` in patch 2 and plugs the last gap that could leave the ref behind after a rebase completes. The commit message explains the logic clearly: the ref only exists if there were conflicts, so it only needs to be removed once, not repeatedly. The accompanying test updates verify that `AUTO_MERGE` is present during conflicts and gone after `--continue`, `--skip`, or `--abort`.

Phillip is a well-known sequencer expert, and the series as a whole is narrowly scoped, well-tested, and uncontroversial. The fix addresses a real user-reported issue (leftover refs confusing tools or users) without changing any on-disk formats or user-visible behavior.
