This is a **design clarification** in the ongoing discussion about the `fetchRefmap` series, now at its twelfth iteration. Phillip Wood and Junio C Hamano are refining the semantics of the new `remote.<name>.fetchRefmap` configuration key, specifically whether forced updates (`+`) should be specified on the "what to fetch" side or the "where to store" side.

The exchange confirms that forced updates (`+`) belong on the refmap side (the "where"), not the `fetchRefmap` side (the "what"). This aligns with the existing refspec design, where `+` controls how fetched refs are stored, not which refs are fetched. Phillip acknowledges the logic but notes a practical inconvenience: if you want to reject non-fast-forward updates for some branches while mapping all refs the same way, you now need two separate refspecs—one with `+` and one without. This is a minor ergonomic trade-off, not a design flaw, and the discussion does not challenge the overall direction of the series.

The thread remains focused on the **v12 implementation**, which is complete and under active review for merging to `master`. No new design questions are raised; this is a clarification of an already-agreed principle. The series is expected to graduate soon once any remaining review feedback is addressed.
