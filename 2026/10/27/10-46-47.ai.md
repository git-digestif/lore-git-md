This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 12-patch refactoring series that modernizes `git rerere`’s subcommand handling. The patch removes the unused `--[no-]rerere-autoupdate` flag from the `status` and `diff` subcommands, which currently ignore it. The change is a preparatory cleanup: it simplifies the code paths for later patches that will convert these subcommands to use Git’s standard `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` mechanism. Only `builtin/rerere.c` is touched, with two lines modified to hard-code `RERERE_READONLY` instead of OR-ing it with the ignored `flags` argument. The patch is mechanical and uncontroversial, aligning with the series’ goal of internal consistency without behavior changes.
