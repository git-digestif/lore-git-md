This is the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 12-patch series modernizing `git rerere`’s subcommand handling. It is a targeted refactoring that removes all remaining uses of the global `the_repository` variable in `builtin/rerere.c` by passing the repository pointer (`repo`) that is already provided to `cmd_rerere()` down to every helper function. The patch is mechanical: thirteen call sites are updated to replace `the_repository` with `repo`, and the `#define USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` is dropped. No behavior changes, new symbols, or test modifications are introduced.

The motivation is straightforward: the command cannot run outside a repository, so the global is unnecessary, and eliminating it is a prerequisite for the later subcommand conversion. The patch is part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort and is uncontroversial; it should interest reviewers who follow that initiative or the rerere subsystem. No open questions or review feedback have surfaced yet.
