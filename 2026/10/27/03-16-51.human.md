This is the first patch in Pablo Sabater’s RFC series lifting the 10 k OID limit for `git cat-file --remote-object-info`. It is a pure refactoring that extracts transport ownership from `get_remote_info()` so the caller (`parse_cmd_remote_object_info()`) can manage the connection across multiple batches in later patches.

The patch moves the transport setup and teardown into the caller, renames the transport variable from `gtransport` to `transport`, and consolidates error handling: `get_remote_info()` now dies on failure instead of returning an error code, and the “smart options required” check is pulled up to the caller with its own error message. The diff touches only `builtin/cat-file.c` and is mechanical in nature—no behavior change, just code motion. It looks clean and is clearly motivated by the needs of the follow-up batching patch.
