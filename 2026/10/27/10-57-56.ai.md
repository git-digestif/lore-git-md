This is the second patch in Phillip Wood’s three-patch series fixing stale `REBASE_HEAD` and `AUTO_MERGE` refs in the sequencer. It removes `REBASE_HEAD` at the end of a rebase by deleting the ref in two places: (1) in `do_commit()` after a successful commit, and (2) at the start of `pick_commits()` before the loop begins, ensuring cleanup even when continuing after conflicts. The patch also updates three existing test scripts (`t3403-rebase-skip.sh`, `t3407-rebase-abort.sh`, `t3418-rebase-continue.sh`) to verify that `REBASE_HEAD` is present during conflicts and absent after the rebase finishes. The changes are small (9 insertions, 3 deletions in `sequencer.c`) and narrowly target the cleanup logic without altering on-disk formats or user-visible behavior. Phillip’s commit message clearly explains the bug’s origin—`REBASE_HEAD` is created during conflict stops but not deleted after the final commit—and cites two user reports, reinforcing the real-world impact. The patch is uncontroversial and appears well-tested, making it a straightforward candidate for merging.
