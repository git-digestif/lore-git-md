This is a documentation discussion in the ongoing thread about Git's terminology for object identifiers. Ben Knoble weighs in on Julia Evans's rejected patch to clarify "object name" vs "object ID" in the glossary. Knoble agrees with Julia's goal of making "object ID" the canonical term for the hash, calling it "simpler" and more intuitive, and notes that he already uses "ID" in his own writing for precision. He suggests that "object name" could be defined as "any of the notations in gitrevisions(1) that resolve to an object ID" rather than leaving it ambiguous, and proposes using "notation" instead of "name" in some contexts. He also points out small wording improvements for the glossary, like mentioning SHA-256 alongside SHA-1. The tone is supportive of Julia's direction but offers constructive tweaks to the approach. No new technical objections are raised; the discussion remains focused on how best to resolve the terminology inconsistency.
