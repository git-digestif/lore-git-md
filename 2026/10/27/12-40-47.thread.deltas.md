# Thread deltas

## 2026/10/27

- [2026/10/27/12-40-47 by Harald Nordgren via GitGitGadget <gitgitgadget@gmail.com>] Harald Nordgren posted a patch to the Makefile exporting `CCACHE_SLOPPINESS += pch_defines,time_macros` for the precompiled header and objects built with it, so ccache can cache those compilations; on his machine a warm-cache `make clean && make -j8` drops from about 100 seconds to under 30, and switching between feature branches builds about 6 times faster (first build of each branch about 25% slower while the cache fills).

- [2026/10/27/20-00-13 by Harald Nordgren <haraldnordgren@gmail.com>] Harald Nordgren agreed putting the export in the two recipes directly could be a good idea, and said Meson is not touched by this patch since his goal is just to speed up his local builds.
