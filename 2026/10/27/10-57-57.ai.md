This is the third and final patch in Phillip Wood's v1 series fixing stale `REBASE_HEAD` and `AUTO_MERGE` refs in the sequencer. It addresses the `AUTO_MERGE` ref, which the merge machinery creates during conflicted picks but which was not being cleaned up after the rebase finished. The fix mirrors the approach taken for `REBASE_HEAD` in patch 2: the ref is now deleted once, at the start of `pick_commits()`, rather than on every loop iteration. The same three test scripts (`t3403-rebase-skip.sh`, `t3407-rebase-abort.sh`, `t3418-rebase-continue.sh`) are extended to verify that `AUTO_MERGE` is present during conflicts and absent after `--continue`, `--skip`, or `--abort`.

The change is minimal (4 insertions, 2 deletions in `sequencer.c`) and uncontroversial: it plugs a small leak of internal state without altering user-visible behavior. The commit message explains the rationale clearly—`AUTO_MERGE` only exists when conflicts occur, so it only needs to be removed once, not repeatedly—and the test updates ensure the fix is exercised in all three rebase exit paths. No new symbols or on-disk formats are introduced. The series as a whole is now complete: patch 1 improved test diagnostics, patch 2 fixed `REBASE_HEAD`, and this patch fixes `AUTO_MERGE`.
