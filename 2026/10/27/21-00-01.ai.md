This is a reply in the ongoing RFC thread introducing `git cat-file --remote-object-info` batching to remove the 10k OID limit. The email is a narrow, substantive follow-up to Junio C Hamano’s review of the first patch, focusing solely on the error message for the "smart options required" condition.

Pablo Sabater acknowledges Junio’s concern that the current message ("smart options required") is cryptic for end users. He proposes an alternative: "object-info is not supported on bundles", which directly explains why the command fails and avoids jargon. The context is that the check only triggers when the remote is a bundle, so the new message would be both accurate and actionable. This change is small but meaningful—it addresses the only concrete critique Junio raised in his review of patch 1, clearing the way for the series to move forward once the wording is updated. No other aspects of the patch or the broader batching design are revisited here.
