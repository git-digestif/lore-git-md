This is a documentation discussion in the ongoing thread about Git's terminology for object identifiers. Ben Knoble weighs in on Julia Evans's rejected patch to make "object ID" the canonical term for the hash identifier in the glossary, while clarifying that "object name" is context-dependent. Knoble largely agrees with Julia's goal of disambiguating the terms but suggests minor refinements to the wording.

He also suggests explicitly mentioning SHA-256 alongside SHA-1 in the glossary definition of "object ID" to account for Git's hash-algorithm agnosticism. Additionally, he wonders whether "object name" could be more precisely defined as "any notation in `gitrevisions(1)` that resolves to an object ID," rather than leaving it as a vague, context-dependent term.

The email is a substantive review of the proposed glossary changes, engaging with the technical and linguistic nuances of the terminology.
