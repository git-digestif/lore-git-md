This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s eight-patch series to clean up Git’s CI infrastructure by consolidating test environment variables across GitHub Actions and GitLab CI. The goal is to reduce duplication, improve maintainability, and ensure consistent test environments—such as default ref storage formats—across platforms. Patches 1 through 7 have gained broad support, with Jeff King calling them "obviously good," and are likely to be merged without further debate. The series also restores test coverage for non-Rust builds, addressing a gap created by Git’s April 2026 default-enable-Rust commit.

The only remaining point of contention is patch 8/8, which introduces a `--feature-testbed` flag to centralize CI environment configuration for local developer use. Jeff King had previously objected to its design as overly complex and inconsistent, particularly because it omits the `OPENSSL_SHA1_UNSAFE` setting, and proposed using a `FEATURE_TESTBED=1` environment variable instead. Junio C Hamano has now endorsed this alternative, calling it "simple" and conceptually coherent, shifting the discussion from a reviewer preference to a maintainer-backed direction. This makes it likely that Patrick will reroll the patch to adopt the environment-variable approach. No other objections or feedback have been raised, and the series remains under review.
