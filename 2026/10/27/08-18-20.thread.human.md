This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch series to clean up Git’s CI infrastructure by consolidating test environment variables across GitHub Actions and GitLab CI. The goal is to reduce duplication, improve maintainability, and ensure consistent test environments, such as aligning with Git’s compiled-in defaults for ref storage format. The series also restores test coverage for non-Rust builds, which was lost after Rust was enabled by default in April 2026.

The patches are uncontroversial and have received strong endorsements from key reviewers: Jeff King called them "obviously good," Toon Claes described them as "noncontroversial and good," and Phillip Wood has now explicitly stated the series is "ready for `next`." The only contentious part of the original series—a proposed `--feature-testbed` command-line flag—was dropped in v2, leaving the remaining changes as straightforward refactoring and consolidation. The series touches CI workflow files, `ci/lib.sh`, and test infrastructure but makes no on-disk format changes.

With all procedural hurdles cleared, the series is now awaiting Junio C Hamano’s pickup for integration testing. The unresolved tension around developer ergonomics versus CI reproducibility (raised by the dropped eighth patch) remains unaddressed, but the immediate discussion is closed. The patches are likely to be merged in their current form.
