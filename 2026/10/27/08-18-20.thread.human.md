This thread is a cleanup series from Patrick Steinhardt aimed at consolidating Git’s CI test environment variables to reduce duplication and improve maintainability across GitHub Actions and GitLab CI. The series, now at its eighth and final patch, introduces a `--feature-testbed` flag to the test suite, allowing developers to locally reproduce the CI environment without manually setting variables. The work is part of an ongoing effort to standardize test environments and addresses a gap in non-Rust build coverage created by the April 2026 default-enable-Rust commit.

Phillip Wood’s review of the final patch was substantive but narrow, focusing on a minor discrepancy: the CI job sets `OPENSSL_SHA1_UNSAFE`, which the new `--feature-testbed` flag omits. He also confirmed the correctness of the shell idiom used to avoid clobbering existing variables. His feedback is constructive and unlikely to require a reroll—just a tweak to the commit message to document the omission. The patch itself is a clean infrastructure improvement, centralizing 11 `GIT_TEST_*` variables into a single flag while preserving existing overrides.

The series is under review with no major objections or requests for changes so far. The refactoring prioritizes resilience to future job additions by using pattern-matching case statements instead of explicit job-name lists, and it aligns test behavior with Git’s compiled-in defaults for ref storage. While the cover letter doesn’t detail specific past bugs or inconsistencies that motivated this work, the changes are mechanical and well-justified as a maintainability win. The next steps are likely minor adjustments to the commit message and continued review of the series as a whole.
