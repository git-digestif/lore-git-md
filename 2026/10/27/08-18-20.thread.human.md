This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s eight-patch series cleaning up Git’s CI infrastructure by consolidating test environment variables across GitHub Actions and GitLab CI. The goal is to reduce duplication, improve maintainability, and ensure consistent test environments, particularly after the April 2026 default-enable-Rust commit introduced gaps in non-Rust test coverage. The series introduces a new `NO_RUST` build variant, centralizes environment variable overrides, and adds a `--feature-testbed` flag to the test suite for local reproduction of CI environments.

The series is under review, with Phillip Wood and Junio C Hamano providing feedback. Phillip called the changes "nice" and highlighted improvements like moving job-dependent variables into `ci/lib.sh` and removing a redundant CI job. Junio suggested a minor shell quoting fix in the final patch. The feedback has been constructive, with only small tweaks expected—primarily documenting an omission in the `--feature-testbed` flag and incorporating Junio’s quoting fix.

The latest development is Jeff King’s (Peff) substantive review of the final patch. He questions the design of the `--feature-testbed` flag, noting it only sets 11 of the 12 environment variables used by the CI job, omitting `OPENSSL_SHA1_UNSAFE`. Peff proposes an alternative: a `FEATURE_TESTBED=1` environment variable to control both build knobs and test variables, avoiding the need for a command-line flag and ensuring consistency. This critique challenges the architectural choice and may prompt a reroll if the alternative gains traction. No other objections or feedback have been raised for the remaining patches.
