This thread is a cleanup series from Patrick Steinhardt aimed at consolidating Git’s CI environment variables across GitHub Actions and GitLab CI. The v2 series consists of seven patches (down from eight in v1) and has broad support from reviewers. The goal is to reduce duplication, improve maintainability, and ensure consistent test environments—for example, by aligning with Git’s compiled-in defaults for ref storage and restoring non-Rust test coverage lost after Rust was enabled by default in April 2026.

The series is now uncontroversial. Jeff King called patches 1–7 "obviously good," while Phillip Wood and Toon Claes described them as "nice" and "noncontroversial." The latest patch (7/7) removes a redundant override of `GIT_TEST_DEFAULT_INITIAL_BRANCH_NAME` in the `linux-feature-testbed` job, as the initial branch name is already controlled by the `WITH_BREAKING_CHANGES` toggle. This aligns with earlier patches that eliminated similar redundancies, such as the now-unnecessary `linux-reftable-sha256` job. The series also introduces a new `NO_RUST` build variant (glibc-based) to restore test coverage for non-Rust builds and centralizes environment variable management in `ci/lib.sh` using pattern-matching rules for future-proofing.

The only design discussion—around a proposed `--feature-testbed` flag for local testing—has been resolved by dropping the patch entirely. Patrick Steinhardt abandoned the idea in favor of the existing `WITH_BREAKING_CHANGES` mechanism, and reviewers (including Junio C Hamano and Jeff King) endorsed this direction. The series is now in the final review phase, awaiting Junio’s confirmation before merging. No further changes are expected, though minor adjustments (e.g., squashing patches) remain possible. The work addresses a gap in CI coverage and sets a cleaner foundation for future job additions.
