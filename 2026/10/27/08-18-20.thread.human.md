This v2 revision of Patrick Steinhardt’s CI cleanup series consolidates test environment variables across GitHub Actions and GitLab CI, reducing duplication and improving maintainability. The series is now down to seven patches after Patrick dropped the controversial eighth patch, which proposed a `--feature-testbed` command-line flag for local re-testing. Reviewers—Jeff King, Junio C Hamano, and Phillip Wood—preferred a `FEATURE_TESTBED=1` environment variable or Makefile flag instead, citing better alignment with existing tooling and CI reproducibility. Patrick abandoned the patch entirely, leaving the core consolidation effort unaffected.

The remaining seven patches are uncontroversial and have received positive feedback from Jeff King ("obviously good"), Phillip Wood ("nice"), and Toon Claes ("noncontroversial and good"). They centralize hardcoded variable overrides in `ci/lib.sh` using pattern matching, eliminate redundant job-specific configurations, and ensure test environments honor Git’s compiled-in defaults, such as the ref storage format. The series also introduces a `NO_RUST` build variant using glibc to restore test coverage for non-Rust builds, addressing a gap created by the April 2026 default-enable-Rust commit. The changes are mechanical but impactful, removing 54 lines of redundant configuration while adding 46 lines of centralized logic, with no on-disk format changes.

The series is now queued for review and likely to be merged as-is, pending Junio C Hamano’s final confirmation. While the unresolved tension between developer ergonomics (quick local re-testing) and CI reproducibility remains, the immediate discussion is closed, and the focus is on the straightforward cleanup patches.
