This is the first patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 12-patch series modernizing `git rerere`’s subcommand handling. It performs a single, mechanical rename: the function `repo_rerere()` becomes `rerere_record()` to align with the naming convention used by every other function in `rerere.h`. The change is purely internal—no behavior, CLI, or on-disk format is altered—and touches nine files where the function is called, including `apply.c`, `builtin/am.c`, `builtin/commit.c`, and `sequencer.c`. The commit message is clear and the diff is minimal, making this a low-risk, high-consistency cleanup that sets the stage for the rest of the series.
