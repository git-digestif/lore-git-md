This is the second patch in Phillip Wood’s three-patch bugfix series for the sequencer. It ensures that the `REBASE_HEAD` ref is deleted at the end of a rebase, not just at the start of each commit pick. The patch moves the cleanup call from the conflict-resolution path (where it was only hit if the last commit had conflicts) to two new places: inside `do_commit()` when a commit succeeds, and at the very beginning of `pick_commits()` so it is also removed when continuing after a conflict. Three existing test scripts are extended to verify that `REBASE_HEAD` is gone after `--continue`, `--skip`, and `--abort`. The change is small (9 insertions, 3 deletions in `sequencer.c`) and uncontroversial—it simply plugs a leak of internal state that could confuse users or tools.
