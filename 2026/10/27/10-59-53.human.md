This is the final review for a four-patch bugfix series targeting `git imap-send`. The series addresses two long-standing interoperability issues: `git imap-send` fails when processing patches where the `Subject` header precedes the `Date` header (a valid but non-canonical order produced by tools like `b4`), and when header field names are not lowercase (e.g., `FROM:`). It also fixes a tunnel-command memory leak and removes a special-case exemption for `git-send-email` messages.

Patrick Steinhardt, one of the most thorough reviewers on the list, has just given his explicit approval to the fourth iteration (`v4`). His earlier feedback focused on defensive coding (now resolved by storing the tunnel as an allocated pointer), documentation clarity (the commit message for the special-case removal now explicitly justifies backward-compatibility implications), and test coverage (a new test verifies case-insensitive header matching with lowercase headers). All of these concerns have been addressed in this version.

The series is now technically complete and ready for integration. The first patch (tunnel process lifecycle fix) was already merged to `next` but has been revised in `v4` to address Patrick’s defensive coding suggestions. The remaining three patches—order-agnostic header parsing, removal of the `git-send-email` special case, and case-insensitive header matching—are uncontroversial and have no open technical concerns. The changes are minimal, focused, and well-tested, with new coverage for mixed-case headers and the `--imap-sent-folder` workflow.

This is a low-risk bugfix with a clear real-world motivation: ensuring interoperability with `b4` and other tools that produce non-canonical email headers.
