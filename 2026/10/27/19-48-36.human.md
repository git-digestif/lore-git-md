This is the first review feedback on Patrick Steinhardt’s 12-patch series modernizing `git rerere`’s subcommand handling. Junio C Hamano responds to the initial patch, which renames `repo_rerere()` to `rerere_record()`.

Junio agrees with the direction—removing the `repo_` prefix is a good step toward eliminating the convention of marking functions that once worked only on `the_repository`. However, he questions the choice of "record" as the new verb, pointing out that `repo_rerere()` is not just about recording: it also resolves conflicts when a recorded resolution exists. He suggests that no single verb perfectly captures this dual behavior and playfully proposes inventing a new verb like "to rerere" or "to perform rerere."

The feedback is substantive but narrowly scoped: it targets the naming choice rather than the mechanical correctness of the patch. No alternative name is firmly endorsed, leaving the door open for Patrick to either defend "record" or propose a different verb in a follow-up. The rest of the series (patches 2–12) remains unreviewed at this point.
