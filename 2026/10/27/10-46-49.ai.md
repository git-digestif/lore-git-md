This is the sixth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 12-patch refactoring series converting `git rerere` to Git’s standard `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` mechanism. It extracts the "clear" action into a dedicated `cmd_rerere_clear()` function that uses `parse_options()` for argument parsing, mirroring the structure already established for the "forget" subcommand in patch 5. The change is confined to `builtin/rerere.c` and is purely mechanical: the existing `rerere_clear()` call is wrapped in a new function, and the top-level dispatch logic is updated to delegate to it. No behavior changes, no new options, and no callers outside the file are affected. The patch is a straightforward, low-risk step in the larger modernization effort.
