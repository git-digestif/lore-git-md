This is a trivial follow-up to the recently merged ODB transaction race-condition fix series. The patch that introduced `write_pack_lockfile()` in `object-file.c` (v4 3/4) uses `unlink()` in its error path, but Karthik Nayak pointed out that `unlink_or_warn()` would be better—it avoids silently failing if the unlink operation itself fails. Justin Tobler agrees and will send a standalone patch to make this change. The fix is purely defensive and does not affect the core logic or correctness of the series, which is already cooking in `next`.
