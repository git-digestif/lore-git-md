This is the third patch in Harald Nordgren’s four-patch bugfix series for `git imap-send`, now in its fourth iteration (`v4`). The series addresses interoperability issues with tools like `b4` that produce non-canonical email header order (e.g., `Subject` before `Date`) and case-insensitive header names (e.g., `FROM:`), while also fixing tunnel-command lifecycle and memory leaks.

This specific patch removes a special-case exemption for messages starting with `From git-send-email`, which was introduced in commit `04133f5bc4` (2025-08-12) to work around `git send-email`’s habit of writing `Subject` before `Date`. With the earlier patches in this series now making header parsing order-agnostic, the exemption is redundant. The patch updates `imap-send.c` to treat all messages uniformly, requiring `From:`, `Date:`, and `Subject:` headers regardless of the `From` line’s content. The commit message explicitly justifies the backward-compatibility implications, noting that RFC 5322 requires `Date` and recommends `Subject`, and that the change aligns with how `imap-send` already handles other messages.

The patch adds a test in `t/t9001-send-email.sh` to verify that `--imap-sent-folder` still works as expected, ensuring the workflow remains functional without the special case. The change is minimal and focused, touching only the counting logic in `count_messages()` and adding test coverage. The series as a whole is now technically complete, with all prior feedback addressed, and this patch is ready for integration. The only potential concern—backward compatibility—is mitigated by the commit message’s rationale and the fact that the new logic still accepts valid messages. The patch looks well-crafted and uncontroversial.
