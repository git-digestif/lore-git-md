This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 12-patch series modernizing `git rerere`’s subcommand handling. The series is a refactoring effort to replace the command’s custom subcommand-parsing logic with Git’s standard `OPT_SUBCOMMAND()` mechanism, aligning it with other multi-subcommand built-ins like `git notes` and `git maintenance`.

This specific patch addresses a minor but unnecessary inconsistency: the `--[no-]rerere-autoupdate` flag is currently passed to `git rerere status` and `git rerere diff`, even though those subcommands ignore it. The flag only affects the default (no-subcommand) behavior of `git rerere`, where it controls whether clean merge resolutions staged in the index are recorded. By removing the flag from the subcommands, the patch simplifies the code and makes future refactorings clearer. The change is purely internal and has no user-visible impact.

The patch is small and mechanical, touching only `builtin/rerere.c` to remove the `flags |` bitwise OR when calling `rerere_cache_setup()` for the `status` and `diff` subcommands. Instead, it hardcodes `RERERE_READONLY`, which is the only flag those subcommands actually need. This is a low-risk cleanup that follows naturally from the series’ goal of standardizing subcommand behavior.
