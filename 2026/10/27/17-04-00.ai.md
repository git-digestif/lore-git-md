This is a **substantive design discussion** in the `fetchRefmap` series thread, now at **v12** and **under active review for merging to `master`**. Phillip Wood and Junio C Hamano are clarifying a **minor but important detail** in the finalized design: where the `+` (forced update) prefix belongs in the new `remote.<name>.fetchRefmap` configuration.

The exchange confirms that **`+` belongs on the refmap side (the "where")**, not the `fetchRefmap` side (the "what"). This aligns with the existing refspec behavior: forced updates only make sense when storing refs, not when deciding which refs to fetch. The discussion also surfaces a **practical inconvenience**: if a user wants to reject non-fast-forward updates for some branches but map all refs the same way, they now need **two separate refspecs** (one with `+` and one without), rather than being able to express this in a single refspec. This is a **low-weight implementation detail**, not a design flaw, and does not block the series from graduating.

The thread remains **focused on the v12 design**, which is **finalized and endorsed by both Junio and Phillip**. The series is **queued in `next` (as v11) but deprecated in favor of v12**, and is **expected to graduate to `master` in the current cycle** once remaining review feedback is addressed.
