This thread is about a bugfix for an inconsistency in Git’s triangular workflow handling. Harald Nordgren identified that when `push.default=simple` and the push remote differs from the upstream remote, `git push` and the `@{push}` revision suffix behave differently: `git push` correctly targets the same-named branch on the push remote, but `@{push}` fails to resolve, causing `git status` to skip the push-branch comparison and show misleading hints. The fix is a small change in `remote.c` that aligns `@{push}`’s resolution logic with `git push` by delegating to `tracking_for_push_dest()` when the remotes differ.
