This thread is about a small but important bugfix to make `@{push}` behave consistently with `git push` in triangular workflows when `push.default=simple`. Harald Nordgren sent a standalone patch that tweaks `remote.c` to align the two behaviors: when the push remote differs from the branch’s upstream, `@{push}` now resolves to the same-named branch on the push remote, just like `git push` does. This fixes a case where `git status` would omit push-branch comparisons and show misleading hints.

The patch is under review and has received a substantive technical review from D. Ben Knoble, who confirmed the inconsistency is real and the fix is correct. Knoble had a minor suggestion about where the new logic should sit in the function but didn’t object to the current placement. Harald acknowledged the feedback but made no changes, leaving the patch as-is. There are no open objections or questions, and the patch is now waiting for either a formal Ack from Knoble or for Junio to pick it up for integration. The change is minimal—just four lines in `remote.c` and a test update in `t/t5516-fetch-push.sh`—and doesn’t touch on-disk formats or introduce new symbols.
