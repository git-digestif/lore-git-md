This is the twelfth and final patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s twelve-patch series modernizing `git rerere`’s subcommand handling. It adds proper, per-subcommand usage strings to the man page and to each of the six subcommand functions (`clear`, `diff`, `forget`, `gc`, `remaining`, `status`) plus the fallback `record` action.

The patch touches two files: `Documentation/git-rerere.adoc` (converting the SYNOPSIS from a single monolithic line to one line per subcommand) and `builtin/rerere.c` (introducing `#define` constants for each usage string and wiring them into the `parse_options()` calls). The diff is purely mechanical: every subcommand now has its own `const char * const rerere_XXX_usage[]` array, and the top-level usage array is updated to list all seven variants.

No new options, flags, or behaviors are introduced; the patch is a documentation and help-text polish that completes the series’ goal of bringing `git rerere` in line with Git’s standard subcommand conventions. The change is uncontroversial and should require only a quick sanity check from reviewers.
