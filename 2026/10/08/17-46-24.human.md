This is a brief follow-up in the `sha1dc-accel` thread, clarifying a licensing detail. D. This thread adds no new information; it simply reflects Knoble’s lingering uncertainty about the MIT-specific case, which Junio had already addressed. The licensing discussion is settled, and the only remaining open question in the series is the provenance clarification Junio requested from Sam Reis.
