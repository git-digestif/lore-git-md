This is a CI/build system discussion that revisits the earlier decision to remove the `linux32` and `linux-TEST-vars` jobs. Patrick Steinhardt pushes back against Junio C Hamano’s proposal to drop both jobs, arguing that i386 coverage (via Debian’s supported images) and the `TEST-vars` job (which has caught regressions in the past) are still valuable. He acknowledges the pragmatic trade-offs but signals willingness to send patches to preserve or adapt the jobs rather than remove them outright. The thread’s status remains open: the Debian 12 update is queued in `seen` and likely to graduate, while the fate of the two Ubuntu-based jobs is now contested.
