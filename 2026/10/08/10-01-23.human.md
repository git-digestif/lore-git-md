This is a CI/build system patch, the fifth in Patrick Steinhardt’s eight-patch series modernizing Git’s CI configurations. It renames the `linux-TEST-vars` job to `linux-exotic` in both GitHub Actions (`.github/workflows/main.yml`) and GitLab CI (`.gitlab-ci.yml`), plus the matching case label in `ci/run-build-and-tests.sh`. The change is purely cosmetic: the job still exercises the same non-default options (`OPENSSL_SHA1_UNSAFE`, `GIT_TEST_SPLIT_INDEX`, `GIT_TEST_FULL_IN_PACK_ARRAY`), but the new name is clearer and avoids the awkward upper-casing. The patch is a straightforward three-line search-and-replace that touches no other logic or behavior.
