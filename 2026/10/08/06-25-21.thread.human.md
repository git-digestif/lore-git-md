This thread is about a small but long-standing bug in `git repack --drop-filtered --dry-run`: the command would incorrectly rebuild packs and delete old ones even when `--dry-run` was specified, violating its documented behavior. The fix arrived in two parts. The first patch (v2) added an early return in `cmd_repack()` to prevent any repository modifications under `--dry-run`; it was reviewed, queued in `next`, and is expected to graduate to `master` shortly. The second patch (v3), just sent, is a follow-up cleanup that removes redundant `!dry_run` checks in two helper functions—checks that became unreachable after the v2 fix. Junio has accepted the v3 patch as-is, calling it “good enough” despite minor nitpicks about the commit message. The series is now complete, with no open questions or objections, and will land in the next integration cycle.
