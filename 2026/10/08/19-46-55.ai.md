This is a surface-level review of the first patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s five-part documentation series converting Git’s `BreakingChanges` document into a proper manpage (`gitbreaking-changes(7)`). The reviewer, D. Ben Knoble, acknowledges the patch’s goal—making the breaking changes document more discoverable via `git help` and `man`—and endorses it in principle. He notes that while the document *is* technically accessible via `git --html-path` (depending on the distribution’s packaging), this method is far less discoverable than a standard manpage, reinforcing the series’ motivation. The comment is purely informational, pointing out an existing but obscure access path without suggesting changes to the patch or series. No technical concerns are raised, and the reviewer explicitly states that a re-roll is unnecessary. The feedback is minor and does not affect the patch’s readiness for integration.
