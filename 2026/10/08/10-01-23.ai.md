This is a CI/build system patch, the fifth in Patrick Steinhardt’s eight-patch series modernizing Git’s CI configurations. It renames the `linux-TEST-vars` job to `linux-exotic` in both GitHub Actions (`.github/workflows/main.yml`) and GitLab CI (`.gitlab-ci.yml`), plus the matching case label in `ci/run-build-and-tests.sh`. The change is purely cosmetic—no logic or behavior is altered—but the new name is clearer: the job exercises Git with non-default options (`OPENSSL_SHA1_UNSAFE`, `GIT_TEST_SPLIT_INDEX`, etc.) that are unusual or “exotic” in normal usage. The patch touches three files, swapping the old name for the new one in each place.
