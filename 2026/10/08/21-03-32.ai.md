This is a **licensing clarification** in the ongoing `sha1dc-accel` thread. Junio C Hamano confirms that Git’s GPL-2-only policy is fully compatible with incorporating MIT-licensed code (such as the existing `sha1dc` submodule and the proposed `sha1dc-accel` subsystem), provided the original license and copyright notices are preserved. The exchange resolves the last policy uncertainty raised by D. Ben Knoble, who had questioned whether the MIT license’s terms could be satisfied within Git’s GPL-2-only codebase. Junio’s response—“the story is the same” as for BSD-licensed code—removes any remaining licensing barrier to the series, leaving only the previously identified **provenance clarification** from Sam Reis (original Rust `sha1dc` crate author) as the sole unresolved blocker. The series itself remains under active review, with all technical objections either addressed or superseded by Johannes Schindelin’s substantive optimization patch and explicit endorsement.
