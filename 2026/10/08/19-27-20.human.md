This is the final patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s five-part documentation series converting Git’s `BreakingChanges` document into a proper manpage (`gitbreaking-changes(7)`). The series is now technically complete and ready for integration after addressing all reviewer feedback.

This patch moves the "Adding new items" discussion—previously placed near the top of the document—to the end. The change reflects the expanded target audience: most readers (end users) care about *what* changes are coming, not *how* to propose new ones. The move was suggested by Patrick Steinhardt and aligns with the series’ goal of improving usability. The diff is minimal (30 lines, 16 insertions, 14 deletions) and purely structural, with no content changes.

The series as a whole is well-motivated and carefully executed. It ensures breaking changes are discoverable via `git help breaking-changes` or `man gitbreaking-changes`, addresses rendering issues with URLs, and clarifies the document’s living-draft status.
