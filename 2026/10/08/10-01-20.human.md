This is a CI/build system patch, part of Patrick Steinhardt’s eight-patch series to tidy up Git’s CI configurations. Patch 2/8 fixes a one-character typo in `ci/run-build-and-tests.sh` that prevented the “fedora-breaking-changes-meson” job from actually enabling breaking changes. The change is trivial (s/musl/meson/) but restores the intended behavior: when the job runs on Fedora with Meson, it now correctly sets `WITH_BREAKING_CHANGES=YesPlease` and passes `-Dbreaking_changes=true` to Meson. No other jobs or files are touched, and there are no user-visible effects—just a quiet reliability improvement for GitLab CI.
