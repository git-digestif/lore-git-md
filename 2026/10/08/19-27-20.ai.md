This is the final patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s five-part v2 series converting Git’s `BreakingChanges` document into the new `gitbreaking-changes(7)` manpage. The series is now technically complete and ready for integration.

This patch moves the “Adding new items” discussion from near the top of the document to the very end. The rationale is that the target audience has expanded beyond developers to include end users, for whom the mechanics of adding entries are less relevant than the actual breaking changes. The change was suggested by Patrick Steinhardt and is a straightforward reorganization: the 14-line discussion block is relocated after the last substantive entry, with no textual edits beyond whitespace. The diff is clean and mechanical.

The series as a whole is documentation-only, touching only `Documentation/gitbreaking-changes.adoc`, the build system files (`Makefile`, `meson.build`), and `command-list.txt`. All review feedback has been addressed, including URL formatting, structural improvements, and the addition of a living-draft admonition. Two merge conflicts with topics already in `master` (`ps/ref-storage-format`) and `seen` (`bc/restrict-hex-to-lowercase`) will require their additions to be moved from `BreakingChanges.adoc` to `gitbreaking-changes.adoc` during integration. No new symbols, CLI options, or on-disk formats are introduced, and no test coverage is needed.
