This is the second patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s five-patch series converting Git’s `BreakingChanges` document into the new `gitbreaking-changes(7)` manpage. The patch performs the actual rename: it moves the content from `Documentation/BreakingChanges.adoc` into the newly created `Documentation/gitbreaking-changes.adoc`, while leaving the original file as a stub that points readers to the new location. The build system (`Makefile`, `meson.build`) and `command-list.txt` are updated to include the new manpage, ensuring it is built and listed in the command reference.

No content changes are made—this is purely a file rename and build-system integration. The patch is mechanical and uncontroversial, addressing the second step in the three-step restructuring plan outlined in the cover letter. Merge conflicts with `ps/ref-storage-format` (in `master`) and `bc/restrict-hex-to-lowercase` (in `seen`) will require their additions to be moved from `BreakingChanges.adoc` to `gitbreaking-changes.adoc` during integration, but those are trivial to resolve. The patch is ready for integration.
