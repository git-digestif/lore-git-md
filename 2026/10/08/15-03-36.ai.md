This is a surface-level review comment on Patrick Steinhardt’s CI/build system patch (4/8 in the series). Todd Zullinger points out a minor factual inaccuracy in the commit message: the phrasing "Ubuntu has dropped support for 32 bit entirely with the 20.04 release" is incorrect, as Ubuntu 20.04 *did* include 32-bit support. The error likely stems from conflating 20.04 with later releases (e.g., 22.04 or 24.04) where 32-bit support was removed. Todd suggests clarifying the wording to avoid implying 20.04 lacked 32-bit support, offering alternatives like "after the 20.04 release" or "in subsequent releases."

The feedback is purely editorial and does not challenge the patch’s technical substance (switching from `i386/ubuntu:20.04` to `i386/debian:latest`). The change itself remains uncontroversial, as the Debian image is a well-motivated replacement for the now-EOL Ubuntu image. Expect Patrick to address the wording in a future revision, but this is unlikely to generate further discussion.
