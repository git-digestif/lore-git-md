This is a design discussion update in the ongoing `git stash create` feature thread. Kazumasa Shigeta responds to Junio C Hamano’s feedback, refining the proposed approach for exposing more of `do_create_stash()`’s capabilities while preserving backward compatibility.

Shigeta now proposes incrementally extending `git stash create` with a minimal subset of options (`-m/--message`, `-q/--quiet`, `-u/--include-untracked`, and `-a/--all`) rather than pursuing a new subcommand or adding `--create-only` to `git stash push`. The rationale is that `stash create` is already script-oriented, and adding option parsing now establishes a foundation for future script-friendly options without revisiting backward-compatibility concerns. The proposed subset avoids options with complex interactions (`--patch`, `--staged`, pathspecs) or unclear use cases, deferring those decisions until concrete needs arise. Shigeta also confirms that `-q/--quiet` would suppress non-error output (e.g., progress messages) while preserving the stash object name on success, aligning with script expectations.

The email clarifies the author’s preference for extending `stash create` over Junio’s "safest" suggestion of `--create-only` in `stash push`, arguing that the latter would complicate future option additions by requiring decisions about whether each new option applies to `push`, `--create-only`, or both. The discussion remains focused on design trade-offs, with no technical objections raised to the proposed subset. Key unresolved questions include whether Junio and Phillip Wood will accept the narrowed scope and whether the exit status mapping (1 → 0) fully addresses backward-compatibility risks. The next step is likely a revised patch or further feedback on the proposed subset.
