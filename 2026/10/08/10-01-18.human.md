This is a CI/build system patch series from Patrick Steinhardt, consisting of eight small housekeeping and modernization changes to Git's continuous integration configurations. The series addresses a mix of issues: skipping a SHA-1-specific test in SHA-256 repositories, fixing a broken Fedora Meson job, removing unused or redundant jobs (including Python 2 coverage and a duplicate `linux-reftable` job), and updating CI jobs that rely on end-of-life Docker images (i386/ubuntu and an older Ubuntu version in the `linux-exotic` job).

The motivation stems from a recent mailing list discussion (linked in the cover letter) about CI maintenance. Patrick notes that the series includes a merge of his earlier `ps/meson-improvements` branch to ensure GitLab CI passes, though that branch isn’t a strict dependency. Test runs for both GitHub Actions and GitLab CI are provided, showing the changes work as intended. The series touches `.github/workflows/main.yml`, `.gitlab-ci.yml`, and a handful of CI helper scripts, with a net reduction of about 30 lines of code.

The patches are narrowly scoped and appear well-motivated: each one either removes dead code, fixes a broken job, or updates an EOL dependency. There’s no new functionality or controversial changes here—just routine maintenance to keep CI reliable and up-to-date. For anyone following Git’s CI health, this series is worth a quick skim to see what’s being cleaned up, but it’s unlikely to spark extended discussion.
