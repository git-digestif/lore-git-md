This is the second patch in Maciej Ciemborowicz’s four-patch series fixing the `reference-transaction` hook’s omission of new refs during branch rename/copy operations. It introduces `ref_transaction_replace_reflog()`, a new API that lets callers replace a ref’s reflog history with explicit entries in the same transaction—without changing the ref itself. The patch keeps the `REF_REPLACE_REFLOG` flag private to prevent accidental misuse and ensures both the files-based and reftable backends handle replacements correctly: files stages logs in temporary files and installs them atomically during commit, while reftable tombstones old entries in the same table. Tests verify null-OID preservation, entry ordering, rollback safety, and duplicate rejection.

The patch is a focused refactoring that lays the groundwork for the heavier lifting in patch 3/4 (copy/rename integration). It adds minimal overhead and is uncontroversial in isolation, though the series’ overall performance regressions (~137% for files-based rename with large reflogs) remain a trade-off for correctness. No test breakages or review feedback have surfaced yet; expect this to proceed smoothly toward `next`.
