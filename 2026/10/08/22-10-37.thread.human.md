This thread is about a three-patch bugfix series (v5) by Karthik Nayak that fixes a performance regression in shallow fetches when backfilling tags. The regression was introduced by a May 2025 change to batched reference updates, which delayed committing references until after tag backfilling, causing the client to report no "have" references during negotiation and forcing the server to send all objects. The fix exposes pending transaction state via the transport’s `negotiation_include_tips` field, restoring correct "have" reporting while preserving atomicity for `--atomic` fetches.

The series is now ready for maintainer review after resolving a memory management blocker in the first patch, which introduced `transport_options_release()` to ensure proper cleanup for all transport types. The core fix (patch 2/3) implements the pending-transaction "haves" design in `builtin/fetch.c` and adds tests to verify "have" reporting during tag backfilling for both atomic and non-atomic flows. The third patch ensures upstream tracking information persists after rejected updates. Patrick Steinhardt, who proposed the design, has reviewed the series and provided only surface-level feedback (commit message wording and commit ordering), which Karthik has addressed or plans to address locally without a re-roll. Justin Tobler, who previously raised substantive questions, now endorses the design. Junio C Hamano’s review is the only remaining blocker before the series can move to `next`. No technical objections remain.
