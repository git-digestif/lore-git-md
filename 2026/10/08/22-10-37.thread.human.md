This thread is about a bugfix patch (v2) by Karthik Nayak that addresses a performance regression in shallow fetches when backfilling tags. The regression was introduced by a May 2025 change that batched reference updates, delaying reference commits until after tag backfilling. This caused shallow clients to report no 'have' references during negotiation, forcing servers to send all objects.

The fix splits the reference transaction into two phases—committing fetched references before backfilling tags—restoring correct negotiation behavior while keeping most of the performance gains. The patch touches `builtin/fetch.c` and includes a test in `t/t5510-fetch.sh` that directly verifies no objects are refetched beyond the shallow depth. Benchmarks show the fix is 17 times faster than the pre-batched-update behavior, with only a 1% slowdown compared to the single-transaction approach.

Reviewers Patrick Steinhardt and Justin Tobler confirmed the two-transaction approach as the correct fix after ruling out simpler alternatives due to protocol constraints. All feedback has been addressed, and the patch is now ready for integration. No open questions remain.
