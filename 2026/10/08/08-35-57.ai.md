This is patch 7/13 of Patrick Steinhardt’s ODB alternates refactoring series, targeting the `tmp-objdir` subsystem. The patch absorbs the temporary-primary-source logic into `tmp-objdir.c`, removing `odb_set_temporary_primary_source()` and `odb_restore_primary_source()` from the public ODB API. The functionality is reimplemented as static helpers in `tmp-objdir.c`, which now directly manipulates the ODB’s source list and `disable_ref_updates` flag.

The change is a straightforward refactoring with no behavior differences. It deletes 62 lines (45 from `odb.c`, 17 from `odb.h`) and adds 34 lines to `tmp-objdir.c`, introducing a new static helper `tmp_objdir_restore_source()`. The patch is preparatory for later work (patches 8/13 and 12/13) that will adapt the quarantine logic to use `struct odb_files_dir` instead of `struct odb_source`. Karthik Nayak’s review of this patch was surface-level, focusing on mechanical correctness rather than edge cases like linked-list invariants or promisor remotes.

This is a clean, well-contained step in the series. The commit message clearly explains the motivation (alternates becoming a files-backend implementation detail) and the mechanical changes. No new symbols are introduced, and the patch does not affect on-disk format or test coverage. If you’re tracking the ODB refactoring, this patch is worth noting for its role in consolidating `tmp-objdir` logic, but it’s not a standalone point of interest.
