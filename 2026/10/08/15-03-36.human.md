This is a surface-level review comment on the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s eight-patch CI/build system series. Todd Zullinger points out a minor factual inaccuracy in the commit message: the commit claims Ubuntu dropped 32-bit support with the 20.04 release, but in reality 20.04 still included 32-bit images, and support was removed in a later release (likely 22.04). Todd suggests rewording the sentence to avoid specifying a version number, or clarifying that support was dropped *after* 20.04.

The comment is purely editorial and does not challenge the patch’s substance. The patch itself—switching the `linux32` job from `i386/ubuntu:20.04` to `i386/debian:latest`—remains uncontroversial and well-motivated. This is a routine nitpick about phrasing, not a technical objection.
