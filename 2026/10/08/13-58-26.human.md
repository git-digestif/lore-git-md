This is a design discussion in the ongoing effort to extend `git stash create` with `--include-untracked` (`-u`) and `--all` (`-a`) options. The author, Kazumasa Shigeta, responds to Junio C Hamano’s feedback and refines the scope of the proposed changes.

Shigeta now favors incrementally extending `git stash create` directly, rather than adding a new subcommand or introducing `--create-only` to `git stash push`. He argues that `stash create` is already script-oriented, and extending it with a minimal subset of options (`-m/--message`, `-q/--quiet`, `-u`, and `-a`) would establish a foundation for future script-oriented features without revisiting backward-compatibility concerns. The untracked modes (`-u` and `-a`) address the original gap, while `-m` and `-q` are justified as useful for scripts. He explicitly rules out `--patch`, pathspec support, and `--staged` for now, citing unclear interaction rules and lack of concrete demand.

The discussion remains focused on design trade-offs: whether to extend `stash create`, add a new subcommand, or reuse `stash push` with `--create-only`. Shigeta’s preference for extending `stash create` is motivated by long-term maintainability, but Junio previously described `--create-only` as "the safest" approach. The thread is still in the design phase, with no revised patch yet. The next step will likely be further feedback or a patch implementing the narrowed scope. The proposal looks well-motivated and grounded in script use cases, but the final direction will depend on consensus around backward-compatibility and interface design.
