This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 13-part refactoring series that moves alternates handling into the "files" ODB backend. The patch itself is trivial: it removes the now-unused `read_alternates` callback from the ODB source interface and all backend implementations (`files`, `inmemory`, `loose`, `packed`), deleting 55 lines across five files.

The change is the logical conclusion of the series’ architectural shift. Alternates are now managed exclusively by the files backend, so the callback is no longer needed. The patch is purely mechanical and introduces no behavior changes—it just cleans up the interface after the real work was done in earlier patches (notably 12/13, which relocated alternates handling into `odb/source-files.c`).

This is a well-crafted series with a clear goal and thorough commit messages. The final patch is a tidy capstone, but the real value was in the preparatory work that made this cleanup possible. If you care about ODB internals or future pluggable backends, the series is worth reviewing as a whole—this patch alone won’t tell you much. The series is now ready for integration, pending resolution of the minor merge conflicts with `seen` noted in the cover letter.
