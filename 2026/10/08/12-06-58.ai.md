This is a refactoring patch in the RFC series introducing "precious files" to Git. It moves the `PATTERN_FLAG_*` macros from `dir.h` into a new `enum pattern_flags` in `attr.h`, where the `struct pattern` they describe is defined. The patch updates the type of the `flags` field in `struct pattern` from `unsigned` to `enum pattern_flags` and adjusts the signatures of `parse_path_pattern()` and `add_pattern()` accordingly. It also adds `#include "attr.h"` to three files (`builtin/check-ignore.c`, `builtin/sparse-checkout.c`, and `dir.c`) that now reference the enum.

The change is purely mechanical and preparatory for the later patches in the series, which will add the `PATTERN_TYPE_PRECIOUS` value to the enum. The patch is small (15 insertions, 9 deletions) and touches only the pattern-handling machinery, making no functional changes. It is clearly well-scoped and uncontroversial as a refactoring step, though its value depends on the eventual acceptance of the precious-files feature. The commit message is concise and accurate, and the diff is clean. No test failures or edge cases are introduced at this stage.
