This is the **v3 cover letter** for a **bugfix series** targeting a data-loss race in `git repack -d`. The series is now down to five patches after the author withdrew the first patch in favor of Justin Tobler’s ODB-focused series, which will handle `.keep` file ownership more cleanly.

The core issue remains: a timing window between when `repack` scans for `.keep` files and when `pack-objects` (spawned with `--honor-pack-keep`) does its own scan. If a concurrent push or fetch finishes migrating its quarantine during this window—linking a `.keep` onto a pack that `repack` has already decided to delete—`pack-objects` will skip the pack’s objects, but `repack` will still delete the pack, leaving dangling refs. The fix (patch 5/5) eliminates the race by passing `pack-objects` a snapshot of the `.keep` packs observed at repack startup, replacing the mid-repack directory scan.

- **Patch 2/5** (the cruft-pack walk fix) now moves the kept-pack cache invalidation logic from `builtin/pack-objects.c` to `packfile.c`, grouping it with existing downcasts.
