This is the third patch in Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s five-part series converting Git’s `BreakingChanges` document into a proper manpage (`gitbreaking-changes(7)`). The series is now technically complete and ready for integration after addressing all reviewer feedback.

This patch replaces the commit-message-style message-IDs (e.g., `<xmqqa59i45wc.fsf@gitster.g>`) with full clickable URLs (e.g., `https://lore.kernel.org/git/xmqqa59i45wc.fsf@gitster.g/`). The change improves usability for end users, who can now jump directly to discussions without manually constructing URLs. The author encountered rendering issues with `asciidoc` and `asciidoctor` for certain message-IDs, requiring URL encoding or fallback links to replies. For example, one problematic message-ID was replaced with a link to its second-level reply to ensure compatibility.

The patch touches only `Documentation/gitbreaking-changes.adoc`, updating 17 references across the document. No other files or build system changes are needed here. The interdiff shows minor adjustments to whitespace and alignment, but these do not affect the rendered output. The series remains documentation-only, with no new symbols, CLI options, or on-disk format changes.

The motivation for this change is clear: the original message-ID format was convenient for developers but less accessible to users unfamiliar with lore.kernel.org’s URL template. The switch to full URLs aligns with the series’ goal of making breaking changes more discoverable. The author’s attention to edge cases (e.g., URL encoding, fallback links) suggests a well-tested contribution.

This patch is uncontroversial and integrates smoothly with the rest of the series.
