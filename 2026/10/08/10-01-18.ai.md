This is a CI/build system patch series from Patrick Steinhardt, consisting of eight small housekeeping and modernization changes to Git's CI configurations. The series targets both GitHub Actions (`.github/workflows/main.yml`) and GitLab CI (`.gitlab-ci.yml`), along with supporting scripts (`ci/install-dependencies.sh`, `ci/lib.sh`, `ci/run-build-and-tests.sh`) and one test adjustment (`t/t5004-archive-corner-cases.sh`).

The motivation stems from a prior discussion (linked as [1]) and focuses on two main themes: updating CI jobs that rely on end-of-life (EOL) Docker images to supported versions, and removing or simplifying redundant or unused logic. The series also incorporates unrelated but necessary Meson build fixes (from `ps/meson-improvements`) to ensure GitLab CI passes, though these are not formal dependencies. Patrick provides links to passing test runs on both GitLab and GitHub, signaling the changes are already validated in the project's CI environments.

Key changes include dropping the unused "linux-clang" job logic, switching away from the unsupported `i386/ubuntu` image, renaming the `linux-TEST-vars` job for clarity, removing Python 2 coverage (now obsolete), and eliminating a redundant `linux-reftable` job. The series also fixes a "fedora-breaking-changes-meson" job and updates the `linux-exotic` job to use a non-EOL Ubuntu version. The only test-related change skips a SHA-1-specific test in SHA-256 repositories, a minor but necessary adjustment for consistency.

The patch series is well-scoped and appears uncontroversial, addressing straightforward maintenance tasks without introducing new functionality or behavioral changes. The net effect is a reduction in CI complexity (14 insertions, 43 deletions) and improved reliability by removing outdated dependencies. The changes are mechanical and unlikely to generate significant discussion, though the removal of Python 2 coverage and the `linux-reftable` job might draw minor attention from contributors who still rely on those configurations.
