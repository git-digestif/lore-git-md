This is a design discussion in the ongoing thread about extending `git stash create` with `--include-untracked` (`-u`) and `--all` (`-a`) options. The author, Kazumasa Shigeta, responds to Phillip Wood’s feedback by narrowing the scope of the proposed changes.

Shigeta agrees to exclude `--patch` and positional pathspecs from `git stash create`, aligning with Phillip’s argument that these options are not script-friendly and could complicate worktree consistency. He also endorses Phillip’s suggestion to use `--pathspec-from-file` as a sufficient alternative for script use cases, noting that `stash create` has existed for 20 years without pathspec support. The discussion confirms that `PARSE_OPT_STOP_AT_NON_OPTION` is a reasonable way to preserve backward compatibility with the existing positional-message grammar, as messages starting with `-` are rare in practice.

The email does not introduce new technical details or code but clarifies the design direction: the patch will focus on exposing `-u` and `-a` (and possibly `-m`/`--message` for consistency with other Git commands) while avoiding interactive or pathspec-related options. Shigeta acknowledges that introducing option parsing to `stash create` has value on its own, even if not all of `do_create_stash()`’s capabilities are exposed immediately. The thread remains in active design review, with no merge status reported. The next steps are likely a revised patch or further discussion about the subset of options to include and whether to pursue one of the three proposed directions (extending `create`, adding a new subcommand, or adding `--create-only` to `push`). Junio C Hamano’s earlier preference for `--create-only` in `stash push` is still on the table but not addressed in this email.
