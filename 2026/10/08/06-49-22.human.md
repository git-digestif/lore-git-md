The optimization introduces an incremental write strategy for `fetch.writeCommitGraph`: instead of rebuilding the commit-graph from all reachable refs after every fetch, Git now writes only the newly fetched commits when possible, falling back to full scans only when necessary (e.g., no existing graph or multi-remote fetches).
