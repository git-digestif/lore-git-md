This thread updates Git's CI configurations to modernize and simplify the build system by removing outdated dependencies, redundant jobs, and unused code. The v3 series, authored by Patrick Steinhardt, consists of eight patches addressing feedback from Junio C Hamano, SZEDER Gábor, and Jeff King (Peff). All substantive technical concerns have been resolved, including a naming dispute over the `linux-TEST-vars` job (now renamed to `linux-feature-testbed`) and a reftable coverage trade-off, where the `linux-reftable-leaks` job was repurposed as `linux-reftable-sha256` to test SHA-256 with reftable.

The series is now uncontested and ready for integration, with Junio having backported it to apply cleanly on Git v2.56.0 for potential inclusion in the `maint` branch. The only remaining open question is a test design discussion between Patrick and Peff regarding patch 1/8, where Peff proposed making the test self-contained by creating a temporary SHA-1 repository. While Patrick tentatively agreed, no reroll has been posted yet. Additionally, Peff and Patrick have deferred broader CI script structural improvements (e.g., wildcard patterns for job categories like `*breaking-changes*`) to a future effort. The series reduces CI complexity while maintaining or improving coverage, with all changes passing linked CI runs.
