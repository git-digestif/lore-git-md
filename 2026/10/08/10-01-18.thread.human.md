This thread is a patch series from Patrick Steinhardt aimed at modernizing and simplifying Git's CI configurations. The series, now at its initial v1 submission, consists of eight patches that remove outdated dependencies, eliminate redundant jobs, and update supporting scripts to improve reliability without changing user-visible behavior.

Key changes so far include dropping the unused "linux-clang" job, switching the `linux32` job to a supported Debian image, renaming the `linux-TEST-vars` job to `linux-exotic` for clarity, and removing Python 2 coverage and the redundant `linux-reftable` job. The latest patch updates the `linux-exotic` job to use the `ubuntu:latest` image instead of the end-of-life Ubuntu 20.04, removing the explicit `gcc-8` requirement. This ensures the job remains on a supported LTS release while maintaining its purpose of testing Git with non-default "exotic" options.

The series has been uncontroversial and mechanical, with no open questions or significant discussion yet. It touches CI configurations for GitHub Actions and GitLab CI, along with supporting scripts and one test file. The net effect is a reduction in CI complexity, with 18 insertions and 53 deletions across the series. The patches are ready for review, with passing CI validation links provided in the initial submission.
