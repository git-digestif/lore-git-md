This thread is a patch series from Patrick Steinhardt updating Git's CI configurations to modernize and simplify the workflows. The series removes outdated dependencies, redundant jobs, and unused logic across GitHub Actions and GitLab CI, with no user-visible changes—just internal reliability improvements. Key updates include switching the 32-bit job to a supported Debian image, renaming a job for clarity, removing Python 2 coverage, and fixing a typo in a Fedora job.

The series was under review with mostly editorial feedback until Junio C Hamano pointed out a substantive issue in the final patch: removing the `linux-reftable` job eliminates the only CI coverage for reftable's foreign-SCM interoperability tests (CVS, SVN, Perforce). The remaining `linux-reftable-leaks` job disables these tests, so the series as written would leave reftable untested against these tools. Junio hasn't objected to the removal itself but wants the trade-off documented or addressed. Patrick will likely revise the series to either restore the coverage or clarify the reasoning in the commit message. The discussion now centers on what the project considers acceptable test coverage for reftable. Other minor feedback on phrasing and grammar has already been noted for future iterations.
