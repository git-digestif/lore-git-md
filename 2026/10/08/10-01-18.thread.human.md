This thread is a patch series from Patrick Steinhardt aimed at modernizing and simplifying Git's CI configurations. The series, now complete with eight patches, removes outdated dependencies (like Python 2 and EOL Docker images), eliminates redundant jobs, and updates supporting scripts to improve reliability without changing user-facing behavior.

The final patch removes the redundant `linux-reftable` job, as its coverage is already provided by the `linux-reftable-leaks` job. The series reduces CI complexity with 20 insertions and 64 deletions across GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, and supporting scripts.

The patches are mechanical and uncontroversial, with no open questions or objections raised so far.
