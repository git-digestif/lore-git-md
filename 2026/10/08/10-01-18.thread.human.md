This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s patch series updating Git’s CI configurations to modernize workflows, remove outdated dependencies, and simplify maintenance. The v2 series, now under review, addresses feedback from Junio C Hamano and Todd Zullinger on the initial submission. Key changes include renaming the `linux-reftable-leaks` job to `linux-reftable-sha256` to test reftable with SHA-256 while preserving foreign-SCM interoperability coverage, fixing a typo in the Fedora Meson job, removing dead code from the `linux-clang` job deletion, and updating Docker images for 32-bit and exotic jobs to supported versions. Python 2 support has also been dropped, and the `linux-TEST-vars` job was renamed to `linux-exotic`.

The series has seen mostly surface-level feedback so far, with Junio’s notable exception: he raised a substantive concern about a regression in reftable test coverage in v1, which Patrick addressed in v2 by splitting coverage between the `linux-reftable` and `linux-reftable-sha256` jobs. However, SZEDER Gábor has now objected to the renaming of `linux-TEST-vars` to `linux-exotic`, arguing the new name is misleading and worse than the original. This feedback is purely about naming semantics and doesn’t affect the technical implementation, but it may require reconsideration if others agree. The series remains under review, with open questions about the trade-offs in test coverage (e.g., SHA-256 vs. foreign-SCM, GCC 8 removal) and the potential impact of dropping Python 2. No maintainer decisions have been made yet.
