This thread is a patch series from Patrick Steinhardt updating Git's CI configurations to modernize dependencies, remove outdated jobs, and improve reliability. The v3 series, now under review, addresses feedback from Junio C Hamano and SZEDER Gábor on earlier versions.

The series consists of eight patches targeting GitHub Actions and GitLab CI workflows, along with supporting scripts and one test file. Key changes include renaming the `linux-TEST-vars` job to `linux-feature-testbed` (resolving a naming dispute), repurposing the `linux-reftable-leaks` job to test reftable with SHA-256 while preserving foreign-SCM coverage, and removing Python 2 logic. The series also updates Docker images, removes dead code, and fixes a typo in the Fedora Meson job that had silently disabled breaking-change testing. This typo fix, the focus of the latest email, is a one-line correction that restores the `WITH_BREAKING_CHANGES` flag, enabling experimental changes on Fedora.

Junio C Hamano has endorsed the series' trade-offs, including the reftable/SHA-256 coverage split, and the naming resolution. SZEDER Gábor's objection to the `linux-exotic` name proposed in v2 was addressed by adopting Junio's preferred `linux-feature-testbed`. The series is uncontested and ready for further review, with no user-visible changes—only internal CI improvements. Open questions remain about coverage trade-offs and the impact of hard-coded Python 3 on legacy workflows.
