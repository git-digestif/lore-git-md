This thread is a patch series from Patrick Steinhardt aimed at modernizing and simplifying Git's CI configurations. The series, now at v1, removes outdated dependencies (like Python 2 and EOL Docker images), eliminates redundant jobs, and updates supporting scripts to improve reliability without changing user-facing behavior. Key changes include switching the `linux32` job to a supported Debian image, renaming the `linux-TEST-vars` job to `linux-exotic`, removing Python 2 coverage, and dropping the redundant `linux-reftable` job.

The series is under review, with one minor correction already noted: Todd Zullinger pointed out a factual error in the commit message for patch 4/8 regarding Ubuntu's 32-bit support timeline. Junio C Hamano recently chimed in with a small but important fix—adding the `SHA1` prerequisite to a test in `t/t5004-archive-corner-cases.sh` to prevent failures in SHA-256 repositories once the Fedora CI job's typo is corrected. His feedback also included a grammatical nit in the commit message, which Patrick will likely address in a future iteration.

So far, the discussion has been light, focusing on surface-level corrections rather than substantive technical concerns. The series is uncontroversial, with no objections raised to its goals or implementation. The main loose ends are the commit message corrections and the potential impact of removing Python 2 coverage and the `linux-reftable` job, though the latter is justified by existing coverage in the `linux-reftable-leaks` job. Expect a v2 to address the minor feedback before the series moves toward integration.
