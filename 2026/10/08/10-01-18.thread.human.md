This thread is a patch series from Patrick Steinhardt aimed at modernizing and simplifying Git's CI configurations. The v3 series, consisting of eight patches, addresses feedback from Junio C Hamano, SZEDER Gábor, and Jeff King (Peff) to remove outdated dependencies, eliminate redundant jobs, and update supporting scripts without introducing new functionality.

Key changes in v3 include renaming the `linux-TEST-vars` job to `linux-feature-testbed` (resolving a naming dispute), repurposing the `linux-reftable-leaks` job as `linux-reftable-sha256` to test reftable with SHA-256, removing Python 2 logic, updating the 32-bit platform image to `i386/debian:latest`, and fixing a typo in the Fedora Meson job. The series also removes dead code and updates the `linux-feature-testbed` job to use `ubuntu:latest`.

Jeff King has raised substantive technical concerns about two patches: the necessity of the `linux-reftable-sha256` job (patch 8/8), which he argues may be redundant given existing coverage in the `fedora-breaking-changes-meson` job, and the long-term maintainability of the `SHA1` prerequisite in `t/t5004-archive-corner-cases.sh` (patch 1/8), where he proposes an alternative test design. Additionally, Peff questions whether the new 32-bit image supports Icelandic locale tests, which could affect gettext-related edge case coverage.

Junio and SZEDER Gábor have already weighed in, with Junio endorsing the SHA-256 coverage trade-off and the `ubuntu:latest` update, while SZEDER’s objection to the `linux-exotic` rename was addressed by adopting Junio’s preferred `linux-feature-testbed` name. The series is now under review, with the most pressing open questions revolving around Peff’s feedback on patches 1/8 and 8/8. No new feedback was introduced in Peff’s latest email, which was a courtesy acknowledgment of the reroll.
