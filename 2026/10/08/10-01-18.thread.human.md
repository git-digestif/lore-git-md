This thread is about a patch series (v4) from Patrick Steinhardt that modernizes Git's CI configurations by removing outdated dependencies, redundant jobs, and dead code while updating supporting scripts. The series has been marked for inclusion in Junio C Hamano's `next` branch after all substantive feedback was addressed. Key changes include renaming the `linux-TEST-vars` job to `linux-feature-testbed`, repurposing a job to test reftable with SHA-256, removing Python 2 logic, updating Docker images, and fixing a typo in a CI script.

The series is uncontested and ready for integration, but one latent issue emerged during review: Rust has been *accidentally* disabled in CI for six months due to a leftover `NO_RUST=UnfortunatelyYes` flag tied to a removed job. Junio proposed restoring intentional coverage of the non-Rust build path by adding the flag to either the `linux-feature-testbed` or `linux32` job, but Patrick has deferred this to a follow-up series. The discussion is now closed on the technical question, with no objections to the proposed fix. The v4 series itself remains on track for `next`, and the Rust coverage gap will be addressed separately. All other open questions—such as broader CI script refactoring and the design of the `WITH_BREAKING_CHANGES` flag—are also deferred to future work.
