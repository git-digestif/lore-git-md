This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s **eight-patch series (v4)** modernizing Git’s CI configurations. The goal is to reduce complexity by removing outdated dependencies (Python 2, EOL Docker images), eliminating redundant jobs, and updating scripts without changing Git’s behavior. All substantive feedback has been addressed, and Junio C Hamano has now marked the series for inclusion in the `next` branch, signaling it’s ready for integration.

Key changes include renaming jobs (`linux-TEST-vars` → `linux-feature-testbed`, `linux-reftable-leaks` → `linux-reftable-sha256`), updating Docker images (`i386/ubuntu:20.04` → `i386/debian:latest`, `ubuntu:20.04` → `ubuntu:latest`), and simplifying CI scripts. The test in `t/t5004-archive-corner-cases.sh` was revised to create a temporary SHA-1 repository, ensuring it works in both SHA-1 and SHA-256 environments. Jeff King (Peff) endorsed the final version, and Junio’s latest note confirms no further rerolls are expected.

Three open questions remain for future work: (1) structural improvements to CI scripts (e.g., wildcard patterns like `*breaking-changes*` to reduce duplication), (2) rationalizing the `WITH_BREAKING_CHANGES` flag’s behavior (currently excludes reftable despite its disruptive nature), and (3) addressing the accidental disabling of Rust in CI (a latent issue surfaced by Kaartic Sivaraam). The series is uncontested and on track for integration, with Junio’s note providing logistical context about `rerere`-assisted conflict resolution.
