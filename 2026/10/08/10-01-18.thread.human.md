This thread is about a patch series from Patrick Steinhardt that modernizes and simplifies Git's CI configurations. The series, now at its fourth version (v4), removes outdated dependencies, eliminates redundant jobs, and updates supporting scripts for GitHub Actions and GitLab CI. All substantive technical concerns have been resolved, and the series is ready for integration.

Key changes include updating Docker images to supported versions, renaming a CI job to `linux-feature-testbed`, repurposing a job to test reftable with SHA-256, and removing Python 2 logic. The series also fixes a typo, removes dead code, and updates the 32-bit platform job. Jeff King (Peff) has reviewed and endorsed the final version, and Junio C Hamano has mechanically backported v3 to Git v2.56.0 for potential maintenance branch integration.

A few loose ends remain, such as potential future refactoring of CI scripts to reduce duplication and a latent issue where Rust was accidentally disabled in CI for the past six months. However, these are deferred to follow-up efforts. Junio's latest email confirms the series is final and ready for integration, with no further rerolls expected. The thread is uncontested and on track for merging.
