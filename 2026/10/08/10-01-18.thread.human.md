This thread is about a patch series from Patrick Steinhardt that modernizes Git's CI configurations by removing outdated dependencies, simplifying job definitions, and updating supporting scripts. The series is now in its third version (v3), addressing feedback from Junio C Hamano, SZEDER Gábor, and Jeff King (Peff), and has been backported by Junio to apply cleanly on Git v2.56.0 for potential inclusion in the `maint` branch.

The series consists of eight patches touching GitHub Actions, GitLab CI workflows, and related scripts. All substantive technical concerns have been resolved, including a naming dispute over the `linux-TEST-vars` job (now renamed to `linux-feature-testbed`) and a trade-off in reftable test coverage (now split between foreign-SCM interoperability and SHA-256 + reftable). The series is uncontested and ready for integration, with only minor open questions remaining.

The most active discussion revolves around patch 1/8, which adds a `SHA1` prerequisite to a test in `t/t5004-archive-corner-cases.sh`. Jeff King proposed an alternative approach—making the test self-contained by creating a temporary SHA-1 repository—while Patrick tentatively agreed but hasn’t rerolled the patch yet. Kaartic Sivaraam recently noted that, with the `SHA1` prerequisite, the test only runs in macOS and AlmaLinux 8 CI jobs, which may reduce the practical impact of Peff’s proposal. This observation adds context but doesn’t resolve the underlying design question. The current implementation remains the baseline, though the discussion is ongoing.

Other patches in the series are uncontested. Patch 2/8 fixes a typo in a job name, with Peff suggesting a wildcard pattern for future-proofing, but Patrick deferred this to a follow-up. The remaining patches update Docker images, remove dead code, and hard-code Python 3 usage, all of which are mechanical changes with no behavioral impact. Junio’s backport confirms the series is in his integration pipeline, and the only remaining work is the unresolved test design question in patch 1/8.
