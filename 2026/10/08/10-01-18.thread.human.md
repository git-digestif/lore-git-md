This thread is a patch series from Patrick Steinhardt updating Git's CI configurations to modernize and simplify them. The v2 series, now at 8 patches, addresses feedback from Junio C Hamano and Todd Zullinger on the initial submission. The goal is to remove outdated dependencies (like Python 2 and EOL Docker images), eliminate redundant jobs, and improve reliability without changing Git's behavior.

Key changes in v2 include preserving foreign-SCM interoperability coverage in the `linux-reftable` job while adding SHA-256 coverage to the `linux-reftable-leaks` job, fixing a typo in the Fedora Meson job, and updating the 32-bit job to use a supported Debian image. The series also renames the `linux-TEST-vars` job to `linux-exotic` for clarity (the subject of this latest email), removes dead code from the `linux-clang` job deletion, and drops Python 2 support. Junio's feedback on v1 was substantive, particularly around test coverage trade-offs, while Todd's was editorial.

The series is under review, with no new objections raised so far. Open questions remain about the long-term implications of the SHA-256 vs. foreign-SCM coverage trade-off and the removal of Python 2 support, though these are largely editorial at this stage. The changes are internal to CI and don't affect user-facing functionality.
