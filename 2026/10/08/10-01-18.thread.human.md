This thread is a patch series from Patrick Steinhardt updating Git's CI configurations to modernize and simplify the setup. The series removes outdated dependencies (like Python 2 and EOL Docker images), eliminates redundant jobs, and updates supporting scripts—all without changing user-visible behavior. The eight-patch v1 submission is under review and has so far drawn only a minor editorial correction from Todd Zullinger on patch 4/8, where the commit message inaccurately stated that Ubuntu 20.04 dropped 32-bit support. The technical changes themselves—such as switching the `linux32` job to `i386/debian:latest` and removing the redundant `linux-reftable` job—remain uncontroversial. The series is ready for broader review, with no substantive objections or open questions yet raised.
