This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s patch series updating Git’s CI configurations to modernize dependencies, remove outdated jobs, and simplify workflows. The series is now at v3, addressing feedback from Junio C Hamano and SZEDER Gábor on earlier versions.

The key changes include repurposing the `linux-reftable-leaks` job to test reftable with SHA-256 (resolving Junio’s concern about coverage trade-offs), updating Docker images to supported versions (e.g., replacing `i386/ubuntu:20.04` with `i386/debian:latest`), and removing Python 2 support. Most patches are uncontroversial, but one rename—changing `linux-TEST-vars` to `linux-exotic`—drew Gábor’s objection. He argues the new name misleadingly implies an exotic platform rather than a job testing non-default Git options. Patrick has acknowledged the critique and proposed alternatives (`linux-nondefault`, `linux-optional-features`), but no consensus has emerged yet. The rest of the series is ready to proceed, pending resolution of this naming dispute.

Junio has endorsed the SHA-256 coverage trade-off, and no other participants have raised technical concerns. The series reduces CI complexity while maintaining or improving test coverage, with no user-visible changes. The only open question is whether the contested rename will be revised or dropped.
