This thread is about a patch series (v4) from Patrick Steinhardt that modernizes Git’s CI configurations by removing outdated dependencies, redundant jobs, and dead code. The series has been marked for inclusion in Junio C Hamano’s `next` branch after all substantive feedback was addressed. Key changes include renaming the `linux-TEST-vars` job to `linux-feature-testbed`, repurposing a reftable job for SHA-256 coverage, removing Python 2 logic, and updating Docker images for 32-bit and feature-testbed jobs.

The latest discussion centers on a latent issue: Rust has been *accidentally* disabled in CI for six months due to a leftover `NO_RUST=UnfortunatelyYes` flag tied to a removed job. Junio proposes adding the flag to either the `linux-feature-testbed` or `linux32` job in this series to restore intentional coverage of the non-Rust build path, framing it as a small, temporary fix. Patrick acknowledges the issue but prefers to defer it to a follow-up series, arguing it aligns with other deferred improvements like CI script structural refactoring. His latest reply confirms he has queued the change for the next series, leaving the v4 series unchanged. The discussion is now logistical, with no technical objections to either approach. The series remains ready for integration in `next`.
