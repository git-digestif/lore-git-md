This patch series from Patrick Steinhardt updates Git's CI configurations to modernize and simplify the testing infrastructure. The v2 series, now consisting of eight patches, addresses feedback from Junio C Hamano and Todd Zullinger on the initial submission. Key changes include removing outdated dependencies (like Python 2 and EOL Docker images), fixing a typo that broke the Fedora Meson job, and cleaning up dead code left from the removal of the `linux-clang` job. The series also updates the 32-bit Linux job to use a supported Debian image and renames the `linux-TEST-vars` job to `linux-exotic` for clarity.

The most notable adjustment is in the final patch, which reworks reftable test coverage. Instead of removing the `linux-reftable` job (which tests foreign-SCM interoperability), the `linux-reftable-leaks` job is repurposed to test reftable with SHA-256, addressing Junio's concern about losing coverage while adding SHA-256 testing. This introduces a new trade-off—prioritizing SHA-256 coverage over leak sanitizer testing—which may spark further discussion among contributors invested in reftable or SHA-256. The series is currently under review, with no substantive objections raised so far, though broader feedback on the coverage trade-off remains an open question.
