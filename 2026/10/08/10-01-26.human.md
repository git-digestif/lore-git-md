This is the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s eight-part CI housekeeping series. It removes the `linux-reftable` job from both GitHub Actions and GitLab CI workflows, leaving only the `linux-reftable-leaks` job (which already exercises the reftable backend with leak sanitizer enabled). The change is a straightforward cleanup: three lines deleted from `.github/workflows/main.yml`, three from `.gitlab-ci.yml`, and a one-line adjustment to `ci/run-build-and-tests.sh` to drop the now-unused job name from a case statement.

The rationale is simple redundancy: the leak-sanitized job already covers reftable functionality, and the unsanitized variant is unlikely to catch additional bugs. The patch is uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ overall goal of reducing CI complexity without sacrificing coverage. If any discussion arises, it will likely focus on whether the remaining `linux-reftable-leaks` job provides sufficient test coverage for the reftable backend.
