This thread is about a documentation rewrite that improves Git’s explanation of detached HEAD state. Julia Evans authored the patch, now at version 3, which replaces the existing section in `git-checkout.adoc` with a new standalone guide (`gitdetached-head(7)`). The goal was to make the concept clearer and more useful by explaining why detached HEAD is valuable, defining it more quickly, clarifying how users might end up in this state accidentally, and removing overly complex diagrams.

The patch is now complete and ready for merging. All feedback from Junio C Hamano, Kristoffer Haugsbakk, and Marie Claire LeBlanc Flanagan has been addressed, including textual refinements, build-system updates, and naming conventions. Junio’s latest feedback positioned his suggested refinements as incremental improvements rather than blocking issues, and Julia has indicated she does not plan to make further changes or conduct user testing. The broader usability concern about Git’s help system—specifically, the inconsistency between internal man page cross-references and the `git help` command—remains noted but is not a blocker for this patch.

Key changes include updating advice messages to reference the new guide, framing detached HEAD as advantageous for experimentation, and specifying the reflog expiry period as 30 days. The patch is self-contained and does not depend on other in-flight work. Julia plans to follow up with a separate patch to hyphenate `gitmergeconflicts` for consistency, but this thread is effectively closed from her side, leaving the merge decision to Junio.
