This thread began as a documentation improvement to rewrite Git’s explanation of detached HEAD state, led by Julia Evans. The patch was merged to `master` on 2024-07-10 after four iterations, addressing feedback from Junio C Hamano, Kristoffer Haugsbakk, and others. The new standalone guide (`gitdetached-head(7)`) and updated advice messages aimed to clarify why detached HEAD is useful, how users might end up in this state, and how to recover—all while removing overly complex diagrams and improving accessibility.

However, Junio recently discovered that the merged patch broke the test suite (`t2020-checkout-detach.sh`) because the advice message included a backtick-quoted command (` `git help detached-head` `), which the test didn’t expect. He posted a trivial follow-up fix: replacing the backticks with single quotes in `advice.c` and updating the test to match. This is a mechanical, uncontroversial change to restore test coverage without altering the patch’s intent. Since the original patch is already in `master`, this is effectively a post-merge bugfix. No further discussion or objections are expected.
