This thread began as an effort to improve Git’s documentation for detached HEAD state, aiming to help confused users understand what happens when they accidentally enter this state. Julia Evans’s original patch series (v1–v6) achieved this goal, with v6 merged to `master` on July 10, 2024. The discussion then shifted to how best to present this information: Junio C Hamano had previously noted that manual pages like `git-checkout` must serve both beginners and advanced users, suggesting a separate "survival guide" for users who find themselves lost. Julia’s follow-up proposed reframing the rewritten explanation as a standalone guide (`git help detached-head`) specifically for that audience, decoupling it from the `git-checkout` manual page.

The thread is now fully concluded. Junio’s latest email approves Julia’s final tweak—a single sentence addition to the merged documentation: *"You can also use this to make experimental commits without being on a branch."* This addresses his earlier feedback about highlighting the practical utility of detached HEAD for temporary work. The change is minimal, uncontroversial, and aligns with the project’s preference for keeping reference documentation precise while providing targeted guidance for common pain points. No further revisions or discussion are expected.
