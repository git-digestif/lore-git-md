This thread began as a documentation improvement for Git’s explanation of detached HEAD state, which was successfully merged to `master` on July 10, 2024. The patch, authored by Julia Evans, addressed four specific shortcomings in the original text and underwent several revisions (v4–v6) to incorporate feedback from Junio C Hamano and D. Ben Knoble. While the technical work is complete, the thread evolved into a meta-discussion about how Git’s documentation should balance accessibility with technical completeness.

Julia’s latest follow-up proposed a procedural resolution to this tension: document the intentional omission of low-level mechanics in the commit message, expand the `gitdatamodel(7)` man page to cover the mechanics of `HEAD` and branches, and add a cross-reference between the two. Junio’s response reaffirms his view that the *practical consequences* of detached HEAD—such as the fact that commits made in this state don’t affect any branch—are essential for users to understand and should not be relegated to a separate, more technical document. He argues that omitting this information would be a "disservice," though he stops short of demanding changes to the merged documentation. The thread is now closed, with no further revisions planned, but the philosophical debate about documentation scope remains unresolved. Julia also plans to submit a separate patch to hyphenate `gitmergeconflicts` for consistency with the new `gitdetached-head(7)` naming convention.
