The v5 patch refines the explanation of `HEAD` to consistently frame it as always referring to the current commit, whether indirectly (via a branch name) or directly (in detached HEAD state). This version avoids anthropomorphism and aligns with Junio’s preference for technical precision while still prioritizing clarity for users. It also adds a note about the practical benefits of detaching HEAD, such as avoiding temporary branches, and tweaks internal wording (e.g., "branch name" instead of "branch"). The implementation remains unchanged: the new content lives in `detached-head.adoc`, included by both `git-checkout.adoc` and the new `gitdetached-head(7)` man page, with updates to `advice.c` to reference the standalone guide.
