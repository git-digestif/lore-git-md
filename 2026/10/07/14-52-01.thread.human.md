This thread is about Julia Evans’s effort to rewrite Git’s documentation for detached HEAD state to make it clearer and more useful. The patch moves the explanation from `git-checkout.adoc` to a new standalone guide, `gitdetachedhead(7)`, and updates advice messages to point to it. The goal is to address four specific shortcomings: the current text doesn’t explain *why* detached HEAD is useful, takes too long to define the concept, doesn’t clarify how users might end up in this state accidentally, and includes overly complex diagrams.

So far, the patch has received substantive feedback from Junio C Hamano and Kristoffer Haugsbakk, focusing on rewording the opening definition of HEAD, clarifying the advantages of detached HEAD for experimentation, and adding a concrete recovery example. The build-system omissions (missing meson/Makefile changes) are the most critical blocker for integration, and Julia has agreed to fix them. The hyphenation of the man page name (`gitdetachedhead(7)` vs. `gitdetached-head(7)`) remains unresolved but is a minor detail. The discussion is still documentation-only, with no objections to the standalone man page or the advice-message pointer change.

Julia has acknowledged most of the feedback and plans to address the build-system issues and reflog expiry period in the next iteration. Some wording refinements, like the opening definition of HEAD and the recovery example, are still under discussion. The patch is under review, with no prior versions, and remains self-contained.
