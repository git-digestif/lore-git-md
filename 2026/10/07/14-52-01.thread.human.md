This thread is about a documentation rewrite for Git’s detached HEAD state, led by Julia Evans. The goal is to make the explanation clearer and more useful by addressing four specific issues: why detached HEAD is useful, how users might end up in this state accidentally, the overly complex diagrams, and the slow definition of the concept. The patch moves the explanation to a new standalone guide (`gitdetached-head(7)`) and updates advice messages to reference it.

The series is now in its third version and is effectively ready to merge. Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, has provided final textual refinements to strengthen the explanation, such as clarifying HEAD’s role as the starting point for changes and emphasizing the advantages of detached HEAD with concrete examples. All prior feedback from Junio, Kristoffer Haugsbakk, and Marie Claire LeBlanc Flanagan has been addressed, including build-system updates, hyphenation conventions, and phrasing improvements. The patch is self-contained and does not depend on other in-flight work.

The only remaining task is to incorporate Junio’s latest suggested refinements, which are minor and focused on polishing the text for clarity. Once these are applied, the patch will be merged. A broader usability concern about Git’s help system—specifically, the inconsistency between internal man page cross-references and the `git help` command—was noted but is not a blocker for this patch. Julia Evans plans to follow up separately to hyphenate `gitmergeconflicts` for consistency with the new guide’s naming convention.
