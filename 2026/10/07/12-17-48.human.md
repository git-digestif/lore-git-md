This is a performance-focused follow-up in the ongoing `sha1dc-accel` thread. Johannes Schindelin responds to Scott Chacon’s v1 series with a **substantive technical review** that both critiques and complements the original patches. The key development is Schindelin’s own patch, which closes a 3.3 % performance gap between the C implementation and the Rust original by avoiding message-schedule reordering in the SHA-NI backend. His patch adds mirrored variants of the SSE2 and AVX2 UBC checks, modifies the SHA-NI spill logic, and extends the unit tests to verify the new code paths. Schindelin reports that with this change the C version is now only 1 % slower than Rust on his Ryzen 7, and he confirms bit-for-bit compatibility across all three implementations (original `sha1dc`, `sha1dc-accel`, and Rust) on both x86-64 and ARM64. He explicitly endorses merging the series, with or without his suggested changes. The thread remains under active review, with no maintainer decision yet.
