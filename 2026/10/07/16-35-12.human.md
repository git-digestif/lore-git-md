This is a **bugfix patch series** targeting the diff subsystem, specifically the handling of `--relative` in combined diffs. The series is a direct response to Junio C Hamano’s substantive review of the original standalone patch, addressing both surface-level style nits and deeper design concerns about path normalization and rename handling.

The v2 series expands the scope to two patches. The first patch, largely unchanged in intent, now ensures that `--relative` consistently strips the prefix from paths in combined diffs, including edge cases like cross-directory renames. It introduces a helper (`strip_relative_prefix()`) that skips all separators at the prefix boundary, avoiding the multiple-slash ambiguity Junio flagged. The second patch fixes a previously unnoticed gap: the fast multi-tree scan (used during rename detection) bypassed the relative-prefix filter entirely, which could lead to inconsistent output when paths outside the prefix appeared in combined diffs.

Test coverage is thorough and now includes explicit checks for cross-directory renames, repeated separators, and raw/NUL-separated output. The author also verified the changes with leak sanitizers and both SHA-1 and SHA-256 builds, noting that an unrelated three-parent coalescing failure in `t4038` remains. The commit messages are clear and link the changes back to Junio’s review, though they could more explicitly state the trade-offs in the rename-handling design (e.g., why printing `../there/file` is preferable to leaving paths unstripped).

The series looks well-crafted and addresses the technical concerns raised in review. The added second patch suggests the author dug deeper into the diff machinery than initially required, which bodes well for the robustness of the fix. The only lingering question is whether the rename-handling approach (stripping only matching paths) will satisfy Junio’s preference for consistency, but the author’s rationale—aligning with ordinary diff’s behavior—is defensible. This is likely ready for `next` once the coding style nits are confirmed fixed.
