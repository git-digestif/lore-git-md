Knoble reproduces the issue on current `master` (commit `6a2b3c4d`), confirming the bug is not limited to the 2.56.0 release. He traces the execution path in `builtin/repack.c`: the `dry_run` flag correctly guards the `delete_redundant` logic, but the function continues past the candidate-printing block and calls `finish_repack` unconditionally. That function writes the new promisor pack and (if `-d` is set) deletes old packs, regardless of `dry_run`. The fix he suggests is surgical: return early after printing candidates when `dry_run` is set, preventing the downstream side effects.
