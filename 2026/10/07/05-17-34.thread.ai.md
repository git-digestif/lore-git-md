Core problem or goal
The `--relative` option in `git diff` ignores the requested prefix when displaying combined diffs (e.g., during merge conflicts), showing paths from the repository root instead. This series ensures both human-readable and machine-readable combined-diff output respects the `--relative` prefix, restoring consistency with non-combined diffs. The v2 series additionally fixes a previously unnoticed bypass in the fast multi-tree scan that ignored `--relative` entirely.
