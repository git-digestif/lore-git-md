# Thread deltas

## 2026/10/07

- [2026/10/07/14-29-53 by Scott Chacon <scott@gitbutler.net>] Scott Chacon submitted an RFC patch to Documentation/SubmittingPatches proposing to allow responsible AI-assisted contributions under the existing DCO, requiring human review/testing and an `Assisted-by:` trailer for substantial assistance, arguing this aligns with current SFC recommendations and the approaches of Linux, Xen, and Debian.
- [2026/10/07/21-42-35 by brian m. carlson <sandals@crustytoothpaste.net>] brian m. carlson objected to the proposed policy, arguing that because major LLMs are trained on code from sources that prohibit copying and are known to reproduce training-set material, no one can honestly assert the DCO without knowing all training sources; as a Git distributor he expressed concern about legal liability and cited Gentoo and NetBSD provenance policies.
- [2026/10/07/22-44-01 by Junio C Hamano <gitster@pobox.com>] Junio C Hamano critiqued the proposal, noting that the claim of legal soundness rests on output from an OpenAI model, that the proposed text is largely unenforceable aspiration, that contributors cannot practically certify the DCO for LLM output under the proposed rule, that the `Assisted-by:` trailer naming specific tools diverges from the kernel's current guidance, and that the proposed text gives maintainers less latitude to reject contributions than the kernel guidelines do.
