This is a documentation review of Julia Evans’s detached HEAD rewrite. Junio C Hamano responds to the patch with line-by-line feedback on the proposed text in `detached-head.adoc` and the surrounding man-page structure.

Junio’s comments are substantive but narrowly focused: he suggests rewording the opening definition of HEAD to avoid circular phrasing, clarifies the *advantages* of detached HEAD for experimentation (rather than framing it as a “problem”), and nudges the text toward more precise language (“unknowingly” instead of “unintentionally” for the tag/checkout case). He also proposes adding a concrete recovery example—quoting the exact warning message Git emits when commits are left behind—so users can act on it without digging into reflogs.

On structure, Junio is neutral about removing the diagrams from `git-checkout(1)` but notes that if they are missed, they could be relocated to the new `gitdetachedhead(7)` guide. The review does not object to the standalone man page itself or to the advice-message pointer change in `advice.c`. No new test coverage or code changes are requested; this remains a documentation-only discussion.
