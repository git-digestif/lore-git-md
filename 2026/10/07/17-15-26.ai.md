This is a substantive review reply in the `pw/stash-optimize-check_changes-calls/v1` refactoring series. Junio C Hamano and Phillip Wood agree on a follow-up direction to eliminate the newly introduced redundant index refresh in `git stash push`. The patch currently causes `do_push_stash()` to call `repo_refresh_and_write_index()` unconditionally, then invoke `do_create_stash()`, which refreshes the index again. Phillip confirms this is a pre-existing inefficiency when there is something to stash, but acknowledges it is now unconditional. Both agree the fix is to make callers responsible for refreshing the index, which would also avoid multiple `preload_index()` calls. No objections are raised; the series remains uncontroversial but now has a clear technical debt item for a follow-up patch. Files touched remain `builtin/stash.c`; functions involved are `do_push_stash()` and `do_create_stash()`. The key technical detail is the redundant index refresh and the proposed caller-responsibility refactor.
