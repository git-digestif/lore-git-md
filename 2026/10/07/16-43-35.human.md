This is a documentation patch, part of Julia Evans’s effort to improve Git’s explanation of detached HEAD state. Junio C Hamano reviews the patch and suggests several refinements to the wording and structure, though he does not object to the overall direction.

Junio’s feedback focuses on precision and tone.
