This is a surface-level review comment on the third patch of Justin Tobler’s four-patch series fixing a race condition in Git’s ODB transaction machinery. The series is part of the ongoing ODB abstraction effort and is now ready for merging, with all substantive feedback addressed and explicit approval from Patrick Steinhardt.

Karthik Nayak’s comment is a minor nit: he suggests replacing `unlink()` with `unlink_or_warn()` in the error path of `write_pack_lockfile()` to ensure the cleanup failure is logged rather than silently ignored. This is a defensive coding practice but does not affect the patch’s correctness or the series’ core goal. The rest of the patch—introducing the `write_pack_lockfile()` helper to prepare for deferred lockfile creation—is unchanged and remains technically sound.

The patch itself is a refactoring step with no user-visible behavior change. It refactors `odb_transaction_files_write_pack()` to explicitly generate `.keep` lockfiles (instead of relying on `git-index-pack --keep`) in preparation for the next patch, which defers lockfile creation to the transaction commit phase. The change is clean, well-motivated, and addresses all prior feedback, including Patrick Steinhardt’s stylistic tweaks. The series as a whole is considered complete and ready for integration.
