This is a **bugfix patch** in the ongoing ODB abstraction effort, specifically the first of four patches in Justin Tobler’s series to fix a race condition in `.keep` lockfile cleanup. The series is **ready for merging**—Junio has signaled readiness, and Patrick Steinhardt has explicitly approved all four patches.

This particular patch updates a comment in `pack-write.c` to be hash-algorithm-agnostic, replacing the hardcoded SHA-1 reference (`%40s`) with a generic `<name>` and referencing `hexsz`. The change is preparatory refactoring for later patches that expose the parsing logic as a public API. Karthik Nayak raised a surface-level concern about comment ambiguity: the updated text describes both `"pack\t<name>\n"` and `"keep\t<name>\n"` as expected output, but the final line (`"In the 'keep' case..."`) could be misread as implying the `"keep"` variant is always expected. Justin clarifies that while all callers use `--keep`, `git-index-pack` may still output `"pack"` if lockfile creation fails (e.g., due to a concurrent process). The ambiguity is resolved in the next patch, which removes the final line entirely, so Justin opts to leave this patch unchanged.

The exchange is minor and unlikely to block the series. The broader fix—deferring `.keep` lockfile creation to the transaction commit phase—remains technically sound and well-tested, with Patrick’s approval and no outstanding objections.
