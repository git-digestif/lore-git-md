The patch updated only the glossary definitions, with no code or behavior changes.

The discussion has now reached a decisive point: Junio Hamano, the maintainer, has rejected the patch. This shifts the burden from the glossary to the broader documentation—resolving the inconsistency would require auditing and updating other man pages to align with the glossary's definition, rather than changing the glossary itself. The thread is effectively blocked unless someone takes on that larger task or presents a new argument for revisiting the glossary. No objections or alternative proposals have been raised yet.
