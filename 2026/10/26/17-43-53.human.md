This is a feature patch series adding degree-of-concern reporting to `git repo structure`. The v2 iteration of Mark C. Chu-Carroll’s three-patch series addresses feedback from the initial posting: plugs a memory leak in the concern-string generation, works around a zlib/zlib-ng CI incompatibility, and—most visibly—resolves the config-key naming mismatch by standardizing on `repo.concern.*` (instead of the v1’s `repo.structure.concern.*` in the docs and `repo.concern.*` in the code).

The feature itself remains unchanged: a `--concern` option that annotates each metric with a star-based or numeric indicator derived from empirical thresholds (base-2 logarithms of the values, compared to a sample-derived mean plus one standard deviation). Users can override the defaults via `repo.concern.<metric>` config keys, and the documentation now matches the implementation. Tests have been updated to reflect the new config prefix and to disable compression to avoid zlib-version flakiness.

The series is well-scoped, touches only the `repo` built-in and its documentation, and comes with thorough test coverage. The empirical methodology for the defaults may still attract discussion, but the configurability insulates the feature from tuning objections. No major design objections surfaced in v1, so this version looks ready for deeper review.
