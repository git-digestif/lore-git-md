This thread introduces a performance optimization for `git send-pack` when force-pushing rebased feature branches, avoiding the re-upload of large unchanged blobs. The series, now complete with its third patch, wires up the new `--inspect-have` option in `pack-objects` to use the locally available old branch tip as a tree-root hint during forced non-fast-forward updates. The optimization is opt-in and narrowly scoped: it applies only to existing branches (`refs/heads/`), skips shallow repositories, and leaves fast-forward or rejected updates unchanged. Marc Strapetz, the author, has provided micro-benchmarks showing significant pack-size and wall-clock reductions for small rewrites, though he acknowledges a worst-case 8% packing-time overhead in some scenarios.

The series is still under review, with no feedback yet from other contributors. The implementation touches several key areas—`pack-objects`, the object database, and `send-pack`—and introduces new tests to verify the behavior. While the change is additive and doesn’t alter existing functionality, the trade-off between performance gains and overhead remains an open question for reviewers to weigh. No maintainer decision has been recorded, and the series is currently at its initial version (v1).
