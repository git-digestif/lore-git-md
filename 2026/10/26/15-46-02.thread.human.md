This thread is about a patch series from Marc Strapetz that aims to optimize `git send-pack` by avoiding re-uploading large unchanged blobs when force-pushing rebased feature branches. The series introduces a new `--inspect-have` option in `pack-objects` and conditional logic in `send-pack.c` to restrict the optimization to forced non-fast-forward updates of existing branches. Marc provided micro-benchmarks showing significant performance improvements for small rewrites, though he also acknowledged a worst-case 8% packing-time overhead in some scenarios.

Junio C Hamano recently replied to the cover letter, noting that the problem the series addresses—resending unchanged blobs after a rebase—is already what the existing push negotiation mechanism was designed to solve. His comment suggests the series might be reinventing an existing solution rather than building on it, though he didn’t propose an alternative or engage with the implementation details. The thread is now waiting for Marc to respond, either to explain why push negotiation isn’t sufficient or to adjust the series to integrate with it.
