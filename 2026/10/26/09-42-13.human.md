This is a **substantive review** of the first patch in Harald Nordgren’s four-part `git imap-send` bugfix series. The patch, already merged to `next` on 2025-06-10, fixes a long-standing tunnel-command process leak by ensuring the child process is properly reaped when the IMAP connection closes.

Patrick Steinhardt’s reply raises two technical concerns about the implementation. First, he questions whether `ictx->cfg->tunnel` is always initialized before `imap_close_server()` is called, suggesting that error paths might leave the `tunnel` field uninitialized and lead to undefined behavior when `finish_command()` is called. Second, he proposes making `imap->tunnel` a dynamically allocated pointer instead of a struct embedded in `struct imap`, which would allow explicit null checks and avoid calling `finish_command()` on uninitialized memory. He also notes a minor stylistic nit: the local variable `tunnel` in `imap_open_store()` is unnecessary and clutters the diff.

The review is substantive because it engages with the patch’s correctness and robustness, not just its style. Patrick’s questions highlight potential edge cases in error handling that could affect stability, and his alternative design (allocated pointer) would make the code more defensive. The concerns are narrowly scoped to this patch and do not challenge the overall goal of fixing the tunnel leak. If Harald addresses them, the patch’s safety would be further improved without changing its behavior.
