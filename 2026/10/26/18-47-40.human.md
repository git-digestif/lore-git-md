This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing review of a bugfix patch targeting the delta compression subsystem. The patch replaces manual integer arithmetic in `diff-delta.c:create_delta_index()` with safe integer helpers (`st_mult()`, `st_add()`, `st_add3()`) to prevent silent overflows during allocation size calculations. The author, Srujith A. Poojary (an Outreachy contributor), acknowledges Junio’s correction to the commit message: the phrase "Passing (hsize + 1) as an argument to st_add3()" was factually wrong because `hsize + 1` is passed to `st_mult()`, not `st_add3()`. This is a minor documentation fix and does not affect the patch code itself, which remains unchanged since v2.

The thread’s central question—whether the patch should proceed as a precautionary measure—remains unresolved. Junio’s feedback on v5 focused on the sufficiency of the commit message’s transparency and rigor, and while the arithmetic and struct-padding justification now meets his standards, the factual error he pointed out must still be corrected. The patch is well-motivated, with a detailed breakdown of the overflow risk under 8-byte struct padding (theoretical but plausible), and aligns with the project’s defensive coding practices. No new objections or review comments have emerged, and the author is expected to post a v6 with the corrected commit message shortly. For now, the patch is still under active review.
