This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing `git reflog` feature thread, responding to Phillip Wood’s UX concerns about Junio C Hamano’s proposal to refactor multi-step commands (like `git cherry-pick` and `git am`) to work on detached HEAD by default. Harald Nordgren agrees with the underlying problem—reflog clutter from intermediate steps—but frames it as a broader usability issue: the fact that experienced users (including himself and Phillip) rely on workarounds (e.g., manually detaching HEAD) signals a need for fundamental simplification. His reply is short and declarative, positioning the proposed refactoring as a step toward "vanilla git" that even power users would prefer over their current tricks.

The email does not engage with the technical specifics of Phillip’s concerns (e.g., `git status` updates) or Junio’s architectural proposal. Instead, it reinforces the series’ original motivation—improving reflog usability—while sidestepping the debate over *how* to achieve it. This leaves the thread’s architectural crossroads unresolved: the community still favors format specifiers (e.g., `%gb`) over dedicated flags, and Junio’s detached-HEAD refactoring remains a long-term proposal without concrete patches. Harald’s reply suggests he sees the refactoring as complementary to his series, but he has not yet addressed how it would interact with the `--operations`/`--historical-branch` redesign.
