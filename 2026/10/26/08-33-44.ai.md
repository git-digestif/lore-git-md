This is the first patch in Harald Nordgren’s v4 series introducing `git branch --delete-integrated`. It is a pure refactoring that extracts the existing `branch.<name>.deleteMerged` opt-out check into a new helper function, `branch_opted_out()`. The helper will be reused in the third patch when the `--delete-integrated` flag is added, ensuring that both flags respect the same per-branch opt-out mechanism. The patch touches only `builtin/branch.c` and makes no behavioral changes. It is uncontroversial and ready for integration.
