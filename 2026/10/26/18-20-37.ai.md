No functional changes to Git’s behavior are introduced; this is purely a CI infrastructure hardening measure. The change aligns with GitHub’s security recommendations and is part of broader CI modernization efforts (`ps/ci-housekeeping-and-modernizations` and `jk/http-curl-strip-creds`).
