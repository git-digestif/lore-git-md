This is a review by Patrick Steinhardt of Aniket Gupta’s four-patch series that moves line-ending configuration variables (`core.autocrlf`, `core.eol`, `core.safecrlf`) from global variables into `struct repo_config_values` as part of the Git libification effort.

Patrick’s feedback is substantive and technical: he argues that the series, while well-intentioned, actually *hinders* libification in its current form. The core issue is that `repo_config_values` can only be populated via `repo_config(git_default_config)`, which hardcodes the values to `the_repository`. As a result, calling `repo_config_values()` on any other repository triggers a BUG. This creates a circular dependency that ties the codebase more tightly to the global `the_repository` rather than reducing it, undermining the libification goal.

The review is clear and direct: Patrick recommends holding off on merging these patches until the underlying issue with `repo_config_values` is resolved. He does not blame the author (noting this is a systemic problem, not Aniket’s fault) but makes it clear the series cannot proceed as-is. This is a significant roadblock for the series, and the discussion will likely shift to how to decouple `repo_config_values` from `the_repository` before revisiting this work. The patches themselves are well-structured and follow established patterns, but the architectural flaw they expose is now the focus.
