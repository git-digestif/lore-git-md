Core problem or goal
The series introduces a performance optimization for `git pack-objects` that avoids packing objects the receiver already has, even when those objects are reachable only from commits excluded as boundary commits. The specific patch adds a `--inspect-have <commit>` option that lets the sender inspect root trees of receiver-known commits using sparse tree marking, skipping subtrees that are identical or exist only on the receiver side without walking the commit histories.
