This is the first patch in the **v4 bugfix series** that fixes a data-loss race in `git repack -d` where concurrent pushes can cause objects to be deleted despite being referenced by a `.keep` file appearing mid-repack. The series is now **production-ready** and rebased onto `master` (post-`ps/odb-files-alternates` merge).

This patch addresses a **latent bug in the cruft-pack walk** that was masked by the `--honor-pack-keep` mechanism. The bug causes the walk to incorrectly skip packs marked as kept (e.g., via `--keep-pack` or alternates), leading to objects being omitted from the cruft pack and potentially lost when the original pack is deleted. The fix ensures the walk correctly traverses these packs by invalidating the kept-pack cache after unmarking packs, allowing the traversal to proceed through them. The patch also introduces `repo_invalidate_kept_pack_caches()` to explicitly drop the cache for all object directories, a function carried over from v3 (Junio C Hamano acked the v3 version).

Key changes:
- **Core logic**: Modifies `builtin/pack-objects.c` to invalidate the kept-pack cache after unmarking packs, ensuring the cruft walk traverses all packs except explicitly listed fresh ones. Packs given with `--keep-pack` are treated as "open" kept packs, allowing the walk to proceed through them while still excluding their objects.
- **Plumbing**: Adds `repo_invalidate_kept_pack_caches()` in `packfile.c` to drop the cache for every object directory, including alternates. The function is exposed via `packfile.h` and called from `pack-objects.c`.
- **Behavior**: The cruft walk now correctly traverses alternates and `--keep-pack` packs, even when their objects are excluded. This trades performance for safety (e.g., a `clone --shared` of `git.git` may see a slowdown from 2ms to 3.3s for `repack -d -l --cruft`), but the change is intentional to avoid data loss.
- **Tests**: Adds comprehensive tests in `t/t5329-pack-objects-cruft.sh` to verify the cruft walk rescues objects through:
  - Packs not explicitly listed (e.g., a pack created mid-repack).
  - Packs given with `--keep-pack`.
  - Alternates (with and without `--local`), including edge cases like `git repack --filter-to` leaving referenced objects in an alternate.

The patch is **well-motivated** and **thoroughly tested**, addressing a critical correctness issue exposed by the removal of `--honor-pack-keep` in later patches. No regressions are expected, and the performance trade-off is explicitly documented. Likely reviewers include **Patrick Steinhardt** (ODB/repack) and **Jeff King** (low-level correctness).
