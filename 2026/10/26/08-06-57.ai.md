Patrick now suggests that if the attribute is never present, the entire `xattr` step could be removed entirely rather than left as a no-op. This would simplify the script and eliminate the need for the `|| true` workaround. The suggestion is framed as a potential preparatory patch, implying it is not urgent and could be addressed separately from the current patch. No objections or counterarguments are raised; the tone is collaborative and low-stakes. The current v2 patch remains ready for final review, with this minor cleanup deferred.
