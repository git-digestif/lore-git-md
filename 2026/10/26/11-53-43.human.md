This is a minor follow-up in the object storage extension series, specifically addressing Karthik Nayak’s review of patch 2/7 in v3. The series introduces a pluggable object database backend mechanism (`extensions.objectStorage`), and this patch refactors the storage format URI into separate `(type, payload)` components (e.g., `("files", "objs")` instead of `"files://objs"`).

The email is a short exchange about two nit-level suggestions from Karthik’s review. Patrick responds to both: he declines to drop `OBJECT_STORAGE_FORMAT_UNKNOWN` from the `object_storage_name_by_format` array (arguing it’s needed for consistency and avoids special-casing `NULL`), and acknowledges a stray newline addition in `setup.c` (not `repository.h`, as Karthik initially misattributed). Both points are purely stylistic and do not affect functionality.

The thread’s substantive discussion—whether user-configurable object storage backends are justified—remains unresolved, but this exchange is just housekeeping. The series is technically approved and functionally complete; this reply confirms Patrick’s intent to send a final version with these minor adjustments. No new concerns are raised.
