This is the third and final patch in Marc Strapetz’s three-part performance series that teaches `git send-pack` to avoid re-uploading unchanged blobs when force-pushing a rebased feature branch. The patch wires up the new `--inspect-have` machinery introduced in the earlier patches by passing the locally available old tip of any forced non-fast-forward branch update to `pack-objects` as a tree-root hint. The logic is deliberately narrow: it only triggers for existing branches (`refs/heads/`), skips shallow repositories (which already use `--objects-edge-aggressive`), and ignores fast-forward or rejected updates. The change is purely additive—no existing behavior is altered—and the test expectation is updated to reflect that only the rebased commit and its tree (two objects) are packed, not the unchanged blob.
