This is a **CI/build system** follow-up in the final review stage of Harald Nordgren’s patch to make Perforce, Git LFS, and JGit installation mandatory on relevant platforms. Patrick Steinhardt acknowledges a minor oversight in his earlier review: the `ci/lib.sh` script already enables `set -e`, so the error-handling additions in the patch are redundant for the purpose of failing the build. He questions whether the explicit error messages are still useful, given that `set -e` will already surface the failing command in CI traces, but concludes that the change is not harmful and does not require further adjustment. The patch remains technically sound and ready for a formal positive review. No substantive objections or changes are proposed.
