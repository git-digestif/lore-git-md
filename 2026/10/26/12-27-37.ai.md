This is a substantive review from Patrick Steinhardt of Aniket Gupta’s four-patch series that moves line-ending configuration variables (`core.autocrlf`, `core.eol`, `core.safecrlf`) into `struct repo_config_values` as part of the Git libification effort. Patrick identifies a critical design flaw: the current implementation *increases* coupling to `the_repository` rather than reducing it, because `repo_config_values()` can only be populated via `git_default_config()`, which hard-codes the global repository. He states that this approach actively works against libification goals and suggests the series should not be merged until the underlying issue is resolved. The review is clear, technically precise, and grounded in the broader architectural direction of the libification effort.
