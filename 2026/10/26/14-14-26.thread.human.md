This thread updates Git's `CodingGuidelines` to officially permit the use of compound literals in C code. Phillip Wood authored the patch, which removes a long-standing cautionary note against the feature after a two-year test period that began in late 2024. During that time, no platform compatibility issues were reported, clearing the way for broader adoption. The change is a small but meaningful step in modernizing Git's C codebase, reflecting the project's cautious but deliberate approach to adopting C99 features. No objections or further discussion are expected, as the decision follows an established process and has already been informally validated.
