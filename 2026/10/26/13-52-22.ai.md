This is the second patch in Julia Evans’s now-merged two-patch series that removes the obsolete `gittutorial-2` document from the Git codebase. The patch updates cross-references in five documentation files (`MyFirstObjectWalk.adoc`, `git.adoc`, `gitcore-tutorial.adoc`, `gitcvs-migration.adoc`, `gitglossary.adoc`) to point to `gitdatamodel[7]` instead of `gittutorial-2[7]`, reflecting that `gitdatamodel` is the modern replacement for the low-level object model explanations originally provided by `gittutorial-2`. It also makes a minimal edit to `gittutorial.adoc` to replace the reference to `gittutorial-2` with a pointer to `gitdatamodel[7]`, though the author notes this change is not carefully thought through since the plan is to delete `gittutorial` entirely in follow-up work.

The patch touches seven files in total (including the removal from `command-list.txt` in the first patch) and is purely mechanical: no new symbols, on-disk format changes, or functional behavior alterations are introduced. The commit message explains the rationale clearly: every reference to `gittutorial-2` was intended to explain Git’s object model, and `gitdatamodel[7]` (added by D. Ben Knoble in 2024) now serves that purpose more effectively. The update to `gittutorial.adoc` is explicitly described as a stopgap to maintain internal consistency until the document is removed.

This patch is uncontroversial and directly addresses the procedural feedback from the v2 series. The broader discussion about the future of `gittutorial` (the older, part-one tutorial) remains unresolved but is outside the scope of this patch. The series as a whole was merged to `master` on 2025-06-10, and this patch is the final step in removing all functional references to `gittutorial-2` from the documentation. The only remaining reference is the stub in `gittutorial-2.adoc` itself, which is preserved for manpage compatibility and explicitly marks the document as obsolete.
