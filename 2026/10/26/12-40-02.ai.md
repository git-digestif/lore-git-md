This is the third patch in the now-merged `gitmergeconflicts(7)` documentation series (v5, 3/6). It updates `Documentation/git-rebase.adoc` to replace a brief, incomplete explanation of merge conflict resolution with a cross-reference to the new centralized guide.

The patch surgically removes four lines of redundant advice (how to find conflict markers, `git add` to mark resolution) while preserving the rebase-specific steps that differ from other commands: the `--skip` option, which is unique to multi-step operations like rebase, revert, and cherry-pick. The new text directs readers to `linkgit:gitmergeconflicts[7]` for a full walkthrough, inserted both in the conflict-resolution section and in the `SEE ALSO` block at the end of the man page. The change is purely editorial—no behavior or command-line interface is altered.

This patch is part of the series’ overall goal of eliminating scattered, inconsistent conflict-resolution advice across Git’s man pages. The rebase man page now defers to the new guide for general concepts while retaining only the details that are specific to rebasing (notably `--skip`).
