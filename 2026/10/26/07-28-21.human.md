This is the final reply in Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch series refactoring Git’s `rerere` subsystem to remove its dependency on the global `the_repository` variable. The series is now complete and cooking in Junio’s integration branch.

Patrick responds to Junio’s minor design curiosity about whether the `rerere_cache_enabled` field (added in patch 4) could have been replaced by the new `rerere_cache` struct. He explains that caching the enabled state in the repository itself felt cleaner, but acknowledges it might be premature optimization. He offers a follow-up patch that removes the caching entirely, instead looking up `rerere.enabled` and checking for `.git/rr-cache` on every call to `rerere_cache_setup()`. This would simplify the code at the cost of an extra `stat()` call per invocation, which Patrick concedes is unlikely to matter in practice.

The tone is collaborative and low-stakes: this is a design preference, not a correctness issue, and Patrick is open to either approach. No further review feedback is requested or implied; the series remains on track for integration. The patch itself is a straightforward cleanup that removes the `rerere_cache_enabled` field from `struct repository` and updates `is_rerere_enabled()` to return a local variable instead of caching the result.
