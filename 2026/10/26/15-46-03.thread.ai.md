Core problem or goal
Extend the existing `t5516-fetch-push.sh` test suite to verify that Git correctly limits object transmission during forced pushes of rebased branches. The goal is to confirm that only the rebased commit, its tree, and any genuinely new blobs are sent, even when the receiver already has the old tip, and to ensure this behavior is consistent across `--force`, `--force-with-lease`, and forced-refspec (`+topic`) variants.
