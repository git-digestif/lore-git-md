Kaartic Sivaraam fixes a minor but annoying usability issue in Git’s new `clar`-based unit tests: when running `meson test --test-args='-ilx'`, the `-l` (long-tests) flag is passed to the unit-test binary, which doesn’t recognize it and fails with an “unknown switch” error. The fix is straightforward—add `-l` and `--long-tests` to the list of ignored (NOOP) arguments in `t/unit-tests/unit-test.c`, following the pattern established in an earlier commit.

The patch is part of the ongoing test modernization effort, specifically the adoption of the `clar` unit-test framework. It’s a two-patch series; the first patch just reorders existing NOOP arguments for clarity, and the second adds the new ignored flags. The change is well-motivated, with a clear reproduction case and a link to the CI results showing the fix works. No controversy or edge cases are apparent—this looks like a clean, uncontroversial fix that should just work.
