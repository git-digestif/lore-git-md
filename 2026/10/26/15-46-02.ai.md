This is a three-patch performance optimization for `git send-pack` that targets a specific workflow: force-pushing a rebased feature branch where large blobs remain unchanged. The series teaches `send-pack` to inspect the old branch tip’s tree before packing, allowing it to exclude objects the receiver already has, even if they lie outside the ordinary revision walk boundary. The change is opt-in, enabled only for forced non-fast-forward updates of existing branches where the old tip is locally available, and requires no server or protocol changes.

The motivation is clear: a rebase that preserves large blobs can still trigger a full re-upload of those blobs, turning a small history rewrite into a large network transfer. The solution leverages the existing sparse tree marking machinery in `pack-objects` by passing the old tip as `--inspect-have`, which lets the packer compare root trees without walking the old tip’s commit history. The implementation is careful to avoid regressions: shallow pushes, partial clones, and bitmap enumeration keep their existing behavior, and replacement refs are disabled during the inspection.

The cover letter includes micro-benchmarks on a Chromium-derived fixture, showing substantial reductions in pack size and wall-clock time for small rewrites (e.g., a single-file reword drops from 2.776 ms to 1.338 ms). The worst-case overhead—an 8 % increase in packing time for a widespread-change case—is presented transparently, and the author explicitly invites feedback on the cost/benefit trade-off. Test coverage is thorough: the series adds 19 new tests to `t5322-pack-objects-sparse.sh` and 43 to `t5516-fetch-push.sh`, covering identical trees, shared blobs, subtree pruning, partial clones, and replacement refs.

Key technical details for follow-up discussion: the new `--inspect-have` option in `pack-objects`, the `odb_has_object_file()` helper, and the conditional logic in `send-pack.c` that enables the inspection only for forced non-fast-forward updates. The patches touch `builtin/pack-objects.c`, `odb.c`, `odb.h`, `odb/source-files.c`, `send-pack.c`, and the two test scripts.
