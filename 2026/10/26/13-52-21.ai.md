This is the first patch in Julia Evans’s now-merged v3 series to remove the obsolete `gittutorial-2` document. The patch reduces `Documentation/gittutorial-2.adoc` to a minimal stub that explicitly marks the document as obsolete and redirects readers to modern alternatives: `gitdatamodel[7]` for low-level object-model explanations and `gitcore-tutorial[7]` for practical internals exploration. The commit message explains the rationale clearly: the tutorial has not been updated in 20 years, contains outdated terminology (e.g., “the index file”), and encourages an approach—inspecting `.git/` internals—that is no longer pedagogically effective for most beginners. By removing the substantive content, the patch paves the way for future tutorial improvements without requiring `gittutorial-2` to remain as a logical sequel to the older `gittutorial`. The preserved stub ensures manpage compatibility while explicitly highlighting the document’s obsolescence. No new symbols or functional behavior changes are introduced; the patch is purely a documentation cleanup.
