This is the v5 cover letter for the `gitmergeconflicts(7)` documentation series, now fully approved and ready for merging. The series introduces a new man page consolidating Git’s merge-conflict resolution guidance, replacing scattered and often duplicated advice in existing command man pages with a single, example-driven guide.

The v5 iteration is a final polish pass incorporating Junio’s last editorial tweaks: the `diff3` example now uses consistent indentation (spaces instead of tabs) and fixes a typo ("removed one fruit to the list" → "from the list"). Julia Evans pushed back on Junio’s suggested rewording of the resolution rationale ("probably" remains in the text), arguing that the word is necessary to acknowledge the artificial nature of the example and the need for judgment in real-world conflict resolution. All other feedback from v4—including Patrick Steinhardt’s concerns about the `zdiff3` explanation—has been addressed or deferred to future work.

The series touches 10 files, adding the new `Documentation/gitmergeconflicts.adoc` (339 lines) and surgically updating existing man pages (`git-merge.adoc`, `git-rebase.adoc`, `git-revert.adoc`, `git-cherry-pick.adoc`, `git-pull.adoc`) to replace redundant conflict-resolution sections with cross-references. The new guide is organized as a practical walkthrough, covering foundational concepts (what a merge conflict is, when it occurs), an exhaustive list of commands that can trigger conflicts, resolution workflows (edit, stage, continue), and tools for handling conflicts (e.g., `git diff AUTO_MERGE`, `git log --merge --patch`). The `diff3` example now demonstrates a scenario where both sides *removed* a fruit from the original list, clarifying how the common ancestor reveals intent.
