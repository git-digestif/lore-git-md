This is a **bugfix series** for the worktree subsystem, specifically addressing a flaw in `git worktree repair` that could corrupt unrelated worktrees. The author, Yoichi NAKAYAMA, responds to Junio C Hamano’s substantive review of the first patch (a refactoring step) with clarifications and a plan to improve the series.

The core issue is that `git worktree repair` previously operated on `.git` files or gitdirs without validating their relationship to the worktree being repaired. In edge cases—like manually swapped worktree directories or copied linked worktrees—this could lead to accidental corruption of unrelated worktrees. The fix introduces validation logic (in patch 2/2) to ensure repairs only proceed when the target is confirmed to belong to the intended worktree.

Yoichi’s reply focuses on patch 1/2, which refactors `infer_backlink()` to reduce redundant `.git` file reads and extract a helper function (`get_worktree_id()`). Yoichi acknowledges these differences and explains his rationale: `worktree repair` prioritizes fixing common relocation issues over repairing malformed `.git` files, so stricter validation is acceptable. He proposes splitting the commit to separate behavioral changes from pure refactoring, making the series easier to review.
