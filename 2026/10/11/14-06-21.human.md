This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing `sha1dc-accel` licensing discussion. D. Ben Knoble thanks Todd Zullinger for the detailed breakdown of how Fedora enumerates Git’s mixed licenses (`BSD-3-Clause`, `GPL-2.0-only`, `GPL-2.0-or-later`, `LGPL-2.1-or-later`, `MIT`) and the specific files involved (`reftable/`, `xdiff/xhistogram.c`, `ewah/`, `compat/obstack.[ch]`, `compat/regex/`). Knoble notes that while he was already aware of `xdiff`, the other license-segregated components were new to him. The exchange reinforces that distributions handle Git’s license diversity transparently by listing all applicable licenses rather than attempting to reduce them to a single “effective” license, and that this approach aligns with Junio’s GPL-2-only stance as long as original notices are preserved. No new technical or policy questions are raised; this is purely a confirmation of the earlier licensing resolution.
