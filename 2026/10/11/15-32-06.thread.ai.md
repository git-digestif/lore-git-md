Subject: [PATCH] SubmittingPatches: discourage splitting test and fix into separate commits

Originating author: Jon Simons
Thread type: documentation update (contribution guidelines)

Core problem or goal
Clarify Git’s contribution guidelines to explicitly discourage the anti-pattern of splitting a test and its corresponding code fix into two separate commits (e.g., one introducing a `test_expect_failure` and a later commit converting it to `test_expect_success`). The goal is to reduce review friction by ensuring the diff context always shows the behavior being tested alongside the fix.
