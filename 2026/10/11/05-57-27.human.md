The regression was introduced in commit `1e746b00aa` (2025), and the commit message now clearly documents this historical context.

The patch touches only `builtin/repack.c` and adds a new test in `t/t7706-repack-drop-filtered.sh` that verifies the pack directory remains unchanged under `--dry-run`, covering both explicit (`-d`) and implicit deletion cases.
