This thread updates Git’s contribution guidelines to explicitly discourage splitting a test and its corresponding bugfix into separate commits. The patch, now in its second version, revises the `Documentation/SubmittingPatches` file to state that a bugfix should include tests in `test_expect_success()` blocks that demonstrate the breakage when the fix is omitted. This change formalizes an existing reviewer preference, making it clear that while `test_expect_failure` may be useful during development, submitted patches should combine the test and fix into a single commit for better reviewability.

Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, provided substantive feedback on the initial version, objecting to phrasing that could imply `test_expect_failure` is acceptable in submitted patches. The revised text removes this ambiguity and aligns with the project’s emphasis on self-contained, reviewable commits. The patch is uncontroversial and reflects established norms rather than introducing new technical concepts. It is now ready for Junio’s final review and is expected to proceed without further discussion. No other participants have weighed in, and the change is unrelated to ongoing efforts like test modernization or `clar` adoption.
