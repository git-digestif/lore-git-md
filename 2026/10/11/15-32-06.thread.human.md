This thread is a straightforward documentation update to Git’s contribution guidelines. Jon Simons proposed a change to `Documentation/SubmittingPatches` to explicitly discourage the practice of splitting a test and its corresponding code fix into separate commits—such as introducing a `test_expect_failure` in one commit and converting it to `test_expect_success` in a later one. The goal is to reduce review friction by ensuring the diff context always shows the behavior being tested alongside the fix, leaving no ambiguity about whether `test_expect_failure` is acceptable in submitted patches.

Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, provided editorial feedback on the proposed text, suggesting a more direct phrasing that removes any implied endorsement of `test_expect_failure` as a submission tool. Jon has since accepted Junio’s revision, which simplifies the guideline to a single sentence: bugfix patches should include tests in `test_expect_success` blocks that demonstrate the breakage when the fix is omitted. With this adjustment, the patch is now likely to proceed as a v2. No technical objections or edge cases were raised, and the discussion remains narrowly focused on wording. The change is self-contained, touching only the contribution guidelines and introducing no new technical concepts or on-disk format changes.
