This thread updates Git’s contribution guidelines to explicitly discourage splitting a test and its corresponding fix into separate commits. The change, proposed by Jon Simons, aims to reduce review friction by ensuring that patches always show the behavior being tested alongside the fix, with no ambiguity about whether `test_expect_failure` is acceptable in submitted patches.

Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, reviewed the patch and suggested minor editorial refinements to the wording in the second version. While he noted that the justification for the rule could be streamlined—since `SubmittingPatches` is meant to state rules, not teach their reasoning—he ultimately decided to queue the patch as-is, calling the discussion close to the point of diminishing returns. The revised text now reads: *"A bugfix patch should add, in the same commit, code change and `test_expect_success()` blocks that demonstrate the breakage when the fix is omitted."* The patch remains uncontroversial and is expected to be integrated into Junio’s `next` branch shortly.

No technical changes to Git’s codebase or test suite are involved; this is purely a documentation update to `Documentation/SubmittingPatches`. The patch reinforces the project’s preference for self-contained commits and may help standardize reviewer expectations in future threads.
