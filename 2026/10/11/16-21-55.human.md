This is a documentation patch to `Documentation/SubmittingPatches`. It adds a new guideline advising contributors to avoid the "add a failing test and then fix it in a subsequent commit" pattern. Instead, it recommends submitting the test and the fix together in a single commit, unless there is a strong reason to split them. The rationale is that separating the test from the fix can make the change harder to review, as the behavior being tested may not be visible in the diff context.

The patch is well-written and clearly motivated. It fits naturally in the existing `[[tests]]` section and complements the earlier `[[separate-commits]]` guidance by cautioning against over-splitting. The advice is practical and aligns with common reviewer preferences on the list. No controversy is evident, and the change is unlikely to generate pushback. A small improvement would be to include links to past mailing list discussions where this preference was stated, as suggested by Kristoffer Haugsbakk in his reply, but the patch stands on its own even without them.
