This is a **substantive design clarification** in the `fetch: introduce +: refspec for matching-inspired fetch mode` series (v12), addressing the **unresolved edge case** about how Git should handle colon-separated `remote.<name>.fetch` entries (e.g., `+refs/heads/master:refs/remotes/cabal/master`) when `remote.<name>.refmap` is also configured. The exchange between Junio C Hamano and Phillip Wood explores three possible behaviors: precedence, dual storage, or erroring out.

Junio’s reply confirms that the **command-line behavior** (`git fetch --refmap=... refs/heads/master:refs/remotes/origin/master`) already implements **dual storage**: the ref is stored in both the refmap’s destination (`alt/master`) and the explicit destination (`origin/master`). He suggests this precedent could apply to the config-based case, but does not explicitly endorse it. The discussion remains **open-ended**, with no decision yet on whether to adopt dual storage, error out, or prioritize one config key over the other. This is a **user-experience and backwards-compatibility question**, not a technical blocker, and will likely be resolved before the series graduates to `master`.
