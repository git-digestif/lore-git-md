This is a refactoring and cleanup series targeting `builtin/commit-graph.c`, part of Patrick Steinhardt’s ongoing effort to make commit-graph backends pluggable. The series consists of seven patches that prepare the codebase by eliminating globals, simplifying option handling, and removing reliance on `the_repository`. All changes are confined to a single file (`builtin/commit-graph.c`) and collectively reduce its line count by a small margin (67 insertions, 64 additions).

The first three patches are mechanical: renaming subcommand functions for clarity, removing an unused `read_replace_refs` declaration, and eliminating the `the_repository` global in favor of explicit repository parameters. These are uncontroversial and align with the broader `the_repository` removal effort.

The fourth patch removes the global `--object-dir=` switch, restricting it to subcommand-specific use. This is the most likely point of contention: the commit message frames it as fixing an outlier, but the change alters user-facing behavior by removing a previously accepted global option. The cover letter explicitly invites discussion on this choice.

The final three patches refactor option handling to avoid global state, replacing scattered option-parsing logic with a more modular approach. These changes are internal and should not affect user-visible behavior, but they lay groundwork for future backend pluggability.

The series is well-scoped and clearly motivated by Patrick’s larger goal. The only potential friction is the global `--object-dir=` removal, which may require justification or rethinking if reviewers object. No test failures or performance regressions are expected, as the changes are purely structural. Key names introduced or modified include the renamed subcommand functions (`cmd_commit_graph_*`), the de-globalized `write_opts` and `opts` structures, and the removal of the `the_repository` usage in this file.
