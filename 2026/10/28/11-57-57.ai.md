This is the fifth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch series refactoring `builtin/commit-graph.c` to prepare for pluggable commit-graph backends. The patch simplifies how common options (specifically `--object-dir=`) are wired up across subcommands by replacing a runtime helper function (`add_common_options`) with a preprocessor macro (`COMMIT_GRAPH_COMMON_OPTIONS`). The change eliminates dynamic allocation of option arrays, reducing the code’s verbosity and removing the need for explicit cleanup (`FREE_AND_NULL(options)`).

The patch is purely internal and does not alter user-visible behavior. It builds on the earlier removal of the global `--object-dir=` switch (patch 4/7) by consolidating the remaining subcommand-specific option handling into a cleaner, macro-based approach. The macro is used consistently across the `verify` and `write` subcommands, and the pattern is clearly extensible to future subcommands.

This is a straightforward refactoring that aligns with the series’ goal of reducing global state and simplifying option management. The change is confined to `builtin/commit-graph.c` and introduces no new symbols or behavioral changes. The macro-based approach is idiomatic in Git’s codebase (e.g., similar patterns exist in other built-in commands) and should be uncontroversial. The patch’s value lies in its cleanup effect, making the code easier to maintain and extend for future backend pluggability work. No test failures or performance implications are expected. Key names modified include the removal of `add_common_options` and `common_opts`, and the introduction of `COMMIT_GRAPH_COMMON_OPTIONS`.
