This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch bugfix series restoring dynamic reload of alternate object databases in Git’s files-based ODB backend. The patch simplifies directory duplication detection by replacing a `hashmap` of `struct odb_files_dir` with a `strset` of paths, since the map’s values were never used. The change is part of a performance optimization effort: the unoptimized fix in patch 3 regressed lookup time for 20k missing objects from 185ms to 310ms, and this patch reduces that to 266ms (a 7.8% improvement over patch 3).

The simplification also restores a long-standing `NEEDSWORK` comment about the dubious benefit of case-insensitive deduplication, which had been removed in v2. The comment notes that the mechanism—relying on `core.ignorecase`—may not reliably deduplicate alternates due to unresolved symlinks, mount points, or misleading configuration values. The patch preserves the case-insensitive logic by downcasing paths once when added to the set, avoiding repeated string comparisons.

The change touches only `odb/source-files.c` and `odb/source-files.h`, renaming `dirs_paths_icase` to `dir_paths_iscase` for consistency and removing the unused `by_path_entry` field from `struct odb_files_dir`. The commit message includes benchmark results showing the performance impact and explicitly acknowledges the trade-off between simplicity and the edge-case limitations of the deduplication mechanism. All feedback from earlier iterations has been addressed, and the patch is ready for final review.
