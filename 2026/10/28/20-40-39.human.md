This is Junio C Hamano’s early feedback on Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch series that refactors `builtin/commit-graph.c` in preparation for pluggable commit-graph backends. The series is part of Patrick’s larger effort to abstract the commit-graph storage layer, and this batch focuses on eliminating globals, simplifying option handling, and removing reliance on `the_repository`.

Junio has reviewed the first four patches and calls them “sensible.” These cover the mechanical steps: renaming subcommand functions for clarity, removing an unused `read_replace_refs` declaration, eliminating `the_repository` in favor of explicit repository parameters, and the contentious removal of the global `--object-dir=` switch. His brief comment does not object to the global option removal, but he has not yet weighed in on whether it should stay or go—only that the patches themselves look reasonable.

The remaining three patches (5–7) refactor option handling to avoid global state, replacing scattered logic with a more modular approach. Junio has not reviewed these yet, so there is no signal on whether they will be accepted as-is or require revision. The series is still under active review, with no indication of merge status or integration branch assignment.

For a busy reader, this is a green light on the first half of the series and a heads-up that the second half is still open. The global `--object-dir=` change remains the most likely flashpoint; if you care about that behavior, now is the time to chime in. Otherwise, the series looks like a solid step toward pluggable backends.
