This is a feature patch series that refines and finalizes the `git submodule foreach` enhancements first proposed in v1. The series now consists of three patches (down from five) that add reverse-order traversal and superproject inclusion to the command, addressing dependency-sensitive workflows in complex submodule hierarchies.

The key changes in this v2 are structural and naming-related, responding to maintainer feedback. Tests are now integrated with their corresponding implementation patches rather than being a separate commit, following Git's conventional style. The `--append-superproject` flag has been renamed to `--include-superproject` to better reflect its behavior (running the command in the superproject after all submodules), and the documentation has been updated to clarify the traversal order and environment handling. The series also adds proper test coverage for edge cases like detached worktrees and alternate indexes.

The implementation touches `builtin/submodule--helper.c` for core logic, `git-submodule.sh` for flag parsing, and adds a comprehensive test file `t/t7427-submodule-foreach-reversive.sh`. The documentation follows the new synopsis style conventions and includes clear examples of the resulting order. The `--reversive` shorthand (combining recursive, reverse, and superproject inclusion) remains as a convenience option.
