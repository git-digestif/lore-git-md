This thread is about a small but useful refactoring to the `git diff-files` command. Edson Wasswa has submitted a patch that replaces the hand-written option-parsing loop in `cmd_diff_files()` with Git's standard `parse-options` API. The change affects only `builtin/diff-files.c`, converting the handling of `--base`, `--ours`, `--theirs`, and `-q` options to use the declarative `OPT_*` macros. This brings the command in line with Git's coding standards, enabling auto-generated help, command-line completion, and option abbreviation without altering any behavior.

The patch is mechanical and well-scoped: it removes 12 lines of manual parsing and adds 16 lines of `parse-options` configuration, preserving all existing functionality. The commit message clearly explains the motivation—avoiding error-prone manual parsing—and the specific benefits of the conversion.
