This thread fixes a performance regression in Git’s object quarantine mechanism during `git push`. When a quarantine environment mistakenly treats the repository’s own object directory as an alternate, `rev-list --alternate-refs` lists every ref twice—including hidden ones—slowing down connectivity checks. Kristofer Karlsson’s patch (now at v2) skips self-referential alternates by marking them as "borrowed" using two heuristics: the alternate’s path is either the repository’s own `<commondir>/objects` or the parent of `GIT_QUARANTINE_PATH`. This delivers a 67.7% speedup in repositories with 200,000 hidden refs and a 17.6% improvement even without hidden refs.

The patch has been merged into Junio C Hamano’s `seen` branch after he resolved a mechanical merge conflict with Patrick Steinhardt’s concurrent `odb-reload-alternates` series. The resolution preserved the patch’s core logic—introducing a `borrowed` flag in `odb/source-files.c`—and the performance gains remain intact. Junio and Jeff King have endorsed the parent-path deduction approach as a pragmatic short-term fix, despite its reliance on "icky" assumptions about the quarantine environment. The patch is now on track for `next` and eventual inclusion in `master`.

Key participants include Kristofer (author), Justin Tobler (who proposed an ODB transaction callback as a long-term solution but accepted the v2 workaround), Junio (who resolved the conflict and framed the fix as minimally invasive), and Peff (who called the approach "quite sensible"). The broader fragility of the alternate interface remains unaddressed, but this patch targets the immediate regression without disrupting ongoing ODB abstraction work. No new objections or open questions have emerged since the merge.
