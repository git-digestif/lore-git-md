This is a memory-leak bugfix in the transport subsystem, part of a three-patch series (v5 1/3). The patch introduces `transport_options_release()` to centralize cleanup of the `git_transport_options` structure, which was previously duplicated in `transport.c` and entirely missing in `transport-helper.c`. The leak affects remote-helper transports, where dynamically allocated fields like `negotiation_restrict_tips` and `filter_options` were never freed. The change is mechanical and confined to the transport layer; it adds one function call in `transport-helper.c` and replaces ten lines of cleanup code in `transport.c` with a single call to the new helper. No behavior change is intended, and the patch includes no tests. The commit message clearly states the problem and solution.
