Core problem or goal
Refactor functions in `bundle-uri.c` to use an explicitly passed `struct repository *r` instead of the global `the_repository`, advancing the project-wide goal of eliminating implicit global state. The change is mechanical and does not alter behavior.

The patch is ready for integration; no open technical questions or objections remain. Patrick Steinhardt has confirmed that v2 incorporates all requested changes and is complete from his perspective.
