This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch series refactoring `git commit-graph` to remove globals and prepare for pluggable backends. The series touches only `builtin/commit-graph.c`, eliminating reliance on `the_repository` and simplifying option handling. Key changes include de-globalizing `write_opts` and `opts` structures, replacing dynamic option arrays with a preprocessor macro, and removing an undocumented global `--object-dir=` switch—this last change is the only user-visible one and is explicitly flagged for discussion, as it could break existing scripts.

Junio Hamano has skimmed the first four patches and called them "sensible," but hasn’t weighed in on the `--object-dir=` removal or the later patches. Mark Chu-Carroll gave a cursory "looks correct" on one patch, but no one has done a deep technical review yet. The series is still under review, with the main open question being whether removing the global `--object-dir=` is justified. If you’re following this, watch for feedback on that change—it’s the only potential blocker.
