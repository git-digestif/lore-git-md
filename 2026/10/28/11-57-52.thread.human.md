This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch series refactoring `git commit-graph` to remove globals and prepare for pluggable backends. The series touches only `builtin/commit-graph.c`, removing three lines of code and eliminating the undocumented global `--object-dir=` switch, which Patrick flags as a potential breaking change that needs discussion.

Junio Hamano has now reviewed the full series. He calls the first five patches “good” but dismisses the last two (6/7 and 7/7) as “Meh,” arguing that the refactoring they introduce—removing file-scope statics and dynamic option-array allocation—doesn’t advance the immediate goal of pluggability because the command isn’t being moved out of `builtin/`. His tone suggests these patches are unlikely to be merged unless Patrick can show a stronger justification. The global `--object-dir=` removal remains unaddressed, so that question is still open. The series is still under review with no merge decision.
