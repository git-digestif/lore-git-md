This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch series refactoring `git commit-graph` to remove globals and prepare for pluggable backends. The series targets `builtin/commit-graph.c`, trimming it by three lines while eliminating reliance on `the_repository` and simplifying option handling.

Junio Hamano has reviewed the series and called the first five patches “good,” but dismissed the last two as “Meh,” saying they don’t advance the goal of making the code reusable outside `builtin/`. The most contentious change is patch 4/7, which removes the undocumented global `--object-dir=` switch; Junio hasn’t weighed in on whether this user-visible change is acceptable. Karthik Nayak’s recent “looks in order” review is purely surface-level and doesn’t address the open questions. The series is still under review, with no merge decision or integration branch assignment.
