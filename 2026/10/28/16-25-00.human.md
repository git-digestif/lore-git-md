This is a short follow-up in the ongoing `git rerere` subcommand modernization series. Junio C Hamano proposes reverting to the original name `rerere()` for the function currently called `repo_rerere()`, arguing that the `repo_` prefix was a temporary measure during the `the_repository` removal effort and is now redundant. The change is purely a symbol rename in `rerere.h` and its callers, with no logic or behavior impact. The series remains blocked on this naming decision; Patrick Steinhardt will likely send a v2 incorporating this suggestion unless further objections arise. All other patches in the series are uncontested and ready for integration once the naming is settled.
