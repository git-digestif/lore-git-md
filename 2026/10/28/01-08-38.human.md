This is a documentation terminology discussion in the now-closed thread about Git's glossary definitions. Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, replies to D. Ben Knoble's email with a brief clarification and a conciliatory tone, but no change in position.

Junio reiterates that the 2005 discussion leading to the current glossary definition ("object name" as the canonical term for the hash) is not a barrier to future improvements—merely context for informed discussion. He acknowledges that inconsistencies exist elsewhere in the documentation (e.g., using "ID" or "Hash" as if they were canonical) and agrees these should be addressed as they are found. However, he does not volunteer to undertake this work himself, nor does he suggest a path forward beyond fixing discrepancies piecemeal.

The email effectively closes the thread on the original patch (Julia Evans's proposal to make "object ID" canonical) while leaving the broader inconsistency unresolved. Junio's stance remains unchanged: "object name" is the authoritative term for the hash, and any documentation using it otherwise is incorrect. The tone is diplomatic, but the outcome is clear—no action will be taken on the glossary until someone audits and corrects the surrounding documentation to match its definition. For now, the status quo prevails.
