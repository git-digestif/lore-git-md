This is a refactoring patch targeting the `add -i` (interactive add) subsystem. The patch makes the `init_add_i_state()` and `clear_add_i_state()` functions, as well as the `struct add_i_state` type, file-scope static in `add-interactive.c`, removing them from the public header `add-interactive.h`. The motivation is encapsulation: external callers only need `run_add_i()`, so the internal initialization and cleanup functions should not be exposed.

The change is mechanical and well-contained, touching only `add-interactive.c` and `add-interactive.h`. The commit message is clear and the diff is straightforward, showing the removal of the public declarations and the addition of `static` to the function definitions. No behavior change is introduced, and the patch aligns with Git's preference for tight encapsulation of implementation details. This looks like a clean, uncontroversial cleanup.
