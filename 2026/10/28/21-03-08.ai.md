This is a refactoring patch in the `unpack-trees` subsystem. Junio C Hamano restores the `verify_uptodate()` function to file-scope static after confirming that the last external caller—introduced in 2018 to support the now-removed "recursive" merge strategy—was eliminated when the strategy itself was debugged and retired in April 2025. The patch touches only `unpack-trees.c` and `unpack-trees.h`, removing the function’s declaration from the header and adding the `static` keyword to its definition. The change is mechanical and uncontroversial, reflecting the ongoing cleanup that follows major subsystem removals.
