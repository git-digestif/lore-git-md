This thread is about a small but important refactoring in Git’s bundle-uri subsystem, part of the broader effort to eliminate the global `the_repository` variable. Okhuomon Ajayi initially sent a patch converting two functions in `bundle-uri.c` to use an explicitly passed repository parameter instead of the global state. The patch was uncontroversial, received a surface-level review from Karthik Nayak, and was queued in Junio’s `next` branch for integration testing.

Patrick Steinhardt then suggested extending the refactoring to cover the last two remaining uses of `the_repository` in the file, which would allow removing the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` define entirely. Okhuomon has now agreed to this proposal, confirmed the follow-up changes build successfully, and plans to send a v2 patch incorporating them. He has asked Junio to hold the current v1 from progressing further in favor of the updated version. The discussion remains purely about completeness of the refactoring—no behavioral changes, correctness concerns, or open questions remain. The patch is expected to land in its final form in the next release cycle.
