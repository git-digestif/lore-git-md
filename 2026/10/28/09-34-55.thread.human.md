This thread is about a small but important refactoring in Git’s `bundle-uri.c` file, part of the long-running effort to remove the global `the_repository` variable. Okhuomon Ajayi’s patch updates two functions to use an explicitly passed repository parameter instead of the global state, and it’s already queued in Junio’s `next` branch for integration testing after a straightforward review from Karthik Nayak.

Patrick Steinhardt now suggests taking the refactoring one step further. While the current patch is correct, it leaves two lingering uses of `the_repository` in the same file—`find_temp_filename()` and `copy_uri_to_file()`—which could be cleaned up with another mechanical change. He provides a ready-to-apply diff to show how this would work, framing it as a matter of completeness rather than correctness. There’s no disagreement here; the discussion remains focused on the project’s established goal of eliminating implicit global state. The patch is uncontroversial and on track for the next release, with this follow-up as a possible but not urgent next step.
