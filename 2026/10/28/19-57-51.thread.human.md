This thread is a small refactoring patch from Junio C Hamano that makes the `init_add_i_state()` and `clear_add_i_state()` functions static in `add-interactive.c`, improving encapsulation after `git add -p` was decoupled from the interactive add internals in an earlier commit. The patch itself is uncontroversial and has received light review from D. Ben Knoble and Patrick Steinhardt, who confirmed its alignment with Git’s interface-narrowing goals and provided historical context.

The only new development is Junio’s observation that `git history reword` does not respect the `notes.rewriteref` configuration, which he raises as a potential edge case or design question. This is now an open question in the thread, though it doesn’t affect the refactoring patch itself. The patch remains under review with no objections or requested changes, and no functional or test coverage changes are involved.
