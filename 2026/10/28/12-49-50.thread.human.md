Mike Beaton reported that `git check-ignore -v` incorrectly matches directory paths with a trailing slash (e.g. `foo/`) against blank lines in `.gitignore` when those lines use CRLF endings or contain only whitespace, violating the documented rule that blank lines never match anything. He confirmed the bug exists in both Git 2.51.1 and 2.56.0, ruling out a recent regression. Aniket Gupta sent a standalone patch that fixes `add_patterns_from_buffer()` in `dir.c` to skip empty lines after trimming, along with a new test in `t/t0008-ignores.sh` covering CRLF and mixed-whitespace cases.

The patch remains under review. Gupta has now sent a follow-up ping noting that it has not yet received maintainer or subsystem-expert attention. No decisions have been made, no Reviewed-by or Acked-by has been recorded, and the series has not entered any of Junio's integration branches. The only open item is maintainer sign-off on whether the single guard clause is sufficient and whether any additional whitespace edge cases need coverage.
