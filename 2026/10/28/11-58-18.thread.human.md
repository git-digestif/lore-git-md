This thread is about a bugfix for `git bundle-uri` that prevents crashes when processing bundle lists containing malformed relative URIs (e.g., excessive `../` traversals). The issue was reported by Aniket Gupta, who posted a second version of the patch after addressing feedback from Patrick Steinhardt.

The fix introduces `relative_url_gently()`, a non-fatal variant of the existing `relative_url()` function, allowing `bundle_list_update()` to skip problematic URIs instead of terminating the entire command. This is particularly important because bundle lists can come from untrusted servers, so graceful error handling is essential. The patch also improves error messages by redacting credentials and naming the problematic URL, and it updates test coverage in `t5750` and `t0060` to verify the new behavior.

While the patch is technically sound and ready for maintainer review, two loose ends remain: some test cases for relative URIs (e.g., `../foo`) were dropped in `t5750`, leaving a coverage gap, and Patrick’s suggestion to refactor `relative_url()`’s error-handling complexity was deferred. The patch is otherwise uncontroversial and aligns with Git’s approach to handling untrusted input. No maintainer decision has been recorded yet.
