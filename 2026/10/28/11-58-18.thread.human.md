This thread is about a bugfix for `git bundle-uri` that prevents a crash when processing bundle lists containing relative URIs with excessive `../` parent traversals. The patch, submitted by Aniket Gupta, introduces a new `relative_url_gently()` function that returns errors instead of calling `die()`, allowing the command to skip malformed URIs and continue processing the rest of the list.

Patrick Steinhardt has reviewed the patch substantively, raising questions about the commit message's clarity (particularly around the origin of bundle lists and the rationale for continuing after errors), the growing complexity of error handling in `relative_url()`, and the removal of certain test cases. While he acknowledges the patch's soundness, he suggests addressing these points before the patch can move forward. No other reviews have been posted yet, and the patch remains under review with no maintainer decision recorded.
