This is a short follow-up in the ongoing RFC thread for the `git cat-file --remote-object-info` batching series. This adjustment also resolves Junio’s earlier critique of the error message wording, as the later check can dynamically include the remote’s URL for clarity.

The thread remains focused on design refinement, with broader questions about server-side OID limits and batching defaults still unresolved.
