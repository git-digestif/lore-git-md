This is a build system follow-up in the ccache/PCH integration thread. Patrick Steinhardt weighs in on the cross-build-system consistency question raised by Ben Knoble, suggesting that ccache sloppiness settings for precompiled headers should ideally be handled upstream in Meson itself rather than in Git’s build scripts. He links to a Meson issue discussing ccache integration improvements, implying that the feature might eventually land there. In the meantime, he leaves the door open for someone to implement the change in Git’s Meson build instructions but does not volunteer or object to the current Makefile-only patch. The thread’s open question about build-system parity remains unresolved, with no immediate action proposed.
