This is the fifth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch bugfix series restoring dynamic reload of alternate object databases in Git’s files-based ODB backend. The patch optimizes performance by avoiding redundant `stat()` syscalls for alternates that are already known to the backend.

The change reorders the logic in `odb_files_dir_is_usable()` to check the `strset` of known alternate paths *before* calling `is_directory()`, eliminating unnecessary syscalls for directories that are already tracked. Benchmarking shows a 3.2% improvement in lookup time for 20,000 missing objects (from 269.2ms to 260.7ms) compared to the previous patch, bringing performance closer to the pre-regression baseline of 185.3ms.

The optimization introduces a subtle behavioral change: when a previously known alternate directory disappears, the error message "object directory %s does not exist" is now suppressed. Patrick acknowledges this trade-off in the commit message, arguing that the suppression is narrow (only disappearing alternates, not missing ones during initial addition) and unlikely to affect real-world debugging. The change is explicitly documented to avoid surprises.

The patch is technically sound and well-motivated, with clear performance data and a candid discussion of the trade-offs. The suppression of the error message was a point of discussion in earlier iterations, but Patrick’s justification—paired with the explicit documentation—resolves the concern. This patch is ready for integration alongside the rest of the series.
