This is a narrow but substantive review follow-up in the ongoing RFC series to lift the 10k OID limit in `git cat-file --remote-object-info` by introducing internal batching. The email focuses solely on the error message that appears when the command is used with a bundle, which lacks "smart options" support.

Junio acknowledges Pablo’s proposed replacement message ("object-info is not supported on bundles") as a clear improvement over the original ("smart options required"), but raises a forward-compatibility concern: if another transport type (besides bundles) ever lacks smart options, the message would become misleading. He suggests a more generic alternative ("object-info is not supported by this remote") and asks whether the remote URL is available at the error site, which would allow a user-recognizable message like "object-info is not supported by %s". The discussion remains open, but the tone is collaborative and the technical point is well-motivated. No other aspects of the patch or series are revisited here.
