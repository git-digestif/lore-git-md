This is a **bugfix** for the `@{push}` revision suffix, aligning its behavior with `git push` in triangular workflows when `push.default=simple`.

The patch addresses a long-standing inconsistency: when a branch’s upstream remote differs from its push remote (a common setup in triangular workflows), `git push` correctly pushes to the same-named branch on the push remote, but `@{push}` fails to resolve. This causes `git status` to omit the push-branch comparison, leading to misleading output. The fix is minimal—just four lines in `remote.c`—and delegates resolution to `tracking_for_push_dest()` when the remotes differ, matching the logic already used for the "current" push mode.
