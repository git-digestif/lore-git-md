This is a maintainer follow-up in the `remote.<name>.followRemoteHEAD` lazy-validation series. Junio C Hamano clarifies the intended refactoring direction for the helper function introduced in v3. The key point is that the helper should not be named with a `lazy_` prefix—its purpose is to determine the `followRemoteHEAD` setting, not to encapsulate laziness. Junio suggests splitting the change into a two-patch series: first extracting the existing logic (currently commented with "See remote.c's handling of ...") into a helper, then extending it to defer validation. He provides a reference branch (`ch/remote-defer-follow-remote-head-validation`) with an example implementation.

The email does not introduce new technical concerns or blocker—it is purely a stylistic and structural suggestion. The dependency on Harald Nordgren’s `hn/fetch-refmap` topic remains the sole integration blocker, as previously noted. Colin Hinton has already agreed to rebase once that topic graduates, and this refactoring adjustment is minor and non-controversial. The patch remains technically sound and ready for integration once the dependency is resolved.
