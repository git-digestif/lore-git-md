This is a maintainer acknowledgment of a small, uncontroversial refactoring patch that advances the `the_repository` removal effort. Junio Hamano accepts Karthik Nayak’s surface-level review and signals the patch will be queued for integration testing in the `next` branch. The patch itself is from Outreachy contributor Okhuomon Ajayi and mechanically replaces uses of the global `the_repository` with an explicitly passed `struct repository *r` in two functions inside `bundle-uri.c`. No behavior changes, no new tests, and no follow-up work are implied; the only loose end—a remaining indirect dependency on `the_repository` via `fetch_pack_fsck_objects()`—was noted by the author but not treated as a blocker. The patch is now cooking in `next` and looks likely to graduate to `master` in the next cycle.
