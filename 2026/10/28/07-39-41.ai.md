This is a **CI/build system** follow-up in Patrick Steinhardt’s series consolidating Git’s CI test environment variables. The email is Patrick’s reply to Jeff King’s **substantive review** of patch 8/8, which proposed a `--feature-testbed` flag to let developers locally reproduce the CI “feature-testbed” job’s environment. Jeff argued the flag was inconsistent because it omitted the `OPENSSL_SHA1_UNSAFE` build knob, making it “half-work,” and suggested replacing it with a `FEATURE_TESTBED=1` environment variable that would control both build knobs and test variables. Junio C Hamano has since endorsed Jeff’s alternative, shifting the discussion from a reviewer preference to a maintainer-backed direction.

Patrick pushes back, explaining his personal workflow: he rarely needs the build knob and instead wants to toggle `GIT_TEST_*` variables without recompiling, often for a single test file. He sees moving the setting into `BUILD-OPTIONS` as incompatible with this use case and is now leaning toward either leaving the patch as-is or dropping it entirely. His response frames the trade-off as one of developer ergonomics versus architectural consistency, but the maintainer’s endorsement of Jeff’s proposal makes a reroll highly likely. The thread’s next step is almost certainly a v2 that adopts the environment-variable design, regardless of Patrick’s personal preference.
