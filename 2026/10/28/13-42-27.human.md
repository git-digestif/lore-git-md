This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing RFC thread for Pablo Sabater’s `git cat-file --remote-object-info` batching series. Junio C Hamano responds to Pablo’s proposal to **remove the early `smart_options` check** in patch 1/3, endorsing the change as a sound design decision. The removal resolves the forward-compatibility concern Junio raised earlier: the later `transport_fetch_object_info()` check (which tests for the actual `fetch_object_info` vtable entry) is more robust and avoids assumptions about `smart_options`. This adjustment also allows the error message to dynamically include the remote’s URL, improving clarity for users.

The thread remains focused on refining the series’ design, with broader questions about server-side OID limits and batching defaults still unresolved. Pablo plans to send a v2 incorporating this change and other feedback. The patch itself is a small but important refactoring step in the larger effort to lift the 10k OID limit for remote object-info queries.
