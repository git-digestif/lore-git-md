This is a maintainer update on Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch series refactoring `builtin/commit-graph.c` to prepare for pluggable commit-graph backends. Junio C Hamano has now reviewed the entire series and finds patches 1 through 5 uncontroversial and well-executed. His only reservation concerns patches 6 and 7, which he describes as "Meh" — not harmful, but also not compelling. He questions the immediate value of eliminating file-scope static variables (`write_opts` and `opts`) when the code remains confined to the `builtin/` directory, where reliance on globals and `the_repository` is still idiomatic. The critique is pragmatic rather than technical: the patches are correct, but their benefit is marginal until the commit-graph logic is extracted from `builtin/` and made reusable.

The series remains under review with no merge decision. Junio’s feedback leaves the door open for Patrick to either drop the last two patches or argue for their inclusion, but the tone suggests the latter would require a stronger case. The earlier open question about removing the global `--object-dir=` switch (patch 4) is now implicitly resolved: Junio’s silence on it in this reply, combined with his earlier "sensible" verdict on the first four patches, signals tacit acceptance of the change. No further discussion or test coverage is requested.

For a reader tracking the pluggable backends effort, this update confirms that the foundational refactoring is on solid ground, but the final two patches may not land in their current form. The series is unlikely to stall on review, but the scope of what gets merged may shrink slightly.
