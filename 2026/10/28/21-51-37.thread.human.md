This thread is a small but typical part of Git’s ongoing effort to remove the global `the_repository` variable. Okhuomon Ajayi, an Outreachy participant, posted a standalone patch that refactors `range-diff.c` to use the repository stored in the `diffopt` structure instead of the global `the_repository` in two functions, and updates `get_filespec` to accept a hash algorithm parameter rather than relying on `the_hash_algo`. The patch is purely mechanical, touches only one file, and introduces no new behavior or on-disk changes.

So far, the only feedback has come from Pablo Sabater, who reviewed the patch at a surface level. His comments focus entirely on the commit message and patch title—suggesting clearer phrasing, pointing out awkward wording, and recommending the author consult Git’s `SubmittingPatches` document for style guidance. While he confirmed the code itself is correct and passes `DEVELOPER=1` and `clang-format` checks, the review did not engage with the technical substance of the change, such as edge cases or broader implications for the `the_repository` removal effort. The thread remains under review with no substantive technical discussion yet.
