This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch bugfix series restoring dynamic reload of alternate object databases in the files-based ODB backend. The patch simplifies directory duplication detection by replacing a `hashmap` of `struct odb_files_dir` with a `strset` of paths, eliminating the need to store and compare entire directory structures. The change is mechanical and focused on the `odb/source-files.c` and `odb/source-files.h` files, with no new tests or user-visible behavior changes.

The simplification removes the `by_path_entry` field from `struct odb_files_dir` and the `dirs_by_path` field from `struct odb_source_files`, replacing them with a `strset dir_paths`. The case-insensitive comparison logic (for `core.ignorecase`) is preserved by downcasing paths once when added to the set, avoiding repeated string comparisons. The patch also restores a long-standing `NEEDSWORK` comment about the dubious benefit of case-insensitive deduplication, which had been removed in v2 and was later identified as an undocumented change by Christian Couder. The lingering reference to "map" in the code comment above `dir_paths` was updated to "set" in this version.

Performance improves slightly: the benchmark shows a 7.8% reduction in lookup time for 20k missing objects (from 293.2ms to 266.5ms) compared to the previous patch, though memory usage increases by about 5%. The patch is part of a larger performance optimization effort in the series, with subsequent patches further reducing lookup time to near pre-regression levels. The change aligns with the ongoing ODB abstraction effort and is narrowly scoped to the files-based backend. All feedback from Junio and Christian has been addressed, and the patch is ready for integration.
