This is a brief follow-up in the `git rerere` subcommand modernization series, focusing on patch 2/12 ("rerere: convert flags into an enum"). Junio C Hamano confirms Patrick Steinhardt’s proposal to extend the enum’s documentation to cover all four flags (`RERERE_AUTOUPDATE`, `RERERE_NOAUTOUPDATE`, `RERERE_READONLY`, and `RERERE_NOWAIT`). This is a purely cosmetic adjustment—no functional or mechanical changes are involved, and the patch remains uncontested. The series’ sole unresolved issue remains the naming of the fallback subcommand in patch 1, with Junio favoring `rerere()` over Patrick’s earlier proposals. All other patches in the series are ready for integration once the naming question is settled.
