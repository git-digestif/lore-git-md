This is the second patch in Elia Pinto’s four-part bugfix series addressing object-size truncation on 32-bit platforms. It converts the fsck subsystem’s internal object-size parameters from `unsigned long` to `size_t`, eliminating truncation when objects exceed 4 GiB. The patch touches `fsck.c` and `fsck.h`, updating function signatures for `fsck_object()`, `fsck_buffer()`, `fsck_tree()`, `fsck_commit()`, `fsck_tag()`, `fsck_tag_standalone()`, `fsck_blob()`, and the helper `verify_headers()`. Callers that still pass `unsigned long` (e.g., pack verification paths and tree-walk routines) are intentionally left for follow-up patches. No tests are included, as reproducing the bug requires >4 GiB objects in memory. The change is mechanical and does not alter on-disk formats or behavior on 64-bit platforms.
