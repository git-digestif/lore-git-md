Thread identity: bugfix series addressing object-size truncation on 32-bit platforms (Windows, LLP64 data model); originating author Elia Pinto; thread type patch series (v1, with follow-up clarification).

Core problem or goal: fix truncation bugs when object sizes exceed 4 GiB on 32-bit platforms by converting function signatures and call sites from `unsigned long` to `size_t` for **in-memory** buffer sizes and indices. The series explicitly distinguishes between in-memory operations (where `size_t` is appropriate) and on-disk sizes (where `off_t` is required). User-visible symptoms include `git fsck` inspecting only a prefix of large objects (e.g., `.gitmodules`), `git tag -v` failing to find signatures, and `copy_subject()` misindexing due to integer overflow.
