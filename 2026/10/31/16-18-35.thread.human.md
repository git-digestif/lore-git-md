This is a call for community participation in the upcoming **Git Rev News edition 140**, the project’s regular newsletter. Christian Couder has shared a draft on GitHub and is inviting contributions—proofreading, article suggestions, interview volunteers, or fixes for open issues—before the November 2, 2026 publication date. The newsletter covers recent mailing list activity, so contributors can help verify summaries or add context. No technical expertise is needed; anyone interested in Git development can take part.
