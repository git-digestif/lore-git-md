The thread initially proposed mechanical refactoring (v1/v2) but pivoted to Junio’s protective measure: wrapping the `notes-utils.h` API in `#ifdef USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` to prevent misuse until the underlying `notes.h` functions (e.g., `write_notes_tree()`, `init_notes()`, `add_note()`) are refactored.
