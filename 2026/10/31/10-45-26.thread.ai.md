Core problem or goal
Remove all implicit uses of the global `the_repository` in `notes-utils.c` by converting `create_notes_commit()`, `commit_notes()`, and `init_copy_notes_for_rewrite()` to use the `struct repository *` parameter they receive. The change is purely mechanical and does not alter behavior.

The v2 patches address all prior feedback and complete the intended scope of the series.
