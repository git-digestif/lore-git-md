This thread is about a patch from Jasmeet Bhatia that improves `git stash` performance by reusing prepared index state and untracked file lists, avoiding redundant computation. The patch shows measurable gains—16-18% for `git stash -p` and 6% for `git stash push` on the Linux kernel repository—but has not yet seen review.

Phillip Wood has now weighed in with a substantive review, noting that his own overlapping series (`pw/stash-change-check-only-once`), currently in `seen`, takes a different approach. Instead of passing prepared state into `do_create_stash()`, his series consolidates the `check_changes()` call to run just once and removes redundant `preload_index()` calls. He suggests that the two approaches may need reconciliation before either can land. The discussion is now focused on architectural direction rather than implementation details, with no objections raised yet but a clear need for coordination.
