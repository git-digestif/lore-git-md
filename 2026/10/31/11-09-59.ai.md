This is the third patch in Elia Pinto’s four-patch series fixing object-size truncation on 32-bit platforms. It converts the buffer-size parameters of the core object-parsing functions—`parse_object_buffer()`, `parse_commit_buffer()`, `parse_tag_buffer()`, and `decode_tree_entry_raw()`—from `unsigned long` to `size_t`. The commit slab that caches commit buffers still stores sizes as `unsigned long`, so `set_commit_buffer()` narrows the incoming `size_t` with `cast_size_t_to_ulong()`, dying on objects ≥4 GiB instead of silently truncating. Seven files are touched (commit.c, commit.h, object-file-convert.c, object.c, object.h, tag.c, tag.h); the change is purely mechanical and does not alter on-disk formats or behavior on 64-bit systems.
