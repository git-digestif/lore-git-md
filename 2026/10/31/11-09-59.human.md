This is the third patch in Elia Pinto’s four-patch series fixing object-size truncation on 32-bit platforms. It converts the core object-parsing functions (`parse_object_buffer`, `parse_commit_buffer`, `parse_tag_buffer`, and `decode_tree_entry_raw`) from `unsigned long` to `size_t` for the buffer-size parameter. On 32-bit systems this prevents silent truncation of objects ≥ 4 GiB, so signatures or other data at the end of large tags, commits, or trees are no longer missed. The patch also updates `set_commit_buffer` to die on oversized objects rather than cache a truncated size, matching the existing behavior in `repo_get_commit_buffer`. Seven files are touched; the change is purely mechanical and does not alter on-disk formats.
