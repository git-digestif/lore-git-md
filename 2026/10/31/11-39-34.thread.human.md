This thread is a four-patch series from Elia Pinto fixing truncation issues with large objects (≥4 GiB) on 32-bit platforms, particularly Windows. The series converts key size and offset parameters from `unsigned long` to `size_t` across the pack-receiving and verification pipeline (`unpack-objects`, `index-pack`, and `git fsck`) to prevent crashes, false SHA-1 collision reports, and partial object checks.

The first three patches addressed crashes in `unpack-objects`, "serious inflate inconsistency" errors in `index-pack --fix-thin`, and false "SHA1 COLLISION FOUND" reports in `index-pack`. The just-arrived fourth patch completes the series by ensuring `git fsck` checks full objects rather than truncated prefixes in packs. All patches are mechanical type conversions with no on-disk format changes, and the series is self-contained and ready for review. No objections have been raised yet, though the lack of test coverage (due to the impracticality of generating 4 GiB objects or testing on 32-bit platforms) has been noted. The series is a high-priority fix for Windows compatibility with large objects.
