This thread concerns Elia Pinto's four-patch v2 series fixing truncation of objects >=4 GiB on platforms where `unsigned long` is 32-bit but `size_t` is 64-bit (notably 64-bit Windows). The patches convert `unpack-objects`, `index-pack`, and `git fsck` from `unsigned long` to `size_t`, addressing inflate failures, crashes, false SHA-1 collision reports, and spurious corruption errors in the pack-receiving and verification pipeline. No tests are included because triggering the bugs requires 4 GiB+ inputs; the author verified the fixes via size-truncation simulation.

Patrick Steinhardt reviewed the series substantively and raised sequencing concerns: because dependent `size_t` conversion series for `odb_write_object()` and `parse_object_buffer()`/`fsck_object()` are already in flight, he proposed deferring the entire series until those land rather than landing it piecemeal, arguing that multiple similar type-conversion series in flight fragment limited reviewing capacity. He also asked for an overflow guard and an EXPENSIVE test around patch 2/4, which the author agreed to add in a future version. In his latest reply, Elia Pinto accepted the full-series deferral, superseding his earlier plan to resend patches 2/4 and 3/4 standalone, and will resend once the dependent series have landed.

The series remains under review with no merge or queue status, and no maintainer decision from Junio C Hamano is recorded. The thread now waits on the landing of the two dependent conversion series or a maintainer ruling on sequencing.
