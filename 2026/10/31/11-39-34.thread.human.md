Elia Pinto's v2 four-patch series fixes truncation and correctness bugs when handling objects of 4 GiB or larger on platforms where `unsigned long` is 32-bit (notably 64-bit Windows), converting the pack-receiving and verification pipeline in `unpack-objects`, `index-pack`, and `git fsck` from `unsigned long` to `size_t`. The series remains under review with no merge or queue status; v1 drew no comments, and v2 was rebased onto a master that already converted `stream_blob()` to `size_t`.

Patrick Steinhardt has provided substantive review on patches 1/4, 2/4, and 4/4. On 1/4 he questioned whether the `cast_size_t_to_ulong()` guards are worth their maintainability cost given that downstream `size_t` conversions are already in flight, and flagged the commit message as overly verbose. On 2/4 he asked for a `signed_add_overflows()` guard in `append_obj_to_pack()` and an `EXPENSIVE`-style test exercising the 4 GiB+ local-base path. On 4/4 he asked whether downstream functions silently validate only a truncated prefix; Elia resolved this by showing that `check_object_signature()` fires first with a spurious corruption error. Elia has accepted all of these points: he plans to defer 1/4 and 4/4 until the dependent `odb_write_object()` and `parse_object_buffer()`/`fsck_object()` conversion series land, and to resend 2/4 and 3/4 standalone as v3 with the overflow guard, the new test, and a condensed commit message.

The newest development is a shift in Patrick's recommendation: rather than splitting the series, he now proposes holding off the entire four-patch series until the two dependent conversion series have landed, arguing that multiple similar type-conversion series in flight fragment limited reviewing capacity and cause some to receive no review at all. No maintainer (Junio) decision on the deferral -- partial or full -- is recorded, so the sequencing question remains open.
