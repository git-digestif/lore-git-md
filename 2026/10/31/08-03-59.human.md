This is a **bugfix** for the `@{push}` revision suffix, now split into a two-patch series. The goal remains the same: align `@{push}` resolution with `git push` behavior when `push.default=simple` in triangular workflows, where the push remote differs from the branch’s upstream remote. The inconsistency causes `git status` to omit push-branch comparisons and display misleading hints.

The refactoring makes the code clearer without changing behavior, and the new logic now sits alongside the existing helper rather than nested inside the original function.
