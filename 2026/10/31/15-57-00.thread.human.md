This thread is about a four-patch series from Ravi Mistry aimed at improving Git’s HTTP transport resilience. The series addresses four gaps: an off-by-one retry counter bug, missing exponential backoff for retries, the inability to retry transient 5xx server errors, and the lack of retries for HTTP POST requests. The patches touch `http.c`, `remote-curl.c`, documentation, and tests, introducing new config options like `http.retryStatusCodes` and helper functions for retry logic.

Junio C Hamano has reviewed the first patch, which fixes the off-by-one retry counter and a race in Retry-After header parsing. His feedback is constructive and focused on a minor clarification about the removal of an early-return guard clause. The review is substantive, confirming the bug and its test coverage, and no objections have been raised yet. The series is still in early review, with no other participant positions recorded. The default set of retryable status codes (currently opt-in for 5xx errors) remains an open question. Earlier proposals in 2020 and 2024 addressed subsets of these issues but did not land.
