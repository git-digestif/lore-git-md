This thread follows Ravi Mistry's five-patch v4 series improving Git's HTTP transport resilience, addressing an off-by-one retry bug, adding exponential backoff, making retry status codes configurable via `http.retryStatusCodes`, extending retries to read-only POST requests (fetch/clone but not push), and changing the default `http.retryAfter` to 10 seconds. After earlier rounds of feedback from Patrick Steinhardt and brian m. carlson were incorporated into v4, Junio C Hamano has now individually approved patches 1/5 through 4/5 and, in his latest note on the cover letter, signaled that he found little to object to in the series overall, though he noted his own limited comfort with the HTTP subsystem. The only patch still awaiting his specific review is 5/5, which changes the default retry delay and adds handling for explicit `Retry-After: 0` headers.

The series remains under review and has not yet been queued for integration. One technical loose end from Junio's review of patch 4/5 remains open: whether the `slot_results` structure in `post_rpc()` should be reset before the retry loop to avoid stale values if a slot fails to start. Otherwise, the architectural direction appears settled, with the maintainer accepting the configurable retry codes as a pragmatic workaround for provider infrastructure that returns non-429 errors when overloaded.
