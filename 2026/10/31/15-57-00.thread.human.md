The thread presents Ravi Mistry's v4 five-patch series aimed at hardening Git's HTTP transport against transient failures, addressing an off-by-one retry bug, adding exponential backoff for 429 responses lacking Retry-After headers, enabling retries for 5xx errors, and allowing safe retries of POST requests during fetch operations. The series remains under review with Junio C Hamano, with v4 specifically resolving Patrick Steinhardt's substantive technical concerns from v3: retry counters now use `size_t`, POST retries are whitelisted to read-only services like `git-upload-pack` while explicitly excluding push, and explicit `Retry-After: 0` headers are handled via raw header inspection. Patch 2/5 details the exponential backoff mechanism itself, computing delays as `http.retryAfter * 2^(attempt-1)` capped at `http.maxRetryTime`, though the default initial delay remains zero in patches 1-4 with the 10-second default deferred to patch 5/5 -- a two-phase structure Junio previously questioned but which the author has retained. Open questions persist around whether configurable retry status codes represent a sound long-term design versus standardizing on 429, and the series awaits final integration decisions.
