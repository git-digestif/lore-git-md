This is the third patch in Elia Pinto’s four-patch **bugfix series** targeting 64-to-32 truncations on Windows (LLP64 platforms). It converts the file-conversion helpers in `convert.c`, `attr.c`, and `read-cache.c` from `unsigned long` to `size_t` to prevent silent misbehavior when Git processes files larger than 4 GiB.

On Windows, where `unsigned long` is 32 bits, the current code truncates file sizes above 4 GiB, causing incorrect binary detection, CRLF handling, and keyword expansion. For example, a file that is binary after the first 4 GiB might be misclassified as text, leading to unwanted line-ending conversions or skipped keyword expansion. The patch also fixes a crash risk in `read_blob_data_from_index()` when `core.autocrlf` is enabled and the staged blob exceeds 4 GiB.

The changes are mechanical type conversions in four files (`attr.c`, `convert.c`, `read-cache-ll.h`, `read-cache.c`), with no behavior change on 64-bit Linux. The patch builds on prior work by Jeff King and Johannes Schindelin and is part of a broader effort to eliminate Windows-specific truncations. No new symbols are introduced, and the on-disk format remains unchanged. The series has no remaining technical objections and appears ready for integration.
