Okhuomon Ajayi has posted a five-patch v2 series that removes implicit uses of the global `the_repository` from the notes subsystem by storing a `struct repository *` in `struct notes_tree` and threading it through the read, write, prune, ref-glob, and combine paths in `notes.c` and `notes.h`. The series is under active review with no merge status; Patrick Steinhardt's explicit confirmation that the remaining `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` guard can stay scoped to functions tied to the global `default_notes_tree` is the gating item before the next iteration. Junio C Hamano has also flagged a version-labeling collision with earlier iterations and directed that the next posting be labeled "v5", which Okhuomon has confirmed she will use.

Review has converged on one remaining correctness bug: `copy_note()` in patch 3/5 dereferences `t->repo->hash_algo` without first handling a NULL `t`. Junio confirmed this is the only unsafe conversion, after Christian Couder corrected the safety argument for patch 2/5 -- the guarantee comes from public entry points substituting `&default_notes_tree` for NULL rather than from individual helper reachability. In her latest reply, Okhuomon accepts Junio's suggestion to factor the repeated NULL-check pattern into a single static helper named `notes_tree_or_default()`, proposed as a prep patch at the front of v5; routing `copy_note()` through it directly fixes the bug. The v5 bundle will also include doc comments for `notes_tree_init()`'s repository and flags parameters, the call-path safety argument in patch 2/5's commit message, and `notes-cache.c` added to the cover letter's list of remaining unconverted code.
