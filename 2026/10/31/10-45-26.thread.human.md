This thread began as a straightforward refactoring to remove implicit uses of the global `the_repository` in `notes-utils.c`, part of the ongoing effort to eliminate the `the_repository` global variable. Okhuomon Ajayi, an Outreachy intern, sent a two-patch series (v2) that mechanically updated `create_notes_commit()`, `commit_notes()`, and `init_copy_notes_for_rewrite()` to accept a `struct repository *` parameter, along with their callers in `builtin/am.c`, `builtin/notes.c`, and `sequencer.c`. The series received positive feedback from Patrick Steinhardt and Karthik Nayak, who confirmed its mechanical correctness and noted it restored the intended state after an earlier automated Coccinelle change.

However, Junio C Hamano’s recent review has shifted the thread’s focus to a fundamental architectural issue. He argues that the `notes-utils.h` API is not truly repository-agnostic, as the underlying functions in `notes.h` (e.g., `write_notes_tree()`, `init_notes()`, `add_note()`) still hardcode `the_repository`. This creates a false abstraction—functions appear to support arbitrary repositories but internally rely on global state. Junio proposes wrapping the entire `notes-utils.h` API in `#ifdef USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` to prevent misuse until `notes.h` is properly refactored, effectively blocking the series in its current form. This feedback challenges the assumption that the API can be incrementally made repository-agnostic and raises broader questions about how to properly abstract the notes subsystem.

The series is now paused while the author and reviewers (Patrick Steinhardt, Karthik Nayak) consider how to address Junio’s concerns. The thread has moved from a mechanical refactoring to an architectural discussion about the correct path forward for the notes subsystem. No further iterations of the patch series are expected until this is resolved.
