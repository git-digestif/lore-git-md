This thread is a small but focused refactoring effort by Outreachy intern Okhuomon Ajayi to remove implicit uses of Git’s global `the_repository` variable in the notes subsystem. The v2 series, now complete, updates three functions in `notes-utils.c`—`create_notes_commit()`, `commit_notes()`, and `init_copy_notes_for_rewrite()`—to explicitly accept a `struct repository *` parameter, along with their callers in `builtin/am.c`, `builtin/notes.c`, and `sequencer.c`. The changes are purely mechanical, restoring the intended state after an automated Coccinelle rule reintroduced `the_repository` references in 2024.

Patrick Steinhardt’s initial feedback on v1 was addressed in full: the series now removes the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` macro entirely, and the author has committed to batching future patches into larger series. Karthik Nayak’s review of v2 confirms the refactoring is correct but remains surface-level, focusing only on mechanical accuracy rather than deeper design or behavioral implications. The patches touch no new CLI options, config keys, or on-disk formats, and no test coverage changes were needed.

The series is under review with no open technical objections, but the author has noted a potential follow-up: passing the repository parameter consistently through `copy()` in `builtin/notes.c`. For now, the patches are ready for maintainer attention as part of the broader `the_repository` removal effort led by René Scharfe.
