This thread is about a six-patch series from Outreachy intern Okhuomon Ajayi that refactors Git’s notes subsystem to remove implicit uses of the global `the_repository` variable. The goal is to make the notes API repository-agnostic by storing the repository in `struct notes_tree` and updating all related functions to use it, addressing a long-standing architectural concern raised by Patrick Steinhardt.

The series is now complete and under active review, with no patches merged yet. It incrementally refactors `notes.c` and `notes-utils.c`, removing the need for downstream guards like `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` in most places. However, one guard remains in `notes.c` itself, which Patrick Steinhardt questioned in his review. Okhuomon’s latest reply clarifies that this guard persists because the series was scoped to code paths that already receive a `struct notes_tree *`, leaving out functions tied to the global `default_notes_tree` or those that don’t propagate a repository parameter. This includes `display_notes()`, `expand_loose_notes_ref()`, and hash-size helpers, which still rely on global state. The explanation positions the series as a well-scoped step forward, with the remaining guard acknowledged as a limitation for future work.

Key participants include Okhuomon Ajayi, who authored the series and is expected to respond further to feedback, and Patrick Steinhardt, whose review suggests interface improvements and questions the remaining guard. René Scharfe, the primary driver of the broader `the_repository` removal effort, is likely to review soon, focusing on whether the repository field and parameters are correctly initialized. Karthik Nayak’s position remains unknown. The series is part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal project and replaces an earlier protective measure with a direct refactoring of the notes API.
