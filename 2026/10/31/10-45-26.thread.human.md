Okhuomon Ajayi is driving a five-patch v2 series that refactors Git's notes subsystem to stop relying on the global `the_repository`, part of the broader `the_repository` removal effort. The series adds a `struct repository *repo` field to `struct notes_tree` and progressively threads the repository through `notes.c`: patches 1/5 through 3/5 converted initialization, the core read path, and the write/prune paths, and the newly posted 4/5 makes the ref glob helpers `string_list_add_refs_by_glob()` and `string_list_add_refs_from_colon_sep()` take an explicit repository parameter, with current callers passing `the_repository` as a stopgap. The final patch 5/5 will convert the `combine_notes_fn` callbacks.

Patrick Steinhardt's interface feedback (function renames and parameter order) has been incorporated, and Christian Couder's two substantive review points are resolved -- the `copy_note()` change moved to patch 3/5 and the problematic sixth patch dropped. The series is reframed as preparatory work, with the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` guard intentionally left in `notes.c` and `notes-utils.[c,h]` for functions still tied to the global `default_notes_tree` or lacking repository propagation. The key open item is Patrick Steinhardt's explicit confirmation that this scoping is acceptable before the next iteration; reviews from René Scharfe and Karthik Nayak are still pending. The series remains under active review with no merge status in `seen`, `next`, or `master`.
