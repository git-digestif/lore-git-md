Okhuomon Ajayi has posted a six-patch v2 series that removes implicit uses of the global `the_repository` from Git's notes subsystem by storing the repository inside `struct notes_tree` and threading explicit `struct repository *` parameters through the notes API. The series, authored as part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort, updates core functions in `notes.c` and `notes-utils.c` while incorporating Patrick Steinhardt's interface feedback on renames like `notes_tree_init()` and `notes_tree_load()`. It remains under active review with no merge status.

Following substantive review from Christian Couder, Okhuomon has now agreed to drop patch 6/6 -- which would have removed the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` guard from `notes-utils.[c,h]` -- and instead limit the next iteration to the first five patches. She will reframe the cover letter as a preparatory step that explicitly enumerates the remaining global dependencies across `notes.c`, `notes-utils.[c,h]`, and `notes-merge.[c,h]`, including the hash-size helpers that still rely on `the_hash_algo`. She has also committed to moving the `copy_note()` hash-algorithm change from patch 5/6 to patch 3/6 to keep commit titles aligned with their contents.

Patrick Steinhardt's question about whether the series could eliminate the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` guard in `notes.c` entirely remains unresolved, though the current trajectory favors incremental progress with clear documentation of remaining work over partial guard removal that spans the `notes.c`/`notes-utils.c` boundary. René Scharfe and Karthik Nayak have not yet weighed in.
