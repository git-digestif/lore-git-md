Okhuomon Ajayi has posted a complete six-patch v2 series that refactors the notes subsystem to store a `struct repository *` inside `struct notes_tree` and eliminate implicit uses of the global `the_repository` in `notes.c` and `notes-utils.c`. The series is part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort led by René Scharfe, and it directly addresses Patrick Steinhardt's architectural concern by making the notes API repository-agnostic rather than papering over the problem with downstream guards. The v2 iteration already incorporates Patrick's requested interface changes, renaming `init_notes()` to `notes_tree_init()` with the tree as its first parameter and `load_notes_trees()` to `notes_tree_load()`, and updating the combine-notes callbacks to accept an explicit repository pointer.

The series remains under active review with no merge status; it has not appeared in `seen`, `next`, or `master`. The central open question is whether Patrick Steinhardt will accept the intentional retention of the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` guard in `notes.c` for the small set of functions that still depend on the global `default_notes_tree` or lack a propagated repository parameter -- specifically `display_notes()`, `expand_loose_notes_ref()`, and the hash-size helpers. Okhuomon's latest email explicitly asks Patrick to confirm that this scoping is acceptable before she posts the v2 series rebased on current `master`, which would gate whether a v3 is needed to eliminate the guard entirely or address the `default_notes_tree` global. René Scharfe's and Karthik Nayak's reviews are still pending, and temporary `the_repository` uses in callers such as `builtin/notes.c` and `notes-cache.c` are deferred to future work.
