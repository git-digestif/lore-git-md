This thread is a small but focused refactoring effort by Outreachy intern Okhuomon Ajayi to remove implicit uses of Git’s global `the_repository` variable in the notes subsystem. The work is part of René Scharfe’s ongoing project to eliminate `the_repository` entirely, making the codebase more modular and testable.

The series started with a v1 that updated two functions in `notes-utils.c` but left the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` macro in place due to a dependency on `init_copy_notes_for_rewrite()`. Patrick Steinhardt reviewed the first version, noting that the macro should be removed entirely and suggesting that future patches be batched into larger series. Okhuomon responded by expanding the change in v2 to cover `init_copy_notes_for_rewrite()` and its callers in `builtin/am.c`, `builtin/notes.c`, and `sequencer.c`, fully removing the macro. The update is purely mechanical—no behavior changes, new options, or test coverage were introduced—and the diff is minimal (18 lines across five files).

The patch is now complete from a technical standpoint, addressing all feedback and leaving no loose ends in the notes subsystem itself. Okhuomon has flagged a potential follow-up in `builtin/notes.c` (`copy()` could pass its repository parameter down to `init_copy_notes_for_rewrite()`), but this is explicitly deferred. The series is under review and appears ready to merge, though no maintainer has yet weighed in on v2. If accepted, it will be a small but clean step forward in the larger `the_repository` removal effort.
