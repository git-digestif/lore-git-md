This thread tracks Okhuomon Ajayi's six-patch v5 series refactoring Git's notes subsystem to store a `struct repository *` inside `struct notes_tree` rather than relying on the global `the_repository`, part of the broader effort to eliminate that global variable. The series is currently under review with no indication of integration into any of Junio's branches. The latest email, patch 1/6, introduces the static helper `notes_tree_or_default()` to centralize the substitution of `&default_notes_tree` for NULL tree pointers across six public entry points, a prerequisite that also fixes the `copy_note()` NULL-dereference bug identified in the previous iteration.

Ajayi, an Outreachy intern, has incorporated feedback from Junio C Hamano—who flagged the version-labeling collision and the `copy_note()` bug—and from Christian Couder, whose correction about `load_subtree()` reachability is now reflected in the commit messages. Patches 2 through 6 progressively thread the repository pointer through initialization, read, write, prune, ref-globbing, and note-combining paths, while intentionally leaving `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` in place for functions tied to the default tree or awaiting further propagation.

What remains open is Patrick Steinhardt's explicit confirmation that the remaining scope of the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` guard is acceptable before further work proceeds, along with pending reviews from Rene Scharfe and Karthik Nayak. Documented future work includes converting `notes-cache.c`, display-notes code, hash-size helpers, and `notes-merge.[c,h]`.
