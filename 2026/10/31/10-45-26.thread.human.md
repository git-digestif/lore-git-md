This thread concerns a six-patch v2 series from Outreachy intern Okhuomon Ajayi that refactors Git's notes subsystem to store a `struct repository *` inside `struct notes_tree` rather than relying on the global `the_repository`. The series is part of the broader `the_repository` removal effort and is currently under review, with no merge to `seen`, `next`, or `master` yet.

Patrick Steinhardt, whose architectural feedback shaped the v2 renames (such as `notes_tree_init()` and `notes_tree_load()`), questioned why the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` guard remains in `notes.c` for functions tied to the global `default_notes_tree`. Okhuomon replied seeking explicit confirmation that this limited scope is acceptable before rebasing. Christian Couder has now added a contrasting proposal: rather than partially removing the guard, he suggests dropping patch 6/6 entirely so that both `notes.c` and `notes-utils.[c,h]` retain their guards consistently, and reframing the series in the cover letter as a preparatory step that enumerates remaining work across `notes.c`, `notes-utils.c`, and `notes-merge.c`.

The series remains open pending Patrick Steinhardt's response to the scoping question and resolution of Christian Couder's organizational suggestion. René Scharfe and Karthik Nayak have not yet weighed in.
