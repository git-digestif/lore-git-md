This thread is about a small but focused refactoring patch from Okhuomon Ajayi, an Outreachy intern, as part of the ongoing effort to remove Git’s global `the_repository` variable. The patch updates two functions in `notes-utils.c`—`create_notes_commit()` and `commit_notes()`—to use the `struct repository *` parameter they already receive instead of implicitly relying on the global. The change is purely mechanical, replacing `the_repository` with the passed `r` in a few internal calls, and makes no behavioral changes.

The patch is well-scoped and aligns with the project’s priority of eliminating `the_repository`, though it retains a `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` guard because `init_copy_notes_for_rewrite()` still depends on the global. No review feedback has arrived yet, so its readiness is still unconfirmed. If accepted, this will be one of many incremental steps toward fully removing the global variable from the notes subsystem.
