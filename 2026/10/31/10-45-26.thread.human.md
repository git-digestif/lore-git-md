This thread is a small but illustrative part of Git’s ongoing effort to remove the global `the_repository` variable. Okhuomon Ajayi, an Outreachy intern, initially sent a patch to make two functions in `notes-utils.c` use an explicitly passed `struct repository *` instead of the global. The change was purely mechanical and uncontroversial, but Patrick Steinhardt noted that the patch didn’t go far enough—it retained `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` because `init_copy_notes_for_rewrite()` still depended on the global. He also asked Ajayi to batch future patches into larger series (5–10 commits) to reduce reviewer overhead.

Ajayi has now replied, agreeing to send a v2 that fully removes `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` by extending the change to `init_copy_notes_for_rewrite()` and its callers. The thread remains straightforward: no technical objections were raised, and the follow-up will address the mechanical completeness of the refactoring. The author has also committed to adjusting their workflow to align with reviewer expectations. The patch is still under review, with no merge or integration status yet.
