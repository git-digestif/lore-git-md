This thread is Okhuomon Ajayi's five-patch v2 series removing implicit uses of the global `the_repository` from Git's notes subsystem by storing a `struct repository *` in `struct notes_tree` and threading it through the read, write, prune, ref-glob, and combine paths in `notes.c` and `notes.h`. It is part of the broader `the_repository` removal effort and is framed as a preparatory step, with the remaining `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` guard intentionally scoped to functions tied to the global `default_notes_tree`, and further conversions in `notes-cache.c`, `notes-utils.[c,h]`, and `notes-merge.[c,h]` enumerated as future work. The series remains under active review with no merge status in any integration branch.

Review has been substantive and largely converging. Christian Couder corrected the safety argument for patch 2/5, and Junio C Hamano accepted the corrected call-path reasoning, confirming that the five core functions converted there are protected by public entry points substituting `&default_notes_tree` for NULL `t`. The one confirmed defect is a NULL-dereference in `copy_note()` (patch 3/5), which Okhuomon Ajayi has now acknowledged and committed to fixing in the next iteration with the standard guard `if (!t) t = &default_notes_tree(); assert(t->initialized);`.

Still open before the next posting: Patrick Steinhardt's explicit confirmation that the scoping of the remaining guard is acceptable, plus the agreed cleanups for v5 -- the `copy_note()` fix, doc comments for the `notes_tree_init()` parameters, and adding `notes-cache.c` to the cover letter's list of remaining work. Per Junio's housekeeping note about a version-labeling collision, the next iteration will be labeled v5, which Okhuomon Ajayi has confirmed.
