This thread is about Okhuomon Ajayi’s six-patch series (v2) refactoring Git’s notes subsystem to remove implicit dependencies on the global `the_repository` variable. The goal is to make the notes API repository-agnostic by storing the repository in `struct notes_tree` and updating all notes functions to use it. This approach directly addresses Patrick Steinhardt’s architectural concern and replaces the earlier protective measure patch, which was withdrawn.

The series is under active review, with no patches merged yet. Patch 1/6 (adding the `repo` field to `struct notes_tree` and updating `init_notes()`/`load_notes_trees()`) laid the groundwork, while patch 2/6—just posted—mechanically updates five core functions in `notes.c` to use `t->repo` instead of `the_repository`. The changes are clean, focused, and maintain the same behavior, making the API more flexible without guards. René Scharfe (the `the_repository` removal driver) and Patrick Steinhardt are likely to review, given their involvement in the architectural discussion. Karthik Nayak’s position remains unknown.

The series is well-structured, with each patch building on the previous one. The remaining patches will address temporary uses of `the_repository` in callers and remove the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` guard entirely. Some follow-up work (e.g., `display_notes()`, `expand_loose_notes_ref()`, and `the_hash_algo` sizes) may be tackled in a future series. The cover letter explicitly ties the work back to the earlier discussion, and the patches reflect careful attention to detail. No merge status yet; the series is still cooking.
