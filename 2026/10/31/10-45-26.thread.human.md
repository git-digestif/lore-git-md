Okhuomon Ajayi is iterating on a five-patch series (currently labeled v2, though Junio C Hamano notes it should have been v4 due to earlier version-label collisions in the thread) that removes implicit uses of the global `the_repository` from Git's notes subsystem. The approach stores a `struct repository *` inside `struct notes_tree` and threads it through the core read, write, prune, ref-glob, and combine paths in `notes.c`, converting functions like `note_tree_find()`, `write_notes_tree()`, and the `combine_notes_fn` combinators to use `t->repo` instead. The remaining `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` guard is intentionally kept for functions still tied to the global `default_notes_tree`. The series is under active review with no merge status; Christian Couder's earlier feedback has been addressed.

Junio C Hamano's latest review of the cover letter endorses the "preparatory series" framing and adds `notes-cache.c` to the inventory of files still needing conversion, noting that `notes_cache_match_validity()`, `notes_cache_write()`, `notes_cache_get()`, and `notes_cache_put()` still reference `the_repository`. He reiterates a NULL-dereference risk in `copy_note()` (patch 3/5), where a NULL `t` could be dereferenced without falling back to `default_notes_tree`, and previously flagged a similar concern in `note_tree_find()` regarding safe NULL handling. He otherwise judges the series to be in good shape.

Before the next iteration, Okhuomon is waiting for Patrick Steinhardt's explicit confirmation that the scoping of the remaining guard is acceptable. The author will also need to address the NULL-safety issues Junio identified and verify that converted functions assume non-NULL, initialized `t`. Per Junio's housekeeping note, the next version should be sent as "v5" to keep the sequence unambiguous for tools like `b4 am`. René Scharfe and Karthik Nayak have not yet weighed in.
