This thread is a six-patch refactoring series by Outreachy intern Okhuomon Ajayi that removes implicit uses of Git’s global `the_repository` variable in the notes subsystem. The goal is to make the notes API repository-agnostic by storing the repository in `struct notes_tree` and updating all notes functions to use it, eliminating the need for downstream guards. The series is now complete and under active review, with no patches merged yet.

Key participants include Okhuomon Ajayi, who posted the series and clarified its scope, and Patrick Steinhardt, whose review suggested interface improvements—renaming `init_notes()` to `notes_tree_init()` and `load_notes_trees()` to `notes_tree_load()`—which Okhuomon has accepted. The series addresses Patrick’s architectural concern by fixing the root cause of unsafe functions, though it leaves the global `default_notes_tree` in `notes.c` as follow-up work. René Scharfe, the primary driver of the broader `the_repository` removal effort, is expected to review, while Karthik Nayak’s position remains unknown.

The refactoring touches `notes.c`, `notes-utils.c`, and their callers, updating function signatures to use `struct notes_tree->repo` or explicit `struct repository *` parameters. The remaining `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` guard in `notes.c` is scoped to functions relying on the global `default_notes_tree` or lacking repository propagation, a limitation acknowledged as future work. No merge status has been reported, and the series remains in review.
