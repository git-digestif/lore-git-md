Okhuomon Ajayi has completed the five-patch v2 series aimed at removing implicit uses of the global `the_repository` from Git's notes subsystem. The series adds a `struct repository *repo` field to `struct notes_tree` and systematically threads that repository through the core read, write, prune, ref-glob, and combine paths in `notes.c`, replacing direct uses of `the_repository` and `the_hash_algo` with `t->repo`. The just-posted final patch (5/5) updates the `combine_notes_fn` callback signature to accept an explicit repository pointer and converts all four built-in combinators, marking the last major piece of the API as repository-agnostic while leaving the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` guard in place for functions still tied to the global `default_notes_tree`.

Patrick Steinhardt's earlier architectural feedback has been incorporated through renames like `notes_tree_init()` and `notes_tree_load()` with the agreed parameter ordering, and Christian Couder's two substantive review points were resolved by dropping the former patch 6/6 and reframing the cover letter as preparatory work. The series remains under active review with no integration status; Okhuomon is explicitly waiting for Patrick Steinhardt's confirmation that the remaining scope of the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` guard -- limited to display-notes helpers and functions depending on the global `default_notes_tree` -- is acceptable before considering any further iteration. René Scharfe and Karthik Nayak have yet to weigh in on this version.
