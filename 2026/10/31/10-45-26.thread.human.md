Okhuomon Ajayi has sent the sixth and final patch of her v5 series, which removes the remaining implicit use of the global `the_repository` from the notes subsystem. This last patch updates the `combine_notes_fn` typedef and all four built-in combinators -- concatenate, overwrite, ignore, and cat_sort_uniq -- to take an explicit `struct repository *` as their first parameter, so that note-combining operations during `note_tree_insert()` act on the repository that actually owns the notes tree. With this, the six-patch series has converted the notes API's read, write, prune, copy, ref-glob, and combine paths to use `t->repo` instead of the global, while intentionally leaving `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` guards in place for functions still tied to the default tree or awaiting further propagation.

The series remains under review; there is no indication it has been queued in `seen`, `next`, or `master`. Patrick Steinhardt's confirmation that the remaining guard scoping is acceptable is still the key open item, and Junio C Hamano's final review of v5 has not yet appeared. The cover letter notes that future work remains in `notes-cache.c`, the display-notes code, hash-size helpers, and `notes-merge.[c,h]`, but the structural foundation -- storing the repository in `struct notes_tree` and threading it through the core operations -- is now complete in this iteration.
