This thread is a small refactoring patch from Outreachy intern Okhuomon Ajayi as part of the ongoing effort to remove the global `the_repository` variable. The patch updates two functions in `notes-utils.c` to use an explicit `struct repository *` parameter instead of the global, a purely mechanical change that doesn’t alter behavior. The patch is under review and hasn’t received any technical feedback yet.

Patrick Steinhardt reviewed the patch and, while he didn’t challenge its correctness, expressed frustration that it didn’t go further to remove `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` entirely—even though the commit message explains this is blocked by another function’s dependency on the global. More notably, he asked the author to slow down and batch future patches into larger series (5–10 commits) to reduce reviewer overhead, noting that multiple small patches from the same contributor in the same subsystem are difficult to manage. The patch itself remains uncontested, but the thread now hinges on whether the author will adjust their workflow in response. No decisions or merges have been made yet.
