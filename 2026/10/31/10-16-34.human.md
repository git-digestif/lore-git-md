This is the third patch in Elia Pinto’s four-patch series fixing 64-to-32 truncations on Windows (LLP64 platforms). It targets the file-conversion subsystem (`convert.c`, `attr.c`, and related helpers) where buffer sizes are passed as `unsigned long` but originate from `size_t` values. On Windows, this truncates files larger than 4 GiB, causing incorrect binary detection, CRLF handling, and keyword expansion.

The patch mechanically converts the buffer-size parameters in `gather_stats()`, `gather_convert_stats()`, `gather_convert_stats_ascii()`, `count_ident()`, and `read_blob_data_from_index()` from `unsigned long` to `size_t`. It also removes the `cast_size_t_to_ulong()` wrapper in `read_blob_data_from_index()`, which was the immediate cause of the crash in `git add` for staged files over 4 GiB when `core.autocrlf` is enabled. The changes are confined to four files and do not alter behavior on 64-bit Linux.

This patch directly addresses the third of the four truncation sites identified in the cover letter. The fix is well-motivated, narrowly scoped, and follows the same pattern as the other patches in the series. No objections remain, and the patch appears ready for integration.
