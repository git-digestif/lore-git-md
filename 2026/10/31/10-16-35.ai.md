This is the fourth and final patch in Elia Pinto’s v3 series fixing 64-to-32 truncations on Windows (LLP64 platforms). It converts the counters in `struct text_stat` and the `count_ident()` helper from `unsigned`/`int` to `size_t` to eliminate a theoretical overflow risk when processing files larger than 4 GiB. The change is purely mechanical: six lines of type conversion in `convert.c`, with no behavior change on 64-bit Linux but a preemptive fix for platforms where `size_t` can exceed 32 bits. The commit message now avoids overstating the risk and simplifies references to `st_add()`/`st_mult()`, addressing Patrick Steinhardt’s feedback from v2. No test is added because triggering the overflow would require impractical input sizes (>4 GiB), but the fix is a logical extension of the series’ goal to harden Git against large-file truncations. The patch is uncontroversial and appears ready for integration.
