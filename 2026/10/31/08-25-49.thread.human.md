This thread is about a two-patch bugfix series from Srujith A Poojary that ensures commit abbreviations in submodule diff headers are checked against the submodule’s object database rather than the superproject’s. This prevents potential collisions where the same abbreviated commit ID could refer to different objects in the two repositories. The series is part of Git’s broader libification effort and reduces reliance on the global `the_repository`.

The first patch, unchanged since v3, fixes the core issue by introducing `strbuf_repo_add_unique_abbrev()` and using `o->repo` as the fallback repository. The second patch, new in v4, adds SHA-256 test coverage to make the test hash-agnostic, addressing Junio’s feedback. The series is now technically complete, with both patches marked for queuing by Junio. No open questions or loose ends remain.

Key participants include Okhuomon Ajayi, who provided surface-level reviews on earlier versions, and Junio, who accepted the series with a minor suggestion about test hash-agnosticism, now fully addressed. The patches touch `submodule.c` and `t/t4041-diff-submodule-option.sh`, and the fix is ready for integration.
