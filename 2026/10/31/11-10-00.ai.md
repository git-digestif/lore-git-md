This is the fourth and final patch in Elia Pinto’s four-patch series fixing object-size truncation on 32-bit platforms. It converts the message-buffer length parameters in `tag.c`, `fmt-merge-msg.c`, and `ref-filter.c` from `unsigned long` to `size_t` so that large tags and commit messages (2 GiB or more) are processed without truncation. The patch also fixes an integer-overflow bug in `copy_subject()` by changing its loop index from `int` to `size_t`, ensuring correct indexing on all platforms. Three files are touched: `fmt-merge-msg.c`, `ref-filter.c`, and `tag.c`, with five lines changed in total. No tests are included because triggering the bug requires objects larger than 2 GiB in memory. The series as a whole is now complete and ready for substantive review.
