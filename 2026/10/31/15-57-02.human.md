This is the second patch in Ravi Mistry’s four-part series improving Git’s HTTP transport resilience. It introduces exponential backoff for HTTP 429 responses when the server does not supply a Retry-After header.

The patch modifies `http.c` to pass the 1-based retry attempt count into `handle_rate_limit_retry()`, where it doubles the configured `http.retryAfter` delay on each subsequent attempt (1 s, 2 s, 4 s, …) up to the `http.maxRetryTime` cap. The documentation (`http.retryAfter` and `http.maxRetryTime`) is updated to reflect the new behavior, and three new tests in `t/t5584-http-429-retry.sh` verify the doubling logic, the clamping at `http.maxRetryTime`, and the continued precedence of server-provided Retry-After headers.

The change is well-motivated: without exponential backoff, a short fixed delay burns through retries too quickly, while a long delay penalizes even brief glitches. The implementation is clean, the tests are thorough, and the commit message clearly explains the rationale and edge cases. No controversy is expected; this is a straightforward improvement to an existing retry mechanism.
