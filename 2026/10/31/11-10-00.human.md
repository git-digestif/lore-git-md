This is the fourth and final patch in Elia Pinto’s four-part bugfix series addressing object-size truncation on 32-bit platforms. It converts the last remaining message-buffer length parameters from `unsigned long` to `size_t` in the tag, fmt-merge-msg, and ref-filter subsystems.

The patch touches three files: `fmt-merge-msg.c`, `ref-filter.c`, and `tag.c`. It updates the signatures of `fmt_tag_signature()`, `copy_subject()`, `append_lines()`, and `run_gpg_verify()` to accept `size_t` instead of `unsigned long`, and changes the loop index in `copy_subject()` from `int` to `size_t` to prevent overflow on subjects larger than 2 GiB. The user-visible symptom on 32-bit platforms is that `git tag -v` inspects only a prefix of large tags and fails to find signatures placed at the end.

The changes are mechanical and do not alter behavior on 64-bit platforms. No new tests are included, as triggering the bug requires processing objects or messages larger than 2 GiB. The series as a whole is now complete and under review; it applies cleanly alongside other in-flight work and does not depend on any follow-up patches.
