This is a substantive design discussion reply in the ongoing `sigchain` race-condition thread. Phillip Wood acknowledges Oswald Buddenhagen’s clarification that SIGPIPE (and four other signals: SIGBUS, SIGFPE, SIGILL, SIGSEGV) are thread-directed rather than process-directed, meaning they target specific threads rather than the entire process. This distinction forces a rethink of the earlier plan to block all signals during thread creation: blocking thread-directed signals could prevent the process from being killed if one of them occurs in a worker thread. Phillip raises the possibility that concurrent unwinding of the `sigchain` stack for SIGPIPE could still happen in practice, and tentatively suggests using an atomic flag in the signal handler to serialize unwinding. The thread remains in the design-proposal stage, with no v2 posted yet; the next step is still awaiting feedback from Jeff King and Junio.
