This thread is about a bugfix patch that resolves a data corruption issue in `git pack-objects` on 32-bit platforms where `long` is 32 bits but `size_t` is larger (e.g., 64-bit Windows). The bug causes blob sizes of 4GiB or larger to be truncated in the pack header, leading to corrupted objects during `git repack` or `git gc` when large loose blobs are pruned. The fix, now in its third version, updates variable types in `builtin/pack-objects.c` to use `size_t` and `off_t` instead of `unsigned long`, ensuring accurate size tracking for large blobs.

The patch is narrowly scoped, well-motivated, and uncontroversial, addressing a clear correctness issue without side effects. All prior feedback from reviewers—including Simon Richter and Junio C Hamano—has been incorporated. Junio has now explicitly marked the patch for queuing, signaling it is ready for integration, likely into the `next` branch for further testing. No test is included due to the difficulty of reproducing the bug (requiring ≥4GiB objects on affected platforms), but the commit message thoroughly documents the edge cases and consequences. The fix is minimal, changing only 7 lines, and does not alter the on-disk format or other `pack-objects` functionality.
