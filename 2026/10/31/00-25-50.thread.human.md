This thread is about a small but important test fix in Git’s test suite (`t5702-protocol-v2.sh`). The issue arose when newer versions of GNU coreutils (9.12+) started quoting environment variable output in `env`, breaking tests that relied on grepping for `GIT_PROTOCOL=version=2`. Jeff King (peff) proposed a targeted fix: replacing the fragile `env`-grepping logic with explicit shell-based variable printing to a trace file, making the tests resilient to quoting changes while strictly validating the expected `GIT_PROTOCOL` value.

The patch is effectively settled. Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, reviewed it and confirmed its correctness, while also clarifying the original test’s intent—fetch operations expect `version=2`, while push and archive tests verify its absence. Todd Zullinger and Collin Funk (a coreutils contributor) chimed in to endorse the approach and provide context: the coreutils quoting change was intentional but later restricted to terminal output, and Debian unstable has already applied the fix. No substantive objections or open questions remain, and the patch is ready for Junio to merge.

The only follow-up was Collin’s email reassuring the list that the coreutils GitHub mirror Todd linked to is trustworthy (restricted to coreutils/gnulib committers), though this was purely informational and didn’t affect the patch itself. The fix is a net improvement, avoiding workarounds like `QUOTING_STYLE=literal` in favor of a cleaner, more robust solution.
