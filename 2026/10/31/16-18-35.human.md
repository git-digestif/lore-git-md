This is a call for contributions to the next **Git Rev News** edition (140). Christian Couder has posted a draft of the upcoming issue on GitHub and invites the community to help refine it before publication on November 2, 2026.

The draft is available [here](https://github.com/git/git.github.io/blob/master/rev_news/drafts/edition-140.md), and contributions can be made via GitHub pull requests or by commenting on the associated [issue](https://github.com/git/git.github.io/issues/872). Feedback is welcome on any section, including proofreading, article suggestions, or links to relevant developments. Christian also encourages volunteers to participate in interviews or other editorial tasks.

This is a routine community update rather than a technical patch or discussion thread, so it doesn’t introduce new features or debates. If you’ve been involved in recent Git development and want to highlight your work—or know of interesting discussions or tools—this is a good opportunity to ensure they’re included. The tone is collaborative and low-pressure, aimed at gathering broad input before finalizing the edition.
