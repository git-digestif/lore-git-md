Thread type: bugfix patch series

Core problem or goal
Fix truncation and correctness issues when handling objects ≥4 GiB on platforms where `unsigned long` is 32-bit (notably Windows). The series converts key components in the pack-receiving and verification pipeline (`unpack-objects`, `index-pack`, `git fsck`) from `unsigned long` to `size_t` to match the underlying pack code, addressing crashes, false SHA-1 collision reports, and partial object checks.

Current status
Under review; no prior versions. The series is self-contained and merges cleanly with other in-flight work, though full correctness for non-streamed objects requires follow-up patches from related series (object parsing and ODB write sizes).
