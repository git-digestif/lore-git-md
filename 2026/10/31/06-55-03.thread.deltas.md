## 2026/11/02

- [2026/11/02/06-17-57 by SZEDER Gábor <szeder.dev@gmail.com>] SZEDER Gábor questioned why the test setup sets `core.commitGraph true` explicitly, noting that in a freshly initialized repository the commit graph has been enabled by default for a long time.
- [2026/11/02/12-46-55 by Patrick Steinhardt <ps@pks.im>] Patrick Steinhardt asked how the kernel computes its localversion, observed it uses `git rev-list --count --left-right $tag` (reimplementing parts of git-describe), and raised the downside that the proposed fix could yield different results depending on whether a recent commit-graph exists (i.e., when git-maintenance last updated it); he proposed also calling `ensure_generations_valid()` in `describe_commit()`, acknowledging it is expensive without a commit graph but asking whether that is better than silently yielding a potentially wrong result.
- [2026/11/02/14-20-25 by Derrick Stolee <stolee@gmail.com>] Derrick Stolee agreed that `ensure_generations_valid()` should be called, adding that even an existing commit-graph can be slightly behind the object database so the newest commits need generation-number fixups, and that the biggest downside is slowness when no commit-graph exists, which is typically better than an incorrect answer.
- [2026/11/02/17-03-25 by Junio C Hamano <gitster@pobox.com>] Junio C Hamano asked whether the huge cost of backfilling generation numbers in a repository without an existing or up-to-date commit-graph could be recouped by amortizing it over time, i.e., writing a commit-graph out.
- [2026/11/02/17-52-23 by Derrick Stolee <stolee@gmail.com>] Derrick Stolee liked that idea, saying the computed structure could be written out as a commit-graph with relatively small overhead, suggested the commit-graph could become an opt-out rather than opt-in feature, and noted the concern that "read only" commands would become mutators of disk state, which could be rectified with appropriate guardrails.
- [2026/11/02/18-37-57 by Linus Torvalds <torvalds@linux-foundation.org>] Linus Torvalds explained the kernel's localversion is historical and configurable (the "setlocalversion" script handles many cases and may predate "git describe"), said the commit-graph-dependent behavior is no more puzzling than the existing odd behavior, suspected `ensure_generations_valid()` is way too expensive in the bad case though big repos likely already have generation numbers, suggested maybe adding a "--exact" flag to force it while documenting that without it the numbers can be slightly odd, and argued that consistently wrong numbers are worse — it took him a week of annoyance to find why they were wrong.
