This is a brief follow-up in the ongoing ZIP archive timestamp bugfix thread. René Scharfe acknowledges Matthew E. Luallen’s test infrastructure fix for his patch that omits the ZIP Extended Timestamp (UT) extra field when commit times exceed the 32-bit signed integer range. The fix—replacing `TIME_IS_64BIT` with `TIME_T_IS_64BIT` in the test prerequisites—correctly handles 32-bit platforms where `time_t` remains 32-bit even if Git’s internal `timestamp_t` is 64-bit. With this adjustment, the patch is now technically sound and ready for integration, though René hints at a deeper question: whether `git archive` should avoid using `time_t` entirely in this context. The broader thread still has unresolved issues (ZIP legacy DOS date overflow and `fast-import` negative timestamp validation), but this exchange unblocks the UT field omission fix without altering its core behavior.
