This patch **refactors `parse_long_opt()`** to extract a reusable `lookup_long_opt()` helper, which performs the lookup part of long-option parsing without side effects (no value assignment, no `die()`/`error()` calls). The extracted function is designed for reuse in the upcoming `early_scan_options()` API, ensuring consistent handling of negated options, unambiguous abbreviations, and aliases.

The refactoring is **clean and mechanical**, splitting `parse_long_opt()` into two parts: `lookup_long_opt()` (pure lookup) and the original function (now a thin wrapper that acts on the lookup result). The new helper returns an enum (`LONG_OPT_EXACT`, `LONG_OPT_ABBREV`, `LONG_OPT_AMBIGUOUS`, or `LONG_OPT_UNKNOWN`) and populates output parameters (`found`, `other`, `stuck`) to convey the lookup result. The patch **preserves all existing behavior**, including exact-match precedence, abbreviation handling, and ambiguity error messages.
