This is the fifth patch in Julia Evans’ v3 series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page consolidating Git’s merge conflict resolution guidance. The patch surgically updates `git-cherry-pick.adoc` to replace its redundant conflict-resolution section with a cross-reference to the new guide.

The change is minimal and mechanical: it removes a vague introductory sentence about merge conflicts ("When it is not obvious how to apply a change...") and the four-step explanation of conflict markers, replacing them with a direct link to `gitmergeconflicts(7)`. The patch also adds the new guide to the `SEE ALSO` section. This aligns with the series’ goal of eliminating scattered, duplicative advice while preserving command-specific details (e.g., `CHERRY_PICK_HEAD` remains documented elsewhere in the page).
