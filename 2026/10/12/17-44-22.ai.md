The exchange does not address the deeper architectural questions Junio raised (e.g., splitting the series into separate patches for rebase and squash detection, or the long-term scalability of the CLI design), nor does it engage with D. Ben Knoble’s counterproposal to use patch IDs for rebase detection in local workflows with edited commit messages.

The email advances the thread only incrementally, confirming that Harald intends to explore `git range-diff`’s commit-matching logic but leaving open whether the series will adopt Junio’s two-phase implementation strategy or how it will handle edge cases like edited commit messages.
