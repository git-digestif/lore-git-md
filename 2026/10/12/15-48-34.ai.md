This is a **feature patch follow-up** in the ongoing `git blame` default ignore-revs series. The email is Ravi Mistry’s reply to Phillip Wood’s review of patch 2/2 (v3), addressing the three usability concerns Phillip raised: uncommitted changes handling, subdirectory support, and blob size limits.

Ravi explains that the current implementation (reading `HEAD:.git-blame-ignore-revs` rather than the working-tree file) was a deliberate choice made in v2 based on Junio’s feedback, not an oversight. He acknowledges Phillip’s point about inconsistency with `.gitattributes` but defers to Junio’s prior guidance. On subdirectory support, Ravi agrees that scanning all directories would be too expensive and aligns with the ecosystem convention of reading only the repository root. For the blob size limit, he clarifies that the `oidset` parser already filters out non-commit OIDs and duplicates, so the set cannot grow larger than the number of local commits, but offers to add an explicit size cap if Junio or Phillip prefer.

The reply keeps the series on track for integration into `next`; no new technical objections are raised, and Ravi remains flexible on the two open refinements (uncommitted changes and size cap). The thread’s next step is Junio’s final call on whether to retain the current behavior or adjust it to match Phillip’s suggestions.
