This is a **bugfix follow-up** to Xiangfei Ding’s patch addressing OpenSSL compatibility in `imap-send.c`. Aniket Gupta points out that the patch’s use of `ASN1_STRING_length()` triggers a deprecation warning in OpenSSL 4.1.0-beta2, where the function has been replaced by `ASN1_STRING_get_length()`. The warning breaks builds with `DEVELOPER=1` enabled, which is a common development setting in the Git project.

The email also notes that the patch introduces a second use of `ASN1_STRING_length()` in the error message, which would need the same update. Aniket suggests coordinating with Beat Bolli’s earlier series from September ("imap-send: future proofing and two correctness fixes"), which also modifies `host_matches()`. This implies the two efforts may overlap or conflict, and merging them cleanly could require adjustments to either or both patches.

The feedback is **surface-level but actionable**, focusing on build compatibility and patch integration rather than the core logic of the fix. The deprecation warning is a real concern for forward compatibility, and the suggestion to align with Beat Bolli’s work is pragmatic. The patch remains uncontroversial in its goal—avoiding assumptions about ASN1 string null-termination—but now needs minor tweaks to address OpenSSL’s API changes and avoid redundancy with existing work. No merge status change; still under review.
