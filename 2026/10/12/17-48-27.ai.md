This is the first patch in D. Ben Knoble’s five-patch series improving `git stash` shell completion. It **refactors** the Bash completion helper (`contrib/completion/git-completion.bash`) to replace a hard-coded list of stash subcommands with a dynamic lookup using Git’s built-in `--git-completion-helper` mechanism. The change aligns `_git_stash` with the pattern used by other completion helpers and reduces duplication, though it preserves the special-case handling of the deprecated `save` subcommand (which remains excluded from default completion). The patch is small, mechanical, and unlikely to be controversial. No behavior changes are introduced.
