This is a contributor housekeeping patch series targeting Git's stash command completion and documentation. D. Ben Knoble addresses several minor but persistent annoyances in how shell completions handle `git stash` subcommands, particularly for Zsh users.

The series starts with a small cleanup to simplify stash subcommand completion logic (patch 1), then extracts common stash listing code to reduce duplication (patch 2). Patch 3 specifically fixes a long-standing Zsh completion issue where stash names (like `stash@{0}`) weren't properly handled, which Knoble documented in a Unix & Linux StackExchange post.

The final two patches are more controversial: they propose removing all remaining references to the deprecated `git stash save` subcommand from both documentation and completion scripts. Knoble notes that while `save` has been deprecated for years, it's still being advertised in the manual and completion systems, creating inconsistency. These patches would fully remove the special-casing of `save` rather than just marking it as deprecated.

The series touches four files: the stash documentation, builtin/stash.c, and both bash/zsh completion scripts. While the first three patches appear straightforward and well-motivated, the last two may spark discussion about whether removing deprecated features from completion is appropriate or premature. The changes are small (22 insertions, 12 deletions) but address real usability paper cuts.
