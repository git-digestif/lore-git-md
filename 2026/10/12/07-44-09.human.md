This is a **revised API refactoring and bugfix series** targeting the `parse-options` subsystem. The v3 series is a **major redesign** of the early argument scanning mechanism, now reusing the existing `parse-options` infrastructure to avoid duplication and ensure consistency. The series converts `git fast-import` to use the new `early_scan_options()` helper, fixing a real bug where `--allow-unsafe-features` was silently ignored when preceded by `--depth 5`.

The v3 redesign addresses all substantive feedback from v2, including:
- **Reusing `lookup_long_opt()`** (extracted from `parse_long_opt()`) to handle negated forms, unambiguous abbreviations, and aliases the same way as `parse_options()`.
- **Stopping at short options, unknown options, ambiguous abbreviations, and low-level callbacks** to avoid misinterpreting an option’s value as another option (e.g., `--export-pack --allow-unsafe-features` now correctly treats `--allow-unsafe-features` as the value of `--export-pack-edges`).
- **Better handling of `PARSE_OPT_LASTARG_DEFAULT`** (now considered as taking a value, matching `parse_options()` behavior).
- **Improved test coverage** for edge cases like stuck values, optional arguments, and negated options.

The series is **well-structured and thoroughly tested**, with clear commit messages explaining the design trade-offs. The `git fast-import` conversion (patch 4/4) is particularly compelling, as it fixes a real-world bug and demonstrates the API’s practical value. The preparatory refactoring (patches 1-2) is clean and uncontroversial, while the core API (patch 3/4) is now more robust and consistent with `parse_options()`.

### Key improvements over v2

- The early scanner now **agrees with `parse_options()` on abbreviations and negated forms**, eliminating the behavioral inconsistencies noted in v2.
- The scan **stops at ambiguous or disallowed abbreviations**, preventing misinterpretation of option values.
- **Better documentation** of the API’s limitations and caller responsibilities in `parse-options.h` and `api-parse-options.adoc`.

### Open questions

- The API’s **stopping behavior** (e.g., at short options or low-level callbacks) may limit its utility for commands with complex early-scan needs, but this is a deliberate trade-off for simplicity and safety.
- The **division of labor** (caller vs. callee for argument skipping) remains unchanged from v2, but this is now well-documented and less likely to cause confusion.

The series is **ready for `next`** once Junio confirms the design. Maintainers of commands with hand-rolled early scans (e.g., `git bisect`, `git rev-parse`) should take note, as this API provides a safer alternative once they adopt `parse-options`. The `git fast-import` bugfix alone justifies the effort, and the API’s design is now mature enough for broader adoption.
