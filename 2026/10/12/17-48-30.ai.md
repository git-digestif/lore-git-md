This is the fourth patch in D. Ben Knoble’s five-patch series improving `git stash` shell completion. It removes the deprecated `git stash save` subcommand from the command’s `-h` usage text and the man page synopsis, while leaving the detailed description intact for users who still rely on it.

The patch touches two files: `Documentation/git-stash.adoc` (removing the synopsis line for `save`) and `builtin/stash.c` (dropping `BUILTIN_STASH_SAVE_USAGE` from the usage array). The commit message notes that `save` was re-added to the documentation in 2022, likely by oversight, and that despite its deprecation since 2017, it has continued to receive new options. The author frames this as a cleanup step, aligning the visible usage with the project’s long-standing deprecation policy.

No behavior changes or test updates are involved. The patch is small and mechanical, but the underlying question—whether to accelerate the removal of a deprecated feature—remains open for discussion. The series’ final patch (5/5) will remove completion support for `save`, making this patch a logical precursor.
