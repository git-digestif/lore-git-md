This is a **substantive review** from Junio Hamano on the RFC patch that refines Curtis Allen Smith’s work to fix a CRLF conversion bug in `read-cache.c`. The patch introduces `size_change_is_conclusive()` to avoid trusting file size changes when clean filters or CRLF conversion are active, shifting verification work from `git add` to `git status`.

Junio’s feedback focuses on two areas: **commit message clarity** and **performance implications**. He suggests minor wording improvements ("in real life" instead of "in real live", "back and forth" instead of "forth and back") and questions whether the examples given (e.g., tools rewriting files with native line endings) truly leave content unchanged or merely alter size. His most significant concern is the overhead of the new `size_change_is_conclusive()` helper, which calls `would_convert_to_git()` unconditionally. He notes that even attribute lookups (required by `would_convert_to_git()`) are not free and asks for data on the performance impact for repositories without clean filters.

Junio also clarifies the collaboration model, asking whether Torsten Bögershausen has taken over the series or is co-authoring it with Curtis. This suggests he may expect a `Co-authored-by` trailer in future iterations if the work is joint. The review is constructive and technical, with no objections to the core approach—only requests for refinement. No alternative solutions are proposed, but the performance question could become a point of contention if the overhead is measurable in large repositories. The patch remains in RFC status, and this feedback will likely shape the next revision.
