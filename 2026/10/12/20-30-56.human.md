This is a small but important compatibility patch for `git imap-send`, part of a larger series addressing OpenSSL correctness and forward-compatibility. The patch replaces the deprecated `SSLv23_method()` with the modern `TLS_method()` in the IMAP TLS setup code. OpenSSL has marked `SSLv23_method()` as deprecated since version 1.1.0, and this change ensures Git continues to build cleanly under newer OpenSSL versions without deprecation warnings.

The change is mechanical and low-risk: `TLS_method()` is the direct replacement recommended by OpenSSL, and the patch updates the error message to match. The commit message is concise and cites the deprecation timeline, making the motivation clear. This is a proactive fix that prevents future build failures, especially under `DEVELOPER=1` where deprecation warnings become errors.

This is the first patch in a two-patch v2 series; the original three-patch series has been split, with the other two patches (addressing ASN1_STRING handling and RFC 6125 compliance) still under discussion. If you care about `imap-send` or OpenSSL compatibility, this patch is straightforward and worth a quick review. The rest of the series remains unresolved due to deeper technical concerns, but this change stands on its own.
