This is a five-patch series from D. Ben Knoble targeting **shell completion** for `git stash`, with two patches also touching **documentation** and **command deprecation**.

The series starts with a small **refactoring** of the stash subcommand completion logic (patch 1), then extracts common stash-listing code to avoid duplication (patch 2). Patch 3 fixes a **Zsh-specific completion bug** where stash names (e.g., `stash@{0}`) were not being handled correctly. The final two patches address the deprecated `git stash save` subcommand: patch 4 removes mentions of it from the `git-stash` man page, and patch 5 removes its completion entirely.

The motivation for patches 4 and 5 is that `git stash save` has been deprecated since Git 2.16 (2018), yet it is still documented and completed as if it were a first-class subcommand. The author frames this as a cleanup opportunity but acknowledges it may be controversial. The changes affect `Documentation/git-stash.adoc`, `builtin/stash.c`, and both Bash and Zsh completion scripts. No tests are added or modified.

The series is well-structured, with clear commit messages and a concise cover letter. The first three patches look uncontroversial and address real usability issues (especially the Zsh fix). The last two patches are more of a policy question—whether to accelerate the removal of a deprecated feature—but the author presents them as a conversation starter rather than a demand. The patches are small, focused, and unlikely to break existing workflows.
