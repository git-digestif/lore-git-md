This thread is about a patch series from D. Ben Knoble that improves shell completion for `git stash`. The series refactors existing logic to reduce duplication, fixes a Zsh-specific bug with stash name completion (like `stash@{0}`), and removes support for the long-deprecated `git stash save` subcommand from both documentation and completion scripts. The series is now at version 2, addressing feedback from Patrick Steinhardt on the first version.

Junio C Hamano has enthusiastically endorsed the second patch, a pure refactoring of stash-listing logic, calling it a clear improvement. He also proposed an enhancement to the fifth patch—automatically rewriting `git stash save --<TAB>` to `git stash push --<TAB>` on the first tab press—but the author has declined to implement it, citing increased complexity and lack of motivation to seek consensus. Patrick Steinhardt, who reviewed the first version, has now signaled approval of the entire series in its current form, explicitly stating he is "happy with this v2." No objections have been raised to the removal of `save` completion, though the thread remains open for further feedback.

The series touches completion scripts for both Bash and Zsh, the `git-stash` man page, and the built-in stash command itself. The Zsh fix is a targeted workaround for a real usability issue, though the broader question of a unified Bash/Zsh solution remains open. The removal of `save` completion and documentation is framed as a cleanup but could be controversial, as it accelerates the deprecation of a feature still in use despite being deprecated since 2018. The series is now effectively ready for merging unless Junio or others raise concerns about the removal of `save` completion.
