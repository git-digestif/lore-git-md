This thread is a five-patch series from D. Ben Knoble aimed at improving `git stash` shell completion. The series refactors existing logic to reduce duplication, fixes a Zsh-specific bug where stash names like `stash@{0}` weren't handled correctly, and removes completion and documentation support for the deprecated `git stash save` subcommand (deprecated since 2018).

The first three patches are technical: they refactor Bash completion logic, extract common stash-listing code, and fix the Zsh bug by ensuring metacharacters in stash names are properly quoted. The last two patches are more contentious—they remove `git stash save` from the man page, usage text, and now, with this latest email, from Bash completion. The author frames this as a cleanup, noting that `save` has been deprecated for over eight years but remains documented and completable, likely due to oversight.
