Patrick also suggested documenting the role of `GIT_COMPLETION_SHOW_ALL` if completion is removed, which the author is likely to address. The only remaining open question is whether the Zsh fix could be made more portable, but the author has noted that no cross-shell solution exists for now.

The series touches completion scripts, documentation, and the stash built-in, with no on-disk format changes. No tests were added, though the Zsh fix addresses a real-world usability issue. The next version should resolve the last design questions and clarify the behavior of the `save` subcommand’s removal.
