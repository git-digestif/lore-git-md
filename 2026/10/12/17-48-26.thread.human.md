This thread is about a patch series from D. Ben Knoble that improves shell completion for `git stash`. The series refactors existing logic to reduce duplication, fixes a Zsh-specific bug with stash name completion (like `stash@{0}`), and removes support for the long-deprecated `git stash save` subcommand from both completion and documentation. The series is now at version 2, addressing all feedback from Patrick Steinhardt, who has approved the entire series.

The most notable change is the removal of `save` from completion and prominent help contexts, which the author acknowledges may be controversial but frames as a cleanup. Junio C Hamano previously endorsed the pure refactoring patch (patch 2/5) and proposed an enhancement to the `save` removal: automatically rewriting `git stash save --<TAB>` to `git stash push --<TAB>` to preserve muscle memory. The author declined to implement this, citing complexity, and Junio has not pushed back. His latest reply is a brief acknowledgment of Patrick’s approval, with no new feedback.

The series is effectively ready to merge, with no open technical objections. The only loose ends are the unresolved design question around a unified Bash/Zsh completion fix (patch 3/5) and the potential controversy around removing `save` support (patches 4/5 and 5/5), though no objections have been raised yet. The author is open to dropping those patches if they prove contentious.
