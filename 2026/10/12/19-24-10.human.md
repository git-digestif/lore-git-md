This is the third and final patch in Isabella Caselli’s three-patch series implementing `includeIf.hostname:<pattern>` for Git’s configuration system. Junio C Hamano’s brief review comment here is surface-level but positive, calling the implementation "neat" and praising the clean extensibility of the `include_condition_is_true()` helper. The patch itself is well-prepared: it adds the core logic, documentation, and test coverage for hostname-based conditional includes, uses the same glob-matching semantics as `includeIf.onbranch:`, and avoids DNS lookups or hostname normalization in line with the 2024 consensus. The only open question—whether matching should be case-sensitive—is noted in the commit message but does not block the current implementation. The series as a whole looks ready for deeper review and appears uncontroversial.
