This is the third patch in Julia Evans’ six-part documentation series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page consolidating Git’s merge conflict resolution guidance. The series is now in its final form (v3) and ready for merging, with Junio’s approval already secured for this patch if it remains unchanged.

This patch surgically edits `Documentation/git-rebase.adoc` to replace a redundant, incomplete explanation of merge conflict resolution with a cross-reference to the new `gitmergeconflicts(7)` guide. The key change is removing four lines of generic advice (e.g., how to find conflict markers with `git diff`, the need to `git add` resolved files) while preserving rebase-specific details: the three resolution paths (`--continue`, `--abort`, `--skip`) and the fact that conflicts can occur during rebasing. The patch adds a prominent link to the new guide in both the conflict-resolution section and the `SEE ALSO` block, ensuring users can easily access the comprehensive, example-driven guidance now available.

The edit is minimal and precise, reflecting the series’ overall strategy: eliminate duplication while retaining command-specific nuances. For `git rebase`, this means keeping the `--skip` option (unique to rebase) and the three-step resolution workflow, both of which are absent from the general guide. The patch also aligns with the series’ broader goal of improving user experience by centralizing scattered advice into a single, well-structured resource.

This is a well-motivated, low-risk documentation improvement. The patch has no behavioral or on-disk implications, and the cross-reference ensures users won’t lose access to critical information.
