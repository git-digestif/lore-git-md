This is the sixth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch series refactoring Git’s `rerere` subsystem to remove its implicit dependency on the global `the_repository` variable. The patch bundles the remaining global state—specifically the `rerere_autoupdate` flag, the `rerere_dirs` strmap, and the `write_lock` lockfile—into a new `rerere_cache` struct defined in `rerere.h`. The lock timeout (`rerere_lock_timeout_ms`) is intentionally left as a local variable in `setup_rerere()` since it is only used there.

The change is mechanical and well-scoped: the new `rerere_cache` struct is declared statically at the top of `rerere.c`, and all references to the former globals are updated to use the struct’s fields. The patch preserves the existing behavior while making the state explicit and easier to manage in a multi-repository context. No new user-facing functionality is introduced, and the on-disk format remains unchanged.

This is a clean, uncontroversial refactoring that fits neatly into the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The patch is likely to receive positive feedback, though reviewers may double-check that the locking semantics remain correct—particularly around `write_lock`, which is now part of the struct. The commit message is clear and functional, though it assumes familiarity with the series’ broader goal. No new tests are needed, as the patch is purely internal.
