This is patch 6/7 in Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch series that removes the `rerere` subsystem’s implicit dependency on the global `the_repository` variable. The patch bundles the remaining global state—`rerere_autoupdate`, the `rerere_dirs` strmap, and the `write_lock`—into a new `struct rerere_cache` named `cache`. The lock timeout (`rerere_lock_timeout_ms`) is intentionally left as a local variable in `setup_rerere()` because it is only used there.

The diff moves the three global variables into the struct, updates all callers in `rerere.c` to use the new `cache` instance, and adds the necessary `#include` directives to `rerere.h`. The change is purely mechanical and does not alter behavior; it simply reorganizes state to prepare for the final patch in the series. No new tests are added, and the on-disk format remains unchanged. This is a clean, uncontroversial refactoring step in the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort.
