This is a feature patch, part 4 of Patrick Steinhardt’s 7-patch series that is exposing the object storage format to users.  It adds two new user-facing knobs: `git rev-parse --show-object-storage-format` and the `objects.storageFormat` field in `git repo`.  The output matches whatever is set in `extensions.objectStorage` (or `files` if the extension is unset).  Documentation, tests, and the plumbing to convert the internal enum to a string are all included.  The patch is clean, well-motivated by the ongoing ODB abstraction effort, and looks ready for review.
