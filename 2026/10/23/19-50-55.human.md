This is a coordination reply in the ongoing `git status` index-locking thread. Jun Wu responds to Junio’s suggestion that he and Mihai Cosma collaborate on a unified patch series. Jun clarifies that the two series are independent: his own work modifies non-`status` commands to wait for the index lock, while Mihai’s focuses on reducing how long `git status` holds the lock. He argues for keeping them separate to avoid conflating distinct race conditions (his fixes read/lock ordering issues, Mihai’s addresses a `skipHash`-related data-loss bug) and notes that the only textual conflict is a single function rename. Jun’s preference is to proceed with parallel series, though he acknowledges Mihai’s `update_index_if_able()` fix is a standalone correctness patch suitable for `maint`. The reply leaves the door open for future coordination but resists merging the efforts at this stage.
