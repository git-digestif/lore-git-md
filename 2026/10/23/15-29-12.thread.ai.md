Core problem or goal

The existing test suite (`t/t1402-check-ref-format.sh`) exercises `--allow-onelevel` but omits coverage for `--no-allow-onelevel`, leaving its behavior and interaction with `--allow-onelevel` untested. The goal is to determine whether adding such tests would be a suitable Outreachy microproject.
