Core problem or goal
Replace manual integer arithmetic in `diff-delta.c:create_delta_index()` with the safe integer helpers `st_mult()`, `st_add()`, and `st_add3()` to prevent silent overflows during allocation size calculations. The patch is justified as a precautionary measure against hypothetical future changes in struct layout or bounds, after Pablo Sabater’s repeated analyses refuted the theoretical overflow risk under current conditions. The v8 commit message now incorporates Pablo’s exact corrections: `struct unpacked_index_entry` is 12 bytes on 32-bit systems (not 16), `entries` is capped at `0xfffffffeU / RABIN_WINDOW`, and `hsize` is explicitly capped at `2²⁶`. These corrections further tighten the bounds analysis, making overflow even more implausible under current conditions.
