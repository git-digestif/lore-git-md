Core problem or goal
Replace manual integer arithmetic in `diff-delta.c:create_delta_index()` with the safe integer helpers `st_mult()`, `st_add()`, and `st_add3()`. The goal is to prevent silent overflows that could corrupt memory; the only behavior change is that overflows now trigger an early NULL return instead of proceeding with an undersized buffer.
