Core problem or goal
Replace manual integer arithmetic in `diff-delta.c:create_delta_index()` with the safe integer helpers `st_mult()`, `st_add()`, and `st_add3()` to prevent silent overflows during allocation size calculations. The original motivation was to guard against overflow, but the analysis has evolved: while the existing bounds on `entries` and `hsize` prevent overflow on 32-bit platforms under the current `struct unpacked_index_entry` layout, Junio’s follow-up arithmetic shows that padding to an 8-byte boundary could still cause `4 * hsize + 16 * entries` to exceed 2³². The patch’s justification is now theoretical but real: defensive coding against a possible (if unlikely) overflow.
