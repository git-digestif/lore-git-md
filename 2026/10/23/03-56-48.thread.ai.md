Core problem or goal
Replace manual integer arithmetic in `diff-delta.c:create_delta_index()` with the safe integer helpers `st_mult()`, `st_add()`, and `st_add3()` to prevent silent overflows during allocation size calculations. The original motivation was to guard against overflow, but the author now confirms that the existing bounds on `entries` and `hsize` already prevent overflow on 32-bit platforms, making the patch’s necessity theoretical rather than practical.

Current status

The discussion has shifted to whether the patch should proceed as defensive coding or be abandoned. No v3 has been posted yet; the author is expected to update the commit message to reflect the new understanding of the overflow risk. Under review, with Junio’s original question now answered.

- File: `diff-delta.c`
