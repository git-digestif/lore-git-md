Core problem or goal
Replace manual integer arithmetic in `diff-delta.c:create_delta_index()` with the safe integer helpers `st_mult()`, `st_add()`, and `st_add3()` to prevent silent overflows during allocation size calculations. The justification now includes a detailed, step-by-step breakdown of the arithmetic and struct layout assumptions: `struct unpacked_index_entry` (12 bytes on 32-bit systems) may be padded to 16 bytes, `entries` and `hsize` are bounded by `0xfffffffeU / RABIN_WINDOW` (≈2²⁶), and `hsize + 1` cannot overflow. The patch documents this struct-padding scenario as a plausible (if unobserved) overflow risk, aligning with the project’s defensive coding standards.
