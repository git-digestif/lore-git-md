Core problem or goal
Replace manual integer arithmetic in `diff-delta.c:create_delta_index()` with the safe integer helpers `st_mult()`, `st_add()`, and `st_add3()` to prevent silent overflows during allocation size calculations. The patch is now justified as a precautionary measure against hypothetical future changes in struct layout or bounds, after Pablo Sabater refuted the original theoretical overflow risk under current conditions. The revised commit message provides transparent arithmetic, showing `entries` capped at 268,435,455 and `hsize` at 67,108,864 (2²⁶), ensuring `hsize + 1` cannot overflow.
