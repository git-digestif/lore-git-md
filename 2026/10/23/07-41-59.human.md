This is a **documentation and terminology cleanup patch** in Andy Koppe’s five-part series to align Git’s codebase with the 2024 redefinition of "pseudoref" (now limited to `FETCH_HEAD` and `MERGE_HEAD`) and "root ref" (refs outside `refs/`, like `HEAD` or `ORIG_HEAD`). This fourth patch updates in-code comments and help text in `builtin/for-each-ref.c`, `contrib/completion/git-completion.bash`, `refs.h`, and `revision.c` to replace outdated uses of "pseudoref" with "root ref."

The review from Patrick Steinhardt is **surface-level**, focusing on commit message clarity rather than technical substance. He suggests trimming redundant explanations (e.g., "this sentence is somewhat pointless") while approving the rationale for leaving `__git_pseudoref_exists()` named as-is. The patch itself remains uncontroversial: it makes no behavioral changes, only correcting terminology in comments and help text. The series as a whole is under review, with Junio C Hamano signaling general approval but no explicit merge status yet.
