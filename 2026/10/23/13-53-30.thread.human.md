This thread is about a regression in `git apply` that causes patch series containing a pure rename followed by an edit to fail when `core.fileMode=false` is set. The issue, introduced between Git 2.44.0 and 2.45.0, was reported by Hannes Brandstätter-Müller, who also proposed a fix and added a regression test. Aniket Gupta later refined the fix to address a security edge case, ensuring the patch cannot maliciously change a regular file into a symlink when `core.fileMode=false`.

All technical concerns raised by Junio C Hamano—whether the scenario is valid input, how the fix behaves in reverse, and whether the patches are applied in a single invocation or separately—have been resolved. The refined fix passes the broader test suite and reproduces the bug in all expected workflows (`git apply`, `git am`, and separate invocations). The patch is now technically uncontested, but Junio has not yet indicated whether he will merge, hold, or drop it.

The only recent development is a process-related note from Patrick Steinhardt, who politely reminded Aniket Gupta to quote relevant parts of emails when replying, as context may not always be visible to all readers. This was a general best-practice suggestion, not a critique of the patch or its discussion. The thread remains under review, with no new technical feedback or maintainer decision.
