This is a maintainer response to a two-patch bugfix series targeting the checkout and branch subsystems. Junio has queued the series, meaning it is now in his integration pipeline and will appear in the next `seen` rebuild. The series addresses the inconsistent state left by `git switch -c` and `git checkout -b` when they fail to write tracking configuration due to a stale `.git/config.lock`: previously, the worktree and index were updated and the new branch ref created, but HEAD remained on the old branch. The patches make the operation non-atomic but consistent, allowing the switch to complete and Git to exit with status 0 even if the tracking config cannot be written, aligning with the behavior of `git push -u`, `git clone`, and `git fetch`.

The series was previously part of a larger effort but was split out at Junio’s request after the direction was agreed in the bug report thread. Patch 1/2 refactors `setup_tracking()` in `branch.c` to return an error instead of calling `exit()`, and patch 2/2 modifies `builtin/checkout.c` to ignore the tracking config failure and finish the switch, while updating the test suite (`t2027-checkout-track.sh`, `t2060-switch.sh`, `t3200-branch.sh`) to verify the new behavior. The author explicitly deferred atomicity (rolling back the switch on failure) to a future topic, keeping this series focused and uncontroversial.

No substantive review feedback was recorded before Junio queued the series, and the thread context indicates no open questions or objections. The patches touch `branch.c`, `branch.h`, `builtin/branch.c`, and `builtin/checkout.c`, and introduce no on-disk format changes. The key symbol modified is `setup_tracking()`, which now returns `int` (error) instead of `void` (exiting). The series is now in `seen` and will cook there until Junio moves it to `next` or drops it.
