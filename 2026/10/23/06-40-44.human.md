This is a **substantive review** of Karthik Nayak’s v4 bugfix patch for the shallow-fetch-with-tags regression. The patch itself was already well-motivated and technically sound, but Patrick Steinhardt’s review raises three targeted questions that could affect the commit message and implementation details.

Patrick asks whether the new logic might now advertise *all* local references during negotiation, rather than just the newly fetched ones, which could change behavior for repositories with millions of refs. He suggests this trade-off should be discussed in the commit message. The second question is about the third test case: it verifies upstream tracking and tag backfilling when a sibling ref is rejected, but Patrick wonders if this edge case is directly related to the fix or just opportunistic coverage. Finally, he proposes a small code simplification: using a local `struct oid_array` instead of dynamically allocating and freeing one, which would make the cleanup unconditional and slightly cleaner.

The review is constructive and focused on clarity and robustness, not on the core design. The questions are precise and actionable, and Patrick explicitly endorses the overall approach ("this version looks much saner"). No new technical concerns are raised, and the patch remains on track for `next` once these minor points are addressed.
