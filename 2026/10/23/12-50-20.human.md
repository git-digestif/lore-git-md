This is the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-part refactoring series that removes `the_repository` dependencies from Git’s `rerere` subsystem. The patch eliminates redundant calls to `repo_config()` in `rerere.c`, addressing both a minor performance inefficiency and a subtle layering violation.

The change is straightforward: `setup_rerere()` and `rerere_gc()` each called `repo_config()` even though their callers had already done so. Worse, the call in `setup_rerere()` could silently overwrite manual config changes made after the initial `repo_config()` call, which is surprising behavior. The patch simply removes both redundant calls, leaving the config parsing to the caller.

This cleanup is a small but necessary step toward the series’ larger goal of encapsulating `rerere` state in a dedicated struct. The commit message is clear and explains both the immediate benefit (removing redundancy) and the forward-looking rationale (preparing for a later patch that caches `is_rerere_enabled()`). No new behavior is introduced, and the change is uncontroversial. The patch touches only `rerere.c` and is part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort.
