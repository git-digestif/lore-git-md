This is a bugfix patch in the delta compression subsystem, part of an Outreachy contribution. The author, Srujith Poojary, has responded to Junio C Hamano’s substantive question about whether the integer arithmetic in `diff-delta.c:create_delta_index()` can actually overflow on 32-bit platforms. After checking the bounds, Srujith confirms that the existing cap on `entries` (derived from `bufsize`) keeps `hsize` small enough to prevent `memsize` from exceeding `SIZE_MAX`, even on 32-bit systems. This means the patch, which replaces manual arithmetic with safe integer helpers (`st_mult()`, `st_add()`, `st_add3()`), addresses a theoretical issue rather than a practical one.

The patch itself remains unchanged in functionality, but the commit message has been corrected in an earlier version to accurately reflect that the helpers call `die()` on overflow rather than returning NULL. Srujith also applied `git clang-format` to fix code alignment as suggested by Phillip Wood. The broader context is the project-wide effort to adopt safe integer helpers, but this specific change now appears to be motivated more by consistency than by an immediate risk.

The thread is now at a crossroads: if the goal is to enforce safe arithmetic uniformly, the patch may still be worth merging despite the lack of a demonstrated overflow risk. If the project prefers to avoid changes that don’t address a concrete problem, this patch might be abandoned. Junio’s next response will likely clarify the project’s stance. For now, the patch is well-explained and technically sound, but its necessity is debatable.
