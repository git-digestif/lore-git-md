This is the first patch in a six-part series introducing an **incremental connectivity check** for Git fetches and pushes. It adds a new technical document (`Documentation/technical/connectivity-check.adoc`) that explains the connectivity invariant, how the existing full connectivity check works, and the exceptions (submodules, promisor objects) that apply.

The document is thorough and well-structured, covering the theoretical foundation (the "connectivity invariant"), the mechanics of the full check (using `git rev-list`), edge cases like deepening fetches and non-commit tips, and even includes a clear example graph illustrating the boundary between incoming and already-connected commits. It’s written for developers who need to understand the subsystem, not end users, and it’s integrated into both the Makefile and Meson build systems.

This patch is purely **documentation** and doesn’t change any code. It’s a prerequisite for the rest of the series, providing the conceptual framework for the incremental mode introduced later. The writing is clear, the examples are helpful, and the scope is appropriately narrow. No controversy or open questions here—just a solid foundation for the technical work that follows.
