This is the fourth patch in Phillip Wood’s seven-patch series `pw/stash-optimize-check_changes-calls/v4`, now cooking in `next` and on track for `master`. The patch eliminates redundant index preloading in `git stash push` and `git stash create` by shifting responsibility for reading the index to the callers of `do_create_stash()`. Previously, `do_push_stash()` and `do_create_stash()` each called `repo_read_index_preload()`, resulting in three full index preloads (each invoking `lstat()` on all files) when only one was needed. The change replaces `repo_read_index_preload()` with `repo_read_index()` in both callers, cutting two unnecessary `lstat()` calls per changed path.

The patch is a straightforward mechanical optimization confined to `builtin/stash.c`. It touches only three lines—removing the redundant `repo_read_index_preload()` call in `do_create_stash()` and replacing it with `repo_read_index()` in `create_stash()` and `do_push_stash()`. No behavior changes are introduced; the goal is purely to reduce filesystem overhead. The commit message clearly explains the redundancy and quantifies the savings (two fewer `lstat()` calls per changed path), making the motivation easy to follow.

This patch is part of the series’ broader effort to streamline stash’s change-detection machinery, which has already eliminated duplicate index refreshes and consolidated `check_changes()` calls in earlier patches.
