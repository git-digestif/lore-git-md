This is the first patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch v2 series restoring dynamic reloading of alternate object databases in the files-based ODB backend. It is a pure refactoring that renames two internal functions: `odb_prepare_alternates` becomes `prepare_alternates`, and `odb_add_alternate_recursively` becomes `add_alternate_recursively`. The change is mechanical—no logic is altered—and is motivated by the fact that these functions now belong to the files-based backend rather than the generic ODB layer, making the old `odb_` prefix misleading.

The patch touches only `odb/source-files.c` and updates nine call sites to match the new names. This preparatory step cleans up the codebase ahead of the substantive fixes in later patches, ensuring the function names accurately reflect their scope. The commit message is clear and concise, explaining the rationale without overstating the impact. There is no controversy here; this is a straightforward, uncontroversial refactoring that sets the stage for the rest of the series.
