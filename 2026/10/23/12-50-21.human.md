This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch series refactoring the `rerere` subsystem to remove its implicit dependency on the global `the_repository` variable. The patch caches the result of `is_rerere_enabled()` in a new `struct repository::rerere_cache_enabled` field, eliminating redundant checks and removing the need for the `GIT_PATH_FUNC()` helper that previously cached the rerere cache directory path.

The change is a straightforward performance and code-cleanliness improvement. `setup_rerere()` currently re-evaluates whether rerere is enabled every time it is called, even though the answer cannot change within a single process. By caching the result in the repository struct, the patch avoids repeated config lookups and filesystem checks. The commit message clearly explains the rationale and the mechanical transformation: the new field is initialized to `-1` (indicating auto-detection is needed), and `is_rerere_enabled()` now consults this field before falling back to the existing logic.

No new user-facing behavior is introduced, and the patch does not add tests, as it is purely a refactoring. The diff is clean and focused, touching only `repository.c`, `repository.h`, and `rerere.c`. The removal of the `GIT_PATH_FUNC()` helper is a nice side effect, simplifying the code further.

This patch looks well-crafted and uncontroversial. It fits neatly into the broader `the_repository` removal effort and should not require significant review discussion, though reviewers may want to verify that the caching logic correctly handles edge cases (e.g., repositories where rerere is disabled after being enabled earlier in the process). The series as a whole remains under review, with no objections raised so far.
