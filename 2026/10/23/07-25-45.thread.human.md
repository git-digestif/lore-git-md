This thread is about the third part of the SHA-1/SHA-256 interoperability effort, implementing pack index v3 to enable bidirectional mapping between repositories using different hash algorithms. The 13-patch v2 series, originally posted by Christian Couder based on brian m. carlson’s earlier work, has been merged to Junio C Hamano’s `next` branch without further technical changes. The series completes the ODB mapping infrastructure, adds pack index v3 writing and reading support, implements packed-object mapping and cross-algorithm disambiguation, and documents the new format.

Key participants include Junio, who merged the series, and Christian, who has now committed to improving threading discipline for future revisions by adopting a `sendemail-validate` hook and helper scripts. This procedural change aims to ensure that cover letters and patches remain properly threaded for automation tools like patchwork and b4. No technical issues remain open, and the series is now in the integration testing phase. The work builds on earlier parts of the interoperability effort (already merged) and Patrick Steinhardt’s ODB refactoring, and will serve as a foundation for brian m. carlson’s remaining tasks, such as automatic v3 selection and `show-index` support.
