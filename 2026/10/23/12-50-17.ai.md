This is a refactoring patch series targeting the `rerere` subsystem as part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The series consists of seven patches that eliminate global state in `rerere.c`, including removing dependencies on `the_repository` and bundling previously global variables into a dedicated struct. The goal is to make the `rerere` subsystem reusable across multiple repositories without relying on implicit global context.

The series is authored by Patrick Steinhardt, a prolific contributor known for large-scale architectural refactorings. It builds on Junio’s integration branch (8th batch, 2026-10-22) with Taylor Blau’s `tb/rerere-wait-for-merge-rr-lock` topic merged in. The changes touch `rerere.c` heavily (309 lines changed), along with smaller adjustments to `builtin/rerere.c`, `hex.c`, `repository.c`, and their headers. Key technical details include caching the result of `is_rerere_enabled()`, removing redundant `repo_config()` calls, and introducing a new `rerere_state` struct to encapsulate what was previously global state.

The motivation is twofold: first, to align with the broader `the_repository` removal effort, and second, to enable `rerere` to be used concurrently for different repositories (e.g., in multi-repository workflows). The commit messages are clear and functional, though they assume familiarity with the `the_repository` removal context. No new user-facing behavior is introduced, and the series does not include new tests, as it is purely a refactoring.

This is a well-scoped, mechanical refactoring that fits cleanly into the ongoing `the_repository` removal work. The changes are likely uncontroversial but will require careful review to ensure no regressions in `rerere`’s behavior, particularly around its locking and caching mechanisms. The series is self-contained and does not appear to depend on other in-flight topics beyond the base branch. Files touched include `rerere.c`, `rerere.h`, and minor adjustments to repository-related plumbing. Key names introduced: `rerere_state` struct, `repo_rerere_path()`, and `is_rerere_enabled()` caching.
