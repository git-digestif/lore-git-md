This is the fifth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch series refactoring Git’s `rerere` subsystem to remove its implicit dependency on the global `the_repository` variable. The series is part of the broader `the_repository` removal effort.

This patch tackles the last few places in `rerere.c` that still rely on `the_repository` by replacing calls to `get_hash_hex()` and `parse_oid_hex()` with their algorithm-aware variants (`get_hash_hex_algop()` and `parse_oid_hex_algop()`), which take an explicit `struct git_hash_algo *` parameter. To make this possible, the patch promotes `get_hash_hex_algop()` from a static helper in `hex.c` to a public function in `hex.h`. The `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` macro is then removed from `rerere.c`, formally cutting the file’s dependency on the global repository.

The changes are mechanical and small—just 12 lines added, 9 removed—but they complete the explicit repository-awareness work for `rerere.c`. The patch leaves one loose end: while the file no longer depends on `the_repository`, it still carries some global state that ties the rerere cache to the first repository used. That issue is flagged in the commit message and will be addressed in the next two patches.

This is a clean, uncontroversial step in the refactoring. No new behavior is introduced, and the patch is unlikely to draw objections beyond possible requests to verify that the new public `get_hash_hex_algop()` function is appropriately documented or tested. Reviewers may focus on ensuring the hash-algorithm-aware variants are used correctly throughout the file.
