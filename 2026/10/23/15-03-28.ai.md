This is the **v4 cover letter** for Phillip Wood’s seven-patch refactoring series `pw/stash-optimize-check_changes-calls`, now **queued for `next`** and on track to graduate to `master` unless integration issues arise. The series targets redundant index loads, refreshes, and change-detection logic in `git stash push` and `git stash create`, eliminating unnecessary `lstat()` calls (four fewer per changed path for `create`, eight for `push`) and avoiding duplicate untracked-file walks. The changes are confined to `builtin/stash.c` and introduce no user-visible behavior changes beyond correcting a minor exit-status anomaly: `create_stash()` now exits 0 when there are no changes to stash, aligning with its documented behavior.

The **core refactoring**—removing redundant `repo_read_preload_index()` and `refresh_index()` calls, consolidating `check_changes()` logic, and introducing `enum create_result` to distinguish success, errors, and no-op cases—remains unchanged from v3. No technical concerns or edge cases were raised during review; Junio’s feedback on v3 was overwhelmingly positive, calling the series "nicely split and described" and "quite well done," with particular praise for patch 5/7’s elimination of in-core index flipping.
