This is a **documentation patch review** in Andy Koppe’s five-part series to align Git’s terminology around "pseudorefs" and "root refs" with the 2024 redefinition. The review is **substantive**, focusing on accuracy and clarity in the updated man pages.

Patrick Steinhardt raises two specific concerns in the third patch (which updates `git-update-ref(1)` and `git-worktree(1)`). First, he points out that the explanation of `core.logAllRefUpdates` in `git-update-ref(1)` is both hard to parse logically and outdated—it still references `$GIT_DIR/logs/<ref>`, which doesn’t apply to the reftable backend. He suggests replacing the detailed explanation with a cross-reference to `git-config(1)`. Second, he recommends converting the prose list of per-worktree refs in `git-worktree(1)` into a bulleted AsciiDoc list for better readability, echoing feedback he gave on an earlier patch in the series.

The review is constructive and targets real issues, but it does not block the patch. The changes Patrick suggests are editorial rather than technical, and the patch’s core goal—replacing outdated "pseudoref" terminology with "root ref"—remains uncontroversial. The series as a whole is still under review, with no merge status reported.
