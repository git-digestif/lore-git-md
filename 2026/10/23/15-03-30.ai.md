This is the second patch in Phillip Wood’s seven-patch series `pw/stash-optimize-check_changes-calls/v4`, which is now cooking in `next` and on track for `master`. The patch removes a redundant change-detection call in `git stash create`, eliminating one of two duplicate `check_changes_tracked_files()` invocations introduced in ef0f0b4509 (2019). The commit message explains that the double-check was never an optimization: the first call (in `create_stash()`) happens before the index is refreshed, making it unreliable, while the second (in `do_create_stash()`) is the only one that matters. By removing the first call, the patch reduces unnecessary work without altering behavior—`do_create_stash()` still performs the authoritative check after refreshing the index.

The patch also cleans up `check_changes_tracked_files()` itself by removing its internal `repo_read_index()` call, since all remaining callers now refresh the index before invoking it. This is a small but logical follow-on to the redundancy removal, ensuring the function’s contract is clearer and its implementation simpler. The diff is minimal (five lines removed from `builtin/stash.c`), and the change is purely mechanical: no new logic, no edge cases, and no user-visible impact beyond the performance gain from avoiding a redundant check. The patch is well-motivated and aligns with the series’ overall goal of streamlining stash’s change-detection machinery.
