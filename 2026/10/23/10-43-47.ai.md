This is the review of the second patch in Igor Wiedler’s two-patch bugfix series (v3) addressing the connectivity-check performance regression in deepening fetches. The patch teaches `odb_for_each_alternate_ref()` to skip refs from shallow or grafted alternates, completing the root-cause fix for the regression.

Patrick Steinhardt’s review is substantive and approving. He confirms the technical rationale—shallow or grafted alternates cannot be trusted as boundaries because their refs point to commits with missing history—and agrees the change is safe in practice. He notes that while the setup is unusual, the fact that `git clone --reference` already rejects such repositories supports the decision to ignore their refs here. His only conceptual reservation is about the fragility of the alternate-ref mechanism itself (relying on the parent directory being a valid repository), but he does not request changes. The review endorses the patch as a correct and necessary fix for the regression, with no outstanding objections or action items.
