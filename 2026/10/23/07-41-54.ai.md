This is a **substantive review** of the third patch in Andy Koppe’s five-part documentation series aligning Git’s terminology around "pseudorefs" and "root refs." Patrick Steinhardt’s feedback focuses on two specific man pages (`git-update-ref(1)` and `git-worktree(1)`) and raises broader concerns about clarity, accuracy, and maintainability.

The review identifies two issues in `git-update-ref(1)`. First, the current phrasing of `core.logAllRefUpdates`’s logic is confusing—it’s unclear whether the config parameter applies to all subsequent conditions or only the first. This ambiguity predates the patch but is now more noticeable. Second, the documentation’s explanation of reflog storage is outdated: it still references `$GIT_DIR/logs/<ref>`, which doesn’t hold for the reftable backend. Patrick suggests replacing the detailed explanation with a cross-reference to `git-config(1)` to avoid inaccuracies.

For `git-worktree(1)`, Patrick reiterates his earlier suggestion (from Patch 2/5) to convert the prose list of per-worktree ref exceptions into a bulleted AsciiDoc list, improving readability and future extensibility. This is an editorial improvement rather than a correctness issue.

The feedback is **not blocking**—the patch’s core goal (terminology alignment) remains valid—but it highlights areas where the documentation could be clearer or more future-proof. The reftable backend’s implications for reflog storage are particularly noteworthy, as they affect multiple man pages beyond this series. No merge status is signaled, but the review suggests the patch may need a v2 to address these points.
