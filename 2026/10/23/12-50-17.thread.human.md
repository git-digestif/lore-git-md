This thread is a seven-patch refactoring series by Patrick Steinhardt to remove Git’s `rerere` subsystem’s dependency on the global `the_repository` variable, part of the broader effort to eliminate global state. The series is now cooking in Junio C Hamano’s integration branch after v1 was posted on 2026-10-22, with Junio reviewing the final patch the same day. The refactoring introduces new structs (`rerere_state` and `rerere_cache`) and explicitly threads repository parameters through all internal functions, touching nearly 500 lines in `rerere.c` and related headers.

Junio’s review of the final patch raised a minor design question about whether the `rerere_cache_enabled` field in `struct repository` (added in patch 4) could have been replaced by the new `rerere_cache` struct. Patrick responded with a proposed patch to remove the caching entirely, arguing that the overhead of checking `.git/rr-cache` and reading the config on each call is negligible and simplifies the code. The discussion is low-stakes—no regressions or blockers have been identified—and the series remains on track for integration. The only open question is whether to prefer the cached or stateless approach for `rerere.enabled`, with Patrick deferring to Junio’s judgment. No other reviewers have weighed in, and the mechanical nature of the refactoring has drawn no substantive objections.
