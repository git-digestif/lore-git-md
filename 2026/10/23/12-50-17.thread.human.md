This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch series refactoring Git’s `rerere` (reuse recorded resolution) subsystem to remove its dependency on the global `the_repository` variable. The goal is to eliminate implicit global state, making the subsystem explicitly repository-aware—a key part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The series was posted on October 22, 2026, and is now complete with the final patch converting the global `rerere_cache` into an explicitly passed struct.

Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, has reviewed the final patch and given it a positive assessment, calling it a "logical conclusion" of the series. While he noted a minor design curiosity about whether the `rerere_cache_enabled` field in `struct repository` could have been replaced by the new `rerere_cache` struct, he did not request changes and has queued the patch for integration. The series is now under active review and is expected to proceed to `next` or `master` without further objections. No regressions or edge cases were raised, though reviewers may still scrutinize the mechanical correctness of the refactoring, particularly around locking and caching mechanisms.

The refactoring touches `rerere.c`, `builtin/rerere.c`, and related headers, introducing new symbols like `rerere_cache_setup()` and `rerere_cache_release()` to manage the cache’s lifecycle. The series is part of the broader `the_repository` removal effort led by René Scharfe and depends on Junio’s integration branch and Taylor Blau’s `tb/rerere-wait-for-merge-rr-lock` topic. No substantive review feedback has been incorporated yet, but the maintainer’s approval signals strong confidence in the direction.
