This is the maintainer's response to the final patch in Patrick Steinhardt's seven-patch series refactoring Git's `rerere` subsystem to remove its dependency on the global `the_repository` variable. Junio C Hamano approves the patch and has queued it for integration, marking the completion of the series' review process.

The patch converts the global `rerere_cache` variable into an explicitly passed struct, introducing `rerere_cache_setup()` and `rerere_cache_release()` to manage its lifecycle. Junio notes that this is a "logical conclusion" of the previous patch's work and expresses mild curiosity about whether the `rerere_cache_enabled` field added to `struct repository` in an earlier patch could have been replaced by this new structure, though he acknowledges it may not be feasible.

The series is now cooking in Junio's integration branches, bringing the `rerere` subsystem fully into the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. This is a clean, well-structured refactoring with no user-facing behavior changes, and Junio's quick approval suggests it is unlikely to encounter further objections. The patch touches `rerere.c`, `builtin/rerere.c`, and `rerere.h`, and introduces two new public functions for cache management.
