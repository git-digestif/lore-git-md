Kaartic Sivaraam proposes an updated wording for the microproject guidelines that incorporates Junio C Hamano’s suggestion to move the `git grep` instruction below the three-dash line (`---`) in commit messages. This preserves the guidance for reviewers while keeping the final commit message clean, aligning with the project’s established conventions.
