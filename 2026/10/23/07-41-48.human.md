This is a documentation patch in Andy Koppe’s five-part series to clean up Git’s terminology around "pseudoref" and "root ref." Patch 2/5 updates the glossary’s "per-worktree ref" entry to reflect the 2024 terminology change: it now lists all root refs (HEAD, ORIG_HEAD, etc.) plus refs under `refs/worktree/` and `refs/rewritten/` as per-worktree, and clarifies that pseudorefs are also worktree-specific. The patch also tweaks the "worktree" entry to avoid duplicating examples.

Patrick Steinhardt’s review is substantive but narrowly focused. He suggests two presentation improvements: (1) converting the list of per-worktree refs into an itemized list for better readability and future extensibility, and (2) removing the explicit examples (HEAD, MERGE_HEAD) from the "worktree" entry to avoid redundancy with the "per-worktree ref" section. Neither suggestion questions the technical content; both are about clarity and consistency. The review does not signal merge status or revisit the glossary phrasing objections raised in Patch 1/5.
