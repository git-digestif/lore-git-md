This is the third patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s seven-patch series refactoring the `rerere` subsystem to remove its implicit dependency on `the_repository`. The patch removes two redundant calls to `repo_config()`: one in `git_rerere_config()` and another in `rerere_gc()`.

The motivation is twofold. First, all callers of `setup_rerere()` already invoke `repo_config()` beforehand, so the call inside `git_rerere_config()` is unnecessary. Second, the call creates a layering violation: `setup_rerere()` is meant to initialize the rerere cache, but calling `repo_config()` here can overwrite global config state that the caller may have manually modified after their own `repo_config()` call. This issue becomes more problematic in a later patch (patch 4), where the series caches the result of `is_rerere_enabled()`, causing `repo_config()` to be called conditionally—further complicating the layering.

The change is minimal: one line removed from `git_rerere_config()` and another from `rerere_gc()`. The patch is purely mechanical and does not alter behavior, but it is a prerequisite for the caching logic introduced in patch 4. The commit message clearly explains the redundancy and the layering concern, though it assumes familiarity with the series’ broader goal of removing `the_repository` dependencies.

This is a well-justified cleanup that aligns with the series’ refactoring goals.
