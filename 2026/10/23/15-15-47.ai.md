This is a surface-level review of the v2 patch that replaces manual integer arithmetic with safe helpers in `diff-delta.c`. Phillip Wood points out two alignment issues in the patch’s diff: the arguments to `st_add()` and `st_add3()` are not vertically aligned, and he provides a link to a clang-format run that shows the expected formatting. He also raises a potential edge case: whether `hsize + 1` could overflow before being passed to `st_mult()`, though he does not claim it is a bug—just a question about the bounds of `hsize`. The review is purely stylistic and mechanical; no substantive concerns about the patch’s logic or safety are raised.
