This thread is about an Outreachy microproject to add test coverage for the `--no-allow-onelevel` option in `git check-ref-format`. Fien Dora, an Outreachy applicant for the December 2026 cohort, identified that the existing test suite (`t/t1402-check-ref-format.sh`) covers `--allow-onelevel` but not its counterpart, leaving the behavior of `--no-allow-onelevel` and its interaction with `--allow-onelevel` untested.

The community has approved the microproject in principle. Phillip Wood, a key reviewer, confirmed the gap is suitable for Outreachy—small in scope but valuable for documenting how the command handles mutually exclusive options (where the last option specified takes effect). Fien manually verified the behavior: `--allow-onelevel foo` succeeds, while `--allow-onelevel --no-allow-onelevel foo` fails because `--no-allow-onelevel` rejects the one-level ref "foo". No objections or requests for changes have been raised, and Fien has now confirmed they will proceed with drafting the patch. The next step is the submission of the test patch itself.
