Core problem or goal
Fix a pluralization bug in the error message shown when a user requests a reflog entry that does not exist (e.g., `HEAD@{5}` when the reflog only has 1 or 2 entries). The current message always uses the plural form ("only has 1 entries"), which is grammatically incorrect in English and prevents proper translation into languages with more complex plural rules.

Current status
Under review; no prior versions. The patch replaces the hardcoded `_()` gettext wrapper with Git’s pluralization macro `Q_()` in `object-name.c` and adds test coverage in `t/t1400-update-ref.sh` for both singular ("1 entry") and plural ("2 entries") cases.
