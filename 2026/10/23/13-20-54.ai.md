This is the fourth patch in D. Ben Knoble’s five-patch v2 series improving `git stash` shell completion. It removes the deprecated `git stash save` subcommand from the command’s `-h` usage text and the man page synopsis, while leaving the detailed description intact for users who still rely on it.

The patch touches two files: `Documentation/git-stash.adoc` (removing the `save` line from the synopsis) and `builtin/stash.c` (dropping `BUILTIN_STASH_SAVE_USAGE` from the usage array). The change is minimal and uncontroversial on its own—Patrick Steinhardt already endorsed it in the v1 review—but it accelerates the deprecation timeline by making `save` invisible in the most prominent help contexts. The commit message acknowledges this as a deliberate choice, framing the patch as part of a broader cleanup effort that may still be debated.

No tests or on-disk formats are affected, and the patch is purely textual. It pairs with the fifth patch, which removes `save` from shell completion, but the two are independent: this one affects only documentation and `-h` output, while the next one targets the completion scripts. The author remains open to dropping both if the removal proves too disruptive.
