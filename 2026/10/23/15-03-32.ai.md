This is the fourth patch in Phillip Wood’s seven-patch refactoring series `pw/stash-optimize-check_changes-calls/v4`, now cooking in `next`. It targets redundant index preloading in `git stash push` and `git stash create` by shifting responsibility for reading the index from `do_create_stash()` to its callers and replacing `repo_read_index_preload()` with `repo_read_index()`. The change eliminates two unnecessary `lstat()` calls per changed path—once in `do_push_stash()` and once in `do_create_stash()`—by ensuring the index is preloaded only once, during the initial `repo_refresh_and_write_index()` call in `do_create_stash()`. The patch is purely mechanical, touching only two lines in `builtin/stash.c` (`create_stash()` and `do_push_stash()`), and preserves all existing behavior while aligning with the series’ broader goal of streamlining stash’s change-detection machinery. No new edge cases or logic are introduced; the focus is strictly on performance hygiene. Junio’s prior reviews of the series have been positive, with no technical objections raised for this patch.
