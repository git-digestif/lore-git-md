This is the maintainer’s call for additional review on a feature series that is otherwise ready to advance. The series, now at v10, introduces a "matching-inspired" fetch mode for shallow repositories to avoid the performance penalty of fetching every branch from a remote with many branches. Junio has reviewed the later iterations and found no remaining issues, but because he is not the target user for this feature, he is uncomfortable merging it without at least one more substantive review from someone else.

The series is technically complete: it adds a `remote.<name>.refmap` configuration, extracts a helper to avoid hard-coded `the_repository` usage, implements dynamic branch inference when no explicit refspec is given, and provides both a `--limited-fetch` flag for `git remote add` and a shallow-repository default. All prior review feedback has been addressed, and the only change in v10 is a rebase to resolve a textual conflict with a recently merged topic. The implementation is clean, backward-compatible for non-shallow repositories, and thoroughly tested.

If no additional review appears, the series will likely stall until someone with a use-case for shallow repositories steps in. For now, it remains in limbo—ready, but not yet approved.
