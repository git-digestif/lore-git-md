This is the second patch in the now-finalized six-part series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page that consolidates Git’s merge conflict resolution guidance. The series is cooking in `next` and expected to graduate to `master` soon.

This patch updates `git-merge.adoc` to remove its existing, now-redundant conflict-resolution sections and replace them with a single cross-reference to the new `gitmergeconflicts(7)` guide. The diff shows a net deletion of 122 lines: the entire “HOW CONFLICTS ARE PRESENTED” and “HOW TO RESOLVE CONFLICTS” sections are dropped, leaving only a brief mention of `--abort` and `--continue` with a link to the new guide. The patch also fixes a 16-year-old bug in the man page by removing references to non-existent steps “2” and “3” in the conflict-resolution workflow.

The change is purely mechanical and uncontroversial; all substantive discussion about the new guide’s content has already been resolved in earlier iterations. Junio’s `Reviewed-by` is present, and the patch carries the same merge status as the rest of the series.
