The next iteration will need to either (1) copy the existing array before appending fetched tips to avoid side effects, (2) enforce exclusive ownership during tag backfilling, or (3) fix the remote-helper cleanup bug in a precursor commit. The patch remains **not ready for `next`** until this is resolved.
