This is a maintainer reply to Mihai Cosma’s two-patch series fixing index locking behavior in `git status`. Junio suggests that Mihai and Jun Wu collaborate to produce a single consolidated series, since their work overlaps and complements each other. Jun Wu’s "Wait for the index lock" series (which makes writers wait for the lock instead of failing) and Mihai’s series (which reduces how often and how long `git status` holds the lock) address the same problem from different angles and currently conflict only on a single lock call in `cmd_status()`.

The suggestion is pragmatic: combining the two efforts would avoid merge conflicts and ensure the final solution is coherent. Mihai has already indicated willingness to rebase onto Jun Wu’s series, and the two approaches are compatible (Mihai’s benchmarks show that applying both reduces `git add` latency from over a second to 160ms in the clean case). No technical objections or concerns are raised; this is purely a coordination request. The next step is for Mihai and Jun Wu to work together on a unified patch series.
