This is the sixth patch in Phillip Wood’s seven-patch refactoring series `pw/stash-optimize-check_changes-calls/v4`, which is now cooking in `next` and on track to graduate to `master`. The series targets redundant index operations in `git stash push` and `git stash create`, eliminating unnecessary `lstat()` calls and index refreshes without changing user-visible behavior.

This patch removes redundant index loads and refreshes in `create_index_from_tree()`. The function is always called from `do_create_stash()`—either directly or indirectly—after `do_create_stash()` has already loaded and refreshed the index. The patch deletes the now-unnecessary `repo_read_index_preload()` and `refresh_index()` calls in `create_index_from_tree()`, saving three `lstat()` calls per changed path. The change is purely mechanical and aligns with the series’ goal of consolidating index operations to avoid duplicate work.

The patch is uncontroversial and well-motivated, reflecting the series’ broader focus on code hygiene and performance. No further discussion or edge cases are expected.
