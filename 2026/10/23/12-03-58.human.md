This is a small i18n patch targeting the ZIP archive backend. The author, Keryle Fondji, submits the fifth iteration of a standalone patch that replaces a hardcoded singular/plural error message in `archive-zip.c` with Git’s `Q_()` macro. The goal is to support proper pluralization in languages with complex rules (like Polish), even though the English singular case (`pathlen == 1`) is unreachable in practice—the error only triggers when a path exceeds the ZIP format’s 65,535-byte limit.

The patch is confined to the `write_zip_entry()` function and makes no changes to logic, tests, or documentation. It splits the existing error message into singular and plural variants without adding new strings to the translation catalog. The commit message clearly explains the pluralization context, addressing earlier feedback from Phillip Wood, who endorsed the approach after the author clarified why `Q_()` is still valuable despite the unreachable singular case.

This version (v5) fixes the final mechanical nit from Junio C Hamano’s review of v4: proper indentation and alignment of function arguments in the `error()` call. Both Junio and Phillip Wood have reviewed and endorsed the patch, and the author explicitly requests it be picked up for `next`. The patch is ready for inclusion and no further revisions are expected. For a busy reader, this is a well-motivated, uncontroversial fix that improves internationalization support with minimal risk.
