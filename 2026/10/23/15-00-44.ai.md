This is the third installment in the interoperability effort; parts 1 and 2 (ODB infrastructure and loose-object mapping) are already in `master`. Key components of the series include ODB mapping infrastructure, v3 index writing/reading, validation logic, and packed-object disambiguation. The implementation is incremental, deferring features like automatic v3 selection and multi-pack-index support to later parts of brian’s work. The series is well-tested, with new tests for v3-specific behavior (e.g., duplicate detection, bounds checking, abbreviated ID resolution) and no expected performance regressions. Reviewers may still scrutinize the v3 validation logic (patch 10) and memory usage during indexing, but the series is otherwise uncontroversial and ready for integration.

Files touched span `pack-write.c`, `packfile.c`, `odb/`, and related headers, with new functions like `odb_map_oid()`, `nth_packed_object_id_algop()`, and `for_each_prefixed_object_in_pack()`.
