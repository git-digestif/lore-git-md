This is a **substantive review** of Karthik Nayak’s v4 bugfix patch for the shallow-fetch-with-tags regression. The review comes from Patrick Steinhardt, the original proposer of the `negotiation_include_tips` design now implemented in the patch.

Patrick’s feedback focuses on three areas: **behavioral implications**, **code clarity**, and **cleanup logic**. He asks whether the new logic could cause the client to advertise *all* local references (not just the newly fetched ones) during negotiation, which might flood the server with millions of "haves" in large repositories. While he acknowledges this *might* be the right trade-off, he wants the commit message to explicitly discuss the potential scalability impact. He also questions the inclusion of the third test (upstream tracking with rejected sibling refs), suggesting it may not be directly related to the fix. On the code side, he proposes minor cleanups: using a local `struct oid_array` variable to simplify memory management, moving the loop variable into the loop, and making the cleanup unconditional. His tone is constructive, and he concludes by calling the new version "much saner" than the previous two-transaction approach.

The review does not identify any correctness issues or regressions, but the scalability question could prompt further discussion or a v5 update to the commit message. The patch remains on track for `next` unless new concerns emerge.
