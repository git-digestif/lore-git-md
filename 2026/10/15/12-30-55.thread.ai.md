Core problem or goal
Fix a correctness bug in `cache_ref_iterator_seek()` where loose-ref iteration can misposition when a directory name shares a prefix with a ref name. Example: with refs `refs/tags/releases-z` and `refs/tags/releases/v1`, seeking after `v1` could incorrectly land on `releases-z` instead of descending into `releases/`, causing `v1` to be returned again. The fix ensures directory components are compared with their trailing slash included, preventing this confusion.
