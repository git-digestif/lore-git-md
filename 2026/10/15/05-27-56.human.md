This is the second patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch series refactoring and optimizing alternates handling in Git’s object database (ODB) "files" backend. It applies the same performance optimization introduced in patch 1/6 — checking packfiles before loose objects — to the `odb_source_files_read_object_stream()` function, which handles streaming large objects.

The change is small (four lines added, two removed) and mirrors the logic already established for reading object info: it reorders the loops to iterate over all packfiles across alternates before checking any loose objects. While the performance impact is expected to be less dramatic for streaming (since reading large objects likely dominates `stat()` overhead), the patch ensures consistent behavior between the two functions. The commit message acknowledges this trade-off but justifies the change on grounds of alignment rather than raw speedup.

This patch is uncontroversial and well-motivated. It builds directly on the optimization introduced in patch 1/6, which was benchmarked at a 1.89x speedup for `git cat-file --batch-check` and is now protected by a regression test (`t/t5615-alternate-env.sh`). The series as a whole has strong reviewer endorsement, with Justin Tobler confirming the design and implementation for all patches. No new feedback or objections are expected here — this is a straightforward follow-through on the established pattern.
