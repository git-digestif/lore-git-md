This is the first patch in a three-part feature series for `git reflog`. It introduces named constants in `reflog.h` for the strings that mark branch switches, renames, and rebase steps—things like `"checkout: moving from "` and `"rebase (start)"`. The patch then updates every place in the codebase that writes or parses these messages to use the new constants instead of hard-coded strings.

The change is purely mechanical and preparatory: no behavior changes, no new features, just eliminating duplication so the next two patches can safely add new options without risking drift between the strings that generate reflog entries and the ones that read them back. The files touched are exactly the ones you’d expect—`builtin/checkout.c`, `builtin/branch.c`, `builtin/rebase.c`, `sequencer.c`, and a couple of low-level helpers (`object-name.c`, `wt-status.c`). The patch is small (30 insertions, 18 deletions) and looks clean, with no obvious oversights or merge conflicts. If the series as a whole is worth doing, this patch is a necessary and uncontroversial first step.
