This is the seventh patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s eight-patch CI housekeeping series, now in its fourth iteration (v4). It removes dead Python 2 logic from the CI scripts after the last Ubuntu 20.04 job was dropped in an earlier patch. The change is purely mechanical: `ci/install-dependencies.sh` now hard-codes `python3` instead of the conditional `PYTHON_PACKAGE` variable, and `ci/lib.sh` simplifies the `MAKEFLAGS` assignment by removing the Ubuntu-20.04-specific branch. The patch is uncontested, aligns with the series’ goal of modernizing CI infrastructure, and has no behavioral impact. It’s ready for integration alongside the rest of the series.
