This is a minor follow-up in the CI housekeeping series, specifically about patch 1/8 which adds a `SHA1` prerequisite to `t/t5004-archive-corner-cases.sh` to prevent it from running in SHA-256 repositories. The patch itself is uncontroversial and ready for integration, but the discussion around it highlights a broader question about test design philosophy.

Patrick Steinhardt responds to Kaartic Sivaraam’s observation that, with the `SHA1` prerequisite, the test now runs in only a few CI jobs (macOS and AlmaLinux 8). Kaartic suggested documenting this limited coverage in the commit message, but Patrick declines, noting that the exact jobs are less important than the principle. This exchange confirms the current behavior is intentional, not accidental, and the patch remains unchanged.

The unresolved question from Jeff King’s earlier review—whether to make the test self-contained by creating a temporary SHA-1 repository—still lingers. Patrick previously signaled openness to the idea ("Hmm. Yeah, I guess that wouldn't hurt"), but no reroll has been posted. Kaartic’s observation that the test already runs in only a few jobs may reduce the urgency of revisiting this, though the technical discussion remains open. For now, the patch stands as-is, and the series as a whole is otherwise uncontested and ready for integration.
