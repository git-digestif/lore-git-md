This is a bugfix for the files ref backend, addressing a review discussion on Andy Koppe’s patch to fix loose ref cache inconsistency with root refs. The patch itself is a well-scoped, well-motivated fix that removes root refs (like `HEAD` or `ORIG_HEAD`) from the shared loose ref cache and instead creates a temporary ref cache for them when requested via the `REFS_FOR_EACH_INCLUDE_ROOT_REFS` flag. This ensures consistent behavior regardless of iteration order.

Patrick Steinhardt’s review raises two technical questions about the design. First, he asks why the fix doesn’t teach the cache itself to skip root refs when the caller isn’t interested in them, rather than keeping them out of the cache entirely. Second, he notes that the current implementation recreates the root ref cache for every iterator that requests root refs, which seems inefficient, and wonders why it isn’t cached like the loose ref cache. These questions suggest Patrick is evaluating whether the proposed solution is the most robust or performant approach, though he doesn’t outright reject it.

The discussion is still open, with no resolution yet. Andy’s patch is technically sound and fixes the inconsistency, but Patrick’s feedback indicates there may be room for refinement in the implementation. The outcome could affect whether the patch is merged as-is or revised to address these points. For now, this is a focused, low-risk bugfix with no broader implications for other backends or user-facing behavior.
