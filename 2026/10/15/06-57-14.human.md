This is a CI/build system patch, part of Patrick Steinhardt’s eight-patch series modernizing Git’s CI configurations. Patch 5/8 is purely mechanical: it renames the `linux-TEST-vars` job to `linux-feature-testbed` in `.github/workflows/main.yml`, `.gitlab-ci.yml`, and `ci/run-build-and-tests.sh`. The new name was chosen by Junio C Hamano from 14 alternatives proposed in the v1 discussion and has already been endorsed by Jeff King (Peff) in earlier rounds.

The job itself exercises Git with non-default options (`OPENSSL_SHA1_UNSAFE`, `GIT_TEST_SPLIT_INDEX`, `GIT_TEST_FULL_IN_PACK_ARRAY`, etc.) to catch edge cases. The old name was awkwardly capitalized and opaque; the new one better describes the job’s purpose as a testbed for experimental features. No behavioral changes are introduced, and the patch is uncontested and ready for integration. The series as a whole is now in Junio’s integration pipeline, with v3 mechanically backported to Git v2.56.0 for potential `maint` inclusion.
