Thread identity: l10n update for `git describe` debug messages, standalone patch by Raj Ojha via GitGitGadget. The patch replaces the `_()` localization wrapper with the `Q_()` pluralization macro in two debug messages in `builtin/describe.c` to support languages with complex pluralization rules.

Core problem or goal: Enable proper grammatical handling of singular and plural forms in debug messages for languages with more complex pluralization rules than English. The change targets two debug messages in `describe_commit()`: `"traversed %lu commits"` and `"found %i tags; gave up search at %s"`. The broader motivation for localizing debug messages is now contested, with Oswald Buddenhagen arguing that debug output is intended for developers (who are likely to understand English) rather than end users, making i18n wrappers here potentially misapplied.
