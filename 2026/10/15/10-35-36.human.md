This is a documentation and usability observation about the experimental `git format-rev` command.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk points out that `git diff-tree --stdin --format=medium` already provides most of the functionality that `git format-rev` was created for: pretty-formatting one revision at a time from standard input. The existing `git diff-tree` approach even has advantages—it supports `--notes` (which `git format-rev` does not) and integrates with the full `rev-list` machinery. The only gap is that `git diff-tree` requires object names rather than revision expressions, but this can be bridged by piping through `git cat-file --batch-command='%(objectname)'`.

The email is not proposing a patch or deprecation, just highlighting that `git format-rev` may be redundant. It’s a concise, well-reasoned observation that could spark discussion about whether the experimental command should be kept, improved, or removed. Worth a quick skim for anyone interested in Git’s revision-formatting tools.
