This is a bugfix patch in the refs subsystem, specifically the files backend, addressing an inconsistency in how root refs (like `HEAD` and `ORIG_HEAD`) are handled in the loose ref cache. The author, Andy Koppe, responds to Junio C Hamano’s proposal for an alternative architectural approach—maintaining two persistent caches (one for refs under `refs/` and one for root refs)—by defending the original design.

Andy argues that the original solution, which creates a temporary ref cache for root refs on demand rather than caching them persistently, is simpler and introduces less overhead. He notes that the `REFS_FOR_EACH_INCLUDE_ROOT_REFS` flag is only used in two places (`git for-each-ref --include-root-refs` and `git refs migrate`), both of which invoke it just once, making persistent caching unnecessary. He also considered modifying the loose ref cache to skip root refs during iteration when the flag is unset but rejected that idea due to its broader impact on the more common case of iterating over non-root refs.

The discussion remains open on whether to adopt Andy’s minimalist approach or Junio’s dual-cache alternative. Andy suggests a third possibility—dropping the flag entirely and requiring callers to explicitly request refs under `refs/`—but acknowledges this would be a larger and riskier change. The patch itself is small and well-scoped, touching only `files-backend.c` and adding a test in `t/t6302-for-each-ref-filter.sh`, but the architectural direction is still under debate. No new implementation has been posted yet, and the trade-offs between simplicity, performance, and maintainability are still being weighed.
