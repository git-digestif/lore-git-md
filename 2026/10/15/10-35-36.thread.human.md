This thread discusses a proposal by Kristoffer Haugsbakk to deprecate the experimental `git format-rev` command, arguing that its functionality—pretty-formatting individual revisions from stdin—is already covered by `git diff-tree --stdin --format=medium`. The replacement pipeline also supports `--notes` (missing in `git format-rev`) and integrates with Git’s full `rev-list` machinery. However, Haugsbakk’s follow-up clarifies a critical limitation: while `git diff-tree` supports NUL-terminated output for basic diff formats, it *cannot* do so for the `--format=medium` pretty-printing mode. This means the proposed pipeline fails to handle multiline commit messages or revision expressions cleanly, a gap that `git format-rev` fills. The thread remains unresolved, with the key question being whether `git format-rev`’s convenience (direct revision expressions, inline object-name annotation) justifies its continued existence despite the overlap with existing tools. No maintainer decision has been recorded.
