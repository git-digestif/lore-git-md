This thread is about whether to deprecate the experimental `git format-rev` command, which pretty-prints Git revisions from stdin. Kristoffer Haugsbakk, the command's original author, initially proposed deprecating it in favor of `git diff-tree --stdin --format=medium`, arguing that the latter covers most of its functionality. However, a critical limitation emerged: while `git diff-tree` supports NUL-separated output, it uses *separators* (not terminators), making it difficult to parse incrementally in scripts that process commits piecemeal. This breaks workflows that rely on unambiguous handling of multiline commit messages or revision expressions, a gap `git format-rev` currently fills.

Kristoffer’s follow-up clarifies that `git diff-tree` cannot fully replace `git format-rev` for his use case—a shell script generating `git log`-like output with interdiffs—because it lacks NUL-terminated diff output for human-readable formats. He experimented with adding a `--use-terminator` option to `git diff-tree` but found the codebase resistant to this change. The thread now hinges on whether the convenience of `git format-rev` (direct revision input, NUL-terminated output) justifies keeping it despite its redundancy with `git diff-tree`, or whether the project should deprecate it and address the functional gap in `git diff-tree` later. No maintainer decision has been recorded, and the discussion remains open.
