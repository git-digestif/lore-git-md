This email is a **feature deprecation proposal** targeting the experimental `git format-rev` command.

Kristoffer Haugsbakk argues that `git diff-tree --stdin --format=medium` already provides the core functionality of `git format-rev`: pretty-formatting one revision at a time from standard input. The existing `git diff-tree` pipeline supports `--notes` (which `git format-rev` lacks) and integrates with the full `rev-list` machinery, including merge display and other log-tree features. While `git format-rev` can accept revision expressions directly and annotate object names in running text, these conveniences can be replicated by prepending `git cat-file --batch-command='%(objectname)'` to the pipeline. The email concludes that `git format-rev`’s raison d’être has been superseded by a combination of existing commands, suggesting it may no longer be needed.
