This thread is about improving `git reflog` usability by adding options to collapse multi-step operations (`--operations`) and show the branch HEAD was on when each entry was recorded (`--historical-branch`). The series, now at v2, was originally proposed by Harald Nordgren to address pain points like obscured reset points during rebasing.

Key participants include Harald (the author), Junio C Hamano (maintainer), Phillip Wood, and Kristoffer Haugsbakk. The discussion has pivoted decisively toward replacing the dedicated flags with format specifiers (e.g., `%gb`) for composability, a shift driven by Phillip’s technical critiques. His review highlighted fundamental flaws in `--operations` (incomplete reflog coverage) and `--historical-branch` (a correctness gap in rebase handling), while Junio proposed broader architectural changes—like making multi-step commands work on detached HEAD—to naturally clean up reflog entries. Harald has accepted Junio’s suggestion to change `git reflog`’s default behavior to operate on the current branch but hasn’t engaged with the larger refactoring.

The series is still under review, with no merge decisions or new patches posted. The latest exchange between Harald and Kristoffer was a low-stakes acknowledgment of feedback, reinforcing that the series may be redesigned or abandoned in favor of the format-specifier approach. Open questions remain about architectural alignment, feature viability, and whether the reflog subsystem is the right place for this functionality.
