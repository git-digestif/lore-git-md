This thread is about a patch series (v5) by Harald Nordgren that adds a `%gb` format placeholder to `git log -g` and `git reflog`, allowing users to see which branch `HEAD` was on for each reflog entry. The feature addresses usability pain points like tracking reset points during rebasing or branch history across renames, without changing default behavior. The series has evolved from earlier proposals for dedicated flags (`--operations` and `--historical-branch`) to a composable format specifier, aligning with Git’s decoration system and addressing prior critiques about correctness and UX.

The v5 series is now split into three patches: (1) a refactoring of hard-coded reflog message strings into `#define` constants, (2) the implementation of `%gb` with fixes for detached HEAD labeling and rebase checkout parsing, and (3) expanded test coverage (now 229 lines in `t/t1414-reflog-walk.sh`).
