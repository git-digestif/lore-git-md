This thread is about Harald Nordgren’s patch series introducing the `%gb` format placeholder to `git log -g` and `git reflog`, allowing users to see branch context in reflog output. The series is now at v3, replacing earlier proposals for dedicated `--operations` and `--historical-branch` flags with a composable format specifier that aligns with Git’s decoration system. The goal is to address usability pain points like tracking reset points during rebasing or branch history across renames without changing default behavior.

The v3 series is under active review, with Phillip Wood’s latest feedback identifying a regression in branch-name detection when `GIT_REFLOG_ACTION` is set. This bug causes incorrect branch labels during rebase operations, breaking real-world workflows that rely on custom reflog messages. The issue is reproducible and actionable, though it doesn’t undermine the `%gb` feature’s core design. Earlier feedback from Phillip and others—such as correctness concerns around `git rebase <upstream> <branch>` and UX trade-offs—has been addressed in v3, and the series is otherwise technically sound. No maintainer decisions have been made yet, and Junio C Hamano’s broader architectural proposals (e.g., refactoring multi-step commands to work on detached HEAD) remain unresolved and orthogonal to this series.

Harald also introduced a new, untriaged proposal in a recent reply: making `git reflog @{0}` the default output to improve usability. This suggestion is separate from the `%gb` series and hasn’t been discussed or implemented. The thread’s key participants include Harald (author), Junio (maintainer), Phillip Wood (reviewer who identified the regression), and D. Ben Knoble (who engaged with Junio’s architectural debate). The series touches 12 files, including core reflog-walking logic, built-in commands, and tests, with no on-disk format changes. The next steps are likely a v4 addressing Phillip’s regression fix, while the broader architectural questions and Harald’s `@{0}` proposal await further discussion.
