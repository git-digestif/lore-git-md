# Thread deltas

## 2026/10/15

- [2026/10/15/21-42-42 by Szymon Wilczek <swilczek.lx@gmail.com>] Szymon Wilczek submitted a patch changing `write_index_as_tree()` in cache-tree.c to take the index lock without `LOCK_DIE_ON_ERROR` (passing flags `0` to `hold_lock_file_for_update`) and to skip `write_locked_index()` when the lock is held (`fd < 0`), so that `git write-tree` and `git am` proceed while another process holds `index.lock`; this fixes a rebase failure where a background `git status` holding the lock caused the sequencer to die before writing `rebase-merge/message`, and the patch adds a test in t/t0090-cache-tree.sh verifying that `git write-tree` succeeds while the index is locked.
