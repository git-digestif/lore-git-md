This is the plumbing patch in the incremental connectivity check series. Patrick Steinhardt raises a small but important architectural concern: the current patch introduces both the `transfer.connectivityCheck` config option (with `incremental` as a valid value) and the `--verify-trees-incremental` flag in the same step, even though the flag does nothing yet. His preference is to split this further: first add the config option but only accept `full` (the default), then introduce the flag in the next patch, and finally enable `incremental` in the last patch. This would make the series even more incremental and easier to review, though it would require a fourth reroll.

The patch itself is otherwise uncontroversial—it is a straightforward setup step that wires up the config plumbing and internal flag. No behavior changes occur at this stage, and the implementation is clean. The question here is purely about reviewability and series structure, not about correctness or functionality. The author has already split the series into four patches to improve reviewability, and Patrick’s suggestion would take that one step further. This is a surface-level review point, but it could help future readers follow the evolution of the feature.
