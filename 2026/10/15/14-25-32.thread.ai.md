Core problem or goal
Eliminate implicit reliance on the global `the_repository` variable by updating functions to accept an explicit `struct repository *` parameter. The original patch targeted `merge.c` (`merge_argument` and `checkout_fast_forward`), but Junio’s review revealed an incomplete dependency: `checkout_fast_forward()` still calls `unpack_trees()`, which itself hardcodes `the_repository`. The goal now extends to refactoring `unpack_trees()` and its helpers to accept an explicit repository parameter, advancing the project-wide `the_repository` removal effort. The thread has also expanded to discuss how to measure and incentivize progress in this effort, with disagreement over the value of the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY` macro and the trade-offs between incremental progress and strict correctness.
