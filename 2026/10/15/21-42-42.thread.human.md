This thread is about a bugfix patch from Szymon Wilczek that prevents `git rebase` and related commands from crashing when another process (like `git status` or Magit) holds the index lock during an in-process commit. The crash leaves rebases in a half-picked state, requiring manual recovery. The patch is minimal, well-motivated, and includes test coverage.

Patrick Steinhardt has reviewed the patch and fully endorses its direction—replacing the fatal `LOCK_DIE_ON_ERROR` flag with a non-blocking lock attempt in `write_index_as_tree()`. He agrees that the cache-tree write-back should be optional and not block the primary operation (computing the tree object). His feedback is mostly structural: he suggests reordering the commit message to follow Git conventions (problem first, then context and solution) and notes a minor stylistic preference for `fd > 0` over `0 <= fd`, though he clarifies this is subjective and not a blocker. The test coverage, which verifies `git write-tree` succeeds while `.git/index.lock` exists, is praised as straightforward and effective.

The patch is now ready to proceed once the commit message is adjusted. No open questions remain, and the fix addresses a real-world annoyance with clear reproduction steps. The series is under review and has no prior versions.
