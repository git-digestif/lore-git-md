This thread began with Okhuomon Ajayi’s patch to remove an implicit dependency on the global `the_repository` variable in Git’s merge machinery, part of the ongoing project-wide effort to eliminate this global. The patch updated two functions in `merge.c` to accept an explicit `struct repository *` parameter, a mechanical but well-scoped change that drew a surface-level "looks good" from Usman Akinyemi and a process note from Junio C Hamano about email headers.

Junio’s latest reply reveals that the original patch was incomplete: `checkout_fast_forward()` still calls `unpack_trees()`, which itself hardcodes `the_repository` in multiple places. His 94-line RFC patch demonstrates how to extend the refactoring into `unpack-trees.c`, introducing a new `unpack_repo()` helper to extract the repository from the options or index state. While not intended for immediate merge, the patch serves as a proof-of-concept for incremental progress and marks the topic as ready for `next` once the deeper dependency is addressed. The thread now centers on whether this follow-up work will be taken up by the original author or another contributor, with no substantive review yet engaging with the behavioral correctness of either patch.
