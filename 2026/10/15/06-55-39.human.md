This is a **surface-level review** of the performance test suite for the incremental connectivity check feature. Patrick Steinhardt points out two mechanical issues in the second patch of the v3 series:

1. The new Perl-based test generator (`generate-repo-p5413-connectivity-check.perl`) and all benchmarks that depend on it must be marked with the `PERL` prerequisite. This ensures the tests are skipped on systems without Perl, maintaining compatibility. The omission is a straightforward oversight and will not affect functionality once fixed.

2. The usage comment in the Perl script still references the old test number (`p5412`) instead of the updated one (`p5413`). This is a leftover from the test renumbering in v3 and should be corrected for consistency.

Both issues are minor and do not impact the substance of the benchmarks. The test suite itself remains well-designed, with clear isolation of the connectivity check overhead and thorough coverage of the three key dimensions (repository size, incoming commit count, and files changed per commit). The fixes are trivial and will likely be addressed in the next reroll without further discussion. The series as a whole is still on track for integration, with Junio C Hamano having signaled strong interest and no substantive objections remaining.
