This is a maintainer's reply in an ongoing bugfix discussion about the files ref backend. Junio C Hamano engages with Patrick Steinhardt's earlier design questions by proposing an alternative approach: instead of creating a temporary root-ref cache for each iterator, maintain two persistent caches—one for refs under `refs/` and one for root refs like `HEAD`—and let callers choose which to use.

The email is brief and exploratory, not a final decision. Junio frames the idea as a curiosity rather than a directive, leaving the door open for further discussion. The thread remains focused on the same subsystem (`files-backend.c`) and the same inconsistency (root refs in the loose ref cache), but now has a concrete alternative on the table. No new code or tests are introduced; this is purely a design-level exchange.
