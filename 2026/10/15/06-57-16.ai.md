This is the seventh patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s **v4 CI/build system housekeeping series** (8 patches total). It **removes dead Python 2 logic** from the CI scripts after the last Ubuntu 20.04 job was dropped in an earlier patch.

The change is mechanical and uncontroversial: `ci/install-dependencies.sh` hard-codes `python3` instead of the now-unused `PYTHON_PACKAGE` variable, and `ci/lib.sh` simplifies its `MAKEFLAGS` assignment by removing the Ubuntu-20.04-specific branch. The commit message explains that Python 2 reached EOL in 2020, no supported CI distro still packages it, and the world has moved to Python 3. No behavioral impact; the patch merely cleans up dead code.

Key symbols: `PYTHON_PACKAGE` (removed), `PYTHON_PATH` (now hard-coded to `/usr/bin/python3`), and the `ubuntu-*` case in `ci/lib.sh`. The patch touches `ci/install-dependencies.sh` and `ci/lib.sh`, with 2 insertions and 11 deletions. It is ready for integration, having passed prior review rounds without substantive objections.
