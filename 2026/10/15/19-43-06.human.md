This is a bugfix targeting the files ref backend, part of an ongoing discussion about how to handle root refs (like `HEAD` and `ORIG_HEAD`) in the loose ref cache. Andy Koppe responds to Junio C Hamano’s suggestion of maintaining two persistent caches—one for refs under `refs/` and another for root refs—by pushing back slightly. He argues that the overhead of caching root refs isn’t justified, since the `REFS_FOR_EACH_INCLUDE_ROOT_REFS` flag is only used in two places (`git for-each-ref --include-root-refs` and `git refs migrate`), and both invoke it just once. His original patch avoids caching root refs entirely, creating a temporary ref cache only when needed, which he sees as simpler and lower-impact.

Andy also briefly considers an alternative: keeping root refs in the loose ref cache but skipping them during iteration if the flag is unset. However, he dismisses this as more disruptive to the common case where root refs are *not* requested. His preferred solution remains the original patch, though he acknowledges that a more sweeping change—dropping the flag and making all callers explicitly request `refs/`—could be cleaner but would be riskier and more invasive.

The discussion is still open, with no clear consensus yet on which architectural approach will win out. The patch itself is small and well-scoped, but the thread has expanded into a design debate about how best to handle root refs in the long term.
