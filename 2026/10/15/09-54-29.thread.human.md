This thread began as a simple l10n update to improve pluralization in two debug messages in `git describe`, replacing `_()` with `Q_()` to better support languages with complex plural rules. Raj Ojha’s patch, though technically sound, overlaps with an earlier submission by Antek Borowski, which Junio C Hamano noted as the likely reference for this change. The patch itself is uncontroversial in its implementation, but its value was already in question due to the prior work.

The discussion has now taken a broader turn with Oswald Buddenhagen’s review, which challenges the very premise of localizing debug messages. He argues that debug output is meant for developers—who are presumed to understand English—rather than end users, suggesting that the effort to improve localization here may be misplaced. This raises a philosophical question about the scope of Git’s i18n efforts: should debug messages be localized at all, or is this a case of over-engineering for an audience that doesn’t need it? The thread now has two open questions: which version of the patch to adopt (Ojha’s or Borowski’s) and whether the change is justified in the first place. No decisions have been made yet, and the discussion is likely to continue before any resolution.
