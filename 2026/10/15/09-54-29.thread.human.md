This thread began as a small l10n improvement to pluralize two debug messages in `git describe`, but has grown into a broader discussion about whether debug output should be localized at all. Raj Ojha proposed replacing `_()` with `Q_()` in two debug messages to support languages with complex pluralization rules, only to discover that Antek Borowski had submitted a nearly identical patch three days earlier. Junio C Hamano pointed out the overlap and encouraged contributors to check the archives before sending patches.

The discussion took a philosophical turn when Oswald Buddenhagen questioned whether debug messages—intended for developers rather than end users—should be localized in the first place. Junio now notes that this question was already raised by Jeff King (Peff) earlier in the thread, leading Borowski to submit a patch that not only fixes the pluralization issue but also removes the `_()` wrapper from *all* debug messages in `builtin/describe.c`, effectively de-localizing them. Borowski’s patch has become the focal point for both the technical change and the policy debate, leaving Ojha’s patch redundant unless the community decides to keep debug messages localized. The thread remains under review, with no clear resolution on whether to adopt Borowski’s approach or revisit the broader i18n scope question.
