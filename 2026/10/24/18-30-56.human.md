This is a **platform-compatibility bugfix** for a test in the recently merged `ps/pack-check-bigfile-threshold` topic. The patch addresses a macOS-specific failure in `t/t1450-fsck.sh` where the BSD `mv` utility refuses to overwrite a read-only loose object file, even when the destination is the same inode. Torsten Bögershausen confirms the root cause: the BSD `mv` (not the shell) prompts for confirmation when overwriting read-only files, and this behavior is not suppressed by POSIX's "standard input is a terminal" exception. The fix is simple and uncontroversial: add the `-f` flag to force the overwrite.

The patch is narrowly scoped to test infrastructure and does not touch the substantive behavior of the original series. It is a follow-up to Junio's "What's cooking" report, which already acknowledged the issue and directed a separate patch rather than a reroll. The change is well-motivated and clearly explained, with debug output and POSIX references included in the commit message. No objections are expected; this should be a straightforward merge once Junio picks it up.
