# Thread deltas

## 2026/10/24

- [2026/10/24/05-24-49 by Tanga Joris <tangajoris@gmail.com>] Tanga Joris posted a patch dropping the NO_PARSEOPT flag from four builtins that now use parse-options—diff-pairs, fast-import, patch-id, and var—so that "git --list-cmds=parseopt" includes them and bash completion can offer their options via "--git-completion-helper"; the patch notes that removing the flag from var does not change its completion because it only has short options (-l and -z), and that rev-parse keeps the flag because its normal options are still parsed by hand.

## 2026/10/25

- [2026/10/25/07-59-07 by René Scharfe <l.s.r@web.de>] René Scharfe endorsed the proposal to drop NO_PARSEOPT from the four builtins, but noted that "git fast-import --git-completion-helper" prints its command-line options only after it receives a line of input, and asked whether the completion scripts still work seamlessly without requiring the user to press <return>.
