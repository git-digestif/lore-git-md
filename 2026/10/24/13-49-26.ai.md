His personal tooling (`git sbup`, `git rdup`, `git div`) demonstrates that this is not just theoretical: he built these helpers *before* Harald’s patch existed, suggesting the need is real and widespread among users who work in triangular setups.

He suggests that his own habits—checking divergence between local and push branches—could help Harald strengthen the commit message by framing the change as a way to bring built-in Git tooling closer to what power users already do manually.
