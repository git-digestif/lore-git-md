Core problem or goal
Git's Bash completion script was not offering command-line options for four built-in commands (`patch-id`, `diff-pairs`, `fast-import`, `var`) because they were still marked with the `NO_PARSEOPT` flag in `git.c` even though they had already been converted to use the `parse-options` API. The patch removes the flag so that completion works as expected.
