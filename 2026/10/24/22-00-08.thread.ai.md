Core problem or goal: Eliminate global state for line-ending configuration to advance Git's libification, making the codebase more modular and reusable. The change follows the established pattern of prior conversions in the libification effort, specifically preparing working tree conversion functions to access repository-specific configuration.

Current status: Under review (all four patches posted). No merge status recorded.

Key technical details:
- Files touched: `convert.c`, `convert.h`, `environment.c`, `environment.h` (primary), plus `combine-diff.c`, `diff.c`, `entry.c`, `object-file.c`, `parallel-checkout.c` (across the series).

The `get_conv_flags()` helper in `object-file.c` is updated to accept an `index_state` parameter to locate the repository.
