Thread identity: Refactoring patch series (4 patches) by Aniket Gupta to move all line-ending configuration variables (`core.autocrlf`, `core.eol`, `core.safecrlf`) from global variables into `struct repo_config_values` as part of the Git libification effort. Series version: v1 (withdrawn by author following critical review; no resubmission planned until underlying architectural issue is resolved).

Core problem or goal: Eliminate global state for line-ending configuration to advance Git's libification, making the codebase more modular and reusable. The change follows the established pattern of prior conversions in the libification effort, specifically preparing working tree conversion functions to access repository-specific configuration. The architectural goal remains valid, but the current implementation is blocked by a critical design flaw that actively undermines libification.

Current status: **Withdrawn from review** (all four patches posted as v1, now explicitly withdrawn by author). Patrick Steinhardt's critical review identified a fundamental design flaw (hard-coded dependency on `the_repository` via `git_default_config()`) that prevents the series from being merged in its current form. Aniket Gupta has acknowledged the issue and withdrawn the series until the underlying architectural problem (decoupling `git_default_config()` from the global repository) is resolved. No merge status recorded; the series is not under active consideration. The architectural blocker is now linked to a prior discussion thread (`<xmqq8q5n1fa2.fsf@gitster.g>`) that documents ongoing work to resolve the hard-coded dependency.
