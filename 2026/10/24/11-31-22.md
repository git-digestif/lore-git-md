> I don't think I converted mergetag entries in my original series.  The
> problem is that if we don't convert them, then we also can't validate
> them because we don't know what data they point to.  We don't make
> assumptions based on the size of the hash because in the future we might
> also have SHA3-256 or BLAKE2b-256.


I'm not sure that is quite correct. Any supported hash function should be unique enough such that we can just try all of them. So, a mergetag or proprietary headers, if we just treat them as text in conversion function, they can be retrieved later using the original function. Hash function doesn't matter for their existence or validity. I'm assuming here that the original repo containing them is not replaced in the meantime.

Basically, I'm assuming that a repo url keeps their original hash function.

The most important part here is to be consistent. These shouldn't be converted when available and skipped otherwise, or history becomes nondeterministic and depends on ingenuity of converting person to find these objects.
