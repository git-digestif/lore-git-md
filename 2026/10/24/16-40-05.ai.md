This is a maintainer's reply in the ongoing `git reflog` feature discussion, weighing in on Phillip Wood’s critique of the `--operations` option. Junio C Hamano endorses the *direction* of the series—making reflog output easier to understand without manual filtering—but sidesteps the architectural debate about whether `--operations` belongs in Git’s core. Instead, he suggests a broader refactoring of multi-step commands (like `git am` and `git cherry-pick`) to work on detached HEAD by default, which would naturally produce cleaner reflog entries without needing a heuristic-based filter. He acknowledges that `git am` predates detached HEAD and users expect it to work on the current branch, but implies this could be revisited for consistency with `git rebase`. His postscript about defaulting `git reflog` to the current branch (rather than HEAD) aligns with Phillip’s earlier suggestion but doesn’t engage with the `--historical-branch` correctness issues or the format-specifier alternative. The tone is pragmatic: he focuses on workflow improvements rather than rejecting the feature outright, but his proposal would require significant changes to long-standing commands, making it a long-term architectural shift rather than a quick fix.
