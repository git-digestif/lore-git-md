This is the second patch in Aniket Gupta's four-patch series refactoring Git's line-ending configuration as part of the libification effort. It moves the `core.autocrlf` setting from the global variable `auto_crlf` into `struct repo_config_values`, following the preparatory groundwork laid in patch 1/4.

The patch is narrowly focused: it relocates the `enum auto_crlf` definition to `environment.h` (to avoid circular dependencies), adds the field to `struct repo_config_values`, initializes it in `repo_config_values_init()`, and introduces a getter function `repo_auto_crlf()`. The bulk of the diff updates `convert.c` to use the new getter instead of the global variable, propagating the `struct repository *` parameter through the call chain. The changes are mechanical and follow the established pattern of prior libification conversions.

A minor complication is the circular dependency that would arise if `enum auto_crlf` were moved to `convert.h`; the patch keeps it in `environment.h` under an existing NEEDSWORK comment, deferring cleanup to a future effort. The commit message clearly explains this trade-off and the reasoning behind it.

This patch is a clear, incremental step in the libification effort. It does not change behavior or on-disk format, and the technical approach is consistent with earlier work in the same area. The main point of discussion for the series as a whole -- whether to pass `repo` to functions or store resolved values in `struct conv_attrs` -- is not directly addressed here but remains open for feedback on the cover letter.
