This thread began as a four-patch series by Aniket Gupta to move Git’s line-ending configuration variables (`core.autocrlf`, `core.eol`, `core.safecrlf`) out of global state and into `struct repo_config_values`, a key step in Git’s libification effort. The series was withdrawn after Patrick Steinhardt identified a critical design flaw: the conversion still relied on `git_default_config()`, which hard-codes a dependency on the global `the_repository`, undermining the goal of making the codebase modular and reusable. Aniket acknowledged the issue and withdrew the series, noting that the underlying architectural problem must be resolved before the conversion can proceed.

The core issue remains unresolved, but Patrick’s follow-up provides a pointer to an earlier discussion thread (`<xmqq8q5n1fa2.fsf@gitster.g>`) that details ongoing work to decouple `git_default_config()` from `the_repository`. Until that effort is completed, the line-ending configuration conversion is blocked. No other participants have weighed in on the architectural question, and the series is not under active review. The broader libification effort continues, but this particular conversion will depend on progress in the referenced thread.
