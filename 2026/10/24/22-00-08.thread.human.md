This thread is about a four-patch series by Aniket Gupta to refactor Git’s line-ending configuration variables (`core.autocrlf`, `core.eol`, `core.safecrlf`) out of global state and into `struct repo_config_values`, as part of the broader libification effort. The goal was to make the codebase more modular by eliminating reliance on global variables, following the pattern of earlier conversions in the same effort.

The series has hit a critical roadblock. Patrick Steinhardt’s review identified a fundamental design flaw: the current implementation *increases* coupling to `the_repository` because `repo_config_values()` can only be populated via `git_default_config()`, which hard-codes the global repository. This directly contradicts the libification goal of making Git more reusable as a library. Patrick explicitly stated the series should not be merged in its current form, and Aniket has withdrawn it pending resolution of this issue. The thread is now focused on next steps—specifically, how to decouple `git_default_config()` from `the_repository`—though no concrete proposals have been made yet.

The technical details of the original series remain unchanged: it touched `convert.c`, `environment.c`, and related files, introduced repository-specific getters for line-ending configuration, and eliminated the `global_conv_flags_eol` global. However, the open question is no longer about implementation details but about the broader architectural challenge of removing hard-coded dependencies on `the_repository`. Aniket has asked for pointers to ongoing work or discussions on this topic, indicating he intends to contribute to that effort. Until this is resolved, the series is effectively blocked.
