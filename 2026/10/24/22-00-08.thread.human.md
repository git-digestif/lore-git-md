This thread began as an attempt by Aniket Gupta to refactor Git’s line-ending configuration variables (`core.autocrlf`, `core.eol`, `core.safecrlf`) out of global state and into a repository-specific structure, part of the broader libification effort to make Git more modular. The four-patch series was withdrawn after Patrick Steinhardt identified a critical design flaw: the conversion still relied on `git_default_config()`, which hard-codes a dependency on the global `the_repository`, undermining the very goal of libification. Aniket acknowledged the issue and withdrew the series, and Junio C Hamano requested a warning comment near the `repo_config_values` structure to prevent similar missteps until the architectural blocker is resolved.

The procedural follow-up here confirms that Junio has already sent the patch adding the requested warning comment, closing that administrative loop. The thread’s focus remains on the unresolved architectural issue—decoupling `git_default_config()` from `the_repository`—which must be addressed before any further configuration-variable conversions can proceed. No new technical discussion or code changes have emerged, and the original series is not under active review. The warning comment is now in flight, but the core blocker persists, leaving the libification effort in a holding pattern for this particular conversion.
