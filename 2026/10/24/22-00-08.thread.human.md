This thread began as a refactoring effort by Aniket Gupta to move Git’s line-ending configuration variables (`core.autocrlf`, `core.eol`, `core.safecrlf`) out of global state and into `struct repo_config_values`, advancing the project’s libification goals. The series was withdrawn after Patrick Steinhardt identified a critical design flaw: the conversion still relied on `git_default_config()`, which hard-codes a dependency on `the_repository`, undermining the very modularity the change aimed to achieve. Aniket acknowledged the issue and paused the work until the underlying architectural problem—decoupling `git_default_config()` from the global repository—is resolved.

The thread has now concluded procedurally. Junio C Hamano sent a patch adding a warning comment near the `repo_config_values` structure to deter further attempts to move configuration variables into it until the blocker is fixed. Patrick Steinhardt acknowledged the patch, effectively closing the loop on this iteration of the effort. The libification of line-ending configuration is on hold pending resolution of the broader architectural issue, which is being tracked in a separate thread. No code from this series has been merged, and the original patches remain withdrawn.
