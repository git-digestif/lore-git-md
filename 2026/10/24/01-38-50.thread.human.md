This thread is about a small but user-visible bug in `git reflog expire --dry-run --verbose`: when you name specific refs on the command line, it incorrectly reports "prune" instead of "would prune", breaking the expected dry-run consistency. Laveesh Bansal sent a minimal one-line patch that fixes the inconsistency by ensuring the `dry_run` flag is properly propagated for named refs, matching the behavior already in place for `--all`. The patch also adds thorough tests to verify the output wording and that no entries are actually removed during dry runs.

Junio C Hamano reviewed the patch and agreed the fix is correct, but suggested a deeper refactoring: instead of maintaining two nearly identical loops (one for `--all`, one for named refs), he proposed consolidating them into a single loop that processes a unified list of refs. This would eliminate the duplication that led to the bug in the first place and reduce the risk of future inconsistencies. The thread is now at a decision point: the original patch is ready to merge as-is, but Junio’s feedback opens the door to a v2 that addresses both the immediate bug and the underlying structural issue. No other reviews or objections have been posted yet.
