This is the third patch in Aniket Gupta’s four-patch series that moves Git’s line-ending configuration (`core.autocrlf`, `core.eol`, `core.safecrlf`) from global variables into `struct repo_config_values` as part of the ongoing libification effort. The patch itself migrates `core.eol`—the setting that controls which line endings (LF or CRLF) are used when files are checked out—into the per-repository configuration structure.

The change is mechanical and follows the exact pattern established in the first two patches: the global `core_eol` variable is removed, the `enum eol` type is kept in `environment.h` to avoid circular dependencies, a new getter function `repo_core_eol()` is added, and every caller in `convert.c` is updated to use the repository-specific value. The patch touches four files (`convert.c`, `convert.h`, `environment.c`, `environment.h`) and is small (29 insertions, 20 deletions), making it easy to verify.

There are no behavior changes—this is purely a refactoring step—and the commit message clearly explains the trade-off (keeping the enum in `environment.h` under a NEEDSWORK comment) that was already discussed in the series cover letter. The patch looks clean and well-scoped, continuing the established libification pattern without introducing new design questions. If the first two patches were uncontroversial, this one is likely to be as well.
