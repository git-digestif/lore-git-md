It replaces manual integer arithmetic in `create_delta_index()` with Git’s safe integer helpers (`st_mult`, `st_add`, `st_add3`) to guard against silent overflows during allocation size calculations. The patch itself is mechanical—no behavior change except that overflows now trigger early failure—and touches only the two allocation calculations in the function.
