This thread is about fixing a Bash completion regression for four Git built-in commands (`patch-id`, `diff-pairs`, `fast-import`, and `var`) that weren’t offering command-line options due to an outdated `NO_PARSEOPT` flag. Tanga Joris’s v4 series (three patches) centralizes the completion-helper logic in `parse-options.c`, removing the need for per-command boilerplate and addressing edge cases like `fast-import` blocking on input or creating temporary files. The series is under review and depends on Christian Couder’s `cc/early-scan-options` series stabilizing in `next`.

Junio Hamano recently reviewed the first patch, suggesting a tweak to the code documentation in `parse-options.h` to avoid overgeneralizing `fast-import`’s unique edge case. The feedback is minor and focuses on clarity rather than technical correctness. All prior review feedback—including substantive input from René Scharfe and Cynthia Uche, and surface-level suggestions from Kaartic Sivaraam—has been addressed. Cynthia’s tested review confirmed the refactoring doesn’t alter existing behavior for any built-in command. The only remaining loose ends are the dependency on `cc/early-scan-options` and minor test-coverage gaps for `patch-id` and `diff-pairs`. The series is otherwise ready for integration once its dependency is resolved.
