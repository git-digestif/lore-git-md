This thread is about a small but user-visible regression in Git’s Bash completion: four built-in commands (`patch-id`, `diff-pairs`, `fast-import`, and `var`) weren’t showing their command-line options during tab completion, even though they had already been converted to use the `parse-options` API. Tanga Joris’s initial patch proposed removing the `NO_PARSEOPT` flag from all four commands in `git.c` to restore completion.

René Scharfe pointed out a wrinkle: `fast-import` behaves differently from the others—it only prints its options *after* receiving a line of input, which could break seamless tab completion. In response, Tanga has now split the fix into two parts. The immediate v2 patch will remove `NO_PARSEOPT` only from the three commands that return immediately (`patch-id`, `diff-pairs`, and `var`), leaving `fast-import` for a later patch. That follow-up will build on Christian Couder’s “early-scan” series to teach `fast-import` to answer `--git-completion-helper` without waiting for input. The split keeps the current fix safe and uncontroversial while deferring the more complex adjustment for `fast-import`. No test coverage has been added yet, and the patch remains under review.
