This thread is about fixing a regression in Git’s Bash completion where four built-in commands (`patch-id`, `diff-pairs`, `fast-import`, and `var`) weren’t showing their available options because they were incorrectly marked with the `NO_PARSEOPT` flag. Tanga Joris’s v3 series (two patches) centralizes the handling of `--git-completion-helper` in `parse-options.c`, removing the need for per-command boilerplate and avoiding side effects like temporary file creation or blocking on input. The series is under review and depends on Christian Couder’s `cc/early-scan-options` series being stable and preferably in `next`.

The refactoring has been validated by Cynthia Uche, who tested both patches across *all* built-in commands and confirmed the changes don’t alter existing behavior. She also proposed a new test case for the `--git-completion-helper-all` variant in `t9300-fast-import.sh`, which improves coverage for an edge case not previously addressed. The series has received substantive feedback from René Scharfe (who identified the `fast-import` edge case) and Junio Hamano (who guided the refactoring), as well as surface-level suggestions from Kaartic Sivaraam. The only remaining loose ends are the dependency on `cc/early-scan-options` and the lack of test coverage for `patch-id`, `diff-pairs`, and `var`—though the latter may not be critical given the broad compatibility testing already performed. The patch is uncontroversial and appears ready for integration once the dependency is resolved.
