This thread is about fixing a Bash completion regression that left four Git built-in commands (`patch-id`, `diff-pairs`, `fast-import`, and `var`) without option completion. Tanga Joris’s v3 series centralizes the `--git-completion-helper` logic in `parse-options.c`, eliminating per-command boilerplate and side effects like temporary file creation or blocking on input. The two-patch series is under review and depends on Christian Couder’s `cc/early-scan-options` series stabilizing in `next`.

Key participants include Tanga (the author), René Scharfe (who identified the `fast-import` edge case), and Junio Hamano (who proposed the refactoring approach). The series is uncontroversial and ready for integration once its dependency is stable. Kaartic Sivaraam’s recent review added two minor suggestions—improving the commit message and adding a code comment—but these are surface-level and don’t affect the technical direction. The only remaining loose ends are the lack of test coverage for three of the four commands and the dependency on `cc/early-scan-options`.
