This thread is about a small but user-visible bug fix to Git’s Bash completion script. Tanga Joris noticed that four built-in commands (`patch-id`, `diff-pairs`, `fast-import`, and `var`) weren’t showing any command-line options during tab-completion, even though they had already been converted to use Git’s `parse-options` API. The fix is simple: remove the `NO_PARSEOPT` flag from these commands in `git.c`.

René Scharfe has reviewed the patch and raised a potential issue with one of the commands, `fast-import`. He points out that when `fast-import` is called with `--git-completion-helper`, it only prints its options *after* receiving a line of input. This could break the expected tab-completion flow, forcing users to press Enter before seeing any options. The other three commands don’t seem to have this problem, so the fix might need to be adjusted for `fast-import` specifically. No other reviews or objections have been posted yet, and the patch is still under review.
