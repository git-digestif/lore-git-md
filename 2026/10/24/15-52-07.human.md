This is a **surface-level review** of Ralf Habacker’s patch adding a “Cherry-pick with -x” context-menu action to `gitk`. Johannes Sixt (Hannes), the current `gitk` maintainer, raises two main concerns.

First, he questions the necessity of the feature: he personally uses `git cherry-pick -x` so rarely that he prefers the command line, and the context menu is already crowded. He invites others to weigh in on whether the feature is worth adding. Second, he suggests UI and implementation tweaks: replacing the jargon “-x” with “cross-reference” in the menu label, avoiding edits to the translation files, and cleaning up the command-line construction so that unused options (`-r` or `-x`) do not appear together. The review is polite and constructive, but remains at the level of wording, menu layout, and code style rather than deeper technical scrutiny.
