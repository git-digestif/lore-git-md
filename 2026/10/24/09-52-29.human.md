This is a brief but substantive clarification in the ongoing Outreachy microproject thread about test coverage for `git check-ref-format --no-allow-onelevel`.

Phillip Wood corrects a minor misunderstanding in Harald Nordgren’s earlier reply: the command `git check-ref-format --allow-onelevel --no-allow-onelevel foo` fails not because the two options are mutually exclusive in general, but because `--no-allow-onelevel` *overrides* `--allow-onelevel` (a standard Git option-parsing convention), and "foo" is a one-level ref that the now-active `--no-allow-onelevel` rejects. This distinction matters for the test suite: the expected behavior is not just an error exit code, but specifically a rejection of the one-level ref format due to the overriding option. The thread remains exploratory, with no objections to the microproject itself.
