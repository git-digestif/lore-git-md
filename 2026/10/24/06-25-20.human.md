This is a surface-level review of the RFC patch proposing `--merged` and `--no-merged` filters for `git worktree list`. Eric Sunshine briefly endorses Junio C Hamano’s earlier suggestion that the current design is too narrowly focused on a specific use case. He draws an analogy to `git submodule foreach`, implying that a more general filtering or iteration mechanism might be a better fit for worktree operations than hard-coded merge-status filters.

The review does not engage with the technical implementation, test coverage, or edge cases of the current patch. Instead, it reinforces the maintainer’s call for a broader design approach—specifically, a primitive like `--branch` that could be combined with existing Git commands rather than introducing new, specialized options. No alternative implementation is proposed, and the feedback remains at the conceptual level. The thread is still in the RFC stage, with no indication of whether the original author plans to revise the patch in response to these design concerns.
