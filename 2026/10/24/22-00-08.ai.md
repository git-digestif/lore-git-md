This is a refactoring patch series (4 patches) that continues the Git libification effort by moving three line-ending configuration variables (`core.autocrlf`, `core.eol`, and `core.safecrlf`) from global variables into `struct repo_config_values`. The series is authored by Aniket Gupta, a first-time contributor to this area.

The motivation is clear: eliminating global state to make Git's internals more modular and reusable. The approach follows the pattern established by earlier conversions in the libification effort. Patch 1/4 passes a `struct repository *` parameter to working tree conversion functions so they can access the repository-specific configuration values. Patches 2-4 each move one variable into `repo_config_values`, mirroring the structure of prior work.

The patch touches nine files, primarily `convert.c` and `environment.c`, with minor adjustments in `combine-diff.c`, `diff.c`, `entry.c`, `object-file.c`, and `parallel-checkout.c`. The changes are mechanical but non-trivial: 177 insertions and 100 deletions across the series. The author notes an alternative design (storing resolved values in `struct conv_attrs`) but chose the repository-passing approach for consistency with prior conversions.

This is part of the broader libification effort, which aims to make Git's codebase more library-like by removing global variables and making functions take explicit parameters. The series appears well-scoped and follows established patterns, suggesting it is a serious contribution. The main point of discussion is likely to be the design choice between passing `repo` and storing resolved values in `conv_attrs`, as the author explicitly invites feedback on this.
