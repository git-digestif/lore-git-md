This is the fourth patch in Jon Simons' **v2 performance-focused feature series** (12 patches) that speeds up client-side refspec matching in `git push`. This patch specifically optimizes `check_push_refs()` by replacing its O(R * N) linear traversal of local refs with `strmap` lookups, reducing the complexity to O(R * rules) for explicit refspecs.

The change introduces a new helper function, `count_refspec_match_in_map()`, which mirrors the logic of the existing `count_refspec_match()` but operates on a pre-built `strmap` of local refs. The patch temporarily adds `match_explicit_lhs_map()` alongside the original `match_explicit_lhs()` to avoid disrupting `match_explicit()` until a later patch in the series. Performance benchmarks in the commit message show measurable improvements for scenarios with many local refs and multiple refspecs, particularly in the "mirror" case (e.g., a 31.1% speedup for 100 refspecs).
