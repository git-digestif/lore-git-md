This is the seventh and final patch in Phillip Wood’s `pw/stash-optimize-check_changes-calls/v2` series, a performance-focused refactoring of `git stash`. The patch simplifies the `reset_tree()` function signature by removing two unused parameters (`update` and `reset`), since the sole caller (`do_apply_stash()`) never needs to update the worktree or reset the index. The change is purely mechanical—no behavior is altered, but the code becomes cleaner and the function’s purpose clearer.

The patch touches only `builtin/stash.c` and is part of a broader effort to eliminate redundant index operations and `lstat()` calls in `git stash push` and `git stash create`. Earlier patches in the series introduced an `enum create_result` to distinguish "no changes to stash" from "nothing stashed," removed duplicate `check_changes()` calls, and consolidated index loads. This patch continues that theme by tidying up a helper function that was carrying unnecessary baggage.
