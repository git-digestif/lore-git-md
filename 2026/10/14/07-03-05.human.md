This is a **CI/build system** patch in the ongoing CI housekeeping series. The thread focuses on the contested rename of the `linux-TEST-vars` job, which tests Git with non-default `GIT_TEST_*` variables like `OPENSSL_SHA1_UNSAFE` and `GIT_TEST_SPLIT_INDEX`. The current proposal (`linux-exotic`) was criticized by SZEDER Gábor as misleading, and Junio C Hamano has now proposed 14 alternatives, grouped into categories like "feature/capability" and "testing/robustness."

In this email, Patrick Steinhardt responds to Junio’s suggestions. He rejects `linux-experimental-features` as inaccurate, since not all tested features are experimental, but accepts `linux-feature-testbed` as a clearer and more precise name. The change is purely mechanical—three one-line substitutions in `.github/workflows/main.yml`, `.gitlab-ci.yml`, and `ci/run-build-and-tests.sh`—with no behavioral impact. The broader series (v3) is otherwise ready, pending this naming resolution.

The patch itself is uncontroversial and well-motivated; the only debate is about naming semantics. Patrick’s adoption of `linux-feature-testbed` resolves the ambiguity Gábor raised while keeping the job’s purpose transparent. If no further objections arise, this rename will likely land in v3. The rest of the series—removing outdated dependencies, simplifying scripts, and adjusting test coverage—remains on track for review.
