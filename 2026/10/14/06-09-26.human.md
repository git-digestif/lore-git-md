This is a bugfix series from Johannes Schindelin, addressing integer overflow risks flagged by Coverity in Git for Windows. The patches touch low-level plumbing (`archive-tar.c`, `http.c`, `wrapper.c`, `date.c`) and introduce helper macros in `git-compat-util.h` to safely detect multiplication overflows.

The motivation is clear: Coverity identified potential overflows in several core functions, and this series plugs those holes. Each patch is small and focused, with the commit messages explaining the specific risk (e.g., zero-length input in `get_path_prefix`, unchecked multiplication in `approxidate`). The new `signed_mult_overflows` and `mult_overflows` macros are reusable and could benefit other parts of the codebase.

This is the fifth Coverity-fix series from Johannes in recent months, and the earlier ones have already been merged to `master`. The patches look well-scoped and uncontroversial—typical defensive programming that should be easy to review. If you care about robustness in edge cases, this series is worth a quick skim.
