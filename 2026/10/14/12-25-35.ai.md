This is a **performance bugfix** for the connectivity check in deepening fetches, reviewed and approved by Patrick Steinhardt. The patch resolves a regression where `--alternate-refs` forced Git to walk the full history of large alternates (e.g., 7M objects) during shallow fetches, turning an instant check into a multi-minute operation (227s in the reported case). The fix moves `--alternate-refs` into the non-deepening branch of `check_connected()`, aligning it with `--all` logic, which already skips unnecessary history walks.

The change is minimal: a one-line move in `connected.c` and two new tests in `t/t5618-alternate-refs.sh`. The tests use `GIT_TRACE2_EVENT` to verify `rev-list` arguments for regular and deepening fetches, confirming alternate refs are excluded during deepening without asserting behavioral differences (since the outcome remains correct for complete alternates). Patrick notes the tests are "roundabout" but accepts them as the best available approach, given the difficulty of testing this behavior directly.

The commit message provides clear historical context (regression introduced by commits 39b44ba771 and cf1e7c0770) and explains the performance impact in CI environments. The solution is targeted and well-motivated, addressing a real-world pain point without disrupting non-deepening fetches.
