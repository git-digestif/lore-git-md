This is a **performance-focused feature series** targeting the client-side refspec matching logic in `git push`. The v2 revision from Jon Simons addresses scalability bottlenecks when pushing many branch deletions to servers with large numbers of refs (e.g., 100k). The series achieves **10–100x speedups** in benchmarked scenarios by replacing linear ref traversals with `strmap` lookups and fixing long-standing behavioral quirks in `refname_match()`.

Key improvements in v2 include squashing failing-test commits into their corresponding fixes (addressing Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s feedback) and plugging a memory leak in `send-pack` exposed by the duplicate-destination rejection logic. The series now consists of 12 patches, down from 15 in v1, with clearer commit boundaries. Performance gains are well-documented: a `git push --dry-run` of 100 delete refspecs to a 100k-ref server now takes **0.15–0.19s** (down from 2.5–8.9s), with the largest speedups coming from removing `mkpath()` overhead in `refname_match()` (patch 3) and converting three linear traversals in `remote.c` to `strmap` lookups (patches 4, 9, and 12).

Notable behavioral changes include rejecting duplicate destinations client-side (patch 8) and stopping the silent stripping of leading `./` in refnames (patch 3), both of which are now thoroughly tested. The series touches **`refs.c`**, **`remote.c`**, and **`builtin/send-pack.c`**, with new tests in **`t5516`**, **`t5408`**, **`t5410`**, and **`t5510`**, plus a perf script (**`t/perf/p5516-push-delete-refspec.sh`**). The commit messages are detailed, and the benchmark table in the cover letter provides granular evidence of the improvements.

The work appears well-scoped and thoroughly tested, addressing both correctness and performance in a high-impact area (large-repository workflows). The series is **under review** and not yet in any integration branch. No substantive technical reviews have been posted yet, but the changes look uncontroversial and align with Git’s ongoing efforts to optimize for large repositories. The removal of `mkpath()` in `refname_match()` might require careful review to ensure no regressions in edge-case refspec matching, but the added test coverage should mitigate this risk.
