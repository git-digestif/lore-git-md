This is a **performance bugfix** targeting the connectivity check in deepening fetches, now entering a **substantive review phase** after Junio C Hamano raised a design concern about the broader implications of the proposed fix.

The patch, originally approved by Patrick Steinhardt, moves `--alternate-refs` into the non-deepening branch of `check_connected()` to avoid forcing a full history walk of large alternates (e.g., 7M objects) during deepening operations. This restores the instant connectivity checks that regressed into multi-minute delays (227s in reported CI cases) after commits 39b44ba771 and cf1e7c0770.

Junio’s reply identifies a **logical gap** in the current approach: the same problem—trusting alternate refs without accounting for their shallow boundaries—exists even in non-deepening fetches. His proposed alternative is a two-step fix: first, apply the one-line move as written (to stop the immediate regression), then extend `odb_for_each_alternate_ref()` to ignore refs from shallow repositories entirely. This would address the root cause rather than just the deepening case.

The review is now **substantive**: Junio’s concern is not about the patch’s correctness for its stated scope, but about whether it goes far enough. The discussion is likely to focus on whether the project should accept a targeted regression fix now and defer the broader issue, or insist on a more comprehensive solution before merging. No new tests or code changes are proposed yet; the next step is conceptual agreement on the fix’s scope.
