This is the third patch in Phillip Wood’s seven-patch series `pw/stash-optimize-check_changes-calls/v2`, a performance-focused refactoring of `git stash push` and `git stash create`. The patch eliminates a redundant `check_changes()` call in `do_push_stash()`, consolidating change detection into `do_create_stash()`. This removes an unnecessary walk of untracked files (saving one full traversal) and avoids two redundant `lstat()` calls per changed path by skipping an extra index refresh.

The patch also refines the return-value convention introduced earlier in the series: `do_create_stash()` now returns `CREATE_NO_CHANGES` (a new enum value) when no changes exist to stash, distinguishing this case from `CREATE_EMPTY` (e.g., `--patch` with no hunks selected). This ensures `git stash push` exits 0 when there are no changes, aligning with its documented behavior, while still exiting 1 for truly empty stashes. The behavioral change is minimal—reflog creation is attempted slightly earlier—but Phillip notes this should not matter in practice.

The diff is clean and mechanical, touching only `builtin/stash.c` and removing 14 lines while adding 9. No new edge cases or controversies are introduced; the patch is a straightforward optimization that builds on the series’ earlier work.
