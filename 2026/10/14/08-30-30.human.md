This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s v3 CI housekeeping series, a targeted platform-compatibility update. It replaces the now-unsupported `i386/ubuntu:20.04` Docker image with `i386/debian:latest` for the `linux32` CI job, ensuring 32-bit platform coverage until at least 2030. The change is purely mechanical: three one-line substitutions in `.github/workflows/main.yml`, `.gitlab-ci.yml`, and `ci/lib.sh`, plus a six-line cleanup in `ci/install-dependencies.sh` that removes Ubuntu-specific logic for SVN and language packs (no longer needed under Debian). The patch is well-motivated—Ubuntu 20.04 was the last 32-bit release and reached end-of-life in April 2025—and the Debian image is a natural successor, already used elsewhere in Git’s CI. No behavioral changes or new functionality are introduced; the `linux32` job continues to exercise Git on a 32-bit platform as before. This looks like a straightforward, uncontroversial maintenance update that should attract little discussion beyond possible confirmation that the Debian image behaves identically in practice.
