This is the second patch in Phillip Wood’s seven-patch series `pw/stash-optimize-check_changes-calls/v2`, a performance-focused refactoring of `git stash`. The patch removes a redundant call to `check_changes_tracked_files()` in `create_stash()`, eliminating a duplicate check for unstaged or uncommitted changes that was introduced in ef0f0b4509 (2019). The earlier check—performed before refreshing the index—was unreliable and unnecessary, as `do_create_stash()` already verifies changes after the index is refreshed. The change reduces overhead by avoiding a second pass over tracked files and simplifies the code by making `check_changes_tracked_files()` no longer responsible for reading the index (since its remaining callers already handle that).

The patch is a straightforward cleanup with no user-visible behavior changes. It aligns with the series’ broader goal of consolidating redundant index operations and `lstat()` calls in `git stash push` and `git stash create`. The change is well-motivated and mechanically sound, touching only `builtin/stash.c` and removing five lines of code. No new edge cases or controversies are introduced.
