This is the sixth patch in Phillip Wood’s seven-patch `pw/stash-optimize-check_changes-calls/v2` series, a performance-focused refactoring of `git stash push` and `git stash create`. The patch removes an unnecessary index load (`repo_read_index_preload()`) and refresh (`refresh_index()`) in `create_index_from_tree()`, a helper used by `git stash create` to build a temporary index file. These operations were redundant because `do_create_stash()`—the caller—already loads and refreshes the index before invoking `create_index_from_tree()`. The change eliminates three `lstat()` calls per changed path, a small but measurable improvement that aligns with the series’ broader goal of reducing redundant filesystem operations.

The patch is confined to `builtin/stash.c` and deletes three lines without introducing new logic or altering user-visible behavior. It builds on the fifth patch in the series, which refactored `stash_working_tree()` to use `create_index_from_tree()` instead of `reset_tree()`, making the redundant index operations here newly visible. The change is mechanical and well-motivated: the commit message clearly explains the redundancy and quantifies the performance gain, and the diff is minimal and targeted.

No edge cases or controversies are introduced. The patch is part of a larger effort to streamline stash internals, and its correctness depends on the earlier patches in the series (particularly the fifth) having already established the correct index state before `create_index_from_tree()` is called. The series as a whole is under active review, with Junio C Hamano’s feedback from v1 fully incorporated, and this patch continues the pattern of eliminating redundant work without altering behavior. If merged, it would land in `next` alongside the rest of the series.
