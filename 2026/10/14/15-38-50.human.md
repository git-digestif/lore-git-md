This is a minor follow-up in the ongoing ODB refactoring series targeting alternates handling. Justin Tobler, who has been providing **substantive, tested reviews** throughout the series, raises a narrow but sensible point about error handling in `do_oid_object_info_extended()`: when the repository is corrupt (i.e., the object exists but cannot be read), the function currently continues to attempt fetching the object from a promisor remote, which may not make sense. He suggests failing immediately with `ODB_READ_ERROR` instead.

Patrick Steinhardt agrees in principle but defers the change to a separate follow-up commit, keeping the current patch (4/6) purely a refactoring with no semantic changes. Justin accepts this approach, and the exchange ends there. The discussion is technical and uncontroversial, with no broader design implications or objections raised. The series’ overall goal—simplifying the ODB source API and optimizing alternates performance—remains on track.
