This is the **v2 performance-focused feature series** that speeds up client-side refspec matching in `git push`, particularly for large-scale branch deletions. The series now consists of **12 patches** (down from 15 in v1) after squashing failing-test commits into their corresponding fixes, addressing Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s feedback. The core goal remains unchanged: eliminating O(n²) linear traversals in favor of `strmap` lookups to achieve **10–100x speedups** in scenarios like pushing 100 delete refspecs to a server with 100k refs (timings drop from **2.5–8.9s to 0.15–0.19s**).

Key technical changes in v2:
- **Behavioral fix in `refname_match()`** (patch 3): Removes `mkpath()` usage, which previously stripped leading `./` from refnames (e.g., `./refs/heads/foo` silently matched `refs/heads/foo`). This change is now guarded by new tests in `t5510` and `t5516` that assert such refspecs are rejected.
- **Memory leak plugged** (patch 5): Fixes a leak in `send-pack` exposed by the duplicate-destination rejection logic.
- **Duplicate destination handling** (patch 8): Now correctly rejects client-side attempts to push multiple refspecs to the same destination on an empty remote, aligning behavior with non-empty remotes. Tests in `t5408` and `t5516` verify this.
- **Performance breakdown**: The benchmark table shows incremental gains:
  - Patch 3 (`refname_match()`) delivers **55–82%** of the total speedup.
  - Patch 4 (`check_push_refs()`) and patch 9 (`match_explicit_refs()`) contribute further gains.
  - Patch 12 (`apply_push_cas()`) optimizes `--force-with-lease` overhead, reducing 100-lease timings from **4.79s to 0.16s**.

Files touched include `refs.c`, `remote.c`, and test scripts (`t5516`, `t5408`, `t5410`, `t5510`, plus a new perf script). The series maintains its disciplined test-before-code structure, with behavioral changes introduced via failing tests that are later resolved. No on-disk format changes are made.
