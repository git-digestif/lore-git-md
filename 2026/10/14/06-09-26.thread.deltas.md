## 2026/10/15

- [2026/10/15/09-27-42 by Phillip Wood <phillip.wood123@gmail.com>] Phillip Wood reviewed the `signed_mult_overflows`/`mult_overflows` macros patch, noting that the type-generic macro is GCC-only and therefore cannot be relied on for safety checks, asking whether any supported platforms lack two's-complement arithmetic (C23 makes it mandatory), correcting that the macro evaluates both operands at least twice, and suggesting a `BUILD_ASSERT_OR_ZERO()` check to enforce `sizeof(a) == sizeof(b)` since the macro requires operands of the same width.
- [2026/10/15/09-28-15 by Phillip Wood <phillip.wood123@gmail.com>] Phillip Wood reviewed the `approxidate` overflow fix, observing that signed integer overflow is undefined behavior rather than necessarily a wrapped value, asking whether the user-supplied number can ever be negative given that "length" is always positive, noting that the proposed fix only prevents overflow when compiling with GCC or Clang because `mult_overflows()` returns 0 on other compilers (leaving undefined behavior on platforms with signed `time_t`), proposing instead to perform the multiplication in `intmax_t` and check that the result converts losslessly to `time_t` to avoid overflow on all platforms regardless of compiler, and questioning why the unsigned-`time_t` range check cannot also apply when `time_t` is signed while suggesting a truncation check that "value" fits in "val".
