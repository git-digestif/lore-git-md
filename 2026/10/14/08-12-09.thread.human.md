This thread is a small, uncontroversial refactoring patch in Git’s ongoing effort to remove the `the_repository` global variable. Okhuomon Ajayi, an Outreachy intern, posted an initial version of the patch to delete the unused `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` macro from `diffcore-break.c`. The macro became redundant after an earlier commit removed the last direct use of `the_repository` in that file.

Usman Akinyemi reviewed the patch and requested a minor tweak to the commit message, dismissing the need for an explanation about preventing future accidental reintroductions of `the_repository`. Okhuomon acknowledged the feedback and plans to post a v2 with the adjusted wording. The change itself is purely mechanical—deleting the macro and its guard—with no runtime impact or test requirements. The patch is self-contained, touching only `diffcore-break.c`, and is expected to be the final version unless new feedback emerges. This work is part of René Scharfe’s broader `the_repository` removal effort.
