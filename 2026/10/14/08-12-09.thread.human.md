This thread is a small but typical part of the long-running effort to remove Git’s `the_repository` global variable. Okhuomon Ajayi, an Outreachy intern, sent a one-file patch that deletes the now-unused `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` macro from `diffcore-break.c`. The macro became redundant after commit 84325f0730 removed the last direct use of `the_repository` in that file, so the change is purely mechanical and has no functional impact. These kinds of cleanups are routine in the `the_repository` removal project; they’re safe, uncontroversial, and help prevent future regressions by making accidental reintroductions of the global fail to compile. No review feedback has appeared yet, but the patch is straightforward and should merge without fuss.
