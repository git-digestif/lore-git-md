This explains why Shashank and others followed the pattern, shifting the issue from contributor behavior to outdated documentation. Kaartic has already submitted a pull request to fix the guidelines. The patch itself remains approved and ready for queueing, but the thread now serves as a reminder that even small, well-intentioned documentation can create friction in the review process.
