This is a small i18n bugfix for `git describe`, already acknowledged as correct by Jeff King. The patch replaces hardcoded English plural forms in two verbose messages with Git’s plural-aware `Q_()` macro, fixing grammatical errors (e.g., “found 1 tags”) and enabling proper translation into languages with different plural rules. The change is confined to `builtin/describe.c` and touches only the two messages in question.

Jeff’s review is brief but positive; he calls the patch “obviously correct” and suitable for a microproject. He does muse about whether the `--debug` messages should be translated at all, but treats that as a separate, larger question and does not block the current fix. No further discussion or objections have surfaced.

This is a clean, uncontroversial improvement that aligns with Git’s ongoing i18n efforts. It should integrate smoothly and is ready for merging.
