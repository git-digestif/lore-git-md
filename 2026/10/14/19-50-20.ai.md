This is the final author update to the first patch in the `gitmergeconflicts(7)` documentation series. Julia Evans incorporates Patrick Steinhardt’s surface-level editorial suggestions from his review of v3, making the following mechanical adjustments to `Documentation/gitmergeconflicts.adoc`:

- **Punctuation consistency**: Adds trailing periods to bullet points in the "3 ways to handle a merge conflict" and "WHAT IS A MERGE CONFLICT?" sections to match the style of the second bullet.
- **Spelling out `--patch`**: Replaces `-p` with `--patch` in the `git log --merge --patch <filename>` command to improve grep-ability in the man page.
- **Removal of the `zdiff3` note**: Drops the brief deprecation note about `zdiff3` entirely, deferring a more thorough explanation to potential future work. This aligns with Julia’s stated uncertainty about how to present `zdiff3` effectively without a compelling example.

The changes are purely stylistic and do not alter the guide’s technical content, structure, or pedagogical intent.
