This is the first patch in a two-patch bugfix series targeting a performance regression in Git's connectivity check for deepening fetches. The patch directly addresses the immediate issue: when performing a shallow fetch (e.g., `git fetch --depth 1`) from a repository with a large alternate (such as a 7M-object bare clone), the connectivity check unnecessarily walks the entire history of the alternate, causing multi-minute delays (227s in the reported case) instead of completing instantly.

The fix is minimal and surgical: it moves the `--not` argument in `check_connected()` so that `--alternate-refs` is negated for *both* regular and deepening fetches, ensuring the walk stops at the alternate's refs rather than traversing its full history. The change touches only `connected.c` (reordering two lines) and adds two tests in `t/t5618-alternate-refs.sh` that verify the `rev-list` arguments via `GIT_TRACE2_EVENT` for both fetch types. The tests confirm the alternate refs are excluded during deepening without asserting behavioral differences, as the outcome remains correct for complete alternates.

This patch retains the approvals from Patrick Steinhardt and Justin Tobler carried over from the v1 series, as it preserves the core regression fix while incorporating Junio C Hamano's design feedback. The commit message is thorough, explaining the historical context (regression introduced by commits 39b44ba771 and cf1e7c0770), the performance impact, and the reasoning behind the fix. The solution is well-motivated and addresses a real-world pain point in CI workflows where fresh repositories use large alternates.

The patch is part of a broader effort to treat alternate refs as a boundary in *all* connectivity checks, not just deepening fetches, which is implemented in the second patch of the series. The undetected edge case of partial clones (promisor remotes) is explicitly deferred to future work, as noted in the cover letter. No merge intent has been stated yet, but the series aligns with Junio's preference for a comprehensive solution.
