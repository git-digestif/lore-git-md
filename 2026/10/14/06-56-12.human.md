This is the fifth and final patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s five-part ODB refactoring series, completing the internal API cleanup begun in earlier patches. The patch removes the `local` flag from the generic `struct odb_source` and moves it into the packed source (`struct odb_source_packed`), where it is still needed. The change is mechanical and confined to the ODB plumbing, with no user-visible behavior changes.

The patch itself is straightforward and well-motivated: the `local` flag no longer makes sense in the generic ODB source now that alternates are handled in the "files" backend, and the packed source is the only remaining site that requires it. The commit message clearly explains the rationale and notes that the `ODB_FOR_EACH_OBJECT_LOCAL_ONLY` flag is now handled entirely within the "files" backend, making the change safe.

Justin Tobler’s **substantive review** raises a design-level concern about the `ODB_FOR_EACH_OBJECT_LOCAL_ONLY` flag, which he calls "leaky" and "awkward" now that the `local` flag is no longer part of the generic ODB source. He questions whether all call sites using the flag have legitimate "files" backend-specific reasons, and whether the in-memory source should ever be iterated when only "local" objects are requested. Patrick’s reply identifies only two callers of the flag—`git pack-objects --local` and `add_unseen_recent_objects_to_traversal()` (used by `git prune`)—and suggests the flag could be removed entirely in a future series. The discussion is collaborative and surfaces a potential follow-up effort, but Justin explicitly states the patch "looks reasonable" and "seems to be moving us in the right direction," with no requests for changes.

The patch is part of Patrick’s broader ODB abstraction effort and follows his earlier `ps/odb-files-alternates` branch, which migrated alternates handling into the "files" backend. The series as a whole is under review, with no major objections raised so far. If the earlier patches in the series are any indication, this one is likely to be uncontroversial and ready for merging once the discussion about the `ODB_FOR_EACH_OBJECT_LOCAL_ONLY` flag is resolved or deferred. The patch is a clean refactoring that simplifies the ODB source API, and the open question about the flag does not appear to be a blocker.
