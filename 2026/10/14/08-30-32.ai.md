This is the sixth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s v3 CI housekeeping series, a **CI/build system** update targeting the `linux-feature-testbed` job. The patch replaces the now-EOL Ubuntu 20.04 image with `ubuntu:latest`, a rolling tag that always points to the most recent Ubuntu LTS release. This change eliminates the need for manual updates while maintaining coverage through other CI jobs (AlmaLinux 8, Debian 12) that test older distributions. The switch also drops the explicit `gcc-8` dependency, as Ubuntu 26.04 no longer supports it, relying instead on the system-provided GCC version.

The patch is purely mechanical, touching only `.github/workflows/main.yml` and `.gitlab-ci.yml` to update the `image` and remove the `cc_package`/`CC_PACKAGE` lines. The change is well-motivated—Ubuntu 20.04’s end-of-life status has already caused issues—and the trade-off (losing GCC 8 coverage) is explicitly justified by the presence of other jobs testing older toolchains. The `latest` tag’s LTS focus ensures stability, unlike the `rolling` tag, which tracks non-LTS releases.

This patch is uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ goal of reducing CI maintenance overhead. Future discussion may reference the `ubuntu:latest` image or the dropped `gcc-8` dependency, but no behavioral changes or new functionality are introduced. The patch is ready for review and unlikely to draw substantive feedback.
