This is the second patch in Phillip Wood’s seven-patch series `pw/stash-optimize-check_changes-calls/v2`, which refactors redundant change-detection logic in `git stash`. This patch specifically targets `git stash create`, removing a duplicate call to `check_changes_tracked_files()` that was introduced in ef0f0b4509 (2019). The duplicate check occurs once in `create_stash()` (before refreshing the index) and again in `do_create_stash()` (after refreshing the index), with no clear optimization benefit. By removing the earlier call, the patch eliminates redundant work while preserving correctness—`do_create_stash()` already handles the definitive change check. The patch also cleans up `check_changes_tracked_files()` by removing its now-unnecessary `repo_read_index()` call, since all remaining callers refresh the index beforehand.

The change is mechanical and well-justified: the commit message explains the history of the duplicate check, its lack of purpose, and why the later call in `do_create_stash()` is the appropriate place to retain the logic. The diff is minimal (five lines removed from `builtin/stash.c`), touching only the `create_stash()` and `check_changes_tracked_files()` functions. No new edge cases or behavioral changes are introduced, and the patch aligns with the series’ broader goal of reducing redundant index operations. Phillip’s reasoning is clear, and the patch addresses a long-standing inefficiency without altering user-visible behavior.
