This is the fourth patch in a five-part series hardening Git against integer overflows flagged by Coverity. It introduces two new macros in `git-compat-util.h`: `signed_mult_overflows` for signed arithmetic and `mult_overflows` as a type-generic wrapper that handles both signed and unsigned operands.

The patch adds about 50 lines to `git-compat-util.h`, including helper macros (`is_unsigned_type`, `minimum_signed_value_of_type`) and the overflow checks themselves. The logic accounts for C’s usual arithmetic conversions and correctly handles the two’s-complement edge case where the signed minimum is representable. On compilers without GNU `typeof`, `mult_overflows` is a no-op stub.

These macros are immediately used in the next patch (5/5) to harden `approxidate` and will likely be reused elsewhere in future overflow-hardening efforts. The implementation is careful and portable, but the absence of test coverage for the new macros may draw review attention. The patch itself is uncontroversial and appears well-crafted.
