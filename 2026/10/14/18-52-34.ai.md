This is a documentation patch under review, specifically the second version of Julia Evans' rewrite of Git’s explanation of detached HEAD state. Junio C Hamano’s reply focuses on minor but important corrections to the patch.

The email is a surface-level review from the maintainer, addressing small technical and stylistic issues. Junio points out that the help command in the advice message and documentation should use `git help detached-head` (hyphenated) rather than `git help detachedhead`. He also requests a sign-off from the co-author, Marie Claire LeBlanc Flanagan, to comply with Git’s contribution guidelines. Additionally, he questions the accuracy of the 90-day reflog expiry period mentioned in the new text, suggesting it might be safer to generalize to "a few weeks" or specify 30 days (the default for `gc.reflogExpireUnreachable`) to avoid misleading users about how long unreachable commits persist. Finally, he notes a whitespace alignment issue in `command-list.txt` where the addition of a hyphen requires adjusting spacing to maintain column alignment.

The patch itself remains well-motivated and addresses a real usability gap in Git’s documentation. The requested changes are minor and do not challenge the patch’s core goals or structure. Once these corrections are made, the patch should be ready for integration. The broader help-system usability concern (internal cross-references vs. `git help`) is not addressed here and remains a potential future improvement.
