This is the fourth patch in Phillip Wood’s seven-patch series `pw/stash-optimize-check_changes-calls/v2`, which refactors `git stash` internals to eliminate redundant index operations. This specific patch removes duplicate calls to `preload_index()` in `git stash push`, cutting two unnecessary `lstat()` calls per changed path.

The patch addresses a performance inefficiency in `do_stash_push()` and `do_create_stash()`: the index is preloaded three times (once in `do_stash_push()` and twice in `do_create_stash()`), each time triggering `lstat()` calls on all tracked files. By shifting responsibility for reading the index to the callers of `do_create_stash()` and replacing `repo_read_index_preload()` with `repo_read_index()`, the patch ensures the index is loaded only once. The change is confined to `builtin/stash.c` and modifies `do_create_stash()`, `create_stash()`, and `do_push_stash()`.

This is a mechanical refactoring with clear performance benefits—reduced `lstat()` overhead—and no user-visible behavior changes. The patch builds on earlier work in the series (e.g., the `enum create_result` introduced in patch 1) and aligns with Junio C Hamano’s feedback from v1, which flagged the redundant index refreshes. The diff is minimal (two lines changed) and well-motivated, targeting a specific bottleneck without introducing new edge cases.
