This thread is about a bug in Git’s files ref backend where root refs (like `HEAD` and `ORIG_HEAD`) are inconsistently included in the loose ref cache depending on when the cache is first created. Andy Koppe identified the issue and proposed a fix that avoids storing root refs in the shared cache entirely, instead creating a temporary cache for them only when the `REFS_FOR_EACH_INCLUDE_ROOT_REFS` flag is used. The patch also adds a test to verify `--include-root-refs` works correctly with `--merged`.

The discussion has centered on three architectural approaches: Andy’s original fix, Junio Hamano’s suggestion to maintain two persistent caches (one for refs under `refs/` and one for root refs), and Andy’s more radical idea to drop the `REFS_FOR_EACH_INCLUDE_ROOT_REFS` flag entirely. Patrick Steinhardt initially raised concerns about the original patch’s efficiency but has since accepted Andy’s explanation that the overhead is negligible, given the flag’s limited use. The thread now turns on whether to proceed with Andy’s minimalist fix or wait for Junio’s alternative, which hasn’t been implemented yet. No decisions have been made, and the patch remains under review.
