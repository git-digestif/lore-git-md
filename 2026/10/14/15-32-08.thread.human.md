This thread is about a bug in Git’s files ref backend where root refs (like `HEAD` and `ORIG_HEAD`) are inconsistently handled in the loose ref cache. The issue arises because root refs are only included in the cache if the `REFS_FOR_EACH_INCLUDE_ROOT_REFS` flag is set during the *first* iteration that creates the cache; later iterations include them regardless of the flag, while earlier ones omit them even if the flag is set. Andy Koppe proposed a fix that avoids storing root refs in the shared cache entirely, instead creating a temporary cache for them when the flag is passed.

The patch is under review, with no prior versions, and targets `files-backend.c` with a new test in `t/t6302-for-each-ref-filter.sh`. The discussion has centered on two approaches: Andy’s original solution (temporary cache for root refs) and Junio C Hamano’s alternative (persistent dual caches for `refs/` hierarchy and root refs). A third option—dropping the `REFS_FOR_EACH_INCLUDE_ROOT_REFS` flag—was raised but not pursued. Patrick Steinhardt initially questioned the original patch’s design and performance but accepted Andy’s explanation, resolving his concerns. Junio’s latest email suggests the patch is technically unblocked but leaves the architectural choice open, implying the discussion may continue before a final decision. No new technical details or participants have emerged.
