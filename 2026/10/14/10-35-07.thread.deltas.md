## 2026/10/15

- [2026/10/15/06-41-58 by Jeff King <peff@peff.net>] Jeff King reviewed Igor Wiedler's v1 patch and said it is exactly the solution he would have expected: commit 39b44ba771 should have treated alternate ref tips like our own existing ref tips, so "--alternate-refs" should be used wherever "--all" is used, and since "--all" was moved into the non-deepening conditional, "--alternate-refs" should go there too. He explained that 39b44ba771's mistake arose because the original 2014 patch (used internally at GitHub) was rebased forward in 2019 without a textual conflict, creating a subtle semantic conflict with the earlier move of "--not"/"--all". He also parsed Junio's question as whether it is a problem for a non-shallow repository to use a shallow alternate for verification, said that sounds likely, framed the issue as whether the refs used for verification come from a shallow repo that might lack objects, and noted he was not sure how that interacts with opt->is_deepening_fetch. He agreed that listing alternate refs behind "--not" is the right thing and that the current code is broken, but was not convinced that disabling "--alternate-refs" only when deepening solves all the problems; he found Junio's proposed two-step fix acceptable, saying moving "--not" out so it is always done should not hurt when deepening without alternates, and that making odb_for_each_alternate_ref() ignore refs from shallow repositories seems reasonable since alternates do not necessarily have a full repo.
- [2026/10/15/06-57-54 by Jeff King <peff@peff.net>] Jeff King, on the v2 odb patch, noted that replace refs exist in addition to grafts and that full connectivity of the original objects is generally insisted upon (fsck ignores replace refs), which he thought was also true of grafts except those from the shallow mechanism. He acknowledged partial-clone corner cases and agreed that punting by ignoring such alternates' refs is fine because alternate-ref use is an optimization and falling back to simpler behavior is good. He said checking the magic "shallow" and "info/grafts" paths is a little gross but there is no real alternative without a repository object for the alternate, and that the same problem applies to advertising alternates for transfer, which Igor's lowest-level fix also covers. He judged the direct "--alternate-refs" test reasonable and the simplest approach, noting a full fetch/push corruption case could be constructed but is not needed for the tests.
