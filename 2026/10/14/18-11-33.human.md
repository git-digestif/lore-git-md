This is the first patch in Igor Wiedler’s two-patch v2 series fixing a performance regression in shallow fetches. It directly addresses the immediate problem: deepening fetches now correctly negate `--alternate-refs` alongside `--all`, preventing Git from walking the entire history of large alternates (e.g., 227-second delays reduced to 0.0s). The change is minimal—moving two lines in `connected.c`—and retains the existing behavior for non-deepening fetches. Tests verify the `rev-list` arguments via `GIT_TRACE2_EVENT` for both regular and deepening cases.

The patch carries forward approvals from Patrick Steinhardt and Justin Tobler (both substantive reviews of v1) and aligns with Junio’s design feedback from the earlier thread. It’s a well-motivated, targeted fix for a real-world CI pain point, with clear commit message context and test coverage. The only loose end is the deferred handling of partial clones (promisor remotes), which is explicitly out of scope here. Worth attention if you care about fetch performance or alternate setups.
