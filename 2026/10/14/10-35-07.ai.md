This is a performance-focused bugfix targeting the connectivity check in deepening fetches. The patch addresses a regression introduced by earlier commits (39b44ba771 and cf1e7c0770) where `--alternate-refs` was unconditionally added to the `rev-list` command, causing Git to walk the entire history of large alternates even when unnecessary.

The problem manifests in CI environments where a fresh repository uses a large alternate (e.g., 7M objects) and performs a shallow fetch. The connectivity check, which should complete instantly, instead takes minutes (227s in the reported case) because it traverses the alternate's full history. The fix moves `--alternate-refs` into the non-deepening branch of the `check_connected()` logic, ensuring deepening fetches only walk the fetched tips down to the new shallow boundary.

The patch touches `connected.c` (moving one line) and adds two tests in `t/t5618-alternate-refs.sh` that verify the `rev-list` arguments via `GIT_TRACE2_EVENT` for both regular and deepening fetches. The tests confirm the alternate refs are excluded during deepening without asserting behavioral differences, as the outcome remains correct for complete alternates.

The commit message is thorough, explaining the historical context, the performance impact, and the reasoning behind the fix. The solution is minimal and targeted, avoiding unnecessary changes to the alternate refs logic for non-deepening cases. This looks like a well-motivated fix for a real-world pain point in CI workflows.
