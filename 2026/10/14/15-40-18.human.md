This is the sixth patch in Phillip Wood’s seven-patch series `pw/stash-optimize-check_changes-calls/v2`, a performance-focused refactoring of `git stash push` and `git stash create`. The series aims to eliminate redundant index loads, refreshes, and change-detection logic, reducing `lstat()` calls and untracked-file walks.

This patch removes an unnecessary index load and refresh in `git stash create`. The function `create_index_from_tree()` is already called after the index is loaded and refreshed in `do_create_stash()`, so the redundant operations here are superfluous. The change saves three `lstat()` calls per changed path, a small but measurable improvement. The patch is mechanical and well-justified: it deletes three lines in `builtin/stash.c` without altering behavior or introducing edge cases.

The series as a whole is under active review and has already incorporated feedback from Junio C Hamano on earlier versions. Phillip, a seasoned contributor with deep expertise in the stash and sequencer subsystems, has expanded the scope of the series to address additional inefficiencies, and this patch is part of that effort. The changes are confined to `builtin/stash.c` and focus on internal performance optimizations rather than user-visible features.

If merged, the series would likely land in `next` as part of the ongoing effort to streamline stash internals. The patch itself is uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ goal of reducing redundant work.
