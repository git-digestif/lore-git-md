This is the v3 reroll of Patrick Steinhardt’s CI/build system housekeeping series, now consisting of eight patches that modernize Git’s GitHub Actions and GitLab CI configurations. The series removes outdated dependencies (EOL Docker images, Python 2), eliminates redundant jobs, and updates supporting scripts (`ci/install-dependencies.sh`, `ci/lib.sh`, `ci/run-build-and-tests.sh`) to reduce complexity and improve reliability. All changes are mechanical and do not alter user-visible behavior.

The most notable update since v2 is the resolution of the naming dispute over the `linux-TEST-vars` job, which is now renamed to `linux-feature-testbed` (Junio C Hamano’s preferred choice among 14 alternatives). This purely mechanical change (three one-line substitutions) clarifies the job’s purpose—testing non-default Git options like `OPENSSL_SHA1_UNSAFE` and `GIT_TEST_SPLIT_INDEX`—without altering its logic. The series also addresses Junio’s earlier concern about reftable test coverage by preserving the `linux-reftable` job (foreign-SCM interoperability) while repurposing the `linux-reftable-leaks` job as `linux-reftable-sha256` to test reftable with SHA-256. Junio has explicitly endorsed this trade-off, removing the blocker for the reroll.

Other key changes include fixing a typo in `ci/run-build-and-tests.sh` (restoring the `WITH_BREAKING_CHANGES` flag for the Fedora Meson job), removing dead code left behind by the deleted `linux-clang` job, switching the `linux32` job to `i386/debian:latest` (supported until at least 2030), and dropping Python 2 support (now obsolete). The `linux-feature-testbed` job now uses `ubuntu:latest` and the system-provided GCC version instead of `gcc-8`, a trade-off that reduces manual updates while preserving coverage through other CI jobs. The series also skips a SHA-1-specific test in SHA-256 repositories (`t/t5004-archive-corner-cases.sh`), a minor but necessary adjustment.

The patches touch `.github/workflows/main.yml`, `.gitlab-ci.yml`, and the `ci/` scripts, with a net reduction of 19 lines (20 insertions, 39 deletions). The changes are well-motivated, thoroughly tested (passing runs linked for GitLab and GitHub), and unlikely to be controversial. For follow-up discussion, expect references to the renamed job (`linux-feature-testbed`), the reftable/SHA-256 coverage split (`linux-reftable` vs. `linux-reftable-sha256`), and the Docker images (`i386/debian:latest`, `ubuntu:latest`). The Meson build fixes (`ps/meson-improvements`) mentioned in the cover letter are prerequisites for GitLab CI but not formal dependencies.
