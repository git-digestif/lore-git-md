This thread is about a two-patch bugfix series by Igor Wiedler that resolves a performance regression in Git’s connectivity checks for deepening fetches. The issue caused multi-minute delays when fetching from large alternates (e.g., 7M objects) due to `--alternate-refs` forcing full history walks. The v3 series, now code-complete and test-stable, implements a two-step fix: patch 1 disables `--alternate-refs` in all connectivity checks, while patch 2 teaches Git to ignore refs from shallow or grafted alternates. The series has broad support from key reviewers, including Patrick Steinhardt and Justin Tobler for the core regression fix, and Peff’s thorough review confirming the technical soundness of both patches. Junio’s earlier design feedback has been fully addressed, and the only procedural blocker—a Windows CI test failure—was resolved in v3.

The patches touch `connected.c`, `odb.c`, and test files, with no on-disk format changes or new symbols introduced. The behavior change is backward-compatible, and the series includes expanded test coverage to verify the fix. The remaining loose ends are minor: the edge case of partial clones (promisor remotes) is explicitly deferred, and Peff noted some conceptual distinctions (like replace refs) that don’t require immediate action.
