This thread is about fixing a performance regression in Git’s connectivity checks for deepening fetches, where `--alternate-refs` was forcing full history walks of large alternate repositories (e.g., 7M objects), causing multi-minute delays. Igor Wiedler’s two-patch series (v2) addresses both the immediate regression and the underlying design flaw by treating alternate refs as a boundary in *all* connectivity checks and teaching `odb_for_each_alternate_ref()` to ignore refs from shallow or grafted alternates. The series retains approvals from Patrick Steinhardt and Justin Tobler for the core regression fix (patch 1) but expands scope in patch 2 to incorporate Junio’s design feedback.

The series is currently paused procedurally after Junio ejected patch 2 from the `seen` branch due to a Windows CI test failure. The failure is mechanical—caused by a path format incompatibility (`file://$TRASH_DIRECTORY`)—and Jeff King (Peff) has proposed two backward-compatible fixes (replacing `$TRASH_DIRECTORY` with `$(pwd)` or rewriting the test more idiomatically). No technical objections to the patch itself have been raised; the ejection is purely to address the test’s portability. Peff’s review praised the low-level fix in `odb.c` and clarified design boundaries (e.g., replace refs, partial clones), though he stopped short of an explicit "Reviewed-by." Junio’s acknowledgment of Peff’s analysis signals agreement with its conclusions.

The patch is backward-compatible, touches `connected.c`, `odb.c`, and `Documentation/rev-list-options.adoc`, and includes expanded tests in `t/t5618-alternate-refs.sh`. The only deferred edge case is partial clones (promisor remotes), which the commit message explicitly punts as future work. The series is otherwise ready to proceed once the test fix is applied. No merge intent has been stated, but the ejection does not reflect dissatisfaction with the patch’s substance—only the test’s portability.
