This thread is about a bugfix for an off-by-one error in Git's pack-check subsystem that caused `git fsck` to incorrectly report corruption for blobs sized exactly at `core.bigFileThreshold`. The issue arose because the streaming path (for large blobs) refused to handle blobs at or below the threshold, while the pack-check code mistakenly routed blobs *equal* to the threshold to that path.

Patrick Steinhardt authored the fix, which aligns the threshold comparison with the rest of the codebase by changing `<=` to `<`. The patch includes a new test in `t/t1450-fsck.sh` to verify the fix. Junio C Hamano reviewed and approved the patch, explicitly stating "Will queue" in his reply, indicating it is now ready for integration into his `seen` branch. No objections or further discussion were raised, and the fix is straightforward with no open questions remaining. The bug was introduced in 2016 and became problematic after a 2026 change to stream creation logic.
