This thread is about a patch series from Johannes Schindelin that fixes integer overflows in Git’s codebase, hardening several subsystems against crashes or corruption when arithmetic exceeds type limits. The series is now at v2, with patches 1 (archive-tar.c) and 2 (http.c) uncontroversial and ready for integration. Patch 3 (wrapper.c) was revised in v2 to address Phillip Wood’s concerns by changing the API contract of `write_in_full()` and introducing `read_in_full_or_error()`, eliminating the overflow risk by design.

The remaining sticking points were patches 4 (git-compat-util.h) and 5 (date.c), which introduced overflow-checking macros and hardened `approxidate_alpha()`. These have now been withdrawn in favor of a simpler, more portable approach for date.c: capping user-supplied numbers at `INT_MAX` and performing the multiplication only after this guard. This avoids compiler-specific overflow detection and handles both signed and unsigned `time_t` uniformly. However, the hardening for date.c is not yet implemented, and the series remains stalled in Junio’s integration branches, marked as “Waiting for response” to Phillip Wood’s review. If no further activity occurs, the series may expire without being merged.

Key participants include Johannes Schindelin (author), Phillip Wood (substantive reviewer who raised platform-compatibility concerns and proposed alternatives), Junio C Hamano (maintainer who confirmed the correctness of some changes and rejected others), and Patrick Steinhardt (reviewer who confirmed the type change in http.c). The thread’s next step depends on whether Johannes posts a v3 with the simplified date.c hardening or abandons it entirely.
