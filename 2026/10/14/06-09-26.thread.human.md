Johannes Schindelin has posted v2 of his five-patch series addressing Coverity-flagged integer overflows in `archive-tar.c`, `http.c`, `wrapper.c`/`wrapper.h`, and `date.c`, with patches 3 through 5 substantially revised to address earlier review feedback. Junio C Hamano has now signaled approval of the series' overall direction, praising v2 as a clear improvement over v1 for removing the ad-hoc overflow macros and cleanly separating `read_in_full_or_error()` from `read_in_full()`. He has previously approved patch 1 (`archive-tar.c`) and patch 2 (`http.c`) pending commit-message corrections, and Phillip Wood approved patch 5 (`date.c`) as "much simpler than the previous version," leaving no outstanding technical objections on that patch.

The series remains in a "waiting for response" state in Junio's "What's cooking" report. Outstanding items are limited to wording: Junio requests commit-message clarifications on patches 1, 2, and 4 (covering the `pathlen` off-by-one description, the `posn` reset behavior, and the `BUG()` contract documentation for `read_in_full()`), plus two trivial cover-letter corrections ("Most callers" overstates the migration scope; "could been simplified" should read "could have been simplified"), which he dismisses as insignificant since cover letters do not enter the permanent commit record.
