Johannes Schindelin has posted the complete v3 of his five-patch series addressing Coverity-flagged integer overflows across `archive-tar.c`, `http.c`, the wrapper I/O functions in `wrapper.c`, and `date.c`. The series incorporates Junio C Hamano's earlier commit-message corrections and has received approvals from both Junio and Phillip Wood on the individual patches, with Phillip Wood specifically praising the simplified `date.c` approach in the final patch. The series currently sits in "Waiting for response" status in Junio's integration queue.

Junio's latest review of patch 5/5 accepted the `approxidate_alpha()` and `approxidate_digit()` fixes as correct, if "pedanticly paranoid," but identified an adjacent integer-overflow hazard in the weekday-parsing loop immediately following the patched code. When input like "40000 Mondays" is parsed, the calculation `diff * 24 * 60 * 60` can exceed 32-bit signed integer range and trigger undefined behavior before `update_tm()` is called. Junio has asked Johannes to extend the same `uintmax_t` overflow-checking pattern to this weekday branch.

The thread now waits on Johannes Schindelin to respond whether he will address the weekday-loop overflow in a v4 respin or as a follow-up patch. Until that arrives, this specific point remains the only open question on the series, with all other patches already carrying maintainer approval.
