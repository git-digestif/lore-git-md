This thread is a five-patch series from Johannes Schindelin, posted in early October, that fixes integer overflows in Git’s codebase flagged by Coverity. The patches target latent issues in archive-tar, http, wrapper, and date subsystems, hardening them against undefined behavior or crashes when arithmetic exceeds type limits. Junio and Patrick Steinhardt have confirmed the fixes are defensive and limited to local user input, so they won’t break history traversal or cross-platform compatibility.

The series is under review, with patch 2 (http.c) uncontroversial and ready to merge. However, patches 3 (wrapper.c), 4 (git-compat-util.h), and 5 (date.c) still have open technical feedback from Phillip Wood, who raised platform-specific concerns about `SSIZE_MAX` vs. `size_t` width mismatches on NonStop and questioned the portability of the new overflow-checking macros. Junio’s latest update flags the series as “Waiting for response” and warns it will expire from his integration branches if Johannes doesn’t address the feedback soon. No new code or arguments have emerged, and the thread is now stalled pending a v2.
