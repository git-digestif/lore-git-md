This thread is a five-patch series from Johannes Schindelin, posted via GitGitGadget, that fixes integer overflows in Git’s codebase flagged by Coverity. The series targets latent issues in several subsystems—archive-tar, http, wrapper, and now date—by introducing defensive checks to prevent undefined behavior, crashes, or silent corruption when arithmetic exceeds type limits. Four similar Coverity-fix branches from the same author were merged to `master` between July and October 2026, so this work follows an established pattern.

The latest patch, the fifth in the series, addresses overflows in `date.c`’s `approxidate` subsystem, where relative-time calculations like "99999 weeks ago" could previously overflow an `int` multiplication. It uses the new `mult_overflows` macro (introduced earlier in the series) to check multiplications and adds platform-specific guards to reject counts exceeding `INT_MAX` or the maximum value of `time_t` on systems with unsigned `time_t`. The change is narrowly scoped to the overflow-prone multiplication and a digit-parsing helper, with no new CLI options, config keys, or on-disk format changes. Like the rest of the series, it lacks test coverage for the hardened edge cases, which may prompt review requests.

The series is under review, with no feedback recorded yet. The patches are mechanical and defensive, hardening user-facing input paths without altering behavior for normal inputs.
