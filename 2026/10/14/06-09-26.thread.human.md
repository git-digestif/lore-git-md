This thread is a five-patch series from Johannes Schindelin (via GitGitGadget) that introduces integer-overflow fixes across Git’s codebase, inspired by Coverity scan findings. The goal is to harden several subsystems—archive-tar, http, wrapper, and date—against undefined behavior, crashes, or silent corruption when arithmetic exceeds type limits. This is the fourth iteration of such efforts from the same author, with four prior Coverity-fix branches already merged to `master` between July and October 2026.

The series is currently under review in its initial (`v1`) form. The latest email focuses on the fourth patch, which adds two new overflow-checking macros to `git-compat-util.h`: `signed_mult_overflows` (for signed integers) and `mult_overflows` (a type-generic wrapper). These macros are infrastructure-only at this stage, with their actual usage appearing in the subsequent patch (5/5) and earlier patches (1–3/5). The macros are designed to be reusable, potentially benefiting future hardening efforts. While the implementation appears uncontroversial, the lack of test coverage for the edge cases may attract review feedback.

Key technical changes include converting the `posn` variable in `http.c` from `int` to `size_t` to prevent heap under-reads during large HTTP responses, and adding upfront checks in `wrapper.c` to reject byte counts exceeding `SSIZE_MAX`. The series also introduces a fallback definition of `SSIZE_MAX` for platforms lacking it. No on-disk format changes, new CLI options, or config keys are introduced. The series is defensive, targeting latent issues rather than observed bugs, and no test coverage has been added for the hardened edge cases yet.
