Johannes Schindelin's five-patch v2 series hardening Git against Coverity-flagged integer overflows -- spanning `archive-tar.c`, `http.c`, `wrapper.c`/`wrapper.h`, and `date.c` -- is nearing convergence. The remaining work is commit-message wording: Junio asked for corrections on patches 1, 2, and 4, and Schindelin has just confirmed he will adopt the suggested rewording for patch 2 clarifying that `posn` resets to zero on every curl callback invocation.
