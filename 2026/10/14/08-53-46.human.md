This is the second patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s six-patch **refactoring and performance series** targeting the Git object database’s "files" backend. It applies the same optimization introduced in patch 1/6—prioritizing packfile checks over loose-object `stat()` calls—but to the object-streaming code path (`odb_source_files_read_object_stream()`).

The change is small (4 insertions, 2 deletions) and mechanical: it splits the single loop that iterates over both packed and loose sources into two separate loops, checking packfiles first. This aligns the streaming behavior with the direct-read optimization in patch 1/6, ensuring consistency across the ODB’s two primary read paths. While Patrick expects the performance impact to be smaller here (since streaming large objects is dominated by I/O time rather than `stat()` overhead), the patch is justified as a matter of internal coherence rather than measurable speedup.

The patch touches only `odb/source-files.c` and carries no user-visible changes or on-disk format modifications. It is uncontroversial and has already received **substantive, tested review** from Justin Tobler, who endorsed the design direction. This is a clean follow-up to the alternates migration work and part of the broader ODB abstraction effort. The series as a whole looks ready for `next` once any remaining nits are addressed.
