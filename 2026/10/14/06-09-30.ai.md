This is the fourth patch in a five-patch series introducing overflow-checking macros to Git’s utility header. It adds two new macros to `git-compat-util.h`: `signed_mult_overflows` for signed-integer multiplication and `mult_overflows` as a type-generic wrapper that dispatches to either the signed or unsigned variant based on the operands’ types. The implementation handles the full range of signed arithmetic, including the representable negative minimum value on two’s-complement targets, and falls back to a no-op on compilers lacking GNU `typeof`.

The patch is purely infrastructure: it defines the macros but does not yet use them in any code path. Those uses appear in the subsequent patch (5/5) and in the earlier patches of the series (1–3/5). The macros are designed to be reusable across the codebase, so future Coverity-driven or manual hardening efforts can adopt them without reinventing the wheel. No tests are included, consistent with the series’ focus on latent issues rather than observed bugs. The implementation is careful and appears uncontroversial, though the absence of test coverage for the edge cases may draw review attention.
