This is the first patch in Jon Simons' **v2 performance-focused series** to speed up `git push` refspec matching for large-scale deletions. It addresses a **preparatory correctness issue** for the upcoming `refname_match()` refactoring (patch 3).

The patch adds validation to `--force-with-lease=<refname>` arguments using `check_refname_format()`, ensuring invalid refnames (like `./refs/heads/main`) are rejected immediately rather than silently ignored. Today, such refnames are unintentionally matched due to `mkpath()`'s `cleanup_path()` stripping leading `./` in `refname_match()`. After patch 3 removes this behavior, the lease would be ignored entirely—this patch ensures consistent failure in both cases.

The test is well-scoped and follows the series' disciplined approach of guarding behavioral changes with explicit coverage. No performance impact is claimed here; this is purely a correctness fix to prevent future regressions.
