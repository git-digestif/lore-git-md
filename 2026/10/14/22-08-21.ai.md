This is Junio C Hamano’s final clarification in the `gitmergeconflicts(7)` thread, responding to Julia Evans’ concern about including `git am -3` in the list of commands that can cause merge conflicts. The exchange is purely explanatory and does not introduce new feedback or block merging.

Junio explains that `git am -3` *is* a three-way merge operation, just like `git cherry-pick` or `git merge`: it uses the working tree’s current state, the preimage from the patch, and the postimage from the patch to attempt a merge. If the preimage blobs are available and the merge stops in conflict, the behavior is functionally identical to other three-way merge commands. His response reinforces that omitting `git am -3` would leave the guide incomplete and potentially misleading, as it is a legitimate source of merge conflicts.

The clarification is aimed at ensuring the guide’s accuracy rather than requesting further changes.
