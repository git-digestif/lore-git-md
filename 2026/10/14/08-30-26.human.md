This is a CI/build system patch series from Patrick Steinhardt, now in its third iteration (v3). The series modernizes and simplifies Git's CI configurations by removing outdated dependencies, eliminating redundant jobs, and updating supporting scripts. The eight patches touch GitHub Actions and GitLab CI workflows, along with minor adjustments to test scripts, reducing CI complexity while maintaining or improving test coverage.

The v3 reroll addresses all prior feedback from Junio C Hamano and SZEDER Gábor. The most notable resolution is the repurposing of the `linux-reftable-leaks` job to `linux-reftable-sha256`, which now tests reftable with SHA-256 while preserving foreign-SCM interoperability coverage in the existing `linux-reftable` job. This trade-off—prioritizing SHA-256 coverage over leak-checking—was explicitly endorsed by Junio, removing the blocker from v2. Another key change is the rename of the `linux-TEST-vars` job to `linux-feature-testbed`, adopting Junio's preferred name after Gábor objected to the earlier `linux-exotic` proposal as misleading.

The series also includes several smaller but practical improvements: fixing a typo in the Fedora Meson job, removing dead code left behind by the deleted `linux-clang` job, switching the 32-bit job to a supported Debian image, and dropping Python 2 coverage (now obsolete). The `linux-feature-testbed` job now uses `ubuntu:latest`, eliminating the need to manually track Ubuntu LTS versions. The net effect is a cleaner, more maintainable CI setup with 19 insertions and 39 deletions across six files.

The patches are well-motivated and appear uncontroversial at this stage. The changes are mechanical and focus on housekeeping rather than new functionality, making them unlikely to generate significant debate. The only potential point of discussion might be the long-term prioritization of SHA-256 coverage over leak-checking in the reftable job, but the immediate trade-off has been settled. The series is ready for final review and should be a straightforward merge.
