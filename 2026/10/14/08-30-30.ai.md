This is the fourth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s v3 CI/build system housekeeping series. It updates the `linux32` job to use `i386/debian:latest` instead of the now-unsupported `i386/ubuntu:20.04` image. The change ensures 32-bit platform coverage until at least 2030, as Debian continues to release 32-bit Docker images despite reducing i386 support in Debian 13.

The patch touches `.github/workflows/main.yml`, `.gitlab-ci.yml`, `ci/install-dependencies.sh`, and `ci/lib.sh`. The diff is mechanical: it replaces the image name in both CI configurations, removes obsolete conditional logic in `ci/install-dependencies.sh` (which previously handled `i386/ubuntu` separately for SVN dependencies), and updates the `CI_OS_NAME` check in `ci/lib.sh` to recognize the new image. The net effect is a reduction of 6 lines (4 insertions, 10 deletions).

This is a straightforward platform-compatibility update with no behavioral impact. The change is well-motivated by the EOL status of Ubuntu 20.04 for i386, and the Debian image provides a stable alternative. The patch is uncontroversial and aligns with the series’ goal of reducing CI complexity while maintaining coverage.
