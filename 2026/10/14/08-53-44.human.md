This is the second iteration of a refactoring and performance series targeting the Git object database’s "files" backend, specifically its handling of alternates. The series now contains six patches (up from five in v1), with the new patch addressing error handling when fetching missing objects from promisor remotes.

The core goal remains unchanged: clean up and optimize the alternates mechanism after its recent migration into the "files" backend. The first two patches (unchanged from v1) fix performance regressions in object reading and streaming when alternates are present, achieving measurable speedups. The third and fourth patches refactor internal logic to simplify the ODB source API, and the fifth patch (new in v2) tightens error handling by avoiding re-fetch attempts for corrupt objects. The final patch removes a redundant `local` flag from the ODB source struct, completing the API cleanup.

The diffstat shows a net reduction of 54 lines, indicating the series is still focused on simplification. The new patch (5/6) is a direct response to Justin Tobler’s feedback on v1, addressing his suggestion to fail early when encountering corrupt repositories rather than attempting to fetch missing objects. This change is scoped narrowly to error handling and does not alter the series’ overall direction.
