This is a performance bugfix for deepening fetches, reviewed and approved by Patrick Steinhardt. The patch moves the `--alternate-refs` flag in `connected.c` so it is only used in non-deepening connectivity checks, preventing Git from walking the full history of large alternates during shallow fetches. The regression was introduced by earlier commits and caused multi-minute delays in CI environments where fresh repositories fetch from large alternates.

The fix is minimal—just one line moved—and includes two new tests in `t/t5618-alternate-refs.sh` that verify the `rev-list` arguments via `GIT_TRACE2_EVENT`. The tests confirm alternate refs are excluded during deepening without asserting behavioral differences, as the outcome remains correct for complete alternates. Patrick notes the tests are somewhat indirect but accepts them as the best available approach.

The commit message is thorough, explaining the historical context, performance impact, and reasoning. The patch is well-motivated and addresses a real-world pain point in CI workflows. With Patrick’s approval and no open questions, this looks ready for integration.
