This is a refactoring patch, part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. Okhuomon Ajayi (an Outreachy intern) removes the now-unused `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` macro from `diffcore-break.c`. The macro became redundant after commit 84325f0730 ("merge,diff: remove the_repository check before prefetching blobs") eliminated the last direct use of `the_repository` in that file.

The change is purely mechanical: two lines deleted from a single file, no functional impact. The commit message is clear and cites the exact commit that made the macro obsolete. This is the kind of small, safe cleanup that the `the_repository` removal effort routinely produces—it prevents future accidental reintroductions of the global variable by making them fail to compile.

Version 2 of the patch incorporates feedback from Usman Akinyemi, who suggested dropping the original justification about preventing future reintroductions. The code itself remains unchanged from v1. The patch is uncontroversial and should be straightforward to review. No tests or documentation changes are needed, as this is internal plumbing only.
