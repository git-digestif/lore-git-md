This is a documentation patch addressing feedback on v2 of Julia Evans' rewrite of Git’s explanation of detached HEAD state. The email is a reply from the author confirming all requested changes from Junio C Hamano’s review.

Julia Evans acknowledges and agrees to fix four minor issues: (1) hyphenating the help command in both the advice message and documentation (`git help detached-head` instead of `git help detachedhead`), (2) adding a sign-off from co-author Marie Claire LeBlanc Flanagan, (3) changing the reflog expiry period from "90 days" to "30 days" (the default for `gc.reflogExpireUnreachable`) to avoid misleading users about unreachable commit persistence, and (4) adjusting whitespace in `command-list.txt` to maintain column alignment after adding the hyphen. These corrections are surface-level and do not alter the patch’s core goals or structure.

The patch remains a well-motivated documentation improvement targeting a real usability gap. Once these changes are incorporated, it should be ready for integration. The broader help-system usability concern (internal cross-references vs. `git help`) is not addressed here and remains a potential future improvement. No new technical details or open questions are introduced in this reply.
