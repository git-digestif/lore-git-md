This is a CI/build system patch, part of Patrick Steinhardt’s eight-patch series modernizing Git’s CI configurations. This specific patch (5/8) renames the `linux-TEST-vars` job to `linux-feature-testbed`—a purely mechanical change that updates three one-line references across `.github/workflows/main.yml`, `.gitlab-ci.yml`, and `ci/run-build-and-tests.sh`.

The rename resolves a naming dispute that surfaced in v2, where SZEDER Gábor objected to the proposed `linux-exotic` as misleading. Junio C Hamano later suggested `linux-feature-testbed` among 14 alternatives, and this version adopts that choice. The new name better reflects the job’s purpose: testing Git with non-default options like `OPENSSL_SHA1_UNSAFE` and `GIT_TEST_SPLIT_INDEX`. There is no behavioral change—only the job name is updated.

The patch is uncontroversial and ready for review. It’s a small but necessary step in the series, which overall reduces CI complexity by removing outdated dependencies (Python 2, EOL Docker images) and redundant jobs. If you’re tracking the series, this is the patch that finalizes the job-name cleanup; the rest of the series focuses on coverage trade-offs (e.g., reftable/SHA-256) and script updates. The linked CI runs confirm the change doesn’t break anything.
