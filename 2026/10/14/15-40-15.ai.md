This is the third patch in Phillip Wood’s seven-patch series `pw/stash-optimize-check_changes-calls/v2`, a performance-focused refactoring of `git stash push` and `git stash create`. The patch eliminates a redundant `check_changes()` call in `do_push_stash()`, reducing the number of untracked-file walks from two to one and cutting two `lstat()` calls per changed path. It also removes an unnecessary index refresh (`repo_refresh_and_write_index()`) that was previously performed before the duplicate change check.

The patch leverages the `enum create_result` introduced in the first patch to distinguish between "no changes to stash" (`CREATE_NO_CHANGES`) and "nothing stashed" (`CREATE_EMPTY`). This ensures consistent exit statuses: `git stash push --patch` (when no hunks are selected) and `git stash push --staged` (when the index matches `HEAD`) still exit with status 1, while a no-op `git stash push` exits 0. The behavioral change is minor—reflog creation now occurs before the "no changes" check—but is deemed inconsequential in practice.

The diff is confined to `builtin/stash.c`, modifying `do_push_stash()` and `do_create_stash()`. The changes are mechanical and well-motivated, aligning with the series’ goal of reducing redundant operations without altering user-visible behavior. No new edge cases or controversies are introduced. The patch is part of a broader effort to streamline stash internals, with prior feedback from Junio C Hamano already incorporated. If merged, it would land in `next` alongside the rest of the series.
