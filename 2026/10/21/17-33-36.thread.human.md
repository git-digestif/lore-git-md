This thread is Jasper Mayone's RFC series on adding `--merged`/`--no-merged` filtering to `git worktree list`, now at version 5 with only three patches remaining. The series originally proposed five patches; patches 4/5 (a `%(currentworktree)` atom for `git for-each-ref`) and 5/5 (a `git branch` output cleanup) have been dropped after Junio C Hamano objected that the "+" marker for bisected branches in long listings is useful, and Phillip Wood argued the atom was redundant with `%(worktreepath)` and could report inconsistent results when a branch is checked out in multiple worktrees.

What remains is a three-patch series considered ready for integration: a safety fix making `git worktree remove` refuse to delete worktrees mid-rebase, bisect, cherry-pick, revert, or `git am` without `--force`; a documentation update warning about safely parsing `%(worktreepath)`; and an enhancement to the `%(worktreepath)` atom that reports paths for rebasing/bisecting branches using O(1) hashmap lookups. Jasper has acknowledged Junio's minor editorial requests (consolidating helper functions, deferring quoting-advice consolidation) but does not plan a v6 unless explicitly asked. Both Jasper and Junio have also sketched a possible follow-up to unify worktree safety checks into a reusable "discardable state" helper in `worktree.c`.
