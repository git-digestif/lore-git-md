This thread is an RFC by Jasper Mayone proposing `--merged` and `--no-merged` filters for `git worktree list`, allowing developers to quickly see which worktrees correspond to topics already merged upstream. The implementation is complete, touching only `builtin/worktree.c` and adding tests, and reuses existing `ref_filter` machinery for consistency. No maintainer feedback has arrived yet, but the patch is technically straightforward and unlikely to be controversial.

Harald Nordgren’s reply shifts focus away from the patch itself, questioning Git’s broader design choice of preventing a branch from being checked out in more than one worktree. He argues this restriction feels arbitrary—since separate clones don’t enforce it—and suggests it may become more frustrating as worktrees grow in popularity, especially with AI-assisted workflows. His comment is brief and doesn’t propose a concrete alternative, but it opens a tangential discussion about whether the one-branch-per-worktree rule should be relaxed or made configurable. For now, the original RFC remains unanswered, with five open design questions still on the table.
