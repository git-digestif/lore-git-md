This thread is about adding filtering capabilities to `git worktree list` by introducing two new ref-filter atoms, `%(worktreepath)` and `%(currentworktree)`, while also addressing safety issues in `git worktree remove`. The five-patch series, now in its fifth and final version (v5), is complete and ready for integration. Jasper Mayone authored the series, which has incorporated all prior feedback, including performance fixes, safety improvements, and composability concerns.

Key changes include making `git worktree remove` refuse to delete worktrees in the middle of operations like rebase, bisect, or cherry-pick without `--force`, and ensuring `%(worktreepath)` now reports paths for rebasing or bisecting branches instead of silently omitting them. The new `%(currentworktree)` atom enables conditional formatting in commands like `git branch`, which now suppresses redundant markers and paths for rebasing/bisecting branches in the current worktree. The series is backward-incompatible for scripts relying on the old behavior of `%(worktreepath)` or the `+` marker in `git branch` output.

The series has been reviewed by Phillip Wood, who proposed the `%(currentworktree)` atom and hashmap refactor, and Ben Knoble, who raised safety concerns about mid-rebase worktrees (now addressed). Eric Sunshine identified an unresolved issue with the main-worktree-first invariant in filtered output, but this does not block integration. The latest email from Ben Knoble confirms his acceptance of a minor documentation wording change, leaving no remaining substantive feedback. The series is now awaiting final review and integration.
