Core problem or goal
Replace hardcoded English plural forms in two (or possibly three) user-facing messages in `builtin/checkout.c` (and optionally `builtin/worktree.c`) with Git’s `Q_()` macro. The goal is to enable translators to handle languages that require different plural forms for small numbers (2–4) versus larger ones (5+), which the current code prevents.

Under review; initial patch posted. No prior versions.

- Messages affected:
  1. Detached HEAD warning: `" ... and %d more.\n"`
  2. Multiple reference error: `"only one reference expected, %d given."`
- No behavior change for English users; no on-disk format changes; no new symbols or CLI options.
