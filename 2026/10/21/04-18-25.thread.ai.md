Core problem or goal
Replace hardcoded English plural forms in user-facing messages in `builtin/checkout.c` and `builtin/worktree.c` with Git’s `Q_()` macro to enable translators to handle languages requiring distinct plural forms for small numbers (2–4) versus larger ones (5+). The goal is i18n correctness without changing English behavior or on-disk formats.
