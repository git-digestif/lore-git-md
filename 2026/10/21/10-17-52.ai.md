This is a follow-up in the four-patch bugfix series restoring dynamic reload of alternate object databases in the files-based ODB backend. The series targets a regression introduced during the ODB abstraction effort, where the ability to reload the alternates list on object lookup failure was accidentally broken.

Patrick responds to Junio’s review comment on the fourth patch, which proposed deferring the `stat_validity` update until after a successful read of the alternates file. Junio’s concern was that updating the validity tracker even on read failures (e.g., transient I/O errors or concurrent modifications) could leave the tracker in an inconsistent state. Patrick agrees the extra safety is worthwhile and posts an adjusted version of the `read_alternates()` helper that clears the validity tracker on failure and updates it only after a successful read and parse. The change is mechanical and confined to the control flow in `odb/source-files.c`; it does not alter the patch’s core logic or performance characteristics.

The discussion remains narrowly focused on correctness and does not reopen broader design questions. Patrick has already queued the change locally, suggesting he intends to include it in a v2 or a follow-up patch. The series as a whole continues to look production-ready, with no other open questions or objections.
