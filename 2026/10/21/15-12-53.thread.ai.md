Subject: [PATCH v3] rev-list: fix `--grep` parsing when followed by another option
Series version: standalone patch, v3
Originating author: Azuama Odinaka Maximus
Thread type: bugfix patch

Core problem or goal
The `git rev-list` command misparses a `--grep` argument that is immediately followed by another option-like argument (e.g., `--missing-only`). The parser treats the next argument as a separate flag rather than as the grep pattern, causing the command to fail or behave unpredictably. The patch ensures that `--grep` is handled early in the argument scan so that the following argument is always treated as the pattern, even if it begins with `--`.
