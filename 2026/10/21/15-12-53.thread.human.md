The issue was that the parser treated the next argument as a separate flag rather than as the pattern, causing failures or unexpected behavior. The fix, now in its fourth iteration, has expanded from addressing just `--grep` to covering all 18 similar options (`--author`, `--committer`, `--since`, `--until`, etc.) by introducing a helper function to recognize these options during the early argument scan.

The patch is uncontroversial in its core logic but has evolved in scope.
