Azuama Odinaka Maximus sent a minimal two-line patch that ensures `--grep` always consumes the next argument as the pattern, even if it starts with `--`, along with a new test to verify the fix.

The patch is under review and hasn’t received any substantive feedback yet. While the feedback is constructive, it doesn’t question the patch’s correctness or identify any deeper problems—just areas for improvement in documentation and test coverage. The patch remains uncontroversial and ready for further review or merging once the commit message is clarified.
