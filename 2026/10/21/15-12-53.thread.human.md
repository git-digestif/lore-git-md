This thread is about a small but important bugfix for `git rev-list`: when `--grep` is immediately followed by another option (like `--missing-only`), the command misparses the arguments, treating the next option as a flag rather than as the grep pattern. Azuama Odinaka Maximus sent a two-line patch in v2 that fixes the issue by ensuring `--grep` always consumes the next argument as the pattern, even if it starts with `--`. The patch also adds tests to confirm the fix works in both edge cases and normal use.
