It includes a **clear example graph** illustrating the boundary between incoming and already-connected commits, which helps clarify the algorithm’s logic. The text also notes the **performance bottleneck** in the full check (marking boundary trees as uninteresting dominates when boundary commits have large trees), setting up the motivation for the incremental mode introduced later in the series.
