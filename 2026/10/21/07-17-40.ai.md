This is the third patch in the v3 series introducing copy-on-write worktree creation. It **permanently disables reflink support on Windows** due to a confirmed silent-corruption bug in ReFS (Git LFS #6312), where `FSCTL_DUPLICATE_EXTENTS_TO_FILE` can expose unwritten zeros if the source data is still cached. The patch removes all Windows-specific reflink code from `copy.c`, replacing it with a stub that always fails with `ENOSYS`. The `builtin/worktree.c` side now emits a warning (unless in `auto` mode) when `--reflink` or `worktree.reflink=true` is used on Windows, citing build 26200 as the affected version. Documentation is updated to reflect the disablement and the corruption risk.

The change is a direct response to **substantive technical feedback** from brian m. carlson, who identified the ReFS bug as a blocker. The patch preserves the filesystem-probing infrastructure (used by patch 4/4) but disables the actual ioctl, ensuring no silent corruption can occur. The test in `t2400-worktree-add.sh` verifies the warning is emitted and the fallback to regular checkout works. This patch resolves the immediate safety concern but leaves the broader reflink design debate (opt-in vs. automatic) unresolved.
