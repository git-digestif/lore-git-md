This thread is an Outreachy microproject where newcomers are working together to improve Git's internationalization by replacing hardcoded English plural messages with the `Q_()` macro. The goal is to enable proper pluralization in languages that distinguish between small and large numbers, without changing how the messages appear in English.

The work has been divided among participants: Tanga Joris will handle two pluralizable messages in `builtin/checkout.c` (lines 1088 and 1492), while Muritala Mudi will address the identical message in `builtin/worktree.c` and an additional one at line 1405 in `checkout.c` ("matched multiple (%d) remote tracking branches"). The patches are still in the coordination phase, with no technical review from maintainers or experienced contributors yet. The next step is for Mudi to submit their patch covering the remaining messages.
