This thread is an Outreachy microproject where Muritala Mudi proposes replacing hardcoded English plural forms in `git checkout` messages with Git’s `Q_()` macro to better support translations. The change targets two user-facing messages in `builtin/checkout.c` that currently prevent translators from handling languages with complex plural rules (e.g., distinguishing between 2–4 items and 5+ items). Muritala also asked whether to include a similar message in `builtin/worktree.c` in the same patch.

So far, the only feedback has come from Keryle Fondji, another newcomer, who welcomed Muritala to the community and endorsed the use of `Q_()` as good practice for internationalization. Keryle suggested keeping the patch scope small—focusing on `checkout.c` first—to simplify review and testing, and shared a link to Junio’s *MyFirstContribution* guide for additional guidance. While supportive, this feedback is surface-level and doesn’t engage with the code’s correctness or edge cases. The thread is still early, with no substantive technical review or maintainer input yet. The next steps will likely involve deeper review or a revised patch addressing the scope question.
