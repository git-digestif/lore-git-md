This thread is about an Outreachy microproject to improve internationalization in Git by replacing hardcoded English plural forms in `git checkout` messages with the `Q_()` macro. The goal is to enable proper pluralization in languages that distinguish between small and large numbers, without changing behavior for English users.

Muritala Mudi proposed the change, initially targeting two messages in `builtin/checkout.c` and optionally one in `builtin/worktree.c`. Two other newcomers, Keryle Fondji and Tuomas Ahola, provided surface-level feedback, endorsing the approach but offering no technical review. Now, Tanga Joris—who already submitted a patch for one of the messages in `checkout.c`—has volunteered to extend their patch to cover the second message in the same file, leaving Muritala to focus on the identical message in `worktree.c`. The division of labor is now clear, but no maintainer or substantive technical review has yet weighed in. The series remains under review with no decisions made about scope or merge readiness.
