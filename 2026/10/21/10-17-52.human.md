This is a follow-up in Patrick Steinhardt’s four-patch bugfix series restoring dynamic reload of alternate object databases in the files-based ODB backend. The series targets a regression introduced during the ODB abstraction effort, where Git lost the ability to reload the alternates list when an object lookup fails.

Junio’s review of the fourth patch identified a minor edge case: the `stat_validity` tracker was updated even if reading or parsing the alternates file failed, which could leave the tracker in an inconsistent state after a transient I/O error. Patrick agrees the extra safety is worth the small change and proposes a revised control flow that defers the validity update until after a successful read. The new version introduces a `successful` flag to track whether parsing completed, then updates or clears the `stat_validity` tracker accordingly. This adjustment is purely internal to `odb/source-files.c` and does not affect the series’ overall design, performance, or test coverage.

The fix remains narrowly scoped, well-explained, and production-ready. The change is uncontroversial and resolves Junio’s concern without introducing new complexity. If Patrick sends a v2, this tweak will likely be the only difference from the original submission.
