This is the second of two reviews Phillip Wood posted on Erik Cervin-Edin’s v3 patch series that completes the `--fixup` non-interactive workflow by allowing `-m`, `-F`, `-c`, and `-C` to work with all `--fixup` variants (`--fixup`, `--fixup=amend:`, `--fixup=reword:`).

Phillip’s feedback on this first patch is surface-level and non-blocking. He notes that the unavoidable code duplication in `prepare_to_commit()` is “not worth refactoring” and approves the core logic and test updates. The only substantive point he raises is a test hygiene concern—calling `test_set_editor()` outside a subshell—which applies to the *second* patch in the series, not this one. On this patch itself, he has no remaining objections and explicitly endorses the feature’s usefulness. The series is now production-ready, awaiting Junio’s final sign-off.
