## 2026/10/21

- [2026/10/21/17-33-36 by Jasper Mayone <me@jaspermayone.com>] Jasper Mayone sent an RFC proposing to add `--merged` and `--no-merged` filters to `git worktree list`, applied to each worktree's HEAD, so users can find worktrees whose work has already landed.
