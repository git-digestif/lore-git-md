This is a **documentation** follow-up in the `gittutorial` replacement series. Junio C Hamano clarifies his earlier stance on AsciiDoc formatting: he does not have a strong preference between Setext-style (underline) and Atx-style (`==` prefix) headings for the new beginner tutorial. He acknowledges that Atx-style may be easier to maintain for a new document, as it avoids character-counting and handles non-ASCII text more gracefully, but leaves the final choice to the author. The mechanical fix (adding blank lines before Setext-style headings or switching to Atx-style) remains the only blocker to merging the series. No content or pedagogical issues are revisited. The series is otherwise ready for submission once the formatting is resolved.
