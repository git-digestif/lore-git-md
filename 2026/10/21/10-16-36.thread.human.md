This thread concerns Elia Pinto's v3 four-patch series fixing 64-to-32-bit truncations that corrupt or crash Git on Windows (LLP64 platforms where `long` is 32-bit), specifically covering object database writes, the word-diff buffer in `diff.c`, file conversion helpers, and text statistics counters. The series remains under review and technically sound, but its progress is stalled by a procedural dispute: Patrick Steinhardt believes the fourth patch's commit message reads as AI-generated and has asked for a genuine rewrite demonstrating human understanding, while Pinto has only sent a ping noting the series has sat since October 31st with two other series blocked behind it. Christian Couder has independently validated the technical approach, implemented a near-identical fix for the ODB write path himself, and explicitly stated the series should merge after minor polishing, though he has not resolved the authorship question. The latest development is a substantive review from Phillip Wood of patch 2/4, who questions whether converting only the allocation counter to `size_t` is sufficient given that the loop indices `i` and `j` remain `int` and are ultimately bounded by `regoff_t` (typically `INT_MAX` on 32-bit), suggesting word-diff buffers larger than roughly 2 GiB may still fail on those platforms and recommending `buffer->orig_nr` and `buffer->orig_alloc` receive the same treatment for consistency.
