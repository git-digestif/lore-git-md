The exchange highlights a core tension in the SHA-256 transition. Neither side has proposed a concrete patch yet, but the discussion now centers on whether the trade-off is acceptable—whether Git should tolerate "broken" headers for the sake of interoperability or enforce stricter consistency, even at the cost of conversion failures.

This thread is likely to draw attention from contributors working on repository conversion tooling and the broader SHA-256 effort. The outcome could shape how Git handles edge cases in mixed-OID repositories for years to come.
