This is the first patch in Harald Nordgren’s v3 series extending `git branch --delete-merged` to detect squash-merged and rebase-merged branches. It is a pure refactoring: the `branch_opted_out()` helper extracts the existing config-based opt-out check (`branch.<name>.deleteMerged=false`) from `delete_merged_branches()` so the same logic can be reused in the next patch, which adds detection for branches whose changes (but not their tips) are upstream. No behavior changes; the patch is mechanical and uncontroversial.
