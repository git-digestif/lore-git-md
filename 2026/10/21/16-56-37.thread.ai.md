Core problem or goal
The thread proposes a multi-layered technical and policy framework to mitigate the sustainability crisis caused by high-frequency, low-delta Git pushes ("comma programmers"), particularly in AI-assisted coding workflows. The goal is to reduce redundant CI/CD builds, cloud compute waste, and environmental harm by introducing push debouncing, batching thresholds, and platform-level rate-limiting guidelines.
