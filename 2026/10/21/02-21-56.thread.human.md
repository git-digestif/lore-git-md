This thread is a small but important i18n fix for `git checkout` that corrects how pluralization is handled in a message showing the number of additional files ("... and %d more."). The patch, submitted by Outreachy applicant Tanga Joris, replaces the singular `_()` gettext wrapper with `Q_()` to ensure proper translation into languages with complex plural rules (like Polish), while leaving the English output unchanged.

Junio C Hamano reviewed the patch and suggested a tweak to the commit message: instead of stating that the English string "stays the same," he recommends acknowledging the trade-off—translators for languages without plural distinctions will now need to provide two identical translations, but this enables correct pluralization for languages that require it. The patch itself remains technically sound and uncontroversial, with no objections raised. The discussion is now focused on refining the commit message for clarity. The fix touches `builtin/checkout.c` and requires no additional test coverage, as it’s purely an i18n plumbing change.
