This thread is a small but well-targeted bugfix for Git's internationalization support. Tanga Joris, an Outreachy applicant, submitted a one-line patch to `builtin/checkout.c` that switches a message from the basic `_()` gettext wrapper to the plural-aware `Q_()` macro. The change addresses a subtle i18n issue: while English treats "1 commit and 2 more" the same as "1 commit and 5 more," languages like Polish require different word forms for small versus large counts. The patch is purely mechanical—it doesn't alter the English output—but ensures translators receive the correct plural context. There's been no discussion yet, but the fix appears uncontroversial and aligns with Git's existing i18n practices.
