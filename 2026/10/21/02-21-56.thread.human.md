This thread is about a small but important i18n fix for `git checkout`. Outreachy applicant Tanga Joris identified two messages in `builtin/checkout.c` that incorrectly used the singular `_()` gettext wrapper for strings that can display plural counts ("... and %d more." and "only one reference expected, %d given."). The patch converts these to the plural-aware `Q_()` macro, enabling proper translation into languages with complex plural rules like Polish, while leaving the English output unchanged.

The patch has now reached v2, incorporating all prior feedback. Junio C Hamano suggested acknowledging the trade-off for languages without plural distinctions (which now require two identical translations), and Tuomas pointed out the second message needing conversion - both are now addressed. Junio has confirmed he will queue the patch, meaning it will appear in `seen` shortly and, barring any last-minute issues, graduate through `next` to `master`. The fix is uncontroversial and complete, touching only i18n plumbing without affecting behavior or on-disk format.
