This thread is about a small but consequential refactoring patch by Okhuomon Ajayi that moves the global `git_commit_encoding` variable into repository-scoped storage as part of the ongoing `the_repository` removal effort. The patch itself is straightforward—it touches `commit.c` and related files to make the encoding repository-specific—but the discussion took an unexpected turn when Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, objected to the approach. He argued that embedding the encoding in the `repo_config_values` struct was overkill and proposed instead passing it as a direct parameter to the functions that need it.

Okhuomon has now agreed to revise the patch along Junio’s lines, keeping the change confined to `commit.c` and avoiding modifications to `repo_config_values`. This shift means the patch will no longer follow the established pattern of the `the_repository` removal series, where globals are consistently moved into the repository struct. The outcome here could set a precedent for how similar globals are handled in future patches. The revised version hasn’t been posted yet, so the thread is effectively paused until the new patch arrives, but the core design question appears to be resolved in favor of Junio’s simpler, more localized approach. No test changes are planned, as the behavior remains unchanged from the user’s perspective.
