This thread is about a patch from Okhuomon Ajayi that moves the global `git_commit_encoding` variable into Git’s `repo_config_values` struct as part of the ongoing effort to remove the `the_repository` global. The patch is a clean, incremental refactoring that touches `commit.c` and `environment.h`, updating `write_commit_tree()` to use a repository-scoped value instead of a global.

The discussion took a turn when Junio C Hamano weighed in with a fundamental critique of the approach. He argues that the patch adds unnecessary complexity by embedding the encoding in the repository struct, when a simpler alternative would be to pass the encoding directly as a `const char *` parameter to the functions that need it. This would avoid touching `repo_config_values` entirely and keep the change localized to the commit subsystem. Junio’s suggestion challenges the established pattern of the `the_repository` removal series, which has consistently moved globals into `repo_config_values`.

The thread is now at a crossroads: the author will need to either defend the current design or revise the patch to align with Junio’s alternative. The outcome could set a precedent for how similar globals are handled in future patches. No other reviews have been posted yet, so this is the first substantive feedback the patch has received. The patch remains under review, and the next steps will depend on how the author and the broader community respond to Junio’s critique.
