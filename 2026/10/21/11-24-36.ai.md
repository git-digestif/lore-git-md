This is a **substantive review** of the second patch in Elia Pinto’s three-patch series fixing 64-to-32 truncations on Windows. Patrick Steinhardt confirms the patch’s correctness: the two allocation counters in `diff.c` (`alloc` in `struct diff_words_buffer` and `alloc` in `diff_words_fill()`) are only ever used by `ALLOC_GROW()`, which expects a `size_t`. The current `unsigned long`/`long` types risk overflow on LLP64 platforms (Windows), where `long` is 32-bit, leading to crashes when processing large files. Steinhardt’s review is narrowly focused on this patch and does not revisit the broader series question about test coverage (which he raised on the first patch). The feedback is purely confirmatory: the type conversion is the right fix, and there are no hidden negative-value dependencies. No further action is needed on this patch.
