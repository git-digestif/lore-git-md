This is the first patch in Harald Nordgren’s two-patch v2 series fixing `git imap-send` interoperability with `b4` and other tools. The patch itself is a one-line memory leak fix in tunnel-command handling, adding `child_process_clear(&tunnel)` after the tunnel process is started. Patrick Steinhardt’s review raises a substantive question: why isn’t `finish_command()` called on the child process at all? His comment suggests the current fix might be incomplete or masking a deeper design issue in how the tunnel process is managed. The thread’s overall goal—ensuring `git imap-send` works with non-canonical email header order—remains on track, but this review introduces a potential follow-up concern about process lifecycle management. The series is still under review, with no merge status recorded.
