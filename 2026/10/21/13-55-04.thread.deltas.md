- [2026/10/21/13-55-04 by Mark C. Chu-Carroll <markchucarroll@fastmail.com>] Mark C. - [2026/10/21/13-55-05 by Mark C. - [2026/10/21/13-55-06 by Mark C. - [2026/10/21/13-55-07 by Mark C. Chu-Carroll <markchucarroll@fastmail.com>] The third patch adds documentation for the `--concern` option in `Documentation/git-repo.adoc` and documents the `repo.structure.concern.*` configuration variables in a new `Documentation/config/repo.adoc`.
