- [2026/10/21/10-16-37 by Elia Pinto <gitter.spiros@gmail.com>] Patch 1 changes `odb_write_object_ext()`, `odb_write_object()`, `odb_source_write_object()`, `write_loose_object()`, and the in-memory source's stored size to take `size_t`, fixing the case where `git add`/`git hash-object -w` of a file over 4 GiB run through a conversion (e.g. core.autocrlf) writes a blob truncated to `size mod 2^32` bytes while the index records the real file's stat data.

- [2026/10/21/10-16-39 by Elia Pinto <gitter.spiros@gmail.com>] Patch 3 changes `gather_stats()`, `gather_convert_stats()`, `gather_convert_stats_ascii()`, `count_ident()`, `read_blob_data_from_index()`, and index attribute reading to use `size_t`, fixing prefix-only scanning of files over 4 GiB and the `git add` failure with core.autocrlf when the staged version exceeds 4 GiB.
