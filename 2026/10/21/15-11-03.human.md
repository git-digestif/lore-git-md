Phillip Wood’s review confirms the patch is well-executed and addresses all prior feedback. The changes unify support for `-m` (inline message) and `-F` (message from file) across all `--fixup` variants (`--fixup`, `--fixup=amend:`, `--fixup=reword:`), removing inconsistent restrictions that previously blocked `-F` entirely and `-m` for amend-style fixups.

The implementation routes `-m` and `-F` through the existing `prepare_to_commit()` logic, eliminating redundant checks and ensuring consistent behavior. The patch maintains backward compatibility—editor launches if no message option is provided—and preserves the existing logic that strips redundant "amend!" lines during autosquash.
