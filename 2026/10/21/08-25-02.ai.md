This is a **substantive review** of the second patch in Harald Nordgren’s two-patch bugfix series for `git imap-send`. Patrick Steinhardt raises two technical concerns about the patch’s scope and design.

First, he questions whether the patch conflates two distinct issues: (1) accepting `Subject` before `Date` headers (the stated goal) and (2) relaxing the special-case logic for messages starting with `From git-send-email`, which previously ignored `Subject` headers entirely. The patch removes this special case without explaining why it is safe to do so, which Patrick flags as potentially risky.

Second, he notes a test design oddity: every test in `t/t9001-send-email.sh` carries the `$PREREQ` guard, which he suggests could be handled more efficiently by skipping the entire script at once if prerequisites are not met.

The review does not challenge the core correctness of the header-parsing logic (which Junio already validated) but highlights unaddressed edge cases and implementation choices that could affect backward compatibility. The author will need to clarify the rationale for removing the `git-send-email` special case and consider whether the test suite’s prerequisite handling should be refactored.
