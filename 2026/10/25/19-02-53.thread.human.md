The patch is part of the broader `the_repository` removal project, which aims to make Git's codebase more modular by eliminating implicit global state. While this specific change is straightforward and introduces no new edge cases, it aligns with the project's architectural goals. So far, the patch has not attracted review comments, but given its clean implementation and adherence to project conventions, it’s likely to be well-received once reviewed.
