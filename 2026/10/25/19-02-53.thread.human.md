This thread is about a small, mechanical patch from Dorine Tipo (an Outreachy participant) that contributes to Git’s long-running effort to remove the global `the_repository` variable. The patch updates two functions—`partial_clone_register()` and `partial_clone_get_default_filter_spec()`—to accept a `struct repository *` parameter instead of relying on the global, and adjusts their callers in `builtin/clone.c` and `builtin/fetch.c` to pass `the_repository` explicitly. The change is purely refactoring; behavior and on-disk format remain unchanged.

So far, the only feedback has come from Okhuomon Ajayi, who tested the patch and noted that the `USE_THE_REPOSITORY_VARIABLE` macro can be safely removed in the same patch since the file no longer references `the_repository`. This is a minor, surface-level observation—no substantive technical concerns have been raised, and the patch is still awaiting review from established contributors. The series remains at its initial version with no decisions or maintainer actions yet.
