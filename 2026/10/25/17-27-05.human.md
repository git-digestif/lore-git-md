This is the third patch in Harald Nordgren’s v10 series introducing a "matching-inspired" fetch mode for shallow repositories. The patch implements the core logic: when a remote has `remote.<name>.refmap` configured but no `remote.<name>.fetch`, a refspec-less `git fetch` now infers which branches to fetch by enumerating the local branches whose `@{upstream}` points to that remote. This addresses the long-standing performance issue where shallow clones fetch unnecessary branches, causing slow or hanging operations.
