This is a **substantive review reply** from D. Ben Knoble on the first patch of Harald Nordgren’s v10 series introducing `remote.<name>.refmap`. The patch modifies `remote_find_tracking()` to ensure it only falls back to `remote->refmap` when `remote->fetch` is either empty or contains no matching refspec for the given query, addressing Knoble’s earlier concern about unintended side effects (e.g., for `--delete-merged`).

Knoble acknowledges the updated logic but expresses uncertainty about whether it is "correct," noting that the fallback to `refmap` might be necessary for the `--limited-fetch` functionality introduced later in the series. He reiterates his earlier questions about the downstream consequences of treating `refmap` as a superset of `fetch` and whether this could affect operations like `--delete-merged`. His reply is not a rejection but a request for further discussion to clarify the intended behavior, suggesting that the current implementation may not yet fully align with the design goals.

The reply does not block the series from proceeding to `next`—Junio and Harald have already addressed the technical feedback—but it highlights a lingering design ambiguity. The series remains uncontroversial and ready for merging, but this exchange underscores that the `remote_find_tracking()` fallback logic may warrant an audit of its callers in follow-up work. The broader goal of the series (dynamic branch selection for shallow repositories) is unaffected.
