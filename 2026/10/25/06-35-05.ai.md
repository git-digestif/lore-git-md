This is the second patch in Tyler Cipriani’s four-patch series optimizing the `test-lint` infrastructure. It updates a single interop test script (`t/interop/i5700-protocol-transition.sh`) to replace bare `grep` invocations with `test_grep`, ensuring compatibility with the `test-grep-lint` check that will soon be integrated into `chainlint.pl`. This patch is uncontroversial and a prerequisite for the later architectural changes in the series.
