This is the third patch in Harald Nordgren’s v10 series implementing a "matching-inspired" fetch mode for shallow repositories. The patch introduces the core logic: when a remote has `remote.<name>.refmap` configured but no `remote.<name>.fetch`, a refspec-less `git fetch` now infers which branches to fetch by enumerating the local branches whose `@{upstream}` points to that remote. This email is Harald’s reply to D. Ben Knoble’s review of that patch, confirming acceptance of all non-blocking suggestions and outlining the planned updates for the next revision.

Harald agrees to rephrase the commit message to better reflect the series’ goal—allowing refmaps to dynamically infer branches to fetch—rather than framing it as a fix for an error case. He also accepts Knoble’s suggestion to clarify the documentation in `fetch-options.adoc`, replacing the vague phrase "branches from the remote that are used as the `@{upstream}` of our local branches" with the more precise "remote-tracking branches from this remote that are also configured as the `@{upstream}` of a local branch are fetched." This change will make it clearer that the refmap’s role is to map the fetched refs, not to narrow the scope of which branches are selected.

On the implementation side, Harald confirms that the next version will replace the `string_list` in `get_ref_map()` with a `strvec`, since the branches are inherently ordered (by refname and configuration) and no sorting is needed. He also agrees to refactor the duplicate code in `do_fetch()` where the `follow_remote_head` logic and `collect_upstream_from_remote()` call appear in two nearly identical blocks, addressing Knoble’s observation about redundancy.

The patch remains ready for merging to `next`. The only remaining work is to incorporate these non-blocking improvements in a final revision or follow-up patches. No technical or procedural blockers remain, and the series is uncontroversial. The key files touched by this patch are `builtin/fetch.c`, `remote.c`, `remote.h`, and the documentation (`fetch-options.adoc`, `config/remote.adoc`), along with new tests in `t/t5586-fetch-refmap.sh`. The behavior change—fetching only the branches tracked by local branches at the remote—is now well-documented and aligned with the series’ goal of improving performance for shallow repositories.
