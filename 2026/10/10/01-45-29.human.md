This is a documentation patch in the ongoing `gitmergeconflicts(7)` series, now in its third iteration. Junio C Hamano, the maintainer, replies to Julia Evans’s v2 cover letter with two targeted refinements for the new man page.

First, he asks for **completeness** in the list of commands that can trigger merge conflicts. Julia had deliberately omitted `` `git am -3` `` and `` `git checkout -m` `` to keep the guide concise for beginners; Junio prefers the full list (`merge`, `rebase`, `cherry-pick`, `pull`, `am -3`, `checkout -m`, `stash pop`, `revert`) without hedging, arguing that an official manual should aim for accuracy over brevity. His suggested phrasing is direct and leaves no room for ambiguity.

Second, he flags **informal contractions** like “Here’s” and “there’s” as out of place in a technical manual. Julia had defended them as more approachable, but Junio insists on consistent formality across Git’s documentation. This is a surface-level stylistic point, not a technical objection, but it carries weight coming from the maintainer.

The exchange is brief and constructive. Julia’s v3 submission will almost certainly adopt both changes, keeping the series on track for merging. The new man page remains a well-motivated, user-focused consolidation of scattered merge-conflict advice; these final tweaks simply polish its tone and completeness.
