This is a respin of the refactoring patch (v8 2/5) in Harald Nordgren’s series introducing a matching-inspired fetch mode for shallow repositories. The patch extracts the existing logic for fetching the current branch’s upstream into a reusable helper, `collect_upstream_from_remote()`, but Junio’s review identified two issues: the control-flow reorganization belongs in the next patch (3/5), and the helper lacks a NULL-check for `branch`, risking a segfault.

Harald acknowledges both points and plans to adjust the patch. The NULL-check oversight is mechanical and easy to fix, but the control-flow change is more fundamental—Junio’s feedback suggests splitting the refactoring into two steps: first, extract the helper without altering the surrounding logic, then introduce the `if/else if/else` cascade in the feature patch (3/5). This keeps the series bisectable and makes each patch’s intent clearer. The respin should be straightforward, and the series remains on track for `next` once this patch is updated. The rest of the series (including the `--limited-fetch` flag and shallow-repository defaults) is uncontroversial and ready for review.
