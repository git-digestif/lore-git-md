This is the author’s reply to Junio’s **substantive review** of the first patch in the two-patch bugfix series that addresses the long-standing inconsistency between `git status` and `git diff`/`git add` when line-ending conversions are active.

Curtis Allen Smith clarifies that the core fix is much simpler than the original patch suggested: a single-line change to `ie_match_stat()` that disables the size-based shortcut whenever `would_convert_to_git()` returns true. This aligns the size branch with the mtime branch’s existing conversion-aware logic. The rest of the original patch—128 lines of new code—was an optimization to avoid unnecessary conversions and hashing when the converted length already mismatches the blob size. He proposes splitting the series: a minimal v2 containing only the one-line fix, followed by the optimization (and the config option in patch 2) as a separate, optional follow-up. Performance measurements show the optimization reduces `git status` time from 507 ms to 3 ms on 200 modified 1 MB files and from 129 ms to 20 ms on 10,000 modified 2.6 KB files under `* text=auto`, with further gains after the first run. The thread is now waiting for Junio’s reaction to the proposed split.
