This is a design discussion reply in the ongoing effort to extend `git stash create` with `--include-untracked` (`-u`) and `--all` (`-a`) options. The author, Kazumasa Shigeta, acknowledges Junio C Hamano’s feedback that `git stash create` should eventually support the same options as `git stash push` where they produce the same stash shape, making the two commands interchangeable for scripting. However, Shigeta proposes limiting the current patch to a minimal subset: `-m/--message`, `-q/--quiet`, `-u/--include-untracked`, and `-a/--all`.

The rationale for this narrower scope is pragmatic: implementing and testing all relevant `stash push` options upfront would require more effort than the author is prepared to invest at this stage. The author explicitly rules out `--patch`, `--staged`, and pathspec support for now, positioning them as follow-up work. This approach preserves backward compatibility (using `PARSE_OPT_STOP_AT_NON_OPTION` for positional message handling) while incrementally expanding `git stash create`’s capabilities. The patch remains under active design discussion, with no merge status reported. The next step is likely a revised patch or further feedback from Junio on whether this subset is sufficient.
