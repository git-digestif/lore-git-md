This is a **CI/build system** follow-up to the earlier fix for the Debian 12 CI flakiness in `t5559.15` and `t5559.16`. Johannes Schindelin acknowledges a quoting bug in the `HAVE_CURL_HTTP2_BUG` test prerequisite that Jeff King identified and fixed. The bug caused the version check to fail silently, allowing the flaky tests to run even on affected curl versions (7.88.0–8.3.0).

The fix itself is straightforward: Jeff’s patch replaces the outer double-quotes with single-quotes in the prereq snippet and escapes the inner single-quotes in the `awk` script, ensuring the `$1` and `$2` variables are passed literally to `awk`. The change is minimal—only four lines in `t/t5551-http-fetch-smart.sh`—and does not alter the logic or scope of the workaround.

Johannes also suggests an alternative approach: moving the version check into a separate shell function (`curl_has_http2_bug`) and referencing it in the prereq. While this would improve readability, it’s not part of the current patch, and the existing fix is sufficient. The discussion about AI involvement is an amusing aside but doesn’t affect the patch’s correctness.

The patch is uncontroversial and ready for integration. It addresses a latent bug that could have undermined the original CI workaround, though its impact was limited to environments with specific curl versions. The commit message clearly explains the issue and the fix, making it easy for future maintainers to understand. No further changes are expected.
