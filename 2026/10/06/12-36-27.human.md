This is the latest exchange in the **incremental connectivity check** patch series, a performance feature that introduces an opt-in mode (`transfer.connectivityCheck=incremental`) to speed up object connectivity verification during fetches and pushes. The series is now in its second iteration (v2), with a third version (v3) planned to address remaining feedback.

The email clarifies a key technical point about how the incremental verifier establishes its trust boundary. The discussion confirms that the incremental mode still relies on `git rev-list --not --all` to identify preexisting ref tips (the "boundary" commits), just like the full connectivity check. The savings come from processing incoming commits in reverse topological order and verifying each commit’s tree only against its parents’ trusted trees, rather than walking the entire object graph. This inductive approach ensures correctness while avoiding the full tree walk of boundary commits, which is where the performance gains (up to 190x faster for small pushes to large repos) come from.
