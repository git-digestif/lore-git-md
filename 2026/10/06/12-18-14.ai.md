This is a reply in the ongoing ODB refactoring series to move alternates handling into the files backend. The author, Patrick Steinhardt, responds to Karthik Nayak’s surface-level review of patch 1/13, which requested clearer motivation for shifting packfile path resolution from `fill_oids_from_packs()` to its caller in `builtin/commit-graph.c`.

Patrick clarifies that the change is preparatory: the current code embeds assumptions in `commit-graph.c` about packfile locations relative to an ODB source, and these assumptions become harder to maintain in the next patch (2/13), which decouples the commit-graph subsystem from `struct odb_source`. By resolving paths early, the patch removes one dependency on the ODB source structure, aligning with the series’ broader goal of making the ODB more backend-agnostic. The explanation is concise and directly addresses the reviewer’s concern, though it remains focused on the immediate technical step rather than the full architectural vision. No new code or behavioral changes are introduced in this reply.
