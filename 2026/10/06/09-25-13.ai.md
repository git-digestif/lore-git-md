This is a follow-up in the ongoing design discussion for extending `git stash create` with `--include-untracked` (`-u`) and `--all` (`-a`) options. The email is a reply from the author, Kazumasa Shigeta, to Phillip Wood’s substantive review of the proposed directions for exposing more of `do_create_stash()`’s functionality.

Shigeta acknowledges Phillip’s feedback on the three potential design directions (extending `stash create`, adding a new subcommand, or adding `--create-only` to `stash push`). He agrees that `--patch` is not a good fit for `stash create` because the command is script-focused and `--patch` can leave the worktree in an inconsistent state. He also agrees that positional pathspec support is unnecessary, proposing `--pathspec-from-file` as a sufficient alternative for scripts. On backward compatibility, he confirms that `PARSE_OPT_STOP_AT_NON_OPTION` is a reasonable way to preserve the existing positional-message grammar, noting that messages starting with `-` are rare in practice. He also endorses Phillip’s suggestion to add `-m`/`--message` for consistency with other Git commands.

The email does not introduce new technical details or commit to a specific direction, but it narrows the scope of the discussion by ruling out `--patch` and positional pathspecs. The thread remains in active design review, with no merge status reported. The next step is likely a revised patch or further discussion based on the feedback, particularly around the subset of options to expose and the preferred design direction. Junio C Hamano’s earlier preference for `--create-only` in `stash push` is still on the table, but this email focuses on refining the `stash create` extension approach.
