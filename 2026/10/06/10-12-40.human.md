This is a **documentation patch update** in Kristofer Karlsson’s two-part series adding an incremental connectivity check for fetch and push. The series aims to reduce the cost of verifying object connectivity by processing incoming commits in topological order, skipping unchanged subtrees.

Kristofer responds to Patrick Steinhardt’s substantive review of the new `Documentation/technical/connectivity-check.adoc` file. He agrees to clarify the connectivity invariant by explicitly stating that unreachable objects cannot be assumed to be connected, even if they exist in the object database. He also plans to remove direct code references (like `check_connected()` and `connected.c`) to avoid staleness and will rework or remove the three-phase breakdown of the full connectivity check, which Patrick found redundant. The discussion about non-commit tips (tags, trees, blobs) and their handling will also be revised for clarity.

The patch is part of a well-motivated performance feature under active review. Kristofer’s willingness to address feedback suggests the documentation will improve in precision and maintainability. This update doesn’t introduce new technical concerns but refines the existing explanation, making it a useful step toward merging. The series remains a strong candidate for attention, especially for those interested in large-repository scalability.
