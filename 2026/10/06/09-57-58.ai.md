This is a substantive design discussion in the ongoing thread about extending `git stash create` with `--include-untracked` (`-u`) and `--all` (`-a`) options. The email is a back-and-forth between Phillip Wood and Kazumasa Shigeta, refining the scope of the proposed changes.

Phillip clarifies his earlier feedback, agreeing with Shigeta that `--patch` is not a good fit for `git stash create` because the command is script-focused and does not modify the worktree. He reinforces that `--patch` (and by extension, interactive options) could leave scripts in an inconsistent state if they later try to remove stashed changes. Phillip also endorses Shigeta’s proposal to use `--pathspec-from-file` instead of positional pathspecs, noting that `stash create` has existed for 20 years without pathspec support and that `--pathspec-from-file` is sufficient for script use cases. He reaffirms that `PARSE_OPT_STOP_AT_NON_OPTION` is a reasonable way to preserve backward compatibility with the existing positional-message grammar, as messages starting with `-` are rare in practice.

The discussion narrows the scope of the proposed changes: `--patch` and positional pathspecs are ruled out, while `--pathspec-from-file` and `-m`/`--message` are endorsed for consistency with other Git commands.
