This thread is about replacing Git’s outdated `gittutorial` man page with a modern, beginner-focused two-part guide authored by Julia Evans. The new tutorial, tested with 112 users, covers local repository creation and commits in Part 1 and pushing to a remote host (GitHub/GitLab) in Part 2. The goal is to provide a practical introduction that reflects current workflows while avoiding confusion about Git’s decentralized model.

The series is now at v2, with Julia addressing all surface-level feedback from Junio C Hamano and D. Ben Knoble, including AsciiDoc formatting, editorial tweaks, and clarifications on platform assumptions and accessibility. The only remaining issues are minor: two AsciiDoc formatting warnings (missing blank lines before section delimiters) reported by Junio in the latest email, which are purely mechanical and will likely be fixed in a trivial v3 update. Julia has also noted plans for post-merge iteration based on user feedback, particularly for branching and collaboration content. The thread remains uncontroversial, with all participants treating the changes as a clear improvement over the outdated tutorial.
