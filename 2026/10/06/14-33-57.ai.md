This is a thread update from the author, Derrick Stolee, acknowledging the substantive feedback from Jeff King (Peff) and Phillip Wood and announcing that he is setting the RFC series aside for now.

Stolee thanks Peff and Phillip for their insightful critiques, which he describes as requiring a fundamental rethink of the approach. He explicitly states that he does not currently have time to pursue Peff’s alternative design (fixed-size, stack-allocated buffers with a sticky error bit) or to address the architectural concerns raised. The email closes the loop on the RFC: the series will not be revised or resubmitted in the near term, though Stolee leaves open the possibility of revisiting the topic in the future.

The tone is gracious and matter-of-fact, treating the feedback as constructive rather than dismissive. There is no pushback or attempt to defend the original design; Stolee simply accepts that the current approach is not viable and steps away. This leaves the broader question of how to make `strbuf` operations safe for critical code paths (like trace2) unresolved, but the thread is now dormant from the author’s side. Future discussion would likely need to start from Peff’s alternative proposal or another new design.
