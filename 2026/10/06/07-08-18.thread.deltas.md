## 2026/11/20

- [2026/11/20/19-02-46 by Aniket Gupta <aniketgupta3001@gmail.com>] Aniket Gupta reported that using `--cherry-mark` makes `git status` slower when branches have diverged because every commit on both sides needs a patch id (e.g., with 3000 commits on both sides status goes from 25ms to 150ms, and with 10000 from 60ms to 500ms), and suggested counting without `--cherry-mark` first and only running the patch id pass when the two counts are small.
- [2026/11/20/19-35-04 by Harald Nordgren <haraldnordgren@gmail.com>] Harald Nordgren acknowledged the performance regression and raised a concern that the suggested approach might produce a weird user experience if people do not understand why the check is suddenly gone.

## 2026/11/21

- [2026/11/21/06-21-20 by Aniket Gupta <aniketgupta3001@gmail.com>] Aniket Gupta agreed with a concern about the check's cost but explained that the extra cost only comes from commits outside the upstream, so a normal rebased branch should be fine and it only gets slow with thousands of commits, making it probably acceptable as is.
