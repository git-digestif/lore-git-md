This is the fourth installment in Taylor Blau’s seven-part series summarizing the Git Contributor’s Summit 2026. It covers the **Outreachy sponsorship** discussion, a short but action-oriented exchange about funding internships.

The core issue is straightforward: the Git project wants to sponsor three Outreachy interns for the December 2026 round at $10 k each, but past corporate sponsors (GitLab and GitHub) declined last year, leaving the Git project itself to cover the cost. The session focused on identifying new sponsors or re-engaging old ones. Christian Couder suggested reaching out to GitLab’s “contributor success” contact; Emily Shaffer volunteered to ask Google’s Open Source Programs Office (OSPO), though she was pessimistic about the outcome. No other companies were named as potential sponsors during the session.

The discussion also touched briefly on **Git Merge 2027**, noting that an early location announcement (Canada was mentioned) would help Outreachy and GSoC interns plan travel. This is a minor procedural point rather than a funding question.

No concrete commitments or proposals emerged; the thread is essentially a call for follow-up outreach. The tone is pragmatic rather than contentious, and the next steps depend on whether any company responds positively.
