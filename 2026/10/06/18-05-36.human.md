This is a summary of the Git Contributor's Summit 2026 notes, posted by Taylor Blau. It serves as a high-level digest of the discussions held at the summit, covering seven key topics that were addressed in separate follow-up replies (not included in this email). The notes are shared to keep the broader community informed and to invite further discussion on the mailing list.

The topics span a mix of process, technical direction, and community concerns: the security mailing list and process, plans for Git 3.0, documentation improvements, Outreachy sponsorship, the future of pluggable object database (ODB) backends, the project's policy on AI-generated contributions, and adoption of Protocol v2 for pushes. The notes are lightly edited for clarity but remain discussion-oriented rather than prescriptive.

This email is not a patch or RFC, but a community update. If you are interested in any of the topics, the linked Google Doc provides more detail, and each topic has its own thread for focused follow-up. The notes reflect the state of discussion as of the summit; no decisions are final until they are debated and resolved on-list.
