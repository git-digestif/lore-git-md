This is a withdrawal notice from Derrick Stolee for his `ds/trace2-tolerate-failed-timestamp` series. The patch set, originally sent in late August 2026, aimed to harden the trace2 API by banning functions like `xstrfmt()` and `xcalloc()` that could call `die()` on allocation failure, replacing them with safer alternatives. The series also introduced a new header, `banned-die.h`, to enforce these restrictions.

Stolee now considers the effort not worth the complexity it introduces. His message suggests that the protections offered by `banned-die.h` are weaker than they appear, and the series does not deliver enough value to justify its presence. The topic is currently marked as "Needs review" in Junio's "What's cooking" report, but Stolee explicitly requests its removal from consideration. No other participants have weighed in on this withdrawal.
