This thread is about a small but user-visible change to Git’s merge conflict advice. Julia Evans proposed updating the message shown during merge conflicts to suggest `git merge --continue` instead of `git commit`, bringing it in line with the advice already given for rebase, revert, and cherry-pick. The patch is a single change to `wt-status.c` and updates two test scripts to reflect the new wording.

The patch is under review and has received positive feedback. Phillip Wood reviewed it first, calling it a "sensible improvement" but suggesting a minor grammatical tweak to the advice text ("to conclude the merge" instead of "to conclude merge"). Junio C Hamano later reviewed the patch and endorsed it, noting that the change aligns the merge advice with the more specific instructions already in place for other conflict-resolution workflows. No technical objections have been raised, and the only remaining action is to incorporate Phillip’s grammatical suggestion.

The patch is ready to move forward once that small adjustment is made. No other open questions or concerns have been identified.
