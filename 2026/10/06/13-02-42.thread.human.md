This thread is about a small but user-visible improvement to Git’s merge conflict advice. Julia Evans proposed a patch to make the advice message during merge conflicts consistent with other conflict-resolution workflows (rebase, revert, cherry-pick) by suggesting `git merge --continue` instead of `git commit`. The change is uncontroversial and touches only the advice text in `wt-status.c`, with updated tests to match.

Phillip Wood reviewed the patch and endorsed the goal, calling it a "sensible improvement." His only feedback was a minor grammatical tweak to the advice text—changing *"to conclude merge"* to *"to conclude the merge"*—which aligns better with Git’s existing phrasing conventions. He also helpfully cited the commit that originally introduced `git merge --continue` as a reference. No objections or technical concerns were raised, and the patch is likely to proceed to a second version incorporating Phillip’s suggestion. The thread remains straightforward, with no open questions or broader implications for Git’s behavior.
