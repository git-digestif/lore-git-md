This is a review of Devi Srinivas Vasamsetti’s clock-skew warning patch.
Junio C Hamano raises fundamental questions about the feature’s usefulness and
limitations. He points out that the warning cannot address the root problem —
clocks on other machines may already be wrong — and asks what the user is
supposed to do when they encounter the warning (wait, ask for a force-push,
or something else). He also notes that the warning only triggers for the
current commit, so it will miss skew in existing history, and that even if
the warning were emitted, date-based traversal (`git log --since`) remains
unreliable because you cannot know which timestamps are correct. His closing
paragraph advises users to avoid date-based options entirely if they need
reliable results, suggesting that the warning may not be worth the added
complexity. The review is substantive, challenging the patch’s premise rather
than its implementation.
