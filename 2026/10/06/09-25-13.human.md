This is a follow-up in the ongoing design discussion about extending `git stash create` to support `--include-untracked` (`-u`) and `--all` (`-a`) options. The thread remains focused on deciding which subset of `git stash push` options should be exposed in a script-focused command and how to preserve backward compatibility.

Kazumasa Shigeta responds to Phillip Wood’s feedback, agreeing that `--patch` is likely unnecessary for `stash create` since the command doesn’t modify the worktree, and that `--pathspec-from-file` is sufficient for pathspec support, avoiding the need for positional arguments. He also confirms that `PARSE_OPT_STOP_AT_NON_OPTION` is a reasonable way to handle message parsing while preserving the existing positional message grammar, and that adding `-m`/`--message` for consistency with other Git commands makes sense. These points narrow the scope of the proposed extension to `-u`, `-a`, and `-m`, with pathspecs limited to `--pathspec-from-file`.

The discussion is now converging on a more concrete design for the first of the three proposed directions (extending `stash create`), but the broader question of whether to extend `create`, add a new subcommand, or introduce `--create-only` to `stash push` remains unresolved. Junio C Hamano’s earlier preference for `--create-only` as the "safest" approach still looms over the thread, but no new input from him appears here. The next step will likely be a revised patch or further discussion clarifying which direction the project prefers. For now, this email advances the design by ruling out interactive options and full pathspec support for `stash create`.
