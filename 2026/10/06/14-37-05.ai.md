The series aims to eliminate `die()`-triggering helpers (e.g., `xsnprintf()`, `xstrdup()`, `ALLOC_ARRAY()`, `xstrfmt()`, `ALLOC_GROW()`, `xcalloc()`) from trace2 to prevent Git crashes during telemetry operations, especially under memory pressure or system call failures.
