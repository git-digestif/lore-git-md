Christian Couder responds to Antonin Delpeuch’s plan to draft a governance document, endorsing the approach but clarifying how the Git project’s review process works. His tone is supportive and pragmatic, emphasizing trust in the community’s review process. No objections to the overall goal are raised, and no new technical or structural details about the document’s content are proposed.
