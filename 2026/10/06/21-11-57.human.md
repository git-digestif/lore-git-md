This is a short follow-up in the ongoing review of Karthik Nayak’s performance patch for the packed-refs backend. The patch, now in its third iteration, aims to speed up operations that rewrite the packed-refs file by avoiding redundant formatting of unchanged references.

Karthik responds to Patrick Steinhardt’s substantive review of v3, agreeing to clarify the commit message and correct the misleading documentation for the `record_start` field in `struct packed_ref_iterator`. The discussion confirms that `pos` and `record_start` advance independently, making the original comment inaccurate. Karthik also accepts Patrick’s suggestion to inline the trivial `write_packed_entry_raw()` wrapper around `fwrite()`. No new technical concerns are raised, and the 20% speedup in reference deletion benchmarks remains the key motivation.

The patch is still under review, with no maintainer decision yet. The next step is a v4 addressing the agreed-upon nits, though Patrick’s feedback did not demand a reroll, implying these are minor enough to be handled during integration. The thread remains focused on implementation clarity rather than revisiting the patch’s core approach or its documented side effects (preserving original formatting, including malformed refnames and peeled OIDs for broken refs). If you care about packed-refs performance or the ref backend’s evolution, this patch is worth tracking—it looks well-motivated and nearly ready.
