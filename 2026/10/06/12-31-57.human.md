This is a performance optimization patch for the packed-refs backend, now receiving a substantive review from Patrick Steinhardt. The patch aims to speed up operations that rewrite the packed-refs file (like deleting references) by avoiding redundant formatting of unchanged refs, achieving a ~20% speedup in benchmarks.

Patrick’s review is mostly positive, calling the 20% improvement "nothing to scoff at" and expressing satisfaction with the change overall. However, he raises two technical nits: first, the commit message could better explain the problem with the current `fprintf()`-based approach before jumping to the solution; second, the comment for the new `record_start` field in `struct packed_ref_iterator` is misleading because it implies `pos` and `record_start` are always equal, when in fact they advance independently. He also notes that the new `write_packed_entry_raw()` helper is a trivial wrapper around `fwrite()` and could arguably be inlined.

The patch remains under review, with no maintainer decision yet. Karthik may choose to address Patrick’s feedback in a follow-up version, though Patrick explicitly said the nits aren’t worth a reroll on their own. The core optimization is uncontroversial and well-motivated by the benchmark results.
