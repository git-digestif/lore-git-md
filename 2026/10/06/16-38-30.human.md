This is a **documentation discussion** in the final stages of the `gitbreaking-changes(7)` manpage RFC series. Kristoffer Haugsbakk posts the formal resolution to the last open question: how to format message-ID references in the new manpage.

The email confirms the consensus reached in the thread: plain URLs (e.g., `https://lore.kernel.org/git/xmqqa59i45wc.fsf@gitster.g/`) will be used instead of the previously debated hybrid URL+message-ID format. Junio C Hamano had proposed the hybrid format for usability and future-proofing, but testing revealed that Asciidoctor renders the message-ID portion as a broken `mailto:` link in HTML output, making the link unusable. Junio conceded that functional, recognizable links take precedence over preserving raw message-IDs, and Kristoffer now accepts this outcome.

Kristoffer also floats a compromise—using footnotes to preserve the original message-IDs for email-only users—but this is presented as a suggestion rather than a demand. The tone suggests he is content with the plain-URL approach and is merely exploring ways to retain the message-IDs for niche use cases. The discussion is now fully resolved, and the series is ready for a v2 that drops the RFC tag and incorporates the plain-URL formatting. No new files or technical changes are introduced; this is purely about documentation presentation in `Documentation/gitbreaking-changes.adoc`.

The broader goal of the series—making Git’s breaking changes more discoverable via a manpage—remains uncontroversial and on track. The next step is for Kristoffer to post the v2, which should be a straightforward update. The series is well-motivated, thoroughly reviewed, and addresses a real usability gap. It looks like a solid contribution that will likely land soon.
