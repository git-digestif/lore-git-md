This is a withdrawal notice for a refactoring series targeting the trace2 subsystem. The author, Derrick Stolee, requests that `ds/trace2-tolerate-failed-timestamp` (7 patches) be dropped from the integration queue. The series aimed to eliminate calls to `die()`-prone helpers (`xstrfmt`, `xcalloc`, `ALLOC_GROW`, `ALLOC_ARRAY`, `xstrdup`) in trace2 code to avoid process termination and recursion during memory allocation failures, introducing a `banned-die.h` header to enforce the restriction. Stolee now considers the trade-off unfavorable: the added complexity (and potential false sense of security from the header) outweighs the benefits. No replacement or follow-up is proposed; the topic is abandoned.
