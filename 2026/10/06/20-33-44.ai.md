This is the maintainer's final word on the message-ID formatting question in the `gitbreaking-changes(7)` manpage series. Junio C Hamano rejects Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s footnote-based compromise, reiterating that raw message-IDs in footnotes add no value over plain URLs. He restates the consensus: the primary link text will be plain URLs (e.g., `https://lore.kernel.org/git/xmqqa59i45wc.fsf@gitster.g/`), which are clickable in terminals and browsers and require only minimal effort from email-only users to extract the message-ID if needed. The thread is now fully resolved; the only remaining step is for Kristoffer to post a v2 of the series incorporating this final decision.

The series itself remains a documentation-only effort to convert the existing `BreakingChanges` document into a proper manpage (`gitbreaking-changes(7)`), making Git’s upcoming breaking changes more discoverable via `git help` and `man`. All review feedback has been addressed, and the series is technically aligned and ready for integration once v2 is posted. Files touched are `Documentation/gitbreaking-changes.adoc`, `Documentation/git.txt`, `command-list.txt`, `Makefile`, and `meson.build`; no new symbols, CLI options, or on-disk formats are involved. The broader goal—improving user visibility of breaking changes—is uncontroversial.
