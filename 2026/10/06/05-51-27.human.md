This is a review of Mark C. Chu-Carroll’s v2 patch adding revision-based filtering to `git repo structure`. The review is substantive and tested: Patrick Steinhardt engages deeply with the implementation, identifies specific edge cases, and suggests concrete improvements to both the code and documentation.

Patrick’s feedback focuses on three areas. First, he critiques the documentation: the synopsis should use `[<revs>...]` instead of `[<include|^exclude>...]` to match Git’s established terminology, and the explanatory text should be streamlined to avoid introducing new jargon (“query clauses” → “revisions”). Second, he questions the `setup_revision_opt` defaults: passing `.def = "HEAD"` and `.revarg_opt = REVARG_COMMITTISH` may be too restrictive, since users might want to filter by trees or blobs; he suggests passing `NULL` instead. Third, he proposes additional test coverage for non-commit objects (trees, blobs) and revision options like `--filter=object:type=blob`, and recommends using `--format=lines` to reduce test boilerplate.

The review is clearly positive on the overall design—Patrick’s earlier proposal for revision-based filtering is now implemented—but he flags several implementation details that need attention before the patch can be considered ready. No new objections to the core concept have surfaced; the discussion remains focused on polishing the execution.
