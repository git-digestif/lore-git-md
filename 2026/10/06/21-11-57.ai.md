This is a short follow-up in the ongoing review of Karthik Nayak’s performance optimization for the packed-refs backend. The patch, now in its third iteration, aims to speed up operations that rewrite the packed-refs file by avoiding redundant formatting of unchanged refs.

Karthik acknowledges Patrick Steinhardt’s feedback on two minor but clear issues: the commit message’s lack of explicit problem statement and the misleading documentation for the `record_start` field in `struct packed_ref_iterator`. He agrees to amend the commit message and correct the comment, and also accepts Patrick’s suggestion to inline the trivial `write_packed_entry_raw()` wrapper around `fwrite()`. No new technical concerns are raised, and the 20% speedup benchmark remains uncontested.

The discussion remains constructive and focused on clarity rather than structural changes. Karthik plans to send a new version once the field documentation is sorted out, but the patch’s core approach—using `fwrite()` for unchanged refs to preserve original formatting—is not in question. The patch is still under review, with no maintainer decision yet.
