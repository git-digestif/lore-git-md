This is a documentation-focused reply in the ongoing `gitbreaking-changes(7)` manpage series. Junio C Hamano responds to Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s proposal to use footnotes for preserving raw message-IDs alongside plain URLs in the new manpage. The thread is now fully aligned on the series’ goals and implementation, with all review feedback resolved.

Junio rejects the footnote idea as unnecessary and visually cluttering. His response reinforces the consensus reached earlier: plain URLs (e.g., `https://lore.kernel.org/git/xmqqa59i45wc.fsf@gitster.g/`) will be the primary format for message-ID references, avoiding the HTML rendering issues that derailed the hybrid URL+message-ID approach.
