This is a thread closure notice for the recently merged bugfix patch that stops `git ls-files` and `git add` from incorrectly stripping the common prefix from negative (exclusion) pathspecs. The author, Diogo Castro, confirms that the patch has been incorporated into Junio C Hamano’s tree and closes the associated GitHub pull request.

The fix itself is now in `master`: it leaves negative pathspecs untouched by the positive-path prefix optimization, ensuring they retain their intended semantics and preventing edge cases where they could degenerate into empty strings or trigger out-of-bounds reads. Three new tests in `t/t6132-pathspec-exclude.sh` cover the regression scenarios. The discussion about potential future optimizations for cases with many negative patterns is acknowledged but explicitly deferred as a separate effort.
