This is a **substantive follow-up** in the ongoing debate over the data structure for `extra_roots` in patch 2/8 of Taylor Blau’s MIDX reachability-closure series. Jeff King (Peff) weighs in on the `oid_array` vs. `oidset` trade-off, siding with Elijah Newren’s argument that preserving pack-order correlation (root trees before subtrees) is valuable for delta compression quality, even if the solution is not perfect. Peff acknowledges Taylor’s point about memory efficiency but notes that the `oidset`’s hash table overhead could make it less efficient in practice if duplicates are rare. He also questions whether cruft packs—where objects are often thrown in without careful ordering—would benefit from this optimization, leaving the door open for further refinement. The discussion remains unresolved, with no hybrid approach proposed yet, but Peff’s feedback reinforces the functional importance of preserving traversal order while acknowledging the memory trade-offs. The next step is likely a revised patch or further discussion balancing these concerns.
