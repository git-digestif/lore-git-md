This is a follow-up in the abandoned `git stash pop` custom conflict-label thread. Junio C Hamano responds to Harald Nordgren’s suggestion of "hiding" the `--label-*` options (keeping them functional but undocumented) by proposing a longer-term architectural improvement: rewriting the in-tree caller (the sequencer) to invoke `git stash apply` functionality via a direct function call rather than spawning a subprocess. This would eliminate the technical constraint that currently requires the options to remain exposed in `stash apply`, allowing them to be removed from the user interface entirely. Junio suggests that labeling the options as "for internal use only" (with a warning that they may disappear) could be a reasonable short-term compromise while the larger refactoring is pursued. The email does not resolve the design question but shifts focus toward a potential future cleanup of the sequencer’s interaction with the stash machinery.
