This is patch 9/13 in Patrick Steinhardt’s ODB refactoring series. It simplifies the tmp-objdir API by merging the two-step quarantine setup (`tmp_objdir_create` + `tmp_objdir_replace_primary_odb`) into a single call.

The patch removes the separate `tmp_objdir_replace_primary_odb` function and folds its logic into `tmp_objdir_create`, which now takes an additional `will_destroy` flag. Callers in `log-tree.c` and `object-file.c` are updated to pass the flag at creation time instead of making a second call. The internal `tmp_objdir` struct replaces its `prev_source` field with `orig_dir` (the original primary directory) and `temp_dir` (the new quarantine directory), and the quarantine directory is inserted at the head of the files backend’s linked list immediately on creation.

The change is mechanical and reduces boilerplate for users of the tmp-objdir subsystem. It also clarifies the lifecycle: the quarantine directory is now active as soon as `tmp_objdir_create` returns, and the `will_destroy` flag is set once at creation rather than being passed separately. The patch touches four files, removing 49 lines while adding 27, and is a straightforward cleanup that aligns with the series’ goal of consolidating alternates handling.
