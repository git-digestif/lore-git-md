The exchange sharpens the disagreement over sequencing. No new patches or technical proposals emerge here—this is purely a scoping discussion about how to approach the rewrite. The tone remains collaborative, but the gap between their positions is now clearer: Julia sees the old tutorial as ineffective and prefers incremental progress, while Junio is open to preserving it if it can be made useful with minimal effort.
