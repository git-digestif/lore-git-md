This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about whether to include a helper script for incremental rebase conflict resolution in Git’s `contrib/` area. Patrick Steinhardt responds to Alejandro Colomar’s query about distributing `git-rebase-walk` separately under the `git-` namespace.

Patrick’s position is pragmatic: while he acknowledges that the existence of multiple tools (like `git-imerge` and Colomar’s script) suggests the workflow is broadly useful, he believes the functionality would be better integrated directly into `git rebase` itself rather than as a standalone script. He notes that this would be a more involved effort, implying he does not expect Colomar to undertake it unless motivated to do so. On the question of distributing the script separately under the `git-` namespace, Patrick clarifies that this is the project’s standard way of extending Git (via third-party `git-*` commands) and does not require explicit permission. However, he sidesteps the question of whether it makes sense to package the script as part of `manpages-utils`, deferring that decision to distributors.

The key takeaway is that Patrick does not oppose the script’s existence or its use of the `git-` namespace, but he stops short of endorsing its inclusion in `contrib/`. His preference for integrating the functionality into `git rebase` itself suggests he sees the current proposal as a stopgap rather than a long-term solution. The thread remains open on whether the project will accept *any* helper script for this workflow, but Patrick’s response reinforces the earlier consensus that `git-imerge` is the more mature (if unmaintained) solution. No new technical objections or alternatives are raised here; the discussion remains focused on project scope and distribution.
