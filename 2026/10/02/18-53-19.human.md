This is a documentation patch thread, part of Julia Evans’ ongoing effort to introduce `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page consolidating merge conflict resolution guidance. The email is a brief follow-up from Julia to Junio C Hamano, acknowledging his surface-level feedback on the proposed "WHAT IS A MERGE CONFLICT?" section for the new guide.

Julia accepts three of Junio’s four suggested wording refinements: replacing "unstaged" with "marks them as conflicted," "it can easily combine them" with "since there’s no overlap," and "of `hello.py`" with "of the same file." However, she pushes back on Junio’s suggestion to replace "will not try to guess" with "cannot guess," arguing that the former better reflects Git’s intentional conservatism in conflict resolution. She contrasts Git’s behavior with more aggressive syntax-aware merge tools like `mergiraf`, which have occasionally produced incorrect merges, to justify the phrasing.
