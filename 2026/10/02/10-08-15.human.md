This prepares the ground for later patches that will enable multiple directories per backend. The patch touches five files (`builtin/count-objects.c`, `builtin/submodule--helper.c`, `diagnose.c`, `odb.c`, and `odb.h`), updating callers to use the new type and its `abspath` field instead of the old `path` field.

No behavior changes are introduced—this is purely a refactoring step. The commit message is clear and concise, and the patch itself is straightforward, with no obvious red flags.
