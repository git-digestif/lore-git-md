This is the final review round for Andrew Pleeter’s four-patch series extending `git var` to provide a unified, scriptable interface for Git’s identity and signing configuration. The series is now in its ninth iteration and has been split into focused, buildable steps as requested by Junio and Phillip Wood.

Phillip’s latest email confirms the patches look good to him. He left minor comments on the test organization in patches 3 and 4 (multi-variable support and the new identity variables), but does not consider them significant enough to warrant a re-roll. The only open question is whether to add a trailing delimiter in `-z` output mode for multi-valued variables, which Phillip suggested but Andrew is open to revisiting. This is not a blocker for integration.

The series is technically complete and addresses all prior feedback. It introduces support for multiple variables, NUL-terminated output (`-z`), and broken-out identity components (`GIT_AUTHOR_NAME`, `GIT_AUTHOR_EMAIL`, `GIT_AUTHOR_DATE`, `GIT_COMMITTER_NAME`, `GIT_COMMITTER_EMAIL`, `GIT_COMMITTER_DATE`). The controversial `GIT_SIGNING_KEY` variable has been removed due to ambiguity across signing mechanisms. Exit-code behavior is now consistent with `GIT_CONFIG_NOSYSTEM` precedent: multi-variable queries exit 0 even if some requested variables are unset, while non-zero codes are reserved for actual errors. The implementation is well-tested, with comprehensive coverage for `-z` output, multi-variable queries, and edge cases.

The patches remain in `seen`; graduation to `next` depends on Junio’s review of the split and the resolution of the trailing delimiter question. The series is ready for integration and appears uncontroversial. If you care about scripting Git’s identity or signing configuration, this is worth tracking.
