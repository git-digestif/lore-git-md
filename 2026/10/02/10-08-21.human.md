This is the tenth patch in Patrick Steinhardt’s 13-patch series refactoring the object database (ODB) to move alternates handling into the files backend. The patch addresses a minor but conceptually important detail: the `will_destroy` flag, which currently lives in the generic `struct odb_source` but is only used by the loose-object backend.

The flag tracks whether an ODB source is part of a transaction that will be discarded, allowing the backend to skip unnecessary work like flushing data to disk. However, as Patrick notes, this assumption is backend-specific and doesn’t belong in the generic ODB layer. The patch moves the flag into `struct odb_source_loose`, where it’s actually used, and updates the quarantine logic in `tmp-objdir.c` to set it directly on the loose source rather than the generic base.

The change is small (4 files, 9 lines removed) and mechanical, but it’s a clean-up that aligns with the series’ broader goal of making the ODB more modular. It doesn’t introduce new behavior or touch user-facing code, but it removes a leaky abstraction that could complicate future backend work. If you’re following the series for its architectural implications, this patch is a good example of the kind of tidying that makes the larger refactor possible. For most readers, it’s safe to skim—this is a detail that matters more to ODB internals than to everyday Git usage.
