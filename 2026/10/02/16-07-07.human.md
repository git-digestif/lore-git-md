This is a documentation patch from Julia Evans that removes empty SYNOPSIS sections from Git's section 7 man pages (conceptual guides and tutorials). The patch updates the `lint-man-section-order.perl` script to exempt section 7 pages from the SYNOPSIS requirement, then strips the now-redundant SYNOPSIS headers from 11 files like `gitcli.adoc` and `gitglossary.adoc`.

The change is motivated by the observation that section 7 man pages (miscellaneous documentation) don’t follow the same conventions as command reference pages (sections 1 and 5), where a SYNOPSIS is standard. Julia tested the patch with `make lint-docs` and a small fish script to verify the removals, and the Perl modification appears correct despite her self-described rusty Perl skills.

The patch is small, self-contained, and well-explained. It touches only documentation files and a single linter script, so it carries no risk of behavioral regression. The motivation aligns with the ongoing effort to standardize man-page formatting, and the change itself is uncontroversial. Worth a quick look for anyone who cares about documentation consistency, but unlikely to spark debate.
