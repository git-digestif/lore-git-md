This is a substantive reply in the ongoing `contrib/` helper thread for incremental rebase conflict resolution. Nico Williams endorses Simon Richter’s conceptual proposal for a bisection-based rebase helper (`bisect-rebase` or `rebase-bisect`) and clarifies that the thread has already converged on this approach—his own `bisect-rebase.sh` and Alejandro Colomar’s `git-bisect-rebase` both implement it. He highlights the practical value of the bisection strategy, noting that he has successfully used his script to rebase across thousands of upstream commits, calling it "very well" in his experience. The reply reinforces the thread’s shift away from the original linear `git-rebase-walk` toward bisection-based scripts, with `git-bisect-rebase` now the leading candidate for `contrib/` inclusion. Nico’s endorsement adds weight to Simon’s proposal, though he does not engage with the optional test-execution feature (`-x 'make check'`), leaving that aspect of the design still open for discussion. The tone is constructive and aligns with the thread’s trajectory toward a minimal, performant shell script.
