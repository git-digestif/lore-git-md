This is the final note in the `git var` extension series, confirming the patches are ready for integration. Andrew Pleeter acknowledges Phillip Wood’s **surface-level review feedback** on test style (replacing `test_env()` with simpler shell constructs and consolidating per-component tests) and agrees to fold those changes into a future cleanup if the series is merged as-is. He also reiterates that dropping `GIT_SIGNING_KEY` was the correct decision, resolving the last open design question.

The series itself remains **technically complete** and **reviewed**, with all substantive concerns addressed in v9. It extends `git var` to provide scriptable access to Git’s identity and signing configuration, introducing six new variables (`GIT_AUTHOR_NAME`, `GIT_AUTHOR_EMAIL`, `GIT_AUTHOR_DATE`, `GIT_COMMITTER_NAME`, `GIT_COMMITTER_EMAIL`, `GIT_COMMITTER_DATE`) and supporting multi-variable queries with `-z` output. The patches are split into four focused commits, touch only `builtin/var.c`, `Documentation/git-var.adoc`, and `t/t0007-git-var.sh`, and maintain backward compatibility. Junio’s review is the only remaining step before graduation to `next`. This is a well-motivated, thoroughly vetted contribution that fills a clear gap in Git’s plumbing.
