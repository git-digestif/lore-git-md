This is a follow-up in the ongoing RFC thread about adding an `includeIf.hostname:<pattern>` condition to Git’s configuration system. Isabella Caselli, the original proposer, responds to Junio C Hamano’s comment that Ignacio Encinas’ 2024 hostname-normalization series was nearly complete before stalling. She expresses enthusiasm about picking up where Ignacio left off, plans to incorporate Jeff King’s suggestion of exposing Git’s hostname interpretation via `git var GIT_HOSTNAME`, and intends to submit an updated patch series based on the 2024 discussion. No new technical questions or objections are raised; the tone remains constructive and forward-looking. The thread is now clearly moving toward implementation, with Isabella positioned to drive the next steps.
