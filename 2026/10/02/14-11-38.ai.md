This is a **surface-level review** of the third patch in Andrew Pleeter’s v9 series extending `git var` to support multi-variable queries. Phillip Wood’s feedback focuses on test style: he notes that `test_env()` is unnecessary in the two test cases where it appears, as the same effect can be achieved with simpler shell constructs (`GIT_CONFIG_GLOBAL= git var ...` or `env GIT_CONFIG_GLOBAL= test_expect_code ...`). The review is purely stylistic and does not identify any functional issues; Phillip explicitly states that the patch is otherwise fine and that these suggestions are not worth a re-roll unless another reason arises.

The patch itself remains technically complete and ready for integration. It implements the core multi-variable functionality for `git var`, allowing multiple variable arguments in a single invocation, printing each as `VARIABLE=value` (or `VARIABLE\nvalue\0` under `-z`), and omitting unset variables without error. The series as a whole is in `seen` and awaiting Junio’s review of v9.
