This is the final exchange in Christian Couder’s five-patch series introducing `uploadpack.lazyFetchTrusted`, a server-side protected configuration variable that lets operators mark repositories as trusted for lazy fetching. The thread is purely about documentation wording: Junio suggested tweaks to the man page phrasing, Christian adopted most of them (“each value of which specifies” instead of “each of which”) but explained why “from which” was not appropriate—lazy fetching happens *for* the repository, not *from* it. All substantive feedback from earlier rounds is now resolved, and the series remains under review with no open technical concerns. The patch itself activates the mechanism by conditionally setting `GIT_NO_LAZY_FETCH=1` only for untrusted repositories, preserving operator control.
