This is a **performance optimization patch** for the packed-refs backend, addressing feedback from Toon Claes’s substantive review. The patch speeds up operations that rewrite the packed-refs file (e.g., deleting references) by avoiding redundant formatting of unchanged refs, instead writing them directly as raw bytes.

Karthik acknowledges Toon’s concerns about side effects—such as preserving original formatting (case, whitespace, malformed refnames, and peeled OIDs for broken refs)—and agrees to document these in the commit message. While these changes are not expected to cause observable issues, they could affect tools parsing packed-refs files (e.g., JGit or libgit2), so transparency is warranted.

On implementation, Karthik accepts Toon’s suggestion to split `write_packed_entry()` into two functions: one for formatted writes (existing behavior) and a new `write_packed_entry_raw()` for raw writes. This separation avoids mixing concerns and clarifies the code’s intent. Karthik also agrees to rename "packfile" to "packed-refs" for consistency, though he defers other stylistic tweaks (e.g., splitting the `if` statement) to avoid distracting from the patch’s core goal.

The patch remains narrowly scoped to `refs/packed-backend.c` and maintains backward compatibility with the on-disk format. The performance benchmark (deleting 100,000 packed refs) shows a ~20% speedup, making this a worthwhile optimization for large repositories. The discussion is now focused on refining the implementation rather than debating the approach, suggesting this patch is likely to land once the minor adjustments are finalized.
