This is a **substantive follow-up review** in the ongoing discussion about patch 2/8 of Taylor Blau’s v2 series fixing MIDX reachability closure corner cases. Jeff King (Peff) raises two technical concerns about the current implementation:

1. **Comment placement**: The explanatory comment about deferring tree/tag roots is located in `read_stdin_packs()`, where the deferred walk actually happens, rather than in `add_object_entry_from_pack()` or `add_loose_object()`, where the deferral decision is made. Peff finds this confusing and suggests moving the comment to the earlier functions, though he acknowledges the challenge of having two separate call sites.

2. **Double `prepare_revision_walk()` call**: The patch calls `prepare_revision_walk()` twice on the same `rev_info` struct—once for the initial commit walk and again for the deferred `extra_roots` walk. Peff questions whether this is safe, noting that while it might work for the limited set of options used here, it could introduce subtle bugs if the struct is reused in unintended ways. He suggests releasing and reinitializing the `rev_info` struct between walks as a more robust alternative.

Neither concern challenges the patch’s core correctness or design, but both are **substantive** implementation details that could affect maintainability or future-proofing. The discussion remains focused on the `oidset` vs. `oid_array` trade-offs from earlier reviews, with Peff’s latest feedback reinforcing the functional importance of preserving traversal order for delta compression quality.
