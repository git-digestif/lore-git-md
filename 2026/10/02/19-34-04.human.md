This is a brief follow-up in the **packfile corruption bugfix thread**, responding to Guillaume Chauvel’s proposal for a deterministic test to replace the original glibc-dependent regression test.

Patrick Steinhardt acknowledges the technical feasibility of Guillaume’s suggestion—manually forcing memory reuse of a `packed_git` struct in a test helper—but pushes back on the cost. He argues that the added infrastructure (a new test helper, enhancements to `test-pack-deltas`, and a new test script) is disproportionate for what he describes as a "very specific edge case." The tone is matter-of-fact, not dismissive, but the message is clear: the complexity of the proposed test does not justify its benefit.

This exchange leaves the test question unresolved. The original series included a regression test that works on some platforms (like Patrick’s NixOS/glibc setup) but not others (Philippe Blain’s WSL), while Guillaume’s follow-up offers a deterministic alternative at the cost of significant new code. Patrick’s reply suggests he would prefer a simpler solution, though none has been proposed yet. The thread remains open on this point, with no substantive review of Guillaume’s patch itself.
