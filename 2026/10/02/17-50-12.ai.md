This is a documentation patch thread, part of Julia Evans’ seven-patch series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page consolidating merge conflict resolution guidance. The email is a surface-level review from Junio C Hamano, the Git maintainer, responding to Julia’s proposed "WHAT IS A MERGE CONFLICT?" section for the new guide. Junio suggests minor wording refinements to improve clarity and precision in the foundational explanation, focusing on directness and readability rather than technical content.

Junio’s feedback targets four specific phrases in Julia’s draft. He proposes replacing "it can easily combine them" with "as there is no overlap" to make the reason for automatic merging explicit. He also suggests rewording "of `hello.py`" to "of the same file" to avoid repetition and improve flow. For the conflict definition, he recommends "cannot guess" over "will not try to guess" for directness. Finally, he critiques the use of "successfully merged" and "unstaged," proposing "merged without conflicts" and "remembers them as conflicted" instead, to better reflect Git’s internal state and avoid misleading implications (e.g., that "unstaged" implies manual intervention).

The review is purely surface-level, addressing phrasing rather than structure or completeness. Junio does not question the section’s overall approach—Julia’s deliberate avoidance of technical jargon like "3-way merge" or "common ancestor"—nor does he suggest adding or removing content. His suggestions are mechanical and aimed at tightening the prose for a man page audience. The feedback is unlikely to be controversial, as it aligns with the series’ goal of user-centric clarity while improving precision. Julia has previously shown receptiveness to such refinements, so these changes are likely to be incorporated into the next revision (v2) without further discussion.
