This is a follow-up in the now-abandoned `git stash pop` custom conflict-label thread. Junio C Hamano responds to Harald Nordgren’s suggestion of "hiding" the `--label-*` options (keeping them functional but undocumented) by proposing a longer-term architectural improvement: rewriting the in-tree caller (the sequencer) to invoke `stash apply` functionality via a direct function call rather than spawning a subprocess. This would eliminate the need for the options to be exposed in the CLI at all, removing the awkwardness of hiding them. Junio suggests that labeling the options as "for internal use only" (with a warning that they may disappear) could be a reasonable short-term compromise while awaiting such a refactor. The tone is pragmatic, acknowledging the technical constraint while keeping the door open for a cleaner solution. No new patch or immediate action is proposed—this is purely a design discussion about how to handle the options going forward.
