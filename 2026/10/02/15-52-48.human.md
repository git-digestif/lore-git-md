This is a brief follow-up from Junio C Hamano in the ongoing RFC thread about embedding SHA-256 tree digests in signed commits and tags. Junio acknowledges Scott Chacon’s concern about the ecosystem impact of switching Git’s default object format to SHA-256 in version 3.0 and the alternative proposal to sign tree digests instead. While Junio has reviewed the implementation and offered minor comments (not detailed in this email), he has not yet formed an opinion on whether the overall approach is sound. The thread remains in the design-discussion phase, with no decisions or merge status recorded. Junio’s tone is neutral, leaving the door open for further feedback before weighing in on the proposal’s viability.
