This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about integrating an incremental rebase conflict resolution helper into Git. Alejandro Colomar responds to Patrick Steinhardt’s earlier email, clarifying his plans and intentions for the `git-rebase-walk` script.

Colomar agrees with Patrick’s suggestion that the functionality would ideally be integrated directly into `git rebase` itself, proposing `--first-conflict` as a potential option name. He expresses interest in pursuing this integration but acknowledges it would take time and likely require assistance. In the meantime, he plans to distribute the script separately under the `git-` namespace (e.g., as part of the `manpages-utils` package), which Patrick confirmed is the project’s standard practice for third-party extensions. Colomar views this as a temporary measure until the functionality can be merged into `git rebase`.

The email does not introduce new technical details or objections but reinforces the thread’s consensus: the script is useful, but its long-term home should be within `git rebase` rather than as a standalone tool. Colomar’s pragmatic approach—providing an immediate solution while working toward a more integrated one—aligns with the project’s incremental development style. The discussion remains open on whether the project will accept any helper script in `contrib/` or prioritize direct integration into `git rebase`. No maintainer decision has been made, but this exchange solidifies the script’s interim distribution path.
