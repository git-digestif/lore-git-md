This is a documentation patch thread in the ongoing review of Julia Evans’ seven-part series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page consolidating Git’s scattered merge-conflict advice. The email shows Julia responding to D. Ben Knoble’s feedback on the second patch, which replaces redundant conflict-resolution content in `git-merge.adoc` with a cross-reference to the new guide.

Julia has now drafted a new “What is a merge conflict?” section for the guide, addressing Knoble’s concern that the original patch removed foundational context. The new section takes a deliberately light approach, avoiding technical terms like “common ancestor” and instead explaining conflicts in plain language: overlapping edits to the same lines of a file trigger Git’s conflict markers, while non-overlapping changes are merged automatically. The tone is conversational and example-driven, matching the rest of the guide’s user-tested style. This addition will appear in the v2 revision, making the guide more self-contained and reducing the abruptness of the updated `git-merge` man page. No other changes are proposed in this email, so the series’ mechanical blockers (build-system integration and `command-list.txt` registration) remain the next hurdles.
