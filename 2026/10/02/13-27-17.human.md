This is the first patch in Souma’s v4 series teaching `git history` to sign rewritten commits. It refactors the replay API (`replay.c` and `replay.h`) to expose a `sign_commit` field in the `replay_revisions_options` struct and plumb it through the commit-creation path (`pick_regular_commit`, `create_commit`). The field accepts either a key ID (empty string for the default key) or `NULL` to disable signing. No behavior changes yet—this is pure infrastructure for the next patch. The diff is small (14 lines added, 5 removed) and mechanical, matching what Patrick Steinhardt requested in earlier rounds. The patch is ready for integration and is expected to land in `next` shortly.
