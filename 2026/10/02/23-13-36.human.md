This is a **substantive follow-up review** from Jeff King (Peff) on patch 2/8 of Taylor Blau’s v2 series fixing MIDX reachability closure corner cases. The patch ensures trees and tags in included or excluded-open packs are treated as roots during `--stdin-packs=follow` traversal to preserve reachability closure in the MIDX.

Peff’s review focuses on two technical points. First, he notes that the comment explaining the deferred traversal of `extra_roots` (trees and tags) is placed in `read_stdin_packs()` rather than where the deferral actually happens (`add_object_entry_from_pack()` and `add_loose_object()`). While the comment’s placement is not incorrect, it could be clearer if split between the two call sites where the deferral logic is implemented. This is a **surface-level** readability concern and does not affect functionality.

Second, Peff raises a **substantive** question about whether it is safe to call `prepare_revision_walk()` twice on the same `rev_info` struct. He acknowledges that it *might* work with the limited set of options used here, but notes that the function was not designed for repeated use and could have hidden corner cases. Since the `rev_info` is local to `read_stdin_packs()`, Peff suggests releasing and reinitializing the struct between walks as a defensive measure. This is a low-risk but technically important point that could prevent subtle bugs in edge cases.

The review is narrowly scoped to these two issues and does not challenge the patch’s core design or test coverage. Peff’s feedback is **tested in spirit**—he is not suggesting functional changes but rather defensive improvements to code clarity and robustness. The patch remains a strict improvement over the status quo, and addressing these points would further solidify its readiness for integration. The unresolved data structure debate (`oidset` vs. `oid_array`) is not revisited here.
