This is a major refactoring patch series from Patrick Steinhardt, part of the ongoing ODB (object database) abstraction effort. The series moves alternates handling into the "files" backend, addressing conceptual mismatches between alternates and pluggable ODBs that have caused performance regressions and design headaches.

The motivation is clear: alternates and pluggable backends don’t play well together. The current design assumes too much about backend internals (e.g., that alternates are filesystem-backed), and data structures like bitmaps and commit graphs can’t cleanly span multiple sources. This series doesn’t fix those problems yet but lays the groundwork for future fixes by consolidating alternates into the files backend.

The series is large (13 patches, ~880 lines added, ~950 removed) and touches core subsystems: `commit-graph`, `pack-objects`, `multi-pack-index`, `tmp-objdir`, and the ODB layer itself. Key changes include:
- Introducing `struct odb_files_dir` to manage multiple object directories.
- Refactoring `odb_for_each_alternate()` and `odb_find_source()` to work with directories instead of sources.
- Moving quarantine logic into `tmp-objdir` as a proper object directory.
- Dropping the `read_alternates` callback in favor of backend-specific handling.

The commit message notes minor merge conflicts with `seen`, which are straightforward to resolve (provided in the email). This looks like a well-motivated, carefully structured series—typical of Patrick’s work—but the size and scope mean it will likely see thorough review. If you care about ODB internals or alternates, this is worth following.
