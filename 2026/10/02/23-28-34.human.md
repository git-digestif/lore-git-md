This is a **substantive review** of the sixth patch in Taylor Blau’s eight-patch bugfix series targeting MIDX reachability closure. Jeff King (Peff) engages with the patch’s design, questioning whether the proposed refactoring is strictly necessary while acknowledging its clarity and maintainability benefits.

The patch introduces a `preferred_pack` field in the `midx_compaction_step` struct to explicitly track the preferred pack, removing the dependency on string-list order and enabling future optimizations such as sorting the list for faster membership checks. Peff’s review notes that the existing code—using `item->util` to mark preferred packs and performing a linear search—would still work even if the string list were sorted, since the search is order-agnostic. However, he endorses the refactoring as a cleaner approach, particularly appreciating the removal of the "gross casts" (`(void *)1`) used to mark preferred packs in the string-list entries.

The review is narrowly scoped to the patch’s internal design and does not challenge its core goal or correctness. Peff’s feedback is constructive and aligns with the series’ broader aim of making MIDX write steps more robust and maintainable. The patch remains uncontroversial and ready for integration, though Peff’s question highlights that the refactoring is more about code clarity than an immediate functional necessity. No further changes are likely needed, but the discussion reinforces the value of the patch’s approach.
