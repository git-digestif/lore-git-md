This is a small feature patch that proposed extending `git stash pop` with custom conflict-label options (`--label-ours`, `--label-theirs`, `--label-base`) already available in `git stash apply`. The author, Harald Nordgren, has now withdrawn the patch after Junio C Hamano and Phillip Wood questioned whether these options belong in user-facing commands at all.

The discussion reveals that the options originated as internal machinery for `git checkout -m` and are technically required by the sequencer for autostash functionality, but neither maintainer sees a compelling reason for users to manually specify custom labels when popping a stash. Harald suggests the alternative of keeping the options in `stash apply` but hiding them from documentation, though he acknowledges this might be a bad idea. No technical flaws were identified in the original implementation—this is purely a design debate about whether the feature is justified in the user interface. The thread is now closed with no further action planned.
