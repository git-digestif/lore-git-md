This is patch 10/13 in Patrick Steinhardt’s ODB alternates refactoring series. It moves the `will_destroy` flag from the generic `struct odb_source` into the loose-object backend (`struct odb_source_loose`), where it is actually used.

The change is a cleanup that tightens the abstraction: the flag was tracking a backend-specific optimization (skipping fsync when the source is ephemeral) but was defined in the generic ODB source structure, implying it was relevant to all backends. In reality, only the loose-object backend ever checked it. The patch removes the field from `odb/source.h`, adds it to `odb/source-loose.h`, and updates the one place in `tmp-objdir.c` that sets it to use the new location. The diff is small (4 files, 5 insertions, 9 deletions) and mechanical.

This patch does not alter behavior; it simply relocates a field to where it belongs. It is a logical follow-up to the earlier patches that introduced `struct odb_files_dir` and absorbed quarantine logic into the files backend, further clarifying the separation between generic ODB infrastructure and backend-specific details. If the series as a whole is of interest, this patch is a minor but necessary step toward that goal.
