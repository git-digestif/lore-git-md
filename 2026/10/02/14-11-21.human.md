This is the final patch in Andrew Pleeter’s four-part v9 series extending `git var` to expose Git’s identity configuration in a scriptable way. The patch adds six new variables—`GIT_AUTHOR_NAME`, `GIT_AUTHOR_EMAIL`, `GIT_AUTHOR_DATE`, `GIT_COMMITTER_NAME`, `GIT_COMMITTER_EMAIL`, and `GIT_COMMITTER_DATE`—so scripts no longer have to parse the monolithic `GIT_AUTHOR_IDENT` or `GIT_COMMITTER_IDENT` strings. The implementation is clean, reusing Git’s existing `split_ident_line()` logic to handle awkward characters in names or emails, and the tests verify that each component can be queried individually, that unset identities are rejected, and that the new variables appear in `git var -l` output.

Phillip Wood’s review is surface-level, suggesting the author and committer identity tests could have been combined into a single test rather than split across three. He explicitly says it is not worth re-rolling the series just for that, so the patch is effectively ready. The rest of the series—string-list conversion, `-z` output mode, and multi-variable support—has already been reviewed and is waiting for Junio to pick up the v9 submission. Once that happens, the topic will graduate to `next` and then `master`.
