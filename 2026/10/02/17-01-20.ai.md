This is a documentation patch thread, part of Julia Evans' seven-patch series introducing `gitmergeconflicts(7)`, a new man page consolidating merge conflict resolution guidance. This specific email responds to D. Ben Knoble’s feedback on the second patch, which replaces redundant conflict-resolution content in `git-merge.adoc` with a cross-reference to the new guide.

Julia incorporates Knoble’s suggestion to add a foundational explanation of what a merge conflict *is*, addressing a gap in the new guide’s completeness. The proposed "WHAT IS A MERGE CONFLICT?" section adopts a deliberately lightweight approach, avoiding technical jargon like "3-way merge" or "common ancestor" in favor of a practical, example-driven explanation. It clarifies that conflicts arise when overlapping changes cannot be automatically combined, and describes Git’s behavior during conflicts (staging successfully merged files, leaving conflicted files unstaged with markers). This addition directly responds to Knoble’s concern about the abruptness of the updated `git-merge` man page for users unfamiliar with merge conflicts, while maintaining the series’ user-centric tone.

The section is likely to be included in the next revision (v2), alongside the previously agreed `git log --merge -p` guidance. No new technical blockers or controversies are introduced; the focus remains on refining the guide’s clarity and completeness. The broader series is still pending mechanical fixes (e.g., `command-list.txt` registration, `.gitattributes` integration) and the resolution of cherry-pick-specific content retention in the cherry-pick man page. Julia’s responsiveness to feedback continues to advance the series toward a more thorough and accessible guide.
