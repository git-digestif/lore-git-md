This is a follow-up in the ongoing discussion about whether to include a helper script for incremental rebase conflict resolution in Git. Alejandro Colomar responds to Patrick Steinhardt’s earlier email, which suggested integrating the functionality directly into `git rebase` rather than as a standalone script.

Colomar agrees with Patrick’s suggestion in principle, proposing `--first-conflict` as a potential option name for `git rebase` to reflect the script’s behavior (rebasing onto the first conflicting commit rather than the target commit). He expresses interest in implementing this integration but acknowledges it would take time and likely require assistance. In the meantime, he plans to distribute the script separately under the `git-` namespace (e.g., as part of `manpages-utils`), with the goal of deprecating it once the functionality is merged into `git rebase`. He notes that this aligns with Git’s standard practice of extending the tool via third-party `git-*` commands and cites precedent in `manpages-utils` for including generally useful utilities.

The email does not introduce new technical objections or alternatives but reinforces the thread’s direction: the script’s functionality is seen as valuable, but the long-term goal is integration into `git rebase` itself. Colomar’s plan to distribute the script separately provides an immediate solution while leaving the door open for a more polished implementation later. The tone remains collaborative, with no signs of disagreement—just a pragmatic acknowledgment of the work required to move the feature into Git’s core.
