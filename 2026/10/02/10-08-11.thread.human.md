This thread is a 13-patch series from Patrick Steinhardt refactoring Git’s object database (ODB) to move alternates handling into the "files" backend, eliminating a long-standing architectural mismatch. The goal is to simplify the ODB subsystem, improve performance, and enable future work like pluggable backends. The series is under initial review (v1), with no substantive objections yet, though Karthik Nayak’s early feedback on the first patch flags a missing explanation in the commit message about *why* the change matters—specifically, how it advances the broader goal of making the ODB more backend-agnostic.

The refactoring touches core ODB code, the files backend, commit-graph subsystem, and quarantine logic, with no on-disk format changes. Key technical shifts include replacing `struct odb_source` with `struct odb_files_dir` for alternates management, dropping the `read_alternates` callback, and consolidating alternates into a files-backend-specific linked list. The final patch (13/13) removes the now-unused callback from all backends, completing the architectural shift. Open questions center on edge cases like promisor remotes, alternates traversal order, and path canonicalization robustness. The series is a prerequisite for future ODB work but currently has minor merge conflicts with `seen`. Reviewers are encouraged to focus on the alternates logic relocation in patch 12/13 and the linked-list traversal updates.
