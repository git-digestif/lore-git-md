This thread is about Patrick Steinhardt’s 13-patch series to refactor Git’s object database (ODB) subsystem, moving alternates handling into the "files" backend. The goal is to eliminate a long-standing architectural mismatch that caused performance regressions and blocked future ODB work, such as pluggable backends. The series is now **technically complete and ready for integration** after a thorough review process.

Patrick authored the series, which touches 52 files, with the bulk of changes in `odb.c` (439 lines removed) and `odb/source-files.c` (482 lines added). Key changes include introducing `struct odb_files_dir` to manage object directories, removing the `read_alternates` callback, and consolidating alternates into the files backend. The v2 reroll was documentation-only, addressing feedback from Karthik Nayak, who reviewed most of the series. Karthik’s feedback was substantive but narrowly scoped, focusing on mechanical correctness and architectural clarity rather than edge cases like promisor remotes or linked-list invariants. His final review acknowledged that all concerns were addressed, including a resolved technical issue about a redundant NULL-check in `clear_midx_file()`.

The series is built on the start of the Git 2.98 cycle and has minor merge conflicts with the `seen` branch, but no further rerolls are expected unless rebasing is needed. It’s now queued for Junio’s integration branches, likely `next` first. If you’re interested in ODB internals or alternates, this is your last chance to review before it lands.
