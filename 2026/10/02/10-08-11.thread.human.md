This thread is a 13-patch refactoring series by Patrick Steinhardt that moves alternates handling into Git’s "files" object database (ODB) backend. The goal is to eliminate a long-standing architectural mismatch where alternates were managed at the ODB source level, which caused performance regressions and blocked future work like pluggable backends. The series consolidates alternates into the files backend, making them a backend-specific detail and simplifying the ODB interface.

The patches are under review, with Karthik Nayak providing the most detailed feedback so far. Karthik’s review is mostly surface-level, but he did flag one unresolved technical concern: a potential NULL-dereference regression in `clear_midx_file()` (patch 12/13) due to the removal of a guard clause. He also previously questioned the architectural decision to eliminate multiple sources per ODB, but retracted that after Patrick clarified the design in a later patch. The series has minor merge conflicts with the `seen` branch, and its size (13 patches, ~500 lines of changes) may pose review challenges for edge cases like promisor remotes or partial clones.

Patrick has responded to Karthik’s earlier feedback, dismissing one concern about a test helper’s implementation as a "test-suite hack" and defending the current approach. The unresolved NULL-check issue in patch 12/13 is the only open blocker, and it may prompt a reroll. No other reviewers have weighed in yet, and the series remains in its initial v1 state. If the NULL-check issue is addressed, the series could proceed toward integration, as the core architectural changes appear to have buy-in from Karthik.
