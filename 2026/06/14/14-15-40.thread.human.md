This thread began as a bugfix series for memory leaks in `git history reword` and related slab management, part of Patrick Steinhardt’s `git history` command work. The original fix for the `git history reword` leak—where `repo_logmsg_reencode()` allocated buffers not freed by `repo_unuse_commit_buffer()` when commits were loaded from the commit-graph—has been merged to `master`. The fix was narrowly scoped to `builtin/history.c` and resolved test failures in `t345x`. A separate leak in the topological walk slab (`init_topo_walk`), exposed in `t4014`, was also fixed and merged earlier.

The discussion revealed broader slab management gaps, particularly how commit-graph parsing bypasses the commit slab cache, but no systemic changes were made. Jeff King (Peff) confirmed the root cause and suggested documenting reproduction recipes for future reference, while Junio C Hamano merged the fix after minor editorial feedback. The thread has now closed with a playful meta-comment from Junio about commit message conciseness, echoing Peff’s earlier stylistic feedback. No open questions or regressions remain, and the fixes are considered complete.
