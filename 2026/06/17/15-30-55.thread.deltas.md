- [2026/08/20/21-39-51 by Junio C Hamano <gitster@pobox.com>] Junio C Hamano corrected Ben Knoble's remark that Zsh accepts empty actions in a `case` statement: it is a common misconception, since POSIX.1 sh grammar explicitly allows `;;` to follow `)` without a compound_list in between, so `bar) ;;` is valid POSIX sh, not a Zsh extension.

## 2026/08/21

- [2026/08/21/12-31-46 by D. Ben Knoble <ben.knoble@gmail.com>] D. Ben Knoble acknowledged that empty case arms (`;;` without a compound_list) are valid in POSIX shell, noting that the Bash manual does not clearly admit this in his reading but it works in implementation, and recalling that Bash and ShellCheck previously complained about empty case arms.
- [2026/08/21/16-42-38 by Junio C Hamano <gitster@pobox.com>] Junio C Hamano explained that the POSIX standard's simplified syntax in Section 2.9.4.3 contributes to the misconception by showing compound-list as required, while the actual grammar in Section 2.10 allows linebreak (zero or more NEWLINEs) in its place.
