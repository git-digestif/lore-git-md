- [2026/08/19/16-49-47 by Justin Tobler <jltobler@gmail.com>] Justin Tobler reviewed [PATCH 3/3], agreeing the skip-when-locked logic makes sense, but suggesting that instead of changing `stack_uptodate()`'s signature, the single call site that wants to skip when locked could simply not invoke `stack_uptodate()` there.

## 2026/08/23
