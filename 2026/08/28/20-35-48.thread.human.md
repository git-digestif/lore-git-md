The thread opened with a bug report from Thomas Haller and a patch from Diogo Castro that fixed an edge case in Git’s pathspec handling: `git ls-files` and `git add` were incorrectly stripping the common prefix of positive pathspecs from *negative* (exclusion) pathspecs, which could turn them into empty strings or trigger out-of-bounds reads. After a brief design discussion—where Junio C Hamano initially questioned whether the optimization should be extended to negatives—Diogo defended the current patch as the correct fix, arguing that the prefix-stripping optimization was intended only for positive pathspecs. Junio accepted the patch as-is, and the fix has now landed in `master`. The patch is minimal (11 lines in `dir.c`), includes three new tests, and preserves the performance benefit of the positive-path prefix optimization. Any future work on optimizing negative pathspecs is deferred. The thread is now closed.
