Subject: [PATCH] dir: avoid prefix-stripping negative pathspecs
Series version: standalone patch (no series)
Originating author: Diogo Castro
Thread type: bugfix patch

Core problem or goal
`git ls-files` and `git add` incorrectly strip the common prefix of positive pathspecs from *all* pathspecs, including negative (exclusion) pathspecs. This can cause negative pathspecs to degenerate into empty strings (matching everything) or trigger out-of-bounds reads when the negative pathspec is shorter than the prefix. The patch fixes this by ensuring negative pathspecs are not affected by the positive-path prefix optimization.

Current status
**Merged to `master`.** Junio C Hamano has accepted and merged Diogo Castro’s original patch. The fix is minimal (11 lines in `dir.c`), includes three new tests in `t/t6132-pathspec-exclude.sh`, and traces the bug to its introduction in 2013 (ef79b1f870). The patch preserves the performance benefit of the positive-path prefix optimization by limiting the working-directory walk to the common prefix of positive pathspecs, then filtering out excluded paths by matching full paths against negative patterns. Discussion about potential future optimizations for cases with many negative patterns is acknowledged but deferred as a separate topic.

Key technical details
- Files touched: `dir.c` (function `do_match_pathspec()`)
- New behavior: negative pathspecs are no longer affected by positive-path prefix optimizations. The common prefix is stripped only from positive pathspecs, preserving the intended semantics of negative patterns.
- Test coverage: three new tests in `t/t6132-pathspec-exclude.sh` covering edge cases (negative pathspecs matching prefix length, shorter than prefix, and interaction with `git add` trailing-slash handling)
- On-disk format: no changes
- Symbols: no new or renamed symbols
- Performance: the fix preserves the performance benefit of the positive-path prefix optimization. No performance regressions are expected.
- Merge date: 2024-07-10 (as confirmed by the author’s thread closure notice)

Open questions and loose ends
- **Future optimizations**: The discussion about recalculating the common prefix to include negative pathspecs (e.g., for cases like `git add -- a/b/c a/b/d ':!a/**/x'`) is explicitly deferred. Such optimizations would require a separate design effort and implementation.
