This thread is about extending `git var` to provide a unified, scriptable interface for Git’s identity and signing configuration, replacing an earlier `git ident` proposal. Andrew Pleeter is the author, and the series is now at version 6 after addressing substantial feedback from Junio C Hamano and Phillip Wood.

The patch adds new variables like `GIT_AUTHOR_NAME`, `GIT_SIGNING_KEY`, and others, supports multiple arguments and NUL-terminated output (`-z`), and maintains backward compatibility. It’s technically sound and review-converged, with all major design concerns resolved. The series remains in Junio’s `seen` branch, indicating it’s under active review but not yet ready for integration.

Phillip Wood’s latest review raises a few remaining edge cases: whether `GIT_SIGNING_KEY` alone is sufficient for callers to determine the signing mechanism, overly strict error handling for unset variables, and documentation clarity around multi-valued variables. He also suggests minor code structure improvements and more robust testing. These are polishing issues, not blockers, and Andrew’s next revision should address them. Once those are resolved, the patch is likely to graduate to `next` and eventually `master`. No fundamental objections remain.
