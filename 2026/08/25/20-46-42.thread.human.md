This thread is about extending Git’s `git var` plumbing command to provide a unified, scriptable interface for querying Git’s identity and signing configuration. The v9 patch series, submitted by Andrew Pleeter, is now complete and ready for maintainer review. The series is split into four patches: (1) a preparatory refactoring to handle multi-valued variables, (2) the addition of `-z` output mode for machine-readable output, (3) support for querying multiple variables in a single invocation, and (4) the new identity variables (`GIT_AUTHOR_NAME`, `GIT_AUTHOR_EMAIL`, `GIT_AUTHOR_DATE`, `GIT_COMMITTER_NAME`, `GIT_COMMITTER_EMAIL`, `GIT_COMMITTER_DATE`).

The core goal—providing a robust, scriptable way to access identity components without parsing monolithic ident strings—has been achieved. The series maintains backward compatibility for single-variable queries while introducing structured output for multi-variable queries, with strict argument ordering and consistent formatting (aligned with `git config -z`). The `GIT_SIGNING_KEY` variable was removed due to ambiguity across signing mechanisms, deferring its reintroduction until a concrete use case emerges. All prior technical feedback from Junio C Hamano, Phillip Wood, Jeff King, and others has been addressed, and the series is technically complete. The only unresolved suggestion—a trailing delimiter in `-z` mode—is not a blocker and can be handled in a follow-up.

The series remains in Junio’s `seen` branch, awaiting his review before graduating to `next`. No maintainer has signaled intent to merge it yet, but the structural and technical concerns raised in earlier iterations have been resolved. Key participants, including Phillip Wood and Junio, have confirmed the design and implementation are sound. The focus now is on final integration.
