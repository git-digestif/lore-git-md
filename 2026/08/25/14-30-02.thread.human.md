This thread is Patrick Steinhardt’s third iteration of a 10-patch series that refactors Git’s object integrity verification (fsck) to make it pluggable per ODB backend. The goal is to move fsck checks out of `builtin/fsck.c` and into their respective backends (loose, packed, etc.), preparing the codebase for future pluggable ODB backends like reftable or cloud storage. The series is a pure refactoring with no user-visible behavior changes, though it fixes a long-standing discrepancy in the `--full` flag’s behavior and a latent progress-meter bug.

The latest email covers patch 9/10, which moves multi-pack index (MIDX) verification into the packed ODB backend. This follows the pattern established in earlier patches for packfiles, reverse indices, and bitmaps. The patch replaces the dedicated `ERROR_MULTI_PACK_INDEX` bit with the generic `ERROR_OBJECT` bit and ensures MIDX checks respect the `--no-full` and `--connectivity-only` flags. A new test verifies these semantics. In response to Toon Claes’s review, Patrick revised the implementation to use early returns and avoid a `BUG()` assertion, addressing stylistic and robustness concerns. The only unresolved question is a minor inconsistency in error-handling between `verify_midx()` and `verify_bitmap_files()`, which Patrick deferred to avoid scope creep.
