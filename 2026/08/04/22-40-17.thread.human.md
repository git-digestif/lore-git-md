This thread is about a bugfix for the `contrib/credential/libsecret` credential helper, authored by Daniel Martí, which prevents crashes and silent data loss caused by a race condition or locked keyring. The issue stems from the use of the `SECRET_SEARCH_LOAD_SECRETS` flag, which silently discards failures (e.g., locked or deleted items), leading to assertion crashes when accessing NULL secrets. Martí’s v2 patch, posted on November 2, removes this flag and replaces it with explicit secret-loading calls, ensuring all errors are surfaced while maintaining the same number of D-Bus round-trips in the common case. The patch aligns with libsecret’s own practices and addresses Junio C Hamano’s feedback on commit message clarity and design rationale.

The patch is self-contained, touching only `contrib/credential/libsecret/git-credential-libsecret.c`, and has no test coverage due to the difficulty of reliably reproducing the race condition. The key unresolved question is whether to retain the `SECRET_SEARCH_LOAD_SECRETS` flag for its perceived optimization benefits (Junio’s preference) or drop it entirely (Martí’s approach). Junio’s recent email nudges the thread, noting the lack of reviews and warning that the topic may be dropped from his `seen` branch if no further feedback emerges. Without additional input, this well-motivated fix risks being abandoned despite its clear value.
