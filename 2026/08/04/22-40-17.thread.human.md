This thread is about a bugfix for the `contrib/credential/libsecret` credential helper, which currently crashes and silently loses data when credential items are locked or deleted concurrently. The issue stems from the use of the `SECRET_SEARCH_LOAD_SECRETS` flag, which silently discards failures, leading to NULL secret values and assertion crashes. Daniel Martí proposed a patch two weeks ago that replaces this flag with an explicit `secret_item_load_secret_sync()` call, arguing this approach is simpler, more reliable, and maintains the same round-trip count in the common case while ensuring all errors are surfaced.

Junio C Hamano reviewed the patch and suggested an alternative that retains `SECRET_SEARCH_LOAD_SECRETS` and conditionally loads secrets only when `secret_item_get_secret()` returns NULL, prioritizing the batch-fetch optimization for the common case. Daniel defended his original design, emphasizing that `SECRET_SEARCH_LOAD_SECRETS` is not a true optimization and silently discards errors, including client-side skipped items. The discussion remains open, with no consensus on whether to prioritize optimization or simplicity and reliability. Junio’s latest email is procedural, clarifying that the next step is for Daniel to revise the patch to address the open questions and improve clarity for future reviewers. The patch targets `contrib/credential/libsecret/git-credential-libsecret.c` and does not include test coverage due to reproducibility challenges. No new technical arguments were introduced, and the next step is likely a v2 patch from Daniel.
