The patch, authored by Grant Moyer, corrects the logic to read and write commit mappings in the proper "from_commit:to_commit" format.

The bug was introduced in Git 2.50.0 during a Perl-removal refactor, and the fix restores the intended behavior for users who rely on incremental repository splitting. The only minor issue is a typo in the subject line, which is unlikely to delay merging.
