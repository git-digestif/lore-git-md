The `git format-rev` series from Kristoffer Haugsbakk, now at v2, adds `--abbrev`, `--color`, and `--date` formatting options to the command to bring it closer to `git log`'s flexibility. The series has addressed all prior feedback, including defensive code improvements, a memory leak fix, and new refactoring patches, but remains under review and at risk of being dropped from Junio’s `seen` branch due to a lack of substantive reviews. While the author initially questioned the command’s necessity, the latest discussion clarifies that `git format-rev --stdin-mode=text` (which turns hashes into references in arbitrary input text) serves a unique purpose that `git diff-tree` cannot easily replicate, justifying its existence. The `--stdin-mode=revs` mode (formatting revision ranges) remains debatable, but no concrete alternative has emerged.

D. Ben Knoble’s recent email shifts the focus to UI/UX design, proposing to split the command’s functionality: move the `--stdin-mode=revs` mode into `git diff-tree` (with NUL-terminated output support) while keeping `git format-rev` solely for the `--stdin-mode=text` mode. This would simplify the command’s scope and improve editor integration, potentially addressing the author’s earlier doubts. The proposal is conceptual, with no technical objections or implementation details yet, but it reopens the question of whether a narrower focus would make the command more maintainable while preserving its unique text-filtering capability. The series remains uncontroversial but still lacks the reviews needed to progress beyond `seen`.
