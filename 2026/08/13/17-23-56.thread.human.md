This thread is about a five-patch series from Kristoffer Haugsbakk that adds `--abbrev`, `--color`, and `--date` formatting options to the experimental `git format-rev` command, bringing it closer to parity with `git log`. The series is now at v2, incorporating all review feedback from v1, including a memory leak fix, defensive code improvements, and two new refactoring patches. However, the author has recently expressed doubts about whether `git format-rev` is needed at all, suggesting that `git diff-tree` could be modified to serve as a replacement.

The series remains under review but is at risk of being dropped from Junio’s `seen` branch due to lack of substantive feedback. Junio has explicitly stated he is not the best reviewer for this command and has invited input from others. Ben Knoble, who had previously acknowledged the series was on his review list, has now engaged with the author’s doubts, asking whether the proposed `git diff-tree` modification would support the stdin filter mode that turns hashes into references—a key feature of `git format-rev`. This question highlights the practical value of the series, but the broader design question remains unresolved. Without further substantive reviews, the series will likely be dropped, though the refactoring patches (e.g., defensive assertion placement, documentation reuse) might still be salvageable for other efforts.

The technical implementation reuses existing `git log` logic (`rev_info` struct and `pretty.c` date formatting) and includes expanded test coverage in `t/t6120-describe.sh`. The series touches `builtin/name-rev.c`, documentation files, and introduces no on-disk format changes. While the patches themselves are uncontroversial, the command’s future is now in question, and the thread’s fate hinges on whether the proposed `git diff-tree` modification can fully replace `git format-rev`.
