The `receive-report` hook series by Karthik Nayak has reached a key milestone: it is now feature-complete, queued in Junio’s `next` branch, and has received final approval from Patrick Steinhardt, one of its most thorough reviewers. The series introduces a new server-side hook that lets administrators intercept and modify the status report sent to clients after ref updates, a feature driven by GitLab’s need for multi-version concurrency control (MVCC). The hook acts as a bidirectional filter, receiving the pkt-line encoded report on stdin and replacing it with its stdout, while forwarding stderr to the client via sideband.

All prior feedback has been addressed, including Junio’s maintainability concern about a boolean parameter (now replaced with an `enum`), Patrick’s substantive review of redundant report-generation functions (now unified), and surface-level nits like code style and documentation clarity. The series is rebased onto the latest `master` and includes 224 lines of test coverage in `t/t5412-receive-report-hook.sh`. The only lingering question is Junio’s philosophical concern about the hook’s ability to report a state divergent from the actual repository state—a documented trade-off justified by GitLab’s use case. With Patrick’s approval and no outstanding technical objections, the series appears ready for graduation to `master`.
