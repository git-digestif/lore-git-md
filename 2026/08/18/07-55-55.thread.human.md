Here’s the human-mode summary for a developer catching up on the thread:

This thread introduces a new `receive-report` hook in Git’s `receive-pack` to let server administrators filter or modify the status report sent to clients after ref updates. The hook is motivated by GitLab’s need for MVCC (multi-version concurrency control), where the server must report a state that may diverge from the actual repository—violating Git’s traditional consistency model but enabling conservative client behavior. The series, now at v9, is feature-complete and queued in Junio’s `next` branch as of September 5, with all prior feedback addressed, including a critical fix in patch 2/4 that could crash clients not requesting a status report.

Karthik Nayak is the author, and the series has drawn reviews from Junio (who raised the consistency concern and a crash-risk flaw), Patrick Steinhardt (who proposed the hook’s design and exit semantics), and others. The hook is now documented, tested (224 lines of coverage), and rebased onto `master` without functional conflicts. The only open question is a gap in test coverage: the suite doesn’t verify behavior when clients omit the `report-status` capability, though the code now handles this case safely. Patrick Steinhardt flagged this as a potential blind spot, but no changes are proposed yet. The series is otherwise ready for final review and graduation to `master`.
