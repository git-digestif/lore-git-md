The thread introduces a new `receive-report` hook for Git’s `receive-pack`, allowing server administrators to intercept and modify the status report sent to clients after ref updates. Authored by Karthik Nayak and motivated by GitLab’s need for MVCC (multi-version concurrency control), the hook acts as a bidirectional filter: it receives the pkt-line encoded status report on stdin, replaces it with its stdout, and forwards stderr to the client via sideband.
