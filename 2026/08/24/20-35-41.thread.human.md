This thread is about Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s `[PATCH v3 0/2]` series adding `--[no-]range-diff-notes` to `git format-patch`. The feature lets users control whether Git notes appear in the range-diff section of patch output independently of the notes shown in the patches themselves. After a usability redesign in v3—adopting Junio C Hamano’s suggestion to drop the toggle mechanism and require explicit arguments—the series is now technically complete and ready for maintainer approval.

The v3 implementation consists of two patches: a preparatory refactoring of `get_notes_args` (1/2) and the new feature (2/2). Junio reviewed v2 and found only a minor test-script redundancy, which Haugsbakk fixed in v3. His feedback on v3’s second patch is limited to editorial improvements: tightening the commit message and documentation wording, and refactoring the option callback for better maintainability. No substantive concerns remain about the design, behavior, or test coverage. The series is now waiting for Junio’s final decision on whether to accept the feature.
