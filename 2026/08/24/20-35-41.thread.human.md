This thread is about Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s `[PATCH v2 0/2]` series adding `--[no-]range-diff-notes` to `git format-patch`. The goal is to let users control Git notes separately in patch output and range-diff sections—for example, suppressing iteration notes (like "v3: [desc]") in range-diffs while keeping them in the patches themselves.

The v2 redesign, posted after v1 was abandoned due to usability concerns, simplifies the interface: `--range-diff-notes=<ref>` requires an explicit notes reference, and `--no-range-diff-notes` suppresses notes in range-diffs without fallback. This replaces v1’s ambiguous toggle mechanism and arg-less option, aligning with Junio Hamano’s feedback. The series is now complete with two patches: the first refactors existing code to prepare for the new options, and the second implements them. Tests cover edge cases like single-patch series and different notes refs for patches versus range-diffs.

The thread remains closed, and no maintainer decision has been made on whether to revive the feature. However, the v2 design resolves the earlier issues and is ready for review if the discussion reopens. Junio’s concerns about v1’s usability are addressed, but he hasn’t explicitly endorsed v2. D. Ben Knoble’s earlier feedback on v1 was surface-level, and no substantive technical review has been posted for v2. The series is self-contained and doesn’t conflict with other in-flight work.
