This thread is about Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s `[PATCH v3 0/2]` series adding `--[no-]range-diff-notes` to `git format-patch`. The feature lets users suppress or specify Git notes separately for the range-diff section while keeping them in the patch output. After Junio C Hamano raised usability concerns with the initial design, the v3 series adopted a cleaner rule: `--no-range-diff-notes` means “no notes for range-diff” without fallback, and `--range-diff-notes=<ref>` requires an explicit ref argument, enforcing a clear separation between patch notes and range-diff notes.

The series is now technically complete. The two patches—one preparatory refactoring, one implementation—touch `builtin/log.c`, `Documentation/git-format-patch.adoc`, and tests in `t/t3206-range-diff.sh`. All of Junio’s feedback on v3 has been addressed: the documentation is rewritten in a concise style, the option callback now wraps `parse_opt_string_list` instead of duplicating its logic, and commit messages are tightened. No substantive concerns remain, and the series awaits Junio’s final decision on whether to accept the feature. The only loose end is a tangential discussion about `format-patch`’s default notes handling, but that is not blocking this series.
