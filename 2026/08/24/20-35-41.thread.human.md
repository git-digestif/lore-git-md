This thread is about Kristoffer Haugsbakk’s patch series adding `--[no-]range-diff-notes` to `git format-patch`, now in its second version. The goal is to let users control whether Git notes appear in the range-diff section of the output independently of the patch notes. After the first version ran into usability concerns—ambiguous option semantics and an unintuitive toggle mechanism—Haugsbakk redesigned the feature following Junio C Hamano’s feedback. The new version simplifies the command-line interface: `--no-range-diff-notes` means exactly that, without fallback, and `--range-diff-notes=<ref>` requires an explicit reference, making the behavior predictable and explicit.

The series is now complete at v2, with two patches: a preparatory refactoring and the new implementation. Junio’s latest reply to the second patch is a light review, noting only a minor redundancy in the test script but otherwise finding nothing questionable. This confirms the patch is technically sound and nearly ready for integration, though the series as a whole remains in limbo. No maintainer decision has been made on whether to revive the feature, and the thread is effectively closed until that changes. The v2 design resolves the usability issues of v1, but without explicit approval, the feature won’t move forward. The only open question is whether the project wants this capability at all.
