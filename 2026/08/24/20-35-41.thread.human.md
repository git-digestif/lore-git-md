This thread is about a small but useful feature addition to `git format-patch`: the ability to control Git notes inclusion separately for the patch output and the range-diff section. Kristoffer Haugsbakk proposed the series, which is now in its fourth iteration (`v4`).

This redesign, suggested by Junio C Hamano, simplifies the earlier toggle-based approach and enforces explicit separation between patch notes and range-diff notes. The series consists of two patches: a preparatory refactoring of `get_notes_args` and the implementation of the new options.
