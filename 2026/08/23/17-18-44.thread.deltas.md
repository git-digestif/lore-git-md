- [2026/08/24/14-08-41 by Oswald Buddenhagen <oswald.buddenhagen@gmx.de>] Oswald Buddenhagen stated he would prefer lib/odb/ over odb/ for the odb sources, and called the quoted-include style shown in the patch's diff ("odb/object-file.h" etc.) the wrong include style — already wrong in many places, but fixable while everything is being rewritten anyway.

- [2026/08/24/20-03-50 by Oswald Buddenhagen <oswald.buddenhagen@gmx.de>] Oswald Buddenhagen answered "why not" lib/odb/ by explaining that it is hugely beneficial for build-system comprehensibility and discoverability when the physical project structure follows the logical one; and confirmed the include-style objection was about double-quoted includes being implementation-defined (so "<>" should be used, as with -I paths), while noting he does not mind the popular interpretation that "" means relative to the including file's directory, which mostly makes "" acceptable for in-module references when headers and sources live side by side.
