**Human-readable summary**

The thread wraps up the now-completed effort to replace Git’s build-time `USE_NSEC` macro with a runtime configuration option, `core.useNanosec`. Authored by D. Ben Knoble, the series—posted as v7 under the topic `dk/use-nsec-runtime`—was merged to `master` on 15 July 2024 after a final polish confirmed all prior feedback had been addressed. The change lets users enable nanosecond precision on supported filesystems without recompiling, reducing “racy Git” problems where timestamps appear identical due to coarse filesystem resolution.

Key participants included Junio C Hamano (who merged the series after routine conflict resolution with `ty/repo-config-cleanups`), Patrick Steinhardt (who endorsed the technical direction), and Jeff King (who surfaced a subtle edge case involving mixed builds but agreed it was not worth blocking the series). The only remaining loose end is a documented architectural constraint: the new call to `repo_config_values()` in `is_racy_stat()` only works for the global repository, so recursive submodule operations may treat `core.useNanosec` as false. This limitation is deferred for future work, but the series itself is complete and no further changes are expected.
