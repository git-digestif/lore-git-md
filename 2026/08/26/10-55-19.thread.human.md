Git is participating in the Outreachy December 2026 cohort, with Christian Couder leading the application. The project has submitted two approved internship ideas—one focused on improving Git’s command-line argument parsing, the other on reducing global state to support libification—and has secured the required two mentors for each. Kaartic Sivaraam recently confirmed that mentor assignments are finalized, with Christian Couder and Siddharth Asthana mentoring the parsing project, and Usman Akinyemi and Pablo Sabater mentoring the libification effort.

With the mentor requirement met, Kaartic has proposed expanding the Outreachy application to include two additional projects originally prepared for Google Summer of Code 2026: improving promisor remote fetch ordering and enhancing the promisor-remote protocol for better-connected remotes. These projects aim to strengthen Git’s partial-clone functionality. No objections have been raised yet, but sponsorship outreach remains ongoing, with a discussion planned at the upcoming Git Contributor’s Summit 2026.
