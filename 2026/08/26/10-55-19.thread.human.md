The Git project’s participation in the Outreachy December 2026 cohort is moving forward, but mentor availability is becoming a challenge. Christian Couder submitted Git’s application with three approved project ideas, all now visible to mentors on the Outreachy site. Two of the original projects—improving Git’s command argument parsing and reducing global state for libification—have confirmed mentors (Christian Couder, Siddharth Asthana, and Usman Akinyemi). The third, implementing configurable fetch ordering for promisor remotes, has Kaartic Sivaraam tentatively signed on as mentor, with Christian available to co-mentor if needed.

However, Pablo Sabater, who was tentatively assigned as a co-mentor for one of the projects, has withdrawn due to time constraints, leaving that role open. He remains willing to contribute informally but won’t officially commit to mentoring. This withdrawal highlights the tight timeline: the Outreachy application deadline is October 5, 2026, and the team still needs to finalize mentor assignments and secure sponsorship. Christian has reached out to potential sponsors like GitLab, GitHub, and OpenAI, and the topic will be discussed at the upcoming Git Contributor’s Summit 2026. For now, the focus is on stabilizing mentor coverage and refining project details before the deadline.
