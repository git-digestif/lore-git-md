This thread is about a two-patch bugfix series from Volodymyr Vriukalo that addresses crashes and incorrect error handling in Git’s experimental `submodule.propagateBranches` feature when `git branch --recurse-submodules` is used with a non-ref start point, such as a raw object ID. The issue manifests in two ways: a BUG abort in repositories without submodules after partially completing the operation, and an earlier failure in repositories with submodules due to a truncated argument list passed to a helper.

The series is currently under review and queued in Junio C Hamano’s `seen` branch as `vv/branch-recurse-no-start-ref`, but Junio has indicated he may drop it unless more reviewers step in. The first patch adds a guard in `create_branches_recursively()` to prevent tracking a start point without a ref, while the second makes the tracking name argument optional in the `submodule--helper create-branch` command to allow recursion to proceed. D. Ben Knoble has provided substantive reviews for both patches, confirming the fixes but suggesting improvements to test clarity and commit messages. No maintainer intent regarding graduation to `next` or `master` has been expressed, and the series remains open for further feedback. The fixes are limited to the experimental feature and do not change the on-disk format.
