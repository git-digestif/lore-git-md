# Thread deltas

## 2026/08/21

- [2026/08/21/06-55-51 by Elijah Newren via GitGitGadget <gitgitgadget@gmail.com>] Elijah Newren submitted a patch to send-pack that adds reachable shallow grafts as negative pack tips to avoid sending the entire toplevel tree when pushing from a shallow clone, controlled by a new `push.shallowExcludeBoundary` config (default true) with an opt-out for `receive.shallowUpdate` receivers.
- [2026/08/21/13-17-32 by Patrick Steinhardt <ps@pks.im>] Patrick Steinhardt reviewed the patch, questioning whether locally amending a shallow graft could send insufficient information to the server, whether tree-level negotiation could verify the grafted tree matches the remote, whether tests cover modified grafted commits, and whether defaulting `push.negotiate` to enabled has downsides; he also suggested marking input parameters `const`, combining the two ref loops, and considering higher-level reachability interfaces.
- [2026/08/21/17-36-04 by Elijah Newren <newren@gmail.com>] Elijah Newren replied that `--depth=2` does not enable negotiation (only `push.negotiate` does, at any depth), that amending a shallow graft creates a new root commit rather than corrupting the repo (a separate foot-gun he will address in another series), that tree negotiation would arrive too late without `push.negotiate` and that `push.negotiate` is more thorough, that the only downside of defaulting `push.negotiate` is added round-trip latency, and agreed to mark parameters `const` and combine the loops; he explained the manual walk is required to stop at peer-known commits and detect graft boundaries.
- [2026/08/21/18-21-50 by Elijah Newren <newren@gmail.com>] Elijah Newren added that `--depth=2` can be guaranteed by certain forge workflows where merge-request refs are advertised and preserved, but GitHub's `receive.hiderefs` for `refs/pull/`, branch deletion on merge, and heavy squash-merge use in large repositories prevent this from helping those cases.

## 2026/08/24

- [2026/08/24/05-30-13 by Patrick Steinhardt <ps@pks.im>] Patrick Steinhardt proposed introducing a new `push.negotiate=shallow` option that would enable negotiation automatically for shallow clones and make it the default, thereby avoiding the latency cost in full clones.
