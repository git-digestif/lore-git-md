This thread is about a patch series from Wesley Schwengle that aims to clean up two long-standing `git-svn` annoyances: suppressing unnecessary migration messages and empty `.git/svn` directory creation in plain Git repositories, and adding a `NO_GIT_SVN` Makefile knob to skip building `git-svn` entirely when SVN support isn’t needed. The series is now at version 2, posted on June 10, 2025, and has addressed the only feedback received so far—a typo fix in an error message.

The patches touch `perl/Git/SVN/Migration.pm`, the `Makefile`, and a test file, introducing no new symbols or on-disk format changes. The changes are uncontroversial and have not attracted substantive technical objections, but the series has been in Junio’s `seen` integration branch since late August without attracting further review. Junio has now flagged it as at risk of being dropped due to the lack of a second reviewer. If this series is important to you, now is the time to step in and review it—otherwise, it may be removed from the queue in the coming weeks.
