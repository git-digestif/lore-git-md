This thread is about a bugfix patch (v2) by Aaron Plattner that preserves `WWW-Authenticate` headers during HTTP redirects in Git. The issue breaks authentication when a redirect target requires credentials (like OAuth2 tokens) not embedded in the URL. The patch introduces a new helper, `credential_update_url()`, to centralize the preservation logic, and includes a test verifying the fix with a custom Apache rewrite rule.

Key participants include Junio C Hamano (maintainer), who requested broader review to avoid the patch being dropped from the `seen` branch, and Jeff King (Peff), who validated the patch’s core premise but suggested improvements to the helper design, commit message clarity, and defensive checks. Matthew Cheetham, the original author of the `wwwauth_headers` logic, confirmed the patch’s correctness and security model, noting headers are preserved from the *final* redirect target, not the original server.

The patch remains under review in `seen` with two open questions: whether to keep the new helper (Peff prefers reverting to the v1’s inline approach for clarity) and whether to add a `BUG()` check to enforce that `wwwauth_headers` are never URL-derived. Michal Suchánek’s recent feedback highlights a minor but important commit message ambiguity about the headers’ origin, though he doesn’t engage with the technical trade-offs. No merge decisions have been made yet.
