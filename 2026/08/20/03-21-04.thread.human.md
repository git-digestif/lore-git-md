This thread is about a bugfix for Git's HTTP layer where `WWW-Authenticate` headers are lost during redirects, breaking authentication when the redirect target requires credential helpers (e.g., OAuth2 Bearer tokens). The patch, now at v2, introduces a helper function `credential_update_url()` in `credential.c` to preserve these headers when updating the credential struct for the new URL.

Aaron Plattner authored the patch, which has been reviewed by Junio C Hamano (who requested broader review to avoid the patch being dropped from `seen`) and Jeff King (Peff). Peff confirmed the patch is technically sound but suggested minor improvements: reverting to the v1’s inline approach for clarity and adding a `BUG()` check to enforce that `wwwauth_headers` are never URL-derived. Matthew Cheetham, the original author of the `wwwauth_headers` mechanism, recently weighed in to validate the patch’s core premise, confirming that the headers from the *final* redirect target are correctly preserved. He also provided historical context, noting that broader refactoring of HTTP state management is out of scope for this patch.

The patch is under review and remains in `seen`. While the technical solution is sound and the test coverage (a new Apache rewrite rule in `t/t5563-simple-http-auth.sh`) is thorough, two open questions remain: whether to keep the `credential_update_url()` helper or revert to the v1’s inline approach, and whether to add Peff’s suggested `BUG()` check. The patch’s credibility is strengthened by Matthew’s input, but these unresolved design choices may delay merging. Junio’s earlier warning about the patch being dropped without further review still applies.
