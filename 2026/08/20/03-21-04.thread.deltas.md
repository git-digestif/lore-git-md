## 2026/11/04

- [2026/11/04/06-24-23 by Jeff King <peff@peff.net>] Jeff King reviewed the v2 patch: agreed the problem description makes sense and identified the root cause as wwwauth_headers not belonging in the credential struct (they are response-derived, not request-sent), but judged the patch's approach of preserving them across the URL update correct and safe, since fwrite_wwwauth() resets the list on each new HTTP status line so the retained headers are by definition from the redirect target; he had a slight preference for the v1 inline treatment over the new credential_update_url() wrapper (which he is OK with), and suggested replacing the trailing strvec_clear() with a BUG() check that credential_from_url() did not populate wwwauth_headers; he cc'd Matthew Cheetham as original author of the wwwauth feature.
- [2026/11/04/06-27-01 by Jeff King <peff@peff.net>] Jeff King summarized his review: the patch is OK as-is with a few small code suggestions, and the "why is this safe" reasoning might be worth adding to the commit message; he attributed the lack of earlier review to wwwauth being poorly understood and the original feature author not being cc'd.
- [2026/11/04/09-34-17 by Matthew John Cheetham <mjcheetham@outlook.com>] Matthew John Cheetham confirmed the awkwardness of carrying WWW-Authenticate challenges in the credential struct (convenient because it already held helper state, but mixing URL-derived and response-derived state), noted his exploratory work toward a reusable client with explicit per-request state is not ready to propose and is a broader cleanup rather than a prerequisite, explained the alternative of expanding credential_fill() was rejected because WWW-Authenticate is HTTP-only while credential_fill() also serves SMTP and certificate passwords, and confirmed fwrite_wwwauth() clears the challenge list on each new status line so preserving it across the redirect URL update is the intended behavior.
