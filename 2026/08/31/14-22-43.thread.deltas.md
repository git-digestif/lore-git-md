Ben Knoble clarified that the quoted single-character `@` rule applies to references generally, observed that the porcelain commands create ref `refs/heads/@` (confusing since `@` is shorthand for HEAD, though not against current documented rules), noted that `git switch @` fails as it resolves to HEAD while `git checkout @` succeeds but does not switch branches, and suggested that `--branch` mode could be stricter and perhaps should reject this case.
